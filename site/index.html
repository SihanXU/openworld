Places

Stone by stone

Each of these places was laid out on paper first, after a real one: Sunwell Abbey after the cathedrals of Reims and Chartres, Dusk Moorings after the stilt village of Ganvie. The game's own renderer draws every building in them.

People

The people you meet first

Centuries ago the Long Night swallowed the sun, until the saint Astra bore five holy torments and it rose again. Now the days are growing short once more, and you are sent out on the pilgrimage. These six are the first people you meet. Open one to see their design sheet.

The world

A thousand maps in one browser tab

1,024 maps, 59 of them made by hand. 1,654 people in 780 households, each with a face, a home and a day of their own. All of it runs in your browser, and the maps you leave far behind go to sleep and catch up when you come back.

Connections

One storm at the Ford

Lightning sets the dead tree alight and rain soaks Hesper's campfire. Vigo takes Marta's bread in the confusion, Holt sees him, and a bounty goes up. Each step is one thing's rules answering the last. The map below follows everything that day touched.

How it works

Asked once

Most of what you do already has a rule, and the game just runs it. When you try something no rule covers, the game asks a language model, once. The answer comes back as a new rule; once it passes its checks it joins the library, and whoever meets the same thing after simply runs it.
