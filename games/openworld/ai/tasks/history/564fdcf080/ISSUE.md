# Pointer beside a small sprite meets only the bare ground; key labels unreadable on paper panels; corner buttons draw emoji

## What is wrong

Three play-side problems:

1. **Campfires cannot be built by hand.** When the pointer is on a tile but beside a small sprite (for example a stone lying there), `Controls.layers` returns only the bare ground (the view's stack). The thing standing on the tile is never met. Putting a second stone there therefore lays it beside the first, instead of combining them into a fire ring.
2. **Key labels are unreadable on paper panels.** In the Options tab and the cover's help, key labels use the dark theme's near-white ink on the light paper panels.
3. **The corner buttons (quests, build, bag, skills) draw emoji** rather than pixel icons in the pack's style.

## Expected behaviour

- With a view stack given that does not include a stone standing on the tile (e.g. an empty stack, `stack: []`), a pointer on that tile still meets the stone. Planning the `put` verb with a second stone held at such a tile targets the stone (`{ e: 'stone' }`), and running the plan turns that stone into a `fixture/build/ring`.
- The order of layers at a spot is: what is drawn under the pointer (the view's stack, when given) first, then what stands on the tile (people and creatures, items with the top of a pile first, fixtures), and the ground last. Airborne things (birds) still come last among the entities, as before.
- Without a stack, the result is what stands on the tile, as before.
- Key labels in the Options tab and the cover's help take the paper's ink and are legible on the paper panels.
- The four corner buttons show 16 px pixel icons instead of emoji: the pack's scroll (quests), hammer (build) and bag (bag), and a star made for this project (skills). The key hint on each button and the badges on the quests and skills buttons are kept.
- The star icon is generated by an art script like the project's other art, and is registered in the asset catalogue so that the asset copy step keeps it.

## Requirements

- `Controls.layers(at, stack?)` always includes the entities standing on the tile, after the stack's entries and before the ground.
- Entries must not be duplicated when the same entity is both in the stack and standing on the tile.
- Entries from the stack that are out of the map or out of bounds are still dropped, as before.
- Planning `put` with a second stone onto a tile holding a stone, with an empty stack, targets that stone and produces a fire ring when run.
- Existing control behaviour (the four inputs, one verb each) is unchanged otherwise.
- Key label colour on paper panels uses the paper's ink.
- Corner buttons render `<img>` icons from catalogue entries rather than emoji glyphs; the asset copy keeps the new star.

## Interface

New catalogue entries in `packages/client/src/assets/catalog.ts`, under `CATALOG.ui`:
- `cornerQuests` — `Items/Scroll/ScrollEmpty.png`, 16×16
- `cornerBuild` — `Items/Tool/Hammer.png`, 16×16
- `cornerBag` — `Items/Object/Bag.png`, 16×16
- `cornerSkills` — `lsw/Ui/Star.png`, 16×16

New file `packages/tools/src/art/hud.ts`: a script (run with `npx tsx packages/tools/src/art/hud.ts`) that writes the 16×16 star icon `Ui/Star.png` under `public/assets/lsw/Ui/` using the existing `writeIcon` helper from `./png.ts`. It exports no names.

No new function signatures; `Controls.layers(at: Pos, stack?: Ref[]): Ref[]` keeps its signature with the changed ordering.
