Welcome to the OpenWorld Wiki

OpenWorld is a 2D pixel role-playing game you play right in your web browser. The world's state and rules are code: the game itself is complete and can be played from start to finish; when you do something the world has never seen, the world learns it and remembers (see The World Learns).

You wake on Cloudtop with a little fairy, Lumi, at your side. The days grow shorter one by one, and the Long Night is returning. The Church of the Five Sufferings calls you onto the Pilgrim's Road, to seek out the five Seal-bearers.

This site is also the game's design proposal: what is built is described as it is now, what is decided but not built is marked “Planned”, and what is not decided is marked “To be designed”. · Play the game · Beginner's Guide · Help edit

Recent work

  • Chapter 1 (the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns) can now be played from start to the chapter's end; the chapters of the later four Seals are still to be designed.
  • The Held Ground has become a working camp: Alberic's trading stall and Dawnmarks (see Money), fishing, feeding, milking and breeding, plus ten more crops.
  • Twenty active skills, the skill bar in the lower left (Shift+1–6) and the skill panel (U).
  • The tutorial: one action per lesson, with the keys not yet taught locked.
  • Storeys: each storey builds only its own level; the ceiling is the only exception.

For the full record of changes, see Version History; for the wiki's own changes, see Special:Recent changes.
