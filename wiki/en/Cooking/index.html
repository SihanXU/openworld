Cooking

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Cooking is turning raw food into cooked. The game has no cooking panel and no recipes that combine several ingredients into a dish: raw food that touches fire becomes the matching cooked food. Cooked food restores more Satiety than raw.

How to cook

The usual way is a lit campfire:

  • Hold raw food and right-click a lit fire: “Roast … on the campfire”, “It sizzles. Done.” The one in your hand becomes cooked food.
  • Hold raw food and press G at a lit fire: the same effect; the cooked food stays on you and does not drop into the fire.
  • Any fire that touches raw food cooks it: for instance a fire burning on the ground, or a weapon or staff with the fire element striking it (see Elements).

One at a time. If you have several stacked in your bag, they are cooked one by one.

The fires you can cook on (cooking fires) must be burning:

Cooking fire Where
campfire Build one yourself (Building), or use the ready-made campfires at the camp at The Ford, in Firstlight Village and on Cinder Pass
brazier The lit braziers in The Old Barrow, The Ossuary of the Thorn, The Warden's Sanctum, The Old Mine and The Dragon's Lair
forge At the smith's in Emberfall
cauldron At the herbalist's in Emberfall

In the rules a fireplace can cook too, but no map has a fireplace yet. A campfire is unlit when built and has to be lit with a torch; see Campfire; a cauldron that has gone out can be lit again too. Other lit things (lamp post, candles, lantern post) are not cooking fires: right-clicking one with raw food or pressing G at it does not cook anything.

  • Raw meat and eggs come from Animals; mushrooms are picked up in the deep woodland of the Wild.
  • Fish are caught by Fishing. All eight kinds of fish cook into the same Grilled Fish.
  • Vegetables, bread, berries, milk and the like cannot be cooked; eat them as they are (milk you drink).
  • Eating raw food does no harm; it just does not fill you up as much.
  • Eating restores only satiety, not health directly. Health comes back slowly from being well fed (satiety at 90% or more); see Satiety and Health.

The numbers for every food are in Food.

Do not drop the wrong thing

What you put down with G at a lit fire:

  • Raw food: on a cooking fire (campfire, brazier, fireplace, forge, cauldron) it is cooked and stays on you; on any other fire, it goes by whether it burns, as below.
  • Things that burn (logs, sticks, cut grass, bread…): thrown into the fire and burnt to Ash.
  • Other things (stones, tools…): put on the fire's cell, and they do not burn.

Fishing

Main article: Fishing

Hold a Fishing Rod and left-click water to cast; when something bites, you land a fish. A fishing rod is made from just 2 sticks and 2 cut grass, or bought at the trading stall on The Held Ground; with Bait on it, the chance of a bite doubles. What fish you catch depends on the hour, the weather and the depth of the water.

What there is not

  • A cooking panel, recipes, dishes made from several ingredients.
  • Stewpots, seasonings. The Cooking Pot is an item in the game, but it cannot be used for cooking.
  • Food spoiling.

Tips

  • Steak restores 8 satiety, two fifths of the satiety maximum of 20. Fell a cow and grill two steaks, and that keeps you going most of the day.
  • Grilling meat by a campfire at night is fairly safe: apart from fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 cells of a lit fire.

History

  • 2026-09-30: The game's first version could already grill fish on a campfire.
  • 2026-10-02: Moved onto the new event model: holding raw food and clicking a lit fire, putting raw food into a fire, and any fire touching raw food all cook it.
  • 2026-10-08: Fishing became a pastime, with seven more kinds of fish to cook, all cooking into Grilled Fish.
  • 2026-10-08: A brazier counts as a cooking fire too; a cauldron that has gone out can be lit again; raw food is cooked only on a cooking fire.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
