Options

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

In the game, the Options panel opens with Esc (when no other panel is open), or by clicking the gear button marked Esc at the lower right of the screen. Time stops while the panel is open.

Contents

Item What it does
Language Switches between Chinese and English; the button reads “中文 → English” or “English → 中文”. The interface, dialogue and item names change to the other language at once
Sound On or off. Turned off, there is no music, no ambience and no sound effects
Music, Ambience Two sliders, each its own level, 80% by default. See Music and Ambience
Quest tracker On or off. Turned off, the tracked quest and its objectives are no longer shown under the clock in the top right. See Tracking and markers
Quest markers (map and screen edge) On or off. Turned off, the diamonds on the minimap, the ★ on the world map and the arrows at the edge of the screen are no longer shown
Companion shows the way when you stand still On or off, on by default. On, stand five seconds doing nothing and Lumi flies to where the tracked quest goes next and calls you (see Showing the way)
Save Saves to the current save slot, showing “Saved to slot N”. See Saving
Save & back to title Saves first, then returns to the title page
Mod console (development) Appears only in the development build; opens the Mod Console
AI Grades the AI the way a graphics setting does: a row of presets, five rows each set to “off / low / medium / high”, and below them the connected model and “Detect”. See AI setting
Controls The lower half of the panel is a key table (see The key table in the game)

Notes

  • The first time you open the game, the language is English. The title page's “Settings” also switch the language, the sound and the world filter, change keys and set the AI.
  • Language, sound, the quest tracker, quest markers, the companion showing the way and the AI setting are all kept in the browser and are still there the next time you open the game, independent of saves.
  • There is no volume slider: the music's volume is fixed and can only be turned on or off as a whole.
  • There are no graphics or resolution settings. The AI setting is the only thing graded in levels.
  • Keys are changed under “Change keys…” beside the key table: every key can be changed except Esc and `; the left and right mouse buttons cannot be changed, and there are no preset key schemes (see Rebinding).
  • Difficulty is chosen when you start a new game and cannot be changed in the options afterward. See Difficulty.

Key table

The table is built from your current bindings (“Change keys…” beside its title opens the keys page; see Rebinding). With the default bindings, the table has one row per key: movement; B build (left click or E places, R rotates, Esc or right click leaves); the left click is the held thing's own use (a weapon strikes, a tool works, and a tool struck against a bed, workbench, wall or the like knocks the whole thing loose; empty-handed, the main-hand weapon or your fist; holding anything else, it is used on yourself: eaten, drunk, worn); the right click uses what is in your hand on what you point at (sowing, unlocking, roasting meat, feeding livestock, and any experiment the world has never seen; holding a wall, floor, bed or the like, it builds it there, and holding a floor, R switches to laying a ceiling; empty-handed it does nothing); E the hand (picks up loose things; talks, opens, reads, sleeps, picks ripe crops); G put (puts down what is in your hand, puts it into something, hands it to someone; a bed or the like put down lies loose and is not built); Space attack; Q dash; X draws or sheathes your weapon; V sneak (pressing E on a person while sneaking picks their pocket); Shift+1–6 casts the skill in that slot of the skill bar (on the skill tree: puts a skill into that slot); a long press of E · G or Alt+click chooses among things stacked together (ceilings included); 1–0 and the wheel choose what is in your hand; Shift+Tab switches the toolbar row; Tab · I inventory; then the panel keys: Esc menu, C crafting, K skill tree, U skill panel, M map, L quests, J codex (with the Threadbook); and a last row saying that any key can be changed under “Options → Keys” (the left and right mouse buttons cannot). The development build has one more row, ` Mod Console. For each key in detail, see Controls.

History

  • 2026-09-30: The game has had Chinese–English switching from the start, with the language kept in the browser.

  • 2026-10-03: “Save & back to title” was added, with three save slots managed from the title page.

  • 2026-10-08: The key table was updated to the current version: G put, the left click used on yourself, the skill bar and U were added, and J reads “codex (with the Threadbook)”.

  • 2026-10-09: The “Quest tracker” and “Quest markers (map and screen edge)” switches were added, both on by default.

  • 2026-10-13: The AI setting was added: presets, a level for each of five kinds, and detection of the model with recommended levels.

  • 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).

  • 2026-10-20: Tab now opens the inventory, with a row of tabs above it switching to the other panels (the old keys such as I still work); switching the toolbar row moved to Shift+Tab or Shift+wheel; every key can be changed in Options, with two presets, scheme A and scheme B.

  • 2026-10-28: The “Companion shows the way when you stand still” switch was added, on by default.
