Reeds

Reeds are a clump of reeds growing by the water. They grow in the marsh and on the riverbanks of the Ford and by the stream at Millbrook. They do not block the way and cannot be taken.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Vesper Ford ×2, Dusklea ×3, Hushwood ×8, Thornwood Hollow ×6, The Reed Bend ×65, Millbrook ×3, The Pilgrim's Road ×3, The Waking Glade ×2, The Held Ground, Stillmere ×4
  • The marsh and riverside of the Ford; the stream at Millbrook.
  • They cannot be planted, and they do not grow back by themselves.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

They are one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with them:

  • Does not block the way: people and creatures can walk over them.
  • G: holding something, press G at them and the thing is put on their tile.
  • Hitting: they have no durability; what happens if you hit them with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
  • Fire: they catch fire. After burning for about 4.5 seconds they become one Ash; while burning, every half second they have a 10% chance to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of them.

What happens if you press E with empty hands to handle them is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to them, the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History
