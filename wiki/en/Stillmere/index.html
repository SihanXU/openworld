The Lakeside is a lake beneath the mists of The Held Ground, opened up in Expanding the Held Ground. The lake is large, ringed with sand and reeds, with deep water in its middle. It is the Held Ground's place for fishing. When Alberic opens it, he says: “Listen. Water. I had forgotten what it sounds like.”

The Lakeside is a peaceful zone; no monsters appear at night.

  • Opening it: on the “Grounds” page of Alberic's Held Ground panel, once the land is at level II, put 20 Stone and 10 Plank into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
  • The card says what it holds: a deep lake with pike, eels and carp; reeds and sand along the shore; water for the fields.

Map

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. Most of the north is an oval lake: an outer ring of sand, water within, and a broad stretch of deep water a little north of the middle. The south is grass, with an opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side.

Areas

  • The opening and the sign: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “The Lakeside — deep water: pike in the middle of the lake, eels at night, carp at dawn.”
  • The lake: neither water nor deep water can be walked across. Now and then the shadows of fish rise in the water.
  • Sand and reeds: a ring of sand round the lake, with a few clumps of reeds growing on it.

  • Fishing: hold a Fishing Rod and cast into the water (see Fishing). Pike can be caught only when you cast into deep water, and the middle of this lake is the largest stretch of deep water in the game; there are also Eels at night, Catfish in the rain, and a Golden Carp at any hour. The sign says “carp at dawn”, but the Golden Carp in fact bites at any time of day; the fish that bites only at dawn (5:00–8:00) is the Dawnscale.
  • Water: click the water with an empty bucket to fill a bucket for watering the fields.
  • Ground yield: sticks, stones and cut grass slowly grow on the grass (up to 6, 5 and 6), with a chance of one more of each every 15 seconds.
