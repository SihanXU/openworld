Satiety

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Satiety is shown as ten drumsticks at the upper right of the toolbar, mirroring the hearts on the left. The maximum is 20, 2 points per drumstick, drawn in halves and emptying from left to right. Hover over the drumsticks to see “Satiety current / 20”. Satiety falls over time and is restored by eating. Well fed, you heal; hungry down to 0, you lose health.

A new game starts with 12 satiety, exactly six tenths.

How fast it falls

Satiety falls in two stages, counted in game time (1 game minute = 1 real second):

State Condition Per point lost
Full Six tenths or more (12 or more) 48 game minutes
hungry Below six tenths (under 12) 6 game hours (7.5 times slower)
asleep While sleeping in a bed Another 4 times slower, multiplied with “hungry”
  • After a full meal it takes about six and a half game hours to fall to six tenths, just in time for the next meal. Eat two or three meals a normal day and you stay above six tenths.
  • Below six tenths you become hungry and satiety falls very slowly: from six tenths to 0 takes about 3 game days.
  • Skipping meals, from full to empty takes about 3 game days.
  • Eat back above six tenths and “hungry” goes away.
  • Over a whole night's sleep, someone well fed loses only two or three points.

Eating

  1. Hold the food in your hand (select its slot on the toolbar).
  2. Left-click (using it on yourself) and confirm “Eat X?”. Pointing the mouse at yourself and right-clicking does the same.
  3. Satiety rises by the food's number, never above the maximum.
  • Raw food can be eaten too, with no harm at all; it just restores less than cooked food. Cooking turns raw meat, raw fish, mushrooms and eggs into cooked food.
  • Ordinary food only restores satiety and does not heal. Only an Herb and drinks heal directly; see Health. Of the drinks, only a Bucket of Milk also restores satiety (4 points).
  • A food's tooltip shows a row of small drumsticks and “+N satiety”.

A food that restores 8 in one meal (Steak, Pie) takes you from six tenths back to completely full.

What satiety does

Satiety Effect
Nine tenths or more (18 or more) 1 health every 8 seconds when not at full health
From nine tenths down to 1 No healing, and no loss of health
0 1 health lost every 8 seconds, but never the last point

If hunger starts costing you health while you sleep, you are woken up, with the message “Hunger wakes you.”

Skills

Several skills in the Survival branch have to do with satiety:

Skill Effect
Iron Stomach Hunger is a third slower: 1 point every 72 game minutes when full
Frugal Slower still: 1 point every 96 game minutes when full
Hearty Meals Food restores 50% more satiety
Second Wind Heals once satiety is above half
Regeneration Heals as long as satiety is at least a quarter

Iron Stomach and Frugal slow both stages by the same proportion.

Who has satiety

Satiety is data: any character with a maximum satiety gets hungry and can be fed. For now only the player character has satiety. Villagers and animals have none, so there is no ready rule for right-clicking them with food; it is something the world has to learn. See The World Learns.

Animals have a system of their own: feed them Animal Feed or Wheat and they are “fed” for a while (they can be milked and can breed), but this is not satiety, and they do not go hungry if not fed. See Feeding.

Why two stages

At first satiety fell at a steady rate, about 2 points per game hour, going from full to empty in ten-odd game hours. A design discussion on 2026-10-05 settled the present model: in real life a person goes from completely full to six-tenths full, and one meal brings them back to full; skip meals altogether and they can still last several days, and that is going from full to empty. So it falls quickly while you are full, just in time for the next meal, and slowly once you are hungry, taking days to reach 0.

Tips

  • Eat up to nine tenths or more before you set out, and you will heal slowly on the way.
  • When you are at about six tenths there is no hurry to eat: once hungry it falls very slowly, though you do not heal either.
  • Berries and apples restore little, good for a quick bite; a proper meal is Steak, Pie or Roast Chicken.
  • The tutorial and the start on Cloudtop both give only 12 points; eat a few berries first.

History

  • 2026-10-02: Satiety was added (a drumstick bar after Minecraft), with “well fed heals, starving at 0 costs health”.
  • 2026-10-05: Hunger is 4 times slower while sleeping.
  • 2026-10-05: Satiety now falls at two speeds: above six tenths, 1 point every 48 game minutes; below six tenths you become “hungry” and lose 1 point every 6 game hours; the slowing from sleep and from “hungry” multiply.
  • 2026-10-08: Drinking a Bucket of Milk also restores 4 satiety.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
