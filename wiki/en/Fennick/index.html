Fennick is an old knight living in Firstlight Village, 62 years old. He was once one of the Seal-bearer's guard, carrying the lance under Alberic, the White Knight. Whenever the villagers speak of “the knight”, he falls silent. By day he sits on the bench by the square mending harness.

  • The Old Templar: available after the Church's letter. He will not let anyone who has not survived a night in the wild go up against the Seal-bearer: after dark, defeat 3 zombies outside the village. The reward is “Fennick's Squire Helm” (of Vowbound quality).

  • The Night-born: he is a guard, and on some evenings he (or Holt) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

Guard

Fennick, the old templar, still keeps watch over Firstlight Village with Holt, one of Firstlight Village's guards: he reports any crime he sees on the spot, goes to stop anyone wanted he sees, and runs to anyone who cries for help. He fights. See Crime.

Lore

Fennick is the one in Chapter 1 who knew the White Knight himself. When he was squire, Alberic had already kept watch for two hundred and fifty years. In the scene of his memory he says:

I carried his lance for six years, when I was young. He had worn the crown two hundred and fifty years by then, and still he would stop the whole column to kneel down and talk to children by the road. Knights don't do that. He did. But the crown kept taking. Every year I was with him, there was a little less of him. One morning he looked at me and asked me my name. I left that night. ...Pilgrim, if he asks your name, tell him. Somebody ought to tell him.

See The White Knight Alberic.

Among the niches of The Ossuary of the Thorn is one carved and still empty: “Fennick, squire to the Commander”. The stone is clean; someone has kept sweeping it.

What he does not say (click to show)

He left the guard on the very night Alberic failed to know him. He also knows the Grey Man: after the flame is stolen and he hears the man wore a grey cloak and had ash on his hands, he says “He would not rob a chapel. If it was him, he had a reason, and I do not like to think what it was.”

Trivia

  • After the White Knight falls, he asks only one thing: “At the end, was he still himself?”
  • Once the player has taken on The Old Templar, Alberic at The Held Ground speaks of a squire whose face he cannot recall: “Tell him I am sorry I asked his name.”
