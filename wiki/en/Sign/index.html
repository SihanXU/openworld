The sign is a wooden board standing by the road, with words written on it. It is one of the few things you can reach from a distance: stand within 2 tiles and press E to read it (“Read sign”). There are 6 on the maps; you can also make one yourself, called a “Signpost” in build mode, which comes out blank.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Vesper Ford, Firstlight Village ×5, Hushwood, The Charcoal Dell, Millbrook, Dawn Hill, The Pilgrims' Rest, The Pilgrim's Road ×6, The Kneeling Field, The Sunrise Road, Woundstone Foot, The Waking Glade, The Held Ground, The Oathstone Quarry, The Wardwood, Stillmere, The Mending Springs
  • Build mode: Stick×1 + Plank×1 + Hammer×1
  • Build mode: press B and choose “Signpost”: 1 Stick and 1 Plank, with a hammer (Smith Hammer, not used up) on you.
  • By hand: put a stick on the ground and press G on it holding a plank: “A plank on a stick: a sign post.” Then strike the sign post with a hammer: “You nail up a blank sign.”

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Reading

  • Stand within 2 tiles of the sign and press E, and the words on it are shown.
  • A blank sign with nothing written on it: “It is blank.”

Writing

In the rules, with an Ink Pot on you, hold a Quill and right-click a blank sign: “You write on the sign.” Reading it afterwards gives “Made by hand.” (you cannot type your own text yet; what is written is always this line). No quill or ink pot is placed in the game, so for now you cannot write.

Breaking and fire

  • A sign cannot be taken (“This can't be taken.”).
  • Durability 4, made of wood. Broken, it leaves 1 stick and 1 plank, each with a half chance (half its makeup; see Durability and Breaking).
  • It can catch fire, burning down to a portion of Ash.
  • Press G on it with something in hand to put the thing on the sign's tile.

History

  • 2026-09-30: The sign has been in the game since the first version; a stick and a plank make a post, a hammer nails it into a blank sign, and a quill and ink write on it.
  • 2026-10-03: Signposts can be put up from the build bar.
  • 2026-10-04: A sign can be read from 2 tiles away; gained durability and a makeup (1 stick, 1 plank).
  • 2026-10-08: The three direction signs at Mountain Foot, the Ford and the East Road gained Chinese text.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
