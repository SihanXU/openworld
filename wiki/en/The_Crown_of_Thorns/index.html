The Crown of Thorns is the last main quest of Chapter 1. With The White Knight Alberic fallen, the First Seal is broken and the Crown of Thorns is in your hands: bring it back to Firstlight Village.

The quest begins by itself once The Held Ground is complete; it completes by itself when you are back in Firstlight Village and the homecoming scene has played, with nothing to hand in. On completion the chapter card for Chapter 1 appears, and the next main quest, The Dragon Road, begins at once.

The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the item Crown of Thorns.

Objectives

  1. Bring the Crown home to Firstlight
    after Chapter 1 ends

The objective looks at the event “back in Firstlight Village, with the homecoming scene played”, and does not check whether the Crown of Thorns is still on you.

Walkthrough

  1. The scene of the Crown of Thorns: once you defeat the White Knight on The East Road, the scene of the Crown of Thorns plays, you receive the Crown of Thorns. Straight after, in the briars where the knight fell, you find a thorn that is still green and receive The Oath-Thorn, and the main line turns first to The Held Ground: go into The Held Ground with it, speak to Alberic, and come back. Then this quest begins. Going back to the village before you have been to the Held Ground does not play the homecoming scene.
  2. Back to the village: follow the East Road west, through The Kneeling Field, The Pilgrim's Road and The Pilgrims' Rest (where Father Caius comes to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. …”), back to the far bank of The Ford, across the river, and on west to Firstlight Village. You can also use the World Map (M) to teleport to the waypoint statue in Firstlight Village (if you have woken it).
  3. The homecoming: as soon as you enter Firstlight Village the homecoming scene plays, the quest completes, and the chapter card “Chapter 1: The First Seal” appears. The Dragon Road begins: east to Emberfall.

Rewards

  • Experience +30 (Levels and Experience)

Besides the experience, this stretch also brings:

  • Crown of Thorns: received on defeating the White Knight, head gear of Stigmatic quality (Defense 4, Attack 2, experience −50%; stigma power “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”).
  • Crown's Insight: Lumi's first boon, experience +100%.
  • The Oath-Thorn: for going to and from the Held Ground.
  • The 4 Iron Ore the White Knight drops, and (if you have chosen a profession) a piece of Legendary Arms.

Dialogue

This quest has no dialogue.

The words are all in the scenes.

The homecoming (click to show)

Rowan sees the Crown of Thorns come back to Firstlight Village, yet is furious that the heretic still has the village's fire; Ilse says “Ever since he was small, he has carried things too heavy for him”; Nessa says the sun set four minutes early tonight, “which I'm sure means nothing”. The chapter card reads: “The White Knight fell to his knees, took off the Crown of Thorns and gave it to me. The Grey Man went east with the light. And the night came a little sooner.”
