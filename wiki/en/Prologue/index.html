Prologue

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Story
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The Prologue is the opening of a new game: the protagonist wakes on Cloudtop at the top of the mountain with a fairy, Lumi, newly at their side, then goes down the mountain, passes the five-wound shrine stone at the Mountain Foot, and reaches the Ford. Reaching the Ford ends the Prologue and begins Chapter 1. The Prologue is also the first half of the main quest Down from the Mountain.

The tutorial is not part of the Prologue. The tutorial is entered on its own from the title screen and takes place in The Clearing (its stages are the main quest Waking); walking out of the clearing returns you to the title screen, and it writes no save. See Tutorial.

Walkthrough

  1. Cloudtop: a new game starts on day 1 at 13:00, with the protagonist in the ramshackle sunwatch hut on Cloudtop. Lumi speaks her first line:

    You're awake! I'm Lumi. I'm a fairy, and from today I go where you go. We're on Cloudtop, at the very top of the mountain. The only way down is through the cut in the rocks to the south.

    In front of the hut there are berries, sticks, stones and cut grass. Cloudtop is a peaceful area. On the hut's table lies the log of the Church's Sunwatch Post (the later quest The Watchman's Log has the player come back to read it), and in the hut is a Cloak the watchman left behind, the “Watchman's Cloak” (Fine).

  2. The first waypoint statue: the first time you see a waypoint statue, Lumi teaches you to press E to wake it; afterward you can teleport back to it from the World Map.

  3. The five-wound shrine stone at the Mountain Foot: from the cut in the south you come down to the Mountain Foot, where the path levels out beside an ancient stone standing among flowers. Beside it, Lumi says:

    It's cold near it. I don't like it. I don't know why I don't like it. ...Try doing something to it, anything. Whatever you do, the world will answer.

  4. Try something the world has never seen: the objective “Try something the world has never seen” of the main quest Down from the Mountain. When the player does something to the shrine stone (or anything else) that the game has not defined, the world learns to answer it. Lumi says: “Did you see that? It answered! Whatever the world learns is kept in the Threadbook: press J.” See The World Learns and Threadbook.

  5. Down the mountain to the Ford: the path goes on south, from the south side of the Mountain Foot down the cliff stair to the Ford. On reaching the Ford, Lumi says, “That's the Ford! Someone down there looks like they need help.” The Prologue ends.

Whatever steps the player skipped, the Prologue ends as soon as they reach the Ford.

After reaching the Ford

The Ford's ferrywoman, Hesper, comes to meet you: the bridge was torn down by a rider in white, and the storm snapped the ferry rope; two people from Firstlight Village to the west have gone missing, so help them first. She gives the player a piece of bread. This is the first scene of Chapter 1.

Objectives

The Prologue is the first three objectives of the main quest Down from the Mountain, shown in the quest tracker at the top right and in the quest log (L):

Objective Done when
Find the shrine stone at the Mountain Foot You walk into the ring of flowers around the shrine stone
Try something the world has never seen Having seen the shrine stone at the Mountain Foot, you do, in front of Lumi, something the world has not defined (whether or not a model is connected)
Take the cliff stair down to the Ford You reach the Ford

The fourth objective of Down from the Mountain, “Go west to Firstlight Village”, is already the beginning of Chapter 1.

Lore

Only one piece of lore appears in the Prologue: the five-wound shrine stone. It is a roadside holy mark of the Five Sufferings, carved with five wounds, and the first (the Crown of Thorns) has cracked open. Pell later draws beside it a grey figure with its hand pressed to the stone.

Why Lumi dislikes the shrine stone (click to show)

What has cracked on the shrine stone is the wound of the First Seal. Lumi is the failed Second Vessel; what remains of her still answers to the Seals, so she feels cold near it but cannot say why (docs/chapter-1.md §3b). The design documents also mention fresh ash on the stone: the Grey Man was checking how far the First Seal had failed (docs/chapter-1.md §5).

Notes

  • In the hint for the Prologue's first objective, the shrine stone is called “an old seal stone”; in the game the object's name is “five-wound shrine stone”, and the quest objective reads “Find the shrine stone at the Mountain Foot”.
  • From 2026-10-04 a new game for a time began in the tutorial's clearing; on 2026-10-06 it went back to beginning on Cloudtop, and the tutorial became a separate mode on the title screen.
