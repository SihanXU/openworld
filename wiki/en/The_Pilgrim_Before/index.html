The Pilgrim Before is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Sister Ilse. Long ago a pilgrim made camp deep in the Forest and never came back. Bring his Scallop Badge to Ilse.

The quest only appears once you have found that camp in the Forest: after you walk into the clearing in the Forest's south-east corner, Ilse will ask this of you, and a “!” appears over her head.

Objectives

  1. The pilgrim's scallop
    Carry Pilgrim's Scallop
  2. Back to Sister Ilse
    Talk to Sister Ilse

The Pilgrim's Scallop is not taken when you hand it in; Ilse lets you keep it. What she wants is Brother Cassian's own scallop from the camp: the Sworn Scallop you receive for taking the vow at The Pilgrims' Rest does not count.

Walkthrough

  1. Find the camp: from Firstlight Village go south across the Grassland into the Forest. In the middle of the Forest the path forks, one branch leading to the clearing in the south-east corner, where there is a rotted tent, a cold fire ring, a cairn, and an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree. Walking into the clearing plays a scene.
  2. Pick up the scallop: a scallop lies on the ground beside the cairn; press E to pick it up. It can be worn round the neck straight away (of Vowbound quality).
  3. Take the quest: go back to Firstlight Village, where Sister Ilse spends the day (6:00–22:00) by the sacred fire on the square. Press E on her and choose “Accept ‘The Pilgrim Before’” in the dialogue box. If the scallop is already on you, talk to her once more after accepting to hand it in.
  4. Hand in: press E to hand it in.

The Forest is a combat zone, with night creatures after dark. Once you have found the camp, coming back to it at night (20:00–04:00) shows another scene.

Rewards

  • Experience +40 (Levels and Experience)
  • Healing Potion×2
  • Cassian's Prayer Cord (Vowbound): Amulet, defence 3, level 3, night defence 1, vow “Vow of Dawn”
  • Regard Sister Ilse +20 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

On top of that you keep the Pilgrim's Scallop (Defense 1, experience +10%, movement speed +15%).

Dialogue

When offering
“A camp in Hushwood, with the Church's proclamation nailed up beside it? ...Then a pilgrim slept there. Would you bring me what he left? If he wore the scallop, bring me that.”
While unfinished
“The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Every one of us is given one when we are named.”
On handing in
“Brother Cassian. He sat in this chapel when I was a novice and asked me to pray for him. The Church named him pilgrim thirty-one years ago. ...They told us he reached the Holy City. Keep it, and wear it on the road. He would want that.”
Brother Cassian (click to show)

The proclamation on the tree at the camp matches, almost word for word, the one the courier read out to you, except that it was written in the year 380 of the Era of the Five Sufferings, and the one it names is “Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village”. Ilse recognizes the scallop: when she was still a novice, Brother Cassian sat in the chapel and asked her to pray for him; thirty-one years ago the Church named him the Pilgrim, and later told the village that he had reached the Holy City.

At night a pale shade kneels by the camp's fire ring, its head bowed, and says: “Do not kneel to him.” Then it is gone.
