Thornwood Hollow lies south of the Forest: a valley ringed with bushes and briars, home to plenty of small monsters. A path cuts across the valley from north to southeast, and at the valley floor, between two standing stones, lies the entrance to The Old Barrow. The first objective of Lost in the Barrow is simply to get here.

Thornwood Hollow is an open-air combat zone: there are monsters even by day, and after nightfall night creatures appear as well.

Map

The map is 40×30, with thick woods and bushes all round. The northern exit (columns 12–14) joins the Forest, and the southern exit (columns 26–28) goes down into the Old Barrow. The valley holds three stretches of briar thicket (west, east and south), a patch of dry grass a little north of the middle, and a patch of mud in the southwest.

Areas

  • The eastern briars: where the briars are thickest. Mia lost her grandmother's silver ring somewhere around here (Mia's Ring).
  • The dry-grass slope: a patch of dry grass a little north of the valley's middle, where fire burns fast. Someone has thrown down a pair of fine Thornproof Boots (defense 2) on the slope.
  • The mud: in the southwest; walk into it and you get wet (see Terrain).
  • The barrow entrance: at the valley floor, where two rocks stand at either side of the end of the path, with the southern exit between them. On each side of the entrance is a carved standing stone (a gravestone); press E to read: “Worn carving: a knight with a crown of thorns, and before him a line of small figures, kneeling. Beneath: THE ORDER OF THE THORN HOLDS.”
  • The waypoint statue: north of the barrow entrance stands a waypoint statue (named “Thornwood Hollow”). Once woken, it makes coming out of the barrow, or coming back next time, easy.

  • At the start there are 3 green slimes (mostly in the open), 3 cave spiders (mostly in the briars) and 2 wolves (on either side of the valley). All of them attack on sight.
  • The slimes and spiders come back: while the player is here, a slime may appear every 90 seconds and a spider every 120 seconds, up to 3 of each. The wolves do not return once felled.

  • Clear the Hollow has you fell 6 monsters here; any monster counts.

Lore

The first time you enter Thornwood Hollow, Lumi says the dead down below are sleeping very uneasily, like a cold draft blowing in under a door.

The Knight on the Standing Stones (click to show)

When you reach the barrow entrance, Lumi notices the carvings on the standing stones: a knight in a crown of thorns, with a row of people kneeling before him. The Order of the Thorn is buried below. The Order's roll is in The Ossuary of the Thorn on the barrow's second level, and its last name is the White Knight Alberic.
