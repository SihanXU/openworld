**Affixes** are the random small bonuses on a piece of gear. A piece's numbers come in two parts: its **main stats** come from its form, [[Substances|material]], [[Quality|quality]] and [[Equipment#Levels|level]] (a monster [[Trophies|trophy]]'s main stats are worked out from its level); its **affixes** are rolled the moment it is made, so no two pieces of the same thing are alike.

## What gear has affixes

Things that can be worn or held in the main hand (weapons, armor, clothing, trinkets, off-hand pieces) roll affixes when they are “born”:

- [[Smithing|forged]] at an [[anvil|anvil]], or [[Crafting|crafted]] (by hand or beside a [[workbench|workbench]] alike);
- the [[Trophies|trophies]] monsters drop;
- the goods merchants restock every morning (see [[Trading]]);
- gear laid out on a map.

A thing that already carries affixes never rolls again. The only gear without affixes is the starting weapon handed out when you choose a [[Professions|profession]]. Named gear from quests rolls too (fixed by its name, the same in every game).

## How they are rolled

How many a piece rolls depends on its [[Quality|quality]]: Crude 2, Common 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5, Stigmatic 6; a monster trophy always 4. No two are the same. First one is drawn from the table below by weight (one drawn is not drawn again), then a value is taken at random in its range, on its steps. The ranges grow with the piece's [[Equipment#Levels|level]]: 10% for every level up (close to 3 times at level 20). Some affixes appear only on some slots.

| Affix | Range (level 1) | Step | Weight | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attack | +1–3 | 1 | 0.7 | All |
| Defense | +1–3 | 1 | 1 | All |
| Critical chance | +1%–5% | 1% | 1 | All |
| Critical damage | +5%–20% | 5% | 1 | All |
| Dodge | +1%–4% | 1% | 1 | All |
| Health per hit | +1 | — | 0.3 | All |
| Cooldown reduction | +2%–6% | 1% | 1 | All |
| Experience | +3%–10% | 1% | 0.8 | All |
| Luck | +3%–10% | 1% | 0.8 | All |
| Movement speed | +2%–6% | 1% | 1 | Feet, waist, shoulders, body, ear |
| Attack speed | +2%–6% | 1% | 1 | Main hand, hands, ring, ear, off hand |

The weights are relative: an affix of weight 1 is twice as common as one of weight 0.5. So attack and health per hit are rarer than defense, critical chance and the like. Boots cannot roll attack speed, and rings cannot roll movement speed.

**Luck**: raises the chance that monsters drop rare materials and trophies; see [[Karma and Faith#Luck|Luck]].

## When they count

- **Worn**: each affix is added to the wearer just like the main stats (defense reduces damage, critical chance adds criticals…).
- **Weapons**: a weapon worn in the main hand or held in the hand counts its affixes too. If the weapon held in the hand is not the main-hand one, the affixes of both count.
- Gear that only sits in the bag does not count its affixes.

## Where to see them

Hover over a piece of gear: the [[Interface#Tooltips|tooltip]] has a section “Affixes:”, one line each, written in blue, such as “+3% Critical Chance”. The numbers at the bottom of the [[Inventory#Character sheet|character panel]] include the affixes.

## Tips

- Make a few more of the same thing, or fell a few more monsters, and pick the piece whose affixes suit you best.
- For attack speed, look at the main hand, gloves, rings, earrings and the off hand; for movement speed, look at boots, belts, mantles, armor and earrings.

## History

- 2026-10-12: Random affixes added: every piece rolls 4 when it is born, with slot limits on attack speed and movement speed.
- 2026-10-12: The stealth, guard reduction and perception affixes removed and replaced by luck (old saves converted automatically).
- 2026-10-13: The count now depends on quality (2 to 6), the ranges grow with the piece's level; named gear from quests rolls affixes too.
