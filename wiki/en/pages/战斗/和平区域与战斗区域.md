Every map is either a **peaceful zone** or a **combat zone**. Below the minimap it says which the current map is: “Peaceful zone”, or “Combat zone · monsters at night”.

The only difference between them: **at night, [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] (zombies and bats) rise on the open-air maps of combat zones**, and not in peaceful zones.

## Where monsters rise at night

Night creatures appear only on maps that are **combat zones** and **open-air**. Dark or indoor maps such as caves, cellars and chapels are combat zones too, but no night creatures rise there at night; they have monsters of their own, which slowly come back.

| Night creatures rise at night | Combat zone, but none rise |
|---|---|
| The Clearing, Mountain Foot, The Grassland, The Forest, Thornwood Hollow, Dawn Hill, The Pilgrim's Road, Abbey of the Thorn, The Kneeling Field, The East Road, Cinder Pass, the Wild | The Old Barrow, The Ossuary of the Thorn, The Warden's Sanctum, The Mill Cellar, Chapel of the First Dawn, Chapel of the Five, The Pilgrims' Hostel, The Chapter Hall, The Abbey Crypt, The Old Mine, The Dragon's Lair |

## Peaceful is not safe

A peaceful zone only means no new monsters rise at night; every other rule is the same:

- The monsters placed on the map from the start are still there. [[The Ford|The Ford]], for example, is a peaceful zone, yet it has a fire slime and a frost slime, and the frost slime attacks of its own accord at night (see [[Factions and Temper]]).
- Striking people, being struck and [[Elements|setting fires]] all work as in a combat zone.

Nor is a combat zone dangerous everywhere: by day there are no night creatures, only the map's own monsters.

## Saving

The autosave does not save when you are “in danger”: hurt within the last 15 seconds, or a hostile creature within 8 cells. This has nothing to do with the zone, only with what is around you. See [[Saving]].

## Tips

- Get back to a peaceful zone before dark, or stay indoors with the door shut in a combat zone (see [[Night Creatures]]).
- The tutorial's [[The Clearing|Clearing]] is a combat zone: zombies and bats come on the very first night, so build your shelter first.

## History

- 2026-10-03: Peaceful and combat zones added: no monsters rise at night in towns and camps, and the current zone is shown below the minimap.
- 2026-10-04: The monsters that rise at night became the world's one “appearing” rule, appearing only on open-air maps of combat zones (W7).
- 2026-10-12: The Pilgrim's Road line added: The Pilgrims' Rest is a peaceful zone; The Pilgrim's Road, Abbey of the Thorn and The Kneeling Field are open-air combat zones, and Chapel of the Five, The Pilgrims' Hostel, The Chapter Hall and The Abbey Crypt are combat zones where no night creatures rise.
