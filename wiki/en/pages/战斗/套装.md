A **set** is a few pieces of gear that belong together. The more pieces of one set you wear, the more bonuses you gain on top. The game's sets come from two sources: the outfit each [[职业|profession]] [[锻造|forges]] at an [[铁砧|anvil]], and the [[专属掉落|trophies]] of one family of monsters.

## Rules

Sets follow those of *Dungeon & Fighter* (DNF), and come in two kinds:

| Set | Pieces | Slots | Bonus tiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Armor set** | 6 | Head, shoulders, body, hands, belt, feet | At 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn |
| **Trinket set** | 3 | Necklace, earring, ring | At 2 and 3 pieces worn |

- **Pieces are counted by slot**: of one set, each slot counts only once. Wear a ring of the same set on each hand and it counts as 1 piece.
- **Bonuses add up**: with 4 pieces worn, you have the bonuses of both the 2-piece and the 4-piece tier.
- **The main hand and off hand are in no set**: weapons and off-hand pieces stand alone, with only their own numbers.
- Only what is worn counts; what sits in the bag does not.
- Hover over a piece of a set and the bottom of the [[界面#提示框|tooltip]] says “◆ Set name Set (pieces worn/total pieces)”, with one tier of bonuses on each line below: green for tiers reached, grey for those not yet.

## Profession sets

Each profession has a forged outfit in its own style, covering the whole body in 11 pieces (two rings can be worn):

- Head, shoulders, body, hands, belt and feet make up the profession's **armor set**; necklace, earring and ring make up its **trinket set**.
- The main hand and off hand are the profession's own gear (the off hand: the Warrior's shield, the Assassin's {{物品|格挡短刃}}, the Ranger's {{物品|箭袋}}, the Mage's {{物品|法典}}), and are in no set. Anyone can wear the other slots.
- All are forged beside an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} in hand; every smithy has an anvil (see [[锻造#铁匠铺|Smithy]]). The first material decides its [[材质|material]] (a jerkin made with leather is leather, an Oathguard piece made with steel bars is steel), and your smithing level and smithing skills decide its [[品质|quality]]. Trinkets (earrings, necklaces, rings, the Starweave Circlet) need {{技能|珠宝匠}} first.
- What you forge has a name of its own (“Shadowfang Jerkin”, “Oathguard Bulwark”); besides its form's main stats, each carries a small bonus of its own (“Own” in the tables below), and it rolls [[词条|affixes]] when it is born all the same (how many depends on its [[品质|quality]]).

## Monster sets

The [[专属掉落|trophies]] of one family of monsters make up a set, under the same rules as the profession sets. For which monster drops each piece and how often, see [[专属掉落#全部专属装备|All trophies]].

|---|---|---|---|
| Gelatin Armour | Armor (6) | Crown of the Slime King, Tideslick Cloak, Gelhide Vest, Emberjelly Gloves, Sparkcord Sash, Rimejelly Boots | 2 pieces, steadfast +10%; 4 pieces, defense +2, movement speed +5%; 6 pieces, each hit draws 1 health, steadfast +15% |
| Wild Hunt Armour | Armor (6) | Frostfang Hood, Wolfpelt Mantle, Carapace Plate, Silkspinner Gloves, Tusked Belt, Batwing Slippers | 2 pieces, attack speed +5%; 4 pieces, critical chance +5%, dodge +3%; 6 pieces, attack +3, critical damage +25% |

| Elemental Court Trinkets | Trinkets (3) | Rimeheart Amulet, Stormspark Earring, Imp's Ember Ring | 2 pieces, cooldown reduction 8%; 3 pieces, attack +2, cooldown reduction 8%, critical chance +4% |
| Wyrmblood Trinkets | Trinkets (3) | The Cyclops' Eye, Cindertear Earring, Whelpfang Ring | 2 pieces, attack +2, defense +2; 3 pieces, attack +4, critical damage +30%, each hit draws 1 health |

**Steadfast**: damage taken while standing still is cut by this much. [[古墓守卫之盔]] and [[石心胸甲]] stand alone, in no set.

## Tips

- Two sets can be mixed: wear Shadowfang Armour on the body and Barrow-born Trinkets as jewelry, for example, and you have the bonuses of both.
- The 2-piece tier of an armor set is the easiest to gather: forge the head and body first, or the head and feet.
- A profession set's main hand and off hand belong to that profession alone: another profession cannot swing them, and an off-hand piece does nothing when worn (see [[装备#职业专属|Profession-only]]).

## History

- 2026-10-11: Four profession sets added (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave), forged at an anvil; at first each set had 12 pieces covering the whole body, with a tier of bonuses at 3, 6, 9 and 12 pieces worn.
- 2026-10-12: Following DNF, sets were split into armor sets (6 pieces, bonuses at 2/4/6) and trinket sets (3 pieces, bonuses at 2/3), and the main hand and off hand no longer count toward a set; pieces are counted by slot, two pieces in the same slot counting once. Five monster sets added.
