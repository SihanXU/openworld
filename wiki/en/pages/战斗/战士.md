The Warrior is one of the four [[职业|professions]], and masters the {{物品|剑}} and the {{物品|盾牌}}. The Warrior stands in front and takes the blows: damage cut, more health, a hard sword stroke back for every hit taken, and fiercer with every enemy felled. Choose it from {{人物|布拉姆}} in [[初原村]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become a Warrior, Bram gives you:
- an **Iron Sword**: power 6, range 1, no element.
- a **Wooden Shield**: defense 2, worn in the off hand. If the off hand is empty, it is put on at once.

Swords (and sabres) and shields are the Warrior's profession weapons: only a Warrior can fight with a sword or wear a shield, and the Warrior's attack talents work only with swords.

## Shields

- A shield is worn in the off hand (the “Off hand” slot of the equipment), and the weapon held in the hand is unaffected.
- A worn shield adds its defense to you: the Wooden Shield +2.
- Some shields also have a block chance: when struck, a chance to block the whole blow (“Blocked!”), at most 50%. Ordinary shields have no block chance; the Legendary shield The Unfallen Wall has 15%.
- A shield can be made beside a [[工作台|workbench]] from 3 planks and 1 branch, but only a Warrior can wear it (“Only a Warrior can wear X.”).

## Legendary Arms

The Warrior's two Legendary weapons are {{物品|誓约之刃}} (a sword) and {{物品|不陷之壁}} (a shield). When a Warrior fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see [[传说武器]], [[首领]]).

## How to play

- **Take one, give one back**: the sword stroke within 2 seconds of being hit gets +40%. With Warlord learned, that stroke also heals 2. So the Warrior does not fear being hit first.
- **Fell them in a row**: Rampage stacks up to 3 times, +15% each, lasting 5 seconds. Against a pack, they fall faster and faster.
- **Bulwark is always on**: whatever you hold, you take 15% less (30% with Warlord).
- The sword's base power is high (6 for the Iron Sword), and the shared branch's {{技能|横扫}} cuts both sides at once, well suited to a Warrior facing a crowd.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]], the Warrior is level 20: health 174, attack 6, defense 2 (plus the Wooden Shield's +2), holding an Iron Sword, with all twenty-two Warrior skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with {{键|空格}}, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned. The most health of the four professions.
