The Warrior is one of the four [[Professions|professions]], and masters the {{item|Sword}} and the {{item|Shield}}. The Warrior stands in front and takes the blows: damage cut, more health, a hard sword stroke back for every hit taken, and fiercer with every enemy felled. Choose it from {{person|Bram}} in [[Firstlight Village]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become a Warrior, Bram gives you:
- an **Iron Sword**: power 6, range 1, no element.
- a **Wooden Shield**: defense 2, worn in the off hand. If the off hand is empty, it is put on at once.

Swords (and sabres) and shields are the Warrior's profession weapons: only a Warrior can fight with a sword or wear a shield, and the Warrior's attack talents work only with swords.

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}} (see [[Skills#Active skills and the skill bar|Active skills and the skill bar]]). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are a Warrior.

## Shields

- A shield is worn in the off hand (the “Off hand” slot of the equipment), and the weapon held in the hand is unaffected.
- A worn shield adds its defense to you: the Wooden Shield +2.
- Some shields also have a block chance: when struck, a chance to block the whole blow (“Blocked!”), at most 50%. Ordinary shields have no block chance; the Legendary shield The Unfallen Wall has 15%.
- A shield can be made beside a [[workbench|workbench]] from 3 planks and 1 branch, but only a Warrior can wear it (“Only a Warrior can wear X.”).

## Legendary Arms

The Warrior's two Legendary weapons are {{item|Oathbrand}} (a sword) and {{item|The Unfallen Wall}} (a shield). When a Warrior fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see [[Legendary Arms]], [[Bosses]]).

## How to play

- **Take one, give one back**: the sword stroke within 2 seconds of being hit gets +40%. With Warlord learned, that stroke also heals 2. So the Warrior does not fear being hit first.
- **Fell them in a row**: Rampage stacks up to 3 times, +15% each, lasting 5 seconds. Against a pack, they fall faster and faster.
- **Bulwark is always on**: whatever you hold, you take 15% less (30% with Warlord).
- The sword's base power is high (6 for the Iron Sword), and the shared branch's {{skill|Sweep}} cuts both sides at once, well suited to a Warrior facing a crowd.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]], the Warrior is level 20: health 174, attack 6, defense 2 (plus the Wooden Shield's +2), holding an Iron Sword, with all twenty-two Warrior skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with {{key|Space}}, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned. The most health of the four professions.
