The Mage is one of the four [[职业|professions]], and masters the {{物品|法杖}}. The Mage casts spells from a few cells away: an elemental staff deals more damage, every fourth spell is a sure critical, you take fewer blows while holding a staff, and felling an enemy with a spell heals you. Choose it from {{人物|布拉姆}} in [[初原村]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become a Mage, Bram gives you a **Wooden Staff**: power 4, range 4, no element.

The staff is the Mage's profession weapon: only a Mage can fight with it, and the Mage's talents work only with staves.

**The wand is not the Mage's weapon.** The {{物品|魔杖}} is a common weapon: anyone can use it, its range is 5, but it takes no Mage talent.

## How to cast

- Press Space and the spell flies the way you face, hitting the first person, the first creature or the first thing in the way along that row (or column).
- When you click a distant target with the left button, it must be in the same row or column as you, and within range.
- With an elemental staff, the target hit reacts to the element (see [[元素]]): fire sets it burning, ice freezes what is wet, lightning shocks what is wet.

## Legendary Arms

The Mage's two Legendary weapons are {{物品|烬心法杖}} (fire) and {{物品|霜冠法杖}} (ice), both elemental, with a longer range than the Wooden Staff. When a Mage fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see [[传说武器]], [[首领]]).

## How to play

- **Keep your distance**: the Wooden Staff's range is 4, 5 with Archmage. Do not let enemies get in your face.
- **Water first, then lightning; wet first, then freeze**: elemental reactions can turn one spell into a whole area (see [[元素]]).
- **Elemental Attunement needs an elemental staff**: the starting Wooden Staff has no element, so this skill does nothing with it; wait until you have an elemental staff (a Legendary staff, for example).
- **Spell Echo counts by itself**: every 4 hits, the 4th is a sure critical; you need not keep to the same target.
- Mana Shield works only while you hold a staff in your hand; switch to anything else and it is gone.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]], the Mage is level 20: health 94, attack 5, defense 1, holding the “Challenge Ember Staff” (a Wooden Staff of fire), with all twenty-two Mage skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with {{键|空格}}, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned. The least health of the four professions.
