Every kind of monster is made of certain things, and when you fell it, it falls apart into those things: a wolf leaves hide and meat, a skeleton leaves bones, a golem leaves stones and ore. They lie where it fell and are not drawn to you by themselves; press {{key|E}} to pick them up.

The amount is half of the materials in its body (at least 1), by the same rule as breaking a stone or felling a tree (see [[Durability and Breaking]]). Each kind of monster also has a small chance to drop its own [[Trophies|trophy]] gear.
