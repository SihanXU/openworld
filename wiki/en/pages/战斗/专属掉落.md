**Trophies** are the gear monsters drop. Every kind of monster has one **trophy** piece that belongs to it (one piece, not a set). Each time you fell one, there is a chance that piece appears straight in the bag of whoever felled it.

## Rules

- **Who gets it**: whoever fells it (the last blow). The piece never lands on the ground; it goes straight into the bag, while an icon flies into the bag on screen; when the player gets one, the lower left says “A trophy: X!”.
- **Drop rate**: 4% for each ordinary monster, 10% for the stronger ones, and certain for the boss-like monsters ([[史莱姆王]], [[独眼巨人]], [[烬峰魔龙]], [[古墓守卫]]).
- **Pity**: if 49 of one kind of monster are felled in a row without the piece dropping, the 50th surely drops it. Each person keeps their own count for each kind of monster; once it drops, the count for that kind starts over.
- **Main stats come from the level**: every trophy has its own [[装备#等级|level]] (the stronger the monster, the higher the level), and its defense and attack are worked out from the level, not from its [[材质|material]] or [[品质|quality]]: a piece of a set counts as Fine (×1.25), a standalone piece as Masterwork (×1.5).
- **Random affixes**: when it drops it rolls 4 [[词条|affixes]] all the same, so the same trophy is different every time it drops.
- **Only from monsters**: it cannot be [[锻造|forged]], and no merchant sells it.
- **Tooltip**: hover over it and it reads “✧ Trophy · dropped by X”.
- Each monster's page names its trophy and drop rate in its “Drops” section.

## Sets

The trophies of one family of monsters make up a [[套装|set]]: a six-piece armor set (head, shoulders, body, hands, belt, feet), or a three-piece trinket set (necklace, earring, ring). Two pieces belong to no set; they stand alone, and their main stats are higher than those of any one piece of a set. For each set's bonuses, see [[套装#怪物套装|monster sets]].

## Not yet obtainable

No map, respawn or story beat yet puts out [[史莱姆王]], [[水史莱姆]], [[雷电史莱姆]], [[巨蟹]], [[冰魔像]] or [[风暴鬼火]], so their trophies cannot be had for now. So at present you can gather at most 3 pieces of the Gelatin Armour, at most 5 of the Wild Hunt Armour, and at most 1 of the Elemental Court Trinkets.

## Tips

- A 4% drop rate gives one piece every 25 kills on average; if your luck is bad, pity makes the 50th certain.
- There is only one each of the [[古墓守卫]], the [[独眼巨人]] and the [[烬峰魔龙]], and each surely drops its piece when felled. The [[幼龙|dragon whelp]] appears only in the dragon fight.
- When gathering a set only the slot counts: two rings of the same set count as one piece; see [[套装]].
