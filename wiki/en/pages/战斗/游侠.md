The Ranger is one of the four [[职业|professions]], and masters the {{物品|弓}}. The Ranger shoots from afar: more damage when standing firm, the farther the more it hurts, and faster on the feet after a hit. Choose it from {{人物|布拉姆}} in [[初原村]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become a Ranger, Bram gives you a **Wooden Bow**: power 2, range 6, no element.

The bow is the Ranger's profession weapon: only a Ranger can fight with it, and the Ranger's talents work only with bows. You can also make a Wooden Bow yourself beside a [[工作台|workbench]] (2 branches, 3 bundles of hay, recipe level 2).

## How to shoot

- Press Space and the arrow flies the way you face, hitting the first person, the first creature or the first thing in the way along that row (or column).
- When you click a distant target with the left button, it must be in the same row or column as you, and within range.
- Range 6 cells; 7 with {{技能|箭术}} learned.

## Legendary Arms

The Ranger's two Legendary weapons are {{物品|疾风之弦}} and {{物品|坠星}}, both bows, with a longer range than an ordinary bow. When a Ranger fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see [[传说武器]], [[首领]]).

## How to play

- **Stand firm first**: stop for 1 second before shooting, +30% damage; 3 cells or more from the target, another +25%. The two add together.
- **Shoot and go**: for 1.5 seconds after a hit you run 20% faster; use it to open the distance, then stop and shoot again.
- **Keep to one**: Hunter's Mark wants three arrows in a row on the same target, and the third is a sure critical. Shoot something else midway and the count starts over.
- The Wooden Bow's power is only 2, so damage is low early on; you rely on Archery and the bonuses for standing still.
- An enemy can be hit only once it is in the same row or column, so line up first as you move.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]], the Ranger is level 20: health 104, attack 5, defense 1, holding a Wooden Bow (range 7), with all twenty-two Ranger skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with {{键|空格}}, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned.
