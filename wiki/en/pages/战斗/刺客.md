The Assassin is one of the four [[Professions|professions]], and masters the {{item|Dagger}}. The Assassin's way of fighting is timing: the first blow after a quiet spell, a blow from behind, the third blow on the same target, all deal much more damage. Choose it from {{person|Bram}} in [[Firstlight Village]] from level 5.

## Starting gear

The first time you become an Assassin, Bram gives you an **Iron Dagger**: power 3, range 1, no element.

The dagger is the Assassin's profession weapon: only an Assassin can fight with it, and the Assassin's talents work only with daggers. Fight with an axe, a mace or another common weapon and none of the Assassin's talents count.

## Skills

The Assassin's first six talents are in the profession branch of the skill tree ({{key|K}}). The root is {{skill|Short-Blade Training}}; two branches lead outward from it, ending in a 2-point ultimate. All of them work only **with a dagger**; Ambush, Relentless, Backstab and Death Dance count only the main target of a melee blow, not the neighbors caught by {{skill|Sweep}}.

| Skill | Requires | Points | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{skill|Short-Blade Training}} | | 1 | Attacks with a dagger gain +1 power. |
| {{skill|Ambush}} | Short-Blade Training | 1 | After 4 seconds without attacking or taking combat damage, the next blow deals +50% damage. This is a gathering of strength, not invisibility. |
| {{skill|Relentless}} | Ambush | 1 | The 3rd blow on the same target deals +60% damage. No more than 2 seconds between blows; changing target, missing or a ranged attack breaks the chain. |
| {{skill|Backstab}} | Short-Blade Training | 1 | A blow from behind the target deals +40% damage. Judged by the way the target faces; the sides and front do not count. |
| {{skill|Shadow Recovery}} | Backstab | 1 | Felling a creature with a dagger restores 10% of your maximum health (rounded up). |
| {{skill|Death Dance}} | Relentless, Shadow Recovery | 2 | A blow that triggers Ambush or Backstab is always a critical hit. |

### Active skills

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}} (see [[Skills#Active skills and the skill bar|Active skills and the skill bar]]). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are an Assassin.

| Skill | Requires | Points | Cooldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {{skill|Shadow Strike}} | Short-Blade Training | 1 | 10 s | Flash behind the nearest enemy within 4 cells and strike (1.5× damage, can trigger Backstab). |
| {{skill|Envenom}} | Short-Blade Training | 1 | 20 s | Your next 5 attacks make the target bleed (1 per second for 4 seconds). |
| {{skill|Smoke Bomb}} | Shadow Strike | 1 | 25 s | For 3 seconds monsters cannot see you and will not attack you. |
| {{skill|Flurry}} | Envenom | 1 | 8 s | Stab the cell ahead 4 times in quick succession (0.6× damage each). |

Learning all twenty-two (ten talents, five skill upgrades, seven active skills) takes 25 points. For the skill tree's rules, see [[Skills]].

## Legendary Arms

The Assassin's two Legendary weapons are {{item|Nightfang}} and {{item|Mirrorshard}}, both daggers. When an Assassin fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see [[Legendary Arms]], [[Bosses]]).

## How to play

- **The opening blow**: hold off for 4 seconds before closing in, and the first blow takes Ambush's full +50%. With Death Dance learned, that blow is also a sure critical.
- **Going round the back**: strike from the side opposite the way the enemy faces. An enemy chasing the player always faces the player, so a backstab is hard; an enemy busy fighting someone else, or one that has just turned, is the easiest prey.
- **The chain of three**: strike the same target three times in a row, and the third blow gets +60%. Do not change target midway.
- **Healing**: Shadow Recovery heals a little every time you fell something with a dagger, good for clearing a pack of small monsters in a row. Fell with another weapon (an axe, bare hands) and you heal nothing.
- The dagger's power is low (3 for the Iron Dagger); the Assassin lives on bonuses. Critical skills of the shared branches such as {{skill|Keen Eye}} and {{skill|Lethal Edge}} suit the Assassin well.

## Boss Challenge

In the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]], the Assassin is level 20: health 114, attack 6, defense 1, holding an Iron Dagger, with all twenty-two Assassin skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with {{key|Space}}, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned.

- 2026-10-08: Shadow Recovery heals only when you fell something with a dagger, like the other Assassin talents.
