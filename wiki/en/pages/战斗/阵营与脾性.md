Who attacks whom in the game does not depend on “is it the player”, but on three things written on every creature: which **faction** it belongs to, what its **temper** is, and who its **foes** are. One set of rules serves the player, the residents and Lumi alike: a monster that attacks the player also attacks a villager walking by.

## Factions

| Faction | Who | Hostile to |
|---|---|---|
| The people | The player, the residents everywhere | No one |
| The wild things | Every monster and animal, bosses included | The people |

- The wild things are hostile to the people; the people are hostile to no one and do not go after monsters of their own accord.
- “Hostile” only means it may strike. Whether it actually does depends on its temper.
- [[Lumi|Lumi]] is a companion and belongs to no faction: she does not fight, and monsters do not fight her.

## Tempers

| Temper | When it strikes | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Aggressive | Goes for any hostile faction it sees | The slimes and spiders of [[Thornwood Hollow]], the wolves of [[The East Road]], the [[Night Creatures|night creatures]], bosses, the slimes, wolves and boars of [[The Wild|the Wild]] |
| Nocturnal | Strikes of its own accord only at night; by day it acts like a wary one | The frost slime of [[The Ford]] |
| Wary | Does not start a fight, but strikes back when struck | The fire slime of [[The Ford]], the boars of [[The East Road]], the chickens of the Wild (a chicken has no attack, so its striking back costs no health) |
| Gentle | Never strikes; flees when struck | The [[Animals|cows and chickens]] of [[The Grassland]], [[The Held Ground]] and [[Emberfall]] |

(The examples are those placed on each map. The same kind of monster can have a different temper in different places.)

## Foes

- **Whoever strikes it becomes its foe**, and from then on it goes for that person on sight (a gentle animal flees on sight). However good its temper.
- This is how a wary monster is provoked: whoever strikes first is chased and struck.

## Sight and pursuit

- A creature strikes only what it can see: the nearest one within its sight (counted in steps over cells).
- It does not chase too far from its own ground: beyond a certain distance (usually 8 cells) it gives up and goes back.
- Some creatures keep to different places by day and by night; the Ford's two slimes, for example, move elsewhere at night.
- With no one to strike, it wanders about its ground, stopping and going.
- No one who is down is struck.

## Fear of fire

Apart from fire monsters, every creature that moves of its own accord fears fire:

- It never comes within 2 cells of a lit fire or anything burning.
- It does not chase anyone standing within 2 cells of a fire.

So a lit [[campfire|campfire]] is a safe place at night. Fire slimes, fire imps, dragon whelps and the other fire creatures do not fear it.

## Health bar colors

The health bar over a creature's head is colored by its attitude to the player:

- **Red**: will attack the player now (hostile and of a temper that strikes at this moment, or already taking the player for a foe).
- **Green**: people and companions.
- **Yellow**: everything else: monsters and animals that will not attack the player of their own accord, and struck trees, walls and other such things.

A nocturnal monster is yellow by day and turns red as soon as night falls.

## Tips

- Leave a monster with a yellow health bar alone and it will not strike; strike it and it turns red.
- When pressed hard in a chase, stand beside a lit fire; most monsters stop 2 cells off.
- Monsters go after residents too. Residents cannot be killed; they only go down for a while.
