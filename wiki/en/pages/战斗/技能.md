Skills are abilities learned with skill points. Every level up gives 1 skill point (see [[Levels and Experience]]); press {{key|K}} to open the skill tree, and click a skill you can learn to learn it. The first time you have a point unspent, [[Lumi|Lumi]] begins the guide quest [[The First Skill Point]] to remind you. Most skills (talents) are always in effect, working by themselves, with no key of their own; {{skill|Dash}} is on {{key|Q}}. There are also **active skills** you cast yourself (4 shared, 7 for each profession): they go on the skill bar in the lower left, are cast with {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}}, and must cool down after use; {{key|U}} opens the skills panel to manage them (see [[#Active skills and the skill bar|Active skills and the skill bar]] below).

## The skill tree

Press {{key|K}} (or the button in the lower right) to open it.

- **Two directions**: the page switches between “Combat” and “Life”. Under Combat are four branches, Swiftness, Survival, Damage and Profession; under Life, Gathering, Agriculture, Animal Husbandry and Smithing. Both directions share the same skill points.
- **Growing outward in four directions from the center**: the center is you, showing your current level; the four branches reach out left, right, up and down (Combat: Swiftness left, Survival right, Damage up, Profession down), each a panel in its own color.
- **Laid out in tiers**: the skills of each branch are set in rows by depth, deeper the farther out; a skill to be learned first is always in the tier before, and the lines run straight across and down. An ultimate that needs two branches learned is set one tier further out, right between those two branches.
- **Branch tabs**: a row of tabs under the direction cards: “All” shows the whole tree, and clicking a branch (or the icon at that branch's center) zooms into that branch's panel.
- Drag to look around, the wheel or the −, + buttons zoom, and “Centre” returns to the whole tree.
- Hover over a skill to see what it does and whether you can learn it:
  - “Learned”;
  - “Needs X”: a skill before it is not yet learned;
  - “Learn · 1 skill point”: click to learn it;
  - “No skill points: level up to earn one”.
- Skills marked “Ability” are effects that trigger under conditions (such as “within 0.6 seconds of a successful move”); the rest are always added to your numbers (such as attack +1).
- Skills marked “Active skill” must be cast yourself. Once learned, hovering shows the tip below: “Active: hover and press Shift+1~6 to set it in the skill bar”.
- The bar at the top shows how many skill points you have.

### The rules of learning

- An ordinary skill costs 1 point. Each branch's **ultimate** costs 2, and needs the two branches before it learned first.
- A skill whose predecessor is not learned cannot be learned.
- What is learned cannot be unlearned. The only exception is changing [[Professions|profession]]: every point of the profession branch comes back, and the other branches are untouched.
- A skill that raises maximum health is full in its extra part the moment it is learned.

Learning everything takes 165 points, that is, level 166. Active skills cost 1 point each; the profession's two ultimates (the ultimate active skill, the ultimate talent) cost 2 each.

## Swiftness

Move fast, strike fast, get out of the way. The root is {{skill|Light Footed}} (movement speed +15%), branching out four ways:

- {{skill|Quick Hands}} → {{skill|Battle Tempo}}: faster attacks, faster still just after a step.
- {{skill|Evasive Step}} → {{skill|Pursuit}}: take fewer blows just after a step; run faster after felling an enemy. Together the two lead to the ultimate {{skill|Windrunner}}: every 3 cells walked, the next melee blow deals +50% damage.
- {{skill|Nimble}} → {{skill|Acrobat}}: dodge, a whole attack missing.
- {{skill|Dash}}: press {{key|Q}} to dash up to 3 cells ahead, cooldown 4 seconds. After it come {{skill|Slipstream}} → {{skill|Tailwind}} (run faster after a dash, dash farther, shorter cooldown) and {{skill|Phantom Step}} (pass through creatures, unharmed for 0.5 seconds after a dash). Acrobat and Phantom Step together lead to the ultimate {{skill|Untouchable}}: the next blow after a dodge is a sure critical.

- The active skill {{skill|Gale Step}}: can be learned once Light Footed is learned; movement speed +50% for 5 seconds, cooldown 20 seconds. It is not the dash: the dash is still on {{key|Q}}, while Gale Step goes on the skill bar.

A “successful move” means an actual step taken: walking into a wall, turning on the spot or teleporting do not count. Movement and attack speed bonuses go up to at most 50%.

## Survival

Take more blows, go hungry slower, heal more, and a few lifesavers. The root is {{skill|Vitality}} (maximum health +4):

- {{skill|Toughness}} → {{skill|Hardy}} → {{skill|Defiance}}: defense, maximum health, fewer blows at low health; further on, {{skill|Iron Will}} (maximum health +8) and {{skill|Adrenaline}} (faster and healing when struck down to low health, once a minute).
- {{skill|Toughness}} → {{skill|Thick Skin}} → {{skill|Fortress}}: defense +4 in all.
- {{skill|Iron Stomach}} → {{skill|Second Wind}} → {{skill|Regeneration}}: hunger comes slower; with satiety above half (Second Wind) or above a quarter (Regeneration) you heal by yourself, without having to be nearly full. Besides these, {{skill|Frugal}} (hunger slower still) and {{skill|Hearty Meals}} (food restores half again as much satiety).

- The active skill {{skill|Mend}}: can be learned once Regeneration is learned; restores 30% of maximum health at once, cooldown 30 seconds.

Two ultimates: {{skill|Last Stand}} (Defiance + Second Wind) leaves 1 health after fatal combat damage, cooldown 30 seconds; {{skill|Phoenix}} (Fortress + Regeneration), once a day, leaves 30% health after any fatal damage (a blow, fire, hunger) and makes you invulnerable for 3 seconds.

“Direct combat damage” means damage dealt by someone striking you; the surroundings' damage, such as hunger, falls and burning, does not count, and most damage-reducing skills do nothing against it. For hunger's rules, see [[Satiety]].

## Damage

Hit harder, land criticals more often, and make them hurt more. The root is {{skill|Strength}} (attack +1):

- {{skill|Heavy Blows}} (attack +2) → {{skill|Sweep}}: melee blows also strike the creatures on either side of the cell ahead; besides, {{skill|Brute}} (attack +3).
- {{skill|Keen Eye}} (critical chance +10%) → {{skill|Executioner}}: +25% damage on targets below 30% health. Further on, {{skill|Precision}} → {{skill|Hemorrhage}} (criticals make the target bleed) and {{skill|Lethal Edge}} → {{skill|Brutal}} (critical damage +25% each).
- {{skill|First Strike}} (+30% on targets at full health) → {{skill|Momentum}} (hits in a row stack more damage, up to +25%).

- The active skills {{skill|Cleave}} (once Strength is learned): bring a heavy blow down on the cell ahead, 2.5× damage, cooldown 6 seconds; {{skill|Whirlwind}} (once Sweep is learned): spin on the spot, striking every enemy in the 8 surrounding cells, 1.2× damage, cooldown 8 seconds.

Two ultimates: {{skill|Deadly Focus}} (Sweep + Executioner) raises criticals from 1.5× to 2×; {{skill|Berserker}} (Brutal + Momentum) gives damage +25% and critical chance +10% while your health is at or below half.

## Profession

This branch grows only once you choose a [[Professions|profession]], showing your profession's twenty-two skills; with no profession, hovering over the branch tells you to find the profession trainer. Each profession's six talents have the same shape: a root, two branches of two skills each, and a 2-point ultimate that needs both branches learned. There are also four active skills: two can be learned once the root is learned, and the other two follow one each after those. Since 2026-10-09 each profession has twelve more: three active skills (the last an ultimate active skill), five **skill upgrades** (once learned, they apply straight to one active skill: a shorter cooldown, more damage, a wider area), three talents and an ultimate talent (see “Advanced” on each profession's page). Profession talents work only with their own profession's weapons.

| Profession | Root | Ultimate | Active skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Assassin]] | {{skill|Short-Blade Training}} | {{skill|Death Dance}} | {{skill|Shadow Strike}}, {{skill|Envenom}} → {{skill|Smoke Bomb}}, {{skill|Flurry}} |
| [[Warrior]] | {{skill|Arms Training}} | {{skill|Warlord}} | {{skill|Shield Bash}}, {{skill|Charge}} → {{skill|War Cry}}, {{skill|Earthsplitter}} |
| [[Ranger]] | {{skill|Archery}} | {{skill|Hunter's Mark}} | {{skill|Multishot}}, {{skill|Leap Shot}} → {{skill|Piercing Arrow}}, {{skill|Arrow Rain}} |

When you change profession, every skill learned in this branch is forgotten and every point comes back, to be learned again in the new profession. For each profession's skills, see its own page.

## Gathering

Twenty skills, branching four ways from {{skill|Long Reach}}:

- **Woodcutting**: {{skill|Woodcutter}}, {{skill|Sharp Axe}} (damage to trees +50%), {{skill|Carpenter}} (one more plank from splitting wood), {{skill|Forester}} (every tree drops acorns), with the ultimate {{skill|Timber Master}}.
- **Mining**: {{skill|Prospector}} (one more ore from a vein), {{skill|Heavy Pick}} (pickaxe force +50%), {{skill|Stonecutter}}, {{skill|Gem Finder}} (veins sometimes hold gems), with the ultimate {{skill|Deep Vein}}.
- **Foraging**: {{skill|Forager}} (one more from mushroom rings and reeds), {{skill|Herbalist}} (herbs eaten restore twice as much), and the older {{skill|Big Pack}} and {{skill|Firemaker}}.
- **Fishing**: {{skill|Angler}} (bites come more easily), {{skill|Bait Saver}}, {{skill|Lucky Catch}} (Golden Carp and Dawnscale doubled), {{skill|Deep Fisher}} (Pike can be caught anywhere), with the ultimate {{skill|Master Angler}} (one more fish every catch).

See [[Foraging]], [[Woodcutting]], [[Mining]], [[Fishing]].

## Agriculture

Twenty skills, starting from {{skill|Farmer}} (one more seed from a harvest): Green Fingers and perennial crops; the wide hoe and watering ({{skill|Wide Hoe}} tills 3×3 at once, {{skill|Irrigation}}, {{skill|Sprinkler}}); compost and rich soil ({{skill|Composter}} lets you make fertilizer yourself); specialties by kind of crop (grains, roots, gourds, fruit and vegetables, berries and herbs each yield more); and the selling and seed-buying {{skill|Market Gardener}} and {{skill|Seed Merchant}}. The ultimates are {{skill|Golden Harvest}} (a 15% chance of a double harvest) and {{skill|Master Farmer}}. For how farming plays, see [[Farming]].

{{skill tree|Agriculture}}

## Animal Husbandry

Twenty skills, starting from {{skill|Herdsman}} (animals you feed stay fed for two days): milking and cheese ({{skill|Cheesemaker}}: milk set over a lit fire becomes cheese); the coop ({{skill|Egg Collector}}: a fed hen lays at once); breeding ({{skill|Breeder}}'s twins, {{skill|Stockman}} feeding a whole herd at once, {{skill|Feed Maker}}); butchering and hides ({{skill|Butcher}}, {{skill|Tanner}}, {{skill|Plucker}}, and {{skill|Leatherworker}} for making jerkins); beekeeping ({{skill|Beekeeper}} goes unstung, {{skill|Apiarist}} makes bee boxes); the selling {{skill|Rancher}}; and the ultimate {{skill|Master Rancher}}. For animals, see [[Animals]].

## Smithing

Twenty skills that go with the [[Smithing|smithing]] gameplay: {{skill|Apprentice Smith}} and {{skill|Fine Craft}} raise the quality of what you forge, {{skill|Forge Builder}} lets you build a forge and an anvil yourself, {{skill|Efficient Smelting}} yields more ingots (a single ore can also be thrown straight into a [[forge#Smelting|forge]] to smelt one ingot), {{skill|Steelworking}}, {{skill|Goldsmith}}, {{skill|Jeweler}}, {{skill|Tool Smith}}, {{skill|Heavy Arms}} and {{skill|Plate Work}} each unlock recipes, {{skill|Weaponsmith}} and {{skill|Keen Edge}} make the weapons you forge stronger, {{skill|Armorsmith}} and {{skill|Reinforced}} make armor harder, and {{skill|Masterwork}} has a chance to raise the quality by one grade. The ultimates are {{skill|Legendary Smith}} (a 4% chance of Vowbound quality) and {{skill|Grandmaster Smith}}.

## Active skills and the skill bar

**Active skills** are cast by yourself, and must cool down for a while after use. Twenty in all:

- **Shared** (anyone can learn them): {{skill|Whirlwind}} (once Sweep is learned), {{skill|Cleave}} (once Strength is learned), {{skill|Gale Step}} (once Light Footed is learned), {{skill|Mend}} (once Regeneration is learned).
- **[[Assassin]]**: {{skill|Shadow Strike}}, {{skill|Envenom}}, then {{skill|Smoke Bomb}}, {{skill|Flurry}}.
- **[[Warrior]]**: {{skill|Shield Bash}}, {{skill|Charge}}, then {{skill|War Cry}}, {{skill|Earthsplitter}}.
- **[[Ranger]]**: {{skill|Multishot}}, {{skill|Leap Shot}}, then {{skill|Piercing Arrow}}, {{skill|Arrow Rain}}.
- **[[Mage]]**: {{skill|Fireball}}, {{skill|Frost Nova}}, then {{skill|Chain Lightning}}, {{skill|Blink}}.

What each skill does, its multiplier and its cooldown are in its description in the skill tree, and listed in the tables of each branch above (its type reads “Active”).

- A skill's damage is “your attack + weapon power” times the skill's multiplier, and criticals and other talents still apply.
- Skills strike only with a weapon: with something else in hand (a torch, food), the weapon in the [[Equipment|main hand]] is used; with no weapon, only your own attack.
- A skill with no target nearby (Shadow Strike, for example) cannot be cast, and does not go on cooldown.
- A learned profession skill can be cast only while you are of that profession; a skill lent by gear ignores profession.
- During a story scene, while answering a yes/no question, while waiting for the world to learn, and while you are held fast yourself ({{status|stunned}}, frozen, kneeling and other states that stop you acting), skills cannot be cast.
- Shield Bash, Charge and Earthsplitter leave the target {{status|stunned}} (unable to move or strike), and Frost Nova freezes it.

### The skill bar

The lower left of the screen has six skill slots, each marked ⇧1–⇧6 in its corner. Press {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}} or click a slot to cast. A slot on cooldown has a dark sweep over it with the seconds left in the middle, and flashes once when ready. Hover over a slot to see the skill's name, cooldown in seconds and description.

- When you learn an active skill in the skill tree, it goes into the first empty slot by itself; skills learned from skill books or lent by gear do not, and must be put in through the skills panel.
- In the skill tree, hover over a learned active skill and press {{key|Shift}}+a number to put it in that slot.
- Slots can be dragged to swap places (drag onto a slot with a skill and the two swap); right-click to clear a slot.
- While a panel is open (the bag, the skill tree and so on), {{key|Shift}}+a number does not cast.
- When a skill cannot be cast, you are told why: “Nothing to use it on.”, “That skill belongs to another profession.”, “You no longer have that skill (what lent it is not worn).” Pressing a skill on cooldown does nothing.

### The skills panel

Press {{key|U}}, or click the button marked U in the lower right, to open the **skills panel** (its title is “Skills”): the six skill slots above, and below them every active skill you now have, each with where it comes from (the talent tree, a skill book, gear) and which slot it is in. Drag a skill into a slot, or click the skill and then the slot; right-click to clear a slot. Press {{key|U}} again, press {{key|Esc}} or click outside the panel to close it.

With no active skills at all, the panel says: “No active skills yet: learn them in the skill tree (K), read a skill book, or wear a piece that lends one.”

### Where active skills come from

- **The skill tree**: learned with skill points.
- **{{item|Skill Book}}**: each book teaches one active skill, and its title is “Skill Book: skill name”. Hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Read Skill Book: X”): “You read the book and learn X.” No skill point is spent, and the book is used up; but the skills before it in the skill tree must be learned first, and if not you are told “You must learn X before Y.”. A profession skill's book can be read only by that profession, and another profession's book says “This book is for another profession.”; a skill you already know says “You know X already.”; in these three cases the book is not used up.
- **Gear**: while you wear gear that carries a skill you can use that skill, whatever your profession; take it off and the skill is gone, and if it stays in a slot it turns grey and cannot be cast.

Skill books are bought from the bookshelf of [[Firstlight Chapel|Firstlight Chapel]] ([[Brother Anselm|Brother Anselm]]); there is no gear that carries skills yet.

## Tips

- Common choices for the first point: {{skill|Vitality}} (4 more health) or {{skill|Strength}} (attack +1).
- For an early dash: after Swiftness's root {{skill|Light Footed}} you can learn {{skill|Dash}}, only 2 points.
- Once Strength is learned you can learn {{skill|Cleave}}: an active skill with your second point.
- If you get hungry fast and heal slowly, look at Survival's {{skill|Iron Stomach}} and {{skill|Second Wind}}.
- The profession branch can be learned only after you choose a profession. To learn its root skill as soon as you choose, keep 1 point in hand when you reach level 5.

## History

- 2026-10-03: The skill tree expanded to two directions, Combat and Life, with eight branches; Swiftness, Survival and Damage each gained two branches and a second ultimate, and the dash ({{key|Q}}), dodge, critical damage, Adrenaline, Phoenix and others went live; professions became a choice made with a trainer after level 5, four professions with six skills each.
- 2026-10-04: All 70 skills got icons of their own.
- 2026-10-05: With hunger changed to two stages, Iron Stomach and Frugal's numbers were adjusted to match (when fed, one point lost every 48 → 72 → 96 game minutes).
- 2026-10-09: The four Life branches each grew to twenty skills; the [[Smithing|smithing]] gameplay (smelting, forging), mushroom rings, wild beehives, cheese and leatherwork added. The skill tree became a tiered panel growing in four directions from the center, with branch tabs; each profession's branch grew to twenty-two nodes (three new active skills, five skill upgrades, three talents, one ultimate talent), and forty-eight more icons drawn in code were added.
- 2026-10-08: Twenty active skills added (four shared, four for each profession), with the skill bar in the lower left ({{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}}) and the skills panel ({{key|U}}); active skills can also come from skill books and gear. The twenty skill icons drawn in code.
- 2026-10-09: The Smithing section made clear that a forge can smelt ore (no skill needed), and that forging did not exist yet.
