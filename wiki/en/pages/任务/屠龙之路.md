**The Dragon Road** is the main quest after [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]]. The knight's kingdom lies open; through it the road runs on east to [[Emberfall]], where the mountain above is smoking: something is burning it. This quest strings the old main line, [[Emberfall and the Dragon|Emberfall and the Dragon]], into one quest: reach Emberfall, find the elder [[Mara]], get [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]] and the [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]] ready, cross [[Cinder Pass]], and defeat [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] in [[The Dragon's Lair]].

Once [[The Crown of Thorns|The Crown of Thorns]] is complete (you bring the Crown of Thorns back to Firstlight Village), this quest begins by itself, with no one to talk to; it completes by itself when the dragon falls, with nothing to hand in. It is currently the last quest of the main line.

This line is older than the present setting of the Church of the Five Sufferings; it is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the chapter of the Second Seal; see [[Chapter 2|Chapter 2]].

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The objectives appear in four batches:

1. **Follow the road east to Emberfall**: walk in at the edge of Emberfall.
2. **Find Mara, the elder of Emberfall**: press {{key|E}} on Mara empty-handed and hear her out about the dragon.
3. **Six objectives of preparation**:
   - Iron ore 5 and coal 3 for Garrick; fireweed 3 and frostcap 3 for Wren. The counts are of what you have already handed them, not of what you carry.
   - Dragonsbane forged by Garrick and the fireproof draught brewed by Wren: press {{key|E}} on them once you have handed over enough.
4. **Climb Cinder Pass to the dragon's lair**, and then **Defeat the dragon of Cinder Peak**.

While you track this quest, the map markers point at Garrick and Wren; they have a “?” over their heads to remind you that you still need to see them.

## Walkthrough

1. **To Emberfall**: enter Emberfall by the eastern exit of [[The East Road]] (this exit only opens once the White Knight has fallen). At the edge of the town a line of narration plays: “Emberfall. Half the roofs are new thatch; the other half are still black.”
2. **Mara**: she is on the square by day. Press E on her empty-handed: she tells you about the dragon on Cinder Peak, says the smith and the herbalist can help, and gives you 2 [[Bread|bread]].
3. **The smith [[Garrick]]** (by the anvil): 5 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[Coal|Coal]]. Both are in [[The Old Mine]] north of the town: break [[iron vein|iron veins]] (2 iron ore each) and [[coal seam|coal seams]] (2 coal each) with a [[Pickaxe|pickaxe]]; there is an old pickaxe at the mine's mouth. The [[cyclops|cyclops]] at the very bottom carries 2 iron ore. The 4 iron ore dropped by the White Knight count too.
4. **The herbalist [[Wren]]** (by the cauldron): 3 [[Fireweed|Fireweed]] and 3 [[Frostcap|Frostcap]]. Fireweed grows in the open ground of [[Firstlight Village]], frostcap in the shady parts of the [[The Forest|Forest]], and both slowly grow back once picked. Both places are west of the river, so you have to go back and cross at the Ford (a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] you have woken saves you the walk).
5. **Hand things over one at a time**: hold a thing and press {{key|G}} on them; each time you hand over the thing in your hand, they take it and say a line. Only what you hand over after talking to Mara counts; once they have enough, handing over more gets “That's enough”, and they take no more.
6. **Get the sword and the draught**: once you have enough, press {{key|E}} on them: Garrick forges Dragonsbane, and Wren brews two Fireproof Draughts. Once you have both, a line of narration plays in Emberfall, the preparation objectives are all ticked, and the next objective appears.
7. **Cinder Pass**: take Emberfall's eastern exit up onto [[Cinder Pass]]. Lower down are two [[frost wolf|frost wolves]], higher up two [[fire imp|fire imps]] (ranged, slowly replenished), and before the summit exit a [[stone golem|stone golem]]. Halfway up there is a lit campfire where you can rest. Walk into the entrance passage of the Dragon's Lair, a line of narration plays, and the last objective appears.
8. **The dragon of Cinder Peak**: before going in, hold a Fireproof Draught and right-click yourself to drink it: for 300 seconds fire does only a quarter of its damage, and you cannot be set alight. Keep the other bottle to drink once the first wears off. The dragon has 700 health, breathes fire at range and bites up close; do not stand in the line of its fire. The first time you hit it while its health is below half, it summons 3 [[dragon whelp|dragon whelps]] (only once). Dragonsbane deals double damage to the dragon and the whelps.
9. **The ending**: the dragon falls and the quest completes. Any whelps left scatter by themselves, and the ending scene and the ending card play. Afterwards you can go on roaming the world freely.

### Out of order

Each step looks only at the state of things. If, say, you burst into the lair's entrance without the sword and the draught, the quest still moves on to “Defeat the dragon of Cinder Peak”, and the unfinished preparation objectives stay in the journal; when the dragon falls, the quest still completes. It is just that without Dragonsbane and the Fireproof Draught this fight is much harder.

## Dialogue

{{quest dialogue}}

The words are all in the scenes and in what the smith and the herbalist say:

- Mara: “A dragon nests on Cinder Peak, up the pass east of here. It comes down every spring. This spring it has come down twice.”
- Garrick, taking iron ore: “Good iron, that! Five ore and three coal, and you'll have your blade.” Forging the sword: “Here. Dragonsbane. Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge this one. Bring it back red with dragon's blood.”
- Wren, taking fireweed: “Fireweed! The books say it only grows where there's been a fire.” Brewing the draught: “Two draughts. Drink one at the lair's mouth, and for a few minutes the fire will barely warm you. The other is for when the first wears off.”

## History

- 2026-10-02: The stretch from Emberfall to the dragon was added as the first main line; at the time there were no quest entries, only the objective box on screen.
- 2026-10-06: Once Chapter 1 was made, this stretch could only be reached after the White Knight fell, and its objective was called “Chapter 2: The Road to Emberfall”.
- 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system, and this stretch became the main quest “The Dragon Road”: it begins by itself once you bring the Crown of Thorns back to the village, and the counts handed to the smith and the herbalist, the sword and the draught are all objectives of the quest, shown in the quest journal and the tracker.
