**The Crown of Thorns** is the last main quest of Chapter 1. With [[The White Knight Alberic]] fallen, the First Seal is broken and the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]] is in your hands: bring it back to [[Firstlight Village]].

The quest begins by itself once the White Knight falls; it completes by itself when you are back in Firstlight Village and the homecoming scene has played, with nothing to hand in. On completion the chapter card for Chapter 1 appears, and the next main quest, [[The Dragon Road]], begins at once.

The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the item [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]].

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The objective looks at the event “back in Firstlight Village, with the homecoming scene played”, and does not check whether the Crown of Thorns is still on you.

## Walkthrough

1. **The scene of the Crown of Thorns**: once you defeat the White Knight on [[The East Road]], the scene of the Crown of Thorns plays, you receive the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]], and this quest begins. Straight after, in the briars where the knight fell, you find a thorn that is still green and receive [[The Oath-Thorn|The Oath-Thorn]], which leads to [[The Held Ground]].
2. **Back to the village**: follow the East Road west, through [[The Kneeling Field]], [[The Pilgrim's Road]] and [[The Pilgrims' Rest]] (where [[Father Caius]] comes to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. …”), back to the far bank of [[The Ford]], across the river, and on west to Firstlight Village. You can also use the [[World Map|World Map]] ({{key|M}}) to teleport to the [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] in Firstlight Village (if you have woken it).
3. **The homecoming**: as soon as you enter Firstlight Village the homecoming scene plays, the quest completes, and the chapter card “Chapter 1: The First Seal” appears. [[The Dragon Road]] begins: east to Emberfall.

## Rewards

{{rewards}}

Besides the experience, this stretch also brings:

- **[[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]]**: received on defeating the White Knight, head gear of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality (Defense 4, Attack 2, experience −50%; stigma power “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”).
- **Crown's Insight**: Lumi's first [[Boons|boon]], experience +100%.
- **[[The Oath-Thorn|The Oath-Thorn]]**: for going to and from the Held Ground.
- The 4 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] the White Knight drops, and (if you have chosen a [[Professions|profession]]) a piece of [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]].
