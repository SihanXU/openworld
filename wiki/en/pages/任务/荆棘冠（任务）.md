**The Crown of Thorns** is the last main quest of Chapter 1. With [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] fallen, the First Seal is broken and the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]] is in your hands: bring it back to [[初原村]].

The quest begins by itself once the White Knight falls; it completes by itself when you are back in Firstlight Village and the homecoming scene has played, with nothing to hand in. On completion the chapter card for Chapter 1 appears, and the next main quest, [[屠龙之路]], begins at once.

The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the item [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]].

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The objective looks at the event “back in Firstlight Village, with the homecoming scene played”, and does not check whether the Crown of Thorns is still on you.

## Walkthrough

1. **The scene of the Crown of Thorns**: once you defeat the White Knight on [[东大路]], the scene of the Crown of Thorns plays, you receive the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]], and this quest begins. Straight after, in the briars where the knight fell, you find a thorn that is still green and receive [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]], which leads to [[誓守之地]].
2. **Back to the village**: follow the East Road west, through [[跪石原]], [[朝圣者之路]] and [[朝圣者驿站]] (where [[凯乌斯司铎]] comes to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. …”), back to the far bank of [[渡口]], across the river, and on west to Firstlight Village. You can also use the [[世界地图|World Map]] ({{键|M}}) to teleport to the [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] in Firstlight Village (if you have woken it).
3. **The homecoming**: as soon as you enter Firstlight Village the homecoming scene plays, the quest completes, and the chapter card “Chapter 1: The First Seal” appears. [[屠龙之路]] begins: east to Emberfall.

## Rewards

{{任务奖励}}

Besides the experience, this stretch also brings:

- **[[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]**: received on defeating the White Knight, head gear of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality (Defense 4, Attack 2, experience −50%; stigma power “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”).
- **Crown's Insight**: Lumi's first [[恩赐|boon]], experience +100%.
- **[[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]]**: for going to and from the Held Ground.
- The 4 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] the White Knight drops, and (if you have chosen a [[职业|profession]]) a piece of [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]].
