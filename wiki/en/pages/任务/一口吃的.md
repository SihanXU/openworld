**A Bite to Eat** is a commission: not a quest anyone wrote in advance, but one that grows out of what is happening in the village right now. Villagers who get hungry look for food on their own (see [[People's Needs|people's needs]]); someone with an empty stomach, not a coin on them and no food found will ask you to bring them something to eat.

Who is going hungry in each "A Bite to Eat" depends on who is actually starving at the time. [[Vigo]], who never has any money, asks this of you most often.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The 〈hungry one〉 in the objectives, the stage table and the dialogue is replaced in the game by the name of whoever is starving at the time.

Anything edible will do: berries, bread, raw meat and cooked dishes all count. The food has to be put into their hands with {{key|G}}; leaving it at their feet does not count.

## Walkthrough

1. **The request**: a yellow “!” appears over the hungry person's head, and the commission is also listed on the [[commission board|commission board]] in [[Firstlight Village]]. Press {{key|E}} on them and they say: “I haven't eaten since yesterday, and I don't have a coin to my name. …If only you had some food to spare.”
2. **Accept or decline**: choose “Accept ‘A Bite to Eat’” in the dialogue box and they say “You would? Anything at all.”; you can also press “Take it” on the commission board. Choose “Decline ‘A Bite to Eat’” and they say “No. Of course not.”, and the commission is withdrawn.
3. **Give them food**: holding something edible, press {{key|G}} on them. They take it and say “Oh — thank you. I won't forget this.” The quest is complete.

If you have accepted but not yet given them anything, talking to them gets “Food is all. Anything at all.”

## Time limits and failure

- If nobody takes the request within 8 in-game hours of it being made, the commission is withdrawn.
- If you do not give them food within 8 in-game hours of accepting, the quest fails.
- If they die in the meantime, the quest fails, and the journal reads “X is gone.”
- There are at most two "A Bite to Eat" commissions at once. The same person will not ask for a second one until the first is settled.

## Tips

- Keep a little food on you and you can settle it on the spot. [[Marta]] in Firstlight Village sells bread, and a single [[Berries|berry]] will do.
- Feed them and they will not need to steal: when someone like [[Vigo]] has eaten, that is one less bit of pilfering.
