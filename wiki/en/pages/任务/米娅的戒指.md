**Mia's Ring** is a side quest of [[Firstlight Village]] given by [[Mia]], the little girl skipping stones by the pond. She sneaked off to [[Thornwood Hollow]] and dropped her grandmother's silver [[Ring|ring]] in the briars on the east side, and she does not want her mother to find out.

The quest has no prerequisites. Thornwood Hollow lies right on the way for [[Lost in the Barrow]], so the two quests can be done together.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (7:00–20:00) Mia is by the pond south of the square; press {{key|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘Mia's Ring’” in the dialogue box.
2. **To Thornwood Hollow**: head south from the village, through [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] and [[The Forest|the Forest]], and enter Thornwood Hollow from the Forest's south-west corner.
3. **Find the ring**: the ring is in the eastern half of Thornwood Hollow, among the briars on the north side: a small silver ring on the ground (called “Mia's Ring”). Press {{key|E}} to pick it up. [[cave spider|Cave spiders]] and [[wolf|wolves]] roam this area, and there are [[green slime|green slimes]] in the open parts of the valley too.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Mia and press {{key|E}} to hand in. After 20:00 she goes home to sleep, and comes out again at 7:00 the next day.

The quest only accepts Mia's own ring, that is, the silver ring called “Mia's Ring” in Thornwood Hollow. If you are also carrying other rings, such as Sister Maren's Ring from [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]] or the Surveyor's Ring from [[The Measure of Days]], they are not taken when you hand in and do not count.
