**The Bell on the Spit** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[希尔德]], the fishwife of [[芦苇湾]]. Nine winters ago her husband Rolf took the boat out into the fog; the boat came back on its own, and he did not. The boat's bell lies on the spit where the boat ran aground, held for nine years by [[水史莱姆|water slimes]]. She does not go there, and asks you to.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

“Rolf's Boat Bell” is a [[铃铛|Bell]]; Hilde keeps it when you hand it in (she says she will hang it on the door).

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: go to the Reed Bend and find Hilde, who stands by the jetty by day (5:00–22:00); press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Bell on the Spit’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Onto the spit**: the spit is south of the jetty (rows 20–22), a strip of sand reaching out into the river. There are 3 [[水史莱姆|water slimes]] on the spit, which attack on sight; once defeated, another may come onto the sand every 1800 seconds.
3. **Pick up the bell**: the bell is at the very end of the spit; press {{键|E}} to pick it up.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Hilde and press {{键|E}} to hand in.

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

{{剧透|The same sound}}
When you hand in, Hilde says: “It still sounds the same. I thought it would have changed.” She gives you the ring Rolf wore when mending nets — it only works on a hand that goes places. Afterwards she says she has hung the bell on the door “so he knows which house is ours”. In the catch book on the table in her cabin, the year nine winters ago has only one line: “The boat came back. He didn't. Fog.”
{{/剧透}}
