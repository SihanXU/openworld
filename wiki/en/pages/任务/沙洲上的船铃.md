**The Bell on the Spit** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[Hilde]], the fishwife of [[The Reed Bend]]. Nine winters ago her husband Rolf took the boat out into the fog; the boat came back on its own, and he did not. The boat's bell lies on the spit where the boat ran aground, held for nine years by [[water slime|water slimes]]. She does not go there, and asks you to.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

“Rolf's Boat Bell” is a [[Bell|Bell]]; Hilde keeps it when you hand it in (she says she will hang it on the door).

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: go to the Reed Bend and find Hilde, who stands by the jetty by day (5:00–22:00); press {{key|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Bell on the Spit’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Onto the spit**: the spit is south of the jetty (rows 20–22), a strip of sand reaching out into the river. There are 3 [[water slime|water slimes]] on the spit, which attack on sight; once defeated, another may come onto the sand every 1800 seconds.
3. **Pick up the bell**: the bell is at the very end of the spit; press {{key|E}} to pick it up.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Hilde and press {{key|E}} to hand in.
