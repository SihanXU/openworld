**The Night-born** is a commission that grows out of what is happening in the village right now. On some evenings the [[夜行生物|night creatures]] outside the fence grow thick again, and a guard asks you to put down three before dawn, for which the watch will pay you.

Every day at 20:00 there is a sixty percent chance that a "The Night-born" arises, the request coming from a guard picked at random (at present [[霍尔特]] or [[芬尼克]]; see [[守卫与悬赏|Guards and Bounties]]).

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The 〈guard〉 in the objectives, the stage table and the dialogue is replaced in the game by the name of the guard who asked.

- Only night creatures put down after you take the quest count: the [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] that appear after dark all count, on any map. Other monsters do not.
- Once you have put down three, go back and talk to the guard who asked.

## Walkthrough

1. **The request**: a yellow “!” appears over the guard's head, and the commission is also listed on the [[委托板|commission board]] in [[初原村]]. Press {{键|E}} on him and he says: “The night-born are thick outside the fence again. Put down three before dawn, and the watch will pay you.”
2. **Accept or decline**: choose “Accept ‘The Night-born’” and he says “Mind the trees. They come out of the dark over there.”; or press “Take it” on the commission board. Choose “Decline ‘The Night-born’” and the commission is withdrawn.
3. **Leave the village**: Firstlight Village is a peaceful zone, with no monsters at night. Go south to [[草原|the Grassland]] or north to [[晨光丘]], and after dark night creatures appear 6 to 10 tiles from you. See [[和平区域与战斗区域|Peaceful and Combat Zones]].
4. **Put down three**: the journal reads “Three put down. Back to X.”, and a “?” appears over the guard's head.
5. **Hand in**: go back and press {{键|E}} on the guard; he says “Good. The village will sleep easier tonight,” pays you, and the quest is complete.

Talk to him before you have put down enough and he says “Three, before dawn.”

## Time limits and failure

- If nobody takes the request within 10 in-game hours of it being made, the commission is withdrawn.
- If you do not hand it in within 10 in-game hours of accepting (around 6 the next morning), the quest fails, and the journal reads “It could not be done.”
- The guard who asked is protected while the quest is under way: when his health reaches zero he only goes down and does not die. See [[人物之死|Death of Characters]].
- There is at most one "The Night-born" at a time.

## Tips

- Do it together with [[老骑士]] and [[草原守夜]]: the zombies you put down count for each of these quests.
- Night creatures crumble to dust at daybreak. If you have not put down enough by dawn, you can only wait for the next request.
