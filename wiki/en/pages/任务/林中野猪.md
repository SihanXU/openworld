**Boars in the Coppice** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[奥林]], the charcoal burner of [[烧炭谷]]. [[野猪|Boars]] have been rooting up the stumps in the coppices around the dell, so no new wood can grow; he asks you to defeat 4 boars in the Charcoal Dell.

Orrin only asks this of you once you reach **level 2**.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

Only boars defeated on the Charcoal Dell map after you take the quest count.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: from level 2, go to the Charcoal Dell and find Orrin, who keeps by the clamp by day (6:00–21:00); press {{键|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘Boars in the Coppice’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Fight the boars**: at the start there are 4 boars in the coppices north and south of the dell, and they charge at you; once defeated, another may appear every 1500 seconds, up to 3 at a time. A boar has 18 health, 4 attack, 1 defense and is fast, so do not go empty-handed. A defeated boar has a chance to drop the [[獠牙腰带|Tusked Belt]] (see [[装备#怪物专属装备|monster trophies]]).
3. **Hand in**: go back to Orrin and press {{键|E}} to hand in.
