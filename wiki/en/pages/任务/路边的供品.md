**Offerings on the Road** is one of [[Lumi]]'s [[Quests#Guides: Lumi speaks up herself|guide quests]], teaching how to light the five [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road]]. Once you have taken the oath at the [[Pilgrims' Rest]] (the step of [[The First Seal]] that asks it), she speaks up as soon as you stand on the Pilgrim's Road; if you walked the road before the oath, it begins when you come back to it after.

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: Lumi points out the dark stones on the squares of flagstones by the road: each shrine asks for something different, and the words cut on it ({{key|E}}) say what; hold what it asks, face the shrine and press {{key|G}}. Each of the five shrines is an objective of its own, and when you track the quest the markers point at the five shrines.
2. **Offerings**: all five can be found on this road; {{key|E}} picks them up:
   - [[Shrine of the Crown]]: something worn on the head; a pilgrim's hat lies on the road;
   - [[Shrine of the Pillar]]: a rope or a cord; a penitent's plaited cord lies on the road, and {{item|Cut Grass}} will do too;
   - [[Shrine of the Scales]]: something to eat; there is a loaf in the abandoned cart;
   - [[Shrine of the Nails]]: something made of metal; a dented helm lies on the road, and {{item|Iron Ore}} will do too;
   - [[Shrine of the Lance]]: a torch or a candle; a candle lies on the road.
3. **The first lit**: Lumi says another line: the others work the same way, and what they ask for is lying about on the road if you look. A lit shrine's marker goes away.
4. **Complete**: once all five shrines are lit, the quest is complete.

A shrine given the wrong thing refuses it and says what it wants. No shrine takes a relic, a keepsake or anything that matters most (see [[wayside shrine]]).
