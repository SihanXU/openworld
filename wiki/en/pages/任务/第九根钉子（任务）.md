**The Ninth Nail** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[乔斯]], the bridge-builder. The three planks of the little bridge at the Ford used nine nails in all, and whoever tore up the bridge laid the nails out on the jetty, but there were only eight. Joss wants the ninth back: it went east with the one who tore up the bridge.

It can only be taken after the Church's letter (see [[第一印]]). The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the item [[第九根钉子|The Ninth Nail]].

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The nail is taken when you hand it in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (6:00–21:00) Joss is planing boards by the workbench on the west side of Firstlight Village. Press {{键|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘The Ninth Nail’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Across the river and onto the East Road**: cross the river at [[渡口]] by any means, and from the eastern exit on the far bank head east, through [[朝圣者驿站]], [[朝圣者之路]] and [[跪石原]], onto [[东大路]].
3. **Find the nail**: the nail is in the kingdom of thorns at the eastern end of the East Road, near the stretch where the road passes north of the ring of thorns: a square-headed nail on the ground. [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] stands in the ring; walking into the ring plays the kingdom's scene, and he will fight you. You can defeat him first and then pick it up, or slip round and pick it up during the fight.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Joss and press {{键|E}} to hand in.

You do not need to have taken the quest to pick up the nail. If you pick it up first and take the quest afterwards, talk to him once more after accepting to hand it in.

The [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]] is a building tool: crates, wooden chests, fences, signposts and the like are first framed up with planks or sticks, then knocked together with the smith hammer (see [[建造|Building]]).

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

{{剧透|What Joss didn't say}}
When you hand in, Joss only says the bridge stood for twenty years. Talk to him again after Chapter 1 ends (once you bring the Crown of Thorns back to the village), and he tells you, once, what happened back then: “Twenty years ago, it was a young man in grey who paid for that bridge. Old coins, with no king's head on them. He said: ‘I want a road that lets the carts get away from here quickly.’ I never did understand what he meant.” As Chapter 1 is designed, those are old coins of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]].
{{/剧透}}
