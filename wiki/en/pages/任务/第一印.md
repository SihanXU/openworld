**The First Seal** is the core main quest of Chapter 1. The days are growing shorter; across the Ford, [[朝圣者之路]] runs east past the Church's [[朝圣者驿站]], five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]] and the abandoned [[荆棘修道院]] to [[跪石原]]; on [[东大路]] beyond it, the first [[持印者|Seal-bearer]], the White Knight Alberic, has made that land his kingdom. This quest strings the second half of Chapter 1 together: the night of thanks in Firstlight Village, the Church's letter the next day, crossing the river at the Ford, walking the Pilgrim's Road, and at last defeating [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]].

The quest begins by itself once [[守夜之备]] is handed in; it completes and pays its rewards by itself when the White Knight falls, with nothing to hand in. Once it is complete, the main quest [[荆棘冠（任务）|The Crown of Thorns]] begins at once.

Take care not to confuse it with the page for the chapter [[第一章|Chapter 1]] (also called “The First Seal”).

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The objectives appear one after another:

- **The night of thanks before the sacred flame**: after The Vigil is handed in, be in [[初原村]] after dark (21:00–24:00).
- **The Church's letter**: the morning after the night of thanks (after 5:00), be in Firstlight Village.
- After the letter, two appear at once:
  - **Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins** (optional): completed by taking the side quest [[朝圣之路]] from [[伊尔丝修女]], or by crossing the river. At this point she has a “!” over her head: she wants to give you The Pilgrim's Road.
  - **Across the river at the Ford**: stand on the far bank of [[渡口]].
- **Take the Pilgrim's Vow at the Pilgrims' Rest**: go to [[五伤礼拜堂]] at [[朝圣者驿站]] and walk up to the altar of the Five.
- **Light the five shrines** (five objectives appearing together): set down ({{键|G}}) what each [[路边圣龛|wayside shrine]] on [[朝圣者之路]] asks for.
- Once all the shrines are lit, two appear at once:
  - **Learn what became of the knight in the Abbey of the Thorn**: get the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] in [[骑士团大厅]] of [[荆棘修道院]].
  - **Take The Order's Seal**: in [[修道院地下墓室]] defeat the last keeper, [[温德尔院长]], and pick up [[荆棘骑士团印章|The Order's Seal]] he drops.
- **On to the Kneeling Field**: walk into [[跪石原]].
- **Bring down the White Knight Alberic on the East Road**: defeat him in the kingdom of thorns on the East Road.

## Walkthrough

1. **The night of thanks**: after The Vigil is handed in, stay in Firstlight Village. At dusk (17:00–21:00) Ilse tells the villagers the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings; after dark (21:00–24:00) the whole village gives thanks before the sacred flame, and then something happens (see the spoiler below). The first objective is complete and “The Church's letter” appears.
2. **Wait for morning**: wait in the village until after 5:00 the next day. [[睡觉|Sleeping]] makes time pass faster, but there is no bed for the player in the village; you can make one yourself and set it down (see [[建造|Building]]). In the morning a scene plays in the village of the letter's arrival, and you receive a [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]]. The second objective is complete, and “Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins” and “Across the river at the Ford” appear.
3. **The Pilgrim's Road (optional)**: [[伊尔丝修女]] now has a side quest, [[朝圣之路]]: go and ask [[芬尼克]], [[乔斯]] and [[赫斯珀]]. Fennick tells you the White Knight's moves, and Joss tells you how to mend the bridge. Accept it and the optional objective is complete. You can carry on without doing this step.
4. **Across the river**: at the Ford, cross the river by any means: lay three planks to mend the little bridge, repair the ferry and have Hesper pole you over, fell the dead tree, freeze the river with the Wand of Frost, or any other way the world learns. For details see [[渡口#区域|“Ways across the river” on the Ford's page]]. Stand on the far bank after the letter, Hesper tells you to follow the road east, the river objective is complete, and the next objective appears, “Take the Pilgrim's Vow at the Pilgrims' Rest”. The eastern exit on the Ford's far bank leads to the Pilgrims' Rest, not straight to the East Road.
5. **The Pilgrim's Road** (added 2026-10-12):
   - **The vow at the Rest**: east of the far bank you come first to [[朝圣者驿站]]. [[凯乌斯司铎]] welcomes you and asks you to take the vow in [[五伤礼拜堂]]: every pilgrim the Holy See has sent east has first knelt before the Five Holy Wounds. Walk up to the altar of the Five, and he leads you in reciting the Five Sufferings, tells you that the Holy See would have you defeat that Seal-bearer and bring back the Crown of Thorns, and adds that “someone should have gone to relieve him”. After the vow you receive the [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]]. The Sworn Scallop is not the [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]] from the camp in the Forest, and cannot be handed in for [[上一位朝圣者]].
   - **The five shrines**: the five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]] on [[朝圣者之路]], one for each Suffering, each want one thing: something worn on the head, a rope, something to eat, a piece of metal, a light. All of these can be picked up on the road: a pilgrim's hat, a grass rope plaited by a penitent, bread in an abandoned cart, a dented helmet, a candle. Hold it and press {{键|G}} on the shrine; the thing is accepted, and the shrine lights up. The shrines take no holy relics, important things, tokens or anything a quest needs you to keep — for example the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]], which can be worn on the head, or the metal [[荆棘骑士团印章|Order's Seal]]. The penitent [[以斯拉修士]] tells of seeing the knight mending his banner on the Kneeling Field.
   - **The Abbey of the Thorn**: once all five are lit, go north from the middle of the road up the hill to [[荆棘修道院]]. In the courtyard there is a statue of Alberic kneeling to a child on the day he was knighted, a memorial to the Order's dead and the gardener's grave; in [[骑士团大厅]] are the Order's roll, four pages of his diary and the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] that was never sent. In [[修道院地下墓室]], the last keeper, [[温德尔院长]], guards The Order's Seal, which he drops when defeated.
   - **The Kneeling Field**: follow the road east into [[跪石原]]. Pilgrims kneel here to pray, then go on their way. Rows of stones carved in kneeling shapes bear the names of earlier pilgrims and the years they held the Crown, and every one records that they would not kneel; the newest stone has no name carved on it yet.
6. **The East Road**: from the east side of the Kneeling Field go up onto the East Road. North of the road is a burned farmstead, where [[艾达]] is hiding in the barn (she has the side quest [[艾达的戒指]]). After the letter, when you reach the stretch of road before the thorns, [[灰衣人]] stands in your way, says his piece, and leaves; there is no fight. There is a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] by the road; wake it before you fight the knight: if you fall you can choose to come back to life here (on Normal difficulty), and beside it you can also change [[恩赐|Lumi's power]].
7. **The White Knight**: walk into the ring of thorns, and once the knight has spoken the fight begins. In the story he has 300 health and 1 armor, and his skills deal four tenths of the damage of the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]] version; before each attack he first marks the tiles where it will land (the prompt reads “Leave the marked area”), and if you step out of the marks before the wind-up ends you will not be hit:
   - **Arrow of Conquest**: shoots a straight line three tiles wide ahead of him. Do not stand directly in front of him.
   - **White-Feather Volley**: rains arrows down on five spots around you.
   - **Thorns Bend the Knee**: he drives his sword into the ground and thorns spring up in a ring around him; anyone pierced is “Entranced” for a moment, unable to move. Stand inside the ring or far away from it, not on the ring itself.
   - When his health drops to {{数值|首领二阶段}} he enters his second phase, with shorter wind-ups and higher damage.
   Defeat him, and the quest is complete and the rewards are yours. By the design document's reckoning, a level 5 character who dodges his attacks can defeat him in 20 to 40 seconds with any profession.

Scattered inside the ring are also [[第九根钉子|The Ninth Nail]], a [[婚戒|Wedding Band]] and two pieces of gear, the things [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] and [[艾达的戒指]] are looking for.

### What happens out of order

- The night of thanks and the letter must both happen in Firstlight Village. They do not happen before The Vigil is handed in.
- You can cross the river before the letter: stand on the far bank and Lumi says “The village still needs us — don't go too far yet.” The crossing is still recorded, but this quest goes step by step: the night of thanks and the letter happen as usual, with not a line of the journal missing; when the letter comes, the objective “Across the river at the Ford” already counts as done the moment it appears, and the quest moves straight on to the next objective, “Take the Pilgrim's Vow at the Pilgrims' Rest”.
- The vow has to come after the letter: Father Caius welcomes the pilgrim named in the letter, and only once he has welcomed you does the vow in the chapel happen.
- No step of the Pilgrim's Road blocks the way: without taking the vow, lighting the shrines or visiting the abbey, you can still walk all the way to the East Road.
- You can go and fight the White Knight at any time. As soon as he falls, this quest completes, all its objectives (those on the Pilgrim's Road included) are ticked together, and the rewards are paid as usual; if it had not yet begun, it completes the moment it begins. [[古墓里的失踪者]], [[守夜之备]] and [[朝圣之路]] are treated as “passed” and end (whether each pays its rewards, see [[任务#被剧情越过的任务|quests the story has passed]]); the night of thanks, the storytelling and the letter no longer happen. The same goes for the welcome and the vow at the Rest: Father Caius instead says “You have come from the east. From him. …” (see [[凯乌斯司铎]]).

## Rewards

{{任务奖励}}

The rewards come in three parts: on taking the vow you receive the [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]] and 20 experience; with both the Writ of Relief and The Order's Seal in hand you get 40 experience; the rest is paid the moment the White Knight falls.

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

The words are all in the scenes. Press {{键|E}} on [[露米]] and she tells you the current objective and a hint for this stage; at the river stage, for example, she tells you the little bridge is three planks short and how to fell trees and split planks with an axe, and reminds you that other ways across count too.

{{剧透|The night of thanks, the letter, the Pilgrim's Road and the Crown of Thorns}}
- **The night of thanks**: Rowan gives thanks for the sacred flame, and for those who went into the barrow for the village; Ilse says “Suffering is not God's abandonment. Suffering is the proof that calamity can be repaid.” The screen goes dark, and a man in a grey cloak, his hands smudged with ash, is standing before the sacred flame, lifting the fire into a shuttered lantern. Holt shouts “Thief!”; the man says “…Not tonight,” and walks away east, looking back once at Lumi. Holt says he made the river freeze under his feet and simply walked across. The sacred flame goes out.
- **The letter**: a courier from the Holy City proclaims: the Seal-bearers are failing, the Long Night is returning, and the Pilgrimage is hereby called; one has come down from the mountain with a fairy, and the Church names that one the Pilgrim. The courier stares at Lumi for a long time and says nothing. Ilse says she does not believe in coincidence.
- **The Rest**: Father Caius says: “At last, a pilgrim — and with a fairy, just as the letter said.” Before setting out you must take the vow: “Every pilgrim the Holy See has sent east knelt there first.” Lumi mutters: “Kneeling again? Everyone round here seems to want someone else on their knees.”
- **The vow**: the priest leads you through the Five Sufferings as the Church explains them (the fourth: “The Nailing. On the Holy Wood he accepted death.”). At the end he says: “That Seal-bearer has kept that road for three hundred years. The Holy See would have you defeat him and bring back the Crown of Thorns — the first of the five Instruments. …Someone should have gone to relieve him. That is no concern of a pilgrim's.” Lumi asks what “someone should have gone to relieve him” means, and the priest says nothing more.
- **The Writ of Relief**: the Holy See's writ says that in the fortieth year of Alberic's bearing the Seal a successor was to be sent. Its wax seal is unbroken; it was never sent. On the back is written: “No successor willing to bear it can be found. Let him keep watch. He will not notice the difference.” The diary on his desk counts on, page after page, past the fortieth year. Lumi says to take it, then: “Someone has to deliver it — late is better than never.”
- **The Kneeling Field**: Lumi reads out the names on the stones: “Elise, Hal, Isolde, Colin… ‘would not kneel’, not one of them knelt. So stubborn! I like them already.” She also wonders who keeps setting the scarecrows straight, every one of them facing east.
- **The kingdom**: the knight says “None cross that do not kneel. Another pilgrim. They always send another. I keep watch. I… keep watch. I do not remember what I keep. Kneel.” Lumi says he is so sad.
- **The Crown of Thorns**: fallen, the knight kneels, takes off the Crown of Thorns and holds it out to you: “Take it. It was never mine to wear.” A cold light flows to Lumi, and she gains her first [[恩赐|boon]]. The Grey Man appears and gathers the rest of the light into the stolen flame: “Every Seal you break makes the night a little longer. Go and ask your Church why.” He tells you to go back and tell Rowan that the light is safer with him than in the village, then leaves to the east. From now on night falls earlier, at 20:00, and day breaks later, at 5:30 (see [[昼夜|Day and Night]]).

## History

- 2026-10-06: Chapter 1 was made: the night of thanks, the letter, the kingdom of thorns on the East Road and the White Knight (weaker in the story than in the Boss Challenge), and the scene of the Crown of Thorns; nights grow longer once the White Knight is defeated.
- 2026-10-07: Added as a main quest, stringing the night of thanks, the letter, the river crossing and the White Knight into one quest; once the White Knight falls, the earlier main quests all end by themselves. After the letter, standing on the far bank advances the story, without needing to hand in The Pilgrim's Road first.
- 2026-10-08: If all four objectives are met when the White Knight falls, the quest completes and pays its rewards (before, it was simply closed, and the rewards were never paid).
- 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system: the former main-line stages (the night of thanks, the next morning, the Pilgrim's Road, mending the broken bridge, the road to the White Knight) became this quest's stages, and the journal records every step; the optional objective “Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins” was added; when the White Knight falls the quest always completes and pays its rewards; if you crossed the river before the letter, the quest still goes in order, and the crossing counts as done when the letter comes.
- 2026-10-12: The Pilgrim's Road was added between the river crossing and the White Knight: the vow at the Rest, the five shrines, the Abbey of the Thorn, the Kneeling Field; the eastern exit on the Ford's far bank now leads to the Pilgrims' Rest.
- 2026-10-13: The reward for the vow became the Sworn Scallop (it used to be the Pilgrim's Scallop, which was mistaken for the one The Pilgrim Before wants); at the vow, Father Caius no longer says that kneeling to the knight will get you let past, but that the Holy See would have you defeat him and bring back the Crown of Thorns.
- 2026-10-13: The shrines no longer accept holy relics, important things, tokens or quest items; once the White Knight has fallen, the welcome and the vow at the Rest no longer happen.
