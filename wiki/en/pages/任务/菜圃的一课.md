**A Lesson in the Garden** is one of [[Lumi]]'s [[Quests#Guides: Lumi speaks up herself|guide quests]], teaching [[Farming|farming]]. The first time you walk into the vegetable garden in the south-east corner of [[Firstlight Village]], she speaks up.

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: in a proper game, walk onto the garden plot, Lumi speaks, and the quest begins by itself.
2. **Tools**: in the east corner of the garden there are “Garden Supplies” (press {{key|E}} to open them), holding a {{item|Hoe}}, a {{item|Sickle}}, a bucket of water and two packets of seeds for each crop. Take the hoe, the sickle and the seeds, and three objectives are complete; take the water bucket while you are at it. When you track the quest, the marker points at this crate.
3. **Harvest**: the garden's three rows of crops are sprouts, seedlings and ripe plants; hold the sickle and left-click a ripe one to get vegetables and seeds. Once there are vegetables in your bag, the objective is complete.
4. **Sow**: the bottom row is empty farmland. Hold a seed and right-click an empty tile of farmland, and the seed becomes a sprout. Once a sprout stands on that row, the objective is complete.
5. **Water**: hold the full bucket and left-click a growing crop; it becomes {{status|watered}}, and grows one stage every one to four in-game hours, as its seed's description says. The well by the square fills an empty bucket.
6. **Complete**: once all three are done, the quest completes, and Lumi gives you two packets of {{item|Turnip Seeds}}.

The hoe can turn grass into farmland, so you can start a garden of your own; the harvest can be eaten, or sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground]]. For what can be grown, see [[Crops|Crops]].
