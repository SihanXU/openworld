**A Letter to Carry** is a commission that grows out of what is happening in the village right now. On some mornings someone in [[初原村]] wants a letter carried to someone else and, not being so quick on their feet, asks you to take it.

Every morning at 6:00 there is a sixty percent chance that an "A Letter to Carry" arises: the writer is someone who is in Firstlight Village at the time (merchants and guards excepted), and the recipient is another person in the village, both picked at random.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The 〈writer〉 and 〈recipient〉 in the objectives, the stage table and the dialogue are replaced in the game by the names of the people at the time.

When you accept, a [[信件|letter]] marked “X's letter to Y” goes into your backpack. Hold it and press {{键|G}} on the recipient; once it is in their hands, the quest is complete.

## Walkthrough

1. **The request**: a yellow “!” appears over the writer's head, and the commission is also listed on the [[委托板|commission board]]. Press {{键|E}} on them and they say: “Could you carry a letter to Y for me? My legs aren't what they were.”
2. **Accept or decline**: choose “Accept ‘A Letter to Carry’” and they say “Here. And no peeking.”, and the letter goes into your backpack; or press “Take it” on the commission board. Choose “Decline ‘A Letter to Carry’” and the commission is withdrawn, leaving nothing behind.
3. **Find the recipient**: track the quest, and the map marker points at the recipient; follow them.
4. **Deliver the letter**: hold the letter and press {{键|G}} on the recipient. They say “A letter? From X? …Thank you for bringing it.” The quest is complete.

If you have accepted but not yet delivered it, talking to the writer gets “That letter, to Y.”

## Quest items

While the commission is under way, the letter is a **quest item**: merchants will not take it. See [[交易|Trading]].

## Time limits and failure

- If nobody takes the request within 12 in-game hours of it being made, the commission is withdrawn.
- If you do not deliver it within 12 in-game hours of accepting, the quest fails.
- If the recipient dies (the journal reads “Y is gone.”), or the letter is lost (burned or destroyed; the journal reads “The letter is lost.”), the quest fails.
- There is at most one "A Letter to Carry" at a time.
