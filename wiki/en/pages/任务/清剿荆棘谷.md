**Clear the Hollow** is a side quest of [[Firstlight Village]] given by [[Holt]], the guard who stands watch at the road junction south of the village. He says the things in Thornwood Hollow are getting bolder and have already strayed onto the Grassland, and wants you to go to [[Thornwood Hollow]] and take out six of them.

It can only be taken from level 2 (see [[Levels and Experience|Levels and Experience]]); before that, Holt does not mention it.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

Only monsters defeated on the Thornwood Hollow map after you take the quest count. Any monster counts: [[green slime|green slimes]], [[cave spider|cave spiders]], [[wolf|wolves]], and the [[zombie|zombies]] and [[bat|bats]] that appear at night too. Those defeated on other maps do not count, nor do those defeated before you took the quest.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (6:00–23:00) Holt stands at the road junction south of the village; press {{key|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘Clear the Hollow’” in the dialogue box.
2. **To Thornwood Hollow**: go south through [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] and [[The Forest|the Forest]].
3. **Fight**: at the start Thornwood Hollow has 3 slimes, 3 spiders and 2 wolves, exactly enough. If that is not enough, while you are in the hollow a slime may be replenished every 90 seconds and a spider every 120 seconds (up to 3 of each); at night there are zombies and bats too.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Holt and press {{key|E}} to hand in.

[[Lost in the Barrow]] also takes you through Thornwood Hollow; take this quest before you go, and everything you defeat on the way counts.
