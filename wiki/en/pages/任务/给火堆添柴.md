**Wood for the Fire** is a commission [[初原村]] has every day, given by [[格斯]] the woodcutter. He wants 4 [[木头|logs]] a day for the village fire. Once you have handed it in, you cannot take it again that day; after midnight, when the new day begins, a “!” appears over his head again.

Gus asks this of you himself; it is not on the [[委托板|commission board]], and it has no time limit: once accepted, you can hand it in any day.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The 4 logs are taken when you hand in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: in the morning (6:00–12:00) Gus is felling trees in the clearing on the north side of [[森林|the Forest]], in the afternoon (12:00–21:00) he is stacking wood by the [[柴堆|wood pile]] on the west side of the village, and after 21:00 he goes home to sleep. Press {{键|E}} on him while he works; he says “Four logs, and my back will thank you. Mind, this fire wants feeding every day,” and you choose “Accept ‘Wood for the Fire’” in the dialogue box. Choosing “Decline” is fine too: the job stays open, and you can come back for it when you want.
2. **Cut logs**: left-click a tree with an [[斧|axe]] to chop it; a stone axe fells a tree in two blows, for 2 logs and 1 stick, so felling two trees is enough. Any tree will do, in the woods around the village or in the Forest. If you have no axe, press {{键|C}} to make a [[斧|stone axe]] from a stick, a stone and a handful of cut grass. You can chop bare-handed too, only much more slowly. See [[伐木|Woodcutting]].
3. **Hand in**: once you have 4 on you, the journal reads “All done. Back to Gus.”, and a “?” appears over Gus's head. Go back to him and press {{键|E}} to hand in.

A felled tree leaves a stump, which grows back into a full tree after {{数值|树木再生}} in-game days.
