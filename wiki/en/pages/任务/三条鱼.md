**Three for the Smoke** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[希尔德]], the fishwife of [[芦苇湾]]. She smokes fish to sell and wants 3 fish for the smoking fire, any kind at all.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

Any kind of [[鱼|fish]] counts: [[生鱼]], [[太阳鱼]], [[河鲈]], [[鳗鱼]], [[狗鱼]], [[鲶鱼]], [[金鳞鲤]], [[晨鳞鱼]]. The fish are taken when you hand them in, the first three she receives.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: cross the [[森林|Forest]] and follow the path from the clearing on its east side to the Reed Bend. In the daytime (5:00–22:00) Hilde stands by the jetty, with a “!” over her head. Press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘Three for the Smoke’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Make a fishing rod**: two [[树枝|sticks]] and two handfuls of [[干草|cut grass]] make a [[鱼竿|fishing rod]] by hand in the [[制作|Crafting]] panel ({{键|C}}); Hilde also sells fishing rods and [[鱼饵|bait]]. Used on a fishing rod, bait makes fish bite twice as fast for the next five casts.
3. **Fish**: with the rod in hand, stand on the bank or the jetty and left-click the water; see [[钓鱼|Fishing]]. By day it is mostly sunfish and perch; after dark (20:00–5:00) there are eels, and casting from the jetty into deep water brings pike.
4. **Hand in**: bring the 3 fish back to Hilde and press {{键|E}} to hand them in.

You can also catch fish in the pond and the lake of [[誓守之地]], or buy them from Hilde (she sells 4 raw fish and 1 eel) — fish bought from her count when you give them back to her.
