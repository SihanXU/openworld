**Night on the Grassland** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[布琳]], the herder. She has lost three cows this month; as soon as it gets dark, the dead walk on [[草原|the Grassland]], and the herd knows it. She asks you to keep watch with her on the Grassland for a night and defeat three of the dead.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

Only the dead defeated on the Grassland map after you take the quest count. The only dead that appear on the Grassland are the [[僵尸|zombies]] that come after dark; the [[蝙蝠|bats]] that appear alongside them do not count.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (6:00–21:00) Brin watches the cattle by the bench in the middle of the Grassland. Press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘Night on the Grassland’” in the dialogue box. The first time you are on the Grassland at dusk (17:00–20:00), you see a scene of the herd kneeling (it plays only once), and at the end of the scene Brin also asks for your help.
2. **Wait for dark**: night begins at {{数值|入夜}} (brought forward to 20:00 once the White Knight has fallen). Brin goes back to the village to sleep at 21:00; you stay on the Grassland.
3. **Fight zombies**: after dark, a night creature may appear on the Grassland 6 to 10 tiles from you every {{数值|夜行生物间隔}} seconds, sixty percent of them zombies, with at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}} at a time. Defeat three zombies. At daybreak they crumble to dust.
4. **Hand in**: go back to the Grassland the next day and press {{键|E}} on Brin to hand in.

[[老骑士]] wants three zombies defeated after dark, anywhere. If you take both quests, every zombie you defeat on the Grassland counts for both. The commission [[清剿夜行者]], which the guards sometimes give, also counts night creatures, so the same zombie counts for all three.
