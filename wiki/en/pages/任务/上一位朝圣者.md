**The Pilgrim Before** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[伊尔丝修女]]. Long ago a pilgrim made camp deep in the [[森林|Forest]] and never came back. Bring his Scallop Badge to Ilse.

The quest only appears once you have found that camp in the Forest: after you walk into the clearing in the Forest's south-east corner, Ilse will ask this of you, and a “!” appears over her head.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The [[朝圣者的扇贝]] is not taken when you hand it in; Ilse lets you keep it. What she wants is Brother Cassian's own scallop from the camp: the [[立誓扇贝]] you receive for taking the vow at [[朝圣者驿站]] does not count.

## Walkthrough

1. **Find the camp**: from [[初原村]] go south across [[草原|the Grassland]] into the Forest. In the middle of the Forest the path forks, one branch leading to the clearing in the south-east corner, where there is a rotted tent, a cold fire ring, a cairn, and an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree. Walking into the clearing plays a scene.
2. **Pick up the scallop**: a scallop lies on the ground beside the cairn; press {{键|E}} to pick it up. It can be worn round the neck straight away (of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality).
3. **Take the quest**: go back to Firstlight Village, where Sister Ilse spends the day (6:00–22:00) by the sacred fire on the square. Press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Pilgrim Before’” in the dialogue box. If the scallop is already on you, talk to her once more after accepting to hand it in.
4. **Hand in**: press {{键|E}} to hand it in.

The Forest is a combat zone, with [[夜行生物|night creatures]] after dark. Once you have found the camp, coming back to it at night (20:00–04:00) shows another scene.

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

{{剧透|Brother Cassian}}
The proclamation on the tree at the camp matches, word for word, the one the courier read out to you, except that it was written in the year 380 of the Era of the Five Sufferings, and the one it names is “Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village”. Ilse recognizes the scallop: when she was still a novice, Brother Cassian sat in the chapel and asked her to pray for him; thirty-one years ago the Church named him the Pilgrim, and later told the village that he had reached the Holy City.

At night a pale shade kneels by the camp's fire ring, its head bowed, and says: “I knelt. I knelt to him. Don't kneel.” Then it is gone.
{{/剧透}}
