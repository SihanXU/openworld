**Waking** is the main quest of the [[新手教程|tutorial]]. You come to in [[林间空地]] with a fairy called [[露米]] beside you. Get through a day and a night here, then follow the path south down the mountain.

Each stage of the tutorial is a stage of this quest: the quest journal ({{键|L}}) and the tracker in the top-right corner show the current objective, and pressing {{键|E}} on Lumi tells you how to do this stage; the tutorial's lessons (the spotlight, the key icons, the locked keys) follow the stages too. See [[新手教程#课程|Lessons]].

This quest exists only in the tutorial entered from the title screen. A proper new game begins on [[云顶台地]], and its first main quest is [[下山]].

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The eight objectives appear one after another: only when one is complete does the next appear in the journal. Each is judged by state, not route:

| Objective | How it is completed |
|---|---|
| Find something to eat | You have eaten something once |
| Gather sticks, stones and grass | You have {{物品|树枝}}, {{物品|石块}} and {{物品|干草}} on you at the same time |
| Make a stone axe | You have an axe on you |
| Fell a tree and split planks | You have {{物品|木板}} on you |
| Build a campfire and light it | There is a lit [[营火|campfire]] within 12 tiles |
| Build a house with a bed | A [[房间|bedroom]] appears on the map |
| Get through the night | The first morning (05:00) after the house is built |
| Follow the path south | Leave the clearing and reach [[山脚]] |

## Walkthrough

1. **Waking**: at 07:00 on day 1 you are lying in the clearing, {{状态|昏迷}}. The quest is there from the start, and the journal's first line reads “I woke in a clearing. I don't remember how I got here.” After about 5 seconds you wake, Lumi introduces herself, and the first objective, “Find something to eat”, appears.
2. **Eat**: press {{键|E}} to pick up the [[浆果|berries]] on the ground, press the number of that slot on the toolbar to take them in hand, and left-click to eat.
3. **Gather**: sticks, stones and cut grass lie scattered around the clearing, and slowly grow back once taken. {{键|Tab}} opens the backpack.
4. **Stone axe**: {{键|C}} opens crafting; make a [[斧|stone axe]] from a stick, a stone and a handful of cut grass.
5. **Planks**: hold the axe and left-click the trees in the woods to the east; a stone axe brings one down in two blows, giving logs; with the axe on you, turn the logs into planks in the crafting panel.
6. **Campfire**: {{键|B}} opens building; set down a campfire; {{键|C}} makes a torch (a stick and grass); hold it and left-click the campfire.
7. **The house**: place walls within the outline drawn on the ground, close them in and fit a door, put a bed inside, and finally place a ceiling overhead (in building mode choose the floor and press {{键|R}} to switch it to a ceiling). Once a bedroom appears on the map, the objective is complete.
8. **The night**: after dark [[夜行生物|night creatures]] come out. Shut the door, eat something before bed, and press {{键|E}} on the bed to sleep. On the first morning (05:00) after the house is built, the path to the south opens.
9. **Down the mountain**: follow the path out of the south of the clearing; once you reach Mountain Foot, the quest is complete. The tutorial played on its own ends here, and you return to the title screen (see [[新手教程#开始与结束|Start and end]]).

For the details of each step, and the keys and locks of each lesson, see [[新手教程|Tutorial]].

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

At each new stage, Lumi says a line:

- Waking: “Hey! You're finally awake! I'm Lumi, I'm a fairy, and from today wherever you go, I go.”
- Having eaten: “Better, right? To make things, we first need sticks, stones and grass.”
- With planks: “Planks! It gets dark early in the woods, and after dark things come out to wander. Light a fire first.”
- With the house built: “A home! Now we just have to get through tonight. Eat something before bed — the berries keep growing back. As soon as it's light, we set off.”
- Daybreak: “It's morning! You made it. The path south is open — down the mountain we go!”

Press {{键|E}} on Lumi and she says “Now: Waking.”, followed by the current objective and how to do this stage, for example “Hold the axe and left-click a tree; it falls and becomes logs. Then left-click the logs with the axe to split them into planks.”

## History

- 2026-10-04: The tutorial was added; at the time its stages were kept on the player and appeared only in what Lumi said.
- 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system: the tutorial's stages became the stages of the main quest “Waking”, the objectives are shown in the quest journal and the tracker, each stage records a line in the journal, and the tutorial's lessons follow the stages.
- 2026-10-20: {{键|Tab}} now opens the backpack, with a row of tabs above it switching to the other panels (the old keys such as {{键|I}} still work); changing the toolbar row became {{键|Shift}}+{{键|Tab}} or {{键|Shift}}+mouse wheel; every key can be changed in [[选项|Options]], with two presets, scheme A and scheme B.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
