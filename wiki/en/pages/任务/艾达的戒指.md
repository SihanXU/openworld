**Edda's Ring** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[艾达]], the farm woman who survived at the burned farmstead on [[东大路]]. When the White Knight passed by, he took everything that shone, including her late husband's wedding band, which she had hung on the doorpost. The ring is in his kingdom at the far end of the road.

The quest has no prerequisites, but you can only meet Edda once you have crossed the river.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The quest only accepts things of the kind [[婚戒|Wedding Band]]. It is taken when you hand it in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Find Edda**: after crossing the river, head east through [[朝圣者驿站]], [[朝圣者之路]] and [[跪石原]] onto the East Road. Halfway along, north of the road, is the burned farmstead; walk into the farmstead, then into the barn in its south-west corner, and Edda comes out to talk to you. After that, press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘Edda's Ring’” in the dialogue box. She hides in the barn all day.
2. **Find the ring**: the wedding band is in the kingdom of thorns at the eastern end of the East Road, in the south-east part of the ring of thorns: a gold ring on the ground (called “Golden Wedding Band”). [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] stands in the ring; walking into the ring plays the kingdom's scene, and he will fight you. You can defeat him first and then pick it up, or slip round and pick it up during the fight.
3. **Hand in**: go back to Edda and press {{键|E}} to hand in. Once the White Knight has fallen, Edda moves to [[初原村]] and works in the vegetable garden in the south-east of the village by day (7:00–21:00); find her there to hand in.

You do not need to have taken the quest to pick up the ring. If you pick it up first and take the quest afterwards, talk to her once more after accepting to hand it in.

## Rewards

{{任务奖励}}

When you hand in, Edda gives you two [[鸡蛋|eggs]], a loaf of [[面包|bread]] and “Edda's Work Gloves” ([[手套|Gloves]], Defense 1). The eggs are raw; you can eat them as they are, or put them on the fire to make [[熟鸡蛋|boiled eggs]].

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

{{剧透|What she says in the barn}}
In the barn Edda says: the days grew too short and the wheat all died; then the things of the night came; and later a knight in white rode by and took the door hinges, the pots and her husband's ring. On the night the barn caught fire, it was a man in grey who pulled her out of the smoke, and she asks you not to tell the village. When you hand in, she says: she had hung the ring on the door so that he could find his way home.
{{/剧透}}
