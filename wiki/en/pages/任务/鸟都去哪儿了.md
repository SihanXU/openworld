**Where the Birds Went** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[Orrin]], the charcoal burner of [[The Charcoal Dell]]. No birdsong is heard in [[The Forest|the Forest]]; Orrin says the birds all settle every dusk on the old oak at the west end of his dell, without a sound. He asks you to ask [[Gus]] whether the Forest has always been this quiet (Gus will not talk to him about it), and to pick up 3 [[Feather|feathers]] from under the old oak for him: he wants to know what birds they are.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The two objectives can be done in either order. The feathers are taken when you hand them in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: go to the Charcoal Dell and find Orrin, who keeps by the clamp by day (6:00–21:00); press {{key|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘Where the Birds Went’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Ask Gus**: in the morning (6:00–12:00) Gus is felling trees in the clearing on the north side of the Forest, and in the afternoon he is stacking wood by the wood pile on the west side of [[Firstlight Village]]. Press E on him and he says: “It went quiet the night the bridge came down. Every bird in the wood went up at once and flew west. I've not told anyone. Who wants to hear a woodcutter talk about birds?”
3. **Pick up feathers**: the old oak is at the west end of the Charcoal Dell, ringed by trees, with a path leading to it. Four feathers lie scattered beneath it; press {{key|E}} to pick up 3.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Orrin and press {{key|E}} to hand in.

## Dialogue

{{quest dialogue}}

{{spoiler|What the birds are waiting for}}
When you hand in, Orrin says: every kind of bird is there — thrushes, goldfinches, jays, birds that never roost together; and Gus says it began the night the bridge went. “Then it isn't my oak they want. It's what can be seen from it.”

Go to the old oak at dusk (17:00–21:00) and a scene plays: hundreds of birds crowd the branches, not one making a sound, all of them facing east. Lumi says that, like the kneeling cattle on [[The Grassland|the Grassland]], the birds know before people do that something has happened. See [[The Charcoal Dell|The Charcoal Dell]].
