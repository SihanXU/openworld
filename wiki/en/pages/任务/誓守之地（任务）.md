**The Held Ground** is a main quest of Chapter 1, between [[The First Seal|The First Seal]] and [[The Crown of Thorns|The Crown of Thorns]]. Once [[The White Knight Alberic]] falls you receive [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]]. This quest shows you how to use it to enter [[The Held Ground]], speak to [[Alberic|Alberic]], who keeps it as its steward, and use it again to go back to where you were.

The quest begins by itself when you receive the Oath-Thorn, and completes by itself when the thorn brings you back out, with nothing to hand in. Then the main quest [[The Crown of Thorns|The Crown of Thorns]] begins. The homecoming scene in Firstlight Village and the end of Part One both wait until this quest is complete.

The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the place [[The Held Ground]].

## Walkthrough

1. **The Oath-Thorn**: once you defeat the White Knight on [[The East Road]] and the scene of the Crown of Thorns has played, you find a thorn that is still green in the briars where the knight fell, and receive [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]]. Lumi says it wants to be used on you. The quest begins here.
2. **Going in**: choose the Oath-Thorn on the toolbar ({{key|1–0}}) so it is in your hand, then left-click: it is used on you, and you enter the Held Ground, standing below the sword planted at its heart. It does not open in the middle of a fight (“Not now, not in the middle of a fight.”); wait a moment and try again. The first time you go in, Alberic's scene plays: he asks your name and gives you 30 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
3. **Speaking to Alberic**: Alberic has a “?” over his head. Press {{key|E}} on him and he tells you to ask him about the Held Ground, where you can see what each ring of mist asks before it lifts and what can be built there, and that you leave by using the thorn on yourself again. For the widenings see [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]].
4. **Coming back**: hold the Oath-Thorn and left-click again. You return to where you stood before you went in, and the quest completes. From now on you can go in and out of the Held Ground this way whenever you like.
