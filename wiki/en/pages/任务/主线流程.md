This page sets out, in order, how to play through the main line. The main line is a chain of main [[任务|quests]]: when one ends the next begins by itself, with no one to talk to, and there is always one under way. A new game wakes on [[云顶台地]] and goes [[下山|down from the mountain]] to [[渡口]] and [[初原村]]; Chapter 1's [[古墓里的失踪者]], [[守夜之备]] and [[第一印]] lead up to the defeat of [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], and [[荆棘冠（任务）|The Crown of Thorns]] brings the Crown of Thorns back to the village; after that comes the old main line from before Chapter 1, [[屠龙之路]], through [[余烬镇]] to [[烬峰魔龙]].

The main quest under way is the tracked quest by default: the tracker in the top-right corner of the screen lists its unfinished objectives, markers on the minimap, the world map and the edges of the screen point to where to go, and the quest journal ({{键|L}}) records every step you have taken. Press {{键|E}} on [[露米]] and she tells you the current objective and a hint. Each step's objective is a state, not a fixed route: whatever way you reach it counts. Besides the main line, Lumi begins a few [[任务#指引：露米自己开口|guide quests]] when the moment is right (skill points, professions, fishing, farming); they are listed alongside the main line in her panel and do not affect the main line's tracking.

Story content is kept inside spoiler blocks. For the story itself see [[主线剧情|Main Story]] and [[第一章|Chapter 1]].

## Tutorial: Waking

{{主条目|醒来}}

The [[新手教程|tutorial]], entered from the title screen, has only one main quest: [[醒来]]. You come to in [[林间空地]], and over one day and night with Lumi you learn to eat, gather, [[制作|craft]], fell trees, light a fire, [[建造|build a house]] and get through the first night; each stage brings a few lessons. At daybreak you walk out along the path to the south, the quest completes, the tutorial ends and you return to the title screen. The tutorial is not saved, and a proper new game does not pass through it.

## Prologue: Down from the Mountain

{{主条目|下山}}

A new game begins at {{数值|新游戏时刻}} on day 1: you wake in a run-down Sunwatch Post hut on Cloudtop, your [[饱食度|satiety]] not full and nothing on you. The main quest [[下山]] begins at once.

1. **Cloudtop**: Lumi introduces herself and points out that the only way down is the gap in the south cliff. Outside the door are [[浆果|berries]], [[树枝|sticks]], [[石块|stones]] and [[干草|cut grass]]: press {{键|E}} to pick things up, and left-click with them in hand to eat. Press {{键|C}} to make a [[斧|stone axe]] from a stick, a stone and a handful of cut grass; you will need it later for felling trees and mending the bridge. The table in the hut holds the log [[守夜人的日志]] wants you to read.
2. **The shrine stone at Mountain Foot**: go down through the gap and follow the gentle slope down below the high ground of Mountain Foot; the path passes the [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stone]] in a ring of flowers, and the objective is complete.
3. **Do something the world has never seen**: right-clicking the stone with something in hand, putting something on it with {{键|G}} or striking it all do. The world gives this act an outcome and records it in the [[织线簿|Threadbook]] (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]); with no AI connected the default outcome is used, which is usually that nothing happens, but the objective is completed all the same. Mountain Foot also has a waypoint statue.
4. **The Ford**: leave Mountain Foot by its southern exit down to the cliff steps north of the Ford. The ferrywoman [[赫斯珀]] comes to meet you and tells you about the Ford: the ferry rope was torn by a storm, and a rider in white tore three planks out of the little bridge. She asks you to go and help in Firstlight Village to the west first, and gives you half a loaf of [[面包|bread]].
5. **Firstlight Village**: from the Ford's camp, follow the road west into the village. [[伊尔丝修女]], [[佩尔]], Lumi and the elder [[罗万]] say a few words, [[下山]] completes and [[古墓里的失踪者]] begins.

The prologue never traps you: do nothing at the shrine stone and go straight down to the Ford, and the earlier objectives are ticked together; however you reach Firstlight Village, Down from the Mountain completes as you enter.

## Chapter 1: The First Seal

### Lost in the Barrow

{{主条目|古墓里的失踪者}}

Rowan says that [[莉娜]] and [[奥斯卡]] from the village are trapped in the barrow.

1. Leave Firstlight Village by its southern exit and head south: [[草原|the Grassland]] → [[森林|the Forest]] (the exit to Thornwood Hollow is in the Forest's south-west corner) → [[荆棘谷]]. Thornwood Hollow has slimes, spiders and wolves even by day. Between two standing stones at the bottom of the hollow is the entrance to the barrow, with a waypoint statue beside it.
2. The first floor of [[古墓|the Old Barrow]]: Lina in the western cell (press E on her); the stairs down are in the eastern cell.
3. [[荆棘骸骨堂]] (the second floor): Oskar in the south-east cell (press E on him); the stairs down are in the north-east corner.
4. [[守墓者圣所]] (the third floor): defeat the warden (a [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]]).
5. The journal reads “Back to Rowan”. Return to Firstlight Village and press {{键|E}} on Rowan to hand in; [[守夜之备]] begins at once.

The Grassland, the Forest and Thornwood Hollow on the way are all combat zones, with [[夜行生物|night creatures]] after dark; Firstlight Village and the Ford are peaceful zones. If you are hurt you can soak in Firstlight Village's [[初原村#区域|healing spring]].

Side quests that fit well into this stretch: [[给锅里添点东西]] (the cows and chickens on the Grassland), [[米娅的戒指]] (Thornwood Hollow), [[清剿荆棘谷]] (from level 2), [[佩尔的涂鸦]] (there is one drawing at the barrow door and one in the village), and the daily commission [[给火堆添柴]]. The [[委托板|commission board]] in Firstlight Village often holds commissions the village has made by itself, too.

### The Vigil

{{主条目|守夜之备}}

Sister Ilse wants three things:

1. Two flasks of lamp oil from the cellar ([[磨坊地窖]]) of [[水车磨坊]] to the west. There are two slimes and a spider in the cellar.
2. Three sprigs of dawnbloom ([[草药|herb]]) on [[晨光丘]] to the north. There are two wolves at the foot of the hill.
3. The old bell rung in the north-east bell tower of [[初晓礼拜堂]] on top of Dawn Hill (just walk into that corner).

With all three gathered, go back to Ilse and hand them in; [[第一印]] begins at once.

### The First Seal

{{主条目|第一印}}

The First Seal strings the second half of Chapter 1 into one quest, its objectives appearing one after another.

#### The night of thanks

- At dusk (17:00–21:00) in Firstlight Village, a scene plays of Ilse telling a story (it can be missed).
- After dark (21:00–24:00) in Firstlight Village, the night of thanks plays. The first objective is complete.

{{剧透|The night of thanks}}
Rowan gives thanks for the sacred flame and for those who went into the barrow, and Ilse recites the Church's words: “Suffering is not God's abandonment.” The screen goes dark, and a man in a grey cloak is standing before the sacred flame, lifting the fire into a lantern. Holt shouts “Thief!”; the man says “Not tonight,” and walks away east, the river freezing under his feet as he crosses. The sacred flame goes out.

In the storytelling scene, when Ilse tells of Astra's heart pierced by the Holy Lance and the sun rising, Lumi is trembling.
{{/剧透}}

#### Next morning: the letter

Wait in Firstlight Village until after 5:00 the next day. There is no bed for you in the village; to make time pass faster, you can make a bed yourself, set it down and sleep (see [[睡觉|Sleeping]], [[建造|Building]]). In the morning a scene plays in the village of the letter's arrival; you receive a [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], and the second objective is complete.

If you go to the Ford after that night and before the morning, Hesper tells you what she saw.

{{剧透|The letter}}
A courier from the Holy City proclaims: the Seal-bearers are failing, the Long Night is returning, and the Pilgrimage is hereby called; one has come down from the mountain with a fairy, and the Church names that one the Pilgrim. The courier stares at Lumi for a long time and says nothing. Ilse says she does not believe in coincidence, and tells you to come to her when you are ready.
{{/剧透}}

#### The Pilgrim's Road (optional)

After the letter, an optional objective appears, “Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins”: Ilse now gives the side quest [[朝圣之路]], and accepting it completes this objective. The Pilgrim's Road sends you to ask [[芬尼克]] (about the White Knight's moves), [[乔斯]] (how to mend the bridge) and [[赫斯珀]] (what she saw); you can carry on without doing it.

After the letter there are a few more side quests: [[老骑士]] (Fennick) and [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] (Joss). Also available at any time are [[日之度量]] and [[守夜人的日志]] (Nessa), [[草原守夜]] (Brin), and, once you have found the camp in the Forest, [[上一位朝圣者]] (Ilse).

#### Across the river

Cross the river at the [[渡口|Ford]]. There are several ways; pick any one:

- **Mend the little bridge**: the broken bridge south of the jetty is three tiles short. Fell trees for logs, and with an axe on you press {{键|C}} to make them into planks (two planks to a log), then stand at the edge of the gap and press {{键|G}} to set a plank down on the water. Lay all three and you can walk across.
- **Take the ferry**: get the rope out of the chest in the camp, press G on the winch at the jetty to fit it, then press E on Hesper empty-handed, and she poles you over on the raft (once only).
- **Fell the dead tree**: stand on the west side of the dead tree at the narrowest part of the river and strike it three times with an axe; it falls across the river.
- **Freeze the river**: trade food to [[托宾]] in Firstlight Village for the Wand of Frost on his cart, hold it and left-click the river water, freezing your way across. The ice melts after a while.
- Any other way the world learns.

Should every one of these ways become unusable, Hesper will pole you across herself. Once you stand on the far bank, the river objective is complete and the next one appears, “Take the Pilgrim's Vow at the Pilgrims' Rest”. For details see [[渡口#区域|“Ways across the river” on the Ford's page]].

#### The Pilgrim's Road

{{另见|朝圣者驿站|朝圣者之路|荆棘修道院|跪石原}}

The eastern exit on the Ford's far bank does not lead straight to the East Road: going east you come in turn to [[朝圣者驿站]], [[朝圣者之路]] and [[跪石原]], with [[荆棘修道院]] north of the middle of the Pilgrim's Road. None of the objectives in this stretch block the way; you can walk straight through to the White Knight. Do them in order and you get extra experience, a [[立誓扇贝]] and [[荆棘骑士团印章|The Order's Seal]].

1. **The vow at the Rest**: the Pilgrims' Rest is a peaceful zone. The first time you come after the letter, [[凯乌斯司铎]] comes to meet you and asks you to take the vow in [[五伤礼拜堂]] to the north. Walk up to the altar of the Five, and he leads you in reciting the Five Sufferings; the objective is complete, and you receive the Sworn Scallop and 20 experience. The Holy See's notice on the square, the prayer of the Five Sufferings in the chapel and the pilgrims' register in [[朝圣者客舍]] can all be read with {{键|E}}. The pilgrim [[莫德]] and her daughter [[温]] stay at the hostel.
2. **The five shrines**: go east into the Pilgrim's Road. Along the road stand five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]], one for each Suffering; hold the thing each one asks for and press {{键|G}} to set it on it: [[荆棘冠之龛]] wants something worn on the head, [[鞭刑柱之龛]] a rope, [[饥刑之秤之龛]] something to eat, [[钉刑圣木之龛]] a piece of metal, [[穿心圣枪之龛]] a light (a torch or a candle). They can all be picked up on the road: a pilgrim's hat, a grass rope plaited by a penitent, bread in an abandoned cart, a dented helmet, a candle. The shrines do not accept holy relics, important things, tokens or anything a quest needs you to keep: neither the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]] nor The Order's Seal can be set on them. The penitent [[以斯拉修士]] kneels halfway along the road. There are two [[狼|wolves]] on the road, and [[僵尸|zombies]] at night.
3. **The Abbey of the Thorn**: once all five are lit, take the path north from the middle of the road up the hill. In the courtyard there are two [[骷髅|skeletons]] and a [[幽灵|ghost]]. In [[骑士团大厅]] to the north you get the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] (there is one more skeleton in the hall); in [[修道院地下墓室]] beneath the shrine to the east, defeat the last keeper, [[温德尔院长]], and pick up The Order's Seal he drops. With both in hand you gain 40 experience.
4. **The Kneeling Field**: go back to the road and keep east; walking into the Kneeling Field completes the objective, and the last objective appears, “Bring down the White Knight Alberic on the East Road”. At night ghosts appear on the dry grass.

{{剧透|The Rest, the abbey and the Kneeling Field}}
- As you take the vow, Father Caius says: “That Seal-bearer has kept that road for three hundred years. The Holy See would have you defeat him and bring back the Crown of Thorns — the first of the five Instruments. …Someone should have gone to relieve him. That is no concern of a pilgrim's.”
- The Writ of Relief says that in the fortieth year of Alberic's bearing the Seal a successor was to be sent; its wax seal is unbroken, and it was never sent. On the back, a line in small writing: “No successor willing to bear it can be found. Let him keep watch. He will not notice the difference.” Once you have found it, talk to Caius again and he admits he knew all along.
- The kneeling stones of the Kneeling Field are carved with the names of earlier pilgrims and the years they held the Crown, and every one records that they would not kneel; the newest stone has no name carved on it yet.
{{/剧透}}

#### The road to the White Knight

1. Take the eastern exit of [[跪石原]] onto [[东大路]]. North of the road is a burned farmstead; walk into the farmstead and then into the barn in its south-west corner and you meet [[艾达]] (the side quest [[艾达的戒指]]). There are two wolves and a boar on the road.
2. On the stretch of road west of the ring of thorns, [[灰衣人]] comes out to stop you (only after the letter). There is no fight; he says his piece and leaves.
3. There is a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] by the road. Wake it: if you fall, you can come back to life here (on Normal difficulty), and beside it you can change Lumi's [[恩赐|boon]].
4. Walk into the ring of thorns, and once [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] has spoken, the fight begins. In the story he has 300 health and 1 armor, and his skills deal only four tenths of the damage they do in the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]]. Before each attack he marks the tiles he will strike; step out of the marks and you will not be hit:
   - Arrow of Conquest: a straight line three tiles wide in front of him. Do not stand directly in front of him.
   - White-Feather Volley: five landing spots around you.
   - Thorns Bend the Knee: a ring of thorns around him. Stand inside the ring or far away from it, not on the ring itself. If the thorns pierce you, you are Entranced for a moment.
   - When his health drops to {{数值|首领二阶段}} he enters his second phase, attacking faster and harder.
   Inside the ring you can also pick up [[第九根钉子|The Ninth Nail]], a [[婚戒|Wedding Band]] and two pieces of gear.
5. Defeat him and the scene of the Crown of Thorns plays; you receive the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]; Lumi gains her first [[恩赐|boon]], “Crown's Insight” (experience +100%); and then you receive [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]]. He also drops 4 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and, if you have chosen a [[职业|profession]], a piece of [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]]. [[第一印]] completes and [[荆棘冠（任务）|The Crown of Thorns]] begins. From now on night begins earlier, at 20:00, and ends at 5:30 (see [[昼夜|Day and Night]]).

{{剧透|The Grey Man on the road and the Crown of Thorns}}
- The Grey Man says: what those Seals hold locked is not the sky but Astra, and the First Long Night wears his face; each Seal-bearer you bring down wakes him a little more, and the ever-longer nights you see are him waking. To Lumi he says “Of all of them, you should know best… No, you won't remember.”
- In the ring of thorns the knight says: “None cross that do not kneel. Another pilgrim. They always send another. I keep watch. I… keep watch. I do not remember what I keep. Kneel.”
- Fallen, the knight kneels and takes off the Crown of Thorns: “Take it. It was never mine to wear.” A cold light spills from the crown and flows to Lumi like water finding an old riverbed. The Grey Man appears and gathers the rest of the light into the stolen flame: “Every Seal you break makes the night a little longer. Go and ask your Church why.” Then he leaves to the east.
{{/剧透}}

### The Crown of Thorns

{{主条目|荆棘冠（任务）}}

Go back to Firstlight Village (on foot, or by teleporting from the world map to the village's statue). Walking back takes you through the Pilgrims' Rest, where [[凯乌斯司铎]] comes to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. …” As soon as you enter the village the homecoming scene plays, [[荆棘冠（任务）|The Crown of Thorns]] completes, the chapter card “Chapter 1: The First Seal” appears, and [[屠龙之路]] begins at once.

{{剧透|The homecoming}}
Rowan is furious that the heretic still has the village's fire; Ilse says “Ever since he was small, he has carried things too heavy for him”; Nessa says the sun set four minutes early tonight. The chapter card: “The White Knight knelt and gave up the Crown of Thorns. The Grey Man went east with the light. And the night came a little sooner.”
{{/剧透}}

### After Chapter 1

- Right-click yourself with [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]] to enter [[誓守之地]]: [[阿尔贝里克]], the Crown of Thorns laid down, is its steward there; press E on the longsword at its center to put materials in and expand the land in three steps (see [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]).
- Once the knight has fallen, the East Road's exit to Emberfall opens. The journal for the first stage of [[屠龙之路]] reads “Chapter 2: the road east to Emberfall.” This “Chapter 2” is a name carried over from the old line, not [[第二章|the chapter of the Second Seal]].

## The old main line: The Dragon Road

{{主条目|屠龙之路}}

The stretch from Emberfall to the dragon is the main line made before Chapter 1, older than the setting of the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]], and unconnected with the story of the Seal-bearers and the Grey Man. It is now one main quest, [[屠龙之路]], which can be played right through to the ending card; it is planned to be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal. See [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]].

### Emberfall

Enter [[余烬镇]] by the East Road's eastern exit; a line of narration plays at the edge of the town, and the first objective is complete. The town's elder, [[玛拉]], is on the square by day; press E on her empty-handed: she tells you about the dragon on Cinder Peak, says the smith and the herbalist can help, and gives you 2 [[面包|bread]].

### Preparing to slay the dragon

Six objectives appear in the journal: iron ore 5 and coal 3 for Garrick, fireweed 3 and frostcap 3 for Wren, Dragonsbane forged by Garrick, and the fireproof draught brewed by Wren.

- **The smith [[加里克]]** (by the anvil): 5 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[煤炭|Coal]]. Both are in [[旧矿坑]] north of the town: break [[铁矿脉|iron veins]] (2 iron ore each) and [[煤层|coal seams]] (2 coal each) with a [[镐|pickaxe]]; there is an old pickaxe at the mine's mouth. The [[独眼巨人|cyclops]] at the very bottom carries 2 iron ore. The 4 iron ore dropped by the White Knight count too.
- **The herbalist [[雷恩]]** (by the cauldron): 3 [[火焰草|Fireweed]] and 3 [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]]. Fireweed grows in the open ground of [[初原村]], frostcap in the shady parts of the [[森林|Forest]], and both slowly grow back once picked. Both places are west of the river, so you have to go back and cross at the Ford (waypoint statues you have woken save you the walk).
- Things must be handed over one at a time: press {{键|G}} on them to hand over the thing in your hand, and they take it and say a line. Only what you hand over after talking to Mara counts. Once you have enough, press {{键|E}} on them: Garrick forges [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]], and Wren brews two [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draughts]].
- Once you have both, a line of narration plays in Emberfall and all six objectives are ticked.

### Cinder Pass

Take Emberfall's eastern exit up onto [[烬岭山道]]. Lower down are two [[霜狼|frost wolves]], higher up two [[火焰小鬼|fire imps]] (ranged, slowly replenished), and before the summit exit a [[石魔像|stone golem]]. Halfway up there is a lit campfire where you can rest. Walk into the entrance passage of [[魔龙巢穴]], a line of narration plays, and the last objective appears.

### The dragon of Cinder Peak

Before going in, hold a Fireproof Draught and right-click yourself to drink it: for 300 seconds fire does only a quarter of its damage, and you cannot be set alight. Keep the other bottle to drink once the first wears off.

[[烬峰魔龙|The dragon of Cinder Peak]] has 700 health, breathes fire at range and bites up close. Do not stand in the line of its fire. The first time you hit it while its health is below half, it summons 3 [[幼龙|dragon whelps]] (only once). Dragonsbane deals double damage to the dragon and the whelps.

Once the dragon falls, [[屠龙之路]] completes, any whelps left scatter by themselves, and the ending scene and the ending card play: “The dragon of Cinder Peak. The dragon is dead. Tonight Emberfall can sleep, and the road east is open. Everything the world learned from you is recorded in the Threadbook (J).” The main line ends here; afterwards you can go on roaming the world freely.

## Skipping and order

- Each step looks only at the state of things, not at the route. Take crossing the river: just standing on the far bank counts, even before the letter. If you cross before the letter, Lumi says “The village still needs us — don't go too far yet”; The First Seal still goes in order, and when the letter comes the river objective already counts as done, so you go straight on to “Take the Pilgrim's Vow at the Pilgrims' Rest”.
- None of the objectives on the Pilgrim's Road (the vow, the five shrines, the abbey, the Kneeling Field) block the way: skip any of them and you can still walk all the way to the East Road. When the White Knight falls, they are all ticked together.
- You can go and fight the White Knight at any time. As soon as he falls, The First Seal completes and pays its rewards; [[古墓里的失踪者]], [[守夜之备]] and [[朝圣之路]] are treated as “passed” and end: Lina and Oskar come home by themselves, and the storytelling, the night of thanks and the letter no longer play. Any of Lost in the Barrow, The Vigil and The Pilgrim's Road whose objectives were all met at that moment still pays its rewards. See [[任务#被剧情越过的任务|quests the story has passed]].
- The Ford has a safeguard rule, so the ways across the river never all break; at worst Hesper takes you across herself.

## Tips

- Wake the [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] first wherever there is one. A woken statue is your [[难度|resting place]], and you can teleport back to it from the [[世界地图|World Map]]; going back and forth across the Ford and returning west of the river for herbs after reaching Emberfall all rely on it.
- Think about where you are going before you head out at night: peaceful zones (Firstlight Village, the Ford, the Pilgrims' Rest, Millbrook, Cloudtop, Emberfall, the Held Ground) have no monsters at night, while the other open-air maps get zombies and bats.
- Forgotten what to do? Check the tracker in the top-right corner, open the quest journal ({{键|L}}), or press {{键|E}} on Lumi.

## History

- 2026-10-02: The first main line: mend the Ford's little bridge, follow the East Road to Emberfall, prepare to slay the dragon, cross Cinder Pass and defeat the dragon, in seven chapters in all. The same day the first prologue was added (Cloudtop, the hillside, Mountain Foot).
- 2026-10-03: Firstlight Village and the rescue in the barrow were added before the little bridge.
- 2026-10-04: The prologue became the tutorial in the clearing; Cloudtop and the hillside were removed.
- 2026-10-06: Chapter 1, “The First Seal”, was made: the night of thanks, the letter, the Pilgrim's Road, the White Knight on the East Road and the Crown of Thorns; Emberfall could only be reached after the White Knight fell. New games went back to starting on Cloudtop, and the tutorial became a separate option.
- 2026-10-07: The main quests were joined into one chain with one always under way (Down from the Mountain, Lost in the Barrow, The Vigil, The First Seal, The Crown of Thorns); the barrow was deepened to three floors; The Vigil and its four maps and the Held Ground were added; The Pilgrim's Road became a side quest.
- 2026-10-08: [[下山]] and [[第一印]] pay their rewards on completion; the main line's stages only move forward (handing in [[朝圣之路]] after crossing the river no longer sends it back to “mend the broken bridge”); the depths of [[旧矿坑]] got a gold vein, so [[扩建誓守之地 III|Expanding the Held Ground III]] can be completed.
- 2026-10-09: The whole main line became quests: the numbered main-line stages (0, 0.5 … 7) were dropped, and the main line is now seven main quests, the tutorial being [[醒来]] and Emberfall to the dragon being [[屠龙之路]]; the objective box on screen was replaced by the quest tracker in the top-right corner, with markers on the map.
- 2026-10-12: The Ford's far bank no longer leads straight to the East Road: The First Seal gained the stretch of the Pilgrim's Road between the river crossing and the White Knight (the vow at the Pilgrims' Rest, the five shrines, the Abbey of the Thorn, the Kneeling Field).
