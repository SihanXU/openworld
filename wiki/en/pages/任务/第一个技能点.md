**The First Skill Point** is one of [[露米]]'s [[任务#指引：露米自己开口|guide quests]]: the first time you have an unspent skill point (usually on reaching level 2), she speaks up by herself and shows you how to open the [[技能|skill tree]] and learn a skill.

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: in a proper game (the [[新手教程|tutorial]] does not count), when you have at least 1 unspent skill point, Lumi pipes up and the quest begins by itself, with the prompt “New guide: The First Skill Point” in the bottom-left corner. The red number on the skills button in the bottom-right corner is the number of unspent points.
2. **Learn a skill**: press {{键|K}} to open the skill tree; the lit skills are the ones you can learn now, and one click learns it. The general skills (the gathering, crafting, combat branches and so on) can be learned by anyone; the profession branches wait until you have [[职业|chosen a profession]].
3. **Complete**: learn any skill, and the quest completes, with a little experience from Lumi.

The main line is not swapped for this quest: what is tracked by default is still the main quest under way. To see its objectives, open the quest journal ({{键|L}}), or press {{键|E}} on Lumi — her “Main guide” page lists the guide quests alongside the main line.
