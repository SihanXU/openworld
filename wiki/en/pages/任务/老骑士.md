**The Old Templar** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[Fennick]], the old knight who sits on the bench on the square. He will not let anyone who has not survived a night in the wild challenge a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]]: after dark, defeat three of the wandering dead outside the village, then walk back on your own.

It can only be taken after the Church's letter (see [[The First Seal]]).

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

Only [[zombie|zombies]] count, on any map, but they must be defeated after you take the quest. Zombies are [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] that appear after dark: they only appear in open-air combat zones, never in peaceful zones such as [[Firstlight Village]] or [[The Ford]].

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (7:00–22:00) Fennick sits on the bench on the south side of the square in Firstlight Village. Press {{key|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘The Old Templar’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Wait for dark**: night begins at {{value|入夜}} (brought forward to 20:00 once the White Knight has fallen). The places nearest the village where zombies appear at night are [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] to the south and [[Dawn Hill]] to the north; [[Millbrook]] to the west and the Ford to the east are peaceful zones, where none appear.
3. **Fight zombies**: after dark, in open-air combat zones a night creature may appear 6 to 10 tiles from you every {{value|夜行生物间隔}} seconds, sixty percent of them zombies and forty percent [[bat|bats]], with at most {{value|夜行生物上限}} at a time. Defeat three zombies. At daybreak they crumble to dust.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Fennick and press {{key|E}} to hand in. After 22:00 he goes off to sleep, and comes out again at 7:00 the next day.

[[Night on the Grassland]] wants three of the dead defeated on the Grassland. If you take both quests, every zombie you defeat on the Grassland counts for both. The commission [[The Night-born]], which the guards sometimes give, also counts night creatures, so the same zombie counts for all three.
