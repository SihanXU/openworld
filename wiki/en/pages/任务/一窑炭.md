**Wood for the Clamp** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[奥林]], the charcoal burner of [[烧炭谷]]. His back can no longer carry logs, and he needs 6 [[木头|logs]] to stack his next clamp; in exchange he offers 4 lumps of [[煤炭|coal]] from the last burn and a hat.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The logs are taken when you hand them in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: cross the [[森林|Forest]] and follow the fork on its west side to the Charcoal Dell. In the daytime (6:00–21:00) Orrin is by the clamp in the middle of the dell, with a “!” over his head. Press {{键|E}} on him and choose “Accept ‘Wood for the Clamp’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Fell trees**: chop trees with an [[斧|axe]]; each tree takes two blows and falls for 2 logs and 1 stick (see [[伐木|Woodcutting]]). The coppices north and south of the dell and the Forest are full of trees; [[格斯]] has ready-cut logs on his wood pile in [[初原村]] too, but those are Gus's.
3. **Hand in**: bring the 6 logs back to Orrin and press {{键|E}} to hand them in.

[[给火堆添柴]] also wants logs (4 a day). Take both quests together and one trip cutting 10 logs covers them both.
