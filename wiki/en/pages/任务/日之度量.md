**The Measure of Days** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[Nessa Quill]], who compiles the almanac. She says the statues are older than the Church and hum at sunset; with readings from just three of them, she can work out how fast the days are shortening. Wake any three [[waypoint statue|waypoint statues]] (press {{key|E}} on each).

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

What counts is the number of statues woken in the whole world, including those woken before you took the quest. The quest description mentions the statues at Mountain Foot, in the village, at the Ford and on the East Road, but in fact any three of the seven will do:

| Statue | Map |
|---|---|
| Cloudtop | The snowfield on the east side of [[Cloudtop]] |
| Mountain Foot statue | South-east of the shrine stone on [[Mountain Foot]] |
| The Ford | Beside the path down from the stone steps at [[The Ford]] |
| Firstlight | West side of the square in [[Firstlight Village]] |
| Thornwood Hollow | North of the barrow entrance in [[Thornwood Hollow]] |
| Dawn Hill | The top of [[Dawn Hill]] |
| The East Road | By the road before the kingdom of thorns on [[The East Road]] |

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day Nessa copies her tables on the east side of the square in Firstlight Village (6:00–17:00), and in the evening goes to the road junction east of the village to time the sunset (17:00–23:00). Press {{key|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Measure of Days’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Wake the statues**: walk up to a sleeping statue and press E to wake it, and the statue lights up. On the way down the mountain in a new game there is one each at Mountain Foot, the Ford and Firstlight Village, none of them far from the main road.
3. **Hand in**: go back to Nessa and press {{key|E}} to hand in. If you had already woken three when you took the quest, talk to her once more after accepting to hand it in.

A statue you have woken becomes your [[Difficulty|resting place]], and you can teleport to it from the [[World Map|World Map]].

## Dialogue

{{quest dialogue}}

{{spoiler|The last pages of the almanac}}
When you hand in, Nessa says the sun is setting earlier week by week, and faster than the old almanacs say; she cannot make out the last few pages of the almanac at all. As Chapter 1 is designed, this almanac was written before the First Long Night fell, and the handwriting on its last pages begs someone to “stop him” (see [[Main Story|Main Story]]). There is nothing in the game yet that lets you read these pages.
