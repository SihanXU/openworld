**Something for the Pot** is a side quest of [[Firstlight Village]] given by [[Marta]], who minds the pot by the fire on the square. She wants 3 [[Raw Beef|Raw Beef]] and 2 [[Egg|eggs]] to make a pot of stew for the whole village. Both the cows and the chickens are on [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] south of the village.

The quest has no prerequisites and can be taken at any time once you are in the village.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

Both things are taken when you hand in. They must be raw: a roasted [[Steak|Steak]] or a cooked [[Boiled Egg|Boiled Egg]] does not count.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day (6:00–22:00) Marta minds the pot by the campfire on the square, with a yellow “!” over her head. Press {{key|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘Something for the Pot’” in the dialogue box.
2. **To the Grassland**: out through the village's southern exit is the Grassland. At the start there are 3 [[cow|cows]] and 4 [[chicken|chickens]]; they do not attack, and run off when struck.
3. **Raw beef**: a defeated cow gives 2 raw beef, so you need to defeat 2. Cows walk more slowly than you, so you can catch them; you can fight bare-handed too (see [[Combat|Combat]]). Once the Grassland's cows are gone they slowly come back: while you are on the Grassland, one may arrive every 120 seconds, up to 4.
4. **Eggs**: a hen lays an egg every {{value|下蛋间隔}} in-game hours; the egg drops on the tile where the hen is standing at the time and does not jump into your backpack by itself, so press {{key|E}} to pick it up. A defeated chicken only gives [[Raw Chicken|Raw Chicken]], never eggs, so do not fight the hens. The hens at the start lay their first eggs at staggered times, so there will be one before long.
5. **Hand in**: go back to Marta, whose marker has turned to “?”, and press {{key|E}} to hand in.

### Other ways

- Raw beef and eggs from anywhere else in the world count too, as long as you have them on you when you hand in.
- [[The Held Ground]] has a cow and two chickens, but livestock there does not come back once defeated.
