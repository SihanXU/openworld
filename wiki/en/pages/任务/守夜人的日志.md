**The Watchman's Log** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[奈莎·奎尔]], who compiles the almanac. The Church once kept a Sunwatch Post on [[云顶台地]] at the mountaintop, where a watchman recorded every sunset; the reports stopped coming years ago. Nessa wants to know what his log says.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{任务目标}}

The log is the table in the Sunwatch Post hut on Cloudtop (called “The Watchman's Log”). Walk within one tile of the table and the scene of reading the log plays, and the objective is complete. Press {{键|E}} to read the words in the log again.

A new game begins in this very hut, and the table is only a few steps from where you start. Many players have already read the log at the start of the game; in that case, after taking the quest from Nessa, talk to her once more to hand it in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day Nessa copies her tables on the east side of the square in Firstlight Village (6:00–17:00), and in the evening goes to the road junction east of the village to time the sunset (17:00–23:00). Press {{键|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Watchman's Log’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Up the mountain**: from Firstlight Village go east to [[渡口]], climb the cliff steps north of the camp to [[山脚]], follow the path up onto the high ground to the north, and enter Cloudtop by the northern exit. Cloudtop has no [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]]; the nearest one is at Mountain Foot.
3. **Read the log**: the hut is in the north-west corner of the plateau, and the table is inside. Walk up to it.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Nessa and press {{键|E}} to hand in.

Mountain Foot is a combat zone, with [[夜行生物|night creatures]] at night; Cloudtop is a peaceful zone.

## Dialogue

{{任务对话}}

The log reads: “Cloudtop Sunwatch Post. Record of sunsets. Year 401: midsummer, 19:42. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — I checked the hourglass twice. Year 411: report sent down the mountain; no answer since. …”

{{剧透|The last page of the log}}
The last page of the log reads: “Found a little sleeping light in the snow by the cairn, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was. I did not know what to tell it.” Reading this, Lumi says she doesn't remember any snow, only that when she woke you were there; and she says that someone counted sunsets here for years and no one ever came to read them, and it is the saddest book she has ever seen. When you hand in, Nessa asks Lumi whether she remembers a watchman, and then answers herself: of course not.
{{/剧透}}
