**The Watchman's Log** is a Chapter 1 side quest given by [[Nessa Quill]], who compiles the almanac. The Church once kept a Sunwatch Post on [[Cloudtop]] at the mountaintop, where a watchman recorded every sunset; the reports stopped coming years ago. Nessa wants to know what his log says.

The quest has no prerequisites.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The log is the table in the Sunwatch Post hut on Cloudtop (called “The Watchman's Log”). Walk within one tile of the table and the scene of reading the log plays, and the objective is complete. Press {{key|E}} to read the words in the log again.

A new game begins in this very hut, and the table is only a few steps from where you start. Many players have already read the log at the start of the game; in that case, after taking the quest from Nessa, talk to her once more to hand it in.

## Walkthrough

1. **Take the quest**: by day Nessa copies her tables on the east side of the square in Firstlight Village (6:00–17:00), and in the evening goes to the road junction east of the village to time the sunset (17:00–23:00). Press {{key|E}} on her and choose “Accept ‘The Watchman's Log’” in the dialogue box.
2. **Up the mountain**: from Firstlight Village go east to [[The Ford]], climb the cliff steps north of the camp to [[Mountain Foot]], follow the path up onto the high ground to the north, and enter Cloudtop by the northern exit. Cloudtop has no [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]]; the nearest one is at Mountain Foot.
3. **Read the log**: the hut is in the north-west corner of the plateau, and the table is inside. Walk up to it.
4. **Hand in**: go back to Nessa and press {{key|E}} to hand in.

Mountain Foot is a combat zone, with [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] at night; Cloudtop is a peaceful zone.

## Dialogue

{{quest dialogue}}

The log reads: “Cloudtop Sunwatch Post. Record of sunsets. Year 401: midsummer, 19:42. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — I checked the hourglass twice. Year 411: report sent down the mountain; no answer since. …”

{{spoiler|The last page of the log}}
The last page of the log reads: “Found a small light asleep in the snow by the cairn, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was.” Reading this, Lumi says only: “It's so cold in here... Let's go.” When you hand in, Nessa says: “A sleeping light in the snow. I do not know what he meant by that. I am sure it means nothing.”
