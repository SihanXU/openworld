**What Was Taken** is a commission that grows out of what is happening in the village right now. When someone has seen a person they know take something of theirs (or of their household's) with their own eyes, and has not got it back, they ask you to get it back for them.

The owner, the taker and the thing in each "What Was Taken" are those of a theft that really happened at the time. Food taken does not count: it has long since been eaten, and only things that keep are worth asking someone to recover. Things you took yourself never give rise to this commission either (that is a [[Crime|crime]], and the owner comes straight to you to settle it).

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The 〈owner〉, 〈taker〉 and 〈thing〉 in the objectives, the stage table and the dialogue are replaced in the game by the names of the people and the thing at the time.

- **Get Y back from X**: complete as soon as the thing is on you, however you got it.
- **Give Y back to X**: hold it and press {{key|G}} on the owner to put it into their hands.

## Walkthrough

1. **The request**: a yellow “!” appears over the owner's head, and the commission is also listed on the [[commission board|commission board]] in [[Firstlight Village]]. Press {{key|E}} on them and they say: “X took my Y right in front of me. If you can get it back, I'll see you right.”
2. **Accept or decline**: choose “Accept ‘What Was Taken’” and they say “Thank you. X won't have gone far.”; or press “Take it” on the commission board. Choose “Decline ‘What Was Taken’” and the commission is withdrawn.
3. **Get it back**: there are several ways; pick any one:
   - **Ask for it**: press {{key|E}} on the taker, and a new option appears at the top of the dialogue box, “Hand over what you took.” Choose it and they say “…Fine. Take it. It's not worth the trouble,” and give you the thing.
   - **Other ways**: lifting it back out of their pocket (see [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing|Sneaking and Pickpocketing]]), finding it somewhere else… as long as the thing ends up on you, it counts. When you use other ways, being seen can still count as theft.
4. **Give it back to the owner**: hold it and press {{key|G}} on the owner. They say “My Y! I thought I'd never see it again.” The quest is complete.

If you have accepted but not yet got it done, talking to the owner gets “My Y. X still has it.”

## Quest items

While the commission is under way, the thing is a **quest item**: merchants will not take it, so you cannot sell it. Once the quest is over, it is an ordinary thing again. See [[Trading|Trading]].

## Time limits and failure

- If nobody takes the request within 24 in-game hours of it being made, the commission is withdrawn.
- If you do not give it back within 24 in-game hours of accepting, the quest fails.
- If the thing is destroyed (burned, say), the quest fails, and the journal reads “Y is gone.”
- There are at most two "What Was Taken" commissions at once. Each theft gives rise to only one.
