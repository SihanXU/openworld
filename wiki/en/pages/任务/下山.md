**Down from the Mountain** is the main quest at the start of a new game. You wake on [[Cloudtop]] with a fairy, [[Lumi]], at your side; the quest takes you past the shrine stone at [[Mountain Foot]], where you first see [[The World Learns|a world that learns]], down the stone steps of the cliff to [[The Ford]], and then west to [[Firstlight Village]].

The quest starts by itself as the game begins, with no one to find; it completes and pays its rewards automatically when you reach Firstlight Village, with nothing to hand in. Once you reach Firstlight Village, the next main quest, [[Lost in the Barrow]], begins at once.

## Objectives

{{objectives}}

The four objectives appear in three batches:

- At the start: **The shrine stone at Mountain Foot** and **Do something the world has never seen**.
  - The shrine stone: walk into the ring of flowers in the middle of Mountain Foot (around the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]]), and Lumi says it is cold beside the stone.
  - Something the world has never seen: after seeing the shrine stone, do to anything something the game has not yet defined an outcome for; the world gives an answer, and the objective is complete.
- After you have done it: **Go down the cliff steps to the Ford**, by entering the Ford.
- Once at the Ford: **Go west to Firstlight Village**, by entering Firstlight Village.

## Walkthrough

1. **Cloudtop**: you wake in the run-down hut of the Sunwatch Post at {{value|新游戏时刻}} on day 1, your [[Satiety|satiety]] not full. The quest begins, and the journal reads “Lumi says the way down lies south, to Mountain Foot.” Outside the door are [[Berries|berries]]: press {{key|E}} to pick them up, and left-click with them in hand to eat. Outside there are also sticks, stones and cut grass, so you can make a [[Axe|stone axe]] first ({{key|C}}). The log on the table inside is what [[The Watchman's Log]] wants you to read; walk up to the table and you read it.
2. **Down the mountain**: the plateau is walled with cliffs all round, and the only way out is the gap in the middle of the south cliff. Follow the path south and walk out off the south edge of the map, and you come to the high ground at the north of Mountain Foot. The tracker and the map marker point at the shrine stone.
3. **The shrine stone**: follow the gentle slope down below the high ground; the path passes a ring of flowers and a stone, and that is the shrine stone. The first objective is complete. Lumi tells you to do something to it.
4. **Something the world has never seen**: right-clicking the stone with something in hand, putting something on it with {{key|G}}, striking it — any of these will do. The world gives this act an outcome and records it in the [[Threadbook|Threadbook]] (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]); with no AI connected the default outcome is used, which is usually that nothing happens, but the objective is completed all the same. Lumi says that everything the world learns is recorded in the Threadbook ({{key|J}}), and the new objective “Go down the cliff steps to the Ford” appears. Mountain Foot is a combat zone with [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] after dark; the Ford below is a peaceful zone, with none at night.
5. **The Ford**: leave Mountain Foot by its southern exit down to the cliff steps north of the Ford; the third objective is complete and the last one, “Go west to Firstlight Village”, appears. The ferrywoman [[Hesper]] comes to meet you, tells you about the Ford and gives you half a loaf of [[Bread|bread]]; she asks you to go and help in the village to the west first.
6. **Firstlight Village**: from the Ford's camp follow the road west, through the opening in the trees into Firstlight Village. A welcome scene plays at the village entrance, the quest is complete, and the rewards are yours.

### Other ways

- Each objective looks only at the state of things, not at how you got there. Falling off the mountain anywhere or using a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] counts all the same.
- You may also do nothing at the shrine stone, or not pass the stone at all, and go straight down to the Ford: as soon as you reach the Ford, the objectives before it that were left undone are all ticked together, and the quest carries on.
- However you get to Firstlight Village, the quest completes the moment you enter it, and the rewards are paid as usual.

## Dialogue

{{quest dialogue}}

Everything on the way is said in scenes:

- When you wake on Cloudtop, Lumi introduces herself, says this is Cloudtop, and that the only way down is the gap in the south cliff.
- As you come near the first waypoint statue, Lumi reminds you to press E to wake it.
- At the shrine stone Lumi says “It's so cold beside it. Five marks, and the first one has cracked… I don't like it,” and asks you to do something to it; once you have, she says “See that? The world answers things it has never seen. Everything it learns goes into the Threadbook — press J. Come on, down to the Ford!”
- At the Ford, Lumi says “Here's the Ford! Those people look like they need help,” followed by Hesper's long introduction.
- On entering Firstlight Village, [[Sister Ilse]], [[Pell]], Lumi and [[Rowan]] each say a few words.

Press {{key|E}} on Lumi and she tells you the current objective and a hint; for example, once you are at the Ford: “Firstlight Village is west of the Ford. Rowan, the village elder, is looking for someone who can fight: people with a yellow "!" over their heads have quests for you (press E).”

## History

- 2026-10-07: Added. From now on there is always a main quest under way: in a new game it is Down from the Mountain, followed by Lost in the Barrow once you reach Firstlight Village.
- 2026-10-08: Reaching Firstlight Village with all three objectives met completes the quest and pays the rewards (before, it was simply closed, and the rewards were never paid).
- 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system: the prologue stages formerly kept on the player (the shrine stone, doing something the world has never seen, going down to the Ford) were folded into this quest, the objectives became four, appearing stage by stage, and the journal records every step; however you get to Firstlight Village, entering it completes the quest and pays the rewards.
