**The First Cast** is one of [[Lumi]]'s [[Quests#Guides: Lumi speaks up herself|guide quests]], teaching [[Fishing|fishing]]. The first time you come to the pond south of the square in [[Firstlight Village]] (the pool of water east of the hot spring), she speaks up.

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: in a proper game, step onto the bank of the pond in Firstlight Village, Lumi speaks, and the quest begins by itself; when you track it, the marker points at the pond.
2. **The fishing rod**: a {{item|Fishing Rod}} can be made by hand — press {{key|C}}, 2 {{item|Stick}} and 2 {{item|Cut Grass}}; the general store in Firstlight Village sells them too. As soon as you have a rod, the objective is complete and Lumi says another line.
3. **Fish**: stand on the bank with the rod in hand and left-click the water (any tile of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). Sometimes a fish bites, sometimes there is nothing; each cast muddies the water, so wait for the surface to settle before the next one. With {{item|Bait}} on (hold the bait and right-click the rod) the chance of a bite doubles. What you catch depends on the hour and the weather; see [[Fishing|Fishing]].
4. **Complete**: once there is any fish in your bag, the quest is complete, and Lumi gives you 3 bait.
