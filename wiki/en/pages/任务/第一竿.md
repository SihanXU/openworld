**The First Cast** is one of [[露米]]'s [[任务#指引：露米自己开口|guide quests]], teaching [[钓鱼|fishing]]. The first time you come to the pond south of the square in [[初原村]] (the pool of water east of the hot spring), she speaks up.

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: in a proper game, step onto the bank of the pond in Firstlight Village, Lumi speaks, and the quest begins by itself; when you track it, the marker points at the pond.
2. **The fishing rod**: a {{物品|鱼竿}} can be made by hand — press {{键|C}}, 2 {{物品|树枝}} and 2 {{物品|干草}}; the general store in Firstlight Village sells them too. As soon as you have a rod, the objective is complete and Lumi says another line.
3. **Fish**: stand on the bank with the rod in hand and left-click the water (any tile of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles). Sometimes a fish bites, sometimes there is nothing; each cast muddies the water, so wait for the surface to settle before the next one. With {{物品|鱼饵}} on (hold the bait and right-click the rod) the chance of a bite doubles. What you catch depends on the hour and the weather; see [[钓鱼|Fishing]].
4. **Complete**: once there is any fish in your bag, the quest is complete, and Lumi gives you 3 bait.
