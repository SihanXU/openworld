**A Road of Your Own** is one of [[露米]]'s [[任务#指引：露米自己开口|guide quests]]: when your character reaches level 5 and still has no [[职业|profession]], she speaks up by herself and takes you to the profession trainer {{人物|布拉姆}} in the drill yard of [[初原村]].

## Walkthrough

1. **Start**: in a proper game, reach level 5 (level 5 is exactly where Bram takes people on; see [[等级与经验|Levels and Experience]]) with no profession, and Lumi speaks; the quest begins by itself. A character who has already chosen a profession never gets it.
2. **Find Bram**: day and night he is in the drill yard beside the hut in the north-east corner of Firstlight Village, with a crossed-swords mark over his head, and when you track the quest there is a marker on the map too. Press {{键|E}} on him.
3. **Choose a profession**: his panel lists four roads — [[刺客|Assassin]] (daggers), [[战士|Warrior]] (sword and shield), [[游侠|Ranger]] (bow), [[法师|Mage]] (staff) — each with its style of play and starting weapon. Pick one.
4. **Complete**: once the profession is chosen, the quest is complete. The profession branch of the [[技能|skill tree]] ({{键|K}}) opens with it; if you have no weapon for your profession, Bram sells them.

You can go back to Bram later to change profession; the price is that the profession branch's skill points are refunded and spent again. See [[职业|Professions]].
