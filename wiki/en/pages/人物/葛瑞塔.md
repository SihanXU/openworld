{{信息框}}
**Greta** keeps the general store of [[初原村]], 52 years old, once the wife of a drover. The drovers stopped coming; the shop is still open. The general store stands on the north side of the road at the east end of the village, with a sign at the door.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

From 7 in the morning to 9 at night she stands behind the counter, and at night she sleeps at the back of the shop.

## The general store
Press {{键|E}} on the counter (“Trade at Greta's counter”) to open the shop panel, with three tabs: **Supplies** (torches, candles, lanterns, rope, flint, knives, empty flasks, buckets, fishing rods, bait), **Provisions** (bread, cheese, apples, ale, healing potions, antidotes) and **Sell** (herbs, berries, mushrooms, feathers, hides, eggs). Prices are fixed and paid in [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]], and you must stand by the counter to buy and sell. See [[金钱]].
