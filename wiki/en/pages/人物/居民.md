The game has 33 characters, plus [[露米|Lumi]], who follows the player everywhere. Each has a home, a schedule and lines of their own, and most of them live in [[初原村]]. This page lists everyone by place and explains the rules of dealing with people.

## Dealing with people

### Talking
Walk up to someone and press {{键|E}} to talk to them. Someone with something to entrust to you has a yellow “!” over their head; someone with a “?” over their head can move one of your quests forward if you talk to them now (a hand-in, or a quest that sent you to them). When you press E, they first say what moves a quest forward, then what they want to entrust to you, then hurry you along with their own errand, and only if there is none of that do they make small talk. See [[任务]]. After that line, the dialogue box lists what you can say to them: first accepting or declining the quest they offer and the quest's topics, then asking for news, chatting, giving a gift, trading, asking them to follow you, bribing, robbing, stealing, spreading unbelief, apologizing and giving things back; see [[对话]]. Everyone has one favorite gift, written on their character page, and they let it slip when chatting; give someone their favorite and their regard rises sharply (see [[友谊#送礼|gifts]]). Everyone has an age: children under 18 cannot be robbed, stolen from, or have unbelief spread to them.

Which line a person says is chosen from the most specific of these classes, in this order:
1. Something that happened lately (a “topic”, for example someone stealing, or the ferry rope just mended);
2. A line for after the story reaches a certain step;
3. The greeting at a first meeting;
4. A line for the current weather;
5. A line for the current time of day: morning 05:00–11:00, day 11:00–17:00, evening 17:00–21:00, night 21:00–05:00;
6. An ordinary line.

Lines marked “said only once”, topics, and first greetings are never said again once said; when a class holds several lines, the one said last time is not repeated straight away. Everyone remembers what they have said, what their last line was, and on which day they spoke with the player.

When the story needs the player to take a stand, the only choices are “Yes / No”.

### Giving things
Hold something and press {{键|G}} on a person, and you hand it to them. They take it and answer: some people have a particular reaction to some class of thing (give [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] rope, for example, and she says “Not to me. To the winch.”); anyone without one says “For me? ...Thank you.”

Giving a thing raises their **opinion** of you a little (more for more valuable things). Everyone has an opinion of everyone else, and its label is written beside the name in the dialogue box (hostile, cold, neutral, friendly, close); regard is also drawn as ten hearts, and someone at full hearts can be asked to live at [[誓守之地]]; see [[友谊]]. The game has **no** gift tastes, birthdays or romance. A few story exchanges have outcomes of their own: give food to [[托宾|Tobin]] and he gives the player the Frost Wand; [[加里克|Garrick]] and [[雷恩|Wren]] take materials to forge a sword and brew a draught.

### Other people's things, and crimes
Things have owners: what a person carries, what is in their house's chests, what is set outside their door, are theirs (their household's); so are a merchant's goods. Taking someone else's thing while seen is theft. People remember who did it (and, if they cannot tell who, what the culprit looked like); they shout, ask for it back, tell others, go to the watch, and [[守卫与悬赏|the guards]] come to stop you. Striking someone, picking pockets, setting fires, picking locks, smashing things and lingering at someone's door in the middle of the night are the same. See [[犯罪]].

These rules are the same for everyone: villagers steal too, get seen and get caught. See [[人们的需求]].

### A household
People who live together are a household: they share their things, and if one is wronged they all bear the grudge: the cook's house ([[玛尔塔]], [[米娅]], [[佩尔]], [[莉娜]]), the Watch House ([[霍尔特]], [[芬尼克]], [[奥斯卡]]), the woodcutter's house ([[格斯]], [[卡丝]]), and the one-person households of [[罗万]], [[艾达]], [[乔斯]], [[伊尔丝修女]], [[托宾]], [[赫斯珀]], [[加里克]], [[玛拉]], [[雷恩]], [[塔姆]], [[奥林]] and [[希尔德]]. [[维戈]] and those who spend the night in the shelter have no home.

### Trade, following and needs
- [[玛尔塔]], [[托宾]], [[加里克]] and [[雷恩]] are merchants you can buy from and sell to; [[托宾]] also fences stolen goods. See [[交易]].
- People friendly enough toward you, or hired hands like [[维戈]], will follow you. See [[跟随与雇佣]].
- Everyone gets hungry; when hungry they go and eat or buy food, and those with no money and no scruples steal. See [[人们的需求]].

### Schedules
Everyone lives by the clock, an hour at a time: sleeping, working, eating, going home. Many have a second schedule for rainy or stormy days, and another for after the story moves on. Some work needs a particular thing, for example [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] tending the fire needs the camp's campfire to be lit; when that thing is not in that state, that stretch of work cannot be done. People walk to other maps, for example [[格斯|Gus]] going back and forth between Firstlight Village and the forest every day.

Stand next to someone, facing them, and they stop and wait a while, so you can talk or hand them something. Walk into someone for about 0.8 seconds and you swap places with them.

### Danger
People belong to the “people” faction, and monsters attack them. When something that will attack them comes near (within 3 cells), they start and run away, keeping as close to a fire as they can; those who fight ([[霍尔特]], [[芬尼克]], [[加里克]], [[布拉姆]], [[维戈]]) strike back when struck. See [[阵营与脾性]].

Someone the story cannot do without does not die when their health falls to zero; they are {{状态|倒地}} for a while, then wake with half their health; someone who is down is not attacked again. Everyone else ([[卡丝]], [[塔姆]], [[维戈]]) can die and becomes a body. See [[人物之死]].

### Codex
Come within {{数值|图鉴视距}} cells of a person and they count as “seen”: they appear on the “People” page of the [[图鉴|Codex]], their card naming the map they live on.
