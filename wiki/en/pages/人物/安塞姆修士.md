**Brother Anselm** is an old brother of [[初原村]], 68 years old, who serves in [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]]'s chapel ([[初原村礼拜堂]]). The chapel stands on the square, just behind the altar of [[阿斯特拉|Astra]] and the holy flame; Ilse keeps the flame outside the door, and he keeps the skill-book shelves inside. The skill books on the shelves were copied before the storm by the monastery over the mountain, and he sells them at what it cost to bring them down. He has read every one of them, yet cannot do a single thing in them; reading, he says, is not learning.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

From 6 in the morning to 9 at night he stands by the bookstand, and at night he sleeps in the chapel.

## The skill bookstand
Press {{键|E}} on the bookstand (“Trade at the skill bookstand”) to open the shop panel “Chapel Bookstand”, with two tabs: **Skill Books** (active skills anyone can learn, 60 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each) and **Profession** (an active skill of one [[职业|profession]], 120 each). Hold a book you have bought and left-click to read it, and you learn the skill in it (see [[技能]]).

He also carries a few things of his own to buy and sell: Holy Water, candles, a book and a quill (see [[交易]]).

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The first time you speak to him, he says: “Welcome to Firstlight's chapel. Sister Ilse keeps the flame; I keep these books. They teach what hands can learn: a strike, a step, a word of fire. Take one into the light and read it, and it will stay with you.”
