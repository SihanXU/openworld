**Joss** is the bridge-builder of [[Firstlight Village]], 58 years old. Twenty years ago he and Aldo, [[Hesper|Hesper]]'s husband, built the little bridge at [[The Ford]]. Now someone has taken that bridge apart, neatly. By day he planes boards at the village [[workbench|workbench]], and he teaches the player how to mend the bridge with planks.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at home from 21:00 to 06:00 and planes boards at the workbench from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
He was a friend of Aldo, Hesper's late husband.

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

The way he tells you to mend the bridge is exactly how it works in the game: “Axe the tree, axe the logs into planks. Three planks and the little bridge is whole.” See [[Woodcutting]] and [[Plank|planks]]. He has also seen that no storm broke the bridge: “The nails were pulled out straight and laid in a row. What storm pulls nails?”

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Joss is the source of the clue that “the bridge was taken apart, not washed away”. See [[Chapter 1]].

Joss never understood what he meant. After Chapter 1 ends, he tells this story, once.
