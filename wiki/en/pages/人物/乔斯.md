**Joss** is the bridge-builder of [[初原村]], 58 years old. Twenty years ago he and Aldo, [[赫斯珀|Hesper]]'s husband, built the little bridge at [[渡口]]. Now someone has taken that bridge apart, neatly. By day he planes boards at the village [[工作台|workbench]], and he teaches the player how to mend the bridge with planks.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps at home from 21:00 to 06:00 and planes boards at the workbench from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
He was a friend of Aldo, Hesper's late husband.

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The way he tells you to mend the bridge is exactly how it works in the game: “Axe the tree, axe the logs into planks. Three planks and the little bridge is whole.” See [[伐木]] and [[木板|planks]]. He has also seen that no storm broke the bridge: “The nails were pulled out straight and laid in a row. What storm pulls nails?”

## Quests

- The Ninth Nail: available after the Church's letter. The three planks of the little bridge held nine nails in all; only eight lie on the jetty, and the ninth is down the road in the White Knight's kingdom. Bring it back and he gives you a [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]] and “Joss's Tool Belt”. See [[第九根钉子]].

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Joss is the source of the clue that “the bridge was taken apart, not washed away”. See [[第一章]].

{{剧透|What he does not say}}
Twenty years ago, the one who paid for that bridge was a young man in grey, with old coins that bore no king's head (the coin of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]]), who said he wanted “a road a cart could leave here by, quickly”. Joss never understood what he meant. After Chapter 1 ends, he tells this story, once.
{{/剧透}}
