**Gus** is the woodcutter of [[Firstlight Village]], 51 years old, and carries an iron [[Axe|axe]]. In the morning he goes to [[The Forest]] to fell trees and gather logs, and in the afternoon he carries the logs back to the village and stacks them on the [[wood pile|log pile]] by the [[workbench|workbench]], for anyone who needs them. His back is not what it was.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

His work is real work: the game does not conjure logs up for him.
- **06:00–12:00, the forest clearing.** First he picks up [[Log|Logs]] lying on the ground within 6 cells, then fells trees within 5 cells with his axe. He keeps working only while he carries fewer than 6 Logs. A tree he fells is like one the player fells: it leaves a stump, and grows back after {{value|树木再生}} game days; see [[Woodcutting]].
- **12:00–21:00, the village log pile.** He puts the logs he carried back into the log pile, one by one.
- **21:00–06:00**, asleep at home.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[Wood for the Fire]]: a daily commission, available once a day. Hand in 4 Logs for experience and 1 [[Bread|Bread]]. Felling a tree with an axe takes two strokes.
- [[Where the Birds Went]] ([[Orrin|Orrin]]'s quest): you must ask Gus why the forest is so quiet. He says that on the night the bridge fell, every bird in the wood rose at once and flew off west, “and who wants to hear a woodcutter on birds”.

## A household
Gus and his wife [[Cass|Cass]] are one household (the woodcutter's house): they share what is in the house, robbing one is robbing both, and wrong one and the other bears the grudge too (see [[Crime]]).

## Lore
Gus is the model of the design that “a person's schedule is real work”: he fells trees, gathers logs and stacks wood, every step an ordinary act, and the rules answer as they always do. See [[Design Pillars]].

## Trivia
- The log pile holds at most 12 Logs and has no owner: taking logs from it is not theft.
- His role in the game is “farmer”, but he carries the “woodcutter” skill tag.
- He shares a character sprite with [[Joss]] and [[Brin]].
