**Hilde** is the fisherwoman of [[芦苇湾]], 47 years old, and plain-spoken. She fishes from the jetty and smokes eels, and sells fish, bait and fishing rods. Nine winters ago her husband Rolf took the boat out into the mist; the boat came back on its own, and he did not. The boat bell still lies on the spit, among the [[水史莱姆|water slimes]], and she does not go there.

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

Her first words are: “If you want fish, I've got fish. If you want talk, the wood's full of trees.” She tells you which fish bite when, and how to make a fishing rod (see [[钓鱼]]). She sells [[生鱼|Raw Fish]], [[鳗鱼|Eel]], [[鱼饵|Bait]] and [[鱼竿|Fishing Rods]].

## Lore
Hilde does not believe in gods, but she is not the kind who [[善恶与信念|spreads unbelief]]: tell her “there's no god watching at all” and she only pauses; she does not get angry. After you have read the sunken stone on the shore, she says a line she says only once: that stone was there before there was a village, “before the Church came to teach us what to repent of”, and whoever carved it thought the gift of one fish worth recording, “and so do I”. See [[芦苇湾]] and [[世界观]].

When you bring back the bell she says: “It still sounds the same. I thought it would have changed.” She gives you Rolf's Net Ring, saying it only works on a hand that goes places.

## Trivia
- She is the first person to sell [[鱼竿|Fishing Rods]]. Making one yourself takes only two sticks and two handfuls of grass.
