**Marta** keeps the fire and the pot of [[初原村]], 44 years old. The whole village eats at her fire; since the storm, there has been little to put in the pot. Her daughter is [[米娅]] and her nephew is [[佩尔]]; both children live with her.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps at home from 22:00 to 06:00 and minds the pot by the fire on the square from 06:00 to 22:00. That fire is the holy flame [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] keeps.

## Relationships
Mia is her daughter, and Pell calls her aunt. When Pell says he has seen the Grey Man, she does not believe him: she says he gets it from his father, who went east and never wrote again.

## Quests

- [[给锅里添点东西]]: she needs 3 [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]] and 2 [[鸡蛋|Eggs]] to make a stew for the whole village. The cows and chickens are on [[草原]] south of the village. Hand them in for [[面包|Bread]] and “Marta's Kitchen Sash”.

## Selling bread
Marta is a merchant of [[初原村]]: every morning she bakes 6 {{物品|面包}}, which you can buy from her (see [[交易]]). Villagers who are hungry and have money come to buy too, so the bread is often sold out before evening (see [[人们的需求]]).

Her bread is carried on her person and is hers: taking it from her pocket is [[潜行与扒窃|pickpocketing]]. When [[维戈|Vigo]] is hungry, he often has designs on that bread. She, [[米娅]], [[佩尔]] and [[莉娜]] are one household (the cook's house); steal from any one of them and the whole family holds a grudge.
