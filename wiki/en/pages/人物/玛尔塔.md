**Marta** keeps the fire and the pot of [[Firstlight Village]], 44 years old. The whole village eats at her fire; since the storm, there has been little to put in the pot. Her daughter is [[Mia]] and her nephew is [[Pell]]; both children live with her.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps at home from 22:00 to 06:00 and minds the pot by the fire on the square from 06:00 to 22:00. That fire is the holy flame [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]] keeps.

## Relationships
Mia is her daughter, and Pell calls her aunt. When Pell says he has seen the Grey Man, she does not believe him: she says he gets it from his father, who went east and never wrote again.

## Quests

- [[Something for the Pot]]: she needs 3 [[Raw Beef|Raw Beef]] and 2 [[Egg|Eggs]] to make a stew for the whole village. The cows and chickens are on [[The Grassland]] south of the village. Hand them in for [[Bread|Bread]] and “Marta's Kitchen Sash”.

## Selling bread
Marta is a merchant of [[Firstlight Village]]: every morning she bakes 6 {{item|Bread}}, which you can buy from her (see [[Trading]]). Villagers who are hungry and have money come to buy too, so the bread is often sold out before evening (see [[People's Needs]]).

Her bread is carried on her person and is hers: taking it from her pocket is [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing|pickpocketing]]. When [[Vigo|Vigo]] is hungry, he often has designs on that bread. She, [[Mia]], [[Pell]] and [[Lina]] are one household (the cook's house); steal from any one of them and the whole family holds a grudge.
