**Haldor** is the smith of [[Firstlight Village]], 29 years old. He learned his trade under [[Garrick|Garrick]] in [[Emberfall]], and when the storm cut the road he came west, to shoe drovers' horses and mend the villagers' tools.

His smithy stands on the north side of the road at the east end of the village, with a sign at the door; inside are a [[forge|forge]] that is always lit and an [[anvil|anvil]]. Anyone may use the forge and anvil without asking him first: smelt ore and coal into ingots by the forge, and with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} in hand beat ingots into gear by the anvil. See [[Smithing]].

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

From 6 in the morning to 9 at night he works at the anvil, and at night he sleeps in the smithy. Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark, showing he is a smith (a quest marker shows first when there is one).

## Trade
Haldor is also a merchant (see [[Trading]]): he sells {{item|Smith Hammer}}, {{item|Coal}}, {{item|Iron Ore}} and {{item|Iron Ingot}}, the things someone new to the forge needs. He fights: struck, he strikes back.

## Relationships
He was taught his trade by [[Garrick|Garrick]]; by his own account, Garrick would call his welds lazy.

## Lore
Haldor lets you take up smithing soon after coming down the mountain: Emberfall lies beyond the White Knight and can only be reached by defeating him, so the smithy in Firstlight Village is the first place where you can smelt and forge.
