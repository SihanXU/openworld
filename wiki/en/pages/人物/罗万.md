**Rowan** is the elder of [[Firstlight Village]], 67 years old. When the storm came, two of the village's foragers, [[Lina]] and [[Oskar]], took shelter in [[The Old Barrow]] beyond [[Thornwood Hollow]], and never came out. He asks for help from everyone who can lift a weapon; the main quest [[Lost in the Barrow]] is his request.

By day he stands on his porch, looking out over the village square.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and watches the square from his porch from 07:00 to 21:00. He has no second schedule for rain or for the story.

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

He has no first-meeting greeting: the first time the player comes into the village, he has already spoken in the welcome scene (“Pell, let them be.”). He has lines of his own for after the holy flame is stolen, after the Church's letter, and after Chapter 1 ends.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[Lost in the Barrow]] (main): begins on its own once the player reaches the village. Find Lina and Oskar, defeat the [[barrow warden|warden]] at the very bottom, and come back to hand it in to Rowan. The rewards include [[Bread|Bread]] and “Rowan's Old Hauberk”. Once it is handed in, Lina and Oskar return to the village.

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Rowan stands for the village's trust in the Church. On the night of thanks he leads the whole village in giving thanks at the holy flame; after the flame is stolen, he grows harder and harder toward that “heretic”: “And if you meet that thief in grey on the road, remember what he took from us.” When the player brings [[Crown of Thorns|the Crown of Thorns]] back to the village, he says: “And that heretic still has our flame.” See [[Chapter 1]].

At the end of Chapter 1, [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] leaves the player a line meant to be passed on to Rowan: “Tell Rowan the light is safer with me than with any of you.”

## Trivia
- He is the elder, but his role in the game is “villager”. The role of “village head” belongs to [[Mara|Mara]] of [[Emberfall]].
- He says “The Church tells us to be patient. To be honest, I am running out of patience.” This is a line for after Chapter 1 ends.
