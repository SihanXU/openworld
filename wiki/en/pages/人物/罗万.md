**Rowan** is the elder of [[初原村]], 67 years old. When the storm came, two of the village's foragers, [[莉娜]] and [[奥斯卡]], took shelter in [[古墓]] beyond [[荆棘谷]], and never came out. He asks for help from everyone who can lift a weapon; the main quest [[古墓里的失踪者]] is his request.

By day he stands on his porch, looking out over the village square.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and watches the square from his porch from 07:00 to 21:00. He has no second schedule for rain or for the story.

## Relationships
[[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] is an old friend of his.

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

He has no first-meeting greeting: the first time the player comes into the village, he has already spoken in the welcome scene (“Pell, let them breathe.”). He has lines of his own for after the holy flame is stolen, after the Church's letter, and after Chapter 1 ends.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

- [[古墓里的失踪者]] (main): begins on its own once the player reaches the village. Find Lina and Oskar, defeat the [[古墓守卫|warden]] at the very bottom, and come back to hand it in to Rowan. The rewards include [[面包|Bread]] and “Rowan's Old Hauberk”. Once it is handed in, Lina and Oskar return to the village.

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Rowan stands for the village's trust in the Church. On the night of thanks he leads the whole village in giving thanks at the holy flame; after the flame is stolen, he grows harder and harder toward that “heretic”: “If you see that grey thief on the road, remember what he took.” When the player brings [[荆棘冠|the Crown of Thorns]] back to the village, he says: “And the heretic still has our flame.” See [[第一章]].

At the end of Chapter 1, [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] leaves the player a line meant to be passed on to Rowan: “Tell Rowan the light is safer with me than with you.”

## Trivia
- He is the elder, but his role in the game is “villager”. The role of “village head” belongs to [[玛拉|Mara]] of [[余烬镇]].
- He says “The Five teach patience. I find I have very little.” This is a line for after Chapter 1 ends.
