The **Church Courier** is a mounted courier from [[圣城|the Holy City]], 27 years old. On the morning after the holy flame is stolen, he rides into [[初原村]], reads out the Holy City's command that the Pilgrimage begin, hands the player the [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], and rides away.

He appears only in this one story scene, and the player cannot talk to him.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He has no schedule. He waits in an “elsewhere” the player cannot reach, until the scene calls him out.

## Relationships
He has no relationships written in the game.

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

His table of lines holds only “Suffering is not God's abandonment.”, which the player never hears. What he says in the scene is:
- “By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers are failing. The Long Night returns. The Pilgrimage is begun.”
- “One came down from the mountain with a fairy. The Church names that one the Pilgrim.”

When he is done, he stares at [[露米|Lumi]] for a long time, and says nothing.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

He gives no quests. His letter is an objective of the main quest [[第一印]] (“The Church's letter”), and it makes [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]]'s [[朝圣之路]] available.

## Lore
The courier formally names the player the Pilgrim, and from then on Chapter 1 turns from the village's troubles to the [[持印者|Seal-bearers]]. See [[第一章]] and [[五苦圣教]].

{{剧透|Why he stares at Lumi}}
Lumi is “the Second Vessel”, the one who could not bear the Five Sufferings long ago. The courier staring at her in silence is the corner of her past that Chapter 1 lets show. See [[露米]].
{{/剧透}}

## Trivia
- Deep in [[森林]], in the old camp, the same Proclamation is nailed up, grey with age; it names Brother Cassian, thirty-one years ago, in words identical to the ones the courier reads.
