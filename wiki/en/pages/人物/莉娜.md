**Lina** is a forager of [[初原村]], 24 years old. When the storm came she ran into [[古墓]] to get out of the rain, and the inner door was shut behind her. The main quest [[古墓里的失踪者]] sends the player to find her. Once rescued, she tends herbs in the village.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

Before she is rescued, she spends all day in a side chamber of the Barrow, waiting for someone to find her. After the player hands in Lost in the Barrow to [[罗万|Rowan]], she returns to the village: she sleeps at home from 22:00 to 07:00 and tends herbs in the vegetable garden from 07:00 to 22:00.

## Relationships
[[奥斯卡|Oskar]] followed her into the Barrow to bring her out, and was shut in there with her.

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

When the player walks into her side chamber, a scene plays: it was not the dead who shut them in, but a man in grey; he pulled the inner door to from the outside, then pushed a loaf of bread in under the door, with a grey mark pressed into it by a thumb. She also tells the player that Oskar went down the stairs in the east cell and never came back.

## Lore
Lina brings out one of Chapter 1's clues: the “loaf stamped with a grey mark” is right there in her side chamber, and can be picked up. See [[灰衣人]] and [[第一章]].

{{剧透|What the door was about}}
The door the Grey Man shut was shutting in the restless [[古墓守卫|wardens]], not Lina and the others. The grey mark on the bread is the sign of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]].
{{/剧透}}
