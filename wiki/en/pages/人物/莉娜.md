**Lina** is a forager of [[Firstlight Village]], 20 years old. She tells the village that when the storm came she ran into [[The Old Barrow]] to get out of the rain, and the inner door was shut behind her. The main quest [[Lost in the Barrow]] sends the player to find her. Once rescued, she tends herbs in the village.

She is like a cat: she comes and goes without a sound, likes high places, puts a price on everything, has a sharp tongue, trusts no one, and denies every kindness she does.

## Appearance
Short, tousled dark brown hair, a few curls sticking up; amber eyes upturned at the corners like a cat's, a sly smile at one corner of her mouth, and a small fang when she grins. A short moss-green cape with the hood down; a cream linen shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a dark brown laced vest and trousers tucked into old soft boots — for climbing barrow shafts, never a dress. Fingerless leather gloves, an old leather satchel across her body with a trowel and a candle stub peeking out, a sheathed knife at her belt; an old tarnished bronze ring on a cord round her neck.

She has a **portrait of her own**: when you talk to her, the dialogue box shows it. It has six expressions — neutral (the sly half smile), happy (eyes shut, grinning with her fang), sad, annoyed (eyes half-lidded, glancing sideways), surprised, and pretending not to care (a blush); every one but happy blinks, and her mouth moves as she speaks. Her map sprite follows: tousled dark brown hair, a moss-green cape, amber eyes. Her design book: [[Art#Portraits of their own and story illustrations|Art]].

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

Before she is rescued, she spends all day in a side chamber of the Barrow, waiting for someone to find her. After the player hands in Lost in the Barrow to [[Rowan|Rowan]], she returns to the village: she sleeps at home from 22:00 to 07:00 and tends herbs in the vegetable garden from 07:00 to 22:00.

## Relationships
- [[Oskar|Oskar]] taught her the mushrooms and the paths when she was small. He grumbles at her, yet on the storm night it was he who followed her into the Barrow to bring her out, and was shut in there with her.
- [[Marta|Marta]]: no house took her in; she has always slept in Marta's cookhouse, and pays for her meals in mushrooms.
- [[Tobin|Tobin]]: whatever she brings up out of the barrows she sells to this peddler, who never asks.
- [[Pell|Pell]]: the best mushrooms keep turning up on Pell's doorstep. The wind, she says.
- [[Sister Ilse]]: it was the sister who sat with her mother at the end. The sister offered to take her into the chapel; she refused, and since then the chapel door is never latched at night; on the coldest nights she slips in to sleep by the flame. She is grateful and fond of the sister and never says so: “Don't tell the sister where I go at night. ...It's not that she'd be angry. It's that she wouldn't be.” She hates the Church that sent her father east, but not the sister.

## Lore
Lina brings out one of Chapter 1's clues: the “loaf stamped with a grey mark” is right there in her side chamber, and can be picked up. See [[The Grey Man]] and [[Chapter 1]].

Her father was one of the pilgrims the Church sends east, and never came back — like [[Pell|Pell]]'s father. Her mother died of a winter fever when she was eight. After that the village fed her by turns, but no house took her in.

{{spoiler|Why she was in the barrow}}
Foraging does not feed her. Lina goes down into the old barrows south of the grassland for the grave goods and sells them to [[Tobin|Tobin]]; the almanac from before the First Night that [[Nessa Quill]] carries came out of a barrow by her hands, and Nessa does not know it. She went down on the storm night because no one would see; "sheltering from the storm" is what she told the village. The bronze ring at her neck is the first thing she ever took from a grave, and the only one she never sold: "Nobody came to see her either."

The Grey Man knew she was in there, yet he shut the door and left the bread. Kindness from a stranger, to someone who trusts no one, is the one thing she cannot work out.
{{/spoiler}}

{{spoiler|What the door was about}}
The door the Grey Man shut was shutting in the restless [[barrow warden|wardens]], not Lina and the others. The grey mark on the bread is the sign of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]].

- She has one schedule for before she is rescued and one for after; while in the Barrow, her schedule reads “waiting to be found”.
