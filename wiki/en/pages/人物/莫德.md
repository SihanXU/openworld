**Maud** is a pilgrim, 34 years old, traveling with her daughter [[Wynn|Wynn]]. They were bound for the Holy City to pray about the ever-shorter days; when they reached [[The Pilgrims' Rest]] they heard of the knight to the east, and dared go no further. Every night she leafs through the page of the hostel register with the names that “went east”.

By day she sits by the fire on the Rest's square, and at night she stays in the Pilgrims' Hostel. After the White Knight falls, she says they can go on their way, and that at last she can teach her daughter the final verse of that rhyme.
