**Fennick** is an old knight living in [[初原村]], 62 years old. He was once one of the [[持印者|Seal-bearer]]'s guard, carrying the lance under Alberic, the White Knight. Whenever the villagers speak of “the knight”, he falls silent. By day he sits on the bench by the square mending harness.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps at the guards' quarters from 22:00 to 07:00 and sits on the bench mending harness from 07:00 to 22:00.

## Relationships
As a young man he carried [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]]'s lance for six years as his squire; by then Alberic had worn the Crown of Thorns for two hundred and fifty years. He sleeps in the same quarters as [[霍尔特|Holt]].

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

After the Church's letter, he talks of the White Knight: “He used to go down on one knee to children. Now he wants the whole world on its knees to him. That is what the crown does to a man.” After the player has been to the entrance of [[古墓]], he says the people buried there are of the Order of the Thorn, and so are the wardens.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

- [[老骑士]]: available after the Church's letter. He will not let anyone who has not survived a night in the wild go up against the Seal-bearer: after dark, defeat 3 [[僵尸|zombies]] outside the village. The reward is “Fennick's Squire Helm” (of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality).

- [[清剿夜行者]]: he is a guard, and on some evenings he (or [[霍尔特|Holt]]) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

## Guard
Fennick, the old templar, still keeps watch over [[初原村]] with [[霍尔特|Holt]], one of [[守卫与悬赏|Firstlight Village's guards]]: he reports any crime he sees on the spot, goes to stop anyone wanted he sees, and runs to anyone who cries for help. He fights. See [[犯罪]].

## Lore
Fennick is the one in Chapter 1 who knew the White Knight himself. When he was squire, Alberic had already kept watch for two hundred and fifty years. In the scene of his memory he says:

> I carried his lance for six years, when I was young. He had worn the crown two hundred and fifty years by then, and still he would stop the whole column to kneel down and talk to children. Knights don't do that. He did.
> But the crown kept taking. A little more of him went every year I was there. One morning he looked at me and asked my name.
> I left that night. ...Pilgrim, if he asks your name, tell him. Somebody ought to tell him.

See [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]].

Among the niches of [[荆棘骸骨堂]] is one carved and still empty: “Fennick, squire to the Commander”. The stone is clean; someone has kept sweeping it.

{{剧透|What he does not say}}
He left the guard on the very night Alberic failed to know him. He also knows [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]]: after the flame is stolen and he hears the man wore a grey cloak and had ash on his hands, he first says “No”, then “Unless he had a reason, and that frightens me more.”

## Trivia

- Once the player has taken on The Old Templar, Alberic at [[誓守之地]] speaks of a squire whose face he cannot recall: “Tell him I am sorry I asked his name.”
