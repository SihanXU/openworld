**Fennick** is an old knight living in [[Firstlight Village]], 62 years old. He was once one of the [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]]'s guard, carrying the lance under Alberic, the White Knight. Whenever the villagers speak of “the knight”, he falls silent. By day he sits on the bench by the square mending harness.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at the guards' quarters from 22:00 to 07:00 and sits on the bench mending harness from 07:00 to 22:00.

## Relationships
As a young man he carried [[Alberic|Alberic]]'s lance for six years as his squire; by then Alberic had worn the Crown of Thorns for two hundred and fifty years. He sleeps in the same quarters as [[Holt|Holt]].

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

After the Church's letter, he talks of the White Knight: “He used to go down on one knee to children. Now he wants the whole world on its knees to him. That is what the crown does to a man.” After the player has been to the entrance of [[The Old Barrow]], he says the people buried there are of the Order of the Thorn, and so are the wardens.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[The Old Templar]]: available after the Church's letter. He will not let anyone who has not survived a night in the wild go up against the Seal-bearer: after dark, defeat 3 [[zombie|zombies]] outside the village. The reward is “Fennick's Squire Helm” (of [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] quality).
- [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)]]: Ilse sends the player to hear him tell of the White Knight. He teaches the player to dodge the thorns: “Stand inside the ring, or well away from it; just never on it. When he draws his bow, don't stand straight in front of him.” Once you have asked him, a memory of his plays when you return to the village.
- [[The Night-born]]: he is a guard, and on some evenings he (or [[Holt|Holt]]) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

## Guard
Fennick, the old templar, still keeps watch over [[Firstlight Village]] with [[Holt|Holt]], one of [[Guards and Bounties|Firstlight Village's guards]]: he reports any crime he sees on the spot, goes to stop anyone wanted he sees, and runs to anyone who cries for help. He fights. See [[Crime]].

## Lore
Fennick is the one in Chapter 1 who knew the White Knight himself. When he was squire, Alberic had already kept watch for two hundred and fifty years. In the scene of his memory he says:

> I carried his lance for six years, when I was young. He had worn the crown two hundred and fifty years by then, and still he would stop the whole column to kneel down and talk to children. Knights don't do that. He did.
> But the crown kept taking. A little more of him went every year I was there. One morning he looked at me and asked my name.
> I left that night. ...Pilgrim, if he asks your name, tell him. Somebody ought to tell him.

See [[The White Knight Alberic]].

Among the niches of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]] is one carved and still empty: “Fennick, squire to the Commander”. The stone is clean; someone has kept sweeping it.

{{spoiler|What he does not say}}
He left the guard on the very night Alberic failed to know him. He also knows [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]]: after the flame is stolen and he hears the man wore a grey cloak and had ash on his hands, he first says “No”, then “Unless he had a reason, and that frightens me more.”
