**Cass** is the weaver of [[初原村]], 47 years old, [[格斯|Gus]]'s wife and [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s friend. In the morning she draws water at the well, by day she weaves at the loom by the square, and in the evening she sits by Marta's fire. No quest sends you to her and no story needs her: she is simply an ordinary person in the village, and, as with the villagers of *The Elder Scrolls*, whatever you do to her, the village remembers.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps at the woodcutter's house from 21:00 to 06:00, draws water at the well on the square from 06:00 to 09:00, weaves at the loom by the square from 09:00 to 17:00, and sits around the fire on the square from 17:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
[[格斯|Gus]] is her husband; the two of them are one household (the woodcutter's house) and share their things: rob Gus and you rob her too. [[玛尔塔|Marta]] is her friend. Her [[友谊|opinion]] of both starts at “friendly”.

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The first time you meet, she introduces herself: “I'm Cass. Gus's wife, though most days I think he's married to that wood.” Give her food and she says she'll keep half for Gus. In the days after someone in the village dies, she talks of how “nobody here will say how it happened”.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Cass gives no quests and appears in none.

## Dealing with her
- **Befriending**: talk with her every day and give her things, and her opinion of you slowly rises to “friendly”. Once she is “friendly”, say “Come with me.” and she follows you (see [[跟随与雇佣]]).
- **Robbing her**: she carries a {{物品|面包}} and a dozen or so {{物品|晨辉币}}. While sneaking you can pick her pocket (see [[潜行与扒窃]]); if she sees you, she asks for it back, and if you don't return it she goes to the watch.
- **She can die**: Cass is not someone the story cannot do without. When her health reaches zero she dies and becomes a body; Gus and Marta hold a grudge against whoever killed her, and the village talks of it for days (see [[人物之死]]).

## Lore
Cass was added to Firstlight Village to test the NPC interaction system: a person with no story protection, who lives only her own life. Every rule of dealing with people (opinion, theft, following, death) holds in full on her.
