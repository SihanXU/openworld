**Mara** is the elder of [[余烬镇]], 71 years old. She has buried two husbands and a season's harvest burned by dragonfire, and refuses to bury this town too. By day she keeps watch over the town square, counting the buckets by the well.

> The line of Emberfall and [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] was written before the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]] setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See [[余烬镇与魔龙]] and [[第二章]].

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and keeps watch on the square from 07:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
She trusts the smith [[加里克|Garrick]] and has given him the town gate to guard; she pays for the coal he smiths with. She taught the herbalist [[雷恩|Wren]] to read.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Mara's part belongs to the main quest [[屠龙之路]]: finding her is its second objective. The first time you press {{键|E}} on her, the “elder's council” scene plays:
- A dragon lives on Cinder Peak, and has come down twice this spring;
- The smith Garrick has the pattern of a sword that can cut through dragonhide, but lacks iron and coal, which are to be found in [[旧矿坑]];
- The herbalist Wren can brew a draught against fire;
- She gives the player two [[面包|Bread]]: “No one fights well on an empty stomach.”

The objectives after that are bringing materials to Garrick and Wren. Once the dragon is dead, she means to carve the player's name on the well.

## Lore
Mara is the guide of the Emberfall line. That line is the old main story “from the broken bridge to the slain dragon”; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Trivia
- Her role is “village head”, and talking to her triggers the elder's council; the role of [[罗万|Rowan]], the elder of [[初原村]], is “villager”.
- She shares a character sprite with [[玛尔塔]] and [[莉娜]].
