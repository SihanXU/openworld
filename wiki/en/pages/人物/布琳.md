**Brin** is the herder of [[Firstlight Village]], 24 years old, who looks after the [[cow|cows]] and [[chicken|chickens]] on [[The Grassland]] south of the village. Week by week more of the things of the night crawl up out of the dark onto the grassland and take her cattle. By day she keeps to her bench on the grassland watching the herd, and at night she goes back to the village to sleep.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps under the market awning in the village from 21:00 to 06:00 and watches the herd on the grassland from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

Her first words are a warning: “Mind the chickens. And don't run; you'll spook the cows.” Speak to her at dusk and she tells the player to watch the cattle kneel.

## Lore
Come to the grassland between 17:00 and 20:00 and the scene of the kneeling herd plays: the light turns amber, and the cattle kneel one after another, all facing east. Brin says it began after the bridge broke. [[Lumi|Lumi]] points out that east is the Ford, and beyond the Ford is that road: the cattle are kneeling to something on the road. That something is [[The White Knight Alberic]].

After the White Knight falls, Brin says the cattle no longer kneel; “now they just stand and look east.” See [[Chapter 1]].
