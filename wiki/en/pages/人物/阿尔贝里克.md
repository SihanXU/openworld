**Alberic** is the White Knight with the Crown of Thorns taken off, the keeper of [[誓守之地]]. The crown took most of him; what is left keeps the same vow turned another way: he holds a piece of land for the one who relieved him, and enlarges it with what the player brings. He cannot keep names in his head, so he asks the player's name.

He also owns the Held Ground's [[货摊|stall]]: “Alberic's stall” stands beside the spot where he keeps watch by day, with three shelves, Farming, Ranching and Fishing, selling seeds, fertilizer, shovels, feed, buckets, crated livestock, fishing rods and bait; its “Sell” page buys what the player grows, raises and catches. The money it takes is the Held Ground's own [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]; for prices see [[金钱]]. Press {{键|E}} on the stall to buy and sell, with no need to talk to him first, whether or not he is nearby, by day or by night.

He is not in the world until the player defeats [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]]. After defeating the White Knight, the player receives [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]]: hold it and right-click yourself to enter the Held Ground, where he is. For the boss fight see [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]]; this page is about the man.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

Before the White Knight falls, he is nowhere the player can reach. After he falls, he lives at the Held Ground: he sleeps in his own tent from 22:00 to 06:00, works at the forge and anvil east of the sword from 06:00 to 14:00, and from 14:00 to 22:00 stands by the planted sword, keeping watch over the land.

He is also the Held Ground's smith: before he was a knight, his father was a smith. Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark. The forge is always lit and anyone may use the anvil (see [[锻造]]); he also sells {{物品|锻锤}}, {{物品|煤炭}}, {{物品|铁锭}} and {{物品|钢条}} (see [[交易]]).

## Relationships
[[芬尼克|Fennick]] was once his squire, who carried his lance for six years as a young man; by then he had worn the Crown of Thorns for two hundred and fifty years. He can no longer recall Fennick's face. His brother by blood was named Hugh; his journal reads “My brother Hugh says it is a sentence”; the warden, Brother Tomas, was his sworn brother in the order, not his brother by blood (see [[古墓守卫]]).

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The first time the player enters the Held Ground, his welcome scene plays: “You are the one who took the crown off me. I could not see your face then. I can now.” He asks the player to tell him their name: “This time, I would like to keep a name.” Finally he hands the player 30 Dawnmarks: “Here: the coin of this land. My stall sells seeds, feed and bait, and buys what you grow, raise and catch. The pond is full of fish.”

Once the player has taken on Fennick's [[老骑士]], he speaks of a squire, once.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

All expansion of the Held Ground goes through him: talk to him and choose “The Held Ground…” to open the steward's panel, and hand him materials to put into the Held Ground's stock, to widen the land, open new grounds and put up working buildings (see [[领地扩建]]). Each time the land is widened, he gives you a sum of Dawnmarks:
- The first (the woodland): Logs and Stone. Reward 20 Dawnmarks.
- The second (the meadow): Planks and [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]]. Reward 40 Dawnmarks.
- The third (the ancient boundary stone): Planks, Stone and [[金矿石|Gold Ore]]. Reward 60 Dawnmarks. Gold Ore is dug from gold veins deep in [[旧矿坑]].

Each time one is finished, a ring of [[雾墙|mist]] around the Held Ground disperses, revealing new ground. See [[誓守之地]].

## Lore
Alberic is [[持印者|the First Seal-bearer]] and the Commander of the Order of the Thorn. On the roll in [[荆棘骸骨堂]], his name is cut the deepest: “Knelt to children. Chosen by the Holy City to bear the Crown of Thorns. He holds.” His vow is carved on the altar of [[守墓者圣所]]: “I will hold until I am relieved.” He was knighted at [[荆棘修道院]], and chosen there to wear the crown, with the promise that someone would relieve him after forty years; the Holy See's [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] was never sent, and he held for three hundred years (Chapter 1's design proposal). See [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] and [[第一章]].

{{剧透|What he does not say}}
The crown took most of his self, and he can no longer remember names. He has turned his vow, “hold until relieved”, around: since the player relieved him, he holds for the player.

The Seal-bearers are among the fulcrums that hold up the vessel that is [[阿斯特拉|Astra]]. Defeating him took away one fulcrum, which is why the nights really did grow longer after he fell. At dusk he apologizes for it: “The sun sets early. Earlier than it did. I am sorry. I think I am sorry.” See [[持印者]] and [[主线剧情]].
