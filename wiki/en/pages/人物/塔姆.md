**Tam** is the miller of [[Millbrook]], 52 years old, who grinds flour and presses lamp oil for [[Firstlight Village]]. Every week his water wheel turns more slowly. After dark, something got into his [[The Mill Cellar]], where the oil for the vigil is kept, and he will not go down.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps in the mill from 21:00 to 06:00 and tends the water wheel in the mill yard from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
He has no relationships in the village written in the game.

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

His very first words are a warning to the player: “Mind the brook. It bites when it's cold.”

## Quests
{{related quests}}

Tam gives no quests himself. The two flasks of vigil oil wanted for [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]]'s main quest [[The Vigil]] are in his cellar, together with the slimes and spiders down there. Once The Vigil is handed in, he says a line he says only once.

## Lore
Tam's water wheel is one more sign that “the days are getting shorter”: the brook depends on the mountain, and the mountain on the sun. Like [[Brin]], he always passes off strange things as “that's all it is”.

Once the vigil oil is handed in, he says his grandmother told him the vigil lamps were first lit from the first light of dawn, “and I believe her more now than I used to.” See [[Chapel of the First Dawn]] and [[Chapter 1]].
