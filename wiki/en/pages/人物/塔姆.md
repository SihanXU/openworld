**Tam** is the miller of [[水车磨坊]], 52 years old, who grinds flour and presses lamp oil for [[初原村]]. Every week his water wheel turns more slowly. After dark, something got into his [[磨坊地窖]], where the oil for the vigil is kept, and he will not go down.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps in the mill from 21:00 to 06:00 and tends the water wheel in the mill yard from 06:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
He has no relationships in the village written in the game.

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

His very first words are a warning to the player: “Mind the brook. It bites when it's cold.”

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Tam gives no quests himself. The two flasks of vigil oil wanted for [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]]'s main quest [[守夜之备]] are in his cellar, together with the slimes and spiders down there. Once The Vigil is handed in, he says a line he says only once.

## Lore
Tam's water wheel is one more sign that “the days are getting shorter”: the brook depends on the mountain, and the mountain on the sun. Like [[布琳]], he always passes off strange things as “that's all it is”.

Once the vigil oil is handed in, he says his grandmother told him the vigil lamps were first lit from the first light of dawn, “and I believe her more now than I used to.” See [[初晓礼拜堂]] and [[第一章]].
