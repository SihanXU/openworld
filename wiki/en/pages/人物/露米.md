**Lumi** is a fairy in the shape of a little winged girl, and the player's companion: a glowing star pinned in her moon-white, lilac-tinged hair, big violet eyes, a white dress, and a pair of clear, butterfly-like wings. When the player wakes on [[云顶台地]] (in the [[新手教程|Tutorial]], in [[林间空地]]), she is right beside them, and from then on she follows the player through the whole game. She is cheerful, curious and bold, and has a remark for everything.

- **Following.** She stays within a few cells behind the player, and when the player stands still she floats to a spot two cells behind them and stops; when the player turns to face her, she does not circle round behind again, and you can talk to her from two cells away. She flies, so she gets past things that block people on foot; when the player goes to another map, or gets too far from her, she follows at once.
- **Takes up no room, and does not fight.** She never blocks the way, and attacks swung forward pass beneath her; but an attack aimed at her on purpose still hits. She attacks nothing.
- **Hints.** Press {{键|E}} on her and she names the [[任务|quest]] being tracked: “Now: the quest's name.” After it come the unfinished objectives and their progress, plus this stage's hint; when no quest is under way she says “Nothing presses now. Go where you like!”
- **Remarks along the way.** When you reach a new place or something new happens, she says something in a bubble over her head; these are the story's “beats”.
- **Witnessing that the world learns.** The first time the player does something to a thing that the world has never seen, she notices. See [[世界会学]].
- **Boons.** When a Seal-bearer falls, part of the power that spills out flows to her, becoming power she carries for the player. See below and [[恩赐]].

She is an essential character: when her health falls to zero she is {{状态|倒地}} for a while, then wakes up; she does not die. On the [[小地图|minimap]] she is a yellow dot.

## Schedule
Lumi has no schedule; she only follows the player.

While she is down she cannot keep up with the player, and reports “cannot keep up” to the world, at most once per game hour. The outcome while she is down is defined: she stays where she fell, a sad face pops up over her head, and a notice at the bottom left reads “Lumi lies where she fell, too weak to keep up.” After 30 seconds she wakes with half her health and flies back to the player.

- She stays on whichever map she fell on. If the player goes to another map meanwhile, the time she spends down stops with that map: only when you return to the map she fell on does she wake and follow again.
- When something else keeps her from moving and she cannot keep up (being frozen, say), the outcome is still undefined, and is decided by [[世界会学|the world that learns]]; with no AI connected, she waits where she is and follows again once she can move.

## Relationships
The one she follows is the player. Whom she follows is a piece of data she carries, not a hard-coded “the player”.

- [[佩尔|Pell]] wants to draw her the first time he sees her.
- The [[圣教信使|Church Courier]], bringing the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, stares at her for a long time and says nothing.
- Before the thorns, [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] says to her: “You of all of them should know, little one. ...No. You would not remember.”
- After reading the sun-watch post's log on Cloudtop, [[奈莎·奎尔|Nessa Quill]] asks her whether she remembers any watchman.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Lumi is the quests' guide: the objectives and hints of each stage of the quest being tracked (by default, the main quest under way) can all be heard again by pressing {{键|E}} on her. In the [[新手教程|Tutorial]], what she tells you is how to do each stage of the main quest [[醒来]]. See [[任务#露米|Quests]].

Once out of the tutorial, she also speaks up on her own to teach you ways of playing you have not learned yet: these are the **guide quests** (see [[任务#指引：露米自己开口|Quests]]). She picks her moment and says a line, the quest begins by itself, and the panel's “The Road” page lists it beside the main quest:

| Guide | When | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|
| [[第一个技能点]] | You have an unspent skill point | Press {{键|K}} to open the [[技能|skill tree]] and learn a skill |
| [[选一条路]] | You reach level 5 with no [[职业|profession]] | Go to the drill yard and see {{人物|布拉姆}} to choose a profession |
| [[第一竿]] | The first time you walk up to the Firstlight Village pond | Make a fishing rod and go [[钓鱼|fishing]] |
| [[菜圃的一课]] | The first time you walk into the Firstlight Village vegetable garden | Pick up tools, harvest, sow, water ([[农耕|farming]]) |

### Boons
She can hold up to five boons, and carries only one at a time. Talking to her (pressing {{键|E}} on her) opens her panel: on the left her portrait, on the right two pages, “The Road” (in her own words, what you should do now, with the main quest's objectives and progress below, and the guide quests under way listed beside them) and “The Seals' Powers” (a slot for each of the five Seals, sealed until you have it). You can look at the powers at any time, but can only change them within {{数值|恩赐切换距离}} cells of an awakened [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] (pressing {{键|E}} on a statue and choosing “Listen” also opens this panel). A boon works only while she and the player are on the same map.

For now there is only one boon: “Crown's Insight”, gained by defeating [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], which doubles the experience you gain. The moment she catches it, a cold light flies from the kneeling knight to her, she glows and rises, and a banner reading “The First Seal” crosses the screen (see [[恩赐]]). For all boons, see [[恩赐]].

## Lore
In the setting, Lumi is not a guide sent by the Church.

{{剧透|Who she is}}
Lumi is what is left of **the Second Vessel**. Long ago, after [[阿斯特拉|Astra]], someone tried to bear [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]], and could not hold. The Five Sufferings stripped her of her power and her memory, leaving a little fairy who does not know what she once was. When and why that attempt happened is left for later chapters.

She can catch the power that spills from each Seal because what remains of her still answers to the Seals: when a Seal-bearer falls, the power that spills out flows to her “like water finding an old riverbed”. She thinks it is a gift.

Chapter 1 shows only a few corners of this: she trembles beside the cracked wound of the five-wound shrine stone; she does not know how old she is; the watchman on Cloudtop once found a little “sleeping light” in the snow, which woke and asked him how old it was; the Church Courier and the Grey Man both stare at her. Her lines are always light: the sadness is left for the player to find alone, and is never spoken by her. See [[主线剧情]] and [[承夜者]].
{{/剧透}}

## Trivia
- Her name comes from “light” (lumen): when a Seal-bearer's power spills out, it flows to her the way water finds an old channel, and she glows as she catches it. In the ever-longer nights, she is a point of light at the player's side.
- Before 2026-10-12 she was called “Pip” (小啾). When a save from before then is loaded, the name is changed automatically.
- Before 2026-10-05 she was a red parrot from the asset pack. When a save from before then is loaded, the parrot becomes her as she is now.
- The first time the player enters Firstlight Village, Pell asks whether she bites, and she answers: “I do not bite. ...Mostly not. Draw my good side.”
- She also has a card on the people page of the [[图鉴|Codex]].
