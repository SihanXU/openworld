**Holt** is the only guard of [[初原村]], 38 years old. He watches the path south out of the village: the things of Thornwood Hollow crawl up through the gap the storm left, and he stands at the head of that path.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps at the guards' quarters from 23:00 to 06:00 and watches the path from his post south of the village from 06:00 to 23:00.

## Relationships
He sleeps in the same quarters as [[芬尼克|Fennick]].

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

At night he warns the player that the undead roam the wilds, “but they never come into the village. Never.” This matches the game's rules: Firstlight Village is a [[和平区域|peaceful zone]], and [[夜行生物|night creatures]] do not appear in the village.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

- [[清剿荆棘谷]]: available from level 2. Defeat 6 monsters in [[荆棘谷]] (slimes, spiders and wolves all count). The reward is a [[长矛|Spear]] and the “Militia Pauldrons”.
- [[清剿夜行者]]: on some evenings he (or [[芬尼克|Fennick]]) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

## Guard
Holt keeps watch over [[初原村]] together with [[芬尼克|Fennick]], one of [[守卫与悬赏|Firstlight Village's guards]]:

- When he sees a crime, he reports it to the watch on the spot; when he sees a killing or an assault, he moves to arrest the culprit himself (see [[犯罪]]).
- When he sees someone wanted, he calls “You there! In the name of the watch, stop!” and goes to stop them: pay the fine, go to jail, talk your way out, bribe him, or resist arrest.
- Walk up to him with your weapon drawn and he tells you to put it away.
- He fights: struck, he strikes back; when someone cries for help, he runs to them.

The cells are behind the guards' quarters.

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Holt swears to catch [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]]. On the night of thanks he is the first to shout “Thief!”; the Grey Man walks across the river on the ice that forms under his feet, and Holt chases him as far as his spear can reach. After Chapter 1 ends, he says he wants to come along when the player goes further east. In the script this is a thread left for later.

## Trivia
- He says the Grey Man “looked at me like I was a child”.
