**Holt** is the only guard of [[Firstlight Village]], 38 years old. He watches the path south out of the village: the things of Thornwood Hollow crawl up through the gap the storm left, and he stands at the head of that path.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at the guards' quarters from 23:00 to 06:00 and watches the path from his post south of the village from 06:00 to 23:00.

## Relationships
He sleeps in the same quarters as [[Fennick|Fennick]].

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

At night he warns the player that the undead roam the wilds, “but they never come into the village. Never.” This matches the game's rules: Firstlight Village is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]], and [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] do not appear in the village.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[Clear the Hollow]]: available from level 2. Defeat 6 monsters in [[Thornwood Hollow]] (slimes, spiders and wolves all count). The reward is a [[Spear|Spear]] and the “Militia Pauldrons”.
- [[The Night-born]]: on some evenings he (or [[Fennick|Fennick]]) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

## Guard
Holt keeps watch over [[Firstlight Village]] together with [[Fennick|Fennick]], one of [[Guards and Bounties|Firstlight Village's guards]]:

- When he sees a crime, he reports it to the watch on the spot; when he sees a killing or an assault, he moves to arrest the culprit himself (see [[Crime]]).
- When he sees someone wanted, he calls “You there! In the name of the watch, stop!” and goes to stop them: pay the fine, go to jail, talk your way out, bribe him, or resist arrest.
- Walk up to him with your weapon drawn and he tells you to put it away.
- He fights: struck, he strikes back; when someone cries for help, he runs to them.

The cells are behind the guards' quarters.

## Lore
In Chapter 1, Holt swears to catch [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]]. On the night of thanks he is the first to shout “Thief!”; the Grey Man walks across the river on the ice that forms under his feet, and Holt chases him as far as his spear can reach. After Chapter 1 ends, he says he wants to come along when the player goes further east. In the script this is a thread left for later.

## Trivia
