**Mia** is [[Marta|Marta]]'s daughter, 9 years old. She followed the foragers all the way to the briars of [[Thornwood Hollow]], and lost her grandmother's silver ring there. By day she skips stones at the village pond.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps at Marta's house from 20:00 to 07:00 and skips stones by the pond from 07:00 to 20:00.

## Relationships
Her mother is Marta; [[Pell]] is her cousin, and calls Marta aunt. The three of them live together.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[Mia's Ring]]: the ring is silver, and lies where the briars are thickest on the east side of Thornwood Hollow. She begs the player not to tell her mother. Once you bring it back, she gives the player her “best stone” (an ordinary [[Stone|Stone]]) and “Mia's Ribbon Earring”.
