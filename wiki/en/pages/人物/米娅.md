**Mia** is [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s daughter, 9 years old. She followed the foragers all the way to the briars of [[荆棘谷]], and lost her grandmother's silver ring there. By day she skips stones at the village pond.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps at Marta's house from 20:00 to 07:00 and skips stones by the pond from 07:00 to 20:00.

## Relationships
Her mother is Marta; [[佩尔]] is her cousin, and calls Marta aunt. The three of them live together.
