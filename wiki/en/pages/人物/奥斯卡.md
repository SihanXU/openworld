**Oskar** is the oldest forager in [[Firstlight Village]], 63 years old. He followed [[Lina|Lina]] into [[The Old Barrow]] to bring her out, and ended up shut in there with her. He went down the stairs looking for another way out, and the dead down there drove him “like sheep” into a cell of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]] on the Barrow's second level. Once rescued, he mends baskets in the village.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

Before he is rescued, he spends all day in the cell in the Ossuary of the Thorn. After the player hands in [[Lost in the Barrow]] to [[Rowan|Rowan]], he returns to the village: he sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and sits on a bench mending baskets from 07:00 to 21:00.

When the player walks into his cell, a scene plays: he speaks of the roll of the order on the altar, where the last name is cut deeper than all the others; and he says the thing that guards this place is further down, “I listened to it walking all night. Back and forth. Like a sentry on a wall.”
