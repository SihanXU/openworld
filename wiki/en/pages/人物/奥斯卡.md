**Oskar** is the oldest forager in [[初原村]], 63 years old. He followed [[莉娜|Lina]] into [[古墓]] to bring her out, and ended up shut in there with her. He went down the stairs looking for another way out, and the dead down there drove him “like sheep” into a cell of [[荆棘骸骨堂]] on the Barrow's second level. Once rescued, he mends baskets in the village.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

Before he is rescued, he spends all day in the cell in the Ossuary of the Thorn. After the player hands in [[古墓里的失踪者]] to [[罗万|Rowan]], he returns to the village: he sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and sits on a bench mending baskets from 07:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
He went into the Barrow for Lina's sake.

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

When the player walks into his cell, a scene plays: he speaks of the roll of the order on the altar, where the last name is cut deeper than all the others; and he says the thing that guards this place is further down, “I listened to it walking all night. Back and forth. Like a sentry on a wall.”
