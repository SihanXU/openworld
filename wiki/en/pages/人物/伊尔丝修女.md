**Sister Ilse** is a sister of [[五苦圣教|the Church of the Five Sufferings]], 56 years old, and the keeper of the holy flame of [[初原村]]. The flame burns on the square beside the altar of [[阿斯特拉|Astra]], and the villagers believe it was taken from the first light of dawn after the First Long Night. Behind the altar and the flame stands her chapel ([[初原村礼拜堂]]), where [[安塞姆修士|Brother Anselm]] serves and keeps the bookstand that sells skill books. She is calm and says little, and she gives the player most of the quests of Chapter 1's main line.

The verse she most often recites is the core creed of the Church of the Five Sufferings: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. Suffering is the proof that calamity can be repaid.”

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps at home from 22:00 to 06:00 and keeps watch by the holy flame from 06:00 to 22:00. After the flame is stolen, she spends her days sweeping the cold altar instead.

## Relationships

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

Her lines change as Chapter 1 goes on: Lina and Oskar rescued, the flame stolen, the Church's letter, the Crown of Thorns brought into the village, the end of Chapter 1; each step has words of its own. She also says that dusk comes earlier and earlier, that every week she lights the lamps a little sooner, “and I write it all down”.

## Lore
Ilse is the Church's voice in Chapter 1. At dusk she tells the village the story of the First Long Night and of Astra (after the player has rescued the two and prepared the vigil); on the night of thanks she leads the whole village in prayer; on the morning after the flame is stolen, the [[圣教信使|Church Courier]] brings the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, and she tells the player: “The same night the flame was taken from us, the Church called for you. I do not believe in coincidence, pilgrim.” See [[第一章]].

The pilgrim before was Brother Cassian. When she was still a novice, he sat in her chapel and asked her to pray for him; thirty-one years ago the Church named him the Pilgrim, and “they told us he reached the Holy City.”

{{剧透|What she does not say}}
The Grey Man is a foundling she raised. On the night the flame was stolen she knew him by his walk, and told no one. She has also read a book of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]] that the Church burned.

That is why, after the flame is stolen, all she says is “Some people take a thing to keep it safe.” When the player brings the Crown of Thorns back to the village, what she says is: “He always carried things too heavy for him, even as a boy.”
{{/剧透}}
