**Sister Ilse** is a sister of [[Church of the Five Sufferings|the Church of the Five Sufferings]], 46 years old, and the keeper of the holy flame of [[Firstlight Village]]. The flame burns on the square beside the altar of [[Astra|Astra]], and the villagers believe it was taken from the first light of dawn after the First Long Night. Behind the altar and the flame stands her chapel ([[Firstlight Chapel]]), where [[Brother Anselm|Brother Anselm]] serves and keeps the bookstand that sells skill books. She is graceful and gentle, a little absent-minded, cares for everyone in the village like a mother, and is deeply devout; she gives the player most of the quests of Chapter 1's main line.

## Appearance
White hair and crimson eyes from birth; she looks much younger than 46. Her long hair is worn in a loose braid over her left shoulder, under a long ivory veil that falls past her waist, pinned at its side with a small five-petalled gold flower. A high-collared ivory habit with wide sleeves; a short madder-red cape with a gold border over her shoulders; at her chest an Amulet of the Five Wounds (a small gold ring set with five red stones, like the one [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)|The Pilgrim's Road]] rewards), and at her waist a prayer cord tied in five knots. Her eyes are weak in bright light and she squints at noon, so she knows people by their step before their face.

She has a **portrait of her own**: when you talk to her, the dialogue box shows it. It has six expressions: neutral (a gentle smile), happy (a smile with her eyes closed; she never laughs too broadly), sad, angry (a slight frown and a pout, a reproach rather than anger), surprised and flustered (a blush). She blinks soon after each line begins, and her mouth opens and closes as she speaks. Her sprite on the map follows the design too: white hair, red eyes. Her design book: [[Art#Portraits of their own and story illustrations|Art]].

The verse she most often recites is the core creed of the Church of the Five Sufferings: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. Suffering is the proof that calamity can be repaid.”

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps in her small house west of the altar of [[Firstlight Chapel|Firstlight Chapel]] from 22:00 to 06:00 and keeps watch by the holy flame from 06:00 to 22:00. After the flame is stolen, she spends her days sweeping the cold altar instead.

## Relationships
[[Rowan]] is an old friend of hers. [[Brother Anselm|Brother Anselm]] serves in her chapel. She and [[Father Caius|Father Caius]] have written to each other for years, and both keep the hour of dusk in a ledger.

She minds everyone: [[Oskar]]'s knees ache before rain, so she leaves a warm stone at his door, and he thinks [[Marta]] left it; [[Nessa Quill|Nessa]] reads until her candle is gone, so she brings a new one and pretends she was only passing; she keeps every drawing of the road east that [[Pell]] gives her.

When [[Lina]]'s mother died of a winter fever, Lina eight, it was Ilse who sat with her through the last nights. She offered to take Lina into the chapel's care; Lina would not. Since then the chapel door has never been latched at night. Lina never admits it, but she is grateful and fond of her: whoever calls the sister forgetful answers to Lina first.

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

Her lines change as Chapter 1 goes on: Lina and Oskar rescued, the flame stolen, the Church's letter, the Crown of Thorns brought into the village, the end of Chapter 1; each step has words of its own. Morning, evening, late night, rain and storm each have a line too, most of them minding the player: soaked through, you are told to stand by the flame a while; on a storm night, “I count the doors I hear close. ...Go home early, will you? I'd like to count yours too.” She says the evenings come sooner and every week she lights the lamps a little earlier and writes each one down, “only I've forgotten which book I wrote them in”.

## Lore
Ilse is the Church's voice in Chapter 1. At dusk she tells the village the story of the First Long Night and of Astra (after the player has rescued the two and prepared the vigil); on the night of thanks she leads the whole village in prayer; on the morning after the flame is stolen, the [[Church Courier|Church Courier]] brings the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, and she tells the player: “The flame taken and the Church calling you, in the same night. Astra never errs, pilgrim, even if I often do.” See [[Chapter 1]].

The pilgrim before was Brother Cassian. When she was still a novice, he sat in her chapel and asked her to pray for him; thirty-one years ago the Church named him the Pilgrim, and “they told us he reached the Holy City.”

{{spoiler|What she does not say}}
The Grey Man is a foundling she raised: when she was sixteen and still a novice, someone left a baby on the chapel step. She raised him until he was ten, when a brother of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]] who came to tend the flame took him into the order. On the night the flame was stolen she knew him by his walk, and told no one. She has also read a book of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]] that the Church burned.

That is why, after the flame is stolen, all she says is “Some people take a thing to keep it safe.” When the player brings the Crown of Thorns back to the village, what she says is: “He always carried things too heavy for him, even as a boy.”

## Trivia
- After the flame is stolen she still sweeps the altar every morning, the taper still in her hand: “My hands are slower than my heart to believe the flame is gone.”
- She tells you to keep the Crown of Thorns “close, but not near your heart”. Nessa says the crown hums at night, so she sang it a hymn for sleeping.
- Some mornings there is dawnbloom on the altar before she gets there: “Astra has a friend who picks flowers very early, and very quietly.” It is [[Lina]].
- Handing in The Vigil, she says: “And this time, I didn't lose a single wick.”
