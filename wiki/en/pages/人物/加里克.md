**Garrick** is the smith of [[Emberfall]], 46 years old. His family has been smiths for twelve generations, and every generation kept the pattern of a dragon-slaying sword, yet never had ore enough to forge it. At the anvil he is loud; away from it he says little.

> The line of Emberfall and [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] was written before the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]] setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See [[Emberfall and the Dragon]] and [[Chapter 2]].

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps in the smithy from 21:00 to 06:00 and works at the anvil from 06:00 to 21:00. The work needs a lit [[forge|forge]].

## Quests
{{related quests}}

Garrick's part belongs to the main quest [[The Dragon Road]]: the iron ore and coal you give him, and the Dragonsbane he forges, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it he has a “?” over his head. After you have heard [[Mara|Mara]]'s council:
1. Give him [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and [[Coal|Coal]] one piece at a time ({{key|G}}). He wants 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal in all; once he has enough, give him more and he says “That's iron enough” or “That's coal enough”, and the piece stays in your hand.
2. Once he has enough of both, press {{key|E}} on him and he forges [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]]: “Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge this one.”

The Iron Ore and Coal are in [[The Old Mine]]: break open an ore vein with a [[Pickaxe|Pickaxe]] and they are yours. The deepest chamber is guarded by a [[cyclops|cyclops]], which carries two Iron Ore of its own.

## Trade
Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark, showing he is a smith.

Garrick is also a merchant (see [[Trading]]): he sells {{item|Pickaxe}}, {{item|Hoe}} and {{item|Dagger}}, restocks every morning, and carries 60 {{item|Dawnmark}} to buy your goods. He fights: struck, he strikes back, and he runs to anyone who cries for help.
