**Garrick** is the smith of [[余烬镇]], 46 years old. His family has been smiths for twelve generations, and every generation kept the pattern of a dragon-slaying sword, yet never had ore enough to forge it. At the anvil he is loud; away from it he says little.

> The line of Emberfall and [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] was written before the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]] setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See [[余烬镇与魔龙]] and [[第二章]].

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Garrick's part belongs to the main quest [[屠龙之路]]: the iron ore and coal you give him, and the Dragonsbane he forges, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it he has a “?” over his head. After you have heard [[玛拉|Mara]]'s council:
1. Give him [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and [[煤炭|Coal]] one piece at a time ({{键|G}}). He wants 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal in all; once he has enough, give him more and he says “That's iron enough” or “That's coal enough”, and the piece stays in your hand.
2. Once he has enough of both, press {{键|E}} on him and he forges [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]]: “Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge this one.”

The Iron Ore and Coal are in [[旧矿坑]]: break open an ore vein with a [[镐|Pickaxe]] and they are yours. The deepest chamber is guarded by a [[独眼巨人|cyclops]], which carries two Iron Ore of its own.

## Trade
Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark, showing he is a smith.

Garrick is also a merchant (see [[交易]]): he sells {{物品|镐}}, {{物品|锄头}} and {{物品|匕首}}, restocks every morning, and carries 60 {{物品|晨辉币}} to buy your goods. He fights: struck, he strikes back, and he runs to anyone who cries for help.
