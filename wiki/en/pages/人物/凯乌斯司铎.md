**Father Caius** is a priest of the Church of the Five Sufferings, 61 years old. The Holy See posted him to [[朝圣者驿站]] to bless the pilgrims it sends east, and to keep the Five Wounds in [[五伤礼拜堂]]. He is gentle, tired, and firm in his faith. When the pilgrim named in the Church's letter reaches the Rest, he is the one who hears the Pilgrim's Vow.

After the vow he looks at your [[善恶与信念|faith]]: at faith 30 or above he blesses you, gives you two flasks of [[圣水|Holy Water]], and asks you to tell him what the Writ of Relief says; at faith −30 or below he has heard that you spread unbelief, and refuses you the Rest's blessing. He is **devout**: spread unbelief to him and he grows angry, regard −8.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps in the Chapel of the Five from 21:00 to 06:00, keeps watch over the Five Wounds in the chapel from 06:00 to 12:00, and from 12:00 to 21:00 stands at the door watching the road for pilgrims.

## Relationships
He and [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] have written to each other for years, and both keep the hour of dusk in a ledger: “Neither of us has written what it means.”

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The first time you speak to him, he says: “Peace on your road, pilgrim. I am Father Caius; the Holy See posted me to this Rest to bless the pilgrims it sends east, and to keep the Five Wounds.” This greeting does not mention the White Knight, and is the same whether you meet him before or after the White Knight falls.

Ordinarily he tells you what to lay at each of the five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]] along the road, and he also says: “They say the Seal-bearer on the East Road was once a gentle man. The Crown asks the most of gentle men.”

After the White Knight falls, he says: “Three hundred years, and in the end it took one pilgrim and one fairy. God forgive us for letting him keep it so long.” And also: “The road east is open. Go on when you are ready, pilgrim: four more Seal-bearers are waiting, and the Crown of Thorns is only the first of five.”

## Trade
He sells candles, Holy Water and bread. Candles can be laid at the [[穿心圣枪之龛|Shrine of the Lance]].

## Lore
What he speaks of is the mission the Holy See proclaimed: seek out the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]], defeat them, and bring the five [[圣刑之器|Instruments of the Sufferings]] back to the Holy City.

- **The welcome** (after the Church's letter and before the White Knight falls, on your first visit to the Rest): “A pilgrim at last, and with a fairy, just as the letter said. Welcome to the Rest. I am Father Caius; the Holy See posted me here to send pilgrims on their way.” “Before you go on, take the vow. Come into the chapel and kneel before the Five Wounds; every pilgrim the Holy See has sent east knelt there first.”
- **The Pilgrim's Vow**: before the altar of the Five Wounds he has the pilgrim kneel, and recites [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] one by one as the Church reads them (the fourth: “The Nailing. On the holy wood He accepted death.”), and says the five shrines on the road stand one for each Suffering, “then the abbey, then the knight”. At the end he says: “That Seal-bearer has kept that road for three hundred years. The Holy See bids you defeat him and bring back the Crown of Thorns, the first of the five Instruments. ...He ought to have been relieved. That is not a pilgrim's concern.” The pilgrim receives the [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]].
- **After the White Knight falls**: the welcome and the vow at the Rest no longer happen. When you come to the Rest again, he comes out to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. ...Then it is done, and the road is open. The Holy See will have its Crown of Thorns, the first of the five Instruments.” “Rest here as long as you like, pilgrim. The chapel is always open, and the Five Wounds are always there.”

{{剧透|What he does not say}}
He knows the First Seal-bearer should have been relieved after forty years, and he knows the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] was never sent. After the player finds the writ in [[荆棘修道院]], he admits it: “You found the writ. ...Yes. I knew. I was told it was not a pilgrim's concern, and I chose to believe it. Go. Don't look at me like that.”
