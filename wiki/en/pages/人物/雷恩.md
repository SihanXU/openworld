**Wren** is the herbalist of [[Emberfall]], 23 years old, young and quick-handed, and the only one in town who has read about dragons rather than only feared them. Her [[cauldron|cauldron]] is always burning.

> The line of Emberfall and [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] was written before the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]] setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See [[Emberfall and the Dragon]] and [[Chapter 2]].

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps in her hut from 22:00 to 06:00 and minds the cauldron from 06:00 to 22:00. Minding the cauldron needs the cauldron lit; if it has been doused, she lights it again herself: “Out again. Pass me the flint.”

## Relationships
Elder [[Mara]] taught her to read.

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

She tells the player where the materials are: “Fireweed grows on the open grassland; frostcaps grow in the forest shade.” She has also read that a wounded dragon calls its whelps.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

Wren's part belongs to the main quest [[The Dragon Road]]: the fireweed and frostcaps you give her, and the Fireproof Draught she brews, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it she has a “?” over her head. After you have heard [[Mara|Mara]]'s council:
1. Give her [[Fireweed|Fireweed]] and [[Frostcap|Frostcaps]] one at a time ({{key|G}}), 3 of each. Fireweed grows in [[Firstlight Village]], frostcaps in [[The Forest]].
2. Once she has enough of both, press {{key|E}} on her, and she brews two flasks of [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]]: drink one at the mouth of the lair, and the second when the first is about to wear off.

## Trade
Wren is also a merchant (see [[Trading]]): she sells {{item|Healing Potion}}, restocking to 3 flasks every morning, and carries 40 {{item|Dawnmark}} to buy your goods.
