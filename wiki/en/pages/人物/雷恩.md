**Wren** is the herbalist of [[余烬镇]], 23 years old, young and quick-handed, and the only one in town who has read about dragons rather than only feared them. Her [[坩埚|cauldron]] is always burning.

> The line of Emberfall and [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] was written before the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]] setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See [[余烬镇与魔龙]] and [[第二章]].

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps in her hut from 22:00 to 06:00 and minds the cauldron from 06:00 to 22:00. Minding the cauldron needs the cauldron lit; if it has been doused, she lights it again herself: “Out again. Pass me the flint.”

## Relationships
Elder [[玛拉]] taught her to read.

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

She tells the player where the materials are: “Fireweed grows on the open grassland; frostcaps grow in the forest shade.” She has also read that a wounded dragon calls its whelps.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Wren's part belongs to the main quest [[屠龙之路]]: the fireweed and frostcaps you give her, and the Fireproof Draught she brews, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it she has a “?” over her head. After you have heard [[玛拉|Mara]]'s council:
1. Give her [[火焰草|Fireweed]] and [[霜盖菇|Frostcaps]] one at a time ({{键|G}}), 3 of each. Fireweed grows in [[初原村]], frostcaps in [[森林]].
2. Once she has enough of both, press {{键|E}} on her, and she brews two flasks of [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]]: drink one at the mouth of the lair, and the second when the first is about to wear off.

## Trade
Wren is also a merchant (see [[交易]]): she sells {{物品|治疗药水}}, restocking to 3 flasks every morning, and carries 40 {{物品|晨辉币}} to buy your goods.

## Lore
The Fireproof Draught makes you {{状态|防火}} for a while: fire damage is greatly reduced, and you cannot be set alight. See [[烬峰魔龙]].
