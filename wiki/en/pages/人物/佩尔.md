**Pell** is [[Marta|Marta]]'s nephew, 9 years old, and he loves drawing on walls in chalk. He says he has seen the Grey Man three times, and nobody believes him. The man gave him a stone that is warm at night; since he got it, the things of the night have not come near his window.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at Marta's house from 20:00 to 07:00, and from 07:00 to 20:00 he is about the village drawing on walls in chalk.

## Relationships
Marta is his aunt; [[Mia]] is his cousin. His father went east and never wrote again (so Marta says).

{{relationships}}

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

The first time the player comes into the village, he spots [[Lumi|Lumi]] at once: “A fairy! Can I draw her? Can I? Don't move, either of you!” At dusk, when [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]] tells the story of the First Long Night, he is the one who begged her to tell it, and the one who notices that Lumi is trembling.

## Quests
{{related quests}}

- [[Pell's Drawings]]: he drew a picture at every place he saw the Grey Man, four in all: beside the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] at [[Mountain Foot]], at the door of [[The Old Barrow]], by the little bridge at [[The Ford]], and by the holy flame in the village. Press {{key|E}} to look at each one. Once you have found them all, he gives the player another [[Warm Ember|warm stone]] and the “Chalk-dusted Cloak”.

## Lore
Laid side by side, the four drawings say that the Grey Man always came first: each time on the night before something bad happened. Pell's conclusion is that “he didn't do the bad things. He just got there first.” See [[The Grey Man]] and [[Chapter 1]].

{{spoiler|What the stone is}}
The warm stone is an ember taken from the dawn's holy flame. It is why the things of the night stay away from Pell's window.
