**Pell** is [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s nephew, 9 years old, and he loves drawing on walls in chalk. He says he has seen the Grey Man three times, and nobody believes him. The man gave him a stone that is warm at night; since he got it, the things of the night have not come near his window.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps at Marta's house from 20:00 to 07:00, and from 07:00 to 20:00 he is about the village drawing on walls in chalk.

## Relationships
Marta is his aunt; [[米娅]] is his cousin. His father went east and never wrote again (so Marta says).

{{人际关系}}

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

The first time the player comes into the village, he spots [[露米|Lumi]] at once: “A fairy! Can I draw her? Don't move. Does she bite?” At dusk, when [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] tells the story of the First Long Night, he is the one who begged her to tell it, and the one who notices that Lumi is trembling.

## Lore
Laid side by side, the four drawings say that the Grey Man always came first: each time on the night before something bad happened. Pell's conclusion is that “he didn't do the bad things. He just got there first.” See [[灰衣人]] and [[第一章]].

{{剧透|What the stone is}}
The warm stone is an ember taken from the dawn's holy flame. It is why the things of the night stay away from Pell's window.
{{/剧透}}
