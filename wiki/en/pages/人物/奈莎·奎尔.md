**Nessa Quill** compiles almanacs for [[The Holy City|the Holy City]], 31 years old. Since the bridge at the Ford broke, she has been stranded in [[Firstlight Village]]. She measures the heavens for the Church's almanac, and every evening at the east end of the village she writes down the moment of sunset. Her figures show that the days are getting shorter; see [[Day and Night]].

## Appearance
Long straight black hair to her waist, heavy blunt bangs cut at the eyebrows; a white feather ornament by her left ear, a star hanging from its gold chain. Thin round silver-grey glasses, violet eyes. An open dark indigo long coat with lapels and wide turned-back cuffs; under it a white shirt, a deep violet satin bow, a cream waistcoat with a row of small brass buttons; taupe straight trousers and brown laced boots. A brass pocket watch is chained to her waistcoat, and she times every sunset with it; the fingertips of her right hand are often ink-stained.

She has a **portrait of her own**: when you talk to her, the dialogue box shows it instead of the bust the [[Looks|look]] system composes. It has six expressions, neutral, happy (a slight smile without a blush — she never smiles wide), sad, exasperated, surprised and shy, and changes with the mood of each line; every expression blinks, and her mouth moves as she speaks. Her figure on the map has the long black hair, the round glasses and the indigo coat too.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

She sleeps under the market awning from 23:00 to 06:00; from 06:00 to 17:00 she sits at her table copying out her tables; from 17:00 to 23:00 she goes to the east end of the village to measure the sunset.

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

Speak to her at dusk and she may be in the middle of counting: “Hush. The sun's touching the treetops. Fourteen. Fifteen. There.” On the day the First Seal falls, she says the sun set four minutes early, “and I'm sure it means nothing”.

## Lore
Nessa is the recorder of Chapter 1's thread that “the days are getting shorter”: her sunset tables, the old almanac's curve, and the log on Cloudtop that no one has read in eleven years all say the same thing. In Chapter 1's last scene she announces those four minutes. See [[Chapter 1]] and [[The Long Night]].

{{spoiler|What she does not say}}
The last pages of the old almanac were written as the First Long Night fell, in a hand begging someone to “stop him”. She cannot make sense of them. In the setting this is a misreading planted for the first reversal of the [[Main Story|Main Story]]. Once she has given you the book, hold it and left-click to use it on yourself to read those pages; see [[Almanac of the First Night]].

## Trivia
- She says the sun set four minutes early on the day the First Seal fell. In the game, after the White Knight falls, nightfall moves from 21:00 to 20:00, and dawn is pushed back to 05:30.
- After reading the watchman's log, she says: “A sleeping light in the snow. I do not know what he meant by that. I am sure it means nothing.”
