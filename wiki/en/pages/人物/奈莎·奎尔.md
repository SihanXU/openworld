**Nessa Quill** compiles almanacs for [[圣城|the Holy City]], 31 years old. Since the bridge at the Ford broke, she has been stranded in [[初原村]]. She measures the heavens for the Church's almanac, and every evening at the east end of the village she writes down the moment of sunset. Her figures show that the days are getting shorter; see [[昼夜]].

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

She sleeps under the market awning from 23:00 to 06:00; from 06:00 to 17:00 she sits at her table copying out her tables; from 17:00 to 23:00 she goes to the east end of the village to measure the sunset.

## Relationships
She bought an old almanac from before the First Long Night from [[托宾|Tobin]].

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

Speak to her at dusk and she may be in the middle of counting: “Hush. The sun's touching the treetops. Fourteen. Fifteen. There.” On the day the First Seal falls, she says the sun set four minutes early, “and I'm sure it means nothing”.

## Lore
Nessa is the recorder of Chapter 1's thread that “the days are getting shorter”: her sunset tables, the old almanac's curve, and the log on Cloudtop that no one has read in eleven years all say the same thing. In Chapter 1's last scene she announces those four minutes. See [[第一章]] and [[极夜]].

{{剧透|What she does not say}}
The last pages of the old almanac were written as the First Long Night fell, in a hand begging someone to “stop him”. She cannot make sense of them. In the setting this is a misreading planted for the first reversal of the [[主线剧情|Main Story]]. Once she has given you the book, hold it and left-click to use it on yourself to read those pages; see [[初夜历书]].
{{/剧透}}

## Trivia
- She says the sun set four minutes early on the day the First Seal fell. In the game, after the White Knight falls, nightfall moves from 21:00 to 20:00, and dawn is pushed back to 05:30.
- After reading the watchman's log, she asks [[露米|Lumi]] whether she remembers any watchman, and answers herself: “No. Of course not.”
