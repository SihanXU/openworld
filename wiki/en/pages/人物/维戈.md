**Vigo** is a drifter the storm washed into [[Firstlight Village]], 29 years old. He has no home, not a single {{item|Dawnmark}} on him, and not much conscience, and he arrived hungry. He sleeps rough at the east end of the village; by day he loafs by the well and mends his boots at his camp, and in the evening he warms his hands at the fire on the square.

## Schedule
{{schedule}}

He sleeps at the camp at the east end of the village from 22:00 to 06:00, loafs by the well from 06:00 to 12:00, goes back to his camp to mend his boots from 12:00 to 18:00, and warms his hands at the fire on the square from 18:00 to 22:00.

## Relationships
Vigo knows no one in the village, and has no home and no friends. Every villager's opinion of him starts at “neutral”; after he has stolen a few times, that is another matter.

## Dialogue
{{dialogue}}

In the days after someone in the village loses something, he says “Don't look at me. …All right, fine. I was hungry.” Give him food and he says “I owe you for this. Ask, and I'll come with you.”

## Quests
{{related quests}}

Vigo has no side quest of his own. He never has money on him, and when he is hungry and can find no food he often asks you to bring him something to eat (the commission [[A Bite to Eat]]); when he takes someone's things and the owner sees him, the owner asks you to get them back (the commission [[What Was Taken]]).

## He steals
Vigo is the one in the village who steals (see [[People's Needs]]):

- As soon as he is hungry (satiety below sixty percent) and has neither food nor money on him, he goes to take any food he can reach within 14 cells: the bread among [[Marta|Marta]]'s goods, what is in the chests.
- His conscience is only twenty percent, so he even picks other people's pockets (except the guards').
- If he is seen, it is just as when you steal: the owner asks him for it back, passers-by shout “Thief!”, and [[Holt|Holt]] and [[Fennick|Fennick]] go to stop him. He cannot pay the fine, so he is locked in a cell.

Stay in the village a day or two and you will often see this. Give him food, and he steals once less.

## Hiring him
Vigo is a **hired hand**: whatever his opinion of you, say “Come with me.” and he follows you (see [[Followers and Hiring]]). He fights: if something turns on you, he rushes in to help. Struck himself, he strikes back.

He is not someone the story cannot do without: when his health reaches zero, he dies (see [[Death of Characters]]).

## Lore
Vigo was added to test that “villagers' behavior goes through the same rules too”: he steals, is seen, is talked about and is caught, through exactly the same events and rules as the player.
