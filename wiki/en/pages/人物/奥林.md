**Orrin** is the charcoal burner of [[烧炭谷]], 58 years old, who has burned charcoal for the smiths and hearths of [[初原村]] for forty years. A clamp takes four days to burn and is ruined if no one sleeps beside it, so he sleeps beside it. His back can no longer carry wood; while [[格斯|Gus]]'s back still held, Gus brought him wood. Boars have rooted up the stools in his coppice, and no new shoots come. Since the night the little bridge broke, every evening at dusk the birds of the whole forest settle on the old oak at the west end of his dell, without a sound; every day he cuts a notch in a board.

## Lore
Orrin is the other end of the clue that “there are no birds in the forest”: the first time you enter [[森林]], Lumi says she cannot hear a single bird, and the answer is in his oak. After you have watched the old oak at dusk (see [[烧炭谷]]), he says a line he says only once: he no longer cuts the notches for the birds, but “for what they're waiting for”. Like the kneeling cattle on [[草原]], the birds are looking east, toward [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]]. See [[第一章]].

## Trivia
- He is the first person in the game to sell Coal; before him, Coal could only be dug in [[旧矿坑]] and [[采石场]], or bought from [[哈尔多|Haldor]].
