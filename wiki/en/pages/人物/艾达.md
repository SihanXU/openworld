**Edda** is the owner of the burned farmstead on [[东大路]], 44 years old. The days were too short and the wheat died; the things of the night came; then a knight in white passed by and took everything that shone, not sparing even her late husband's ring, which hung on the doorpost. When the player first meets her, she is hiding in the barn.

After [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] falls, she moves to [[初原村]] and works the village's vegetable garden.

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

Before the White Knight falls, she spends all day hiding in the barn on the East Road. After he falls, she sleeps at her lodgings in Firstlight Village from 21:00 to 07:00 and tends the village garden from 07:00 to 21:00.

## Relationships
Her husband is dead. She hung his ring on the door “so he could find his way home”.

## Lore
Edda is the witness of the East Road in [[第一章]]: the short days ruined the harvest, the things of the night followed, and a knight in white took everything that shone. On the night her barn burned, a man in grey pulled her out of the smoke. See [[灰衣人]].

{{剧透|What she does not say}}
She owes that “heretic” her life, but dares not say so. So every time she mentions the Grey Man, she adds: “Don't tell the village I told you.”
{{/剧透}}
