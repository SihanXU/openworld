**Tobin** is a peddler who works the road to the east, 34 years old. Twice a year he brings charms, lamp oil and sundries from the east, talking all the way. The storm stopped the ferry, so he moved his [[手推车|handcart]] to [[初原村]]. Nobody here buys what is on the cart, and he has nothing to eat himself.

On the cart are a [[魔杖|Frost Wand]] and a [[一瓶灯油|flask of lamp oil]], both his. Give him any food ({{键|G}}) and he gives the player the Frost Wand; from then on, taking the wand is not theft. It is one of the game's rare barters; see [[金钱]].

## Schedule
{{日程表}}

He sleeps by his cart from 22:00 to 06:00; from 06:00 to 09:00 and from 11:00 to 19:00 he watches the cart (not while the cart is on fire); from 09:00 to 11:00 he draws water at the [[水井|well]] on the square; from 19:00 to 22:00 he warms his hands at the fire on the square. That fire is the village's holy flame; when it is out (after the flame is stolen, for example), he cannot warm his hands then. On stormy days he spends all day sheltering under the market awning.

## Relationships
He owes [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] at the Ford the fare for a crossing. He says the river owes them both; Hesper does not laugh.

[[布拉姆|Bram]] in the drill yard complains that he talks nonstop on the sidelines at every drill.

He sold [[奈莎·奎尔|Nessa Quill]] an old almanac from before the First Long Night, and last winter sold a frost charm to a man in grey.

## Dialogue
{{对话}}

He is happiest when given food: “Two days. Two whole days.” When he sees someone stealing, he says he is a peddler, and one always sees these things.

## Quests
{{相关任务}}

Tobin gives no quests. Trading food for the Frost Wand is one of the ways to cross at the Ford: the wand's cold freezes the water's surface into {{状态|薄冰}}, and you walk across on the ice. See [[渡口]].

## Trade and fencing
Tobin is a merchant of [[初原村]] (see [[交易]]): he sells {{物品|一瓶灯油}}, {{物品|兜帽}}, {{物品|开锁器}} and {{物品|爆竹}}, restocks every morning, and carries 40 {{物品|晨辉币}} to buy your goods.

He is also the village's only **fence**: stolen goods other merchants will not take, he does, only at half the price again. The hood and lockpick he sells are just what that line of work needs (see [[犯罪]] and [[潜行与扒窃]]).

He minds his own business: when he sees others stealing, he does not shout or go to the watch. Steal from him, though, and he will still ask for it back.

## Lore
Chapter 1 gave him two touches: the old almanac sold to Nessa, and the frost charm sold to the Grey Man. Only after the flame is stolen does he work out that the Grey Man crossed the river with that charm. See [[灰衣人]].

{{剧透|What he does not say}}
He hopes Hesper has forgotten that fare; she has not. He is afraid of the slimes that crawl out of their burrows at night.

The old coins the Grey Man paid him bear no king; they are the coins of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]].

## Trivia
- Before 2026-10-03 he camped at the Ford and slept by his cart; his line “better than sleeping by the hole at the Ford with one eye open” is about those days.
- In thunder he shouts not to stand under the trees, then mentions his uncle, and will not say more.
