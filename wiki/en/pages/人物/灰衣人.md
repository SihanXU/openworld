**The Grey Man** is a man in Chapter 1 in a grey travelling cloak, with ash on his hands, carrying a shuttered brass lantern. The villagers have each glimpsed him at the edge of their own corner. On the night of thanks he walks into [[初原村]], gathers the holy flame into his lantern, and crosses the river eastward over ice that forms beneath his feet.

He appears only in story scenes: a scene puts him on the map, and once he has spoken he leaves. He does not live anywhere the player can reach, and the player cannot talk to him or fight him.

He has no schedule. He waits in an “elsewhere” the player cannot reach, until a story scene calls him out.

- [[乔斯|Joss]]: twenty years ago, a young man in grey paid for the little bridge at the Ford.
- [[赫斯珀|Hesper]], [[霍尔特|Holt]]: watched him walk across the river.

## Lore
In the game he is only ever called “the Grey Man”. In the setting his name is **Severin, the Greyvowed**, about 40 years old, once a brother of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]]. That order of the Church guarded the holy flame taken from the first light of dawn; twenty years ago the Church dissolved it, and its last few brothers were declared heretics. In the villagers' eyes, he is the heretic who scratched the shrine stone, lured the foragers into the Barrow and stole the holy flame.

His rules: he never harms anyone, never explains in more than one sentence, and everything he does protects someone. The player never fights him.

{{剧透|What he is really doing}}
From the very start he believes the first reversal of the [[主线剧情|Main Story]]: that [[阿斯特拉|Astra]] is the monster, the sealed First Night now waking; and that the Church, by sending the power of each Seal to the Holy City, is waking him. That is why he works against the Pilgrimage. He takes the holy flame drawn from the dawn so as to gather the power that spills out when each Seal-bearer falls, and keep it from reaching the Holy City.

He is a foundling Sister Ilse raised from infancy. Fennick knows him too.

For each of his acts in Chapter 1, what is seen, what the village makes of it, and the truth all differ:

| What is seen | What the village makes of it | The truth |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh ash on the first cracked wound of the five-wound shrine stone | A heretic desecrating the holy stone | He was checking how far the First Seal had decayed |
| “The grey man shut the door from the outside” | He trapped people in the Barrow | He shut the restless wardens in, and left the people bread stamped with the Watch's mark |
| Pell's warm stone | A wicked thing from a stranger | An ember taken from the dawn's holy flame; the things of the night stay away from Pell's window |
| He takes the holy flame and crosses on ice | Theft of a holy thing; sorcery | He needs this flame of the dawn to gather the power the Seal-bearers spill, and took it before the Pilgrimage began |
| He gathers the knight's spilled power into his lantern | A heretic devouring a Seal | He keeps this power from reaching the Holy City; the flame gutters, and his hands shake |
| “Every seal you break makes the night longer” | Heresy | It is true |

In the setting, by the middle of the game the hero comes round to his view, and the two of them hunt Astra together; on the way they discover the second reversal: Astra is not the First Night but its cage, and Severin mistook his enemy. This part is not yet in the game (to be designed).

How he will use this flame of the dawn is not yet settled in the setting.
