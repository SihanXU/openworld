**The Grey Man** is a man in Chapter 1 in a grey travelling cloak, with ash on his hands, carrying a shuttered brass lantern. The villagers have each glimpsed him at the edge of their own corner. On the night of thanks he walks into [[Firstlight Village]], gathers the holy flame into his lantern, and crosses the river eastward over ice that forms beneath his feet.

He appears only in story scenes: a scene puts him on the map, and once he has spoken he leaves. He does not live anywhere the player can reach, and the player cannot talk to him or fight him.

## Appearance
Tall and lean, dark unkempt hair falling over his brow, gold eyes, a faint stubble. A long grey travelling cloak frayed into strips at the hem, its hood usually up, a line of rust-orange lining at its edge; under it the dark charcoal high-collared tunic of an old order. His fingers and the backs of his hands are grey, as if with ash that will not wash off. He carries a small hooded brass lantern whose warm golden flame is the only warm colour on him, and wears a pale blue crystal pendant at his chest. He carries no weapon.

He has a **portrait of his own** (with the hood up): when he speaks in a scene, the dialogue box shows it. It has six expressions: neutral (calm), happy (a rueful half smile), sad (weary, eyes lowered), angry (a cold stare), surprised (restrained) and uneasy (looking aside); he blinks, and his mouth opens and closes as he speaks. His sprite on the map follows: dark hair, gold eyes. His design book: [[Art#Portraits of their own and story illustrations|Art]].

## Lore
In the game he is only ever called “the Grey Man”. In the setting his name is **Severin, the Greyvowed**, 30 years old, once a brother of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]]. That order of the Church guarded the holy flame taken from the first light of dawn; ten years ago the Church dissolved it, and its last few brothers were declared heretics. He was the youngest of them, twenty at the time. In the villagers' eyes, he is the heretic who scratched the shrine stone, lured the foragers into the Barrow and stole the holy flame.

His rules: he never harms anyone, never explains in more than one sentence, and everything he does protects someone. The player never fights him.

{{spoiler|What he is really doing}}
From the very start he believes the first reversal of the [[Main Story|Main Story]]: that [[Astra|Astra]] is the monster, the sealed First Night now waking; and that the Church, by sending the power of each Seal to the Holy City, is waking him. That is why he works against the Pilgrimage. He takes the holy flame drawn from the dawn so as to gather the power that spills out when each Seal-bearer falls, and keep it from reaching the Holy City.

He is a foundling Sister Ilse raised from infancy: when he was left on the step of Firstlight's chapel, Ilse was sixteen and still a novice. At ten, a brother of the Watch who came to tend the flame took him into the order; the boy Joss remembers carrying the bridge-payer's purse was him. Fennick knows him too.

For each of his acts in Chapter 1, what is seen, what the village makes of it, and the truth all differ:

| What is seen | What the village makes of it | The truth |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh ash on the first cracked wound of the five-wound shrine stone | A heretic desecrating the holy stone | He was checking how far the First Seal had decayed |
| “The grey man shut the door from the outside” | He trapped people in the Barrow | He shut the restless wardens in, and left the people bread stamped with the Watch's mark |
| Pell's warm stone | A wicked thing from a stranger | An ember taken from the dawn's holy flame; the things of the night stay away from Pell's window |
| He takes the holy flame and crosses on ice | Theft of a holy thing; sorcery | He needs this flame of the dawn to gather the power the Seal-bearers spill, and took it before the Pilgrimage began |
| He gathers the knight's spilled power into his lantern | A heretic devouring a Seal | He keeps this power from reaching the Holy City; the flame gutters, and his hands shake |
| “Every seal you break makes the night longer” | Heresy | It is true |

In the setting, by the middle of the game the hero comes round to his view, and the two of them hunt Astra together; on the way they discover the second reversal: Astra is not the First Night but its cage, and Severin mistook his enemy. This part is not yet in the game (to be designed).

How he will use this flame of the dawn is not yet settled in the setting.
