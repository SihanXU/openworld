**Wynn** is [[Maud|Maud]]'s daughter, 8 years old, and she will ask anything. She sings the **White Knight's rhyme** her grandmother taught her, about the days when [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight]] still knelt to children. Her grandmother never sang it to the end, saying “the end went wrong”.

By day she plays by the well at the Rest, and at night she stays in the hostel. After the White Knight falls, she says how the rhyme ends: “and when they come, he kneels again.”
