# Welcome to the OpenWorld Wiki

**OpenWorld** is a 2D pixel role-playing game you play right in your web browser. The world's state and rules are code: the game itself is complete and can be played from start to finish; when you do something the world has never seen, the world learns it and remembers (see [[世界会学]]).

You wake on Cloudtop with a little fairy, [[露米|Lumi]], at your side. The days grow shorter one by one, and [[极夜|the Long Night]] is returning. [[五苦圣教|The Church of the Five Sufferings]] calls you onto the Pilgrim's Road, to seek out the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]].

## Recent work

- [[第一章]] (the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns) can now be played from start to the chapter's end; the chapters of the later four Seals are still [[分类：待设计|to be designed]].
- [[誓守之地]] has become a working camp: Alberic's [[货摊|trading stall]] and [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] (see [[金钱]]), [[钓鱼|fishing]], [[动物|feeding, milking and breeding]], plus ten more [[作物|crops]].
- Twenty [[技能#主动技能与技能栏|active skills]], the skill bar in the lower left ({{键|Shift}}+{{键|1}}–{{键|6}}) and the skill panel ({{键|U}}).
- The [[新手教程|tutorial]]: one action per lesson, with the keys not yet taught locked.
- [[楼层]]: each storey builds only its own level; the ceiling is the only exception.

For the full record of changes, see [[版本历史]]; for the wiki's own changes, see [[特殊：最近更改]].

## Help edit

Every page on this site is a file under `wiki/pages/` in the GitHub repository. Everyone on the project team can edit it, and the site updates automatically once a change is merged into the main line. Start with [[编辑入门]]; for how to write, see the [[格式手册]] and the [[风格指南]]; for pages not yet finished, see [[特殊：待完善]].
