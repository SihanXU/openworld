# Welcome to the OpenWorld Wiki

**OpenWorld** is a 2D pixel role-playing game you play right in your web browser. The world's state and rules are code: the game itself is complete and can be played from start to finish; when you do something the world has never seen, the world learns it and remembers (see [[The World Learns]]).

You wake on Cloudtop with a little fairy, [[Lumi|Lumi]], at your side. The days grow shorter one by one, and [[The Long Night|the Long Night]] is returning. [[Church of the Five Sufferings|The Church of the Five Sufferings]] calls you onto the Pilgrim's Road, to seek out the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]].

## Recent work

- [[Chapter 1]] (the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns) can now be played from start to the chapter's end; the chapters of the later four Seals are still [[Category:To be designed|to be designed]].
- [[The Held Ground]] has become a working camp: Alberic's [[trading stall|trading stall]] and [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] (see [[Money]]), [[Fishing|fishing]], [[Animals|feeding, milking and breeding]], plus ten more [[Crops|crops]].
- Twenty [[Skills#Active skills and the skill bar|active skills]], the skill bar in the lower left ({{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}}) and the skill panel ({{key|U}}).
- The [[Tutorial|tutorial]]: one action per lesson, with the keys not yet taught locked.
- [[Storeys]]: each storey builds only its own level; the ceiling is the only exception.

For the full record of changes, see [[Version History]]; for the wiki's own changes, see [[Special:Recent changes]].

## Help edit

Every page on this site is a file under `wiki/pages/` in the GitHub repository. Everyone on the project team can edit it, and the site updates automatically once a change is merged into the main line. Start with [[Editing Basics]]; for how to write, see the [[Format Manual]] and the [[Style Guide]]; for pages not yet finished, see [[Special:Stubs]].
