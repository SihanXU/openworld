Time in OpenWorld is always running: people keep to their schedules, monsters come out at night, and storms arrive on time. **One minute of game time is one real second**, an hour is one real minute, and a whole day is 24 real minutes. The date counts only the day, starting from Day 1, and the day changes at midnight, 00:00.

The game has no weeks, seasons or years. See [[Seasons]].

## The clock

The wooden frame at the upper right of the screen is the clock:

- The first line is the date, such as “Day 3”.
- In the middle is a half-round dial, whose hand sweeps from 06:00 (left) through noon (top) to 02:00 (right); beside it is the [[Weather|weather]] icon.
- At the bottom is the time, which ticks over every 10 game minutes. In Chinese it is shown on the 24-hour clock (such as 21:30), in English on the 12-hour clock.

## A day

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 05:00 | Dawn; night creatures begin to fade away |
| 06:00 | A player sleeping in bed wakes |
| 13:00 | A new game begins at this hour on Day 1 |
| From 18:00 | The sky grows gradually darker |
| 21:00 | Night falls; night creatures begin to appear in the wild in combat zones |

The [[Tutorial|tutorial]] begins at 07:00 on Day 1. The hours of nightfall and dawn change as the story moves on; see [[Day and Night]]. Villagers keep to an hourly routine, with another schedule for rain and storms; see [[Residents]].

## When time pauses

At these times the world stands completely still:

- any panel is open: the [[Inventory|bag]], [[Crafting|crafting]], [[Skills|skills]], the [[World Map]], [[Quests|quests]], the [[Codex|codex]], the [[Options]], a chest;
- a dialogue box, a yes/no question, a full-screen story card, or the selection list brought up by a long press;
- while the world is learning the result of what you just did (it waits at most 20 seconds; see [[The World Learns]]);
- the death screen, the title page, the development build's [[Mod Console]].

[[Building|Build mode]] does not pause; time flows as usual.

## When time skips ahead

| Situation | Skips ahead to |
|---|---|
| [[Sleeping|Sleeping]]: stand on a bed and press {{key|E}} | The next 06:00. Whatever the hour you lie down, you sleep until 6 the next morning |
| Taking a step on the [[The Lands of the Five Seals|overworld]] | 30 game minutes per step, more or less depending on terrain and weather. See [[World Map]] |

If you are hurt while time is skipping ahead, it stops at once: when sleeping the message is “Hunger wakes you.” or “Something hurts you awake.”, and on the overworld “Hurt, you stop on the way.”

## Other maps

Only the map the player is on runs in real time. A map you leave is paused, and when you walk back in, the timers there are brought up to date for the time that has passed (such as watered crops, or the statuses on people and things).

## Tips

- One real minute is one game hour. From the 13:00 start to nightfall at 21:00 is only 8 real minutes.
- Opening your bag to sort things costs no time, so sort away.
- To skip the night, go to sleep.

## History

- 2026-10-01: Added a 24-hour clock.
- 2026-10-02: Removed weeks and seasons, keeping only the day count and day and night.
- 2026-10-04: A step on the overworld takes time, skipping the clock ahead at once.
- 2026-10-05: Sleep always lasts until 06:00, with hunger slowing meanwhile; being hurt wakes you.
- 2026-10-06: Time pauses while the world learns the result of the player's act.
