There are three things in the game to do with maps:

- **The world map** ({{key|M}}): the whole continent with its six regions and its places; switch to “Nearby” for a patchwork of the maps you have visited and how they lie to each other. You can click a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] to travel.
- **The minimap**: in the top-left corner of the screen, showing the map you are on.
- **The overworld**: the [[The Lands of the Five Seals|Lands of the Five Seals]], a continent surrounded by the sea. It is itself a map you can walk, and every cell of the continent leads to a map.

## The world map (M)

Open it with {{key|M}} or by clicking the minimap in the top-left corner; press it again or press {{key|Esc}} to close it. Time stops while it is open.

- When it opens it shows the whole continent, the [[The Lands of the Five Seals|Lands of the Five Seals]], drawn in the colors of the land. Each region is outlined in its own color with its name written in the middle; backdrop regions that have no story yet are dimmed, with “no tale here yet” written under their names.
- Places are drawn as small diamonds: capitals and towns are larger and always named, the rest show their names only when you zoom in; places you have been to have a bright ring; sealed places are dark.
- A marker shows where you are now: on the overworld it is the cell you stand on; inside a map it is that map's cell (for an interior or an underground level, the cell of the map it belongs to). Click ◎ You to zoom in and come back to yourself.
- Click “Nearby” for another kind of drawing: every map you have visited, pieced together by where it lies in the world, drawn in the colors of its terrain and labeled with its name; click “The continent” to switch back.
- Hold the mouse button and drag to move the view; scroll the wheel to zoom.
- The map marks every [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] in the maps you have visited. Woken statues glow; hovering over one says “click to travel,” and a click takes you there, onto the cell just below the statue, facing it. Statues not yet woken say “not woken yet,” and clicking them does nothing.
- The top of the panel says “Drag · wheel to zoom · click a glowing statue to travel.”
- Where a tracked [[Quests|quest]] wants you to go, a yellow ★ is drawn; hovering over it shows which objective it is. See [[Quests#Tracking and markers|Tracking and markers]].

“Nearby” draws only maps that you have visited and that are joined to each other by exits at their edges. It does not draw: interiors entered through a door, such as houses and chapels; the deeper levels beneath [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]]; [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; the overworld itself; and the [[The Wild|wild]] on the overworld. While you stand in these places, the world map shows no marker for you.

You cannot travel during a cutscene, while a yes/no question is open, or while the world is learning from your action.

## The minimap

A small square in the top-left corner of the screen shows the terrain of the current map, with M marked in its lower left. Click it to open the world map.

| Color | What it is |
|---|---|
| White dot (black outline) | You |
| Yellow dot | [[Lumi]] |
| Blue dot | People |
| Beige dot | Animals |
| Red dot | Monsters |
| Bright cyan | A woken waypoint statue |
| Dark cyan | A waypoint statue not yet woken |
| Yellow diamond | The person or place a tracked [[Quests|quest]] wants you to go to |

Changes to the ground (a felled tree, planks laid down) are drawn on it too. Below the minimap it says whether the current map is a “Peaceful zone” (green) or a “Combat zone · monsters at night” (red). See [[Peaceful and Combat Zones]].

## The overworld: the Lands of the Five Seals

{{main|The Lands of the Five Seals}}

The [[The Lands of the Five Seals|Lands of the Five Seals]] are a continent surrounded by the sea, 64×48 cells, divided into six regions. Each cell of the continent is a place that leads to a map (the overworld has its own hand-drawn map style, with an icon for each story place): the hand-made maps ([[The Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Forest]]…) sit on the cells that match the exits between them; the other five regions hold some two hundred backdrop places; and every remaining land cell is [[The Wild|wild]]. A river and a mountain range enclose the west bank of the Ford, which can be crossed only by going through the Ford's own map.

### How to get onto the overworld

Walk off the edge of a map where there is no exit to another map, and you come out on the overworld, standing on the cell next to that map's cell in the direction you walked out (on its own cell when that side is sea or mountains).

- This works only for maps that have a place on the overworld. Interiors and underground maps do not.
- Most hand-made maps are ringed with trees and rocks. There are only a few places where you can walk out without clearing a way: the treeless stretches on the north, east and south sides of the far bank of the [[The Ford|Ford]] (except the east exit that leads to [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]]), the one cell on the south side of the Ford's west bank right next to the river, and the one cell beside the stone steps on the south side of [[Mountain Foot]]; see [[The Lands of the Five Seals#How to get onto the world map|the Lands of the Five Seals]] for details. Elsewhere you must first fell trees (axe) or break rock (pickaxe) to open a way, and then walk off the edge. See [[Woodcutting]], [[Mining]].
- The four edges of a wilderness map are open: walk out of any side and you are back on the overworld.

### Traveling the overworld

The overworld is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]] with no monsters. Every step on it takes time, and the clock jumps ahead each time:

| Terrain | Per step |
|---|---|
| Road | 15 game minutes |
| Plains | 30 game minutes |
| Forest, hills, snowfield | 45 game minutes |
| Sea, mountains, river | Impassable |

In rain and storms each step takes half again as long. If you are hurt on the way you stop, with the notice “Hurt, you stop on the way.” See [[In-game Time]].

### Entering a place

- Stand on a place and press {{key|E}}; the prompt reads “Enter X,” and in you go, whichever way you face.
- The cell under your feet is outlined, with the place's name written on it, such as “The wild forest” or “Emberfall.”
- You enter the map from the side you came onto the cell from: walk in from the east, and you enter from the map's east side.
- The [[The Ford|Ford]]'s cell is river and cannot be stood on: stand on the cell beside it, face it and press {{key|E}}, and you enter by the crossing between its two maps.
- Any other input on a place (left click, right click, {{key|G}}) says “Out of reach — go in first (E).”
- The place you have just walked out of is drawn with a blinking gold frame and a small marker bobbing above it, so you can find your way back.

### Places not yet open

Places the story has not reached yet are drawn on the overworld with their icons, but locked:

- **How they look**: the icon is greyed out, with a small padlock in its upper right corner.
- **You cannot enter**: standing on one and pressing {{key|E}}, the prompt reads “Sealed: place name”; pressing it only shows a line saying why, and the place does not open. When what is in front of you is only open ground, {{key|E}} still acts on the locked place under your feet, so standing on it and pressing shows the reason (see [[Controls#Pointing and targets|Aim and target]]).
- **Locked now**: what lies east of the East Road, held by [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]]: [[Emberfall]], [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]], [[Cinder Pass]], [[The Dragon's Lair|the Dragon's Lair]]. Pressing {{key|E}} says “The White Knight still holds the East Road. There is no way past him yet.” They all open when the White Knight falls. The crossing on the east side of [[The East Road|the East Road]] that leads to Emberfall is the same: you cannot pass it before then.

## The wild

{{main|The Wild}}

Every land cell on the overworld without a hand-made map is wild. Each patch of wild is decided by the world's seed and the cell's coordinates; it is generated the first time you go in and is the same every time within the same world. A wilderness map is 40×30 cells, a combat zone, with [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] after dark.

| Overworld terrain | Cell name | Map name inside | What is there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plains | The wild plains | Open Plains | Grass, flowers, scattered trees and rocks; green slimes, chickens; berries, sticks, stones |
| Forest | The wild forest | Deep Woods | Dense woods; boars, green slimes; mushrooms, sticks |
| Hills | The wild hills | Rolling Hills | Dry grass, gravel, rocks; wolves, green slimes; stones, herbs |
| Snowfield | The wild snowfield | Snowfield | Snow, ice, pine trees; frost wolves, frost slimes; stones, sticks |
| Road | The wild road | The Road | A road running from west to east; green slimes; sticks |

## Tips

- Whenever you reach a new place, first find its waypoint statue and wake it; afterwards one click on the world map brings you back.
- On the overworld, following the roads is fastest.
- If you want to forage in the wild, first open a gap in the edge of a map near home, and use it to go out to and come back from the overworld.

- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
