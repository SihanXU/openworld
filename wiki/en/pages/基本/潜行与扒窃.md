## Sneaking
Press {{key|V}} to start or stop **sneaking**. While sneaking, “Sneaking” is shown at the upper left of the screen, and you move more slowly (about 60% slower), but are harder to see:

- only someone within half their sight range of you (usually 4 cells) who does not have you **behind** them can notice you;
- even then it takes luck: the sharper their eyes, the easier it is for them, and the higher your level, the harder;
- when you are not sneaking, everyone within sight can see what you do (see [[Crime]]).

So if you want to steal, do it from behind someone, while they turn away, or once they are asleep. A sleeping person sees nothing.

## Pickpocketing
While sneaking, walk up to a person and press {{key|E}} on them (the prompt reads “Pick someone's pocket”) to open their **pockets** panel:

- On the left, “What they carry”: everything they have on them, money included. Things they wear are marked “worn”; they can be taken too, but are more likely to be noticed.
- On the right, “What you carry”: you can click “Slip in” to put a thing quietly into their pocket.

Every reach of the hand, whether to take or to slip in, is a separate act, and each may be noticed:

- **the person themselves** is the most likely to notice (a base of 60%), people nearby less so (30%);
- taking something worn doubles the chance of being noticed;
- the higher your level and the poorer their eyesight, the less likely you are to be noticed.

If you are noticed, that is a case of **pickpocketing** (a fine of 25, and their opinion of you −25): they cry out, demand the thing back, and go to report it; see [[Crime]] and [[Guards and Bounties]]. If you are not noticed, the thing is yours to keep, but it **still belongs to them**: when you go to sell it, ordinary merchants will not take it, and only [[Tobin]] will (see [[Trading]]); if the guards catch you, it is taken back.

When not sneaking, you can also press {{key|G}} on a person to give them a thing (that is a gift; see [[Friendship]]); press G while sneaking, and the thing goes silently into their pocket: they neither thank you nor know.

## Villagers pick pockets too
[[Vigo]], penniless and short on conscience, goes through other people's pockets for food when he is hungry (though not the guards' pockets). Pickpocketing among villagers is seen, gossiped about and caught by the guards too; see [[People's Needs]].
