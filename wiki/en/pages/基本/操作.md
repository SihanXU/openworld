OpenWorld is played with keyboard and mouse, left hand on the keyboard and right hand on the mouse; there is no controller support. Keys can be changed in the [[Options]] (see [[#Rebinding keys|rebinding keys]]); this page describes the default bindings. There are **four inputs** for doing things to the things in the world, each with one job:

- **Left click**: the held thing's **own use**. A tool or weapon is swung (strike, chop, dig, hoe, draw water, light a fire); with an empty hand, your fist; anything else you hold (food, potions, clothes, letters) is used on yourself (eat, drink, put on, read).
- **Right click**: the held thing **used on what you point at** (sowing, feeding a cow, unlocking, roasting meat, and every kind of experiment the world has never seen). Holding a wall, a bed or the like builds it there.
- {{key|E}}: **the hand**. A thing lying loose on the ground is picked up; a thing set up is handled (talk, open a door, read a notice, sleep, pick a ripe crop).
- {{key|G}}: **put**. Put down, put in or put on top what you hold, or hand it to someone; whatever you put down lies loose and is never built.

The other keys move, attack, cast skills, switch what you hold and open panels.

## Movement

- Move with {{key|W}}{{key|A}}{{key|S}}{{key|D}} or the arrow keys; when several directions are held at once, the last one pressed wins.
- Hold {{key|Shift}} to walk slowly, taking a little more time per cell.
- Walking into a person first turns you to face them; keep pushing against them for about 0.8 seconds and the two of you swap places.
- When you are not moving, pointing the mouse at an adjacent cell turns your character to face it. Stand next to a person facing them and they pause their schedule for a while, so you can talk or hand them something.
- Only the player can walk into the exits at a map's edge; walk off the edge of a map that has a place on the [[The Lands of the Five Seals|overworld]] and you are on the overworld (see [[World Map]]).
- Stepping down a drop of more than one level hurts you; see [[Health]].

## Inputs

| Input | Name | What it does | Reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left click | Own use | Swings a tool or weapon at what you point at; with an empty hand, the main-hand weapon or a fist; anything else is used on yourself | Depends on **what you hold**: mostly 1 cell; {{item|Spear}} and {{item|Halberd}} 2 cells; {{item|Fishing Rod}} 3 cells; {{item|Staff}} 4 cells; {{item|Wand}} 5 cells; {{item|Bow}} 6 cells |
| Right click | Use on | Uses the held thing on what you point at: sow with a seed, unlock with a key, roast raw meat over a campfire, feed a cow wheat, give someone food or clothes; holding anything else and clicking a creature hands it to the creature (a rope or cut grass ties it up, and something edible it swallows); holding something that can be built, builds it there | Same as left click |
| {{key|E}} | The hand | Picks up loose things; touches set-up things by hand: talking to people, opening chests and doors, reading notices, waking a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]], sleeping by standing on a bed, entering a place on the overworld, warming yourself by a lit fire, picking ripe crops; pressed on terrain such as ground, water or trees, nothing happens; reach out to a wild monster and it bites | Depends on **the target**: most things 1 cell; beds and places on the overworld 0 cells (you must stand on them); notice boards 2 cells; picking things up 1 cell |
| {{key|G}} | Put | Puts the held thing on the ground, on a surface, into an open container, into a fire, or hands it to a person; laying planks across a river is putting too | Always 1 cell |

Some key points:

- Reach is counted in cells, and a diagonal counts as 1 cell too (a square centered on yourself). Every character is held to the same reach, NPCs included.
- Ranged attacks (bows, staves, wands and spears, with a reach above 1) can only strike along your own row or column. A fishing rod is not a weapon, and casting is not bound by this: any water cell within 3 will do, diagonals included (see [[Fishing]]).
- The [[Ranger]]'s {{skill|Archery}} and the [[Mage]]'s {{skill|Archmage}} add 1 to the reach of their profession's weapons.
- On **people** and companions, the left click does not strike, whether you hold a weapon, a tool or nothing; only after drawing your weapon with {{key|X}} does a left click hit them (a torch, too, only sets a person alight once your weapon is drawn). Right-clicking a person while holding a weapon “uses the weapon on them”; only with the weapon drawn does it cut them. When {{key|Space}} swings in front of you, a person standing in the way is hit too.
- Another profession's own weapons cannot be swung; you get the message “Only X fight with Y”. See [[Professions]].
- Picking up with {{key|E}} and putting with {{key|G}} are physical acts with results that are always certain: if a thing can be taken it is taken, and if it can be put down it is put down. They never set off [[The World Learns|the world learning]]. The {{key|G}} prompt is shown only when it would change something.
- When the right click (or {{key|E}} on a set-up thing) meets something no rule defines, that is where the world learns, and a “?” appears after the prompt. See [[#Action prompts and “?”|action prompts and “?”]].
- A left click swung at a patch of open ground (grass, dirt) that the world has never seen struck does nothing, so a careless click does not go off to ask the world; to try it, use the right click.

## Left click

The left click looks only at what you hold:

- **A weapon or tool in hand** (sword, bow, axe, pickaxe, hoe, sickle, fishing rod, torch, bucket, watering can…): it is swung at what you point at and does its own job: a weapon strikes, an axe fells trees, a pickaxe mines, a hoe tills, a sickle reaps, a bucket draws and pours water, a torch lights fires, a fishing rod casts.
  - **An axe, pickaxe or hammer striking a built thing** (bed, workbench, chest, crate, smith's anvil, smith's forge, bee box, wall, door, floor, stairs): it **comes loose whole**, falls where it stood as a single loose thing, is drawn into your bag automatically when you come near, and can also be picked up with {{key|E}}. A chest spills its contents first. A locked chest or door cannot be knocked down; it only loses durability. A weapon such as a sword only damages it (for durability see [[Durability and Breaking]]). Things too heavy to move (tables, fences) are not knocked down.
- **An empty hand**: you strike with the weapon equipped in your main hand, or with a punch if there is none.
- **Anything else in hand** (bread, a potion, a helmet, a letter, a stone…): it is **used on yourself**, wherever you point: eat, drink, put on, read. A use the world has never seen (using a stone on yourself, say) first asks “Use Stone on yourself?”, and only after you confirm does it go to the world.
- **What gets struck**: with an empty hand, first a person or creature in the cell you point at, then things too heavy to move (trees, chests, walls, boulders), never things lying loose on the ground; with a tool, the topmost thing you point at. **People** and companions are struck only after you draw your weapon ({{key|X}}).
- **A swing is never aimed at yourself.** To choose something stacked underneath, use {{key|Alt}}+left click.
- **It acts on the press and goes on while held**: against monsters it strikes again and again at your attack speed; for work it keeps the same rhythm, and moving the mouse carries it from cell to cell (hoeing a row of ground, felling a stand of trees). Holding repeats only the same known act: things the world has never seen (with a “?”) and things that need confirming are not repeated, nor is what you just did undone (a full bucket is not poured out again); things used on yourself are used one per press.

## Right click

The right click uses the held thing on what you point at, and chooses nothing else for you:

- **Using it on things**: a seed on empty farmland sows it, ash or fertilizer on an unripe crop fertilizes it, a key on a locked chest unlocks it, raw meat on a burning fire roasts it, ore on a burning furnace smelts it, wheat on a cow feeds it, bait on a fishing rod baits it, a potion on someone gives it to them to drink.
- **Experiments**: a use the world has never seen (an apple on a well, a stone on a slime) has a “?” after its prompt, and pressing it goes to ask the world (see [[The World Learns]]). The right click is the place for experiments.
- **Pointing at yourself**: uses it on yourself, the same as a left click with it in hand (eat, drink, put on); a weapon pointed at yourself is equipped in your main hand.
- **Building**: holding a wall, floor, door, stairs, bed, workbench, fence or the like, right-click open ground, a floor or a built thing to **build it there**; hold and drag to build a row, one per cell; holding a floor, press {{key|R}} to lay it as the [[Storeys#Ceilings|ceiling]] overhead instead, or use {{key|Alt}}+right click and choose “Ceiling” from the list. Clicking water, a tree or a person is still a place for the world to learn. You can also use build mode ({{key|B}}).
- **An empty hand**: the right click does nothing.

## E: The Hand

{{key|E}} looks at the **form** of what you point at:

- **Loose things**: items on the ground, things on a table, monster drops, and beds, walls and the like that were knocked down or put down with {{key|G}}. Press {{key|E}} to pick them up into your hand. **Other people's things** first ask “Steal …?”, and are taken only when you confirm; that is [[Crime|theft]].
- **Set-up things**: people, doors, chests, beds, notice boards, ripe crops, workbenches… Press {{key|E}} to touch them by hand: talk, open and close, open up, read, sleep, pick a ripe crop.
- **On a person while sneaking**: go through their pockets (“Pick someone's pocket”); see [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing]].
- Pressing {{key|E}} on empty ground does nothing.

Even if the world learns “turning a stone over with an empty hand”, that does not change what {{key|E}} does to a stone: a loose stone is always picked up.

## Two forms of things

As with blocks and dropped items in Minecraft, a thing that can be built has two forms on the ground:

| | Loose | Set up |
|---|---|---|
| Which | Every item; beds, walls, workbenches and chests knocked down or put down with {{key|G}} | Things built with the right click or in build mode; crops, trees, rocks |
| Look | Drawn as the item's small icon, not in the way | Standing, in the way, can serve as a workstation or a container |
| {{key|E}} | Picks it up | Handles it |
| How it changes | Built with the right click or in build mode → set up | Struck with an axe, pickaxe or hammer by left click → comes loose whole, lying where it stood |

When a chest is knocked down, its contents spill out first.

## Pointing and targets

Every input acts on the cell the mouse points at.

- **By default, on the topmost layer.** A cell may hold several things stacked: a person, things on a table, the table, a tree's crown, the ground. A short press acts on whatever is drawn on top ({{key|E}} first picks up what is on the table, and only once that is gone the table itself). When you stand on something and point at yourself, the left and right clicks act on yourself, and {{key|E}} and {{key|G}} act on the topmost thing in the cell under your feet (berries, for example).
- **You can point only at cells within reach.** A cell can be chosen only if at least one input can do something there. A farther cell shows only the inputs that reach it, such as a single line “Left click Strike the wolf with the bow”.
- **When the mouse is beyond reach**, the game looks back along the line from you to the mouse and chooses the farthest cell within reach.
- **When you are not using the mouse** (your last move was walking with the keyboard), {{key|E}} and {{key|G}} act on the cell in front of you; the left and right clicks are mouse buttons and always act on the cell the mouse points at. {{key|E}} has one exception: when the cell in front of you has nothing defined for {{key|E}} (its prompt shows “?”), or is only empty ground or floor, and under your feet there is something that does something when pressed (a bed, a place on the overworld), {{key|E}} acts underfoot. When the cell in front is only open ground, {{key|E}} acts on what is underfoot even if pressing it changes nothing: standing on a place on the overworld that is not open yet, for example, pressing {{key|E}} tells you why you cannot enter (see [[World Map#Places not yet open|places not yet open]]).
  - On a place on the overworld, you can press {{key|E}} facing any direction: the overworld's terrain is never “felt”.
  - Standing on a bed with empty ground or floor in front of you, {{key|E}} sleeps; with something else in front of you (a chest, a door, a person), {{key|E}} goes to it first, so to sleep, point the mouse at the bed.
- **Only on your own storey.** When you are [[Storeys|upstairs]], you can act only on things on that storey. The floor of the storey above (the ceiling overhead, that is) can be acted on only when chosen specifically in the selection list or in build mode.
- The chosen cell is outlined. A place on the overworld covers 3×3 cells and is outlined as a whole.

## Choosing among stacked things

Hold {{key|E}} or {{key|G}} for about 0.4 seconds, or hold {{key|Alt}} and left- or right-click, and an “On which?” list pops up:

- It lists everything stacked in that cell (that this input can reach), each line saying what pressing it will do. Lines no rule defines have a “?” after them. When choosing with the right click under your own feet, the first line is “Yourself”; with an empty hand, {{key|Alt}}+left click pointed at yourself lets you choose “Yourself” and punch yourself.
- Press {{key|1}}–{{key|9}} or click a line to choose; press {{key|Esc}} or click elsewhere to close it.
- With a floor in hand, {{key|Alt}}+right click has “Ceiling” as its last line: lay a floor overhead. With an axe, pickaxe or hammer, {{key|Alt}}+left click also has “Ceiling” as its last line when there is a floor overhead: knock it down, and it falls whole onto the storey where you stand. See [[Storeys]].
- The list includes only things on your own storey.
- Time pauses while the list is open. An ordinary click never brings it up, so being slow on the buttons in a fight does not stop the game.

## Action prompts and “?”

The cell to be acted on is marked on the map by four small corners: the frame slides to the next cell, is faint over open ground, and is orange for an act the world has never defined.

**The cursor** changes with {{key|E}} and the right click: a hand over things you can pick up, open or read, a speech bubble over a person, and a hand too when holding a wall or bed over a place it can be built; when the world has never seen the right click's use it carries an orange “?” (pressing goes to ask the world), when {{key|E}} would take someone else's thing it carries a red “!” (that is theft), and when out of reach the cursor fades (the act will land on the outlined cell).

**The prompt box** sits above the frame and says what each input will do when pressed. It appears only once your character stops and the frame has settled for a moment, so it does not jump about while you walk or sweep the mouse across:

- Using the mouse: left click and right click (not repeated when the right click does the same as the left), plus {{key|E}} and {{key|G}}.
- Not using the mouse: {{key|E}} and {{key|G}} for the cell in front of you. The line for an empty hand touching open ground the world has never seen is left out (it would come up at every step, and the frame's orange already says so).

For example, pointing at a stone with bread in hand:

- Left click Eat Bread
- Right click Use Bread on Stone ?
- {{key|E}} Pick up Stone

The wording of the prompts comes from the world's [[The World Learns|rules]]. The same thing is described differently in different situations: a helmet on yourself is “Put on Helmet”, on someone else “Put Helmet on Tobin”; a place on the overworld is “Enter the forest wild”.

**“?”** means the world has never defined the result of this act. The line is faded and followed by an orange “?”. Press it and the world learns it (when an AI is connected), or it is handled by default (usually nothing happens). When AI for this kind of thing is turned off in the [[Options]], the “?” is no longer shown: pressing only applies the default, and nothing is asked. See [[The World Learns]].

No prompt is shown while your character cannot act (during a cutscene, while down, while the world is learning), nor while a dialogue box or a yes/no question is open.

## Attacking and dashing

- {{key|Space}}: attacks once in the direction you face, striking the first thing in the way within reach. Hold to keep attacking, at least 0.5 seconds apart; bonuses to attack speed shorten the interval. When what you hold is not a weapon and no weapon is equipped in your main hand: a tool (torch, hoe, bucket…) is swung, and anything else (a stone, a rope, bread…) gives way to a punch; you never strike with odds and ends you picked up. You can fight with food in hand just by pressing Space.
- A left click on a creature is an attack too, landing on the one you point at: with a weapon in hand you use the weapon, with an empty hand the main-hand weapon or your fist. To hand something to a creature, use the right click (with things it knows how to take, such as wheat for a cow); see [[Monsters#Handing things to creatures|handing things to creatures]].
- An attack with an empty hand is a punch, which can slowly break plants and wood; on stone it says “Too hard.” See [[Durability and Breaking]].
- {{key|Q}}: once you have learned {{skill|Dash}}, you dash up to 3 cells in the direction you face; it cannot pass solid obstacles, and has a 4-second cooldown.
- {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}}: casts the active skill in that slot of the skill bar at the lower left; after casting you must wait out the cooldown. Nothing is cast while a panel is open; while the skill tree is open, these keys put the learned active skill under the mouse into that slot. See [[Skills#Active skills and the skill bar|active skills and the skill bar]].
- For the detailed rules see [[Combat]].

## Sneaking and drawn weapons

- {{key|V}}: start or stop **sneaking**. While sneaking you move slowly, and only people a short way in front of you can see you. While sneaking, press {{key|E}} on a person (“Pick someone's pocket”) to open their pockets, or {{key|G}} to slip what you hold quietly into their pocket. See [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing]].
- {{key|X}}: draw or put away your weapon. With your weapon drawn, a left click on a person is an attack (wounding someone is [[Crime|assault]], killing them is murder); walk up to a guard and he tells you to put it away. When guards are chasing you, put your weapon away and stop fighting back, and they stop. See [[Guards and Bounties]].
- While sneaking or with your weapon drawn, a small tag at the upper left of the screen reminds you. When you are wanted, the bounty is shown there too.

## What you hold and the toolbar

- The toolbar is the first row of the [[Inventory|bag]], ten slots for {{key|1}}–{{key|0}}. The chosen slot is what you hold in hand. What you hold is not drawn on your character; when a sword, bow or staff attacks, the swing, the drawn bowstring or the raised staff is drawn for that moment.
- The mouse wheel jumps to the next (previous) slot that holds something. Clicking a toolbar slot chooses it too.
- {{key|Shift}}+{{key|Tab}} brings the next row of the bag up to serve as the toolbar; rolling the wheel with {{key|Shift}} held does it too, and rolling back gives the previous row. The chosen column stays the same.
- Drops drawn into your bag automatically go into empty slots, never into the chosen one: fight a monster with an empty hand, and your hand is still empty afterwards.
- The single slot beside the toolbar is the off hand, for a shield and the like. See [[Equipment]].

## Panels and menus

- {{key|Tab}} opens the [[Inventory|bag]]. Along the top of the panel is a row of tabs: bag, crafting, skill tree, skills, quests, codex, world map; each tab shows its own key, and clicking one switches to it.
- You can also press each one's own key directly: {{key|I}} [[Inventory|bag]], {{key|C}} [[Crafting|crafting]], {{key|K}} [[Skills|skill]] tree, {{key|U}} [[Skills#The skills panel|skill panel]], {{key|M}} [[World Map]], {{key|L}} [[Quests|quests]], {{key|J}} [[Codex|codex]]. Press the same key again, press {{key|Esc}}, click ✕ or click the empty space outside the panel to close it.
- The [[trading stall|trading stall]] panel has no key: press {{key|E}} on the stall in [[The Held Ground]] to open it.
- The [[Trading|trade]] panel and the pockets panel have no key either: talk to a merchant and choose “Let's trade.” to open trade; while sneaking, press {{key|E}} on a person to open their pockets (see [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing]]).
- {{key|Esc}} closes, in order: build mode, the developer console, an open panel. With nothing open, it opens the [[Options]].
- Time pauses while a panel is open, except in build mode. See [[In-game Time]].
- For mouse actions in the bag and chest panels, see [[Inventory]] and [[Chests and Containers]].

## Build mode

Press {{key|B}} or click the build button at the lower right to enter. {{main|Building}}

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left click or {{key|E}} | Builds the chosen thing in the cell you point at |
| Hold left click and drag | Builds one in every cell along the way, a row at a time |
| {{key|R}} | Turns stairs; with a floor chosen, switches between “this storey” and “the ceiling” |
| {{key|B}}, {{key|Esc}} or right click | Leaves build mode |
| {{key|W}}{{key|A}}{{key|S}}{{key|D}} | Move as usual |

In build mode {{key|G}}, {{key|Space}}, the number keys and the wheel do nothing; {{key|Tab}} still opens the bag (and leaves build mode). Time flows as usual. Opening any panel leaves build mode automatically.

## Dialogue, questions and cutscenes

| Situation | Keys |
|---|---|
| Dialogue box | {{key|E}}, {{key|Esc}} or a click: the first shows all the text, the second turns to the next line |
| Options below the dialogue box (see [[Dialogue]]) | Click, or press {{key|1}}–{{key|9}} for the matching line; {{key|Esc}} or “Goodbye.” ends the conversation |
| Stopped by a guard | Only the watch's options (pay the fine, go to jail…), chosen with {{key|1}}–{{key|9}} or a click; {{key|Esc}} cannot close it |
| A remark someone makes in passing | {{key|G}} dismisses it at once, and carries out this put as usual |
| Yes/no questions (such as “Eat Bread?” or “Steal Bread?”) | Arrow keys or {{key|A}}{{key|D}}{{key|W}}{{key|S}} to switch, {{key|E}} to confirm, {{key|Esc}} for “No”; or click a button directly |
| Full-screen story cards | {{key|E}} or a click to continue |

Time pauses while dialogue, questions or story cards are open.

## While the world is learning

When you press an act with a “?” and the world is learning its result, the middle of the screen shows “The world is learning this…” and the seconds waited. The world is paused meanwhile; press {{key|Esc}} to stop waiting, and the default result applies at once. See [[The World Learns]].

## When keys are not accepted

- The title page, the load panel and the death screen accept only mouse clicks on their buttons, not keys. See [[Difficulty]] and [[Saving]].
- During a cutscene, your character cannot act and no action prompts are shown.
- The first lessons of the [[Tutorial|tutorial]] lock the inputs they have not taught yet: pressing them does nothing, and the key icon on screen shakes.

## The key table in the game

The “Controls” panel on the title page and the [[Options]] each have a short key table, made from your current bindings, which follows when you change keys. With the default bindings its contents match this page: the left click is the held thing's own use, the right click uses it on what you point at, {{key|E}} picks up or handles, {{key|G}} puts, {{key|Shift}}+{{key|1}}–{{key|6}} casts skills, and the last line is “{{key|Esc}} · {{key|I}} · {{key|C}} · {{key|K}} · {{key|U}} · {{key|M}} · {{key|L}} · {{key|J}}: menu · bag · crafting · skill tree · skill panel · map · quests · codex (with the Threadbook)”. The development build has one more line, {{key|`}} Mod Console.

## Rebinding keys

In the [[Options]], click “Change keys…” to open the keys page:

- Each action has a row with two columns, **key** and **alternate key**; both keys work (moving up, for example, is {{key|W}} and ↑ by default). Click one, then press the new key; press {{key|Esc}} to cancel, or Backspace to clear that key.
- Changes start as a draft: the top of the page says “Not saved yet”, and they take effect only when you click **Save**; click **Discard changes** to go back to how things were. Click “← Options” without saving and you are asked whether to save or discard.
- If the new key is already taken by another action, you are asked first: “G is “…”'s key. Trade the two?”; click “Trade” to swap them, or “Cancel” to change nothing.
- A row that differs from the default is marked, with “↺ Default E” on its right; click it to restore that row's default (and the action whose key it took also gets its own default back where possible). **Restore all defaults** at the top sets every key back to its default (which must also be saved).
- Skills can be bound to {{key|Shift}}+a key; movement and slow walking cannot use {{key|Shift}} ({{key|Shift}} is the slow-walk key).
- What cannot be changed: the left and right mouse buttons (the left and right clicks belong to the mouse alone, with no keyboard version), {{key|Esc}}, {{key|`}}, and the browser's own keys such as {{key|Ctrl}}, {{key|Alt}}, {{key|F5}}, {{key|F11}} and {{key|F12}} ({{key|Ctrl}}+{{key|W}}, for instance, closes the web page outright).
- Once you change a key, everywhere the game names keys follows: the action prompts, the key table, the keys on the lower-right buttons and the toolbar, the skill bar, and “press X” in the tutorial and in quests.
- Bindings are kept in this computer's browser, not in the [[Saving|save]]; switch browsers or clear the site's data and they return to the defaults.

## Tips

- When you are not sure what a press will do, look at the prompt bar above your character first. An act with a “?” is something this world has never seen, and just right for trying: right-click with something in hand.
- To act on something stacked underneath (the table rather than the cup on it), hold {{key|E}}, or {{key|Alt}}+click.
- To move a bed somewhere else: strike it once with an axe by left click, and it falls whole; pick it up, then right-click to build it elsewhere.
- Ranged weapons cannot hit targets on a diagonal; first get into the same row or column.
