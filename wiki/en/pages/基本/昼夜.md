The world has day and night. By default **night falls at 21:00 and dawn comes at 05:00**. At night, zombies and bats appear in the wild in [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zones]], people go home to sleep, and sight grows shorter. As the [[持印者]] fall one by one, the nights grow longer and longer.

## The hours of night

With each Seal-bearer who falls, night falls 1 hour earlier and dawn comes half an hour later.

| Seal-bearers fallen | Nightfall | Dawn | Length of night |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (the start) | 21:00 | 05:00 | 8 hours |
| 1 (after defeating the White Knight in [[第一章]]) | 20:00 | 05:30 | 9½ hours |

For now, only the first Seal-bearer, [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], can be defeated in the game. The nights grow longer after the scene in which the Crown of Thorns is taken; see [[第一章]].

The screen begins to darken 3 hours before nightfall and is darkest at nightfall; it begins to brighten half an hour before dawn and returns to daytime brightness 2 hours after dawn. These two times move with the length of the night as well.

In clear weather, the weather icon on the [[界面|clock]] shows the moon ☾ from 2 hours before nightfall to 1 hour after dawn, and the sun ☀ the rest of the time: 19:00–06:00 at the start, and 18:00–06:30 after the first Seal falls.

## Why the nights grow longer

From the very start of the game, the sun has been setting earlier year by year:

- On [[云顶台地]], the watchman's log records the time of the midsummer sunset year after year, each earlier than the last; its last page says that “a sleeping light” was found in the snow.
- {{人物|赫斯珀}} at the Ford says: “The evenings come sooner every week.”
- The [[五苦圣教]] has proclaimed that the five Seal-bearers can no longer bear the Five Sufferings, and that if the five Seals fail completely, the Second [[极夜|Long Night]] will fall. That is why the player sets out on the Pilgrimage.

After the White Knight is defeated, {{人物|灰衣人}} says to the player: “Every seal you break makes the night longer. Ask your Church why.” Back in Firstlight Village, {{人物|奈莎·奎尔}} counts the time: “Four minutes. The sun set four minutes early tonight.”

{{剧透|The real reason the nights grow longer}}
According to the original setting, the five Seal-bearers are the five Fulcrums of [[阿斯特拉]], the Vessel, each bearing a share of the Five Sufferings. Within Astra the [[原夜|First Night]] is locked away. Defeating a Seal-bearer both frees them from their suffering and takes away a Fulcrum that holds up the Vessel, and so the nights grow longer and longer. See [[主线剧情]] and [[承夜者]].
{{/剧透}}

## What happens at night

- **[[夜行生物|Night creatures]]**: after dark, on outdoor maps in combat zones, every 6 seconds a {{怪物|僵尸}} (60%) or a {{怪物|蝙蝠}} (40%) appears on open ground 6–10 cells from the player, at most 4 on one map. After dawn they crumble into dust and fade away a few at a time. Both their appearing and their fading follow the current hours of nightfall and dawn.
- Peaceful zones ([[初原村]], [[渡口]], [[云顶台地]] and others), indoor maps and dark underground maps have no night creatures.
- Shut the door and stay inside, and they cannot get in.
- Some creatures attack of their own accord only at night; see [[阵营与脾性]].
- Villagers go home to sleep on their schedules; see [[居民]].
- **Sight halved**: after dark, every character's sight is halved, unless they stand within 3 cells of firelight. This affects how far away monsters notice you, and also who sees what you do.

## Lighting

Every map has one kind of lighting:

| Lighting | Maps | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Outdoor | Most maps | Changes with day and night; a little darker again when cloudy, raining, stormy or foggy |
| Dark | [[古墓]], [[荆棘骸骨堂]], [[守墓者圣所]], [[旧矿坑]], [[魔龙巢穴]], [[磨坊地窖]] | Always very dark, day or night |
| Indoor | [[初晓礼拜堂]] | Follows day and night, but only 40% as dark as outdoors |

It does not rain on dark or indoor maps. See [[天气]].

## Light sources

- Lit or burning things give off a warm glow: a lit [[营火|campfire]], [[火盆|brazier]], [[路灯|lamp post]], [[烛台|candles]], [[灯笼柱|lantern post]], [[壁炉|fireplace]] or [[熔炉|forge]], and trees, grass and houses on fire.
- An awakened [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] glows too.
- A {{物品|火把}}, {{物品|提灯}} or {{物品|蜡烛}} the player holds draws a glow around the character, but that is only a visual effect: they do not count as “a lit fire”, do not scare creatures off, and do not restore sight.
- To light a campfire: hold a torch and left-click it. Once you learn {{技能|钻木取火}}, an ordinary {{物品|树枝}} will light it too. See [[营火]].

## Firelight keeps creatures away

Creatures that do not belong to fire (those without the “fire” property) fear fire:

- they will not come within 2 cells of anything lit or burning;
- they will not chase a person standing in firelight.

So beside a lit campfire is a safe place at night. Rain does not put out a lit campfire, but it does put out things that are burning.

## Tips

- Before the first night, get ready a house whose door you can shut, or a lit campfire.
- At night, stay by firelight: monsters keep away, and you can see farther too.
- If you would rather not fight at night, go to sleep before dark and sleep straight through to 06:00.
