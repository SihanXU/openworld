Villagers have [[Satiety|satiety]] just as you do, only it drops half as fast.

## Two meals a day
Everyone eats twice a day: in the hour after they get up and in the hour before bed. Those who live in a home of their own eat beside their household's larder; those with no home (who sleep at the shelter or out of doors) eat wherever mealtime finds them, carrying on with what they were doing ([[Hesper]] eats by her fire). At mealtime, anyone not full (satiety under ninety percent) eats what they carry, or takes food from their own or their household's chests and barrels. They never take from another household's, and never buy or steal for a meal.

- **The larder**: every household with a house keeps a barrel or chest of bread and apples, by head: two loaves and an apple per person per day. Every morning it is topped back up, from what the household's work brings in. Take from it, and that household goes a meal short that day.
- **While you are away**: when you go to another map, the people there keep their days and their meals. When you come back, their satiety runs from their last meal, so they are not half-starved on your return.
- In the backdrop's towns, people eat one meal at noon at their own table, and their rooms' larders are topped up each day too.

## Hungry
When a villager's satiety falls below sixty percent they are “hungry,” and they put down what they are doing to look for food, in this order:

1. **If they carry food**, they eat it.
2. **If they carry money**, they go to someone within 14 cells who sells food and buy whatever they can afford. This is how [[Marta|Marta]]'s bread gets sold (see [[Trading]]).
3. **If they have no money**: an honest person (fifty percent conscience or more) goes without and looks again an hour later; **someone with little conscience**, or **someone close to starving** (satiety at zero), goes to take food lying on the ground or stored in chests within 14 cells.
4. **Someone with very little conscience** (thirty percent or less) also picks other people's pockets (except the guards').

If they find no food, they look again an hour later. Someone without a single coin who has found no food asks you to bring them something to eat: the commission [[A Bite to Eat]].

It is not only hunger: someone who is uncomfortable, or below half their health, with no way to put it right at hand, buys or takes whatever would put it right in the same order — a wounded person with money goes and buys a healing potion to drink. What puts what right is up to the world's rules; see [[Keeping Safe]].

When they take what belongs to others, it is just like when you do: if seen, it is [[Crime|theft]] or [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing|pickpocketing]]. The owner chases after them to get it back, passers-by shout “Thief!”, go to report it and gossip about it in the village, and the [[Guards and Bounties|guards]] go to stop them: if they have enough money they pay the fine, and if not they are locked in a cell. They are held in grudge, and others think worse of them.

## Money
Everyone carries a few {{item|Dawnmark}}. Every morning their money is topped back up to what it was: that is their takings for the day (merchants take more, see [[Trading]]). If they are not on your map at six o'clock, it is topped up the next time you see them. Someone with no income (such as [[Vigo]]) is still penniless in the morning.

## Who steals
| Person | Money | Conscience | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Vigo]] | 0 | Twenty percent | Steals when hungry, even picks pockets; keeps at it even after being caught |
| [[Tobin]] | 40 | Fifty percent | Has money, and usually buys; doesn't care to mind other people's business |
| Everyone else | Some | One hundred percent | Buys if they have money, goes without if not, unless close to starving |

[[Vigo]] came into the village hungry. Spend a day in [[Firstlight Village]] and you will often see him slip up to Marta's goods or put his hand into someone's pocket. Giving him food, or hiring him to follow you, makes him steal less often.
