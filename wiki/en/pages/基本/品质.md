**Quality** is the grade of how well a weapon, tool or piece of gear is made, in six grades (a different thing from [[装备#等级|gear level]]: level 1–20 is how strong it is, quality how well made). The higher the quality, the higher its attack and defense, and the more [[装备#词条|affixes]] gear rolls. Quality is written before the name (such as “Fine Stone Axe”; Normal quality isn't written), and the color of the name in the tooltip also shows the quality. The first four grades can be [[制作|crafted]]; the top two can only be found.

## The six grades

{{品质表}}

| Quality | Name color in the tooltip | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Crude | Grey | The crafter's level is below the recipe's requirement |
| Normal | White | The crafter's level meets the recipe's requirement |
| Fine | Yellow | The crafter's level is 3 or more above the recipe's requirement |
| Masterwork | Cyan | The crafter's level is 6 or more above the recipe's requirement |
| [[遗誓|Vowbound]] | Purple | Found only: things left by those who swore a vow |
| [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] | Orange-red | Found only: things the Seal-bearers carry |

For each grade's multiplier see the quality table at the start of this section. A thing's attack and defense = the form's own stat × the [[材质|substance]]'s multiplier × the quality's multiplier × the [[装备#等级|level]]'s multiplier, rounded, at least 1. The number of affixes gear rolls: Crude 2, Normal 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5, Stigmatic 6. So at the same level, gear of higher quality is always better than gear of lower quality.

## Quality when crafting

When you make something, its quality is decided by **how far the crafter's [[等级与经验|level]] is above the level the recipe requires**. Most recipes require level 1; the {{物品|弓}} requires level 2.

| Level − recipe requirement | Quality made |
|---|---|
| Below 0 | Crude |
| 0–2 | Normal |
| 3–5 | Fine |
| 6 or more | Masterwork |

For example, at level 1 a bow you make can only be Crude; at level 4 a stone axe you make is Fine, and at level 7 Masterwork.

## Vowbound

Things left by those who swore a vow, such as gear left behind by the Order of the Thorn or by pilgrims of old. Under the name in the tooltip it says: “Vowbound — left by one who swore a vow. It still remembers.” Besides a ×1.75 multiplier and 5 minor affixes, every piece of Vowbound gear carries a **Vow**: a special effect with a condition and a reward (Vigil, Dawn, the Held Ground, the Thorn, Mercy, Silence, the Road, the Shield, the Hunt, the Lone Road: ten in all), written in purple in the tooltip as “✦ Vow name · Vow.” Randomly rolled Vowbound gear carries one at random; the few named pieces in the story each have a set one. See [[遗誓]], [[装备#遗誓与圣痕|Equipment]].

Vowbound gear comes from quest rewards and certain enemies, such as Tomas's Belt of Vigil carried by the {{怪物|古墓守卫}}. See [[遗誓]].

## Stigmatic

Things that bear one of the First Martyr's five wounds. Only the [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] carry them; each is unique in the world and has a power of its own. The tooltip says: “Stigmatic — it bears one of the First Martyr's five wounds. Suffering brings one nearer to God.” See [[圣痕]], [[传说武器]].

## Tips

- If you want to make Fine tools and weapons, gain a few levels before making them.
- A tool's power is multiplied by its quality too, so a Fine axe also fells trees faster. For how well tools work on different materials, see [[耐久与破坏]].

## History

- 2026-10-04: The Crude, Fine and Masterwork grades were added, with quality decided by level when crafting; the quality multiplier applies to attack and defense.
- 2026-10-07: The Vowbound and Stigmatic grades were added, found only and never crafted; the tooltip tells where they come from.
- 2026-10-13: Quality decides the number of affixes on gear (2 to 6); the stat formula includes gear level.
- 2026-10-13: Vowbound gear's random minor traits were replaced by ten Vows, and it rolls minor affixes like other gear.
