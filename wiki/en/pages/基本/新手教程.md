The **tutorial** is a separate entry on the title page, apart from both the main story and saves. The player wakes from unconsciousness in [[The Clearing]] and, following [[Lumi]] over a day and a night, learns the basics of this world: finding food, gathering materials, [[Crafting|crafting]], felling trees, making a fire, [[Building|building a house]] and getting through the first night. Each lesson teaches a single action, and uses a spotlight, key icons and locked keys to have the player do it with their own hands rather than read about it. Walk out along the path to the south of the Clearing and the tutorial ends, returning you to the title page.

## Beginning and end

- Click “Tutorial” on the title page (subtitle “Learn the ropes · Separate from the story and saves”) to begin. There is no save slot or [[Difficulty|difficulty]] to choose.
- At 07:00 on Day 1, the player lies on the ground in the Clearing, {{status|out cold}}, and wakes after about 5 seconds. Lumi says: “Hey! You are awake at last! I am Lumi…”
- The tutorial **writes no save**. Clicking “Save” in the [[Options]] only shows “The tutorial keeps no save.”
- [[Threadbook|Threads]] the world learns in the tutorial stay in the shared rule library, common to the real game. See [[The World Learns]].
- Falling in the tutorial is handled as on Normal difficulty: you revive beside the bed you last slept in (or where you woke, if you have not slept yet), and what you carried (except worn equipment and the weapon in hand) is bundled into a bundle left where you fell. See [[Difficulty]].
- After dawn, walk out along the path to the south of the Clearing; the screen says “Tutorial complete! Start a new game from the title for the story.” and you return to the title page. Nothing from the tutorial is carried into the main story.
- A real new game does not pass through the tutorial; it starts straight from [[Cloudtop]]. See [[Beginner's Guide]].

## The Clearing

{{main|The Clearing}}

- A clearing of 36×26 cells, surrounded by woods that cannot be left. To the east is a small stand of trees, the place to cut wood.
- Berries, sticks, stones and cut grass lie scattered on the ground. Once taken, they all slowly grow back (at most 6 berries at once), enough to eat your fill before dark.
- The house has a set place: the bed in the middle, walls on the north, west and east sides, and the door on the south. The house-building lessons mark these cells on the ground.
- Beside the house stands a [[training dummy|training dummy]], which stands itself up again after being knocked down.
- The Clearing is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]: after dark, [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] (zombies and bats) come out.
- The only way out is a path two cells wide to the south. Before dawn it is just ordinary ground; walk there too early and Lumi says “Not that way yet”.

## Stages

The tutorial has 8 stages, which are the stages of the main quest [[Waking]]. Each stage has one objective, shown in the quest tracker at the upper right and in the quest journal ({{key|L}}); once you achieve it, the next stage begins, the journal records a line, and Lumi says something. Press {{key|E}} on Lumi and she repeats how to do this stage. What is judged is the “state”, not the route: once it is achieved it counts, however you did it.

| Stage | Objective | Condition for the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | (Unconscious) | Wake up |
| 1 | Find something to eat | Have eaten once |
| 2 | Gather sticks, stones and grass | Carry a {{item|Stick}}, a {{item|Stone}} and {{item|Cut Grass}} at the same time |
| 3 | Make a stone axe | Carry an axe |
| 4 | Fell a tree and split planks | Carry a {{item|Plank}} |
| 5 | Build a campfire and light it | A lit [[campfire|campfire]] within 12 cells |
| 6 | Build a house with a bed | A [[Rooms|bedroom]] appears on the map |
| 7 | Get through the night | The first morning (05:00) after the house is built |
| 8 | Take the path south | Walk out of the Clearing; the tutorial ends |

Stage 7 waits for “the first morning after the house is built”, not dawn on the second day. Build the house in the afternoon, and you must get through that night; finish it only in the small hours, and the path opens at 05:00 that same day. A player who does nothing is not let out on the second day.

## Lessons

Each stage is divided into several lessons, each teaching a single action:

1. Lumi says a short line.
2. The thing to act on is circled, with everything around it darkened (a spotlight). It may be a thing on the ground, an outline, an interface button or a toolbar slot.
3. The key to press is drawn above that thing, with “lesson number / lessons in this stage” beside it, such as “E 2/4”.
4. Once you have really done it, a green check mark appears, and the next lesson begins.

There are 24 lessons in all. Lessons appear in order, but their completion looks at the state: if, when a lesson's turn comes, it has already been achieved, it completes at once. Once a stage moves on, its remaining lessons no longer appear.

## Locks

- The first, groundwork lessons **lock the inputs they have not taught**: the surroundings darken, other keys do nothing, and the key icon on screen shakes.
- What is locked is the keys, not the character. A locked input produces no action at all.
- In the crafting panel only the thing being taught (the stone axe, planks) can be made, and the build bar offers only the campfire or walls; the first wall must go inside the outline drawn on the ground.
- A lock never blocks what the lesson itself needs: in the campfire lesson you can still pick things up, and in the wall lesson you can still fell trees and pick things up, so running short of materials never leaves you stuck.
- Closing a panel is never locked.
- The game pauses while panels such as crafting or the bag are open, so after making something in a panel, you must close the panel for the lesson to show as done.

## Skipping

- **Skipping a lesson**: hold {{key|Esc}} for two seconds. A short press of {{key|Esc}} still opens the menu. Skipping removes only this lesson's lock and spotlight; the stage's objective must still be achieved to move on.
- **Skipping the whole tutorial**: just start a new game from the title page.

## The first night

- The weather on Day 1 is fixed: cloudy by day, a thunderstorm from 17:00 to 20:00, and rain after 20:00. See [[Weather]].
- Night falls at 21:00; zombies and bats appear 6–10 cells from the player, at most 4 at once. They fade away after dawn. See [[Day and Night]].
- Shut the door and stay inside, and they cannot get in. Within 2 cells of a lit campfire, creatures that fear fire will not come near.
- Press {{key|E}} on the bed to sleep, all the way to 06:00. See [[Sleeping]].

## Tips

- You start with only 12/20 satiety; one berry restores 2, so 4 berries fill you up. Save the rest to eat before bed. See [[Satiety]].
- A tree falls after two blows of the stone axe, giving 2 logs and 1 stick. One log makes 2 planks in the crafting panel (you must carry an axe).
- Walls, doors, beds and floors can all be made and placed on the spot in build mode from the materials you carry, without going to the crafting panel first.
- Shut the door before dark. With the door open the house still counts as a bedroom, but it does not keep out the things of the night.
