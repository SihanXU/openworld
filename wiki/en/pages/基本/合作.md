OpenWorld is a **single-player game**, played alone in the browser, with no online, co-op or versus mode. Everyone's saves are kept only in their own browser.

The one thing players share is **what the world learns**. All players connected to the same AI service share one rule library: when someone does something the world has never seen and the world learns its outcome, the other players' worlds learn it too within about 15 seconds, and the next time the same kind of thing meets the same event, no one has to wait for the AI. So every player is helping everyone teach this world to be more complete. See [[世界会学]], [[织线簿]].

## History

- 2026-10-01: The AI service gained a shared rule library, and every game connected to it syncs regularly.
- 2026-10-09: A learned rule belongs to only one kind of thing (see [[世界会学]]).
