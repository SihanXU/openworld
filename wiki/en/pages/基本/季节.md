OpenWorld **has no seasons**, and no weeks, months or years either. The date counts only “day N”, each day 24 hours long; see [[In-game Time]].

- Crops do not keep to seasons: as long as they are in the open and watered on time, they grow. See [[Farming]] and [[Crops]].
- Each day's [[Weather|weather]] is decided at random (the first two days are fixed), and does not change with the seasons.
- The villagers' schedules change only with the hour, the weather and the story; there is no difference between days of the week.

The one long-term change in the world is the night: as the [[Seal-bearers]] fall one by one, dark comes earlier and dawn comes later. See [[Day and Night]].

## History

- 2026-10-01: The early version modeled on Stardew Valley had days of the week and seasons.
- 2026-10-02: The team decided not to copy Stardew Valley's gameplay systems; weeks and seasons were removed, leaving only the day count and day and night.
- 2026-10-06: Added nights that grow longer with the story.
