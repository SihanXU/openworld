A **container** is a piece of furniture that holds things: chests, barrels, crates, clay pots, wardrobes, shelves, bookshelves, sacks and wood piles. Press {{键|E}} on a container to open it; the chest panel appears on screen, and in it you move things between the container and your inventory. A container stays put on the ground and cannot be carried off whole.

## Kinds

| Container | Capacity (slots) | Materials (half returned when broken) |
|---|---|---|
| [[箱子|Chest]] | 12 | 4 Planks |
| [[衣柜|Wardrobe]] | 12 | 4 Planks |
| [[柴堆|Wood pile]] | 12 | 2 Logs |
| [[货架|Shelf]] | 8 | 3 Planks |
| [[书架|Bookshelf]] | 8 | 3 Planks |
| [[板条箱|Crate]] | 6 | 2 Planks |
| {{物品|fixture/container/barrel}} (barrel) | 4 | 2 Planks |
| [[麻袋|Sack]] | 4 | 1 Cloth |
| [[陶罐|Clay pot]] | 2 | — |

Some containers have their own capacity on the map: {{人物|赫斯珀}}'s chest at the Ford holds 8 things, and the Garden Supplies beside the kitchen garden in [[初原村]] hold 24 (its durability is also higher than an ordinary crate's, at 12).

Capacity counts pieces, not slots: a container holds at most that many **pieces**, and pieces of the same kind are shown stacked in one slot in the panel.

## Opening and moving things

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| {{键|E}} | Opens the container and shows the chest panel; if the container is already open, just shows the panel |
| Closing the panel ({{键|Esc}}, ✕ or clicking outside it) | The container closes with it |
| {{键|G}} on an open container | Puts what is in your hand into it; when it is full, it says “It is full.” |
| {{键|G}} on a closed container | It says “It is closed.” |

Time stops while the panel is open. The panel closes by itself when the container is burned, broken, or closed by a rule.

## The chest panel

The top of the panel holds the container's slots, with “pieces held / capacity” beside the title; below is your own inventory.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Click a slot | Moves the whole stack to the other side |
| {{键|Shift}}+click | Moves only one |
| Right-click a slot | A menu: use what is in your hand on it, put what is in your hand into it, handle it empty-handed, move one |
| ⇣ Add to existing stacks | Puts in everything in your inventory of a kind the container already holds |
| ⇅ Organize | Organizes your inventory |

## Building your own container

In the “Buildings” section of [[建造|build mode]] ({{键|B}}):

| Name | Needs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Crate | 2 Planks, and a hammer on you | A crate (6 pieces) |
| Wooden chest | 4 Planks, and a hammer on you | A chest (12 pieces) |

The hammer only has to be carried; it is not used up. You can make a [[锻锤|Stone Smith Hammer]] (2 Stone, 1 Stick) with your bare hands. You can also do without build mode: put two Planks together and you have a frame; strike it once with a hammer and it is nailed into a crate; see [[建造]].

An axe can break crates and chests, giving back half the Planks. See [[耐久与破坏]].

## Other people's containers

- Many containers in the village, and the things in them, have owners. An item with an owner says “Belongs to X” in its tooltip.
- Take something from someone's container in front of them and they say “Hey — that is mine!”, and the story goes round the village for two days. There is no other punishment. See [[居民]].
- The rules have locking and unlocking with keys: a locked container cannot be opened, and a key can unlock or lock it. For now there are no locked containers on the map.

## Tips

- When your inventory is nearly full, put a chest somewhere you often go. A wooden chest holds twice as much as a crate for only 2 more Planks.
- Back at the chest, press “Add to existing stacks” to put your materials away in one go.
- Most containers are made of wood and are flammable; do not put them next to a campfire.
