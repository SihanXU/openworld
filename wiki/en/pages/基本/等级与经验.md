**Levels and experience**: defeating creatures and completing [[任务|quests]] earns experience; when enough experience builds up you go up a level, and every level gives 1 skill point to spend on learning skills on the [[技能|skill]] tree. The experience bar sits below the hearts and drumsticks and above the toolbar, showing your current level. Hover over it to see “Level N · Experience current / needed”, along with the number of unspent skill points if you have any.

## Earning experience

| Source | Experience |
|---|---|
| Defeating a creature or animal | Its maximum health, times your experience bonus |
| Completing a quest | The experience stated in the quest's reward, times your experience bonus. See [[任务]] |

- Only the character who lands the killing blow earns experience.
- Characters that “do not die” (people, [[露米]]) are not defeated when they collapse, and give no experience.
- Breaking things, such as felling trees, breaking stone or knocking down walls, gives no experience.
- For the maximum health of each creature, see [[怪物]] and [[动物]]. For example, a {{怪物|蝙蝠}} has 6, a {{怪物|僵尸}} 14, a {{怪物|狼}} 14.

## Leveling up

Going from level L to the next takes 20 × L experience. Experience left over carries into the next level.

| Level | Needed for the next level | Total experience from level 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | 0 |
| 2 | 40 | 20 |
| 3 | 60 | 60 |
| 4 | 80 | 120 |
| 5 | 100 | 200 |
| 6 | 120 | 300 |
| 7 | 140 | 420 |
| 8 | 160 | 560 |
| 9 | 180 | 720 |
| 10 | 200 | 900 |

- When you level up, “★ Level up! Level N · +1 skill point” appears at the lower left. Gaining a lot of experience at once can raise several levels in a row.
- There is no level cap.
- Leveling up adds no health or attack in itself; you grow stronger through skill points and [[装备|equipment]].
- Unspent skill points show as a red number on the skills button ({{键|K}}) at the lower right.

## Experience bonuses

All the experience bonuses you carry are added together and multiplied onto the experience you gain. A negative total counts as 0, so you always get at least the original amount.

| Source | Bonus |
|---|---|
| The [[恩赐|Boon]] “Crown's Insight” (while Lumi carries it) | +100% |
| Earring [[饰品|accessories]] | Mostly +10%; the Star-glass Earring from a quest reward gives +20% |
| {{物品|荆棘冠}} (worn on the head) | −50%: it only cancels other bonuses, never bringing experience below the original |

## What levels are for

- **Skill points**: 1 per level; see [[技能]]. The first time you have an unspent point, [[露米]] begins guiding you through the quest [[第一个技能点]].
- **Professions**: from level 5 you can see the profession master {{人物|布拉姆}} in [[初原村]] and choose a profession. See [[职业]]. If you reach level 5 without a profession, Lumi begins guiding you through the quest [[选一条路]], taking you to him.
- **Crafting quality**: when you make something, being 3 levels above the recipe's requirement gives [[品质|Fine]], 6 levels above gives Masterwork, and below the requirement you can only make Crude. See [[品质]], [[制作]].
- **Quest thresholds**: a few quests can only be taken at a certain level; for example [[清剿荆棘谷]] needs level 2.

## Tips

- At night in the wild of a combat zone, zombies and bats keep appearing: an early source of experience, and a danger. See [[夜行生物]].
- After defeating the first Seal-bearer, remember to have Lumi carry “Crown's Insight” beside an awakened waypoint statue; from then on your experience is doubled. See [[恩赐]].
