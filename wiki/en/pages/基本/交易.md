Everyone is a **merchant** who buys and sells with you for {{物品|晨辉币}}. Talk to them ({{键|E}}) and choose “Let's trade.” to open the trade panel:

- On the left, “Their goods”: what they have left today, each marked with its price; click “Buy” to buy it, and the money goes from you to them.
- On the right, “What they would pay”: what you carry that they will take and the price they offer; click “Sell” to sell it to them, and they pay you.
- Along the top it says how much money you carry and how much they carry. When they don't have enough to pay, the “Sell” on that row is greyed out.

Time stands still while the trade panel is open. Close it with {{键|Esc}} or ✕.

### What things are worth

The basis of every price is a thing's **value**, set by “how much can be earned in a day”: a villager carries a few dozen [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] a day, a commission pays six to twelve, and each project finished in [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] pays twenty to eighty. Roughly: a stick or a stone is 1, a loaf of bread 6, a healing potion 20, an iron sword 40, a skill book 60. Crops, fish, eggs and milk are worth twice what the [[货摊|trading stall]] pays for them, so you get the same money whoever you sell them to. Things of iron, steel, gold or wood have their value multiplied by their material (iron 1, steel 1.6, gold 5, wood 0.6). The prices marked at the general store in [[初原村]] are reckoned by the same values.

Villagers buy here too: someone who is hungry and has money goes to buy from whoever sells food (see [[人们的需求]]), so Marta's bread is sometimes sold out before evening.

## Prices
Every thing has a **value**, decided by what it is, what material it is made of and its quality (for example bread 6, a lockpick 8, a dagger 20, a healing potion 20; better materials and [[品质|quality]] are worth more).

- **Buying**: the value plus a quarter, then discounted by their [[友谊|regard]] for you: every 10 points of regard makes it 3% cheaper, and at full hearts (100) it is 30% cheaper; when regard is negative they charge more, up to a fifth more at −50.
- **Selling**: half the value; they pay more when regard is high (a fifth more at full hearts) and less when it is negative (a fifth less at −50).

So being on good terms with a merchant makes both buying and selling pay.

## What they won't take
- Money itself, what you are wearing, [[剧情物品|story items]] and bound things are never taken.
- **Quest items are not taken**: things an active [[任务|quest]] needs you to bring back or deliver (the things in [[失物]], the letter in [[捎封信]]) won't be taken by a merchant until the quest is over.
- **What isn't yours is not taken**: something stolen, or taken off someone, an ordinary merchant refuses with “I don't touch what isn't yours.”
- **[[托宾]] is a fence**: he takes stolen goods too, only at a further half off.

What you buy is yours from then on; what you sell belongs to the merchant. Steal a merchant's goods and try to sell them back, and they will know their own things and won't take them.

## Regard and prices
A merchant's regard for you changes their prices: every 10 points of regard makes things 3% cheaper, and at full hearts (100) 30% cheaper; they also pay more for your things, up to a fifth more. When regard is negative they charge more instead, up to a fifth more at −50. The top of the trade panel shows the current “♥ Friend’s price” or “Cold price.” See [[友谊]].
