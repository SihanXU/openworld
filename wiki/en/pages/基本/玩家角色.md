The **player character** is the person the player controls. In the game this person's name is simply “you”, and it cannot be changed; you make their look yourself when you start a new game ([[外观|Looks]]). They wake in the ruined outpost on [[云顶台地]] with only the fairy [[露米]] beside them. Once they come down the mountain, the people of [[初原村]] call them the traveler, and later the Pilgrim.

## Base stats

At the start of a new game:

| Stat | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[生命值|Health]] | 40 / 40 | Ten hearts, 4 points each |
| Attack | 2 | The power of a bare-handed punch; with a weapon, the weapon's power is added |
| Defense | 0 | Subtracted from every hit you take, to a minimum of 1 |
| Movement | 0.2 seconds per tile | Hold {{键|Shift}} to walk slowly |
| [[饱食度|Satiety]] | 12 / 20 | You start a little hungry |
| [[等级与经验|Level]] | 1 | 0 experience, 0 skill points |
| Sight | 7 tiles | Halved away from firelight after dark; see [[昼夜]] |
| Inventory | 30 slots | See [[背包]] |

You start with nothing on you. [[技能|Skills]], [[装备|equipment]], [[职业|professions]] and [[恩赐|Boons]] change these numbers.

## Looks

When you start a new game, the top of the “New game” panel is “Your look”: choose a body (woman or man), flip through hairstyles, facial features, beards, marks, glasses, hats and outfits one by one, click the color swatches to change hair color, eye color, skin color and the colors of the clothes, or roll the dice for a random look. The little figure on the map, the portrait beside the dialogue box and the tags that describe how you look all come from this one look record; see [[外观]]. The default is a traveler with a ponytail in adventurer's clothes. What you hold in your hands is not drawn on the body: a sword, bow or staff is drawn only in the moment you strike with it. [[装备|Equipment]] such as helmets, breastplates and cloaks is drawn on the body once worn.

## The player in the rules

OpenWorld's rules never ask “is this the player?”. In the rules the player character is an ordinary “person”, following the same rules as the villagers:

- They belong to the folk [[阵营与脾性|faction]], and the wild attacks them just as it would attack any villager.
- They carry the property “does not die”: when their health reaches zero they collapse instead of vanishing. A villager who collapses gets up again after a while; when the player collapses, the death screen appears; see [[难度]].
- Eating, wearing clothes, sleeping, getting hurt in a fall, catching fire, getting soaked in the rain: the rules treat them exactly as they treat anyone else. They can also hold a piece of clothing and right-click someone else to dress them in it.
- For now only the player character has [[饱食度|satiety]]. Villagers and animals have none, so there is no ready rule for feeding them ordinary food; it is something the world has to learn. Animals only take [[饲料|Animal Feed]] and [[麦子|Wheat]]; see [[动物#喂食|Feeding]].
- What they do is seen by the people nearby. Take someone's things in front of the owner and the owner says “Hey — that is mine!”, and the story goes round the village for two days.
- The engine checks the reach of every action, the same for every character. See [[操作#输入|Inputs]].

Only the input knows which character the keyboard is moving.

## Growth

- **[[等级与经验|Levels]]**: defeating creatures earns experience, and every level gained gives 1 skill point.
- **[[技能|Skills]]**: spend skill points on the skill tree ({{键|K}}) to learn skills that raise health, attack, defense or speed, or slow hunger.
- **[[职业|Professions]]**: from level 5 you can see {{人物|布拉姆}} in Firstlight Village and choose [[刺客|Assassin]], [[战士|Warrior]], [[游侠|Ranger]] or [[法师|Mage]].
- **[[装备|Equipment]]**: ten slots plus the off hand, affecting attack, defense and other stats. The higher the [[品质|quality]], the higher the numbers.
- **[[恩赐|Boons]]**: after you defeat a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]], Lumi catches part of their power, and you can choose one kind to carry with you.

## Setting

The protagonist is sent onto the Pilgrim's Road by the Church of the Five Sufferings to find the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] and bring back the [[圣刑之器|Instruments of the Sufferings]]. See [[主线剧情]].

{{剧透|The real reason the protagonist was chosen}}
According to the original lore document, the Church of the Five Sufferings did not choose the protagonist for courage alone. To restart the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings, it needs not only the five Instruments of the Sufferings but also a new Bearer of the Sufferings: someone able to defeat the five Seal-bearers and bear the power of the five Seals. In the intended ending, the protagonist inherits the Five Sufferings and becomes the new [[承夜者|Nightbearer]]. See [[结局]].
{{/剧透}}

## History

- 2026-10-02: The player's figure became a ninja in a dog mask; the inventory, satiety, levels and experience were added.
- 2026-10-02: The rules stopped asking “is this the player”: faction, temper and collapsing became data each character carries.
- 2026-10-03: The skill tree and professions were added.
- 2026-10-05: The off-hand slot for equipment and Lumi's Boons were added.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
