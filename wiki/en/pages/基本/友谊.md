Everyone has an **opinion** of everyone else, from −100 (bitter hatred) to 100 (as close as family). Their opinion of you is your **regard** with them. You can tell it from what they do: whether they will follow you, whether they sell to you dear, whether persuading them or apologizing works, whether they avoid you when they see you. When you talk to them, beside the name in the dialogue box there is a word and **a row of ten hearts**: one heart for every 10 points of regard, rounded; a heart more than half full (5 points or more) is drawn whole and one less than half is drawn as half, so 95 points is ten full hearts. Hover over the hearts to see the exact number.

| Opinion | Label |
|---|---|
| −60 and below | hostile |
| −59 to −20 | cold |
| −19 to 19 | neutral |
| 20 to 49 | friendly |
| 50 and above | close |

Opinions belong to **each pair of people separately**: Marta's opinion of you, her opinion of [[Vigo]], and Vigo's opinion of Marta are each their own. The same goes between villagers.

## Where it starts
Strangers start at 0 (neutral). Family and friends (as written in their relationships) and members of the same household start at 30 (friendly).

## What raises it
| What you do | Change |
|---|---|
| Talk to them | +2 the first time each day |
| Give them something ({{key|G}}, or “I have something for you.” in conversation) | Thirty percent of the thing's value plus 2 (a loaf of bread is about +3) |
| Give them their **favorite gift** | +30, once per person per day (giving the same thing again that day counts as an ordinary gift) |
| Return something you took from them | +5 (+8 by choosing “Give back what I took.” in conversation) |
| Bribe them (10 {{item|Dawnmark}}) | +12 |
| Apologize successfully | +5 |

The higher the opinion, the harder it is to raise further (at 50, the same good deed raises it only half as much).

## What lowers it
- Being seen by them doing wrong, or their hearing of it from others: see the table of offenses in [[Crime]]. The victim's household loses the most, family and friends sixty percent, guards half, passers-by a quarter; hearsay halves it again.
- A failed apology −2; a successful [[Dialogue|robbery]] −40, a failed one −60; being caught [[Dialogue|stealing]] −40 (unchanged if you aren't found out).

Bad deeds take their full toll at once; there is no “the lower it is, the slower it falls.”

## It fades
Opinions slowly drift back toward where they started, but **goodwill lasts longer than resentment**: liking falls back only one hundredth a day (100 points of regard is still 95 after five days, still full hearts; after ten days still more than nine hearts), while a quarrel fades by a tenth each day.

## What regard gets you
- **Better deals**: the higher a merchant's regard for you, the cheaper they sell to you: 30% cheaper at full hearts, 15% cheaper at half (50); they also pay more for your things, up to a fifth more. Someone cold to you charges more instead, up to a fifth more at −50. The trade panel shows “♥ Friend’s price −X%” or “Cold price +X%.” See [[Trading]].
- **Inviting them to move in**: at full hearts (10 hearts, 95 points or more), once you have the [[The Held Ground|Held Ground]] (after defeating [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]]), conversation gains the line “Come and live in the Held Ground?”. If they agree, they pack up at once and move there, with a bed of their own; by day they go about near where they live (7:00–21:00), and at night they sleep (21:00–7:00); you can still buy what they used to sell from them on your land. Someone following you stops following you once they have moved there. People the main story can't do without (Sister Ilse, Rowan, Mara, Garrick, Wren, Hesper, Fennick, Edda, Pell, Lina, Oskar, Alberic, Severin, Father Caius and the Church Courier) won't move away; and if you insist on asking anyway when you aren't at full hearts, when the land isn't yours yet, or when they already live there, they explain why they won't move. See [[Held Ground Residents]].
- **How many can live there**: at first the land has only one bed, beside the camp. Ask Alberic to put up a **residents' tent** under “Expanding the Held Ground”: each adds two beds, three in all, for at most seven people. Invite someone when the beds are full, and they will say you have no bed for them yet. See [[The Held Ground]].

## Giving gifts
Pressing {{key|G}} on someone while holding something gives it to them; or choose “I have something for you.” in [[Dialogue|conversation]] to open the **gift panel**. The top of the panel shows the thing they like best: a “?” if you have never heard them say it, prompting you to chat with them more; once they have said it, a picture and the name of that thing, noting if you have already given it today. Below are the things in your backpack you can give (coins, quest items and bound things aren't listed), sorted by how much they will like them: ♥ A trifle (worth less than 5), ♥♥ A kindness (less than 20), ♥♥♥ A treasure, and ★ for the thing they like best. Click “Give” to give it.

They accept it and say a line: some people have their own words for certain things (give [[Cass]] food, and she says she'll keep half for Gus); otherwise it is “For me? … Thank you.” Pressing G while sneaking slips the thing into their pocket instead, which doesn't count as a gift (see [[Sneaking and Pickpocketing]]).

**Everyone has one favorite gift**, decided by their id (the same in every game), and written in the infobox on their character page; children like sweets and toys. How to find out in the game: choose “Let's talk a while.” in conversation and they say a random bit of small talk; everyone has at least two lines, one of which lets their liking slip (“Between us — I've always had a soft spot for… Don't tell anyone.”; some people have their own words: [[Hesper]], [[Hilde]], [[Mia]], [[Bram]], [[Sister Ilse]]). Once said, it isn't repeated, and the gift panel shows it from then on. Give them the thing they like best and they are delighted (with their own words, or “You remembered. …Thank you. Truly.”), +30 regard, once a day.

## What there isn't
There are no heart events, no disliked gifts, no birthdays, and no romance or marriage.

## History
- 2026-10-01: The early version modeled on Stardew Valley had friendship points and gift giving.
- 2026-10-02: The team decided not to copy Stardew Valley's gameplay systems, and friendship points and gift tastes were removed.
- 2026-10-09: Opinions between every pair of people were added (after the way *The Elder Scrolls* and Elin do it: decided by actions, numbers unseen, fading over time).
- 2026-10-18: Everyone got one favorite gift (let slip in chat, shown in the gift panel, +30 when given), and the gift panel was added. This changed the October 2 decision “no gift tastes”: there is only one favorite, with no lists of likes and dislikes.
- 2026-10-12: Opinions of you show as ten hearts; regard falls back more slowly; full hearts make things 30% cheaper; at full hearts you can invite people to move into the Held Ground, and residents' tents add beds. Fixed the same day: the threshold for inviting changed from 100 points to 95 (regard falls back every day, and 100 points lasted only a little over an hour); hearts are drawn rounded, so 95 points is ten hearts; insisting on inviting someone when the conditions aren't met doesn't make them move; someone following you stops following once they have moved in.
