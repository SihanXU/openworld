**Equipment** is what you wear on your body: helmets, armour, boots, rings, earrings, shields and so on. The body has ten slots, plus an off hand. What you put on gives defense, attack, experience bonuses and other traits. Open the [[Inventory|inventory]] ({{key|I}}); the [[Inventory#Character sheet|character sheet]] on the left shows the slots on your body.

## Slots

{{slot table}}

On the left of the inventory panel is a character sheet: the player's anime-style portrait sits in the middle, with a column on the left for head, earring, necklace, shoulders and armour, a column on the right for hands, ring, ring, belt and feet, and the main hand and off hand below the portrait. The top of the sheet shows your level and profession, and below are listed Health, Food, Attack, Weapon, Defense and Critical. See [[Inventory#Character sheet|Character sheet]].

**The main hand** holds a weapon: with a weapon in the main hand, when the toolbar is switched to something else, pressing {{key|Space}} to fight still uses the main-hand weapon; the left click with empty hands does the same. With a torch or tool in hand, the left click uses it for its own purpose (lighting, working); with anything else in hand, the left click uses it on yourself; with another weapon in hand, you use the one in your hand. A weapon's numbers count only once, when you strike with it. An empty slot shows a relief outline of that slot (helmet, earring, sword, shield…). Rings have two places.

The off hand is shown on its own at the bottom of the screen, to the right of the toolbar.

## Putting on and taking off

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hold it and left-click (or right-click yourself) | With something wearable in hand, the left click uses it on yourself, shown as “Put on X”; once put on, it leaves your hand and goes onto your body. A weapon's left click is a swing, so to ready it in the main hand, right-click yourself |
| Click it in the inventory to pick it up, then click a slot on the character sheet on the left | Puts it on |
| Click something worn on the character sheet | Takes it off |
| Right-click in the inventory | The menu has “Wear”, “Ready in the main hand” or “Wear in the off hand” |

- A thing can only be worn in its own slot. If that slot already holds something, the old piece comes off by itself.
- What you wear is on you and **takes no inventory slot**: the slot empties when you put it on, and it returns to the inventory when you take it off (falling at your feet if the inventory is full). It can only be taken off by clicking it on the character sheet.
- Putting on and taking off show “You put on X.” and “You take off X.” at the lower left.

## Dressing someone else

Hold something wearable and right-click a person, and the prompt reads “Put Y on X”: the thing goes into their hands and they put it on (talking to people is {{key|E}}). Handing it over with {{key|G}} only gives it to them: they take it and keep it on them, say something, but do not put it on. See [[Controls#Inputs|Inputs]].

## Stats

- **Defense**: subtracted from every hit you take, to a minimum of 1. The defense of all slots adds up.
- **Attack**: some equipment (gloves, rings and so on) adds attack.
- **Experience**: earring accessories mostly carry an experience bonus; see [[Levels and Experience]].
- **Off hand**: a shield gives defense and block rate, with block rate capped at 50%.

A piece of equipment's attack and defense = its form's own number × the material's factor × the [[Quality|quality]] factor × the [[#Levels|level]] factor, rounded, at least 1. Of the same kind of helmet, an iron one is harder than a wooden one, a Fine one stronger than a Common one, a level 5 one stronger than a level 1 one. See [[Substances]], [[Quality]].

Hover over a piece of equipment and the tooltip says “When worn (X):” and the numbers it gives; increases are written in green, costs in red.

**The stats panel in the inventory** (below the portrait) shows all your stats as they are now, by the same reckoning combat actually uses: your own, what you wear (including each piece's random affixes), set bonuses, statuses, the power Lumi carries, and what the weapon in your hand brings. There are 16 in all: Health, Food, Attack, Weapon, Defense, critical chance, critical damage (150% base), dodge, lifesteal, cooldown reduction, experience gain, luck, movement speed, attack speed, karma and faith (for karma and faith, see [[Karma and Faith]]). Hovering over some of them shows an explanation.

**Slot frame colors**: in the inventory, the equipment panel and the toolbar, equipment sits in dark slots whose frame color shows the quality (after DNF): Crude gray, Common white, Fine blue, Masterwork purple, Vowbound pink, Stigmata orange, trophies gold; Stigmata and trophy frames also have a streak of light running round them. Other items keep plain slots.

**Base stats of clothing** (since 2026-10-12): Hat, defense +1; Hood, defense +1, dodge +1%; Cloak, defense +1; Boots, defense +1, movement speed +3%; Gloves, defense +1, critical chance +2%. Defense scales with material and quality.

## Levels

Every piece of equipment worn or held has a **level**, from 1 to 20, written in the tooltip under its name (“Level 5”). Level is one axis of equipment's numbers; quality is the other:

- **Main stats grow with level**: each level adds 0.25 to the factor for attack, defense and power (level 1 ×1, level 5 ×2, level 10 ×3.25, level 20 ×5.75); percentage stats (critical chance, dodge, experience, movement speed and so on) gain 0.125 per level.
- **Quality multiplies on top of level**: at the same level, the higher the quality, the higher the main stats (Crude ×0.8, Common ×1, Fine ×1.25, Masterwork ×1.5, Vowbound ×1.75, Stigmata ×2), and the more [[#Affixes|minor affixes]] are rolled. So at the same level the higher grade is always better, and at the same quality the higher level is always better.
- **Affixes widen with level**: an affix's range grows 10% per level (nearly 3 times as wide at level 20).
- **Level does not restrict wearing**: equipment of any level can be worn; level only decides how strong it is.

An iron Sword: level 1 Common, power 6; level 5 Fine, 15; level 10 Masterwork, 29; level 20 (Ringsteel, Masterwork), 86.

A piece of equipment's level comes from where it came from:

| Source | Level |
|---|---|
| [[Smithing|Smithing]], [[Crafting|crafting]] | The maker's level (no lower than the recipe's level, at most 20) |
| Bought from merchants | The merchant's own stock level: the village merchants 1, [[Fennick]] 2, [[Orrin]] and [[Hilde]] 2, [[Garrick]] 4 |
| Placed on the map | The map's level: around the Ford and Firstlight Village 1; Thornwood Hollow, the Old Barrow, the Charcoal Dell, the Reed Bend, Dawn Hill 2; the Ossuary of the Thorn, the Pilgrim's Road 3; the Warden's Sanctum, the Abbey of the Thorn, the Kneeling Field, the East Road, Emberfall 4; the Abbey Crypt, the Old Mine 5; Cinder Pass 6; the Dragon's Lair 8 (named equipment can be set otherwise) |
| [[#Monster trophies|Monster trophies]] | That monster's level (slime 1, wolf 2, boar 3, ghost 4, frost wolf 5, golem 6, cyclops 7, dragon 10; see the table below) |
| Quest rewards | The level the quest sets: errands in the village 1–2, the pilgrimage 3, the knights' kingdom 4 |
| [[Stigmata|Stigmata]] equipment | 10, the Seal-bearers' |

Equipment without a level in an old save counts as level 1, with its numbers unchanged.

## Affixes
{{main|Affixes}}

Every piece of equipment has two parts to its numbers:

- **Main stats**: from the form itself, times the factors for material, [[Quality|quality]] and [[#Levels|level]]; a monster trophy's main stats are worked out from its level and do not change.
- **Minor affixes**: a few random ones rolled when the piece is “born”: forged, stocked by a merchant, placed on the map or dropped by a monster all count. Quality decides how many: Crude 2, Common 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5, Stigmata 6; monster trophies 4. No two are alike, and each takes a value in steps within its own range, which widens with level.

| Affix | Range (level 1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | +1–3 | Less common |
| Defense | +1–3 | |
| Critical chance | +1%–5% | |
| Critical damage | +5%–20% | |
| Dodge | +1%–4% | |
| Health per hit | +1 | Rare |
| Cooldown reduction | +2%–6% | |
| Experience gain | +3%–10% | |
| Luck | +3%–10% | Monsters drop things more often (see [[Karma and Faith#Luck|Luck]]) |
| Movement speed | +2%–6% | Only on feet, belt, shoulders, body and ear |
| Attack speed | +2%–6% | Only on main hand, hands, ring, ear and off hand |

When worn, affixes add to you just as main stats do; a weapon's affixes also count when you strike with it (held in hand or readied in the main hand). The tooltip writes them in blue under “Affixes:”. See [[Affixes]] for details. A profession's starting weapon has no affixes; named equipment in quests keeps the numbers its author gave it.

## Monster trophies
{{main|Trophies}}

Every kind of monster has a **trophy** of its own. Defeat one, and there is a chance it goes straight into the inventory of whoever defeated it: 4% for ordinary monsters, 10% for stronger ones, and always for boss-like ones; if you have defeated 50 of the same kind without one, the 50th always drops it. Trophies can only be had by fighting monsters; their main stats are worked out from their level (see [[Trophies]]), and they still roll 4 affixes. The tooltip reads “✧ Trophy · dropped by X”.

## Sets
{{main|Gear Sets}}

Pieces of the same set worn together give an extra bonus. **Armour sets** (head, shoulders, body, hands, belt, feet) gain a tier at 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn, and **jewelry sets** (necklace, earring, ring) at 2 and 3. Pieces are counted by slot, so two rings of the same set count as 1; the main hand and off hand are not part of sets. Each profession has a set forged at an anvil (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave), and the trophies of each monster family also form a set. The bottom of the tooltip lists the set's bonus tiers, with the tiers reached in green.

For trophies' levels, drop rates and main stats, see [[Trophies]].

## Profession-only

- Some equipment can be used only by one [[Professions|profession]], and its tooltip says “X only”. The line is orange when it is your profession, red when it is not.
- Another profession's shield in your off hand does nothing, and the off-hand slot is grayed out.
- Another profession's own weapon cannot be swung; the game says “Only a X can fight with Y.”
- A weapon with no profession restriction reads “Common weapon: no profession talents”.

## Vowbound and Stigmata

The two highest grades of [[Quality|quality]] can only be found, never made:

- **[[Vowbound|Vowbound]]**: things left behind by those who swore a vow. Besides higher numbers and 5 minor affixes, each carries a **vow**: a special effect with a condition and a reward, written in purple in the tooltip as “✦ vow name · Vow” with its description. The ten vows:

{{vow table}}

  Vowbound equipment found at random carries one at random; the named pieces in the story each have their own (Brother Odo's Pauldrons carry the Vow of the Held Ground, Sister Maren's Ring the Vow of Mercy, Tomas's Belt of Vigil the Vow of Vigil, the scallop the Vow of the Road, Fennick's helmet the Vow of the Shield, Cassian's Prayer Cord the Vow of Dawn, the Order's Seal the Vow of the Thorn). Two pieces with the same vow do not stack. See [[Vowbound]].
- **[[Stigmata|Stigmata]]**: things bearing one of the First Martyr's five wounds, found only on the [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]]. Each has a power all its own, written in the tooltip as “✦ power name · Unique”. Some also demand a price: wearing the {{item|Crown of Thorns}}, for example, gives an experience bonus of −50%. See [[Stigmata]], [[Legendary Arms]].

## What there is not

- Each profession has one armour set and one jewelry set, and there are also five monster sets; see [[Gear Sets]].
- Equipment has no durability: it does not wear out and needs no repair.
- Helmets, hats, circlets, the Crown of Thorns, breastplates, pauldrons and cloaks are drawn on the body once worn, on the map figure and the dialogue portrait alike, colored by their material (an iron helmet iron-gray, a bronze one bronze), and they give the wearer the matching look tags (such as “armoured” `look:armoured`). Take them off and you look as before. The other slots (boots, gloves, belts, jewelry) are not shown. See [[Looks#What is worn|Looks]].

## Tips

- The easiest equipment to get early on comes from quest rewards, such as the Traveller's Boots for completing [[Down from the Mountain]] and Rowan's Old Hauberk for [[Lost in the Barrow]]. See [[Quests]].
- When you collapse on Normal difficulty, the equipment you wear (including the main-hand weapon) stays on you; the things in your inventory are bundled into [[Lost Belongings|Lost Belongings]] left where you fell. See [[Difficulty]].
- What you put on someone else is theirs.

## History

- 2026-10-13: Equipment gained levels (1–20): main stats and affix ranges grow with level, the number of affixes grows with quality, and at the same level the higher quality is always better; forged equipment takes the maker's level, and merchants, maps, monsters and quests each have levels of their own; the main stats of monster trophies and quest rewards are now worked out from their level; equipment you wear can also show its stats when hovered over.
- 2026-10-12: The inventory's stats panel now shows all 16 stats (including affixes, sets, statuses and Lumi's power); gloves, boots, hats, hoods and cloaks gained base stats.
- 2026-10-12: Random affixes (4 per piece), monster trophies (one per kind of monster, guaranteed after 50) and five monster sets were added; sets were split after DNF into armour sets and jewelry sets.
- 2026-10-11: The inventory panel became the character sheet, with the slots on either side of the portrait and empty slots showing a relief outline.
- 2026-10-11: Sets were added: four profession sets, with bonuses for several pieces of the same set; new body forms, the Jerkin and the Robe; the Assassin, Ranger and Mage got off-hand pieces of their own (the Parrying Dagger, the Quiver, the Tome).
- 2026-10-09: Worn things no longer take inventory slots.

- 2026-10-04: Wearing was added: the slots on the body, with defense and attack reckoned from form, material and quality; wearable things can also be put on someone else with the left click.
- 2026-10-05: The off hand and profession-only equipment were added.
- 2026-10-07: The Vowbound and Stigmata grades of quality were added, along with a ten-slot equipment panel beside the inventory; named equipment was added to quest rewards.
- 2026-10-08: The main hand was added; when you collapse on Normal difficulty, the equipment you wear stays on you.
- 2026-10-12: Steadfastness, stealth and perception were removed from the stats panel, and luck, karma and faith added; those three stats on equipment were exchanged for defense, dodge or luck (old saves are converted automatically).
- 2026-10-13: Vowbound equipment carries a vow (ten special effects) and rolls minor affixes like any other equipment; Vowbound equipment in old saves is given a vow and affixes automatically, with its numbers raised to the level Vowbound should have.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
