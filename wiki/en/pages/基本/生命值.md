**Health** is shown as ten hearts at the upper left of the toolbar. The player starts with 40 health; each heart stands for a tenth of maximum health (4 points each at the start), is drawn in quarters, and empties from right to left. Hover the mouse over the hearts to see “Health current / maximum”. When health reaches zero the player collapses and the death screen appears; see [[难度]].

## Taking damage

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Attacks | The attacker's attack plus the weapon's power, minus your defense, at least 1 point. Dodge (capped at 50%) can make an attack miss entirely, and a shield in the off hand blocks attacks at its block rate (capped at 50%). See [[战斗]] for details |
| Elements | An attack with an element also has that element's effect; elemental resistance reduces the damage by a percentage. See [[元素]] |
| {{状态|燃烧}} | While you are on fire you lose about 1 point per second, and the fire spreads to flammable things nearby. Becoming {{状态|潮湿}} (rain, a splash of water, walking into a mud pool) puts it out |
| {{状态|流血}} | 1 point per second, ignoring defense |
| Falls | A drop of up to one storey does no harm; every storey beyond that costs 6 points. See [[楼层]] |
| Starving | At 0 [[饱食度|satiety]] you lose 1 point every 8 seconds, but it never takes the last point |
| Lightning | In a thunderstorm, lightning strikes things nearby. See [[天气]] |

## Recovery

| Method | Amount |
|---|---|
| Being well fed | With satiety at nine tenths or more (18/20) and health not full, 1 point every 8 seconds |
| [[睡觉|Sleeping]] | Lying down at once restores half the missing health; in a bed in a [[房间|bedroom]], all of it |
| Healing spring | The hot spring in [[初原村]]: stepping in restores 1 point, and you keep healing while you soak in it ({{状态|泡温泉}}) |
| {{物品|治疗药水}} | 16 |
| {{物品|解毒剂}} | 5 |
| {{物品|葡萄酒}} | 4 |
| {{物品|一杯麦酒}} | 3 |
| {{物品|浑浊药水}} | 1 |
| Drinking water ({{物品|一瓶水}}, {{物品|水袋}}, a Water Bucket) | 2, and the container is emptied |
| {{物品|一桶牛奶}} | 4, plus 4 satiety; the bucket is emptied |
| {{物品|草药}} | Eaten, restores 4 points, plus 1 satiety |
| Revival | On Easy and Normal difficulty, health is full again after revival |

- **Ordinary food does not heal.** Food such as Bread and Steak only restores [[饱食度|satiety]]; once you are well fed, health comes back slowly through “being well fed”. See [[食物]].
- Drinks and herbs first ask “Drink the X?” or “Eat the X?” and are used up only once you confirm.
- Hold a potion or drink and right-click someone else to give it to them; it restores their health.
- Campfires do not heal.

### Skills

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| {{技能|自愈}} | Health regenerates on its own once satiety is above half |
| {{技能|再生}} | Health regenerates on its own as long as satiety is at least a quarter |
| {{技能|肾上腺素}} | When an attack leaves you at 30% health or less, you regain 1 point per second for 5 seconds and move 30% faster; at most once a minute |
| {{技能|影中汲取}} ([[刺客|Assassin]]) | Defeating an enemy with a dagger restores a tenth of your maximum health |
| {{技能|奥术汲取}} ([[法师|Mage]]) | Defeating an enemy with a staff restores 3 points (wands do not count) |

Weapons and [[遗誓|Vowbound]] equipment with the “lifesteal” property draw back health with every hit; see [[装备]].

## Maximum health

Maximum health can only be raised through [[技能|skills]]:

| Skill | Maximum health |
|---|---|
| {{技能|体魄}} | +4 |
| {{技能|强健}} | +6 |
| {{技能|钢铁意志}} | +8 |
| {{技能|不破之身}} ([[战士|Warrior]]) | +10 |

The extra health is filled in at once when you learn the skill. There are always ten hearts; the higher the maximum, the more health each heart stands for.

## At zero

- When the player's health reaches zero, they collapse, the whole world stops, and the death screen appears. See [[难度]].
- Villagers and Lumi collapse for 30 seconds at zero, then get up with half their health.
- Monsters and animals at zero are defeated, and whoever defeated them earns experience. See [[等级与经验]].

## Other health bars

A thin health bar appears above anything that is hurt (creatures hostile to you show it even unhurt):

| Color | Whose |
|---|---|
| Red | Those who will attack you |
| Green | People and Lumi |
| Amber | Everything else: creatures that do not mean to attack you, and things such as a tree that has been chopped or a wall (showing durability) |

A boss's health bar is shown at the top of the screen. See [[首领]].

## Tips

- To recover health, first eat until satiety is at nine tenths or more, then find a safe place and wait a while.
- A night's sleep in a bedroom restores all of it at once. Build a house with a bed, a door and a ceiling; see [[房间]].
- The healing spring in Firstlight Village costs nothing; when you are hurt, go back and soak in it.

## History

- 2026-10-02: Health became ten hearts above the toolbar; satiety and “being well fed” healing were added.
- 2026-10-04: Damage taken is reckoned from the weapon's efficiency against the material and from elemental resistance; things that are defeated break into materials.
- 2026-10-05: The health restored by sleeping depends on whether you are in a bedroom.
- 2026-10-08: The Fireproof Draught lost its healing (drinking it only gives fireproofing); a Bucket of Milk restores health as well as satiety; Shadow Recovery only counts daggers, and Arcane Recovery only counts staves.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
