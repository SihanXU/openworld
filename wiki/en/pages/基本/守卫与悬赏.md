[[初原村]] has its own **watch** (the Firstlight watch), kept by [[霍尔特]] and [[芬尼克]]. For [[犯罪|crimes]] committed in the village and reported, the fines add up to your **bounty** with the watch. The upper left of the screen shows “Firstlight watch bounty N”. A bounty is recorded only where the crime was committed: the wild outside the village has no watch.

Anyone can be wanted, villagers included. When [[维戈]], starving, steals bread and is seen, the guards arrest him just the same (see [[人们的需求]]).

## How the guards arrest people
- A guard who sees a crime reports it to the watch on the spot; other witnesses report it a while later.
- A guard who sees a wanted person, whether **someone he knows** (has dealt with) or **someone within 3 cells**, calls out “You there! Stop in the name of the watch!” and walks over to stop them: “Stop right there! The watch wants a word.”
- When you are stopped, the dialogue box offers only the watch's choices, time stands still, and you must choose one.

## Choices when you are stopped

| Choice | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pay the fine | You carry enough {{物品|晨辉币}} to pay the bounty | You pay and the bounty is cleared; whatever you carry that is not yours is taken back and returned to its owner |
| Go to jail | Always available | You are locked in the cell behind the guards' house, 1 hour for every 20 of bounty (at least 1 hour, at most a whole day), and the time passes at once; the bounty is cleared and stolen goods are taken back |
| Talk your way out | You are not a murderer; you have not already talked this guard round today | Success: “…Fine. Get out of my sight.” The bounty is wiped clean. Failure: his opinion of you drops, and you can only pay or go to jail |
| Bribe | You are not a murderer; you carry one and a half times the bounty | You pay one and a half times the amount, the bounty is cleared, and **the stolen goods stay with you** |
| Resist! | Always available | “Then you pay in blood!” The guard draws his weapon and attacks, and calls the other guards within 10 cells |

The chance of talking him round depends on his [[友谊|opinion]] of you and your level: a base of 30%, plus 10% for every 20 points of opinion, plus 3% for every level.

**Murder** carries a bounty of 1000, beyond what the watch can pardon: you cannot talk your way out or bribe, only go to jail or resist.

If you choose “Pay the fine” without enough money, the guard says “You can't pay that. The cells, then.” and takes you straight to jail.

## After resisting
The guards keep fighting you. Put your weapon away ({{键|X}}) and stop fighting back for a while, and the guards stop and come up to stop you again, so you can choose once more. If you are knocked down, the death screen appears as usual (see [[难度]]). Villagers stopped by the guards never resist: if they have the money they pay the fine, and if not they go to jail.

## Turning yourself in
While you still have a bounty, talking to a guard adds the option “I want to settle what I owe the watch”: the guard offers all the choices, as if he had stopped you.

## Blade drawn
Walk within 3 cells of a guard with your weapon drawn ({{键|X}}) and he warns you: “Put that away before someone gets hurt.” It is only a warning, not a crime.

## The guards' commissions
Some evenings (20:00), when night creatures gather outside the fence, a guard asks you to put down three of them before dawn and come back for your pay: the commission [[清剿夜行者]]. Once you take it, that guard can only be knocked down, not killed, until the quest ends.
