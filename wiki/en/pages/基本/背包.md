The **inventory** holds everything the player carries. It has 30 slots in three rows of ten. The first row is the **toolbar** at the bottom of the screen, on the keys {{key|1}}–{{key|0}}; whatever is in the selected slot is what you hold in your hand. Press {{key|Tab}} (or {{key|I}}) or click the inventory button at the lower right to open the inventory panel; the tabs above the panel switch to the other panels, such as crafting, the skill tree and quests.

## Slots and stacking

- 30 slots in three rows. Learning {{skill|Big Pack}} in the [[Skills#Gathering|Gathering]] branch adds a row, making 40 slots.
- Things of the same kind stack in one slot: they count as the same only when their type, name, tags, statuses and the properties they carry themselves are all alike (so a wet Plank and a dry Plank are kept apart, and so are two letters with different words). Whose a thing is does not matter: a loaf you bought and a loaf you found stack together.
- **Tools, weapons and anything you can wear take one slot each** and never stack: two axes take two slots, each with its own wear and affixes. The same holds in a chest.
- A slot can hold any number; there is no cap, and no weight.
- When the inventory is full (no empty slot), a new kind of thing you get falls at your feet. Kinds you already have still stack in.

## Toolbar

- The ten slots at the bottom of the screen, labeled 1–0 in their top left corners. Choose one with the number keys, by clicking a slot or by scrolling the mouse wheel. The wheel only jumps between slots that hold something.
- One of the stack in the selected slot is held in your hand, and it is what the left and right clicks use: the left click is its own use (a weapon strikes, a tool works, anything else is used on yourself: eaten, drunk, put on), and the right click uses it on what you point at; empty-handed, the left click uses your main-hand weapon or your fist. What you hold is not drawn on the character; when a sword, bow or staff is used to attack, the swing, the drawn bow or the raised staff is drawn in the moment of the strike. See [[Controls]].
- Press {{key|Shift}}+{{key|Tab}} to bring the next row of the inventory up as the toolbar; holding {{key|Shift}} and turning the wheel works too, and scrolling back brings up the last row. The selected column stays the same.
- When the character walks near the bottom edge of the screen, the toolbar moves to the top of the screen so it does not cover the character.
- The single slot to the right of the toolbar is the **off hand**, which normally shows a gray shield outline. With a shield in it, it shows the shield; another profession's shield can be put in but does nothing, and the slot is grayed out. See [[Equipment]].

## The inventory panel

The panel is in two halves: the **character sheet** on the left and the **pack** on the right. When the window is too narrow, the character sheet goes above the pack. Time stops while the panel is open.

### Character sheet

The left side is like a character card:

- **Top line**: a green “Level N” on the left, and the [[Professions|profession]] name on the right (“No profession” before you have chosen one).
- **Middle**: the player's anime-style portrait (the face made in [[Looks|Looks]]) in a copper-edged frame. The column to the left of the portrait is head, earring, necklace, shoulders and armour; the column to the right is hands, ring, ring, belt and feet; below the portrait, side by side, are the main hand and the off hand. Each slot has its name written below it.
- **An empty slot** shows a relief outline of that slot (helmet, earring, necklace, pauldron, breastplate, glove, ring, belt, sword, boots, shield); once something is worn there the slot turns gold and shows it. When another profession's off-hand piece is in the off-hand slot, the slot is grayed out.
- **The stats below**, six in two columns: Health (current / maximum), Food (current / maximum), Attack, Weapon (the power of the main-hand weapon, or “—” without one), Defense and Critical. Attack, Defense and Critical are the character's own numbers plus the numbers written on the equipment worn.

For how to put things on and take them off, see the table below and [[Equipment#Putting on and taking off|Equipment]].

### Pack

On the right are three rows of slots (with the toolbar row set apart). The title line reads “Pack” on the left and shows the money you carry on the right: a {{item|Dawnmark}} icon, the amount and the word “Dawnmarks” (see [[Money]]). Below the slots are two buttons, “⇅ Organize” and “⤓ Drop”, and a line of instructions.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Click a slot | Picks up this stack, which follows the mouse |
| Click another slot while holding a stack | Puts it down or swaps |
| Drag a slot onto another | Puts it down or swaps |
| Drag something wearable onto the character sheet on the left | Puts it on |
| Drag a slot out of the panel | Drops the whole stack in front of you |
| Click empty space outside the panel while holding a stack | Drops the whole stack in front of you |
| {{key|Shift}}+click | Moves this stack between the toolbar and the other rows of the inventory |
| ⇅ Organize | Tools go first, everything else is sorted by kind, and empty slots move to the end |
| ⤓ Drop | Drops the stack you have picked up in front of you (at your feet if there is no room in front) |
| Pick up something wearable and click a slot on the character sheet on the left | Puts it on (in its own slot) |
| Click something worn on the character sheet | Takes it off |
| Right-click a slot | Opens a menu; see below |
| Hover over a slot | Shows this thing's tooltip; see [[Interface#Tooltips|Tooltips]] |

### Right-click menu

Right-click something in the inventory or a chest and you can use several inputs on it, just as if it lay on the ground:

- **Use X on it**: uses what is in your hand on this thing (the same as the right click).
- **Put X into / onto it**: puts what is in your hand into or onto it (the same as {{key|G}}).
- **Handle it**: the same as pressing {{key|E}} on it.
- **Wear / Wear in the off hand / Take off**: only for things that can be worn.
- **Drop**: drops it in front of you.

This way you can use what is in your hand on another thing in your inventory without first putting it on the ground. A combination no rule defines makes [[The World Learns|the world learn]] here too.

## Drawing things in

- Things dropped by rules (Logs from a felled tree, Planks from a smashed crate and so on) that land within 2 tiles fly into your inventory by themselves, and “+1 name” shows at the lower left of the screen. Picking up the same kind of thing in a row adds up the number.
- {{skill|Long Reach}} widens the range by another 2 tiles.
- Things lying quietly are not drawn in: what you leave behind when you collapse on Normal difficulty and what a defeated creature scatters both have to be walked to and picked up with {{key|E}}.
- Nothing is drawn in while the character is busy (sleeping or collapsed, for example).
- A new thing drawn in does not go into the empty slot you have selected (as long as there is another empty slot): fighting empty-handed, you will not suddenly find a dropped thing in your hand. Things picked up with {{key|E}} still go straight into your hand.

## What happens to the things in your inventory

- Equipment you wear is on you and takes no inventory slot: the slot empties when you put it on, and it comes back to the inventory when you take it off. See [[Equipment]].
- When you collapse on Normal difficulty, the things in your inventory are bundled into [[Lost Belongings|Lost Belongings]] left where you fell (the equipment you wear, the weapon in your hand and important items stay with you). Go back and press {{key|E}} to take it all back at once. See [[Difficulty]].
- The things in your inventory are kept in the [[Saving|save]].

## Tips

- Keep the tools and weapons you use often on the toolbar and materials in the rows below, and bring them up with {{key|Shift}}+{{key|Tab}} when you need them.
- When your inventory is nearly full, put a [[Chests and Containers|chest]] at home.
- The organize button puts tools first, so after pressing it the toolbar may have changed; watch what is in your hand.
