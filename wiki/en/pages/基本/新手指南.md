{{引言|What the world has never seen, it learns — and remembers.|Title page}}

**OpenWorld** is a 2D pixel-art role-playing game you can play right in your browser. Every thing and every rule in the world is written code: chopping a tree drops wood, rain puts out burning things, and the undead roam at night. The player can do anything to anything. When you meet something the world has never defined, the world learns its result on the spot if an AI is connected, and remembers it forever; with no AI it is handled by default, and the game can still be played from start to finish. See [[世界会学]].

The story takes place in [[五印之地]]. The [[持印者]] of the [[五苦圣教]] are weakening, and the nights grow longer year by year. The player wakes on [[云顶台地]] at the mountain's top with a fairy called [[露米]] at their side, is drawn into the events of [[初原村]] on the way down, and sets out on the Pilgrim's Road. For the story see [[主线剧情]] and [[第一章]]; for a step-by-step walkthrough see [[主线流程]].

The first time you open it, the game is in English. Click “中文” at the lower right of the title page, or switch in the in-game [[选项]]. The language is remembered in the browser.

## Starting a new game

1. Click “New game” and first choose a difficulty: **Easy** (you get up where you fell), **Normal** (the default: you revive at your resting place, and what you carried is bundled into a bundle left where you fell; equipment and key items are not dropped), or **Hardcore** (one life only). See [[难度]].
2. Choose one of the three save slots and click “Start here”. If the slot already holds progress, the button reads “Overwrite & start” and asks you to confirm first.
3. The game saves automatically; see [[存档]].

If you have never played a game of this kind, you may want to play through the [[新手教程|tutorial]] first.

## The first hour

Here is an opening without detours. The order is not binding: the game's goals are all “states”, and any way of reaching them counts.

### 1. Waking on Cloudtop

- It is Day 1, 13:00. The player wakes in a ruined watch post in the northwest corner of the plateau, with Lumi beside them; she tells you this is Cloudtop, and the only way down is the cut in the rock face to the south. The first main quest, [[下山]], begins: the quest tracker at the upper right shows the current objective, and the yellow diamond on the minimap points to where you need to go.
- You carry nothing; satiety is 12/20 and health 40/40. See [[饱食度]] and [[生命值]].
- The watchman's log lies open on the post's table; press {{键|E}} to read it. A watchman's cloak still hangs on the hook. Later, {{人物|奈莎·奎尔}} of Firstlight Village will ask you to come and read this log (the side quest [[守夜人的日志]]).
- On the open ground before the door are 3 berries, 2 sticks, 2 stones and 2 bundles of cut grass.
- Cloudtop is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters spawn there at night.

### 2. Eating

1. Walk over to the berries and press {{键|E}} to pick them up.
2. Press the number of the berries' toolbar slot ({{键|1}}–{{键|0}}) to take them in hand.
3. Left click, and confirm “Eat Berries?”. One berry restores 2 satiety.

Health regenerates on its own only when satiety is above 90%; see [[饱食度]].

### 3. Making a stone axe

Press {{键|C}} to open the [[制作|crafting]] panel. A [[斧|stone axe]] takes 1 stick, 1 stone and 1 bundle of cut grass, and can be made by hand. A stone axe fells trees, and it is a weapon too. See [[伐木]].

### 4. Waking the waypoint statue

A short way east of the post stands a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]]. Lumi reminds you as you come near. Press {{键|E}} to wake it; the statue lights up and becomes your **resting place**: on Normal difficulty, you revive here after falling. From then on, click it on the [[世界地图]] ({{键|M}}) to travel back to it from anywhere.

### 5. Down to the Mountain Foot

Go down the path from the cut on the south side of the plateau and you reach the [[山脚]]. The Mountain Foot is a combat zone, with monsters after dark.

Where the path reaches level ground, an ancient [[五伤路碑]] stands among the flowers. No rule in the world has ever defined it. Do something to it: right-click it with something in hand, put something on it with {{键|G}}, or strike it. A “?” appears after the action prompt; this is where the world learns. Once you have done it, Lumi tells you that everything the world learns is kept in the [[织线簿]] ({{键|J}}).

Below the shrine stone is another waypoint statue, the Mountain Foot's own; remember to wake it.

### 6. The Ford

Follow the path south and down the cliff steps and you reach [[渡口]]. The ferrywoman {{人物|赫斯珀}} tells you that a storm snapped the ferry rope, and a rider all in white tore the planks off the little bridge. She sends you west to Firstlight Village first, to help out there. Just then you find half a loaf left over from the road in your pack (you get a {{物品|面包}}).

### 7. Firstlight Village

Head west to [[初原村]]. {{人物|伊尔丝修女}}, {{人物|佩尔}} and the elder {{人物|罗万}} come to meet you.

- With this, the main quest [[下山]] is complete, rewarding 15 experience and a pair of traveler's boots. See [[任务]].
- Rowan's main quest [[古墓里的失踪者]] begins right away: two people from the village went missing in the storm.
- The village has a steaming healing spring; soak in it to recover health. Lumi points it out the first time you see it.
- Firstlight Village is a peaceful zone. People with something to ask of you have a yellow “!” over their heads: press {{键|E}} on them to hear them out, and choose “Accept” in the dialogue box. The [[委托板|commission board]] in the square lists the village's current commissions.

For what comes after, see [[主线流程]].

## How to stay alive

| System | Key points |
|---|---|
| [[生命值|Health]] | Ten hearts. While you are well fed (satiety above 90%) it slowly regenerates; you can also sleep, soak in a hot spring or drink a potion |
| [[饱食度|Satiety]] | Ten drumsticks. After eating your fill, it takes about six and a half game hours to fall to 60%; after that you are “hungry” and it falls very slowly; at 0 you lose health |
| [[昼夜|Day and night]] | Night falls at 21:00, dawn comes at 05:00. After dark, the undead and bats come out in the wild in combat zones |
| [[睡觉|Sleeping]] | Stand on a bed and press {{键|E}} to sleep until 06:00. The bed becomes your resting place |
| [[营火|Campfire]] | Within 2 cells of a lit campfire, creatures that fear fire will not come near |
| [[和平区域与战斗区域]] | Below the minimap it says which kind the current map is |
| [[难度|Death]] | When health reaches zero the death screen appears, where you can revive, load a save or return to the title |

One minute of game time is one real second, and a whole day is 24 real minutes. Time pauses while menus and dialogue are open. See [[游戏内时间]].

## Lumi

[[露米]] is the fairy who follows you everywhere. She flies, takes up no space, does not fight, and follows you across maps. Press {{键|E}} on her and she tells you what to do “now” and how. The current objective is also written in the quest tracker at the upper right; if you forget what to do, glance at the tracker, ask her, or open the [[任务|quest]] journal ({{键|L}}).

She also picks her moments to teach you new things of her own accord, in **guide quests**: the first time you have an unspent skill point ([[第一个技能点]]), reaching level 5 without a profession ([[选一条路]]), the first time you walk to the village pond ([[第一竿]]), the first time you walk into the vegetable garden ([[菜圃的一课]]). She says a line and the quest begins; it is listed alongside the main quest on “The Road” page of her panel.

## Tips

- Read the action prompt above the cell before pressing a key. Things with a “?” are things the world has never seen; go ahead and try them. See [[操作#动作提示与「?」|action prompts]].
- Wake the [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] in every place that has one: it lets you travel there, and serves as a resting place too.
- Before dark, get back to a peaceful zone, shut yourself indoors, or keep by a lit campfire.
- When you fall on Normal difficulty, what you carry (except worn equipment, the weapon in hand and key items) is bundled into a {{物品|遗落的包裹}} left where you fell. Go back and press {{键|E}} to take it all back at once.
- When your bag is full, newly picked-up things drop at your feet. Use a [[箱子与容器|chest]] to store what you do not need.
- To see more of the world, open the [[世界地图]] ({{键|M}}); walk off a map's edge and you can also reach the overworld of [[五印之地]].

## History

- 2026-10-03: The opening became a prologue that goes from the mountaintop all the way down to the Ford; waypoint statues and the world map were added.
- 2026-10-04: A new game first entered the tutorial in the Clearing, which could be skipped; Cloudtop was removed for the time being.
- 2026-10-04: A new game lets you choose a difficulty.
- 2026-10-06: Cloudtop was restored as the starting point of a new game, and the tutorial became a separate mode on the title page.
- 2026-10-07: The opening joined Chapter 1's main story: going from Cloudtop to Firstlight Village is the main quest “Down from the Mountain”, and Rowan's quest begins automatically on arrival.
- 2026-10-08: On Normal difficulty, what is left where you fell is bundled into one bundle, taken back at once. Fishing and money were added to the systems at a glance.
- 2026-10-09: Quests are now accepted in the dialogue box; a quest tracker appeared at the upper right of the screen, with quest markers on the map; a commission board went up in Firstlight Village.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held thing on something became the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things became {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
