---
title: Festivals
summary: OpenWorld has no festivals held by the calendar; gatherings in the village happen only once, at particular moments of the story.
aliases: festival, celebrations, feasts, gatherings
---
OpenWorld **has no festivals**. The game has no fixed days on a calendar (see [[季节]]), so there are no celebrations or gatherings held on time every year or every week.

Gatherings in the village happen only once, as part of the story. In [[第一章|Chapter 1]], for example, after the missing villagers are brought back and the vigil is prepared, the people of [[初原村]] gather by the holy fire at dusk, Sister Ilse tells the story of the First Long Night, and the whole village gives thanks that night (the “Night of Vigil and Thanksgiving” after the main quest [[守夜之备]]). Scenes like this are set off by the story and are not repeated. See [[主线流程]].

## History

- 2026-10-01: An early version modeled on Stardew Valley had weekly gatherings and a planned “Kindling Festival”.
- 2026-10-02: The team decided not to copy Stardew Valley's gameplay systems, and the weekly gatherings and festivals were removed.
