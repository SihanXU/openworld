OpenWorld **has no achievements**, and no statistics page recording play time, monsters defeated and the like.

To see how far you have come, look in these places:

- The [[Codex]]: the items, people and creatures you have seen; each page shows “seen / total”.
- The [[Threadbook]]: every rule the world has learned; the ones signed “you” are the ones you taught it.
- The [[Quests|quest]] journal: quests under way, completed and failed, each recording every step taken.
- Save cards: day, level and current objective. See [[Saving]].

The [[Boss Challenge]] keeps a count for the session, “Defeated this session: N / 4”, which resets when you close the page.

## History

The game has never had an achievement system.
