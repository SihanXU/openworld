In [[Dialogue|dialogue]], say “Come with me.” to someone:

- **Someone the story cannot do without** cannot leave: “I can't leave — I'm needed here.” (For who the story cannot do without, see [[Death of Characters]].)
- If their [[Friendship|regard]] for you has reached “Friendly” (above 25), or they are a **hired hand**, they come with you: “Lead on, then.”
- Otherwise: “I don't know you well enough for that.”

For now the ones who can go with you are [[Cass]], [[Tam]] (once you are on good terms) and [[Vigo]] (a hired hand, who goes with anyone who asks).

## While they follow you
- They set aside their own schedule and keep within 2 tiles of you.
- If you get far ahead (more than 12 tiles), they come straight to your side.
- If you walk into another map, they cross over with you and appear beside you.
- **Those who fight** (among the ones who can go with you, for now [[Vigo]]) rush in to fight for you when they see something within 4 tiles of you doing you harm. Those who do not fight just follow.
- When hungry they still eat and buy things (see [[People's Needs]]); when a monster comes near, those who do not fight get out of the way.

## What to say to someone following you
| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| Wait here. | They stay where they are and wait for you, and do not cross maps with you |
| Let's go on. | They stop waiting and follow you again |
| Go home. | They stop following you and go back to their own life |

Followers can die too (none of them is someone the story cannot do without): take [[Cass]] somewhere dangerous and she may not come back. See [[Death of Characters]].
