The **codex** opens with {{key|J}}, or by clicking the book button in the lower right of the screen. It lists every kind of item, every person and every kind of creature in the world: those you have seen show their picture and name, those you haven't are only a black silhouette named “???”. The codex is the player's own record and goes with the [[Saving|save]]. The last page of the codex is the [[Threadbook|Threadbook]].

Time stops while the codex is open. When a new entry appears, the codex button in the lower right flashes, and stops once you open the codex.

## Four tabs

| Tab | Contents | What counts as “seen” |
|---|---|---|
| Items | Every kind of item, grouped by kind | Having carried it: in your backpack, in your hand, or in a container in your backpack |
| People | Every person, and Lumi | Coming within 6 cells on the same map |
| Monsters | Every kind of creature, including animals and the White Knight; the three bosses who appear only in the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]] are not included | Coming within 6 cells on the same map |
| Threadbook | Every rule the world has learned | See [[Threadbook]] |

The labels of the first three tabs show “seen / total,” and so does the heading of each group on the Items page.

## Cards

- **Seen**: drawn as it looks, with its name.
  - A creature's card shows its ❤ health, ⚔ attack and 🛡 defense below.
  - A person's card shows which map they live on below.
- **Not seen**: only a black silhouette, named “???”.

Items are recorded by kind, not one by one: see one stone axe and the “Axe” slot lights up; an iron axe and a Fine axe all count as the same kind.

## Item groups

Weapons, Armour, Clothing, Tools, Lights, Vessels, Food, Potions, Materials, Seeds, Gems, Valuables, Relics, Documents, Keys, Odds and ends.

## Known issues

At present the codex can't be completed:

- Several kinds of creature never appear on any map: the {{monster|Slime King}}, {{monster|giant crab}}, {{monster|ice golem}}, {{monster|storm wisp}}, {{monster|water slime}} and {{monster|spark slime}}.
- Some items can't be got in the game, such as the {{item|Coin Pouch}} and the {{item|Skill Book}}.

## Tips

- To find out where someone lives, look for their card in the codex first. People you haven't seen are only a silhouette, so you have to meet them once.
- A creature's health, attack and defense can only be seen after you have seen it. Being within 6 cells of it counts as seeing it; no need to fight.
- New items and creatures the world creates as it learns are not on the codex's list.

## History

- 2026-10-06: The codex ({{key|J}}) was added: three pages for Items, People and Monsters, with the old Threadbook becoming the codex's fourth page. A codex button was added in the lower right.
