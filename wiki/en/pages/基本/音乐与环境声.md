The **music** follows what is happening in the game: a map does not loop one piece for ever. In the wilds a piece plays and then falls silent for a while, leaving only the **ambience**: the wind, the rain, running water, a fire, birds by day and crickets by night. The ambience is made as you play; there are no recordings behind it.

## What plays when

From the top down, the first that holds decides:

| When | What plays |
|---|---|
| The title screen | the title's own piece, whatever place shows behind it |
| A scene | the music the scene asks for, or silence, until the scene ends |
| A [[Bosses|boss]] fight | that boss's own fight music; [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]] changes to another at his second phase |
| A [[Combat|fight]] | the moment something comes at you, or you strike someone, the fight music cuts in; after the fight it plays on for 7 seconds, then fades back to what was playing |
| Otherwise | the place's music, in its version for the hour and the [[Weather|weather]] |

While a fight, a scene or a boss plays over a place's music, the place's piece is only held, and goes on afterwards from where it stopped.

While you lie fallen (see [[Difficulty|Difficulty]]) the music stops; only the fall's jingle sounds.

## Places and hours

Each place has its own mood, and a place can have a version of its own for dawn, day, dusk, night, rain and storm:

| Place | By day | Dusk and night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Cloudtop]] | airy, lonely | at night as the wilds | tense in a storm |
| The wilds ([[The Grassland]], the mountain foot, the roads) | a piece now and then, with long rests | sad at dusk; at night almost only the ambience | sad in the rain, tense in a storm |
| [[Firstlight Village]] | the village's music, short rests | at dusk a quiet piece by the fire; at night almost silent | a softer version |
| [[Millbrook]] | pastoral | as the village | as the village |
| [[The Reed Bend]] | music of the river | sadder at dusk; at night as the wilds | as the wilds |
| [[The Ford]] | a forlorn piece | as the wilds | as the wilds |
| [[The Forest]], [[The Charcoal Dell]] | mysterious | after dark, the dark forest's music | as the wilds |
| [[Thornwood Hollow]] | like ruins | dark at night | — |
| Indoors | quiet pieces, long rests | quieter at night | — |
| A shop | light | — | — |
| [[Firstlight Chapel]] | like chant | — | — |
| Caves and mines | deep | — | — |
| [[The Old Barrow]] and its crypts | grim | — | — |
| The [[World Map|World Map]] | the road's music | — | — |

The hours of dusk and night follow [[Day and Night|Day and Night]]: every seal that falls makes the night longer, and dusk's music comes earlier with it.

## Music in the story

A few great scenes have music of their own: the night the flame is stolen, which turns to the grey man's theme when he appears; the morning the Church's courier comes; the White Knight's fall; the cards that end Part One, Chapter One and the dragon. As you step into the White Knight's ring of thorns the music falls silent first, and the fight music comes only when he strikes.

## The ambience

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| Wind | always a little outdoors; stronger on snow and high ground, with a thin whistle; strongest in a storm |
| Rain | in rain and storms; indoors it is the dull rain on the roof |
| Thunder | a moment after the lightning in a storm |
| Running water | the more water nearby, the louder |
| A fire | the nearer a lit fire, the louder its crackle; a hearth indoors counts |
| Birds | by day outdoors, more in the morning; none in the rain or on snow |
| Crickets, an owl | at night outdoors, when it is neither cold nor raining; the owl calls only now and then |
| Drips and a low hum | in dark places such as caves and the barrow |
| A room's quiet | a very faint hush indoors |

## Jingles

Gaining a level, finishing a [[Quests|quest]] and [[Lumi|Lumi]] taking up a seal's power each have a short jingle; the music steps back while it sounds.

## Volume

The [[Options|Options]] and the title screen's “Settings” each have two sliders, **Music** and **Ambience**, both 80% by default. The “Sound” switch turns the music, the ambience and the effects off together. The settings are remembered in the browser.
