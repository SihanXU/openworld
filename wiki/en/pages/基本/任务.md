A **quest** is something the world wants you to do. Main quests begin by themselves as the story goes; side quests and commissions need someone to entrust them to you: a person with a yellow “!” over their head has something to ask of you. Press {{键|E}} on them, they explain what they want, and below the dialogue box “Accept: quest name” and “Not now (quest name)” are listed; only choosing to accept takes it on. Every quest is divided into several **stages**: when things in the world change, the quest moves forward a step and writes a line in its **journal**. Press {{键|L}} to open the quest journal; the tracker in the top right of the screen, the minimap, the world map and the markers at the screen's edge point to where the quest you are tracking wants you to go.

## Four kinds of quest

| Kind | Where it comes from | How it begins | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main | The story itself | By itself, without talking to anyone: when one main quest ends, the next takes over | [[下山]], [[第一印]] |
| Side | Someone's own business | Talk to them and accept in the dialogue box | [[米娅的戒指]], [[老骑士]] |
| Commission | Something happening in the village right now, or a job there is every day | Talk to the person asking and accept, or take it at the [[委托板|commission board]] | [[一口吃的]], [[给火堆添柴]] |
| Guide | [[露米]] teaching you a way to play | She speaks up herself: it begins when the moment comes | [[选一条路]], [[第一竿]] |

In the quest journal, every quest name has a small tag in front saying which kind it is.

## Taking on quests

### Main quests: they begin by themselves

Main quests don't need to be taken. As soon as their condition is met (the previous main quest ends, you reach a certain place, the story reaches a certain step), they begin, and the lower left says “Quest taken: X.” If you haven't chosen another quest to track in the journal, the newly begun main quest is the one tracked. Main quests follow one another, and there is always one under way. See [[主线流程]].

### Guides: Lumi speaks up herself

**Guides** are Lumi's teaching quests. She waits for the right moment and says a line herself, and the quest begins, with the notice “New guide: X” in the lower left: when you have unspent skill points it is [[第一个技能点]]; when you reach level 5 without a profession, [[选一条路]]; the first time you reach the pond in Firstlight Village, [[第一竿]]; the first time you walk into the vegetable garden, [[菜圃的一课]]. They don't take the main quest's place: the main quest is still tracked by default; press {{键|E}} on Lumi, and her “The Road” page lists the guides under way beside the main quest, each with her hint. Every objective is a state (a fishing rod in your bag, having talked to Bram, a sprout standing in the clearing), and however you reach it counts. There are no guides in the [[新手教程|tutorial]]: its lessons are in the stages of [[醒来]].

### Side quests and commissions: accepted in conversation

1. A person with a yellow “!” over their head has something to entrust to you. Walk up to them and press {{键|E}}, and they first explain what they want.
2. At the top of the list below the dialogue box is each thing they are asking of you, with two choices each: **Accept: quest name** and **Not now (quest name)**. Click one or press its number key.
3. Choose accept: the quest begins, the lower left says “Quest taken: X,” and they may say another line.
4. Choose not now:
   - A side quest: they don't take offense, and the matter stays open. The “!” over their head doesn't go away; come back and take it whenever you have made up your mind.
   - A commission the village gave rise to ([[一口吃的]], [[失物]], [[捎封信]], [[清剿夜行者]]): declining withdraws it, and this commission won't come to you again.

Some side quests have prerequisites: a certain level, the story reaching a certain step, finishing another quest. Until the conditions are met there is no “!” over their head, and they won't bring it up. Side quests the story has already passed won't be brought up again either (for example, once the White Knight has fallen, no one will entrust [[朝圣之路]] to you any more).

### What people say first when you talk to them

Press {{键|E}} on someone, and they pick one thing to say in this order:

1. **Words that move a quest forward**: a quest you are to hand in to them (with everything done), or a quest that sends you to talk to them. They say that stage's line, and the quest moves on. This comes before everything else.
2. **Entrusting**: if they have something to ask of you, they explain it, and the dialogue box lists accept and not now.
3. **Words while they wait**: if you have taken their quest and not finished it, they give you a nudge, such as [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s “Three beef, two eggs. The pot is waiting.”
4. Only if there is none of these do they say their everyday line.

After that line, the things you can say to them are listed below the dialogue box. Choices to do with quests come first: commissions to accept or decline, and the topics a quest under way adds for this person (for example, in [[失物]], saying “Give back what you took.” to the one who took the thing). For the other choices see [[对话]].

## Markers over heads

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Yellow “?” | Talking to them now will move one of your quests forward: handing in, or a quest that sends you to them |
| Yellow “!” | They have something to entrust to you (a side quest or commission) |
| “?” (quest under way) | One of your quests' objectives is to talk to them, even if it isn't time yet (for example, you have everything but left it in a chest) |

When someone has several of these at once, only one is shown, taken from the top of the table down. Markers are drawn above everything in the world, so you can see them even when the person stands behind a house. See [[界面#头顶的东西|What appears over heads]].

## Objectives

Every quest has several objectives, listed in the journal and the tracker:

| Objective | How it counts as done |
|---|---|
| Bring | You carry a number of a certain kind of thing. The count looks at how many you carry **now**: drop them or use them, and the count goes back down |
| Defeat | After taking on the quest, defeat a number of a certain kind of creature (some only on a certain map). Those defeated before you took the quest don't count |
| Talk | Go to someone and press {{键|E}}, and they say a particular line |
| Give | Hold something and press {{键|G}} on someone to put it into their hands |
| Reach, achieve | Something holds true in the world: you reach a place, ring a bell, read a journal, wake a number of waypoint statues, the story reaches a certain step |

- Objectives look at states, not routes: however you do it counts. Some objectives already hold before you take the quest (for example, you woke three statues long ago), and are done the moment you take it.
- Some objectives are released stage by stage: the later ones only appear in the journal after the earlier ones are done.
- Objectives marked “optional” don't affect whether the quest is completed.
- An objective that can no longer be done (for example, because the story has moved past it) is struck out in the journal.

## Stages and the journal

Every quest is made up of several stages. A stage waits on a state of the world: a thing coming into your hands, someone falling, you entering a place, a step of the story happening. As soon as the state holds, however it came about, the quest enters that stage:

- a line is written in the journal, such as “All of it done. Back to Marta.”;
- new objectives are released, or objectives are ticked or struck out;
- sometimes someone says a line, takes things from you, or hands out a reward;
- sometimes the quest is completed or failed then and there.

Quests only move forward, never back. Each quest page's “Stages and journal” section lists all its stages, what each writes in the journal, and when it is entered.

## Handing in

Side quests and most commissions must be handed back to the person who asked, once done:

1. When all the objectives are done, the journal writes “All of it done. Back to X.”, an objective “Back to X” appears, and a yellow “?” appears over their head.
2. Press {{键|E}} on them: they say their hand-in line, take what they asked for from you (a few things they let you keep, such as the [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]]), the reward is yours, and the lower left says “Quest complete: X.”
3. At hand-in the objectives are checked once more: things for a “Bring” objective must be on you. If you put them down after gathering them all, you have to bring them along again to hand in.

Some commissions don't need you to go back: in [[一口吃的]], [[失物]] and [[捎封信]] you put the thing into the other person's hands with {{键|G}}, and the quest is completed on the spot. Most main quests complete by themselves when the last objective is achieved; [[古墓里的失踪者]] has you go back to [[罗万]], and [[守夜之备]] has you go back to [[伊尔丝修女]].

## Rewards

Rewards can be:

- **Experience**, multiplied by your experience bonus. See [[等级与经验]].
- **Items**, put straight into your backpack. Some are named gear of a given quality, such as Rowan's old mail. See [[装备]], [[品质]].
- **Money**: [[失物]] gives 10 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]], [[捎封信]] 6, [[清剿夜行者]] 12. Other quests give no money. (The money for expanding the Held Ground is not a quest reward; it is paid when a project of [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] is finished.) See [[金钱]].
- **Regard**: the person who asked (and sometimes others) thinks better of you. See [[友谊]].
- The story moving forward.

Each quest page's “Rewards” section lists all its rewards.

## Quests the story has passed

The main story never breaks off and never leaves you stuck. If you jump ahead some other way, the quests passed over come to an end instead of hanging in the journal forever. At present only one thing passes over other quests: [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]] falling.

| Quest | If the White Knight falls first |
|---|---|
| [[古墓里的失踪者]] | It ends, and Lina and Oskar make their own way back to the village. If by then both have been found and the warden has fallen, it is completed and rewarded as usual; otherwise there is no reward |
| [[守夜之备]] | It ends. If by then you carry the lamp oil and the dawn flowers and have rung the bell, Ilse accepts them all the same and the reward is given; otherwise there is no reward |
| [[第一印]] | Completed, rewarded as usual |
| [[朝圣之路]] | It ends. If by then all three people have been asked, the reward is given as usual; otherwise there is no reward. If you never took it, no one will entrust it to you any more |

Quests that end this way all go on the journal's “Done” page, with the last journal line reading “The road went past it.”, or, when everything was done, “All of it done as the road went on.”

## Commissions

**Commissions** arise from what is happening in the village right now: someone is hungry, someone has watched their things being taken, there is a letter to carry, the things of the night are growing in number again. They come and go, and the people and things in each are the ones really there at the time.

| Commission | When it appears | Who asks | What to do | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[一口吃的]] | Someone is hungry, has no money and can't find food | The hungry person | Give them something to eat ({{键|G}}) | 8 hours |
| [[失物]] | Someone has seen a person they know take something of theirs (not food) | The one who lost it | Get it back and return it to them ({{键|G}}) | 24 hours |
| [[捎封信]] | Some mornings (6:00) someone in Firstlight Village has a letter to send | The one who wrote the letter | Give the letter to its recipient ({{键|G}}) | 12 hours |
| [[清剿夜行者]] | Some evenings (20:00) a guard thinks there are too many things about in the night | A guard | Defeat 3 night creatures and go back to them | 10 hours |
| [[给火堆添柴]] | Every day | [[格斯]] | Bring back 4 logs and hand them to him | None |

- Someone with a commission has a “!” over their head, and it is also listed on the [[委托板|commission board]] in [[初原村]].
- **Deadlines** run in game time: once a commission is offered, if no one takes it within the deadline it is withdrawn; once taken, if it isn't done within the deadline it **fails**. Commissions without a deadline never fail.
- There are at most two of the same kind of commission at once (one each of [[捎封信]] and [[清剿夜行者]]); the same person or the same thing never gives rise to two of the same commission at once.
- [[给火堆添柴]] can be taken once a day; after handing it in you have to wait until the next day. Gus asks it of you in person, so it isn't on the commission board.

## The commission board

{{主条目|委托板}}

On the square of [[初原村]], beside the [[布告栏|notice board]], stands a commission board. Stand within 2 cells and press {{键|E}} (“Look at the commission board”) to open the commission board panel: every commission in the village that no one has taken yet, each with its title and description and a “Take it on” button beside it. Click “Take it on” and it is yours, with no need to go and find the person asking; handing in and the outcome are the same as taking it on face to face. When there are no commissions it says “Nobody wants anything done just now. Come back later.”

## Failure

A quest fails when:

- a commission passes its deadline without being done;
- a person the quest can't do without dies, or the thing it needs is gone: the hungry person in [[一口吃的]] dies, the recipient in [[捎封信]] dies or the letter is gone, the thing in [[失物]] is destroyed.

On failure the lower left says “Quest failed: X” (in red), and the quest moves to the journal's “Failed” page, with its last line giving the reason for the failure, or “It came to nothing.”

## Protected people and quest items

- **People the story cannot do without** (people who speak in cutscenes, people who give quests, profession trainers) don't die when their health reaches zero; they only fall down. Besides, while a quest is under way, the people it names have the same protection, such as the guard who asks in [[清剿夜行者]]. See [[人物之死]].
- **Quest items**: while a quest is under way, the thing it needs you to bring back or deliver (the thing in [[失物]], the letter in [[捎封信]]) is a quest item, and merchants won't take it. See [[交易]]. Once the quest is over it is an ordinary thing.

## The quest journal (L)

Open it with {{键|L}} or by clicking the quest button in the lower right; time stops while it is open.

- At the top are three tabs: **Under way**, **Done** and **Failed**, each showing how many quests it holds.
- On the left are this page's quests, newest first; each has its kind's tag in front (Main, Side, Favour), and the tracked one has a ★ in front.
- On the right are the details of the selected quest:
  - the title, with “✓ Done” for completed quests and “✗ Failed” for failed ones;
  - **From**: who asked (left out for main quests with no one asking);
  - the description;
  - the objectives: unfinished ones first, with their progress (such as “Log 2/4”), “optional,” and “→ map name” when the objective is on another map; finished ones ticked ✓, struck-out ones marked ✗;
  - the **Journal**: one line for each stage reached, in the order they happened.
- A quest under way has a “☆ Track” button in its upper right; click it and it becomes “★ Tracked,” and this quest is the one tracked. Click again to cancel and return to the default.
- When you haven't taken any quest, “Under way” says: “No quests taken on. Look for a yellow "!" over someone's head, and press E.” When you have taken quests but they are all done for now, it says: “Nothing under way. Look for a yellow "!" over someone's head for more.” Other empty pages say “None.”
- The number on the quest button in the lower right is the number of quests under way. It has a red background when someone in sight has a “?” over their head, and a brown one otherwise. The button flashes when a quest enters a new stage, and stops when you open the journal.

## Tracking and markers

Only one quest is tracked at a time: the one you chose in the journal; if you haven't chosen one, the most recently begun main quest still under way (or, with no main quest, the most recently begun quest).

- **The tracker**: in the top right of the screen, below the clock, it shows the tracked quest's name and its unfinished objectives (up to four), with their progress; when an objective is on another map it says “→ map name.”
- **Quest markers**: the people or places that the tracked quest's unfinished objectives point to:
  - a yellow diamond on the [[世界地图#小地图|minimap]];
  - a yellow ★ on the [[世界地图|world map]] ({{键|M}}); hovering over it shows the objective;
  - for one on the current map but off the screen, an arrow at the screen's edge pointing its way; once it is on the screen, the arrow turns into a diamond floating above it;
  - when the objective is a person, the marker follows them; when it is a place, the marker sits in the middle of that place.
- Both can be turned off in [[选项|Options]]: “Quest tracker” and “Quest markers (map and screen edge).”

## Lumi

Press {{键|E}} on [[露米]], and she tells you the tracked quest: “Now: quest name.” Then come the unfinished objectives and their progress, plus this stage's hint, such as how to do it and where to look. When no quest is under way, she says “Nothing presses now. Go where you like!” In the [[新手教程|tutorial]], what she says is the hint for each stage of the main quest [[醒来]].

## Tips

- When you reach a village, first look for people with a “!” over their heads. [[初原村]] has several side quests, and there are often commissions on the commission board.
- Hand-ins usually take away the things for “Bring” objectives. Think it over before handing in something important you have only one of.
- Commissions have deadlines. After taking one, check its description in the journal, and don't set off only after the deadline has passed.
- If you want to know where to find an objective, track that quest: the tracker says which map to go to, and the maps carry markers.
- [[给火堆添柴]] can be done every day, a steady source of experience and bread.
- For each quest's detailed walkthrough see its quest page and [[主线流程]].

## History

- 2026-10-03: The quest system was added: Firstlight Village's commissions, the quest journal ({{键|L}}), the “!” and “?” over heads, daily repeating quests.
- 2026-10-06: Chapter 1's main and side quests were added; main quests are taken up automatically and complete by themselves, and quests the story passes over close automatically.
- 2026-10-07: Chapter 1's main quest runs through to the White Knight, and quest rewards include named gear.
- 2026-10-08: The quests for expanding the Held Ground reward Dawnmarks. Quests passed over by the story whose objectives were all achieved (such as Down from the Mountain and The First Seal) give their rewards as usual.
- 2026-10-09: The quest system was rebuilt to be driven by events: side quests and commissions are accepted or declined in the dialogue box; quests are divided into stages, with the journal recording each stage; the quest journal has three pages, Under way, Done and Failed, and one quest can be tracked; the tracker in the top right and quest markers on the minimap, the world map and the screen's edge were added; what happens in the village gives rise to commissions ([[一口吃的]], [[失物]], [[捎封信]], [[清剿夜行者]]), and Firstlight Village put up a [[委托板|commission board]]; commissions have deadlines and fail when they expire; people a quest names are protected, and merchants don't take quest items; the “repeatable” kind became “commission,” and [[给火堆添柴]] was filed under commissions; the stages of the [[新手教程|tutorial]] became the main quest [[醒来]]; the old main line from Emberfall to the dragon became the main quest [[屠龙之路]].
