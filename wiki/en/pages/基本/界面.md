This page describes the interface elements on the game screen. The interface follows the practice of a few games: the clock and the buttons at the lower right take after Stardew Valley; the hearts, drumsticks, experience bar and item tooltips take after Minecraft. The interface text follows the language set in [[选项|Options]].

## Layout

| Position | Contents |
|---|---|
| Top left | The [[世界地图#小地图|minimap]], with the zone written below it |
| Top right | The clock: date, dial, weather, time; the quest tracker below it |
| Top center | The boss health bar (when near a boss); the place name that fades in when you enter a map |
| Center | The notice shown while the world is learning |
| Bottom left | The skill bar (against the bottom edge of the screen), with messages above it |
| Bottom center | Hearts, drumsticks, the experience bar, the toolbar, the off hand |
| Bottom right | Eight buttons |
| Above center, over the character's head | The action prompt |

When the character walks near the bottom edge of the screen, the toolbar group moves to the top of the screen.

## Clock

The wooden frame in the top right corner. The first line is the date (“Day N”); in the middle is the dial, whose hand sweeps from 06:00 (left) to 02:00 (right), with the weather icon beside it (☀ ☾ ☁ ☂ ⚡ ≋); hover over the icon to see the weather's name. At the bottom is the time, which ticks every 10 game minutes. See [[游戏内时间]], [[天气]].

In clear weather the weather icon is the moon ☾ from 2 game hours before nightfall until 1 game hour after dawn, and the sun ☀ the rest of the time. At first that is 19:00–06:00; as the story moves on and the nights grow longer, the moon comes out earlier with them (see [[昼夜]]).

## Hearts, drumsticks and the experience bar

Above the toolbar, the ten hearts on the left are [[生命值|health]] and the ten drumsticks on the right are [[饱食度|satiety]]. The drumsticks shake when satiety is 0. Below them is a thin, long experience bar showing your current level. Hover over any of these to see the exact numbers. See [[等级与经验]].

## Toolbar and off hand

Ten slots, labeled 1–0 in the top left corner; the selected slot has a frame. Each slot shows the item's icon and count. The single slot on the right is the off hand: empty, it shows a gray shield outline, and it is grayed out when it holds another profession's shield. Click a slot to select it. See [[背包]], [[装备]].

## Skill bar

In the very bottom left corner of the screen is a row of six square skill slots, with a dark background and beveled frames, each labeled ⇧1–⇧6 in its corner. It is not shown until you have learned an active skill, and appears once you learn the first. A slot holding an active skill shows the skill's icon; an empty slot is dark.

- Press {{键|Shift}}+{{键|1}}–{{键|6}} or click a slot to cast the skill in it.
- While a skill is cooling down, a dark sweep passes over the slot like a clock face, with the seconds remaining written in the middle (with one decimal place under 10 seconds); when the cooldown ends, the slot flashes.
- A skill lent by a piece of equipment stays in its slot after you take the equipment off, but is grayed out and cannot be cast.
- Hover over a slot to see the skill's name, its cooldown in seconds, its description and “Right click: take it out”; hover over an empty slot for a hint on how to put a skill in.
- Drag slots to swap them; right-click to clear one. Full management is in the skill panel ({{键|U}}).

See [[技能#主动技能与技能栏|Active skills and the skill bar]].

## Panel tabs

When the inventory, crafting, the skill tree, the skill panel, quests, the codex or the world map is open, a row of tabs runs across the top center of the screen; the one that is open is highlighted, and each tab shows its key. Click a tab to close the current panel and open that one. In a game without the classes module, the skill tree and skill panel tabs are not shown.

## Minimap and zone

At the top left, showing the current map and the people, animals, monsters and waypoint statues on it. A strip below it reads “Peaceful zone” (on green) or “Combat zone · monsters at night” (on red). Click the minimap to open the world map. See [[世界地图#小地图|Minimap]], [[和平区域与战斗区域]].

## Quest tracker and markers

The small translucent box under the clock is the **quest tracker**: its first line is the name of the [[任务|quest]] being tracked (in yellow), and below it are its unfinished objectives, up to four, with their progress (such as “Log 2/4”); an objective on another map is followed by “→ map name”. You choose which quest to track in the quest journal ({{键|L}}); if you have chosen none, it is the main quest begun most recently.

The people or places that the unfinished objectives of the tracked quest point to are drawn here:

- a yellow diamond on the minimap;
- a yellow ★ on the [[世界地图|world map]];
- one on the current map but off screen gets an arrow at the edge of the screen pointing its way; once on screen, it becomes a diamond floating above it.

The tracker and the markers can both be turned off in [[选项|Options]]. To find out what to do, you can also press {{键|E}} on [[露米]]: she says “Now: …” followed by the objective, its progress and how to do it. See [[任务#追踪与标记|Tracking and markers]].

## Messages

Little paper slips in the bottom left corner of the screen, up to 6 at a time, fading after 5 seconds.

| Border color | Contents |
|---|---|
| Green | Good news: picking something up (“+1 Berries”; picking up the same kind in a row adds up), leveling up, accepting and completing quests, a new thread woven in |
| Orange | Anything to do with [[世界会学|the world learning]]: “✦ The world pauses…”, “✦ The world is making something new: …” |
| Red | Warnings; a quest failed |
| None | Plain narration: “You eat the Bread.”, “The tree falls!” and so on |

When you pick something up, the item's icon also flies in an arc from where it lay into the character.

## Place names

When you walk into a map, the map's name fades in at the top center of the screen, stays a moment, and fades out.

## Action prompt

The tile the mouse points at (or the one you face) is framed by four small corner brackets, and the frame slides to the next tile. The cursor shows what {{键|E}} will do: over something that can be picked up, opened or read it is a hand, and over a person a speech bubble; it is also a hand where {{键|E}} has nothing to do but a wall, bed or similar thing in your hand can be built there with the right click. When the world has never seen the right click's use there, it carries a “?”; when {{键|E}} would take someone else's things (stealing), it carries a red “!”. Above the frame is a small prompt box, one line per input (with the mouse: the left click and the right click, plus {{键|E}} and {{键|G}}), saying what pressing it will do; it appears only once the character stops and the frame has settled a moment, and it is hidden while you walk, so it does not jump about with every step. An action the world has not yet defined is shown faded, followed by an orange “?”. It is not shown during dialogue, questions and cutscenes. See [[操作#动作提示与「?」|Action prompts and the “?”]].

The “On which?” list that a long press of {{键|E}} or {{键|G}}, or Alt+click, brings up is also shown below that tile, with a number on each line. See [[操作#选择叠着的东西|Long press: choosing a target]].

| Kind | Weapon, tool, food, potion, material, seed, armour, clothing, light, container, valuable, reading |

| When in the off hand | Defense, block rate |
| When worn somewhere | Defense, attack, experience bonus; negative numbers (costs) are written in red |
| Traits | Purple: dodge, movement speed, bonuses after dark and so on |
| Vow | Purple: a Vowbound piece's “✦ vow name · Vow” and a line describing its effect |
| Stigma power | “✦ power name · Unique” and its description |
| Profession | “X only” (orange for your own profession, otherwise red), or “Common weapon: no profession talents” |
| Food | A row of small drumsticks and “+N satiety”; one that heals says “+N Health” |

## Things overhead

- **Speech bubbles**: when someone farther from you speaks, the words appear in a bubble over their head and vanish after a few seconds.
- **Mood symbols**: ♥ happy, … sad, !! angry, ! surprised, ♡ shy.
- **Quest markers**: a yellow “!” (someone has a quest to entrust to you) and “?” (talking to them now moves your quest forward: handing something in, or a quest has sent you to find them), bobbing up and down. See [[任务#头顶的标记|Markers overhead]].
- **The profession master's marker**: the [[职业|profession]] master ([[布拉姆]] in Firstlight Village) has a sword-shaped icon bobbing over his head, so he is easy to find. When he has a quest marker, the quest marker is shown first.
- **Health bars**: things that are hurt and creatures hostile to you have a thin health bar overhead: red for those who will attack you, green for people and Lumi, amber for everything else. See [[生命值#别人的生命条|Other health bars]].
- **Floating numbers**: taking damage floats up a “-N”, colored by element; healing floats up a green “+N”.
- **The world is learning**: while the world learns the outcome of an action, the one doing it has a spinning ✦ and three dots overhead, and the thing acted on flickers translucent with a “…” overhead.

Quest markers, the profession master's marker and health bars are drawn above everything else in the world: when someone stands behind a tree or a house, these markers can still be seen.

## Dialogue box

When someone within 2 tiles of you speaks, the dialogue box appears at the bottom of the screen: a portrait on the left, the name above, the text typed out letter by letter, and a ▼ at the lower right meaning there is more. Press {{键|E}} or {{键|Esc}}, or click: the first press shows the whole text, the second turns the page. An ordinary single line can be dismissed at once with {{键|G}}. Dialogue in a cutscene has to be paged through line by line. See [[操作#对话、提问与剧情|Dialogue, questions and the story]].

## Yes/no questions

Before you eat or drink something, the game asks a question such as “Eat X?”. Two buttons, “Yes” and “No”, appear in the dialogue box. See [[操作#对话、提问与剧情|Dialogue, questions and the story]].

## Story cards and fades to black

During a cutscene the screen may go entirely dark and then light up again (a fade to black), or a full-screen card of text may appear, such as the one at the end of Chapter 1. Press {{键|E}} or click to continue.

## The world is learning

When you have done something the world has never seen and the world is learning its outcome, the center of the screen shows: “✦ The world is learning this… N seconds (20 at most) · Esc to stop waiting”. See [[世界会学]].

## Boss health bar

Within 12 tiles of a [[首领|boss]], the boss health bar appears at the top center of the screen:

- at the top, a small crown and the word “B O S S”, with the boss's name below;
- the current phase, “I / ARRIVED” or “II / AWAKENED”, and the exact health numbers;
- a wide health bar: when the boss is hurt, a bright segment drops first and a dark segment then slowly falls after it; a notch on the bar marks the health line at which the second phase begins;
- the bottom line is the move bar: while the boss winds up, it shows the move's name and “WIND-UP / EVADE”; otherwise it reads “Watch the wind-up · Strike during recovery”.

The boss health bar is hidden during cutscenes.

## The tutorial's overlay

In the [[新手教程|tutorial]] the screen is dimmed, leaving only the thing to be used lit; the key to press is drawn above it, with “Lesson N of M” beside it; pressing a locked key makes the key icon shake; when a lesson is complete, a green ✓ pops up in the center of the screen.

## Stall panel

Opened by pressing {{键|E}} on the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], titled “🏪 Stall”. Along the top are four tabs (Farming, Ranching, Fishing, Sell), with the number of [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] you carry on the right; below is one row per thing, with its icon on the left, what you give and how much in the middle (shown in red when you do not have enough), and a “Buy ×N” or “Sell → N” button on the right, plus an “All ×times” button when it can be done several times over. More than one tile away from the stall, the panel reads “Stand beside the trading stall to trade.”. See [[金钱]].
