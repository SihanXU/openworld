As in *The Elder Scrolls*, the people of OpenWorld are of two kinds:

- **Those the story cannot do without** (people named in quests, cutscenes and story rules, and the profession trainer [[Bram]]) don't die when their health drops to zero; they only fall {{status|down}} for half an hour and then wake with half their health. Someone who is down is not attacked again.
- **People a quest names to protect**: while a [[Quests|quest]] is under way, the people it names have the same protection, until the quest ends. For example, the person who gives a side quest and the person you are sent to find, or the guard entrusted to you in [[The Night-born]].
- **Everyone else can die.** At present that is [[Cass]], [[Tam]] and [[Vigo]]. Ordinary villagers added later can die by default too.

When the player's health reaches zero you fall down and the death screen appears; see [[Health]] and [[Difficulty]].

## After death
When someone dies:

1. Whoever struck them down gains experience (as much as their maximum health).
2. They become a **body** lying where they fell. Everything they carried or held in their hands is inside the body.
3. The body belongs to their household (to themselves, if they had no home): taking things from it is taking what belongs to others, and if seen it counts as [[Crime|theft]].
4. Two days later the body turns to **bones**, with the things still inside.
5. Their schedule, their lines and their following all end with them. They never come back.
6. Commissions that can't do without them fail: if the hungry person in [[A Bite to Eat]] or the recipient of the letter in [[A Letter to Carry]] dies, the quest fails; commissions they had offered and no one had taken are withdrawn too. See [[Quests#Failure|Failure]].

Press {{key|E}} on a body or bones (the prompt reads “Search…”) to open it and take things as from a [[Chests and Containers|chest]].

## Killing
- Being seen striking someone dead is **murder**: a fine of 1000, which the [[Guards and Bounties|watch]] won't pardon, and you can neither talk your way out nor bribe. The victim's household's opinion of you drops to the lowest.
- Whenever someone dies (whoever did it, whether or not anyone saw), the village talks about it for four days. People like [[Cass]] will bring it up: “Someone died, and nobody here will say how.”
- A left click on a friendly person first asks “Attack so-and-so?”, and strikes only on “Yes”; with your weapon drawn ({{key|X}}) it does not ask. The right click uses what is in your hand on them, and asks first too: right-clicking a person while holding a weapon is doing something to them with it, answered by [[The World Learns|the world that learns]]; empty-handed, the right click does nothing (see [[Controls#Friendly people are asked about first|Friendly people are asked about first]]). Wounding someone is assault.

## History
- 2026-10-09: Ordinary villagers can now die; bodies and bones were added. Before this, everyone whose health reached zero only fell down.
- 2026-10-09: While a quest is under way, the people it names only fall down; commissions that can't do without someone fail after that person dies.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
