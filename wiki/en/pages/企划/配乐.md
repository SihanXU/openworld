The game's music and sound effects come from the asset pack Ninja Adventure (CC0). Music plays by map, and boss fights switch to boss music. In the [[选项|Options]], sound only has an on/off switch, with no volume; music is fixed at 35% volume.

## Music by place

{{配乐表}}

Every map has its own track: the wilds and [[五印之地]] play Field; [[林间空地|The Clearing]] and [[森林|The Forest]] play Forest; [[初原村]], [[水车磨坊|Millbrook]], [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] and [[余烬镇|Emberfall]] play Village; barrows, mines and cellars play Cave; [[魔龙巢穴|The Dragon's Lair]] plays Battle; the [[初晓礼拜堂|Chapel of the First Dawn]] plays Temple. A new game starts with Field at [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]].

## Tracks

The tracks the asset pack includes and the game uses: Village, Village (Night), Field, Forest, Interior, Cave, Battle, Boss Battle, Shop, Temple, Dungeon, Dark Forest, Title. Those the maps actually use at present are Village, Field, Forest, Cave, Battle, Boss Battle and Temple.

## History

- 2026-10-08: every map's music name now matches a track: a new game is no longer silent, the Clearing and the Forest play the forest track, and the villages play the village track.

## Sound effects

Footsteps, pick up, put down, hit, hurt, attack, cast, fire, water, ice, thunder, wind, explosion, door opening, eating, healing, coins, undefined (doing something the world has never seen), learned (the world weaves in a new thread), death.
