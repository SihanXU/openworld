This page lists the game's known issues, for players' reference and for development to follow up. When an issue is fixed, it is removed from here and noted in the “History” of the page concerned.

On 2026-10-08 most of the issues found while writing the wiki were fixed, including the controls guide, background music, longer nights, gold ore, quest rewards, fire and cooking, explosives, and the Held Ground's animals and trading stall. Each change is recorded in the “History” of the related page; the merge record is at [[Version History]].

## Things you cannot obtain

| Thing | Effect |
|---|---|
| {{item|Key}} | The rules for locking chests exist, but the world has no keys and no locked chests |
| {{item|Quill}}, {{item|Ink Pot}} | Inscribing signposts cannot be used |

- The weights in the item catalogue for “where each thing should appear” are not used by any code, so roughly half of the items cannot be obtained in normal play (each item page says so at the top), and the [[Codex|Codex]] cannot be completed.
- The second [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arm]] of each profession ([[Mirrorshard]], [[The Unfallen Wall]], [[Starfall]], [[Rimecrown]]) cannot be obtained at present: there is only one Seal-bearer, the White Knight, and he drops only the first.

## Lumi collapsed on another map

When [[Lumi]] collapses, she stays where she fell. If you walk to another map in the meantime, her collapse timer stops too; only when you return to the map where she fell does she wake and follow you again.

## Monsters that never appear

The [[Slime King|Slime King]], [[giant crab|giant crab]], [[ice golem|ice golem]], [[storm wisp|storm wisp]], [[water slime|water slime]] and [[spark slime|spark slime]] have entries in the [[Codex|Codex]] but never appear in the game, so the Codex cannot be completed.

## No signposts to the world map

[[The Lands of the Five Seals]] (the world map) is built, but nothing in the game tells you how to get onto it: only a few edges, across the river from [[The Ford|the Ford]] and beside the stone steps at [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]], lead straight out; elsewhere you must first cut down trees or break through rock. See [[World Map]].

## Designs not yet settled

These are not bugs, but they differ from what players expect and wait for the design to be settled:

- Once you have crossed the river, [[Hesper|Hesper]] will not ferry you back: the ferry is only a fallback for when you cannot cross.
- Things put into someone else's chest count as the chest owner's. So if you put your own things into [[Hesper|Hesper]]'s chest and take them back in front of her, she says you stole.
- The [[cooking rack|cooking rack]] is only a counter; you cannot cook on it.
- Grass tufts, wildflowers, pebbles and moss can neither be picked up nor broken; only fire clears the ones that burn.
- An ordinary [[Shield|shield]] only adds defense; only [[The Unfallen Wall|The Unfallen Wall]] can block.
- A creature tied up with rope or cut grass carries the “frozen” status: it is drawn under a shell of ice, and when hit with a blunt weapon it shatters like ice (see [[Monsters#Handing things to creatures|Handing things to creatures]]).
- The rope or cut grass used to tie a creature stays on it and drops out again when you fell it.
- The rules say a frozen person thaws by warming up at a fire, but while frozen you cannot press {{key|E}}, so the rule never applies (see [[campfire#Warming up|Warming up]]).
