## Perspective

Top-down 3/4 view, 16 pixels to a cell. One storey of height equals one cell of depth: things on the second storey are drawn one cell higher (see [[Storeys|Storeys]]).

## Asset pack

The ground, buildings, most people, monsters and item icons come from **Ninja Adventure** (by Pixel-boy and AAA, CC0 license).

## Art direction

On 2026-10-02 the team discussed switching to anime-style, higher-quality assets: no existing asset pack met all three of “anime style, high quality, releasable with open-source code”, so Ninja Adventure was kept at that time.

From 2026-10-09, people are drawn in anime style, with assets generated by the project itself ([[Looks|Looks]]):
- Portrait parts are painted one by one by the open-source model Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Apache-2.0), running locally and editing the same base figure; a program then splits them into layers and turns them into recolorable shading maps. The generation pipeline and the prompts are in the repository, and the output is released with the code.
- People on the map became 16×32 anime-style chibi pixel figures, assembled from pixel parts in code, one cell taller than Ninja Adventure's 16×16 people. Monsters and terrain still use Ninja Adventure.
- From 2026-10-17, the figures became 24×36 in FFBE style (the style and palette set after the Cloud pixel art the user provided): a local model draws the draft, which is set on the pixel grid and touched up by hand; walking swings the arms in turn, and standing has a frame of its own.
- The portrait, the map figure and the appearance tags come from the same look record, so they always agree.
