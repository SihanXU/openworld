Crafting turns the materials you carry into new things. Press {{键|C}} to open the crafting panel. The panel lists every recipe in the game; nothing has to be unlocked, and when you have the materials you can make it. It is not called “combining”.

## Where recipes come from

Everything that can be made says two things: what it is made of, and where it is made. The recipe comes from these two; there is no separate recipe table:

- **Made of**: a stone axe is 1 stone, 1 stick and 1 cut grass; a wall is 2 planks.
- **Where it is made**: “by hand” can be made anywhere, any time; “workbench” needs you to stand beside a {{物品|工作台}}.

A new thing the world learns (see [[世界会学|the world learns]]) can be made in the panel from the moment it appears, as long as it says these two things as well.

## Using the panel

Each row is a recipe:
- On the left is what it makes; one marked “at a workbench” after its name needs you to stand beside a workbench (one within the ring of cells around you); when you are too far, the label turns red.
- In the middle are the materials, written “on you / needed”; any you are short of turn red.
- A tool you need to carry is drawn as its icon, with ✓ if you have it and ✗ if not. Hovering shows “A tool you need to carry; it is not used up.”
- On the right is the “Craft” button. A recipe that makes more than one says “Craft ×2”.
- When you can make several, an “All ×N” button appears: it keeps crafting from what you carry until it can make no more. Shift-clicking “Craft” does the same.

Rules:
- Only what is in your bag and in your hands counts. Materials in a chest beside you do not.
- Tools are not used up. The {{物品|木板}} recipe needs an axe on you, and the axe is not used up.
- What you make goes into your bag, and “+1 name” floats up on the screen.

## Material and quality

What you make:
- **Material** follows the first ingredient. A tool that starts with stone is of stone (stone axe, stone pickaxe); one that starts with a log, plank or stick is of wood (wooden bow, wooden shield, wooden bed). The material sets the multiplier of a weapon's power and armor's defense; see [[材质|Substances]].
- **Quality** depends on how far the maker's level is above the recipe's level:

| Level − recipe level | Quality | Power, defense multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Below 0 | Crude | ×0.8 |
| 0 to 2 | Common | ×1 |
| 3 to 5 | Fine | ×1.25 |
| 6 and above | Masterwork | ×1.5 |

Every recipe is level 1 except the {{物品|弓}} (recipe level 2). So a bow made at level 1 is a “Crude Wooden Bow”, from level 2 it is common, from level 4 tools are Fine, and from level 7 Masterwork. The two qualities Vowbound and Stigmatic cannot be made, only found (see [[品质|Quality]]).

For example: a stone axe has power 5; a Fine one made at level 4 has 6, a Masterwork one made at level 7 has 8.

## Structures and furniture

{{物品|墙}}, {{物品|地板}}, {{物品|门}}, {{物品|楼梯}}, a wooden bed and a wooden workbench can be made in the crafting panel first and carried, then held and right-clicked to build them (putting one down with {{键|G}} only leaves it loose on the ground); or they can be placed directly in [[建造|Building]] mode, which makes one from materials on the spot when you carry none. Campfires, chests, fences and signs are not in the crafting panel; they are built in Building mode.

## Profession gear

Anyone can make a {{物品|盾牌}} or a {{物品|弓}}, but only a [[战士|Warrior]] can wear a shield, and only a [[游侠|Ranger]] can fight with a bow (see [[职业|Professions]]).

## What you get back when it breaks

When something you made is broken, only half of what it was made of comes back (see [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]). Things that can be picked up, such as walls, floors and beds: knock one with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; then press {{键|E}} to pick it up, and you get the whole piece back with nothing lost.

## What there is not

- No cooking recipes: food can only be cooked over a fire; see [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- No smelting or smithing recipes. Smelting is not in the crafting panel: hold iron ore or gold ore and right-click a lit {{物品|熔炉}}, and each ore melts into one {{物品|铁锭}} or {{物品|金锭}} (see [[熔炉#熔炼|Smelting]]). No recipe uses ingots yet, and the [[技能#锻造|Smithing]] branch has no skills yet.
- No recipes for workstations other than the workbench. [[余烬镇]] has an {{物品|铁砧}}, a {{物品|熔炉}} and a {{物品|坩埚}} standing there, but the crafting panel has nothing to be made beside them (the forge smelts ore, and a lit forge or cauldron can cook things; see [[烹饪|Cooking]]).
- The [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]] also counts as a “workstation” in the game: buying and selling are worked out the way a recipe is (what you give is used up, and what you get goes into your bag). But these trades are not in the crafting panel; they are in the stall's own panel; see [[金钱|Money]].

## Tips

- The third lesson of the tutorial is making a stone axe: press {{键|C}}; 1 stick, 1 stone, 1 cut grass.
- You need not put logs down to split them: carry an axe and click “All” on the plank row in the panel.
- [[初原村]] has a workbench ready to use.

- 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
