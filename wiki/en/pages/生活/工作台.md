The workbench is a wooden bench. Three things can only be [[制作|crafted]] standing beside a workbench: the {{物品|棍棒}}, the {{物品|盾牌}} and the {{物品|弓}}. The workbench itself can be made anywhere from 4 {{物品|木板}}, and what comes out is called a “wooden workbench”. Knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself) and you can carry it with you. Hold it and right-click a cell and it stands there, ready to use (putting it down with {{键|G}} only leaves it loose on the ground; it does not stand up).

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make one yourself**: press {{键|C}}; 4 planks, no tool needed, anywhere.
- **Place one in Building mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Wooden workbench”. If you carry one, it is put down directly; if not, one is first made from 4 planks you carry and then put down (see [[建造|Building]]).
- **Ready-made**: [[初原村]] has one, standing on the west side of the village.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Making things beside it

Standing beside a workbench, that is, with a workbench within the ring of cells around you (8 cells), you can make the recipes marked “at a workbench” in the crafting panel. When you are too far away, the “Craft” button is gray, and hovering over it says “Stand beside a workbench”.

| Recipe | Materials | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden club | 1 {{物品|木头}} | A blunt weapon anyone can use |
| Wooden shield | 3 planks, 1 {{物品|树枝}} | Only a [[战士|Warrior]] can wear it |
| Wooden bow | 2 sticks, 3 {{物品|干草}} | Recipe level 2; only a [[游侠|Ranger]] can fight with it |

### Other uses

- **Bench top**: hold something and press {{键|G}} at it, and the thing is put on the bench top.
- **Room use**: a [[房间|room]] with a workbench inside counts as a “workshop”. For now this use has no effect.
- **It can be taken away**: knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and the whole bench comes loose where it stood; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself). It is not damaged.

### It cannot be broken, but it burns

A workbench has no durability, so hitting it with a weapon takes none off (an axe, pickaxe or hammer knocks the whole bench loose; see above). Whatever you do to it with something that the rules do not define is [[世界会学|something the world has not seen]]. It is made of wood and catches fire: burnt out, it becomes {{物品|灰烬}}, and what was on the bench top falls where it stood.
