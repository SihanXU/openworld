Cooking is turning raw food into cooked. The game has no cooking panel and no recipes that combine several ingredients into a dish: raw food that touches fire becomes the matching cooked food. Cooked food restores more [[饱食度|Satiety]] than raw.

## How to cook

The usual way is a lit {{物品|营火}}:

- **Hold raw food and right-click a lit fire**: “Roast … on the campfire”, “It sizzles. Done.” The one in your hand becomes cooked food.
- **Hold raw food and press {{键|G}} at a lit fire**: the same effect; the cooked food stays on you and does not drop into the fire.
- **Any fire that touches raw food cooks it**: for instance a fire burning on the ground, or a weapon or staff with the fire element striking it (see [[元素|Elements]]).

One at a time. If you have several stacked in your bag, they are cooked one by one.

The fires you can cook on (cooking fires) must be burning:

| Cooking fire | Where |
|---|---|
| {{物品|营火}} | Build one yourself ([[建造|Building]]), or use the ready-made campfires at the camp at [[渡口]], in [[初原村]] and on [[烬岭山道]] |
| {{物品|火盆}} | The lit braziers in [[古墓]], [[荆棘骸骨堂]], [[守墓者圣所]], [[旧矿坑]] and [[魔龙巢穴]] |
| {{物品|熔炉}} | At the smith's in [[余烬镇]] |
| {{物品|坩埚}} | At the herbalist's in [[余烬镇]] |

In the rules a fireplace can cook too, but no map has a fireplace yet. A campfire is unlit when built and has to be lit with a torch; see [[营火|Campfire]]; a cauldron that has gone out can be lit again too. Other lit things ({{物品|路灯}}, {{物品|烛台}}, {{物品|灯笼柱}}) are not cooking fires: right-clicking one with raw food or pressing {{键|G}} at it does not cook anything.

- Raw meat and eggs come from [[动物|Animals]]; mushrooms are picked up in the deep woodland of [[荒野|the Wild]].
- Fish are caught by [[钓鱼|Fishing]]. All eight kinds of fish cook into the same Grilled Fish.
- Vegetables, bread, berries, milk and the like cannot be cooked; eat them as they are (milk you drink).
- Eating raw food does no harm; it just does not fill you up as much.
- Eating restores only satiety, not health directly. Health comes back slowly from being well fed (satiety at 90% or more); see [[饱食度|Satiety]] and [[生命值|Health]].

The numbers for every food are in [[食物|Food]].

## Do not drop the wrong thing

What you put down with {{键|G}} at a lit fire:
- Raw food: on a cooking fire (campfire, brazier, fireplace, forge, cauldron) it is cooked and stays on you; on any other fire, it goes by whether it burns, as below.
- Things that burn (logs, sticks, cut grass, bread…): thrown into the fire and burnt to {{物品|灰烬}}.
- Other things (stones, tools…): put on the fire's cell, and they do not burn.

## Fishing
{{主条目|钓鱼}}

Hold a {{物品|鱼竿}} and left-click water to cast; when something bites, you land a fish. A fishing rod is made from just 2 sticks and 2 cut grass, or bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]]; with {{物品|鱼饵}} on it, the chance of a bite doubles. What fish you catch depends on the hour, the weather and the depth of the water.

## What there is not

- A cooking panel, recipes, dishes made from several ingredients.
- Stewpots, seasonings. The {{物品|锅}} is an item in the game, but it cannot be used for cooking.
- Food spoiling.

## Tips

- Steak restores 8 satiety, two fifths of the satiety maximum of 20. Fell a cow and grill two steaks, and that keeps you going most of the day.
- Grilling meat by a campfire at night is fairly safe: apart from fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 cells of a lit fire.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The game's first version could already grill fish on a campfire.
- 2026-10-02: Moved onto the new event model: holding raw food and clicking a lit fire, putting raw food into a fire, and any fire touching raw food all cook it.
- 2026-10-08: Fishing became a pastime, with seven more kinds of fish to cook, all cooking into Grilled Fish.
- 2026-10-08: A brazier counts as a cooking fire too; a cauldron that has gone out can be lit again; raw food is cooked only on a cooking fire.
- 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
