The game's animals are the {{怪物|牛}} and the {{怪物|鸡}}, with their young, the {{怪物|小牛}} and the {{怪物|小鸡}}. They wander about on the grass and never attack anyone; when hit, they run.

- Feed them {{物品|饲料}} or {{物品|麦子}} and they are **fed**.
- A fed cow can be milked with an empty {{物品|木桶}} for a {{物品|一桶牛奶}}.
- Two fed animals of the same kind side by side bring forth a young one; the young one grows up after a while.
- A chicken lays an {{物品|鸡蛋}} every {{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours, fed or not.
- The [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]] sells feed, buckets, and hens and calves in crates, and buys eggs and milk.

There is no shearing, no barns, no penning with fences and no taming; there are no Animal Husbandry skills either.

## Behavior

- Animals belong to the “wild” faction, but they are mild-tempered: they never attack anyone of their own accord (see [[阵营与脾性|Factions and Temper]]). The same goes for young born to them and animals let out of crates.
- An animal remembers whoever hits it, and from then on runs from that person on sight.
- Normally it ambles about near its own patch, stopping and starting, and comes back if it strays too far.
- It fears fire: it will not walk within 2 cells of a lit fire.
- Press {{键|E}} at one with empty hands and no rule says what happens (“Interact with cow ?”). It does not bite an outstretched hand the way wild monsters do (see [[怪物#空手伸手|Reaching out bare-handed]]).
- Right-click one with {{物品|干草}} or {{物品|绳索}} and it is tied up for 3 seconds; the cut grass or rope stays on it and drops out only when it is felled. Do not feed animals cut grass; use feed.

The chicken on the open plains of [[荒野|the Wild]] is a little different: when hit, it turns and pecks whoever hit it, but chickens have no attack, so the pecks do no damage.

## Feeding

{{物品|饲料}} and {{物品|麦子}} are both what animals eat.

1. Hold the feed (or wheat).
2. Right-click an animal (“Give the cow feed”), or press {{键|G}} at it.
3. One feed is used up, the prompt says “The cow eats happily.”, a heart rises over the animal's head, and it gets {{状态|吃饱了}} for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours.

- Cows, chickens, calves and chicks can all be fed. Other creatures (wolves, boars…) do not eat feed: right-clicking them with feed is not defined in the rules (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]).
- Right-click an animal with ordinary food (a carrot, bread…) and it gulps it down (“The cow gulps down the carrot.”), but this is not feeding: it does not become {{状态|吃饱了}}, gives no milk, and does not breed because of it; what it swallowed is simply gone. Only things with the nature of “feed” (feed and wheat) count as feeding. See [[怪物#递东西给生物|Giving things to creatures]].
- Feed is bought on the “Ranching” page of the stall on the Held Ground, 5 for 6 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]. Wheat you grow yourself; see [[农耕|Farming]].
- Being fed does not change how an animal behaves in itself; it matters for milking and breeding.

## Milking

1. First feed a {{怪物|牛}}.
2. Hold an empty {{物品|木桶}} and left-click it (“Milk the cow”).
3. “You milk the cow.” The bucket in your hand becomes a {{物品|一桶牛奶}}, and the cow gets {{状态|挤过奶}}.

- A cow that is not fed, or has already been milked, gives no milk; the prompt only says: “The cow won't give milk — feed it first, and it can only be milked once a day.”
- The milked status lasts {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours, during which the cow cannot be milked again. After that, feed it once more and it can be milked again.
- Calves cannot be milked.
- You can make a bucket yourself (3 planks, 1 cut grass; see [[制作|Crafting]]) or buy one at the stall (8 Dawnmarks).
- **Bucket of Milk**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself and you drink it (“Drink the Bucket of Milk?”), restoring 4 [[生命值|Health]] and 4 [[饱食度|Satiety]]; the bucket goes back to being empty, ready for more milking. You can also give it to someone else to drink. The stall buys one for 12 Dawnmarks, and then the bucket is sold with it.

## Laying eggs

- A chicken lays an egg at its own feet every **{{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours** (3 real minutes), wherever it is, fed or not.
- The egg lies quietly on the ground and does not fly into your bag by itself; press {{键|E}} to pick it up.
- Chickens lay only while someone is on that map; leave and come back, and the eggs due in the meantime are made up.
- Chicks do not lay; they start once they grow into chickens.
- The stall buys eggs for 4 Dawnmarks each.

## Breeding

Cows and chickens both breed, in the same way:

1. Feed one (a cow, say).
2. Within {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours, feed another of the same kind not far from it: the steps between the two, across and down added together, no more than 3 cells.
3. The one fed second calls to fed animals of its kind within that range; the one that hears gives birth to a young one at its own feet: “A calf is born!” or “A chick has hatched!”, with a heart and a flash of light.
4. Both get {{状态|刚生过崽}} for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. Feeding them again during that time only fills them; they will not breed again.

- Only animals of the same kind breed: cows bring forth calves, chickens chicks.
- One that has just bred neither calls nor answers.
- If the one fed second has several fed animals of its kind beside it that have not just bred, each of them gives birth to a young one.
- Young animals cannot breed.

## Young animals

| Young | Health | Grows into | When felled |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{怪物|小牛}} | 5 | {{怪物|牛}} | An even chance of leaving 1 {{物品|生牛肉}} |
| {{怪物|小鸡}} | 2 | {{怪物|鸡}} | An even chance of leaving 1 {{物品|羽毛}} |

- The young are smaller than their parents, drawn as smaller copies of their parents' pictures.
- After {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours, a young animal grows up by itself. A grown cow can be milked and can breed; a grown chicken starts laying and can breed.
- Until they grow up, calves cannot be milked, chicks do not lay, and neither breeds.
- As with eggs, the young do not grow while you are off the map; when you return, the time is made up.

## Animals in crates

The “Ranching” page of the [[货摊|trading stall]] sells animals packed in crates:

| Item | Price | What comes out |
|---|---|---|
| {{物品|装箱的母鸡}} | 40 Dawnmarks | A {{怪物|鸡}} |
| {{物品|装箱的小牛}} | 120 Dawnmarks | A {{怪物|小牛}} |

Hold the crate and press {{键|G}} at a cell of ground (“Let out the Crated Hen”): “The crate opens, and the animal inside walks out.” The animal appears in that cell, and the crate is gone.

A calf let out of a crate is just like one born: after {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours it grows into a cow.

## When felled

Like any other creature, an animal falls when its health reaches 0 and leaves half of what it is made of (see [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]). What it leaves lies quietly where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick it up.

| Animal | Health | Leaves | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cow | 10 | 2 {{物品|生牛肉}} | 10 |
| Chicken | 4 | 1 {{物品|生鸡肉}} | 4 |
| Calf | 5 | An even chance of 1 {{物品|生牛肉}} | 5 |
| Chick | 2 | An even chance of 1 {{物品|羽毛}} | 2 |

Raw meat can be eaten as it is or cooked: Raw Beef cooks into {{物品|牛排}}, Raw Chicken into {{物品|烤鸡肉}}, and an egg boils into a {{物品|熟鸡蛋}} (see [[烹饪|Cooking]]).

## What there is not

- **Shearing, barns, penning with fences, taming**: none of these. Animals walk about near their own patch and do not follow people.
- **Animals' satiety**: “fed” is only a timed status; animals have no satiety, and unfed they do not starve.
- **Animal Husbandry skills**: the [[技能#牧业|Animal Husbandry]] branch has no skills yet (the skill tree says “Skills to be designed”).

## Tips

- If you want eggs rather than meat, do not hit the chickens; wait beside them.
- The Held Ground starts with only one cow, which cannot bring forth a calf. Buy a crated calf and raise it, and you have a pair; after that, feed both cows whenever they wander close together (within 3 cells across and down) and they breed again.
- Milk a fed cow first and then let it breed; the two do not conflict.
- Cows on the Grassland come back slowly even if they are all felled, and chickens come back faster than cows.
