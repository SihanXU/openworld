**Held Ground residents** are people who move from elsewhere to live on [[誓守之地]]. When someone's hearts for you are full (all ten hearts of [[友谊|regard]] lit), you can ask them to move there; they get a bed of their own on the Held Ground and live there from then on.

## Who can be asked

When you talk to them ({{键|E}}), the dialogue options gain the line “Come and live in the Held Ground?”, when all of these hold:

- Their hearts for you are **full**: all ten hearts beside the name in the dialogue box are lit (see [[友谊|Friendship]]);
- You already have the Held Ground: [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] has fallen;
- They have not moved there already;
- They are not someone the main story cannot do without.

Those the main story cannot do without cannot be persuaded, and the line never appears in their dialogue: [[伊尔丝修女]], [[罗万]], [[玛拉]], [[加里克]], [[雷恩]], [[赫斯珀]], [[芬尼克]], [[艾达]], [[佩尔]], [[莉娜]], [[奥斯卡]], [[阿尔贝里克]], [[塞弗林|Severin]], [[凯乌斯司铎]], [[圣教信使]].

## Beds

| Beds | Where | How you get them |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beside the camp southwest of the sword | There from the start |
| 2 | In front of the Residents' Tent | Build the “Residents' Tent” in [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] (from land level I) |
| 2 | In front of the Second Residents' Tent | Build the “Second Residents' Tent” (from land level I) |
| 2 | In front of the Third Residents' Tent | Build the “Third Residents' Tent” (from land level II) |

So at most seven people can live there. The tents' materials and rewards are in [[领地扩建#建筑|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Once they agree

- **Yes**: when there is a free bed, they say “...Live there? With you? — Yes. Give me a moment to pack. I'll see you in the Held Ground.” and set off at once, arriving straight on the Held Ground.
- **No bed**: when every bed is taken, they say “I'd like that. But you've no bed for me there yet — ask Alberic to raise another tent.” and do not move.
- **Schedule**: once moved, they spend 7:00–21:00 every day near their own bed (whoever has the bed by the camp spends the day beside the sword), and sleep in their bed 21:00–7:00. Their old schedule is no longer used.
- **Home**: the Held Ground becomes their home, and the bed is their bed.
- **Trade as before**: what they used to sell, you can still buy from them on the Held Ground (see [[交易|Trading]]).
- People who have moved there never move away again.

## Tips

- To ask more people, first raise residents' tents in Expanding the Held Ground: tents need [[布匹|Bolts of Cloth]], and cloth is woven from cut grass at a [[织布机|loom]].
- Ask the people you often buy from to move over, and you need not run to the village.
- Regard slowly ebbs; whoever you want to ask, ask while their hearts are full; see [[友谊#会淡下去|It fades]].
