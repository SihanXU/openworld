**Held Ground residents** are people who move from elsewhere to live on [[The Held Ground]]. When someone's hearts for you are full (all ten hearts of [[Friendship|regard]] lit), you can ask them to move there; they get a bed of their own on the Held Ground and live there from then on.

## Who can be asked

When you talk to them ({{key|E}}), the dialogue options gain the line “Come and live in the Held Ground?”, when all of these hold:

- Their hearts for you are **full**: all ten hearts beside the name in the dialogue box are lit (see [[Friendship|Friendship]]);
- You already have the Held Ground: [[The White Knight Alberic]] has fallen;
- They have not moved there already;
- They are not someone the main story cannot do without.

Those the main story cannot do without cannot be persuaded, and the line never appears in their dialogue: [[Sister Ilse]], [[Rowan]], [[Mara]], [[Garrick]], [[Wren]], [[Hesper]], [[Fennick]], [[Edda]], [[Pell]], [[Lina]], [[Oskar]], [[Alberic]], [[The Grey Man|Severin]], [[Father Caius]], [[Church Courier]].

## Beds

| Beds | Where | How you get them |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beside the camp southwest of the sword | There from the start |
| 2 | In front of the Residents' Tent | Build the “Residents' Tent” in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] (from land level I) |
| 2 | In front of the Second Residents' Tent | Build the “Second Residents' Tent” (from land level I) |
| 2 | In front of the Third Residents' Tent | Build the “Third Residents' Tent” (from land level II) |

So at most seven people can live there. The tents' materials and rewards are in [[Expanding the Held Ground#Buildings|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Once they agree

- **Yes**: when there is a free bed, they say “...Live there? With you? — Yes. Give me a moment to pack. I'll see you in the Held Ground.” and set off at once, arriving straight on the Held Ground.
- **No bed**: when every bed is taken, they say “I'd like that. But you've no bed for me there yet — ask Alberic to raise another tent.” and do not move.
- **Schedule**: once moved, they spend 7:00–21:00 every day near their own bed (whoever has the bed by the camp spends the day beside the sword), and sleep in their bed 21:00–7:00. Their old schedule is no longer used.
- **Home**: the Held Ground becomes their home, and the bed is their bed.
- **Trade as before**: what they used to sell, you can still buy from them on the Held Ground (see [[Trading|Trading]]).
- People who have moved there never move away again.

## Resident work

On the Residents page of the steward's panel, assign a **hand harvester** to a resident aged 18 or older who lives at an open bed on the Held Ground and is not protected or committed to another duty. Each resident can have one job at a time.

- Choose a real available warehouse, then explicitly check the crop cells the resident may harvest. No cells are authorized by default. Crops planted later in an authorized cell are included; crops in other cells are left alone.
- Residents keep their usual day and night schedule and rest from 21:00 to 7:00. They work only while you are on the Held Ground and time is running. They pause with a status message when you leave the map, pause the job, a route is blocked, or the warehouse is unavailable. Close the steward's panel to let time advance.
- The job uses the existing hand-pick rule: one crop, plus the resident's own `pick` value, and one returned seed. It uses no sickle and does not sow, water, fertilize, milk or sell. Produce becomes physical task cargo carried by the resident before it is placed in the selected entity warehouse. The job does not read or write land-upgrade stock and does not use or move the resident's private belongings.
- Capacity includes both the produce and returned seed, accounts for other in-transit job cargo, and splits matching items into stacks of at most 999; the resident leaves the crop intact if all output will not fit. Task cargo in transit is saved with the game.
- If the warehouse disappears or the job is revoked while cargo is in transit, choose **Put task cargo down for recovery**. The resident leaves only tracked job cargo still in their custody at their feet, and the panel shows the map and coordinates. Go to that ground tile and pick the items up yourself. Private items are left alone, and cargo already transferred out of the resident's custody is never moved remotely.

You can also assign a **feeder**. Choose a real available warehouse and explicitly select owned livestock one animal at a time. None are selected by default, and newborns are not added automatically. A fed animal can remain selected; the resident fetches exactly one physical portion of feed only when that animal is hungry, then follows the usual feeding rule. Eggs and breeding keep their normal effects. The job never milks or sells anything, and the manual Stockman action keeps its usual nearby-herd behavior.

If an animal dies, leaves the Held Ground, or is fed by someone else while its feed is in transit, the resident returns that same feed to the selected warehouse. If the warehouse is locked or full, the resident keeps carrying the tracked item for later recovery. When you leave the map, the resident and cargo stay where they are and the job pauses. Unowned livestock cannot be assigned directly, and animals owned by someone else cannot be assigned.

Older saves may contain livestock with no recorded owner. While beside the steward, open the Residents page, choose one eligible animal, and confirm the claim. The animal's own event checks again that it is unowned, alive, on the Held Ground, unfrozen and eligible livestock. Ownership is not inferred from its former map or birthplace, and claiming an animal does not assign it to a feeder. You must select it for feeding yourself; other unowned animals remain unchanged.

## Tips

- To ask more people, first raise residents' tents in Expanding the Held Ground: tents need [[Bolt of Cloth|Bolts of Cloth]], and cloth is woven from cut grass at a [[loom|loom]].
- Ask the people you often buy from to move over, and you need not run to the village.
- Regard slowly ebbs; whoever you want to ask, ask while their hearts are full; see [[Friendship#It fades|It fades]].
