To sleep, stand on a {{物品|床}} and press {{键|E}}. Once you lie down, time speeds ahead to 6:00 in the morning, and you wake with some of your health restored. A bed you have slept in becomes your [[难度|rest point]]: on Normal difficulty, when you fall you get back up here. The game has no stamina (see [[能量|Energy]]), so sleep is not required; it is the way to pass the night, recover health and set your rest point.

## How to sleep

- You have to stand on a bed to reach it: walk onto the bed and press {{键|E}}; the prompt is “Sleep”.
- “You lie down.” Time immediately speeds ahead to 6:00 in the morning.
- You can sleep at any time: lie down at 22:00 and you sleep 8 game hours; lie down at 7 in the morning and you sleep until 6 the next day.
- While asleep you have the {{状态|睡着}} status and cannot move.

The game has no bed lying ready; you make your own: 2 planks and 2 cut grass (the “wooden bed” in [[制作|Crafting]]), or place one directly in [[建造|Building]] mode. A bed can be carried: knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself); when you get where you are going, hold it and right-click a cell, and it is set up for sleeping.

## When you wake

- **Health restored**: your missing health × the rest value, rounded up.
  - The bed is in a [[房间|bedroom]] (a bed inside a house closed in on every side and covered overhead): rest value 1, everything you were missing is restored. “You sleep deeply and wake refreshed.”
  - The bed is anywhere else: rest value 0.5, half is restored. “You get some sleep, after a fashion.”
- **Rest point**: this bed becomes your rest point. On Normal difficulty, when you fall you return to the bed you last slept in or the waypoint statue you last woke (see [[难度|Difficulty]], [[传送雕像|Waypoint statues]]).

## Hunger slows while you sleep

While asleep, satiety falls 4 times more slowly, and this stacks with the 7.5 times of “hungry” (see [[饱食度|Satiety]]). A night's sleep (8 game hours) on a full stomach costs about 2 to 3 satiety.

## Being woken

While time speeds ahead, you wake at once whenever your health drops:

- Satiety reaches 0 and health starts to go: “Hunger wakes you.”
- Something hurts you (a monster out at night, say): “Pain wakes you.”

So do not sleep on an empty stomach, and do not sleep in the open: shut the door and sleep indoors (see [[夜行生物|Night Creatures]]).

## How others sleep

[[居民|Residents]] sleep by their own schedules, going back to where they sleep when it is time; rain and the story change their schedules too. They do not need beds to sleep.

## Tutorial

The last lesson of the [[新手教程|Tutorial]]: build a house with a bed, shut the door before dark, and go to bed. Lumi reminds you: “Don't go to sleep hungry, or you'll wake up starving in the middle of the night.”

## Tips

- Eat before you sleep: with satiety at 90% or more, you keep slowly regaining health after you wake too.
- A night in a bedroom is as good as full health. Badly hurt? Going indoors for a sleep is the easiest cure.
- Take a bed when you explore far away: a bed can be knocked loose and carried, set up with a right-click when you arrive, and slept in, and it becomes your new rest point.
