Mining is breaking stony things with a pickaxe ({{item|Pickaxe}}). Rocks and boulders give {{item|Stone}}; ore veins give {{item|Iron Ore}}, {{item|Coal}} and {{item|Gold Ore}}. The mining ground you can reach in Chapter 1 is [[Dawn Hill Mine]] on the west slope of [[Dawn Hill]], north of [[Firstlight Village]] (iron and coal, with a stone pickaxe at the entrance); later there are [[The Old Mine]] and the [[The Quarry|Quarry]] on [[The Held Ground]]. Stones grow on the ground by themselves anyway (see [[Foraging|Foraging]]); mining is a bigger, faster source of stone, and one of the only sources of iron ore.

**Veins grow back**: break open a vein and it drops 2 ore, leaving a [[spent iron vein|spent face]] where it stood — bedrock that no tool can touch — which turns back into a vein after one game day (24 game hours, about 24 minutes), in the same place. The clock keeps running while you are off that map. Boulders and rocks are gone once broken.

## How to mine

Hold a pickaxe, point the mouse at a rock and press the left button. The prompt bar at the top reads “Mine …”, and the rock's cell is outlined.

- Each blow deals your own attack plus the pickaxe's power. A pickaxe's efficiency against both stone and ore is 1.
- A new player has attack 2 and a [[Pickaxe|stone pickaxe]] has power 4, so a blow does 6 points.
- When durability reaches 0, it shatters and drops materials by “what it is made of × half” (see [[Durability and Breaking|Durability and Breaking]]). What drops pops out onto the ground; walk close and it flies into your bag.

**Fists cannot break stone.** The body's material is softer than stone, so hitting it only shows “Too hard.” with no damage. An axe can break it, but its efficiency is only 0.25: 2 points a blow with a stone axe.

## What can be mined

| Thing | Where | Durability | Stone pickaxe blows | Drops when broken |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rock | Every map, often at the map's edge | 10 | 2 | 1 stone |
| Boulder | The north slope of [[The Ford]] and elsewhere | 12 | 2 | 4 stones |
| Cave wall | The walls of cave maps | 16 | 3 | 1 stone |
| Stone wall | Stone-built walls on maps | 24 | 4 | 2 stones |
| Iron vein | [[The Old Mine]] | 12 | 2 | 2 iron ore |
| Coal seam | [[The Old Mine]] | 12 | 2 | 2 coal |
| [[gold vein|Gold vein]] | The deepest part of [[The Old Mine]] | 12 | 2 | 2 gold ore |

The amount dropped is what it is made of times the salvage rate of 0.5: a boulder is made of 8 stones and drops 4 when broken.

## The Old Mine

[[The Old Mine]] is north of [[Emberfall]], a dark cave map.

- The main tunnel has four coal seams and four iron veins along its sides, and a {{monster|cyclops}} guards the iron vein at the deepest point. The deepest chamber also has two [[gold vein|gold veins]], just below the iron vein the cyclops guards; this is the only place in the whole world with gold.
- On the ground at the mine's entrance lies an “Old Pickaxe” (an iron pickaxe); if you have no pickaxe, take that one for now.
- Veins grow back: iron veins and coal seams up to 4 each, with one more appearing on empty cave floor every 3 game hours (3 real minutes); gold veins up to 2, with one more every 6 game hours. They grow only while someone is in the mine.
- The mine has {{monster|bat}}, {{monster|cave spider}} and {{monster|skeleton}}; the spiders and bats come back too.

Other sources of iron ore: the cyclops carries 2, which drop when you fell it; every Seal-bearer drops 4 when they fall (see [[Bosses|Bosses]]).

## What ore is for

- **Iron ore**: [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expand the Held Ground II]] needs 5; the smith [[Garrick]] in [[Emberfall]] wants 5 iron ore and 3 coal to forge you [[Dragonsbane]] (see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]]).
- **Coal**: used only when Garrick forges Dragonsbane (3).
- **{{item|Gold Ore}}**: [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expand the Held Ground III]] needs 3. Mining out both gold veins gives 4, enough.
- **Smelting**: hold iron ore or gold ore and right-click the lit [[forge|forge]] in Emberfall, and each ore melts into one {{item|Iron Ingot}} or {{item|Gold Ingot}} (see [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]]). Ingots are hammered on an [[Smithing|anvil]] into weapons, armor, tools and jewelry, or sold to merchants: an iron ingot is worth 15 and a gold ingot 75, more than the ore (6 and 20). Quests and Garrick want ore, so keep enough before you smelt.

How ingots are made into things is in [[Smithing|Smithing]]; the skills of the [[Skills#Smithing|Smithing]] branch smelt more and forge better.

## Tips

- To walk off the edge of a map onto [[The Lands of the Five Seals]], you often have to break the rocks at the edge first.
- A stone pickaxe breaks a rock in two blows, as fast as a stone axe fells a tree; but a cave wall takes three.
- Bare hands against rock only get “Too hard”; make a stone pickaxe first: 2 stones, 2 sticks, 1 cut grass (see [[Crafting|Crafting]]).

## Related skills

{{skill|Prospector}} and {{skill|Deep Vein}} make veins drop more ore, {{skill|Heavy Pick}} makes the pickaxe stronger, {{skill|Stonecutter}} makes stone drop more, and {{skill|Gem Finder}} sometimes digs up gems (see [[Skills#Gathering|Gathering skills]]). The ore you dig can be taken to [[Smithing|Smithing]].
