Woodcutting is chopping trees with an axe. A felled tree leaves a stump, two {{物品|木头}}, one {{物品|树枝}}, and sometimes an {{物品|橡子}}. Logs split into {{物品|木板}}, and planks are the material most used in [[建造|building]] and [[制作|crafting]].

## Chopping a tree

Hold an axe ({{物品|斧}}), point the mouse at a tree (a tree or a pine tree) and press the left button. The prompt bar at the top reads “Chop …”, and the tree's cell is outlined.

- Every chop takes some durability off the tree and leaves an axe mark on it (status {{状态|砍了一斧}}).
- The mark lasts 10 seconds. If you do not chop again within 10 seconds, the tree heals and its durability is full again.
- A tree has 10 durability. Each chop deals your own attack plus the axe's power, and an axe's efficiency against wood is 1 (see [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]). A new player has attack 2 and a [[斧|stone axe]] has power 5, so a chop does 7 points: **two chops fell a tree**.
- When the tree falls you see “The tree falls!”, it turns into a stump where it stood, and it drops 2 logs and 1 stick, with a 25% chance of 1 acorn as well.
- With {{技能|伐木工}} learned, every tree drops 1 more log. After you learn it, trees no longer drop acorns.

What drops pops out onto the ground; walk close and it flies into your bag.

### Without an axe

- **Bare hands**: a punch on a tree does 1 damage, so ten punches fell a tree. Fists leave no axe mark, so the durability you knock off never grows back.
- **Other weapons**: blades such as swords and daggers have an efficiency of 0.5 against wood, blunt weapons 0.25, much slower than an axe (efficiencies in [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]).

### Burnt trees

- A tree that catches fire and burns out, or is chopped down while it burns, becomes a charred tree: it leaves no stump and drops no logs.
- One chop of an axe on a charred tree breaks it into 1 {{物品|煤炭}} (“The charred tree crumbles into coal.”).
- [[烬岭山道]] has charred trees already standing.

### Bushes and other plants

- A bush has 4 durability and drops 1 stick when broken: four punches bare-handed, one chop with an axe.
- A briar has 6 durability, is woody, and has a chance to drop 1 stick when broken.

## Stumps

- A stump in the open with no one standing on it grows back into a tree after **two game days** (48 game hours, 48 real minutes). While it is covered or someone stands on it, it looks again every game hour.
- Chop the stump too (6 durability, one chop of a stone axe) and you get 1 log, and that cell never grows a tree again.
- A {{物品|铲子}} digs the whole stump out (for 1 more stick); a dug-out stump never grows back into a tree. Shovels are sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]].

So, to keep a wood full of trees to chop, chop only the trees and leave the stumps.

## Logs and planks

A log splits into two planks, in one of two ways:

1. **In the crafting panel**: press {{键|C}} and choose {{物品|木板}}. You need an axe on you; it is not used up. Each craft makes 2 planks; “All ×N” splits every log you carry at once.
2. **On the ground**: put a log on the ground, hold an axe and left-click it: “You split the log into planks.” Two planks pop out.

The uses of logs and planks are on their own pages. The most common:

| Use | Logs | Planks |
|---|---|---|
| [[楼层|Walls, floors, doors]] | | 2 each |
| {{物品|楼梯}} | | 3 |
| {{物品|床}}, {{物品|工作台}} | | 2, 4 |
| {{物品|棍棒}} (at a workbench) | 1 | |
| [[给火堆添柴]] (once a day) | 4 | |
| [[扩建誓守之地 I|Expand the Held Ground I]] | 10 | |

A plank put on shallow water lays a stretch of wooden bridge (see [[地形|Terrain]]).

## Acorns and oaks

- Chopping a tree has a 25% chance to drop an {{物品|橡子}} (none after you learn {{技能|伐木工}}).
- Hold an acorn and right-click a cell that can be hoed (grass, dirt, sand, snow, farmland); if no other crop is on it, you plant it: “You plant an acorn.”
- What you plant is an oak sapling. Like a stump, in the open with no one standing on it, it grows into a tree after two game days.

## The dead tree at the Ford

Where the river at [[渡口]] is narrowest stands a dead tree. Chop it three times; on the third chop it falls away from the player. If it falls across the river, its trunk becomes a stretch of wooden bridge, and a stump is left where it stood. This is one of the Ford's solutions; see [[渡口]].

## Tips

- The edge of a map is ringed with woods or rocks. To walk off the map onto [[五印之地]], you first have to chop (or dig) a way out.
- A stone axe fells a tree in two chops. Do not wander off too long between them: the axe mark heals in 10 seconds.
- Keep logs as they are and split them when you need them: the crafting panel splits logs without your putting them down.

## Related skills

## History

- 2026-09-30: First version of woodcutting: an axe chops a tree; the tree falls and leaves a stump and logs.
- 2026-10-04: Trees got durability and a material, and a stone axe fells a tree in two chops; stumps use a “growth stage” instead and grow back into trees after two days (W2b, W7).
- 2026-10-05: In the crafting panel, an axe on you turns logs into planks; “All” splits them all at once.
- 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
