**Smithing** is the craft of turning ore into gear, in two steps: first smelt ore and coal into **ingots** beside a **forge**, then stand beside an **anvil** with a {{物品|锻锤}} and hammer the ingots into things. What you make is of the ingot's metal: an iron ingot makes an iron sword, a {{物品|钢条}} a steel sword; every thing has one anvil recipe for iron and one for steel (jewelry is iron and gold). The smithies of [[初原村]], [[誓守之地]] and [[余烬镇]] have a forge and anvil ready to use; once you learn {{技能|筑炉}} you can build your own at home. Ore comes from [[晨光丘矿洞]] north of Firstlight Village: reachable in Chapter 1, its iron veins and coal seams grow back one game day after being mined out; see [[采矿|Mining]].

## Smelting (forge)

| Recipe | Materials | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| 2 {{物品|铁锭}} | 2 {{物品|铁矿石}}, 1 {{物品|煤炭}} | Nothing |
| 2 {{物品|金锭}} | 2 {{物品|金矿石}}, 1 {{物品|煤炭}} | {{技能|金匠}} |
| 2 {{物品|钢条}} | 3 {{物品|铁矿石}}, 3 {{物品|煤炭}} | {{技能|炼钢术}}, level 3 |

{{技能|高效冶炼}} doubles the ingots each smelting yields. You can also do without a recipe: hold one iron ore or gold ore and right-click a lit forge, one ore for one ingot, with no coal used (see [[熔炉#熔炼|Smelting]]).

## Forging (anvil + smith hammer)

- Anyone can forge: daggers, spears, swords, maces, helmets, shields.
- Skills required: warhammers and halberds ({{技能|重兵器}}), pauldrons and breastplates ({{技能|板甲}}), metal axes, pickaxes, hoes, sickles and shovels ({{技能|工具匠}}), rings and amulets ({{技能|珠宝匠}}).
- **Level**: the gear you forge has your level (no lower than the recipe's level, at most 20); see [[装备#等级|Gear level]]: a level 10 smith forges a level 10 sword.
- **Quality** is set by your level plus your smithing skills ({{技能|学徒铁匠}}, {{技能|精工}}, {{技能|宗师铁匠}}) minus the recipe's difficulty: 3 above is Fine, 6 above Masterwork. {{技能|杰作}} has a 15% chance of one grade higher, and {{技能|传奇锻造}} a 4% chance of coming out Vowbound outright.
- {{技能|武器锻造}} and {{技能|武器大师}} give forged weapons more power, and {{技能|锋刃}} adds critical chance; {{技能|护甲锻造}}, {{技能|加固}} and {{技能|护甲大师}} give armor more defense.

## Gear sets
{{主条目|套装}}

Every profession has a set of forged gear covering the whole body: head, earring, necklace, shoulders, body, hands, ring, belt, feet, main hand and off hand, all forged at an anvil with a {{物品|锻锤}} in hand. The first material decides the material, smithing level and skills decide the quality, and the names follow the profession's gear (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave). Jewelry (earrings, necklaces, rings, circlets) needs {{技能|珠宝匠}} first. Head, shoulders, body, hands, belt and feet make up the **armor set** (bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn); necklace, earring and ring make up the **jewelry set** (2 and 3 pieces); the main hand and off hand belong to the profession alone and are not part of a set. Each piece's materials and bonuses are in [[套装|Gear Sets]].

The newly added forms of piece: {{物品|皮甲}} (body), {{物品|法袍}} (body), {{物品|格挡短刃}} (Assassin's off hand), {{物品|箭袋}} (Ranger's off hand), {{物品|法典}} (Mage's off hand). They can only be forged at an anvil as a piece of a set.

Everything forged rolls [[词条|affixes]]: how many is set by its [[品质|quality]] (Crude 2, Common 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5), and the range of their values grows with the maker's [[装备#等级|level]].

## Selling

The stall buys iron ore, gold ore and coal; {{技能|矿石商}} sells them for more.
