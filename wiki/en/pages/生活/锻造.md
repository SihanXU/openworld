**Smithing** is the craft of turning ore into gear, in two steps: first smelt ore and coal into **ingots** beside a **forge**, then stand beside an **anvil** with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} and hammer the ingots into things. What you make is of the ingot's metal: an iron ingot makes an iron sword, a {{item|Steel Bar}} a steel sword; every thing has one anvil recipe for iron and one for steel (jewelry is iron and gold). The smithies of [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Held Ground]] and [[Emberfall]] have a forge and anvil ready to use; once you learn {{skill|Forge Builder}} you can build your own at home. Ore comes from [[Dawn Hill Mine]] north of Firstlight Village: reachable in Chapter 1, its iron veins and coal seams grow back one game day after being mined out; see [[Mining|Mining]].

## Smithies

There are three places where you can smelt and forge; the forges are always lit, and anyone may use the anvils:

| Place | Smith | What they sell |
|---|---|---|
| The smithy by the East Road in [[Firstlight Village]] | [[Haldor]] | {{item|Smith Hammer}}, {{item|Coal}}, {{item|Iron Ore}}, {{item|Iron Ingot}} |
| East of the sword on [[The Held Ground]] | [[Alberic]] (the White Knight, at the anvil in the mornings) | {{item|Smith Hammer}}, {{item|Coal}}, {{item|Iron Ingot}}, {{item|Steel Bar}} |
| The smithy in [[Emberfall]] | [[Garrick]] | {{item|Pickaxe}}, {{item|Hoe}}, {{item|Dagger}}, {{item|Sword}}, {{item|Helmet}}, {{item|Iron Ingot}} |

A smith has an anvil-and-hammer mark over their head. Haldor is at the anvil 6:00–21:00, Alberic 6:00–14:00; when the smith is away, the forge and anvil can be used all the same. Once you learn {{skill|Forge Builder}} you can also build a forge and anvil of your own at home.

## Smelting (forge)

| Recipe | Materials | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| 2 {{item|Iron Ingot}} | 2 {{item|Iron Ore}}, 1 {{item|Coal}} | Nothing |
| 2 {{item|Gold Ingot}} | 2 {{item|Gold Ore}}, 1 {{item|Coal}} | {{skill|Goldsmith}} |
| 2 {{item|Steel Bar}} | 3 {{item|Iron Ore}}, 3 {{item|Coal}} | {{skill|Steelworking}}, level 3 |

{{skill|Efficient Smelting}} doubles the ingots each smelting yields. You can also do without a recipe: hold one iron ore or gold ore and right-click a lit forge, one ore for one ingot, with no coal used (see [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]]).

## Forging (anvil + smith hammer)

- Anyone can forge: daggers, spears, swords, maces, helmets, shields.
- Skills required: warhammers and halberds ({{skill|Heavy Arms}}), pauldrons and breastplates ({{skill|Plate Work}}), metal axes, pickaxes, hoes, sickles and shovels ({{skill|Tool Smith}}), rings and amulets ({{skill|Jeweler}}).
- **Level**: the gear you forge has your level (no lower than the recipe's level, at most 20); see [[Equipment#Levels|Gear level]]: a level 10 smith forges a level 10 sword.
- **Quality** is set by your level plus your smithing skills ({{skill|Apprentice Smith}}, {{skill|Fine Craft}}, {{skill|Grandmaster Smith}}) minus the recipe's difficulty: 3 above is Fine, 6 above Masterwork. {{skill|Masterwork}} has a 15% chance of one grade higher, and {{skill|Legendary Smith}} a 4% chance of coming out Vowbound outright.
- {{skill|Weaponsmith}} and {{skill|Master Weaponsmith}} give forged weapons more power, and {{skill|Keen Edge}} adds critical chance; {{skill|Armorsmith}}, {{skill|Reinforced}} and {{skill|Master Armorsmith}} give armor more defense.

## Gear sets
{{main|Gear Sets}}

Every profession has a set of forged gear covering the whole body: head, earring, necklace, shoulders, body, hands, ring, belt, feet, main hand and off hand, all forged at an anvil with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} in hand. The first material decides the material, smithing level and skills decide the quality, and the names follow the profession's gear (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave). Jewelry (earrings, necklaces, rings, circlets) needs {{skill|Jeweler}} first. Head, shoulders, body, hands, belt and feet make up the **armor set** (bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn); necklace, earring and ring make up the **jewelry set** (2 and 3 pieces); the main hand and off hand belong to the profession alone and are not part of a set. Each piece's materials and bonuses are in [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]].

The newly added forms of piece: {{item|Jerkin}} (body), {{item|Robe}} (body), {{item|Parrying Dagger}} (Assassin's off hand), {{item|Quiver}} (Ranger's off hand), {{item|Tome}} (Mage's off hand). They can only be forged at an anvil as a piece of a set.

Everything forged rolls [[Affixes|affixes]]: how many is set by its [[Quality|quality]] (Crude 2, Common 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5), and the range of their values grows with the maker's [[Equipment#Levels|level]].

## Selling

The stall buys iron ore, gold ore and coal; {{skill|Ore Merchant}} sells them for more.

- 2026-10-12: After DNF, sets were split into armor sets (6 pieces, bonuses at 2/4/6) and jewelry sets (3 pieces, bonuses at 2/3), and the main hand and off hand no longer count toward a set; two pieces for the same slot count as one.

- 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls|Controls]]).
