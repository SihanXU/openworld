Fishing is casting a {{物品|鱼竿}} into water and seeing whether anything bites. When something bites, you land a fish, now and then a golden carp or a soggy old boot; which fish depends on the hour, the weather and the depth of the water. The fish you land can be eaten, grilled, or sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]]. The first time you walk up to the pond in [[初原村]], [[露米]] begins the guide quest [[第一竿]] and walks you through it step by step.

## Getting ready

### Fishing rod

There are two ways to get a {{物品|鱼竿}}:

- **Make one yourself**: press {{键|C}} to open [[制作|Crafting]]; 2 {{物品|树枝}} and 2 {{物品|干草}}, made by hand.
- **Buy one**: on the “Fishing” page of the stall on the Held Ground, 25 {{物品|晨辉币}} each.

A rod casts up to {{数值|鱼竿距离}} cells, into any cell of water within the square centered on you, diagonals included; it need not be in the same row or column. A fishing rod is not a weapon, and clicking a creature with it does not attack.

### Bait

{{物品|鱼饵}} is bought on the “Fishing” page of the stall, 5 for 5 Dawnmarks. Bait only works once it is put on the rod:

1. Hold the bait (select the bait's slot on the hotbar).
2. Press {{键|Tab}} to open the [[背包|Inventory]], right-click the rod and choose “Use Bait on it” (“Bait the fishing rod”). With the rod lying on the ground, right-clicking it while holding bait works too.
3. “You bait the hook.” That bait is used up, and the rod keeps {{数值|鱼饵次数}} casts' worth of bait.

- The bait stays on the rod; holding something else or putting it in a chest does not lose it.
- The rod's item tooltip shows how many baited casts are left, 0 when it has no bait.
- You can bait it several times over, and the casts add up.
- Every baited cast uses one bait, whether or not anything is caught.

## Casting

Hold the rod and left-click a cell of water (the prompt is “Cast into shallow water”). The water you can fish is the **shallow water** and **deep water** of [[地形|Terrain]], on any map.

| Situation | Chance of a bite | When nothing bites |
|---|---|---|
| Bait on the rod | 70% | “The bait's been nibbled clean. Nothing caught.” |
| No bait on the rod | 35% | “The float bobs. Nothing yet.” |

- When something bites, what you catch goes straight into your bag, and you are told what you caught (see the next section).
- Caught or not, that cell of water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds (10 game minutes). Cast into the same cell again during that time and you only get “The water's stirred up. Wait for it to settle.”, and no bait is used. Move to another cell of water and you can keep fishing.

## What you catch

Once something bites, the game checks the lines below from top to bottom, and the first one that holds is what you catch:

| Order | You catch | Condition | Chance of this line | Message |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | {{物品|晨鳞鱼}} | 05:00–08:00 (first light) | 3% | “The line comes out of the water glowing — a dawnscale, scales like the first morning!” |
| 2 | {{物品|金鳞鲤}} | Any time | Shallow water 6%, deep water 10% | “A flash of gold in the water — a golden carp!” |
| 3 | {{物品|泡烂的靴子}} | Any time | 8% | “Heavy… it's an old boot.” |
| 4 | {{物品|鲶鱼}} | Rain or storm | 40% | “A whiskered head pokes out of the rain — a catfish!” |
| 5 | {{物品|鳗鱼}} | 20:00–05:00 (night) | 45% | “It writhes on the line in the dark — an eel!” |
| 6 | {{物品|狗鱼}} | Cast into deep water | 45% | “Long and toothy, up from the deep — a pike!” |
| 7 | {{物品|太阳鱼}} | 06:00–19:00 (day) | 45% | “A shiny sunfish!” |
| 8 | {{物品|河鲈}} | Any time | 45% | “A striped perch!” |
| 9 | {{物品|生鱼}} | None of the above | The rest | “A tug — you haul up a fish!” |

A line whose condition does not hold is simply skipped. A time span includes the hour it starts on and excludes the hour it ends on: 05:00–08:00 means 05:00 to 07:59. Weather is in [[天气|Weather]], the hours in [[游戏内时间|In-game Time]].

Worked out in this order, the shares of what you land once something bites:

| When and where | Shares of the catch |
|---|---|
| Day (12:00), shallow water, no rain | Sunfish 38.9%, Raw Fish 26.2%, Perch 21.4%, Soggy Boot 7.5%, Golden Carp 6.0% |
| Night (22:00), shallow water, no rain | Eel 38.9%, Raw Fish 26.2%, Perch 21.4%, Soggy Boot 7.5%, Golden Carp 6.0% |
| Early morning (06:00–07:59), shallow water | Sunfish 37.7%, Raw Fish 25.4%, Perch 20.8%, Soggy Boot 7.3%, Golden Carp 5.8%, Dawnscale 3.0% |
| Day, deep water | Pike 37.3%, Sunfish 20.5%, Raw Fish 13.8%, Perch 11.3%, Soggy Boot 7.2%, Golden Carp 10.0% |
| Rainy day, shallow water | Catfish 34.6%, Sunfish 23.3%, Raw Fish 15.7%, Perch 12.8%, Soggy Boot 7.5%, Golden Carp 6.0% |

Bait only raises the chance of a bite; it does not change what you catch.

## Where to fish

Any shallow or deep water can be fished, and what you catch depends only on the conditions above, not on which map you are on. The expanded [[湖畔|Lakeside]] has broad stretches of deep water, worth visiting for pike and a better chance of golden carp.

- **The Held Ground's fish pond**: a stretch of shallow water north of the planted sword, with a sign beside it: “Fish pond — two sticks and two handfuls of grass make a rod. Bait at the stall.” It is close to the stall, handy for buying bait and selling fish.
- **The pond east of the Held Ground**: beyond the first ring of mist, the pond itself lying where the second ring of mist stands, with a little deep water in the middle where pike can be caught. Once you complete [[扩建誓守之地 I|Expand the Held Ground I]] and the first ring of mist draws back, you can walk to the pond's west bank. You cannot stand in deep water, so stand on the bank and cast into deep water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} cells: from the very edge of the west bank it is just within reach.
- **Water elsewhere**: the river at [[渡口]] and the ponds and streams all over can be fished.

Now and then a fish shadow surfaces in the Held Ground's water, lingers, and vanishes. The shadow is only a hint on screen; shadow or no shadow, it makes no difference to what you catch; no shadow appears in water that has just been stirred up.

## Once it is landed

- **Eat**: every fish can be eaten raw, restoring different amounts of [[饱食度|Satiety]]; see each fish's page and [[食物|Food]].
- **Grill**: every fish put on a lit cooking fire grills into the same {{物品|烤鱼}} (satiety +5), more filling than raw. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: take them to the “Sell” page of the stall on the Held Ground. A dawnscale fetches 80 Dawnmarks, a golden carp 30, and a soggy boot only 1. Prices are in [[金钱#货摊收什么|What the stall buys]].
- **Give**: as with other food, a hungry person thanks you for it, such as {{人物|托宾}} at the Ford.

## Tips

- Bait doubles the chance of a bite. With no money for bait you can fish with a bare hook, only more slowly.
- After a cast, a cell of water needs 10 seconds; casting by turns into several cells is faster.
- For eels, go at night; for a chance at a dawnscale, keep watch by the water at first light.
- On rainy days you can catch catfish, 20 coins each, worth more than most fish.
