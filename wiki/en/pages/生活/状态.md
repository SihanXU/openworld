A status is a mark that hangs on a thing, a person or a creature, such as “burning”, “wet” or “asleep”. Most statuses have a time limit and disappear by themselves when it runs out; some stay until something else removes them. Statuses are the same for everyone: a tree, a chest and a person can all burn.

Durations are given in real seconds; 1 second is 1 game minute.

## All statuses

{{状态表}}

When you hover the mouse over a thing, its description lists its statuses in red. Some statuses have a mark on screen: the flames of burning, the droplets of wet, the shell of ice when frozen, the sparks of electrified, oil stains, and the green crosses rising while soaking in a hot spring.

## From the elements

The rules of the elements are in [[元素|Elements]].

- **{{状态|燃烧}}**: 4.5 seconds for things, 2.5 seconds for bodies. Every half second a burning thing has an even chance of losing 1 durability (defense does not count), and it passes the fire on to burnable things beside it. Water, rain and ice put fire out. When it burns out, ground becomes ash, a tree becomes a charred tree, furniture and items become ash; structures such as walls, floors, stairs and doors are still there when the fire goes out, and become ash only if their durability runs out while they burn. Catching fire thaws something frozen. Fire creatures do not catch fire from fire; they regain health instead.
- **{{状态|潮湿}}**: 40 seconds. Splashed water, rain and stepping into mud all make you wet. A wet thing does not catch fire (fire only dries it); it can be frozen by ice; and when lightning strikes, the shock runs along wet things. Wet removes burning. If you do not want to wait, press {{键|E}} with empty hands at a lit fire to warm up, and you are dried at once (see [[营火#取暖|Warming up]]).
- **{{状态|冰冻}}**: 4 seconds, unable to act. The ice element can only freeze wet things. A creature tied up with fiber such as {{物品|绳索}} or {{物品|干草}} is frozen too, for only 3 seconds (see [[怪物#递东西给生物|Giving things to creatures]]). Frozen removes burning and wet. A frozen body hit by a blunt weapon or by earth takes damage × 1.5, and the ice shatters. Fire thaws it (and thawed, it is wet). A frozen person cannot act, and so cannot press {{键|E}} to warm up at a fire.
- **{{状态|刚解冻}}**: 8 seconds, starting at the same time as frozen. During this time it cannot be frozen again, so no one stays frozen on and on in the rain.
- **{{状态|感电}}**: 0.8 seconds, unable to act. Lightning striking wet things, conductive things (metal, water) and most creatures electrifies them. Electrified ground shocks whoever stands on it.
- **{{状态|油污}}**: 90 seconds. Splashed with lamp oil. An oiled thing explodes when fire touches it: it catches fire itself, and the things around it may catch fire too.
- **{{状态|防火}}**: 300 seconds (5 game hours). Comes from drinking a {{物品|防火药剂}}. 75% fire resistance, and you cannot catch fire; if you are burning when you get it, the fire goes out.
- **{{状态|薄冰}}**: 150 seconds. The ice that ice freezes from shallow water carries this status; when the time is up it melts back into water, and whoever stands on it is put ashore. Ice that is on a map from the start does not melt.
- **{{状态|搅浑}}**: 10 seconds. After you cast into water, that cell of water is stirred up; casting there again during this time catches nothing and uses no bait, and no fish shadows appear in it (see [[钓鱼|Fishing]]).
- **{{状态|泡温泉}}**: 0.8 seconds, renewed for as long as you are in the healing spring in [[初原村]]. While soaking you keep regaining health, and it also removes burning and frozen (see [[地形|Terrain]]).

## The body

- **{{状态|饿了}}**: you get it when satiety falls below 60% of its maximum (12 points), and it goes once you eat back above 60%. Once you are hungry, satiety falls 7.5 times more slowly. See [[饱食度|Satiety]].
- **{{状态|睡着}}**: while sleeping in a bed. Lying down, you cannot move, and satiety falls 4 times more slowly. It goes when you wake. See [[睡觉|Sleeping]].
- **{{状态|倒地}}**: 30 seconds, unable to act. People who “cannot die” (residents, Lumi) go down when their health reaches 0, and wake when the time is up with half their health. No one attacks someone who is down. [[露米]], when down, cannot keep up with the player and stays where she fell until she wakes. When the player's health reaches 0, the death screen appears; see [[难度|Difficulty]].
- **{{状态|昏迷}}**: 5 seconds, unable to act. At the start of the [[新手教程|Tutorial]], the player wakes from being out cold.
- **{{状态|流血}}**: 4 seconds, losing 1 health a second, with defense not counting. Comes from the skill {{技能|撕裂}}: a critical hit makes the target bleed. Someone felled by bleeding counts as felled by whoever dealt the wound.
- **{{状态|跪地}}**: 1 second, unable to move. The stigma power of the {{物品|荆棘冠}}, “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”: attacking while wearing it has a 20% chance of bringing the other to their knees.
- **{{状态|肾上腺素}}**: 5 seconds, regaining 1 health a second, with +30% movement speed. Comes from the skill {{技能|肾上腺素}}: it triggers when a single blow takes you below 30% health, at most once a minute.
- **{{状态|眩晕}}**: unable to move or strike. Comes from active skills: {{技能|盾击}} 1.5 seconds, {{技能|冲锋}} 0.5 seconds, {{技能|裂地}} 0.8 seconds. See [[技能#主动技能与技能栏|Active skills]].

## Bosses' skills

See [[首领|Bosses]].

- **{{状态|迟滞}}**: 2.5 seconds; movement slows (each step takes × 1.7 the time), attack speed unchanged. Comes from “Thorn Covenant” of [[绯愿兰后]].
- **{{状态|迷眩}}**: 0.8 seconds, unable to act. Comes from “Thorns Bend the Knee” of [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]] and “False Promise” of the Velvet Lure.

## Monsters' skills

See [[怪物技能|Monster Skills]].

- **{{状态|中毒}}**: 3 seconds, losing 1 health a second, with defense not counting. One in every 4 ordinary attacks of the [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spider]] carries poison. Drinking an [[解毒剂|Antidote]] cures it at once.
- **{{状态|缠丝}}**: 2.5 seconds; every step takes 3 times as long as usual. Comes from the cave spider's “Web Spit”.
- **{{状态|狂热}}**: 8 seconds; faster movement, +1 attack. A [[狼|wolf]]'s “Howl” when wounded frenzies it and every beast within 6 cells.
- **{{状态|缩壳}}**: 4 seconds; +4 defense, but very slow movement. A [[巨蟹|giant crab]] withdraws into its shell when its health falls below half.
- Monsters can also make people **bleed** (a [[狼|wolf]]'s Lunge), **stunned** (a [[野猪|boar]]'s Charge, a [[幽灵|ghost]]'s Wail) and **mired** (a [[僵尸|zombie]]'s grasp), with the same effects as written above.

## Things and mechanisms

- **{{状态|点燃}}**: when something that can be lit, such as a campfire, brazier, lamp, candle, forge or cauldron, is burning. No time limit; water puts it out. Once lit, a thing that can be lit does not change when fire touches it again, and does not catch fire. The [[干草垛|haystack]] at the burned farmstead on [[东大路]] is always lit too: a smoldering fire that does not spread and never burns itself out; it is not a thing that can be lit, so if fire touches it again it truly catches fire. Monsters (except fire ones) do not come within 2 cells of a lit thing. Raw food has to be cooked on a lit cooking fire. See [[营火|Campfire]].
- **{{状态|打开}}**: when a chest is open. Press {{键|E}} to open and shut it. Things can only be put in while it is open.
- **{{状态|已共鸣}}**: a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] that has been woken; it glows, and you can travel to it on the map.
- **{{状态|坏了}}**: a mechanism that needs mending, such as the winch at [[渡口]] with its rope snapped.
- **{{状态|砍了一斧}}**: 10 seconds. A tree has been chopped with an axe. If it has not fallen when the time is up, the tree heals and its durability is full again. See [[伐木|Woodcutting]].

## Crops and growth

- **{{状态|浇过水}}**: the current growth stage has water, used up when the stage is grown; each stage's time is set by the crop (1, 2 or 4 game hours), and it needs open sky too. Irrigation keeps water for the next stage as well, at the same growth speed. See [[农耕|Farming]].
- **{{状态|施过肥}}**: the crop has had ash or fertilizer worked in, giving 1 more when cut.
- **{{状态|生长中}}**: the timer of things that change with time: stumps and oak saplings growing into trees, hens laying eggs, calves and chicks growing up.

## Animals

See [[动物|Animals]].

- **{{状态|吃饱了}}**: {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. An animal fed [[饲料|Animal Feed]] or [[麦子|Wheat]]. A fed cow can be milked; two fed animals of the same kind side by side bring forth a young one.
- **{{状态|挤过奶}}**: {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. A cow just milked, which cannot be milked again during this time.
- **{{状态|刚生过崽}}**: {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. The two animals that have just brought forth a young one, which will not breed again during this time.

## Lumi's power

- **{{状态|荆冠之悟}}**: hangs on [[露米]]. While she carries this power, the player she follows gains +100% experience. See [[恩赐|Boons]].

## History

- 2026-09-30: The game's first version already had burning, wet, frozen, electrified, oiled, lit, open and other statuses.
- 2026-10-03: Added bleeding and adrenaline (skills), mired and entranced (bosses), fireproof (the Fireproof Draught).
- 2026-10-04: Watered and growing are driven by the “growth stage” instead (W7); fireproof became a status giving fire resistance (W8).
- 2026-10-05: Added hungry (satiety in two bands); asleep changed to lying still and getting hungry slowly. Lumi's power became a status on her.
- 2026-10-07: Added kneeling (Crown of Thorns), soaking (the healing spring) and thawing.
- 2026-10-08: Added fed, milked and bred (animals) and stunned (active skills).
- 2026-10-08: A lit thing does not catch fire when fire touches it again; the haystack on the East Road became lit (smoldering) instead of burning and spreading for good; fed, milked and bred each last a whole game day.
- 2026-10-09: Warming up beside a lit fire removes wet; a creature tied up with rope or cut grass is frozen for 3 seconds; Lumi stays where she fell when down.
