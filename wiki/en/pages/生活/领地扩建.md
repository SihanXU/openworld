**Expanding the Held Ground** is how [[The Held Ground]] grows. It has three lines, all handled through [[Alberic]]:

- **Land**: push back the mist, and the ground grows larger.
- **New grounds**: open up a place with a particular use, such as a quarry or a lake.
- **Buildings**: raise facilities on the staked plots around the sword, and residents' tents for the people who move in.

## Opening the panel

Press {{key|E}} to talk to Alberic and choose “The Held Ground…” to open the **management** panel (titled “The Held Ground”). Time pauses while it is open. The sword planted in the middle can only be read; it no longer opens the panel.

- **Left**: a round map of the ground (the part already held in green, the rings of mist not yet pushed back as gray dashed lines), “Land N / 3”, and the **stock** (how much of each material is stored).
- **Right**: three tabs, “Land”, “Grounds” and “Buildings”, each saying how many are done; when something can be built right away, a small dot lights in the tab's corner. Each card says:
  - what you get once it is built;
  - which materials it needs, each with a material slot, a progress bar (in stock / needed) and “how many you carry”;
  - its state: **Done**, **Ready to build** (enough in stock, the card glowing gold), **Collecting**, **Locked** (land level too low, the card saying “Needs land N”).
- **Bottom**: the “Pack”, listing what you carry, with what the expansions can use lit up.

## Putting materials in

Materials are not handed to one project; they go to Alberic, into the **stock**, and every project draws from the stock.

- **Drag**: drag a material from the pack onto a card's material slot to put in as many as that card still lacks.
- **Click**: click a material in the pack to put in as many as all the projects being collected still lack, together; clicking a material slot on a card works too.
- What goes into the stock is handed over and never comes back to your bag, but the stock stays, so you can put things in over many visits.
- Something that is on no project's list, handed to him, is just an ordinary gift.
- Handing him a listed material with {{key|G}} at any time puts it into the stock as well.

## Building

When the stock is enough, the card's button lights up: “Widen!” for land, “Open!” for grounds, “Build!” for buildings. Press it and the materials are taken from the stock, and Alberic says a line.

### Land

| Level | Name | Materials | Reward | Once pushed back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | The Grove | 10 {{item|Log}}, 10 {{item|Stone}} | 20 Dawnmarks | The first ring of mist lifts: a small wood, scattered stones, more open ground |
| II | The Meadow | 20 {{item|Plank}}, 5 {{item|Iron Ore}} | 40 Dawnmarks | The second ring: a flowering meadow and the waterside |
| III | The Old Bounds | 30 {{item|Plank}}, 30 {{item|Stone}}, 3 {{item|Gold Ore}} | 60 Dawnmarks | The last ring: the old boundary stones, iron veins in the rock |

Land has to go one level at a time.

### New grounds

Each ground is a separate place. Once it is opened, press “Go ➜” on its card and Alberic sends you there; a path on the south side of the ground leads back out to the sword.

| Ground | Land needed | Materials | What it has |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Quarry]] | I | 15 planks, 10 stones | Boulders to break, iron veins, coal seams and a gold vein; stones keep coming up out of the ground |
| [[The Timberland]] | I | 15 logs, 5 stones | A dense wood (it grows back when chopped), a mushroom ring and berries, sticks and cut grass on the ground |
| [[The Lakeside]] | II | 20 stones, 10 planks | A deep lake: you can fish (pike in the middle of the lake, eels at night), with a sandy shore and reeds |
| [[The Hot Springs]] | III | 25 stones, 2 {{item|Iron Ingot}} | Three hot springs that heal you when you soak (like the spring in [[Firstlight Village]]), with herbs and frostcaps on the slopes |

### Buildings

Eight [[staked plot|staked plots]] are laid out in advance around the sword (each a ring of stakes, named “plot for a …”). Once one is built, after a while the plot becomes that thing, with a flash of light.

| Building | Where the plot is | Land needed | Materials | Becomes | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workshop | West of the sword | — | 8 logs, 4 stones | A [[workbench|workbench]]; unlocks [[Crafting|crafting]] recipes: {{item|Club}}, {{item|Shield}}, {{item|Bow}}, made anywhere from the bag | — |
| Workshop II | (upgrade) | I | 16 planks, 4 {{item|Iron Ingot}} | Unlocks recipes: {{item|Candle}}, {{item|Lantern}}; a Forge Builder's [[smith's forge|smith's forge]], an Apiarist's bee box | — |
| Smithy: Bellows | (upgrade, no plot) | I | 4 {{item|Leather Hide}}, 8 planks, 3 iron ingots | A smith with the skill forges warhammers, halberds, pauldrons, breastplates, metal tools, rings and amulets at any [[anvil|anvil]] | — |
| Smithy: Hardened Anvil | (upgrade) | II | 2 {{item|Steel Bar}}, 12 stones, 6 {{item|Coal}} | The four professions' [[Gear Sets|sets]] can be forged at an anvil | — |
| Arcane Workshop | South-east of the sword | I | 12 planks, 4 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}}, 2 {{item|Gem}} | An [[arcane workbench|arcane workbench]]: wands, staves, healing and fireproof draughts, a mage's first spell books | — |
| Arcane Workshop II | (upgrade) | II | 4 gems, 4 {{item|Frostcap}}, 4 {{item|Fireweed}}, 2 {{item|Gold Ingot}} | Fire and frost staves, the deeper spell books | — |
| Kitchen Hearth | South of the sword | — | 10 stones, 4 logs | A [[campfire|campfire]] that never goes out, for cooking | — |
| Storehouse | South of the sword | I | 12 planks, 6 logs | A 40-slot [[storehouse|storehouse]] | — |
| Weaving Shed | South of the sword | I | 8 planks, 6 sticks | A [[loom|loom]]; unlocks recipes: {{item|Bolt of Cloth}} (and a Leatherworker's leather gear), made anywhere from the bag | — |
| Apothecary | Northwest of the sword | II | 8 stones, 6 {{item|Herb}} | A [[cauldron|cauldron]] | — |
| Residents' Tent | Northeast of the sword | I | 6 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}}, 10 planks | A [[tent|tent]], with two beds in front | 30 Dawnmarks |
| Second Residents' Tent | East of the sword | I | 8 Bolts of Cloth, 14 planks, 6 logs | A tent, with two more beds | 50 Dawnmarks |

The residents' tents are for people who move in: there is already one bed beside the camp, and with all three tents built there are seven in all. Who can be asked to live there is in [[Held Ground Residents|Held Ground Residents]].

## History

- 2026-10-07: The three expansions were three quests of Alberic's (Expand the Held Ground I, II, III), with the materials handed to him.
- 2026-10-11: Expansion became a panel on the sword: put in the materials and expand on the spot.
- 2026-10-11: The panel moved into Alberic's dialogue (“The Held Ground…”), and materials go into the ground's stock; besides land, new grounds (the Quarry, the Timberland, the Lakeside, the Hot Springs) and working buildings (workshop, kitchen hearth, storehouse, weaving shed, apothecary) were added. Expansion quests in progress in old saves are cancelled; land levels already reached are kept.
- 2026-10-12: Three residents' tents added to the buildings, for people who move in; the Held Ground in old saves gets their plots staked out too.
- 2026-10-21: Buildings began unlocking crafting recipes: the Workshop the club, shield and bow, the Weaving Shed cloth and leather gear; once unlocked they are made in the crafting panel anywhere, with no workbench or loom beside you.
- 2026-10-21: Buildings can be upgraded: Workshop II (candles, lanterns, a smith's forge, a bee box), the smithy's bellows and hardened anvil (skill recipes and the professions' sets wait for them); the Arcane Workshop and its level II were added (wands, staves, draughts, spell books).
