Foraging is getting materials from the world. Things on the ground are picked up with {{键|E}}; trees are chopped ([[伐木|Woodcutting]]), rocks are broken ([[采矿|Mining]]), crops are harvested ([[农耕|Farming]]). At the start of the game you carry nothing, and your first tools are all made from {{物品|树枝}}, {{物品|石块}} and {{物品|干草}} picked up from the ground ([[制作|Crafting]]).

## Picking things up

- Point the mouse at something lying loose on the ground and press {{键|E}}. You can reach your own cell and the ring around it, that is, 1 cell (see [[操作|Controls]]).
- Picking up is a physical rule, always defined, and never asks the AI (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]). Things that stand (doors, chests, beds…) and people are not picked up: pressing {{键|E}} at them handles them, or talks to the person.
- When several things are stacked on one cell, holding {{键|E}} lists every layer of that cell, and you choose one to take (only the things on your own storey are listed; see [[楼层|Storeys]]).
- Other people's things can be taken too, but pressing {{键|E}} first asks “Steal …?”, and only takes it once you confirm: this is theft; if the owner sees it, they say “Hey — that's mine.” (see [[犯罪|Crime]]). Hovering the mouse over it, the item's description says “Belongs to …”.

## Drawn in by itself

What a rule makes drop (the {{物品|木头}} from a felled tree, the {{物品|木板}} from a smashed chest, the crops you cut) pops out and flies into your bag by itself when you come within 2 cells. With {{技能|长臂}} from the [[技能#采集|Gathering]] branch, the range grows by 2 cells more.

Things that do not fly to you by themselves:
- What a creature leaves when it is felled, and what a person drops when they fall, lie quietly where they are; press {{键|E}} to pick them up.
- An {{物品|鸡蛋}} a chicken lays lies where it is too.
- On [[难度|Normal difficulty]], after you come back to life, the belongings left where you fell are not drawn in either.

When your bag is full, a new kind of item you pick up drops at your feet; kinds you already have stack in as usual. Items have no weight and no stack limit (see [[背包|Inventory]]).

## What grows again

The ground of outdoor maps grows scattered materials by itself. Every so often, as long as the map has not reached its limit of that thing, one more appears on empty ground. They never appear under the player's feet or in the cells right beside the player.

| Place | What grows (most on the map) | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Most outdoor maps ([[渡口]], [[初原村]], [[草原]], [[山脚]], [[云顶台地]], [[林间空地]], [[荆棘谷]], [[东大路]], [[余烬镇]], [[水车磨坊]], [[晨光丘]], [[誓守之地]]) | {{物品|树枝}} 6, {{物品|石块}} 5, {{物品|干草}} 6 | 15 seconds |
| [[森林]] | {{物品|树枝}} 10, {{物品|石块}} 3, {{物品|干草}} 4; {{物品|霜盖菇}} 3 | 15 seconds; Frostcap 90 seconds |
| [[初原村]] | {{物品|火焰草}} 3 as well | 90 seconds |
| [[林间空地]] ([[新手教程|Tutorial]]) | {{物品|浆果}} 6 as well | 15 seconds |
| [[晨光丘]] | {{物品|草药}} 3 as well | 120 seconds |
| [[荒野]] | {{物品|树枝}} 4, {{物品|石块}} 3, {{物品|干草}} 4 | 15 seconds |

Sticks and stones grow on grass, cut grass on grass and dry grass (in the Wild, sticks and stones grow on snow too). Time is game time: 15 seconds is 15 game minutes.

Only the map the player is on grows things. Maps you have left are frozen, and do not catch up on growing when you return.

The veins of [[旧矿坑]] also grow back slowly; see [[采矿|Mining]]. The cows and chickens of [[草原]] come back; see [[动物|Animals]].

## Things placed only once

Some things are placed when a map is made, and once taken they are gone:
- The scattered items in [[荒野|the Wild]]: berries, sticks and stones on the open plains, {{物品|蘑菇}} and sticks in the deep woodland, stones and {{物品|草药}} in the rolling hills, stones and sticks on the snowfields, sticks on the roads.
- Materials lying on maps from the start, such as the sticks under the trees west of [[渡口]], the stones on the slope and the cut grass on the grass.

## Gathering skills

The [[技能#采集|Gathering]] branch has four skills, learned in a line down from {{技能|长臂}}:

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| {{技能|长臂}} | The range at which drops are drawn in grows by 2 cells. |
| {{技能|伐木工}} | A felled tree drops one more log (see [[伐木|Woodcutting]]). |
| {{技能|大背包}} | One more row in the bag (10 slots). |
| {{技能|钻木取火}} | An ordinary stick lights a campfire, brazier or candles (see [[营火|Campfire]]). |

## Tips

- The first tool to make is a [[斧|stone axe]]: 1 stick, 1 stone, 1 cut grass.
- When you are short of sticks, chopping trees is faster than waiting for the ground: a felled tree drops 1 stick too.
- A new game begins on [[云顶台地]], where the yard already has 3 berries, 2 sticks, 2 stones and 2 cut grass lying about, enough for your first stone axe.
- What grows on the ground has a limit; once you have picked it all, you wait for it to grow again slowly. To stock up on materials, stay on one map and pick things up as you work.
