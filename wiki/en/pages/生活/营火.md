A campfire is a ring of stones around two sticks. It is unlit when built; once lit with a torch it keeps burning, needs no fuel, and rain cannot put it out. A lit campfire cooks raw food ([[Cooking|Cooking]]), burns what is thrown into it to ash, and at night monsters will not come within 2 cells of it. It is built in [[Building|Building]] mode.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Campfire”; 2 {{item|Stone}} and 2 {{item|Stick}}, no tool needed. It can be built on empty ground on your own storey, or on a floor already laid.
- **Built by hand**: hold a stone and press {{key|G}} at another stone on the ground: “The two stones make a fire ring.” Then put two sticks into the ring: the first gives “One more stick.”, the second “The campfire is built, ready to light.” (see [[Building#Building by hand|Building by hand]]).
- **Ready-made**: the camp at [[The Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]], and the last camp on [[Cinder Pass]] below the Dragon's Lair each have a campfire burning. In the clearing southeast of [[The Forest]] there is a “cold fire ring”, the camp left by the pilgrim before you.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Lighting and putting out

- **Lighting**: hold a {{item|Torch}} and left-click the campfire. Any fire can light it, such as a fire-element weapon or something burning. With {{skill|Firemaker}} learned, right-clicking it with an ordinary stick lights it too.
- **Once lit, it stays lit**: a campfire uses no fuel and never goes out by itself.
- **Putting out**: hold a container full of water and left-click it, and the fire goes out; it can be lit again.
- **Rain does not put it out**: rain only wets people and creatures and puts out burning things, and a lit campfire does not count as “burning”.
- When [[Residents|residents]]' fires go out, they light them again themselves (not while they are wet): “Out again. Hand me the flint.”

### Cooking

Hold raw food and right-click a lit campfire, or press {{key|G}} at it, and the raw food is cooked (“Roast … on the campfire”). What can be cooked is in [[Cooking|Cooking]].

### Burning things

Hold something that burns (a log, a stick, cut grass, bread…) and press {{key|G}} at a lit campfire: “… is thrown into the fire.” It lands on the fire's cell and burns to {{item|Ash}}. Ash fertilizes crops (see [[Farming|Farming]]).

### A safe place at night

Apart from fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 cells of a lit fire (or a burning thing), and do not chase anyone standing there. Stand by a campfire at night and most [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] only prowl outside.

### Warming up

Press {{key|E}} at a lit campfire with empty hands (“Warm yourself by the campfire”):

- **When you are wet**: the fire touches you with a force that does no harm and dries you: “You warm yourself by the campfire, and the damp steams away.” {{status|wet}} goes at once, with no health lost. Caught in the rain, splashed with water, or through the mud, and you do not want to wait 40 seconds to dry by yourself? Come to the fire and press once. Wet people can be frozen by ice and shocked by lightning (see [[Elements|Elements]]), so dry off before fighting frost slimes.
- **When you are not wet**: only “The campfire's warmth seeps into your palms.”, with a little glow; nothing changes. The game has no temperature, and warming up does not restore health.
- **When you are frozen**: in the rules, a frozen person warming up thaws (and is wet once thawed, needing another warm-up to dry). But while {{status|frozen}} you cannot act and cannot press {{key|E}}, so you cannot use this yourself.
- Every lit thing that can be lit can be used to warm up like this: the campfire, [[brazier|brazier]], [[lamp post|lamp post]], [[candles|candles]], [[lantern post|lantern post]], [[fireplace|fireplace]], [[forge|forge]], [[cauldron|cauldron]]. Pressing {{key|E}} bare-handed at an unlit campfire is not covered by any rule (the prompt carries a “?”).

### Other

- **Kitchen**: a campfire is a cooking fire. A [[Rooms|room]] with a campfire inside counts as a kitchen (no effect for now).
- **Light**: at night there is a ring of light around a campfire, a visual effect only.
- **No healing**: a campfire does not restore health, and neither does warming up.

### Breaking

A campfire is stone, with 6 durability. Fists get “Too hard.”; a {{item|Pickaxe}} breaks it in one blow, dropping 1 stone and 1 stick (half of what it is made of). A fire ring not yet finished, taken apart with a pickaxe, gives back every material put into it.

## Crafting

The campfire is not in the crafting panel ({{key|C}}); it is built in Building mode or by hand; see [[#Source|Source]] above.

## Trivia

- The same thing is called “Campfire” in Building mode and “campfire” standing in the world.
- [[Wood for the Fire]] is a daily quest in [[Firstlight Village]]: [[Gus]] wants 4 logs a day to feed the village fire. Adding wood does not change the fire itself; a campfire never goes out anyway.
