The game has six elements: fire, water, ice, lightning, wind and earth. An element reacts with what it touches: fire sets hay alight, water puts fire out, ice freezes a wet slime, lightning runs across wet ground. These reactions are all rules, and they treat the player, residents, monsters, items and the ground alike.

## The six elements

{{element table}}

A weapon or staff that carries an element has the element's title in its name: of Embers (fire), of Tides (water), of Frost (ice), of Storms (lightning), of Gales (wind), of Stone (earth).

## Where elements come from

- **Weapons**: a weapon, staff or wand with an element puts its element on whoever it hits (the blow's own damage still counts).
- **Creatures**: some monsters carry an element of their own, and their attacks carry it: {{monster|fire slime}}, {{monster|fire imp}}, {{monster|dragon whelp}}, {{monster|the dragon of Cinder Peak}} (fire); {{monster|frost slime}}, {{monster|frost wolf}}, {{monster|ghost}} (ice); {{monster|stone golem}} (earth); and others.
- **Torches, flint and steel, candles**: hold one and left-click a thing, and you touch it with fire.
- **Water**: a full bucket, flask or waterskin; left-click a thing to splash water on it.
- **Weather**: rain is water without force, falling on people, creatures and burning things in the open; in a thunderstorm a bolt of lightning strikes every so often (see [[Weather|Weather]]).
- **The ground**: stepping into mud gets you wet; a burning thing sets alight what touches it; electrified ground shocks whoever stands on it.

## Fire

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Something oiled | Explodes: it catches fire itself, and burnable things within 1 cell have a 70% chance of catching fire too. |
| Something wet | Dried (it steams); it does not catch fire. A wet creature also takes damage (force × 0.8). |
| Something frozen | Thawed, and becomes wet. A frozen creature also takes damage (force × 0.5). |
| Something that can be lit (campfire, brazier, lamp post, candles, lantern post, forge, cauldron) | Lit ({{status|lit}}) if it was not; one already lit does not change, and a wooden lantern post does not catch fire from it. |
| Something burnable (wood, hay, trees, wooden walls, hides, cloth…) | Catches fire ({{status|burning}}). |
| Something explosive ({{item|Firecracker}}, {{item|Blackpowder Bomb}}) | Bursts the moment it catches fire: everything in the four cells beside it (up, down, left, right) takes a fire blow (force 1 for a firecracker, 8 for a bomb), burnable things in those cells are set alight, and it is gone. |
| A fire creature | Restores 2 health. |
| Other creatures and people (not wet, not frozen, not already burning) | Catch fire, for 2.5 seconds. |
| Raw food | Cooked (see [[Cooking|Cooking]]). |
| Ice | Melts into water. |
| Anything else | No change. |

**Warming yourself**: with empty hands, press {{key|E}} at a lit fire (a campfire, brazier, forge…) and the fire touches you with a force of 0: if you are wet you are dried, if you are frozen you thaw, and you take no damage either way (damage goes by force). While frozen you cannot act, so in practice it is the drying that is useful. See [[campfire#Warming up|Warming up]].

**Once something burns**:
- Every half second a burning thing has an even chance of losing 1 durability (see [[Durability and Breaking|Durability and Breaking]]).
- Every half second it may pass the fire to burnable things beside it: the ground 7%, dry ground (dry grass) 30%, furniture 10%, dry furniture 30%, structures such as walls, floors, stairs and doors 10%. Wet or frozen things do not catch it.
- When wind blows on it, the fire leaps about within 2 cells (50% chance).
- When it burns out: ground becomes ash, a tree becomes a charred tree, a wooden wall becomes a burnt wall, furniture becomes a heap of {{item|Ash}} (what was in it falls out), and an item becomes ash.
- Structures ({{item|Wall}}, {{item|Floor}}, {{item|Stairs}}, {{item|Door}}) are still there when the fire goes out, only with less durability; only if their durability runs out while they burn do they become a heap of ash.
- Nothing catches fire on water.

## Water

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Something burning | Put out, and made wet ({{status|wet}}). |
| Something lit (a campfire, a lamp) | Goes out. |
| Water itself | No change. |
| A fire creature | Takes damage (force × 1.2) and becomes wet; rain only makes them wet and smoking. |
| A crop still growing | Watered (see [[Farming|Farming]]). |
| Anything else | Becomes wet. |

A wet thing takes 40 seconds to dry. Wet things cannot be set alight by fire, but they can be frozen by ice and shocked by lightning.

## Ice

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Something burning | Puts out the fire. |
| Something wet | Frozen ({{status|frozen}}, unable to move for 4 seconds), and at the same time it begins 8 seconds of {{status|thawing}}: it cannot be frozen again in those 8 seconds. |
| Shallow water | Freezes into thin ice that people can walk on; water within 1 cell has a 70% chance of freezing too. Thin ice melts back into water after 150 seconds, and whoever stands on it is put ashore. |
| An ice creature | Restores 2 health. |
| Anything else | No change. |

**A frozen body fears blunt weapons and earth**: hit by a blunt weapon or an earth attack, it takes damage × 1.5, and the ice shatters.

## Lightning

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Something wet | No longer wet but {{status|electrified}} (unable to move for 0.8 seconds); the lightning runs on to wet things within 2 cells (90% chance). A wet creature also takes damage (force × 0.8). |
| Something conductive (metal, water) | Electrified. Electrified water passes the lightning on to conductive and wet things within 2 cells. |
| Something dry and burnable (dry grass, a haystack) | Catches fire. |
| A lightning creature | No change. |
| A golem | Takes damage (force × 0.5); it is not electrified. |
| Other creatures | Electrified. |

Half the time, a bolt in a thunderstorm strikes a tall thing (a dead tree, say) within 12 cells of the player.

## Wind

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Something burning | The fire leaps about within 2 cells, with a 50% chance of setting burnable things alight. |
| Creatures and people | Blown 2 cells away, in the direction away from where the wind came. |
| Anything else | No change. |

Wind is one of the few things in the game that pushes people away (the other is bosses' skills).

## Earth

| Touches | Result |
|---|---|
| Fragile furniture (a clay pot and the like) | Shatters, and what was in it falls out. |
| A frozen creature | The blow's damage × 1.5, and the ice shatters. |
| Anything else | No change. |

## Oil

Oil is not an element, but it belongs with fire: hold a flask of lamp oil and left-click a thing, and the oil splashes on it; it is {{status|oiled}} for 90 seconds. An oiled thing explodes when fire touches it; see above. There is little lamp oil in the game: [[Tobin]]'s wagon has a flask (it is Tobin's), and [[The Mill Cellar]] holds the vigil lamp oil a quest needs.

## Resistances

Every element has its own resistance, which reduces that element's damage by a percentage: 100% means no damage at all, and a negative value means more damage. Drink a {{item|Fireproof Draught}} and for 300 seconds (5 game hours) you have 75% fire resistance and cannot catch fire ({{status|fireproof}}). Damage from elemental reactions counts as damage of that element.

## Tips

- Splash water first, then lightning: a crowd of wet enemies is shocked together, and the shock spreads.
- Strike a frozen enemy with a blunt weapon for half again the damage.
- Fire spreads fast on dry grass. On a rainy day a fire hardly takes.
- Do not play with fire in your own wooden house: the walls, floors and bed are all wood, and all burn.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Elemental reactions were in the game's first version: fire ignites, water douses, ice freezes wet things, lightning shocks wet things, oil explodes in fire.
- 2026-10-02: Ice freezes water into thin ice, which melts back into water after a while.
- 2026-10-04: Elements got their resistances; the Fireproof Draught became a status giving 75% fire resistance (W8).
- 2026-10-07: After being frozen once, a spell of “thawing” follows, so nothing is frozen again and again.
- 2026-10-08: Firecrackers and Blackpowder Bombs burst when they catch fire; burning structures spread fire and become ash when burnt through; a lit lantern post touched by fire does not catch fire.
- 2026-10-09: Warming up beside a lit fire: the fire touches a person with a force of 0, drying the wet and hurting no one.
