{{quote|Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.|The Church of the Five Sufferings}}

The **Church of the Five Sufferings** is the religion founded by [[Astra|Astra]]'s followers, and the prevailing faith of this world. People saw with their own eyes Astra bear the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]], and after the fifth they saw the sun again, so they came to believe that “Astra repaid the world's calamity with his own pain.” His five instruments of torment were enshrined as the highest relics (the [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]]), and so the Church of the Five Sufferings was founded.

## Doctrine

**The core doctrine** (§1):

> Suffering is not God's abandonment.
> Suffering is the proof the debt is being paid.

**The creed** (§5). Over time, the Church's most central creed became:

> “Suffering brings one nearer to God.”
> “As long as someone goes on bearing the Five Sufferings, the Long Night will never fall again.”

The original lore text says the second line is “not entirely wrong.”

**The theology of creation** (§2): God did not truly create light; he only separated light from [[The First Night|the First Night]]. The sun is the boundary that keeps them apart.

**The meaning of the Five Sufferings**: the Church has its own reading of each Suffering; the Crowning, for instance, is “giving up human pride and will”, and the Nailing is “accepting death”. See [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]].

In the game, the doctrine is spoken mostly by [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]]. She recites it on the night of thanks and at ordinary times:

> Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.

The Litany of the Five in the Chapel of the Five at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] reads: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid. As long as one bears the Five, the Long Night shall not return.” [[Father Caius|Father Caius]], stationed at the Rest, says: “I say it at the altar every morning, and on the hard mornings I say it twice.” The [[Church Courier|Church Courier]]'s only everyday line is also “Suffering is not God's abandonment.” At the end of Chapter 1, [[Rowan|Rowan]] says: “The Church tells us to be patient. To be honest, I am running out of patience.”

## Relics

The five instruments of torment used when Astra bore the Five Sufferings are the Church's highest relics: the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]], the Pillar of Scourging, the Scales of Hunger, the Holy Wood of the Nails and the Lance That Pierced the Heart. See [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]].

## Organization

### Seal-bearers

As time passed, it became hard for one person's body to bear the whole strain. The Church set up a new system: the power of the five Instruments of the Sufferings was divided among five “Seal-bearers”, who each share part of Astra's pain. See [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]].

### The Holy City

The center of the Church. The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage is issued “by order of the Holy City”; the end of the pilgrimage is to hold the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings again in the Holy City. The place is not yet in the game. See [[The Holy City|the Holy City]].

The Church's highest body is called the **Holy See** (a proposal of Chapter 1's design, docs/chapter-1.md §14). It posts notices at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] and has stationed [[Father Caius|Father Caius]] there to bless the pilgrims sent east.

### The Pilgrimage and pilgrims

When the Long Night returns and the Seal-bearers fail, the Church calls a pilgrimage and names a pilgrim. The pilgrim must find the five Seal-bearers, defeat them now that their Seals have twisted them, recover the five holy instruments, and finally hold the **Great Rite of the Five Sufferings** again in the Holy City (§7).

The workings of the pilgrimage that the game shows:

- **The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage**: the document the courier reads out and hands to the protagonist; see [[Proclamation of the Pilgrimage|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]].
- **The Scallop Badge**: Ilse says, “The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Every one of us is given one when we are named.” See [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]].
- **The previous pilgrim**: Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village was named pilgrim in the year 380 of the Era of the Five Sufferings and never came back. His camp is deep in [[The Forest|the Forest]]. See the quest [[The Pilgrim Before|The Pilgrim Before]].
- **The Holy See's notice**: the notice on the board at the Pilgrims' Rest: “BY THE HOLY SEE OF THE FIVE. The Seal-bearers, saints who bear what Astra bore, can bear it no longer. Pilgrims of the Five: seek out the five Seal-bearers, overcome them, and bring the five instruments home to the Holy City for the Great Rite. Before you go on, take the oath at the five wounds.” This one mission is all the Church tells its pilgrims.
- **The Pilgrim's Vow**: kneeling before the altar of the five wounds in the Rest's [[Chapel of the Five|Chapel of the Five]], the pilgrim recites the Five Sufferings after the priest, as the Church reads them. “Every pilgrim the Holy See sends east has knelt there first.” A pilgrim who has sworn the vow receives a [[Sworn Scallop|Sworn Scallop]].
- **Wayside shrines**: the five [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], one for each Suffering; the pilgrim lays on each shrine what its Suffering asks for, lighting them one by one along the way.
- **Penitents**: [[Brother Ezra|Brother Ezra]] whips himself: “The Seal-bearers bear Astra's pain for all of us, and I take a little of theirs, a stroke at a time.” He says this is what they teach at the Rest.
- **The pilgrims' book**: the register at [[The Pilgrims' Hostel|the Pilgrims' Hostel]]; beside each name is drawn a scallop, and beside many names is written “Went east. Did not come back.”
- **Kneeling stones**: in the White Knight's kingdom on [[The East Road|the East Road]] and on [[The Kneeling Field|the Kneeling Field]] stand “kneeling stones”, carved as pilgrims kneeling, raised for the pilgrims who would not kneel to him. The ones in the kingdom bear no names, and their scallop badges are worn almost smooth; the ones on the Kneeling Field are carved with names and with the year of the Crown-holder, the oldest dating from the 212th year of the Crown.

### The Order of the Thorn

An order of knights buried beneath [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]]. Every wall of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]] is a knight's burial niche, and the roll on the altar lists forty-one names, the last of them:

> ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER, WHO KNELT TO CHILDREN. TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN. HE HOLDS.

[[Fennick|Fennick]] was the Knight-Commander's squire; he says he “carried a lance in the Seal-bearers' escort once.” In the ossuary there is an empty niche carved with his name.

The order's abbey is [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]], north of the Pilgrim's Road, long abandoned. The roll in [[The Chapter Hall|the Chapter Hall]] likewise holds forty-one names, the last being “ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER — BEARER OF THE CROWN”, and “No knight was made after him.” The [[Writ of Relief|Writ of Relief]], under the Order's wax seal, saying another should be sent to relieve him after forty years, is also there, the wax whole, never sent.

### The Ashen Watch

An old order of the Church that kept the fire taken from the first ray of dawn. The Church dissolved it ten years ago and declared its last few brothers heretics. See [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]].

### The Church in the provinces

- **Firstlight's sacred fire**: the fire in the square of [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]]. The villagers call it the First Fire and believe it was taken from the first ray of dawn after the First Long Night. Beside it stands Astra's altar, carved: “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.” Its keeper is [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]], a sister of the Five Sufferings, who tends the sacred fire every dawn and dusk. In the game the fire is a lit [[campfire|campfire]]; in Chapter 1 the Grey Man takes it, and the altar has been cold ever since.
- **[[Firstlight Chapel|Firstlight Chapel]]**: Sister Ilse's chapel, just behind the sacred fire and Astra's altar. On the stone wall around Astra's figure are carved the five holy wounds, and the villagers kneel here to give thanks for the sacred fire outside. [[Brother Anselm|Brother Anselm]] serves inside and keeps the bookstand of skill books for sale.
- **[[The Pilgrims' Rest|The Pilgrims' Rest]]**: the Church's waystation on the east bank of the Ford's river, with the [[Chapel of the Five|Chapel of the Five]] and [[The Pilgrims' Hostel|the Pilgrims' Hostel]], kept by Father Caius.
- **[[Chapel of the First Dawn|The Chapel of the First Dawn]]**: an old roofless chapel on top of [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]]. Inside is “the first fire altar”, where the First Fire was lit, and in its bell tower hangs the bell that rang on the morning the sun came back.
- **[[five-wound shrine stone|Five-wound shrine stones]]**: roadside holy marks of the Five Sufferings, carved with five wounds. On the one at the [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]] the first wound has cracked open. Below the dawnstone on [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]] there are also five small wounds carved, and a flame.
- **The Sunwatch Post**: the Church once kept a Sunwatch Post on [[Cloudtop|Cloudtop]], where one person did nothing but record the time of sunset for the almanac. The reports later stopped. See [[The Watchman's Log|The Watchman's Log]].
- **The almanac**: [[Nessa Quill|Nessa Quill]] compiles the almanac for the Holy City and measures the heavens.

### The calendar

Documents in the game are dated by the Era of the Five Sufferings. The old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree in the forest is dated “the 380th year of the Era of the Five Sufferings”.

## What the Church hides

{{spoiler|What the Church does not know, or only its highest ranks know}}

- The Church does not know (or only a very few at the very top know) that what the Five Sufferings really keep up is not some holy covenant but “the vessel that is Astra” (§5).
- In the ruins the protagonist discovers many structures underground that carry power toward the Holy City, and that the pain the five Seal-bearers bear all ends up flowing to the same place (§9).
- The most deeply hidden doctrine of the Church of the Five Sufferings is: “Someone must always bear the Five Sufferings.” In other words: “There must always be an Astra.” (§11)
- The Church sends the protagonist on the pilgrimage not only because they are brave. The Great Rite of the Five Sufferings also needs a new Bearer of the Sufferings, and the whole pilgrimage is also a Sifting (§13). See [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]].
