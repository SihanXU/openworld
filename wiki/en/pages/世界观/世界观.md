OpenWorld's story takes place in a world that believes in the [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]]. Several hundred years ago the sun vanished for a time, an event known as [[The Long Night|the Long Night]]. The saint [[Astra|Astra]] bore five holy torments, and the sun rose again. As the game begins, the days are once more growing shorter by the day. The player comes down from the mountain with the fairy [[Lumi|Lumi]] at their side, is named a pilgrim by the Church, and sets out to find the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]].

The authority for the lore is the collaborator's document *The Church of the Five Sufferings: Setting and Main Story*; the full text is at [[Original Lore Document]]. This site only splits that text into pages, arranges them and links them to one another; it does not write new lore. Anything not yet settled in the original or in the team's discussions is marked “To be designed”.

## The official history

This is the account every piece of evidence supports early in the game, and the account the game's characters believe (the original, §8, calls it “the first layer of understanding”):

- Long ago the days grew shorter and shorter until the sun vanished altogether. Crops withered, beasts turned strange, and the dead found no rest. This calamity is called [[The Long Night|the Long Night]]; the people in the game call it “the First Long Night”.
- Astra willingly bore five holy torments, the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]]: [[The Five Sufferings#The Crowning|the Crowning]], [[The Five Sufferings#The Scourging|the Scourging]], [[The Five Sufferings#The Starving|the Starving]], [[The Five Sufferings#The Nailing|the Nailing]] and [[The Five Sufferings#The Piercing|the Piercing]]. When the fifth torment was complete, the sun rose again. Later ages honored him as “Astra, the First Martyr”.
- His followers founded the [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]] and enshrined the five instruments of torment as its highest relics, the [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]].
- Later, the power of the five holy instruments was divided among five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]], who shared Astra's suffering between them.
- Now the nights are lengthening again. The Church has declared that the five Seal-bearers can no longer hold. The protagonist is sent on a pilgrimage: to defeat the Seal-bearers twisted by their Seals, recover the five holy instruments, and hold the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings again in [[The Holy City|the Holy City]].

From the protagonist's point of view, this is a pilgrimage to save the world.

## The two reversals

The story is understood in three layers, and the last two are reversals. See [[Main Story|Main Story]] for details.

{{spoiler|The first and second reversals}}

- **The first reversal**: as the protagonist defeats the Seal-bearers and goes deeper into the Church's ruins, the records grow stranger and stranger. The protagonist arrives at a new explanation: “Astra was not the one who ended the Long Night. He may be the Long Night itself.” The five holy torments look more like five seals, and the goal becomes stopping the First Martyr's return.
- **The second reversal**: only at the very end does the protagonist understand: Astra carries [[The First Night|the First Night]], but he did not make it; rather, “he locked the First Night inside himself.” The nights are lengthening because “Astra can barely hold any longer”: “the cage is breaking.”
- **The rule of the world**: “The First Night cannot be killed.” The world must always have a [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]]. The most deeply hidden doctrine of the Church of the Five Sufferings is “someone must always bear the Five Sufferings.”
- **Why the protagonist was chosen**: besides the five holy instruments, the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings needs a new Bearer of the Sufferings. The whole pilgrimage is also a “Sifting”.

A decision the team added (2026-10-05): [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] (Severin) believes the first reversal from the start and works against the protagonist; by the middle of the game the protagonist comes to believe it too, the two of them hunt Astra together, and during the hunt they discover the second reversal. [[Lumi|Lumi]] is the failed Second Vessel.

1. The section “The official history” on this page.
2. [[The Long Night|The Long Night]], [[Church of the Five Sufferings|the Church of the Five Sufferings]] and [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]] (only each section's “The Church's reading”).
3. [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]] and the walkthrough parts of [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]].

1. [[The First Night|The First Night]] → [[The Long Night|the Long Night]] → [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]] → [[Instruments of the Sufferings|the Instruments of the Sufferings]], to understand what the Five Sufferings really do in the lore.

## How much the game shows

The game currently goes only as far as [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]] (the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns), so only the first layer of understanding appears in it:

- The people of [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] believe the official history. [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]] tells the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings and recites the Church's prayers.
- The days are getting shorter: [[Hesper|Hesper]], [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]] and [[Nessa Quill|Nessa Quill]] all say it gets dark earlier every week. After the first Seal-bearer falls, the nights really do lengthen (see [[Day and Night|Day and Night]]).
- The dead will not rest: the dead in [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]] “can't lie down”.
- The reversals show only at the edges: the Grey Man's actions, Lumi's reactions to the five-wound shrine stone and to the story of the holy lance, the previous pilgrim's camp. These clues are listed in [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]].
