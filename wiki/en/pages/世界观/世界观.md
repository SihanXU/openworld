OpenWorld's story takes place in a world that believes in the [[五苦圣教|Church of the Five Sufferings]]. Several hundred years ago the sun vanished for a time, an event known as [[极夜|the Long Night]]. The saint [[阿斯特拉|Astra]] bore five holy torments, and the sun rose again. As the game begins, the days are once more growing shorter by the day. The player comes down from the mountain with the fairy [[露米|Lumi]] at their side, is named a pilgrim by the Church, and sets out to find the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]].

The authority for the lore is the collaborator's document *The Church of the Five Sufferings: Setting and Main Story*; the full text is at [[世界观原文]]. This site only splits that text into pages, arranges them and links them to one another; it does not write new lore. Anything not yet settled in the original or in the team's discussions is marked “To be designed”.

## The official history

This is the account every piece of evidence supports early in the game, and the account the game's characters believe (the original, §8, calls it “the first layer of understanding”):

- Long ago the days grew shorter and shorter until the sun vanished altogether. Crops withered, beasts turned strange, and the dead found no rest. This calamity is called [[极夜|the Long Night]]; the people in the game call it “the First Long Night”.
- Astra willingly bore five holy torments, the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]]: [[五苦#戴冠|the Crowning]], [[五苦#受鞭|the Scourging]], [[五苦#受饥|the Starving]], [[五苦#受钉|the Nailing]] and [[五苦#受枪|the Piercing]]. When the fifth torment was complete, the sun rose again. Later ages honored him as “Astra, the First Martyr”.
- His followers founded the [[五苦圣教|Church of the Five Sufferings]] and enshrined the five instruments of torment as its highest relics, the [[圣刑之器|Instruments of the Sufferings]].
- Later, the power of the five holy instruments was divided among five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]], who shared Astra's suffering between them.
- Now the nights are lengthening again. The Church has declared that the five Seal-bearers can no longer hold. The protagonist is sent on a pilgrimage: to defeat the Seal-bearers twisted by their Seals, recover the five holy instruments, and hold the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings again in [[圣城|the Holy City]].

From the protagonist's point of view, this is a pilgrimage to save the world.

## The two reversals

The story is understood in three layers, and the last two are reversals. See [[主线剧情|Main Story]] for details.

{{剧透|The first and second reversals}}

- **The first reversal**: as the protagonist defeats the Seal-bearers and goes deeper into the Church's ruins, the records grow stranger and stranger. The protagonist arrives at a new explanation: “Astra was not the one who ended the Long Night. He may be the Long Night itself.” The five holy torments look more like five seals, and the goal becomes stopping the First Martyr's return.
- **The second reversal**: only at the very end does the protagonist understand: Astra carries [[原夜|the First Night]], but he did not make it; rather, “he locked the First Night inside himself.” The nights are lengthening because “Astra can barely hold any longer”: “the cage is breaking.”
- **The rule of the world**: “The First Night cannot be killed.” The world must always have a [[承夜者|Nightbearer]]. The most deeply hidden doctrine of the Church of the Five Sufferings is “someone must always bear the Five Sufferings.”
- **Why the protagonist was chosen**: besides the five holy instruments, the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings needs a new Bearer of the Sufferings. The whole pilgrimage is also a “Sifting”.

A decision the team added (2026-10-05): [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] (Severin) believes the first reversal from the start and works against the protagonist; by the middle of the game the protagonist comes to believe it too, the two of them hunt Astra together, and during the hunt they discover the second reversal. [[露米|Lumi]] is the failed Second Vessel.

{{/剧透}}

## Reading order

For players who want to avoid spoilers:

1. The section “The official history” on this page.
2. [[极夜|The Long Night]], [[五苦圣教|the Church of the Five Sufferings]] and [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] (only each section's “The Church's reading”).
3. [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] and the walkthrough parts of [[第一章|Chapter 1]].

For team members who want the whole lore:

1. [[原夜|The First Night]] → [[极夜|the Long Night]] → [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] → [[圣刑之器|the Instruments of the Sufferings]], to understand what the Five Sufferings really do in the lore.
2. [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] → [[阿斯特拉|Astra]] → [[承夜者|the Nightbearer]], to understand the whole system.
3. [[主线剧情|Main Story]] → each chapter → [[结局|Ending]].
4. When in doubt, look things up in the [[年表|Timeline]] and the [[术语表|Glossary]].

## How much the game shows

The game currently goes only as far as [[第一章|Chapter 1]] (the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns), so only the first layer of understanding appears in it:

- The people of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] believe the official history. [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] tells the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings and recites the Church's prayers.
- The days are getting shorter: [[赫斯珀|Hesper]], [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] and [[奈莎·奎尔|Nessa Quill]] all say it gets dark earlier every week. After the first Seal-bearer falls, the nights really do lengthen (see [[昼夜|Day and Night]]).
- The dead will not rest: the dead in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]] “can't lie down”.
- The reversals show only at the edges: the Grey Man's actions, Lumi's reactions to the five-wound shrine stone and to the story of the holy lance, the previous pilgrim's camp. These clues are listed in [[第一章|Chapter 1]].

The game's terms differ from the original lore text in a few places:
