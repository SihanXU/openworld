**Matthias, Giver of War** is the second of the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]], holder of the Second Seal, tied to the Second Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Scourging|the Scourging]], and to the holy instrument [[Instruments of the Sufferings#The Pillar of Scourging|the Pillar of Scourging]]. Matthias's figure is modeled on the rider on the red horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, Matthias is the boss of [[Chapter 2|Chapter 2]].

## Lore

{{image|seal2-war-giver-mattia.png|Character art of Matthias, Giver of War (drawn by the collaborator)|right}}

- **Seal**: the Second Seal.
- **Torment**: the Scourging. Astra was bound to a pillar and scourged at length; legend says that even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall. The Church reads it as “repaying everyone's sins with the flesh”.
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: the Pillar of Scourging.
- Like the other Seal-bearers, Matthias shares part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.

{{spoiler|What Matthias bears}}

- Matthias is one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bears “the bodily pain of the scourging” (§12).
- What the Scourging really does is let the flesh gradually adapt to the constant pain and transformation [[The First Night|the First Night]] brings (§4).

## In the game

{{tbd}}

Matthias is not yet in the game: there is no chapter, place, boss skill or dialogue for this Seal-bearer, and no [[Boons|Boon]] that Lumi receives when Matthias falls. The original lore text says nothing more about Matthias than the above.

After [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]], what currently follows in the game is an older storyline that predates this setting, [[Emberfall and the Dragon|Emberfall and the Dragon]], which will later be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal.
