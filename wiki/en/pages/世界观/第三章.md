**Chapter 3**, by the structure of the main story, is the chapter of the Third Seal: the protagonist faces the third [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]], [[Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale|Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale]], and recovers the third [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instrument of the Sufferings]], the Scales of Hunger. The chapter's places, people, quests and boss fight have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

## What is already decided

- **Seal-bearer**: Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale, holder of the Third Seal, modeled on the rider on the black horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation (§6).
- **Torment**: the Third Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Starving|the Starving]]. Astra gave up food and drink and let his body be weighed on the scales against grain, against sin and against life. Scripture preserves one line: “His flesh at last weighed less than sin.” The Church reads it as “freedom from the desires of the flesh” (§4).
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: [[Instruments of the Sufferings#The Scales of Hunger|the Scales of Hunger]].
- **Chapter structure**: one Seal-bearer per chapter; when the Seal-bearer falls, [[Lumi|Lumi]] catches part of the Seal's power and gains a [[Boons|Boon]]; and the nights grow a little longer again. The Third Seal's Boon and how much longer the nights grow are both to be designed.

{{spoiler|What Ehle bears}}

Ehle bears the hunger and the wasting of life (§12). What the Starving really does is cut the bearer's dependence on food, sleep and the ordinary needs of life (§4).
