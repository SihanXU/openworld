**Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins** is the fourth of the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]], holder of the Fourth Seal, tied to the Fourth Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Nailing|the Nailing]], and to the holy instrument [[Instruments of the Sufferings#The Holy Wood of the Nails|the Holy Wood of the Nails]]. The figure is modeled on the rider on the pale horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, Nacht is the boss of [[Chapter 4|Chapter 4]].

## Lore

- **Seal**: the Fourth Seal.
- **Torment**: the Nailing. Astra's hands and feet were pierced by holy nails, and he was fixed to the holy wood. The Church reads it as “accepting death”.
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: the Holy Wood of the Nails.
- Like the other Seal-bearers, Nacht shares part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.

{{spoiler|What Nacht bears}}

- Nacht is one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bears “the erosion of death the holy nails stand for” (§12).
- What the Nailing really does is fasten Astra firmly to the real world: even as his soul begins to be eroded by [[The First Night|the First Night]], he does not slip out of reality entirely (§4).
