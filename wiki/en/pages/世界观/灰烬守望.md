**The Ashen Watch** is an order of the [[五苦圣教|Church of the Five Sufferings]] whose duty was to keep the fire taken from the first ray of dawn burning. Twenty years ago the Church dissolved it and declared its last few brothers heretics. [[灰衣人|The Grey Man]] of Chapter 1 (his name in the setting: Severin, the Greyvowed) was once one of them.

## Lore

- The Ashen Watch kept the fire taken from the first ray of dawn, that is, the light of the dawn that ended the first [[极夜|Long Night]]. The First Fire of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] is such a fire.
- The Church dissolved it twenty years ago and declared its last brothers heretics.
- Severin, the Greyvowed, was one of its brothers; in the game he is called [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]]. The people of Firstlight Village take him for a heretic and a thief.

The name “Ashen Watch” does not yet appear in the game's text.

## Traces in the game

In Chapter 1, nothing the Ashen Watch left behind says its name:

- **Old coins**: [[乔斯|Joss]] says the one who paid for the footbridge twenty years ago was a young man in grey, who paid in “old coin, no king on it.” [[托宾|Tobin]] says that last winter he sold an amulet of frost to a man in grey, who paid in the same coin. The design documents say these are the Watch's coins.
- **A grey thumbprint**: the loaf of bread in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]] has a grey mark pressed into it (see [[剧情物品#印着灰痕的面包|Loaf marked with ash]]); in [[守墓者圣所|the Warden's Sanctum]], a grey thumbprint is also pressed beneath the Knight-Commander's oath. The design documents say the mark on the bread is the Watch's sign.
- **A warm ember**: the stone that grows warm at night, which the Grey Man gave [[佩尔|Pell]]. The design documents say it is an ember left from a fire taken from the dawn. See [[温暖的余烬|Warm Ember]].
- **The First Fire**: the Grey Man gathers Firstlight Village's First Fire into a hooded lantern and carries it east.

{{剧透|What goes unsaid}}

- Severin is a foundling taken in by Firstlight's fire-keeper: [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] raised him. On the night of thanks she recognized his walk but told no one. She once read a book of the Watch that the Church burned (docs/chapter-1.md §7). In the game, at the end of Chapter 1, she says: “He always did carry things that were too heavy for him.”
- [[芬尼克|Fennick]] knows Severin too. After the First Fire is stolen, he says: “I knew a man like that. He would not. Unless he had a reason, and that scares me more.”
- From the start, Severin believes Astra is a monster: the sealed First Long Night itself, waking as the seals weaken; and the Church, by sending the seals' power to the Holy City, is waking him. So he works against the pilgrimage (the team's decision).
- He takes the First Fire to hold what each broken seal leaves behind, so that it never reaches the Holy City. How exactly he uses the flame is not yet decided (docs/chapter-1.md §5 marks it as a proposal).
- His rules: he never harms anyone and never explains a word more than he must. Each of his acts also protects someone. The player never fights him.
- By the middle of the game the protagonist comes to share his belief, the two of them hunt Astra together, and during the hunt they discover the second reversal: Astra is not the night but the night's cage, and Severin had been wrong all along about what he was fighting.
