{{引言|The White Knight knelt, and gave up the Crown. The grey man took the light east. And the night came a little sooner.|Chapter 1's end card}}

**Chapter 1, “The First Seal: the Crown of Thorns”**, is the main story's first chapter, and the one chapter fully built in the game so far. The days grow shorter by the day. The protagonist comes down from the mountain with [[露米|Lumi]] and arrives at [[初原村|Firstlight Village]], a village named for the first dawn after the First Long Night, whose sacred fire is said to have been taken from that day's first ray of dawn. The footbridge to the east has been torn apart plank by plank by a rider in white wearing a crown of thorns. The protagonist helps the village rescue two people from the barrow; on the night of thanks, a man in grey takes the sacred fire; the next morning, the Church's courier brings the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage. The protagonist crosses the river, takes the Pilgrim's Vow at the Church's [[朝圣者驿站|Pilgrims' Rest]], lights the five shrines along [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], reads the knight's past in the abandoned [[荆棘修道院|Abbey of the Thorn]], crosses [[跪石原|the Kneeling Field]], finds the first [[持印者|Seal-bearer]], [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]], on [[东大路|the East Road]], defeats him, and wins the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]. That evening, the sun sets early.

This chapter tells only the first layer of understanding: it never questions the official history and never lies (see [[主线剧情|Main Story]]). For a step-by-step walkthrough, see [[主线流程|Main Quest Walkthrough]].

## Walkthrough

Below, the chapter is written section by section in the order actually built in the game. The trigger conditions are in parentheses.

### Down from the Mountain

A new game begins on [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]]. Go down to the [[山脚|Mountain Foot]], past the [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stone]] with its first wound cracked, and on down to [[渡口|the Ford]]. See [[序章|Prologue]].

### The Ford

The first time you reach the Ford, the ferrywoman [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] comes to meet you:

> The storm took the ferry rope. The footbridge it did not take: a rider did. All in white, a crown of thorns grown through his helm. He pulled the planks out with his hands and laid the nails on my pier like a toll paid.

She says there is an old axe by the stumps and three planks would mend the bridge; but two people from Firstlight Village to the west have gone missing, and the bridge can wait, so help them first. She gives the player a piece of bread. Thorns are driven into the two broken stumps of the footbridge (“Thorns driven into the stump”).

### Firstlight Village

The first time you enter the village, Sister Ilse welcomes the player by the sacred fire: “the flame was lit from the first dawn after the First Long Night.” The boy Pell wants to draw Lumi; the village head, Rowan, says two people have gone missing and asks you to come to him once you have warmed up. Rowan's quest [[古墓里的失踪者|Lost in the Barrow]] then begins.

In the village you can see: the sacred fire and Astra's altar beside it; Pell's chalk drawing by the sacred fire, “HE WAS HERE FIRST”; Nessa's table where she records sunset times; the waypoint statue at the edge of the square.

### To the barrow

The barrow is south of the village; on the way you pass through, in order:

- **[[草原|The Grassland]]**: on your first visit, Lumi notices the cows are all bunched up at the north end, away from the trees. At dusk (17:00–20:00), the whole herd kneels, all facing east. The herder Brin says: “Since the bridge went.” After dark the dead come up out of the trees (her quest [[草原守夜|Night on the Grassland]]).
- **[[森林|The Forest]]**: no birds sing. Deep in the forest's southeast is a camp long unused: a rotted tent, a cold fire ring, and a cairn with a pilgrim's staff leaning on it; nailed to a tree is an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, word for word the same as the one the protagonist later receives, only with a different name. Beside the cairn lies a [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]]. Come back after dark (20:00–04:00), and a pale shade kneels by the fire ring: “I knelt. I knelt to him. Do not kneel.”
- **[[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]]**: Lumi senses that “the dead are sleeping badly” down below. On the carved standing stones on either side of the barrow mouth is a knight wearing a crown of thorns with a row of small figures kneeling before him: “THE ORDER OF THE THORN HOLDS”. There is a waypoint statue beside the entrance.

### The barrow's three levels

- **[[古墓|The Old Barrow]]**: find [[莉娜|Lina]] in the small cell to the west. She says it was not the dead who shut them in, but a man in grey, who pulled the inner door to from outside and then pushed a loaf of bread through the gap, with a grey thumbprint on the bread. Oskar went down the stairs in the east cell. This “Loaf marked with ash” lies in the cell. On the ground at the barrow's door is Pell's drawing of the door.
- **[[荆棘骸骨堂|The Ossuary of the Thorn]]**: a whole order of knights is buried here, every niche carved with a name, and the dead are not sleeping either. There is an empty niche carved “Fennick, squire to the Commander”, swept clean. The roll on the altar ends with Alberic's name. Find [[奥斯卡|Oskar]] in the southeast cell; he heard something below walking all night, back and forth, “like a guard on a wall.”
- **[[守墓者圣所|The Warden's Sanctum]]**: the [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]] stands guard before an altar: “it still thinks it has a post to keep.” When you defeat it, it says: “The night... is coming back. I could not... lie down.” The altar is carved: “I will hold until I am relieved. — Alberic.” Below is a grey thumbprint. Lumi says: “He swore to hold... and nobody ever came to relieve him.”

Return to the village and hand the quest in to Rowan.

### The Vigil

Once Lina and Oskar are home, Ilse wants to keep a vigil at the sacred fire for those who came back (the quest [[守夜之备|The Vigil]]):

- **[[水车磨坊|Millbrook]]** (west of the village): the waterwheel turns for a while and then stops. The miller, [[塔姆|Tam]], says something has got into the cellar.
- **[[磨坊地窖|The Mill Cellar]]**: slimes and spiders, and two flasks of vigil oil on the shelves.
- **[[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]]** (north of the village): this is said to be where the sun rose again after the First Long Night, and the flowers all face east. There is the dawnstone carved “HERE THE SUN ROSE AGAIN”, and dawnbloom grows all around. On the hill are wolves and a waypoint statue.
- **[[初晓礼拜堂|The Chapel of the First Dawn]]** (on top of Dawn Hill): roofless and cold. “The first fire altar” is where the First Fire was lit. Step into the bell tower in the northeast corner and ring the old bell, and its sound rolls all the way to Firstlight Village.

### The tale at dusk

(After the two are rescued and the vigil is ready, in Firstlight Village between 17:00 and 21:00) Ilse tells the villagers the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings. When she reaches the spear going through Astra's heart, Lumi is shivering; she says she is only cold. Ilse recites: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.”

### The night of thanks

(Same conditions, in Firstlight Village between 21:00 and 24:00) Rowan leads the whole village in giving thanks. The screen goes dark and comes up again: a man in a grey cloak stands before the sacred fire and gathers the flame into a hooded lantern. The sacred fire goes out. Holt shouts “thief”; the grey man says only “...Not tonight.” and walks away east. Lumi asks: “Why did he look at me like that?” Holt says he made the river freeze under his feet and crossed it.

After this night, Ilse goes every morning to sweep the cold altar. If you go to the Ford now, Hesper says she too saw the man who walked across on the ice, and far off to the east a light bobbed along the road and went out.

### The letter

(The first morning after the night of thanks, in Firstlight Village) The [[圣教信使|Church Courier]] arrives:

> By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers fail. The Long Night returns. Let the pilgrimage be called.
> One has come down from the mountain with a fairy at their side. The Church names them pilgrim.

The courier stares at Lumi for a long time, says nothing, and hands the protagonist a [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]]. Ilse says: “The flame is gone and the Church calls you in the same night. I do not believe in coincidence, pilgrim.”

### The Pilgrim's Road (side quest)

Ilse's side quest [[朝圣之路|The Pilgrim's Road]] sends the protagonist to ask three people:

- Fennick explains how the White Knight fights: when he drives his sword into the ground, thorns grow in a ring, so stand inside the ring or keep well away, but never on it; when he draws his bow, don't stand straight in front of him. Afterward Fennick remembers: as a young man he carried Alberic's lance for six years, when Alberic had already worn the crown for two hundred and fifty years and would still halt the whole column to kneel on one knee and speak to a child; but the crown kept taking, and one morning he asked Fennick his name.
- Joss explains the bridge: three planks fill the gap, “Or go round some other way — the river does not care how.”
- Hesper tells of the rider; come to the Ford again afterward and she talks about the thorns in the stumps: “Like a man marking what is his.”

### Crossing the river

The goal of the objective “Mend the broken bridge” is a state: standing on the far bank. The ways built are:

- **Mend the bridge**: fell a tree with an axe, split it into planks, and press {{键|G}} to lay three planks on the water.
- **The ferry**: fit the rope from Hesper's chest to the ferry winch, then ask her to take you across.
- **The dead tree**: fell the tree at the narrows, dead for ten years, so that it lies across the river.
- **Freeze it**: trade food to [[托宾|Tobin]] for the [[剧情物品#寒霜魔杖|Frost Wand]] on his cart, freeze the river and walk across.
- Any way of getting someone across the river that [[世界会学|the world learns]] counts too.

You can cross before the courier comes, but the main story waits for the pilgrimage to begin before it moves on. Once you are on the far bank, Hesper says the road east comes first to the Church's Rest; the rider went on far past it, beyond the burned farm: “If you find him — mind his bow.”

### The Pilgrim's Road

The east exit on the far side of the Ford does not lead straight to the East Road. Heading east, there are four places in turn, all objectives of The First Seal, but none of them blocks the way.

- **[[朝圣者驿站|The Pilgrims' Rest]]**: the Church's stopping place for pilgrims. On the notice board is the Holy See's notice: “BY THE HOLY SEE OF THE FIVE. The Seal-bearers are saints: they carry what Astra carried, and they can carry it no longer. Pilgrims of the Five: seek out the five Seal-bearers, overcome them, and bring the five instruments home to the Holy City for the Rite. Take the oath at the five wounds before you go on.” (After the letter) [[凯乌斯司铎|Father Caius]] comes to meet you: “A pilgrim, at last — and with a fairy, as the letter said.” Lumi says: “Kneel again? Everyone out here wants someone to kneel.”
- **The oath**: before the altar of the five wounds in the [[五伤礼拜堂|Chapel of the Five]], the priest leads the protagonist through the Five Sufferings as the Church reads them (the Nailing: “Upon the holy wood he accepted death”), and finally says: “The Seal-bearer has held that road three hundred years. The Holy See asks you to overcome him and bring home the Crown, the first of the five. ...He was due relief, once. That is not a pilgrim's concern.” The protagonist receives a [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]]. The Litany of the Five in the chapel reads: “As long as one bears the Five, the Long Night shall not return.” In the book at [[朝圣者客舍|the Pilgrims' Hostel]], beside many names is written “Went east. Did not come back.” The pilgrim [[莫德|Maud]] and her daughter [[温|Wynn]] have stopped at the Rest, and Wynn sings the White Knight's nursery rhyme: “he kneels to the small and he stands for the weak—”
- **[[朝圣者之路|The Pilgrim's Road]]**: five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]], one for each Suffering, each of which lights only when what its Suffering asks for is laid on it: something worn on the head, a cord, something to eat, a piece of metal, a light. The signposts are wound with thorns: “EAST: THE KINGDOM. NONE CROSS THAT DO NOT KNEEL.” The white pennants along the road grow more tattered the further east they are, and on the last, “Whoever mended them stopped remembering how.” The penitent [[以斯拉修士|Brother Ezra]] whips himself to share the Seal-bearers' pain; he once got as far as the Kneeling Field and saw the knight mending a pennant, whose hands stopped when he saw him. Lumi says: “A knight who mends flags? I'd like to see that. ...From a safe distance.” When all five shrines are lit, the light of the middle one points to the path leading north to the abbey.
- **[[荆棘修道院|The Abbey of the Thorn]]**: the order's abandoned abbey, where dead brothers still keep the hours of prayer. The statue in the courtyard shows Alberic on the day he was knighted, kneeling on one knee before a child and holding out a mended wooden horse. In the courtyard stand memorials to the order's dead (who are buried in [[荆棘骸骨堂|the Ossuary of the Thorn]] beneath Thornwood Hollow), one of them to the warden, Brother Tomas: “sworn brother to the Commander, sent to ward the Order's dead under the hollow.” In [[骑士团大厅|the Chapter Hall]] are the order's roll (forty-one names, the last being “ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER — BEARER OF THE CROWN”, and “No knight was made after him”), his journal (mending a kite; “My brother Hugh says it is a sentence”; “Forty years, and then relief”; in the forty-first year, “I forgot Hugh's face today”; at the end only “Hold.”), and the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] that was never sent: “Let him hold. He will not know the difference.” In [[修道院地下墓室|the Abbey Crypt]], [[温德尔院长|Prior Wendel]], who held back the writ, guards [[荆棘骑士团印章|the Order's Seal]]: “We hold, that others need not.” If you see Father Caius again after finding the writ, he admits: “Yes. I knew.”
- **[[跪石原|The Kneeling Field]]**: pilgrims kneel here to pray and then go on their way. Rows of stones carved in kneeling figures bear the names of earlier pilgrims and the year of the Crown-holder, each one carved with their refusal to kneel; the oldest is from the 212th year of the Crown, and the newest has no name carved on it yet. Between the stones, scarecrows in pilgrims' coats are bound kneeling on the ground, all facing east, and someone keeps setting them upright. Lumi says: “‘would not kneel’, every one of them. Stubborn! I like them already.”

### The East Road

- The first time you walk onto [[东大路|the East Road]], Lumi says the sun is lower than it was this morning, and that someone has planted the thorns along the road in a line.
- **The burned farmstead**: everything has burned except the house. In the ashes are claw marks longer than an arm (a clue for the older storyline [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]]).
- **The barn**: [[艾达|Edda]] is hiding inside. The days were too short and the wheat died; the things of the night came; a knight in white took everything that shone, including her late husband's ring that she had hung on her doorpost. On the night the barn burned, a man in grey pulled her out of the smoke: “Don't tell them in the village.”
- **The Grey Man's warning** (after the courier has come, on the stretch of road before the thorns): the Grey Man stops the protagonist: “Pilgrim. Turn back.” He says that what the seals lock away is not the sky but Astra: “The night you see getting longer — that is him, waking up.” To Lumi he says: “You of all of them should know, little one. ...No. You would not remember.” Finally he says: “The knight past those thorns is a good man in a cage he did not choose. ... But when the crown comes off, what it lets go is mine.” Then he leaves. There is no fight.
- **The waypoint statue before the thorns**: Lumi says you can rest here, and later, once there is more than one power, change it here too; past the thorns it is even colder than at the shrine stone.
- **The knight's kingdom**: a ring of thorns encloses a field, and in the field stand kneeling stones carved as kneeling pilgrims. Also in the kingdom are [[第九根钉子|the Ninth Nail]], Edda's [[婚戒|Wedding Band]], a pilgrim's earring by a kneeling stone, and the gauntlets of one of the White Knight's retainers. When you step into the ring of thorns, the White Knight speaks:

  > None cross that do not kneel.
  > Another pilgrim. They always send another.
  > I hold. I... hold. I do not remember what. Kneel.

### The White Knight

{{主条目|白骑士·阿尔贝里克}}

The White Knight uses three skills: Arrow of Conquest (a straight line ahead of him), White-Feather Volley (five spots around the target) and Thorns Bend the Knee (a ring around him, with safety both inside and outside the ring; anyone hit kneels for a moment). Before each attack, the cells it will land on are marked first; when his health drops to half, he enters the second phase.

The White Knight in Chapter 1 is weaker than the one in the [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]]: 300 health (1200 in the challenge), 1 armor, and his skills deal 40% of the challenge's damage. When he falls he drops 4 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]]; if the player has a [[职业|Profession]], also one of that profession's [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]]. Only after he falls does the road to [[余烬镇|Emberfall]] open.

### The Crown of Thorns

(After the White Knight falls) The knight kneels and, for the first time in a very long time, takes the Crown of Thorns off his head with his own hands and holds it out: “Take it. It was never mine to hold.” The protagonist receives the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]].

A cold light comes loose from the crown and runs to Lumi “like water finding an old riverbed.” She receives her first [[恩赐|Boon]], “Crown's Insight”. Lumi asks: “It's mine? Is that allowed?”

The Grey Man steps out of the smoke: “...So it has started.” He opens his lantern, and the remaining light flows into the stolen sacred fire; his hands are shaking.

> Every seal you break makes the night longer. Ask your Church why.

He tells the protagonist to keep what Lumi caught, to go home and tell Rowan “the light is safer with me than with you,” and walks away east.

From this point the nights grow longer (see below). In the briars where the knight fell there is still one green thorn; the protagonist receives [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]], with which they can enter [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]].

### Return

(Back in Firstlight Village with the Crown of Thorns) Rowan says: “The Crown of Thorns. In Firstlight. ...And the heretic still has our flame.” Ilse says: “He always did carry things that were too heavy for him.” Nessa says: “Four minutes. The sun set four minutes early tonight. I am sure it means nothing.”

The chapter's end card appears on screen:

> Chapter One · The First Seal
>
> The White Knight knelt, and gave up the Crown.
> The grey man took the light east.
> And the night came a little sooner.

### Afterward

- The main quest [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]] begins (the first stage's log: “Chapter 2: the road east to Emberfall.”), continuing into the older storyline [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]]. By the setting, Chapter 2 should be the chapter of the second Seal; see [[第二章|Chapter 2]].
- Edda moves to Firstlight Village. Brin's cows no longer kneel; they just stand and look east.
- Alberic appears as steward of [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]. See [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]].

## The nights grow longer

After you defeat the White Knight, the nights really do grow longer: from then on night falls at 20:00 and day breaks at 05:30 (previously 21:00 and 05:00). The hours when [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear lengthen with them. In the story, Nessa says the sun set four minutes early. See [[昼夜|Day and Night]].

## Skipping and safety nets

- Each step's goal is a state, not a path.
- The player can ignore everything before and go straight to fight the White Knight. Once he falls, the earlier main quests (the barrow, the vigil, The First Seal) and The Pilgrim's Road all count as complete, and Lina and Oskar are home too; the tale at dusk, the night of thanks and the letter no longer play, and the welcome and the oath at the Pilgrims' Rest no longer happen.
- The oath, the shrines, the abbey and the Kneeling Field along the Pilgrim's Road never block the way; you can walk straight past them. The shrines do not take holy relics or important things, so the Crown of Thorns and the Order's Seal cannot be offered up by mistake.
- When every way across at the Ford is cut off, Hesper takes the player across the river herself.

## Clues

This chapter tells its story with things. The table below lists the traces the Grey Man leaves and how the villagers understand them.

| What is seen | How the villagers understand it |
|---|---|
| Pell's drawing of the shrine stone: the first wound blacked out, a grey figure with its hand on it | The heretic is defiling the holy stone |
| Lina: “A man in grey pulled the inner door to, from outside.” | He trapped people in the barrow |
| Pell's stone that grows warm at night | An uncanny thing from a stranger |
| He takes the First Fire and walks east on the ice | Theft of a relic; sorcery |
| He gathers the knight's scattered power into his lantern | The heretic is devouring the Seal |
| “Every seal you break makes the night longer. Ask your Church why.” | Heresy |

Other clues: Hesper and Ilse saying “it gets dark earlier every week”; Nessa's sunset table and the [[初夜历书|Almanac of the First Night]]; the kneeling herd; the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage in the forest, thirty-one years old; the kneeling stones in the kingdom and on the Kneeling Field; the roll in the ossuary and the oath in the sanctum; the journal in the abbey and the Writ of Relief that was never sent.

## Lore

{{剧透|The truth behind each of the Grey Man's acts}}

| What is seen | The truth |
|---|---|
| The grey figure by the shrine stone | He was checking how far the First Seal had failed. |
| He shut the door from outside | He was shutting the restless warden in, and he left the trapped people bread, pressed with the Ashen Watch's sign. |
| Pell's warm stone | An ember left from a fire taken from the dawn: the things of the night stay away from Pell's window because of it. |
| He takes the First Fire | He needs this dawn flame to hold what each broken seal leaves behind, so he takes it before the pilgrimage begins. How exactly he uses the flame is not yet decided. |
| He gathers the knight's power | He does not want it to reach the Holy City; he believes that sending it there will wake Astra. The flame flares gold, and his hands are shaking. |
| “The night grows longer” | It is true: the Seal-bearers are the Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra (§12). |

The Grey Man's rules: he never harms anyone and never explains a word more than he must; each of his acts also protects someone. The player never fights him.

{{/剧透}}

{{剧透|What the characters leave unsaid}}

- [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]] raised the Grey Man (Severin). On the night of thanks she recognized his walk but told no one.
- [[芬尼克|Fennick]] left the escort the night Alberic did not know his name. He also knew Severin.
- [[乔斯|Joss]]: the one who paid for the bridge twenty years ago was a young man in grey, with the Ashen Watch's old coins, wanting “a road out of here a cart could take in a hurry.” Joss tells this after Chapter 1 ends.
- [[奈莎·奎尔|Nessa Quill]]: the last pages of the old almanac were written as the First Long Night fell, in a hand begging someone to “stop him”; she cannot make sense of it. This is the first misreading behind the setting's first reversal. Once you have the [[初夜历书|Almanac of the First Night]], hold it and left-click to use it on yourself to read these pages.
- [[艾达|Edda]] owes this heretic her life but dares not say so.
- The amulet of frost [[托宾|Tobin]] sold to the Grey Man is how the Grey Man walked across the river on ice.
- [[露米|Lumi]] is the failed Second Vessel. That is why she shivers by the shrine stone, shivers at the story of the holy lance, and catches the Seals' power. The courier stares at her for a long time and says nothing; the Grey Man knows what a fairy who drinks in Seals once was. See [[承夜者|Nightbearer]].
- The White Knight dies, in the end, as a man, not a monster.
