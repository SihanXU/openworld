**Chapter 4**, by the structure of the main story, is the chapter of the Fourth Seal: the protagonist faces the fourth [[持印者|Seal-bearer]], [[死之执辔者·纳赫特|Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins]], and recovers the fourth [[圣刑之器|Instrument of the Sufferings]], the Holy Wood of the Nails. The chapter's places, people, quests and boss fight have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

## What is already decided

- **Seal-bearer**: Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins, holder of the Fourth Seal, modeled on the rider on the pale horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation (§6).
- **Torment**: the Fourth Suffering, [[五苦#受钉|the Nailing]]. Astra's hands and feet were pierced by holy nails, and he was fixed to the holy wood. The Church reads it as “accepting death” (§4).
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: [[圣刑之器#钉刑圣木|the Holy Wood of the Nails]].
- **Chapter structure**: one Seal-bearer per chapter; when the Seal-bearer falls, [[露米|Lumi]] catches part of the Seal's power and gains a [[恩赐|Boon]]; and the nights grow a little longer again. The Fourth Seal's Boon and how much longer the nights grow are both to be designed.

{{剧透|What Nacht bears}}

Nacht bears the erosion of death the holy nails stand for (§12). What the Nailing really does is fasten Astra firmly to the real world, so that even as his soul begins to be eroded by the First Night, he does not slip out of reality entirely (§4).

{{/剧透}}
