One-sentence explanations of the lore's terms, grouped by theme. The explanations use only the first layer of understanding (the account everyone believes early in the game); terms that would spoil the reversals are gathered in the spoiler block at the end of this page. For the game's system terms (fullness, waypoint statues…), see their own pages.

## Heaven, earth and calamity

| Term | Explanation | See |
|---|---|---|
| The First Night | The darkness that existed before the world was formed. The sun keeps light apart from it. | [[原夜|The First Night]] |
| The Long Night | The calamity in which the First Night seeps into reality, the sun vanishes, and the world sinks into a long night. | [[极夜|The Long Night]] |
| The First Long Night | What the game calls the first Long Night. | [[极夜|The Long Night]] |
| The Second Long Night | The Church's warning: once the five Seals fail completely, the Long Night will come again. | [[极夜|The Long Night]] |
| The First Fire | The fire of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]], said to have been taken from the first ray of dawn after the First Long Night. In Chapter 1 the Grey Man takes it. | [[五苦圣教|Church of the Five Sufferings]] |

## Faith

| The Five Sufferings | The five holy torments Astra bore. | [[五苦|The Five Sufferings]] |
| The Crowning | The First Suffering: wearing the crown of thorns. The Church says it means giving up human pride and will. | [[五苦#戴冠|The Crowning]] |
| The Scourging | The Second Suffering: being bound to a pillar and scourged. The Church says it means repaying everyone's sins with the flesh. | [[五苦#受鞭|The Scourging]] |

## Orders and institutions

| Term | Explanation | See |
|---|---|---|
| The Holy City | The center of the Church of the Five Sufferings and the end of the pilgrimage. | [[圣城|The Holy City]] |
| The Holy See | The Church's highest body; it issues notices and has stationed a priest at the Pilgrims' Rest. | [[五苦圣教#圣城|The Holy City]] |
| The Pilgrimage | The mission the Church calls when the Long Night returns. | [[五苦圣教#朝圣与朝圣者|The Pilgrimage and pilgrims]] |
| The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage | The document with which the Church calls the pilgrimage and names the pilgrim. | [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]] |
| The Scallop Badge | The pilgrim's mark; everyone who is named receives one. | [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]] |
| The Pilgrim's Vow | The oath a pilgrim takes kneeling before the five holy wounds before setting out, led by the priest at the Pilgrims' Rest. | [[五伤礼拜堂|Chapel of the Five]] |
| Crown-holder | The reckoning of years on the kneeling stones, counted from when the first Seal-bearer put on the Crown of Thorns. He has held for three hundred years. | [[跪石原|The Kneeling Field]] |
| The Ashen Watch | The old order that kept the fire taken from the first ray of dawn, dissolved twenty years ago. | [[灰烬守望|The Ashen Watch]] |
| Heretic | What the Church declared the last few brothers of the Ashen Watch; the people of Firstlight Village also call the Grey Man this. | [[灰烬守望|The Ashen Watch]] |
| The Order of the Thorn | An order of knights buried beneath Thornwood Hollow; the White Knight was its Knight-Commander, and its abbey lies north of the Pilgrim's Road. | [[五苦圣教#荆棘骑士团|The Order of the Thorn]] |
| The Sunwatch Post | A post the Church once kept on Cloudtop to record the time of sunset. | [[守夜人的日志|The Watchman's Log]] |
