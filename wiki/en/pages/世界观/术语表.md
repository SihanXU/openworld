One-sentence explanations of the lore's terms, grouped by theme. The explanations use only the first layer of understanding (the account everyone believes early in the game); terms that would spoil the reversals are gathered in the spoiler block at the end of this page. For the game's system terms (fullness, waypoint statues…), see their own pages.

## Heaven, earth and calamity

| Term | Explanation | See |
|---|---|---|
| The First Night | The darkness that existed before the world was formed. The sun keeps light apart from it. | [[The First Night|The First Night]] |
| The Long Night | The calamity in which the First Night seeps into reality, the sun vanishes, and the world sinks into a long night. | [[The Long Night|The Long Night]] |
| The First Long Night | What the game calls the first Long Night. | [[The Long Night|The Long Night]] |
| The Second Long Night | The Church's warning: once the five Seals fail completely, the Long Night will come again. | [[The Long Night|The Long Night]] |
| The First Fire | The fire of [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]], said to have been taken from the first ray of dawn after the First Long Night. In Chapter 1 the Grey Man takes it. | [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]] |

| The Scallop Badge | The pilgrim's mark; everyone who is named receives one. | [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]] |

| The Sunwatch Post | A post the Church once kept on Cloudtop to record the time of sunset. | [[The Watchman's Log|The Watchman's Log]] |
