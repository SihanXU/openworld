**Chapter 2**, by the structure of the main story, is the chapter of the Second Seal: the protagonist faces the second [[持印者|Seal-bearer]], [[赐战者·马提亚|Matthias, Giver of War]], and recovers the second [[圣刑之器|Instrument of the Sufferings]], the Pillar of Scourging. The chapter's places, people, quests and boss fight have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

## What is already decided

- **Seal-bearer**: Matthias, Giver of War, holder of the Second Seal, modeled on the rider on the red horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation (§6).
- **Torment**: the Second Suffering, [[五苦#受鞭|the Scourging]]. Astra was bound to a pillar and scourged at length; legend says that even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall. The Church reads it as “repaying everyone's sins with the flesh” (§4).
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: [[圣刑之器#鞭刑柱|the Pillar of Scourging]].
- **Chapter structure**: one Seal-bearer per chapter; when the Seal-bearer falls, [[露米|Lumi]] catches part of the Seal's power and gains a [[恩赐|Boon]]; and the nights grow a little longer again (docs/chapter-1.md §10; “Agreed additions”). What the Second Seal's Boon is and how much longer the nights grow are both to be designed.
- **The Grey Man**: works against the pilgrimage from Chapter 1 onward; by the middle of the game the protagonist will come round to his side (“Agreed additions”). What he does in Chapter 2 is to be designed.

{{剧透|What Matthias bears}}

Matthias bears the bodily pain of the scourging (§12). What the Scourging really does is let the flesh gradually adapt to the constant pain and transformation the First Night brings (§4).

{{/剧透}}

## The game's current “Chapter 2”

In the game, the main quest after Chapter 1 ends is [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]], whose first log line reads “Chapter 2: the road east to Emberfall.” It continues into the older storyline [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]], which predates this setting: [[余烬镇|Emberfall]], [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]], [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]] and [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]]. That storyline is only a placeholder and will later be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal. The claw marks in the burned farmstead on [[东大路|the East Road]] in Chapter 1 are also a clue left for that older storyline.
