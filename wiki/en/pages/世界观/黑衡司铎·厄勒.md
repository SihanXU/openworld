**Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale** is the third of the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]], holder of the Third Seal, tied to the Third Suffering, [[五苦#受饥|the Starving]], and to the holy instrument [[圣刑之器#饥刑之秤|the Scales of Hunger]]. Ehle's figure is modeled on the rider on the black horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, Ehle is the boss of [[第三章|Chapter 3]].

## Lore

{{图|seal3-priest-of-the-black-scales-ele.png|Character art of Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale (drawn by the collaborator)|right}}

- **Seal**: the Third Seal.
- **Torment**: the Starving. Astra gave up food and drink and let his body be weighed on the scales against grain, against sin and against life. Scripture preserves one line: “His flesh at last weighed less than sin.” The Church reads it as “freedom from the desires of the flesh”.
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: the Scales of Hunger.
- Like the other Seal-bearers, Ehle shares part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.

{{剧透|What Ehle bears}}

- Ehle is one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bears “the hunger and the wasting of life” (§12).
- What the Starving really does is cut the bearer's dependence on food, sleep and the ordinary needs of life (§4).

{{/剧透}}

## In the game

{{待设计}}

Ehle is not yet in the game: there is no chapter, place, boss skill or dialogue for this Seal-bearer, and no [[恩赐|Boon]] that Lumi receives when Ehle falls. The original lore text says nothing more about Ehle than the above.
