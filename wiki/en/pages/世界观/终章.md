The **Final Chapter** is the main story's last chapter, after all five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] have fallen. The protagonist carries the five [[圣刑之器|Instruments of the Sufferings]] into [[至圣大殿|the Most Holy Hall]] and faces the final boss, [[阿斯特拉|Astra]]. The original lore text sets out what happens in this chapter and Astra's lines; the map, the walkthrough and the boss fight itself have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

## What is already decided

- The end of the pilgrimage is [[圣城|the Holy City]], where the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings is held again (§7).
- The protagonist carries the five holy instruments into the Most Holy Hall (§14).
- The final boss is Astra, the First Martyr (§14).
- The current version makes only the first ending, the preview ending (§16). See [[结局|Ending]].

{{剧透|What happens in the Final Chapter (§14–§15)}}

- At the center of the Most Holy Hall lies Astra's body. As the five holy instruments return to their places (the Crown of Thorns set on again; the scourge marks split open again; the Scales of Hunger rising once more; the holy nails fixing his body again; the Lance That Pierced the Heart entering his chest once more), Astra opens his eyes (§14).
- Astra knows he can no longer go on bearing the First Night, but he cannot simply make the protagonist the new [[承夜者|Nightbearer]]: if the new vessel cannot withstand it, the world will only be destroyed sooner. Only “one who can kill the present Nightbearer” is fit to succeed him. The final battle is not meant to stop the player from saving the world; it is “the last trial” (§15).
- Early in the fight, Astra still demands that the player leave: “Leave this place.” As the fight goes on: “You do not know what you are asking for.” At last, he is satisfied the player is strong enough: “Good.” “At least this time...” “I can die.” (§15)

{{/剧透}}

## Not to be confused with the game's “Final Chapter”

In the game, the last main objective of the older storyline [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]] used to be called “Final Chapter” as well: after you defeated [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]], the objective box showed “Final Chapter”. Now that storyline is the main quest [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]], which is complete as soon as the dragon falls, and the last line of its log is “The dragon is dead, and Emberfall can sleep.” That storyline predates this setting and has nothing to do with the Final Chapter this page is about.
