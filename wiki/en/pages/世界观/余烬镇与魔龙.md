**Emberfall and the Dragon** is the stretch of the main story that follows [[第一章|Chapter 1]] in the game: the protagonist travels east along [[东大路|the East Road]] to [[余烬镇|Emberfall]], helps the smith and the herbalist ready a dragon-slaying sword and a fireproofing draught, crosses [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]], and defeats [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] in [[魔龙巢穴|the Dragon's Lair]].

This storyline was written on 2026-10-02, before the current *Church of the Five Sufferings* setting. The setting has no dragon, and this storyline has nothing to do with the [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] or the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]]. It is now only placeholder content, kept in the game so there is still a road to walk after Chapter 1; it will later be replaced by the chapter of the second Seal (see [[第二章|Chapter 2]]). This page records it as it is in the game.

## Place in the main story

- Until [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]] is defeated, the East Road's exit toward Emberfall is closed. Once he falls, it opens.
- After you return to Firstlight Village with the Crown of Thorns and Chapter 1 ends, the main quest [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]] begins. Its first log line is “**Chapter 2: the road east to Emberfall.**”, and Lumi's hint is “The knight's kingdom is open. Beyond it, the road runs east to Emberfall.” The “Chapter 2” here is a name the old storyline kept, not the second Seal of the setting.
- The burned farmstead on the East Road in Chapter 1 (claw marks longer than an arm in the ashes) is a clue left for this storyline.

## Walkthrough

The whole storyline is one main quest, [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]]; the table below lists its stages.

| Stage | Where | What to do | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| East to Emberfall | The East Road → Emberfall | Go through the knight's kingdom and keep heading east | You walk in at Emberfall's gate |
| Find Mara | Emberfall | Find the elder, [[玛拉|Mara]] | You talk to Mara |
| Prepare to slay the dragon | The Old Mine; Firstlight Village; the Forest | Give the smith [[加里克|Garrick]] 5 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[煤炭|Coal]], give the herbalist [[雷恩|Wren]] 3 [[火焰草|Fireweed]] and 3 [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]], then talk to each of them | You have [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]] and the [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]] |
| Cinder Pass | Cinder Pass | Cross the mountain pass east of Emberfall | You reach the lair's entrance |
| The dragon of Cinder Peak | The Dragon's Lair | Defeat the dragon | The dragon falls and the quest is complete |

### Emberfall

On entering at the gate, the protagonist sees: “Half its roofs are new thatch; the rest are still black.” Talk to Mara: she tells you about the dragon on Cinder Peak, sends you to find the smith and the herbalist, and gives you two pieces of [[面包|Bread]]: “Nobody fights hungry.”

### The smith and the herbalist

- **Garrick** wants 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal. [[旧矿坑|The Old Mine]] is north of the town, and its ore veins must be broken open with a [[镐|Pickaxe]]; the richest iron vein is at the very bottom, guarded by a [[独眼巨人|cyclops]], which drops 2 Iron Ore when it falls. There is an “Old Pickaxe” at the mine's mouth.
- **Wren** wants 3 Fireweed (on the grass of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]]) and 3 Frostcap (in [[森林|the Forest]]).
- Hold each thing in your hand and press {{键|G}} to hand them over one at a time; once you have given enough, press {{键|E}} to talk to them. The quest tracker in the top right shows how many of each you have handed in.
- Garrick forges Dragonsbane: “Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge that one.” Wren brews two Fireproof Draughts: drink one at the lair's entrance, and the second when the first is about to wear off.

With both in hand, the protagonist gets ready in town and sets out, and the objective “Up Cinder Pass to the dragon's lair” appears.

### Cinder Pass

A snowy uphill road with more and more scorched rock and ash the higher it climbs. The lower stretch has [[霜狼|frost wolves]]; the upper stretch has [[火焰小鬼|fire imps]] and a [[石魔像|stone golem]]. Halfway up there is a lit [[营火|campfire]] where you can roast food and rest.

### The Dragon's Lair

When you step into the lair's entrance, “Something vast breathes in the dark,” and the last objective appears: “Defeat the dragon of Cinder Peak”.

- The dragon has 700 health; it breathes fire from 4 cells away and also bites.
- When it is hit while below half health, it gives a shriek and summons 3 [[幼龙|dragon whelps]] (only once).
- After you drink a Fireproof Draught, fire deals only a quarter of its damage, and you cannot be set alight.
- Dragonsbane deals double damage to the dragon and its whelps.
- Once the dragon is dead, the remaining whelps flee into the dark one by one.

When the dragon falls, an end card plays:

> The dragon of Cinder Peak
>
> The dragon is dead.
> Emberfall can sleep tonight, and the road east is open.
>
> Everything the world learned from you is kept in the Threadbook (J).

[[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]] is complete when the dragon falls. There used to be an objective after this called “Final Chapter”, unrelated to the setting's [[终章|Final Chapter]]; it is gone now.

## People

- [[玛拉|Mara]]: Emberfall's elder.
- [[加里克|Garrick]]: the smith, who forges Dragonsbane.
- [[雷恩|Wren]]: the herbalist, who brews the Fireproof Draught.

## Items

- [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]]: the sword Garrick forges, of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality, with the power “Dragonslayer”.
- [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]]: for a while after drinking it, fire damage is cut to a quarter and you cannot be set alight.
- Materials to hand in: [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]], [[煤炭|Coal]], [[火焰草|Fireweed]], [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]].
