**The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar** are the fifth of the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]], holder of the Fifth Seal, tied to the Fifth Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Piercing|the Piercing]], and to the holy instrument [[Instruments of the Sufferings#The Lance That Pierced the Heart|the Lance That Pierced the Heart]]. The figure is modeled on the martyrs of the fifth seal in the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, the White Witnesses are the boss of [[Chapter 5|Chapter 5]], and the last Seal-bearer before the [[Final Chapter|Final Chapter]].

## Lore

{{image|seal5-white-robed-witnesses.webp|Character art of the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar (drawn by the collaborator)|right}}

- **Seal**: the Fifth Seal.
- **Torment**: the Piercing. At last a holy lance pierced Astra's chest. At that very moment the sun rose again, and the Long Night ended.
- **Instrument of the Sufferings**: the Lance That Pierced the Heart.
- Like the other Seal-bearers, the White Witnesses share part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.

{{spoiler|What the White Witnesses bear}}

- The White Witnesses are one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bear “the wound to the soul the holy lance left” (§12).
- What the Piercing really does: when the blade went through his heart, Astra's body became, for the first time, an “entrance” joining reality and [[The First Night|the First Night]], and the First Night began to pour into his body (§4).
