**The Most Holy Hall** is where the main story's [[Final Chapter|Final Chapter]] takes place. The pilgrim walks in here carrying the five [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]], and the main story ends here. The place is not yet in the game; its map, appearance and location have not yet been designed. The original lore text says only that in the closing image it is underground (§17).

{{spoiler|What happens in the Most Holy Hall}}

- **The holy instruments return** (§14): the protagonist carries the five Instruments of the Sufferings into the Most Holy Hall. At its center lies [[Astra|Astra]]'s body. As the five holy instruments return to their places (the Crown of Thorns set on again; the scourge marks split open again; the Scales of Hunger rising once more; the holy nails fixing his body again; the Lance That Pierced the Heart entering his chest once more), Astra opens his eyes.
- **The last trial** (§15): Astra fights the protagonist to find out whether the protagonist can bear what he has borne for centuries. See [[Astra#As the final boss|As the final boss]].
- **The First Night runs out of control** (§16): after Astra dies, the First Night sealed within him for centuries begins to run out of control, and darkness spreads swiftly from the hall. The protagonist chooses to inherit the Five Sufferings, and the whole of the First Night enters the protagonist.
- **The new Nightbearer** (§16): outside, in the world, the sun rises again. But in the depths of the Most Holy Hall, the player can no longer leave. The protagonist takes Astra's place, becoming the new [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]].
- **The closing image** (§17): the camera returns to the Most Holy Hall underground. No music. Only the protagonist sitting in the dark, the holy lance still in their chest, the darkness behind them slowly writhing.
