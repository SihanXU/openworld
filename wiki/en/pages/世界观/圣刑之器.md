The **Instruments of the Sufferings** are the five instruments of torment used when [[Astra|Astra]] bore the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]]. The [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]] enshrines them as its highest relics. Later, the Church divided the power of the five holy instruments among five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]]. One of the pilgrim's tasks is to take them back from the Seal-bearers whom their Seals have twisted.

## The Crown of Thorns

{{main|Crown of Thorns}}

A crown of thorns covered in barbs. When Astra put it on, the thorns pierced his skull, yet it was never taken off. It is the first holy instrument, belonging to the First Seal.

In the game, the Crown of Thorns is the goal of [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]]: after [[The White Knight Alberic|Alberic, the White Knight]] falls, he kneels and, for the first time, takes the crown off with his own hands and holds it out to the protagonist: “Take it. It was never mine to hold.” It is head gear of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality, carrying a power all its own, “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”, and it also slows your experience.

## The Pillar of Scourging

Astra was bound to this pillar and scourged at length; even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall. It belongs to the Second Seal and is borne by Matthias, Giver of War.

Not yet in the game; see [[Chapter 2|Chapter 2]].

## The Scales of Hunger

Astra gave up food and drink and let his body be weighed on these scales against grain, against sin and against life. Scripture preserves the words: “His flesh at last weighed less than sin.” It belongs to the Third Seal and is borne by Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale.

Not yet in the game; see [[Chapter 3|Chapter 3]].

## The Holy Wood of the Nails

Astra's hands and feet were pierced by holy nails and fixed to this holy wood. It belongs to the Fourth Seal and is borne by Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins.

Not yet in the game; see [[Chapter 4|Chapter 4]].

## The Lance That Pierced the Heart

The holy lance that at last pierced Astra's chest. At the moment its blade went through his heart, the sun rose again. It belongs to the Fifth Seal and is borne by the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar.

Not yet in the game; see [[Chapter 5|Chapter 5]].

## The holy instruments and the Seal-bearers

As time passed, it became hard for Astra alone to go on bearing the whole strain. The Church of the Five Sufferings channeled the power of the five Instruments of the Sufferings into five Seal-bearers, who each bear part of Astra's pain (§6). Centuries of bearing it twisted them in the end. In Chapter 1, the Crown of Thorns has worn away the White Knight's self, and he remembers only that he must “hold”.

When a Seal-bearer falls, the power of the Seal scatters. [[Lumi|Lumi]] catches part of it, which becomes her [[Boons|Boon]]; in Chapter 1, [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] gathers the rest into his lantern.

## The holy instruments in the Final Chapter

{{spoiler|Where the five holy instruments end up}}

- The protagonist finally carries the five Instruments of the Sufferings into [[The Most Holy Hall|the Most Holy Hall]]. At its center lies Astra's body. The five holy instruments return to their places: the Crown of Thorns is set on again; the scourge marks split open again; the Scales of Hunger rise once more; the holy nails fix his body again; the Lance That Pierced the Heart enters his chest once more. Astra opens his eyes (§14).
- After the protagonist defeats Astra, the five Instruments of the Sufferings appear before the protagonist. The protagonist chooses to “inherit the Five Sufferings”: the Crown of Thorns settles on the protagonist's head, the scourge's wounds appear on their body, the Scales of Hunger come back into balance, the holy nails fix their flesh, and the Lance That Pierced the Heart enters their chest (§16).
- In the closing image, the protagonist sits in the darkness of the Most Holy Hall, the holy lance still in their chest (§17).
- In the truth of the setting, the five instruments of torment are not there to “commemorate Astra” but to hold him in place; they are more like five seals (§9, §10).
