The **Seal-bearers** are five offices established by the [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]]. Astra alone could hardly go on bearing the whole strain, so the Church divided the power of the five [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]] among five Seal-bearers, who each share part of Astra's pain. Centuries of bearing it twisted them in the end. As the game begins, the Church declares that the Seal-bearers are failing and sends out a pilgrim to defeat them and recover the holy instruments.

The figures of the five Seal-bearers come from the first five seals of the Book of Revelation. Each is the boss of one chapter of the main story.

“One Seal-bearer per chapter, and each leaves Lumi a Boon on falling” is the structure the team decided on.

## The Seal-bearers are failing

Several hundred years after the first [[The Long Night|Long Night]], the nights have begun to lengthen again. The Church of the Five Sufferings declares that the five Seal-bearers can no longer go on bearing the Five Sufferings; once the five Seals fail completely, the Second Long Night will come. So the protagonist is sent on the pilgrimage, to find the five Seal-bearers, defeat them now that their Seals have twisted them, and recover the five Instruments of the Sufferings (§7).

Chapter 1 shows what the failing looks like: on the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] at the [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]], the first wound has cracked open; the first Seal-bearer, the White Knight, remembers only that he must “hold”; he treats the land beyond the Ford as his own kingdom, and none cross that do not kneel.

## When a Seal-bearer falls

In the game, defeating a Seal-bearer brings about the following (so far only the White Knight):

- **The Seal's power scatters**: [[Lumi|Lumi]] catches part of it, which becomes one of her [[Boons|Boons]]. The First Seal gives “Crown's Insight” (experience +100%). The Boons panel reads: “Lumi takes up part of a seal's power when its bearer falls.”
- **The Instrument of the Sufferings**: the White Knight hands over the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]].
- **Drops**: 4 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]]; if the player has a [[Professions|Profession]], also one of that profession's [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]] (each appears only once per world).
- **The nights grow longer**: the rule the team decided is that each Seal-bearer who falls makes the night longer. After the First Seal falls, night runs from 20:00 to 05:30 (previously 21:00 to 05:00). How much each later Seal adds is to be designed. See [[Day and Night|Day and Night]].
- In Chapter 1, [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] gathers the remaining power into his lantern.

Besides the White Knight, the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]] has three more bosses: [[The Velvet Lure|The Velvet Lure]], [[The Face Thief|The Face Thief]] and [[The Crown Without a King|The Crown Without a King]]. They are left over from boss designs that predate this setting; none of them is currently assigned to a Seal-bearer, and they have no place in the story.

{{spoiler|What the Seal-bearers really mean}}

- The Seal-bearers are not merely the guardians of five relics. All along they have been sharing [[The First Night|the First Night]] with Astra. Centuries of bearing it twisted them, and that is the real reason the five bosses have become what they are now (§6).
- They are the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra (§12):
  - Alberic, the White Knight: bears part of the strain of the Crown of Thorns.
  - Matthias, Giver of War: bears the bodily pain of the scourging.
  - Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale: bears the hunger and the wasting of life.
  - Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins: bears the erosion of death the holy nails stand for.
  - The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar: bear the wound to the soul the holy lance left.
- So when the player defeats them, they are both ending their suffering and dismantling the five Fulcrums that hold Astra. This is also why the further the journey goes, the worse the night may become (§12).
- In the ruins the protagonist discovers that the pain the five Seal-bearers bear all ends up flowing to the same place (§9).
- Lumi can catch the Seals' power because she is the failed Second Vessel: what remains of her still answers to the Seals, like water finding an old riverbed (the team's decision; docs/chapter-1.md §3b). See [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]].
- The Grey Man says that every seal broken “makes the night longer”, and that when the Crown comes off, “something will come out of it. That, I will take with me.”
