The **Five Sufferings** are the five holy torments [[Astra|Astra]] willingly bore during the first [[The Long Night|Long Night]]. When the fifth torment was complete, the sun rose again. The [[Church of the Five Sufferings|Church of the Five Sufferings]] takes its name and its doctrine from these five. Each Suffering corresponds to an instrument of torment, one of the [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]], and later also to a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]].

Each section first gives the Church's reading (the account everyone believes early in the game), then puts what the original lore text says each one really does inside a spoiler block.

When [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]] tells the story of the Five Sufferings in the game, she says:

> Then Astra came. He took a crown of thorns. He was scourged at the pillar. He starved upon the scales. He was nailed to the holy wood.
> And when the spear went through his heart — the sun rose.

## The Crowning

**The First Suffering**. Instrument of the Sufferings: the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]].

A crown of thorns covered in barbs was set on Astra's head. The thorns pierced his skull, yet it was never taken off.

**The Church's reading**: giving up human pride and will.

{{spoiler|What the Crowning really does}}

It wears down the bearer's boundaries of self, so that his mind can withstand the intrusion of [[The First Night|the First Night]] (§4).

In Chapter 1, this is exactly what the crown does to [[The White Knight Alberic|Alberic, the White Knight]]: after three hundred years (a proposal of Chapter 1's design), he remembers only that he must “hold”, not what he holds, nor anyone's name. [[Fennick|Fennick]] was his squire in the two hundred and fiftieth year of his wearing the crown: “But the crown kept taking. A little more of him went every year I was there. One morning he looked at me and asked my name.”

{{/剧透}}

**In the game**: Chapter 1, “The First Seal: the Crown of Thorns”, is built entirely around this Suffering. The [[Shrine of the Crown|Shrine of the Crown]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] reads: “THE FIRST SUFFERING: THE CROWN. He let them press the thorns into his brow, and did not lift his hand. — Lay down what you wear on your head, and be lowly.” The Crown of Thorns is the only Instrument of the Sufferings that has appeared in the game so far, and it can be worn on the head; its experience penalty (see [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]]) and the [[Boons|Boon]] Lumi catches from it, “Crown's Insight” (experience +100%), both come from the same thing: the Crown of Thorns wears the self thin.

## The Scourging

**The Second Suffering**. Instrument of the Sufferings: the Pillar of Scourging.

Astra was bound to a pillar and scourged at length. Legend says that even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall.

**The Church's reading**: repaying everyone's sins with the flesh.

{{spoiler|What the Scourging really does}}

It lets the flesh gradually adapt to the constant pain and transformation the First Night brings (§4). The second Seal-bearer, Matthias, Giver of War, bears the bodily pain of the scourging (§12).

{{/剧透}}

**In the game**: Ilse's story and the oath at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] mention it. The [[Shrine of the Pillar|Shrine of the Pillar]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] reads: “THE SECOND SUFFERING: THE SCOURGE. Bound to the pillar, he bled for every one of us. — Lay down a cord, the scourge you were spared.” The penitent [[Brother Ezra|Brother Ezra]] whips himself and says, “Every stroke I take is one Astra need not.” The corresponding [[Chapter 2|Chapter 2]] has not been designed yet.

## The Starving

**The Third Suffering**. Instrument of the Sufferings: the Scales of Hunger.

Astra gave up food and drink and let his body be weighed on the scales against grain, against sin and against life. Scripture preserves one line:

> “His flesh at last weighed less than sin.”

**The Church's reading**: freedom from the desires of the flesh.

{{spoiler|What the Starving really does}}

It cuts the bearer's dependence on food, sleep and the ordinary needs of life (§4). The third Seal-bearer, Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale, bears the hunger and the wasting of life (§12).

{{/剧透}}

**In the game**: Ilse's story and the oath at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] mention it. The [[Shrine of the Scales|Shrine of the Scales]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] reads: “THE THIRD SUFFERING: HUNGER. Upon the scales his flesh grew lighter than sin. — Lay down a meal you will not eat.” The corresponding [[Chapter 3|Chapter 3]] has not been designed yet.

## The Nailing

**The Fourth Suffering**. Instrument of the Sufferings: the Holy Wood of the Nails.

Astra's hands and feet were pierced by holy nails, and he was fixed to the holy wood.

**The Church's reading**: accepting death.

{{spoiler|What the Nailing really does}}

It fastens Astra firmly to the real world. Even as his soul begins to be eroded by the First Night, he does not slip out of reality entirely (§4). The fourth Seal-bearer, Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins, bears the erosion of death the holy nails stand for (§12).

{{/剧透}}

**In the game**: Ilse's story and the oath at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] mention it, both giving the Church's reading. During the oath [[Father Caius|Father Caius]] recites: “The fourth: the Nails. Upon the holy wood he accepted death.” The [[Shrine of the Nails|Shrine of the Nails]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] reads: “THE FOURTH SUFFERING: THE NAILS. Nailed to the holy wood, he accepted death. — Lay down metal, as he laid down his life.” The corresponding [[Chapter 4|Chapter 4]] has not been designed yet.

## The Piercing

**The Fifth Suffering**. Instrument of the Sufferings: the Lance That Pierced the Heart.

At last a holy lance pierced Astra's chest. At that very moment the sun rose again, and the Long Night ended.

The original lore text gives no separate Church reading for this Suffering.

{{spoiler|What the Piercing really does}}

When the blade went through his heart, Astra's body became, for the first time, an “entrance” joining reality and the First Night, and the First Night began to pour into his body. It was at exactly this moment that the sun rose again (§4). The fifth Seal-bearer, the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar, bears the wound to the soul the holy lance left (§12).

In the game, when Ilse reaches the lance piercing the heart, Lumi shivers. When Pell asks her about it, she says, “I'm not. It's cold. It's just cold.” Lumi is the failed Second Vessel; see [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]].

{{/剧透}}

**In the game**: Ilse's story ends with it, and so does the oath at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]]. The [[Shrine of the Lance|Shrine of the Lance]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] reads: “THE FIFTH SUFFERING: THE LANCE. When it pierced his heart, the sun rose. — Lay down a light, and the dawn will remember you.” The corresponding [[Chapter 5|Chapter 5]] has not been designed yet.

## The Great Rite of the Five Sufferings

The Church wants the protagonist to recover the five holy instruments and finally, in [[The Holy City|the Holy City]], “hold again the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings” (§7). From the protagonist's point of view, this repeats Astra's miracle of long ago.

{{spoiler|What the Great Rite really needs}}

The Great Rite of the Five Sufferings needs not only the five Instruments of the Sufferings but also a new Bearer of the Sufferings. Only one who can defeat the five Seal-bearers and bear the power of the five Seals is fit to become the new vessel (§13). In the Final Chapter the five holy instruments return to their places in [[The Most Holy Hall|the Most Holy Hall]]: the Crown of Thorns is set on again, the scourge marks split open again, the Scales of Hunger rise once more, the holy nails fix his body again, and the Lance That Pierced the Heart enters his chest once more (§14). In the preview ending, the same Five Sufferings fall on the protagonist one after another (§16). See [[Ending|Ending]].

{{/剧透}}

## The five wounds

In the game the Five Sufferings are often drawn as five wounds:

- [[five-wound shrine stone|Five-wound shrine stones]]: roadside holy marks of the Five Sufferings, carved with five wounds. On the one at the [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]] the first wound (the crown) is cracked open, and when Lumi comes near she says, “It's colder near it.”
- Below the dawnstone on [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]] are carved five small wounds and a flame.
- The altar of [[Firstlight Chapel|Firstlight Chapel]], “the Five Holy Wounds”: on the stone wall around Astra's figure are carved the crown of thorns, the scourge, the scales, the holy nails and the holy lance.
- The altar of the five wounds in the [[Chapel of the Five|Chapel of the Five]] at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]], where pilgrims take their oath; the Litany of the Five on the bookstand beside it reads: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid. As long as one bears the Five, the Long Night shall not return.”
- The five [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], one for each Suffering, each of which lights only when something is laid on it.
- The “Amulet of the Five Wounds”, the reward of the quest [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)|The Pilgrim's Road]].
- Items of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality “bear one of the First Martyr's five wounds”.
