{{信息框}}
**The Reed Bend** lies east of [[森林|the Forest]], at the end of the path from the Forest's eastern clearing. The river of [[渡口|the Ford]] bends here and flows south, taking up a whole strip along the east of the map: shallow water near the bank, deep water farther out. On the bank lives the fisherwoman [[希尔德]]; her [[希尔德的小屋|cabin]] and jetty are by the shore, and a fire for smoking fish is always burning. South of the jetty a sandbar runs out into the water, where three [[水史莱姆|water slimes]] guard a bronze ship's bell: nine winters ago Hilde's husband Rolf put out in the fog, and the boat came back by itself, but he did not. North of the jetty, at the water's edge, a sunken stone leans askew, its carvings older than the village.

The Reed Bend is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]; after nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear. There are fish in the river for [[钓鱼|fishing]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30. An opening on the west side at rows 16–18 leads to the Forest; a path comes in there, runs northeast to the cabin's door, then east to the jetty. The river flows from north to south, its bank lying between columns 28 and 32 and curving westward in the middle stretch. Along the bank are grass and patches of sand, with reeds growing.

## Areas

- **The fisher's home**: the cabin, the salt barrel by the door, the nets hung to dry, and the fire for smoking fish (a [[营火|campfire]] that is always lit, on which you can cook). The cabin's door leads into [[希尔德的小屋]].
- **The jetty**: three planks running out into the water at row 12. By day [[希尔德]] stands at the edge of the jetty fishing. Stand on the jetty and cast east, and you reach deep water.
- **The sandbar**: a strip of sand running out into the river south of the jetty (rows 20–22). “Rolf's ship's bell” (a [[铃铛|Bell]]) lies at the very tip of the sandbar, with three water slimes round it. [[沙洲上的船铃]] has you bring it back.
- **The sunken stone**: a toppled [[墓碑|standing stone]] at the water's edge north of the jetty (row 6); press {{键|E}} to read it: “A boat; the one aboard hands a fish to a child on the shore. No thorns. No wounds. No one kneeling.”
- **The river**: the river to the east. The three tiles nearest the bank are shallow water, and beyond that is deep water all the way to the edge of the map. The far bank is not on this map.

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[希尔德]] is the only person here. From 5:00 to 22:00 she fishes from the jetty, and from 22:00 to 5:00 she sleeps in the cabin.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

At the start there are 3 [[水史莱姆|water slimes]] on the sandbar, which attack on sight; once they are felled, another may come onto the sand every 1800 seconds, up to 2 at a time. Farther south along the bank from the sandbar, 1 [[巨蟹|giant crab]] keeps guard, and does not return once felled. After nightfall there are also [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]].

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- **Fish**: the river's fish come by the hour and the depth, as everywhere else: [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]] by day, [[鳗鱼|Eels]] after dark, [[狗鱼|Pike]] in deep water, [[鲶鱼|Catfish]] on rainy days, the odd [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]], and, very rarely, a [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]] at dawn. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]]. A fishing rod takes just two [[树枝|Sticks]] and two bundles of [[干草|Cut Grass]] to make; Hilde sells bait and fishing rods.
- **Rolf's ship's bell**: the [[铃铛|Bell]] on the sandbar, a quest item.
- Sticks and stones lie on the ground at the start, and afterwards the ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- West (rows 16–18): [[森林]].
- The cabin's door: [[希尔德的小屋]].

There is no road on the river's east bank. On [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], the Reed Bend occupies the cell of the river itself: it is a camp on the river's west bank and is drawn as a camp, but that cell, like the river, cannot be stood on, and it can only be entered from the Forest — the river remains a barrier that can be crossed only at the Ford.

## Lore

The first time you enter the bend, Lumi says: it's the river! It's the Ford's river; it bends round this way; even the water is quiet here.

{{剧透|The Sunken Stone}}
Walk up to the sunken stone and a scene plays: carved on the stone is a boat, and the one aboard is handing a fish to a child on the shore — no thorns, no wounds, no one kneeling. Lumi says no one on this stone is suffering; someone is only giving another person a fish. “Why does that feel so strange to look at?” Afterwards Hilde says the stone was there before there was a village, “before the Church came and told us what to be sorry for”.

This is the first place in the game that hints at another faith before [[五苦圣教|the Church of the Five Sufferings]]: one that honored giving, not suffering. See [[世界观|Lore]].
{{/剧透}}
