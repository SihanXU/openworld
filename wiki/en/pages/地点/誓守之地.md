**The Held Ground** is a field ringed by mist, lying nowhere on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]]. Once [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]] has fallen, you receive a thorn that is still green, [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]]; hold it and right-click yourself to enter the Held Ground, and use it once more inside to return to where you were standing.

This is the player's own camp, and a place for work too: you can build, farm, raise livestock and fish. [[阿尔贝里克]], the Crown of Thorns laid down, serves as steward here, and he has set up a [[货摊|trading stall]] as well: it sells seeds, fertilizer, shovels, animal feed, buckets, crated livestock, fishing rods and bait, and buys crops, eggs, milk and fish, in this place's own coin, the [[晨辉币|Dawnmark]] (see [[金钱|Money]]). East of the sword stand Alberic's forge and anvil as well; he works iron there in the mornings, the forge is always lit, and anyone may use them (see [[锻造|Smithing]]). The land reaches as far as he can hold. Hand him materials to put in the Held Ground's stock, and ring by ring he pushes back the mist, opens new grounds and raises working buildings (see [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]).

The Held Ground is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night. It is not drawn on the [[世界地图|world map]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 48×40, its two outermost rings old trees. Alberic's sword is planted at the very center, and you arrive standing due south of it. Around the sword is a stretch of open ground, enclosed by three rings of mist ([[雾墙|banks of mist]], which cannot be walked through), about 8, 12 and 16 tiles from the sword. At first you can move only within the first ring.

## Areas

- **[[插在地上的长剑|The planted sword]]**: at the center, Alberic's sword. Press {{键|E}} to read: “Alberic's longsword, point down in the earth, a little crooked. The ground holds as far as he holds it.”
- **Alberic's tent**: northwest of the sword. Press {{键|E}} at the tent to go in (“Enter Alberic's tent”); inside is [[阿尔贝里克的帐篷里]]: his camp bed, his old armor on a stand, and a chest.
- **The chest**: an open chest northeast of the sword, named “the Held Ground's chest”, which anyone may use.
- **The forge and anvil**: east of the sword, Alberic's [[熔炉|forge]] (always lit) and [[铁砧|anvil]], which anyone may use (see [[锻造|Smithing]]).
- **The trading stall**: southeast of the sword, “Alberic's stall”; see [[货摊|trading stall]].
- **The farmland**: a small patch of turned earth southwest of the sword, ready to sow.
- **[[地基|Staked plots]]**: eight staked plots round the sword, on which [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] raises the workshop, the kitchen hearth, the storehouse, the weaving shed, the apothecary and three residents' tents.

## Expanding the Held Ground

Speak to [[阿尔贝里克]] and choose “The Held Ground…” to open the Held Ground's panel: push back the mist to widen the land, open new grounds ([[采石场|the Quarry]], [[林场|the Timberland]], [[湖畔|the Lakeside]], [[温泉谷|the Hot Springs]]), and raise working buildings on the plots. Materials are handed to him for the Held Ground's stock. For details, see [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[阿尔贝里克]] comes here after the White Knight falls: from 6:00 to 14:00 he works iron at the anvil east of the sword, from 14:00 to 22:00 he keeps watch beside the sword (the stall is right beside him), and from 22:00 to 6:00 he sleeps in front of his tent. He is also the one to see about expanding the Held Ground: choose “The Held Ground…” when speaking to him (see [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]).

## People Who Move In
{{主条目|领地居民}}

People at full regard can be invited to live here (see [[领地居民|Held Ground Residents]]). At first there is only one bed, by the camp; build residents' tents through Alberic's “The Held Ground…”, and each adds two berths in front of the tent:

| Building | Land needed | Materials | Reward when built | Beds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residents' tent | I (The Grove) | {{物品|布匹}} 6, {{物品|木板}} 10 | 30 Dawnmarks | +2 |
| Second residents' tent | I (The Grove) | Bolt of Cloth 8, Plank 14, {{物品|木头}} 6 | 50 Dawnmarks | +2 |
| Third residents' tent | II (The Meadow) | Bolt of Cloth 10, Plank 18, {{物品|铁锭}} 2 | 80 Dawnmarks | +2 |

At most seven people can live here. Those who move in spend 7:00–21:00 near their own berths and sleep there from 21:00 to 7:00; what they used to sell can still be bought from them.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There is no livestock here at the start, and none grows by itself; it has to be bought at the stall. [[牛|Cows]] and [[鸡|chickens]] do not attack people and run off when struck; a hen lays an egg every {{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours. Ways to raise livestock:

- Buy a [[装箱的母鸡|Crated Hen]] or a [[装箱的小牛|Crated Calf]] at the stall and set it on the ground for one more animal (a calf takes {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours to grow into a cow).
- Feed two animals of the same kind standing side by side until they are fed, and they produce a [[小牛|calf]] or a [[小鸡|chick]], which grows up in {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours.
- Livestock that is bought or born here is as tame as the original animals and does not chase people.
- A fed cow can be milked with an empty bucket.

See [[动物|Animals]].

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- The ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6, with a chance of one more of each every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds).
- The four new grounds opened through [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] each have their own resources: [[采石场|the Quarry]] (stone, iron, coal, gold), [[林场|the Timberland]] (trees that grow back, mushrooms, berries), [[湖畔|the Lakeside]] (fish in a deep lake), [[温泉谷|the Hot Springs]] (healing springs, herbs, Frostcap).
- The woods beyond the first ring of mist can be felled for timber; the iron veins beyond the third ring can be mined for [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]], once the mist has drawn back entirely.
- You can fish in the fish pond and in the pond to the east; their water can also be drawn into a bucket for watering the fields.
- For what the stall sells and what it pays, see [[金钱#货摊卖什么|what the stall sells]].
- The chest, the farmland, the livestock and all the open ground are free to use: nothing here has an owner.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

The Held Ground has no exit to the outside; it can be entered and left only with [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]]: hold it and right-click yourself (the label is “Enter / leave the Held Ground”). Used outside, it brings you in standing due south of the sword; used inside, it returns you to where you stood before you came in.

It cannot be used in combat: if in the last 5 seconds you have struck or been struck, or if an enemy is chasing you, using it only says “Not now — not with a fight on me.” So you cannot bring a pursuing monster in with you, nor use it to slip out of a fight.

For the new grounds you have opened ([[采石场|the Quarry]], [[林场|the Timberland]], [[湖畔|the Lakeside]], [[温泉谷|the Hot Springs]]), press “Go ➜” on their cards in the expansion panel and Alberic sends you there; each ground has a road on its south side, and walking out by it brings you back due south of the sword.

## Lore

{{剧透|Alberic's Vow}}
The first time you come to the Held Ground, Alberic, his helm off, recognizes you as the one who took the crown from him: “I swore I would hold until I was relieved. You relieved me. So I hold for you now — this ground, as far as the mist.” He asks you to tell him your name: “I would like to keep one, this time.” Lumi says that without his helm he looks kind; tired, but kind. Finally he hands you 30 Dawnmarks: “Here — the coin of this ground. My stall keeps seeds, feed and bait; it buys back what you grow, raise and catch. The pond is full of fish.”

His vow is carved on the altar in [[守墓者圣所]], the lowest level of the Old Barrow: “I will hold until I am relieved.” After the second expansion he says he remembers there once being fields like these, but cannot recall whose they were; in his lines at the last expansion he says, “And nobody kneels here.”
{{/剧透}}

Each time you come back after an expansion, Lumi says the mist has drawn back and tells you what has appeared beyond it.
