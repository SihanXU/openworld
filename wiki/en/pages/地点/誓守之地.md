**The Held Ground** is a field ringed by mist, lying nowhere on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]]. Once [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]] has fallen, you receive a thorn that is still green, [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]]; hold it and right-click yourself to enter the Held Ground, and use it once more inside to return to where you were standing.

This is the player's own camp, and a place for work too: you can build, farm, raise livestock and fish. [[Alberic]], the Crown of Thorns laid down, serves as steward here, and he has set up a [[trading stall|trading stall]] as well: it sells seeds, fertilizer, shovels, animal feed, buckets, crated livestock, fishing rods and bait, and buys crops, eggs, milk and fish, in this place's own coin, the [[Dawnmark|Dawnmark]] (see [[Money|Money]]). East of the sword stand Alberic's forge and anvil as well; he works iron there in the mornings, the forge is always lit, and anyone may use them (see [[Smithing|Smithing]]). The land reaches as far as he can hold. Hand him materials to put in the Held Ground's stock, and ring by ring he pushes back the mist, opens new grounds and raises working buildings (see [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]).

The Held Ground is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night. It is not drawn on the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 48×40, its two outermost rings old trees. Alberic's sword is planted at the very center, and you arrive standing due south of it. Around the sword is a stretch of open ground, enclosed by three rings of mist ([[bank of mist|banks of mist]], which cannot be walked through), about 8, 12 and 16 tiles from the sword. At first you can move only within the first ring.

## Areas

- **[[planted sword|The planted sword]]**: at the center, Alberic's sword. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Alberic's longsword, driven point down into the earth, a little crooked. Its grip is wrapped in new cloth.”
- **Alberic's tent**: northwest of the sword. Press {{key|E}} at the tent to go in (“Enter Alberic's tent”); inside is [[Inside Alberic's Tent]]: his camp bed, his old armor on a stand, and a chest.
- **The chest**: an open chest northeast of the sword, named “the Held Ground's chest”, which anyone may use.
- **The forge and anvil**: east of the sword, Alberic's [[forge|forge]] (always lit) and [[anvil|anvil]], which anyone may use (see [[Smithing|Smithing]]).
- **The trading stall**: southeast of the sword, “Alberic's stall”; see [[trading stall|trading stall]].
- **The farmland**: a small patch of turned earth southwest of the sword, ready to sow.
- **[[staked plot|Staked plots]]**: eight staked plots round the sword, on which [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] raises the workshop, the kitchen hearth, the storehouse, the weaving shed, the apothecary and three residents' tents.

## Expanding the Held Ground

Speak to [[Alberic]] and choose “The Held Ground…” to open the Held Ground's panel: push back the mist to widen the land, open new grounds ([[The Quarry|the Quarry]], [[The Timberland|the Timberland]], [[The Lakeside|the Lakeside]], [[The Hot Springs|the Hot Springs]]), and raise working buildings on the plots. Materials are handed to him for the Held Ground's stock. For details, see [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Residents

{{residents}}

[[Alberic]] comes here after the White Knight falls: from 6:00 to 14:00 he works iron at the anvil east of the sword, from 14:00 to 22:00 he keeps watch beside the sword (the stall is right beside him), and from 22:00 to 6:00 he sleeps in front of his tent. He is also the one to see about expanding the Held Ground: choose “The Held Ground…” when speaking to him (see [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]).

## People Who Move In
{{main|Held Ground Residents}}

People at full regard can be invited to live here (see [[Held Ground Residents|Held Ground Residents]]). At first there is only one bed, by the camp; build residents' tents through Alberic's “The Held Ground…”, and each adds two berths in front of the tent:

| Building | Land needed | Materials | Reward when built | Beds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Residents' tent | I (The Grove) | {{item|Bolt of Cloth}} 6, {{item|Plank}} 10 | 30 Dawnmarks | +2 |
| Second residents' tent | I (The Grove) | Bolt of Cloth 8, Plank 14, {{item|Log}} 6 | 50 Dawnmarks | +2 |
| Third residents' tent | II (The Meadow) | Bolt of Cloth 10, Plank 18, {{item|Iron Ingot}} 2 | 80 Dawnmarks | +2 |

At most seven people can live here. Those who move in spend 7:00–21:00 near their own berths and sleep there from 21:00 to 7:00; what they used to sell can still be bought from them.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There is no livestock here at the start, and none grows by itself; it has to be bought at the stall. [[cow|Cows]] and [[chicken|chickens]] do not attack people and run off when struck; a hen lays an egg every {{value|下蛋间隔}} game hours. Ways to raise livestock:

- Buy a [[Crated Hen|Crated Hen]] or a [[Crated Calf|Crated Calf]] at the stall and set it on the ground for one more animal (a calf takes {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours to grow into a cow).
- Feed two animals of the same kind standing side by side until they are fed, and they produce a [[calf|calf]] or a [[chick|chick]], which grows up in {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours.
- Livestock that is bought or born here is as tame as the original animals and does not chase people.
- A fed cow can be milked with an empty bucket.

See [[Animals|Animals]].

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- The ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6, with a chance of one more of each every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds).
- The four new grounds opened through [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] each have their own resources: [[The Quarry|the Quarry]] (stone, iron, coal, gold), [[The Timberland|the Timberland]] (trees that grow back, mushrooms, berries), [[The Lakeside|the Lakeside]] (fish in a deep lake), [[The Hot Springs|the Hot Springs]] (healing springs, herbs, Frostcap).
- The woods beyond the first ring of mist can be felled for timber; the iron veins beyond the third ring can be mined for [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]], once the mist has drawn back entirely.
- You can fish in the fish pond and in the pond to the east; their water can also be drawn into a bucket for watering the fields.
- For what the stall sells and what it pays, see [[Money#What the stall sells|what the stall sells]].
- The chest, the farmland, the livestock and all the open ground are free to use: nothing here has an owner.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

The Held Ground has no exit to the outside; it can be entered and left only with [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]]: hold it and right-click yourself (the label is “Enter / leave the Held Ground”). Used outside, it brings you in standing due south of the sword; used inside, it returns you to where you stood before you came in.

It cannot be used in combat: if in the last 5 seconds you have struck or been struck, or if an enemy is chasing you, using it only says “Not now, not in the middle of a fight.” So you cannot bring a pursuing monster in with you, nor use it to slip out of a fight.

For the new grounds you have opened ([[The Quarry|the Quarry]], [[The Timberland|the Timberland]], [[The Lakeside|the Lakeside]], [[The Hot Springs|the Hot Springs]]), press “Go ➜” on their cards in the expansion panel and Alberic sends you there; each ground has a road on its south side, and walking out by it brings you back due south of the sword.

## Lore

{{spoiler|Alberic's Vow}}
The first time you come to the Held Ground, Alberic, his helm off, recognizes you as the one who took the crown from him: “I swore to hold until I was relieved. You relieved me. So now I hold for you: this ground, as far as the mist.” He asks you to tell him your name: “This time, I would like to keep one.” Finally he hands you 30 Dawnmarks: “Take this, the coin of this ground. My stall keeps seeds, feed and bait, and buys back what you grow, raise and catch. There are fish in the pond.”

His vow is carved on the altar in [[The Warden's Sanctum]], the lowest level of the Old Barrow: “I will hold until I am relieved.” After the second expansion he says he remembers there once being fields like these, but cannot recall whose they were; in his lines at the last expansion he says, “and no one here kneels to me.”

Each time you come back after an expansion, Lumi says the mist has drawn back and tells you what has appeared beyond it.
