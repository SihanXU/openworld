**The Dragon's Lair** lies at the top of [[Cinder Pass|Cinder Pass]], a great cave on Cinder Peak. Inside is a floor of scorched stone, with [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] at its center. Defeat it, and the old main story ends here with an ending card.

The lair is a dark underground [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]], with battle music. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 30×24. The entrance is in the south (columns 14–16), and a short passage leads into an oval arena. Four rock pillars stand along the arena's walls, a little north of its middle is a stretch of ash, and a lit brazier stands in each corner.

## Areas

- **The lair entrance**: the mouth of the passage in the south. Walking into this stretch plays a line of narration (“Heat, and the smell of scorched stone”), and the main quest advances to “The Dragon of Cinder Peak”. It is best to drink a [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]] before coming in.
- **The arena**: where the dragon is, with four rock pillars along the walls.

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is in the lair.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|The dragon of Cinder Peak]]: 700 health; it breathes fire at range and also bites. The first time you strike it while its health is below half (350), it shrieks and calls up 3 [[dragon whelp|dragon whelps]] (only once). When it falls, the remaining whelps flee shrieking into the dark one by one.
- How to fight it: drink a Fireproof Draught (the fireproof status lasts 300 seconds; fire deals only a quarter of its damage and cannot set you alight), and keep out of the straight line of its fire. [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]] deals double damage to the dragon and its whelps. For details, see [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]].
