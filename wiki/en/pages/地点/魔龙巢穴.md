**The Dragon's Lair** lies at the top of [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]], a great cave on Cinder Peak. Inside is a floor of scorched stone, with [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] at its center. Defeat it, and the old main story ends here with an ending card.

The lair is a dark underground [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]], with battle music. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 30×24. The entrance is in the south (columns 14–16), and a short passage leads into an oval arena. Four rock pillars stand along the arena's walls, a little north of its middle is a stretch of ash, and a lit brazier stands in each corner.

## Areas

- **The lair entrance**: the mouth of the passage in the south. Walking into this stretch plays a line of narration (“Heat, and the smell of scorched stone”), and the main quest advances to “The Dragon of Cinder Peak”. It is best to drink a [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]] before coming in.
- **The arena**: where the dragon is, with four rock pillars along the walls.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one is in the lair.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- [[烬峰魔龙|The dragon of Cinder Peak]]: 700 health; it breathes fire at range and also bites. The first time you strike it while its health is below half (350), it shrieks and calls up 3 [[幼龙|dragon whelps]] (only once). When it falls, the remaining whelps flee shrieking into the dark one by one.
- How to fight it: drink a Fireproof Draught (the fireproof status lasts 300 seconds; fire deals only a quarter of its damage and cannot set you alight), and keep out of the straight line of its fire. [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]] deals double damage to the dragon and its whelps. For details, see [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]].

## Lore

{{待设计}}

{{剧透|The Ending}}
When the dragon falls, the fires in the lair slowly die and an ending card appears: “The dragon of Cinder Peak. The dragon is dead. Tonight Emberfall can sleep, and the road east is open. Everything the world has learned from you is kept in the Threadbook (J).” The main quest [[屠龙之路|The Dragon Road]] is complete.
{{/剧透}}

This content predates the setting of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]]; it was the end of the main story before Chapter 1 was made. It is planned to be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see [[第二章|Chapter 2]]).
