**Cinder Pass** climbs from the east of [[余烬镇]] up to [[魔龙巢穴]] on Cinder Peak. Rock walls line both sides; snow lies at the bottom, and the higher you go, the more the ground is burned earth and ash. Along the way are frost wolves, fire imps and a stone golem; there is an ice golem in the snow, and a storm wisp drifts over the open road. Halfway up burns a campfire, the last place to rest before the lair.

Cinder Pass is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]], and after nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear as well. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 30×40, tall and narrow. Rock walls line both sides, the ground is snow, and the upper half holds a wide stretch of ash. A mountain road two tiles wide comes in from the west (rows 34–36), zigzags north and leaves by the north (columns 14–16). A few charred trees stand along the way.

## Areas

- **The snow slope at the bottom**: the lower half, where frost wolves roam and the ice golem keeps watch in the snow.
- **The last camp**: a lit [[营火|campfire]] halfway up. Apart from fire creatures, monsters will not come within two tiles of a lit fire, so you can catch your breath here and cook food (see [[营火|campfire]], [[烹饪|Cooking]]).
- **The scorched earth**: the ash ground of the upper half, where the fire imps are.
- **The summit**: before the northern exit, guarded by the stone golem.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives on the pass.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- 2 [[霜狼|frost wolves]] in the lower half, 1 [[冰魔像|ice golem]] in the snow at the bottom, 2 [[火焰小鬼|fire imps]] in the upper half (ranged attacks), 1 [[风暴鬼火|storm wisp]] on the middle stretch of road (ranged attacks), and 1 [[石魔像|stone golem]] before the summit exit.
- The fire imps slowly come back on the ash ground: one may appear every 240 seconds, up to 2. When a [[天气|storm]] rises, storm wisps appear on the snow and the road: one may appear every 60 seconds, up to 2, and only in a storm. The rest do not return once felled.
- After nightfall [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] appear.

## Lore

{{待设计}}

In the old main story this pass was the road from Emberfall to the dragon's lair on Cinder Peak; Mara, the head of the town, said the dragon lived at the top of the mountain road east of town. This content predates the setting of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] and is planned to be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see [[第二章|Chapter 2]]).
