**Thornwood Hollow** lies south of [[森林|the Forest]]: a valley ringed with bushes and briars, home to plenty of small monsters. A path cuts across the valley from north to southeast, and at the valley floor, between two standing stones, lies the entrance to [[古墓]]. The first objective of [[古墓里的失踪者]] is simply to get here.

Thornwood Hollow is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]: there are monsters even by day, and after nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear as well.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30, with thick woods and bushes all round. The northern exit (columns 12–14) joins the Forest, and the southern exit (columns 26–28) goes down into the Old Barrow. The valley holds three stretches of briar thicket (west, east and south), a patch of dry grass a little north of the middle, and a patch of mud in the southwest.

## Areas

- **The eastern briars**: where the briars are thickest. [[米娅]] lost her grandmother's silver ring somewhere around here ([[米娅的戒指]]).
- **The dry-grass slope**: a patch of dry grass a little north of the valley's middle, where fire burns fast. Someone has thrown down a pair of fine [[靴子|Thornproof Boots]] (defense 2) on the slope.
- **The mud**: in the southwest; walk into it and you get wet (see [[地形|Terrain]]).
- **The barrow entrance**: at the valley floor, where two rocks stand at either side of the end of the path, with the southern exit between them. On each side of the entrance is a carved standing stone (a [[墓碑|gravestone]]); press {{键|E}} to read: “Worn carving: a knight with a crown of thorns, and before him a line of small figures, kneeling. Beneath: THE ORDER OF THE THORN HOLDS.”
- **The waypoint statue**: north of the barrow entrance stands a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] (named “Thornwood Hollow”). Once woken, it makes coming out of the barrow, or coming back next time, easy.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives in Thornwood Hollow.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- At the start there are 3 [[绿史莱姆|green slimes]] (mostly in the open), 3 [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spiders]] (mostly in the briars) and 2 [[狼|wolves]] (on either side of the valley). All of them attack on sight.
- The slimes and spiders come back: while the player is here, a slime may appear every 90 seconds and a spider every 120 seconds, up to 3 of each. The wolves do not return once felled.
- After nightfall there are also [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]].
- [[清剿荆棘谷]] has you fell 6 monsters here; any monster counts.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

At the start there are 2 [[树枝|Sticks]] on the northern slope. The ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6). Both the bushes and the trees can be cut down.

## Lore

The first time you enter Thornwood Hollow, Lumi says the dead down below are sleeping very uneasily, like a cold draft blowing in under a door.

{{剧透|The Knight on the Standing Stones}}
When you reach the barrow entrance, Lumi notices the carvings on the standing stones: a knight in a crown of thorns, with a row of people kneeling before him. The Order of the Thorn is buried below. The Order's roll is in [[荆棘骸骨堂]] on the barrow's second level, and its last name is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]].
{{/剧透}}
