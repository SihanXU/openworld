**The Pilgrims' Rest** stands on the east bank of [[渡口|the Ford]], a stopping place the Church of the Five Sufferings keeps for pilgrims. Head east from the Ford's east exit and this is the first place you reach; farther east is [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]]. [[凯乌斯司铎]], stationed here, welcomes the pilgrim named in the Church's letter.

The Rest is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]. It is the only road east to [[余烬镇|Emberfall]], so after defeating the White Knight, going back to the village and heading east again both take you through here.

## Map

36×26. It joins the east bank of [[渡口|the Ford]] on the west and [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]] on the east. A dirt road runs through the Rest from west to east, with a flagstone square in the middle.

## Areas
- **The Chapel of the Five**: the stone chapel to the north, its door facing south. Inside are the altar of the five wounds and the bookstand with the Litany of the Five; see [[五伤礼拜堂]].
- **The Pilgrims' Hostel**: the long house to the south. Inside are a row of beds and the pilgrims' book; see [[朝圣者客舍]].
- **The square**: the flagstones in the middle of the road. On its west side stands **the Holy See's notice**; press {{键|E}} to read: “BY THE HOLY SEE OF THE FIVE. The Seal-bearers are saints: they carry what Astra carried, and they can carry it no longer. Pilgrims of the Five: seek out the five Seal-bearers, overcome them, and bring the five instruments home to the Holy City for the Rite. Take the oath at the five wounds before you go on.”
- Northeast of the square is a well; the signpost on the east reads “THE PILGRIM'S ROAD → five shrines, one road. Light them as you go.”
- South of the square are the pilgrims' fire (always lit) and two log benches.

## Residents
{{居民}}

- [[凯乌斯司铎]]: in the morning he keeps the five holy wounds in the chapel, from noon to evening he watches the road from the chapel door, and at night he sleeps in the chapel.
- [[莫德]] and her daughter [[温]]: pilgrims bound for the Holy City who stopped here when they heard about the White Knight. By day Maud sits by the fire and Wynn plays by the well; at night they sleep in the hostel.

## Lore
- The first time you come here (after receiving [[教会的来信|the Church's letter]] and before the White Knight falls), Father Caius comes out to greet you: “A pilgrim, at last — and with a fairy, as the letter said.” He asks you to the chapel to take the vow: “Every pilgrim the Holy See sends east has knelt there first.” Lumi says: “Kneel again? Everyone out here wants someone to kneel.”
- For the taking of the vow before the altar of the five wounds, see [[五伤礼拜堂]].
- After the White Knight falls, the welcome and the vow no longer appear. The first time you come to the Rest after that, Father Caius says: “You came from the east. From him. ...Then it is done, and the road is open. The Holy See will have its Crown, the first of the five.”

## History
