**The Scarred March** is one of the six regions of [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], in the north and northeast of the continent. It is the land of the [[Seal-bearers|Second Seal]], where [[Matthias, Giver of War|Matthias, Giver of War]] bears the torment of [[Instruments of the Sufferings|the Pillar of Scourging]]: the fighting never stops.

It is a **backdrop region**: you can walk into it, its towns, fortresses and caves are all there, and its wilds hold the monsters of these parts, but no one lives here yet, and there is no story and no commission. On the world map, beneath its name, it reads “Story not yet open”. Later versions will fill it with playable content.

## Landscape

Red rock badlands (drawn as mesas on the world map) and steppe, with ridges between the regions. Its wilds are the Open Steppe (wolves, boars), the Red Badlands (fire imps, fire slimes) and the Red Hills.

## Locations

Its capital is Crimsonhold. The War-Giver's Bastion, at the far east, is the Seal-bearer's seat, sealed for now.
