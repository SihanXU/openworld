**The Lands of the Five Seals** is the game's world map: a continent ringed by sea and divided into six regions, the five [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]] each keeping one, with [[The Holy City|the Holy City]] at the center (see [[#Regions|Regions]] below). It is drawn as a hand-drawn map in inked lines (beaches and coastlines, clusters of woods, snow-capped mountains, rolling hills), in a style nothing like the ground you walk on, so you know at a glance that you are on the world map. Every cell of the continent stands for one map; each story location is drawn as its own icon (a village, a town, a ford, a mill, a mine...), the story locations take their own cells, and the remaining cells are [[The Wild|the Wild]]. Walk out over the edge of a map and you stand on that map's own cell of the Lands of the Five Seals; stand on a location and press {{key|E}} to walk into its map.

All of the playable story is now in the northwest, on [[Frostfang Coast|Frostfang Coast]]. The other five regions are **backdrop**: you can walk into them, and their towns, fortresses and caves are all there, but they have no story or commissions yet, and the world map marks them “no tale here yet”.

The Lands of the Five Seals is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]. The [[World Map|world map]] ({{key|M}}) shows it by default.

## Map

{{map}}

The continent is 64 cells across and 48 down, one cell to a location. The outermost ring is all sea; the land is roughly a square with rounded corners, with a winding coastline. All the hand-made maps are on Frostfang Coast in the northwest; some two hundred more backdrop locations are spread over the six regions, and the rest of the land is [[The Wild|the Wild]]. Several rivers run from the high ground down to the sea or to lakes, and highways link the regions' capitals in a ring, each with a road of its own into the Holy City; where a highway crosses a river there is a bridge.

Backdrop locations have icons by their kind: fortress (walls and a flag), ruin (broken walls), graveyard (gravestones), abbey (a spire), city quarter (white walls and a golden dome), tower, old battlefield (crossed spears and a torn banner), cave (a cave mouth in the mountains), and the Seat of a Seal-bearer (a black spire, sealed); villages, towns, farmsteads, mills, camps and shrines use the same icons as on Frostfang Coast.

Cells of [[The Wild|the Wild]] carry no mark; you read them by their land: woods, hills, snowfields, plains, and in the other regions grassland, red-rock badlands (red mesas), fields, dust flats, salt flats, marsh (reeds), dead woods (bare trees), white-stone plateau and city outskirts.

The location you have just walked out of is marked: a gold frame round its cell, blinking on and off, and a gold arrow bobbing over it, meaning “you just came out of here”.

### Locations not yet open

Locations the story has not reached yet have a dimmed icon with a lock drawn at the upper right. The cell is like a wall: it can be neither stepped onto nor crossed; walk toward it and you bump into it, and the reason is shown at the lower left (each place in its own words). Others travelling the world map go round it as well. If an older save has you standing on a locked cell, pressing {{key|E}} reads “Sealed: X” and only shows the reason; one step aside and you are off it.

In Part One the road east of the Ford is locked: [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], [[The Pilgrim's Road]], [[Abbey of the Thorn]], [[The Kneeling Field]] and [[The East Road]], with the message “The road east of the Ford is not open yet: its tale is told in Part Two.” The east behind the White Knight is locked too: [[Emberfall]], [[The Old Mine]], [[Cinder Pass]] and [[The Dragon's Lair]], with the message “The White Knight still holds the East Road. There is no way past him yet.” Once you defeat the White Knight on [[The East Road|the East Road]], their locks are gone and you can step onto them and go in straight from the world map.

A few places are locked for good (in the current version): the four Seats of the Seal-bearers (the War-Giver's Bastion in [[The Scarred March|the Scarred March]], the Black Balance in [[The Vale of Scales|the Vale of Scales]], the Pale Rider's Ossuary in [[The Pale Fens|the Pale Fens]], and the Altar Beneath in [[The White Altar Heights|the White Altar Heights]]) and the Most Holy Hall at the heart of the Holy City, with the message “The way in is shut. Its tale is not yet told.”

Each step on the world map skips time by the minutes in the table above, times 1.5 in rain or storm (see [[Weather]]). While the time is skipped, [[Satiety|satiety]] falls as usual.

Each region has its own lands (see [[#Regions|Regions]] above): the northernmost rows of Frostfang Coast are mostly snowfield, and to the south lie plains, forest and hills; the other regions each divide into their own few lands by height and wetness.

### River and mountains: the only crossing

North of the Ford runs a mountain range all the way to the northern sea; south of the Ford runs a river, which flows south past the Old Barrow's cell, then turns west and runs all the way to the western sea; the Ford's own cell is river too, and cannot be stood on. So the west bank (the original part of Frostfang Coast) is enclosed: **between it and the rest of the continent, the only way is through the map of the Ford**. To enter the Ford, stand on a cell beside it (Firstlight Village, Mountain Foot or the Pilgrims' Rest), face the Ford and press {{key|E}}: going in from the west takes you to the west bank, and going in from the Pilgrims' Rest to the far bank. Leaving the Ford from the west bank puts you on the west side, and leaving from the far bank puts you on the east side. It is designed this way so that you cannot walk out to the west, circle round on the world map and come in from the east, skipping the Ford's river crossing (see [[The Ford]]).

The west bank is a narrow valley: peaks run along the north and the west, there is snow above Cloudtop, and a bay takes the south-west; only ten wild cells are left between the hand-made maps, each there for a reason — the high snow above Cloudtop, Dawn Hill's crag, Millbrook's fields, the wayside between the mill, the grassland and the dell, the Westwood behind the dell and the hollow, and the river meadow below the barrow. Anything an older save had standing on a cell that is now closed is moved to the nearest open one when it loads.

The game's first part is played on the west bank alone: the road east of the Ford opens in Part Two, and [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], [[The Pilgrim's Road]], [[Abbey of the Thorn]], [[The Kneeling Field]] and [[The East Road]] are locked locations on the world map (see [[#Locations not yet open|Locations not yet open]], [[The First Seal|Part One]]).

- West bank: [[Cloudtop]], [[Mountain Foot]], [[Dawn Hill]], [[Millbrook]], [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Grassland]], [[The Forest]], [[Thornwood Hollow]], [[The Old Barrow]], [[The Charcoal Dell]], [[The Reed Bend]], and the Ford's west bank.
- East of the river (the rest of the continent): the Ford's far bank, [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], [[The Pilgrim's Road]], [[Abbey of the Thorn]], [[The Kneeling Field]], [[The East Road]], [[Emberfall]], [[The Old Mine]], [[Cinder Pass]], [[The Dragon's Lair]], and the other five regions.

Entering the Ford from Mountain Foot, you take the stone steps between Mountain Foot and the Ford, and arrive on the west bank.

### How to get onto the world map

Walk out over the edge of a map that has a cell on the continent (not by stepping on its exit tiles), and you stand on the world map on **that map's own cell** (the mark of the place you just left is under your feet); from there you choose where to go: step onto a neighbouring cell, or press {{key|E}} where you stand to go back in. Cells that cannot be stood on are the exception: the cells of [[The Ford]] and [[The Reed Bend]] are river, so walking out of them lands you on the cell beside them in the direction you walked, and walking out of the Ford only ever lands you on a cell on the same bank; a locked location (only ever left from inside after the developer console took you in) also puts you beside it.

Most hand-made maps are ringed by woods, brush or rock, all of which can be felled or broken (see [[Woodcutting|woodcutting]], [[Mining|mining]]); cut a way through and you can walk out over the edge. The places where you can now walk out over the edge without cutting are:

- one tile on the south edge of [[The Ford]]'s west bank right by the river (the far bank's north, east and south edges are woods but for the road east, and in Part One that road's exit is sealed);
- one tile beside the stone-steps exit on the south edge of [[Mountain Foot]].

[[The Wild|The Wild]] is open on all four sides; you can walk back to the world map from any edge.

### How to enter a place

Stand on a location and press {{key|E}} (the label reads “Enter X”), facing any way. Whichever side you stepped onto the cell from, you enter the map from that side: walk over from the west and you appear on the map's west side; if that way is blocked, you are moved to the nearest open ground. Just out of a map by its edge, press {{key|E}} before taking a step and you go back in where you came out. The Ford is the exception: its cell cannot be stood on, so stand beside it, face it and press {{key|E}}, and you enter at the crossing between the two maps.

## Lore

The six regions are laid out after the original text of the [[Lore|lore]]: the five Seal-bearers each keep one quarter and bear one of the Five Sufferings, and the land wears the look of that suffering (the bloody weals of the Scourging, the barren fields of the Starving, the dead woods of the Nailing, the white altar of the Lance); the Holy City is at the center, and the Most Holy Hall is still shut.
