**The Lands of the Five Seals** is the game's world map: a continent ringed by sea and divided into six regions, the five [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] each keeping one, with [[圣城|the Holy City]] at the center (see [[#地区|Regions]] below). It is drawn as a hand-drawn map in inked lines (beaches and coastlines, clusters of woods, snow-capped mountains, rolling hills), in a style nothing like the ground you walk on, so you know at a glance that you are on the world map. Every cell of the continent stands for one map; each story location is drawn as its own icon (a village, a town, a ford, a mill, a mine...), the story locations take their own cells, and the remaining cells are [[荒野|the Wild]]. Walk out over the edge of a map and you stand on the Lands of the Five Seals; stand on a location and press {{键|E}} to walk into its map.

All of the playable story is now in the northwest, on [[霜牙海岸|Frostfang Coast]]. The other five regions are **backdrop**: you can walk into them, and their towns, fortresses and caves are all there, but they have no story or commissions yet, and the world map marks them “no tale here yet”.

The Lands of the Five Seals is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]. The [[世界地图|world map]] ({{键|M}}) shows it by default.

## Map

{{地图}}

The continent is 64 cells across and 48 down, one cell to a location. The outermost ring is all sea; the land is roughly a square with rounded corners, with a winding coastline. All the hand-made maps are on Frostfang Coast in the northwest; some two hundred more backdrop locations are spread over the six regions, and the rest of the land is [[荒野|the Wild]]. Several rivers run from the high ground down to the sea or to lakes, and highways link the regions' capitals in a ring, each with a road of its own into the Holy City; where a highway crosses a river there is a bridge.

Backdrop locations have icons by their kind: fortress (walls and a flag), ruin (broken walls), graveyard (gravestones), abbey (a spire), city quarter (white walls and a golden dome), tower, old battlefield (crossed spears and a torn banner), cave (a cave mouth in the mountains), and the Seat of a Seal-bearer (a black spire, sealed); villages, towns, farmsteads, mills, camps and shrines use the same icons as on Frostfang Coast.

Cells of [[荒野|the Wild]] carry no mark; you read them by their land: woods, hills, snowfields, plains, and in the other regions grassland, red-rock badlands (red mesas), fields, dust flats, salt flats, marsh (reeds), dead woods (bare trees), white-stone plateau and city outskirts.

The location you have just walked out of is marked: a gold frame round its cell, blinking on and off, and a gold arrow bobbing over it, meaning “you just came out of here”.

### Locations not yet open

Locations the story has not reached yet have a dimmed icon with a lock drawn at the upper right. You can step onto the cell, but press {{键|E}} and the label reads “Sealed: X”; you cannot go in, and the reason is shown at the lower left. What is sealed now is the east behind the White Knight: [[余烬镇]], [[旧矿坑]], [[烬岭山道]] and [[魔龙巢穴]], with the message “The White Knight still holds the East Road. There is no way past him yet.” Once you defeat the White Knight on [[东大路|the East Road]], the locks are gone and you can go in straight from the world map.

A few places are locked for good (in the current version): the four Seats of the Seal-bearers (the War-Giver's Bastion in [[赤痕边境|the Scarred March]], the Black Balance in [[衡谷平原|the Vale of Scales]], the Pale Rider's Ossuary in [[苍白林沼|the Pale Fens]], and the Altar Beneath in [[白坛高地|the White Altar Heights]]) and the Most Holy Hall at the heart of the Holy City, with the message “The way in is shut. Its tale is not yet told.”

Each step on the world map skips time by the minutes in the table above, times 1.5 in rain or storm (see [[天气]]). While the time is skipped, [[饱食度|satiety]] falls as usual.

Each region has its own lands (see [[#地区|Regions]] above): the northernmost rows of Frostfang Coast are mostly snowfield, and to the south lie plains, forest and hills; the other regions each divide into their own few lands by height and wetness.

### River and mountains: the only crossing

North of the Ford runs a mountain range all the way to the northern sea; south of the Ford runs a river, which flows south past the Old Barrow's cell, then turns west and runs all the way to the western sea; the Ford's own cell is river too, and cannot be stood on. So the west bank (the original part of Frostfang Coast) is enclosed: **between it and the rest of the continent, the only way is through the map of the Ford**. To enter the Ford, stand on a cell beside it (Firstlight Village, Mountain Foot or the Pilgrims' Rest), face the Ford and press {{键|E}}: going in from the west takes you to the west bank, and going in from the Pilgrims' Rest to the far bank. Leaving the Ford from the west bank puts you on the west side, and leaving from the far bank puts you on the east side. It is designed this way so that you cannot walk out to the west, circle round on the world map and come in from the east, skipping the Ford's river crossing (see [[渡口]]).

- West bank: [[云顶台地]], [[山脚]], [[晨光丘]], [[水车磨坊]], [[初原村]], [[草原]], [[森林]], [[荆棘谷]], [[古墓]], and the Ford's west bank.
- East of the river (the rest of the continent): the Ford's far bank, [[朝圣者驿站]], [[朝圣者之路]], [[荆棘修道院]], [[跪石原]], [[东大路]], [[余烬镇]], [[旧矿坑]], [[烬岭山道]], [[魔龙巢穴]], and the other five regions.

Entering the Ford from Mountain Foot, you take the stone steps between Mountain Foot and the Ford, and arrive on the west bank.

### How to get onto the world map

Walk out over the edge of a map that has a cell on the continent (not by stepping on its exit tiles), and you appear on the world map on the cell **beside** that map's cell, in the direction you walked out; where that is sea or mountains, you stand on the map's own cell. Walking out of the Ford only ever lands you on a cell on the same bank.

Most hand-made maps are ringed by woods, brush or rock, all of which can be felled or broken (see [[伐木|woodcutting]], [[采矿|mining]]); cut a way through and you can walk out over the edge. The places where you can now walk out over the edge without cutting are:

- the treeless stretches of the north, east and south edges of [[渡口]]'s far bank (except the three tiles of the east exit to the Pilgrims' Rest), and one tile on the west bank's south edge right by the river;
- one tile beside the stone-steps exit on the south edge of [[山脚]].

[[荒野|The Wild]] is open on all four sides; you can walk back to the world map from any edge.

### How to enter a place

Stand on a location and press {{键|E}} (the label reads “Enter X”), facing any way. Whichever side you stepped onto the cell from, you enter the map from that side: walk over from the west and you appear on the map's west side; if that way is blocked, you are moved to the nearest open ground. The Ford is the exception: its cell cannot be stood on, so stand beside it, face it and press {{键|E}}, and you enter at the crossing between the two maps.

## Lore

The six regions are laid out after the original text of the [[世界观|lore]]: the five Seal-bearers each keep one quarter and bear one of the Five Sufferings, and the land wears the look of that suffering (the bloody weals of the Scourging, the barren fields of the Starving, the dead woods of the Nailing, the white altar of the Lance); the Holy City is at the center, and the Most Holy Hall is still shut.

## History

- 2026-10-04: Two levels of map made: Frostfang Coast was an ordinary map, 16×12 cells, each cell 3×3 blocks; the hand-made maps sat on cells reckoned from their exits, and the rest of the land was the Wild; rivers and mountains blocked the way on the world map as well, and the Ford was the only crossing.
- 2026-10-04: The way to enter a place changed to standing on the location and pressing E.
- 2026-10-06: With Cloudtop restored, it returned to the cell north of Mountain Foot (land: snowfield).
- 2026-10-12: The cell east of the Ford became the Pilgrims' Rest; the Pilgrim's Road, the Abbey of the Thorn and the Kneeling Field were set east of it, and the line of the East Road and Emberfall moved three cells east with them.
- 2026-10-08: The edges of the snowfield no longer show black borders (grass lies under the snow); the music changed to the open-country theme.
- 2026-10-11: The world map marks the location you just walked out of; Emberfall, the Old Mine, Cinder Pass and the Dragon's Lair behind the White Knight are sealed until he is defeated.
- 2026-10-11: The world map changed to one location per cell, in its own hand-drawn map style (coastline, woods, mountain ranges, an icon per location); one cell takes 30 minutes to walk; the Ford's cell is river, entered from beside it; walking out over a map's edge lands you on the cell beside it.
- 2026-10-18: The world map grew into a whole continent and was renamed the Lands of the Five Seals: 64×48 cells, six regions, some two hundred backdrop locations, ten new lands, rivers, bridges and highways; the old Frostfang Coast became a region in the northwest, its cells unchanged.
