**Mountain Foot** lies south of [[Cloudtop|Cloudtop]] and north of [[The Ford|the Ford]]. The path down the mountain comes from the upland in the north along a gentle slope, passes an ancient [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] in a ring of flowers, and goes on through the woods to the cliff steps north of the Ford.

The shrine stone is something the game leaves on purpose as “not yet learned by the world”: whatever you do to it, the outcome is undefined, and [[The World Learns|the world learns to answer]]. Here the prologue first teaches you this.

## Map

{{map}}

The north of the map is upland two levels higher than the rest, edged by a cliff; only the two tiles the path crosses are stairs cut into the rock; the cliff is two levels high, so you can neither jump down it anywhere else nor climb it (see [[Terrain#Height|Terrain]]). Woods lie all round. The path comes down from the north exit (three tiles wide), passes the shrine stone, and leaves south by the two-tile exit on the map's south edge.

## Areas

- **The shrine stone and the flowers**: a broad bed of flowers in the middle of the map, with a [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] standing in it. Walk into this area and [[Lumi]] says it's cold near the stone and asks you to do something to it. On the ground beside the stone is a [[chalk drawing of a stone|chalk drawing of a stone]] (one of [[Pell's Drawings|Pell's Drawings]]; press {{key|E}} to look at it).
- **Waypoint statue**: southeast of the shrine stone stands a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] (named “Mountain Foot statue”).
- **The hunter's lodge**: a long-abandoned wooden cabin to the west, wood-walled and wood-floored, its door long gone. On the floor inside lies a pair of [[Gloves|Hunter's Gloves]].
- **Signpost**: by the path to the south stands a [[sign|sign]] reading “The Ford ↓”.
- **The cliff steps**: the south exit joins the stone steps on the cliff north of the Ford.

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one lives at Mountain Foot.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There are no monsters at Mountain Foot by day. It is an open-air [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]; after dark, [[zombie|zombies]] and [[bat|bats]] appear (see [[Night Creatures|night creatures]]), crumbling to dust at daybreak.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

The ground at Mountain Foot keeps growing [[Stick|sticks]], [[Stone|stones]] and [[Cut Grass|Cut Grass]] (up to 6, 5 and 6 portions at once); every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds one portion may appear. No [[Berries|berries]] grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

- The north exit leads to [[Cloudtop]].
- The south exit leads to the cliff steps north of [[The Ford|the Ford]], down to the Ford's camp.
- Walking out of [[The Clearing|the Clearing]] of the [[Tutorial|tutorial]] also brings you to the top of the path at the north of Mountain Foot; but in the standalone tutorial, the tutorial ends as soon as you walk out of the clearing.
- Beside the stone-steps exit in the south, one tile of dirt path is not an exit; walk south from there off the edge of the map and you reach the world map, [[The Lands of the Five Seals]].

## Lore

{{spoiler|The five marks on the shrine stone}}
The shrine stone is cut with five wound-marks, standing for [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]]. Lumi says the first (the Crown of Thorns) is cracked. In Pell's chalk drawing this mark is colored black, and a grey figure has his hand on it. As Chapter 1 is designed, this is [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] checking how far the First Seal has decayed, though the villagers take it for a heretic desecrating a holy thing (see [[Chapter 1]]).
{{/剧透}}

In the prologue, before the shrine stone, Lumi first asks you to “try something the world has never seen”. Once you have, she says that what the world learns is kept in the [[Threadbook|Threadbook]] ({{key|J}}).
