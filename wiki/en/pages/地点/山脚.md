**Mountain Foot** lies south of [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]] and north of [[渡口|the Ford]]. The path down the mountain comes from the upland in the north along a gentle slope, passes an ancient [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stone]] in a ring of flowers, and goes on through the woods to the cliff steps north of the Ford.

The shrine stone is something the game leaves on purpose as “not yet learned by the world”: whatever you do to it, the outcome is undefined, and [[世界会学|the world learns to answer]]. Here the prologue first teaches you this.

## Map

{{地图}}

The north of the map is upland two levels higher than the rest, edged by a cliff; only the two tiles the path crosses are a gentle slope, and jumping down anywhere else hurts you (see [[生命值|health]]). Woods lie all round. The path comes down from the north exit (three tiles wide), passes the shrine stone, and leaves south by the two-tile exit on the map's south edge.

## Areas

- **The shrine stone and the flowers**: a broad bed of flowers in the middle of the map, with a [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stone]] standing in it. Walk into this area and [[露米]] says it's cold near the stone and asks you to do something to it. On the ground beside the stone is a [[画着石碑的粉笔画|chalk drawing of a stone]] (one of [[佩尔的涂鸦|Pell's Drawings]]; press {{键|E}} to look at it).
- **Waypoint statue**: southeast of the shrine stone stands a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] (named “Mountain Foot statue”).
- **The hunter's lodge**: a long-abandoned wooden cabin to the west, wood-walled and wood-floored, its door long gone. On the floor inside lies a pair of [[手套|Hunter's Gloves]].
- **Signpost**: by the path to the south stands a [[告示牌|sign]] reading “The Ford ↓”.
- **The cliff steps**: the south exit joins the stone steps on the cliff north of the Ford.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives at Mountain Foot.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no monsters at Mountain Foot by day. It is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]; after dark, [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] appear (see [[夜行生物|night creatures]]), crumbling to dust at daybreak.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

The ground at Mountain Foot keeps growing [[树枝|sticks]], [[石块|stones]] and [[干草|Cut Grass]] (up to 6, 5 and 6 portions at once); every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds one portion may appear. No [[浆果|berries]] grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The north exit leads to [[云顶台地]].
- The south exit leads to the cliff steps north of [[渡口|the Ford]], down to the Ford's camp.
- Walking out of [[林间空地|the Clearing]] of the [[新手教程|tutorial]] also brings you to the top of the path at the north of Mountain Foot; but in the standalone tutorial, the tutorial ends as soon as you walk out of the clearing.
- Beside the stone-steps exit in the south, one tile of dirt path is not an exit; walk south from there off the edge of the map and you reach the world map, [[五印之地]].

## Lore

{{剧透|The five marks on the shrine stone}}
The shrine stone is cut with five wound-marks, standing for [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]]. Lumi says the first (the Crown of Thorns) is cracked. In Pell's chalk drawing this mark is colored black, and a grey figure has his hand on it. As Chapter 1 is designed, this is [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] checking how far the First Seal has decayed, though the villagers take it for a heretic desecrating a holy thing (see [[第一章]]).
{{/剧透}}

In the prologue, before the shrine stone, Lumi first asks you to “try something the world has never seen”. Once you have, she says that what the world learns is kept in the [[织线簿|Threadbook]] ({{键|J}}).
