**Dawn Hill** lies north of [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]]. Legend says that after [[The First Night|the First Long Night]], this is where the sun rose again. The hill is thick with flowers that face east, among them the dawnbloom [[The Vigil|The Vigil]] asks for; on its crown stands an abandoned chapel, [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]], where the old bell hangs.

Dawn Hill is an open-air [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]: there are wolves by day, two [[spark slime|spark slimes]] on the hilltop, and [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] after dark.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 40×30, ringed by woods. The north half is the crown of the hill, one level higher than the ground below, its south edge a cliff; only the three middle tiles are a gentle slope up the hill. The path comes in at the south exit (columns 19–21), crosses the sea of flowers below, winds up the slope and leads to the door of the chapel on the crown.

## Areas

- **The sea of flowers**: a broad stretch of flowers in the middle of the lower ground.
- **The crown**: the high ground north of the cliff.
- **The Chapel of the First Dawn**: a stone chapel on the north of the crown, whose door leads into [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]].
- **The Dawnstone**: an ancient stone on the crown (of the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] kind), named “The Dawnstone”. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Cut deep, and old: HERE THE SUN ROSE AGAIN. Below, newer: five small wounds, and a flame.”
- **Waypoint statue**: on the west side of the crown stands a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] (named “Dawn Hill”).

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one lives on Dawn Hill.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- 2 [[wolf|wolves]] roam the east and west of the lower ground, attack on sight, and do not come back once defeated.
- 2 [[spark slime|spark slimes]] at either end of the open hilltop, where storms strike first; they are far from both the waypoint statue and the Dawnstone, so they will not set on you the moment you arrive by waypoint. They do not come back once defeated.
- After dark, [[zombie|zombies]] and [[bat|bats]] appear (up to {{value|夜行生物上限}}), crumbling to dust at daybreak.

## Lore

The first time you come to Dawn Hill, Lumi says: this is where they say the sun came back, after the First Night; the flowers up here still face east, every one of them, and those must be the dawnbloom.

The ancient words on the Dawnstone mark the place where the sun rose again, and below them are the newer five wounds ([[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]]) and a flame. According to the words on the hearth in the chapel, Firstlight's holy flame was first kindled there from the light of dawn and carried down the hill.
