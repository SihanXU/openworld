**The Old Barrow** lies under the two standing stones at the bottom of [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]]. On the day of the storm, [[Lina]] and [[Oskar]] of Firstlight took shelter here and were trapped by the dead. The barrow goes down three levels in all: this level is the barrow itself, below it is [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]], and at the very bottom is [[The Warden's Sanctum|the Warden's Sanctum]]. [[Lost in the Barrow]] takes you through all three.

The barrow is a dark underground [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]], always in the same dim light, day or night; no night creatures appear underground, but it has its own dead.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 30×24, cave wall on every side. The entrance down from Thornwood Hollow is in the north (columns 13–15), and a flagstone passage three tiles wide runs south and opens into a great hall, with a cell off its west side and another off its east side. Braziers burn along both sides of the passage.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: the head of the steps in the north. On the ground near the entrance is a [[chalk drawing of a door|chalk drawing of a door]] (one of [[Pell's Drawings|Pell's Drawings]]): “A door, shut, a hand-mark on it. Behind it, something with a crown of bones. In front, two small people.”
- **The great hall**: the flagstone hall at the end of the passage, where skeletons roam.
- **The west cell**: where [[Lina]] is trapped. Walk into the cell and a scene plays. On the ground near the cell lie a [[Bread|Loaf marked with ash]] and a [[Helmet|Rusted Helm of the Thorn]] (Crude).
- **The east cell**: in the cell is a [[Terrain|downward stair]] cut into the rock, leading to the second level, [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]].

## Residents

{{residents}}

[[Lina]] is trapped in the west cell, waiting for someone to find her. Once you have spoken with her (one of the objectives of [[Lost in the Barrow]]), she stays where she is; only after the quest is handed in does she go back to [[Firstlight Village]].

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- At the start there are 3 [[skeleton|skeletons]] and 1 [[bat|bat]], all of which attack on sight.
- Skeletons slowly come back: while you are on this level, one may come out every 240 seconds (4 game hours), up to 2.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

The barrow's ground yields nothing, and there are no trees to fell. The cave walls can be broken with a pickaxe (see [[Mining|mining]]), but this level has its own cell on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], so breaking through the edge takes you out onto the world map.

## Lore

{{spoiler|What Lina says}}
Walk into the west cell and Lina says it was not the dead who shut them in here but a man in grey, who pulled the inner door to from outside and then pushed a loaf of bread under it. There was a grey thumbprint on the bread. Oskar went down the stairs in the east cell to look for another way out and never came back. Lumi says the dead in here want to lie down but can't, like a bed that's too cold.

As Chapter 1 is designed, the villagers believe [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] trapped the two; in truth what he shut in was the warden who will not rest, and the bread he left bears the mark of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]].
