**The Timberland** is a stretch of old woods under the mist of [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], opened up through [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]. It is where the Held Ground gets its wood: the trees stand thick and many, and felled trees grow back. When Alberic opens it he says: “Trees. Old ones. They were here before the crown, and they will be here after me.”

The Timberland is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[阿尔贝里克]]'s Expanding the Held Ground panel, once the land is at level I, put 15 {{物品|木头}} and 5 {{物品|石块}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: dense woods to fell, that grow back; mushrooms and berries under the trees; sticks and grass on the ground.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 32×24, its outer two tiles all old trees and pines. Inside is grassland, with trees and pines in thick rows, and only a five-tile-wide clearing down the middle from the south entrance all the way to the north.

## Areas

- **The entrance and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the entrance stands a sign: “THE TIMBERLAND — fell what you need; the old woods grow back.” Beside the sign is a [[柴堆（自然物）|wood pile]].
- **Mushroom ring**: a [[蘑菇圈|mushroom ring]] in the woods to the east.
- **The dense woods**: trees on both sides of the middle clearing.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- **Logs**: fell trees with an axe for [[木头|Logs]] (see [[伐木|woodcutting]]). A felled tree leaves a stump, which grows back into a tree after two game days (if it has light and nothing lies on top of it).
- At the start, 3 portions of {{物品|浆果}} lie on the ground.
- **The ground's yield**: the grass slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6 portions), and {{物品|蘑菇}} (up to 4); every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds one of each may appear.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The three-tile entrance in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out to return to [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], standing due south of the longsword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this ground's card in the [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] panel, and [[阿尔贝里克]] sends you to just inside the Timberland's south entrance. While the ground has not been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Timberland is not on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]] and is not drawn on the [[世界地图|world map]]. [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]] works here as usual, taking you back to where you stood before entering the Held Ground.
