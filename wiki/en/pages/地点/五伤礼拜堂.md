**The Chapel of the Five** is the stone chapel north of [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]]. [[Father Caius]] keeps the five holy wounds here, takes **the Pilgrim's Vow** from pilgrims, and sleeps here at night as well.

## Map

{{map}}

16×12, one stone-walled room, with its entrance in the south wall. Under the north wall stands the altar of the five wounds, with a lit candlestand on either side; a bookstand stands to the west; two benches stand in the nave; the priest's cot is in the northeast corner.

## Areas

- **The altar of the five wounds**: the [[altar|altar]] under the north wall. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Astra on the holy wood, carved in white stone: a crown of thorns, the scourge's marks, the ribs of the fast, the nails, the lance in the side. Below: WHO BEARS, IS NEAR TO GOD.”
- **Before the altar**: the three rows of floor tiles in front of the altar. Until the White Knight falls, the first time you step here you take the vow (see below).
- **The Litany of the Five**: the [[bookstand|bookstand]] on the west side. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid. As long as one bears the Five, the Long Night shall not return.” This bookstand is only for reading; it sells no books.
- **The priest's cot**: the northeast corner.

## Residents

{{residents}}

- [[Father Caius]]: from 6:00 to 12:00 he stands before the altar, and from 21:00 to 6:00 he sleeps on the cot; from noon to evening he is at the chapel door in the Rest.

## Lore

{{spoiler|The Pilgrim's Vow}}
Once the priest has welcomed you at the Rest, walk up to the altar and the scene of the vow plays. The priest leads you through [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]]:

> Kneel. — Say after me. The first suffering: the Crown. He let them press the thorns into his brow, and did not lift his hand.
> The second: the Scourge. Bound to the pillar, he bled for every one of us. The third: Hunger. On the scales his flesh grew lighter than sin.
> The fourth: the Nails. Upon the holy wood he accepted death. The fifth: the Lance. When it pierced his heart, the sun rose.
> Five shrines stand on the road east, one for each. Light them as you go — lay at each what its suffering asks. Then the abbey, then the knight.
> The Seal-bearer has held that road three hundred years. The Holy See asks you to overcome him and bring home the Crown, the first of the five. ...He was due relief, once. That is not a pilgrim's concern.

Lumi asks: “‘Was due relief’? What does that mean? — He went quiet. I don't like it when grown-ups go quiet.” After the vow you receive the [[Sworn Scallop|Sworn Scallop]], and [[The First Seal]] moves on to the stage of lighting the [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]].

After the White Knight falls, the vow scene no longer plays.
