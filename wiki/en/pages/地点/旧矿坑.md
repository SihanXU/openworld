**The Old Mine** is an abandoned mine in the mountain north of [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]. Its tunnels hold [[铁矿脉|iron veins]] and [[煤层|coal seams]]; break them with a [[镐|pickaxe]] for [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and [[煤炭|Coal]], just what the smith [[加里克]] needs to forge [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]]. The deepest chamber is richest in iron and also has two [[金矿脉|gold veins]], guarded by a [[独眼巨人|cyclops]] (the only other gold vein is in [[采石场|the Quarry]] of the Held Ground).

The Old Mine is a dark underground [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]], the same by day and night. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30, cave wall on every side. The entrance is in the south (columns 19–21), and a main tunnel three tiles wide runs north to the deepest chamber, with four lit braziers along the way. From the middle of the main tunnel one tunnel branches west and one east, and there is a short side passage in the southwest; each tunnel ends in a small chamber.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: in the south. On the ground just inside lies an [[镐|Old Pickaxe]] (iron pickaxe).
- **The west tunnel**: leads to the northwest chamber, with coal seams and one iron vein.
- **The east tunnel**: leads to the east chamber, with coal seams and an iron vein.
- **The southwest passage**: a small chamber with one coal seam.
- **The deep chamber**: at the north end of the main tunnel, with two iron veins and, below them, two [[金矿脉|gold veins]], guarded by the [[独眼巨人|cyclops]].

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one is in the mine.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- At the start there are 2 [[蝙蝠|bats]], 3 [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spiders]], 1 [[骷髅|skeleton]], and the [[独眼巨人|cyclops]] in the deep chamber. The cyclops carries 2 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]], which drop when it is defeated.
- While you are in the mine, a spider may come back every 150 seconds (up to 3), and a bat every 180 seconds (up to 2). The skeleton and the cyclops do not come back.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- At the start there are 4 [[铁矿脉|iron veins]], 4 [[煤层|coal seams]] and 2 [[金矿脉|gold veins]]. Break an iron vein with a pickaxe for 2 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]], a coal seam for 2 [[煤炭|Coal]], and a gold vein for 2 [[金矿石|Gold Ore]]. A stone pickaxe will do too, breaking a vein in two blows; strike one bare-handed and you are told “Too hard.” See [[采矿]].
- A vein broken open leaves a spent face in its place (which cannot be broken), and after one game day it turns back into a vein in the same spot.
- Dragonsbane takes 5 iron ore and 3 coal. In [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]], Land II takes 5 iron ore and Land III takes 3 gold ore.
- Veins elsewhere: [[晨光丘矿洞|Dawn Hill Mine]], open already in Chapter 1, has 5 iron veins and 4 coal seams; there are 3 iron veins beyond the third ring of mist in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], and the Held Ground's [[采石场|Quarry]] has 3 iron veins, 3 coal seams and 1 gold vein. Veins grow back the same way everywhere.

## Lore

{{待设计}}

In the old main story, the iron and coal the smith needs for Dragonsbane are all in this old mine. Bats, spiders and dead miners live in the mine, and a cyclops holds the best iron ore at its deepest point. This content is older than the setting of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] and, as planned, will be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see [[第二章]]).
