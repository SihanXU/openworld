**The Old Mine** is an abandoned mine in the mountain north of [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]. Its tunnels hold [[iron vein|iron veins]] and [[coal seam|coal seams]]; break them with a [[Pickaxe|pickaxe]] for [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and [[Coal|Coal]], just what the smith [[Garrick]] needs to forge [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]]. The deepest chamber is richest in iron and also has two [[gold vein|gold veins]], guarded by a [[cyclops|cyclops]] (the only other gold vein is in [[The Quarry|the Quarry]] of the Held Ground).

The Old Mine is a dark underground [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]], the same by day and night. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 40×30, cave wall on every side. The entrance is in the south (columns 19–21), and a main tunnel three tiles wide runs north to the deepest chamber, with four lit braziers along the way. From the middle of the main tunnel one tunnel branches west and one east, and there is a short side passage in the southwest; each tunnel ends in a small chamber.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: in the south. On the ground just inside lies an [[Pickaxe|Old Pickaxe]] (iron pickaxe).
- **The west tunnel**: leads to the northwest chamber, with coal seams and one iron vein.
- **The east tunnel**: leads to the east chamber, with coal seams and an iron vein.
- **The southwest passage**: a small chamber with one coal seam.
- **The deep chamber**: at the north end of the main tunnel, with two iron veins and, below them, two [[gold vein|gold veins]], guarded by the [[cyclops|cyclops]].

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is in the mine.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- At the start there are 2 [[bat|bats]], 3 [[cave spider|cave spiders]], 1 [[skeleton|skeleton]], and the [[cyclops|cyclops]] in the deep chamber. The cyclops carries 2 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]], which drop when it is defeated.
- While you are in the mine, a spider may come back every 150 seconds (up to 3), and a bat every 180 seconds (up to 2). The skeleton and the cyclops do not come back.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- At the start there are 4 [[iron vein|iron veins]], 4 [[coal seam|coal seams]] and 2 [[gold vein|gold veins]]. Break an iron vein with a pickaxe for 2 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]], a coal seam for 2 [[Coal|Coal]], and a gold vein for 2 [[Gold Ore|Gold Ore]]. A stone pickaxe will do too, breaking a vein in two blows; strike one bare-handed and you are told “Too hard.” See [[Mining]].
- A vein broken open leaves a spent face in its place (which cannot be broken), and after one game day it turns back into a vein in the same spot.
- Dragonsbane takes 5 iron ore and 3 coal. In [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]], Land II takes 5 iron ore and Land III takes 3 gold ore.
- Veins elsewhere: [[Dawn Hill Mine|Dawn Hill Mine]], open already in Chapter 1, has 5 iron veins and 4 coal seams; there are 3 iron veins beyond the third ring of mist in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], and the Held Ground's [[The Quarry|Quarry]] has 3 iron veins, 3 coal seams and 1 gold vein. Veins grow back the same way everywhere.

## Lore

{{tbd}}

In the old main story, the iron and coal the smith needs for Dragonsbane are all in this old mine. Bats, spiders and dead miners live in the mine, and a cyclops holds the best iron ore at its deepest point. This content is older than the setting of [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]] and, as planned, will be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see [[Chapter 2]]).
