**The Ford** is a crossing on a river, below the cliffs of [[Mountain Foot]] and east of [[Firstlight Village]]. The ferrywoman [[Hesper]] camps on the west bank. A storm tore the ferry rope loose, and someone has pulled three planks out of the little bridge over the river, so there is no ready way across.

The river splits the map in two: the west bank joins Mountain Foot and Firstlight Village, while the far bank leads east to [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], and beyond it to [[The Pilgrim's Road]], [[The Kneeling Field]] and [[The East Road]]. On [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] this river runs all the way to the sea, and the Ford is the only crossing between the eastern and western halves. Reaching the far bank is one goal of the quest [[The First Seal]], and it judges only the result, never the route: mending the little bridge, taking the ferry, felling the dead tree, freezing the river, or any other way the world learns — all of them count as crossing.

The Ford is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]; no [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] appear at night.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 48×30. Along the north runs a mossy cliff with a single flight of stone steps two tiles wide, a little west of the middle, leading up to Mountain Foot. The river comes down from the north, narrows to six tiles at the dead tree, widens before the jetty into a stretch of still water ten tiles across, then bends south off the map. The west and south are wooded; the western woods leave a gap at rows 18 to 20, the way to Firstlight Village.

From the camp two things can be seen in the distance: the jetty with the ferry, and a lone dead tree standing where the river runs narrowest.

## Areas

- **The camp**: a clearing in the middle of the west bank. Here are Hesper's [[tent|tent]] (press {{key|E}} at it to crawl inside, where there are a bedroll, a crate and a candle; walk down to come out), her [[chest|chest]] (8 slots, holding a [[Rope|Rope]] and one of Hesper's [[Bread|Bread]] loaves), a burning [[campfire|campfire]], a log bench and cooking rack by the fire, and firewood and a [[stump|stump]] beside the tent. By the stump lies an [[Axe|old axe]] (an Iron Axe), by the fire there is a [[Torch|Torch]], and where the road enters the camp stands a lantern post.
- **The slime burrow**: a rocky mound at the foot of the cliff in the northwest corner, with a hole in it (a [[slime burrow|slime burrow]]); in front are trampled bare mud, two [[Bone|Bones]] and a few boulders. The [[fire slime|fire slime]] lives here.
- **The hay slope**: a wide stretch of dry grass north of the camp, with a haystack beside it. Fire spreads fast over dry grass (see [[Elements|Elements]]).
- **The frosted bank**: by the river in the northeast corner snow has gathered, and a few tiles of the river's edge are iced over. The [[frost slime|frost slime]] stays here by day.
- **The jetty**: east of the camp a short pier juts from the bank, with the ferry (a [[ferry raft|ferry raft]]) moored beside it. On the bank stand the [[ferry winch|ferry winch]] with its rope snapped and an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]]. There is a pier on the far bank too.
- **The dead tree**: on a small spit of bank where the river is narrowest stands a [[dead tree|dead tree]], with rock to its north and south, so it can be cut only from the west.
- **The marsh**: a few pools south of the jetty, ringed with reeds and ferns. Lightning travels through the water (see [[Elements|Elements]]).
- **The broken bridge**: a little wooden bridge east of the marsh, on row 22. Its piers still stand at both ends, but the planks of the middle three tiles are gone. Thorns are nailed into the bridge posts (“thorns nailed into the bridge posts”), and on the ground by the bridge is a [[chalk drawing of a bridge|chalk drawing of a bridge]] (one of [[Pell's Drawings|Pell's Drawings]]).
- **The waypoint statue**: beside the path down from the stone steps to the camp stands a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]] (named “The Ford”).
- **The far bank**: the whole stretch east of the river from column 36, bright with wildflowers, with a few clumps of trees around a path heading east. At the end of the path stands a [[sign|sign]] reading “→ Emberfall”; the path leaves by the eastern exit for [[The Pilgrims' Rest]]. Set foot on the far bank and you have crossed the river.

### Ways Across

Each of the ways below will take you to the far bank. Lumi's hints and [[Joss]]'s words both say that crossing some other way counts too.

1. **Mend the little bridge**: the broken bridge is missing three tiles in the middle. Fell a tree with an [[Axe|Axe]] (a Stone Axe brings it down in two blows) to get [[Log|Logs]]; with the axe on you, press {{key|C}} to make the logs into [[Plank|Planks]] (two planks per log), or left-click a log with the axe. Stand at the edge of the gap and press {{key|G}} to lay a plank on the water, and that tile becomes a wooden bridge. Fill all three and you can walk across. A plank laid on any shallow water becomes a wooden bridge; it need not be in the gap.
2. **Take the ferry**: the rope in the camp's chest belongs to no one. Hold it and press {{key|G}} at the winch: the rope is joined, and the ferry line pulls taut again. Then, empty-handed, press {{key|E}} at Hesper (the label is “Ask Hesper to take you across”), and she poles the raft to the pier on the far bank. The ferry takes you only once: look for her again after you have crossed, and she will not pole the raft again.
3. **Fell the dead tree**: stand west of the dead tree and left-click it with an axe. The first two blows only bite in; on the third it falls away from the axe, across the river, and the fallen trunk becomes a stretch of wooden bridge, leaving a stump where the tree stood.
4. **Freeze the river**: in [[Firstlight Village]], [[Tobin]]'s handcart holds a [[Wand|Wand of Frost]]. Press {{key|G}} at Tobin with anything edible to give it to him, and he lets you have the wand; after that you can take it with {{key|E}} without it counting as theft. Hold the wand and left-click the river: the water freezes over and the ice spreads to the sides; freeze ahead of you blow by blow and you can walk across. After a while the ice melts back to water, and anyone standing on it is pushed ashore.
5. **Other ways**: do something no rule defines to the river, the ferry, the winch or the dead tree, and [[The World Learns|the world learns]] an outcome; if the outcome puts you on the far bank, that counts just the same.

### Never Stuck

The Ford has two safeguard rules so that every way across can never be cut off at once:

- Until the winch is mended, the Ford always has a rope. If the original is burned or destroyed, Hesper says “That was my last good rope. … No — there is the old one off the raft,” and a new rope appears on the ground.
- If you have not yet crossed and the rope, the dead tree and the Wand of Frost are all gone, without the ferry or the dead tree having been used, Hesper says “Come, I will take you over on the raft myself, line or no line,” and takes you straight to the far bank.

## Residents

{{residents}}

[[Hesper]] is the Ford's only resident. She sleeps by the campfire, tends it early in the morning, at noon and at dusk (the campfire must be kept lit), watches the river from the jetty in the morning and mends fishing nets in the afternoon; in a storm she waits at the mouth of her tent for the rain to stop. Once you have crossed, she spends her days watching the river from the jetty.

[[The Grey Man]] passes through the Ford in the story, but does not live here.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

The two slimes are the Ford's own creatures; once defeated, they do not come back:

- The [[fire slime|fire slime]] lives in the slime burrow and fights back only when struck. At night it wanders over toward the hay slope.
- The [[frost slime|frost slime]] stays on the frosted bank by day; after nightfall it turns hostile, wanders north of the jetty and attacks anyone it meets. It will not come near a lit campfire, so the camp is safe at night.

The Ford is a peaceful zone; no zombies or bats appear here at night.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

There are 3 [[Stick|Sticks]] under the western woods, 3 [[Stone|Stones]] on the slope north of the camp and 3 bundles of [[Cut Grass|Cut Grass]] on the hay slope; afterwards the ground slowly grows more (sticks up to 6, stones up to 5, cut grass up to 6, with a chance of one more of each every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds). Every tree in the woods can be felled, and the stumps grow back into full trees in {{value|树木再生}} game days. Boulders can be broken with a pickaxe for stones.

## Lore

Hesper has ferried this river for thirty-one years. The storm tore the ferry rope loose; the little bridge, though, was not washed away by any storm. She says a rider all in white took it apart, a crown of thorns grown into his helm: he pulled the planks out one by one and laid the nails out on the jetty, as though paying a toll. That rider is [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]]. Only eight nails lie on the jetty; for what became of the ninth, see [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]].

The first time you come down to the Ford, Hesper tells you this, sends you on to Firstlight Village to lend a hand first, and gives you half a loaf of [[Bread|Bread]] for the road.

{{spoiler|After the Crossing}}
- On the Night of Thanksgiving, after [[The Grey Man]] steals Firstlight Village's sacred fire, he walks across this river, the water freezing under his feet as he goes and the ice melting away behind him. Come to the Ford after that night and before the Church's letter arrives, and Hesper speaks of it; far off to the east, a point of light goes out along the road.
- Once you have asked Hesper about it during the quest [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)|The Pilgrim's Road]], she shows you the thorns in the bridge posts: the knight nailed thorns into the places where the planks had been, as if marking something as his own. That bridge was built by her husband Aldo together with Joss, and in the second year after it was finished the river took Aldo.
- Once the Church has summoned you, set foot on the far bank and Hesper tells you the road east leads first to [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Church's waystation]]; the rider went much farther than that, past the burned farmstead. “If you find him — mind his bow.”
