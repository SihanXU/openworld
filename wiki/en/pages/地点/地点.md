The game world is a continent ringed by sea, called [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]]. Every cell of the continent is a map: the places the story passes through are hand-made maps, and the remaining cells are [[荒野|the Wild]], generated from the land. The game now has 30 hand-made maps (not counting small interiors such as houses, shops, farmhouses and tents), plus the world map itself. They are all on [[霜牙海岸|Frostfang Coast]] in the northwest; the continent's other five regions ([[赤痕边境|the Scarred March]], [[衡谷平原|the Vale of Scales]], [[苍白林沼|the Pale Fens]], [[白坛高地|the White Altar Heights]] and [[圣城|the Holy City]]) hold some two hundred **backdrop locations**: you can walk into them, but they have no story yet; they have no pages of their own and are listed on their regions' pages.

{{地区表}}

The hand-made maps are joined by exits on their edges: walk onto an exit's tiles and you enter the neighboring map. Interiors and the underground levels are not on the edges, and are entered by doors or stairs. Two maps are not on the continent: [[林间空地|the Clearing]], used by the tutorial, and [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], reached with [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]].

The game's course runs roughly north to south, then east: you wake at [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]], go down by [[山脚|Mountain Foot]] to [[渡口|the Ford]], west into [[初原村|Firstlight Village]], south through [[草原|the Grassland]], [[森林|the Forest]] and [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]] down into [[古墓|the Old Barrow]], back to the village and on to [[水车磨坊|Millbrook]] and [[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]], then back to the Ford to cross the river, east through [[朝圣者驿站|the Pilgrims' Rest]], [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]] (with [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]] to its north) and [[跪石原|the Kneeling Field]], and onto [[东大路|the East Road]]. For the whole route, see [[主线流程]]. West and east of the Forest lie two places of side quests: the charcoal burner's [[烧炭谷|Charcoal Dell]] and the fisherwoman's [[芦苇湾|Reed Bend]].

## Connections

The diagram below lays out the maps by their places on the world map: north up, south down; horizontal and vertical lines are exits on the maps' edges, arrows are stairs or doors.

```
                                 Cloudtop
                                    │
           Dawn Hill          Mountain Foot                                      Abbey of the Thorn                                      Old Mine   Dragon's Lair
               │                    │                                                   │                                                    │            │
Millbrook ── Firstlight ──── The Ford (west bank ≈river≈ far bank) ── Pilgrims' Rest ── Pilgrim's Road ── Kneeling Field ── East Road ── Emberfall ── Cinder Pass
   ↓              │      ↓
Mill Cellar   Grassland   Chapel of the First Dawn (on Dawn Hill)
                  │
Charcoal Dell ── Forest ── Reed Bend (the river bend, the river's own cell)
     ↓              │          ↓
 Orrin's Hut   Thornwood Hollow   Hilde's Cabin
                    │
               Old Barrow → Ossuary of the Thorn → Warden's Sanctum
```

- **On the mountain**: [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]] has a single exit to the south, leading to [[山脚|Mountain Foot]]. The path south of Mountain Foot goes down to the cliff steps north of the Ford.
- **The Ford**: a river splits [[渡口|the Ford]] in two. The west bank joins Mountain Foot (north) and Firstlight Village (west); the far bank joins the Pilgrims' Rest (east). For the ways across the river, see [[渡口#区域|the Ford]].
- **Round Firstlight Village**: [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] has an exit on each side: the Ford to the east, [[水车磨坊|Millbrook]] to the west, [[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]] to the north, and [[草原|the Grassland]] to the south. Behind Astra's altar on the village square is the door of [[初原村礼拜堂|Firstlight Chapel]]. Millbrook's mill door leads to [[磨坊地窖|the Mill Cellar]]; the chapel door on the crown of Dawn Hill leads to [[初晓礼拜堂|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] (not the same place as Firstlight Chapel).
- **South**: south of the Grassland is [[森林|the Forest]], south of the Forest is [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]], and between the two standing stones at the bottom of Thornwood Hollow is the entrance to [[古墓|the Old Barrow]]. The fork west of the Forest leads to [[烧炭谷|the Charcoal Dell]] (the thatched hut's door leads to [[奥林的小屋|Orrin's Hut]]), and the path from the clearing on the east leads to [[芦苇湾|the Reed Bend]] (the cabin's door leads to [[希尔德的小屋|Hilde's Cabin]]). The Reed Bend takes the river's own cell on the world map; that cell cannot be stood on, and you can only walk in from the Forest. The stairs in the Old Barrow's east cell go down to [[荆棘骸骨堂|the Ossuary of the Thorn]], and the stairs in the ossuary's northeast corner go down again to [[守墓者圣所|the Warden's Sanctum]].
- **The Pilgrim's Road**: east of the Ford's far bank comes first [[朝圣者驿站|the Pilgrims' Rest]], whose doors lead to [[五伤礼拜堂|the Chapel of the Five]] and [[朝圣者客舍|the Pilgrims' Hostel]]. East of the Rest is [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]]; from the middle of the road a path climbs north up the hill to [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]], whose doors lead to [[骑士团大厅|the Chapter Hall]] and [[修道院地下墓室|the Abbey Crypt]]. East of the Pilgrim's Road is [[跪石原|the Kneeling Field]], and east of the Kneeling Field is [[东大路|the East Road]].
- **East**: the exit at the east end of [[东大路|the East Road]] to [[余烬镇|Emberfall]] opens only once [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]] falls. North of Emberfall is [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]], east of it [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]], and at the top of the pass [[魔龙巢穴|the Dragon's Lair]]. The stretch east of Emberfall is old content from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].
- **Maps of their own**: [[林间空地|the Clearing]] is the ground of the [[新手教程|tutorial]]; its only way out leads to Mountain Foot, but walking out ends the tutorial and returns you to the title screen. [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] is not on the continent: defeat the White Knight to gain [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]], then hold it and right-click yourself to go there and back.
- **The world map**: walk out over a map's edge where there is no exit and no trees or rocks in the way, and you are on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]]; stand on a location and press {{键|E}} to enter it. The continent's cells with no hand-made map are all [[荒野|the Wild]].

The [[世界地图|world map]] ({{键|M}}) draws the maps you have visited by the relative positions above and marks the [[传送雕像|waypoint statues]] you have woken; click a statue to travel to it. Interiors, the two levels below the Old Barrow, the Held Ground and the Lands of the Five Seals are not drawn on the world map.

## Peaceful and Combat Zones

The minimap under the clock says which kind the current map is (“Peaceful zone” or “Combat zone · monsters at night”). For the rules, see [[和平区域与战斗区域]].

- **Peaceful zones**: [[云顶台地]], [[渡口]], [[初原村]], [[水车磨坊]], [[朝圣者驿站]], [[余烬镇]], [[誓守之地]], [[五印之地]]. No [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear at night. The Ford's own two slimes are not affected and go about as usual.
- **Combat zones**: all other maps. Of these, the open-air maps ([[山脚]], [[林间空地]], [[草原]], [[森林]], [[烧炭谷]], [[芦苇湾]], [[荆棘谷]], [[晨光丘]], [[朝圣者之路]], [[荆棘修道院]], [[跪石原]], [[东大路]], [[烬岭山道]] and all of [[荒野|the Wild]]) have [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] after dark. The barrow's three levels, the Mill Cellar, the Abbey Crypt, the Old Mine and the Dragon's Lair are dark underground places, and the Chapel of the First Dawn, the Chapel of the Five, the Pilgrims' Hostel and the Chapter Hall are indoors; night creatures do not appear in any of them.

## Waypoint Statues

Six maps each have a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]]: [[山脚]], [[渡口]], [[初原村]], [[荆棘谷]], [[晨光丘]], [[东大路]]. Wake one with {{键|E}} and it becomes a [[难度|resting place]], and you can also travel to it from the world map.

## History

- 2026-10-02: The Ford made as a single-map demo; the same day, the meadow to its west and the forest to its south were added, along with the old main story's five maps from the East Road to the Dragon's Lair.
- 2026-10-02: The first prologue: Cloudtop, the slope and Mountain Foot, all the way down the mountain to the Ford.
- 2026-10-03: The meadow became Firstlight Village, and the cows and chickens moved to the new Grassland; Thornwood Hollow and the Old Barrow added south of the village.
- 2026-10-04: Two levels of map: every map placed on the world map of Frostfang Coast, with the Wild generated in the other cells. Cloudtop and the slope removed, and the tutorial moved to the new Clearing.
- 2026-10-05: Chapter 1: the White Knight's Thorn Kingdom added on the East Road; the road to Emberfall opens only once he falls.
- 2026-10-06: Cloudtop restored as the start of a new game.
- 2026-10-07: The Ossuary of the Thorn and the Warden's Sanctum added as two levels below the Old Barrow; Millbrook and the Mill Cellar added west of Firstlight Village, and Dawn Hill and the Chapel of the First Dawn to its north; the Held Ground added.
- 2026-10-08: Every map given its own music (see [[配乐]]); five signs and the notice board gained Chinese text.
- 2026-10-12: The line of the Pilgrim's Road added between the Ford and the East Road: the Pilgrims' Rest (the Chapel of the Five, the Pilgrims' Hostel), the Pilgrim's Road, the Abbey of the Thorn (the Chapter Hall, the Abbey Crypt) and the Kneeling Field. The east exit of the Ford's far bank now leads to the Pilgrims' Rest instead of straight to the East Road.
- 2026-10-13: Firstlight Village's church merged into Sister Ilse's chapel and moved onto the square behind Astra's altar, as Firstlight Chapel.
- 2026-10-13: [[烧炭谷|The Charcoal Dell]] and [[芦苇湾|the Reed Bend]] added on either side of the Forest, each with a hut.
