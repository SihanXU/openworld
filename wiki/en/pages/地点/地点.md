The game world is a continent ringed by sea, called [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]]. Every cell of the continent is a map: the places the story passes through are hand-made maps, and the remaining cells are [[The Wild|the Wild]], generated from the land. The game now has 30 hand-made maps (not counting small interiors such as houses, shops, farmhouses and tents), plus the world map itself. They are all on [[Frostfang Coast|Frostfang Coast]] in the northwest; the continent's other five regions ([[The Scarred March|the Scarred March]], [[The Vale of Scales|the Vale of Scales]], [[The Pale Fens|the Pale Fens]], [[The White Altar Heights|the White Altar Heights]] and [[The Holy City|the Holy City]]) hold some two hundred **backdrop locations**: you can walk into them, but they have no story yet; they have no pages of their own and are listed on their regions' pages.

{{region table}}

The hand-made maps are joined by exits on their edges: walk onto an exit's tiles and you enter the neighboring map. Interiors and the underground levels are not on the edges, and are entered by doors or stairs. Two maps are not on the continent: [[The Clearing|the Clearing]], used by the tutorial, and [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], reached with [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]].

The game's course runs roughly north to south, then east: you wake at [[Cloudtop|Cloudtop]], go down by [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]] to [[The Ford|the Ford]], west into [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]], south through [[The Grassland|the Grassland]], [[The Forest|the Forest]] and [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]] down into [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]], back to the village and on to [[Millbrook|Millbrook]] and [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]], then back to the Ford to cross the river, east through [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]], [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] (with [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]] to its north) and [[The Kneeling Field|the Kneeling Field]], and onto [[The East Road|the East Road]]. For the whole route, see [[Main Quest Walkthrough]]. West and east of the Forest lie two places of side quests: the charcoal burner's [[The Charcoal Dell|Charcoal Dell]] and the fisherwoman's [[The Reed Bend|Reed Bend]].

## Connections

The diagram below lays out the maps by their places on the world map: north up, south down; horizontal and vertical lines are exits on the maps' edges, arrows are stairs or doors.

```
                                 Cloudtop
                                    │
           Dawn Hill          Mountain Foot                                      Abbey of the Thorn                                      Old Mine   Dragon's Lair
               │                    │                                                   │                                                    │            │
Millbrook ── Firstlight ──── The Ford (west bank ≈river≈ far bank) ── Pilgrims' Rest ── Pilgrim's Road ── Kneeling Field ── East Road ── Emberfall ── Cinder Pass
   ↓              │      ↓
Mill Cellar   Grassland   Chapel of the First Dawn (on Dawn Hill)
                  │
Charcoal Dell ── Forest ── Reed Bend (the river bend, the river's own cell)
     ↓              │          ↓
 Orrin's Hut   Thornwood Hollow   Hilde's Cabin
                    │
               Old Barrow → Ossuary of the Thorn → Warden's Sanctum
```

- **On the mountain**: [[Cloudtop|Cloudtop]] has a single exit to the south, leading to [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]]. The path south of Mountain Foot goes down to the cliff steps north of the Ford.
- **The Ford**: a river splits [[The Ford|the Ford]] in two. The west bank joins Mountain Foot (north) and Firstlight Village (west); the far bank joins the Pilgrims' Rest (east). For the ways across the river, see [[The Ford#Areas|the Ford]].
- **Round Firstlight Village**: [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] has an exit on each side: the Ford to the east, [[Millbrook|Millbrook]] to the west, [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]] to the north, and [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] to the south. Behind Astra's altar on the village square is the door of [[Firstlight Chapel|Firstlight Chapel]]. Millbrook's mill door leads to [[The Mill Cellar|the Mill Cellar]]; the chapel door on the crown of Dawn Hill leads to [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] (not the same place as Firstlight Chapel).
- **South**: south of the Grassland is [[The Forest|the Forest]], south of the Forest is [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]], and between the two standing stones at the bottom of Thornwood Hollow is the entrance to [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]]. The fork west of the Forest leads to [[The Charcoal Dell|the Charcoal Dell]] (the thatched hut's door leads to [[Orrin's Hut|Orrin's Hut]]), and the path from the clearing on the east leads to [[The Reed Bend|the Reed Bend]] (the cabin's door leads to [[Hilde's Cabin|Hilde's Cabin]]). The Reed Bend takes the river's own cell on the world map; that cell cannot be stood on, and you can only walk in from the Forest. The stairs in the Old Barrow's east cell go down to [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]], and the stairs in the ossuary's northeast corner go down again to [[The Warden's Sanctum|the Warden's Sanctum]].
- **The Pilgrim's Road**: east of the Ford's far bank comes first [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]], whose doors lead to [[Chapel of the Five|the Chapel of the Five]] and [[The Pilgrims' Hostel|the Pilgrims' Hostel]]. East of the Rest is [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]]; from the middle of the road a path climbs north up the hill to [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]], whose doors lead to [[The Chapter Hall|the Chapter Hall]] and [[The Abbey Crypt|the Abbey Crypt]]. East of the Pilgrim's Road is [[The Kneeling Field|the Kneeling Field]], and east of the Kneeling Field is [[The East Road|the East Road]].
- **East**: the exit at the east end of [[The East Road|the East Road]] to [[Emberfall|Emberfall]] opens only once [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]] falls. North of Emberfall is [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]], east of it [[Cinder Pass|Cinder Pass]], and at the top of the pass [[The Dragon's Lair|the Dragon's Lair]]. The stretch east of Emberfall is old content from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].
- **Maps of their own**: [[The Clearing|the Clearing]] is the ground of the [[Tutorial|tutorial]]; its only way out leads to Mountain Foot, but walking out ends the tutorial and returns you to the title screen. [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] is not on the continent: defeat the White Knight to gain [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]], then hold it and right-click yourself to go there and back.
- **The world map**: walk out over a map's edge where there is no exit and no trees or rocks in the way, and you are on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]]; stand on a location and press {{key|E}} to enter it. The continent's cells with no hand-made map are all [[The Wild|the Wild]].

The [[World Map|world map]] ({{key|M}}) draws the maps you have visited by the relative positions above and marks the [[waypoint statue|waypoint statues]] you have woken; click a statue to travel to it. Interiors, the two levels below the Old Barrow, the Held Ground and the Lands of the Five Seals are not drawn on the world map.

## Peaceful and Combat Zones

The minimap under the clock says which kind the current map is (“Peaceful zone” or “Combat zone · monsters at night”). For the rules, see [[Peaceful and Combat Zones]].

- **Peaceful zones**: [[Cloudtop]], [[The Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]], [[Millbrook]], [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], [[Emberfall]], [[The Held Ground]], [[The Lands of the Five Seals]]. No [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] appear at night. The Ford's own two slimes are not affected and go about as usual.
- **Combat zones**: all other maps. Of these, the open-air maps ([[Mountain Foot]], [[The Clearing]], [[The Grassland]], [[The Forest]], [[The Charcoal Dell]], [[The Reed Bend]], [[Thornwood Hollow]], [[Dawn Hill]], [[The Pilgrim's Road]], [[Abbey of the Thorn]], [[The Kneeling Field]], [[The East Road]], [[Cinder Pass]] and all of [[The Wild|the Wild]]) have [[zombie|zombies]] and [[bat|bats]] after dark. The barrow's three levels, the Mill Cellar, the Abbey Crypt, the Old Mine and the Dragon's Lair are dark underground places, and the Chapel of the First Dawn, the Chapel of the Five, the Pilgrims' Hostel and the Chapter Hall are indoors; night creatures do not appear in any of them.

## Waypoint Statues

Six maps each have a [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]]: [[Mountain Foot]], [[The Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]], [[Thornwood Hollow]], [[Dawn Hill]], [[The East Road]]. Wake one with {{key|E}} and it becomes a [[Difficulty|resting place]], and you can also travel to it from the world map.

## History

- 2026-10-02: The Ford made as a single-map demo; the same day, the meadow to its west and the forest to its south were added, along with the old main story's five maps from the East Road to the Dragon's Lair.
- 2026-10-02: The first prologue: Cloudtop, the slope and Mountain Foot, all the way down the mountain to the Ford.
- 2026-10-03: The meadow became Firstlight Village, and the cows and chickens moved to the new Grassland; Thornwood Hollow and the Old Barrow added south of the village.
- 2026-10-04: Two levels of map: every map placed on the world map of Frostfang Coast, with the Wild generated in the other cells. Cloudtop and the slope removed, and the tutorial moved to the new Clearing.
- 2026-10-05: Chapter 1: the White Knight's Thorn Kingdom added on the East Road; the road to Emberfall opens only once he falls.
- 2026-10-06: Cloudtop restored as the start of a new game.
- 2026-10-07: The Ossuary of the Thorn and the Warden's Sanctum added as two levels below the Old Barrow; Millbrook and the Mill Cellar added west of Firstlight Village, and Dawn Hill and the Chapel of the First Dawn to its north; the Held Ground added.
- 2026-10-08: Every map given its own music (see [[Music]]); five signs and the notice board gained Chinese text.
- 2026-10-12: The line of the Pilgrim's Road added between the Ford and the East Road: the Pilgrims' Rest (the Chapel of the Five, the Pilgrims' Hostel), the Pilgrim's Road, the Abbey of the Thorn (the Chapter Hall, the Abbey Crypt) and the Kneeling Field. The east exit of the Ford's far bank now leads to the Pilgrims' Rest instead of straight to the East Road.
- 2026-10-13: Firstlight Village's church merged into Sister Ilse's chapel and moved onto the square behind Astra's altar, as Firstlight Chapel.
- 2026-10-13: [[The Charcoal Dell|The Charcoal Dell]] and [[The Reed Bend|the Reed Bend]] added on either side of the Forest, each with a hut.
