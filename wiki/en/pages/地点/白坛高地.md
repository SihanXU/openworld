**The White Altar Heights** is one of the six regions of [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], in the west and southwest of the continent. It is the land of the [[持印者|Fifth Seal]], where [[祭坛之下的白衣万证|the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar]] bear the wound in the soul left by [[穿心圣枪|the Lance That Pierced the Heart]].

It is a **backdrop region**: you can walk into it, its towns, fortresses and caves are all there, and its wilds hold the monsters of these parts, but no one lives here yet, and there is no story and no commission. On the world map, beneath its name, it reads “Story not yet open”. Later versions will fill it with playable content.

## Landscape

White highland, mountains and pine forest. Its wilds are the White Highland (stone golems, storm wisps), the Highland Pines and the Stony Pasture.

## Locations

Its capital is Candlecrest. The Altar Beneath is the Seal-bearer's seat, sealed for now.
