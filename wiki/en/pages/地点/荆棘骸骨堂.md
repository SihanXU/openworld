**The Ossuary of the Thorn** is the second level down in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]]. The Order of the Thorn buried its own dead here, every set of bones laid in a niche carved with a name. [[奥斯卡]] came down looking for a way out and was driven by the undead into the cell in the southeast corner. Down the stairs in the northeast corner lies the lowest level, [[守墓者圣所]].

The Ossuary is a dark underground [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]; it knows no day or night, and no night creatures appear. It hangs beneath the Old Barrow and has no place of its own on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or on the [[世界地图|world map]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 32×24, with cave walls on every side. Down the stairs up in the northwest corner, a passage runs south and then east into the hall of niches in the middle; southeast of the hall is Oskar's cell, and from its northeast a corridor leads to the stairs down. Five lit braziers stand along the way.

## Areas

- **The stairs up**: the northwest corner, back to the cell on the east side of the Old Barrow.
- **The hall of niches**: the hall in the middle, its four walls lined with the Order's burial niches ([[墓碑|gravestones]], named “the Order's niches”); press {{键|E}} to read them:
  - “BROTHER ODO OF THE THORN. He held the gate at Varn three days. He did not kneel.” Before the niche lie [[护肩|Brother Odo's Pauldrons]] (Vowbound, defense 5, with the Vow of the Held Ground: while you stand still, attack +25% and damage taken −30%).
  - “SISTER MAREN OF THE THORN, who carried water to both armies.” Before the niche lies [[戒指|Sister Maren's Ring]] (Vowbound, attack 3, defense 1, life steal 1, with the Vow of Mercy: striking an enemy below three-tenths of its health drains 2 health).
  - “BROTHER TOMAS OF THE THORN, the Commander's brother. Warden of this house until the night comes back.”
  - “Carved, and left empty: FENNICK, SQUIRE TO THE COMMANDER. The stone is clean. Someone keeps it swept.”
- **The Order's roll**: the [[祭坛|altar]] in the middle of the hall, named “the Order's roll”. It reads: “THE ORDER OF THE THORN. Forty-one names, the oldest worn smooth. The last, cut deeper than the rest: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER, WHO KNELT TO CHILDREN. TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN. HE HOLDS.”
- **Oskar's cell**: the southeast corner. Walk in and Oskar's scene plays.
- **The stairs down**: the northeast corner, leading to [[守墓者圣所]].

For the terms Vowbound, vow and life steal, see [[品质|Quality]] and [[遗誓|Vowbound]].

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[奥斯卡]] is trapped in the cell in the southeast corner. After you have spoken with him he stays where he is; only once [[古墓里的失踪者]] has been handed in does he go back to [[初原村]].

## Lore

The first time you come down to this level, Lumi says every wall is full of bones, a name carved on each of them: a whole order of knights is buried down here, and they aren't sleeping either.

{{剧透|The Empty Niche and the Roll}}
- Entering the hall of niches, Lumi discovers that the niche carved “Fennick, squire to the Commander” is empty, and swept perfectly clean. Fennick is the old knight on the bench in Firstlight Village.
- In the cell, Oskar says: he read the roll on the altar, some forty knights of the Order of the Thorn, and the last name carved deeper than the rest; the thing that guards this place is farther down, at the very bottom of the stairs in the northeast corner, and he listened all night to it pacing back and forth, like a sentry on a city wall.
- The last name on the roll is Alberic, that is, [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]]. Brother Tomas of the niches was his sworn brother in the Order (not his brother by blood), and is the warden on the lowest level. The memorial stones of Odo, Maren and Tomas stand in the court of [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]]; they themselves are buried here.
{{/剧透}}
