**Frostfang Coast** is one of the six regions of [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], in the northwest of the continent. It is the land of the [[持印者|First Seal]], the kingdom of [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]], who bears the burden of [[荆棘冠|the Crown of Thorns]].

All the story you can play in the game today is here: from [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]], [[渡口|the Ford]] and [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] to, east of the river, [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], [[东大路|the East Road]], [[余烬镇|Emberfall]] and [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]]. For every hand-made map, see [[地点|Locations]].

## Landscape

A cold coast, pine forests and hills, with snowfields in the northernmost rows. The mountains north of the Ford and the river to the south enclose the river's west bank, and only the Ford crosses it (see [[五印之地#河与山：唯一的过河处|the Lands of the Five Seals]]). Its wilds use the original five recipes (see [[荒野|The Wild]]).
