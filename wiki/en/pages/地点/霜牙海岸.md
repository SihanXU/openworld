**Frostfang Coast** is one of the six regions of [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], in the northwest of the continent. It is the land of the [[Seal-bearers|First Seal]], the kingdom of [[The White Knight Alberic|the White Knight Alberic]], who bears the burden of [[Crown of Thorns|the Crown of Thorns]].

All the story you can play in the game today is here: from [[Cloudtop|Cloudtop]], [[The Ford|the Ford]] and [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] to, east of the river, [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], [[The East Road|the East Road]], [[Emberfall|Emberfall]] and [[Cinder Pass|Cinder Pass]]. For every map, see [[Locations|Locations]].

## Landscape

A cold coast, pine forests and hills, with snowfields in the northernmost rows. The mountains north of the Ford and the river to the south enclose the river's west bank, and only the Ford crosses it (see [[The Lands of the Five Seals#River and mountains: the only crossing|the Lands of the Five Seals]]). Its wilds are the original five grounds (see [[The Wild|The Wild]]).
