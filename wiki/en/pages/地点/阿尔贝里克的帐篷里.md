**Inside Alberic's Tent** is the inside of the tent northwest of the sword on [[誓守之地]]. Press {{键|E}} at the tent (“Enter Alberic's tent”) to crawl in. It is small inside: a camp bed, a stand holding the armor he used to wear, and a chest.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 8×6, wooden walls and a wooden floor, a single small room. The exit is in the south wall.

## Areas

- **Alberic's camp bed**: against the west end of the north wall.
- **His old armor, on a stand**: against the east end of the north wall. Press {{键|E}} to read: “White plate, the crown's thorns still caught in the joints. He keeps it polished and does not wear it.”
- **The chest**: a [[箱子|chest]] by the east wall.

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[阿尔贝里克]] does not sleep inside the tent at night, but in front of it (22:00–6:00).

## Lore

The White Knight's armor is white plate. Since laying down the Crown of Thorns, Alberic has never worn it again (see [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]]).
