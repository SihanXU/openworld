{{信息框}}
**The Chapter Hall** is the long house northwest of the court of [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]]. The Order of the Thorn held council here; in the northeast corner is the cell where [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic]] once lived, and in the west the scriptorium.

The hall is indoors, but counts as a [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]: there is a [[骷髅|skeleton]].

## Map

{{地图}}

22×14, the whole of it a single stone-walled room, with the entrance in the south wall. In the north are the long table holding the roll and two chairs, with a lit brazier in each corner; in the west are bookshelves and emptied weapon racks; in the southwest the scriptorium desk; in the northeast corner Alberic's cot and desk.

## Areas

- **The Roll of the Order**: the long [[桌子|table]] in the north. Press {{键|E}} to read: “Forty-one names in forty-one hands, the knights of the Order. The last, larger, the ink still black: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER — BEARER OF THE CROWN. HE HOLDS FOR US ALL. No knight was made after him.” The same roll is carved on the altar of [[荆棘骸骨堂]].
- **Alberic's cell**: the northeast corner, a cot and a desk, with the four leaves of his journal ([[信件|Letters]]) scattered on the floor. The first time you enter this corner, Lumi says: “His bed is so small. — There are pages on the desk. Read them… no, I'll read them with you. I don't want to read them alone.”
- **The scriptorium desk**: the table in the southwest, with the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]] on the floor beside it.
- In the west, “The Order's books” (a [[书架|bookshelf]]) and “Empty weapon racks”.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- **Alberic's journal**, four leaves; press {{键|E}} to pick one up, and with it in hand, left-click to use it on yourself and read it:
  - Leaf I: “Mended the miller's girl's kite today. She says knights do not mend kites. I told her this one does. — A.”
  - Leaf II: “They have chosen me for the Crown. The Prior says it is an honor. My brother Hugh says it is a sentence. They are both right. Forty years, and then relief. I can hold for forty years. — A.”
  - Leaf III: “The forty-first year. No one has come. The thorns are into the bone now. I forgot Hugh's face today and wept, and could not remember why I wept. — A.”
  - Leaf IV: “Hold. Hold. Hold. Hold until relieved. Hold. Hold. Hold. Hold.”
- **[[替任文书|Writ of Relief]]**: beside the scriptorium desk. Picking it up completes “Learn the knight's past at the Abbey of the Thorn” in [[第一印|The First Seal]].

## Lore

The brother Hugh of the journal is Alberic's brother by blood. Brother Tomas, who guards the Order's dead beneath Thornwood Hollow, is his sworn brother in the Order, not his brother by blood (see [[守墓者圣所]]).
