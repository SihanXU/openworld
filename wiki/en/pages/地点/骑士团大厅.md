**The Chapter Hall** is the long house northwest of the court of [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]]. The Order of the Thorn held council here; in the northeast corner is the cell where [[The White Knight Alberic|Alberic]] once lived, and in the west the scriptorium.

The hall is indoors, but counts as a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]: there is a [[skeleton|skeleton]].

## Map

{{map}}

22×14, the whole of it a single stone-walled room, with the entrance in the south wall. In the north are the long table holding the roll and two chairs, with a lit brazier in each corner; in the west are bookshelves and emptied weapon racks; in the southwest the scriptorium desk; in the northeast corner Alberic's cot and desk.

## Areas

- **The Roll of the Order**: the long [[table|table]] in the north. Press {{key|E}} to read: “The knights of the Order, forty-one names in forty-one hands. The last is written larger, the ink still black: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER — BEARER OF THE CROWN. HE HOLDS FOR US ALL. No knight was made after him; the rest of the page is empty.” The same roll is carved on the altar of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]].
- **Alberic's cell**: the northeast corner, a cot and a desk, with the four leaves of his journal ([[Letter|Letters]]) scattered on the floor. The first time you enter this corner, Lumi says: “This room is tiny, and so cold. There are pages lying by the desk.”
- **The scriptorium desk**: the table in the southwest, with the [[Writ of Relief|Writ of Relief]] on the floor beside it.
- In the west, “The Order's books” (a [[bookshelf|bookshelf]]) and “Empty weapon racks”.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- **Alberic's journal**, four leaves; press {{key|E}} to pick one up, and with it in hand, left-click to use it on yourself and read it:
  - Leaf I: “Mended the miller's girl's kite today. She told me knights do not mend kites. I told her this one does. — A.”
  - Leaf II: “They have chosen me for the Crown. The Prior says it is an honor. My brother Hugh says it is a sentence. Forty years, and then I am to be relieved. I can hold for forty years. — A.”
  - Leaf III: “The forty-first year. No one has come. The thorns are into the bone now. Today I could not remember Hugh's face, and I wept, and then I could not remember why I wept. — A.”
  - Leaf IV: “Hold. Hold. Hold. Hold until relieved. Hold. Hold. Hold. Hold.”
- **[[Writ of Relief|Writ of Relief]]**: beside the scriptorium desk. Picking it up completes “Learn the knight's past at the Abbey of the Thorn” in [[The First Seal|The First Seal]].

## Lore

The brother Hugh of the journal is Alberic's brother by blood. Brother Tomas, who guards the Order's dead beneath Thornwood Hollow, is his sworn brother in the Order, not his brother by blood (see [[The Warden's Sanctum]]).
