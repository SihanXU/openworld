**The Quarry** is a quarry beneath the mists of [[誓守之地]], opened up in [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]. The ground is bare earth and gravel, with rock faces in the corners and boulders and veins scattered about: this is where the Held Ground gets its stone and ore. When Alberic opens it, he says: “There was a quarry under the mist. Every kingdom has one; it is where its walls come from.”

The Quarry is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[阿尔贝里克]]'s Held Ground panel, once the land is at level I, put 15 {{物品|木板}} and 10 {{物品|石块}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: boulders to be broken; iron veins, coal seams and a gold vein; stones that crop up from the ground again.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. The ground is bare earth, with a broad stretch of gravel in the middle; there are two rock faces at the top left and top right, and one more along each of the left and right sides. In the middle of the south side is an opening three tiles wide, and a path runs north from it to the middle.

## Areas

- **The opening and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “THE QUARRY — stone for the walls of a kingdom that kneels to nobody.”
- **The gravel yard**: the broad stretch of gravel in the middle, with 7 [[巨石|boulders]] scattered over it.
- **The veins**: 1 [[金矿脉|gold vein]] due north, with a [[煤层|coal seam]] on each side of it and 1 more coal seam in the southeast; 1 [[铁矿脉|iron vein]] in each of the northwest, northeast and southwest corners.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- 7 [[巨石|boulders]]: each gives 4 {{物品|石块}} when broken with a pickaxe.
- 3 [[铁矿脉|iron veins]], 3 [[煤层|coal seams]] and 1 [[金矿脉|gold vein]]: each gives 2 {{物品|铁矿石}}, {{物品|煤炭}} or {{物品|金矿石}} respectively when broken with a pickaxe. A worked-out vein leaves a spent face where it stood, which grows back after one game day; boulders do not grow back. See [[采矿|Mining]].
- **Stones**: stones keep cropping up on the bare earth and gravel, up to 10 at a time, with a chance of one more every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds (only while someone is in the Quarry). No sticks or cut grass grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out and you are back on [[誓守之地]], standing due south of the sword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this ground's card in the [[领地扩建|Held Ground]] panel, and [[阿尔贝里克]] sends you to just inside the opening on the Quarry's south side. Until the ground has been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Quarry is not on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], nor drawn on the [[世界地图|world map]]. [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]] works here all the same, taking you back to where you stood before you entered the Held Ground.
