**The Quarry** is a quarry beneath the mists of [[The Held Ground]], opened up in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]. The ground is bare earth and gravel, with rock faces in the corners and boulders and veins scattered about: this is where the Held Ground gets its stone and ore. When Alberic opens it, he says: “There was a quarry under the mist. Every kingdom has one; it is where its walls come from.”

The Quarry is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[Alberic]]'s Held Ground panel, once the land is at level I, put 15 {{item|Plank}} and 10 {{item|Stone}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: boulders to be broken; iron veins, coal seams and a gold vein; stones that crop up from the ground again.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. The ground is bare earth, with a broad stretch of gravel in the middle; there are two rock faces at the top left and top right, and one more along each of the left and right sides. In the middle of the south side is an opening three tiles wide, and a path runs north from it to the middle.

## Areas

- **The opening and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “THE QUARRY — stone for the walls of a kingdom that kneels to nobody.”
- **The gravel yard**: the broad stretch of gravel in the middle, with 7 [[boulder|boulders]] scattered over it.
- **The veins**: 1 [[gold vein|gold vein]] due north, with a [[coal seam|coal seam]] on each side of it and 1 more coal seam in the southeast; 1 [[iron vein|iron vein]] in each of the northwest, northeast and southwest corners.

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- 7 [[boulder|boulders]]: each gives 4 {{item|Stone}} when broken with a pickaxe.
- 3 [[iron vein|iron veins]], 3 [[coal seam|coal seams]] and 1 [[gold vein|gold vein]]: each gives 2 {{item|Iron Ore}}, {{item|Coal}} or {{item|Gold Ore}} respectively when broken with a pickaxe. A worked-out vein leaves a spent face where it stood, which grows back after one game day; boulders do not grow back. See [[Mining|Mining]].
- **Stones**: stones keep cropping up on the bare earth and gravel, up to 10 at a time, with a chance of one more every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds (only while someone is in the Quarry). No sticks or cut grass grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

- The opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out and you are back on [[The Held Ground]], standing due south of the sword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this ground's card in the [[Expanding the Held Ground|Held Ground]] panel, and [[Alberic]] sends you to just inside the opening on the Quarry's south side. Until the ground has been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Quarry is not on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], nor drawn on the [[World Map|world map]]. [[The Oath-Thorn|The Oath-Thorn]] works here all the same, taking you back to where you stood before you entered the Held Ground.
