**Orrin's Hut** is the inside of the thatched hut on the east side of [[The Charcoal Dell|the Charcoal Dell]], entered by the hut's door. The charcoal burner [[Orrin]] sleeps here at night and keeps watch by the kiln by day.

The hut is indoors, and no night creatures appear in it. It hangs under the Charcoal Dell and has no place of its own on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 11×8, one wood-walled room, with its door in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **Bed**: a [[bed|bed]] in the northwest corner.
- **Fireplace**: in the northeast corner, always lit.
- **Table and chairs**: a table and two chairs in the middle of the room.
- **A sack of charcoal**: the [[sack|sack]] beside the bed, holding 2 [[Coal|Coal]].
- **Chest and barrel**: a [[chest|chest]] in the southeast corner and a [[barrel|barrel]] in the southwest corner, both empty.

## Residents

{{residents}}

[[Orrin]] sleeps here from 21:00 to 6:00.
