**Orrin's Hut** is the inside of the thatched hut on the east side of [[烧炭谷|the Charcoal Dell]], entered by the hut's door. The charcoal burner [[奥林]] sleeps here at night and keeps watch by the kiln by day.

The hut is indoors, and no night creatures appear in it. It hangs under the Charcoal Dell and has no place of its own on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or the [[世界地图|world map]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 11×8, one wood-walled room, with its door in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **Bed**: a [[床|bed]] in the northwest corner.
- **Fireplace**: in the northeast corner, always lit.
- **Table and chairs**: a table and two chairs in the middle of the room.
- **A sack of charcoal**: the [[麻袋|sack]] beside the bed, holding 2 [[煤炭|Coal]].
- **Chest and barrel**: a [[箱子|chest]] in the southeast corner and a [[木桶（容器）|barrel]] in the southwest corner, both empty.

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[奥林]] sleeps here from 21:00 to 6:00.

No creatures.
