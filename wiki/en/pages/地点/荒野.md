**The Wild** is the land cells of [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]] that hold no story location. Each is a 40×30 map, generated from that cell's ground and the world seed. In a given world, the same cell always generates the same map; only the places the player has changed inside it are remembered.

Frostfang Coast has five kinds of wild, one for each of its five walkable grounds; the other regions' grounds each have their own wilds, and the same ground may also differ from region to region (see [[#其他地区的荒野|Wilds of the Other Regions]] below). On the world map, the location markers bear names like “The wild plains” and “The wild forest”; once you walk in, the map itself is called “Open Plains”, “Deep Woods” and so on.

## Map

Wild maps have no fixed layout, so there is no map template. Every one is 40×30, open on all four sides with no exit tiles: walk off any edge and you are back on that cell of the world map. Within a radius of 3 tiles of the map's center no trees grow and no rocks are placed, so there is always open ground under your feet when you walk in from the world map.

The Wild is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]. After nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear (at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}}).

## Areas

The five kinds of wild each have their own base ground, patches and plant life:

| Map name | Marker on the world map | Ground | Patches | What grows (chance per tile) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open Plains | The wild plains | grass | 4 patches of flowers, 3 of dry grass | tree 3%, bush 1.5%, rock 0.8% |
| Deep Woods | The wild forest | grass | 2 patches of flowers | tree 20%, pine tree 6%, bush 4% |
| Rolling Hills | The wild hills | grass | 5 patches of dry grass, 2 of gravel | rock 5%, bush 2%, tree 1.5% |
| Snowfield | The wild snowfield | snow | 1 patch of ice | pine tree 8%, rock 2% |
| The Road | The wild road | grass | 2 patches of flowers | tree 2%, bush 1% |

Through the middle of the road wild runs a dirt road two tiles wide, from the west edge all the way to the east, bending at random.

## Residents

No one lives in the Wild.

## Creatures

The creatures placed at generation, by kind:

| Map name | Creatures |
|---|---|
| Open Plains | [[绿史莱姆|green slime]] ×2, [[鸡|chicken]] ×1 |
| Deep Woods | [[野猪|boar]] ×1, [[绿史莱姆|green slime]] ×1 |
| Rolling Hills | [[狼|wolf]] ×1, [[绿史莱姆|green slime]] ×1 |
| Snowfield | [[霜狼|frost wolf]] ×1, [[冰霜史莱姆|frost slime]] ×1 |
| The Road | [[绿史莱姆|green slime]] ×1 |

Chickens fight back only when struck; every other creature gives chase as soon as it sees someone (sight 4 tiles). Note that the boars of the Wild differ from those of the East Road: the East Road's boars fight back only when struck, while the Wild's attack on sight. These creatures do not come back once felled; the [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] of the night appear as usual and crumble to dust at daybreak.

## Items and Resources

What is placed on the ground at generation:

| Map name | Items |
|---|---|
| Open Plains | [[浆果|Berries]] ×3, [[树枝|Stick]] ×3, [[石块|Stone]] ×2 |
| Deep Woods | [[蘑菇|Mushroom]] ×4, [[树枝|Stick]] ×4 |
| Rolling Hills | [[石块|Stone]] ×4, [[草药|Herb]] ×2 |
| Snowfield | [[石块|Stone]] ×2, [[树枝|Stick]] ×2 |
| The Road | [[树枝|Stick]] ×2 |

Besides this, the ground of every wild slowly grows sticks, stones and [[干草|Cut Grass]]: a chance of one more of each every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds, up to 4 sticks, 3 stones and 4 bundles of cut grass. Trees, pine trees, bushes and rocks can all be felled or broken (see [[伐木|Woodcutting]], [[采矿|Mining]], [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]), and stumps grow back into full trees after {{数值|树木再生}} game days.

## Connected Locations

The Wild connects only to [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]]: walk onto a wild cell on the world map and press {{键|E}} to enter; walk off any edge of the wild and you are back on the world map. There are no direct exits between wilds, or between a wild and a hand-made map.

## Lore

The Wild is part of the world model's “two-tier map”: story locations are made by hand, and everywhere else is generated from its ground, with the aim of building the world up to hundreds of maps (see [[设计支柱|Design Pillars]], [[路线图|Roadmap]]). For now they hold no story content.
