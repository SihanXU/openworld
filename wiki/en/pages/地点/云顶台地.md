{{信息框}}
**Cloudtop** is a high plateau on the mountaintop, where a new game begins. You wake in a ramshackle hut on the plateau with [[露米]] at your side. The plateau is ringed with rock, and the only way down is a gap in the cliff to the south, leading to [[山脚]].

Cloudtop is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

## Map

{{地图}}

The north half of the plateau is snowy upland, with a few rocks and pines showing through the snow. A cliff runs east to west across the plateau, leaving a single gap at its center; the upland stands two levels above the ground to the south, and at the gap a gentle slope leads down. Below the slope a path runs south all the way to the map's south edge, where the exit is three tiles wide.

## Areas

- **The Sunwatch Post hut**: a wood-walled hut in the northwest corner of the plateau, with a wooden floor, its door facing south and no roof. A new game starts inside, at {{数值|新游戏时刻}} on day 1. On the table lies the watchman's log open, and on a peg by the wall hangs a [[斗篷|Watchman's Cloak]] (Fine, defense 1).
- **The clearing before the door**: scattered outside the door are 3 portions of [[浆果|Berries]], 2 [[树枝|sticks]], 2 [[石块|stones]] and 2 handfuls of [[干草|Cut Grass]]: enough for a meal, and enough to make a [[斧|stone axe]]. A path runs slantwise from the door to the gap.
- **The gap and the slope**: the only break in the middle of the cliff, and the way down the mountain. Lumi points it out in her very first words when you wake.

### The Watchman's Log

The table in the hut is “the watchman's log”. Walk up to the table and a scene of reading the log plays; you can also read it with {{键|E}}. The log says:

> Cloudtop Sunwatch, sunset record. Year 401: midsummer 19:42. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — checked the hourglass twice. Year 411: report sent down the mountain; no reply. ...

Reading the log completes the objective of the quest [[守夜人的日志]]. Because the table is right beside the starting point, many players have read it before they are given the quest; in that case, take the quest from [[奈莎·奎尔]] and talk to her once more to hand it in.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives at Cloudtop.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no creatures on the plateau. It is a peaceful zone, and no [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear even at night.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

Once the berries before the door are gone, there are no more; no berries grow on the plateau. The ground slowly grows [[树枝|sticks]], [[石块|stones]] and [[干草|Cut Grass]]: every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds one portion of each may appear, up to 6 sticks, 5 stones and 6 handfuls of cut grass. The pines and rocks can all be felled and broken.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The gap to the south leads to the upland at the north of [[山脚]]. It is the plateau's only exit.
- The rocks round the plateau wall off the map's edges; break through them and you can walk off the edge onto the world map, [[五印之地]].

## Lore

The Church once kept a Sunwatch Post at Cloudtop, where a watchman recorded the time of sunset year after year, and the reports went down the mountain for the almanac. The log shows the sun setting earlier every year, and the reports went unanswered years ago. [[奈莎·奎尔]] wants to know what the log says ([[守夜人的日志]]).

{{剧透|The last page of the log}}
The last page of the log reads: the watchman found in the snow by the cairn “a small light asleep, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was.” When you have read it, Lumi says she remembers no snow, only that when she woke, you were beside her. For Lumi's origins, see [[露米]] and [[主线剧情]].
