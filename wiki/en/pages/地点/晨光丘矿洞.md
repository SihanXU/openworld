**Dawn Hill Mine** opens into the mountainside of [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]]'s western slope, entered by the path on Dawn Hill's west side (rows 18–19). It is Chapter 1's mine: the [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and [[Coal|Coal]] that the [[forge|forge]] and [[anvil|anvil]] of the [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] smithy need are dug closest to hand here. The tunnels hold 5 [[iron vein|iron veins]], 4 [[coal seam|coal seams]] and 3 [[boulder|boulders]], and a worn stone [[Pickaxe|pickaxe]] lies on the ground at the entrance. There are no gold veins: the gold is deep in [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]] and in [[The Quarry|the Quarry]] of [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].

A vein broken open does not vanish: it leaves a [[spent iron vein|spent face]] in its place, which no tool can break, and after **one game day** (24 game hours, about 24 minutes) it turns back into a vein. So the ore here is steady: come once a day, and each vein gives 2 ore (more if you have learned {{skill|Prospector}}). See [[Mining]].

The mine is a dark underground [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]], the same by day and night, gear level 2.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 30×22, cave wall on every side. The entrance is in the east, and a tunnel three tiles wide runs west to a mining hall; from the hall one tunnel climbs north to a small chamber, and another drops south to a chamber in the south. Three braziers burn in the tunnels.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: rows 11–12 on the east side, joining the path on Dawn Hill's west side. On the ground just inside lies the “Worn Pickaxe” (stone pickaxe, power 4, breaks a vein in two blows). You can also make a pickaxe yourself: {{key|C}} opens [[Crafting|crafting]]; 2 stones, 2 sticks and 1 handful of grass, made by hand.
- **The mining hall**: the west end of the tunnel, with 2 iron veins and 1 coal seam.
- **The north chamber**: 2 iron veins and 1 coal seam, and a [[bat|bat]].
- **The south chamber**: 1 iron vein and 2 coal seams, and a bat.
- **Along the tunnels**: 3 boulders, each giving 4 [[Stone|stones]] when broken (boulders do not grow back).

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- Two bats. Once they are killed, another may come every 180 seconds, up to 2 at once.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- 5 iron veins and 4 coal seams: break one with a pickaxe and each drops 2 iron ore or coal. Once broken it becomes a spent face in place, and grows back after one game day.
- 3 boulders: each drops 4 stones, and does not grow back. Stones also turn up on the ground from time to time (up to 4).
- The Worn Pickaxe at the entrance.

## Using the Ore Here

1. Break a vein with a pickaxe; the ore pops out and flies into your bag.
2. Go back to the [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] smithy (see [[Smithing|smithing]]): standing by the [[forge|forge]], smelt 2 iron ore and 1 coal into 2 [[Iron Ingot|Iron Ingots]].
3. Holding a {{item|Smith Hammer}}, stand by the [[anvil|anvil]] and forge weapons, armor and tools from the ingots.
4. Come back the next day, and the veins have all grown back.
