{{信息框}}
**The East Road** runs east from [[跪石原]] to [[余烬镇]]. Coming from [[渡口]], you first head east through [[朝圣者驿站]], [[朝圣者之路]] and the Kneeling Field. North of the road stands a burned farmstead, where the farmer [[艾达]] hides in the barn; the eastern stretch of the road is a field ringed with thorns, the “kingdom” of the First Seal-bearer, [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]]. He stands at its center, and none cross that do not kneel. Defeating him is the last objective of the quest [[第一印]].

The exit to Emberfall opens only once the White Knight falls. The East Road is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]; after dark, [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 48×24, ringed with woods. A road two tiles wide enters from the west (rows 11–13, from the Kneeling Field), winds east and leaves by the east edge (rows 10–12). The burned farmstead lies north of the road's middle stretch; the road's eastern stretch is ringed by a circle of briars, and the road runs straight through the ring.

## Areas

- **Signpost**: a [[告示牌|sign]] at the western junction reads “Emberfall →”.
- **The clearing south of the road**: a patch of trodden earth south of the road. The [[首领挑战|Boss Challenge]] and the development boss sparring ground set up their arenas here.
- **The burned farmstead**: north of the road's middle stretch. The house still stands, but the yard, the hay and the orchard have burned to ash; a few trees are charred stumps, and one [[干草垛|haystack]] is still smoldering ({{状态|点燃}}: the fire does not spread and never burns itself out; a splash of water puts it out). Walking into the farmstead plays a scene. You can go inside the farmhouse: the room is thick with soot, and two plates sit on the table, one of them never used.
- **The barn**: in the southwest corner of the farmstead, where [[艾达]] hides. Once you have seen the farmstead's scene, walk into the barn and she comes out to talk.
- **The road before the thorns**: the stretch of road west of the kingdom.
- **Waypoint statue**: by the road before the thorns stands a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] (named “The East Road”). You can wake it here before fighting the White Knight and change [[恩赐|Lumi's powers]] beside it.
- **The Thorn Kingdom**: the eastern stretch of the road, where a ring of “the knight's thorns” ([[荆棘丛|briars]]) encloses a field, with gaps left only where the road passes. Walking in plays a scene; [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]] stands inside the ring.
  - **Kneeling stones**: inside and outside the ring stand 5 stones carved as kneeling pilgrims ([[墓碑|gravestones]]). Press {{键|E}} to read: “A stone carved as a pilgrim kneeling. No name. A scallop shell, the pilgrim's mark, worn almost away.” Like the kneeling stones of [[跪石原|the Kneeling Field]], they stand for the pilgrims before you who would not kneel to him. Beside one of them lies [[耳环|A Kneeling Pilgrim's Earring]] (Fine, experience +10%).
  - **What the knight took**: scattered in the ring are [[第九根钉子|the Ninth Nail]] and a gold [[婚戒|Wedding Band]], the things sought by [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] and [[艾达的戒指]]; there is also a pair of [[手套|Gauntlets of the White Host]] (Masterwork, defense 2, attack 1).

## Residents

{{居民}}

- [[艾达]] hides in the barn all day. After the White Knight falls, she moves to [[初原村]].
- [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|The White Knight Alberic]] stands at the center of the Thorn Kingdom; he is a boss, not a resident. See his page.
- [[灰衣人]] appears in the story on the road before the thorns and in the kingdom, but does not live here.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- 2 [[狼|wolves]] roam the area south of the kingdom and attack on sight; wolves slowly come back, one possibly every 240 seconds, up to 2.
- 1 [[野猪|boar]] in the brush south of the road, which fights back only when struck.
- After dark, [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] appear (up to {{数值|夜行生物上限}}), crumbling to dust at daybreak.
- Boss: [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]]. The knight of the story is weaker than the one in the Boss Challenge: 300 health, armor 1, and his skills deal only four tenths of the challenge version's damage; at {{数值|首领二阶段}} health he enters his second phase. For his three skills and how to answer them, see his page and [[首领]].

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

At the start, 2 [[树枝|sticks]] and 2 [[石块|stones]] lie by the road, and the ground slowly grows more (up to 6, 5 and 6 portions). The earring, the gauntlets, the nail and the wedding band in the kingdom do not come back once taken. When the White Knight falls he drops 4 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]]; if you have already chosen a [[职业|profession]], he also drops that profession's piece of [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]] (only one of each in the whole world).

## Lore

{{剧透|Scenes on the East Road}}
- **On the road**: the first time you set foot on the East Road after crossing the river, Lumi says the sun is lower than it was this morning, though it is barely past noon; and the thorns along the road were planted in a line by someone.
- **The burned farmstead**: everything here has burned, and in the ash are claw marks longer than an arm. They are what the dragon left in the old main story (see [[余烬镇与魔龙]]).
- **The barn**: Edda says the days grew too short and the wheat died; then the things of the night came; and later a knight in white took everything that shone, her husband's ring hanging on the doorpost among it. The night the barn caught fire, it was a man in grey who pulled her out of the smoke, and she dares not tell the village.
- **The road before the thorns**: once the Church's letter has come, walk onto this stretch and [[灰衣人]] stands in your way and tells you to turn back. He says what the seals lock away is Astra, that the First Long Night wears his face, and that with every Seal-bearer you defeat he wakes a little more. To Lumi he says “You of all of them should know... No. You would not remember,” and then leaves. You never fight him.
- **The kingdom**: walk into the ring of thorns and the knight says: “None cross that do not kneel. Another pilgrim. They always send another. I hold. I... hold. I do not remember what. Kneel.”
- **The Crown of Thorns**: when the knight falls, he kneels, lifts off the Crown of Thorns and holds it out to you: “Take it. It was never mine to hold.” A cold light streams from the crown to Lumi, and she gains her first [[恩赐|Boon]], Crown's Insight. The Grey Man appears and gathers the rest of the light into the holy fire he stole: “Every seal you break makes the night longer. Ask your Church why.” From then on night falls earlier (see [[昼夜]]). Then, where the knight fell, you find a thorn still green, [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]], which leads to [[誓守之地]].
{{/剧透}}
