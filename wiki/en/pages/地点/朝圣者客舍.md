**The Pilgrims' Hostel** is the wooden house south of [[朝圣者驿站|the Pilgrims' Rest]], where pilgrims passing through spend the night. [[莫德]] and her daughter [[温]] sleep here at night.

## Map

{{地图}}

14×9, one wood-walled room, with its entrance in the south wall. Five beds stand under the north wall, a lit fireplace is in the northeast corner, the table with the book is in the middle, and a chest stands in the southeast corner.

## Areas

- **Beds**: five [[床|beds]] under the north wall. The two on the west are Maud's and Wynn's; the other three are called “An empty cot”.
- **The pilgrims' book**: the [[桌子|table]] in the middle. Press {{键|E}} to read: “Names, years, a scallop drawn beside each. The oldest pages are brown. Next to many, in one hand after another down the years: ‘Went east. Did not come back.’”
- The [[壁炉|fireplace]] in the northeast corner is always lit; a [[箱子|chest]] stands in the southeast corner.

## Residents

- [[温]]: sleeps here from 20:00 to 7:00.
