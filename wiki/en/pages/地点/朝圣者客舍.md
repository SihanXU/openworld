**The Pilgrims' Hostel** is the wooden house south of [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]], where pilgrims passing through spend the night. [[Maud]] and her daughter [[Wynn]] sleep here at night.

## Map

{{map}}

14×9, one wood-walled room, with its entrance in the south wall. Five beds stand under the north wall, a lit fireplace is in the northeast corner, the table with the book is in the middle, and a chest stands in the southeast corner.

## Areas

- **Beds**: five [[bed|beds]] under the north wall. The two on the west are Maud's and Wynn's; the other three are called “An empty cot”.
- **The pilgrims' book**: the [[table|table]] in the middle. Press {{key|E}} to read: “A register of names and years, a scallop drawn beside each name. The oldest pages have gone brown. After many of the names the same words are added, in one hand after another down the years: ‘Went east. Did not come back.’”
- The [[fireplace|fireplace]] in the northeast corner is always lit; a [[chest|chest]] stands in the southeast corner.
