**The Pale Fens** is one of the six regions of [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], in the south of the continent. It is the land of the [[持印者|Fourth Seal]], where [[死之执辔者·纳赫特|Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins]] bears the creeping death of [[钉刑圣木|the Holy Wood of the Nails]]: the dead find no rest.

It is a **backdrop region**: you can walk into it, its towns, fortresses and caves are all there, and its wilds hold the monsters of these parts, but no one lives here yet, and there is no story and no commission. On the world map, beneath its name, it reads “Story not yet open”. Later versions will fill it with playable content.

## Landscape

Marsh (reeds), dead wood (dead trees) and lakes, in a dusk that never lifts. Its wilds are the Marsh (water slimes, zombies), the Dead Wood (skeletons, bats), the Grey Woods (ghosts) and the Fen Meadow.

## Locations

Its capital is Dusk Moorings. The Pale Rider's Ossuary is the Seal-bearer's seat, sealed for now.
