**Firstlight Village** is a small village ringed by woods, west of [[渡口|the Ford]]. It is named for the first light of dawn after [[原夜|the First Long Night]], and the fire on its square is said to have been taken from that very light. Most of Chapter 1's people live here, and most quests are taken here.

Firstlight Village is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night. It has an exit on each side and is the hub of the country round the Ford: the Ford to the east, [[水车磨坊|Millbrook]] to the west, [[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]] to the north, and [[草原|the Grassland]] to the south.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 60×40, ringed by woods two to four tiles deep. The road from the Ford enters the village from the east; north of the road stand the general store and the smithy; it goes on past Tobin's handcart to the flagstone square in the middle. North of the square, behind Astra's altar, stands Firstlight Chapel. Three cottages stand in a row to the north and one to the west; south of the square is the pond, west of the pond a hot spring lies in the woods, and the vegetable garden is in the southeast corner. On either side of the lane south toward the forest are the two houses in the lane. Other lanes lead to the west, north and south exits.

## Areas

- **The square and the holy flame**: the flagstone square in the middle of the village. A campfire burns on the square: the village's holy flame (also called the First Fire), which [[伊尔丝修女]] keeps by day. Beside the fire is [[祭坛|Astra's altar]]; press {{键|E}} to read: “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. — Suffering is not God's abandonment.” By the fire is a log bench, and on the ground a [[画着灰衣人的粉笔画|chalk drawing of a grey man]] (one of [[佩尔的涂鸦|Pell's Drawings]]).
- **Firstlight Chapel**: behind the altar and the holy flame is Sister Ilse's chapel ([[初原村礼拜堂]]); step out of its door and one step down is Astra's altar. The [[告示牌|sign]] by the door reads: “FIRSTLIGHT CHAPEL — Sister Ilse keeps the flame and Astra's altar; inside, Brother Anselm keeps the bookstand of skill books.” Inside are an altar carved with the five holy wounds, candlestands and benches; [[安塞姆修士]] keeps the chapel bookstand and sells skill books. It is not the same place as [[初晓礼拜堂|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] on [[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]].
- **The well, the notice board and the commission board**: north of the square is a [[水井|well]], and the [[布告栏|notice board]] beside it reads: “FIRSTLIGHT VILLAGE — Every journey begins with a first step. East: The Ford. West: Millbrook. North: Dawn Hill. South: the grassland, and the forest beyond.” Left of the notice board is the [[委托板|commission board]], listing the village's commissions no one has taken yet (see [[任务#委托|commissions]]). Two lit street lamps also stand on the square.
- **Waypoint statue**: on the west side of the square stands a [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] (named “Firstlight”).
- **Cottages**: three to the north, one to the west, and two more in the lane to the south. You can walk into each one: walk up from the doorway to go in, and inside are a bed, table and chairs, a fireplace and a chest. The middle one to the north is the Elder's House (the elder [[罗万]] stands before its porch by day), the northwest one is Marta's House, the northeast one the Watch House, the west one the Woodcutter's House, and to the south are the House in the Lane and the Little Cabin.
- **Tobin's handcart**: by the road on the east side of the square. On the cart lie a [[魔杖|Frost Wand]] and a [[一瓶灯油|Flask of Oil]], both [[托宾]]'s. For trading food for the wand, see [[渡口#区域|“Ways across” on the Ford's page]].
- **The shops by the east road**: on the north side of the road into the village from the Ford, two houses you can enter, west to east, each with a sign at its door, and two street lamps by the road:
  - **General Store**: [[葛瑞塔]] stands behind the counter, selling sundries, food and potions, and buying herbs, hides and eggs.
  - **The Smithy**: inside are a lit [[熔炉|forge]] and an [[铁砧|anvil]] anyone may use (see [[锻造|smithing]]); the smith [[哈尔多]] works there by day.
- **The training ground**: the open ground beside the northeast cottage, where the profession mentor [[布拉姆]] stands. Once you reach level {{数值|职业等级}}, choose a [[职业|profession]] beside him.
- **The carpenter's corner**: south of the west cottage, with a [[工作台|workbench]] and an open [[柴堆|wood pile]]. [[乔斯]] planes boards at the workbench by day, and [[格斯]] stacks the wood he has cut on the pile in the afternoon. Beside the workbench you can make things that need a workbench (see [[制作|crafting]]).
- **The pond**: south of the square, where [[米娅]] skips stones by day.
- **The healing spring**: a hot spring in the woods west of the pond; the sign beside it reads “Warm spring — sit a while, and it mends you.” Step into the spring and you regain 1 health at once, and 1 more every half second you stay in (the status “soaking”). The first time you come near, [[露米]] points it out to you.
- **The vegetable garden**: a 4×4 plot of [[农耕|farmland]] in the southeast corner. Its four columns are carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins; the first three rows are at the sprout, young plant and ripe stages, and the last row is empty, ready for sowing. Beside it, the Garden Supplies ([[板条箱|crate]], 24 slots, durability 12) holds 2 portions of seed for each of the four crops, a steel [[锄头|Hoe]], a bronze [[镰刀|Sickle]], a [[一桶水|Water Bucket]], a steel [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]], and 4 each of planks, sticks and stones. See [[农耕]], [[作物]].
- **The lane south**: the head of the lane to the Grassland, where the guard [[霍尔特]] keeps watch.

## Residents

{{居民}}

- On and round the square by day: [[伊尔丝修女]] keeps the holy flame; [[玛尔塔]] minds the pot by the fire; [[芬尼克]] sits on the bench mending harness; [[佩尔]] draws in chalk near the notice board; [[托宾]] keeps his handcart, fetches water at the well in the morning and warms his hands at the fire toward evening.
- About the village: [[罗万]] watches the square from his porch; [[米娅]] is by the pond; [[乔斯]] is at the workbench; [[奈莎·奎尔]] copies tables on the east side of the square by day and times the sunset at the east road's head toward evening; [[布拉姆]] is on the training ground; [[葛瑞塔]] is in the general store; [[哈尔多]] is in the smithy by day; [[安塞姆修士]] keeps the bookstand in the chapel; [[霍尔特]] is at the head of the lane south.
- Out of the village to work by day: [[格斯]] goes to [[森林|the Forest]] to fell trees in the morning and comes back to stack wood in the afternoon; [[布琳]] herds cattle on [[草原|the Grassland]] by day and comes back to the village to sleep at night.
- Moved here as the story goes on: [[莉娜]] and [[奥斯卡]] come back to the village once rescued from the barrow (Lina tends herbs, Oskar weaves baskets by the bench); after the White Knight falls, [[艾达]] moves here from the East Road and works in the vegetable garden.
- Seen only in story scenes: [[灰衣人]], [[圣教信使]].

Once the holy flame is stolen, Sister Ilse's schedule changes to “sweeping the cold altar”.

## Lore

The village's name, “Firstlight”, means the first dawn after the First Long Night: the morning [[阿斯特拉|Astra]]'s fifth suffering ended. Sister Ilse says the fire on the square was taken from that day's first light; in [[初晓礼拜堂|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] on Dawn Hill stands the fire altar that lit it. Behind the fire and Astra's altar is Sister Ilse's own chapel, where the villagers kneel before the five holy wounds and give thanks for the holy flame outside its door. Firstlight believes what the Church teaches: suffering is not God's abandonment.

{{剧透|What happens in the village in Chapter 1}}
- The first time you come into the village, Sister Ilse welcomes you by the fire, Pell wants to draw Lumi, and the elder Rowan says two of the villagers have gone missing ([[古墓里的失踪者]]).
- Once the two are rescued and [[守夜之备|The Vigil]] is handed in, at dusk (17:00–21:00) Ilse tells the villagers the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings; after dark (21:00–24:00) the whole village gives thanks before the holy flame. As the thanksgiving ends, a man in a grey cloak scoops the holy flame into a lantern; Holt cries thief, and the man walks off east, the river freezing under his feet to let him cross. From then on the holy flame is out.
- The next morning, the Holy City's courier comes to the village, reads out the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, names you a pilgrim, and leaves a copy of the [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]].
- When you come back with the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]], Rowan is furious that the heretic still holds the holy flame, Ilse says “He always did carry things that were too heavy for him,” and Nessa says the sun set four minutes early tonight. Chapter 1 ends here, and the chapter card appears.
