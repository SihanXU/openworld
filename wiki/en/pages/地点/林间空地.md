**The Clearing** is the ground of the [[新手教程|tutorial]]. Choose “Tutorial” on the title screen and you wake in this clearing: you lie out cold for a few seconds, and once you are up, [[露米]] teaches you step by step to eat, gather, make an axe, fell a tree, light a fire and build a hut with a bed, and then to get through a night. Only after the first morning does the path to the south open.

The Clearing is not on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], and no road leads back to it. The tutorial takes no save; walk out of the clearing and the tutorial ends, returning you to the title screen. A new game begins at [[云顶台地]] and never passes through here.

The Clearing is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]: after dark [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] come out, which is why the tutorial has you finish the hut and shut its door before nightfall.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 36×26, with a ring of forest all round. On the east side of the clearing stands a small copse (7 trees), and to the northeast a patch of flowers. In the south a path two tiles wide runs from the clearing to the southern edge of the map.

## Areas

- **Where you wake**: the west of the clearing, where the tutorial begins, at 7:00 in the morning.
- **The copse to the east**: 7 trees, for learning to fell trees and split planks.
- **The outline of the house**: a little east of the clearing's middle an outline is drawn: the bed's place in the center, walls on the north, west and east, the door on the south. The lesson on walls lets you set the first wall only on the outline.
- **Training dummy**: a [[训练假人|training dummy]] east of the house. Left-click it until it falls, and it stands back up.
- **The path south**: the clearing's only way out, leading to [[山脚]]. Before the first morning the end of the path is plain ground; walk over and Lumi says “Not that way yet.”

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives in the clearing; there is only [[露米]], who follows you everywhere.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no creatures by day. After nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear (at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}}); shut the door and stay inside, and they cannot get in.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- At the start the ground holds 6 [[浆果|Berries]], 3 [[树枝|Sticks]], 4 [[石块|Stones]] and 5 bundles of [[干草|Cut Grass]]: enough for one meal, and enough to make your first [[斧|Stone Axe]] and a [[火把|Torch]].
- The ground slowly grows more berries, sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 6, 5 and 6), with a chance of one more every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds. This is what Lumi means, once the hut is built, when she says “the berries will keep growing back”.
- When the copse's trees are felled, their stumps slowly grow back, so you can fell them more than once.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The path south (columns 17–18): the top of the path on the north side of [[山脚]]. It opens only after the first morning. When you play the tutorial on its own, the tutorial ends as soon as you walk out of the clearing: the screen shows “Tutorial complete! Start a new game from the title screen to begin the main story.” and you return to the title screen.

## Lore

The Clearing is only the tutorial's ground and has no story. Here Lumi introduces herself for the first time: “I'm Lumi, a fairy — from today I go where you go.”

The tutorial has eight parts in all; for each one's lessons, which keys are locked and how to skip it, see [[新手教程|Tutorial]].
