**The Clearing** is the ground of the [[Tutorial|tutorial]]. Choose “Tutorial” on the title screen and you wake in this clearing: you lie out cold for a few seconds, and once you are up, [[Lumi]] teaches you step by step to eat, gather, make an axe, fell a tree, light a fire and build a hut with a bed, and then to get through a night. Only after the first morning does the path to the south open.

The Clearing is not on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], and no road leads back to it. The tutorial takes no save; walk out of the clearing and the tutorial ends, returning you to the title screen. A new game begins at [[Cloudtop]] and never passes through here.

The Clearing is an open-air [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]: after dark [[zombie|zombies]] and [[bat|bats]] come out, which is why the tutorial has you finish the hut and shut its door before nightfall.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 36×26, with a ring of forest all round. On the east side of the clearing stands a small copse (7 trees), and to the northeast a patch of flowers. In the south a path two tiles wide runs from the clearing to the southern edge of the map.

## Areas

- **Where you wake**: the west of the clearing, where the tutorial begins, at 7:00 in the morning.
- **The spring**: a pool under the north trees, rising beneath an old wall of the Five: in its niche a hooded figure worn smooth, five marks cut above it, the first of them black, and a spout running into the pool; knee-high runs of wall and broken columns either side, a saint fallen by the water.
- **The old oak**: on the woods' west edge, older than the wall, its crown over the trees.
- **The copse to the east**: 7 trees, for learning to fell trees and split planks.
- **The outline of the house**: a little east of the clearing's middle an outline is drawn: the bed's place in the center, walls on the north, west and east, the door on the south. The lesson on walls lets you set the first wall only on the outline.
- **Training dummy**: a [[training dummy|training dummy]] east of the house. Left-click it until it falls, and it stands back up.
- **The path south**: the clearing's only way out, leading to [[Mountain Foot]]. Before the first morning the end of the path is plain ground; walk over and Lumi says “Not that way yet.”

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one lives in the clearing; there is only [[Lumi]], who follows you everywhere.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There are no creatures by day. After nightfall [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] appear (at most {{value|夜行生物上限}}); shut the door and stay inside, and they cannot get in.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- At the start the ground holds 6 [[Berries|Berries]], 3 [[Stick|Sticks]], 4 [[Stone|Stones]] and 5 bundles of [[Cut Grass|Cut Grass]]: enough for one meal, and enough to make your first [[Axe|Stone Axe]] and a [[Torch|Torch]].
- The ground slowly grows more berries, sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 6, 5 and 6), with a chance of one more every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds. This is what Lumi means, once the hut is built, when she says “the berries will keep growing back”.
- When the copse's trees are felled, their stumps slowly grow back, so you can fell them more than once.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

- The path south (columns 17–18): the top of the path on the north side of [[Mountain Foot]]. It opens only after the first morning. When you play the tutorial on its own, the tutorial ends as soon as you walk out of the clearing: the screen shows “Tutorial complete! Start a new game from the title screen to begin the main story.” and you return to the title screen.

## Lore

The Clearing is only the tutorial's ground and has no story. Here Lumi introduces herself for the first time: “I'm Lumi, a fairy — from today I go where you go.”

The tutorial has eight parts in all; for each one's lessons, which keys are locked and how to skip it, see [[Tutorial|Tutorial]].
