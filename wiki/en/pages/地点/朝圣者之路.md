**The Pilgrim's Road** runs east from [[朝圣者驿站|the Pilgrims' Rest]] to [[跪石原|the Kneeling Field]]. From the middle of the road a path goes north, up the hill to [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]]; this path is there from the start. Five [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]] stand by the road, each for one of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]].

This is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]: there are wolves on both sides of the road, and [[僵尸|zombies]] appear after dark.

## Areas
- **The penitent's camp**: a little southeast of the road's middle, a lit campfire. [[以斯拉修士]] kneels by the road by day and sleeps by the campfire at night. The first time you come this way, his scene plays.

## The Five Shrines
Each shrine is cut with the scripture of its suffering, which you can read with {{键|E}}. Hold what it asks and press {{键|G}} to lay it there; the shrine lights up and takes the thing (see [[路边圣龛]]).

| Shrine | What to lay on it | Found along the road |
|---|---|---|
| [[荆棘冠之龛]] | Something worn on the head | A pilgrim's hat (in the grass on the west of the road) |
| [[鞭刑柱之龛]] | A cord | A penitent's plaited cord (east of the first shrine) |
| [[饥刑之秤之龛]] | Something to eat | A pilgrim's loaf (on the abandoned cart south of the road) |
| [[钉刑圣木之龛]] | A piece of metal | A dented helm (iron, north of the road's middle) |
| [[穿心圣枪之龛]] | A light: a torch or a candle | A candle (south of the road's eastern stretch) |

A wrong offering is not accepted, and the shrine says what it asks for. Holy relics and key items such as the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]] and [[荆棘骑士团印章|the Order's Seal]], and tokens too, are refused by every shrine. Once all five are lit a scene plays, completing this stage of [[第一印]].

## Lore
- **The White Knight's traces**: the signposts are bound with thorns. Three signposts, west to east, read: “EAST: THE KINGDOM. NONE CROSS THAT DO NOT KNEEL.” “KNEEL, AND BE SPARED.” “HE HOLDS. KNEEL.” Three white pennants stand by the road: the western one “mended many times, by someone patient”, the middle one where “the mending stops halfway up”, and the easternmost only a few rags: “Whoever mended them stopped remembering how.”
- The first time you set foot on this road, Lumi says: “Thorns tied round the signposts, and white flags. Somebody looked after these flags once. Somebody stopped.”
- **The penitent**: Brother Ezra believes that every stroke of the scourge he takes is one Astra need not. He once went as far as the Kneeling Field and saw the knight mending a flag: “He saw me and his hands stopped — like he had forgotten what hands were for. Then he told me to kneel.” Lumi says: “A knight who mends flags? I'd like to see that. ...From a safe distance.”
- **All five lit**: as the fifth mark lights, five points of light shine out together, and the middle one points to the path north to the abbey. Lumi says the crown shrine is the coldest: “I touched it and it touched back.”
