**The Pilgrim's Road** runs east from [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] to [[The Kneeling Field|the Kneeling Field]]. From the middle of the road a path goes north, up the hill to [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]]; this path is there from the start. Five [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]] stand by the road, each for one of [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]].

This is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]: there are wolves on both sides of the road, and [[zombie|zombies]] appear after dark.

## Areas
- **The penitent's camp**: a little southeast of the road's middle, a lit campfire. [[Brother Ezra]] kneels by the road by day and sleeps by the campfire at night. The first time you come this way, his scene plays.

## The Five Shrines
Each shrine is cut with the scripture of its suffering, which you can read with {{key|E}}. Hold what it asks and press {{key|G}} to lay it there; the shrine lights up and takes the thing (see [[wayside shrine]]).

| Shrine | What to lay on it | Found along the road |
|---|---|---|
| [[Shrine of the Crown]] | Something worn on the head | A pilgrim's hat (in the grass on the west of the road) |
| [[Shrine of the Pillar]] | A cord | A penitent's plaited cord (east of the first shrine) |
| [[Shrine of the Scales]] | Something to eat | A pilgrim's loaf (on the abandoned cart south of the road) |
| [[Shrine of the Nails]] | A piece of metal | A dented helm (iron, north of the road's middle) |
| [[Shrine of the Lance]] | A light: a torch or a candle | A candle (south of the road's eastern stretch) |

A wrong offering is not accepted, and the shrine says what it asks for. Holy relics and key items such as the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]] and [[The Order's Seal|the Order's Seal]], and tokens too, are refused by every shrine. Once all five are lit a scene plays, completing this stage of [[The First Seal]].

## Lore
- **The White Knight's traces**: the signposts are bound with thorns. Three signposts, west to east, read: “EAST: THE KINGDOM. NONE CROSS THAT DO NOT KNEEL.” “KNEEL, AND BE SPARED.” “HE HOLDS. KNEEL.” Three white pennants stand by the road: the western one “mended many times, by someone patient”, the middle one where “the mending stops halfway up”, and the easternmost only a few rags: “Whoever mended them stopped remembering how.”
- The first time you set foot on this road, Lumi says: “Thorns tied round the signposts, and white flags. Somebody looked after these flags once. Somebody stopped.”
- **The penitent**: Brother Ezra believes that every stroke of the scourge he takes is one Astra need not. He once went as far as the Kneeling Field and saw the knight mending a flag: “He saw me and his hands stopped — like he had forgotten what hands were for. Then he told me to kneel.” Lumi says: “A knight who mends flags? I'd like to see that. ...From a safe distance.”
- **All five lit**: as the fifth mark lights, five points of light shine out together, and the middle one points to the path north to the abbey. Lumi says the crown shrine is the coldest: “I touched it and it touched back.”
