{{信息框}}
**Emberfall** lies at the east end of [[东大路|the East Road]], its back to the great mountain; half the houses in town have just been rethatched, and the other half are still burned black. The mayor [[玛拉]], the smith [[加里克]] and the herbalist [[雷恩]] live here. At the foot of the mountain north of town is the entrance to [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]], and the mountain road east of town leads to [[烬岭山道|Cinder Pass]] and [[魔龙巢穴|the Dragon's Lair]] at the top.

Emberfall is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]. The exit into town from the East Road opens only once [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]] falls, so you cannot get here before the end of Chapter 1.

The stretch from Emberfall to the dragon is the old main story from before Chapter 1, older than the world setting of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]]. It is still in the game and can be played through to its end; as planned, it will be replaced by the content of [[第二章|Chapter 2]] (the Second Seal). See [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30. The top four rows are the mountain's cave wall, with the Old Mine's entrance left in the middle (columns 19–21). Woods lie on the west, south and east. A road enters town from the west (rows 14–16) and leads to the flagstone square in the middle; a road runs north from the square to the mine mouth; and a road leaves town east from the square (rows 8–10) toward Cinder Pass.

## Areas

- **The town gate**: where you come into town from the west. The first time you get here a line of narration plays, and the main story moves on to “Emberfall”.
- **The square**: the flagstone square in the middle of town, with a [[水井|well]], two street lamps and a [[布告栏|notice board]]. The notice board reads: “DRAGON SEEN OVER CINDER PEAK. KEEP THE BUCKETS FULL.”
- **The mayor's house**: the teahouse south of the square, where [[玛拉]] lives at night; by day she stands in the square. Walk up from the doorway to go in: on the tea table is a list of the houses the dragon has burned.
- **The smithy**: in the northeast, with a lit [[熔炉|forge]], an [[铁砧|anvil]] and a wooden barrel before the door. [[加里克]] works at the anvil by day. You can go into the smithy: inside are a tool rack, a weapon rack and a quenching tub, and spread on the table is the pattern for the dragon-slaying sword, traced over by twelve generations of smiths.
- **The herbalist's hut**: a thatched hut in the west, with a lit [[坩埚|cauldron]] and a crate before the door. [[雷恩]] tends the cauldron by day. You can go into the hut: inside are books about dragons, a row of herb jars and a second cauldron.
- **Houses**: four more houses round the square, which you can also enter, each with a bed, table and chairs, a fireplace and a chest.
- **Houses**: two in the northwest, one in the southwest, one in the southeast.
- **Scorched ground**: a small patch of ash in the east, where the dragon breathed fire.

The forge and the cauldrons are all lit: hold raw food and right-click them to cook it (see [[烹饪|cooking]]), or press {{键|E}} empty-handed to warm yourself beside them and dry off (see [[营火#取暖|warming up]]). Hold iron ore or gold ore and right-click the forge to smelt it into an iron or gold ingot (see [[熔炉#熔炼|smelting]]); the smithies of [[初原村]] and [[誓守之地]] have lit forges too (see [[锻造|smithing]]).

## Residents

{{居民}}

- [[玛拉]]: the mayor, keeping the square from 7:00 to 21:00. Press {{键|E}} on her empty-handed to hear her tell of the dragon (once only), and she gives you 2 [[面包|Bread]].
- [[加里克]]: the smith, at his anvil from 6:00 to 21:00. Hand him iron ore and coal one piece at a time ({{键|G}}); once there is enough, talk to him ({{键|E}}) and he forges [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]].
- [[雷恩]]: the herbalist, at her cauldron from 6:00 to 22:00. Hand her fireweed and frostcaps; once there is enough, talk to her and she brews two [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draughts]].

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are 2 [[鸡|chickens]] in town, which attack no one and run off when struck. It is a peaceful zone; no monsters appear at night.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

Nothing lies on the ground at the start; later the ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6 portions). The materials the town needs are all elsewhere: [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and [[煤炭|Coal]] in [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]], [[火焰草|Fireweed]] in [[初原村]], and [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]] in [[森林|the Forest]].

## Lore

{{待设计}}

The old main story: a dragon lives on Cinder Peak and comes down the mountain once every spring; this year it came down twice. The burned farmstead on the East Road was its doing. Garrick's family has handed down the pattern of a sword that can cut through dragonhide, and twelve generations have waited to forge it; Wren can brew a draught against fire.

This story is older than the world setting of the Five Sufferings and was never joined to the main story of the [[持印者|Seal-bearers]] and [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]]. As planned, it will be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal, which has not been designed yet (see [[第二章]]).
