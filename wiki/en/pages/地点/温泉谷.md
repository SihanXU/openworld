{{信息框}}
**The Hot Springs** is a snow-covered valley at the edge of [[誓守之地]], the last area that can be opened up in [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]. Three warm springs well up from the cold stone, and soaking in them heals you. When Alberic opens it, he says: “Warm water out of cold rock. Sit in it a while. I will keep watch.”

The Hot Springs is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[阿尔贝里克]]'s Held Ground panel, once the land is at level III, put 25 {{物品|石块}} and 2 {{物品|铁锭}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: springs that heal you as you soak; herbs and Frostcap on the slopes; a quiet place at the edge of the Held Ground.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. The ground is snow, with two rock faces at the top left and top right; in the middle lies an oval of grass, and the three springs are scattered across the middle of the valley. In the middle of the south side is an opening three tiles wide.

## Areas

- **The opening and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “THE HOT SPRINGS — sit a while, and it mends you.”
- **The springs**: the three springs in the middle of the valley (healing springs). Stepping into a spring restores 1 health at once, and soaking in one keeps restoring it ({{状态|泡温泉}}), just like the hot spring in [[初原村]]. See [[生命值|Health]].

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- At the start 3 {{物品|草药}} and 2 {{物品|霜盖菇}} lie on the ground.
- **Ground yield**: herbs slowly grow on the grass, up to 4 at a time, with a chance of one more every {{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds. No sticks, stones or cut grass grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{相连地点}}

- The opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out and you are back on [[誓守之地]], standing due south of the sword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this area's card in the [[领地扩建|Held Ground expansion]] panel, and [[阿尔贝里克]] sends you to just inside the opening on the Hot Springs' south side. Until the area has been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Hot Springs is not on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], nor drawn on the [[世界地图|world map]]. [[誓约之棘|The Oath-Thorn]] works here all the same, taking you back to where you stood before you entered the Held Ground.
