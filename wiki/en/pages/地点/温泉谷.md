**The Hot Springs** is a snow-covered valley at the edge of [[The Held Ground]], the last area that can be opened up in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]. Three warm springs well up from the cold stone, and soaking in them heals you. When Alberic opens it, he says: “Warm water out of cold rock. Sit in it a while. I will keep watch.”

The Hot Springs is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[Alberic]]'s Held Ground panel, once the land is at level III, put 25 {{item|Stone}} and 2 {{item|Iron Ingot}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: springs that heal you as you soak; herbs and Frostcap on the slopes; a quiet place at the edge of the Held Ground.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. The ground is snow, with two rock faces at the top left and top right; in the middle lies an oval of grass, and the three springs are scattered across the middle of the valley. In the middle of the south side is an opening three tiles wide.

## Areas

- **The opening and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “THE HOT SPRINGS — sit a while, and it mends you.”
- **The springs**: the three springs in the middle of the valley (healing springs). Stepping into a spring restores 1 health at once, and soaking in one keeps restoring it ({{status|soaking}}), just like the hot spring in [[Firstlight Village]]. See [[Health|Health]].

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- At the start 3 {{item|Herb}} and 2 {{item|Frostcap}} lie on the ground.
- **Ground yield**: herbs slowly grow on the grass, up to 4 at a time, with a chance of one more every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds. No sticks, stones or cut grass grow here.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

- The opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out and you are back on [[The Held Ground]], standing due south of the sword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this area's card in the [[Expanding the Held Ground|Held Ground expansion]] panel, and [[Alberic]] sends you to just inside the opening on the Hot Springs' south side. Until the area has been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Hot Springs is not on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], nor drawn on the [[World Map|world map]]. [[The Oath-Thorn|The Oath-Thorn]] works here all the same, taking you back to where you stood before you entered the Held Ground.
