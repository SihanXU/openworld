{{信息框}}
**The Grassland** lies south of [[初原村]]: open grass ringed by thin woods, with a pond in the middle. The village's cows and chickens are all here, and by day the herder [[布琳]] sits on a bench watching over them. Cross the Grassland southward and you reach [[森林|the Forest]].

The Grassland is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]. After dark [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] appear, which is why the quests [[老骑士]] and [[草原守夜]] both have you fight the undead here at night.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30, and the woods round it are thinner than the village's. A path winds across the Grassland from the northern exit (columns 22–24) to the southern exit (also columns 22–24). A few lone trees are scattered over the middle of the Grassland, and there is a pond in the southwest.

## Areas

- **The herd**: the broad stretch of grass in the middle of the Grassland, where the cows and chickens graze.
- **Brin's bench**: a log bench in the middle of the Grassland, where [[布琳]] sits by day watching the cattle.
- **The pond**: in the southwest, where the livestock drink.
- **The southern woods**: the Grassland's southern exit leads to the Forest. Brin says that after dark the undead come up from over there, from the woods to the south.

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[布琳]] herds the cattle here by day (6:00–21:00) and goes back to [[初原村]] to sleep at night. [[格斯]] crosses here every morning on his way to fell trees in the Forest.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- At the start there are 3 [[牛|cows]] and 4 [[鸡|chickens]]. They do not attack people and run off when struck. A felled cow leaves 2 [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]], and a chicken leaves [[生鸡肉|Raw Chicken]]; a hen lays an [[鸡蛋|Egg]] every {{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours. See [[动物|Animals]].
- The livestock slowly come back: while the player is on this map, a cow may come every 120 seconds, up to 4, and a chicken every 90 seconds, up to 5.
- The [[夜行生物|night creatures]] that appear after nightfall (zombies and bats, at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}}) crumble to dust at daybreak.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

At the start there are 3 [[树枝|Sticks]], 3 [[石块|Stones]] and 3 bundles of [[干草|Cut Grass]] on the ground, and more slowly grow afterwards (up to 6, 5 and 6). The raw beef and eggs [[给锅里添点东西]] asks for both come from here.

## Lore

{{剧透|The Herd at Dusk}}
Come to the Grassland at dusk (17:00–20:00) and you see the cows, one after another, bend their forelegs and kneel, all of them facing east. Brin says this began after the bridge broke, and in her whole life her grandmother never saw a cow kneel. Lumi says the Ford lies east, and past the Ford that great road: whatever is on that road, the cattle are kneeling to it. At the road's end is the thorn kingdom of [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]], whose law is “None cross that do not kneel.”
{{/剧透}}

The first time you come to the Grassland, Lumi notices that the cows are all huddled at the north end, well away from the woods.
