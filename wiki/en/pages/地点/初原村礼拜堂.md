**Firstlight Chapel** is [[伊尔丝修女]]'s chapel, on the square of [[初原村]], standing right behind [[祭坛|Astra's altar]] and the holy flame: step out of its door and one step down is the altar. Ilse keeps the holy flame outside the door by day; inside the chapel, [[安塞姆修士]] keeps the bookstand and sells skill books.

The chapel is indoors and no night creatures appear in it, but it counts as a [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]] (the village itself is a peaceful zone). It is not [[初晓礼拜堂|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] on [[晨光丘|Dawn Hill]].

## Map

{{地图}}

14×11, one stone-walled room, with its entrance in the south wall. Under the north wall is the altar carved with the five holy wounds, with a lit candlestand on either side; four benches stand in the nave; the bookstand is on the east side, with Brother Anselm standing beside it; his bed is in the northwest corner.

## Areas

- **The five wounds**: the [[祭坛|altar]] under the north wall. Press {{键|E}} to read: “Five wounds carved in the stone round a figure of Astra, the First Martyr: the crown, the scourge, the scales, the nails, the lance. Here the village kneels to give thanks for the flame outside.”
- **The skill bookstand**: the [[教堂书架|bookstand]] on the east side. Press {{键|E}} (“Trade at the bookstand”) to open the shop panel “Chapel Bookstand”, with two tabs: **Skill Books** (active skills anyone can learn, 60 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each) and **Profession** (a [[职业|profession]]'s active skills, 120 each). Hold a book you have bought and left-click to read it, and you learn the skill in it (see [[技能|skills]]).
- The sign by the door (outside on the square) reads: “FIRSTLIGHT CHAPEL — Sister Ilse keeps the flame and Astra's altar; inside, Brother Anselm keeps the bookstand of skill books.”
