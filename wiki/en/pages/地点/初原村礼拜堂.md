**Firstlight Chapel** is [[Sister Ilse]]'s chapel, on the square of [[Firstlight Village]], standing right behind [[altar|Astra's altar]] and the holy flame: step out of its door and one step down is the altar. Ilse keeps the holy flame outside the door by day; inside the chapel, [[Brother Anselm]] keeps the bookstand and sells skill books.

The chapel is indoors and no night creatures appear in it, but it counts as a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]] (the village itself is a peaceful zone). It is not [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] on [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]].

## Map

{{map}}

14×11, one stone-walled room, with its entrance in the south wall. Under the north wall is the altar carved with the five holy wounds, with a lit candlestand on either side; four benches stand in the nave; the bookstand is on the east side, with Brother Anselm standing beside it; his bed is in the northwest corner.

## Areas

- **The five wounds**: the [[altar|altar]] under the north wall. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Five wounds carved in the stone round a figure of Astra, the First Martyr: the crown, the scourge, the scales, the nails, the lance. Here the village kneels to give thanks for the flame outside.”
- **The skill bookstand**: the [[bookstand|bookstand]] on the east side. Press {{key|E}} (“Trade at the bookstand”) to open the shop panel “Chapel Bookstand”, with two tabs: **Skill Books** (active skills anyone can learn, 60 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each) and **Profession** (a [[Professions|profession]]'s active skills, 120 each). Hold a book you have bought and left-click to read it, and you learn the skill in it (see [[Skills|skills]]).
- The sign by the door (outside on the square) reads: “FIRSTLIGHT CHAPEL — Sister Ilse keeps the flame and Astra's altar; inside, Brother Anselm keeps the bookstand of skill books.”
