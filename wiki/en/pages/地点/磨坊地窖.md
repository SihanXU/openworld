**The Mill Cellar** lies beneath [[Millbrook]], reached through the mill's door. It is a stone-built storeroom, and at the back stand two flasks of vigil oil, the ones [[The Vigil|The Vigil]] has you bring back to [[Sister Ilse]]. Things of the night have crept in: two slimes and a spider.

The cellar is a dark indoor [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]; it knows no day or night, and no night creatures appear. It hangs beneath the mill and has no place of its own on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or on the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 16×12, the whole of it a single stone-walled room. The entrance is in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: the middle of the south wall, back to Millbrook. North of the entrance burns a set of candles.
- **The barrels in the corners**: each of the room's four corners holds a [[barrel|barrel]] (a container, empty).
- **The crates by the north wall**: two [[crate|crates]] by the north wall (empty).
- **The oil**: on either side, against the north wall, stands a flask of vigil oil (a [[Flask of Oil|Flask of Oil]]).

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is in the cellar.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

At the start there are 2 [[green slime|green slimes]] and 1 [[cave spider|cave spider]], all of which attack on sight. Once felled, they do not return.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

Once the two flasks of vigil oil are taken, there are no more. If you find only one, there is another flask of oil on [[Tobin]]'s handcart in [[Firstlight Village]], but it belongs to Tobin: take it while someone is watching and it counts as theft (you hear “Hey — that is mine!”, and the village talks about it for two days; there are no other consequences). Nothing grows from the ground in the cellar.

The first time you enter the cellar, Lumi says: there are slimes in the cellar; the things of the night creep further in week by week; the oil flasks are on the shelf at the very back.
