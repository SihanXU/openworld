**The Abbey Crypt** lies under the small chapel in the northeast of [[荆棘修道院|the Abbey of the Thorn]]: the crypt of the abbey's priors, one after another. The Order's knights are not buried here; they lie in [[荆棘骸骨堂|the Ossuary of the Thorn]]. At the north end of the crypt waits the last keeper, [[温德尔院长]].

The crypt is indoors and very dark, and counts as a [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]].

## Map

{{地图}}

30×20. Two walls running east to west divide the crypt into a south, a middle and a north chamber, with a gap in the walls every six tiles. The entrance is in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **The priors' niches**: a row of six [[墓碑|niches]] under the north wall of the south chamber. Press {{键|E}} to read: “A prior of the abbey, a thorn ring carved over the niche. The name has been chiselled off and cut again, smaller, lower down, as if by someone who kept forgetting.”
- **The deepest part**: the middle of the north chamber. Walk in and the scene of Prior Wendel rising plays.
- Lit [[火盆|braziers]] stand in each chamber.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one living.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- [[温德尔院长]]: the last keeper, a skeleton, in the north chamber. Defeat him and he drops [[荆棘骑士团印章|the Order's Seal]].
- 1 [[骷髅|skeleton]] and 1 [[幽灵|ghost]] in the middle chamber, which attack on sight.

## Lore

{{剧透|The last keeper}}
When you walk into the deepest part, Prior Wendel says: “Who... comes for the seal? The Crown went east. The relief stays here. We hold, that others need not. We HOLD.” Lumi says: “He's the one who kept the writ! Careful — he's not going to give it up nicely.”
