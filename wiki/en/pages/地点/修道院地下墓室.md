**The Abbey Crypt** lies under the small chapel in the northeast of [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]]: the crypt of the abbey's priors, one after another. The Order's knights are not buried here; they lie in [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]]. At the north end of the crypt waits the last keeper, [[Prior Wendel]].

The crypt is indoors and very dark, and counts as a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]].

## Map

{{map}}

30×20. Two walls running east to west divide the crypt into a south, a middle and a north chamber, with a gap in the walls every six tiles. The entrance is in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **The priors' niches**: a row of six [[gravestone|niches]] under the north wall of the south chamber. Press {{key|E}} to read: “A prior of the abbey, a thorn ring carved over the niche. The name has been chiselled off and cut again, smaller, lower down, as if by someone who kept forgetting.”
- **The deepest part**: the middle of the north chamber. Walk in and the scene of Prior Wendel rising plays.
- Lit [[brazier|braziers]] stand in each chamber.

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one living.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- [[Prior Wendel]]: the last keeper, a skeleton, in the north chamber. Defeat him and he drops [[The Order's Seal|the Order's Seal]].
- 1 [[skeleton|skeleton]] and 1 [[ghost|ghost]] in the middle chamber, which attack on sight.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

Apart from the seal Prior Wendel drops, there is nothing to take.
