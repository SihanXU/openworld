**The Warden's Sanctum** is the lowest level of [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]], reached by the stairs in the northeast corner of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]]. There is only one round stone chamber here, where the warden (a [[barrow warden|barrow warden]]) stands guard before an altar. Defeating it is the last objective of [[Lost in the Barrow]].

The sanctum is a dark underground [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]]. It hangs under the Old Barrow and has no place of its own on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 24×18. Down the upward stair in the middle of the north side, a short passage leads into a round stone chamber, with a lit brazier in each of its four corners.

## Areas

- **The upward stair**: in the north, back to the Ossuary of the Thorn.
- **The round chamber**: where the warden and two skeletons are.
- **The Commander's vow**: the [[altar|altar]] on the south side of the chamber, named “The Commander's vow”. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Cut into the altar, in a knight's plain hand: ‘I WILL HOLD UNTIL I AM RELIEVED. — ALBERIC.’ Below it, newer, pressed into the soot with a thumb: a mark of ash.”

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is in the sanctum.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

- The [[barrow warden|barrow warden]] stands before the altar and attacks anyone it sees, but does not stray far from the altar. Defeated, it drops the [[Belt|Tomas's Belt of Vigil]] it wore (Vowbound, defense 3, plus 2 more at night; vow: Vow of Vigil, after dark, critical chance +20% and movement speed +15%).
- 2 [[skeleton|skeletons]] guard the two sides of the chamber.
- The monsters on this level do not come back once defeated.

## Lore

The first time you come down into the sanctum, Lumi says: it's standing guard in front of an altar; not attacking, guarding; whatever it was, it still thinks it has a post to keep.

{{spoiler|When the warden falls}}
As the warden falls apart, its jaw moves and it speaks: “The night... is coming back. I could not... lie down.” Lumi starts: the dead don't talk. On the altar behind it is cut Alberic's vow, and below it is pressed a grey thumbprint. Lumi realizes this is the White Knight Hesper spoke of: he swore to hold, and no one ever came to relieve him.

The warden is Brother Tomas of the Thorn, sworn brother in the Order (not brother by blood) to the Commander, Alberic, sent about a hundred years ago to keep the Order's dead here (see the niches of [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|the Ossuary of the Thorn]] and the memorial of [[Abbey of the Thorn|the Abbey of the Thorn]]). The grey thumbprint under the vow, like the mark on the bread Lina spoke of, is a sign that [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] has been here. Alberic's vow is answered later, at [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
