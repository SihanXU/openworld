**The Lakeside** is a lake beneath the mists of [[The Held Ground]], opened up in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]. The lake is large, ringed with sand and reeds, with deep water in its middle. It is the Held Ground's place for fishing. When Alberic opens it, he says: “Listen. Water. I had forgotten what it sounds like.”

The Lakeside is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

- **Opening it**: on the “Grounds” page of [[Alberic]]'s Held Ground panel, once the land is at level II, put 20 {{item|Stone}} and 10 {{item|Plank}} into the Held Ground's stock and press “Open!”.
- The card says what it holds: a deep lake with pike, eels and carp; reeds and sand along the shore; water for the fields.

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 32×24, with old trees and pines two tiles deep along all four sides. Most of the north is an oval lake: an outer ring of sand, water within, and a broad stretch of deep water a little north of the middle. The south is grass, with an opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side.

## Areas

- **The opening and the sign**: in the middle of the south side. North of the opening stands a sign: “The Lakeside — deep water: pike in the middle of the lake, eels at night, carp at dawn.”
- **The lake**: neither water nor deep water can be walked across. Now and then the shadows of fish rise in the water.
- **Sand and reeds**: a ring of sand round the lake, with a few clumps of [[reeds|reeds]] growing on it.

## Residents

{{residents}}

No one is here.

## Creatures

{{creatures}}

There are no animals here, and no monsters.

## Items and Resources

{{map items}}

- **Fishing**: hold a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] and cast into the water (see [[Fishing|Fishing]]). [[Pike|Pike]] can be caught only when you cast into **deep water**, and the middle of this lake is the largest stretch of deep water in the game; there are also [[Eel|Eels]] at night, [[Catfish|Catfish]] in the rain, and a [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] at any hour. The sign says “carp at dawn”, but the Golden Carp in fact bites at any time of day; the fish that bites only at dawn (5:00–8:00) is the [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]].
- **Water**: click the water with an empty bucket to fill a bucket for watering the fields.
- **Ground yield**: sticks, stones and cut grass slowly grow on the grass (up to 6, 5 and 6), with a chance of one more of each every {{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds.

## Connected Locations

{{connections}}

- The opening three tiles wide in the middle of the south side (columns 15–17): walk out and you are back on [[The Held Ground]], standing due south of the sword.
- From the Held Ground: press “Go ➜” on this area's card in the [[Expanding the Held Ground|Held Ground expansion]] panel, and [[Alberic]] sends you to just inside the opening on the Lakeside's south side. Until the area has been opened, the card has no such button.
- The Lakeside is not on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]], nor drawn on the [[World Map|world map]]. [[The Oath-Thorn|The Oath-Thorn]] works here all the same, taking you back to where you stood before you entered the Held Ground.
