**The Chapel of the First Dawn** stands on the crown of [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]], a cold, abandoned stone chapel. Firstlight's holy flame was first kindled here, on its hearth, from the light of dawn. In the bell tower in the northeast corner an old rope still hangs, and ringing that bell is one of the objectives of [[The Vigil|The Vigil]].

The chapel is indoors and no night creatures appear in it, but it counts as a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]], and a bat lives inside. It hangs under Dawn Hill and has no place of its own on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 14×12, one stone-walled room. The entrance is in the middle of the south wall. Under the north wall is the hearth, with a candlestand on either side; a bench stands on each side of the middle; the bell tower is in the northeast corner.

## Areas

- **The first hearth**: the [[altar|altar]] under the north wall, named “The first hearth”. Press {{key|E}} to read: “A stone hearth, cold for centuries. Soot in the cracks: the Firstlight was kindled here from the dawn, and carried down the hill.”
- **Candlestands and benches**: two unlit candlestands on either side of the hearth, and two benches in the nave.
- **The bell tower**: the northeast corner. Walk into this corner and you take hold of the old rope and ring the bell; a scene plays, and The Vigil's “ring the bell” is done. No key is needed.

## Lore

{{spoiler|The bell}}
When you ring the bell, it swings once, twice, and its voice rolls down the hill all the way to Firstlight. Lumi says the village must be able to hear it; it sounds like morning, though it isn't. She says too: Ilse told her the First Fire was lit on this hearth, from the sun's own light; it is so cold now, as though it has been waiting for something.

Sister Ilse says someone rang this bell the morning the sun came back; and a vigil for those coming home should ring it too.
