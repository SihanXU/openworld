{{信息框}}
**The Old Barrow** lies under the two standing stones at the bottom of [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]]. On the day of the storm, [[莉娜]] and [[奥斯卡]] of Firstlight took shelter here and were trapped by the dead. The barrow goes down three levels in all: this level is the barrow itself, below it is [[荆棘骸骨堂|the Ossuary of the Thorn]], and at the very bottom is [[守墓者圣所|the Warden's Sanctum]]. [[古墓里的失踪者]] takes you through all three.

The barrow is a dark underground [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]], always in the same dim light, day or night; no night creatures appear underground, but it has its own dead.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 30×24, cave wall on every side. The entrance down from Thornwood Hollow is in the north (columns 13–15), and a flagstone passage three tiles wide runs south and opens into a great hall, with a cell off its west side and another off its east side. Braziers burn along both sides of the passage.

## Areas

- **The entrance**: the head of the steps in the north. On the ground near the entrance is a [[画着门的粉笔画|chalk drawing of a door]] (one of [[佩尔的涂鸦|Pell's Drawings]]): “A door, shut, a hand-mark on it. Behind it, something with a crown of bones. In front, two small people.”
- **The great hall**: the flagstone hall at the end of the passage, where skeletons roam.
- **The west cell**: where [[莉娜]] is trapped. Walk into the cell and a scene plays. On the ground near the cell lie a [[面包|Loaf marked with ash]] and a [[头盔|Rusted Helm of the Thorn]] (Crude).
- **The east cell**: in the cell is a [[地形|downward stair]] cut into the rock, leading to the second level, [[荆棘骸骨堂|the Ossuary of the Thorn]].

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[莉娜]] is trapped in the west cell, waiting for someone to find her. Once you have spoken with her (one of the objectives of [[古墓里的失踪者]]), she stays where she is; only after the quest is handed in does she go back to [[初原村]].

## Creatures

{{生物}}

- At the start there are 3 [[骷髅|skeletons]] and 1 [[蝙蝠|bat]], all of which attack on sight.
- Skeletons slowly come back: while you are on this level, one may come out every 240 seconds (4 game hours), up to 2.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

The barrow's ground yields nothing, and there are no trees to fell. The cave walls can be broken with a pickaxe (see [[采矿|mining]]), but this level has its own cell on [[五印之地|the Lands of the Five Seals]], so breaking through the edge takes you out onto the world map.

## Lore

{{剧透|What Lina says}}
Walk into the west cell and Lina says it was not the dead who shut them in here but a man in grey, who pulled the inner door to from outside and then pushed a loaf of bread under it. There was a grey thumbprint on the bread. Oskar went down the stairs in the east cell to look for another way out and never came back. Lumi says the dead in here want to lie down but can't, like a bed that's too cold.

As Chapter 1 is designed, the villagers believe [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] trapped the two; in truth what he shut in was the warden who will not rest, and the bread he left bears the mark of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]].
{{/剧透}}
