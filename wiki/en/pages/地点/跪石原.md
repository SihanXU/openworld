**The Kneeling Field** lies between [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]] and [[The East Road|the East Road]], on the outskirts of the White Knight's kingdom. Pilgrims would kneel on this field to pray, then go on east.

It is a [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zone]].

## Map
{{map}}

44×24. To the west it joins [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], to the east [[The East Road|the East Road]]. A road runs west to east through the dry grass in the middle of the field.

## Areas
- **The kneeling stones**: a row on each side of the road, 8 in all, each carved as a kneeling pilgrim. Like the kneeling stones in the East Road's kingdom, they bear the names of those who would not kneel to the knight. Press {{key|E}} to read them, for example: “Aelis of Varrow, pilgrim. She would not kneel. The 212th year of the Crown.” The others are Brother Hal, Ysolde of the Salt Coast, Odile the Smith, Corin (“sent with the blessing of the Holy See”), the sisters Petra and Joan, an unnamed one (“A scallop and a child's shoe”), and the newest: “The carving is fresh. The name is not cut yet.”
- **Kneeling “subjects”**: the 6 [[scarecrow|scarecrows]] between the stones. Press {{key|E}} to read: “Straw in a pilgrim's coat, bent at the knees and tied there with briar. Someone set them in rows, facing east, and keeps setting them straight.”
- At the field's east end grow the knight's thorns, and a sign reads “THE KINGDOM. KNEEL.”

## Lore
- The first time you enter, a scene plays and completes “Go to the Kneeling Field” in [[The First Seal|The First Seal]]. Lumi reads out the names on the stones: “Aelis, Hal, Ysolde, Corin… ‘would not kneel’, every one of them. Stubborn! I like them already.” Then she asks: “And somebody keeps setting the straw ones straight, all facing east. Who tidies a field like this?”
- The years on the stones are counted in the reckoning of the Crown, from the year Alberic put on the Crown of Thorns. The oldest stone is from the 212th year.
