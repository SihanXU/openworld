**Hilde's Cabin** is the inside of the wooden cabin on the shore of [[The Reed Bend|the Reed Bend]], entered by the cabin's door. The fisherwoman [[Hilde]] sleeps here at night.

The cabin is indoors, and no night creatures appear in it. It hangs under the Reed Bend and has no place of its own on [[The Lands of the Five Seals|the Lands of the Five Seals]] or the [[World Map|world map]].

## Map

{{map}}

The map is 10×8, one wood-walled room, with its door in the middle of the south wall.

## Areas

- **Bed**: a [[bed|bed]] in the northwest corner.
- **Fireplace**: in the northeast corner, always lit.
- **Hilde's table**: in the middle of the room; press {{key|E}} to read: a tally of catches, year by year; nine winters back, one line is not a tally: “Boat came home. He did not. Fog.” Nothing else is written that year.
- **Smoked eels**: the [[crate|crate]] in the southeast corner, holding 2 [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]].
- **Barrel**: a [[barrel|barrel]] in the southwest corner, empty.

## Residents

{{residents}}

[[Hilde]] sleeps here from 22:00 to 5:00.
