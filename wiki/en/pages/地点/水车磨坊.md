{{信息框}}
**Millbrook** lies west of [[初原村]]. A brook runs through it from north to south, and the mill stands on the brook's east bank. The miller [[塔姆]] supplies Firstlight Village with flour and lamp oil. The mill's door leads down to [[磨坊地窖]], and the lamp oil [[守夜之备|The Vigil]] needs is down there.

Millbrook is a [[和平区域与战斗区域|peaceful zone]]; no monsters appear at night.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30, with woods all round. In the west a brook three tiles wide flows from the northern edge to the southern, with a little wooden bridge across it at row 14. The mill is a long house in the north. The road from Firstlight Village comes in from the east, passes a sign and reaches the yard in front of the mill; another path runs west from the yard over the bridge. South of the mill lies a stretch of farmland.

## Areas

- **The mill**: the long house in the north, whose door leads to [[磨坊地窖]].
- **The yard**: before the mill's door, where [[塔姆]] tends the waterwheel by day.
- **The sign**: beside the road in the east, reading “MILLBROOK — flour and lamp oil for Firstlight.”
- **The brook and the wooden bridge**: the brook in the west, with reeds along its banks. The wooden bridge over it leads to the west bank.
- **The wheat field**: a broad stretch of farmland south of the mill. It was a wheat field once, but in the ever shorter days nothing will grow there any more; it is bare ground now, and you can sow it yourself (see [[农耕|Farming]]).

## Residents

{{居民}}

[[塔姆]] is the miller. From 6:00 to 21:00 he tends the waterwheel in the yard, and at night he sleeps in the mill.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

There are no creatures at the mill itself. Whatever got in is underground, in [[磨坊地窖]].

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

Nothing lies on the ground at the start; afterwards the ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass (up to 6, 5 and 6). The brook's water can be drawn with a bucket.

## Lore

The first time you come to the mill, Lumi says: listen to that waterwheel; it turns a while, then stops, as if it were tired. Tam says something has got into his cellar, and the lamp oil is down there. In the design of Chapter 1, Tam's waterwheel turns more slowly week by week.
