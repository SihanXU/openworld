{{信息框}}
**The Forest** lies south of [[草原|the Grassland]]: a very dense wood, with a path threading between a few clearings and running south to [[荆棘谷]]. It is a good place for timber, and [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]] grows here. In a clearing in the southeast corner is a camp no one has used in a long time.

The Forest is an open-air [[和平区域与战斗区域|combat zone]]; after nightfall [[夜行生物|night creatures]] appear.

## Map

{{地图}}

The map is 40×30. The woods are thick all round, and inside them about two tiles in five hold a tree. The path comes in at the northern exit (columns 22–24), passes the northern clearing, bends southwest, skirts the middle and eastern clearings, then runs west to the clearing in the southwest corner and leaves the map southward from there (columns 12–14). Three side paths branch off: one west through the woods, leaving the map on the west side (rows 14–16) for [[烧炭谷]]; one east from the eastern clearing, leaving the map on the east side (rows 16–18) for [[芦苇湾]]; and one to the clearing in the southeast corner.

## Areas

- **The northern clearing**: the first clearing you reach coming in from the Grassland. [[格斯]] fells trees here every morning.
- **The middle clearings**: the few forest clearings the path runs through, the ground strewn with fallen leaves, ferns and moss.
- **The Pilgrim's Camp**: the clearing in the southeast corner. A rotted [[帐篷|tent]], a cold fire ring (a dead [[营火|campfire]]), an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree (a [[布告栏|notice board]]; press {{键|E}} to read it), and beside it a cairn with a pilgrim's staff leaning on it; on the ground by the cairn lies a [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]]. The paper on the tree reads: “By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearer wanes; let the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage go out. Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village is named the Pilgrim. — Year 380 of the Era of the Five Sufferings. (The paper has gone gray with age. Its words are exactly those the courier read to you.)”
- **The way to Thornwood Hollow**: the southwest corner, where the path leaves the Forest southward.

## Residents

{{居民}}

No one lives in the Forest. [[格斯]] fells trees in the northern clearing every day from 6:00 to 12:00, then goes back to [[初原村]] to stack the wood.

## Creatures

{{生物}}

The Forest has no monsters by day. After nightfall [[僵尸|zombies]] and [[蝙蝠|bats]] appear (at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}}), and they crumble to dust at daybreak.

## Items and Resources

{{物品分布}}

- **Logs**: the Forest has more trees than anywhere. Felling one gives 2 [[木头|Logs]] and 1 [[树枝|Stick]], and the stump it leaves grows back in {{数值|树木再生}} game days. See [[伐木|Woodcutting]].
- **Frostcap**: at the start 3 [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]] grow in the shade; once taken, one may grow back every 90 seconds, up to 3 at a time. It is one of the ingredients [[雷恩]], the herbalist of [[余烬镇]], asks for.
- **Sticks**: they lie thickest on the ground here, up to 10 at a time. Stones reach at most 3, and cut grass at most 4.

## Lore

The first time you enter the Forest, Lumi says that a forest this big ought to be full of noise in the afternoon, yet there is not a single bird. Where the birds went is answered in [[烧炭谷|the Charcoal Dell]] to the west: since the night the little bridge broke, every bird in the Forest has flown each dusk to the old oak of the charcoal burner [[奥林]] to roost, all of them facing east, not one making a sound (see [[鸟都去哪儿了]]).

{{剧透|The Pilgrim Before}}
Walking into the camp in the southeast corner plays a scene: Lumi reads out the paper on the tree and finds it word for word the same as the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage the courier read to you, only very old, and the name on it is not yours. The scallop by the cairn is a pilgrim's token; [[伊尔丝修女]] will know whose it is. After this you can take up the quest [[上一位朝圣者]]: it was Brother Cassian, named thirty-one years ago, for whom Ilse prayed when she was still a novice.

Once you have found the camp, come back at night (20:00–04:00) and a pale shade kneels beside the cold fire ring, head bowed, and says: “I knelt to him. Do not kneel.” Then it vanishes.
