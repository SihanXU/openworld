**Documents** are items with writing on them: the [[Book|Book]], the [[Scroll|Scroll]], the [[Letter|Letter]], the [[Map|Map]], and Chapter 1's [[Proclamation of the Pilgrimage|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]] and [[Almanac of the First Night|Almanac of the First Night]]. They are made of paper and can burn.

Much of the world's writing is not on items but on things that stand: signs, notice boards, gravestones, altars, chalk drawings on the ground. Walk up to one and press {{key|E}} to read it; a sign can be read even from 2 cells away. What you read appears on the screen.

| [[Book]], [[Scroll]], [[Map]] | No fixed source on the maps at present |

Hold a document with writing on it and left-click to use it on yourself (label “Read …”), and its words appear in the messages at the bottom left. Those with writing at present are the [[Proclamation of the Pilgrimage|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]] (full text at [[Proclamation of the Pilgrimage#The proclamation|the text of the proclamation]]), the [[Almanac of the First Night|Almanac of the First Night]], the [[Writ of Relief|Writ of Relief]], and Alberic's four journal pages in the Chapter Hall (full text at [[The Chapter Hall#Items and Resources|the Chapter Hall]]). Other [[Book|books]], [[Scroll|scrolls]], [[Letter|letters]] and [[Map|maps]] hold nothing written, and the rules do not define reading them; an undefined act on a document is left to [[The World Learns|the world learns]].

## Things to read in the world

Below are the texts that can be read with {{key|E}} around Chapter 1 (the game's own words).

### Around Firstlight Village

- **Astra's altar** ([[Firstlight Village]], the [[altar|altar]] beside the flame): “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. — Suffering is not God's abandonment.”
- **The sign by the hot spring** (Firstlight Village): “Warm spring — sit a while, and it mends you.”
- **Millbrook's sign** ([[Millbrook]]): “MILLBROOK — flour and lamp oil for Firstlight.”
- **The Dawnstone** (the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]] on [[Dawn Hill]]): “Cut deep, and old: HERE THE SUN ROSE AGAIN. Below, newer: five small wounds, and a flame.”
- **The first hearth** (the altar of the [[Chapel of the First Dawn]]): “A stone hearth, cold for centuries. Soot in the cracks: the Firstlight was kindled here from the dawn, and carried down the hill.”

### Pell's chalk drawings

Four chalk drawings of the grey man, the things the quest [[Pell's Drawings]] has you find. They are drawn on the ground; you have to look down.

| Drawing | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [[chalk drawing of a stone]] | [[Mountain Foot]], by the shrine stone | “The shrine stone, five marks on it, the first scribbled over in black. A grey figure with its hand on it.” |
| [[chalk drawing of a door]] | At the door of [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]] | “A door, shut, a hand-mark on it. Behind it, something with a crown of bones. In front, two small people.” |
| [[chalk drawing of a bridge]] | [[The Ford|The Ford]], by the little bridge | “A bridge with its middle gone. A tall figure in white beside it, and a smaller grey one watching from the trees.” |
| [[chalk drawing of a grey man]] | Firstlight Village, beside the flame | “A hooded man with a lantern, beside a fire. Underneath, in a child's letters: HE WAS HERE FIRST.” |

### The three levels of the Old Barrow

- **The carved standing stones** ([[Thornwood Hollow]], the [[gravestone|gravestones]] on either side of the Old Barrow's entrance): “Worn carving: a knight with a crown of thorns, and before him a line of small figures, kneeling. Beneath: THE ORDER OF THE THORN HOLDS.”
- **The Order's niches** ([[The Ossuary of the Thorn]], four of them):
  - “BROTHER ODO OF THE THORN. He held the gate at Varn three days. He did not kneel.”
  - “SISTER MAREN OF THE THORN, who carried water to both armies.”
  - “BROTHER TOMAS OF THE THORN, the Commander's brother. Warden of this house until the night comes back.”
  - “Carved, and left empty: FENNICK, SQUIRE TO THE COMMANDER. The stone is clean. Someone keeps it swept.”
- **The Order's roll** (the altar of the Ossuary of the Thorn): “THE ORDER OF THE THORN. Forty-one names, the oldest worn smooth. The last, cut deeper than the rest: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER, WHO KNELT TO CHILDREN. TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN. HE HOLDS.”
- **The knight-commander's vow** (the altar of the [[The Warden's Sanctum]]): “Cut into the altar, in a knight's plain hand: "I WILL HOLD UNTIL I AM RELIEVED. — ALBERIC." Below it, newer, pressed into the soot with a thumb: a mark of ash.”

### The camp in the Forest

- **The proclamation nailed to a tree** (the southeast of [[The Forest|the Forest]], a [[notice board|notice board]]): “BY ORDER OF THE HOLY CITY. The Seal-bearers weaken. Let the pilgrimage be called. Brother Cassian of Firstlight is named pilgrim. — In the 380th year of the Five. (The paper is grey with age. The words are the ones the courier read to you.)”
- **A cairn of stones** (a gravestone in the same place): “Stones piled over something long. A pilgrim's staff leans on it, its tip worn flat by many miles. No name.”

### Cloudtop and the East Road

- **The watchman's log** (a [[table|table]] on [[Cloudtop]]): see [[Story Items#The watchman's log|Story Items]].
- **The kneeling stones** ([[The East Road|The East Road]], in the knight's kingdom, five of them): “A stone carved as a pilgrim kneeling. No name. A scallop shell, the pilgrim's mark, worn almost away.” They are carved of those who would not kneel to the knight.

### The Pilgrim's Road

What can be read at the Pilgrims' Rest, on the Pilgrim's Road, in the Abbey of the Thorn and on the Kneeling Field: the Holy See's notice at [[The Pilgrims' Rest]], the five-wound altar and the litany of the Five Sufferings in the [[Chapel of the Five]], the pilgrims' register in [[The Pilgrims' Hostel]], the verses of the [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]], the statue and the memorial stones of the [[Abbey of the Thorn]], the Order's roll in the [[The Chapter Hall]], the abbots' niches in the [[The Abbey Crypt]], the kneeling stones and the scarecrows of the [[The Kneeling Field]]. The texts are on each page.

### The Held Ground

- **The planted sword** (the middle of [[The Held Ground]]): “Alberic's longsword, point down in the earth, a little crooked. The ground holds as far as he holds it.”
- **The boundary stones** (the four sides of the Held Ground): “A boundary stone, its carving worn: a thorn ring, and inside it, nothing kneeling.”

For what is written on signs and notice boards, see [[sign|Sign]] and [[notice board|Notice Board]].
