**Furniture and fixtures** are the things that stand in the world: beds, workbenches, chests, campfires, lamps, wells, signs, altars… Most of them cannot be picked up; only beds and workbenches can be taken away and set down again: left-click one with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; press {{键|E}} to pick it up. Build mode ({{键|B}}) can also put up campfires, chests, fences and signs.

## Furniture you can take away

| Furniture | Materials | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [[床]] | 2 planks, 2 cut grass | Stand on the bed and press {{键|E}} to sleep till morning; the bed becomes your resting place. A room with a bed in it is a bedroom, where you sleep better. See [[睡觉|Sleeping]]. |
| [[工作台]] | 4 planks | Standing beside it, the crafting panel offers the things made at a workbench: Wooden Club, Wooden Shield, Wooden Bow. |

Both are made by hand in the crafting panel ({{键|C}}), and can also be made on the spot and set down in build mode. There is a ready-made workbench by the wood pile in [[初原村]].

## Built in build mode

| Fixture | Materials | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [[营火]] | 2 stones, 2 sticks | Light it with a torch: warmth, cooking, light at night |
| [[板条箱]] | 2 planks, finished with a hammer | Small storage, 6 slots |
| Wooden Chest | 4 planks, finished with a hammer | Large storage, 12 slots |
| [[栅栏]] | 2 sticks, finished with a hammer | Blocks passage; several can be joined into a ring to fence a garden |
| Sign | 1 stick, 1 plank, finished with a hammer | A blank sign; carrying ink, you can write on it with a quill (the game has no source of ink or quills yet) |

For those that need a hammer, put down the materials first, and finish by left-clicking once with a [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]] in hand. One not yet hammered is “unfinished work”, which a tool can take apart. Left-click a crate or a Wooden Chest with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops (what is inside spills out first); pick it up and it can be built somewhere else.

## Containers

Press {{键|E}} to open one, and things can be put in and taken out (see [[箱子与容器|Chests and Containers]]). How many slots each container holds:

| Container | Slots |
|---|---|
| [[箱子]] | 12 |
| [[衣柜]] | 12 |
| [[柴堆]] | 12 |
| [[货架]] | 8 |
| [[书架]] | 8 |
| [[板条箱]] | 6 |
| [[木桶（容器）]] | 4 |
| [[麻袋]] | 4 |
| [[陶罐]] | 2 |

Barrels, crates and clay pots are flimsy and break in one blow. A room holding three or more containers counts as a storeroom.

Note: the storage barrel and the [[木桶|bucket]] for carrying water share one Chinese name in the game. The one for water is a [[工具|tool]].

## Light sources

- **[[营火|Campfire]]**: gives light only once lit. Left-click it holding a [[火把|Torch]] and it catches; with the skill Firemaker an ordinary stick lights it. A lit campfire can cook; rain does not put it out.
- **Braziers, lamp posts, candles, lantern posts, [[壁炉|fireplaces]]**: the fixed lights of the towns and the underground, most of them lit from the start. A lit [[火盆|brazier]] or fireplace can also cook; lamp posts, candles and lantern posts cannot.
- **Lights held in the hand**: the [[火把|Torch]] (1 stick, 1 cut grass, made by hand), the [[蜡烛|Candle]], the [[提灯|Lantern]]. Held in the hand, they make you glow, as a visual effect only.

Within 2 cells of anything lit or burning, wild things that fear fire do not come near, and do not chase you in. So beside a lit campfire is a safe place at night. See [[昼夜|Day and Night]] and [[夜行生物|Night Creatures]].

A lit campfire, brazier, lamp post, candles, lantern post, fireplace, forge or cauldron can be warmed at by pressing {{键|E}} empty-handed: if you are wet, you dry at once. See [[营火#取暖|warming up]].

## Other fixtures

- **Living**: [[桌子|tables]], [[椅子|chairs]], [[长椅|benches]], [[原木长凳|log benches]], [[炊架|cooking racks]], [[手推车|handcarts]], [[水井|wells]] (for drawing water), [[帐篷|tents]].
- **Workstations**: the [[铁砧|anvil]], the [[熔炉|forge]], the [[坩埚|cauldron]] (forge and cauldron can cook when lit; a lit forge can smelt ore into ingots, see [[熔炉#熔炼|smelting]]).
- **Readable**: [[告示牌|signs]] (readable even from 2 cells away), [[布告栏|notice boards]], [[墓碑|gravestones]], [[祭坛|altars]]. See [[文书|Documents]].
- **Holy sites**: [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stones]], [[传送雕像|waypoint statues]].
- **Training**: the [[训练假人|training dummy]], which wobbles back up by itself once knocked down; the tutorial uses it for attack practice.

## Rooms

The fixtures in a room decide what room it is: a bed makes a bedroom, a cooking fire a kitchen, a workbench or forge a workshop, three or more containers a storeroom. See [[房间|Rooms]].
