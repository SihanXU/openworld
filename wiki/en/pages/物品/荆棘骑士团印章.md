**The Order's Seal** is a bronze seal hung on a chain: a ring of thorns, and inside it a knight on one knee — kneeling not to a lord, but to a child. Its rim is engraved “We hold, so that others need not.” It is a neck accessory of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality.

## Source

{{来源}}

- [[温德尔院长|Prior Wendel]] of [[修道院地下墓室]] drops it where he falls. Press {{键|E}} to pick it up.
- There is only this one, and it cannot be crafted.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Picking it up**: plays a scene and completes the objective of [[第一印]] “Take the Order's seal from the last guardian of the Abbey Crypt”. Once you have it and the [[替任文书|Writ of Relief]], The First Seal moves to its next stage.
- **Worn at the neck**: defense +2; stand-firm damage reduction +10% (less damage taken while standing still; see [[装备]]).
- It counts as an important item: on Normal difficulty it stays on you when you fall (see [[难度]]).
- The [[钉刑圣木之龛|Shrine of the Nails]] wants metal, but does not take this seal: shrines do not take relics or important items, and it only says “The Shrine of the Nails does not light. The Order's Seal is not something to give away.”

## Lore

{{剧透|Scene}}
The scene when you pick it up:

> (A bronze seal: a knight on one knee — not to a lord, but to a child. Around the rim: WE HOLD, SO THAT OTHERS NEED NOT. It is warm, as if someone had been holding it all along.)

Lumi says: “Maybe if he sees this, he'll remember. Not ‘hold’. ‘Kneel’ — the good kind.”
{{/剧透}}
