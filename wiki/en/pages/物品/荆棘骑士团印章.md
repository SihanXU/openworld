**The Order's Seal** is a bronze seal hung on a chain: a ring of thorns, and inside it a knight on one knee — kneeling not to a lord, but to a child. Its rim is engraved “We hold, so that others need not.” It is a neck accessory of [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] quality.

## Source

{{sources}}

- [[Prior Wendel|Prior Wendel]] of [[The Abbey Crypt]] drops it where he falls. Press {{key|E}} to pick it up.
- There is only this one, and it cannot be crafted.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Picking it up**: plays a scene and completes the objective of [[The First Seal]] “Take the Order's seal from the last guardian of the Abbey Crypt”. Once you have it and the [[Writ of Relief|Writ of Relief]], The First Seal moves to its next stage.
- **Worn at the neck**: defense +2; stand-firm damage reduction +10% (less damage taken while standing still; see [[Equipment]]).
- It counts as an important item: on Normal difficulty it stays on you when you fall (see [[Difficulty]]).
- The [[Shrine of the Nails|Shrine of the Nails]] wants metal, but does not take this seal: shrines do not take relics or important items, and it only says “The Shrine of the Nails does not light. The Order's Seal is not something to give away.”
