**Stigmata** (Stigmatic) is the highest item [[Quality|quality]], its name shown in red-gold. In the setting, a Stigmatic item bears one of the five wounds of the First Martyr, [[Astra]], and only [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]] carry them. Stigmatic items never appear at random and cannot be crafted; each has a power of its own, and every power exists only once in the whole world.

## Rules

- **Multiplier**: power and defense count ×2, the highest of all qualities.
- **A power of its own**: the tooltip reads “✦ power name · Unique” and says what it does. The power travels with the blow to the thing struck: kneeling, burning and frozen become that thing's own statuses.
- **The cost**: by design, every Stigmatic item takes something back from whoever holds it, the cost written in red in the tooltip. At present only the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]] has a cost: while you wear it, you gain only half the experience.
- A profession's Legendary weapons work only for their own profession; see [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]].

In the game's data, the Crown of Thorns is the only Stigmatic item marked with the wound it answers to: the first Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Crowning|the Crowning]]. Dragonsbane comes from an old storyline older than this setting; its quality is Stigmatic too.

## History

- 2026-10-07: The highest quality, “Legendary”, was renamed “Stigmatic” and no longer appears at random; every Stigmatic item gained a power of its own; the Crown of Thorns became a Stigmatic headpiece.
