**Legendary Arms** are the weapons that belong to the four [[Professions|professions]], two to each, of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality, each with a power of its own. They never lie on the ground and cannot be crafted: when a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]] falls, he leaves one Legendary weapon belonging to the profession of whoever felled him.

## How to obtain

- When you defeat a Seal-bearer after choosing a profession, he drops a Legendary weapon of your profession, plus 4 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]].
- The one dropped is the first of the profession's two that has not yet appeared in the world. Each Legendary weapon appears only once in a world.
- If you have not chosen a profession, the Seal-bearer drops only Iron Ore.
- At present the game holds only the first Seal-bearer, [[The White Knight Alberic]], so each profession can obtain only its first weapon: the Assassin's Nightfang, the Warrior's Oathbrand, the Ranger's Galestring and the Mage's Emberheart. The second ones wait for later Seal-bearers.
- Nothing obtained in the [[Boss Challenge|Boss Challenge]] is saved.

## Overview

| Profession | First | Second |
|---|---|---|
| [[Assassin]] | Nightfang (dagger) | Mirrorshard (dagger) |
| [[Warrior]] | Oathbrand (sword) | The Unfallen Wall (shield, worn in the off hand) |
| [[Ranger]] | Galestring (bow, wind element) | Starfall (bow) |
| [[Mage]] | Emberheart (staff, fire element) | Rimecrown (staff, ice element) |

Like other profession weapons, a Legendary weapon works only for its own profession: in another profession's hands it makes no attack, and only a Warrior can wear the Unfallen Wall. Profession talents apply to them.

A Legendary bow has a longer range than an ordinary bow, and a Legendary staff a longer range than an ordinary staff.
