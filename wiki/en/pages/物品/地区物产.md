**Regional goods** are the things each [[五印之地|region]] of the continent makes and sells itself. All of them are **variants** of some base item: a “Fenland Sword” handles just like a [[剑|Sword]], a “Smoked Herring” eats just like [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]] — the world's rules for them are exactly those for the original; what differs is the name, the icon, the [[材质|substance]], the worth, and where they can be found.

Each region has three kinds:

- **Gear and tools made locally**: a full set of weapons, armour, clothes, tools and jewelry that the local smiths, tailors and jewelers make in their own style (“Frostfang Sword”, “Redmarch Pauldrons”, “Hallowed Ring”), from the substances produced there.
- **Local goods**: things found only there — the Whaling Harpoon and Stockfish of [[霜牙海岸]], the Chaff Bread and Loaded Dice of [[衡谷平原]], the Mourning Veil and Jellied Eel of [[苍白林沼]].
- **Famous pieces**: a region's renowned craft, rare and dear (three times the price of the original); famous gear is always of [[品质|Fine]] quality.

They are not placed on the playable maps but in each region's [[未开放的地点|backdrop places]]: the chests and storage barrels at every household's door, the shops' shelves, the smithies' weapon chests, the inns' casks, the temples' candlesticks and bookshelves, the camps' crates, the market stalls, and the chests deep in ruins and caves. A place mostly keeps its own region's things; to [[圣城|the Holy City]] pilgrims come from every quarter, and goods of other regions can be found there too. Across the whole continent, more than twenty thousand things are kept this way.
