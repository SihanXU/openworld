{{信息框}}

The **Writ of Relief** is the decree of the Holy See of the Five Sufferings to Alberic, the First Seal-bearer: in the fortieth year of his bearing the Seal, a successor would be sent to relieve him. It has lain all along by the scriptorium desk of the [[骑士团大厅]] in the [[荆棘修道院]], its seal unbroken, never sent.

## Source

{{来源}}

- In the west of the [[骑士团大厅]], on the floor beside the scriptorium desk. Press {{键|E}} to pick it up.
- There is only this one; it cannot be crafted and does not come back.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Taking it**: plays a scene and completes [[第一印|The First Seal]]'s objective “Learn what became of the knight in the Abbey of the Thorn”. Once you have it and [[荆棘骑士团印章|The Order's Seal]] as well, The First Seal moves to its next stage (go to the [[跪石原]]).
- **Read**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, and you read the full text:

> BY THE HOLY SEE OF THE FIVE. To Brother-Knight Alberic of the Thorn, First Seal-bearer: in the fortieth year of your bearing you shall be relieved of the Crown, and a successor sent to take it up. — The seal is unbroken. It was never sent. On the back, in a later, smaller hand: “No successor could be found who would bear it. Let him hold. He will not know the difference.”

- It counts as an important item: on normal difficulty, when you fall it stays with you and does not go into the [[遗落的包裹|Lost Belongings]] (see [[难度|Difficulty]]).
- It is made of paper and burns. The wayside shrines do not take it.

## Lore

{{剧透|The scene}}
The scene when you take it:

> (The writ of relief. In the fortieth year of his bearing, a successor sent. The wax is whole: it was never sent. On the back: “No successor could be found who would bear it. Let him hold. He will not know the difference.”)
> (On his desk, the leaves of his journal count on past the fortieth year.)

Lumi says: “A letter nobody ever sent? Then we'll take it with us. Somebody ought to deliver it — late is better than never.”

The one who held it back was the abbey's prior, [[温德尔院长]]. Once you have been here, [[凯乌斯司铎]] says: “You found the writ. ...Yes. I knew. I was told it was not a pilgrim's concern, and I decided to believe it.”
{{/剧透}}

The forty-year term and the Holy See's withholding of the writ are setting that Chapter 1's design added beyond the original lore document (docs/chapter-1.md §14, marked as a proposal).
