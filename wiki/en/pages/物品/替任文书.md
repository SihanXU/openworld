The **Writ of Relief** is a writ under the wax seal of the Order: Alberic, Bearer of the Crown, has borne the seal forty years, and another shall be sent to relieve him. It has lain all along by the scriptorium desk of the [[The Chapter Hall]] in the [[Abbey of the Thorn]], its wax whole, never opened and never sent.

## Source

{{sources}}

- In the west of the [[The Chapter Hall]], on the floor beside the scriptorium desk. Press {{key|E}} to pick it up.
- There is only this one; it cannot be crafted and does not come back.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Taking it**: plays a scene and completes [[The First Seal|The First Seal]]'s objective “At the Abbey of the Thorn, north of the road, find out what became of the knight”. Once you have it and [[The Order's Seal|The Order's Seal]] as well, The First Seal moves to its next stage (go to the [[The Kneeling Field]]).
- **Read**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, and you read the full text:

> A writ of relief under the wax seal of the Order. It reads: Alberic, Bearer of the Crown, has borne the seal forty years; another shall be sent to relieve him. — The wax is whole. It was never opened, and never sent. On the back, one line in fresher ink: “No one could be found who would bear it. Let him go on holding.”

- It counts as an important item: on normal difficulty, when you fall it stays with you and does not go into the [[Lost Belongings|Lost Belongings]] (see [[Difficulty|Difficulty]]).
- It is made of paper and burns. The wayside shrines do not take it.

## Lore

{{spoiler|The scene}}
The scene when you take it:

> (A writ of relief under the Order's wax seal. It reads: Alberic, bearer of the Crown, has borne it forty years; another shall be sent to take it up from him.)
> (The seal is whole. It was never opened, and never sent. On the back is a line in newer ink: “No one can be found who will bear it. Let him go on holding.”)

Lumi says: “It was never sent... Let's take it with us.”

The one who held it back was the abbey's prior, [[Prior Wendel]]. Once you have been here, [[Father Caius]] says: “You found the writ. ...Yes. I knew. I was told it was not a pilgrim's concern, and I decided to believe it. Go on. Don't look at me like that.”
{{/剧透}}

The forty-year term and the Holy See's withholding of the writ are setting that Chapter 1's design added beyond the original lore document (docs/chapter-1.md §14, marked as a proposal).
