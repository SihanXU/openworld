The **Sworn Scallop** is the hero's own mark of pilgrimage: the reward of [[The First Seal]] for taking the Pilgrim's Vow in the [[Chapel of the Five|Chapel of the Five]] at [[The Pilgrims' Rest]]. It is a neck accessory of Fine quality.

## Source

{{sources}}

- [[The First Seal]]: take the vow before the altar of the Five Wounds (stage 50 of The First Seal).
- Cannot be crafted.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Worn at the neck**: defense +1, movement speed +10%.
- It is not the [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]]. The one at the forest camp belonged to the pilgrim before, Brother Cassian, and that is the one [[The Pilgrim Before]] asks about; carrying the Sworn Scallop does not count.
- It counts as a keepsake, and [[wayside shrine|wayside shrines]] do not accept it.

## Lore

Sister Ilse once said: “The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Every one of us is given one when we are named.”

## History

- 2026-10-13: Added. Before this, the vow gave the Pilgrim's Scallop itself, which made “The Pilgrim Before” count as found at once.
