The **Sworn Scallop** is the hero's own mark of pilgrimage: the reward of [[第一印]] for taking the Pilgrim's Vow in the [[五伤礼拜堂|Chapel of the Five]] at [[朝圣者驿站]]. It is a neck accessory of Fine quality.

## Source

{{来源}}

- [[第一印]]: take the vow before the altar of the Five Wounds (stage 50 of The First Seal).
- Cannot be crafted.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Worn at the neck**: defense +1, movement speed +10%.
- It is not the [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]]. The one at the forest camp belonged to the pilgrim before, Brother Cassian, and that is the one [[上一位朝圣者]] asks about; carrying the Sworn Scallop does not count.
- It counts as a keepsake, and [[路边圣龛|wayside shrines]] do not accept it.

## Lore

Sister Ilse once said: “The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Everyone who is named receives one.”

## History

- 2026-10-13: Added. Before this, the vow gave the Pilgrim's Scallop itself, which made “The Pilgrim Before” count as found at once.
