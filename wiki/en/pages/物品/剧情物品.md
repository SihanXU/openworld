**Story items** are items the story places in the world or hands to the player. Most of them cannot be crafted and exist only once in the world; some are to be handed to whoever gave a quest, others stay with you for good.

A story item is also an ordinary thing: it goes into the inventory, can be put down or given to someone, and one made of paper can be burned. Most of the meaning the story gives them is not written as rules: what you do with the ember, what you touch with the Crown of Thorns — these uses the game does not define are exactly where [[The World Learns|the world learns]] answers. With no model connected, nothing happens.

## Named props

Some story props are not item kinds of their own but one piece of an ordinary item, with a name of its own.

### Loaf marked with ash

A [[Bread|loaf of bread]] on the floor of Lina's cell in [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]], named “Loaf marked with ash”. Lina says a grey man pulled the inner door shut from outside, then pushed a loaf of bread under the gap: “It has a mark on it. Ash. Like a thumb pressed in.”

It can be eaten like ordinary bread.

{{spoiler|The ash mark on the bread}}

The grey man shut the restless warden inside and at the same time left food for the ones trapped there; the mark pressed into the bread is the sign of the Ashen Watch (docs/chapter-1.md §5). Beneath the knight-captain's vow in the [[The Warden's Sanctum]] there is the same grey thumbprint. See [[The Ashen Watch]].

### Vigil Oil

On the innermost shelf of [[The Mill Cellar|the Mill Cellar]] stand two [[Flask of Oil|flasks of oil]] named “Vigil Oil”, the things Ilse's quest [[The Vigil]] asks for. The miller [[Tam]] dares not go down: “Something got into the cellar after dark. Slimes, and worse.” His grandmother said the vigil lamps were lit from the first dawn.

The quest accepts only the item kind “Flask of Oil”; the flask sold on [[Tobin]]'s cart counts as well. Oil can be splashed on things to leave them “oiled”, and an oiled thing flares up when fire meets it.

### Dawnbloom

The [[Herb|herbs]] on [[Dawn Hill]], named “Dawnbloom”. It is said that after the First Long Night the sun rose again over Dawn Hill, and “the flowers up here still face east, every one of them”. The quest [[The Vigil]] asks for 3; it accepts only the item kind “Herb”. The map starts with 4 Dawnbloom; afterwards new herbs slowly grow on Dawn Hill's grass, named simply “Herb”, and they count for the quest just the same.

### Mia's ring

In the thickest of the briars in the east of [[Thornwood Hollow]] lies a silver [[Ring|ring]] named “Mia's ring”; it belonged to Mia's grandmother. See the quest [[Mia's Ring]]. The quest accepts only this one; no other ring you carry is taken.

### The watchman's log

A [[table|table]] in the sun-watching hut on [[Cloudtop]], named “The watchman's log”; press {{key|E}} to read it:

> SUNSET, CLOUDTOP WATCH. Year 401: 19:42 at midsummer. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — I have checked the glass twice. Year 411: the reports go down the mountain and nothing comes back. Last page: Found a small light asleep in the snow by the cairn, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was. I did not know what to tell it.

Walking up to the table plays a scene. [[Nessa Quill]]'s quest [[The Watchman's Log]] asks the player to read it. It is a table and cannot be taken.

### Frost Wand

A Wooden [[Wand|Wand]] of the frost element on [[Tobin]]'s cart, belonging to Tobin. Give Tobin some food and he lets you take it: “A deal is a deal, and that bread was a very good deal.” It can freeze water into thin ice, one of the ways across the river at the Ford. See [[Chapter 1]].

## How quests recognize items

Most quests that ask for items look only at the item's kind, not its name; [[Mia's Ring]] is the exception and accepts only Mia's own ring:

| Quest | What it asks for | Once handed over |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Vigil]] | 2 flasks of oil, 3 herbs | Taken |
| [[Mia's Ring]] | Mia's ring | Taken |
| [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]] | The Ninth Nail | Taken |
| [[Edda's Ring]] | Wedding Band | Taken |
| [[The Pilgrim Before]] | Pilgrim's Scallop | Not taken; Ilse tells you to keep it |
