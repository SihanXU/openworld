**Story items** are items the story places in the world or hands to the player. Most of them cannot be crafted and exist only once in the world; some are to be handed to whoever gave a quest, others stay with you for good.

A story item is also an ordinary thing: it goes into the inventory, can be put down or given to someone, and one made of paper can be burned. Most of the meaning the story gives them is not written as rules: what you do with the ember, what you touch with the Crown of Thorns — these uses the game does not define are exactly where [[世界会学|the world learns]] answers. With no model connected, nothing happens.

## Named props

Some story props are not item kinds of their own but one piece of an ordinary item, with a name of its own.

### Loaf marked with ash

A [[面包|loaf of bread]] on the floor of Lina's cell in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]], named “Loaf marked with ash”. Lina says a grey man pulled the inner door shut from outside, then pushed a loaf of bread under the gap: “It has a mark on it. Ash. Like a thumb pressed in.”

It can be eaten like ordinary bread.

{{剧透|The ash mark on the bread}}

The grey man shut the restless warden inside and at the same time left food for the ones trapped there; the mark pressed into the bread is the sign of the Ashen Watch (docs/chapter-1.md §5). Beneath the knight-captain's vow in the [[守墓者圣所]] there is the same grey thumbprint. See [[灰烬守望]].

{{/剧透}}

### Vigil Oil

On the innermost shelf of [[磨坊地窖|the Mill Cellar]] stand two [[一瓶灯油|flasks of oil]] named “Vigil Oil”, the things Ilse's quest [[守夜之备]] asks for. The miller [[塔姆]] dares not go down: “Something got into the cellar after dark. Slimes, and worse.” His grandmother said the vigil lamps were lit from the first dawn.

The quest accepts only the item kind “Flask of Oil”; the flask sold on [[托宾]]'s cart counts as well. Oil can be splashed on things to leave them “oiled”, and an oiled thing flares up when fire meets it.

### Dawnbloom

The [[草药|herbs]] on [[晨光丘]], named “Dawnbloom”. It is said that after the First Long Night the sun rose again over Dawn Hill, and “the flowers up here still face east, every one of them”. The quest [[守夜之备]] asks for 3; it accepts only the item kind “Herb”. The map starts with 4 Dawnbloom; afterwards new herbs slowly grow on Dawn Hill's grass, named simply “Herb”, and they count for the quest just the same.

### Mia's ring

In the thickest of the briars in the east of [[荆棘谷]] lies a silver [[戒指|ring]] named “Mia's ring”; it belonged to Mia's grandmother. See the quest [[米娅的戒指]]. The quest accepts only this one; no other ring you carry is taken.

### The watchman's log

A [[桌子|table]] in the sun-watching hut on [[云顶台地]], named “The watchman's log”; press {{键|E}} to read it:

> SUNSET, CLOUDTOP WATCH. Year 401: 19:42 at midsummer. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — I have checked the glass twice. Year 411: the reports go down the mountain and nothing comes back. Last page: Found a small light asleep in the snow by the cairn, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was. I did not know what to tell it.

Walking up to the table plays a scene. [[奈莎·奎尔]]'s quest [[守夜人的日志]] asks the player to read it. It is a table and cannot be taken.

### Frost Wand

A Wooden [[魔杖|Wand]] of the frost element on [[托宾]]'s cart, belonging to Tobin. Give Tobin some food and he lets you take it: “A deal is a deal, and that bread was a very good deal.” It can freeze water into thin ice, one of the ways across the river at the Ford. See [[第一章]].

## How quests recognize items

Most quests that ask for items look only at the item's kind, not its name; [[米娅的戒指]] is the exception and accepts only Mia's own ring:

| Quest | What it asks for | Once handed over |
|---|---|---|
| [[守夜之备]] | 2 flasks of oil, 3 herbs | Taken |
| [[米娅的戒指]] | Mia's ring | Taken |
| [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] | The Ninth Nail | Taken |
| [[艾达的戒指]] | Wedding Band | Taken |
| [[上一位朝圣者]] | Pilgrim's Scallop | Not taken; Ilse tells you to keep it |
