**Accessories** are gear worn on the ears, neck, shoulders, waist and fingers. What they give varies: earrings add experience, rings add attack, amulets, pauldrons and belts add defense, and the named ones carry traits of their own as well. For gear on the head, body, hands, feet and off hand see [[Armour]].

## Slots

| Slot | Forms | What it usually gives |
|---|---|---|
| Ears | [[Earring|Earrings]] | +10% experience |
| Neck | [[Amulet|Amulets]] | Defense |
| Shoulders | [[Cloak|Cloaks]], [[Pauldrons|pauldrons]] | Cloaks have no defense; pauldrons do |
| Waist | [[Belt|Belts]] | Defense |
| Fingers (two slots) | [[Ring|Rings]] | Attack |

Two rings can be worn at once. You put them on the same way as armour: hold one in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself, or swap it in the equipment slots of the pack.

The [[Wedding Band]] and the [[Pilgrim's Scallop]] can be worn too: the Wedding Band on a finger, the scallop hung at the neck. The treasure [[Circlet|circlet]] is worn on the head and counts under the head slot of [[Armour]].
