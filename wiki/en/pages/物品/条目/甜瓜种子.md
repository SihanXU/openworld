**Melon Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Melon|Melon]], bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]], 3 for 24 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland, they grow after two waterings. Harvesting gives seeds back, so your seeds multiply the more you plant.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The stall**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]], press {{key|E}}, and click them on the “Farming” shelf to buy 3 for 24 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]; they go straight into your inventory. Step away from the stall and you cannot buy.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe melon plant with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking by hand gives 1.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sowing**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow Melon Seeds.” It becomes a melon sprout. There is a ready-made field southwest of the planted sword on the Held Ground; elsewhere, a [[Hoe|Hoe]] turns diggable ground such as dirt or grass into farmland.
- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Sow melon” on the “Planting” page; the seeds only need to be in your inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering, in the open, makes it grow one stage two game hours later: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[melon (crops)|melon (crop)]], [[Farming|Farming]] and [[Crops|Crops]].
