A **Smith Hammer** is a hammer for building. Half-finished things put together from planks and sticks need a knock of the hammer to be completed: chests, crates, fences and signs are all made this way ([[Building|Building]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Make it yourself**: press {{key|C}}; 2 {{item|Stone}} and 1 {{item|Stick}} make a **Stone Smith Hammer**, with no [[workbench|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: the “Garden Supplies” crate beside the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds a **Steel Smith Hammer** (attack 5).
- **Quest**: when [[Joss|Joss]]'s side quest [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]] is completed, he gives you “a proper hammer” and his tool belt.

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip gives the smith hammer's use as “Finishes what is built”. Hold it and left-click:

- **A wooden frame**: a frame of two planks is nailed into a {{item|crate}}, one of four into a {{item|chest}}; one of three gives the message “One more plank.”
- **Stakes**: nailed into a stretch of {{item|fence}}.
- **A sign post**: nailed into a blank {{item|sign}}; you need a {{item|Quill}} to write on it.
- **Build mode**: when you press {{key|B}} to place a crate, a wooden chest, a fence or a signpost, the hammer must be in your pack; it is not used up.
- **Knocking built things loose**: left-click something built that can be carried off, such as a wall, door, floor, stairs, bed or workbench, and the whole of it comes loose and drops where it stood; press {{key|E}} to pick it up (if you are close it is drawn into your pack by itself), and you can carry it elsewhere and build it again. See [[Structures|Structures]].
- Clicking anything else knocks it with the hammer, and the blow counts as “blunt”: full effect on bone and crystal, half on wood, stone and ore (see [[Durability and Breaking#Efficiency table|the efficiency table]]).

How frames, stakes and posts are put up: see [[Building|Building]].
