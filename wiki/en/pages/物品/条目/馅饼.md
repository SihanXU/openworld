A **Pie** is a baked pastry; eating it restores 8 [[Satiety|satiety]], as much as a [[Steak|Steak]]. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it; nor is there any [[Cooking|cooking]] recipe in the game that makes pie.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Pie?” Confirm, and it restores 8 satiety.
- Pie burns: thrown into a fire it burns to ash.
