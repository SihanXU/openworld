A **Coin Pouch** is a pouch of money. The game's money is the [[Dawnmark|Dawnmark]] of [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] (see [[Money|Money]]); a coin pouch is not Dawnmarks: it cannot be opened, cannot be spent at the [[trading stall|trading stall]], and the stall will not take it either. You cannot get one in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Trivia

- The coin pouch's data records a value of 20. As with everything else, whatever it is worth is reckoned from this number (multiplied by material and quality): a merchant's buying and selling prices (see [[Trading|Trading]]), and the fine for stealing it (see [[Crime|Crime]]).
- The roadmap planned “use a coin pouch on yourself to empty its money onto you”; it has not been built (see [[Money#Not yet|Money]]).
- On the front page's portal, the “Inventory” entry uses the coin pouch's picture.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue.
- 2026-10-09: Its value of 20 began to matter: buying, selling and fines for theft are all reckoned from a thing's value.
