An **Oil Bucket** is a [[木桶|Bucket]] full of oil. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it; in the rules an empty bucket cannot be filled with oil either.

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

In the rules it works like the {{物品|一瓶灯油}}:

- **Pour oil**: left-click something (“Pour oil on …”): “Slick oil coats ….” It gets {{状态|油污}} for 90 seconds; the bucket is an empty Bucket again. Used on yourself or on water, the rules do not define it.
- **Flare-up**: an oiled thing flares up as soon as fire touches it: it catches fire itself, with a 70% chance of setting alight anything flammable within one cell. See [[元素#油|Oil]].

The Oil Bucket itself can catch fire too.
