**Raw Fish** is the commonest catch when fishing: once something bites and none of the other fish is landed, it is a Raw Fish. Eating it restores 2 [[饱食度|satiety]], and grilled it becomes [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]]. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] into the water; once something bites, the Dawnscale, Golden Carp, Soggy Boot, Catfish, Eel, Pike, Sunfish and Perch are checked in turn, and if none hits it is a Raw Fish: “A tug — you reel in a fish!” It can be caught at any time and in any water. In the daytime in shallow water, about a quarter of bites are a Raw Fish. See [[钓鱼#钓上什么|What you catch]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, restoring 2 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm; it is just less filling than grilled.
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]] (“Cook Raw Fish over campfire”), or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes Grilled Fish. Any fire that reaches it cooks it. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] buys it, 6 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue. Under the rules, fishing had a 35% chance of landing a Raw Fish, but the fishing rod could not be obtained in the game.
- 2026-10-08: Fishing rods can be made and bought and fishing became a pastime; Raw Fish became the catch when “nothing else was landed” after a bite, and the stall buys it.
- 2026-10-21: The controls settled on four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
