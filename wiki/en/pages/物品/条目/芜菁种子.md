**Turnip Seeds** are the seeds of the [[芜菁|turnip]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow up. A harvest gives seeds back, so the seeds multiply as you plant.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Garden Supplies**: the “Garden Supplies” crate beside the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] holds 2.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe turnip with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking it by hand gives 1. The turnip in the garden that is already ripe can be harvested at once.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sow**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the Turnip Seeds.” It becomes a turnip sprout.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Sow Turnip” on the “Farming” page; it is enough to have the seeds in your pack.
- **Growing**: each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later, if in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[农耕]] and [[作物]].

## History

- 2026-10-03: Added to the game, along with the Firstlight Village garden.
