A **Flask of Oil** is a glass flask holding lamp oil. The main quest [[The Vigil]] needs two, and [[The Mill Cellar|the Mill Cellar]] holds two flasks of “Vigil Oil”. Oil can also be splashed on things, so that they flare up as soon as fire touches them.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The Mill Cellar**: the cellar beneath [[Millbrook|Millbrook]] holds two flasks of **Vigil Oil**.
- **Tobin's cart**: [[Tobin]]'s cart in [[Firstlight Village]] has a flask, which is Tobin's. If he sees you take it, he says “Hey — that is mine!”

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **The vigil**: [[Sister Ilse]]'s main quest [[The Vigil]] needs two flasks of lamp oil, taken when you hand in the quest. Any flask of lamp oil counts.
- **Pour oil**: left-click something (“Pour oil on …”): “Slick oil coats ….” It gets {{status|oiled}} for 90 seconds; an [[Empty Flask|Empty Flask]] is left in your hand. Used on yourself or on water, the rules do not define it, and [[The World Learns|the world learns]] answers.
- **Flare-up**: an oiled thing flares up as soon as fire touches it: it catches fire itself, with a 70% chance of setting alight anything flammable within one cell. Oiled monsters too. See [[Elements#Oil|Oil]].
- The lamp oil itself can catch fire too.
