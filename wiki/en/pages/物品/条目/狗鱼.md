The **Pike** is a long fish full of teeth that bites only in deep water. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]], and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] pays 16 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] for each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[Bait|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- **Where**: only a cast into **deep water** can bring up a Pike. The game has deep water in two places, both on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]]:
  - The middle of the pond east of the planted sword (4 tiles). The pond lies beyond the first ring of mist; once the land has grown to level I in [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]], you can walk to the pond's west shore. A rod casts {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles, so from the west shore you can reach the middle.
  - The deep lake at [[The Lakeside|The Lakeside]]: a broad stretch of deep water in the middle of the lake. You can go there once you open the Lakeside when expanding the Held Ground (from land level II).
- Once you have learned {{skill|Deep Fisher}}, a Pike can be caught in any water.
- When none of the ones before it hit, there is a 45% chance it is a Pike.
- When you land it: “Long and toothy, from the deep — a pike!” The fish goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] → [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] → [[Soggy Boot|Soggy Boot]] → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → **Pike** → [[Sunfish|Sunfish]] → [[Perch|Perch]] → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- In the daytime without rain, about 39% of bites in deep water are a Pike (94% × 92% × 45%). At night the [[Eel|Eel]] is checked first, and in rain the [[Catfish|Catfish]], so there are fewer Pike.
- After a cast, that patch of water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another patch of water to keep fishing. See [[Fishing|Fishing]].
- The stall does not sell Pike; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Pike?” Confirm, and it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish grills into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 16 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.
