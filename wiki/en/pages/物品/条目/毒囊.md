The **Venom Sac** is the swollen venom gland of a [[cave spider|cave spider]]. The spider's bite leaves you [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|poisoned]], and the cure is made from this very thing.

## Source

{{sources}}

- [[cave spider|Cave spider]]: leaves 1 when felled.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- Together with 1 [[Herb|Herb]], it makes a flask of [[Antidote|Antidote]] with bare hands (see [[Crafting|Crafting]]).
- It can also be sold at a [[trading stall|trading stall]].

## History

- 2026-10-19: Added to the game as the cave spider's drop; the antidote is made from it.
