The **Venom Sac** is the swollen venom gland of a [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spider]]. The spider's bite leaves you [[状态#怪物的技能|poisoned]], and the cure is made from this very thing.

## Source

{{来源}}

- [[洞穴蜘蛛|Cave spider]]: leaves 1 when felled.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- Together with 1 [[草药|Herb]], it makes a flask of [[解毒剂|Antidote]] with bare hands (see [[制作|Crafting]]).
- It can also be sold at a [[货摊|trading stall]].

## History

- 2026-10-19: Added to the game as the cave spider's drop; the antidote is made from it.
