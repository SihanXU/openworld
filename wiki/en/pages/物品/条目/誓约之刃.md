**Oathbrand** is the [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s first [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arm]], a [[Sword|sword]] of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 10, and it drains 1 health with every hit; its stigma power “Oath Kept” triples the health drained while its holder is below 30% health.

## Source

{{sources}}

- When a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]] falls, if the one who felled them is a Warrior and Oathbrand has not yet appeared in this world, they leave Oathbrand behind, along with 4 {{item|Iron Ore}}.
- At present the only Seal-bearer you can fight in the game is [[The White Knight Alberic|Alberic, the White Knight]], so a Warrior who fells him on [[The East Road|the East Road]] gets Oathbrand. There is only one in each world.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Combat**: as with an ordinary sword, only a Warrior can fight with it, and all of the Warrior's attack talents apply to it.
- **Lifesteal**: drains 1 health with every hit.
- **Stigma power “Oath Kept”**: below 30% health, the health drained is tripled.
- It is made of metal, conducts electricity, and also carries the “holy” trait.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Warrior's Legendary Arm, left by a fallen Seal-bearer.
- 2026-10-07: The quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Oath Kept”.
