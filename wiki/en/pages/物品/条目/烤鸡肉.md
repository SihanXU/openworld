{{信息框}}
**Roast Chicken** is cooked [[生鸡肉|Raw Chicken]]; eating it restores 6 [[饱食度|satiety]], 4 more than raw chicken.

## Source

{{来源}}

Hold raw chicken and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{键|G}} facing the fire, and it roasts into Roast Chicken. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Roast Chicken?” Confirm, and it restores 6 satiety.

## History

- 2026-10-02: Added to the game, together with chickens.
- 2026-10-21: The controls settled on four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
