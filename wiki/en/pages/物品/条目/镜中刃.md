**Mirrorshard** is the [[刺客|Assassin]]'s second [[传说武器|Legendary Arm]], a [[匕首|dagger]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 7, and it drains 1 health with every hit; its stigma power “Stolen Face” drains 3 more on a critical hit.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, they leave the first of the Legendary Arms of their slayer's profession that has not yet appeared in this world. The Assassin's first is [[夜牙|Nightfang]], so Mirrorshard appears only when an Assassin fells a second Seal-bearer.
- At present the only Seal-bearer you can fight in the game is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]], so Mirrorshard cannot yet be obtained in normal play.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: only an Assassin can fight with it, and all of the Assassin's talents apply to it.
- **Lifesteal**: drains 1 health with every hit.
- **Stigma power “Stolen Face”**: drains 3 more health on a critical hit.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Assassin's second Legendary Arm.
- 2026-10-07: The quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Stolen Face”.
