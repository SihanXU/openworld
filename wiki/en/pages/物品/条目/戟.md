The **Halberd** is a polearm. Like the [[长矛|Spear]] it reaches 2 tiles, and its base attack of 5 is 1 higher than the Spear's. It belongs to no [[职业|profession]], so anyone can fight with it. None lies on any map and no quest gives one; once you have learned {{技能|重兵器}}, [[锻造|smith]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 3 iron ingots or steel bars plus 2 sticks (recipe level 4).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{键|空格}}, or left-click a monster within 2 tiles. An attack 2 tiles away can only go out along your own row or column. It may also carry an [[元素|element]].
- **Pierce**: like the Spear, the Halberd strikes with its point, and its edge is “pierce” (see the [[耐久与破坏#效率表|efficiency table]]). It is not a blunt weapon, so hitting a {{状态|冰冻}} target does not shatter the ice.
