The **Halberd** is a polearm. Like the [[Spear|Spear]] it reaches 2 tiles, and its base attack of 5 is 1 higher than the Spear's. It belongs to no [[Professions|profession]], so anyone can fight with it. None lies on any map and no quest gives one; once you have learned {{skill|Heavy Arms}}, [[Smithing|smith]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 3 iron ingots or steel bars plus 2 sticks (recipe level 4).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{key|Space}}, or left-click a monster within 2 tiles. An attack 2 tiles away can only go out along your own row or column. It may also carry an [[Elements|element]].
- **Pierce**: like the Spear, the Halberd strikes with its point, and its edge is “pierce” (see the [[Durability and Breaking#Efficiency table|efficiency table]]). It is not a blunt weapon, so hitting a {{status|frozen}} target does not shatter the ice.
