A **Shovel** is a tool for digging earth. It can dig a stump out by the roots, and it can dig a crop back up into a seed. No shovel is placed on any map, and it cannot be [[制作|crafted]]; you buy it on the “Farming” page of the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], 15 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] apiece.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip gives the shovel's use as “Digs up stumps and plants”.

- **Digging out a stump**: left-click a stump left by a felled tree: “You dig out the stump.” The stump is gone for good and will never grow back into a tree; it leaves 1 {{物品|树枝}}. See [[伐木#树桩|Stumps]].
- **Digging up a crop**: left-click a crop (at any stage): “You dig up …” The crop turns back into one of its seeds. See [[农耕]].
- Clicking anything else digs at it with the shovel: full effect on earth and plants, very poor on other materials (see [[耐久与破坏#效率表|the efficiency table]]).
