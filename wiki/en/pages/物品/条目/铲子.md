A **Shovel** is a tool for digging earth. It can dig a stump out by the roots, and it can dig a crop back up into a seed. No shovel is placed on any map, and it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]]; you buy it on the “Farming” page of the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], 15 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] apiece.

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip gives the shovel's use as “Digs up stumps and plants”.

- **Digging out a stump**: left-click a stump left by a felled tree: “You dig out the stump.” The stump is gone for good and will never grow back into a tree; it leaves 1 {{item|Stick}}. See [[Woodcutting#Stumps|Stumps]].
- **Digging up a crop**: left-click a crop (at any stage): “You dig up …” The crop turns back into one of its seeds. See [[Farming]].
- Clicking anything else digs at it with the shovel: full effect on earth and plants, very poor on other materials (see [[Durability and Breaking#Efficiency table|the efficiency table]]).
