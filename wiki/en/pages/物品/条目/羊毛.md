**Wool** is a tuft of soft wool. There are no sheep in the game, and you cannot get wool in normal play either: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about wool, and no recipe uses it. Wool burns.
