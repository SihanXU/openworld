A **Mace** is a blunt weapon that belongs to no [[Professions|profession]]: anyone can fight with it. None is placed on any map and no quest gives one; [[Smithing|smith]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 3 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars plus 1 Log (recipe level 2).

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{key|Space}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile. Base attack 4; it may also carry an [[Elements|element]].
- **Shattering frozen targets**: a blunt weapon that hits a creature that is {{status|frozen}} shatters the ice, and that blow deals ×1.5 damage.
