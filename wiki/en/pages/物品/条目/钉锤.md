A **Mace** is a blunt weapon that belongs to no [[职业|profession]]: anyone can fight with it. None is placed on any map and no quest gives one; [[锻造|smith]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 3 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars plus 1 Log (recipe level 2).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{键|空格}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile. Base attack 4; it may also carry an [[元素|element]].
- **Shattering frozen targets**: a blunt weapon that hits a creature that is {{状态|冰冻}} shatters the ice, and that blow deals ×1.5 damage.
