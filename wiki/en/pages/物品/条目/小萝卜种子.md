**Radish Seeds** are the seeds of the [[小萝卜|Radish]], bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]], 5 for 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow ripe. Harvesting gives the seeds back, so the more you plant, the more seeds you have.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Trading stall**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]] and press {{键|E}}, click them in the “Farming” column, and 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] buys 5, straight into the inventory. Away from the stall you cannot buy.
- **Harvest**: a ripe radish reaped with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds, picked by hand 1.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sow**: holding the seeds, right-click an empty cell of farmland: “You sow Radish Seeds.” It becomes a radish sprout. There is ready farmland southwest of the sword on the Held Ground; elsewhere a [[锄头|Hoe]] can turn diggable ground such as dirt and grass into farmland.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}}, and choose “Sow Radish” on the “Farming” page; the seeds only need to be in the inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering makes it grow one stage two game hours later in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[小萝卜（作物）]], [[农耕|Farming]] and [[作物|Crops]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]]; the seeds are bought in the stall's “Farming” column.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作|Controls]]).
