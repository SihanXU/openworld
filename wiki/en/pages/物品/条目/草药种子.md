**Herb Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Herb|herb]]. They can be sown on farmland and grow into herbs. No Herb Seeds are placed on any map, and no herbs are planted anywhere; you buy them at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sow**: hold them and right-click an empty tile of farmland; it becomes a herb sprout. Each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later, if in the open: sprout, herb seedling, ripe herb. See [[Farming]].
- **Harvest**: reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 herbs and 2 seeds; picking by hand gives 1 and 1.
- The “Farming” page of build mode has no herbs: the seeds have to be sown from the hand.
