A **Snowball** is a packed ball of snow. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it; nor can you pack a snowball from snowy ground.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about the snowball: it cannot be thrown, and it does not melt. It carries the tags “ice”, “cold” and “water”, but on an item these tags have no effect.
