A **Snowball** is a packed ball of snow. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it; nor can you pack a snowball from snowy ground.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the snowball: it cannot be thrown, and it does not melt. It carries the tags “ice”, “cold” and “water”, but on an item these tags have no effect.
