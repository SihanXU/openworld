A **Breastplate** is armor worn on the body, with a base defense of 3, the highest of the ordinary armor. The only one in the game is [[Rowan|Rowan]]'s old hauberk.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Quest**: when the main quest [[Lost in the Barrow]] is completed, [[Rowan|Rowan]] gives you **Rowan's Old Hauberk** (Fine, defense 4) and 2 Bread.
- **Smithing**: with {{skill|Plate Work}} learned, [[Smithing|smith]] one at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 5 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars (recipe level 4); with {{skill|Leatherworker}} learned you can also make one from leather at a [[workbench|workbench]]. The [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s set includes an “Oathguard Plate” (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- No breastplate is placed on any map.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Put on Breastplate”); use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. It takes the “armor” slot; see [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Dress someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and they put it on; it becomes theirs.
