A **Key** locks and unlocks chests. In the rules one key opens every lock; there is no such thing as one key to one lock. But you cannot get a key in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it; nor are there any locked chests on the maps.

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip says the key “Opens what is locked”. In the rules, holding a key and using it on a chest (any [[Chests and Containers|container]]):

- **Locked**: right-click it to unlock it, and the chest opens at the same time (“Unlock …”): “Click. It opens.”
- **Closed and not locked**: right-click it to lock it (“Lock …”): “Click. It is locked.” To open a chest that is not locked, press {{key|E}}.
- Pressing {{key|E}} empty-handed at a locked chest only says “Locked.”; an axe cannot break open a locked crate or chest either.
