A **Helmet** is armour worn on the head, with a base defense of 1 that varies with [[Substances|substance]] and [[Quality|quality]]. The game has two named helmets: one in [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]], one a reward from [[Fennick]]'s quest.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rusted Helm of the Thorn | Crude (iron) | Defense 1 | Lies in [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]] |
| Fennick's Squire Helm | [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] | Defense 4, evasion +10%, the vow “Shield” | The reward of the side quest [[The Old Templar]] |

**Smithing**: [[Smithing|forge]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] carrying a {{item|Smith Hammer}}: 2 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars (recipe level 2); with {{skill|Leatherworker}} learned it can also be made of leather beside a [[workbench|workbench]]. The [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s set includes a helmet, the “Oathguard Helm” (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]). [[Garrick]] and [[Fennick]] also sell helmets (see [[Trading|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Put on Helmet”); use it again to take it off. It can also be equipped in the [[Inventory|inventory]]. The head can wear only one thing; see [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Put it on someone else**: holding it, right-click someone, and it goes on them, theirs from then on; see [[Equipment#Dressing someone else|dressing others]].
- The helmet takes the same slot as the [[Hat|Hat]] and the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]].
