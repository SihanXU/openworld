A **Helmet** is armour worn on the head, with a base defense of 1 that varies with [[材质|substance]] and [[品质|quality]]. The game has two named helmets: one in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]], one a reward from [[芬尼克]]'s quest.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rusted Helm of the Thorn | Crude (iron) | Defense 1 | Lies in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]] |
| Fennick's Squire Helm | [[遗誓|Vowbound]] | Defense 4, evasion +10%, the vow “Shield” | The reward of the side quest [[老骑士]] |

**Smithing**: [[锻造|forge]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] carrying a {{物品|锻锤}}: 2 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars (recipe level 2); with {{技能|皮匠}} learned it can also be made of leather beside a [[工作台|workbench]]. The [[战士|Warrior]]'s set includes a helmet, the “Oathguard Helm” (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]). [[加里克]] and [[芬尼克]] also sell helmets (see [[交易|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Put on Helmet”); use it again to take it off. It can also be equipped in the [[背包|inventory]]. The head can wear only one thing; see [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Put it on someone else**: holding it, right-click someone, and it goes on them, theirs from then on; see [[装备#给别人穿|dressing others]].
- The helmet takes the same slot as the [[帽子|Hat]] and the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]].
