A **Herb** is an edible medicinal plant. It is the only food that restores [[Health|health]] directly when eaten: one herb restores 4 health and also 1 [[Satiety|satiety]]. The “dawnbloom” that opens facing east on [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]] is this herb, and [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]]'s vigil needs three.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Dawn Hill**: [[Dawn Hill|Dawn Hill]] has 4 **dawnbloom** placed on it; more herbs slowly grow in its grass and flowers, up to 3 at a time.
- **The wild**: each “Rolling Hills” map of [[The Wild|the Wild]] has 2 scattered on it; once picked, they do not grow back.
- Grow your own: buy [[Herb Seeds|Herb Seeds]] at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; sow them, water twice, and harvest herbs.
- The trading stall also buys herbs, 3 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Herb?” Confirm, and it restores 4 health and 1 satiety.
- **The vigil**: the main quest [[The Vigil]] asks for 3 dawnbloom (the objective “Pick dawnbloom on Dawn Hill”), taken when you turn the quest in. Herbs that later grow on Dawn Hill count too.
- Herbs burn.
