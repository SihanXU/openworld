A **Hoe** is the first tool of farming: hold it and left-click grass, dirt, sand or snow, and that tile is turned into farmland; only farmland can be sown ([[Farming|Farming]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Make it yourself**: press {{key|C}}; 2 {{item|Stone}}, 2 {{item|Stick}} and 1 {{item|Cut Grass}} make a **Stone Hoe**, with no [[workbench|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: the “Garden Supplies” crate beside the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds a **Steel Hoe** (attack 4), together with seeds, a bucket and a sickle.
- **Buy**: the “Farming” page of the trading stall at the Held Ground, 12 Dawnmarks.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Tilling**: left-click a tile of diggable ground (grass, dirt, sand, snow), and it becomes farmland. The tooltip gives the hoe's use as “Tills soil”.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}}; “Till Soil” on the “Farming” page needs a hoe in your pack, not in your hand. See [[Farming#Farming in Building mode|Farming in build mode]].
- Clicking anything else digs at it with the hoe: full effect on earth and plants, very poor on other materials.
