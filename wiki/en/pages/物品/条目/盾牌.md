{{信息框}}
The **Shield** is armor worn in the off hand; only the [[战士|Warrior]] can wear it. It adds defense. A **Wooden Shield** can be made from planks at a [[工作台|workbench]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make one yourself**: stand by a workbench and press {{键|C}}: 3 {{物品|木板}} and 1 {{物品|树枝}} make a Wooden Shield.
- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose the [[战士|Warrior]] with [[布拉姆|Bram]], you receive a **Wooden Shield** (defense 2) along with an iron [[剑|Sword]]; if your off hand is empty, it is put on at once.
- The Warrior's second [[传说武器|legendary weapon]], [[不陷之壁|The Unfallen Wall]], is also a shield.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Worn in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it to the off hand from the [[背包|inventory]]. The tooltip reads “In the off hand: +N defense”.
- **Warriors only**: anyone of another profession, or who has not chosen a profession yet, who tries to wear it is told “Only a Warrior can wear the Wooden Shield.” When you change to another profession, a shield you are wearing is taken off by itself and stays in your inventory.
- An ordinary shield only adds defense, with no blocking; only The Unfallen Wall has a block chance.
- The shield is made of wood and can catch fire.
