**Rope** is a length of sturdy rope. It has one important use at [[The Ford|The Ford]]: the storm snapped the ferry's line, and once a new length is spliced onto the ferry winch, [[Hesper|Hesper]] can take you across the river.

## Source

{{sources}}

A Rope lies in the chest in the camp at [[The Ford|The Ford]]; it belongs to no one, and you can simply take it.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Mending the ferry line**: hold the Rope and press {{key|G}} facing the broken ferry winch, or left-click it (“Mend the ferry winch with Rope”): “You splice the rope onto the drum. The line runs taut across the water again.” The Rope is used up. Then press {{key|E}} facing Hesper with empty hands (“Ask Hesper for the crossing”), and she poles you to the far bank.
- Before it is mended, looking at the winch with {{key|E}} and empty hands tells you the broken line needs a length of sturdy rope.
- **Tying it to things**: hold the Rope and left-click a fixture, and the rule is that the rope is handed to it: a chest puts the rope inside, a tabletop sets it on top, and anything else has it laid on its tile.
- **Binding creatures**: hold the Rope and left-click a monster or animal (“Bind green slime with Rope”): “The green slime is lashed down with Rope.” It cannot move or attack for 3 seconds. Binding uses the {{status|frozen}} status, with a shell of ice drawn over it; a blunt weapon then deals × 1.5 damage to it, and fire also sets it loose. The Rope stays on it and drops where it falls when it is felled. What happens if you hand it over again to one already frozen or bound is not in the rules; bosses are not affected (the rules do not say). See [[Monsters#Handing things to creatures|Handing things to creatures]].
- What happens when you hold the Rope and left-click a **person** is not in the rules (“Use Rope on so-and-so” with a “?”). Pressing {{key|G}} facing a creature does nothing: {{key|G}} can only give things to people.
- Give the Rope to Hesper herself and she says, “Not to me. To the winch.”
- Rope catches fire.

## Trivia

- Until the winch is mended, the Ford always has a rope. If this rope is burned or lost, Hesper says, “That was my last good rope. … No — there is the old one off the raft. Frayed, but it will splice.” and brings out a new one.
- Mending the ferry line is one of four ways across the river; the other three are laying a bridge, felling the dead tree and freezing the river. See [[The Ford|The Ford]].
- Bind a slime with the Ford's rope and the rope stays on the slime: the Ford still counts as having a rope, so Hesper will not bring out a new one, and you have to fell that slime to get it back.
