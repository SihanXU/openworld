The **Pumpkin** is a gourd you can grow; eating it restores 6 [[饱食度|satiety]], tied with the [[甜瓜|Melon]] as the most filling of the fourteen vegetables.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Firstlight's garden**: the garden in [[初原村]] has a row of pumpkins planted, and the bottom one is already ripe, ready to harvest.
- **Grow your own**: sow [[南瓜种子|Pumpkin Seeds]] on farmland; watered twice, it grows ripe; reaped with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] it gives 2 pumpkins and 2 seeds, picked by hand 1 pumpkin and 1 seed. See [[农耕|Farming]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Pumpkin?” Confirm, and it restores 6 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{键|G}}.
