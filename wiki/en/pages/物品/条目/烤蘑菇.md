The **Roasted Mushroom** is a cooked [[Mushroom|Mushroom]]; eating it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]], four times as much as a raw one.

## Source

{{sources}}

Hold a mushroom and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{key|G}} facing the fire, and it roasts into a Roasted Mushroom. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Roasted Mushroom?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety.
