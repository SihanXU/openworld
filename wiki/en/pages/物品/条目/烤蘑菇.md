The **Roasted Mushroom** is a cooked [[蘑菇|Mushroom]]; eating it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]], four times as much as a raw one.

## Source

{{来源}}

Hold a mushroom and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{键|G}} facing the fire, and it roasts into a Roasted Mushroom. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Roasted Mushroom?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety.
