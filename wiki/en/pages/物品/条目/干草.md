**Cut Grass** is a handful of grass that has been cut, to be picked up off the ground anywhere outdoors. It is the material for binding things: the Stone Axe, Stone Pickaxe, torch and bucket each need one, the Wooden Bow three, and the straw mattress of a [[bed|bed]] two.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Picked up off the ground**: cut grass keeps growing on the grass and dry grass of most outdoor maps, at most 6 at a time. See [[Foraging|Foraging]].
- It grows on the maps of [[The Wild|the Wild]] too.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Crafting**: the Stone Axe, Stone Pickaxe, Stone Hoe, Stone Sickle, torch, bucket, Wooden Bow and bed all need cut grass; see the “Made into” row above and [[Crafting|Crafting]].
- **Burning**: cut grass catches fire, and the tooltip says “Flammable”. Thrown into a lit fire, it burns to [[Ash|Ash]].
- **Binding creatures**: cut grass is fiber, and works like the {{item|Rope}}: holding it, right-click a monster or animal (“Bind green slime with Cut Grass”), and it is bound for 3 seconds ({{status|frozen}}); the handful of grass stays on it and drops when it is felled. Bosses are not caught by this. See [[Monsters#Handing things to creatures|handing things to creatures]].
- **Not feed**: clicking a cow or chicken with cut grass does not feed it, it binds it. Animals are fed with [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] or [[Wheat|Wheat]] (see [[Animals#Feeding|feeding]]).
