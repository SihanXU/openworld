**Gold Ore** is a lump of ore that holds gold. You get it by breaking open a [[gold vein|gold vein]] with a {{item|Pickaxe}}, 2 per vein. Gold veins are in the chamber at the very bottom of [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]], and there is one in [[The Quarry|the Quarry]] of [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] as well. The third stage of land in [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3 Gold Ore. Hold it and right-click a lit [[forge|forge]], and it is smelted into a {{item|Gold Ingot}}.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Breaking gold veins**: there are 2 at the bottom of the Old Mine, each dropping 2 when broken; once they are spent, a new one grows back in the mine every 6 game hours (2 at most).
- **The Quarry**: once you open up [[The Quarry|the Quarry]] by [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]], there is a gold vein to its north that gives 2 when broken. This one does not grow back once spent.
- No monster carries it, and it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smelting**: hold the gold ore and right-click a lit forge (the smithies in [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] and [[Emberfall|Emberfall]] each have one; see [[Smithing|Smithing]]) (“Smelt Gold Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The lump in your hand becomes a {{item|Gold Ingot}}. When the forge has been put out, you only get the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.” See [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]].
- **Expanding the Held Ground**: hand it to [[Alberic|Alberic]] to go into the ground's stock; the third stage of land, “The Old Bounds”, needs 3 (plus 30 Planks and 30 Stones).
- A gold ingot is worth 75 and a lump of gold ore 24, so selling it to a merchant smelted is far better (see [[Trading|Trading]]). But the expansion asks for the ore itself, so keep 3 back before you smelt.
