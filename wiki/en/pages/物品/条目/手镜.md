The **Hand Mirror** is a small mirror you can hold in your hand. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the Hand Mirror: it carries the “reflective” tag, but it shows nothing, and it cannot reflect light or spells. Its tooltip says it is “Fragile”, but under the rules items do not break when dropped.
