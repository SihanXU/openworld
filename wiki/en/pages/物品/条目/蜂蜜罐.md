A **Honey Jar** is a clay jar of honey; eating it restores 6 [[Satiety|satiety]]. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Honey Jar?” Confirm, and it restores 6 satiety; the jar goes with it.

## Trivia

- The game has a fixture called the “beehive”, but no rule lets you take honey from it.
