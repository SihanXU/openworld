A **Gem** is a crystal stone. It can carry an [[Elements|element]], which adds an element word to its name (“Gem of Embers”, for example). It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rule about gems: a gem cannot be set into anything, and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]] does not buy gems, so they cannot be sold (see [[Money|Money]]). The tooltip calls it “Precious”.

## Trivia

- In the [[Codex|Codex]] the gem is a category of its own, “Gems”, with only itself in it.
