A **Gem** is a crystal stone. It can carry an [[元素|element]], which adds an element word to its name (“Gem of Embers”, for example). It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rule about gems: a gem cannot be set into anything, and the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]] does not buy gems, so they cannot be sold (see [[金钱|Money]]). The tooltip calls it “Precious”.

## Trivia

- In the [[图鉴|Codex]] the gem is a category of its own, “Gems”, with only itself in it.
