**Coal** is a mineral fuel. [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]'s smith [[Garrick|Garrick]] needs 3 Coal to forge [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Coal seams**: [[The Old Mine|The Old Mine]] has 4 coal seams; break one with a [[Pickaxe|Pickaxe]] and it leaves 2 Coal. The seams slowly grow back. See [[Mining|Mining]].
- **Charred wood**: left-click a tree charred by fire with an [[Axe|Axe]]: “The charred wood crumbles to coal.” You get 1.
- A blackened wooden wall also leaves Coal when broken.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Forging the sword**: in the Emberfall line, hold Coal and press {{key|G}} facing the smith Garrick to hand it over, one at a time; once you have handed over 3, and then 5 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]], press {{key|E}} facing him with empty hands, and he forges [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]]. The Emberfall and dragon line is the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon|Emberfall and the Dragon]].
- **Burning**: Coal catches fire; the tooltip says “Flammable”. Thrown into a lit fire, it burns to [[Ash|Ash]].
