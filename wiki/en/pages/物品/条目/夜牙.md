**Nightfang** is the first of the [[Assassin|Assassin]]'s [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]], a [[Dagger|Dagger]] of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 8, with a built-in 15% critical chance; its stigma power, “Tooth of the Night”, gives its holder a further +25% critical chance after nightfall.

## Source

{{sources}}

- When a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]] falls, if whoever felled him is an Assassin and no Nightfang has yet appeared in the world, he leaves Nightfang, plus 4 {{item|Iron Ore}}.
- The only Seal-bearer that can be fought in the game at present is [[The White Knight Alberic]], so an Assassin who defeats him on [[The East Road|the East Road]] receives Nightfang. There is only one in each world.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fighting**: like an ordinary dagger, only an Assassin can fight with it, and all the Assassin's talents work with it.
- **Critical hits**: the weapon has a built-in +15% critical chance.
- **Stigma power “Tooth of the Night”**: +25% critical chance after nightfall. For what hours count as night see [[Day and Night|Day and Night]]; as the Seal-bearers fall, the nights grow longer.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Assassin's Legendary weapon, left by a fallen Seal-bearer.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Tooth of the Night”.
