**Nightfang** is the first of the [[刺客|Assassin]]'s [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]], a [[匕首|Dagger]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 8, with a built-in 15% critical chance; its stigma power, “Tooth of the Night”, gives its holder a further +25% critical chance after nightfall.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, if whoever felled him is an Assassin and no Nightfang has yet appeared in the world, he leaves Nightfang, plus 4 {{物品|铁矿石}}.
- The only Seal-bearer that can be fought in the game at present is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], so an Assassin who defeats him on [[东大路|the East Road]] receives Nightfang. There is only one in each world.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fighting**: like an ordinary dagger, only an Assassin can fight with it, and all the Assassin's talents work with it.
- **Critical hits**: the weapon has a built-in +15% critical chance.
- **Stigma power “Tooth of the Night”**: +25% critical chance after nightfall. For what hours count as night see [[昼夜|Day and Night]]; as the Seal-bearers fall, the nights grow longer.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Assassin's Legendary weapon, left by a fallen Seal-bearer.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Tooth of the Night”.
