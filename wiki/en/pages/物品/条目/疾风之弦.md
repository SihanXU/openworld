**Galestring** is the [[游侠|Ranger]]'s first [[传说武器|legendary weapon]], a [[弓|Bow]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 8, it shoots 7 tiles, it carries the wind [[元素|element]], and its attack speed is +20%; its stigma power “Running Wind” gives +30% movement speed for 2 seconds after every hit.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, if the one who felled them is a Ranger and no Galestring has yet appeared in this world, they leave Galestring behind, along with 4 {{物品|铁矿石}}.
- For now the only Seal-bearer you can fight in the game is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|The White Knight Alberic]], so a Ranger who fells him on [[东大路|The East Road]] gets Galestring. There is only one in each world.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: like an ordinary bow, only a Ranger can fight with it, and all of the Ranger's talents apply to it; {{技能|箭术}} gives it another +1 range.
- **Wind element**: a creature it hits is blown back 2 tiles; when it hits something {{状态|燃烧}}, the wind carries the fire to the tiles around. See [[元素|Elements]].
- **Attack speed**: +20%.
- **Stigma power “Running Wind”**: every hit gives +30% movement speed for 2 seconds.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Ranger's legendary weapon, left by a fallen Seal-bearer.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Running Wind”.
