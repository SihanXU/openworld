**Raw Chicken** is the meat a felled [[鸡|chicken]] leaves. Eaten, it restores 2 [[饱食度|satiety]]; roasted, it becomes [[烤鸡肉|Roast Chicken]], which restores 6.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Chickens**: there are chickens on [[草原|The Grassland]], [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] and the open plains of [[荒野|the Wild]]. A felled chicken leaves 1 Raw Chicken, which falls to the ground; press {{键|E}} to pick it up. A living chicken lays an [[鸡蛋|Egg]] every 3 game hours; see [[动物|Animals]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, restoring 2 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes Roast Chicken. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
