**Raw Chicken** is the meat a felled [[chicken|chicken]] leaves. Eaten, it restores 2 [[Satiety|satiety]]; roasted, it becomes [[Roast Chicken|Roast Chicken]], which restores 6.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Chickens**: there are chickens on [[The Grassland|The Grassland]], [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] and the open plains of [[The Wild|the Wild]]. A felled chicken leaves 1 Raw Chicken, which falls to the ground; press {{key|E}} to pick it up. A living chicken lays an [[Egg|Egg]] every 3 game hours; see [[Animals|Animals]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, restoring 2 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes Roast Chicken. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
