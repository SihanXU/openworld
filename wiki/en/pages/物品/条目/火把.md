{{信息框}}
The **Torch** is a tool for lighting fires: 1 {{物品|树枝}} and 1 tuft of {{物品|干草}} make one anywhere. A newly built [[营火|campfire]] is unlit; it lights only when you left-click it with a torch. A torch can also set other things that burn alight.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make one yourself**: press {{键|C}}: 1 stick and 1 tuft of cut grass make a **Wooden Torch**, with no [[工作台|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: a torch lies in the camp at [[渡口|The Ford]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip says the Torch “Sets things alight”. Hold it and left-click:

- **Things that can be lit**: a [[营火|campfire]], brazier, lamp post, candlestick, lantern post, fireplace, forge or [[坩埚|cauldron]]; once lit, they do not go out by themselves; see [[营火#点火和灭火|Lighting and putting out]]. Lighting one that is already lit changes nothing.
- **Things that burn**: dry grass, trees, wooden walls, wooden chests, haystacks… catch fire, and the fire spreads.
- **Things that explode**: a [[爆竹|Firecracker]] or a [[黑火药炸弹|Blackpowder Bomb]] goes off the moment it is lit, and anyone standing right next to it is caught in the blast.
- **Monsters**: it sets them {{状态|燃烧}} for a while, losing health as they burn; fire monsters instead recover 2 health.
- Wet things are dried, frozen things thaw, ice melts into water, and things doused in oil flare up. See [[元素|Elements]].

The torch is not used up. It cannot be used on yourself.

While you hold it, a ring of light shines around the player; this is only a visual effect: monsters keep away from lit fires and things that are burning, not from the torch in your hand. To get through the night, stay by a lit campfire.

## Trivia

- The torch itself is made of wood and can catch fire; throw it into a fire and it burns to {{物品|灰烬}}.
- In the [[新手教程|tutorial]], the fifth objective of the main quest [[醒来|Waking]] is “Build a campfire and light it”: B builds the campfire, C makes a torch, and you left-click the campfire with the torch in hand.
