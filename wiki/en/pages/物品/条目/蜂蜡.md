**Wax** is a lump of beeswax. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about wax: it cannot be made into a [[Candle|Candle]]. Wax burns.
