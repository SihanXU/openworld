**Rimecrown** is the [[法师|Mage]]'s second [[传说武器|Legendary Arm]], a [[法杖|staff]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 10, it reaches 6 tiles, it carries the ice [[元素|element]], and it has a built-in 10% critical chance; its stigma power “Crown of Rime” gives each hit a 20% chance of briefly freezing the target.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, they leave the first of the Legendary Arms of their slayer's profession that has not yet appeared in this world. The Mage's first is [[烬心法杖|Emberheart]], so Rimecrown appears only when a Mage fells a second Seal-bearer.
- At present the only Seal-bearer you can fight in the game is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]], so Rimecrown cannot yet be obtained in normal play.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Casting**: only a Mage can cast with it, and all of the Mage's talents apply to it.
- **Ice element**: hitting a {{状态|潮湿}} target freezes it; hitting shallow water turns it to ice. Ice monsters hit by the ice element instead recover 2 health. See [[元素|Elements]].
- **Critical hits**: the weapon has a built-in +10% critical chance.
- **Stigma power “Crown of Rime”**: each hit has a 20% chance to make the target {{状态|冰冻}} for 1 second. A frozen target hit again by a blunt weapon or the earth element shatters the ice and takes half again as much damage (see [[元素|Elements]]).

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Mage's second Legendary Arm.
- 2026-10-07: The quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Crown of Rime”.
