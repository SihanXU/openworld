{{信息框}}
A **Steel Bar** is refined steel. Only a smith who has learned {{技能|炼钢术}} can make it: at a [[熔炉|forge]], 3 {{物品|铁矿石}} and 3 {{物品|煤炭}} make 2 bars.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smelting**: the [[锻造|smithing]] recipe “Steel Bar” at the forge, which needs {{技能|炼钢术}}, from level 3. {{技能|高效冶炼}} doubles what comes out.
- Clicking a forge with iron ore directly only smelts an iron ingot, never steel.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Smithing**: at an [[铁砧|anvil]], with a smith hammer on you; every weapon, armor and tool has a steel recipe, and what you make is of steel, harder than iron (see [[材质|Substances]]).
- **Sell**: worth 25.
