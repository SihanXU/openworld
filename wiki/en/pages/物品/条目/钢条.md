A **Steel Bar** is refined steel. Only a smith who has learned {{skill|Steelworking}} can make it: at a [[forge|forge]], 3 {{item|Iron Ore}} and 3 {{item|Coal}} make 2 bars.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smelting**: the [[Smithing|smithing]] recipe “Steel Bar” at the forge, which needs {{skill|Steelworking}}, from level 3. {{skill|Efficient Smelting}} doubles what comes out.
- Clicking a forge with iron ore directly only smelts an iron ingot, never steel.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smithing**: at an [[anvil|anvil]], with a smith hammer on you; every weapon, armor and tool has a steel recipe, and what you make is of steel, harder than iron (see [[Substances|Substances]]).
- **Sell**: worth 25.
