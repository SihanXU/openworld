A **Gold Ingot** is a bar of refined gold. Hold {{item|Gold Ore}} and right-click a lit [[forge|forge]], and the ore in your hand melts into a gold ingot. It is very valuable, and it is the material for [[Smithing|smithing]] jewelry and the [[Gear Sets|profession sets]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smelting**: hold gold ore and right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Gold Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The lump of gold ore in your hand becomes a gold ingot. One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting. See [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]].
- **Smelting in batches**: once you have learned {{skill|Goldsmith}}, stand by a forge and press {{key|C}}: 2 Gold Ore plus 1 Coal make 2 gold ingots. See [[Smithing#Smelting (forge)|Smelting]].
- Gold ore comes from the [[gold vein|gold veins]] at the bottom of [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]] and in the Held Ground's [[The Quarry|quarry]]; lit forges stand in the smithies of [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] and [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]. When the forge has been put out, you only get the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”; light it first with a {{item|Torch}}.
- Only a forge can smelt it: clicking another fire with gold ore is not covered by the rules (the message carries a “?”). Pressing {{key|G}} at the forge only puts the ore down beside it.
- No ready-made gold ingots are placed on any map, and no one carries one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smithing**: at an [[anvil|anvil]], with a smith hammer on you, hammer it into weapons, armor, tools or jewelry by the [[Smithing|smithing]] recipes; what you make is of this metal.
- **Sell**: a gold ingot is worth 75: the ingot itself is worth 15, and gold's value multiplier is 5 (see [[Substances|Substances]]). A lump of gold ore is worth only 24, so smelting before selling is far better. A merchant gives about 37 {{item|Dawnmark}} for it, up or down depending on what he thinks of you (see [[Trading|Trading]]). The [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] does not buy it.
- A gold ingot is a conductive metal and does not burn.

## Trivia

- The gold ingot's icon is the same picture as the gold ore's.
- Smelted ore is no longer ore: the third stage of land in [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3 Gold Ore, so keep enough back before you smelt.

## History

- 2026-10-09: Added to the game: a lit forge smelts gold ore into a gold ingot.
- 2026-10-10: It can be hammered into gear on an anvil; the forge can also smelt in batches by recipe.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
