A **Gold Ingot** is a bar of refined gold. Hold {{物品|金矿石}} and right-click a lit [[熔炉|forge]], and the ore in your hand melts into a gold ingot. It is very valuable, and it is the material for [[锻造|smithing]] jewelry and the [[套装|profession sets]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smelting**: hold gold ore and right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Gold Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The lump of gold ore in your hand becomes a gold ingot. One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting. See [[熔炉#熔炼|Smelting]].
- **Smelting in batches**: once you have learned {{技能|金匠}}, stand by a forge and press {{键|C}}: 2 Gold Ore plus 1 Coal make 2 gold ingots. See [[锻造#冶炼（熔炉）|Smelting]].
- Gold ore comes from the [[金矿脉|gold veins]] at the bottom of [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]] and in the Held Ground's [[采石场|quarry]]; lit forges stand in the smithies of [[初原村]], [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] and [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]. When the forge has been put out, you only get the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”; light it first with a {{物品|火把}}.
- Only a forge can smelt it: clicking another fire with gold ore is not covered by the rules (the message carries a “?”). Pressing {{键|G}} at the forge only puts the ore down beside it.
- No ready-made gold ingots are placed on any map, and no one carries one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Smithing**: at an [[铁砧|anvil]], with a smith hammer on you, hammer it into weapons, armor, tools or jewelry by the [[锻造|smithing]] recipes; what you make is of this metal.
- **Sell**: a gold ingot is worth 75: the ingot itself is worth 15, and gold's value multiplier is 5 (see [[材质|Substances]]). A lump of gold ore is worth only 24, so smelting before selling is far better. A merchant gives about 37 {{物品|晨辉币}} for it, up or down depending on what he thinks of you (see [[交易|Trading]]). The [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] does not buy it.
- A gold ingot is a conductive metal and does not burn.

## Trivia

- The gold ingot's icon is the same picture as the gold ore's.
- Smelted ore is no longer ore: the third stage of land in [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3 Gold Ore, so keep enough back before you smelt.
