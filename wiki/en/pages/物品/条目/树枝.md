{{信息框}}
The **Stick** is the most common material: it can be picked up anywhere on the ground outdoors, and new ones slowly grow back once picked. The first stone [[斧|Axe]], [[火把|torches]], the various stone tools, [[营火|campfires]] and [[栅栏|fences]] all need it.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Picked up from the ground**: on most outdoor maps, sticks keep growing on the grass, up to 6 at a time, 10 in the [[森林|forest]]. See [[采集|Foraging]].
- **Felling trees**: a fallen tree leaves 1 stick. A broken bush also leaves sticks.
- **The Wild**: sticks also lie scattered on the maps of [[荒野|the Wild]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Crafting**: the stone axe, stone pickaxe, stone hoe, stone sickle, stone smith hammer, torch, wooden bow and wooden shield all need sticks; see the “Makes” row above and [[制作|Crafting]].
- **Building a campfire**: the “Campfire” in building mode ({{键|B}}) takes 2 stones and 2 sticks. You can also build one by hand: stack two stones on the ground into a stone ring, then lay two sticks on the ring. See [[营火|campfire]].
- **Building a fence**: lay a stick on another stick on the ground to stand them up as stakes, and strike them with a [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]] to make a length of [[栅栏|fence]].
- **Building a sign**: lay a plank on a stick on the ground to stand it up as a sign post, and strike it with the Smith Hammer to make a [[告示牌|sign]].
- **Fire by friction**: once you have learned {{技能|钻木取火}}, hold an ordinary stick and right-click a campfire, brazier or candlestick that is unlit and not wet: “You work the stick until … catches.”
- **Burning**: sticks catch fire; the tooltip says “Flammable”. Thrown into a lit fire, a stick burns to [[灰烬|Ash]], and ash can fertilize crops.
