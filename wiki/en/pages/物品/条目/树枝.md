The **Stick** is the most common material: it can be picked up anywhere on the ground outdoors, and new ones slowly grow back once picked. The first stone [[Axe|Axe]], [[Torch|torches]], the various stone tools, [[campfire|campfires]] and [[fence|fences]] all need it.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Picked up from the ground**: on most outdoor maps, sticks keep growing on the grass, up to 6 at a time, 10 in the [[The Forest|forest]]. See [[Foraging|Foraging]].
- **Felling trees**: a fallen tree leaves 1 stick. A broken bush also leaves sticks.
- **The Wild**: sticks also lie scattered on the maps of [[The Wild|the Wild]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Crafting**: the stone axe, stone pickaxe, stone hoe, stone sickle, stone smith hammer, torch, wooden bow and wooden shield all need sticks; see the “Makes” row above and [[Crafting|Crafting]].
- **Building a campfire**: the “Campfire” in building mode ({{key|B}}) takes 2 stones and 2 sticks. You can also build one by hand: stack two stones on the ground into a stone ring, then lay two sticks on the ring. See [[campfire|campfire]].
- **Building a fence**: lay a stick on another stick on the ground to stand them up as stakes, and strike them with a [[Smith Hammer|Smith Hammer]] to make a length of [[fence|fence]].
- **Building a sign**: lay a plank on a stick on the ground to stand it up as a sign post, and strike it with the Smith Hammer to make a [[sign|sign]].
- **Fire by friction**: once you have learned {{skill|Firemaker}}, hold an ordinary stick and right-click a campfire, brazier or candlestick that is unlit and not wet: “You work the stick until … catches.”
- **Burning**: sticks catch fire; the tooltip says “Flammable”. Thrown into a lit fire, a stick burns to [[Ash|Ash]], and ash can fertilize crops.
