**Carrot Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Carrot|carrot]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow up. A harvest gives seeds back, so the seeds multiply as you plant.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Garden Supplies**: the “Garden Supplies” crate beside the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds 2.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe carrot with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking it by hand gives 1. The carrot in the garden that is already ripe can be harvested at once.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sow**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland that has been [[Hoe|hoed]]: “You sow the Carrot Seeds.” It becomes a carrot sprout.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Sow Carrot” on the “Farming” page; it is enough to have the seeds in your pack.
- **Growing**: each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later, if in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[Farming]] and [[Crops]].
