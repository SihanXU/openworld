A **Water Bucket** is a [[Bucket|Bucket]] full of water. Farming cannot do without it: a crop grows one stage only for each watering. It can also put out fires, wet things, or simply be drunk. Use it once, and the bucket is empty.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Drawing water**: holding an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]], left-click shallow water, deep water, a well, a fountain or a trough.
- **Ready-made**: the “Garden Supplies” box by the garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds a Water Bucket.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Water**: left-click a crop that is still growing and has not been watered (“Water …”). The crop gets {{status|watered}}, and in the open it grows a stage two game hours later. See [[Farming|Farming]].
- **Splash**: left-click anything else (“Splash …”): “You pour water on ….” The water acts on it: something {{status|burning}} is put out, a lit campfire or brazier goes out, and anything else becomes {{status|wet}}. Fire monsters splashed with it are hurt. See [[Elements#Water|Water]].
- **Drink**: a bucket is a tool, and its left click draws, waters and splashes; to drink, hold it and right-click yourself; you are asked “Drink Water Bucket?” Confirm, and it restores 2 health: “Refreshing.”
- Whichever way it is used, once the water is gone an empty bucket is left in your hand, ready to be filled again.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}}; “Water” on the “Farming” page needs a container full of water in the inventory.

## Trivia

- A Water Bucket waters only one crop. If you plant a lot, break ground by the water.
