**Iron Ore** is the ore you get by breaking open an iron vein. Hold it and right-click a lit [[forge|forge]], and it is smelted into an {{item|Iron Ingot}}; but the people who want iron all take the ore itself: the smith [[Garrick|Garrick]] in [[Emberfall|Emberfall]], and [[Alberic|Alberic]] at [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Iron veins**: [[The Old Mine|The Old Mine]] has 4 iron veins, there are 3 beyond the third ring of mist at [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], and the Held Ground's [[The Quarry|quarry]] has 3 as well. Breaking one with a [[Pickaxe|Pickaxe]] leaves 2 Iron Ore. Only the Old Mine's veins slowly grow back. See [[Mining|Mining]].
- **Buy**: Firstlight Village's smith [[Haldor|Haldor]] sells iron ore (see [[Trading|Trading]]).
- **Cyclops**: the [[cyclops|cyclops]] deep in the Old Mine carries 2, which drop to the ground when it is felled.
- **Seal-bearers**: fell a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]] and they leave 4 Iron Ore.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Forging a sword**: in the Emberfall line, hold iron ore and press {{key|G}} at the smith Garrick to hand it over one at a time; once you have given 5, and then 3 [[Coal|Coal]], press {{key|E}} at him empty-handed and he forges [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]].
- **[[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]**: the second stage needs 5 Iron Ore and 20 Planks.
- **Smelting**: hold iron ore and right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Iron Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The lump in your hand becomes an {{item|Iron Ingot}}. When the forge has been put out, you only get the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.” See [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]]. An iron ingot is worth 15, more than the ore (8), and is also a material for [[Smithing|smithing]]; but Garrick and the expansion want the ore, so keep enough back before you smelt.
