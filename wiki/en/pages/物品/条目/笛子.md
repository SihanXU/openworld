The **Flute** is a flute made of wood, a musical instrument. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about the Flute, and none about musical instruments: it makes no sound. The Flute catches fire.
