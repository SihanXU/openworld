{{信息框}}
The **Tomato** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 2 [[饱食度|satiety]]. The tomato is perennial: after reaping, the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Firstlight's vegetable garden**: a row of tomatoes grows in the vegetable garden of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]]; the bottom plant is already ripe and ready to harvest.
- **Grow your own**: sow [[番茄种子|Tomato Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice to grow; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 tomatoes and 2 seeds, and picking by hand gives 1 and 1. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[农耕|Farming]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Tomato?” Confirm, and it restores 2 satiety. It cannot be grilled.
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
