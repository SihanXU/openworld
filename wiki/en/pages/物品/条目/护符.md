An **Amulet** is a piece of jewelry worn around the neck, with a base defense of 1. Both amulets in the game come from [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]]'s quests.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amulet of the Five Wounds | Masterwork | Defense 2 | Reward of the side quest [[朝圣之路|The Pilgrim's Road]] |
| Cassian's Prayer Cord | [[遗誓|Vowbound]] | Defense 3, night defense +1, vow “Dawn” | Reward of the side quest [[上一位朝圣者|The Pilgrim Before]] |

**Smithing**: once you have learned {{技能|珠宝匠}}, [[锻造|smith]] one at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: an iron ingot or a gold ingot makes an amulet. Each of the four professions' sets includes a necklace (Shadowfang Pendant, Oathguard Gorget, Windrunner Token, Starweave Amulet), part of that set's jewelry set (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. It takes the “necklace” slot, so you can wear either it or the [[朝圣者的扇贝|Pilgrim's Scallop]], not both. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Night defense**: Cassian's Prayer Cord adds 1 more defense after nightfall.
