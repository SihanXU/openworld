An **Amulet** is a piece of jewelry worn around the neck, with a base defense of 1. Both amulets in the game come from [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]]'s quests.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amulet of the Five Wounds | Masterwork | Defense 2 | Reward of the side quest [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)|The Pilgrim's Road]] |
| Cassian's Prayer Cord | [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] | Defense 3, night defense +1, vow “Dawn” | Reward of the side quest [[The Pilgrim Before|The Pilgrim Before]] |

**Smithing**: once you have learned {{skill|Jeweler}}, [[Smithing|smith]] one at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: an iron ingot or a gold ingot makes an amulet. Each of the four professions' sets includes a necklace (Shadowfang Pendant, Oathguard Gorget, Windrunner Token, Starweave Amulet), part of that set's jewelry set (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. It takes the “necklace” slot, so you can wear either it or the [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]], not both. See [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Night defense**: Cassian's Prayer Cord adds 1 more defense after nightfall.
