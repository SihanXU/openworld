An **Antidote** is a flask of green potion. Drinking it cures [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|poison]] and restores 4 [[Health|health]]. It is [[Crafting|crafted]] by hand from 1 [[Venom Sac|Venom Sac]] (dropped by the [[cave spider|cave spider]]) and 1 [[Herb|Herb]].

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it cures poison and restores 4 health.
- **Give someone a drink**: hold it and right-click someone; they are cured and recover 4 health.

## Trivia

- One bite in four from a [[cave spider|cave spider]] poisons you, and the antidote is made from that very spider's venom sac.
