An **Antidote** is a flask of green potion. Drinking it cures [[状态#怪物的技能|poison]] and restores 4 [[生命值|health]]. It is [[制作|crafted]] by hand from 1 [[毒囊|Venom Sac]] (dropped by the [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spider]]) and 1 [[草药|Herb]].

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it cures poison and restores 4 health.
- **Give someone a drink**: hold it and right-click someone; they are cured and recover 4 health.

## Trivia

- One bite in four from a [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spider]] poisons you, and the antidote is made from that very spider's venom sac.
