{{信息框}}
The **Soggy Boot** is an old boot now and then hooked while fishing. It is not a fish; it cannot be worn or eaten, and the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] pays 1 [[晨辉币|Dawnmark]] for it.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[鱼饵|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- Any hour, any weather, any water. On every bite, the [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]] (early morning only) and the [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]] are checked first; if neither hits, there is an 8% chance it is this boot: “Something heavy... an old boot.” It goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]] → [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]] → **Soggy Boot** → [[鲶鱼|Catfish]] → [[鳗鱼|Eel]] → [[狗鱼|Pike]] → [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]] → [[河鲈|Perch]] → [[生鱼|Raw Fish]].
- All told, about 7.5% of bites are the boot (94% × 8%). A baited cast still uses up 1 bait.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 1 Dawnmark.
- It is not clothing like [[靴子|Boots]] and cannot be worn. It cannot be eaten, and it does not catch fire.
- The rules do not say what happens if you do anything else with it; the world may learn a new answer (see [[世界会学|the world learns]]).

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the fish pond, bait and stall on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]]; fishing catches different fish by the hour, the weather and the depth of the water.
