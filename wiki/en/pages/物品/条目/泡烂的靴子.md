The **Soggy Boot** is an old boot now and then hooked while fishing. It is not a fish; it cannot be worn or eaten, and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] pays 1 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmark]] for it.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[Bait|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- Any hour, any weather, any water. On every bite, the [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] (early morning only) and the [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] are checked first; if neither hits, there is an 8% chance it is this boot: “Something heavy... an old boot.” It goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] → [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] → **Soggy Boot** → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → [[Pike|Pike]] → [[Sunfish|Sunfish]] → [[Perch|Perch]] → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- All told, about 7.5% of bites are the boot (94% × 8%). A baited cast still uses up 1 bait.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 1 Dawnmark.
- It is not clothing like [[Boots|Boots]] and cannot be worn. It cannot be eaten, and it does not catch fire.
- The rules do not say what happens if you do anything else with it; the world may learn a new answer (see [[The World Learns|the world learns]]).

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the fish pond, bait and stall on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]]; fishing catches different fish by the hour, the weather and the depth of the water.
