**Corn Seeds** are the seeds of [[Corn|Corn]], bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]], 3 for 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland, they grow after two waterings. Corn is perennial: plant it once, and after harvest the plant is still there; water it again and it fruits again, with no need to sow anew.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The stall**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]], press {{key|E}}, and click them on the “Farming” shelf to buy 3 for 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]; they go straight into your inventory. Step away from the stall and you cannot buy.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe corn plant with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking by hand gives 1.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sowing**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow Corn Seeds.” It becomes a corn sprout. There is a ready-made field southwest of the planted sword on the Held Ground; elsewhere, a [[Hoe|Hoe]] turns diggable ground such as dirt or grass into farmland.
- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Sow corn” on the “Planting” page; the seeds only need to be in your inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering, in the open, makes it grow one stage two game hours later: sprout, young plant, ripe. After harvest it goes back to the young plant, and one more watering ripens it again. See [[corn (crops)|corn (crop)]], [[Farming|Farming]] and [[Crops|Crops]].
