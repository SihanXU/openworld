A **Mug of Ale** is a big mug of ale. It counts as a drink: drinking it restores 3 [[Health|health]], but no [[Satiety|satiety]]. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink Mug of Ale?” Confirm, and it restores 3 health.
- **Give someone a drink**: right-click someone holding it, and they recover 3 health.
- In the rules, drinking does not make you drunk.
