**Ectoplasm** is a wisp of something pale cyan, glowing from within: the “stuff” of the dead who will not rest and of the things that come in the night. [[ghost|Ghosts]] and [[storm wisp|storm wisps]] leave it when felled.

## Source

{{sources}}

- [[ghost|Ghost]]: 1 wisp.
- [[storm wisp|Storm wisp]]: 1 wisp.

## Uses

{{uses}}

For now it can only be sold at a [[trading stall|trading stall]], where it is the most valuable of the monster materials.

## History

- 2026-10-19: Added to the game as the drop of ghosts and storm wisps.
