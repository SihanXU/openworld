{{信息框}}
The **Bucket** is an empty pail. Hold it and left-click at the water's edge, and it becomes a bucket of water you can take to water crops, put out fires or drink. The large wooden barrels standing on the ground for storage are a different thing; see [[木桶（容器）|barrel (container)]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make one yourself**: press {{键|C}}: 3 {{物品|木板}} and 1 tuft of {{物品|干草}} make a bucket, with no [[工作台|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: an empty bucket stands in the camp at [[渡口|The Ford]].
- **Buy**: on the “Ranching” page of the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]], 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.
- Once a bucket of water is used, the bucket is left behind and becomes an empty bucket again.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fetching water**: hold the empty bucket and left-click shallow water, deep water, a {{物品|水井}}, a {{物品|喷泉}} or a {{物品|水槽}} (“Fill Bucket”): “Filled with water.” It becomes a {{物品|一桶水}}.
- **Building mode**: press {{键|B}}; “Fetch water” on the “Planting” page needs an empty vessel in your inventory. See [[农耕#水从哪来|Where water comes from]].
- **Milking**: hold the empty bucket and left-click a [[牛|cow]] that has been fed (“Milk cow”): “You milk the cow.” It becomes a {{物品|一桶牛奶}}. The cow must first have been fed [[饲料|Animal Feed]] or [[麦子|Wheat]], and once milked it cannot be milked again for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. See [[动物#挤奶|Milking]].
- Once you drink a bucket of milk, the bucket becomes an empty bucket again; sell one to the stall and the bucket goes with it.
- An empty bucket can only fetch water and milk cows. Nowhere in the rules can it hold oil.
