The **Bucket** is an empty pail. Hold it and left-click at the water's edge, and it becomes a bucket of water you can take to water crops, put out fires or drink. The large wooden barrels standing on the ground for storage are a different thing; see [[barrel|barrel (container)]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Make one yourself**: press {{key|C}}: 3 {{item|Plank}} and 1 tuft of {{item|Cut Grass}} make a bucket, with no [[workbench|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: an empty bucket stands in the camp at [[The Ford|The Ford]].
- **Buy**: on the “Ranching” page of the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]], 8 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.
- Once a bucket of water is used, the bucket is left behind and becomes an empty bucket again.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fetching water**: hold the empty bucket and left-click shallow water, deep water, a {{item|well}}, a {{item|fountain}} or a {{item|trough}} (“Fill Bucket”): “Filled with water.” It becomes a {{item|Water Bucket}}.
- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}}; “Fetch water” on the “Planting” page needs an empty vessel in your inventory. See [[Farming#Where water comes from|Where water comes from]].
- **Milking**: hold the empty bucket and left-click a [[cow|cow]] that has been fed (“Milk cow”): “You milk the cow.” It becomes a {{item|Bucket of Milk}}. The cow must first have been fed [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] or [[Wheat|Wheat]], and once milked it cannot be milked again for {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours. See [[Animals#Milking|Milking]].
- Once you drink a bucket of milk, the bucket becomes an empty bucket again; sell one to the stall and the bucket goes with it.
- An empty bucket can only fetch water and milk cows. Nowhere in the rules can it hold oil.
