A **Flask of Water** is an [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] filled with water. Its uses are exactly those of the {{物品|一桶水}}: water one crop, put out a fire, wet things, or be drunk. Use it once, and the flask is empty.

## Source

{{来源}}

No Flask of Water is placed on the maps. Holding an Empty Flask, left-click the water's edge, a well, a fountain or a trough to fill it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Water**: left-click a crop that is still growing and has not been watered; it gets {{状态|浇过水}}, and in the open it grows a stage two game hours later. See [[农耕|Farming]].
- **Splash**: left-click anything else: something burning is put out, a lit fire goes out, and anything else becomes {{状态|潮湿}}. See [[元素#水|Water]].
- **Drink**: a flask is a tool, and its left click waters and splashes; to drink, hold it and right-click yourself; you are asked “Drink Flask of Water?” Confirm, and it restores 2 health.
- Once used, an Empty Flask is left.
