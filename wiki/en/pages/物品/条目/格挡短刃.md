The **Parrying Dagger** is a short blade worn in the off hand to turn aside the enemy's blows. It is the [[Assassin|Assassin]]'s off-hand piece, as the [[Shield|Shield]] is the [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s: base defense 1, dodge +3%. It is made of iron or steel.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you and press {{key|C}}: 1 {{item|Steel Bar}} and 1 {{item|Leather Hide}} make a **Shadowfang Parrying Dagger**, recipe level 3. The first material is the steel bar, so it comes out steel (steel's defense factor is 1.75). Its [[Quality|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and its [[Affixes|affixes]] are rolled when it is made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- None lie on any map, and merchants do not sell it.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Worn in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it to the off hand from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. The off-hand slot to the right of the hotbar shows it.
- **Assassins only**: worn by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, it does nothing: the off-hand slot turns grey, and “Assassin only” in the tooltip is red. See [[Equipment#Profession-only|Profession-only gear]].
- **Not part of a set**: neither the main hand nor the off hand counts toward any set, so the Shadowfang Parrying Dagger has only its own stats (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- It is not a weapon: attacks use the weapon in your main hand (see [[Controls#Attacking and dashing|Attacking and dashing]]).
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put on them; it then belongs to them.
