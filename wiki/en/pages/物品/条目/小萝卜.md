The **Radish** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 2 [[Satiety|satiety]]. Its seeds are sold only at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]]; harvested radishes can be sold back to the stall, 5 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[Radish Seeds|Radish Seeds]] on farmland; watered twice, it grows ripe; reaped with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] it gives 2 radishes (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picked by hand 1 radish and 1 seed. See [[radish (crops)]] and [[Farming|Farming]].
- **Seeds**: [[Radish Seeds|Radish Seeds]] are bought in the “Farming” column of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], 5 for 8 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell radishes themselves, it only buys them. The garden in [[Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no radishes.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Radish?” Confirm, and it restores 2 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}} to open it, and sell in the “Sell” column, 5 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]]; the seeds are bought in the stall's “Farming” column.
