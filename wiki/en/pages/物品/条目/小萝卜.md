{{信息框}}
The **Radish** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 2 [[饱食度|satiety]]. Its seeds are sold only at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]]; harvested radishes can be sold back to the stall, 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[小萝卜种子|Radish Seeds]] on farmland; watered twice, it grows ripe; reaped with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] it gives 2 radishes (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picked by hand 1 radish and 1 seed. See [[小萝卜（作物）]] and [[农耕|Farming]].
- **Seeds**: [[小萝卜种子|Radish Seeds]] are bought in the “Farming” column of the [[货摊|trading stall]], 5 for 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell radishes themselves, it only buys them. The garden in [[初原村]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no radishes.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Radish?” Confirm, and it restores 2 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}} to open it, and sell in the “Sell” column, 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{键|G}}.

## History
