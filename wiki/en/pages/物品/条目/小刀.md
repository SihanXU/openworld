A **Knife** is a sharp little tool. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The knife carries the tool ability “knife”, but no rule in the rule library uses it, and the efficiency table has no row for it. It is not a weapon; left-clicking something with it is something the world has not yet seen.
