The **Sword** is the [[战士|Warrior]]'s profession weapon. Only someone who has chosen the Warrior profession can fight with it, and the Warrior's attack talents work only with swords. Its attack varies with [[材质|substance]] and [[品质|quality]], and it may carry an [[元素|element]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[战士|Warrior]] with [[布拉姆]], you receive an **Iron Sword** (attack 6), together with a Wooden [[盾牌|Shield]], worn in the off hand automatically. Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- **Smithing**: [[锻造|forge]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] carrying a {{物品|锻锤}}: 2 Iron Ingots or Steel Bars plus 1 plank (recipe level 2). The [[战士|Warrior]]'s set includes a sword, the “Oathguard Blade” (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- **Buying**: [[加里克]] and [[布拉姆]] sell swords (see [[交易|Trading]]).
- No swords are placed on the maps.
- The Warrior's [[传说武器|Legendary weapon]] [[誓约之刃|Oathbrand]] is a sword too.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fighting**: hold it and press {{键|空格}} to slash in front of you, or left-click a monster within 1 cell. A sword is a blade, and its blow counts as a “cut”. For the combat rules see [[战斗|Combat]].
- **Only a Warrior can use it**: held by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, its left click reads “Only a Warrior can fight with a sword”, and it makes no attack. It can still be held, put in a chest or given away.
- **The Warrior's talents**: {{技能|武器训练}} gives swords +1 attack; attack talents such as {{技能|以牙还牙}} and {{技能|狂暴}} also work only with swords; see [[战士|Warrior]].
- **Cutting things**: a Warrior left-clicking a tree, a chest or another thing that is not a person with a sword also strikes a blow. The damage follows the efficiency of a “cut” against the substance: on wood only half an axe's, on stone almost useless (see [[耐久与破坏#效率表|the efficiency table]]).
- With no weapon drawn ({{键|X}}), a left click on a person does not attack; right-clicking a person with a sword uses the sword on them, which the rules do not define, and [[世界会学|the world learns]] answers.
