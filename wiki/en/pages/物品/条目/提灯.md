The **Lantern** is a metal hand lantern. Like the [[Torch|Torch]], it can set things alight. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one. The “lantern post” in the camp at [[The Ford|The Ford]] is a different thing: a lamp that stands on the ground.

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip says the Lantern “can set things alight”. Under the rules, holding it and left-clicking something that can be lit lights it, and left-clicking something that can burn sets it on fire, the same as a Torch; see [[Elements|Elements]]. The Lantern is not used up. While you hold it, a ring of light shines around you.

The Lantern is made of metal: it does not catch fire itself, but it conducts electricity.
