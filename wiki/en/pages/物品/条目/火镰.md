**Flint and Steel** is a tool for striking fire; like the [[火把|Torch]], it can set things alight. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one. To light a fire, use a torch you make yourself.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip says Flint and Steel “Sets things alight”. Under the rules, hold it and left-click:

- **Things that can be lit** ([[营火|campfire]], brazier, lamp post, candlestick, fireplace…): it lights them.
- **Things that burn**: it sets them on fire.
- Wet things are dried, frozen things thaw, and things doused in oil flare up. See [[元素|Elements]].

Flint and Steel is not used up. It is made of metal and does not catch fire itself.
