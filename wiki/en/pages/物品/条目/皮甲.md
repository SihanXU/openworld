The **Jerkin** is light armor worn on the body, made of leather, with a base defense of 2: lower than the [[胸甲|Breastplate]] (3), but anyone can wear it. It is a form added for the [[套装|profession sets]]: the [[刺客|Assassin]]'s “Shadowfang Jerkin” and the [[游侠|Ranger]]'s “Windrunner Jerkin” are both jerkins.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you and press {{键|C}}:
  - **Shadowfang Jerkin**: 4 {{物品|皮革}} and 1 {{物品|钢条}}, recipe level 3; it comes with dodge +3% and belongs to the Shadowfang armor set.
  - **Windrunner Jerkin**: 4 leather hides and 1 {{物品|铁锭}}, recipe level 3; it belongs to the Windrunner armor set.
- The first material is leather, so both come out leather. Their [[品质|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and their [[词条|affixes]] are rolled when they are made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- No jerkin lies on any map, and merchants do not sell them.
- The [[绿史莱姆|green slime]]'s [[专属掉落|trophy]] “Gelhide Vest” is also a jerkin; see [[凝胶软甲|Gelhide Vest]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Put on Jerkin”); you can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. It takes the “armor” slot, competing for it with the [[胸甲|Breastplate]] and the [[法袍|Robe]]; see [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Sets**: the Shadowfang Jerkin and the Windrunner Jerkin each count as the “body” piece of their own armor set (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- Once worn, it is not drawn on the body: neither the map figure nor the portrait shows that a jerkin is worn.
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put on them; it then belongs to them.
- It is leather, and can catch fire.
