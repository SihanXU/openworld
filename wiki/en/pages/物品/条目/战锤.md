The **Warhammer** is a heavy blunt weapon with a base attack of 5, tied with the [[戟|Halberd]] as the highest of the general weapons. It belongs to no [[职业|profession]], so anyone can fight with it. None lies on any map and no quest gives one; once you have learned {{技能|重兵器}}, [[锻造|smith]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 4 iron ingots or steel bars plus 1 log (recipe level 4).

## Uses

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{键|空格}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile. It may also carry an [[元素|element]].
- **Shattering frozen targets**: a blunt weapon that hits a creature that is {{状态|冰冻}} shatters the ice, and that blow deals ×1.5 damage.
