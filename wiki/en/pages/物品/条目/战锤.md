The **Warhammer** is a heavy blunt weapon with a base attack of 5, tied with the [[Halberd|Halberd]] as the highest of the general weapons. It belongs to no [[Professions|profession]], so anyone can fight with it. None lies on any map and no quest gives one; once you have learned {{skill|Heavy Arms}}, [[Smithing|smith]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 4 iron ingots or steel bars plus 1 log (recipe level 4).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{key|Space}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile. It may also carry an [[Elements|element]].
- **Shattering frozen targets**: a blunt weapon that hits a creature that is {{status|frozen}} shatters the ice, and that blow deals ×1.5 damage.
