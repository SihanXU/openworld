**Ash** is what is left when something flammable burns away. It is a fertilizer: work it into the soil of a crop that is still growing, and the harvest gives one more crop. The [[Fertilizer|Fertilizer]] sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] works the same way.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Burning things**: a flammable item burns into one Ash; flammable fixtures such as chests and wood piles also leave one where they stood once they burn down. The simplest way is to hold a stick or a tuft of cut grass and press {{key|G}} facing a lit [[campfire|campfire]], throwing it into the fire. See [[Elements|Elements]].
- The “ash” left on burnt ground is a terrain, not this item, and cannot be picked up.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fertilizing**: hold Ash and right-click a crop that is still growing (a sprout or a young plant, not yet fertilized): “You work ash into the soil.” The crop becomes {{status|fertilized}}, and gives 1 more crop when reaped. The Ash is used up. A perennial crop's fertility is spent once it is harvested, and it must be fertilized again. See [[Farming#Fertilizing|Fertilizing]].
