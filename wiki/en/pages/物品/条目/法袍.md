{{信息框}}
The **Robe** is a long robe worn on the body, made of cloth or silk, with a base defense of 1, the lowest of the armor slot. It is a form added for the [[套装|profession sets]]: the [[法师|Mage]]'s “Starweave Robe” is a robe.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you and press {{键|C}}: 5 {{物品|布匹}} and 1 {{物品|金锭}} make a **Starweave Robe**, recipe level 3, which comes with skill cooldowns −3% and belongs to the Starweave armor set.
- The first material is cloth, so it comes out cloth (cloth's defense factor is 0.5; rounded, it is still at least 1). Its [[品质|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and its [[词条|affixes]] are rolled when it is made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- No robe lies on any map, and merchants do not sell it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (“Put on Robe”); you can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. It takes the “armor” slot, competing for it with the [[胸甲|Breastplate]] and the [[皮甲|Jerkin]]; see [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Set**: the Starweave Robe counts as the “body” piece of the Starweave armor set. Wearing 2, 4 and 6 pieces of Starweave armor gives experience +10%, skill cooldowns −10%, and attack +3 with crit chance +5%, respectively (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- Anyone can wear it, not only Mages.
- Once worn, it is not drawn on the body.
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put on them; it then belongs to them.
- It is cloth, and can catch fire.
