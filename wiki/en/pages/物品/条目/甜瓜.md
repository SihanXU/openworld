The **Melon** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 6 [[饱食度|satiety]]. The seeds are sold only at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]]; the melons you harvest can be sold back to the stall, 22 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[甜瓜种子|Melon Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice to grow; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 melons (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, and picking by hand gives 1 melon and 1 seed. See [[甜瓜（作物）|melon (crop)]] and [[农耕|Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[甜瓜种子|Melon Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[货摊|trading stall]], 3 for 24 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].
- The stall does not sell melons themselves; it only buys them. The vegetable garden in [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no melons.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Melon?” Confirm, and it restores 6 satiety. It cannot be grilled.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}} to open it, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 22 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
