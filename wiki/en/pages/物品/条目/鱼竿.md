A **Fishing Rod** is the tool for fishing. Hold it and left-click a tile of water to cast into it, and if a fish bites you land it. The rod casts as far as {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles, and it can take [[鱼饵|Bait]], which doubles the chance of a bite. How to fish and what you can catch: see [[钓鱼|Fishing]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make it yourself**: press {{键|C}} to open [[制作|crafting]]; 2 {{物品|树枝}} and 2 {{物品|干草}} make one by hand, with no workbench needed.
- **Buy**: the “Fishing” page of the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], 25 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] apiece.
- The sign by the Held Ground's fish pond gives the recipe: “FISH POND — make a rod from two sticks and two grass. Buy bait at the stall.”

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Casting**: hold it and left-click a tile of shallow or deep water (“Cast into the shallow water”). Without bait there is a 35% chance of a bite, with bait 70%. When something bites, the catch goes straight into your pack: mostly fish, now and then a {{物品|金鳞鲤}} or a {{物品|泡烂的靴子}}; which fish you catch depends on the hour, the weather and the depth of the water; see [[钓鱼#钓上什么|What you catch]]. When nothing bites, the message is “The float bobs. Nothing bites — yet.” (unbaited) or “The bait is taken clean. Nothing.” (baited).
- **Stirred up**: whether or not you catch anything, that tile of water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds, and casting there again in the meantime only gives the message “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.”. You can keep fishing in another tile of water.
- **Range**: any tile of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles will do, diagonals included; it is not a weapon, so it need not be in the same row or column as the water.
- **Baiting**: hold bait, then in the inventory right-click the rod and choose “Use Bait on it” (“Bait Fishing Rod”): “You bait the hook.” One portion of bait gives the rod {{数值|鱼饵次数}} more baited casts, and you can keep adding more. The bait is recorded on this rod, and each cast uses one, whether or not anything bites.
- The fishing rod is not a weapon: clicking a creature with it does not attack.
- The tooltip shows the baited casts left and hints on the catch for the water's depth and the time of day. The fishing rod is made of wood, and it burns.
