The **Perch** is a fish with stripes along its body, which may be caught at any time and in any water. Eaten raw it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]], and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] pays 8 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] for each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[Bait|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- **When**: any time, any water, but it comes after almost every other fish: when none of the ones before it hit, there is a 45% chance it is a Perch, and otherwise it is a [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- When you land it: “A striped perch!” The fish goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] → [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] → [[Soggy Boot|Soggy Boot]] → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → [[Pike|Pike]] → [[Sunfish|Sunfish]] → **Perch** → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- In the daytime, in shallow water and without rain, about 21% of bites are a Perch; from 19:00 to 19:59, an hour for neither Sunfish nor Eel, about 39%.
- After a cast, that patch of water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another patch of water to keep fishing. See [[Fishing|Fishing]].
- The stall does not sell Perch; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Perch?” Confirm, and it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish grills into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 8 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History
