{{信息框}}
The **Perch** is a fish with stripes along its body, which may be caught at any time and in any water. Eaten raw it restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]], and the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] pays 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] for each.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[鱼饵|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- **When**: any time, any water, but it comes after almost every other fish: when none of the ones before it hit, there is a 45% chance it is a Perch, and otherwise it is a [[生鱼|Raw Fish]].
- When you land it: “A striped perch!” The fish goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]] → [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]] → [[泡烂的靴子|Soggy Boot]] → [[鲶鱼|Catfish]] → [[鳗鱼|Eel]] → [[狗鱼|Pike]] → [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]] → **Perch** → [[生鱼|Raw Fish]].
- In the daytime, in shallow water and without rain, about 21% of bites are a Perch; from 19:00 to 19:59, an hour for neither Sunfish nor Eel, about 39%.
- After a cast, that patch of water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another patch of water to keep fishing. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]].
- The stall does not sell Perch; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Perch?” Confirm, and it restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish grills into the same Grilled Fish. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 8 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
