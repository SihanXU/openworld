A **Cooking Pot** is a metal stewpot. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about the pot: it cannot be used for cooking. [[Cooking|Cooking]] in the game means taking raw food to a lit fire and roasting it, with no pot. The pot is made of metal and conducts electricity.
