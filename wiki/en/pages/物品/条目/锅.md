A **Cooking Pot** is a metal stewpot. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the pot: it cannot be used for cooking. [[烹饪|Cooking]] in the game means taking raw food to a lit fire and roasting it, with no pot. The pot is made of metal and conducts electricity.
