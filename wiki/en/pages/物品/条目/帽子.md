A **Hat** is clothing worn on the head. An ordinary hat has no defense and no other bonus. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. It takes the same slot as the [[Helmet|Helmet]] and the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]]; see [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- Hats of cloth and leather can catch fire.

## Trivia

- It has a slot in the [[Codex|Codex]]; since a normal game cannot obtain it at present, that slot always shows “???”.
