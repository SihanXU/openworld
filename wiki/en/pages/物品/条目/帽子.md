A **Hat** is clothing worn on the head. An ordinary hat has no defense and no other bonus. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. It takes the same slot as the [[头盔|Helmet]] and the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]; see [[装备|Equipment]].
- Hats of cloth and leather can catch fire.
