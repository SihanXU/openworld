The **Empty Waterskin** is an empty leather bag. Like an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]] it can hold water, and full it is a [[Waterskin|Waterskin]]. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one; you only get one by emptying a Waterskin.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fetching water**: hold it and left-click shallow water, deep water, a well, a fountain or a trough, and it becomes a [[Waterskin|Waterskin]].
- **Building mode**: “Fetch water” on the “Planting” page can use it.
