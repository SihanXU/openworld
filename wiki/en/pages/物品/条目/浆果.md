{{信息框}}
**Berries** are small fruits growing in the wild; eating them restores 2 [[饱食度|satiety]]. They are the first thing you eat in a new game: three servings lie in the yard on [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]]. In the [[新手教程|tutorial]], the first objective of the main quest [[醒来|Waking]] is “Find something to eat”.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Cloudtop**: 3 servings in the yard where the game begins.
- **Tutorial**: 6 servings lie in [[林间空地|The Clearing]], and berries keep growing on the grass, up to 6 at a time, enough to eat your fill and last the night. See [[采集|Foraging]].
- **The Wild**: 3 servings lie scattered on each “Open Plains” map of [[荒野|the Wild]]; once picked, they do not grow back.
- **Grow your own**: buy [[浆果种子|Berry Seeds]] at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]]; the [[浆果丛|berry bush]] they grow can be harvested for berries once it fruits, and fruits again after harvest.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; it asks “Eat Berries?” Confirm, and they restore 2 satiety. When the [[新手教程|tutorial]] teaches “left-click to eat what you hold”, it uses berries.
- Like any other food, you can give them to someone with {{键|G}}, or trade them to [[托宾|Tobin]] for the Frost Wand.
- **Sell**: the Held Ground's stall buys berries, 1 [[晨辉币|Dawnmark]] each.
