The **Staff** is the [[法师|Mage]]'s profession weapon; its attacks reach 4 tiles. Only someone who has chosen the Mage profession can cast with it, and the Mage's talents apply only to staves.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose the [[法师|Mage]] with [[布拉姆|Bram]], you receive a **Wooden Staff** (attack 4). Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- **Smithing**: the [[法师|Mage]]'s set includes a “Starweave Staff”, [[锻造|smithed]] at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 2 planks plus 1 gold ingot (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- **Buy**: [[布拉姆|Bram]] sells staves (see [[交易|Trading]]).
- No staff lies on any map.
- The Mage's two [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]], [[烬心法杖|Emberheart]] and [[霜冠法杖|Rimecrown]], are both staves.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Casting**: a Mage holding it presses {{键|空格}} to send a spell the way they face, or left-clicks a target within 4 tiles. A long-range attack can only go out along your own row or column. Each blow counts as a “spell” and carries the staff's [[元素|element]].
- **Mages only**: held by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, the left click's prompt reads “Only a Mage can fight with the staff”, and no attack comes out.
- **The Mage's talents**: {{技能|奥术专注}} gives staff attacks +1; {{技能|大法师}} another +2, and +1 range; {{技能|法力护盾}} reduces the damage you take while you hold a staff; {{技能|奥术汲取}} restores 3 health when you fell a monster with a staff. See [[法师|Mage]].
