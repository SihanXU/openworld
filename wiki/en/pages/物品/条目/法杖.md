The **Staff** is the [[Mage|Mage]]'s profession weapon; its attacks reach 4 tiles. Only someone who has chosen the Mage profession can cast with it, and the Mage's talents apply only to staves.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose the [[Mage|Mage]] with [[Bram|Bram]], you receive a **Wooden Staff** (attack 4). Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- **Smithing**: the [[Mage|Mage]]'s set includes a “Starweave Staff”, [[Smithing|smithed]] at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 2 planks plus 1 gold ingot (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- **Buy**: [[Bram|Bram]] sells staves (see [[Trading|Trading]]).
- No staff lies on any map.
- The Mage's two [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]], [[Emberheart|Emberheart]] and [[Rimecrown|Rimecrown]], are both staves.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Casting**: a Mage holding it presses {{key|Space}} to send a spell the way they face, or left-clicks a target within 4 tiles. A long-range attack can only go out along your own row or column. Each blow counts as a “spell” and carries the staff's [[Elements|element]].
- **Mages only**: held by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, the left click's prompt reads “Only a Mage can fight with the staff”, and no attack comes out.
- **The Mage's talents**: {{skill|Arcane Focus}} gives staff attacks +1; {{skill|Archmage}} another +2, and +1 range; {{skill|Mana Shield}} reduces the damage you take while you hold a staff; {{skill|Arcane Recovery}} restores 3 health when you fell a monster with a staff. See [[Mage|Mage]].
