**Animal Feed** is dry fodder for livestock, bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]. Feed it to any [[Animals|animal]] and it is “fed” for a while: a fed [[cow|cow]] can be milked, and two fed cows (or two fed [[chicken|chickens]]) side by side will have a [[calf|calf]] (or hatch a [[chick|chick]]). [[Wheat|Wheat]] can be fed too, with exactly the same effect.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Ranching” shelf, 5 for 6 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Feeding animals**: hold the feed and right-click an animal, or face it and press {{key|G}} (the label reads “Feed …”). The feed is eaten, and the animal shows a happy face: “… eats happily.” It gets {{status|fed}} for {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours. Cows, chickens, calves and chicks can all be fed.
- **Milking**: once a cow is fed, left-click it with an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]] and you milk a [[Bucket of Milk|Bucket of Milk]]. A cow that is not fed will not give milk.
- **Breeding**: as soon as a cow or chicken is fed, it calls to its own kind nearby: if one within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down together) is already fed and has not bred recently, it hears and has a [[calf|calf]] or hatches a [[chick|chick]] beside itself. After that both are {{status|bred}} and will not breed again for {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours. An animal that has just bred can still be fed and milked as usual; it just does not call to its kind. See [[calf|Calf]] and [[chick|Chick]].
- Being fed or not does not affect a hen's laying: a chicken lays an [[Egg|Egg]] every {{value|下蛋间隔}} game hours, fed or not.
- Feed is not for people: using it on yourself with the right click is not covered by the rules. The same goes for using it on people or anything else, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|the world learns]]).
- Feed is dry fodder and burns; thrown into a lit fire it burns to [[Ash|Ash]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the trading stall at the Held Ground, feeding, milking and breeding.
- 2026-10-08: One feeding lasts a whole game day.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
