{{信息框}}
**Animal Feed** is dry fodder for livestock, bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]. Feed it to any [[动物|animal]] and it is “fed” for a while: a fed [[牛|cow]] can be milked, and two fed cows (or two fed [[鸡|chickens]]) side by side will have a [[小牛|calf]] (or hatch a [[小鸡|chick]]). [[麦子|Wheat]] can be fed too, with exactly the same effect.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Ranching” shelf, 5 for 6 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Feeding animals**: hold the feed and right-click an animal, or face it and press {{键|G}} (the label reads “Feed …”). The feed is eaten, and the animal shows a happy face: “… eats happily.” It gets {{状态|吃饱了}} for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. Cows, chickens, calves and chicks can all be fed.
- **Milking**: once a cow is fed, left-click it with an empty [[木桶|Bucket]] and you milk a [[一桶牛奶|Bucket of Milk]]. A cow that is not fed will not give milk.
- **Breeding**: as soon as a cow or chicken is fed, it calls to its own kind nearby: if one within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down together) is already fed and has not bred recently, it hears and has a [[小牛|calf]] or hatches a [[小鸡|chick]] beside itself. After that both are {{状态|刚生过崽}} and will not breed again for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours. An animal that has just bred can still be fed and milked as usual; it just does not call to its kind. See [[小牛|Calf]] and [[小鸡|Chick]].
- Being fed or not does not affect a hen's laying: a chicken lays an [[鸡蛋|Egg]] every {{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours, fed or not.
- Feed is not for people: using it on yourself with the right click is not covered by the rules. The same goes for using it on people or anything else, and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学|the world learns]]).
- Feed is dry fodder and burns; thrown into a lit fire it burns to [[灰烬|Ash]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the trading stall at the Held Ground, feeding, milking and breeding.
- 2026-10-08: One feeding lasts a whole game day.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
