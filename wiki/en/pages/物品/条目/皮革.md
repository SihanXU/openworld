**Leather Hide** is a tanned animal hide. [[wolf|Wolves]], [[frost wolf|frost wolves]], [[boar|boars]], [[dragon whelp|dragon whelps]] and [[cyclops|cyclopes]] leave one when felled (see [[Monster Drops|Monster Drops]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about Leather Hide, and no recipe uses it. Leather Hide catches fire. The leather gloves and leather boots in the game are found or given by quests, not made from Leather Hide.
