{{信息框}}
**Bait** goes on a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]]. One portion of bait lasts {{数值|鱼饵次数}} casts; a baited rod has a 70% chance of a bite on each cast, against 35% unbaited. It is bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Fishing” shelf, 5 for 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]. The same shelf also sells fishing rods (25).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Baiting**: hold the bait and use it on a fishing rod (the label reads “Bait Fishing Rod”): “You bait the hook.” The bait is used up, and the rod gains {{数值|鱼饵次数}} more baited casts. If the rod is in your pack, press {{键|I}} to open the inventory, right-click the rod and choose “Use Bait on it”; if the rod lies on the ground, right-click it.
- **You can keep adding more**: each portion adds another {{数值|鱼饵次数}} casts, with no limit.
- **How it is used up**: each cast with a baited rod uses 1, whether or not a fish bites. When a fish bites, you land something; when nothing bites, the message is “The bait is taken clean. Nothing.” Casting into water that is still stirred up (“The water is stirred up. Let it settle.”) does not count as a cast and uses no bait.
- Once the baited casts run out, the rod goes back to the unbaited 35%.
- Bait is only of use on a fishing rod. Using it on anything else is not covered by the rules, and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学|the world learns]]).
