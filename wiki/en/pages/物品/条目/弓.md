The **Bow** is the [[游侠|Ranger]]'s profession weapon, its attack reaching 6 cells. Only someone who has chosen the Ranger profession can fight with it. A bow needs no arrows: held in the hand, it shoots.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make your own**: stand by a [[工作台|workbench]], press {{键|C}}, and make a **Wooden Bow** from 2 {{物品|树枝}} and 3 {{物品|干草}}. The recipe level is 2; made at level 1, it comes out Crude; see [[品质#制作时的品质|quality when crafting]].
- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[游侠|Ranger]] with [[布拉姆]], you receive a **Wooden Bow** (attack 2). Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- Both of the Ranger's [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]], [[疾风之弦|Galestring]] and [[坠星|Starfall]], are bows.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fighting**: a Ranger holding it presses {{键|空格}} to loose an arrow the way they face, or left-clicks a monster within 6 cells. A ranged attack can only be shot along your own row or column; it cannot reach diagonally. For the combat rules see [[战斗|Combat]].
- **What flies is an arrow**: when you shoot, an arrow flies across the screen; a bow with an element looses arrows tinted with the element's color. The bow can also be equipped in the [[装备|main hand]], and shoots just the same while you hold something else.
- **Only a Ranger can use it**: held by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, its left click reads “Only a Ranger can fight with a bow”, and it makes no attack. A bow made before choosing a profession cannot be used either.
- **The Ranger's talents**: {{技能|箭术}} gives bows +1 attack and +1 range; talents such as {{技能|稳定瞄准}}, {{技能|狙击}} and {{技能|快速拉弓}} all work only with bows; see [[游侠|Ranger]].
