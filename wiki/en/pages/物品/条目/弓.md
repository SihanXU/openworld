The **Bow** is the [[Ranger|Ranger]]'s profession weapon, its attack reaching 6 cells. Only someone who has chosen the Ranger profession can fight with it. A bow needs no arrows: held in the hand, it shoots.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Make your own**: stand by a [[workbench|workbench]], press {{key|C}}, and make a **Wooden Bow** from 2 {{item|Stick}} and 3 {{item|Cut Grass}}. The recipe level is 2; made at level 1, it comes out Crude; see [[Quality#Quality when crafting|quality when crafting]].
- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[Ranger|Ranger]] with [[Bram]], you receive a **Wooden Bow** (attack 2). Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- Both of the Ranger's [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]], [[Galestring|Galestring]] and [[Starfall|Starfall]], are bows.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fighting**: a Ranger holding it presses {{key|Space}} to loose an arrow the way they face, or left-clicks a monster within 6 cells. A ranged attack can only be shot along your own row or column; it cannot reach diagonally. For the combat rules see [[Combat|Combat]].
- **What flies is an arrow**: when you shoot, an arrow flies across the screen; a bow with an element looses arrows tinted with the element's color. The bow can also be equipped in the [[Equipment|main hand]], and shoots just the same while you hold something else.
- **Only a Ranger can use it**: held by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, its left click reads “Only a Ranger can fight with a bow”, and it makes no attack. A bow made before choosing a profession cannot be used either.
- **The Ranger's talents**: {{skill|Archery}} gives bows +1 attack and +1 range; talents such as {{skill|Steady Aim}}, {{skill|Sniper}} and {{skill|Quick Draw}} all work only with bows; see [[Ranger|Ranger]].
