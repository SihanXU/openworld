A **Feather** is a bird's feather. Its only source is the [[小鸡|chick]]: a chick is made of one feather, and when it is felled there is a one-in-two chance it leaves that feather behind. A grown [[鸡|chicken]] leaves only Raw Chicken when felled, never a feather; no feathers are placed on any map, and they cannot be [[制作|crafted]].

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about feathers: you cannot make arrows or a quill from one. Feathers burn.
