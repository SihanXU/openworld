A **Feather** is a bird's feather. Its only source is the [[chick|chick]]: a chick is made of one feather, and when it is felled there is a one-in-two chance it leaves that feather behind. A grown [[chicken|chicken]] leaves only Raw Chicken when felled, never a feather; no feathers are placed on any map, and they cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]].

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about feathers: you cannot make arrows or a quill from one. Feathers burn.
