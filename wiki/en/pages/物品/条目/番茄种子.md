**Tomato Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Tomato|Tomato]]. The tomato is perennial: plant it once, and after harvest the plant is still there; water it again and it fruits again, with no need to sow anew.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Garden Supplies**: the “Garden Supplies” crate by the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] holds 2.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe tomato plant with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking by hand gives 1.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sowing**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow Tomato Seeds.” It becomes a tomato sprout.
- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Sow tomato” on the “Planting” page; the seeds only need to be in your inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering, in the open, makes it grow one stage two game hours later: sprout, young plant, ripe. After harvest it goes back to the young plant, and one more watering ripens it again. See [[Farming|Farming]] and [[Crops|Crops]].
