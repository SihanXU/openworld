The **Sunfish** is a bright little fish that bites by day. Eaten raw it restores 2 [[Satiety|satiety]], and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]] pays 6 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (holding the rod, left-click a stretch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} cells); with [[Bait|Bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time, without bait 35%.
- **When**: 06:00 to 18:59, any water will do. When none of the earlier entries hits, there is a 45% chance it is a Sunfish.
- When you land one: “A bright little sunfish!” The fish goes straight into the inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks one fish after another in this order, a later one coming up only when the earlier ones miss: [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] → [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] → [[Soggy Boot|Soggy Boot]] → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → [[Pike|Pike]] → **Sunfish** → [[Perch|Perch]] → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- By day in shallow water, when it is not raining, about 39% of bites are Sunfish (94% × 92% × 45%), the fish most often caught by day.
- After a cast, that stretch of water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds, and casting there again in that time only tells you “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another stretch of water to keep fishing. See [[Fishing|Fishing]].
- The trading stall does not sell Sunfish, it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Sunfish?” Confirm, and it restores 2 [[Satiety|satiety]].
- **Roast**: holding it, right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish roasts into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, and sell in the “Sell” column, 6 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the fish pond, bait and trading stall on [[The Held Ground]]; fishing catches different fish by the hour, the weather and the depth of the water.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls|Controls]]).
