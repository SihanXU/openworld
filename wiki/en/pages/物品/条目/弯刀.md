The **Scimitar** is a bladed weapon. Like the [[剑|Sword]], it belongs to the [[战士|Warrior]]: only a Warrior can fight with it. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: a Warrior holding it presses {{键|空格}}, or left-clicks a monster within 1 tile. Each blow counts as a “cut”, with a base attack of 4, the same as the Sword.
- Held by any other profession, the left click's prompt reads “Only a Warrior can fight with the scimitar”, and no attack comes out.
- The Warrior's combat talents also apply to it: the talents recognize the Warrior's own profession weapons; see [[战士|Warrior]].
