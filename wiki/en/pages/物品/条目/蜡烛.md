A **Candle** is a taper made of wax. Like the [[火把|Torch]], it can set things alight. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it. The lit “candles” standing on maps are a different thing and cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip says the candle “Sets things alight”. In the rules, holding it and left-clicking something that can be lit (a campfire, a brazier, candles…) lights it, and clicking something flammable sets it on fire, just like a torch; see [[元素|Elements]]. The candle is not used up. While you hold it, a ring of light surrounds you.

The candle is made of wax, and it burns.
