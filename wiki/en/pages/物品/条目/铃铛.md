A **Bell** is a small metal bell. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the bell: it carries the tag “rings”, but shaking it makes no sound. The bell is made of metal and conducts electricity.

## Trivia

- The bell rung in the quest [[守夜之备]] is the great bell in the tower of [[初晓礼拜堂|the Chapel of the First Dawn]]: walk into the bell tower and the hero pulls that old rope to ring it. That bell is not this item.
