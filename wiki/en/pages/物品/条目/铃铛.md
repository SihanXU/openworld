A **Bell** is a small metal bell. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about the bell: it carries the tag “rings”, but shaking it makes no sound. The bell is made of metal and conducts electricity.

## Trivia

- The bell rung in the quest [[The Vigil]] is the great bell in the tower of [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]]: walk into the bell tower and the hero pulls that old rope to ring it. That bell is not this item.
