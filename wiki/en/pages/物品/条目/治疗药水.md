The **Healing Potion** is a flask of red potion; drinking it restores 16 [[Health|health]], the most of any item. There are only two in the game.

## Source

{{sources}}

[[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]]'s side quest [[The Pilgrim Before|The Pilgrim Before]]: bring her the [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]] left behind by the pilgrim deep in the [[The Forest|forest]], and she gives you two Healing Potions and “Cassian's Prayer Cord”.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Drink Healing Potion?” Confirm, and it restores 16 health.
- **Give someone else a drink**: hold it and right-click another person or creature (“Give Healing Potion to … to drink”), and they recover 16 health.
- Potions do not restore satiety.
