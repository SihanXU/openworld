A **Rag Doll** is a doll sewn of cloth. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rule about rag dolls. Rag dolls can catch fire.
