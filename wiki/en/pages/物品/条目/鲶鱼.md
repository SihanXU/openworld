The **Catfish** is a whiskered fish that bites only in rain or storm. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|Satiety]], and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] at [[The Held Ground]] buys each one for 20 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast into water with a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[Bait|Bait]] on the hook something bites 70% of the time; without bait, 35%.
- **When**: whenever the [[Weather|weather]] is rain or storm, at any hour, in any water. When none of the three before it has hit, there is a 40% chance it is a catfish.
- When you land one: “A whiskered head breaks the rain — a catfish!” The fish goes straight into your bag.
- Once something bites, the game checks these one by one in this order, each only if the ones before it missed: [[Dawnscale]] → [[Golden Carp]] → [[Soggy Boot]] → **Catfish** → [[Eel]] → [[Pike]] → [[Sunfish]] → [[Perch]] → [[Raw Fish]].
- In rain, about 35% of bites are catfish (94% × 92% × 40%). On a rainy night the catfish is also checked first, then the eel.
- After a cast the patch of water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up; let it settle.” You can keep fishing in another patch. See [[Fishing]].
- The trading stall does not sell catfish; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat the catfish?” Confirm and it restores 4 [[Satiety|Satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles. Cooked.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish cooks into the same grilled fish. See [[Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]], press {{key|E}} and sell it in the “Sell” column, 20 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money]].
- Like any other food, it can be handed to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the fishpond, bait and trading stall of [[The Held Ground]]; fishing yields different fish by hour, weather and depth of water.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
