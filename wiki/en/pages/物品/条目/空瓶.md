The **Empty Flask** is an empty glass flask. Like an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]] it can hold water, and full it is a {{item|Flask of Water}}.

## Source

{{sources}}

No Empty Flask lies on any map, but bottled things leave one once used up:

- Splash a {{item|Flask of Oil}} on something, and the flask is empty.
- A Flask of Water drunk or splashed also leaves an Empty Flask.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fetching water**: hold the Empty Flask and left-click shallow water, deep water, a well, a fountain or a trough: “Filled with water.” It becomes a {{item|Flask of Water}}.
- **Building mode**: press {{key|B}}; “Fetch water” on the “Planting” page can use it.
- An Empty Flask can only hold water; nowhere in the rules can it hold oil.
