**Pauldrons** are armor worn on the shoulders, with a base defense of 1. The game has two pairs: one in [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|The Ossuary of the Thorn]], and one as the reward of [[Holt|Holt]]'s quest.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brother Odo's Pauldrons | [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] (steel) | Defense 5, vow “Held Ground”, 5 minor affixes | Lies in [[The Ossuary of the Thorn|The Ossuary of the Thorn]] |
| Militia Pauldrons | Fine | Defense 2 | Reward of the side quest [[Clear the Hollow|Clear the Hollow]] |

**Smithing**: once you have learned {{skill|Plate Work}}, [[Smithing|smith]] them at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 3 iron ingots or steel bars (recipe level 3); with {{skill|Leatherworker}} you can also make them from leather at a [[workbench|workbench]]. The [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s set includes a pair of “Oathguard Pauldrons” (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; use them again to take them off. You can also equip them from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. See [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Sharing a slot with the cloak**: Pauldrons and the [[Cloak|Cloak]] are both worn on the “shoulders”, so you can wear only one of them at a time.
- **Vow of the Held Ground**: Brother Odo's Pauldrons carry the [[Vowbound|vow]] “Held Ground”: once you have stood still for a full second, your attacks deal +25% damage and you take −30% damage.
