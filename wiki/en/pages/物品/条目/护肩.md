**Pauldrons** are armor worn on the shoulders, with a base defense of 1. The game has two pairs: one in [[荆棘骸骨堂|The Ossuary of the Thorn]], and one as the reward of [[霍尔特|Holt]]'s quest.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brother Odo's Pauldrons | [[遗誓|Vowbound]] (steel) | Defense 5, vow “Held Ground”, 5 minor affixes | Lies in [[荆棘骸骨堂|The Ossuary of the Thorn]] |
| Militia Pauldrons | Fine | Defense 2 | Reward of the side quest [[清剿荆棘谷|Clear the Hollow]] |

**Smithing**: once you have learned {{技能|板甲}}, [[锻造|smith]] them at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 3 iron ingots or steel bars (recipe level 3); with {{技能|皮匠}} you can also make them from leather at a [[工作台|workbench]]. The [[战士|Warrior]]'s set includes a pair of “Oathguard Pauldrons” (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Wear**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; use them again to take them off. You can also equip them from the [[背包|inventory]]. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Sharing a slot with the cloak**: Pauldrons and the [[斗篷|Cloak]] are both worn on the “shoulders”, so you can wear only one of them at a time.
- **Vow of the Held Ground**: Brother Odo's Pauldrons carry the [[遗誓|vow]] “Held Ground”: once you have stood still for a full second, your attacks deal +25% damage and you take −30% damage.
