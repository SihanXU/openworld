**Holy Water** is consecrated water in a flask. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

In the rules holy water is simply water, used like the {{物品|一瓶水}}: water one crop, splash it on things (putting out fire, wetting), or drink it for 2 health, leaving an [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] behind. It carries the “holy” trait, but no rule in the rule library looks at that.
