**Parsnip Seeds** are the seeds of the [[防风草|parsnip]], bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], 5 for 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow up. A harvest gives seeds back, so the seeds multiply as you plant.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Trading stall**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], press {{键|E}}, and click them on the “Farming” shelf: 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] buys 5, which go straight into your pack. Once you step away from the stall you can no longer buy.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe parsnip with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking it by hand gives 1.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sow**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the Parsnip Seeds.” It becomes a parsnip sprout. There is a ready plot of farmland southwest of the planted sword at the Held Ground; elsewhere you can turn diggable ground such as dirt or grass into farmland with a [[锄头|Hoe]].
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Sow Parsnip” on the “Farming” page; it is enough to have the seeds in your pack.
- **Growing**: each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later, if in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[防风草（作物）|the parsnip crop]], [[农耕]] and [[作物]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the [[货摊|trading stall]] at [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]; the seeds are bought on the stall's “Farming” shelf.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
