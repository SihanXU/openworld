The **Acorn** is the seed of an oak. A felled tree sometimes drops one. It need not go in farmland, and it needs no watering: plant it in any diggable ground and it grows into a tree in two game days.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Felling trees**: fell a tree with an [[斧|Axe]], and there is a 25% chance of an extra acorn. See [[伐木|Woodcutting]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Planting a tree**: hold the acorn and right-click a tile of diggable ground (grass, dirt, sand, snow, farmland) with nothing growing on it: “You plant an acorn.” It becomes an oak sapling.
- **Growing**: in the open with no one standing on it, an oak sapling grows into a tree after two game days; if someone is standing on it or something covers it overhead, it waits another game hour and checks again. The grown tree can be felled like any other.
- A [[铲子|Shovel]] digs an oak sapling back up into an acorn.

## Trivia

- Once you have learned {{技能|伐木工}}, a felled tree drops an extra log instead, and no more acorns.
