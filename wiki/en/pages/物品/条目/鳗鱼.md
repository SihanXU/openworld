{{信息框}}
The **Eel** is a fish that bites only at night. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[饱食度|Satiety]], and the [[货摊|trading stall]] at [[誓守之地]] buys each one for 18 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast into water with a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[鱼饵|Bait]] on the hook something bites 70% of the time; without bait, 35%.
- **When**: from 20:00 to 04:59 the next day, in any water. When none of those before it has hit, there is a 45% chance it is an eel.
- When you land one: “It writhes on the line in the dark — an eel!” The fish goes straight into your bag.
- Once something bites, the game checks these one by one in this order, each only if the ones before it missed: [[晨鳞鱼]] → [[金鳞鲤]] → [[泡烂的靴子]] → [[鲶鱼]] → **Eel** → [[狗鱼]] → [[太阳鱼]] → [[河鲈]] → [[生鱼]].
- On a night without rain, about 39% of bites are eels (94% × 92% × 45%); in rain the [[鲶鱼|catfish]] is checked first, so there are fewer eels. Fishing deep water at night, the eel is also checked before the [[狗鱼|pike]].
- After a cast the patch of water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up; let it settle.” You can keep fishing in another patch. See [[钓鱼]].
- The trading stall does not sell eels; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat the eel?” Confirm and it restores 4 [[饱食度|Satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles. Cooked.” It becomes [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish cooks into the same grilled fish. See [[烹饪]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]], press {{键|E}} and sell it in the “Sell” column, 18 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱]].
- Like any other food, it can be handed to someone with {{键|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the fishpond, bait and trading stall of [[誓守之地]]; fishing yields different fish by hour, weather and depth of water.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
