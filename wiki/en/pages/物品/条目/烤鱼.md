**Grilled Fish** is a cooked fish; eating it restores 5 [[Satiety|satiety]]. All eight kinds of fish you can catch ([[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]], [[Sunfish|Sunfish]], [[Perch|Perch]], [[Catfish|Catfish]], [[Eel|Eel]], [[Pike|Pike]], [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]], [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]]) grill into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Fishing|Fishing]], [[Cooking|Cooking]].

## Source

{{sources}}

Hold a raw fish and right-click lit cookware ([[campfire|campfire]], brazier, fireplace, forge, cauldron), or let fire reach it, and it is cooked. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].

| Raw fish | Eaten raw | Grilled |
|---|---|---|
| Raw Fish, Sunfish | +2 | +5 |
| Perch | +3 | +5 |
| Catfish, Eel, Pike, Golden Carp, Dawnscale | +4 | +5 |

Every fish is more filling grilled.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Grilled Fish?” Confirm, and it restores 5 satiety.
- The stall does not buy Grilled Fish. Only raw fish can be sold; see [[Money#What the stall buys|What the stall buys]].
