{{信息框}}
**Grilled Fish** is a cooked fish; eating it restores 5 [[饱食度|satiety]]. All eight kinds of fish you can catch ([[生鱼|Raw Fish]], [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]], [[河鲈|Perch]], [[鲶鱼|Catfish]], [[鳗鱼|Eel]], [[狗鱼|Pike]], [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]], [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]]) grill into the same Grilled Fish. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]], [[烹饪|Cooking]].

## Source

{{来源}}

Hold a raw fish and right-click lit cookware ([[营火|campfire]], brazier, fireplace, forge, cauldron), or let fire reach it, and it is cooked. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].

| Raw fish | Eaten raw | Grilled |
|---|---|---|
| Raw Fish, Sunfish | +2 | +5 |
| Perch | +3 | +5 |
| Catfish, Eel, Pike, Golden Carp, Dawnscale | +4 | +5 |

Every fish is more filling grilled.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Grilled Fish?” Confirm, and it restores 5 satiety.
- The stall does not buy Grilled Fish. Only raw fish can be sold; see [[金钱#货摊收什么|What the stall buys]].
