The **Steak** is cooked [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]]; eating it restores 8 [[饱食度|satiety]], two fifths of the satiety maximum of 20. Of the foods you can get for yourself, it is the most filling.

## Source

{{来源}}

Hold raw beef and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{键|G}} facing the fire, and the raw beef roasts into a Steak. Any fire that reaches raw beef on the ground also cooks it. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Steak?” Confirm, and it restores 8 satiety.

## Trivia

- A cow leaves 2 Raw Beef; cooked, that is 16 satiety, close to the satiety maximum.
