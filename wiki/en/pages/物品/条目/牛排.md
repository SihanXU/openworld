The **Steak** is cooked [[Raw Beef|Raw Beef]]; eating it restores 8 [[Satiety|satiety]], two fifths of the satiety maximum of 20. Of the foods you can get for yourself, it is the most filling.

## Source

{{sources}}

Hold raw beef and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{key|G}} facing the fire, and the raw beef roasts into a Steak. Any fire that reaches raw beef on the ground also cooks it. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Steak?” Confirm, and it restores 8 satiety.

## Trivia

- A cow leaves 2 Raw Beef; cooked, that is 16 satiety, close to the satiety maximum.
