A **Log** is the raw timber you get from felling a tree. Its main use is becoming {{item|Plank}}: with an [[Axe|Axe]] on you, one log makes two planks, and the walls, floors, doors, beds and workbenches you build all need planks.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Felling trees**: fell a tree with an [[Axe|Axe]] and it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; with {{skill|Woodcutter}}, 1 more. The stump grows back into a tree after two game days. See [[Woodcutting|Woodcutting]].
- **Wood pile**: logs are stacked on the [[wood pile|wood pile]] in [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]]. The woodcutter [[Gus|Gus]] goes to the [[The Forest|forest]] to fell trees every morning and stacks the logs there in the afternoon; anyone who needs them can take them.
- Things made of wood, such as stumps and wood piles, also leave some logs when they are broken (see [[Durability and Breaking|Durability and Breaking]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Making planks**: with the axe in your inventory, press {{key|C}} and choose “Plank”: 1 log makes 2 planks. Or lay the log on the ground and left-click it with the axe to split it into 2 planks.
- **Making a club**: standing by a [[workbench|workbench]], 1 log makes a wooden [[Club|club]].
- **Quests**: [[Gus|Gus]]'s daily quest [[Wood for the Fire|Wood for the Fire]] asks for 4 logs; the first level of [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] asks for 10.
- **Burning**: logs catch fire (the tooltip says “Flammable”). A [[campfire|campfire]] needs no feeding; a log thrown into a lit fire just burns to [[Ash|Ash]].
