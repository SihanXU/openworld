{{信息框}}
A **Log** is the raw timber you get from felling a tree. Its main use is becoming {{物品|木板}}: with an [[斧|Axe]] on you, one log makes two planks, and the walls, floors, doors, beds and workbenches you build all need planks.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Felling trees**: fell a tree with an [[斧|Axe]] and it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; with {{技能|伐木工}}, 1 more. The stump grows back into a tree after two game days. See [[伐木|Woodcutting]].
- **Wood pile**: logs are stacked on the [[柴堆|wood pile]] in [[初原村|Firstlight Village]]. The woodcutter [[格斯|Gus]] goes to the [[森林|forest]] to fell trees every morning and stacks the logs there in the afternoon; anyone who needs them can take them.
- Things made of wood, such as stumps and wood piles, also leave some logs when they are broken (see [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Making planks**: with the axe in your inventory, press {{键|C}} and choose “Plank”: 1 log makes 2 planks. Or lay the log on the ground and left-click it with the axe to split it into 2 planks.
- **Making a club**: standing by a [[工作台|workbench]], 1 log makes a wooden [[棍棒|club]].
- **Quests**: [[格斯|Gus]]'s daily quest [[给火堆添柴|Wood for the Fire]] asks for 4 logs; the first level of [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] asks for 10.
- **Burning**: logs catch fire (the tooltip says “Flammable”). A [[营火|campfire]] needs no feeding; a log thrown into a lit fire just burns to [[灰烬|Ash]].
