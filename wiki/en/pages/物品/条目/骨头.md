A **Bone** is a bone. Two lie on the ground at [[The Ford|the Ford]]; they can be picked up, but they have no use.

## Source

{{sources}}

[[skeleton|Skeletons]] (2), [[zombie|zombies]] (1) and [[barrow warden|barrow wardens]] (3) leave bones when felled (see [[Monster Drops|Monster Drops]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about bones, and no recipe uses them. Right-clicking something else with a bone is something the world has not yet seen.
