A **Bone** is a bone. Two lie on the ground at [[渡口|the Ford]]; they can be picked up, but they have no use.

## Source

{{来源}}

[[骷髅|Skeletons]] (2), [[僵尸|zombies]] (1) and [[古墓守卫|barrow wardens]] (3) leave bones when felled (see [[怪物掉落|Monster Drops]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about bones, and no recipe uses them. Right-clicking something else with a bone is something the world has not yet seen.
