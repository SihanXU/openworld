A **Bolt of Cloth** is a roll of woven cloth. Stand by a [[loom|loom]] and press {{key|C}} to weave 1 bolt from 4 {{item|Cut Grass}} (Cass's loom in [[Firstlight Village]], or the weaving shed built on the Held Ground). [[Cass]] also sells bolts of cloth (see [[Trading|Trading]]). It is the material for the cloth pieces of the [[Gear Sets|profession sets]] and for the residents' tents of [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{sources}}

A sack is made from a bolt of cloth, and breaking a sack has a one-in-two chance of leaving a bolt of cloth; but no sacks are placed on the maps.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smithing**: in the Assassin's, Ranger's and Mage's sets, the hoods, cloaks, robes, gloves, belts, slippers and tomes need bolts of cloth (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- **Expanding the Held Ground**: the three residents' tents need 6, 8 and 10 bolts, given to [[Alberic]] to be kept in the Held Ground's stores (see [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]).
- Bolts of cloth can catch fire.
