A **Bolt of Cloth** is a roll of woven cloth. Stand by a [[织布机|loom]] and press {{键|C}} to weave 1 bolt from 4 {{物品|干草}} (Cass's loom in [[初原村]], or the weaving shed built on the Held Ground). [[卡丝]] also sells bolts of cloth (see [[交易|Trading]]). It is the material for the cloth pieces of the [[套装|profession sets]] and for the residents' tents of [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{来源}}

A sack is made from a bolt of cloth, and breaking a sack has a one-in-two chance of leaving a bolt of cloth; but no sacks are placed on the maps.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Smithing**: in the Assassin's, Ranger's and Mage's sets, the hoods, cloaks, robes, gloves, belts, slippers and tomes need bolts of cloth (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- **Expanding the Held Ground**: the three residents' tents need 6, 8 and 10 bolts, given to [[阿尔贝里克]] to be kept in the Held Ground's stores (see [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]).
- Bolts of cloth can catch fire.
