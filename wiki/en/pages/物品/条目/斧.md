The **Axe** is a tool for felling trees, and a weapon too. Hold it and left-click a tree to [[Woodcutting|fell]] it, or left-click a {{item|Log}} on the ground to split it into two {{item|Plank}}; with an axe on you, the [[Crafting|crafting]] panel can turn logs into planks. It belongs to no [[Professions|profession]]: anyone can fight monsters with it.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Make one yourself**: the **stone axe** is the first thing you can make: 1 {{item|Stick}}, 1 {{item|Stone}} and 1 tuft of {{item|Cut Grass}}; press {{key|C}} and make it anywhere, with no [[workbench|workbench]] needed. A common-quality stone axe has attack 5. It is the first tool the [[Tutorial|tutorial]] teaches.
- **Ready-made**: an **Old Axe** (iron, attack 6) lies by the stump in the camp at [[The Ford|The Ford]]. [[Hesper|Hesper]] mentions it when you first meet.

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip gives the Axe's use as “Fells trees, splits logs”. What it can do under the rules:

- **Fell trees**: left-click a tree: “The axe bites into the tree.” The tree becomes {{status|notched}}. Two blows of a stone axe fell it, leaving a stump, 2 logs and 1 stick, with a 25% chance of an extra {{item|Acorn}}; once you have learned {{skill|Woodcutter}}, you get an extra log instead. Stop after one blow and the cut heals in 10 seconds, leaving the tree whole again. See [[Woodcutting|Woodcutting]].
- **Split logs**: left-click a log lying on the ground: “You split the log into planks.” It becomes 2 planks.
- **Make planks**: with the axe in your inventory, the crafting panel's “Plank” recipe can be made: 1 log makes 2 planks. The axe only has to be on you; it is not used up.
- **Charred wood**: left-click a tree charred by fire: “The charred wood crumbles to coal.” You get 1 {{item|Coal}}.
- **The dead tree at the Ford**: the dead tree at the narrows of [[The Ford|The Ford]] takes three blows to fall, and falls away from you; the river lies on that side, so the trunk ends up across it as a bridge. See [[Woodcutting#The dead tree at the Ford|The dead tree at the Ford]].
- **Taking things apart**: left-click to knock an unlocked crate or chest, and what is inside spills out while the container comes loose whole and drops; pick it up and you can build it elsewhere. A wooden frame, stakes or a sign post that has not been nailed together yet comes apart with one blow of the axe, and the planks and sticks that went into it come back as they were (see [[Building|Building]]).
- **Knocking built things loose**: left-click a built wall, door, floor, stairs, bed, workbench or anything else of the kind you can carry off, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; press {{key|E}} to pick it up (close by it is drawn into your inventory by itself), and you can carry it elsewhere and build it again. See [[Structures|Structures]].
- **Combat**: the Axe is a general weapon anyone can use, with a base attack of 4. Hold it and press {{key|Space}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile.

## Trivia

- In the item catalogue the Axe is a weapon, not a tool: it is a bladed weapon that also carries the “axe” tool ability. So when you click a tree or a log with it you are using a tool, and when you click a monster you are attacking.
- An axe cannot dig up a stump; that takes a {{item|Shovel}}; see [[Woodcutting#Stumps|Stumps]].
