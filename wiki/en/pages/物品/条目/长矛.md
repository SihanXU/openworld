A **Spear** is a polearm whose attack reaches 2 tiles. It belongs to no [[职业|profession]]: anyone can fight with it. The only one in the game comes from [[霍尔特|Holt]]'s quest.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Quest**: the side quest [[清剿荆棘谷]] from [[霍尔特|Holt]] in [[初原村]] (taken from level 2): fell 6 monsters in [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]] and turn it in, and you get a spear and a pair of “Militia Pauldrons”. This spear has no material, so its attack is just the base 4.
- No spear is placed on any map, and it cannot be [[制作|crafted]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{键|空格}}, or left-click a monster within 2 tiles. An attack at 2 tiles can only go out along your own row or column; it cannot reach diagonally. For the rules of combat see [[战斗|Combat]].
- General weapons get no bonus from profession talents.
- **Thrust**: a spear strikes with its point, and its edge is “piercing” (as with the pickaxe). Against things such as wood and stone it is reckoned by the efficiency of piercing (see [[耐久与破坏#效率表|the efficiency table]]). It is not a blunt weapon: hitting a {{状态|冰冻}} target does not shatter the ice, and it gets none of the blunt weapon's 1.5× damage.
