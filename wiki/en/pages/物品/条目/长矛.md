A **Spear** is a polearm whose attack reaches 2 tiles. It belongs to no [[Professions|profession]]: anyone can fight with it. The only one in the game comes from [[Holt|Holt]]'s quest.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Quest**: the side quest [[Clear the Hollow]] from [[Holt|Holt]] in [[Firstlight Village]] (taken from level 2): fell 6 monsters in [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]] and turn it in, and you get a spear and a pair of “Militia Pauldrons”. This spear has no material, so its attack is just the base 4.
- No spear is placed on any map, and it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{key|Space}}, or left-click a monster within 2 tiles. An attack at 2 tiles can only go out along your own row or column; it cannot reach diagonally. For the rules of combat see [[Combat|Combat]].
- General weapons get no bonus from profession talents.
- **Thrust**: a spear strikes with its point, and its edge is “piercing” (as with the pickaxe). Against things such as wood and stone it is reckoned by the efficiency of piercing (see [[Durability and Breaking#Efficiency table|the efficiency table]]). It is not a blunt weapon: hitting a {{status|frozen}} target does not shatter the ice, and it gets none of the blunt weapon's 1.5× damage.
