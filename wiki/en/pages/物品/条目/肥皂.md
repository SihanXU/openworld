**Soap** is a bar of soap. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about soap: it carries the tags “slippery” and “cleaning”, but it does not wash off {{状态|油污}}, nor does it make the ground slippery.
