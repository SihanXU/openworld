A **Belt** is clothing worn at the waist, with a base defense of 1. There are three in the game: one carried by the [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]], and two as quest rewards.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomas's Belt of Vigil | [[遗誓|Vowbound]] (leather) | Defense 3, defense +2 at night, vow “Vigil” | Carried by the [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]] in [[守墓者圣所|The Warden's Sanctum]]; it drops to the ground when the warden is felled |
| Marta's Kitchen Sash | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[给锅里添点东西]] |
| Joss's Tool Belt | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] |

When the barrow warden falls, what it carries drops where it stood, and you press {{键|E}} to pick it up. **Smithing**: each of the four professions' sets has a belt (Shadowfang Belt, Oathguard Girdle, Windrunner Belt, Starweave Sash), [[锻造|smithed]] at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Night defense**: Tomas's Belt of Vigil adds 2 more defense after nightfall.
