A **Belt** is clothing worn at the waist, with a base defense of 1. There are three in the game: one carried by the [[barrow warden|barrow warden]], and two as quest rewards.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomas's Belt of Vigil | [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] (leather) | Defense 3, defense +2 at night, vow “Vigil” | Carried by the [[barrow warden|barrow warden]] in [[The Warden's Sanctum|The Warden's Sanctum]]; it drops to the ground when the warden is felled |
| Marta's Kitchen Sash | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[Something for the Pot]] |
| Joss's Tool Belt | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]] |

When the barrow warden falls, what it carries drops where it stood, and you press {{key|E}} to pick it up. **Smithing**: each of the four professions' sets has a belt (Shadowfang Belt, Oathguard Girdle, Windrunner Belt, Starweave Sash), [[Smithing|smithed]] at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. See [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Night defense**: Tomas's Belt of Vigil adds 2 more defense after nightfall.
