The **Egg** is laid by a hen. Eaten raw it restores 1 [[Satiety|Satiety]]; put on the fire it becomes a [[Boiled Egg|Boiled Egg]], which restores 3. [[Marta|Marta]]'s quest needs two eggs.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Hens**: every [[chicken|chicken]] lays an egg at its feet every 3 game hours. There are chickens on the open plains of [[The Grassland]], [[The Held Ground]] and [[The Wild]]. See [[Animals]].
- The egg lands on the ground; press {{key|E}} to pick it up.
- **Quest reward**: Edda gives 2 when you hand in the side quest [[Edda's Ring]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it restores 1 Satiety.
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles. Cooked.” It becomes a Boiled Egg. See [[Cooking]].
- **Quest**: the side quest [[Something for the Pot]] of [[Marta|Marta]] in [[Firstlight Village]] needs 2 eggs and 3 [[Raw Beef|Raw Beef]], taken when you hand it in.
