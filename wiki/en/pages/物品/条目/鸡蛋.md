{{信息框}}
The **Egg** is laid by a hen. Eaten raw it restores 1 [[饱食度|Satiety]]; put on the fire it becomes a [[熟鸡蛋|Boiled Egg]], which restores 3. [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s quest needs two eggs.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Hens**: every [[鸡|chicken]] lays an egg at its feet every 3 game hours. There are chickens on the open plains of [[草原]], [[誓守之地]] and [[荒野]]. See [[动物]].
- The egg lands on the ground; press {{键|E}} to pick it up.
- **Quest reward**: Edda gives 2 when you hand in the side quest [[艾达的戒指]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it restores 1 Satiety.
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles. Cooked.” It becomes a Boiled Egg. See [[烹饪]].
- **Quest**: the side quest [[给锅里添点东西]] of [[玛尔塔|Marta]] in [[初原村]] needs 2 eggs and 3 [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]], taken when you hand it in.
