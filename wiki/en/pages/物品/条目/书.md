A **Book** is an ordinary book. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it. The books of the story are other items, such as the [[初夜历书|Almanac of the First Night]].

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

A book carries the “readable” mark. Documents with writing on them (the [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], the [[初夜历书|Almanac of the First Night]]) can be read by holding them and left-clicking to use them on yourself; this ordinary book has nothing written in it, and holding it and left-clicking it on yourself is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]). Press {{键|E}} empty-handed on a book lying on the ground and you are only told “It is Book.” Books can catch fire.
