A **Book** is an ordinary book. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it. The books of the story are other items, such as the [[Almanac of the First Night|Almanac of the First Night]].

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

A book carries the “readable” mark. Documents with writing on them (the [[Proclamation of the Pilgrimage|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], the [[Almanac of the First Night|Almanac of the First Night]]) can be read by holding them and left-clicking to use them on yourself; this ordinary book has nothing written in it, and holding it and left-clicking it on yourself is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]). Press {{key|E}} empty-handed on a book lying on the ground and, with no AI connected, you are only told “You run a hand over Book.” Books can catch fire.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue.
- 2026-10-08: Documents with writing can be read in hand; an ordinary book holds no writing, and reading it is still the world's to answer.
