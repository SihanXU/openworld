A **Cloak** is clothing worn over the shoulders. An ordinary cloak gives no defense by itself; all three cloaks in the game are named, and each carries defense.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watchman's Cloak | Fine (cloth) | Defense 1 | Lies in the Sunwatch Post on [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]] |
| Chalk-dusted Cloak | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[佩尔的涂鸦|Pell's Drawings]] |
| Cloak of the Dawn Vigil | Masterwork | Defense 2, night defense +1 | Reward of the main quest [[守夜之备|The Vigil]] |

**Smithing**: the Assassin's, Ranger's and Mage's sets each include a cloak worn over the shoulders (Shadowfang Cloak, Windrunner Mantle, Starweave Mantle), [[锻造|smithed]] at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you, from bolts of cloth and leather or feathers (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]). [[卡丝|Cass]] also sells cloaks (see [[交易|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Sharing a slot with pauldrons**: the Cloak and [[护肩|Pauldrons]] are both worn on the “shoulders”, so you can wear only one of them at a time.
- **Night defense**: the Cloak of the Dawn Vigil adds 1 more defense after nightfall.
- A cloth cloak can catch fire.
