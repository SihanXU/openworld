The **Broom** is a broom of bound straw. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the Broom: it cannot sweep the floor. The Broom is made of wood and straw, and it can catch fire.
