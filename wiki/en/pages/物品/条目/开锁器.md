A **Lockpick** is a tool for picking locks. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

In the rules only a [[钥匙|Key]] can open and lock locks. The lockpick carries the “lockpick” mark, but no rule in the rule library uses it: left-clicking a locked chest with it is something the world has not yet seen.

## Trivia

- In the game the lockpick uses the key's picture.
