A **Fireproof Draught** is a flask of orange potion. Drinking it gives you {{status|fireproof}}: for 5 game hours (5 real minutes), fire deals only a quarter of its damage, and you cannot catch fire. [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]'s herbalist [[Wren|Wren]] brews it to face the fire of [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]].

## Source

{{sources}}

In the [[Emberfall|Emberfall]] line, after the elder [[Mara|Mara]]'s council, the herbalist [[Wren|Wren]] asks for 3 [[Fireweed|Fireweed]] and 3 [[Frostcap|Frostcap]]:

1. Hold the fireweed and frostcap and press {{key|G}} at Wren to hand them over one at a time, until you have given 3 of each.
2. Then press {{key|E}} at him empty-handed: he brews two Fireproof Draughts and hands them to you: “Drink one at the lair's mouth… The other is for when the first wears off.”

These two are the only ones. The Emberfall and dragon line is the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink Fireproof Draught?” Confirm, and: “A cool calm spreads under your skin: fire will hardly touch you now.” You get {{status|fireproof}} for 5 game hours:
  - damage from the fire element falls to a quarter (75% fire resistance);
  - any fire burning on you goes out at once, and you cannot be set alight for that time.
- **Give someone a drink**: hold it and right-click someone, and they get the same fireproofing.
- It restores no health; it only gives fireproofing.
