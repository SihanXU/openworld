The **Cup** is a cup for drinking from, made of wood, clay, silver, gold or glass. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about the Cup: it cannot hold water, and you cannot drink from it. To carry water, use a [[Bucket|Bucket]], an [[Empty Flask|Empty Flask]] or an [[Empty Waterskin|Empty Waterskin]].
