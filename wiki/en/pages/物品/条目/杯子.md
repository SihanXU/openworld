The **Cup** is a cup for drinking from, made of wood, clay, silver, gold or glass. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the Cup: it cannot hold water, and you cannot drink from it. To carry water, use a [[木桶|Bucket]], an [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] or an [[空水袋|Empty Waterskin]].
