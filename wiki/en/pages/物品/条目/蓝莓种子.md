**Blueberries Seeds** are the seeds of [[Blueberries|blueberries]], bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], 3 for 24 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow up. Blueberries are perennial: plant them once, and after a harvest the plant is still there; water it again and it fruits again, with no need to sow anew.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], press {{key|E}}, and click them on the “Farming” shelf: 24 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] buys 3, which go straight into your pack. Once you step away from the stall you can no longer buy.
- **Harvest**: reaping a ripe blueberry plant with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds; picking it by hand gives 1.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sow**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the Blueberries Seeds.” It becomes a blueberry sprout. There is a ready plot of farmland southwest of the planted sword at the Held Ground; elsewhere you can turn diggable ground such as dirt or grass into farmland with a [[Hoe|Hoe]].
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Sow Blueberries” on the “Farming” page; it is enough to have the seeds in your pack.
- **Growing**: each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later, if in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. After a harvest it goes back to a young plant, and one more watering ripens it again. See [[blueberries (crops)|the blueberry crop]], [[Farming]] and [[Crops]].
