**The Unfallen Wall** is the second of the [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]], a [[Shield|Shield]] of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality. It is worn in the off hand, with 5 defense and a 15% block chance; its stigma power, “Unfallen”, stops a deadly attack once a minute.

## Source

{{sources}}

- When a [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]] falls, he leaves the first of the felling hero's profession's Legendary weapons that has not yet appeared in the world. The Warrior's first is [[Oathbrand|Oathbrand]], so the Unfallen Wall appears only when a Warrior fells a second Seal-bearer.
- The only Seal-bearer that can be fought in the game at present is [[The White Knight Alberic]], so the Unfallen Wall cannot yet be obtained in a normal game.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Worn in the off hand**: like an ordinary shield, only a Warrior can wear it.
- **Block**: every blow you take has a 15% chance of being blocked entirely, with “Blocked!” over your head.
- **Stigma power “Unfallen”**: when an attack would fell you, it is blocked entirely, with “Unfallen!” over your head. After that it takes 60 seconds before it can block again.
- It is made of metal and conducts electricity.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Warrior's second Legendary weapon.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Unfallen”.
