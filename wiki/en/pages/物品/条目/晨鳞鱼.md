The **Dawnscale** is a rare fish that bites only in the early morning, its scales like the light of the first morning. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]], and grilled into [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]] it restores 5; the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] pays 80 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] for each, the highest price the stall pays for anything.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[鱼饵|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- **When**: only between 05:00 and 07:59. In that window, every bite first has a 3% chance of being a Dawnscale. Any water will do, whatever the weather.
- When you land it: “The line glows as it comes up — a Dawnscale, scales like the first morning!” The fish goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: **Dawnscale** → [[金鳞鲤|Golden Carp]] → [[泡烂的靴子|Soggy Boot]] → [[鲶鱼|Catfish]] → [[鳗鱼|Eel]] → [[狗鱼|Pike]] → [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]] → [[河鲈|Perch]] → [[生鱼|Raw Fish]].
- With bait, each cast in that window has about a 2.1% chance of catching one (70% × 3%); without bait, about 1%.
- After a cast, that patch of water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another patch of water to keep fishing. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]].
- The stall does not sell Dawnscales; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Dawnscale?” Confirm, and it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click lit cookware ([[营火|campfire]], [[火盆|brazier]]…), or press {{键|G}} facing it: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]], which restores 5 satiety when eaten, 1 more than raw. Every fish grills into the same Grilled Fish. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 80 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.

## History
