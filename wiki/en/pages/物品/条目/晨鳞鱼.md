The **Dawnscale** is a rare fish that bites only in the early morning, its scales like the light of the first morning. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]], and grilled into [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]] it restores 5; the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]] pays 80 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] for each, the highest price the stall pays for anything.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click a patch of water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles). With [[Bait|bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time; without, 35%.
- **When**: only between 05:00 and 07:59. In that window, every bite first has a 3% chance of being a Dawnscale. Any water will do, whatever the weather.
- When you land it: “The line glows as it comes up — a Dawnscale, scales like the first morning!” The fish goes straight into your inventory.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each tried only if the ones before it missed: **Dawnscale** → [[Golden Carp|Golden Carp]] → [[Soggy Boot|Soggy Boot]] → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → [[Pike|Pike]] → [[Sunfish|Sunfish]] → [[Perch|Perch]] → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- With bait, each cast in that window has about a 2.1% chance of catching one (70% × 3%); without bait, about 1%.
- After a cast, that patch of water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds; casting there again in that time only says “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” Move to another patch of water to keep fishing. See [[Fishing|Fishing]].
- The stall does not sell Dawnscales; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Dawnscale?” Confirm, and it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]].
- **Grill**: hold it and right-click lit cookware ([[campfire|campfire]], [[brazier|brazier]]…), or press {{key|G}} facing it: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]], which restores 5 satiety when eaten, 1 more than raw. Every fish grills into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 80 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History
