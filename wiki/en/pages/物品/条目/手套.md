**Gloves** are clothing worn on the hands. Ordinary gloves give no bonus by themselves; the game has three named pairs, each with its own bonuses.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Gloves | Common (leather) | None | Lies at the [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]] |
| Edda's Work Gloves | Fine | Defense 1 | Reward of the side quest [[Edda's Ring|Edda's Ring]] |
| Gauntlets of the White Host | Masterwork (leather) | Defense 2, attack +1 | Lies in the knight's kingdom on [[The East Road|The East Road]] |

**Making**: once you have learned {{skill|Leatherworker}}, make them from leather at a [[workbench|workbench]]. Each of the four professions' sets includes a pair of gloves (Shadowfang Gloves, Oathguard Gauntlets, Windrunner Bracers, Starweave Gloves), [[Smithing|smithed]] at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]). [[Cass|Cass]] also sells gloves (see [[Trading|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; use them again to take them off. You can also equip them from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. See [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- Besides defense, the Gauntlets of the White Host add 1 attack.
- Gloves made of leather or cloth can catch fire.

## Trivia

- The Hunter's Gloves have no stats at all: putting them on changes nothing.
