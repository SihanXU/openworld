An **Ink Pot** is a small pot of ink. To write on a blank [[sign|sign]] with a [[Quill|Quill]], you need one in the inventory. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Writing**: right-clicking a blank sign with a quill writes on it only if an Ink Pot is in the inventory. The ink is not used up.
- The description of the “Sign” in build mode reads: “A blank sign; write on it with a quill and ink.”
- Beyond that, no rule in the rule library uses it.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue; writing on a sign needs ink carried.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls|Controls]]).
