**Dice** are a pair of dice made of bone. You cannot get them in normal play: none are placed on any map, they cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives them.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rules about dice: the game has no dice-rolling minigame.
