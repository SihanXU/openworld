An **Eggplant** is a vegetable you can grow; eating one restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]]. Eggplant is perennial: after a harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]; the eggplants you harvest can be sold back to the stall for 6 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[茄子种子|Eggplant Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 eggplants (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 eggplant and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[茄子（作物）|the eggplant crop]] and [[农耕]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[茄子种子|Eggplant Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[货摊|trading stall]], 3 for 16 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell eggplants themselves; it only buys them. The garden in [[初原村]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no eggplant.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Eggplant?” Confirm, and it restores 3 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}} to open it, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 6 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
