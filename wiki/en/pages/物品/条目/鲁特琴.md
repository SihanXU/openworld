The **Lute** is a plucked string instrument made of wood. It cannot be obtained in normal play: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rule library has no rule about the lute, and none about instruments: it makes no sound. The lute can catch fire.

## Trivia

- On the portal of the wiki's main page, the “Music” entry uses the lute's picture.
