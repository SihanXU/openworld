The **Lute** is a plucked string instrument made of wood. It cannot be obtained in normal play: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rule about the lute, and none about instruments: it makes no sound. The lute can catch fire.
