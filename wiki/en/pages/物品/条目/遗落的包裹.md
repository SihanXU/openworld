**Lost Belongings** is a bundle of things left where the player fell. On Normal [[Difficulty|difficulty]], when you fall and choose “Respawn” to return home, what you carried does not come with you; it is tied into a bundle like this and left on the spot. Walk back and open it, and everything inside returns to your pack.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Respawning on Normal difficulty**: after the player falls, choosing “Respawn” on the death screen (standing up again at home) gives the message “You get up again. What you carried is bundled up where you fell; what you wear and your important items are still on you.”
- **What goes into the bundle**: the things in your pack and the thing in your hand.
- **What stays on you**: the gear you wear, the weapon in your main hand, and items marked “important” (such as [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]]).
- If you carry nothing besides these, no bundle is left.
- On Easy difficulty you get up where you fell with everything still on you, and there is no bundle. See [[Difficulty#Respawning|Respawning]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Take it back**: walk up to the bundle and press {{key|E}} (the label reads “Gather Lost Belongings”): “You gather up your belongings.” Everything inside goes into your pack, and the bundle disappears.
- Anyone can open it: the things go into the pack of whoever opens it.
- The bundle stays where it is; it never disappears, and it never flies into your pack on its own. Fall again, and another bundle is left at the new spot, while the old one stays where it was.
- An empty bundle can be picked up with {{key|E}} like any other thing.
- The bundle does not burn.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game: on Normal difficulty, what you carry no longer scatters all over the ground when you fall, but is tied into one bundle.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
