**Frostcap** is a blue mushroom with a chill about it. [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]'s herbalist [[雷恩|Wren]] needs 3 to brew the [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]]: “Frostcap, from the cold side of the forest. Good.” It is not the same thing as the edible [[蘑菇|Mushroom]].

## Source

{{来源}}

3 lie on the grass of [[森林|the Forest]]; once picked they slowly grow back, up to 3 at a time.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Brewing**: in the Emberfall line, hold frostcap and press {{键|G}} at Wren to hand it over one at a time; once you have given 3, and then 3 [[火焰草|Fireweed]], press {{键|E}} at him empty-handed and he brews two Fireproof Draughts. The Emberfall and dragon line is the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].
- Frostcap cannot be eaten, and it does not burn.
