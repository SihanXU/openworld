**Frostcap** is a blue mushroom with a chill about it. [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]'s herbalist [[Wren|Wren]] needs 3 to brew the [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]]: “Frostcap, from the cold side of the forest. Good.” It is not the same thing as the edible [[Mushroom|Mushroom]].

## Source

{{sources}}

3 lie on the grass of [[The Forest|the Forest]]; once picked they slowly grow back, up to 3 at a time.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Brewing**: in the Emberfall line, hold frostcap and press {{key|G}} at Wren to hand it over one at a time; once you have given 3, and then 3 [[Fireweed|Fireweed]], press {{key|E}} at him empty-handed and he brews two Fireproof Draughts. The Emberfall and dragon line is the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].
- Frostcap cannot be eaten, and it does not burn.
