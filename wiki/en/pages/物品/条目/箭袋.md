The **Quiver** is a leather quiver hung on the body and worn in the off hand. It is the [[游侠|Ranger]]'s off-hand piece, as the [[盾牌|Shield]] is the [[战士|Warrior]]'s: no defense, +3% crit chance. It is made of leather.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you and press {{键|C}}: 3 {{物品|皮革}} and 6 {{物品|羽毛}} make a **Windrunner Quiver**, recipe level 3. Its [[品质|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and its [[词条|affixes]] are rolled when it is made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- None lie on any map, and merchants do not sell it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Worn in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it to the off hand from the [[背包|inventory]]. The off-hand slot to the right of the hotbar shows it.
- **Rangers only**: worn by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, it does nothing: the off-hand slot turns grey, and “Ranger only” in the tooltip is red. See [[装备#职业专属|Profession-only gear]].
- **Not part of a set**: neither the main hand nor the off hand counts toward any set, so the Windrunner Quiver has only its own stats (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- Shooting needs no arrows in the game: the [[弓|Bow]] shoots by itself, and the quiver is just an off-hand piece that adds crit chance.
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put on them; it then belongs to them.
- It is leather, and can catch fire.
