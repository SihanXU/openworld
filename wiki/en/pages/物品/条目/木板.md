{{信息框}}
The **Plank** is the material building uses most. [[墙|Walls]], [[地板|floors]], [[楼梯|stairs]], [[门|doors]], [[床|beds]], [[工作台|workbenches]], [[木桶|buckets]] and [[盾牌|shields]] all need planks; press {{键|G}} with one facing shallow water and you can also lay a tile of bridge.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Making**: with an [[斧|Axe]] on you, press {{键|C}} and choose “Plank”: 1 {{物品|木头}} makes 2. The axe is not used up. “All ×n” on the panel, or a click with {{键|Shift}}, turns every log in your inventory into planks at once.
- **Splitting**: lay a log on the ground and left-click it with the axe to split it into 2 planks.
- **Ready-made**: the “Garden Supplies” crate by the vegetable garden in [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] holds 4.
- **Taking apart**: things made of wood give back some of their planks when broken or taken apart; see [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Crafting and building**: for the recipes that use planks, see the “Makes” row above; so do the wall, floor, stairs, door, bed, workbench, crate, wooden chest and signpost in building mode ({{键|B}}). See [[制作|Crafting]] and [[建造|Building]].
- **Laying a bridge**: hold a plank and press {{键|G}} facing shallow water (“Lay Plank across shallow water”), and that tile of water becomes a wooden bridge; the plank is used up. Three planks have been taken from the little bridge at [[渡口|The Ford]]; lay three and you can cross the river. Deep water cannot be bridged.
- **Building a box**: lay a plank on another plank on the ground to make a wooden frame; keep laying them on the frame, up to four. Strike it once with a [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]]: two planks make a [[板条箱|crate]], four make a [[箱子|chest]].
- **Building a sign**: lay a plank on a stick on the ground to stand it up as a sign post, and strike it with the Smith Hammer to make a blank [[告示牌|sign]].
- **[[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]**: the second level asks for 20, the third for 30.
- Planks catch fire.
