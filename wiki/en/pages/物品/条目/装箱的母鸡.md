A **Crated Hen** is a [[chicken|chicken]] packed in a wooden crate, bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]. Hold it and press {{key|G}} at a tile of ground: the crate opens, the hen walks out, and the crate is gone.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Ranching” shelf, 1 crate for 40 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Let it out**: hold the crate and press {{key|G}} at a tile beside you (the label reads “Let Crated Hen out”): “The crate opens, and out it comes.” A grown chicken appears on that tile (or on the nearest free tile if it cannot stand there), and the crate disappears. It can be let out on any map.
- A chicken let out this way is like any other: it lays an [[Egg|Egg]] every {{value|下蛋间隔}} game hours, can be fed [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]], and can hatch a [[chick|chick]] together with a well-fed hen.
- It is as docile as the chickens already on the map: it never attacks anyone, runs when struck, does not chase people to peck them, and never keeps [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]] from opening because a fight is “still going on”. See [[chicken#Behavior|the chicken's behavior]].
- The crate is made of wood but does not burn. Other uses are not covered by the rules, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|the world learns]]).

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the trading stall at the Held Ground.
- 2026-10-08: A chicken let out is as docile as the chickens already there, and no longer chases people to peck them.
