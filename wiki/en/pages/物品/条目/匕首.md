The **Dagger** is the [[刺客|Assassin]]'s profession weapon. Only someone who has chosen the Assassin profession can fight with it, and the Assassin's talents work only with daggers. Its base attack is 2, the lowest among weapons alongside the [[棍棒|Club]]; the Assassin makes up the damage with the first strike, backstabs and combos.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Choosing a profession**: the first time you choose [[刺客|Assassin]] with [[布拉姆]], you receive an **Iron Dagger** (attack 3). Each profession's starting gear is given only once.
- **Smithing**: [[锻造|forge]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] carrying a {{物品|锻锤}}: 1 Iron Ingot or Steel Bar plus 1 plank (recipe level 1). The [[刺客|Assassin]]'s set includes a dagger, the “Shadowfang” (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- **Buying**: [[加里克]] and [[布拉姆]] sell daggers (see [[交易|Trading]]).
- No daggers are placed on the maps.
- Both of the Assassin's [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]], [[夜牙|Nightfang]] and [[镜中刃|Mirrorshard]], are daggers.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fighting**: hold it and press {{键|空格}} to stab in front of you, or left-click a monster within 1 cell. A dagger is a blade, and its blow counts as a “cut”. For the combat rules see [[战斗|Combat]].
- **Only an Assassin can use it**: held by another profession, or by someone who has not chosen one, its left click reads “Only an Assassin can fight with a dagger”, and it makes no attack.
- **The Assassin's talents**: {{技能|短兵精通}} gives daggers +1 attack; {{技能|暗影先手}}, {{技能|背刺}}, {{技能|连环刺击}}, {{技能|影中汲取}} and {{技能|死亡之舞}} all work only with daggers; see [[刺客|Assassin]].
- With no weapon drawn ({{键|X}}), a left click on a person does not attack; right-clicking a person with a dagger uses the dagger on them, which the rules do not define, and [[世界会学|the world learns]] answers.
