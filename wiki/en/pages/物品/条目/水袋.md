The **Waterskin** is a leather bag full of water. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

Under the rules a Waterskin works like a {{item|Water Bucket}}: it waters a crop, splashes onto something (putting out a fire, wetting it), or restores 2 health when drunk. Once used, an [[Empty Waterskin|Empty Waterskin]] is left, which can be filled again.
