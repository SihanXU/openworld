The **Waterskin** is a leather bag full of water. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

Under the rules a Waterskin works like a {{物品|一桶水}}: it waters a crop, splashes onto something (putting out a fire, wetting it), or restores 2 health when drunk. Once used, an [[空水袋|Empty Waterskin]] is left, which can be filled again.
