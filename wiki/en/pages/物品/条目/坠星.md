**Starfall** is the second of the [[游侠|Ranger]]'s [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]], a [[弓|Bow]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 10 and it shoots 8 cells, the longest range of any weapon, with a built-in 10% critical chance; its stigma power is also called “Falling Star”: every fifth hit is a sure critical.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, he leaves the first of the felling hero's profession's Legendary weapons that has not yet appeared in the world. The Ranger's first is [[疾风之弦|Galestring]], so Starfall appears only when a Ranger fells a second Seal-bearer.
- The only Seal-bearer that can be fought in the game at present is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], so Starfall cannot yet be obtained in a normal game.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Fighting**: only a Ranger can fight with it, and all the Ranger's talents work with it.
- **Critical hits**: the weapon has a built-in +10% critical chance.
- **Stigma power “Falling Star”**: every fifth hit is a sure critical, and then the count starts again.

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Ranger's second Legendary weapon.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Falling Star”.
