**Wheat** is the ears harvested from a ripe wheat field. No wheat grows on any map: you buy [[麦种|Wheat Seeds]] at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地]], sow them, water them until they grow, and harvest them. Wheat can be given to animals as fodder, or sold.

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Feed animals**: hold wheat and right-click a cow or chicken (or press {{键|G}} facing it); just like [[饲料|Animal Feed]], it becomes {{状态|吃饱了}}. A fed cow can be milked, and two fed animals of the same kind side by side will have young. See [[动物#喂食|feeding]].
- **Sell**: the trading stall buys it at 3 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] a portion.
- It cannot be ground into flour, nor made into [[面包|Bread]]. Wheat is dry and flammable, and catches fire faster than most things.

## Trivia

- In the rules ripe wheat is dry: one torch can burn a field of ripe wheat to ash, and the ash is just what you need to fertilize.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue; the rules could grow wheat, but there were no Wheat Seeds to get.
- 2026-10-08: Wheat also became animal fodder; the trading stall at the Held Ground sells Wheat Seeds and buys wheat.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
