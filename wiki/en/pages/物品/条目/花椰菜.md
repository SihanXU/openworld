A **Cauliflower** is a vegetable you can grow; eating one restores 5 [[Satiety|satiety]]. The seeds are sold only at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; the cauliflowers you harvest can be sold back to the stall for 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[Cauliflower Seeds|Cauliflower Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 cauliflowers (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 cauliflower and 1 seed. See [[cauliflower (crops)|the cauliflower crop]] and [[Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[Cauliflower Seeds|Cauliflower Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], 3 for 20 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell cauliflowers themselves; it only buys them. The garden in [[Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no cauliflower.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Cauliflower?” Confirm, and it restores 5 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}} to open it, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the [[trading stall|trading stall]] at [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; the seeds are bought on the stall's “Farming” shelf.
