**Raw Beef** is the meat a felled [[cow|cow]] leaves. Eaten, it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]]; roasted over a fire it becomes a [[Steak|Steak]], which restores 8. [[Marta|Marta]]'s quest asks for three Raw Beef.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Cows**: there are cows on [[The Grassland|The Grassland]] and [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]]. A felled cow leaves 2 Raw Beef, which fall to the ground and do not fly into your inventory by themselves; press {{key|E}} to pick them up. Once the Grassland's cows are all felled, more slowly come back; see [[Animals|Animals]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, restoring 3 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]] (“Cook Raw Beef over campfire”), or press {{key|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes a Steak. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Quest**: the side quest [[Something for the Pot|Something for the Pot]] from [[Marta|Marta]] in [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] asks for 3 Raw Beef and 2 [[Egg|eggs]], taken when you hand in the quest.
- What happens when you right-click a wolf with raw meat is not defined by the rules; [[The World Learns|the world learns]] answers it.
