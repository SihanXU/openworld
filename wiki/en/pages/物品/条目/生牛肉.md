{{信息框}}
**Raw Beef** is the meat a felled [[牛|cow]] leaves. Eaten, it restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]]; roasted over a fire it becomes a [[牛排|Steak]], which restores 8. [[玛尔塔|Marta]]'s quest asks for three Raw Beef.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Cows**: there are cows on [[草原|The Grassland]] and [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]]. A felled cow leaves 2 Raw Beef, which fall to the ground and do not fly into your inventory by themselves; press {{键|E}} to pick them up. Once the Grassland's cows are all felled, more slowly come back; see [[动物|Animals]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, restoring 3 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]] (“Cook Raw Beef over campfire”), or press {{键|G}} facing the fire: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes a Steak. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Quest**: the side quest [[给锅里添点东西|Something for the Pot]] from [[玛尔塔|Marta]] in [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] asks for 3 Raw Beef and 2 [[鸡蛋|eggs]], taken when you hand in the quest.
- What happens when you right-click a wolf with raw meat is not defined by the rules; [[世界会学|the world learns]] answers it.
