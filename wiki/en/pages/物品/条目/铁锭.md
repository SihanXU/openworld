An **Iron Ingot** is a bar of refined iron. Hold {{item|Iron Ore}} and right-click a lit [[forge|forge]], and the ore in your hand melts into an iron ingot. It is worth more than the ore, and can be hammered into gear on an [[Smithing|anvil]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smelting**: hold iron ore and right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Iron Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The lump of iron ore in your hand becomes an iron ingot, and the forge gives off a puff of smoke. One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting. See [[forge#Smelting|Smelting]].
- The only forge in the game is the one in front of the smithy in [[Emberfall|Emberfall]], lit from the start. When the forge has been put out, you only get the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”, and the ore stays as it is; light it first with a {{item|Torch}}.
- Only a forge can smelt it: clicking other fires such as a [[campfire|campfire]] or a [[brazier|brazier]] with iron ore is not covered by the rules (the message carries a “?”). Pressing {{key|G}} at the forge only puts the ore down beside it.
- No ready-made iron ingots are placed on any map, and no one carries one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smithing**: at an [[anvil|anvil]], with a smith hammer on you, hammer it into weapons, armor, tools or jewelry by the [[Smithing|smithing]] recipes; what you make is of this metal.
- **Sell**: an iron ingot is worth 15 (a lump of iron ore is 6); a merchant gives about 7 {{item|Dawnmark}} for it, up or down depending on what he thinks of you (see [[Trading|Trading]]). The [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] does not buy it.
- An iron ingot is a conductive metal and does not burn.

## Trivia

- The item catalogue says iron ingots “often appear in smithies”, but no code uses that weighting (see [[Known Issues|Known Issues]]).

## History

- 2026-10-09: Added to the game: a lit forge smelts iron ore into an iron ingot.
- 2026-10-10: It can be hammered into gear on an anvil; the forge can also smelt in batches by recipe.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
