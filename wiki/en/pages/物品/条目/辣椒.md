A **Hot Pepper** is a vegetable you can grow; eating one restores 1 [[Satiety|satiety]]. Hot pepper is perennial: after a harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; the peppers you harvest can be sold back to the stall for 4 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[Hot Pepper Seeds|Hot Pepper Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 peppers (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 pepper and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[hot pepper (crops)|the hot pepper crop]] and [[Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[Hot Pepper Seeds|Hot Pepper Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], 3 for 14 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell peppers themselves; it only buys them. The garden in [[Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no peppers.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Hot Pepper?” Confirm, and it restores 1 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}} to open it, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 4 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.
