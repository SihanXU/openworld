{{信息框}}
**Slime Gel** is a lump of green gel, still faintly quivering. Every kind of [[史莱姆|slime]] leaves it when felled, and the [[史莱姆王|Slime King]] leaves a great heap.

## Source

{{来源}}

- [[绿史莱姆|Green slime]], [[火焰史莱姆|fire slime]], [[冰霜史莱姆|frost slime]], [[雷电史莱姆|spark slime]], [[水史莱姆|water slime]]: 1 each.
- [[史莱姆王|Slime King]]: 4.

## Uses

{{用途}}

At present it can only be sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]].

## History

- 2026-10-19: Added to the game as the slimes' drop.
