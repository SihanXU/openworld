**Slime Gel** is a lump of green gel, still faintly quivering. Every kind of [[green slime|slime]] leaves it when felled, and the [[Slime King|Slime King]] leaves a great heap.

## Source

{{sources}}

- [[green slime|Green slime]], [[fire slime|fire slime]], [[frost slime|frost slime]], [[spark slime|spark slime]], [[water slime|water slime]]: 1 each.
- [[Slime King|Slime King]]: 4.

## Uses

{{uses}}

At present it can only be sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]].
