The **Golden Carp** is a golden carp that now and then takes the hook while you fish, whatever the hour, the weather or the water. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]], and the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] buys it for 30 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[鱼竿|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click water within {{数值|鱼竿距离}} tiles); with [[鱼饵|Bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time, without bait 35%.
- **When**: at any time, in any water. Each time something bites, there is a 6% chance in shallow water and 10% in deep water that it is a golden carp; from 05:00 to 07:59 the Dawnscale is checked first, which makes these about 5.8% and 9.7%.
- When you land one: “Gold flashes in the water — a golden carp!” The fish goes straight into your pack.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each later one only if the earlier ones missed: [[晨鳞鱼|Dawnscale]] → **Golden Carp** → [[泡烂的靴子|Soggy Boot]] → [[鲶鱼|Catfish]] → [[鳗鱼|Eel]] → [[狗鱼|Pike]] → [[太阳鱼|Sunfish]] → [[河鲈|Perch]] → [[生鱼|Raw Fish]].
- Outside the early morning, each baited cast has about 4.2% in shallow water and 7% in deep water; unbaited, about 2.1% and 3.5%.
- After a cast, that water is {{状态|搅浑}} for 10 seconds, and casting there again in the meantime only gives the message “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” You can keep fishing in other water. See [[钓鱼|Fishing]].
- The trading stall does not sell golden carp; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Golden Carp?” Confirm, and it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]].
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or face the fire and press {{键|G}}: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[烤鱼|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish roasts into the same Grilled Fish. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}}, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 30 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.

## History
