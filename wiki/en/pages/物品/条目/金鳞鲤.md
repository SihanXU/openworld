The **Golden Carp** is a golden carp that now and then takes the hook while you fish, whatever the hour, the weather or the water. Eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]], and the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] buys it for 30 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Fishing**: cast a [[Fishing Rod|Fishing Rod]] into the water (hold the rod and left-click water within {{value|鱼竿距离}} tiles); with [[Bait|Bait]] on, something bites 70% of the time, without bait 35%.
- **When**: at any time, in any water. Each time something bites, there is a 6% chance in shallow water and 10% in deep water that it is a golden carp; from 05:00 to 07:59 the Dawnscale is checked first, which makes these about 5.8% and 9.7%.
- When you land one: “Gold flashes in the water — a golden carp!” The fish goes straight into your pack.
- Once something bites, the game checks the catches one by one in this order, each later one only if the earlier ones missed: [[Dawnscale|Dawnscale]] → **Golden Carp** → [[Soggy Boot|Soggy Boot]] → [[Catfish|Catfish]] → [[Eel|Eel]] → [[Pike|Pike]] → [[Sunfish|Sunfish]] → [[Perch|Perch]] → [[Raw Fish|Raw Fish]].
- Outside the early morning, each baited cast has about 4.2% in shallow water and 7% in deep water; unbaited, about 2.1% and 3.5%.
- After a cast, that water is {{status|stirred up}} for 10 seconds, and casting there again in the meantime only gives the message “The water is stirred up. Let it settle.” You can keep fishing in other water. See [[Fishing|Fishing]].
- The trading stall does not sell golden carp; it only buys them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Golden Carp?” Confirm, and it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]].
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or face the fire and press {{key|G}}: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes [[Grilled Fish|Grilled Fish]]. Every fish roasts into the same Grilled Fish. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}}, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 30 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the fish pond, bait and the trading stall at [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; fishing catches different fish by hour, weather and depth of water.
