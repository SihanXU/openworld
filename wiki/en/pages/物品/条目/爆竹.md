The **Firecracker** is a string of paper-rolled crackers. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

The tooltip says the Firecracker “Goes off when it catches fire”.

- **Going off**: a Firecracker is made of paper and goes off the moment it catches fire: everything in the four tiles directly next to it, above, below, left and right (people, creatures, walls, furniture…), takes a fire blow of power 1 (less defense, at least 1); anything flammable and not wet in those tiles catches fire, and the Firecracker itself is gone, leaving no [[Ash|Ash]]. Its own tile and the diagonal tiles are not hit.
- **How to light it**: left-click the Firecracker on the ground with a fire source such as a [[Torch|Torch]]; throw it into a lit [[campfire|campfire]]; or let fire reach it (spreading fire, a blow carrying the fire element). A wet Firecracker touched by fire is only dried.
- Whoever lights it standing right next to it is also hit and catches fire; lighting it from a diagonal tile is safe.
- The blast has only an eighth of the force of a [[Blackpowder Bomb|Blackpowder Bomb]]. See [[Elements#Fire|Fire]].
