An **Apple** is a fruit; eating one restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]]. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, there are no apple trees, and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Apple?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety.
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}, and you can trade it to [[托宾|Tobin]] for the Frost Wand.
