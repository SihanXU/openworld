A **Circlet** is a slender crown ring, a valuable, made of silver, gold or ringsteel. It can be worn on the head, +1 defense. None is placed on the maps and no quest gives one; the only way to get one is to [[Smithing|forge]] the [[Mage|Mage]] set's **Starweave Circlet** at an [[anvil|anvil]] carrying a {{item|Smith Hammer}}: 1 {{item|Gold Ingot}}, with {{skill|Jeweler}} learned first, with a built-in −3% skill cooldown (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, and it goes on your head; use it again to take it off. It can also be changed in the equipment slots of the inventory ({{key|I}}). Whatever was on your head before (a [[Helmet|Helmet]], a [[Hat|Hat]], the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]]) comes off.
- The +1 defense is the form's number, then multiplied by the [[Substances|substance]] and [[Quality|quality]] (see [[Armour#How defense is counted|how defense is calculated]]).
- The rules say nothing of other uses, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]).

## Trivia

- A circlet and the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]] are not the same kind of thing. The Crown of Thorns is a relic that wears down whoever wears it; a circlet is only a valuable.
- Of the circlet's three substances, ringsteel is an ancient rune metal; see [[Substances|Substances]].
