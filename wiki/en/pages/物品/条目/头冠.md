A **Circlet** is a slender crown ring, a valuable, made of silver, gold or ringsteel. It can be worn on the head, +1 defense. None is placed on the maps and no quest gives one; the only way to get one is to [[锻造|forge]] the [[法师|Mage]] set's **Starweave Circlet** at an [[铁砧|anvil]] carrying a {{物品|锻锤}}: 1 {{物品|金锭}}, with {{技能|珠宝匠}} learned first, with a built-in −3% skill cooldown (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, and it goes on your head; use it again to take it off. It can also be changed in the equipment slots of the inventory ({{键|I}}). Whatever was on your head before (a [[头盔|Helmet]], a [[帽子|Hat]], the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]) comes off.

- The rules say nothing of other uses, and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]).

## Trivia

- A circlet and the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]] are not the same kind of thing. The Crown of Thorns is a relic that wears down whoever wears it; a circlet is only a valuable.
- Of the circlet's three substances, ringsteel is an ancient rune metal; see [[材质|Substances]].
