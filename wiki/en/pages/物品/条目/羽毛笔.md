A **Quill** is a pen for writing. With an [[墨水瓶|Ink Pot]] on you, right-click a blank [[告示牌|sign]] with it and you write on it. But you cannot get a quill, or ink, in normal play: none is placed on any map, they cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Write on a sign**: a sign made with the [[锻锤|Smith Hammer]] is blank; looked at empty-handed, it only says “It is blank.” Right-click it with the quill in hand (“Write on the sign”) and, with an ink pot in your pack: “You write on the sign.” From then on, whoever reads it sees “Made by hand.” You cannot type your own words yet; what gets written is always this one line.
- Without ink, the rules do not say what happens: it is something the world has not yet seen.
- **On anything else**: in the rules, the quill is “a hand holding a pen”: using it on anything else is the same as handling that thing empty-handed with {{键|E}}.
- The quill burns.
