A **Quill** is a pen for writing. With an [[Ink Pot|Ink Pot]] on you, right-click a blank [[sign|sign]] with it and you write on it. But you cannot get a quill, or ink, in normal play: none is placed on any map, they cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives them.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Write on a sign**: a sign made with the [[Smith Hammer|Smith Hammer]] is blank; looked at empty-handed, it only says “It is blank.” Right-click it with the quill in hand (“Write on the sign”) and, with an ink pot in your pack: “You write on the sign.” From then on, whoever reads it sees “Made by hand.” You cannot type your own words yet; what gets written is always this one line.
- Without ink, the rules do not say what happens: it is something the world has not yet seen.
- **On anything else**: in the rules, the quill is “a hand holding a pen”: using it on anything else is the same as handling that thing empty-handed with {{key|E}}.
- The quill burns.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue; with ink, the quill writes on a sign.
- 2026-10-08: Holding one no longer makes you glow.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
