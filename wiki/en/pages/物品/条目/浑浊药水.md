The **Murky Potion** is a flask of cloudy purple potion. It carries the “unknown” tag, but under the rules it is just a potion: drinking it restores 1 [[Health|health]]. It cannot be obtained in normal play: none lies on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Drink Murky Potion?” Confirm, and it restores 1 health.
- **Give someone else a drink**: hold it and right-click someone, and they recover 1 health.
