A **Wine Bottle** is a bottle of wine. It counts as a drink: drinking it restores 4 [[Health|health]] but no [[Satiety|satiety]]. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink Wine Bottle?” Confirm, and it restores 4 health.
- **Give someone a drink**: hold it and right-click someone (“Give Wine Bottle to … to drink”), and they recover 4 health.
- In the rules, drinking does not make anyone drunk.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue.
