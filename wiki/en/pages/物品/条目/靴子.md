**Boots** are clothing worn on the feet. Ordinary boots have no defense of their own; both pairs of boots in the game are named, and each carries its own defense.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traveller's Boots | Common | Defense 1 | Reward of the main quest [[Down from the Mountain]], yours as soon as you reach [[Firstlight Village]] |
| Thornproof Boots | Fine (leather) | Defense 2 | Placed in [[Thornwood Hollow|Thornwood Hollow]] |

**Making**: with {{skill|Leatherworker}} learned, make them from leather at a [[workbench|workbench]]; each of the four professions' sets has a pair (Shadowfang Boots, Oathguard Greaves, Windrunner Boots, Starweave Slippers), [[Smithing|smithed]] at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]). [[Fennick|Fennick]] also sells boots (see [[Trading|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Put on**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; use them again to take them off. You can also equip them from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. See [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- Boots made of leather or cloth burn.
