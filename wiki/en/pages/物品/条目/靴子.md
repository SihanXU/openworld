{{信息框}}
**Boots** are clothing worn on the feet. Ordinary boots have no defense of their own; both pairs of boots in the game are named, and each carries its own defense.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traveller's Boots | Common | Defense 1 | Reward of the main quest [[下山]], yours as soon as you reach [[初原村]] |
| Thornproof Boots | Fine (leather) | Defense 2 | Placed in [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]] |

**Making**: with {{技能|皮匠}} learned, make them from leather at a [[工作台|workbench]]; each of the four professions' sets has a pair (Shadowfang Boots, Oathguard Greaves, Windrunner Boots, Starweave Slippers), [[锻造|smithed]] at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]). [[芬尼克|Fennick]] also sells boots (see [[交易|Trading]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Put on**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; use them again to take them off. You can also equip them from the [[背包|inventory]]. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- Boots made of leather or cloth burn.
