{{信息框}}
A **Ring** is a piece of jewelry worn on a finger. Every ring adds 1 attack, whatever its material or quality. You have two ring slots, so you can wear two at once.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mia's Ring | Common (silver) | Attack +1 | In the briars on the east side of [[荆棘谷|Thornwood Hollow]]; the quest [[米娅的戒指|Mia's Ring]] asks you to return it to [[米娅|Mia]] |
| Sister Maren's Ring | [[遗誓|Vowbound]] (silver) | Attack +3, defense +1, lifesteal 1, vow “Mercy” | Lies in [[荆棘骸骨堂|The Ossuary of the Thorn]] |
| Surveyor's Ring | Fine | Attack +1 | Reward of the side quest [[日之度量|The Measure of Days]] |
| Brin's Bone Ring | Fine | Attack +1 | Reward of the side quest [[草原守夜|Night on the Grassland]] |

**Smithing**: once you have learned {{技能|珠宝匠}}, [[锻造|smith]] one at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: an iron ingot or a gold ingot makes a ring. Each of the four professions' sets includes a ring, part of that set's jewelry set (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. The first ring goes on an empty ring slot; when both are full, it replaces the ring in the first slot. See [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Lifesteal and Mercy**: Sister Maren's Ring drains 1 health with every hit, and it also carries the [[遗誓|vow]] “Mercy”: hitting an enemy below 30% health drains 2 more.
- **Quest**: Mia's Ring is a quest item, and it is taken from you when you return it to Mia. You can wear it before then.

## Trivia

- The [[婚戒|Wedding Band]] is a different item and does not count as a ring.
