A **Turnip** is a root vegetable you can grow; eating one restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The Firstlight Village garden**: the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] has a row of turnips, and the bottom one is already ripe and can be harvested at once.
- **Grow your own**: sow [[Turnip Seeds|Turnip Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 turnips and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 turnip and 1 seed. See [[Farming]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Turnip?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.

## History
