**Wheat Seeds** are the seeds of [[麦子|wheat]]. Like other seeds they can be sown on farmland and grow into wheat. No Wheat Seeds are placed on any map, and no wheat grows there; you buy them at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地]].

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sow**: hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the wheat seeds.” It becomes a wheat sprout. Each time it is watered, it grows one stage two game hours later in the open: sprout, green wheat, ripe wheat. See [[农耕]].
- **Harvest**: reaping ripe wheat with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 [[麦子|Wheat]] and 2 Wheat Seeds; picking it by hand gives 1 and 1.
- The “Farming” page of build mode ({{键|B}}) has only the fourteen vegetables, not wheat: Wheat Seeds have to be sown from the hand.
- Ripe wheat is dry and flammable, and catches fire faster than other crops.
