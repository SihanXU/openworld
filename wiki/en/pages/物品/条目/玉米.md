**Corn** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]]. Corn is perennial: after harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]]; the corn you harvest can be sold back to the stall, 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] an ear.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[玉米种子|Corn Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice to grow; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 ears of corn (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, and picking by hand gives 1 ear and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[玉米（作物）|corn (crop)]] and [[农耕|Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[玉米种子|Corn Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[货摊|trading stall]], 3 for 18 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].
- The stall does not sell corn itself; it only buys it. The vegetable garden in [[初原村|Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no corn.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Corn?” Confirm, and it restores 3 satiety. It cannot be grilled.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}} to open it, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 5 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] an ear. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
