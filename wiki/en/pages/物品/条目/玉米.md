**Corn** is a vegetable you can grow; eating it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]]. Corn is perennial: after harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|The Held Ground]]; the corn you harvest can be sold back to the stall, 5 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] an ear.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[Corn Seeds|Corn Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice to grow; reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 ears of corn (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, and picking by hand gives 1 ear and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[corn (crops)|corn (crop)]] and [[Farming|Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[Corn Seeds|Corn Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], 3 for 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
- The stall does not sell corn itself; it only buys it. The vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village|Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no corn.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Corn?” Confirm, and it restores 3 satiety. It cannot be grilled.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}} to open it, and sell it on the “Sell” shelf for 5 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] an ear. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any other food, you can give it to someone with {{key|G}}.
