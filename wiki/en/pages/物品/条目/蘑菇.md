A **Mushroom** is an edible fungus that grows in the woods. Eaten raw it restores only 1 [[Satiety|satiety]]; roasted into a [[Roasted Mushroom|Roasted Mushroom]] it restores 4, so roasting makes a big difference. It is not the same thing as the [[Frostcap|Frostcap]] of [[The Forest|the Forest]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The wild**: each “Deep Woods” map of [[The Wild|the Wild]] has 4 mushrooms scattered on it. They are placed only once, when the map is generated, and do not grow back once picked.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it restores 1 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or face the fire and press {{key|G}}: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes a Roasted Mushroom. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].
