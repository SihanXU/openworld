{{信息框}}
A **Mushroom** is an edible fungus that grows in the woods. Eaten raw it restores only 1 [[饱食度|satiety]]; roasted into a [[烤蘑菇|Roasted Mushroom]] it restores 4, so roasting makes a big difference. It is not the same thing as the [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]] of [[森林|the Forest]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The wild**: each “Deep Woods” map of [[荒野|the Wild]] has 4 mushrooms scattered on it. They are placed only once, when the map is generated, and do not grow back once picked.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it restores 1 satiety. Eating it raw does no harm.
- **Roast**: hold it and right-click a lit [[营火|campfire]], or face the fire and press {{键|G}}: “It sizzles and cooks.” It becomes a Roasted Mushroom. See [[烹饪|Cooking]].
