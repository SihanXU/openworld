The **Blackpowder Bomb** is a bomb packed with blackpowder. It cannot be obtained in normal play: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip says the bomb “bursts when it catches fire”.

- **Bursting**: the bomb bursts the moment it catches fire. Everything in the four tiles directly above, below, left and right of it (people, creatures, walls, furniture…) takes a fire blow of force 8 (minus defense); anything in those tiles that can burn and is not wet is set alight; the bomb itself is gone and leaves no [[灰烬|Ash]]. Creatures and people caught in the blast also catch fire. Its own tile and the diagonal tiles are not hit.
- **How to light it**: left-click the bomb on the ground with a fire source such as a [[火把|Torch]]; throw it into a lit [[营火|campfire]]; or let fire reach it (spreading fire, a blow carrying the fire element). A wet bomb touched by fire is only dried.
- Whoever lights it standing right beside it is caught in the blast too; lighting it from a diagonal tile is safe.
- It is eight times as strong as a [[爆竹|Firecracker]]. The other thing that bursts into flame is something soaked in oil; see [[一瓶灯油]] and [[元素#油|oil]].
