A **Scroll** is a roll of paper with an arcane air about it. It can carry an [[Elements|element]], which adds an element word to its name (“Scroll of Frost”, for example). It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

A scroll cannot cast spells. Nothing is written on it: holding it and left-clicking it on yourself (reading it) is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]). Only documents with writing on them can be read for their content; see [[Documents|Documents]]. Scrolls can catch fire.
