**Fireweed** is a red herb with a breath of fire about it. [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]'s herbalist [[雷恩|Wren]] needs 3 to brew the [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]]: “Fireweed. It only grows where the ground remembers fire.”

## Source

{{来源}}

3 lie on the grass of [[初原村|Firstlight Village]]; once picked, they slowly grow back, up to 3 at a time.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Brewing**: in the Emberfall line, hold Fireweed and press {{键|G}} facing Wren to hand it over, one at a time; once you have handed over 3, and then 3 [[霜盖菇|Frostcap]], press {{键|E}} facing him with empty hands, and he brews two Fireproof Draughts. The Emberfall and dragon line is the old main story from before Chapter 1; see [[余烬镇与魔龙|Emberfall and the Dragon]].
- Fireweed cannot be eaten. It catches fire.
