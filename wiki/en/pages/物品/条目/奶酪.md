**Cheese** is a food; eating it restores 4 [[饱食度|satiety]]. There are only two pieces of cheese in the game, a quest reward from [[布琳]].

## Source

{{来源}}

The side quest [[草原守夜]] of [[布琳]], the herder on [[草原|the Grassland]]: fell 3 wandering undead on the Grassland after dark, and when you hand in the quest she gives you two pieces of cheese and a bone ring: “Here, cheese from the last of the good milk. Thank you.”

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Cheese?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety.
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{键|G}}.

- 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue.
