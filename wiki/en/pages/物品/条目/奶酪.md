**Cheese** is a food; eating it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]]. There are only two pieces of cheese in the game, a quest reward from [[Brin]].

## Source

{{sources}}

The side quest [[Night on the Grassland]] of [[Brin]], the herder on [[The Grassland|the Grassland]]: fell 3 wandering undead on the Grassland after dark, and when you hand in the quest she gives you two pieces of cheese and a bone ring: “Here, cheese from the last of the good milk. Thank you.”

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Cheese?” Confirm, and it restores 4 satiety.
- Like other food, it can be handed to someone with {{key|G}}.
