**Fertilizer** is farm manure sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]. Work it into the soil of a crop that is still growing, and when you reap that crop with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] you get 1 more of its produce. It does exactly the same as [[Ash|Ash]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Farming” shelf, 3 for 10 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Fertilize**: hold the fertilizer and right-click a crop that is not yet ripe (a sprout or a young plant; the label reads “Fertilize …”): “You work fertilizer into the soil.” The fertilizer is used up, the crop gets {{status|fertilized}}, and it gives 1 more at harvest (2 becomes 3).
- **Once per plant**.
- **Only reaping with a sickle benefits**: picking by hand gives only 1, fertilized or not.
- **Perennial crops** ([[Tomato|tomato]], [[Corn|corn]], [[Blueberries|blueberries]], [[Strawberry|strawberry]], [[Hot Pepper|hot pepper]], [[Eggplant|eggplant]], [[berry bush|berry bush]]) spend the fertilizer at their first harvest and must be fertilized again.
- Wheat, herbs, berry bushes and every vegetable can be fertilized while they are growing. Using fertilizer on a crop already fertilized, on a ripe crop, on an [[oak|oak sapling]] or on anything else is not covered by the rules, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|the world learns]]).
- For the full rules see [[Farming#Fertilizing|Fertilizing]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, along with the trading stall at the Held Ground.
- 2026-10-08: The message when fertilizing became “You work fertilizer into the soil.”
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
