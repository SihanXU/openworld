**Emberheart** is the [[法师|Mage]]'s first [[传说武器|legendary weapon]], a [[法杖|Staff]] of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality. Its attack is 11, it reaches 5 tiles, and it carries the fire [[元素|element]]; its stigma power “Heart of Embers” gives each hit a 25% chance of setting the target alight.

## Source

{{来源}}

- When a [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] falls, if the one who felled them is a Mage and no Emberheart has yet appeared in this world, they leave Emberheart behind, along with 4 {{物品|铁矿石}}.
- For now the only Seal-bearer you can fight in the game is [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|The White Knight Alberic]], so a Mage who fells him on [[东大路|The East Road]] gets Emberheart. There is only one in each world.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Casting**: like an ordinary staff, only a Mage can cast with it, and all of the Mage's talents apply to it.
- **Fire element**: hitting something that burns sets it on fire, and hitting raw food on the ground cooks it; a fire monster hit by the fire element instead recovers 2 health. See [[元素|Elements]].
- **Stigma power “Heart of Embers”**: each hit has a 25% chance of setting the target alight ({{状态|燃烧}} for 3 seconds).

## History

- 2026-10-05: Added to the game as the Mage's legendary weapon, left by a fallen Seal-bearer.
- 2026-10-07: Its quality was renamed “Stigmatic”, and it gained the stigma power “Heart of Embers”.
