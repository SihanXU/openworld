{{信息框}}
**Bread** is the most common food in the game. Eating one loaf restores 5 [[饱食度|satiety]]. It cannot be grown or made, but people hand it out everywhere: [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] gives you one when you first meet, [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]'s [[玛拉|Mara]] gives two, and six quests include it among their rewards.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Hesper**: the first time you reach [[渡口|the Ford]], Hesper gives you a loaf after she has finished talking (“(Still in your pack: the end of a travel loaf.)”).
- **Mara**: in the council scene at Emberfall, Mara gives you two loaves: “Nobody fights hungry.”
- **The chest at the Ford**: the camp chest holds one, and it is Hesper's. If she sees you take it, she says “Hey — that is mine!”
- **The Old Barrow**: a “Loaf marked with ash” lies in [[古墓|the Old Barrow]].
- The daily commission [[给火堆添柴]] gives one too, and you can claim it every day.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Bread?” Confirm, and it restores 5 satiety. Eating does not restore health directly; health only slowly comes back while satiety is above 90%; see [[生命值|Health]].
- **Trade**: hold it and press {{键|G}} at [[托宾|Tobin]], and he takes it and gives you the frost [[魔杖|wand]] from his cart (once only). Any food will do.
- **Give it away**: press {{键|G}} to hand it to someone; they take it and say something.
- Bread burns: thrown into a fire it burns to [[灰烬|Ash]].

{{剧透|The loaf in the barrow}}
The “Loaf marked with ash” was left by [[灰衣人|the Grey Man]] for [[莉娜|Lina]] and [[奥斯卡|Oskar]], trapped in the Old Barrow. According to the design document (docs/chapter-1.md), the ash mark is the sign of [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]].
{{/剧透}}
