**Bread** is the most common food in the game. Eating one loaf restores 5 [[Satiety|satiety]]. It cannot be grown or made, but people hand it out everywhere: [[Hesper|Hesper]] gives you one when you first meet, [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]'s [[Mara|Mara]] gives two, and six quests include it among their rewards.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Hesper**: the first time you reach [[The Ford|the Ford]], Hesper gives you a loaf after she has finished talking (“(Still in your pack: the end of a travel loaf.)”).
- **Mara**: in the council scene at Emberfall, Mara gives you two loaves: “Nobody fights hungry.”
- **The chest at the Ford**: the camp chest holds one, and it is Hesper's. If she sees you take it, she says “Hey — that is mine!”
- **The Old Barrow**: a “Loaf marked with ash” lies in [[The Old Barrow|the Old Barrow]].
- The daily commission [[Wood for the Fire]] gives one too, and you can claim it every day.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Bread?” Confirm, and it restores 5 satiety. Eating does not restore health directly; health only slowly comes back while satiety is above 90%; see [[Health|Health]].
- **Trade**: hold it and press {{key|G}} at [[Tobin|Tobin]], and he takes it and gives you the frost [[Wand|wand]] from his cart (once only). Any food will do.
- **Give it away**: press {{key|G}} to hand it to someone; they take it and say something.
- Bread burns: thrown into a fire it burns to [[Ash|Ash]].

{{spoiler|The loaf in the barrow}}
The “Loaf marked with ash” was left by [[The Grey Man|the Grey Man]] for [[Lina|Lina]] and [[Oskar|Oskar]], trapped in the Old Barrow. According to the design document (docs/chapter-1.md), the ash mark is the sign of [[The Ashen Watch|the Ashen Watch]].
