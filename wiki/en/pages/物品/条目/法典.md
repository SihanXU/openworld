{{信息框}}
The **Tome** is a thick leather-bound book held in the off hand. It is the [[法师|Mage]]'s off-hand piece, as the [[盾牌|Shield]] is the [[战士|Warrior]]'s: no defense, skill cooldowns −5%. Leather covers, paper pages.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you and press {{键|C}}: 1 {{物品|皮革}}, 2 {{物品|布匹}} and 2 {{物品|羽毛}} make a **Starweave Tome**, recipe level 3. Its [[品质|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and its [[词条|affixes]] are rolled when it is made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- None lie on any map, and merchants do not sell it.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Held in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it to the off hand from the [[背包|inventory]]. The off-hand slot to the right of the hotbar shows it.
- **Mages only**: held by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, it does nothing: the off-hand slot turns grey, and “Mage only” in the tooltip is red. See [[装备#职业专属|Profession-only gear]].
- **Skill cooldowns**: the wait after an active skill is cast is 5% shorter (see [[技能#主动技能与技能栏|Active skills and the skill bar]]).
- **Not part of a set**: neither the main hand nor the off hand counts toward any set, so the Starweave Tome has only its own stats (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).
- It is not a book you can read, nor a [[技能书|Skill Book]].
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put in their hand; it then belongs to them.
- Leather and paper: it can catch fire.
