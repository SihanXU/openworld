The **Tome** is a thick leather-bound book held in the off hand. It is the [[Mage|Mage]]'s off-hand piece, as the [[Shield|Shield]] is the [[Warrior|Warrior]]'s: no defense, skill cooldowns −5%. Leather covers, paper pages.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Smithing**: stand by an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you and press {{key|C}}: 1 {{item|Leather Hide}}, 2 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}} and 2 {{item|Feather}} make a **Starweave Tome**, recipe level 3. Its [[Quality|quality]] depends on the smith's level and smithing skills, and its [[Affixes|affixes]] are rolled when it is made (how many depends on the quality, from 2 at Crude to 5 at Masterwork).
- None lie on any map, and merchants do not sell it.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Held in the off hand**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, or equip it to the off hand from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. The off-hand slot to the right of the hotbar shows it.
- **Mages only**: held by any other profession, or by someone who has not chosen a profession yet, it does nothing: the off-hand slot turns grey, and “Mage only” in the tooltip is red. See [[Equipment#Profession-only|Profession-only gear]].
- **Skill cooldowns**: the wait after an active skill is cast is 5% shorter (see [[Skills#Active skills and the skill bar|Active skills and the skill bar]]).
- **Not part of a set**: neither the main hand nor the off hand counts toward any set, so the Starweave Tome has only its own stats (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).
- It is not a book you can read, nor a [[Skill Book|Skill Book]].
- **Dressing someone else**: hold it and right-click someone, and it is put in their hand; it then belongs to them.
- Leather and paper: it can catch fire.
