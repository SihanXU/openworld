A **Carrot** is a vegetable you can grow; eating one restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Firstlight Village garden**: the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] has a row of carrots, and the bottom one is already ripe and can be harvested at once.
- **Grow your own**: sow [[胡萝卜种子|Carrot Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 carrots and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 carrot and 1 seed. See [[农耕]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat Carrot?” Confirm, and it restores 3 satiety. It cannot be roasted.
- Like any food, you can give it to someone with {{键|G}}.
