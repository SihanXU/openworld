{{信息框}}
An **Earring** is a piece of jewelry worn on the ear. It adds no attack or defense; what it adds is [[等级与经验|experience]]: an ordinary earring raises the experience you gain by 10%.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Kneeling Pilgrim's Earring | Fine (silver) | Experience +10% | Placed by the kneeling stone on [[东大路|the East Road]] |
| Mia's Ribbon Earring | Fine | Experience +10% | Reward of the side quest [[米娅的戒指]] |
| Star-glass Earring | Masterwork | Experience +20% | Reward of the side quest [[守夜人的日志]] |

**Smithing**: each of the four professions' sets has an earring; once you have learned {{技能|珠宝匠}}, [[锻造|smith]] it at an [[铁砧|anvil]] with a {{物品|锻锤}} on you: 1 Gold Ingot (see [[套装|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[背包|inventory]]. You have only one earring slot; see [[装备|Equipment]].
- **Experience bonus**: all the experience you gain (from felling things and from quest rewards) is multiplied by (1 + all experience bonuses). The earring's bonus is added together with Lumi's [[恩赐|boon]] “Crown's Insight” and the −50% of the [[荆棘冠|Crown of Thorns]]; the total never falls below the normal rate.
