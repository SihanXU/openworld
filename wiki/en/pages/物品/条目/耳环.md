An **Earring** is a piece of jewelry worn on the ear. It adds no attack or defense; what it adds is [[Levels and Experience|experience]]: an ordinary earring raises the experience you gain by 10%.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Name | Quality | Stats | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Kneeling Pilgrim's Earring | Fine (silver) | Experience +10% | Placed by the kneeling stone on [[The East Road|the East Road]] |
| Mia's Ribbon Earring | Fine | Experience +10% | Reward of the side quest [[Mia's Ring]] |
| Star-glass Earring | Masterwork | Experience +20% | Reward of the side quest [[The Watchman's Log]] |

**Smithing**: each of the four professions' sets has an earring; once you have learned {{skill|Jeweler}}, [[Smithing|smith]] it at an [[anvil|anvil]] with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} on you: 1 Gold Ingot (see [[Gear Sets|Gear Sets]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; use it again to take it off. You can also equip it from the [[Inventory|inventory]]. You have only one earring slot; see [[Equipment|Equipment]].
- **Experience bonus**: all the experience you gain (from felling things and from quest rewards) is multiplied by (1 + all experience bonuses). The earring's bonus is added together with Lumi's [[Boons|boon]] “Crown's Insight” and the −50% of the [[Crown of Thorns|Crown of Thorns]]; the total never falls below the normal rate.

## History
