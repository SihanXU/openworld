A **Crated Calf** is a [[calf|calf]] packed in a wooden crate, bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]. Hold it and press {{key|G}} at a tile of ground: the crate opens, the calf walks out, and the crate is gone. After {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours the calf grows into a [[cow|cow]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: the “Ranching” shelf, 1 crate for 120 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. It is the most expensive thing at the stall.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Let it out**: hold the crate and press {{key|G}} at a tile beside you (the label reads “Let Crated Calf out”): “The crate opens, and out it comes.” A calf appears on that tile (or on the nearest free tile if it cannot stand there), and the crate disappears. It can be let out on any map.
- A calf let out this way can be fed [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]], but it cannot be milked yet, nor will it breed; after {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours (two game days) it grows into a cow, and only then can it be milked (see [[Bucket of Milk|Bucket of Milk]]) and have a calf with another well-fed cow. The Held Ground starts with a single cow; buy a crate and raise it, and you have a second.
- It is as docile as the cows already on the map: it never attacks anyone, runs when struck, does not chase people to butt them, and never keeps [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]] from opening because a fight is “still going on”. See [[calf#Behavior|the calf's behavior]].
- The crate is made of wood but does not burn. Other uses are not covered by the rules, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|the world learns]]).
