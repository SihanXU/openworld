**Pumpkin Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Pumpkin|Pumpkin]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow ripe. Harvesting gives the seeds back, so the more you plant, the more seeds you have.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Garden Supplies**: the “Garden Supplies” box by the garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds 2.
- **Harvest**: a ripe pumpkin reaped with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds, picked by hand 1. The ripe pumpkin in the garden can be harvested straight away.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sow**: holding the seeds, right-click an empty cell of farmland: “You sow Pumpkin Seeds.” It becomes a pumpkin sprout.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}}, and choose “Sow Pumpkin” on the “Farming” page; the seeds only need to be in the inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering makes it grow one stage two game hours later in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[Farming|Farming]] and [[Crops|Crops]].

## History

- 2026-10-03: Added to the game, together with Firstlight's garden.
