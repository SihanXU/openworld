**Blueberries** are a vegetable you can grow; eating them restores 1 [[Satiety|satiety]]. Blueberries are perennial: after a harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; the blueberries you harvest can be sold back to the stall for 3 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] a portion.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[Blueberries Seeds|Blueberries Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 portions of blueberries (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 portion and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[blueberries (crops)|the blueberry crop]] and [[Farming]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[Blueberries Seeds|Blueberries Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], 3 for 24 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell blueberries themselves; it only buys them. The garden in [[Firstlight Village]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no blueberries.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; you are asked “Eat Blueberries?” Confirm, and they restore 1 satiety. They cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] and press {{key|E}} to open it, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 3 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] a portion. See [[Money|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give them to someone with {{key|G}}.
