{{信息框}}
**Blueberries** are a vegetable you can grow; eating them restores 1 [[饱食度|satiety]]. Blueberries are perennial: after a harvest the plant does not disappear but goes back to the young-plant stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again. The seeds are sold only at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]; the blueberries you harvest can be sold back to the stall for 3 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] a portion.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Grow your own**: sow [[蓝莓种子|Blueberries Seeds]] on farmland and water them twice; reaping with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 portions of blueberries (3 if fertilized) and 2 seeds, picking by hand gives 1 portion and 1 seed. After that, every further watering gives another harvest. See [[蓝莓（作物）|the blueberry crop]] and [[农耕]].
- **Seeds**: buy [[蓝莓种子|Blueberries Seeds]] on the “Farming” shelf of the [[货摊|trading stall]], 3 for 24 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].
- The trading stall does not sell blueberries themselves; it only buys them. The garden in [[初原村]] grows only carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins, no blueberries.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Eat**: hold them and left-click to use them on yourself; you are asked “Eat Blueberries?” Confirm, and they restore 1 satiety. They cannot be roasted.
- **Sell**: stand by the [[货摊|trading stall]] and press {{键|E}} to open it, then sell on the “Sell” shelf: 3 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] a portion. See [[金钱|Money]].
- Like any food, you can give them to someone with {{键|G}}.
