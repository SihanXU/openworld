A **Goblet** is a cup of silver or gold, counted among the valuables. You cannot get it in normal play: none is placed on any map, it cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

The rule library has no rules about the goblet: it cannot hold water, and you cannot drink from it. What holds water is the [[木桶|Bucket]], the [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] and the [[空水袋|Empty Waterskin]].
