A **Wand** is a casting weapon whose attack reaches 5 tiles. It belongs to no [[职业|profession]]: anyone can cast with it. The only one in the game is the **Frost Wand** on [[托宾|Tobin]]'s cart, which carries the ice [[元素|element]].

## Source

{{来源}}

The **Frost Wand** (wood, attack 2, ice element) lies on Tobin's cart in [[初原村]], and it belongs to Tobin:

- **Trade food for it**: hold any piece of food and press {{键|G}} at Tobin to give it to him; he takes it and says the blue wand on the cart is yours. If you go to take the wand after that, he only warns you to mind the cold. The trade happens only once.
- If you take it without trading, Tobin says “Hey — that is mine!” if he sees you, and the village talks about it for two days, with no other consequence.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Casting**: hold it and press {{键|空格}} to send a spell the way you face, or left-click a target within 5 tiles. A ranged attack can only go out along your own row or column. The blow counts as “spell” and carries the wand's element. For the rules of combat see [[战斗|Combat]].
- **Freezing the river**: left-click shallow water with the Frost Wand, and the surface freezes into walkable ice that also spreads around; this {{状态|薄冰}} melts back into water after 150 seconds, and anyone standing on it then is pushed to the bank. This is one of the ways to cross the river at [[渡口|the Ford]]; see [[元素|Elements]].
- **Freezing wet things**: the ice element hitting a {{状态|潮湿}} target makes it {{状态|冰冻}}, unable to move for a while. Ice monsters hit by the ice element instead recover 2 health.
- The wand is a general weapon: the [[法师|Mage]]'s talents apply only to staves, not to wands.
- When your weapon is not drawn ({{键|X}}), left-clicking a person with the wand does not attack them.

## Trivia

- As long as you have not yet crossed the river, the Frost Wand counts as one of [[渡口|the Ford]]'s ways across. If the rope to mend the ferry, the dead tree that can be felled and this wand are all gone, [[赫斯珀|Hesper]] poles you across on the raft herself.
- The Frost Wand first belonged to a peddler at the Ford; later it became Tobin's, and the cart moved to Firstlight Village.
