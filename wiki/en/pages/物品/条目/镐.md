{{信息框}}
A **Pickaxe** is a tool for breaking stone. Hold it and left-click rock, a boulder, a cave wall or an ore vein, and you break it open, getting {{物品|石块}}, {{物品|铁矿石}} and {{物品|煤炭}} ([[采矿|Mining]]). Striking stone with bare hands only gives the message “Too hard.”; to get stone you first need a pickaxe.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make it yourself**: press {{键|C}}; 2 Stones, 2 {{物品|树枝}} and 1 {{物品|干草}} make a **Stone Pickaxe**, with no [[工作台|workbench]] needed. A stone pickaxe of common quality has attack 4.
- **Ready-made**: an **Old Pickaxe** (iron, attack 5) lies in [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

The tooltip gives the pickaxe's use as “Breaks rock and boulders”. Whatever you left-click takes a blow from the pick's point, with damage reckoned by the pickaxe's efficiency against its material: stone, ore, bone and crystal all take full effect (see [[耐久与破坏#效率表|the efficiency table]]).

- **Breaking stone**: rock (durability 10) breaks after two blows of an ordinary stone pickaxe and leaves 1 Stone; the boulder at [[渡口|the Ford]] (durability 12) leaves 4 Stones when broken; cave walls and stone walls are tougher and take more blows. See [[采矿#能凿的东西|What can be broken]].
- **Breaking ore veins**: the iron veins and coal seams in [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]] each leave 2 Iron Ore or 2 Coal when broken; the veins slowly grow back. [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]] has iron veins too.
- **Taking apart a stone ring**: a stone ring not yet built into a campfire comes apart with one blow of the pick, and the stones and any sticks put on it are returned as they were (see [[建造|Building]]).
- **Knocking built things loose**: left-click something built that can be carried off, such as a wall, door, floor, stairs, bed or workbench, and the whole of it comes loose and drops where it stood; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (if you are close it is drawn into your pack by itself), and you can carry it elsewhere and build it again. See [[建筑件|Structures]].
- **Fighting**: the pickaxe is not a weapon, but left-clicking a monster with it is still a blow, and against flesh it also takes full effect.
