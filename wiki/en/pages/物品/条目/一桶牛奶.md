A **Bucket of Milk** is milk drawn from a fed [[cow|cow]], held in a [[Bucket|Bucket]]. Drinking it restores 4 [[Health|health]] and 4 [[Satiety|satiety]], and the bucket is left empty; it can also be sold to the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]] for 12 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Milking**: holding an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]], left-click a cow (label “Milk …”). The cow must be fed first: given [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] or [[Wheat|Wheat]], with {{status|fed}} not yet worn off, and not yet milked since this feeding. If it works: “You milk ….” The empty bucket in your hand becomes a Bucket of Milk, and the cow gets {{status|milked}}.
- If the cow is not fed, or already milked: “… will not give milk — feed it first, and only once a day.”
- “Milked” lasts {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours (a whole game day), so a cow can be milked only once a day; after that, feed it again and it can be milked again.
- Only a grown cow can be milked, not a [[calf|calf]] (the rules do not say what happens when you click a calf with a bucket).
- An empty bucket can be bought in the trading stall's “Ranching” column (8 Dawnmarks), or made by hand from 3 [[Plank|planks]] and 1 [[Cut Grass|cut grass]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drink**: hold it and right-click yourself (a bucket is a tool, and its left click is for clicking other things with it); you are asked “Drink Bucket of Milk?” Once confirmed: “You drink Bucket of Milk.” It restores 4 health and 4 satiety, and the milk in your hand becomes an empty [[Bucket|Bucket]] again, ready for more milking.
- **Give someone a drink**: right-click someone who gets hungry (label “Give Bucket of Milk to … to drink”); they recover 4 health and 4 satiety, and the bucket is likewise left empty.
- **Sell**: stand by the trading stall and press {{key|E}}, and sell it in the “Sell” column for 12 Dawnmarks a bucket, bucket and all. See [[Money|Money]].
- Used on anything else (poured on a crop, given to an animal to drink), the rules do not say what happens, and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the trading stall on the Held Ground and milking.
- 2026-10-08: Drinking it also restores 4 satiety, and the bucket is left empty; “milked” now lasts a whole game day.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls|Controls]]).
