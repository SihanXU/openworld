The **Club** is the simplest blunt weapon: at a [[工作台|workbench]], a single {{物品|木头}} makes a **Wooden Club**. It belongs to no [[职业|profession]], so even someone who has not chosen a profession yet can fight monsters with it.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make one yourself**: stand by a workbench and press {{键|C}}: 1 log makes a Wooden Club. No tool is needed.
- No club lies on any map, and no quest gives one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Combat**: hold it and press {{键|空格}}, or left-click a monster within 1 tile. A common-quality Wooden Club has attack 2. For the combat rules, see [[战斗|Combat]].
- **Shattering frozen targets**: a blunt weapon that hits a creature that is {{状态|冰冻}} shatters the ice; that blow deals ×1.5 damage, and the target thaws at once.
- General weapons get no bonus from profession talents; the tooltip says “Common weapon: no profession talents”.
