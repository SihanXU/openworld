A **Map** is a map drawn on paper, an item. It has nothing to do with the [[世界地图|World Map]] opened with {{键|M}}. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

Nothing is drawn on this map: holding it and left-clicking it on yourself (reading it) is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]). Nor does it mark anything on the world map. Maps can catch fire.
