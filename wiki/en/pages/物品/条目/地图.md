A **Map** is a map drawn on paper, an item. It has nothing to do with the [[World Map|World Map]] opened with {{key|M}}. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[Crafting|crafted]], and no quest gives it.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

Nothing is drawn on this map: holding it and left-clicking it on yourself (reading it) is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[The World Learns|The World Learns]]). Nor does it mark anything on the world map. Maps can catch fire.
