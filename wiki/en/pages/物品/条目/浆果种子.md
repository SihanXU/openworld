**Berry Seeds** are the seeds of the [[浆果丛|berry bush]]. They can be sown on farmland and grow into a berry bush; the berry bush is perennial and fruits again after harvest. No Berry Seeds lie on any map and no berry bushes grow there; you have to buy them at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|The Held Ground]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Sowing**: hold them and right-click an empty tile of farmland, and it becomes a berry bush sprout. Each watering, in the open, makes it grow one stage two game hours later: sprout, berry bush, fruiting berry bush. See [[农耕|Farming]].
- **Harvesting**: reaping it with a [[镰刀|Sickle]] gives 2 [[浆果|Berries]] and 2 seeds; picking by hand gives 1 and 1. After harvest the bush goes back to the “berry bush” stage, and one more watering makes it fruit again.
- The “Planting” page of building mode has no berries: the seeds have to be held in your hand to sow.
