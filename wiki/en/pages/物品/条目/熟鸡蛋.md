The **Boiled Egg** is an [[Egg|Egg]] cooked over a fire; eating it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]], three times as much as a raw egg.

## Source

{{sources}}

Hold an egg and right-click a lit [[campfire|campfire]], fireplace or forge, or press {{key|G}} facing the fire, and it becomes a Boiled Egg. See [[Cooking|Cooking]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Eat**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; it asks “Eat Boiled Egg?” Confirm, and it restores 3 satiety.

## Trivia

- Its name is Boiled Egg, but there is no pot in the game to boil it in: it is roasted.
