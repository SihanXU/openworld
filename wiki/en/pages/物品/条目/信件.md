A **Letter** is an ordinary letter. It cannot be obtained in a normal game: it is not placed on any map, cannot be [[制作|crafted]], and no quest gives it. The Church's letter in Chapter 1 is the [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], another thing.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

Nothing is written in this letter: holding it and left-clicking it on yourself (reading it) is not defined by the rules, something the world has not yet seen (see [[世界会学|The World Learns]]); only the [[朝圣令|Proclamation of the Pilgrimage]], which has writing, can be read for its content. Letters cannot be sent either. Letters can catch fire.
