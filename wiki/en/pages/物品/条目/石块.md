{{信息框}}
The **Stone** is one of the most common materials: it can be picked up anywhere on the ground outdoors, and breaking rock also yields it. All stone tools are made with it, and a [[营火|campfire]] needs two.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Picked up from the ground**: on most outdoor maps, stones keep appearing on the grass, up to 5 at a time. See [[采集|Foraging]].
- **Breaking rock**: break a rock with a [[镐|Pickaxe]] and it leaves 1; break the boulder at [[渡口|The Ford]] and it leaves 4. See [[采矿|Mining]].
- **Quest**: when [[米娅|Mia]]'s quest [[米娅的戒指|Mia's Ring]] is completed, she gives you her “best stone”, which is a Stone.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Crafting**: the stone axe, stone pickaxe, stone hoe, stone sickle and stone smith hammer all need stones; see the “Makes” row above and [[制作|Crafting]].
- **Building a campfire**: the “Campfire” in building mode ({{键|B}}) takes 2 stones and 2 sticks. You can also build one by hand: lay one stone on another on the ground to make a stone ring, then add two sticks. See [[营火|campfire]].
- **[[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]]**: the first level asks for 10, the third for 30.
- The tooltip says it is “Heavy”, but the game has no carrying weight: however many you carry, it is all the same.

## Trivia

- The stone Mia gives you is “lucky. Mostly.”, but it is no different from any other Stone.
