**Potato Seeds** are the seeds of the [[Potato|Potato]], bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]], 5 for 10 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. Sown on farmland and watered twice, they grow ripe. Harvesting gives the seeds back, so the more you plant, the more seeds you have.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Trading stall**: stand by the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]] and press {{key|E}}, click them in the “Farming” column, and 10 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] buys 5, straight into the inventory. Away from the stall you cannot buy.
- **Harvest**: a ripe potato reaped with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] gives 2 seeds, picked by hand 1.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Sow**: holding the seeds, right-click an empty cell of farmland: “You sow Potato Seeds.” It becomes a potato sprout. There is ready farmland southwest of the sword on the Held Ground; elsewhere a [[Hoe|Hoe]] can turn diggable ground such as dirt and grass into farmland.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}}, and choose “Sow Potato” on the “Farming” page; the seeds only need to be in the inventory.
- **Growing**: each watering makes it grow one stage two game hours later in the open: sprout, young plant, ripe. See [[potato (crops)]], [[Farming|Farming]] and [[Crops|Crops]].

## History

- 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]]; the seeds are bought in the stall's “Farming” column.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls|Controls]]).
