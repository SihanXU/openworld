A **Sickle** is a tool for bringing in crops. Hold it and left-click a ripe crop, and you take in the produce and the seeds, one more of each than picking by hand ([[农耕|Farming]]).

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Make it yourself**: press {{键|C}}; 2 {{物品|石块}}, 1 {{物品|树枝}} and 1 {{物品|干草}} make a **Stone Sickle**, with no [[工作台|workbench]] needed.
- **Ready-made**: the “Garden Supplies” crate beside the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] holds a **Bronze Sickle** (attack 3).
- **Buy**: the “Farming” page of the trading stall at the Held Ground, 10 Dawnmarks.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Harvesting**: left-click a ripe crop: “You harvest …” It drops 2 of the produce and 2 seeds; a fertilized crop gives 1 more of the produce. An annual crop is gone once harvested, leaving empty farmland; a perennial one (tomato, berry bush) goes back to the young-plant stage and fruits again when watered. The tooltip gives the sickle's use as “Harvests crops”.
- **Compared with picking by hand**: without a sickle, pressing {{键|E}} to pick a ripe crop gives only 1 of the produce and 1 seed.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}}; “Harvest” on the “Farming” page needs a sickle in your pack.
- Clicking anything else cuts at it with the sickle, and the blow counts as “cutting”.
