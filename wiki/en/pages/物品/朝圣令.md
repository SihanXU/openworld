The **Proclamation of the Pilgrimage** is the document by which the Holy City calls the Pilgrimage and names the Pilgrim. In [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]], on the first morning after the night of thanks, the [[Church Courier]] reaches [[Firstlight Village]], reads it out before everyone, and hands it to the hero. From that moment the hero is the Pilgrim named by the Church.

## Source

{{sources}}

In Firstlight Village, on the first morning after the night of thanks (the night the Grey Man took the First Fire), in the scene of the courier's arrival. If you have already defeated the White Knight by then, the scene no longer plays.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Read**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (label “Read Proclamation of the Pilgrimage”), and its words appear in the messages at the bottom left. For the full text see [[#The proclamation|The proclamation]] below.
- It is made of paper and can burn.
- As you receive it, “The Church's letter” of the main quest [[The First Seal|The First Seal]] is completed, and the optional objective “Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins” appears: Sister Ilse gives you the side quest [[The Pilgrim's Road (quest)]].

## The proclamation

The full text, read with it in hand:

> By the Church of the Five Sufferings, to every parish and every road: the Seal-bearers can no longer carry what Astra laid upon them. Dusk comes earlier, dawn later; should the five Seals fail, the Long Night falls a second time. The pilgrimage is called. Let the pilgrim seek out the five Seal-bearers, bring back the five instruments, and come to the Holy City for the Rite of the Five Sufferings. Named to this road: the one who came down from the mountain with a fairy. — Sealed in the Holy City.

## Lore

What the courier reads out:

> By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers fail. The Long Night returns. Let the pilgrimage be called.
> One has come down from the mountain with a fairy at their side. The Church names them pilgrim.

The courier stares at Lumi a long moment, and says nothing. Ilse says: “The flame taken and the Church calling you, in the same night. Astra never errs, pilgrim, even if I often do.”

In the camp in the southeast of [[The Forest|the Forest]], another Proclamation of the Pilgrimage is nailed to a tree, the paper grey with age:

> BY ORDER OF THE HOLY CITY. The Seal-bearers weaken. Let the pilgrimage be called. Brother Cassian of Firstlight is named pilgrim. — In the 380th year of the Five.

Having read it, Lumi says: “That's YOUR letter. Word for word. Except it's old. Really old. And the name on it isn't yours.” See [[Pilgrim's Scallop|Pilgrim's Scallop]].

{{spoiler|What the proclamation is really looking for}}

The Church does not send out a pilgrim only because pilgrims are brave. The Great Rite of the Five Sufferings also needs a new Bearer of the Sufferings: one who can defeat the five Seal-bearers and bear the power of the five Seals is fit to become the new Vessel. The whole Pilgrimage is also the Sifting (§13). See [[Nightbearer|Nightbearer]].
