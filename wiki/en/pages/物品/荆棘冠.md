The **Crown of Thorns** is the first of the five [[Instruments of the Sufferings|Instruments of the Sufferings]], belonging to the first Suffering, [[The Five Sufferings#The Crowning|the Crowning]], and to the First Seal. It sits on the head of the first [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearer]], [[The White Knight Alberic|Alberic, the White Knight]], its thorns grown into his helmet. When the White Knight is defeated he kneels, takes it off and hands it to the hero himself.

It is a piece of head gear of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality: wearing it makes you stronger, and at the same time makes experience come more slowly. Its power, “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”, is the law of the White Knight's kingdom.

## Source

{{sources}}

There is only one Crown of Thorns, and it can only be obtained at the end of [[Chapter 1|Chapter 1]]: once you defeat the White Knight in the knight's kingdom on [[The East Road]], a scene puts it in the hero's hands.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Wear it on the head**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself. The tooltip shows its defense and attack bonuses, and in red the price: experience gained −50%.
- **Halved experience only cancels bonuses**: the experience multiplier is 1 plus all bonuses, and never goes below normal speed. So with no other bonus, wearing the Crown of Thorns does not slow your experience; with Lumi's [[Boons|Boon]] “Crown's Insight” (+100%), the two together leave only +50%.
- **Stigma power “None Cross That Do Not Kneel”**: the tooltip says “Your attacks have a 20% chance to make the target kneel (unable to move for 1 second).” A target struck this way takes the status “kneeling”.
- **Quest**: the main quest [[The Crown of Thorns|The Crown of Thorns]] is “Bring the Crown of Thorns back to Firstlight Village”. Returning to [[Firstlight Village]] plays the homecoming scene and completes the quest; the game does not check whether you actually carry the crown.
- Doing something undefined with it to other things is answered by [[The World Learns|the world learns]].

## Lore

The Crown of Thorns is the instrument of the first Suffering that Astra bore: a crown of barbed thorns, the thorns driven into the skull, yet never taken off. The Church explains its meaning as “casting off human pride and will”. Later, its power was given to the first Seal-bearer.

In Chapter 1, people speak of it like this:

- [[Fennick|Fennick]]: “When I served him, he would kneel to talk to children, so his face was level with theirs. Now everyone who passes must kneel to him. The crown did that to him.”
- The White Knight as he falls: “Take it. It was never mine to wear.”
- [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]], seeing it for the first time: “The Crown of Thorns, here in our village. I never thought I would see it with my own eyes. Keep it close — but not near your heart.”
- [[Rowan|Rowan]]: “Our village holds the Crown of Thorns, and the altar is cold.”

{{spoiler|What the Crown of Thorns really does}}

- The true work of the Crowning is to wear down the bearer's bounds of self, so that the mind can bear the intrusion of the First Night (§4). The White Knight wore it for hundreds of years; he remembers only that he must “hold”, not what he holds, and cannot keep anyone's name.
- Its price in the game (less experience) and the boon Lumi catches from it (more experience) come from the same cause: the Crown of Thorns wears the self thin, and a thinner self learns faster (docs/chapter-1.md §8).
- In the final chapter, when the five Instruments return to their places, the Crown of Thorns is set again on Astra's head (§14); in the preview ending, it is set on the hero's head (§16).

## Trivia

- The White Knight's death animation is the handing over of the Crown of Thorns: kneeling, taking off the crown, holding it out, falling, the crown landing by his gauntlet.
- [[Brin|Brin]]'s cows kneel to the east every evening at dusk; the day the hero comes back with the Crown of Thorns, they stop kneeling: “Now they just stand, looking east.”
