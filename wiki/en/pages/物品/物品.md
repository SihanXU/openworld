An **item** is something you can pick up and carry: weapons, tools, clothing, food, materials, seeds, documents… They go in your [[背包|bag]], are held in your hand, and deal with the world through the four inputs (see [[操作]]). Walls, beds, chests and other things that stand on the ground once put down are [[建筑件|structures]] and [[家具与设施|furniture and fixtures]]; some of them can also be picked up and put down again.

## How to use items

- **Pick up**: point at it lying loose on the ground and press {{键|E}} to put it in your bag. Only things within 1 tile can be picked up.
- **Hold in hand**: press {{键|1}}–{{键|0}} or turn the mouse wheel to choose a toolbar slot.
- **Left click**: the held thing's own use. Tools and weapons swing at what the mouse points to (felling trees, cutting stone, hoeing, fighting monsters…), and keep swinging while held; anything else is used on yourself: eating, drinking, wearing, reading, and using [[誓约之棘|the Oath-Thorn]] to enter and leave the Held Ground all work this way.
- **Right click**: use the held thing on what the mouse points to (sowing, feeding animals, unlocking, cooking…); pointing at yourself also uses it on yourself. Holding a wall, a bed or the like, right-click a tile to build it there. The reach depends on the thing in your hand: mostly 1 tile, a spear 2, a staff 4, a wand 5, a bow 6.
- **Put down, put in, hand to someone**: press {{键|G}}.
- When a thing has no defined use on a target, the key prompt shows a purple “?”. The world then goes to learn this, see [[世界会学]].

## How names are made

One kind of item (one “form”, such as “sword”) can have many variants in the world. A variant is decided by three things, which are written into its name:

- **Material**: wood, stone, iron, steel, silver… It sets the multiplier of power and defense, and also whether it can cut other things. See [[材质|Substances]].
- **Quality**: Crude, Common, Fine, Masterwork, Vowbound, Stigmatic. Common quality is not written into the name. See [[品质]].
- **Element**: forms such as weapons, wands, gems and scrolls can carry one element, adding of Embers, of Tides, of Frost, of Storms, of Gales or of Stone to the name. See [[元素]].

The Chinese name runs element + quality + material + form, as in 「炽焰精良铁剑」; the English name is “Fine Iron Sword of Embers”. A thing you make yourself takes the material of its main ingredient (an axe made from stones is a “Stone Axe”, a shield made from planks a “Wooden Shield”), and its quality depends on how far the maker's level is above the recipe's (see [[制作]]).

A weapon's power, a piece of gear's defense = the form's value × the material's multiplier × the quality's multiplier.

The world holds some items with names of their own, such as the “Old Axe” at the Ford, the “Watchman's Cloak” on Cloudtop and “Sister Maren's Ring” in the Ossuary. Each is one piece of an ordinary form, with its name and numbers set on purpose.

## Stacking, ownership and damage

- Items of the same kind, name, marks and statuses stack in one slot, with no limit on the count and no weight.
- When your bag is full, a new kind you get falls at your feet.
- Someone else's things say “Belongs to X” in the tooltip. Take one in front of its owner and you are told “Hey — that's mine.” See [[居民]].
- Things that can burn burn away when fire touches them, turning into [[灰烬|Ash]]. See [[元素]].
- Weapons, tools and gear do not wear out, and there is no repairing.

## Groups in the codex

Press {{键|J}} to open the “Items” page of the [[图鉴|codex]]; items are listed in the game's own groups: weapons, armor, clothing, tools, lighting, containers, food, potions, materials, seeds, gems, treasures, relics, documents, keys, miscellany. An item you have held once lights up; ones you have not show “???”.

This wiki's category pages do not quite match the codex's groups: for example, the codex's “armor” and “clothing” are split here by slot into [[防具|Armour]] and [[饰品|Accessories]], and “potions” are on the [[食物|Food]] page.

## About sources

The item catalogue has more forms than can currently be obtained on the maps. Many forms are there for the rules, for [[世界会学|the world learns]] and for maps to come, and have no fixed source now, such as the [[钱袋|Coin Pouch]] and the [[技能书|Skill Book]]. Where each item can be obtained is in the “Source” section of its own page. New rules the world learns can also create brand-new items, each with its own 16×16 icon.
