The **Pilgrim's Scallop** is a scallop shell on a cord, the pilgrim's mark. It lies in a cold camp deep in the southeast of [[The Forest|the Forest]], beside a cairn. The camp belonged to Brother Cassian, the pilgrim named thirty-one years ago. It is a necklace of [[Vowbound|Vowbound]] quality.

## Source

{{sources}}

There is only one in the world, in the camp in the southeast of the Forest. Walking into the camp for the first time plays a scene, whose last line is:

> (Something glints by the cairn: a scallop shell on a cord, the pilgrim's mark.)

What you receive when swearing the vow at [[The Pilgrims' Rest|the Pilgrims' Rest]] is a different thing: the [[Sworn Scallop|Sworn Scallop]], the Pilgrim's own scallop. It is not this one, and giving it to Ilse does not count.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Worn at the neck**: besides 2 defense, movement speed +26%, experience +18%, and 5 minor affixes; it also carries the [[Vowbound|vow]] “Road”: +30% movement speed while walking on a road, +10% evasion while moving.
- **Quest**: once you have found the camp, [[Sister Ilse]] gives you the side quest [[The Pilgrim Before]]. Bring her the scallop to see; she does not take it: “Keep it, and wear it on the road. He would want that.” The quest's reward also holds another Vowbound item, “Cassian's Prayer Cord”. The quest accepts only this one; the [[Sworn Scallop|Sworn Scallop]] does not count.

## Lore

Ilse says: “The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Every one of us is given one when we are named.”

On a tree beside the camp is nailed an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, almost word for word the one the courier read to the hero, except that the one named is “Brother Cassian of Firstlight”, dated “the 380th year of the Five”. When you hand over the scallop, Ilse says:

> Brother Cassian. He sat in this chapel when I was a novice and asked me to pray for him. The Church named him pilgrim thirty-one years ago. ...They told us he reached the Holy City.

Go back to the camp after dark (20:00–04:00), and a pale shape kneels by the cold fire ring and, without looking up, says: “Do not kneel to him.” The kneeling stones in the White Knight's kingdom on [[The East Road|the East Road]] are carved with the scallop badge too, worn almost away.
