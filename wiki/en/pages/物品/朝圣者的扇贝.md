The **Pilgrim's Scallop** is a scallop shell on a cord, the pilgrim's mark. It lies in a cold camp deep in the southeast of [[森林|the Forest]], beside a cairn. The camp belonged to Brother Cassian, the pilgrim named thirty-one years ago. It is a necklace of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality.

## Source

{{来源}}

There is only one in the world, in the camp in the southeast of the Forest. Walking into the camp for the first time plays a scene, whose last line is:

> (Something glints by the cairn: a scallop shell on a cord. The pilgrim's mark. Sister Ilse would know whose it was.)

What you receive when swearing the vow at [[朝圣者驿站|the Pilgrims' Rest]] is a different thing: the [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]], the Pilgrim's own scallop. It is not this one, and giving it to Ilse does not count.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Worn at the neck**: besides 2 defense, movement speed +26%, experience +18%, and 5 minor affixes; it also carries the [[遗誓|vow]] “Road”: +30% movement speed while walking on a road, +10% evasion while moving.
- **Quest**: once you have found the camp, [[伊尔丝修女]] gives you the side quest [[上一位朝圣者]]. Bring her the scallop to see; she does not take it: “Keep it. He would want it walking.” The quest's reward also holds another Vowbound item, “Cassian's Prayer Cord”. The quest accepts only this one; the [[立誓扇贝|Sworn Scallop]] does not count.

## Lore

Ilse says: “The scallop is the pilgrim's mark. Every one of us is given one when we are named.”

On a tree beside the camp is nailed an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, word for word the one the courier read to the hero, except that the one named is “Brother Cassian of Firstlight”, dated “the 380th year of the Five”. When you hand over the scallop, Ilse says:

> Brother Cassian. He sat in this chapel when I was a novice and asked me to pray for him. The Church named him pilgrim thirty-one years ago. ...They told us he reached the Holy City.

Go back to the camp after dark (20:00–04:00), and a pale shape kneels by the cold fire ring: “I knelt. I knelt to him. Do not kneel.” The kneeling stones in the White Knight's kingdom on [[东大路|the East Road]] are carved with the scallop badge too, worn almost away.
