**Dragonsbane** is the sword [[Garrick]], the smith of [[Emberfall|Emberfall]], forged to face [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]]. It belongs to the old storyline after Chapter 1, [[Emberfall and the Dragon]], which predates the present setting and will be replaced.

Dragonsbane is a general weapon of [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] quality: anyone can use it, and it belongs to no profession. Its power, “Dragonslayer”: double damage against dragons and dragon whelps.

## Source

{{sources}}

In the old storyline's objective “Prepare to slay the dragon”: give Garrick 5 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[Coal|Coal]], one piece at a time ({{key|G}}), then talk to him ({{key|E}}), and he hands you Dragonsbane. There is only this one in the world.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- Held in the hand to attack. It looks like a sword, but it is not the Warrior's profession weapon: anyone can use it, and profession talents do not boost it.
- Deals double damage to [[the dragon of Cinder Peak|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] and to [[dragon whelp|dragon whelps]].

## Lore

Garrick says as he hands over the sword:

> There it is. Dragonsbane. My family has waited twelve generations to forge that blade. Come back with the dragon's blood on it!

Afterwards he says: “There. Twelve of us kept the pattern, and I'm the one who forged it. Swing it hard. It was made for that dragon.” [[Mara]], the elder of Emberfall, says she heard the hammer all night.

Neither Dragonsbane nor the dragon is part of the setting of the Church of the Five Sufferings.

## History

- 2026-10-03: Added to the game with the Emberfall part of the old main story, its quality then the highest, “Legendary”.
- 2026-10-07: The highest quality was renamed “Stigmatic”; Dragonsbane gained the power “Dragonslayer”.
