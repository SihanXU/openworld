**Dragonsbane** is the sword [[加里克]], the smith of [[余烬镇|Emberfall]], forged to face [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]]. It belongs to the old storyline after Chapter 1, [[余烬镇与魔龙]], which predates the present setting and will be replaced.

Dragonsbane is a general weapon of [[圣痕|Stigmatic]] quality: anyone can use it, and it belongs to no profession. Its power, “Dragonslayer”: double damage against dragons and dragon whelps.

## Source

{{来源}}

In the old storyline's objective “Prepare to slay the dragon”: give Garrick 5 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[煤炭|Coal]], one piece at a time ({{键|G}}), then talk to him ({{键|E}}), and he hands you Dragonsbane. There is only this one in the world.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- Held in the hand to attack. It looks like a sword, but it is not the Warrior's profession weapon: anyone can use it, and profession talents do not boost it.
- Deals double damage to [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] and to [[幼龙|dragon whelps]].

## Lore

Garrick says as he hands over the sword:

> There. Dragonsbane. Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge that one. Bring it back red.

Afterwards he says: “Swing it like you mean it. It was made to mean it.” [[玛拉]], the elder of Emberfall, says she heard the hammer all night.

Neither Dragonsbane nor the dragon is part of the setting of the Church of the Five Sufferings.

## History

- 2026-10-03: Added to the game with the Emberfall part of the old main story, its quality then the highest, “Legendary”.
- 2026-10-07: The highest quality was renamed “Stigmatic”; Dragonsbane gained the power “Dragonslayer”.
