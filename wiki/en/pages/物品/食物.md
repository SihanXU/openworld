**Food** is any item that can be eaten or drunk. Eating restores [[Satiety|satiety]]; drinking potions and drinks restores [[Health|health]]. Raw meat, fish, eggs and mushrooms can be cooked over a fire, and cooked food is more filling.

## Eating

- Hold the food in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat X?”, and confirming eats it.
- Eating restores only satiety, not health directly. With satiety at 90% or more, health slowly recovers by itself. See [[Satiety]] and [[Health]].
- [[Herb|Herbs]] are the exception: they can be eaten and also restore a little health.
- Raw food does no harm when eaten; it is just less filling than cooked.
- Food does not spoil.
- Skills can make food more filling or make satiety drop more slowly; see [[Skills]].

## Drinking

- Hold a potion or drink in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink X?”, and drinking it restores health.
- A [[Healing Potion|Healing Potion]] restores the most; [[Wine Bottle|Wine Bottle]], [[Mug of Ale|Mug of Ale]], [[Antidote|Antidote]] and [[Murky Potion|Murky Potion]] restore less.
- A [[Bucket|Bucket]], [[Empty Flask|Empty Flask]] or [[Empty Waterskin|Empty Waterskin]] holding water can be drunk too, restoring 2 health; the container is left behind empty.
- A [[Bucket of Milk|Bucket of Milk]] restores both 4 health and 4 satiety; once drunk, the bucket is left behind empty.
- Buckets, flasks and waterskins are tools: their left click fills them with water, waters or splashes; to drink what is in one, hold it and right-click yourself.
- A [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]] restores no health; for 5 game hours after drinking it, fire does only a quarter of its damage and cannot set you alight (the status “fireproof”).
- Using a drink on someone else makes them drink it.

## Cooking

{{main|Cooking}}

- Holding raw food, right-click a lit cooking fire: a [[campfire|campfire]], [[brazier|brazier]], [[fireplace|fireplace]], [[forge|forge]] or [[cauldron|cauldron]]. The raw food in your hand is cooked: “It sizzles and cooks.”
- Pressing {{key|G}} at a lit cooking fire while holding raw food does the same; letting the fire burn it cooks it too. Lamp posts, candles and lantern posts are not cooking fires; food put on them does not cook.
- What can be cooked: [[Raw Fish]] → [[Grilled Fish]], [[Raw Beef]] → [[Steak]], [[Raw Chicken]] → [[Roast Chicken]], [[Mushroom]] → [[Roasted Mushroom]], [[Egg]] → [[Boiled Egg]].
- There is no cooking interface, and no recipes with several ingredients.

## Where to get it

- **On the ground**: [[Berries|Berries]] grow on [[Cloudtop]] and in the tutorial's [[The Clearing|Clearing]]; the ones in the Clearing slowly grow back.
- **Farming**: fourteen vegetables: [[Carrot]], [[Turnip]], [[Tomato]] and [[Pumpkin]], plus [[Parsnip]], [[Potato]], [[Radish]], [[Cauliflower]], [[Melon]], [[Corn]], [[Blueberries]], [[Strawberry]], [[Hot Pepper]] and [[Eggplant]], whose seeds are bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. See [[Farming]], [[Crops]] and [[Seeds]].
- **Animals**: [[cow|Cows]] on [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] and [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] leave Raw Beef when knocked down, and [[chicken|chickens]] leave Raw Chicken; a hen lays an egg every 3 game hours; a well-fed cow can be milked for a [[Bucket of Milk|Bucket of Milk]] (drinking it restores health and satiety too). See [[Animals]].
- **Fishing**: [[Raw Fish]], [[Sunfish]], [[Perch]], [[Catfish]], [[Eel]], [[Pike]], [[Golden Carp]] and [[Dawnscale]], all of which cook into [[Grilled Fish]]. See [[Fishing]].
- **Quests and people**: [[Bread|Bread]] is the most common reward, and Hesper also gives you a loaf the first time you meet; [[Brin]] gives [[Cheese|Cheese]]; [[Sister Ilse]] gives two Healing Potions in the quest [[The Pilgrim Before]]; the herbalist [[Wren]] brews Fireproof Draughts.
- **Dawn Hill**: the dawnbloom there is a kind of herb.

Forms such as [[Apple|Apple]], [[Pie|Pie]], [[Honey Jar|Honey Jar]], wine and ale have no fixed source on the maps at present.

## Giving it to others

Use {{key|G}} to hand food to a person, and they take it. [[Tobin]] is starving and will trade the Frost Wand on his cart for any one portion of food. Right-clicking a person while holding food feeds it to them (pressing {{key|E}} talks to them): their satiety goes up (and herbs restore a little health too).

Right-click a **creature** (a monster or animal) while holding something edible: “Feed Apple to green slime”, and it gulps it down (“The green slime snaps up the apple.”), with a happy emote over its head. Creatures have no satiety; swallowing does not heal them and does not change how they feel about you: an aggressive monster still attacks you after eating. What is swallowed is gone, and does not drop again if you knock the creature down. [[Bosses|Bosses]] eat nothing. Animals also swallow ordinary food this way, but only [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] and [[Wheat|Wheat]] count as feeding them (see [[Animals#Feeding|Feeding]]). Pressing {{key|G}} at a creature does nothing. See [[Monsters#Handing things to creatures|Handing things to creatures]].

Harvests, eggs, milk and raw fish can be sold to the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; see [[Money]].
