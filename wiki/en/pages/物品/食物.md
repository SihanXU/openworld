**Food** is any item that can be eaten or drunk. Eating restores [[饱食度|satiety]]; drinking potions and drinks restores [[生命值|health]]. Raw meat, fish, eggs and mushrooms can be cooked over a fire, and cooked food is more filling.

## Eating

- Hold the food in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat X?”, and confirming eats it.
- Eating restores only satiety, not health directly. With satiety at 90% or more, health slowly recovers by itself. See [[饱食度]] and [[生命值]].
- [[草药|Herbs]] are the exception: they can be eaten and also restore a little health.
- Raw food does no harm when eaten; it is just less filling than cooked.
- Food does not spoil.
- Skills can make food more filling or make satiety drop more slowly; see [[技能]].

## Drinking

- Hold a potion or drink in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink X?”, and drinking it restores health.
- A [[治疗药水|Healing Potion]] restores the most; [[葡萄酒|Wine Bottle]], [[一杯麦酒|Mug of Ale]], [[解毒剂|Antidote]] and [[浑浊药水|Murky Potion]] restore less.
- A [[木桶|Bucket]], [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] or [[空水袋|Empty Waterskin]] holding water can be drunk too, restoring 2 health; the container is left behind empty.
- A [[一桶牛奶|Bucket of Milk]] restores both 4 health and 4 satiety; once drunk, the bucket is left behind empty.
- Buckets, flasks and waterskins are tools: their left click fills them with water, waters or splashes; to drink what is in one, hold it and right-click yourself.
- A [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]] restores no health; for 5 game hours after drinking it, fire does only a quarter of its damage and cannot set you alight (the status “fireproof”).
- Using a drink on someone else makes them drink it.

## Cooking

{{主条目|烹饪}}

- Holding raw food, right-click a lit cooking fire: a [[营火|campfire]], [[火盆|brazier]], [[壁炉|fireplace]], [[熔炉|forge]] or [[坩埚|cauldron]]. The raw food in your hand is cooked: “It sizzles and cooks.”
- Pressing {{键|G}} at a lit cooking fire while holding raw food does the same; letting the fire burn it cooks it too. Lamp posts, candles and lantern posts are not cooking fires; food put on them does not cook.
- What can be cooked: [[生鱼]] → [[烤鱼]], [[生牛肉]] → [[牛排]], [[生鸡肉]] → [[烤鸡肉]], [[蘑菇]] → [[烤蘑菇]], [[鸡蛋]] → [[熟鸡蛋]].
- There is no cooking interface, and no recipes with several ingredients.

## Where to get it

- **On the ground**: [[浆果|Berries]] grow on [[云顶台地]] and in the tutorial's [[林间空地|Clearing]]; the ones in the Clearing slowly grow back.
- **Farming**: fourteen vegetables: [[胡萝卜]], [[芜菁]], [[番茄]] and [[南瓜]], plus [[防风草]], [[土豆]], [[小萝卜]], [[花椰菜]], [[甜瓜]], [[玉米]], [[蓝莓]], [[草莓]], [[辣椒]] and [[茄子]], whose seeds are bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. See [[农耕]], [[作物]] and [[种子]].
- **Animals**: [[牛|Cows]] on [[草原|the Grassland]] and [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] leave Raw Beef when knocked down, and [[鸡|chickens]] leave Raw Chicken; a hen lays an egg every 3 game hours; a well-fed cow can be milked for a [[一桶牛奶|Bucket of Milk]] (drinking it restores health and satiety too). See [[动物]].
- **Fishing**: [[生鱼]], [[太阳鱼]], [[河鲈]], [[鲶鱼]], [[鳗鱼]], [[狗鱼]], [[金鳞鲤]] and [[晨鳞鱼]], all of which cook into [[烤鱼]]. See [[钓鱼]].
- **Quests and people**: [[面包|Bread]] is the most common reward, and Hesper also gives you a loaf the first time you meet; [[布琳]] gives [[奶酪|Cheese]]; [[伊尔丝修女]] gives two Healing Potions in the quest [[上一位朝圣者]]; the herbalist [[雷恩]] brews Fireproof Draughts.
- **Dawn Hill**: the dawnbloom there is a kind of herb.

Forms such as [[苹果|Apple]], [[馅饼|Pie]], [[蜂蜜罐|Honey Jar]], wine and ale have no fixed source on the maps at present.

## Giving it to others

Use {{键|G}} to hand food to a person, and they take it. [[托宾]] is starving and will trade the Frost Wand on his cart for any one portion of food. Right-clicking a person while holding food feeds it to them (pressing {{键|E}} talks to them): their satiety goes up (and herbs restore a little health too).

Right-click a **creature** (a monster or animal) while holding something edible: “Feed Apple to green slime”, and it gulps it down (“The green slime snaps up the apple.”), with a happy emote over its head. Creatures have no satiety; swallowing does not heal them and does not change how they feel about you: an aggressive monster still attacks you after eating. What is swallowed is gone, and does not drop again if you knock the creature down. [[首领|Bosses]] eat nothing. Animals also swallow ordinary food this way, but only [[饲料|Animal Feed]] and [[麦子|Wheat]] count as feeding them (see [[动物#喂食|Feeding]]). Pressing {{键|G}} at a creature does nothing. See [[怪物#递东西给生物|Handing things to creatures]].

Harvests, eggs, milk and raw fish can be sold to the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]; see [[金钱]].

## History
