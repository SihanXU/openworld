**The Ninth Nail** is a nail from the little bridge at [[The Ford]]: square-headed, pulled out straight. When the rider in white took the bridge apart he laid the nails out on the dock, but only eight of them; he took the ninth with him. It is in the White Knight's kingdom on [[The East Road]].

## Source

{{sources}}

There is only one in the world, lying in the ring of thorns in the knight's kingdom on the East Road.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Quest**: give it to [[Joss|Joss]] to complete the side quest [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]]. Joss keeps it. The quest can only be taken after the courier has come.
- It is iron, conducts electricity, and is sharp.

## Lore

Joss says:

> Eight nails on the dock. Eight. I drove nine. Whoever tore up my bridge kept one — if you're headed that way, find it for me.
> The ninth nail. Square head, pulled out straight. You'll know it when you see it.

[[Hesper|Hesper]] says the rider laid the nails out on her dock “as if he were collecting a toll”. Joss says: “Nails pulled out straight, laid out in a row. What storm pulls nails? Someone wanted that bridge gone, and wanted us to know it.”

Joss built the bridge twenty years ago with Hesper's husband, Aldo. When you return the nail, Joss says: “The ninth. … That bridge stood twenty years.”

{{spoiler|Who paid for the bridge}}

What Joss leaves unsaid: twenty years ago, the one who paid for the bridge was a man in grey, a boy at his heel carrying the purse, with the old coin of the Ashen Watch; he wanted “a road that would let the wagons get away from here, fast” (docs/chapter-1.md §7). In the design this is one of the quest's rewards; in the game, Joss tells it only after Chapter 1 ends.
