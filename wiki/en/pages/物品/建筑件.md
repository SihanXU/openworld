**Structures** are the four parts houses are built from: the [[墙|Wall]], the [[地板|Floor]], the [[楼梯|Stairs]] and the [[门|Door]]. Like other items they can be carried; once built, a part takes up a cell and becomes part of the house; left-click it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood, so it can be taken back in one piece. For how to build a house see [[建造|Building]], for the rules of several storeys [[楼层|Storeys]], and for what counts as a room [[房间|Rooms]].

## The four parts

| Part | Materials | Once placed |
|---|---|---|
| [[墙]] | 2 planks | Blocks passage and sight; holds up floors within two cells above it; a wall of the storey above can stand on a wall of the storey below |
| [[地板]] | 2 planks | Laid on ground you can stand on, it covers the ground; laid on an upper storey, it needs walls to hold it up. Seen from below, it is the ceiling of the storey beneath |
| [[楼梯]] | 3 planks | A flight one storey high, placed in front of the higher level; the cell above the stairs must be left open |
| [[门]] | 2 planks | Press {{键|E}} to open and close it. Shut, it blocks passage and sight like a wall; open, it can be walked and seen through; open or shut, it counts as a room's edge |

Parts made of planks are called “Wooden Wall”, “Wooden Floor”…; the substance in the name follows the materials. All four parts are wood, and can burn.

## How to make them

- Press {{键|C}} to make them by hand in the [[制作|crafting]] panel; the finished parts go into the inventory.
- Or skip that: select one in build mode ({{键|B}}), and if you carry the materials it is made and placed on the spot.

## How to place them

- Holding one, right-click a cell to build it there, and hold and drag to build a whole row; or in build mode ({{键|B}}) place it with the left click or {{键|E}}, holding and dragging for a whole row too.
- Holding one and pressing {{键|G}} only puts it on the ground, lying loose, not built; press {{键|E}} to pick it up again.
- Each storey builds only on itself. In build mode, {{键|R}} switches between “floor” and “ceiling”, and also turns the stairs; with a floor in hand, {{键|R}} makes the right click lay a ceiling instead.
- At most {{数值|楼层上限}} storeys can be built.
- When it cannot be placed, you are told why: “Something is there already.” “There is a floor there already.” “A floor cannot be laid on this ground.” “Stairs keep the storey over them open.” “Nothing would hold it up.” “Out of reach.”

## How to take them down

- Left-click a built part once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; then press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into the inventory by itself).
- A floor with something on it can be taken down only once that thing is removed.
- When a wall holding up a floor is taken down, the floor falls.
- Struck with any other tool or a weapon (a sword and the like), a part only loses durability; broken, it gives back only half its materials. See [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]].
