A **weapon** is an item held in the hand to attack with. Hold a weapon and left-click a target, or press {{键|空格}} to attack in front of you: that is one blow. Attack power = your own attack + the weapon's power. For the rules of fighting see [[战斗]].

## Profession weapons and common weapons

Some weapons belong to a single [[职业|profession]]. Others can hold them, store them and give them away, but cannot attack with them; they are told “Only an X can fight with a Y”.

| Profession | Profession weapons |
|---|---|
| [[刺客]] | [[匕首]] |
| [[战士]] | [[剑]], [[弯刀]], and the off-hand [[盾牌]] |
| [[游侠]] | [[弓]] |
| [[法师]] | [[法杖]] |

The other weapons are common weapons that anyone can use: [[斧]], [[棍棒]], [[钉锤]], [[战锤]], [[长矛]], [[戟]], [[魔杖]], [[屠龙剑]]. A profession's talents work only on that profession's weapons; the tooltip of a common weapon says “Common weapon: no profession talent bonus”.

Before you have chosen a profession (which waits until level 5), only common weapons can be used to fight. When you first choose a profession you get a starting weapon: an Iron Dagger for the Assassin, an Iron Sword and a Wooden Shield for the Warrior, a Wooden Bow for the Ranger, a Wooden Staff for the Mage.

## Attack range

How far a left-click attack reaches depends on the weapon in your hand:

| Weapon | Range |
|---|---|
| Most weapons, bare hands | 1 tile |
| [[长矛]], [[戟]] | 2 tiles |
| [[法杖]] | 4 tiles |
| [[魔杖]] | 5 tiles |
| [[弓]] | 6 tiles |

A ranged attack can only go out along your own row or column. Bows, wands and staves fire a projectile in the direction you face. Some skills and [[传说武器|Legendary Arms]] reach farther.

## Bare hands

With nothing in hand, the left click strikes with the weapon in your main hand, or, if the main hand is empty too, with a punch. When you hold something that is neither a tool nor a weapon, such as food or a material, the left click uses it on yourself and does not swing. A fist can slowly break things softer than itself (grass, wood); hitting stone only says “Too hard.” See [[耐久与破坏]].

When your weapon is not drawn ({{键|X}}), left-clicking a person does not attack them, whatever you hold.

## Elements

A weapon that can carry an element gains a word in its name: of Embers (fire), of Tides (water), of Frost (ice), of Storms (lightning), of Gales (wind), of Stone (earth). When it hits, the element acts on the target: fire can set it alight, water wets it, ice freezes wet things, lightning jumps between wet things, wind blows the target back two tiles, earth can shatter brittle things. See [[元素]].

The ready-made element weapons in the game are the Wand of Frost on [[托宾|Tobin]]'s cart (traded for food), and among the Legendary Arms Galestring, Emberheart and Rimecrown.

## The axe is both weapon and tool

The [[斧|axe]] is in the weapon catalogue, and is also the tool for felling trees and splitting planks. Your first axe is usually a Stone Axe you make yourself. See [[工具]].

## Where to get them

- **Make them yourself**: press {{键|C}} to open [[制作|crafting]]. A Stone Axe can be made by hand; at a [[工作台|workbench]] you can make a Wooden Club, a Wooden Shield and a Wooden Bow (the bow needs level 2).
- **On the map**: the “Old Axe” by the stump at [[渡口]].
- **Quests**: [[霍尔特|Holt]]'s quest [[清剿荆棘谷]] gives a [[长矛|Spear]].
- **Trade**: [[托宾|Tobin]] trades the Wand of Frost for a portion of food.
- **Profession**: the starting weapon when you first choose a profession.
- **Seal-bearers**: a defeated Seal-bearer drops a [[传说武器|Legendary weapon]] of your profession.
- **Old story line**: [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]], forged by the smith [[加里克|Garrick]].

Forms in the catalogue such as the [[钉锤|Mace]], [[战锤|Warhammer]] and [[戟|Halberd]] have no fixed source on the maps for now.
