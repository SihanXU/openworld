The **Almanac of the First Night** is an old almanac written before the First Long Night (the first [[极夜|Long Night]]). [[托宾]] sold it to [[奈莎·奎尔]], who makes almanacs. Nessa found that its sunset curve matches exactly the one she measures now. Once you help her read three waypoint statues, she gives you the book.

## Source

{{来源}}

The reward of the side quest [[日之度量]]: wake three [[传送雕像|waypoint statues]] and go back to Nessa.

> Lower every week, and faster than the old almanac says. Take it — I have copied what I need. Read the last pages. I cannot make sense of them.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Read**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself (label “Read the Almanac of the First Night”), and the book's words appear in the messages at the bottom left: line after line of sunset times, and a few sentences on the last pages. The full text is a spoiler; it is in the spoiler block under [[#设定|Lore]] below.
- It is made of paper and can burn.

## Lore

Nessa says:

> Tobin sold me an almanac from before the First Night. Its curve matches mine. Exactly.
> Every month the sun sets earlier. Four minutes, five, six. I write it all down. The numbers make a curve, and I do not like where it goes.

When she gives you the quest, she says the waypoint statues are older than the Church, that they hum at sunset, “lower every week”.

{{剧透|What the last pages say}}

The full text, read with the book in hand:

> Sunset after sunset in a neat column, each a little earlier than the last. On the last pages the hand changes — hurried, pressed so hard the pen has torn the paper: “No sun today. None today. He says he will take it all. Whoever reads this — stop him.” After that, nothing.

The almanac's last pages were written as the First Long Night fell, in a hand begging someone to “stop him”. Nessa cannot make sense of it. It is the first misreading of the setting's first reversal, planted here in advance (docs/chapter-1.md §7; for the first reversal see [[主线剧情|Main Story]]).

{{/剧透}}
