{{信息框}}
The **sign** is a wooden board standing by the road, with words written on it. It is one of the few things you can reach from a distance: stand within 2 tiles and press {{键|E}} to read it (“Read sign”). There are 6 on the maps; you can also make one yourself, called a “Signpost” in [[建造|build]] mode, which comes out blank.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Signpost”: 1 {{物品|树枝}} and 1 {{物品|木板}}, with a hammer ({{物品|锻锤}}, not used up) on you.
- **By hand**: put a stick on the ground and press {{键|G}} on it holding a plank: “A plank on a stick: a sign post.” Then strike the [[牌子立柱|sign post]] with a hammer: “You nail up a blank sign.”

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Reading

- Stand within 2 tiles of the sign and press {{键|E}}, and the words on it are shown.
- A blank sign with nothing written on it: “It is blank.”

### Writing

In the rules, with an {{物品|墨水瓶}} on you, hold a {{物品|羽毛笔}} and right-click a blank sign: “You write on the sign.” Reading it afterwards gives “Made by hand.” (you cannot type your own text yet; what is written is always this line). No quill or ink pot is placed in the game, so for now you cannot write.

### Breaking and fire

- A sign cannot be taken (“This can't be taken.”).
- Durability 4, made of wood. Broken, it leaves 1 stick and 1 plank, each with a half chance (half its makeup; see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- It can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}.
- Press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on the sign's tile.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The sign has been in the game since the first version; a stick and a plank make a post, a hammer nails it into a blank sign, and a quill and ink write on it.
- 2026-10-03: Signposts can be put up from the build bar.
- 2026-10-04: A sign can be read from 2 tiles away; gained durability and a makeup (1 stick, 1 plank).
- 2026-10-08: The three direction signs at Mountain Foot, the Ford and the East Road gained Chinese text.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
