{{信息框}}
A **door** is a wooden door for building houses, made from 2 {{物品|木板}} without any tools. Press {{键|E}} to open or shut it. A shut door blocks the way and sight like a wall; an open door lets anyone walk through. Open or shut, it counts as an edge of a [[房间|room]] just as a wall does, and like a wall it can hold up the floor above. See [[楼层#门|Storeys]]; for how to put one up, see [[建造]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Crafting**: press {{键|C}} and choose “Door”: 2 planks, no tools.
- **Building mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Door”; if you have none on you, it is made on the spot from planks.
- **Holding a door**: right-click a tile and the door is fitted there. {{键|G}} only puts it down loose on the ground; that does not count as fitted, and {{键|E}} picks it up again.
- [[新手教程|Tutorial]] lesson six: “Walls round, and a door.”

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Opening and shutting

- Press {{键|E}}: a shut door opens (“Open the door”), an open door shuts (“Shut the door”).
- A shut door is solid: it blocks the way like a wall and blocks sight too, so what is done behind it cannot be seen by people outside. An open door can be walked through and seen through by anyone.
- Creatures do not open doors: shut the door and stay inside, and the monsters outside cannot get in (see [[夜行生物]]). Tutorial lesson seven: “Before dark, shut the door: {{键|E}}.”

### Rooms and floors

- Open or shut, a door counts as an edge of a room, so a house is a [[房间|room]] even with its door open.
- Like a wall, a door holds up the floor of the storey above within 2 tiles of it, and can be stacked on a wall.

### Picking it up and breaking it

- Hit it once with the left click holding an axe, pickaxe or hammer and it comes loose whole and drops where it was; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (if you are close it is drawn into your pack), with no material lost.
- Durability 10, wood. Tools only knock it loose; only hitting it with a weapon or bare hands takes durability. Broken, it leaves 1 plank (half of 2, see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- It catches fire: burning takes away its durability, and may spread the fire to burnable things within one tile of it. When the fire goes out the door is still there; if its durability burns away, it burns into one {{物品|灰烬}}, leaving no plank.
