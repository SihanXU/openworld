The **log pile** (natural object) is the class of natural objects that are wood stacked into a pile. It never appears on a map by itself; every log pile in the game is a kind of it: the single [[firewood|firewood]] stack west of the tent in the camp at [[The Ford|the Ford]]. What the class has in common: it blocks the way, it is made of wood, it catches fire, and the wood in it cannot be taken out.

The pile where the woodcutter Gus stacks wood in [[Firstlight Village]] is the [[wood pile|wood pile]] (in Chinese the two share a name), but it is a container, not the same thing as this class.

## Source

{{sources}}

- No map has a log pile of this class on its own, only its one kind, [[firewood|firewood]] (1 stack in the Ford's camp).
- You cannot make one, and no rule produces one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

The rules this class of log pile shares (and [[firewood|firewood]] follows):

- **Blocks the way**: natural objects are all solid and cannot be walked through.
- The wood cannot be taken out: for wood, go and fell trees (see [[Woodcutting]]).
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and after burning for about 4.5 seconds becomes one {{item|Ash}}; while burning it spreads the fire to flammable things nearby.
- Hold something and press {{key|G}}, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
