{{信息框}}
A **stone ring** is a fire ring made of two stones, a half-built [[营火|campfire]]. Put two sticks in it and it becomes an unlit campfire. Building a campfire in [[建造|build]] mode goes through these steps.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Laying one**: put a {{物品|石块}} on the ground, hold another stone and press {{键|G}} on it (“Lay a ring of stones”): “Two stones make a fire ring.”
- No map has a ready-made stone ring. The “cold fire ring” in the forest is a campfire gone out, not a stone ring.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Adding sticks**: hold a {{物品|树枝}} and press {{键|G}} on it (“Build a campfire”). The first: “One more stick.” The second: “A campfire, ready to light.” The stone ring becomes an unlit campfire; light it with a torch (see [[营火]]).
- **Taking it apart**: hold a {{物品|镐}} and left-click it (“Take the stone ring apart”): both stones and any sticks already put in come back.
- The stone ring is solid and blocks the way; it cannot be taken.
- It has no durability. Apart from taking it apart with a pickaxe, the rules do not say what happens when you hit it. Made of stone, it does not catch fire.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version of building already had the stone ring: two stones make a fire ring, two sticks in it make a campfire, and a pickaxe takes it back apart into its materials.
