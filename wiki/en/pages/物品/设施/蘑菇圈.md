A **mushroom ring** is a ring of small mushrooms grown on the ground. It is only scenery: it does not block the way, cannot be picked and does not catch fire. At present no map in the game has a mushroom ring. The edible {{物品|蘑菇}} is an item and has nothing to do with the mushroom ring.
