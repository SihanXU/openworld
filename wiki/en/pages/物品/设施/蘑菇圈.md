A **mushroom ring** is a ring of small mushrooms grown on the ground. It is only scenery: it does not block the way, cannot be picked and does not catch fire. At present no map in the game has a mushroom ring. The edible {{item|Mushroom}} is an item and has nothing to do with the mushroom ring.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **{{key|G}}**: the thing is put on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it does not catch fire.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
