{{信息框}}
The **storehouse** is a large wooden store cupboard, a container holding 40 things, more than three times a [[箱子|chest]] (12). There is only one in the game: in [[誓守之地]], built through [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Expanding the Held Ground**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and on the “Buildings” page build the “Storehouse”: after Land level I (The Grove), 12 {{物品|木板}} and 6 {{物品|木头}}, given to him for the Held Ground's stores; press “Build!”. Alberic says: “Walls and a roof for what you keep. Nothing in it will be taken; I sleep lightly.”
- The [[储物棚地基|plot for a storehouse]] south of the sword becomes the storehouse a little later, and is open when built.
- Cannot be made yourself, nor placed in [[建造|build]] mode.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{键|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{键|G}} puts the thing in your hand into it, up to 40 things; opening it shows the chest panel, where clicking a thing moves it between the storehouse and your bag (see [[箱子与容器]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers is a storeroom (no effect for now).
- **Fire**: it is wooden and can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}; what is inside drops on the spot.
- The storehouse has no owner: taking things from it is not theft.
