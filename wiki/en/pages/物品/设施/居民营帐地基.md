The **plot for a residents' tent** is a staked patch of open ground northeast of the sword in [[The Held Ground]], where the “Residents' Tent” of [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] is to stand. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, it becomes a [[tent|tent]] a little later.

## Source

{{sources}}

- The Held Ground has it from the start, northeast of the sword. Cannot be made, nor taken away.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[Alberic|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find the “Residents' Tent” on the “Buildings” page. It requires Land level I (The Grove); the materials are 6 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}} and 10 {{item|Plank}}, given to him for the Held Ground's stores. When the stores are enough, press “Build!”. When it is built, Alberic also gives you 30 {{item|Dawnmark}}.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “Two more beds: two more people may come and live here”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “A tent with two cots in it. A knight once had a hundred men under canvas. Two will do, to begin with.” Every so often the plot checks whether it has been built; once it has, it becomes a [[tent|tent]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: a tent with a bed on each side in front of it, for the people you invite to live there (see [[Held Ground Residents]]).
- The plot does not block the way; you can walk over it. Press {{key|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- The stakes can catch fire. A plot that burns is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[staked plot]].
