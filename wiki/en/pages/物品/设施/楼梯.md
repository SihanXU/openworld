**Stairs** are a block one storey high with steps on top, made from 3 {{item|Plank}} with nothing but your hands. Set them in front of the floor one level up, walk up and take one more step forward, and you are upstairs. You choose their direction when you place them. For the full rules see [[Storeys#Stairs|Storeys]]; for how to place them, see [[Building]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Crafting**: press {{key|C}} and choose “Stairs”: 3 planks, no tool needed.
- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Stairs”; if you have none on you, they are made on the spot from planks.
- **Stairs in hand**: right-click a tile, and the stairs are placed there. Pressing {{key|G}} only puts them down loose on the ground, where they cannot be climbed; pressing {{key|E}} picks them up again.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Placing them

- **Choosing the direction**: in build mode, the arrow on the preview points the way up; press {{key|R}} to turn it clockwise. If you have not turned it, the stairs face away from the player.
- **Set in front of the floor one level up**: the tile the arrow points at must have floor laid on the level above (the ground floor's ceiling, for instance).
- **Keep the space above clear**: the tile directly above the stairs cannot have a ceiling laid on it; that is the way up.
- Stairs take up one tile's volume slot.

### Going up and down

- Step onto the stairs from any side and you are half a storey higher; take one more step in the direction of the arrow, and you stand on the floor of the level above.
- Going down: on the upper level, walk toward the stairs' tile, and you step back onto the stairs and then walk down.
- Someone standing on the stairs counts as being on the stairs' own level.
- In a [[Rooms|room]], the tile above a stairwell counts as covered, so the room is not broken open by it.

### Picking them up and breaking them

- Knock them once with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and they come loose whole where they stand; then press {{key|E}} to pick them up (close by, they are drawn into your bag by themselves), losing no materials.
- Durability 8, wood. Tools only knock them loose; only hitting them with a weapon or bare-handed takes off durability. Broken, they leave 1 plank, with a 50% chance of 2 (half of the 3, see [[Durability and Breaking]]).
- They catch fire: while burning they lose durability and may spread the fire to burnable things within one tile. Once the fire is out the stairs are still there; if their durability burns away, they burn down to one {{item|Ash}}, leaving no planks.

## History

- 2026-10-04: Stairs became a thing you can hold in your hand and place (W6).
- 2026-10-05: Stairs changed into a block one storey high with steps on top, turned with {{key|R}}; no ceiling can be laid above stairs.
- 2026-10-08: Burning stairs spread the fire to their neighbors; burned out, they become ash and no longer fall apart into planks.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
