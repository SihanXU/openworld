A **washing line** is a rope strung between two wooden posts, with cloth hanging from it. At present no map in the game has a washing line on it, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

In the rules a washing line is just something that blocks the way:

- **{{key|G}}**: the thing is put down on its tile (“You set X down by the washing line.”).
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wood and cloth, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
