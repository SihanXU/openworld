**Reeds** are a clump of reeds growing by the water. They grow in the marsh and on the riverbanks of [[The Ford|the Ford]] and by the stream at [[Millbrook]]. They do not block the way and cannot be taken.

## Source

{{sources}}

- The marsh and riverside of the Ford; the stream at Millbrook.
- They cannot be planted, and they do not grow back by themselves.

## Uses

{{uses}}

They are one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with them:

- **Does not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over them.
- **{{key|G}}**: holding something, press {{key|G}} at them and the thing is put on their tile.
- **Hitting**: they have no durability; what happens if you hit them with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: they catch fire. After burning for about 4.5 seconds they become one {{item|Ash}}; while burning, every half second they have a 10% chance to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of them.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle them is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to them, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
