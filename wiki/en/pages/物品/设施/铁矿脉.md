An **iron vein** is a seam of iron ore in the rock. Broken open with a {{item|Pickaxe}}, it drops 2 {{item|Iron Ore}}. [[Dawn Hill Mine]], open from Chapter 1, has 5; [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]] has 4; the outermost ring of [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] has 3, and the Held Ground's [[The Quarry|Quarry]] another 3. Once broken open it leaves a [[spent iron vein|spent iron vein]] in its place, which grows back after one game day. Iron Ore is what [[Smithing|smithing]], the Emberfall smith's forging of [[Dragonsbane]] and [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] need.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Dawn Hill Mine**: 5, in the mine hall and its two chambers. A worn stone pickaxe lies on the ground at the entrance. It can be visited as soon as you reach the village in Chapter 1.
- **The Old Mine**: 4, in the tunnels on either side and in the deepest chamber; a {{monster|cyclops}} guards the iron veins in the deepest chamber. An old pickaxe lies on the ground at the mine entrance.
- **The Held Ground**: 3, outside the third ring of [[bank of mist|banks of mist]]. They can be reached only once the land has been widened to level III in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] and the last ring of mist has lifted.
- **The Quarry**: 3. Reachable once the [[The Quarry|Quarry]] has been opened in Expanding the Held Ground.
- **Grows back**: iron veins are the same everywhere: once broken open each leaves a [[spent iron vein|spent iron vein]] in its place (bedrock, which no tool can chip), which turns back into an iron vein after one game day (24 game hours).

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Breaking it open

- Durability 12, material ore (hardness 2).
- Holding a pickaxe, left-click it (“Mine iron vein”): a stone pickaxe takes 6 points a blow, breaking it open in two. Punching it: “It's too hard.”
- Broken open it drops 2 Iron Ore (half of 4), which pop out and fly into your pack when you walk near; a spent iron vein is left in its place. See [[Mining]] and [[Durability and Breaking]].

### What Iron Ore is used for

- The [[Emberfall]] smith {{person|Garrick}}: 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal forge [[Dragonsbane]].
- Land II, “The Meadow”, in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]]: 5 Iron Ore.
- Smelted into Iron Ingots in a [[forge|forge]], then [[Smithing|smithed]] at an [[anvil|anvil]].

### Other

An iron vein is solid and blocks the way; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.
