A **wardrobe** is a wooden wardrobe. It is a container and, like a [[箱子|chest]], holds 12 things. At present no map in the game has a wardrobe, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{键|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{键|G}} puts what you hold into it, up to 12 things (see [[箱子与容器]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).
- **Breaking**: durability 10, made of wood. Broken, it falls apart into 2 {{物品|木板}} (half of 4), and what is inside drops out (see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- **Fire**: it catches fire and when burnt out becomes one {{物品|灰烬}}; what is inside drops where it stood.

The wardrobe has nothing to do with clothes: you can put anything in it, and it does not equip anything for you.

## History
