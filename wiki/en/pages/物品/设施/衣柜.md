A **wardrobe** is a wooden wardrobe. It is a container and, like a [[chest|chest]], holds 12 things. At present no map in the game has a wardrobe, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{key|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{key|G}} puts what you hold into it, up to 12 things (see [[Chests and Containers]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[Rooms|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).
- **Breaking**: durability 10, made of wood. Broken, it falls apart into 2 {{item|Plank}} (half of 4), and what is inside drops out (see [[Durability and Breaking]]).
- **Fire**: it catches fire and when burnt out becomes one {{item|Ash}}; what is inside drops where it stood.

The wardrobe has nothing to do with clothes: you can put anything in it, and it does not equip anything for you.
