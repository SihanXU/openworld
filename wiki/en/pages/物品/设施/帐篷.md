A **tent** is a cloth tent, 3×3 tiles in size. It is solid, blocking the way and also the view. There are three in the game at the start: Hesper's tent at the camp at [[渡口]], the “rotted tent” left by the pilgrim before deep in [[森林]], and “Alberic's tent” in [[誓守之地]].

All three can be entered: press {{键|E}} facing the tent (“Enter X”) to duck inside, where there is a small room ([[渡口营地的帐篷]], [[朽烂的帐篷里]], [[阿尔贝里克的帐篷里]]); walk down out of the tent's mouth from inside and you are back in front of the tent.

Once the residents' tent, the second and the third residents' tents are built in [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]], the Held Ground's [[地基|plots]] become new tents, each with two beds in front, for the people who come to live there (see [[领地居民]]). These tents have no room and cannot be entered.
## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Ford's camp**: the tent of the ferrywoman {{人物|赫斯珀}}. On stormy days she keeps to the tent's doorway during the day instead of going to the dock.
- **The Forest**: in a clearing deep in the southeast, beside the “rotted tent” are a ring of cold fire, a [[布告栏|Proclamation]] nailed to a tree and a cairn of stones. Once you walk into this camp, you can get the side quest [[上一位朝圣者]] from [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]].
- **The Held Ground**: northwest of the planted sword, “Alberic's tent”. [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]] sleeps in front of the tent at night.
- Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Blocks the way**: a tent takes up 3×3 tiles, and people and creatures walk round it.
- **Blocks the view**: what happens behind a tent cannot be seen by people on its other side. For example, taking something in front of its owner gets you scolded, but behind a tent you are not seen (see [[居民]]).
- **Not a room**: a tent itself does not count as a [[房间|room]], and does not keep off rain; the three that can be entered hold another small map inside.
- **{{键|G}}**: press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on the tent's tile.
- **Hitting**: a tent has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: a tent is cloth and can catch fire; after burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}, and the tent is gone.

What happens when you fiddle with it bare-handed with {{键|E}} is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
