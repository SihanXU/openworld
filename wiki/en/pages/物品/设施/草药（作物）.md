A **herb** (crop) is a herb planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Herb Seeds}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{item|Herb}} and seeds. The harvested {{item|Herb}} can be eaten and restores both satiety and health; it can also be picked up in the wild (see [[Foraging]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{item|Herb Seeds}}, right-click an empty farmland tile; the seeds become a “herb sprout”.
- **Where the seeds come from**: the “Farming” page of the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], 3 to a lot. No map has herb crops already growing (see [[Crops]]). Building mode has no entry for sowing herbs; the seeds must be sown from your hand.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | herb sprout | Water once; 2 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | herb plant | Water again; 2 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe herbs | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 2 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{item|Ash}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- See [[Farming]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 herbs (3 if fertilized) and 2 Herb Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 herb straight into your hands, 1 Herb Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- Digging it up with a {{item|Shovel}} turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”). The shovel is sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
- Crops do not catch fire.
