A **berry bush** is a berry shrub planted in farmland. It is perennial: once its fruit is harvested as {{item|Berries}}, it goes back to having no fruit, and one more watering makes it fruit again. {{item|Berry Seeds}} are bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]; no map has a berry bush growing on it, and you have to plant your own (see [[Crops]]). The berries on the ground in the wild are items you can pick up directly and have nothing to do with berry bushes; the “bushes” on maps are terrain, not berry bushes either.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding berry seeds, right-click an empty farmland tile, and the seeds become a “berry bush sprout”.
- **Where the seeds come from**: the “Farming” page of the trading stall on the Held Ground, 3 to a lot. Build mode has no entry for sowing berries; the seeds must be sown from your hand.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | berry bush sprout | Water once; 2 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | berry bush | Water again; 2 game hours later it bears fruit |
| 3 | berry bush in fruit | Harvest |

Watering, open sky and fertilizing work as for other crops: left-click it holding a container full of water, and 2 game hours later it grows one stage; it does not grow while something covers it overhead; fertilizing with ash gives 1 more at harvest. See [[Farming]].

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click the berry bush in fruit | 2 berries (3 if fertilized) and 2 berry seeds |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick the berry bush in fruit | 1 portion of berries straight into your hands, 1 lot of berry seeds dropped on the ground |

The berry bush is **perennial**: after harvesting or picking, it goes back to the “berry bush” stage with no need to sow again; one more watering and it fruits again. Fertilizer is used up at harvest and must be applied again. The harvest also brings back berry seeds, so as long as you have one bush, you can grow more.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way and do not catch fire.
- Digging it up with a {{item|Shovel}} turns it back into one lot of berry seeds. The shovel is sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
