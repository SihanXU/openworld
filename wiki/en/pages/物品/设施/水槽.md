A **trough** is a wooden trough full of water. In the rules it is a water source like the {{item|well}}: click it holding an empty container and the container fills with water. The animals in the game do not need to drink (see [[Animals]]). At present no map has a trough on it, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drawing water**: holding an empty container (an empty bucket, an {{item|Empty Flask}}, an {{item|Empty Waterskin}}), left-click it: “Filled with water.” “Refill Water” in [[Building|build]] mode can use it too.
- The trough is solid and blocks the way.
- The trough has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it. Although it is wooden, the rules do not count it as flammable, and it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
