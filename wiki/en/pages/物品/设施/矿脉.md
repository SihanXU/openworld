An **ore vein** is a vein of ore in the rock. Every vein placed on a map is one of its three kinds: the [[iron vein|iron vein]], the [[coal seam|coal seam]] and the [[gold vein|gold vein]], most of them in [[The Old Mine]]. This page describes the rules the kinds share; no plain vein of no stated kind is on any map at present.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Iron veins: 4 in [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]], 3 on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
- Coal seams: 4 in the Old Mine.
- Gold veins: 2 in the deepest chamber of the Old Mine.
- The veins in the Old Mine grow back: iron veins and coal seams up to 4 each, a new one appearing on empty cave floor every 3 game hours; gold veins up to 2, one every 6 game hours (see [[Mining#The Old Mine|the Old Mine]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Mining it

- Durability 12; its material is ore (hardness 2).
- **Pickaxe**: hold a pickaxe and left-click it (“Mine X”). A pickaxe works on ore at efficiency 1: a stone pickaxe deals 6 points a blow, so two blows break it open.
- **Axe**: it can mine, but at an efficiency of only 0.1.
- **Fist**: the body is softer than ore, and all you get is “Too hard.”
- Mined, it drops half of its makeup: an iron vein drops 2 {{item|Iron Ore}}, a coal seam 2 {{item|Coal}}, a gold vein 2 {{item|Gold Ore}}; a plain ore vein drops 1 {{item|Stone}}.

### Other

An ore vein is solid and blocks the way; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken. Hold something and press {{key|G}}, and the thing is put down on its tile.
