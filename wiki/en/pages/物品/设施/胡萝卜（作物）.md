{{信息框}}
A **carrot** (crop) is a carrot planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{物品|胡萝卜种子}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{物品|胡萝卜}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{物品|胡萝卜}}: eaten raw it restores 3 [[饱食度|satiety]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{物品|胡萝卜种子}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Carrot Seeds.” The seeds become a “carrot sprout”. “Sow Carrot” on the “Farming” page of [[建造|build]] mode works too.
- **Firstlight Village's vegetable garden**: the garden has a row of carrots, from top to bottom one sprout, one young plant and one ripe plant; the ripe one can be harvested right away. The Garden Supplies next to it holds 2 Carrot Seeds (see [[农耕#初原村的菜圃|Firstlight Village's vegetable garden]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | carrot sprout | Water once; 2 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young carrot | Water again; 2 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe carrot | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{状态|浇过水}} and grows one stage 2 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{物品|灰烬}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- See [[农耕]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{物品|镰刀}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 carrots (3 if fertilized) and 2 Carrot Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{键|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 carrot straight into your hands, 1 Carrot Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- The rules do not say what happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe.
- Digging it up with a {{物品|铲子}} turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”). The shovel is sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]].
- Crops do not catch fire.
