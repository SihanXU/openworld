{{信息框}}
The **handcart** belongs to the peddler {{人物|托宾}} and stands on the east side of [[初原村]]. When the ferry at the Ford broke down, he pushed his cart into the village. On it lie a [[魔杖|Frost Wand]] and a {{物品|一瓶灯油}}, both Tobin's.

## Source

{{来源}}

- Tobin's is the only one. The cart belongs to Tobin. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Tobin's cart

- Tobin minds his cart every day from 6:00 to 9:00 and from 11:00 to 19:00, and sleeps beside it at night. While the cart is on fire, he cannot mind it.
- The things on the cart are Tobin's. Take one in front of him and he says “Hey — that is mine!”
- **The Frost Wand**: hold anything edible and press {{键|G}} on Tobin; he accepts it and gives you the wand: “Bless you. The wand on my cart — the blue one — it is yours.” After that he says nothing when you take the wand from the cart. The wand can freeze the river, one of the ways to cross at [[渡口|the Ford]].
- The lamp oil cannot be given away; it stays Tobin's.

### Putting things on it

The handcart is a surface: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the cart (“You set X on the handcart.”). Pressing {{键|E}} picks up what is on the cart first; the cart itself cannot be taken.

### Unbreakable, but it burns

The handcart has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it. It is wooden and catches fire, burning down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
