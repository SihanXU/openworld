A **turnip** (crop) is a turnip planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Turnip Seeds}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{item|Turnip}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{item|Turnip}}: eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|satiety]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{item|Turnip Seeds}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Turnip Seeds.” The seeds become a “turnip sprout”. “Sow Turnip” on the “Planting” page of [[Building]] mode works too.
- **Firstlight Village's vegetable garden**: the garden has a row of turnips, from top to bottom one sprout, one young plant and one ripe plant; the ripe one can be harvested right away. The garden toolbox next to it holds 2 Turnip Seeds (see [[Farming#Firstlight's vegetable garden|Firstlight Village's vegetable garden]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | turnip sprout | Water once; 1 game hour later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young turnip | Water again; 1 game hour later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe turnip | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 1 game hour later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{item|Ash}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- See [[Farming]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 turnips (3 if fertilized) and 2 Turnip Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 turnip straight into your hands, 1 Turnip Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- Digging it up with a {{item|Shovel}} turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”). The shovel is sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
- Crops do not catch fire.
