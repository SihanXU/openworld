A **boulder** is a big rock that blocks the way. Seven lie scattered on the slope north of [[The Ford]]. Broken up with a {{item|Pickaxe}} it gives 4 {{item|Stone}}, the most stone of anything (see [[Mining]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- 7 at the Ford, all on the slope to the north.
- Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Breaking it up

- Durability 12, stone (hardness 2).
- **Pickaxe**: hold the pickaxe and left-click it (“Mine boulder”). A pickaxe works stone at efficiency 1: a Stone Pickaxe does 6 a blow and breaks it in two.
- **Axe**: can chip it, but at efficiency only 0.25.
- **Fist**: the body is softer than stone; striking it only gives “Too hard.”, with no damage.
- Broken, it drops 4 stones (half of 8), which bounce out and fly into your bag when you come near. See [[Durability and Breaking]].

### Otherwise

- A boulder is solid and blocks the way; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken (“This can't be taken.”).
- Press {{key|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile (“You put the something beside the boulder.”).
- What happens when you fiddle with it bare-handed with {{key|E}} is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The boulder has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-04: The boulder gained durability 12, the material stone and a makeup (8 stones), and is broken up with a pickaxe (W2b).
