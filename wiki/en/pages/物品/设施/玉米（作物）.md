**Corn** (crop) is corn planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Corn Seeds}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{item|Corn}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{item|Corn}}: eaten raw it restores 3 [[Satiety|satiety]]. Corn is **perennial**: after the harvest it does not disappear but goes back to a young plant, and one more watering ripens it again.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{item|Corn Seeds}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Corn Seeds.” The seeds become a “corn sprout”. “Sow Corn” on the “Farming” page of [[Building|build]] mode works too.
- The seeds are bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] (the “Farming” shelf, 3 for 18 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]). No map has corn already planted, and neither does the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | corn sprout | Water once; 4 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young corn | Water again; 4 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe corn | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{item|Fertilizer}} or {{item|Ash}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (with fertilizer, “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- All crops grow equally fast: from sowing to ripe takes at least 4 game hours and two waterings. See [[Farming]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 corn (3 if fertilized) and 2 Corn Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 corn straight into your hands, 1 Corn Seeds dropped on the ground |

Corn is **perennial**: after harvesting or picking, it goes back to “young corn” with no need to sow again; one more watering, and 4 game hours later it is ripe again. Fertilizer is used up at harvest and must be applied again.

Harvested corn can be sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] for 5 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- The rules do not say what happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe.
- In the rules, digging it up with a [[Shovel|shovel]] turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.
