The **Shrine of the Crown** is the first, westernmost [[wayside shrine|wayside shrine]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is something worn on the head.

## Source

{{sources}}

On [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], the first and westernmost. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: press {{key|E}} to read: “THE FIRST SUFFERING: THE CROWN. They pressed the thorns into his brow, and he did not lift his hand. Pilgrim, lay down what you wear upon your head, and your pride with it.”
- **Lighting**: holding something that can be worn on the head (a hat, a helmet, a circlet), press {{key|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a pilgrim's hat (on the grass on the south side of the road's west end).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Crown asks for something worn on the head.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[wayside shrine]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Crown” of [[The First Seal]].

## Background

The [[Crown of Thorns]] can be worn on the head, but it is a relic, and this shrine does not take it. Lumi says this one is the coldest: “The crown one is cold. Colder than the others...”

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: Relics and important items are no longer taken.
