The **Shrine of the Crown** is the first, westernmost [[路边圣龛|wayside shrine]] on [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is something worn on the head: pride laid down.

## Source

{{来源}}

On [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], the first and westernmost. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: press {{键|E}} to read: “THE FIRST SUFFERING: THE CROWN. He let them press the thorns into his brow, and did not lift his hand. — Lay down what you wear on your head, and be lowly.”
- **Lighting**: holding something that can be worn on the head (a hat, a helmet, a circlet), press {{键|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a pilgrim's hat (on the grass on the south side of the road's west end).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Crown asks for something worn on the head: pride laid down.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[路边圣龛]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Crown” of [[第一印]].

## Background

The [[荆棘冠]] can be worn on the head, but it is a relic, and this shrine does not take it. Lumi says this one is the coldest: “I touched it and it touched back.”

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: Relics and important items are no longer taken.
