The **fireplace** is a stone-built fireplace. Like the {{item|campfire}} it is a cooking fixture: lit, it can cook raw food, and a [[Rooms|room]] with a fireplace counts as a kitchen. For now no map in the game has a fireplace, and you cannot make one yourself.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Lighting and putting out

- **Lighting**: left-click it holding a {{item|Torch}}, or strike it with the fire element. Once you have learned {{skill|Firemaker}}, right-clicking it with an ordinary stick lights it too.
- **Putting out**: left-click it holding a container full of water.

### Cooking

- Right-click the lit fireplace holding raw food and the food is cooked (“Cook X on the fireplace”). Pressing {{key|G}} at it holding raw food does the same (see [[Cooking]]).
- Press {{key|G}} holding something burnable, and the thing is thrown into the fire and burns to {{item|Ash}}.

### Warming yourself

Press {{key|E}} bare-handed at the lit fireplace (“Warm yourself by the fireplace”): if you are {{status|wet}}, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself by the fireplace; the damp steams away.”); if not, only “The fireplace's warmth seeps into your palms.” It restores no health. What happens when you press {{key|E}} bare-handed at a cold fireplace is not written in the rules. See [[campfire#Warming up|warming yourself]].

### Otherwise

- **Kitchen**: the fireplace is a cooking fixture, and a [[Rooms|room]] with a fireplace counts as a kitchen (no effect for now).
- **Monsters keep away**: apart from fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 tiles of something lit.
- The fireplace is stone and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The fireplace has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-04: Cooking fixtures make a room a kitchen (W5).
- 2026-10-09: Pressing {{key|E}} bare-handed warms you by a lit fireplace.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
