{{信息框}}
**Blueberries** (crop) are blueberries planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{物品|蓝莓种子}}, water them twice, and once ripe harvest {{物品|蓝莓}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{物品|蓝莓}}: eaten raw it restores 1 [[饱食度|satiety]]. Blueberries are **perennial**: after a harvest they do not disappear but go back to a young plant, and one more watering ripens them again.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{物品|蓝莓种子}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Blueberries Seeds.” The seeds become a “blueberries sprout”. “Sow Blueberries” on the “Planting” page of [[建造]] mode works too.
- The seeds are sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] (“Farming” shelf, 3 for 24 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]). No map has blueberries already planted, and the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] has none either.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | blueberries sprout | Water once; 4 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young blueberries | Water again; 4 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe blueberries | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click them: “You water X.” They get {{状态|浇过水}} and grow one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers them overhead, they do not grow when the time comes; they check again every game hour and grow only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but they never wither either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{物品|肥料}} or {{物品|灰烬}}, right-click them before they are ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (With fertilizer it is “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- All crops grow equally fast: at least 4 game hours and two waterings from sowing to ripeness. See [[农耕]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{物品|镰刀}}, left-click them when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 blueberries (3 if fertilized) and 2 Blueberries Seeds, dropped on their tile |
| Press {{键|E}} to pick them when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 portion of blueberries straight into your hands, 1 Blueberries Seeds dropped on the ground |

Blueberries are **perennial**: after harvesting or picking, they go back to “young blueberries”, with no need to sow again; water them once more and they ripen again 4 game hours later. Fertilizer is used up at the harvest and must be applied again.

Harvested blueberries can be sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] for 3 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] per portion.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit them with a weapon or sickle before they are ripe is not written in the rules.
- In the rules, digging them up with a [[铲子|shovel]] turns them back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.
