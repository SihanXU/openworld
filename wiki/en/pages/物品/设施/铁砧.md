An **anvil** is the block a smith works on, where the second step of [[Smithing|smithing]] is done: with a {{item|Smith Hammer}} in your hand (or pack), stand beside an anvil, and the [[Crafting|crafting]] panel lets you beat ingots into weapons, armour, tools, jewellery and [[Gear Sets|profession sets]]. There are three in the game, each beside a lit [[forge|forge]]: Haldor's anvil in the smithy of [[Firstlight Village]], Alberic's anvil east of the sword on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], and the anvil in front of the smithy in the northeast of [[Emberfall]]. An anvil is also a surface that things can be put on.

## Source

{{sources}}

- One each: in the smithy beside the East Road in Firstlight Village (named “Haldor's anvil”), east of the sword on the Held Ground (“Alberic's anvil”), and in front of the smithy in Emberfall. They can be used when the smith is away.
- You cannot make this kind of anvil; once you have learned {{skill|Forge Builder}}, you can make a movable [[smith's anvil|smith's anvil]] from 3 Iron Ingots beside a forge.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Smithing**: stand beside an anvil with a smith hammer and press {{key|C}}; the crafting panel lists everything made at an anvil: daggers, spears, swords, maces, helmets and shields, and, once the skills are learned, warhammers, halberds, pauldrons, breastplates, metal tools, rings and amulets, as well as the four professions' sets. What you make is of the ingot's metal; its [[Quality|quality]] depends on your level and smithing skills, and it rolls [[Affixes|affixes]] as it is made (how many depends on quality, from 2 for Crude to 5 for Masterwork). See [[Smithing]] and [[Gear Sets]].
- **Putting things on it**: holding something, press {{key|G}} at it and the thing is put on the anvil (“You put X on the anvil.”). {{key|E}} first picks up what is on it; the anvil itself cannot be taken.
- **Workshop**: the anvil is a workstation, and a [[Rooms|room]] with an anvil in it counts as a workshop (no effect at present).
- **Smiths**: Haldor, Alberic and Garrick work at their anvils, and need the forge beside them lit. [[Dragonsbane]] is got by handing Iron Ore and Coal to Garrick and then talking to him; by the rules it needs no anvil.
- **Cannot be broken**: the anvil is metal and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire. Struck by the lightning element it becomes charged ({{status|electrified}}), which does nothing to it.

For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
