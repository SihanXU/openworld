A **staked plot** is a patch of open ground ringed with stakes round the sword in [[誓守之地]]. There are eight, each labelled with what is to be built there (“plot for a …”): once that building is built in [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]]'s [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] panel, the plot becomes it a little later.

## Source

{{来源}}

All eight plots are in the Held Ground from the start; they cannot be made, nor taken away:

|---|---|---|---|

| [[厨房灶台地基]] | South of the sword | A lit [[营火|campfire]] | — |
| [[储物棚地基]] | South of the sword | A [[储物棚|storehouse]] (open) | I |

For materials and rewards see [[领地扩建#建筑|Expanding the Held Ground]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Becoming a building**: every so often a plot checks whether its building has been built in Expanding the Held Ground; once it has, the plot becomes that thing with a flash of light. You need not walk over to it or do anything else.
- **Does not block the way**: you can walk over it; press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- **Fire**: the stakes can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}. A plot that burns is gone: that item in Expanding the Held Ground can still be built and still takes the materials, but nothing will appear here anymore. Keep fire away.
- Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).

## History
