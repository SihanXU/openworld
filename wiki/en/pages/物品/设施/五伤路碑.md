The **five-wound shrine stone** is an ancient stone set up by the wayside by the Church of the Five Sufferings, carved with five wounds (see [[The Five Sufferings]]). There are two in the game: the one at [[Mountain Foot]] has no name, and is the stone Lumi asks the player to “do something to” in the prologue; the one on [[Dawn Hill]] is called “the Dawnstone”, is carved with words, and can be read.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Mountain Foot**: on the path down the mountain. Walk up to it and the first objective of the quest [[Down from the Mountain]], “The wayside stone at the foot of the mountain”, is complete. One of [[Pell|Pell]]'s [[chalk drawing of a stone|chalk drawings]] is right beside it.
- **Dawn Hill**: on the hill, by the path to the [[Chapel of the First Dawn|Chapel of the First Dawn]], with dawnbloom growing around it.
- Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### The stone at Mountain Foot: try it

Walk up to the stone at Mountain Foot and {{person|Lumi}} says: “It's cold next to it. Five marks, and the first one's cracked… I don't like it. I don't know why I don't like it. — Do something to it: use what's in your hand, put something on it, hit it. Whatever you do, the world will learn to answer.”

Apart from “put down”, this stone has no defined response at all:

- Fiddling with it bare-handed with {{key|E}}, right-clicking it with something in hand, and hitting it bare-handed are all [[The World Learns|things the world has never seen]]. With AI connected the label carries a “?”, and the world writes a new rule for the act and records it in the [[Threadbook|Threadbook]]. Without AI the label has no “?”, and it only says “You run a hand over the five-wound shrine stone.” or “Nothing happens.”
- A learned rule belongs to the kind of thing the act falls on: touching it bare-handed or hitting it bare-handed teaches the stone; using a stone on it teaches the stone you hold, and switching to a log means teaching it again.
- After seeing the first such attempt, Lumi says: “See? The world answers things it's never seen before. Everything it learns goes in the Threadbook — press J.”
- Pressing {{key|G}} with something in hand puts it on the stone's tile (“You put the something beside the five-wound shrine stone.”). This is defined and never asks the AI.

### The Dawnstone

The stone on Dawn Hill is carved with words; walk up and press {{key|E}} to read it: “Cut deep, and old: HERE THE SUN ROSE AGAIN. Below, newer: five small wounds, and a flame.”

### Otherwise

The stone is stone: it has no durability and cannot be broken; it does not burn; it cannot be taken.

## History

- 2026-10-06: Renamed the five-wound shrine stone and carved with five wounds (Chapter 1).
- 2026-10-07: The Dawnstone was raised on Dawn Hill.
- 2026-10-09: A learned rule belongs to one kind of thing only: what a stone taught must be taught again with a log.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
