The **chalk drawing of a stone** is a picture the child {{person|Pell}} drew in chalk on the ground at [[Mountain Foot|Mountain Foot]], beside the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stone]]. It lies flat on the ground and does not block the way. Walk up to it and press {{key|E}} to see what it shows; once you have looked at it, the drawing counts as found.

## Source

{{sources}}

- There is only this one, drawn by Pell. You cannot draw one yourself.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Looking at the drawing

- Stand within 1 tile of the drawing and press {{key|E}}. What it shows: “The shrine stone, five marks on it, the first scribbled over in black. A grey figure with its hand on it.”
- Once you have looked at it, the objective “The shrine stone” of the side quest [[Pell's Drawings]] is complete. Looking at it before taking the quest counts too.
- Pell says he drew one in every place where he saw the Grey Man. The four drawings are: [[chalk drawing of a stone]] (by the shrine stone at the mountain's foot), [[chalk drawing of a door]] (at the barrow's door), [[chalk drawing of a bridge]] (by the footbridge at the Ford) and [[chalk drawing of a grey man]] (by the flame in Firstlight Village).

### Other

- The chalk drawing cannot be taken (“That stays where it is.”), nor wiped away: water only makes it wet, and fire cannot burn it.
- It has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- Hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on the drawing's tile.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
