The **plot for a kitchen hearth** is a staked patch of open ground south of the sword in [[The Held Ground]], where the “Kitchen Hearth” of [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] is to stand. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, it becomes a lit [[campfire|campfire]] a little later, already burning when built.

## Source

{{sources}}

- The Held Ground has it from the start, south of the sword. Cannot be made, nor taken away.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[Alberic|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find the “Kitchen Hearth” on the “Buildings” page. No requirement (it can be built from the start); the materials are 10 {{item|Stone}} and 4 {{item|Log}}, given to him for the Held Ground's stores. When the stores are enough, press “Build!”.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “A hearth that is always lit: cook what you catch and grow”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “A fire that stays lit. Eat. A steward who lets his lord starve is no steward.” Every so often the plot checks whether it has been built; once it has, it becomes a lit [[campfire|campfire]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: hold raw food and right-click it to cook it (see [[Cooking]]); press {{key|E}} bare-handed to warm yourself.
- The plot does not block the way; you can walk over it. Press {{key|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- The stakes can catch fire. A plot that burns is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[staked plot]].
