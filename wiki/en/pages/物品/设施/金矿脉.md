A **gold vein** is a seam of gold ore in the rock. Broken open with a {{item|Pickaxe}}, it drops 2 {{item|Gold Ore}}. There are two in the deepest chamber of [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]], just below the two [[iron vein|iron veins]] guarded by the {{monster|cyclops}}; [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]]'s [[The Quarry|Quarry]] has one too. Land III, “The Old Bounds”, in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3 Gold Ore.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The Old Mine**: 2, in the deepest chamber (at the north end, one tile below the iron veins).
- **The Quarry**: 1, on the north side of the Quarry. It can be reached only after the Quarry is opened in [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]].
- **Grows back**: once broken open it leaves a [[spent gold vein|spent gold vein]] in its place (bedrock that cannot be chipped), which turns back into a gold vein after one game day, just like iron veins and coal seams. [[Dawn Hill Mine]] has no gold veins.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Breaking it open

- Durability 12, material ore, like the other [[ore vein|ore veins]].
- Holding a pickaxe, left-click it (“Mine gold vein”): a stone pickaxe takes 6 points a blow, breaking it open in two. Punching it: “It's too hard.”
- Broken open it drops 2 Gold Ore (half of 4), which pop out and fly into your pack when you walk near. Mining out both veins in the Old Mine is enough for the 3 that Land III of Expanding the Held Ground needs. See [[Mining]] and [[Durability and Breaking]].

### Other

A gold vein is solid and blocks the way; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.
