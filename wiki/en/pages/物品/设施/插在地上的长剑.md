The **planted sword** is [[Alberic|Alberic]]'s longsword, planted point-down in the earth at the very center of [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], a little crooked. It is the center of the Held Ground: when you arrive by [[The Oath-Thorn|the Oath-Thorn]], you stand three tiles due south of it, and all three rings of mist are centered on it. There is only one in the game.

## Source

{{sources}}

- At the center of the Held Ground, there from the start. You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Read**: walk up to it and press {{key|E}} (“Read the planted sword”): “Alberic's longsword, driven point down into the earth, a little crooked. Its grip is wrapped in new cloth.”
- **Expanding the Held Ground is not done here**: to expand the Held Ground, go to Alberic himself, talk to him and choose “The Held Ground…” (see [[Expanding the Held Ground]]). The sword can only be read.
- It blocks the way, is metal and conducts electricity; it does not catch fire.
- Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## Lore

{{spoiler|Alberic's vow}}
Alberic's vow is “I will hold until I am relieved.” Once the player has taken the Crown of Thorns from him, he turns the vow around: he holds this ground for the one who relieved him. Where the sword stands, there he holds; the ground reaches as far as he can hold it — the mist that expanding the Held Ground pushes back is the edge of what he holds, growing wider.
