A **lantern post** is a wooden post with a lantern hanging from it. There is one south of the camp at [[The Ford|the Ford]], unlit. Like a lamp post, it can be lit and put out. It is made of wood, but fire touching it only lights the lamp: if it is already lit and fire touches it again, nothing happens.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this one, at the Ford. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Lighting and putting out

- **Lighting**: holding a {{item|Torch}}, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn {{skill|Firemaker}}, right-clicking it with an ordinary stick lights it too. If it is wet, the first touch of fire only dries it.
- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water, left-click it. Rain does not put out a lit lantern.

### Fire

The lantern post is wooden and flammable, but it is a thing that can be lit: when fire touches it, it lights if unlit and stays as it is if lit, and the post does not catch fire from it. Only when it has been doused with [[Flask of Oil|lamp oil]] and then touched by fire does it flare up and burn whole ({{status|burning}}): after burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes one {{item|Ash}}, and the lantern post is gone; while burning it may also spread the fire to something flammable within one tile (see [[Elements#Oil|Oil]]).

### Once it is lit

- Except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit (see [[Factions and Temper]]).
- Hold something that burns and press {{key|G}} on a lit lantern post, and the thing goes into the fire.
- Press {{key|E}} empty-handed to warm yourself beside it (“Warm yourself at the lantern post”): if you are {{status|wet}}, the damp is dried off; if not, it only warms your hands, with no health restored. The Ford's post starts out unlit, and the rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} empty-handed on it while it is unlit. See [[campfire#Warming up|Warming yourself]].
- The lantern post is not a cooking fixture and cannot cook anything: holding raw food and pressing {{key|G}} on it only puts the food down on the post's tile; the rules do not say what happens when you right-click it holding raw food. For cooking, see [[Cooking]].

### Unbreakable

The lantern post has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
