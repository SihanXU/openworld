{{信息框}}
The **log bench** is a bench made of a whole log laid on its side, 3 tiles wide, and it blocks the way. Things can be put on it. There is one by the campfire of the camp at [[渡口]], one by the fire in [[初原村]], and one on [[草原]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Ford**: south of the camp's campfire.
- **Firstlight Village**: by the village fire. {{人物|芬尼克}} mends harness by the bench every day from 7:00 to 22:00 (“Fennick by the bench”).
- **The Grassland**: “Brin's bench”, beside the herd. The herder {{人物|布琳}} watches the herd here every day from 6:00 to 21:00.
- Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Putting things on it**: press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on the bench (“You put the something on the log bench.”).
- **{{键|E}}**: first picks up what is on the bench; the bench itself cannot be taken.
- **Blocks the way**: the bench is solid, and people and creatures walk round it.
- **Hitting**: the bench has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it is wooden, can catch fire, and burns down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}.

The game has no “sit down”. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
