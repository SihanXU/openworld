The **log bench** is a bench made of a whole log laid on its side, 3 tiles wide, and it blocks the way. Things can be put on it. There is one by the campfire of the camp at [[The Ford]], one by the fire in [[Firstlight Village]], and one on [[The Grassland]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The Ford**: south of the camp's campfire.
- **Firstlight Village**: by the village fire. {{person|Fennick}} mends harness by the bench every day from 7:00 to 22:00 (“Fennick by the bench”).
- **The Grassland**: “Brin's bench”, beside the herd. The herder {{person|Brin}} watches the herd here every day from 6:00 to 21:00.
- Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: press {{key|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on the bench (“You put the something on the log bench.”).
- **{{key|E}}**: first picks up what is on the bench; the bench itself cannot be taken.
- **Blocks the way**: the bench is solid, and people and creatures walk round it.
- **Hitting**: the bench has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it is wooden, can catch fire, and burns down to a portion of {{item|Ash}}.

The game has no “sit down”. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
