{{信息框}}
A **bank of mist** is mist standing tile by tile on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]]. The banks form three rings around the sword planted in the ground, closing the Held Ground in at their centre; a bank of mist is solid and cannot be walked through. Each time the steward, [[阿尔贝里克]], widens the Held Ground, one ring of mist lifts by itself and uncovers the land beyond.

## Source

{{来源}}

Only on the Held Ground. The three rings lie ever farther from the sword at the centre:

| Ring | When it lifts | What lies beyond |
|---|---|---|
| First ring | On completing [[扩建誓守之地 I]] | A small grove |
| Second ring | On completing [[扩建誓守之地 II]] | A flower meadow and a pond |
| Third ring | On completing [[扩建誓守之地 III]] | Four [[墓碑|boundary stones]] and three [[铁矿脉|iron veins]] |

Widening the Held Ground III needs 3 {{物品|金矿石}}, dug from the [[金矿脉|gold veins]] in the deepest part of [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Lifting

Each ring of mist checks every 3 seconds: once the Held Ground has been widened as far as its ring, it lifts with a “poof” and vanishes. So when you come back to the Held Ground after handing in the task, that ring of mist soon clears.

### Blocking the way

- A bank of mist is solid; neither people nor creatures can walk through it.
- It has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.
- Holding something, press {{键|G}} at it and the thing is put on its tile.

What happens if you press {{键|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
