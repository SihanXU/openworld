A **haystack** is hay piled into a big stack. It blocks the way, but no grass can be taken out of it. It is very dry and catches fire most easily of all: a single spark can set it burning, and while it burns it spreads the fire beside it. There is one north of the camp at [[The Ford]]; another, in the burnt farmstead north of [[The East Road]], keeps smouldering.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **The Ford**: north of the camp, one stack.
- **The East Road**: in the burnt farmstead north of the road, the yard already burnt to ash, the haystack still smoulders ({{status|lit}}).
- Cannot be made. The grass you can pick up off the ground is {{item|Cut Grass}}, and has nothing to do with the haystack.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Fire

- A haystack is dry and flammable: fire or the fire element sets it burning at a touch; lightning striking it in a storm sets it burning too.
- While burning, it has a 30% chance every half second to spread the fire to something flammable within one tile, much faster than ordinary things.
- After burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes a portion of {{item|Ash}}, and the haystack is gone.
- The stack on the East Road is a smouldering fire ({{status|lit}}), like a campfire: it does not spread, and does not burn itself out; monsters keep 2 tiles away from it, and it lights its surroundings at night. Rain does not put it out; left-click it holding a container full of water and the fire goes out, after which it is an ordinary haystack.
- A smouldering haystack is not something that can be lit: let fire touch it again (a torch, the fire element) and it truly bursts into flame, spreading and burning out as above.

### Otherwise

- A haystack is solid and blocks the way.
- It has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- Press {{key|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.

What happens when you fiddle with it bare-handed with {{key|E}} is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History
