The **dead tree** is a dead tree at the narrowest point of the river at [[The Ford|the Ford]]. It is one of the ways across: chop it three times with an axe and it falls away from the player; chop it standing on its west side and it falls across the water, its trunk becoming a stretch of wooden bridge. A [[Woodcutting#Stumps|stump]] is left behind, which grows into a living tree two days later.

## Source

{{sources}}

- There is only this one, at the Ford, on the west bank at the river's narrowest point. You cannot plant one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Felling it into a bridge

- Hold an axe and left-click it (“Chop the dead tree”). The first two blows: “The axe bites into the dead wood. The trunk creaks.”
- The third: “The dead tree creaks, leans, and comes down with a crash.” It falls away from the player, up to 8 tiles long.
- Along the direction it falls, every shallow-water tile becomes wooden bridge, stopping at the first tile that is not water. So stand on its **west** side to chop it, so that it falls east across the river.
- Once felled, it becomes a stump. While the stump is under open sky and no one stands on it, it grows into a tree after two game days (see [[Woodcutting]]).
- The people of the Ford talk about it for a few days.

The rules do not say what happens if you hit it with anything but an axe: the dead tree has no durability and answers only to the axe.

### Ways across the river

The Ford has several ways across: mend the [[ferry winch|ferry winch]] and ask {{person|Hesper}} to ferry you, fell the dead tree, or freeze the river with the Frost [[Wand|Wand]]. The world always makes sure at least one of them remains; if none were left, Hesper would pole you across on the raft herself (see [[The Ford]]).

### It can be struck by lightning

- The dead tree is tall and dry. In a thunderstorm, half the time lightning falls on something tall within 12 tiles of the player, and the dead tree is just such a thing.
- Struck by lightning or touched by fire, it catches fire; after burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes one {{item|Ash}}, and the dead tree is gone, and that way across with it. Rain puts out what is burning; if the rain puts it out before it burns away, it survives.

### Other

- The dead tree is solid; it blocks the way and blocks sight.
- Hold something and press {{key|G}}, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”).
