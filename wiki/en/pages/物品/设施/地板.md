{{信息框}}
A **floor** is a wooden floor piece for building houses, made from 2 {{物品|木板}} on the spot. Laid on the ground, it covers the terrain that was there; laid upstairs, it is a floorboard and needs walls on the storey below to hold it. A storey's ceiling is the floor of the storey above: the same piece of floor is a ceiling seen from below and a floor seen from above. For the detailed rules see [[楼层#地板|Storeys]] and [[楼层#天花板|ceilings]]; for how to place it see [[建造]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Crafting**: press {{键|C}} and choose “Floor”: 2 planks, no tool needed.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Floor”. If you have none on you, it is made from planks on the spot. Press {{键|R}} to switch between “Lay on this storey” and “Lay as ceiling”. Drag to lay a row.
- **Holding a floor**: right-click a tile to lay it (hold and drag to lay a row). Pressing {{键|G}} only puts it down loose on the ground, not laid, and {{键|E}} picks it up again.
- The last step of lesson six of the [[新手教程|tutorial]]: choose the floor in build mode, press {{键|R}} to switch to ceiling, and lay the roof on.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Laid on the ground

- It covers the terrain that was there and becomes the surface of the tile; laid on [[地形|flowers]] or dry grass, it also covers the flowers and tall grass drawn on the ground. Take the floor away and the terrain and plants show again.
- It can only be laid on ground you can stand on: not on a tile of water, lava, tree or rock (“You can't lay a floor on this ground.”).
- A tile can have only one floor per storey (“There is a floor here already.”). If a bed, stairs or a chest already stands on the tile, the floor is laid underneath them.

### Laid upstairs, laid as a ceiling

- A floor upstairs needs a wall or door on the storey below, within 2 tiles of the tile, to hold it (“Nothing could hold it up.”).
- To lay a ceiling over your own storey you have to choose it on purpose: press {{键|R}} in build mode; holding a floor, press {{键|R}} to switch the right click to laying a ceiling, or Alt+right-click and choose “Ceiling”.
- No ceiling can be laid directly above stairs (“The space above the stairs must stay open.”).
- A tile with a floor overhead stays out of the rain; a house walled all round and fully ceilinged becomes a [[房间|room]]. Crops do not grow under a ceiling.

### Taking it up

- Strike a floor on your storey once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole piece comes loose and lies on the spot; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself).
- To take a ceiling, hold an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, Alt+left-click the tile and choose “Ceiling” to knock it loose.
- A floor upstairs with something on it (a wall, a bed, a person standing there) cannot be knocked loose; first take away what is on it.

### Falling in

When the walls holding a floor upstairs are taken away or broken and no other wall nearby holds it, the floor falls in: “The floor gives way.” It breaks into 1 plank, and the people and things on it fall to the first storey below that has a floor. Falling 1 storey does no harm; each storey more costs 6 health (see [[耐久与破坏#摔落|falling]]).

### Breaking and fire

- Durability 8, wood. Broken, it leaves 1 plank (half of 2).
- It can catch fire: while burning it has a half chance every half second to lose 1 durability, and a 10% chance to spread the fire to something burnable within one tile. When the fire goes out the floor remains, with less durability; when its durability burns away, it burns to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}, leaving no plank.

## History

- 2026-10-04: The floor became something you can hold in your hand, lay down and take up; a floor upstairs needs walls to hold it, and falls in when the walls are gone (W6).
- 2026-10-05: A ceiling has to be chosen on purpose with {{键|R}} or a long press; no ceiling can be laid above stairs.

- 2026-10-08: A burning floor spreads the fire beside it; burnt away, it turns to ash instead of falling apart into planks.
- 2026-10-08: A floor laid on the ground covers the plants on that tile; flowers and grass no longer poke up through the floor.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
