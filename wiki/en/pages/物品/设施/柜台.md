A **counter** is a length of wooden counter that things can be put on. A counter is not for buying and selling: the only place in the game to buy and sell is the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] (see [[Money]]). At present no map has a counter on it, and you cannot make one.

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: a counter is a surface. Hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the counter (“You set X on the counter.”).
- **{{key|E}}**: picks up what is on the counter first; the counter itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: the counter has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
