The **trading stall** is a stall on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], named “Alberic's stall”. Press {{键|E}} to open the stall panel and buy seeds, fertilizer, a shovel, feed, buckets, crated livestock, bait and a fishing rod with [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]], or sell it what you grow, raise and catch in exchange for Dawnmarks. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, see [[金钱]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- There is only this one, on the Held Ground, southeast of the sword planted in the ground, two tiles east of where [[阿尔贝里克]] stands by day.
- You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Trading

- **Opening**: walk up to the stall and press {{键|E}} (label “Trade at Alberic's stall”). This opens the stall page of the menu, with four shelves: **Farming**, **Ranching**, **Fishing** and **Sell**, and above them how many Dawnmarks you carry.
- **Buying**: click a thing on one of the first three shelves; its price is taken and what you bought goes straight into your pack (how many you get per purchase is in the tables below). Without enough money you cannot buy.
- **Selling**: click a thing on the “Sell” shelf; one of it is taken from your pack or hands, and the Dawnmarks it fetches go into your pack. One at a time.
- **You must stand beside it**: you can trade only while standing in the ring of 8 tiles around the stall. When you are not beside it, the stall page says “Stand beside the stall to trade.” and the buy and sell buttons are grey; a trade sent while not beside it does not go through, and you are only told “That has to be done at the stall.”
- **No one needs to mind it**: the stall does its own trade; you can buy and sell even when Alberic is not nearby or has gone to bed for the night.

### Farming

{{货摊表|农业}}

[[胡萝卜]], [[芜菁]], [[番茄]] and [[南瓜]] also grow in the vegetable garden in [[初原村]]; the seeds of the other ten vegetables ([[防风草]], [[土豆]], [[小萝卜]], [[花椰菜]] and [[甜瓜]], and [[玉米]], [[蓝莓]], [[草莓]], [[辣椒]] and [[茄子]], which bear again after a harvest) are sold only here. See [[作物]].

The [[铲子|shovel]] too is sold only here: with it you dig crops back up into seeds and grub out tree stumps by the roots (see [[农耕#铲子|Shovel]] and [[伐木]]).

### Ranching

{{货摊表|牧业}}

A [[装箱的小牛]] lets out a [[小牛|calf]], which grows into a [[牛|cow]] after two game days; a [[装箱的母鸡]] lets out a full-grown [[鸡|chicken]]. For milking, feeding and breeding see [[饲料]], [[一桶牛奶]], [[小牛]] and [[小鸡]].

### Fishing

{{货摊表|渔业}}

A fishing rod can also be made by hand from 2 [[树枝|Sticks]] and 2 [[干草|Cut Grass]]. For fishing see [[钓鱼]] and [[鱼饵]].

### Sell

{{货摊表|出售}}

The stall buys only the things in this table; nothing else can be sold. It takes what you grow (wheat, vegetables, berries, herbs), what you raise (eggs, milk) and what you catch (every kind of fish, and the [[泡烂的靴子]] too), but not the seeds, feed and tools it sells itself.

### Other

- **Putting things on it**: it is a counter. Holding something, press {{键|G}} at it and the thing is put on the counter; {{键|E}} first picks up what is on the counter, and the stall itself cannot be taken.
- **Blocks the way**: the stall is solid.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: this stall on the Held Ground always has {{状态|防火}} and will not burn. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, so fire cannot touch it.
- For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
