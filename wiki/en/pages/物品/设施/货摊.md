The **trading stall** is a stall on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], named “Alberic's stall”. Press {{key|E}} to open the stall panel and buy seeds, fertilizer, a shovel, feed, buckets, crated livestock, bait and a fishing rod with [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]], or sell it what you grow, raise and catch in exchange for Dawnmarks. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, see [[Money]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- There is only this one, on the Held Ground, southeast of the sword planted in the ground, two tiles east of where [[Alberic]] stands by day.
- You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Trading

- **Opening**: walk up to the stall and press {{key|E}} (label “Trade at Alberic's stall”). This opens the stall page of the menu, with four shelves: **Farming**, **Ranching**, **Fishing** and **Sell**, and above them how many Dawnmarks you carry.
- **Buying**: click a thing on one of the first three shelves; its price is taken and what you bought goes straight into your pack (how many you get per purchase is in the tables below). Without enough money you cannot buy.
- **Selling**: click a thing on the “Sell” shelf; one of it is taken from your pack or hands, and the Dawnmarks it fetches go into your pack. One at a time.
- **You must stand beside it**: you can trade only while standing in the ring of 8 tiles around the stall. When you are not beside it, the stall page says “Stand beside the stall to trade.” and the buy and sell buttons are grey; a trade sent while not beside it does not go through, and you are only told “That has to be done at the stall.”
- **No one needs to mind it**: the stall does its own trade; you can buy and sell even when Alberic is not nearby or has gone to bed for the night.

### Farming

{{stall table|farm}}

[[Carrot]], [[Turnip]], [[Tomato]] and [[Pumpkin]] also grow in the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]]; the seeds of the other ten vegetables ([[Parsnip]], [[Potato]], [[Radish]], [[Cauliflower]] and [[Melon]], and [[Corn]], [[Blueberries]], [[Strawberry]], [[Hot Pepper]] and [[Eggplant]], which bear again after a harvest) are sold only here. See [[Crops]].

The [[Shovel|shovel]] too is sold only here: with it you dig crops back up into seeds and grub out tree stumps by the roots (see [[Farming#Shovel|Shovel]] and [[Woodcutting]]).

### Ranching

{{stall table|ranch}}

A [[Crated Calf]] lets out a [[calf|calf]], which grows into a [[cow|cow]] after two game days; a [[Crated Hen]] lets out a full-grown [[chicken|chicken]]. For milking, feeding and breeding see [[Animal Feed]], [[Bucket of Milk]], [[calf]] and [[chick]].

### Fishing

{{stall table|fish}}

A fishing rod can also be made by hand from 2 [[Stick|Sticks]] and 2 [[Cut Grass|Cut Grass]]. For fishing see [[Fishing]] and [[Bait]].

### Sell

{{stall table|sell}}

The stall buys only the things in this table; nothing else can be sold. It takes what you grow (wheat, vegetables, berries, herbs), what you raise (eggs, milk) and what you catch (every kind of fish, and the [[Soggy Boot]] too), but not the seeds, feed and tools it sells itself.

### Other

- **Putting things on it**: it is a counter. Holding something, press {{key|G}} at it and the thing is put on the counter; {{key|E}} first picks up what is on the counter, and the stall itself cannot be taken.
- **Blocks the way**: the stall is solid.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: this stall on the Held Ground always has {{status|fireproof}} and will not burn. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, so fire cannot touch it.
- For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
