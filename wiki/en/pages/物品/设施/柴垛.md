**Firewood** is a stack of split wood, west of the tent in the camp at [[The Ford|the Ford]]. It is just one of the camp's things: it blocks the way, and the wood cannot be taken out. The pile where Gus stacks wood in [[Firstlight Village]] is the [[wood pile|wood pile]], a container whose wood anyone may take.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this stack, in the Ford's camp. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- The wood cannot be taken out: for wood, go and fell trees (see [[Woodcutting]]).
- **Blocks the way**: the firewood stack is solid.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and after burning for about 4.5 seconds becomes one {{item|Ash}}; while burning, every half second it has a 10% chance to spread the fire to something flammable within one tile. The camp's tent and chest are right beside it.
- Hold something and press {{key|G}}, and the thing is put down on its tile.

The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
