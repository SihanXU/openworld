**Wildflowers** are a cluster of small flowers growing on the grass, one of the most common small things on the maps. They are only scenery: they do not block the way and cannot be picked. The flower-covered terrain “flowers” is something else: that is the ground itself.

## Source

{{来源}}

- On the grass by the roadside and across the river at [[渡口|the Ford]], and at [[初原村]], [[草原|the Grassland]], [[水车磨坊]] and [[余烬镇]].
- They cannot be planted, and they do not grow back by themselves.

## Uses

{{用途}}

They are one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with them:

- **Does not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over them.
- **{{键|G}}**: holding something, press {{键|G}} at them and the thing is put on their tile.
- **Hitting**: they have no durability; what happens if you hit them with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Cannot be picked**: the game has no “picking flowers”, and wildflowers are not an item.
- **Fire**: they catch fire. After burning for about 4.5 seconds they become one {{物品|灰烬}}; while burning, every half second they have a 10% chance to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of them.

What happens if you press {{键|E}} with empty hands to handle them is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to them, the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).

## History

- 2026-10-02: The small things on the ground went from scenery in the picture to things in the world: each one is an object, and every input reaches it.
