The **Shrine of the Nails** is the fourth [[wayside shrine|wayside shrine]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is something made of metal.

## Source

{{sources}}

On [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], the fourth. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: press {{key|E}} to read: “THE FOURTH SUFFERING: THE NAILS. Nailed by his hands and feet to the holy wood, he accepted death. Pilgrim, lay down metal, as he laid down his life.”
- **Lighting**: holding something made of metal, press {{key|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a dented helm (iron, on the north side midway along the road).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Nails asks for something made of metal.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[wayside shrine]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Nails” of [[The First Seal]].

## Background

[[The Order's Seal]] is metal, but it is an important item, and this shrine does not take it.

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: The inscription changed to the Church's wording (it used to be “They nailed him to the holy wood so that he would not leave us”); the Order's Seal is no longer taken.
