The **Shrine of the Nails** is the fourth [[路边圣龛|wayside shrine]] on [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is something of iron or metal: the nails.

## Source

{{来源}}

On [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], the fourth. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: press {{键|E}} to read: “THE FOURTH SUFFERING: THE NAILS. Nailed to the holy wood, he accepted death. — Lay down metal, as he laid down his life.”
- **Lighting**: holding something made of metal, press {{键|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a dented helm (iron, on the north side midway along the road).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Nails asks for something of iron or metal: the nails.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[路边圣龛]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Nails” of [[第一印]].

## Background

[[荆棘骑士团印章]] is metal, but it is an important item, and this shrine does not take it.

## History
