**Moss** is a patch of moss growing on the ground, found under the trees of [[森林|the Forest]] and beside the rocks north of [[渡口|the Ford]]. It does not block the way, cannot be taken and does not catch fire.

## Source

{{来源}}

- 7 patches in the Forest, near trees; 1 beside the boulder north of the Ford.
- It cannot be planted, and it does not grow back by itself.

## Uses

{{用途}}

It is one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with it:

- **Does not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over it.
- **{{键|G}}**: holding something, press {{键|G}} at it and the thing is put on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: moss does not catch fire.

What happens if you press {{键|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
