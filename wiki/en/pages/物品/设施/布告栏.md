The **notice board** is a wooden board with notices pinned to it. Like the {{item|sign}}, you read it by pressing {{key|E}} from within 2 tiles. There are three in the game: on the square of [[Firstlight Village]], in the old camp deep in [[The Forest]], and on the square of [[Emberfall]]. To the left of the one in Firstlight Village is the [[commission board|commission board]], listing the village's current commissions.

## Source

{{sources}}

| Where | It says |
|---|---|
| Firstlight Village's square, by the well | “FIRSTLIGHT VILLAGE. East: the Ford. West: Millbrook. North: Dawn Hill. South: the grassland, and the forest beyond.” |
| Deep in the southeast of the Forest, at the camp of the pilgrim before | Named “Proclamation nailed to a tree”: “BY ORDER OF THE HOLY CITY. The Seal-bearers weaken. Let the pilgrimage be called. Brother Cassian of Firstlight is named pilgrim. — In the 380th year of the Five. (The paper is grey with age. The wording is almost the same as the courier's.)” |
| Emberfall's square | “DRAGON SEEN OVER CINDER PEAK. KEEP THE BUCKETS FULL.” |

Cannot be made; the “Signpost” of build mode makes a sign.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: press {{key|E}} from within 2 tiles (“Read notice board”), and the notice's contents are shown.
- **The Proclamation**: the old camp where the Forest's board stands is the starting point of the side quest [[The Pilgrim Before]]: once you walk into the camp, you can get this quest from [[Sister Ilse|Sister Ilse]], and bring her the scallop the pilgrim left behind.
- Press {{key|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- **Breaking**: durability 4, made of wood. Broken, it leaves 1 stick and 1 plank, each with a half chance (see [[Durability and Breaking]]).
- **Fire**: it can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{item|Ash}}.
