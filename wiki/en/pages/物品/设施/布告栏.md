{{信息框}}
The **notice board** is a wooden board with notices pinned to it. Like the {{物品|告示牌}}, you read it by pressing {{键|E}} from within 2 tiles. There are three in the game: on the square of [[初原村]], in the old camp deep in [[森林]], and on the square of [[余烬镇]]. To the left of the one in Firstlight Village is the [[委托板|commission board]], listing the village's current commissions.

## Source

{{来源}}

| Where | It says |
|---|---|
| Firstlight Village's square, by the well | “FIRSTLIGHT VILLAGE — Every journey begins with a first step. East: The Ford. West: Millbrook. North: Dawn Hill. South: the grassland, and the forest beyond.” |
| Deep in the southeast of the Forest, at the camp of the pilgrim before | Named “Proclamation nailed to a tree”: “By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers fail, and a Proclamation of the Pilgrimage shall go forth. Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village is named the Pilgrim. — Year 380 of the Era of the Five Sufferings. (The paper is so old it has gone grey. The words on it are exactly the ones the courier read to you.)” |
| Emberfall's square | “DRAGON SEEN OVER CINDER PEAK. KEEP THE BUCKETS FULL.” |

Cannot be made; the “Signpost” of build mode makes a sign.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: press {{键|E}} from within 2 tiles (“Read notice board”), and the notice's contents are shown.
- **The Proclamation**: the old camp where the Forest's board stands is the starting point of the side quest [[上一位朝圣者]]: once you walk into the camp, you can get this quest from [[伊尔丝修女|Sister Ilse]], and bring her the scallop the pilgrim left behind.
- Press {{键|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- **Breaking**: durability 4, made of wood. Broken, it leaves 1 stick and 1 plank, each with a half chance (see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- **Fire**: it can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}.

## History
