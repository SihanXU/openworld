{{信息框}}
A **cauliflower** (crop) is a cauliflower planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{物品|花椰菜种子}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{物品|花椰菜}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{物品|花椰菜}}: eaten raw it restores 5 [[饱食度|satiety]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{物品|花椰菜种子}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Cauliflower Seeds.” The seeds become a “cauliflower sprout”. “Sow Cauliflower” on the “Planting” page of [[建造]] mode works too.
- The seeds are sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] (“Farming” shelf, 3 for 20 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]). No map has cauliflowers already planted, and the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] has none either.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | cauliflower sprout | Water once; 4 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young cauliflower | Water again; 4 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe cauliflower | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{状态|浇过水}} and grows one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{物品|肥料}} or {{物品|灰烬}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (With fertilizer it is “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- All crops grow equally fast: at least 4 game hours and two waterings from sowing to ripeness. See [[农耕]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{物品|镰刀}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 cauliflowers (3 if fertilized) and 2 Cauliflower Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{键|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 cauliflower straight into your hands, 1 Cauliflower Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

Harvested cauliflowers can be sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] for 18 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- In the rules, digging it up with a [[铲子|shovel]] turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.
