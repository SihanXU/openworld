A **well** is a stone well built into the ground. There is one each on the squares of [[Firstlight Village]] and [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]. Left-click it holding an empty container and it fills with water; the water you draw can water the ground, put out fires and be drunk.

## Source

{{sources}}

- One on the north side of Firstlight Village's square. The peddler {{person|Tobin}} goes to the well to draw water every day from 9:00 to 11:00.
- One on Emberfall's square. Once the dragon is slain, Emberfall's elder {{person|Mara}} says your name will be carved on the well.
- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Drawing water

- Holding an empty container, left-click the well: “Filled with water.” (label “Fill X”)
- Containers that can be filled: the {{item|item/vessel/bucket/empty}} (becomes a {{item|Water Bucket}}), the {{item|Empty Flask}} (becomes a {{item|Flask of Water}}) and the {{item|Empty Waterskin}} (becomes a {{item|Waterskin}}).
- “Refill Water” on the “Farming” page of [[Building|build]] mode can use a well too: choose “Refill Water” and left-click the well, as long as the empty container is in your bag.
- A well's water never runs out.

What the water can do: water crops (see [[Farming#Where water comes from|where water comes from]]), be thrown to put out a fire, or, held in your hand and left-clicked, be drunk to restore 2 health (see [[Health]]).

### Other

- The well is solid and blocks the way.
- The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”).
- The well is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
