{{信息框}}
A **well** is a stone well built into the ground. There is one each on the squares of [[初原村]] and [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]. Left-click it holding an empty container and it fills with water; the water you draw can water the ground, put out fires and be drunk.

## Source

{{来源}}

- One on the north side of Firstlight Village's square. The peddler {{人物|托宾}} goes to the well to draw water every day from 9:00 to 11:00.
- One on Emberfall's square. Once the dragon is slain, Emberfall's elder {{人物|玛拉}} says your name will be carved on the well.
- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Drawing water

- Holding an empty container, left-click the well: “Filled with water.” (label “Fill X”)
- Containers that can be filled: the {{物品|item/vessel/bucket/empty}} (becomes a {{物品|一桶水}}), the {{物品|空瓶}} (becomes a {{物品|一瓶水}}) and the {{物品|空水袋}} (becomes a {{物品|水袋}}).
- “Refill Water” on the “Farming” page of [[建造|build]] mode can use a well too: choose “Refill Water” and left-click the well, as long as the empty container is in your bag.
- A well's water never runs out.

What the water can do: water crops (see [[农耕#水从哪来|where water comes from]]), be thrown to put out a fire, or, held in your hand and left-clicked, be drunk to restore 2 health (see [[生命值]]).

### Other

- The well is solid and blocks the way.
- The rules do not say what happens when you press {{键|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”).
- The well is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
