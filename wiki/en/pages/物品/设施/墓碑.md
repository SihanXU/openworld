A **gravestone** is a stone carved with words; walk up to it and press {{key|E}} to read it (“Read X”). Every gravestone in the game has a name of its own: the kneeling stones on [[The East Road]], the boundary stones of [[The Held Ground]], the carved standing stones of [[Thornwood Hollow]], the Order's tomb niches in [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]], and the cairn deep in [[The Forest]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Reading

- **Kneeling stone**: “A stone carved as a kneeling pilgrim. No name. The pilgrim's scallop badge is worn almost smooth.” A pilgrim's earring lies beside one of them.
- **Carved standing stone**: “Worn carvings: a knight crowned with thorns, a row of small figures kneeling before him. Beneath: THE ORDER OF THE THORN KEEPS WATCH HERE.”
- **A cairn of stones**: “Stones heaped over something long. A pilgrim's staff leans against it, its tip worn flat by many miles. No name.” Beside it are a rotted [[tent|tent]] and a Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree (see [[The Pilgrim Before]]).
- **Boundary stone**: “A boundary stone, its carving worn: a ring of thorns, and no kneeling figure inside the ring.” The boundary stones lie beyond the third [[bank of mist|bank of mist]], and can only be reached once [[Expanding the Held Ground|Expanding the Held Ground]] is done and the mist has cleared.

{{spoiler|The Order's tomb niches}}
The four tomb niches in the Ossuary of the Thorn:
- “BROTHER ODO OF THE THORN. He held the gate at Varn three days. He did not kneel.”
- “SISTER MAREN OF THE THORN, who carried water to both armies.”
- “BROTHER TOMAS OF THE THORN, the commander's brother. Warden of this house until the night comes back.”
- “Carved, and left empty: FENNICK, SQUIRE TO THE COMMANDER. The stone is clean. Someone keeps it swept.”
{{/剧透}}

### Otherwise

- Press {{key|G}} on a gravestone with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- A gravestone is stone and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
