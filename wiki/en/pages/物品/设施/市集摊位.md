The **market stall** is a wooden stall with a cloth awning, and things can be laid on its counter. An ordinary market stall cannot buy or sell; the one that can is a kind beneath it, Alberic's [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground]] (see [[Money]]). For now no map has an ordinary market stall, and you cannot make one yourself.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: the stall is a counter. Press {{key|G}} on it with something in hand to lay the thing on the counter.
- **{{key|E}}**: first picks up what is on the counter; the stall itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: the stall has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: made of wood and cloth, it can catch fire, and burns down to a portion of {{item|Ash}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The market stall has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-08: A kind beneath it was added: the [[trading stall|trading stall]], which buys and sells.
