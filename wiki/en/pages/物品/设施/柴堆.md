The **wood pile** is a pile of stacked wood on the west side of [[Firstlight Village]], and also a container that holds 12 things. Every afternoon the woodcutter {{person|Gus}} stacks the {{item|Log}} he has cut on it, for anyone who needs it. It starts out open.

The split wood beside the tent at [[The Ford|the Ford]] is the [[firewood|firewood]] stack, a different thing: it is not a container, and its wood cannot be taken out.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this one, in Firstlight Village, on the west side of the village near Gus's house, beside the woodworking bench.
- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Gus's wood

- In the morning Gus goes to [[The Forest|the Forest]] to fell trees and gather wood, carrying up to 6 logs at a time; between 12:00 and 21:00 he comes back to the village and puts the wood in the wood pile.
- Gus only puts wood into the pile while it is **open**. Press {{key|E}} to close the pile, and he cannot put any in.
- The wood in the pile belongs to no one; take it and nobody says a word. You can take it to hand in for [[Wood for the Fire]], or split it into planks.

### Storing and taking

- **{{key|E}}**: open or close it; when open, the chest panel appears (see [[Chests and Containers]]).
- **{{key|G}}**: while it is open, put the thing in your hand into it, up to 12 things.

### Breaking and fire

- Durability 6, made of wood. Broken, it falls apart into 1 log (half of its 2), and everything inside drops out.
- It catches fire and burns down to {{item|Ash}}; the wood inside drops where it stood (and the dropped wood may go on burning).
