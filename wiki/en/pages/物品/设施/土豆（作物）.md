The **potato** (crop) is a potato planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Potato Seeds}}, water twice, and once it is ripe harvest {{item|Potato}} and seeds. For the vegetable once harvested see {{item|Potato}}: eaten raw it restores 4 [[Satiety|Satiety]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: hold {{item|Potato Seeds}} and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the potato seeds.” The seeds become a “potato sprout”. “Sow Potato” on the “Farming” page of [[Building|build]] mode works too.
- The seeds are bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground]] (the “Farming” column, 5 for 10 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]). No potatoes grow on any map, nor in the vegetable garden of [[Firstlight Village]].

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growing

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | potato sprout | Water once; it grows to the next stage 2 game hours later |
| 2 | young potato | Water once more; it ripens 2 game hours later |
| 3 | ripe potato | Harvest |

- **Watering**: hold a container full of water and left-click it: “You water the something.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 2 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs the open sky**: while something covers it overhead it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once it is in the open.
- **No water, no growth**, and it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: hold {{item|Fertilizer}} or {{item|Ash}} and right-click it while it is not yet ripe: “You work the ash into the soil.” (with fertilizer: “You work the fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest gives 1 more portion. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- Every crop grows equally fast: at least 4 game hours and two waterings from sowing to ripe. For the detailed rules see [[Farming]].

### Harvest

| How | You get |
|---|---|
| Hold a {{item|Sickle}} and left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 potatoes (3 if fertilized) and 2 Potato Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 potato straight into your hand, and 1 Potato Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

Harvested potatoes can be sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] for 6 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

### Otherwise

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or a sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- In the rules, digging it up with a [[Shovel|Shovel]] turns it back into one portion of seeds (“You dig up the something.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.

## History

- 2026-10-08: Ten crops were added, each with three stages: parsnip, potato, radish, cauliflower and melon, and corn, blueberries, strawberry, hot pepper and eggplant, which fruit again after harvest; their seeds are bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] in [[The Held Ground]].
- 2026-10-08: Fertilizing with fertilizer says “You work the fertilizer into the soil.”
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
