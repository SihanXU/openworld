{{信息框}}
A **coal seam** is a band of coal in the rock. Mine it with a {{物品|镐}} and it drops 2 {{物品|煤炭}}. [[晨光丘矿洞]], open from Chapter 1, has 4; the galleries on both sides of [[旧矿坑]] have 4; [[采石场]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] has 3. Once mined, it leaves a [[挖空的煤层|spent coal seam]] in place, which grows back after one game day.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Old Mine**: 4, in the galleries on both sides.
- **Dawn Hill Mine**: 4: 1 in the mine hall, 1 in the north chamber, 2 in the south chamber.
- **The Quarry**: [[采石场]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] has 3, reachable once the Quarry is opened through [[领地扩建]].
- **Grows back**: wherever the coal seam is, once mined it leaves a [[挖空的煤层|spent coal seam]] in place (bedrock, unbreakable), which turns back into a coal seam after one game day.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Mining it

- Durability 12; its material is ore (hardness 2).
- Hold a pickaxe and left-click it (“Mine the coal seam”): a stone pickaxe deals 6 points a blow, so two blows break it open. Punching it: “Too hard.”
- Mined, it drops 2 coal (half of its 4). See [[采矿]], [[耐久与破坏]].

### What coal is for

The smith {{人物|加里克}} of [[余烬镇|Emberfall]] needs 3 coal (plus 5 iron ore) to forge [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]]. Smelting in batches at a [[熔炉|forge]] needs coal too: 2 ore and 1 coal make 2 ingots (see [[锻造]]). Coal catches fire, and can be thrown into a fire to burn to ash.

### Other

A coal seam is solid and blocks the way; the seam itself does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.
