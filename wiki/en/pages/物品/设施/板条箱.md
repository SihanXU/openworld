A **crate** is a small wooden box that holds 6 things. Press {{键|E}} to open it and store or take things. It is the cheapest container, made from just 2 {{物品|木板}} and a hammer; but its durability is only 1, and one blow breaks it. The “Garden Supplies” by the vegetable garden in [[初原村]] is a crate too, only it holds more and is sturdier.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Crate”: 2 planks, with a hammer on you (the {{物品|锻锤}}, not used up).
- **By hand**: put one plank on another plank on the ground to make a [[木框架|wooden frame]], then knock it once with a hammer: “You nail together a crate.”
- **The Garden Supplies**: by the vegetable garden in Firstlight Village; it holds 24 things and has durability 12. Inside are 2 each of carrot, turnip, tomato and pumpkin seeds, a hoe, a sickle, a bucket of water and a smith hammer, plus 4 planks, 4 sticks and 4 stones (see [[农耕#初原村的菜圃|Firstlight Village's vegetable garden]]).
- **The Mill Cellar**: two by the north wall of [[磨坊地窖|the Mill Cellar]], empty.
- **Emberfall**: one in front of the cottage of the herbalist {{人物|雷恩}}.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Storing and taking

- **{{键|E}}**: open or close it (“Open the crate”). After building one in build mode, leave build mode before pressing {{键|E}}.
- **{{键|G}}**: while it is open, put the thing in your hand into it; when it is full, “It is full.”
- A [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).

### Breaks at one blow

- **Axe, pickaxe, hammer**: left-click an unlocked crate and what is inside spills out first, then the whole crate comes loose and lies where it stood; walk close and it is drawn into your bag by itself, or press {{键|E}} to pick it up. Holding it, right-click open ground to build it again.
- **Hitting it with anything else** (a weapon, a fist): durability 1, made of wood. One blow breaks it into 1 plank, and what is inside drops out. The Garden Supplies is the exception: its durability is 12, and it takes several blows to break.
- See [[耐久与破坏]].

### Fire

Crates catch fire. The first time a burning crate loses durability, it breaks into planks; if it burns out before that, it burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}, and what is inside drops where it stood.
