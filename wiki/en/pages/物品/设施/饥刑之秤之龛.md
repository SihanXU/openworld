The **Shrine of the Scales** is the third [[路边圣龛|wayside shrine]] on [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], midway along the road beside the path north to the abbey. What it asks for is something to eat: a meal fasted.

## Source

{{来源}}

On [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], the third, midway along the road beside the path north to the abbey. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: press {{键|E}} to read: “THE THIRD SUFFERING: HUNGER. Upon the scales his flesh grew lighter than sin. — Lay down a meal you will not eat.”
- **Lighting**: holding something edible, press {{键|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a pilgrim's loaf (on the abandoned cart on the south side of the road).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Scales asks for something to eat: a meal fasted.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[路边圣龛]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Scales” of [[第一印]].

## Background

When all five are lit, this one's light points up the path north to the [[荆棘修道院]].

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
