{{信息框}}
The **pumpkin** (crop) is a pumpkin planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{物品|南瓜种子}}, water twice, and once it is ripe harvest {{物品|南瓜}} and seeds. For the vegetable once harvested see {{物品|南瓜}}: eaten raw it restores 6 [[饱食度|Satiety]], the most among vegetables, tied with the melon.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Sowing**: hold {{物品|南瓜种子}} and right-click an empty tile of farmland: “You sow the pumpkin seeds.” The seeds become a “pumpkin sprout”. “Sow Pumpkin” on the “Farming” page of [[建造|build]] mode works too.
- **The vegetable garden in Firstlight Village**: the garden has a row of pumpkins, one each of sprout, young plant and ripe from top to bottom, and the ripe one can be harvested at once. The Garden Supplies beside it hold 2 Pumpkin Seeds (see [[农耕#初原村的菜圃|the vegetable garden in Firstlight Village]]).

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Growing

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | pumpkin sprout | Water once; it grows to the next stage 4 game hours later |
| 2 | young pumpkin | Water once more; it ripens 4 game hours later |
| 3 | ripe pumpkin | Harvest |

- **Watering**: hold a container full of water and left-click it: “You water the something.” It gets {{状态|浇过水}} and grows one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs the open sky**: while something covers it overhead it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once it is in the open.
- **No water, no growth**, and it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: hold {{物品|灰烬}} and right-click it while it is not yet ripe: “You work the ash into the soil.” The harvest gives 1 more portion. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- For the detailed rules see [[农耕]].

### Harvest

| How | You get |
|---|---|
| Hold a {{物品|镰刀}} and left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 pumpkins (3 if fertilized) and 2 Pumpkin Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{键|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 pumpkin straight into your hand, and 1 Pumpkin Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

### Otherwise

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or a sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- Digging it up with a {{物品|铲子}} turns it back into one portion of seeds (“You dig up the something.”). Shovels are bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] in [[誓守之地]].
- Crops do not catch fire.

## History

- 2026-10-03: Four vegetables were added, carrot, turnip, tomato and pumpkin, each with three stages; the vegetable garden in Firstlight Village was planted with them.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
