**Stakes** are something half set up from sticks, not yet finished. Two sticks put together stand up as stakes; one knock of a hammer nails them into a length of [[fence|fence]]. Building a fence in [[Building|build]] mode goes through these two steps.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Setting them up**: put a {{item|Stick}} on the ground, hold another stick and press {{key|G}} on it (“Set stakes”): “Two sticks make stakes.”
- No map has ready-made stakes.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Nailing them into a fence**: hold a hammer (the {{item|Smith Hammer}}, not used up) and left-click them (“Build a fence”): “You nail up a fence.”
- **Taking them apart**: hold an axe and left-click them (“Take the stakes apart”): both sticks come back.
- Stakes are solid and block the way; they cannot be taken.
- They have no durability. Apart from taking them apart with an axe and nailing them with a hammer, the rules do not say what happens when you hit them.
- They are wooden and catch fire, burning down to one {{item|Ash}}.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version of building already had stakes: two sticks make stakes, and a hammer nails them into a fence.
