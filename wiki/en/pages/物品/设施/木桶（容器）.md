{{信息框}}
A **barrel** is a wooden barrel that holds 4 things. It is a container: press {{键|E}} to open it and store or take things. It is fragile, with a durability of only 1, and breaks at a single blow. The {{物品|item/vessel/bucket/empty}} for carrying water is a different thing that you hold in your hand (in Chinese the two share a name); the barrel on this page stands on the ground and stays put.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Mill Cellar**: there is one in each of the four corners of [[磨坊地窖|the Mill Cellar]], empty.
- **Emberfall**: there is one in front of the smithy in [[余烬镇|Emberfall]].
- Barrels cannot be made. The “Bucket” in the crafting panel ({{键|C}}) is the bucket for carrying water.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Storing and taking

The same as a [[箱子|chest]] (see [[箱子与容器]]):

- **{{键|E}}**: open or close it.
- **{{键|G}}**: while it is open, put the thing in your hand into it, up to 4 things; while it is closed, “It is closed.”
- A [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).

### Breaks at one blow

A barrel has durability 1 and is made of wood. A fist, a weapon or a tool — anything no softer than wood — breaks it at one blow: it falls apart into 1 {{物品|木板}} (half of its 2), and what is inside drops out. See [[耐久与破坏]].

### Fire

Barrels catch fire. The first time a burning barrel loses durability, it breaks into planks; if it burns out before that, it burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}, and what is inside drops where it stood.
