The **cooking rack** is a wooden rack beside the campfire in the camp at [[The Ford|the Ford]]. Its name says “cooking”, but in the rules it is only a surface: things can be put on it, but nothing can be cooked on it, and it does not make a room a kitchen. To cook, use a lit {{item|campfire}} (see [[Cooking]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this one, in the Ford's camp, east of the campfire. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the rack (“You set X on the cooking rack.”).
- **{{key|E}}**: picks up what is on the rack first; the rack itself cannot be taken.
- **Not a cooking fixture**: the rules do not say what happens when you right-click it holding raw food; it does not count as a cooking fixture, and cannot make a [[Rooms|room]] a kitchen.
- **Hitting**: the cooking rack has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-10-02: Added the cooking rack: everything in the Ford's camp that looks like a thing is a thing, and every input reaches it.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
