A **table** is a wooden table; hold something and press {{键|G}} to put it on the tabletop. There is only one in the game: the one in the ramshackle hut on [[云顶台地|Cloudtop]], with “the watchman's log” lying open on it, which you can read by pressing {{键|E}}.

## Source

{{来源}}

- Only this one, on Cloudtop. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### The watchman's log

The table on Cloudtop is named “the watchman's log”, and it can be read:

- Come within one tile of the table and a scene plays: the hero leafs through the log, and {{人物|露米}} speaks of the light in the snow. It plays only once.
- After that, press {{键|E}} (“Read the watchman's log”) to read the full text again.
- It is what [[奈莎·奎尔|Nessa Quill]]'s side quest [[守夜人的日志]] asks you to read.

The log begins like this: “The Cloudtop Sunwatch Post: sunset record. Year 401: midsummer, 19:42. Year 405: 19:31. Year 409: 19:12 — I checked the hourglass twice. Year 411: report sent down the mountain; no answer ever came.”

{{剧透|The last page of the log}}
“The last page: found a small light asleep in the snow by the cairn, warm as a coal. It woke and asked me how old it was. I did not know how to answer.”
{{/剧透}}

### Putting things on it

The table is a surface: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the table (“You set X on the table.”). Pressing {{键|E}} picks up what is on the table first; the table itself cannot be taken (“That stays where it is.”).

### Unbreakable, but it burns

The table has no durability: the rules do not say what happens when you hit it with a weapon, a tool or your fist — it is [[世界会学|something the world has not seen]]. It is wooden and catches fire, burning down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
