{{信息框}}
A **bed** is for sleeping. Stand on a bed and press {{键|E}} to lie down and sleep until 6 in the morning, and the bed you slept in becomes your resting place (see [[睡觉]]). No map in the game has a ready-made bed; you have to make one: 2 {{物品|木板}} and 2 {{物品|干草}}, no tool and no workbench, and it comes out named “Wooden Bed”. A bed can be carried: strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole bed comes loose and lies on the spot, then press {{键|E}} to pick it up; holding it, right-click a tile to set it up, and wherever you set it you can sleep.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Crafting**: press {{键|C}} and choose “Wooden Bed”: 2 planks and 2 cut grass, made on the spot.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Wooden Bed”. If you carry a bed it is put down directly; if not, one is first made on the spot from the materials on you and then put down (see [[建造#放下的东西|things put down]]).
- **Setting it up**: holding a bed, right-click an empty tile. Pressing {{键|G}} only puts the bed on the ground loose, like an item, and you cannot sleep in it: pressing {{键|E}} on it picks it up.
- Lesson six of the [[新手教程|tutorial]] asks you to put a bed in the house.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Sleeping

- A bed is only within reach when you **stand on it**: walk onto the bed and press {{键|E}}; the prompt is “Sleep”.
- “You lie down.” Time skips ahead to 6:00 in the morning, and you wake with some health restored: fully if the bed is in a [[房间|bedroom]], half elsewhere.
- While asleep your Satiety drops more slowly; being woken by hunger or by a blow wakes you at once.
- For the detailed rules see [[睡觉]].

### Resting place

The bed you slept in becomes your resting place. On [[难度|Normal difficulty]], when you fall, you get up again at the bed you last slept in or the [[传送雕像|waypoint statue]] you last woke.

### Bedroom

A [[房间|room]] closed all round and covered overhead with a bed in it is a bedroom. The bedroom is for now the only room use with a real effect: sleep a night in it and your health is fully restored.

### Taking up and putting down

- **Taking up**: strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole bed comes loose and lies on the spot; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself), with no loss of material.
- A bed does not block the way, and people can stand on it; nor does it take up a tile's space slot, so it can share a tile with a floor: putting the bed down first and laying the floor afterwards works too, the floor being laid under the bed (see [[楼层]]).
- Press {{键|G}} on a bed with something in hand to put the thing on the bed's tile.
- [[居民|Residents]] do not use beds to sleep; when the time comes they go back to where they sleep.

### Cannot be broken, but burns

A bed has no durability. Axes, pickaxes and hammers only knock the whole bed loose; what happens if you hit it with a weapon is not written in the rules (see [[世界会学]]). It is made of wood and cloth, can catch fire, and burns down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}. Do not put your bed next to a campfire.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The bed has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-04: Beds can be slept in: you sleep until morning, health is restored by the room, fully in a bedroom; the bed you slept in becomes your resting place (W5). Beds can be made on the spot from planks and cut grass, and carried.
- 2026-10-05: Build mode can place beds directly; sleeping gives the status “asleep”, with hunger 4 times slower.
- 2026-10-07: A bed put down is drawn as the whole bed.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
