The **plot for a third residents' tent** is a staked-out patch of empty ground northwest of the sword on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], the place where the “Third Residents' Tent” of [[领地扩建]] is to be built. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, after a while it becomes a [[帐篷|tent]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- On the Held Ground from the start, northwest of the sword. You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find “Third Residents' Tent” on the “Structures” page. It requires land tier II (the Meadow); materials: 10 {{物品|布匹}}, 18 {{物品|木板}} and 2 {{物品|铁锭}}, handed to him for the Held Ground's store. Once the store has enough, press “Build!”. When it is finished, Alberic also gives you 80 {{物品|晨辉币}}.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “Two more beds: seven in all”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “A village, nearly. Nobody kneels in it.” Every so often the plot checks whether the building is done; when it is, it turns into a [[帐篷|tent]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: one bed on each side in front of the tent, for the people you invite to live there (see [[领地居民]]).
- The plot does not block the way, and you can walk over it; hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- The stakes catch fire. A plot burned away is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[地基]].
