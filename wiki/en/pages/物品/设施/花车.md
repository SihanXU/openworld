A **flower cart** is a handcart loaded with fresh flowers. It is a kind of [[handcart|handcart]]: things can be put on the cart. At present no map in the game has a flower cart, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: holding something, press {{key|G}} at it and the thing is put on the cart.
- **{{key|E}}**: first picks up what is on the cart; the cart itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: the cart is wooden, catches fire, and when burnt out becomes one {{item|Ash}}.

For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
