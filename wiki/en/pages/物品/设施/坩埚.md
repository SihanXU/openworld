The **cauldron** is a metal pot hung over a fire, in front of the cottage of the herbalist {{person|Wren}} on the west side of [[Emberfall]], burning from the start. It is a cooking fixture: while it burns, right-click it holding raw food, or press {{key|G}} facing it, to cook the food (see [[Cooking]]). Wren tends the cauldron every day from 6:00 to 22:00, and wants it burning.

## Source

{{sources}}

- There is only this one, in Emberfall. Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Cooking

- Right-click the burning cauldron holding raw food and the food is cooked (“Cook X on the cauldron”).
- Pressing {{key|G}} at the burning cauldron holding raw food does the same: the food is cooked and stays on you.
- Pressing {{key|G}} at the burning cauldron holding something burnable (a log, cut grass…): “The something is thrown into the fire.” The thing lands on the cauldron's tile and burns to {{item|Ash}}.

### Lighting and putting out

- **Putting out**: left-click the cauldron holding a container full of water, and the fire goes out. Rain does not put it out.
- **Lighting**: a cold cauldron can be lit again: left-click it holding a {{item|Torch}}, or strike it with the fire element. Once you have learned {{skill|Firemaker}}, an ordinary {{item|Stick}} lights it too.
- **Wren lights it herself**: if she finds the fire out while tending the cauldron (6:00–22:00), she lights it herself: “Out again. Pass me the flint.”
- A cauldron already burning does not change when fire touches it again.

### Warming yourself

Press {{key|E}} bare-handed at the lit cauldron (“Warm yourself by the cauldron”): if you are {{status|wet}}, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself by the cauldron; the damp steams away.”); if not, only “The cauldron's warmth seeps into your palms.” It restores no health. What happens when you press {{key|E}} bare-handed at a cold cauldron is not written in the rules. See [[campfire#Warming up|warming yourself]].

### Otherwise

- **Fireproof Draught**: give Fireweed and Frostcap to Wren and she brews a [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draught]]. This is something you hand to her; in the rules it does not use the cauldron.
- **No smelting**: what happens when you right-click the cauldron with Iron Ore or Gold Ore is not written in the rules (the prompt carries a “?”); smelting needs a [[forge|forge]].
- **Kitchen and workshop**: the cauldron is both a cooking fixture and a workstation, so a [[Rooms|room]] with a cauldron counts as both a kitchen and a workshop (no effect for now).
- **Monsters keep away**: apart from fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 tiles of something lit.
- The cauldron is metal and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire. Struck by the lightning element, it becomes charged ({{status|electrified}}).

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The cauldron has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-03: A burning cauldron was placed in front of the cottage of Emberfall's herbalist.
- 2026-10-08: A cauldron that goes out can be lit again; pressing G at a burning cauldron also cooks raw food.
- 2026-10-09: Pressing {{key|E}} bare-handed warms you by a burning cauldron.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
