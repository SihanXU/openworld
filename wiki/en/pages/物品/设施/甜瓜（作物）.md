{{信息框}}
A **melon** (crop) is a melon planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{物品|甜瓜种子}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{物品|甜瓜}} and seeds. For the harvested fruit see {{物品|甜瓜}}: eaten raw it restores 6 [[饱食度|satiety]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{物品|甜瓜种子}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Melon Seeds.” The seeds become a “melon sprout”. “Sow Melon” on the “Farming” page of [[建造|build]] mode works too.
- The seeds are bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] (the “Farming” shelf, 3 for 24 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]]). No map has melons already planted, and neither does the vegetable garden in [[初原村]].

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | melon sprout | Water once; 4 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young melon | Water again; 4 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe melon | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{状态|浇过水}} and grows one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{物品|肥料}} or {{物品|灰烬}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (with fertilizer, “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- All crops grow equally fast: from sowing to ripe takes at least 4 game hours and two waterings. See [[农耕]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{物品|镰刀}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 melons (3 if fertilized) and 2 Melon Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{键|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 melon straight into your hands, 1 Melon Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

Harvested melons can be sold at the [[货摊|trading stall]] for 22 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]] each.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- The rules do not say what happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe.
- In the rules, digging it up with a [[铲子|shovel]] turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.
