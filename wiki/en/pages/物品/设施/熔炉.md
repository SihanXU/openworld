A **forge** is a stone-built furnace, where the first step of [[Smithing|smithing]] is done: press {{key|E}} on it to open the forge's own panel, and smelt ore and coal into ingots there. There are three in the game, all lit from the start: Haldor's forge in the smithy of [[Firstlight Village]], Alberic's forge east of the sword on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], and the forge in front of the smithy in [[Emberfall|Emberfall]]. Holding {{item|Iron Ore}} or {{item|Gold Ore}}, right-click it, and one ore melts straight into one {{item|Iron Ingot}} or {{item|Gold Ingot}}; it is also a cooking fixture: right-click it holding raw food to cook it (see [[Cooking]]). The three smiths ({{person|Haldor}}, {{person|Alberic}} and {{person|Garrick}}) work at the [[anvil|anvil]] beside it, and can only keep working while the forge is lit.

## Source

{{sources}}

- One each: in the smithy by the East Road in Firstlight Village (named “Haldor's forge”), east of the sword on the Held Ground (“Alberic's forge”), and in front of the smithy in Emberfall.
- You cannot make this kind of forge; once you learn {{skill|Forge Builder}}, you can make a [[smith's forge|smith's forge]] that can be moved, from stone and coal.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Smelting

- Holding {{item|Iron Ore}} or {{item|Gold Ore}}, right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Iron Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The one ore in your hand becomes one {{item|Iron Ingot}} or {{item|Gold Ingot}}, and the forge puffs out smoke.
- One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting.
- **Smelting in batches**: press {{key|E}} on the forge (“Smelt at the forge”) to open the forge panel: 2 iron ore and 1 coal make 2 iron ingots; with {{skill|Forge Builder}} a [[smith's anvil|smith's anvil]] is made here from 3 iron ingots; learn {{skill|Goldsmith}} to smelt gold ingots, and {{skill|Steelworking}} to smelt {{item|Steel Bar}}. For details see [[Smithing#Smelting (forge)|Smelting]].
- When the forge has been put out, clicking it with ore only gives the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”, and the ore stays as it is. Light it first (see [[#Lighting and putting out|Lighting and putting out]] below).
- Only the right click smelts: holding ore and pressing {{key|G}} on the forge only puts the ore down on the forge's tile (“Set Iron Ore down by the forge”).
- Other fires cannot smelt ore: the rules do not say what happens when you click a [[campfire|campfire]], a [[brazier|brazier]] or a [[cauldron|cauldron]] with ore (the prompt shows “?”).
- The ingots you smelt are made into gear at an [[anvil|anvil]] (see [[Smithing]]); but Garrick's Dragonsbane and [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] want the ore itself, so keep enough back before smelting.

### The panel

- Press {{key|E}} on the forge, lit or cold, and the forge panel opens: what is made in a forge (the smelting recipes, the smith's anvil), from what you carry; you need not stand beside it while you make. Closing the panel and walking off is fine.
- To warm yourself, go to a [[campfire|campfire]] or [[brazier|brazier]]: {{key|E}} on the forge opens its panel.

### Cooking and burning things

- Holding raw food, right-click a lit forge and the food is cooked (“Cook X over the forge”); pressing {{key|G}} on it does the same.
- Hold something that burns and press {{key|G}}, and the thing goes into the fire and burns to {{item|Ash}}.

### The smiths' fire

- All three smiths need the forge lit while they work at the anvil: Haldor every day 6:00–21:00, Alberic 6:00–14:00, Garrick 6:00–21:00.
- After the forge has been put out, Garrick lights it again himself after a while (“Out again. Pass me the flint.”); he does not light it while it is wet.
- [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]] is obtained by handing Garrick 5 iron ore and 3 coal and then talking to him; in the rules, forging the sword does not use the forge.

### Lighting and putting out

- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water, left-click it. Rain does not put out a lit forge.
- **Lighting**: holding a {{item|Torch}}, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn {{skill|Firemaker}}, an ordinary stick works too.

### Other

- **Kitchen and workshop**: the forge is both a cooking fixture and a workstation, so a [[Rooms|room]] with a forge in it counts as both a kitchen and a workshop (no effect at present).
- **Monsters keep away**: except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit.
- The forge is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History
