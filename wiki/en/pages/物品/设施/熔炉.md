{{信息框}}
A **forge** is a stone-built furnace, where the first step of [[锻造|smithing]] is done: stand beside it, and in the [[制作|crafting]] panel you can smelt ore and coal into ingots. There are three in the game, all lit from the start: Haldor's forge in the smithy of [[初原村]], Alberic's forge east of the sword on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], and the forge in front of the smithy in [[余烬镇|Emberfall]]. Holding {{物品|铁矿石}} or {{物品|金矿石}}, right-click it, and one ore melts straight into one {{物品|铁锭}} or {{物品|金锭}}; it is also a cooking fixture: right-click it holding raw food to cook it (see [[烹饪]]). The three smiths ({{人物|哈尔多}}, {{人物|阿尔贝里克}} and {{人物|加里克}}) work at the [[铁砧|anvil]] beside it, and can only keep working while the forge is lit.

## Source

{{来源}}

- One each: in the smithy by the East Road in Firstlight Village (named “Haldor's forge”), east of the sword on the Held Ground (“Alberic's forge”), and in front of the smithy in Emberfall.
- You cannot make this kind of forge; once you learn {{技能|筑炉}}, you can make a [[铁匠炉|smith's forge]] that can be moved, from stone and coal.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Smelting

- Holding {{物品|铁矿石}} or {{物品|金矿石}}, right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Iron Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The one ore in your hand becomes one {{物品|铁锭}} or {{物品|金锭}}, and the forge puffs out smoke.
- One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting.
- **Smelting in batches**: stand beside the forge and press {{键|C}} to open the crafting panel: 2 iron ore and 1 coal make 2 iron ingots; learn {{技能|金匠}} to smelt gold ingots, and {{技能|炼钢术}} to smelt {{物品|钢条}}. For details see [[锻造#冶炼（熔炉）|Smelting]].
- When the forge has been put out, clicking it with ore only gives the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”, and the ore stays as it is. Light it first (see [[#点着和熄灭|Lighting and putting out]] below).
- Only the right click smelts: holding ore and pressing {{键|G}} on the forge only puts the ore down on the forge's tile (“Set Iron Ore down by the forge”).
- Other fires cannot smelt ore: the rules do not say what happens when you click a [[营火|campfire]], a [[火盆|brazier]] or a [[坩埚|cauldron]] with ore (the prompt shows “?”).
- The ingots you smelt are made into gear at an [[铁砧|anvil]] (see [[锻造]]); but Garrick's Dragonsbane and [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] want the ore itself, so keep enough back before smelting.

### Warming yourself

- Press {{键|E}} empty-handed on a lit forge (“Warm yourself at the forge”). If you are {{状态|潮湿}}, the damp is dried off: “You warm yourself at the forge; the damp steams off you.” If not, just “Warmth from the forge seeps into your hands.”, with no health restored. For details see [[营火#取暖|Warming yourself]].
- When the forge has been put out, the rules do not say what happens when you press {{键|E}} on it empty-handed (the prompt shows “?”).

### Cooking and burning things

- Holding raw food, right-click a lit forge and the food is cooked (“Cook X over the forge”); pressing {{键|G}} on it does the same.
- Hold something that burns and press {{键|G}}, and the thing goes into the fire and burns to {{物品|灰烬}}.

### The smiths' fire

- All three smiths need the forge lit while they work at the anvil: Haldor every day 6:00–21:00, Alberic 6:00–14:00, Garrick 6:00–21:00.
- After the forge has been put out, Garrick lights it again himself after a while (“Out again. Pass me the flint.”); he does not light it while it is wet.
- [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]] is obtained by handing Garrick 5 iron ore and 3 coal and then talking to him; in the rules, forging the sword does not use the forge.

### Lighting and putting out

- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water, left-click it. Rain does not put out a lit forge.
- **Lighting**: holding a {{物品|火把}}, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn {{技能|钻木取火}}, an ordinary stick works too.

### Other

- **Kitchen and workshop**: the forge is both a cooking fixture and a workstation, so a [[房间|room]] with a forge in it counts as both a kitchen and a workshop (no effect at present).
- **Monsters keep away**: except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit.
- The forge is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).

## History
