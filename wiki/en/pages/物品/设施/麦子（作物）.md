**Wheat** (crop) is wheat planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Wheat Seeds}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{item|Wheat}} and seeds. The harvested {{item|Wheat}} is a material: it can be fed to cows and chickens as feed, and sold to the trading stall on the Held Ground.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{item|Wheat Seeds}}, right-click an empty farmland tile; the seeds become a “wheat sprout”.
- **Where the seeds come from**: the “Farming” page of the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], 5 to a lot. No map has wheat already growing (see [[Crops]]). Building mode has no entry for sowing wheat; Wheat Seeds must be sown from your hand.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | wheat sprout | Water once; 2 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young wheat | Water again; 2 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe wheat | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 2 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{item|Ash}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- See [[Farming]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 Wheat (3 if fertilized) and 2 Wheat Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 Wheat straight into your hands, 1 Wheat Seeds dropped on the ground |

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- Digging it up with a {{item|Shovel}} turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”). The shovel is sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
- The sprout and young wheat do not catch fire; **ripe wheat is dry**: fire lights it at a touch, lightning can strike it too, and while burning it has a 30% chance every half second to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of it. Burnt out, it becomes one {{item|Ash}}.

## History

- 2026-09-30: Wheat was among the crops from the first version.
- 2026-10-08: The trading stall on the Held Ground sells Wheat Seeds; wheat can be used as feed.
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
