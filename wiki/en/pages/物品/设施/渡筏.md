The **ferry raft** is a wooden raft on the water beside the landing at [[The Ford|the Ford]], built by Aldo, the husband of the ferrywoman {{person|Hesper}}. Once the [[ferry winch|ferry winch]] is mended, talk to Hesper and she poles you across the river on the raft.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this one, at the Ford, on the water off the landing. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Crossing**: the raft cannot be poled by you. Once the ferry winch is mended, press {{key|E}} to talk to Hesper, and she takes you across (see [[ferry winch]]).
- **Putting things on it**: the raft is a surface; hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the raft.
- **{{key|E}}**: picks up what is on the raft first; the raft itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
