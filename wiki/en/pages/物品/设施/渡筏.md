The **ferry raft** is a wooden raft on the water beside the landing at [[渡口|the Ford]], built by Aldo, the husband of the ferrywoman {{人物|赫斯珀}}. Once the [[渡口绞盘|ferry winch]] is mended, talk to Hesper and she poles you across the river on the raft.

## Source

{{来源}}

- Only this one, at the Ford, on the water off the landing. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Crossing**: the raft cannot be poled by you. Once the ferry winch is mended, press {{键|E}} to talk to Hesper, and she takes you across (see [[渡口绞盘]]).
- **Putting things on it**: the raft is a surface; hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the raft.
- **{{键|E}}**: picks up what is on the raft first; the raft itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

The rules do not say what happens when you press {{键|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
