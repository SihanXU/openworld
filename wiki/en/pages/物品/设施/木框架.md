{{信息框}}
A **wooden frame** is something half-built from planks, not yet finished. Two planks put together make a frame; one knock of a hammer nails a 2-plank frame into a [[板条箱|crate]] and a 4-plank frame into a [[箱子|chest]]. Building a crate or a wooden chest in [[建造|build]] mode goes through these same steps.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Putting one up**: put a {{物品|木板}} on the ground, hold another plank and press {{键|G}} on it (“Start a frame”): “Two planks make a frame.”
- No map has a ready-made frame.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Adding planks, nailing it together

| Planks on the frame | Holding a plank, press {{键|G}} | Holding a hammer, left-click it |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Adds one: “A frame of 3 planks.” | Nails it into a crate: “You nail together a crate.” |
| 3 | Adds one: “A frame of 4 planks.” | “One more plank.” |
| 4 | “The frame holds no more planks.” | Nails it into a chest: “You nail together a chest.” |

- The hammer is the {{物品|锻锤}}, and it is not used up.
- Press {{键|E}} empty-handed to look at it (“Look at the wooden frame”); it tells you how many planks it has now and what it can still become, for example “A frame of 2 planks. A hammer makes a crate; two more make a chest.”

### Taking it apart

Hold an axe and left-click it (“Take the wooden frame apart”): “The wooden frame comes apart.” Every plank you put in comes back, not one missing.

### Other

- The frame is solid and blocks the way; it cannot be taken.
- It has no durability. Apart from taking it apart with an axe and nailing it with a hammer, the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- It is wooden and catches fire: it burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}, and the planks you put in are lost.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version of building already had the wooden frame: two planks make a frame, a hammer nails it into a crate or a chest, and an axe takes it back apart into planks.
