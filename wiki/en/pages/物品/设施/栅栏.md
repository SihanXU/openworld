A **fence** is a length of wooden fence that blocks the way; neither people nor creatures can get through. You have to build it yourself: in [[Building|build]] mode choose “Fence”, 2 {{item|Stick}}, with a hammer on you; hold the left button and drag to build a whole row, fencing in a garden or a pen. At present no map has a ready-made fence.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Fence”: 2 sticks, with a hammer on you (the {{item|Smith Hammer}}, not used up).
- **By hand**: put a stick on the ground, hold another stick and press {{key|G}} on it: “Two sticks make stakes.” Knock the [[stakes|stakes]] with a hammer: “You nail up a fence.” (see [[Building#Building by hand|building by hand]])

## Direction

A fence has a direction: across (running east–west) or upright (running north–south).

- **Build mode**: the arrow on the preview is its facing; press {{key|R}} to turn it. When the arrow points left or right the fence stands north–south; when it points up or down, east–west. If you have not turned it, it runs between the player and the tile (its face toward the player).
- **By hand**: the fence faces whichever way the player faced when setting the stakes, likewise with its face toward the player.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Blocks the way**: a fence is solid; people and creatures have to go around it. [[Animals|Animals]] cannot get out either.
- A fence cannot be taken, and an axe, pickaxe or hammer cannot knock it loose; to remove it, break it.
- **Breaking**: durability 4, wood. An axe cuts it open in one blow; a bare-handed punch takes off 1 point. Broken, it leaves 1 stick (half of its 2, see [[Durability and Breaking]]).
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}; along a row of fence, the fire may run on down the line.
- Hold something and press {{key|G}}, and the thing is put down on its tile.
