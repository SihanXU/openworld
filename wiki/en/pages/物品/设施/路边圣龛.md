{{信息框}}
The **wayside shrines** are five stone shrines beside [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], shaped like the [[五伤路碑|five-wound shrine stones]]; each stands for one of [[五苦|the Five Sufferings]] and is carved with that suffering's text. The five are the [[荆棘冠之龛]], the [[鞭刑柱之龛]], the [[饥刑之秤之龛]], the [[钉刑圣木之龛]] and the [[穿心圣枪之龛]].

## Source

{{来源}}

Only on the Pilgrim's Road, in a line from west to east. They cannot be built.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: walk up to one and press {{键|E}} to read the carved text.
- **Lighting**: holding what that suffering asks for, press {{键|G}} to lay it on. The shrine takes it (the thing is gone), a small light kindles in the carving, and you are told “X is laid on Y. A light kindles in the carving.” Once lit it stays lit, and laying anything else on it only says it is “already lit”.

| Shrine | What it asks for | What it accepts |
|---|---|---|
| [[荆棘冠之龛]] | Something worn on the head | Anything worn on the head: a hat, a helmet, a circlet… |
| [[鞭刑柱之龛]] | A rope | Fibers such as Rope and Cut Grass |
| [[饥刑之秤之龛]] | Something to eat | Anything edible |
| [[钉刑圣木之龛]] | Something of metal | Things made of metal |
| [[穿心圣枪之龛]] | A light | Light sources such as a torch or a candle |

- **The wrong thing**: it is not taken, and the shrine says what it asks for, for example “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Crown asks for something worn on the head: pride laid down.”
- **What is never taken**: relics ([[荆棘冠]], [[誓约之棘]]), important items ([[荆棘骑士团印章]], [[替任文书]]), things that never leave you, and keepsakes ([[朝圣者的扇贝]], [[立誓扇贝]], [[米娅的戒指]]). Laid on a shrine, they only get “X stays dark. Y is not a thing to give away.”, and the thing stays with you.
- All five lit: completes the “Light the five shrines” stage of [[第一印]] and plays a scene.

The Pilgrim's Road itself has five offerings ready; see [[朝圣者之路#五座圣龛|the Pilgrim's Road]].

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: Relics, important items, things that never leave you and keepsakes are no longer taken. Before this, the Crown of Thorns laid on the Shrine of the Crown, or the Order's Seal laid on the Shrine of the Nails, was taken and could never be got back.
