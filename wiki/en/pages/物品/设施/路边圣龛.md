The **wayside shrines** are five stone shrines beside [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], shaped like the [[five-wound shrine stone|five-wound shrine stones]]; each stands for one of [[The Five Sufferings|the Five Sufferings]] and is carved with that suffering's text. The five are the [[Shrine of the Crown]], the [[Shrine of the Pillar]], the [[Shrine of the Scales]], the [[Shrine of the Nails]] and the [[Shrine of the Lance]].

## Source

{{sources}}

Only on the Pilgrim's Road, in a line from west to east. They cannot be built.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: walk up to one and press {{key|E}} to read the carved text.
- **Lighting**: holding what that suffering asks for, press {{key|G}} to lay it on. The shrine takes it (the thing is gone), a small light kindles in the carving, and you are told “X is laid on Y. A light kindles in the carving.” Once lit it stays lit, and laying anything else on it only says it is “already lit”.

| Shrine | What it asks for | What it accepts |
|---|---|---|
| [[Shrine of the Crown]] | Something worn on the head | Anything worn on the head: a hat, a helmet, a circlet… |
| [[Shrine of the Pillar]] | A rope | Fibers such as Rope and Cut Grass |
| [[Shrine of the Scales]] | Something to eat | Anything edible |
| [[Shrine of the Nails]] | Something of metal | Things made of metal |
| [[Shrine of the Lance]] | A light | Light sources such as a torch or a candle |

- **The wrong thing**: it is not taken, and the shrine says what it asks for, for example “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Crown asks for something worn on the head: pride laid down.”
- **What is never taken**: relics ([[Crown of Thorns]], [[The Oath-Thorn]]), important items ([[The Order's Seal]], [[Writ of Relief]]), things that never leave you, and keepsakes ([[Pilgrim's Scallop]], [[Sworn Scallop]], [[Mia's Ring]]). Laid on a shrine, they only get “X stays dark. Y is not a thing to give away.”, and the thing stays with you.
- All five lit: completes the “Light the five shrines” stage of [[The First Seal]] and plays a scene.

The Pilgrim's Road itself has five offerings ready; see [[The Pilgrim's Road#The Five Shrines|the Pilgrim's Road]].

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: Relics, important items, things that never leave you and keepsakes are no longer taken. Before this, the Crown of Thorns laid on the Shrine of the Crown, or the Order's Seal laid on the Shrine of the Nails, was taken and could never be got back.
