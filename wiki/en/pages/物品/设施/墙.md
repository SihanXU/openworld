{{信息框}}
A **wall** is a wooden wall for building houses, made from 2 {{物品|木板}} on the spot. It can be held in the hand: right-click a tile to stand it up; strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole wall comes loose and lies on the spot, then press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself). A wall blocks the way and the view, forms the edge of a [[房间|room]], and holds up the floor above. For how walls stack and hold up floors, see [[楼层#墙|Storeys]]; for how to place one see [[建造]].

The stone walls and wooden walls already on the maps are terrain, not this thing (see [[地形]]).

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Crafting**: press {{键|C}} and choose “Wall”: 2 planks, no tool, can be made anywhere.
- **Build mode**: press {{键|B}} and choose “Wall”. If you carry a wall it is put down directly; if not, one is made on the spot from the planks on you and then put down. Hold the left button and drag to place a row.
- Lesson six of the [[新手教程|tutorial]]: put the first wall on the drawn outline, then wall it all round.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Putting down and taking up

- **Standing it up**: holding a wall, right-click a tile next to you (hold and drag to stand a row), or place it in build mode. A wall takes up a tile's space slot: it cannot go on the tile you stand on, and cannot go where the tile already has a wall, stairs or furniture (“Something is already here.”).
- Pressing {{键|G}} holding a wall only puts it on the ground, loose, like an item, not standing; {{键|E}} picks it up again.
- **Stacking**: on the second storey, put a wall right above a ground-floor wall and it stands on the wall below.
- **Taking up**: strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole wall comes loose and lies on the spot; press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself), with no loss of material. Knock out a wall holding up a floor, and the floor that has lost its support falls in.
- Press {{键|G}} on a wall with something in hand and the thing lands in the free place on the storey above the wall.

### What a wall does

- **Blocks the way**: a wall is solid.
- **Blocks the view**: what happens behind a wall cannot be seen by people on its other side.
- **Encloses a room**: walled and doored all round, with a cover overhead, it is a [[房间|room]].
- **Holds up floors**: each wall holds the floors and ceilings on the storey above within 2 tiles of itself (see [[楼层#支撑与坍塌|support and collapse]]).

### Breaking

Durability 12, wood. Axes, pickaxes and hammers do not wear away its durability; they only knock the whole wall loose. Only hitting it with a weapon or bare hands wears it down (a punch does 1). Broken, it leaves 1 plank (half of 2). To get it back as it was, knock it loose with a tool rather than breaking it (see [[耐久与破坏]]).

### Fire

A wall is wooden and can catch fire. It burns for about 4.5 seconds, with a half chance every half second to lose 1 durability, and a 10% chance to spread the fire to something burnable within one tile (other walls, floors, doors, furniture, dry grass…). When the fire goes out the wall remains, with somewhat less durability; if it burns until its durability is gone, it burns to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}} left on that tile, and leaves no plank.
