The **plot for a second residents' tent** is a staked-out patch of empty ground east of the sword on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], the place where the “Second Residents' Tent” of [[Expanding the Held Ground]] is to be built. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, after a while it becomes a [[tent|tent]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- On the Held Ground from the start, east of the sword. You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[Alberic|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find “Second Residents' Tent” on the “Structures” page. It requires land tier I (the Grove); materials: 8 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}}, 14 {{item|Plank}} and 6 {{item|Log}}, handed to him for the Held Ground's store. Once the store has enough, press “Build!”. When it is finished, Alberic also gives you 50 {{item|Dawnmark}}.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “Two more beds”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “Voices at the fire of an evening. I had forgotten that, too.” Every so often the plot checks whether the building is done; when it is, it turns into a [[tent|tent]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: one bed on each side in front of the tent, for the people you invite to live there (see [[Held Ground Residents]]).
- The plot does not block the way, and you can walk over it; hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- The stakes catch fire. A plot burned away is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[staked plot]].
