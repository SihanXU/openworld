{{信息框}}
The **slime burrow** is a stone hole in the northwest corner of [[渡口]], 3×3 tiles, and it blocks the way. The {{怪物|火焰史莱姆}} stays just south of its mouth. In the rules no new slimes come out of the burrow; it is only a place.

## Source

{{来源}}

- There is only this one, at the Ford. Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Blocks the way**: the stone hole is solid and takes up 3×3 tiles.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it is stone and does not catch fire.
- Press {{键|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.

What happens when you fiddle with it bare-handed with {{键|E}} is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
