A **brazier** is a metal fire basin standing on the ground. In the dark maps — [[The Old Barrow]], [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]], [[The Warden's Sanctum]], [[The Old Mine]] and [[The Dragon's Lair]] — 21 of them burn in all along the passages and in the chambers. The 2 tiles around a lit brazier are a safe place: except for fire monsters, monsters will not come in.

## Source

{{sources}}

- 4 in the Old Barrow, 5 in the Ossuary of the Thorn, 4 in the Warden's Sanctum (at the four corners of the chamber), 4 in the Old Mine (along the main gallery) and 4 in the Dragon's Lair. All start out lit.
- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Monsters keep away

- Except for fire monsters (fire slimes, fire imps, dragon whelps, the dragon of Cinder Peak and the like), monsters will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing there (see [[Factions and Temper]]).
- When something is hard on your heels, fall back beside a brazier. The {{monster|barrow warden}} in [[The Warden's Sanctum|the Warden's Sanctum]] and the {{monster|cyclops}} in the Old Mine fear fire too.
- At night, people and creatures within 3 tiles of a lit fire see as far as by day.

### Lighting and putting out

- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water (a {{item|Water Bucket}}, for instance), left-click it, and the fire goes out. Any water element will do.
- **Lighting**: holding a {{item|Torch}}, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn {{skill|Firemaker}}, right-clicking it with an ordinary {{item|Stick}} lights it too. If the brazier is wet, the first touch of fire only dries it.
- Once lit it stays lit: it needs no fuel, and rain does not put it out.

### Cooking

A brazier is a cooking fixture:

- Holding raw food, right-click a lit brazier (“Cook X over the brazier”), or press {{key|G}} on it, and the raw food is cooked, staying with you (see [[Cooking]]).
- A [[Rooms|room]] with a brazier in it counts as a kitchen.

### Warming yourself

Press {{key|E}} empty-handed on a lit brazier (“Warm yourself at the brazier”): if you are {{status|wet}}, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself at the brazier; the damp steams off you.”); if not, just “Warmth from the brazier seeps into your hands.”, with no health restored. The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} empty-handed on an unlit brazier (the prompt shows “?”). See [[campfire#Warming up|Warming yourself]].

### Throwing things in

- Hold something that burns (logs, sticks, cut grass…) and press {{key|G}}: “X goes into the fire.” It burns down to {{item|Ash}}.
- Anything else, with {{key|G}}, is put down on the brazier's tile.

### Unbreakable, and it does not burn

The brazier is metal and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire either. Hit by the lightning element, it becomes charged ({{status|electrified}}), which does nothing to it.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
