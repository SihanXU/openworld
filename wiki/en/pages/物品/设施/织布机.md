A **loom** is a wooden loom. It is a workstation: stand beside it, and in the [[制作|crafting]] panel ({{键|C}}) you can weave cloth. There are two in the game: [[卡丝|Cass]]'s in [[初原村]], and the Weaving Shed built on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Firstlight Village**: one on the east side of the Woodcutter's House (the home of [[格斯|Gus]] and [[卡丝|Cass]]) on the west side of the village. It is Cass's, and she sits at it weaving every day 9:00–17:00. Weaving beside it does not count as touching her things.
- **The Held Ground's Weaving Shed**: build the “Weaving Shed” in [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]]'s [[领地扩建|Held Ground expansion]] panel (from land tier I, 8 {{物品|木板}} and 6 {{物品|树枝}}), and after a while the [[织坊地基|plot for a weaving shed]] south of the sword becomes a loom.
- You cannot make one, nor place one in [[建造|build]] mode.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Weaving**: stand beside a loom and press {{键|C}} to open the crafting panel; weave 4 {{物品|干草}} into 1 {{物品|布匹}} (recipe level 1). The row is marked “needs a loom nearby”, and it cannot be made when you are not beside one.
- Cloth is the material for the hoods, cloaks, robes, gloves, belts, slippers and codices of the [[套装|profession sets]], and also for the residents' tents in [[领地扩建]].
- **Workshop**: a [[房间|room]] with a loom in it counts as a workshop.
- **{{键|G}}**: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
