A **loom** is a wooden loom. It is a workstation: stand beside it, and in the [[Crafting|crafting]] panel ({{key|C}}) you can weave cloth. There are two in the game: [[Cass|Cass]]'s in [[Firstlight Village]], and the Weaving Shed built on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Firstlight Village**: one on the east side of the Woodcutter's House (the home of [[Gus|Gus]] and [[Cass|Cass]]) on the west side of the village. It is Cass's, and she sits at it weaving every day 9:00–17:00. Weaving beside it does not count as touching her things.
- **The Held Ground's Weaving Shed**: build the “Weaving Shed” in [[Alberic|Alberic]]'s [[Expanding the Held Ground|Held Ground expansion]] panel (from land tier I, 8 {{item|Plank}} and 6 {{item|Stick}}), and after a while the [[plot for a weaving shed|plot for a weaving shed]] south of the sword becomes a loom.
- You cannot make one, nor place one in [[Building|build]] mode.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Weaving**: stand beside a loom and press {{key|C}} to open the crafting panel; weave 4 {{item|Cut Grass}} into 1 {{item|Bolt of Cloth}} (recipe level 1). The row is marked “needs a loom nearby”, and it cannot be made when you are not beside one.
- Cloth is the material for the hoods, cloaks, robes, gloves, belts, slippers and codices of the [[Gear Sets|profession sets]], and also for the residents' tents in [[Expanding the Held Ground]].
- **Workshop**: a [[Rooms|room]] with a loom in it counts as a workshop.
- **{{key|G}}**: hold something and press {{key|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
