A **briar** is a clump of thorny brambles left by [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|the White Knight Alberic]]. Two clumps are nailed to the piles of the broken footbridge at [[渡口|the Ford]]; on [[东大路|the East Road]] a ring of “the knight's briars” surrounds the White Knight's fields, with a gap left only where the road passes through. Briars are solid and block the way, but they can be cut open or burnt away.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **The Ford**: “briars nailed into the bridge piles”, 2 clumps, nailed to the piles of the footbridge whose planks the White Knight took away.
- **The East Road**: “the knight's briars”, 24 clumps forming a ring; inside the ring are the White Knight's fields, and the road runs through the middle of it.
- They cannot be planted.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Blocking the way

Briars are solid; people and creatures must go around them. Touching one does not hurt.

### Cutting

- Durability 6, wood (hardness 1). An axe works on wood at efficiency 1: a stone axe takes 7 points a blow, cutting one open in a single stroke. A bare-handed punch takes only 1 point.
- Once cut open, it has a one-in-two chance to leave 1 {{物品|树枝}} (half its makeup, see [[耐久与破坏]]).

### Burning

Briars catch fire. After burning for about 4.5 seconds a clump becomes one {{物品|灰烬}}; while burning, every half second it has a 10% chance to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of it, and it loses durability too. Since the ring of briars is all joined up, the fire may burn through it one clump after another.

### Other
