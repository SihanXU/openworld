{{信息框}}
The **fountain** is a stone-built fountain. In the rules it is a water source like the {{物品|水井}}: point at it holding an empty container and it fills with water. For now no map in the game has a fountain, and you cannot make one yourself.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Drawing water**: hold an empty container (an empty bucket, an {{物品|空瓶}}, an {{物品|空水袋}}) and left-click it: “You fill it with water.” “Draw water” in [[建造|build]] mode can use it too (see [[农耕#水从哪来|where water comes from]]).
- The fountain is solid and blocks the way.
- The fountain is stone and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The fountain has been in the game since the first version.
