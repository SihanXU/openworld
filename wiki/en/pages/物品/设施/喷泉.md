The **fountain** is a stone-built fountain. In the rules it is a water source like the {{item|well}}: point at it holding an empty container and it fills with water. For now no map in the game has a fountain, and you cannot make one yourself.

## Source

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Drawing water**: hold an empty container (an empty bucket, an {{item|Empty Flask}}, an {{item|Empty Waterskin}}) and left-click it: “You fill it with water.” “Draw water” in [[Building|build]] mode can use it too (see [[Farming#Where water comes from|where water comes from]]).
- The fountain is solid and blocks the way.
- The fountain is stone and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

## History

- 2026-09-30: The fountain has been in the game since the first version.
