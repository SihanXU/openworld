A **spent iron vein** is the rock face left in place after an [[iron vein|iron vein]] has been mined out with a pickaxe. Its material is bedrock (hardness 9), harder than any tool: whatever you strike it with only raises a puff of dust and the message “Bare rock. The vein will show again in a day.” It cannot be taken either, and it still blocks the way.

## Source

{{sources}}

Mining out any one of them: in [[Dawn Hill Mine]], [[The Old Mine]], the outer ring of [[The Held Ground]] and [[The Quarry]].

## Growing back

It carries a timer of one game day (24 game hours, about 24 minutes of real time). When the time is up, it turns back into an iron vein in place, at full durability, and mining it again gives 2 more {{item|Iron Ore}}. The timer keeps running while you are off the map and catches up when you come back. See [[Mining]].
