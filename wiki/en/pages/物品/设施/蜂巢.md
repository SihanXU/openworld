A **beehive** is a wooden hive. At present no map in the game has a beehive, and you cannot make one. In the rules no honey can be taken from a beehive, and it does not attract bees; the game's {{item|Honey Jar}} is a different thing.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Blocks the way**: the beehive is solid.
- **{{key|G}}**: the thing is put on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it is wooden, catches fire, and when burnt out becomes one {{item|Ash}}.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
