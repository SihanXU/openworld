The **plot for a weaving shed** is a staked-out patch of empty ground south of the sword on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], the place where the “Weaving Shed” of [[领地扩建]] is to be built. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, after a while it becomes a [[织布机|loom]].

## Source

{{来源}}

- On the Held Ground from the start, south of the sword. You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find “Weaving Shed” on the “Structures” page. It requires land tier I (the Grove); materials: 8 {{物品|木板}} and 6 {{物品|树枝}}, handed to him for the Held Ground's store. Once the store has enough, press “Build!”.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “A loom for cloth”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “A loom. Someone used to weave the order's banners. I do not remember who. It does not matter now.” Every so often the plot checks whether the building is done; when it is, it turns into a [[织布机|loom]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: stand beside it and weave 4 cut grass into 1 bolt of cloth.
- The plot does not block the way, and you can walk over it; hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
- The stakes catch fire. A plot burned away is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[地基]].
