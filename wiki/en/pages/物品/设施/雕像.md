A **statue** is an ordinary stone statue. At present no map in the game has a statue, and you cannot make one. The one you can awaken and use to teleport on the world map is the [[waypoint statue|waypoint statue]], a different thing; to avoid confusing the two, ordinary statues are not placed on the maps.

## Uses

{{uses}}

In the rules a statue is just a stone thing that blocks the way:

- **{{key|G}}**: the thing is put on its tile (“You put X beside the statue.”).
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules. It does not catch fire.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
