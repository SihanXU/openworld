**Pebbles** are a patch of small round stones by the river, on the bank at [[The Ford]]. They are just scenery: they do not block the way and cannot be picked up. The stone you can pick up and use is {{item|Stone}}.

## Source

{{sources}}

- By the river at the Ford, in 2 places.
- They cannot be planted, and do not appear by themselves.

## Uses

{{uses}}

They are small things on the ground, and the rules let you do very little to them:

- **Do not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over them.
- **{{key|G}}**: press {{key|G}} on them with something in hand to put it on their tile.
- **Hitting**: they have no durability; what happens if you hit them with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Not stones**: pebbles cannot be picked up, and no stone can be knocked out of them.
- **Fire**: pebbles do not catch fire.

What happens when you fiddle with them bare-handed with {{key|E}} is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). Do something else to them and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).

- 2026-10-02: The small things on the ground went from picture decoration to things in the world: each one an object that every input can reach.
