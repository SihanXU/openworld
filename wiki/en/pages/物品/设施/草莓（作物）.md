A **strawberry** (crop) is a strawberry planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow {{item|Strawberry Seeds}}, water it twice, and once ripe harvest {{item|Strawberry}} and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see {{item|Strawberry}}: eaten raw it restores 2 [[Satiety|satiety]]. The strawberry is **perennial**: after a harvest it does not disappear but goes back to a young plant, and one more watering ripens it again.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Sowing**: holding {{item|Strawberry Seeds}}, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Strawberry Seeds.” The seeds become a “strawberry sprout”. “Sow Strawberry” on the “Planting” page of [[Building]] mode works too.
- The seeds are sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] (“Farming” shelf, 3 for 20 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]). No map has strawberries already planted, and the vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] has none either.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growth

| Stage | Name | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | strawberry sprout | Water once; 2 game hours later it grows into the next stage |
| 2 | young strawberry | Water again; 2 game hours later it ripens |
| 3 | ripe strawberry | Harvest |

- **Watering**: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets {{status|watered}} and grows one stage 2 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
- **Needs open sky**: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
- **No water, no growth**, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
- **Fertilizing**: holding {{item|Fertilizer}} or {{item|Ash}}, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (With fertilizer it is “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
- All crops grow equally fast: at least 4 game hours and two waterings from sowing to ripeness. See [[Farming]] for the full rules.

### Harvest

| Method | You get |
|---|---|
| Holding a {{item|Sickle}}, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) | 2 strawberries (3 if fertilized) and 2 Strawberry Seeds, dropped on its tile |
| Press {{key|E}} to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) | 1 strawberry straight into your hands, 1 Strawberry Seeds dropped on the ground |

The strawberry is **perennial**: after harvesting or picking, it goes back to “young strawberry”, with no need to sow again; water it once more and it ripens again 2 game hours later. Fertilizer is used up at the harvest and must be applied again.

Harvested strawberries can be sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] for 6 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each.

### Other

- Crops do not block the way.
- What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
- In the rules, digging it up with a [[Shovel|shovel]] turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
- Crops do not catch fire.
