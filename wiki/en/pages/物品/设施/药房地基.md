The **plot for an apothecary** is a staked-out patch of empty ground northwest of the sword on [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]], the place where the “Apothecary” of [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] is to be built. Before it is built it is only a ring of stakes; once it is built, it turns into a [[坩埚|cauldron]] a little later.

## Source

{{来源}}

- It is on the Held Ground from the start, northwest of the sword. You cannot make it, and it cannot be taken.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克]] and choose “The Held Ground…”, then find “Apothecary” on the “Buildings” page. It requires Land II (The Meadow); the materials, 8 {{物品|石块}} and 6 {{物品|草药}}, are handed to him and kept in the Held Ground's store. Once the store has enough, press “Build!”.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “A cauldron for brewing”.
- When you press it, Alberic says: “A pot for remedies. I have been wounded enough times to know which ones work.” The plot checks every so often whether it has been built; once it has, it turns into a [[坩埚|cauldron]] in a burst of sparkles.
- What it turns into: see [[坩埚|cauldron]].
- The plot does not block the way, and you can walk over it; holding something, press {{键|G}} at it and the thing is put on its tile.
- The stakes catch fire. A burnt plot is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[地基]].
