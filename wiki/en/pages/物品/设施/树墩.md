The **stump** is the wooden chopping block beside the tent in the camp at [[渡口|the Ford]], with an “Old Axe” lying on the ground next to it. Things can be put on it. The [[伐木#树桩|stump]] left by felling a tree is terrain and grows back into a tree two days later; this stump is a thing, and never grows again.

## Source

{{来源}}

- Only this one, in the Ford's camp. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Putting things on it

The stump is a surface: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the stump (“You set X on the stump.”). Pressing {{键|E}} picks up what is on it first; the stump itself cannot be taken.

### Splitting it

- Durability 6, wood (hardness 1). An axe works on wood at efficiency 1: a stone axe deals 7 points a blow, so one blow splits it. A bare-handed punch takes off only 1 point.
- Split, it leaves 1 {{物品|木头}} (half of its 2, see [[耐久与破坏]]). Once split, it is gone.

### Fire

Wooden, it catches fire. While burning it loses durability; burned out, it becomes one {{物品|灰烬}}.
