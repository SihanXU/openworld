A **scarecrow** is a straw figure propped up on a wooden frame and dressed in old clothes. At present no map in the game has a scarecrow on it, and you cannot make one. In the rules it scares nothing away, and there are no animals in the game that come to steal the crops.

{{sources}}

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Blocks the way**: the scarecrow is solid.
- **{{key|G}}**: the thing is put down on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: straw, cloth and wood, it catches fire and burns down to one {{item|Ash}}.

The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
