{{信息框}}
A **sign post** is a half-made sign, a plank set on a stick. One knock of a hammer nails it into a blank [[告示牌|sign]]. Building a signpost in [[建造|build]] mode goes through these steps.

## Source

{{来源}}

- **Putting one up**: put a {{物品|树枝}} on the ground, hold a {{物品|木板}} and press {{键|G}} on it (“Put up a post”): “A plank on a stick: a sign post.”
- No map has a ready-made post.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Nailing it into a sign**: hold a hammer (the {{物品|锻锤}}, not used up) and left-click it (“Make a sign”): “You nail together a blank sign.” For how to write on a blank sign, see [[告示牌]].
- **Taking it apart**: hold an axe and left-click it (“Take the sign post apart”): the plank and the stick both come back.
- The post is solid and blocks the way; it cannot be taken.
- It has no durability. Apart from taking it apart with an axe and nailing it with a hammer, the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- It is wooden and catches fire, burning down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version of building already had the sign post: a plank set on a stick, nailed into a sign with a hammer.
