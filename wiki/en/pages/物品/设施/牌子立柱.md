A **sign post** is a half-made sign, a plank set on a stick. One knock of a hammer nails it into a blank [[sign|sign]]. Building a signpost in [[Building|build]] mode goes through these steps.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Putting one up**: put a {{item|Stick}} on the ground, hold a {{item|Plank}} and press {{key|G}} on it (“Put up a post”): “A plank on a stick: a sign post.”
- No map has a ready-made post.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Nailing it into a sign**: hold a hammer (the {{item|Smith Hammer}}, not used up) and left-click it (“Make a sign”): “You nail together a blank sign.” For how to write on a blank sign, see [[sign]].
- **Taking it apart**: hold an axe and left-click it (“Take the sign post apart”): the plank and the stick both come back.
- The post is solid and blocks the way; it cannot be taken.
- It has no durability. Apart from taking it apart with an axe and nailing it with a hammer, the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- It is wooden and catches fire, burning down to one {{item|Ash}}.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version of building already had the sign post: a plank set on a stick, nailed into a sign with a hammer.
