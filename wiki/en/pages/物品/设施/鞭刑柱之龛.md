The **Shrine of the Pillar** is the second [[路边圣龛|wayside shrine]] on [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is a rope or a cord: the scourge.

## Source

{{来源}}

On [[朝圣者之路|the Pilgrim's Road]], the second. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Reading**: press {{键|E}} to read: “THE SECOND SUFFERING: THE SCOURGE. Bound to the pillar, he bled for every one of us. — Lay down a cord, the scourge you were spared.”
- **Lighting**: holding a fiber such as a rope or Cut Grass, press {{键|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a penitent's plaited cord (northeast of the first shrine).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Pillar asks for a rope or a cord: the scourge.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[路边圣龛]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Pillar” of [[第一印]].

## Background

The penitent [[以斯拉修士]] says: “The crown, something for the head. The scourge, a cord. I have given them all my cords.”
