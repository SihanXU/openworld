The **Shrine of the Pillar** is the second [[wayside shrine|wayside shrine]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it asks for is a rope or a cord: the scourge.

## Source

{{sources}}

On [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], the second. It cannot be built.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: press {{key|E}} to read: “THE SECOND SUFFERING: THE SCOURGE. Bound to the pillar, he bled for every one of us. — Lay down a cord, the scourge you were spared.”
- **Lighting**: holding a fiber such as a rope or Cut Grass, press {{key|G}} to lay it on; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself has one ready: a penitent's plaited cord (northeast of the first shrine).
- The wrong thing is not taken, and the shrine says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Pillar asks for a rope or a cord: the scourge.” Relics and important items laid on it are not taken either (see [[wayside shrine]]).
- Lighting it completes the objective “Light the Shrine of the Pillar” of [[The First Seal]].

## Background

The penitent [[Brother Ezra]] says: “The crown, something for the head. The scourge, a cord. I have given them all my cords.”

## History
