The **bookshelf** is a wooden bookshelf, a container that holds 8 things. For now no map in the game has one, and you cannot make one yourself. A bookshelf holds anything, not only books.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{键|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{键|G}} puts the thing in your hand into it, up to 8 things (see [[箱子与容器]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers is a storeroom (no effect for now).
- **Breaking**: durability 8, made of wood. Broken, it falls apart into 1 {{物品|木板}}, with a half chance of 2 (half of 3), and what is inside falls out (see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- **Fire**: it can catch fire, burning down to a portion of {{物品|灰烬}}; what is inside drops on the spot.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The bookshelf has been in the game since the first version.
- 2026-10-04: Gained durability and a makeup (3 planks).
