An **oak** is a tree grown from an {{item|Acorn}}. Plant an acorn and first there is an “oak sapling” on the ground; under open sky, with no one standing on it, two game days later it grows into an ordinary tree that can be felled like the trees already on the map (see [[Woodcutting]]). An oak sapling does not need to be planted in farmland and does not need watering.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Planting**: holding an acorn, right-click a tile of ground that can be hoed: grass, dirt, sand, snow or farmland. If nothing else is growing on that tile: “You plant an acorn.” The acorn becomes an oak sapling.
- **Where acorns come from**: felling a tree has a 25% chance to drop one. Once you learn {{skill|Woodcutter}}, felling a tree gives 1 more log but no longer drops acorns.
- No map has a ready-made oak sapling.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Growing into a tree

- While under open sky with no one standing on it, an oak sapling grows into a tree after **two game days** (48 game hours, 48 minutes of real time).
- If it is covered or someone is standing on it when the time comes, it does not grow, and checks again every game hour.
- No watering, no fertilizer. Watering only makes it wet.
- The grown tree is the “tree” of the terrain: two blows of an axe fell it, it drops logs and sticks, and the stump left behind grows back into a tree two days later (see [[Woodcutting]]).

### Other

- Oak saplings do not block the way and do not catch fire.
- Digging one up with a {{item|Shovel}} turns it back into an acorn. The shovel is sold at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]].
- The rules do not say what happens when you hit it with a weapon.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The first version already had the oak: felling trees may drop acorns, and planted acorns grow into oaks.
- 2026-10-04: Oak saplings and stumps share one growth clock: two game days under open sky and they grow into trees (W7).
- 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{key|E}} (see [[Controls]]).
