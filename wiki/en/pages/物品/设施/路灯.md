{{信息框}}
A **lamp post** is a metal post with a lamp burning at the top. [[初原村]] and [[余烬镇]] have two each, lit from the start. Like every lit fire, it keeps monsters away and lights up its surroundings at night.

## Source

{{来源}}

- Two in Firstlight Village: one on the east side of the square, one on the west side of the village.
- Two in Emberfall, near the square.
- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Monsters keep away

Except fire monsters, monsters do not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase a person standing there (see [[阵营与脾性]]). At night, people and creatures standing within 3 tiles of a lit fire see as far as by day. On screen a ring of warm light surrounds a lamp post (see [[昼夜]]).

### Lighting and putting out

- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water, left-click it and the lamp goes out. Rain does not put out a lit lamp post.
- **Lighting**: holding a {{物品|火把}}, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you have learned {{技能|钻木取火}}, right-clicking it holding an ordinary stick lights it too. When it is wet, the first touch of fire only dries it.
- Once lit it stays lit, with no need to add oil.

### Warming yourself

Press {{键|E}} with empty hands at a lit lamp post (“Warm yourself at the lamp post”): if you are {{状态|潮湿}}, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself at the lamp post; the damp steams off you.”); if you are not wet, only “The lamp post's warmth seeps into your hands.”, and it restores no health. What happens if you press {{键|E}} with empty hands at an unlit lamp post is not written in the rules (the prompt shows “?”). See [[营火#取暖|Warming yourself]].

### Throwing things in

- Holding something burnable, press {{键|G}}: the thing is thrown into the fire and burns to {{物品|灰烬}}.
- A lamp post is not cookware and cannot cook anything: holding raw food and pressing {{键|G}} at it only puts the food on the tile beside the lamp post (“You put X beside the lamp post.”), still raw; what happens if you right-click it holding raw food is not written in the rules. For cooking see [[烹饪]].

### Cannot be broken, does not burn

A lamp post is metal and has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules; nor does it catch fire. Struck by the lightning element it becomes charged ({{状态|感电}}), which does nothing to it.

For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
