**Fallen leaves** are a heap of dead leaves under a tree, most common in [[The Forest|the Forest]]. They do not block the way and cannot be taken. Fallen leaves are dry: they catch fire more easily than the other small things, and the fire spreads faster.

## Source

{{sources}}

- The Forest (the most), [[The Ford|the Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]] and [[The Grassland|the Grassland]], always beside trees.
- They cannot be planted, and they do not grow back by themselves.

## Uses

{{uses}}

They are one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with them:

- **Does not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over them.
- **{{key|G}}**: holding something, press {{key|G}} at them and the thing is put on their tile.
- **Hitting**: they have no durability; what happens if you hit them with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Lightning**: lightning in a thunderstorm sets alight the dry flammable things it strikes, and fallen leaves are one of them.
- **Fire**: fallen leaves catch fire and after burning for about 4.5 seconds become one {{item|Ash}}; because they are dry, while burning they have a 30% chance every half second to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of them. In the Forest the leaves lie in one stretch, so take care with fire.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle them is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to them, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
