A **shelf** is a wooden rack. It is a container and holds 8 things. At present no map in the game has a shelf, and you cannot make one. Shelves are not used for selling either: the only place in the game to buy and sell is the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]] (see [[Money]]).

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{key|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{key|G}} puts what you hold into it, up to 8 things (see [[Chests and Containers]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[Rooms|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).
- **Breaking**: durability 6, made of wood. Broken, it falls apart into 1 {{item|Plank}}, or 2 with a one-in-two chance (half of 3), and what is inside drops out (see [[Durability and Breaking]]).
- **Fire**: it catches fire and when burnt out becomes one {{item|Ash}}; what is inside drops where it stood.
