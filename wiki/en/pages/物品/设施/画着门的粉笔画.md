{{信息框}}
The **chalk drawing of a door** is a picture the child {{人物|佩尔}} drew in chalk on the ground at the door of [[古墓]]. It lies flat on the ground and does not block the way. Walk up to it and press {{键|E}} to see what it shows; once you have looked at it, the drawing counts as found.

## Source

{{来源}}

- There is only this one, drawn by Pell. You cannot draw one yourself.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Looking at the drawing

- Stand within 1 tile of the drawing and press {{键|E}}. What it shows: “A door, shut, a hand-mark on it. Behind it, something with a crown of bones. In front, two small people.”
- Once you have looked at it, the objective “The barrow door” of the side quest [[佩尔的涂鸦]] is complete. Looking at it before taking the quest counts too.
- Pell says he drew one in every place where he saw the Grey Man. The four drawings are: [[画着石碑的粉笔画]] (by the shrine stone at the mountain's foot), [[画着门的粉笔画]] (at the barrow's door), [[画着桥的粉笔画]] (by the footbridge at the Ford) and [[画着灰衣人的粉笔画]] (by the flame in Firstlight Village).

### Other

- The chalk drawing cannot be taken (“That stays where it is.”), nor wiped away: water only makes it wet, and fire cannot burn it.
- It has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- Hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on the drawing's tile.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
