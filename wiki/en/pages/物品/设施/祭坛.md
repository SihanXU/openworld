{{信息框}}
An **altar** is a stone-built altar that things can be put on. Every altar in the game has a name of its own and words carved on it; walk up to one and press {{键|E}} to read it (“Read X”). The [[插在地上的长剑|planted sword]] at the center of [[誓守之地|the Held Ground]] is not an altar but a thing of its own.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Reading

- **Astra's altar**: “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. — Suffering is not God's abandonment.” {{人物|伊尔丝修女}} keeps the sacred flame every day from 6:00 to 22:00; after the flame is stolen, she sweeps this cold altar every morning. See [[阿斯特拉]].
- **The first hearth**: “A stone hearth, cold for centuries. Soot in the cracks: the First Fire was kindled here from the dawn, and carried down the hill.” On each side of the hearth stands an unlit set of [[烛台|candles]].

- **The altar of the five wounds**: “Astra on the holy wood, carved in white stone: a crown of thorns, the scourge's marks, the ribs of the fast, the nails, the lance in the side. Below: WHO BEARS, IS NEAR TO GOD.”

{{剧透|The three deep in the barrow and in the Abbey of the Thorn}}
- **The roll of the Order**: “THE ORDER OF THE THORN. Forty-one names, the oldest worn smooth. The last, cut deeper than the rest: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER, WHO KNELT TO CHILDREN. TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN. HE HOLDS.”
- **The Commander's vow**: “Cut into the altar, in a knight's plain hand: "I WILL HOLD UNTIL I AM RELIEVED. — ALBERIC." Below it, newer, pressed into the soot with a thumb: a mark of ash.”
- **The statue in the thorn yard**: “A young knight on one knee before a child, holding out a wooden horse he has mended. The face is worn smooth by hands. The plinth: ALBERIC, ON THE DAY HE WAS MADE A KNIGHT.”
{{/剧透}}

### Putting things on it

An altar is a surface: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is set on the altar (“You set X on the altar.”). Nothing else happens when you do.

### Unbreakable, and it does not burn

An altar is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
