A **chair** is a wooden chair. It does not block the way; people can walk right over it. There is no “sitting down” in the game: the chair has no rules of its own. At present no map in the game has a chair on it, and you cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

In the rules there is little a chair can do:

- **{{键|G}}**: hold something and press {{键|G}} on it, and the thing is put down on the chair's tile (“You set X down by the chair.”). A chair is not a surface.
- **Hitting**: the chair has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
- **Fire**: the chair is wooden, catches fire and burns down to one {{物品|灰烬}}.

Press {{键|E}} on it empty-handed and the label shows “?”: this is something the world has not seen. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see [[世界会学]]).
