The **plot for a workshop** is a staked patch of open ground west of the sword in [[誓守之地]], where the “Workshop” of [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] is to stand. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, it becomes a [[工作台|workbench]] a little later.

## Source

{{来源}}

- The Held Ground has it from the start, west of the sword. Cannot be made, nor taken away.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find the “Workshop” on the “Buildings” page. No requirement (it can be built from the start); the materials are 8 {{物品|木头}} and 4 {{物品|石块}}, given to him for the Held Ground's stores. When the stores are enough, press “Build!”.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “A workbench: what is made at one is made here”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “A bench, and room to work at it. Make what you need; I will not ask what it is for.” Every so often the plot checks whether it has been built; once it has, it becomes a [[工作台|workbench]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: everything that needs a workbench can be made beside it (see [[制作]]).
- The plot does not block the way; you can walk over it. Press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- The stakes can catch fire. A plot that burns is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[地基]].
