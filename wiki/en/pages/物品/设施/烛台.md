**Candles** are a candlestand set with candles. One burns north of the entrance to [[The Mill Cellar|the Mill Cellar]]; in [[Chapel of the First Dawn|the Chapel of the First Dawn]] there is one on each side of the hearth below the north wall, unlit. Lit candles, like any other fire, keep monsters from coming close.

## Source

{{sources}}

- 1 in the Mill Cellar (lit), 2 in the Chapel of the First Dawn (unlit).
- You cannot make them.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Lighting and putting out

- **Lighting**: holding a {{item|Torch}}, left-click them, or hit them with the fire element. Once you learn {{skill|Firemaker}}, right-clicking them with an ordinary stick lights them too (“You work the stick until the candles catch.”). If they are wet, the first touch of fire only dries them.
- **Putting out**: holding a container full of water, left-click them.
- Once lit they stay lit; the candles never burn down.

### Monsters keep away

Except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit, nor chase someone standing there. The slimes and spiders in the Mill Cellar behave this way too. At night, people and creatures within 3 tiles of a lit fire see as far as by day.

### Warming yourself

Press {{key|E}} empty-handed on lit candles (“Warm yourself at the candles”): if you are {{status|wet}}, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself at the candles; the damp steams off you.”); if not, just “Warmth from the candles seeps into your hands.”, with no health restored. The rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} empty-handed on unlit candles (such as the two in the Chapel of the First Dawn; the prompt shows “?”). See [[campfire#Warming up|Warming yourself]].

### Throwing things in

Hold something that burns and press {{key|G}} on lit candles, and the thing goes into the fire and burns to {{item|Ash}}. Candles are not a cooking fixture and cannot cook anything: holding raw food and pressing {{key|G}} on them only puts the food down on their tile, still raw; the rules do not say what happens when you right-click them holding raw food. For cooking, see [[Cooking]].

### Unbreakable, and they do not burn

Candles have no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit them; the candlestand itself does not catch fire.

Do something else to them and the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
