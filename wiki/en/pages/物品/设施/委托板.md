The **commission board** is a wooden board with commissions pinned to it. Press {{key|E}} from within 2 tiles (“Look at the commission board”) to open the commission board panel: every [[Quests#Commissions|commission]] in the village that no one has taken yet is listed there, and clicking “Accept” takes it, with no need to go and find the person who asked. There is only one in the game, on the square of [[Firstlight Village]], beside the [[notice board|notice board]].

## Source

{{sources}}

It stands on the north side of Firstlight Village's square, one tile left of the notice board by the well. Cannot be made.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Looking at commissions**: press {{key|E}} from within 2 tiles to open the commission board panel, titled “Commission Board”. Each commission has a card: the quest name, a description (who asked, and what needs doing), and an “Accept” button in the top right corner. With no commissions it says “Nobody wants anything done just now. Come back later.”
- **Accepting**: click “Accept”, and the bottom left says “Quest accepted: X”, and the quest appears in the quest log ({{key|L}}). From then on it is the same as one accepted in person: handing in, time limits and failure are unchanged, and you hand it in to the person who asked.
- The board lists the commissions the village gives rise to: [[A Bite to Eat]], [[What Was Taken]], [[A Letter to Carry]], [[The Night-born]]. Wherever the person who asked may be, any that no one has taken are listed here. The side quests people give you in person, and [[Wood for the Fire]], are not on the board; for those, find the people themselves.
- You cannot refuse a commission at the board. Leave one you do not want alone, and it is withdrawn by itself once its time limit passes.
- Press {{key|G}} with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- **Breaking, fire**: like the [[sign|sign]] it is made of wood, has durability 4, and can catch fire. See [[Durability and Breaking]].
