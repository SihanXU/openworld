The **plot for a storehouse** is a staked patch of open ground south of the sword in [[誓守之地]], where the “Storehouse” of [[领地扩建|Expanding the Held Ground]] is to stand. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, it becomes a [[储物棚|storehouse]] a little later, open when built.

## Source

{{来源}}

- The Held Ground has it from the start, south of the sword. Cannot be made, nor taken away.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Building it**: talk to [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], choose “The Held Ground…”, and find the “Storehouse” on the “Buildings” page. It requires Land level I (The Grove); the materials are 12 {{物品|木板}} and 6 {{物品|木头}}, given to him for the Held Ground's stores. When the stores are enough, press “Build!”.
- The card says what you get once it is built: “A storehouse of forty places for what you keep”.
- Once you press it, Alberic says: “Walls and a roof for what you keep. Nothing in it will be taken; I sleep lightly.” Every so often the plot checks whether it has been built; once it has, it becomes a [[储物棚|storehouse]] with a flash of light.
- What it becomes: something that holds 40 things.
- The plot does not block the way; you can walk over it. Press {{键|G}} on it with something in hand to put the thing on its tile.
- The stakes can catch fire. A plot that burns is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See [[地基]].
