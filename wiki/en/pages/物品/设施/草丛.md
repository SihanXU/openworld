A **grass tuft** is a small tuft of tall grass in a meadow; every grassy map has some. It is only scenery: it does not block the way and cannot be taken. The grass you can pick up from the ground and use for making things is {{item|Cut Grass}}, a different thing, which grows back on the grass by itself (see [[Foraging]]).

## Source

{{sources}}

- On the grass of [[The Ford|the Ford]], [[Firstlight Village]], [[The Grassland|the Grassland]] (the most), [[The Forest|the Forest]] and [[Emberfall]].
- The vegetable garden in [[Firstlight Village]] has no grass tufts, leaving room for sowing.
- It cannot be planted, and it does not grow back by itself.

## Uses

{{uses}}

It is one of the small things on the ground, and the rules let you do little with it:

- **Does not block the way**: people and creatures can walk over it.
- **{{key|G}}**: holding something, press {{key|G}} at it and the thing is put on its tile.
- **Hitting**: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it with a weapon or tool is not written in the rules.
- **Not Cut Grass**: a grass tuft cannot be cut, and it does not turn into Cut Grass.
- **Fire**: it catches fire. After burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes one {{item|Ash}}; while burning, every half second it has a 10% chance to spread the fire to flammable things within one tile of it.

What happens if you press {{key|E}} with empty hands to handle it is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
