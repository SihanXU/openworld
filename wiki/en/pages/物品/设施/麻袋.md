{{信息框}}
A **sack** is a cloth bag. It is a container and holds 4 things. It is soft: lying on the ground it does not block the way, and people can walk over it. At present no map in the game has a sack, and you cannot make one.

## Source

{{来源}}

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Storing and taking**: {{键|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{键|G}} puts what you hold into it, up to 4 things (see [[箱子与容器]]).
- **Rooms**: a [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).
- **Breaking**: durability 3, made of cloth (hardness 0); a fist hits it differently from wood, 2 points a punch. Torn open, it leaves 1 {{物品|布匹}} with a one-in-two chance, and what is inside drops out (see [[耐久与破坏]]).
- **Fire**: cloth catches fire; when burnt out it becomes one {{物品|灰烬}}, and what is inside drops where it lay.
