The **Shrine of the Lance** is the fifth and easternmost [[wayside shrine|wayside shrine]] on [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]]. What it wants is a light — a torch or a candle: the dawn.

## Source

{{sources}}

On [[The Pilgrim's Road|the Pilgrim's Road]], the fifth, easternmost. You cannot build one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Reading**: press {{key|E}} to read: “THE FIFTH SUFFERING: THE LANCE. When it pierced his heart, the sun rose. — Lay down a light, and the dawn will remember you.”
- **Kindling it**: hold a light such as a torch or a candle and press {{key|G}} to lay it on the shrine; the shrine takes it and lights up. The road itself provides one: a candle (on the south side of the road's eastern stretch).
- It does not take the wrong thing, and says: “Nothing kindles. The Shrine of the Lance asks for a light — a torch or a candle: the dawn.” It does not take holy relics or key items either (see [[wayside shrine]]).
- Kindling it completes the objective “Kindle the Shrine of the Lance” of [[The First Seal|The First Seal]].

## History

- 2026-10-12: Added with the Pilgrim's Road.
- 2026-10-13: No longer takes holy relics or key items.
