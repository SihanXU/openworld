A **bench** is a wooden bench. It does not block the way, and people can walk over it; holding something, press {{key|G}} to put it on the seat. The only ones in the game are the two along either side of the hall of the [[Chapel of the First Dawn|Chapel of the First Dawn]].

The bench made of a whole log in villages and camps is the [[log bench|log bench]], a different thing.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Two in the Chapel of the First Dawn. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Putting things on it**: the bench is a surface. Holding something, press {{key|G}} at it and the thing is put on the seat (“You put X on the bench.”).
- **{{key|E}}**: first picks up what is on the seat; the bench itself cannot be taken.
- **Hitting**: the bench has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
- **Fire**: it is wooden, catches fire, and when burnt out becomes one {{item|Ash}}.

The game has no “sitting down”. For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see [[The World Learns]]).
