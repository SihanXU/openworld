The **ferry winch** is the winch that hauls the ferry rope on the landing at [[The Ford|the Ford]], built by Aldo, the husband of the ferrywoman {{person|Hesper}}. It starts out broken: the storm in the night tore the ferry rope apart. Splice it with a {{item|Rope}} and Hesper can pole the [[ferry raft|ferry raft]] to carry you across the river; this is one of the ways to cross at the Ford.

## Source

{{sources}}

- Only this one, on the Ford's landing. It starts out {{status|broken}}.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Mending it

1. **Look**: press {{key|E}} empty-handed (“Look at the ferry winch”): “The ferry rope snapped at the drum; the frayed end trails in the water. A good length of rope would mend it.”
2. **Find a rope**: the [[chest|chest]] beside the camp's tent has a rope in it (Hesper: “The rope is in my chest. The winch is on the landing. The rest is hands.”). That rope belongs to no one; take it and nobody says a word.
3. **Splice it**: stand on the landing with the rope in hand and press {{key|G}} on the winch (“Mend the ferry winch with the rope”): “You splice the rope onto the drum. The line runs taut across the water again.” The rope is used up, and the winch is no longer broken.
4. **Cross**: talk to Hesper ({{key|E}}, “Ask Hesper for the crossing”), and she poles you across on the raft.

- Giving the rope to Hesper herself does nothing: “Not to me. To the winch.”
- Once it is mended, the people of the Ford talk about it for a few days.
- As long as the winch is broken, there is always a rope at the Ford: if the one in the chest burns, Hesper strips an old one off the raft for you.

### Other

- Once it is mended, the rules do not say what happens when you press {{key|E}} on it empty-handed (the label shows “?”).
- The winch has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it cannot be taken.
- It is wooden and catches fire, burning down to one {{item|Ash}}.
