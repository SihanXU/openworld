A **chest** is a wooden chest that holds 12 things. Press {{key|E}} to open it and move things between the chest panel and your bag (see [[Chests and Containers]]). There is one in the camp at [[The Ford|the Ford]] and one on [[The Held Ground|the Held Ground]], and you can make your own; in [[Building|build]] mode it is called the “Wooden Chest”.

## Source

{{sources}}

- **Build mode**: press {{key|B}} and choose “Wooden Chest”: 4 {{item|Plank}}, with a hammer on you (the {{item|Smith Hammer}}, not used up).
- **By hand**: put one plank on another plank on the ground to make a [[wooden frame|wooden frame]]; keep adding planks to the frame until it has 4, then knock it once with a hammer: “You nail together a chest.” (see [[Building#Building by hand|building by hand]])
- **The Ford's camp**: the chest beside the tent belongs to {{person|Hesper}} and holds only 8 things. In it are a {{item|Rope}} and a {{item|Bread}}. The rope belongs to no one, and mending the [[ferry winch|ferry winch]] depends on it; the bread is Hesper's.
- **The Held Ground**: “the Held Ground's chest” beside [[Alberic|Alberic]]'s tent, open from the start and empty.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Storing and taking

- **{{key|E}}**: open or close it (“Open the chest”, “Close the chest”). When it opens, the chest panel appears; closing the panel closes the chest too.
- **{{key|G}}**: while the chest is open, put the thing in your hand into it (“You put X in the chest.”); when it is full, “It is full.”; when it is closed, “It is closed.”
- Take things from someone else's chest in front of its owner and the owner says “Hey — that is mine!” (see [[Chests and Containers#Other people's containers|other people's containers]]).
- When a thing in a chest has no owner written on it, it counts as the chest owner's. So if you put something in Hesper's chest and then take it out in front of her, she says it is hers too.

### Locks

In the rules, holding a {{item|Key}} and right-clicking a closed chest locks it (“Click. It is locked.”); right-clicking a locked chest holding the key unlocks and opens it. Pressing {{key|E}} on a locked chest only gives “Locked.”; an axe cannot take it apart at one blow, and it can only be broken blow by blow like anything else. At present no map has a locked chest, and no key is placed anywhere.

### Rooms

A [[Rooms|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom. For now a storeroom is only a mark on the room, with no effect.

### Taking it apart and breaking it

- **Axe, pickaxe, hammer**: left-click an unlocked chest and what is inside spills out first, then the whole chest comes loose and lies where it stood (“Knock the chest loose”); walk close and it is drawn into your bag by itself, or press {{key|E}} to pick it up. Holding it, right-click open ground to build it again. A locked one only loses durability.
- **Hitting it with anything else** (weapons such as swords, a fist): durability 8, made of wood. At 0 it falls apart into 2 planks (half of its makeup), and what is inside drops out. A bare-handed punch takes off only 1 point of durability.
- See [[Durability and Breaking]].

### Fire

The chest is wooden and catches fire (see [[Elements#Fire|Fire]]). After burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes one {{item|Ash}}, and what is inside drops where it stood. While burning, every half second it has a 50% chance to lose 1 point of durability; if its durability runs out first, it breaks into planks as if broken.
