A **clay pot** is an earthenware jar. It is a container and holds only 2 things. It is the most fragile container: durability 1, and clay is soft, so anything can break it, and it leaves no shards. At present no map in the game has a clay pot.

## Source

{{来源}}

- You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{用途}}

### Storing and taking

As with other containers (see [[箱子与容器]]): {{键|E}} opens or closes it; while it is open, {{键|G}} puts things in, up to 2. A [[房间|room]] with 3 or more containers in it is a storeroom (no effect at present).

### Breaking

- **Breaking**: durability 1, clay of hardness 0. A fist, weapon or tool breaks it in one blow. The clay pot has no makeup written for it, so when it breaks only what it held drops out, and no material is left.
- **Earth element**: the clay pot is fragile; struck by the earth element it breaks at once, and what is inside drops out (see [[元素#土|Earth]]).
- The clay pot does not catch fire.
