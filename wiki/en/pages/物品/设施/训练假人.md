The **training dummy** is a target of straw bound to a wooden post, east of the tutorial hut in [[The Clearing|the Clearing]]. Lesson seven of the [[Tutorial|tutorial]] has you knock it down once with the left click. Once knocked down it stands itself back up with its durability full again, so you can hit it as often as you like.

## Source

{{sources}}

- There is only one, in the Clearing of the tutorial, beside the hut the tutorial has you build. You cannot make one.

## Uses

{{uses}}

### Practice

- Tutorial lesson seven: “There's a dummy by the house. Left click it until it falls over.”
- Durability 12, wood (hardness 1). A bare-handed punch takes 1 point; a stone axe takes 7 a blow, knocking it down in two.
- At 0 durability: “The training dummy topples, and rocks upright again.” Its durability is full again.
- Knocking the dummy down gives no experience: it is not a creature.

### Other

- The dummy is solid and blocks the way; it cannot be taken.
- It is made of wood and straw and catches fire: burning takes away its durability, and when burnt out it becomes one {{item|Ash}} and the dummy is gone. If the dummy is burnt in the tutorial, you can skip this lesson by holding {{key|Esc}} for 2 seconds.
- Holding something, press {{key|G}} and the thing is put on its tile.

## History

- 2026-09-30: The training dummy was in the first version.
- 2026-10-07: The tutorial was changed to one action per lesson, and a dummy was placed beside the hut in the Clearing: durability 12, standing itself up again after being knocked down.
