**Armour** is gear worn on the body that adds defense, filling five slots: head, body, hands, feet and off hand. For gear in the other five slots (earrings, necklaces, shoulders, belts and rings) see [[Accessories]]. The equipment slots number eleven in all; see [[Equipment]].

## How defense is counted

- A piece of armour's defense = the form's defense × the [[Substances|material]]'s multiplier × the [[Quality|quality]]'s multiplier. For example, among helmets an iron one is harder than a leather one, and a Fine one harder than a Common one.
- The defense of everything you wear is added together. When you are attacked, the damage is reduced by your defense, but at least 1 point always remains.
- Clothing (hats, boots, gloves) has no defense of its own, except the named pieces.
- Armour does not wear out, there is no repairing, and there are no set bonuses.

## Putting it on

- Hold it in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself, and you put it on.
- You can also click the figure's equipment slots in the pack ({{key|I}}), or right-click an item and choose “Equip”.
- Only one piece can be worn in each slot.
- Gear exclusive to a profession works only for that profession: only a [[Warrior|Warrior]] can wear a [[Shield|shield]] in the off hand.

## Named armour in the game

Most of the armour in Chapter 1 is a piece with a name of its own, placed in a particular spot or given by a quest.

| Slot | Name | Quality | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head | Rusted Helm of the Thorn | Crude | [[The Old Barrow|The Old Barrow]], beside Lina's cell |
| Head | Fennick's Squire Helm | [[Vowbound]] | Quest [[The Old Templar]] |
| Head | [[Crown of Thorns]] | [[Stigmata|Stigmatic]] | Got after defeating [[The White Knight Alberic]] |
| Body | Rowan's Old Hauberk | Fine | Quest [[Lost in the Barrow]] |
| Hands | Hunter's Gloves | Common | [[Mountain Foot]], the hunter's old cabin |
| Hands | Edda's Work Gloves | Fine | Quest [[Edda's Ring]] |
| Hands | Gauntlets of the White Host | Masterwork | [[The East Road|The East Road]], inside the knight's kingdom |
| Feet | Traveller's Boots | Common | Quest [[Down from the Mountain]] |
| Feet | Thornproof Boots | Fine | [[Thornwood Hollow]], where the briars are thickest |
| Off hand | Wooden Shield | Common | Got the first time you choose the Warrior profession; can also be made at a [[workbench|workbench]] from 3 planks and 1 stick |
| Off hand | The Unfallen Wall | Stigmatic | The Warrior's [[Legendary Arms|Legendary Arms]] |

Fennick's Squire Helm carries the vow trait “+10% dodge”. Wearing the Crown of Thorns slows your experience; see its page.
