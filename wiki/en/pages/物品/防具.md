**Armour** is gear worn on the body that adds defense, filling five slots: head, body, hands, feet and off hand. For gear in the other five slots (earrings, necklaces, shoulders, belts and rings) see [[饰品]]. The equipment slots number eleven in all; see [[装备]].

## How defense is counted

- A piece of armour's defense = the form's defense × the [[材质|material]]'s multiplier × the [[品质|quality]]'s multiplier. For example, among helmets an iron one is harder than a leather one, and a Fine one harder than a Common one.
- The defense of everything you wear is added together. When you are attacked, the damage is reduced by your defense, but at least 1 point always remains.
- Clothing (hats, boots, gloves) has no defense of its own, except the named pieces.
- Armour does not wear out, there is no repairing, and there are no set bonuses.

## Putting it on

- Hold it in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself, and you put it on.
- You can also click the figure's equipment slots in the pack ({{键|I}}), or right-click an item and choose “Equip”.
- Only one piece can be worn in each slot.
- Gear exclusive to a profession works only for that profession: only a [[战士|Warrior]] can wear a [[盾牌|shield]] in the off hand.
