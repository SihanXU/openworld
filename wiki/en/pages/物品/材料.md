**Materials** are items for crafting, building and handing in for quests. The five most used are the [[Stick|Stick]], [[Stone|Stone]], [[Cut Grass|Cut Grass]], [[Log|Log]] and [[Plank|Plank]]: the first axe, the campfire, walls and beds all begin with them.

Materials are a category of items; what a thing is made of (wood, stone, iron…) is its “substance”, see [[Substances|Substances]].

## Common materials

| Material | Where it comes from | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| [[Stick]] | Keeps growing on the ground; a felled tree drops 1 | Stone tools, torches, campfires, fences, signs, the Wooden Shield, the Wooden Bow |
| [[Stone]] | Keeps growing on the ground; break rock (1) or a boulder (4) with a pickaxe | Stone tools, campfires, [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] |
| [[Cut Grass]] | Keeps growing on the ground (grass that has been cut) | Stone tools, torches, beds, buckets, the Wooden Bow |
| [[Log]] | A tree felled with an axe drops 2 (1 more with Woodcutter learned) | Split into planks; clubs; Gus's quest [[Wood for the Fire]]; [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] |
| [[Plank]] | Click a log on the ground with an axe, or make them in the crafting panel carrying an axe: 1 log gives 2 planks | Walls, floors, doors, stairs, beds, workbenches, buckets, shields, chests; mending the Ford's bridge |
| [[Iron Ore]] | Break the iron veins of [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]] with a pickaxe (2 each); dropped when the [[cyclops|cyclops]] or a Seal-bearer falls | Garrick forging Dragonsbane; Land II of [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]]; smelted into Iron Ingots in a lit forge |
| [[Iron Ingot]] | Right-click a lit [[forge|forge]] with iron ore, one smelted for one; or smelt them in batches beside the forge by a [[Smithing|smithing]] recipe | Forged at the anvil into weapons, armour, tools and jewelry; sold to merchants |
| [[Gold Ingot]] | Right-click a lit forge with gold ore, one smelted for one; once you learn Goldsmith, also by recipe | Forged into rings and amulets; sold to merchants (worth 75) |
| [[Steel Bar]] | Once you learn Steelworking, 3 iron ore and 3 coal make 2 beside the forge | Forged at the anvil into steel weapons, armour and tools; sold to merchants |
| [[Coal]] | Break the coal seams of the Old Mine with a pickaxe (2 each); left when charred trees and burnt walls are broken | Garrick forging Dragonsbane |
| [[Herb]] | The Dawnbloom on [[Dawn Hill]], which slowly grows back; grow your own (seeds bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]]) | Eaten (restores a little health); Ilse's quest [[The Vigil]]; sold to the trading stall |
| [[Fireweed]] | On the grass of [[Firstlight Village]], slowly growing back | Wren brewing the Fireproof Draught |
| [[Frostcap]] | In [[The Forest|the Forest]], slowly growing back | Wren brewing the Fireproof Draught |
| [[Bone]] | Two lie on the ground at [[The Ford|the Ford]] | — |
| [[Ash]] | Left when something that burns has burned out | As fertilizer: a fertilized crop reaped with a sickle gives 1 more |
| [[Fertilizer]] | Bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]] | Fertilizes like ash |
| [[Wheat]] | Grow your own (Wheat Seeds bought at the trading stall) | Feeding animals; sold to the trading stall |
| [[Animal Feed]] | Bought at the trading stall | Feeding animals: a fed cow can be milked, and two fed animals of a kind have young |

Sticks, stones and cut grass on the ground grow back one at a time every so often ({{value|地面产出间隔}} seconds), until a map holds a certain number of them.

## Materials from broken things

When something is broken, it leaves half the materials it is made of ({{value|回收比例}}): a tree is 4 logs and 2 sticks, and felled it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; a wooden wall is 2 planks, and broken it leaves 1. A thing you made yourself also gives back only half its materials when broken; knocking a wall, a floor or another structure loose with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer gets it back whole. See [[Durability and Breaking|Durability and Breaking]].

## Materials with no source

Forms such as the [[Bolt of Cloth|Bolt of Cloth]], [[Leather Hide|Leather Hide]], [[Wool|Wool]], [[Wax|Wax]] and [[Salt|Salt]] have no fixed source on the maps at present.

- [[Gold Ore|Gold Ore]] is dug from the gold veins deep in [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]]; the third level of [[Expanding the Held Ground|expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3; it can also be smelted into Gold Ingots in the forge.
- [[Wheat|Wheat]] has to be grown: Wheat Seeds are bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] on [[The Held Ground]].
- A [[Feather|Feather]] is left only by a felled [[chick|chick]], with a one-in-two chance.
- [[Fertilizer|Fertilizer]] and [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] are bought at the trading stall; see [[Farming#Fertilizing|fertilizing]] and [[Animals#Feeding|feeding]].
