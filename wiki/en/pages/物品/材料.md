**Materials** are items for crafting, building and handing in for quests. The five most used are the [[树枝|Stick]], [[石块|Stone]], [[干草|Cut Grass]], [[木头|Log]] and [[木板|Plank]]: the first axe, the campfire, walls and beds all begin with them.

Materials are a category of items; what a thing is made of (wood, stone, iron…) is its “substance”, see [[材质|Substances]].

## Common materials

Sticks, stones and cut grass on the ground grow back one at a time every so often ({{数值|地面产出间隔}} seconds), until a map holds a certain number of them.

## Materials from broken things

When something is broken, it leaves half the materials it is made of ({{数值|回收比例}}): a tree is 4 logs and 2 sticks, and felled it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; a wooden wall is 2 planks, and broken it leaves 1. A thing you made yourself also gives back only half its materials when broken; knocking a wall, a floor or another structure loose with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer gets it back whole. See [[耐久与破坏|Durability and Breaking]].

## Materials with no source

Forms such as the [[布匹|Bolt of Cloth]], [[皮革|Leather Hide]], [[羊毛|Wool]], [[蜂蜡|Wax]] and [[盐|Salt]] have no fixed source on the maps at present.

- [[金矿石|Gold Ore]] is dug from the gold veins deep in [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]]; the third level of [[领地扩建|expanding the Held Ground]] needs 3; it can also be smelted into Gold Ingots in the forge.
- [[麦子|Wheat]] has to be grown: Wheat Seeds are bought at the [[货摊|trading stall]] on [[誓守之地]].
- A [[羽毛|Feather]] is left only by a felled [[小鸡|chick]], with a one-in-two chance.
- [[肥料|Fertilizer]] and [[饲料|Animal Feed]] are bought at the trading stall; see [[农耕#施肥|fertilizing]] and [[动物#喂食|feeding]].
