The **Warm Ember** is a stone that grows warm at night. [[佩尔|Pell]] says the Grey Man gave him one, and since he has had it the things of the night stay away from his window. Find all four of Pell's chalk drawings and he gives you his other one.

## Source

{{来源}}

The reward of the side quest [[佩尔的涂鸦]]:

> You see? He's there every time — always the night before something bad happens. He doesn't do the bad things. He just gets there first. Here, have my other warm stone.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- While you hold it, a little light shines on the player. This is only a visual effect: it does not count as something lit, and wild things do not keep away because of it.
- It is not a fire source and cannot light anything.
- The game defines no other use for it. What you do with it is answered by [[世界会学|the world learns]]; with no model connected, nothing happens. It is left to the world this way by design.

## Lore

Pell says:

> I've seen the grey man. Three times! Nobody believes me. He gave me a stone that gets warm at night.
> Ever since I had that stone, the night things don't come near my window. My aunt says that's nonsense.

{{剧透|What this stone is}}

It is an ember left by a fire taken from the dawn. The things of the night really do stay away from Pell's window because of it. The villagers think it something sinister a stranger gave a child (docs/chapter-1.md §5). The Grey Man who gave it comes from [[灰烬守望|the Ashen Watch]], the order that keeps the fire taken from the first light of dawn.
