**Tools** are items for changing the world: felling trees, cutting stone, tilling soil, reaping, finishing what you build, carrying and pouring water. Holding a tool, left-click the target to use it. Like weapons, tools have “power”, and their efficiency differs from one material to another.

## What each tool can do

The tooltip says in blue what a tool can do.

| Tool | What it can do | See |
|---|---|---|
| [[斧]] | Fells trees and splits wood: chop a tree down for a [[木头|Log]], and click a log on the ground with the axe to split it into two [[木板|planks]]. Carrying an axe, you can also split logs into planks in the crafting panel. | [[伐木|Woodcutting]] |
| [[镐]] | Breaks rock, boulders and ore veins, for [[石块|Stone]], [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]] and [[煤炭|Coal]]. | [[采矿|Mining]] |
| [[锄头]] | Turns grass, dirt, sand or snow into farmland. | [[农耕|Farming]] |
| [[镰刀]] | Reaps ripe crops, for 2 of the harvest and 2 seeds. | [[农耕|Farming]] |
| [[锻锤]] | Finishes building: the last step of the crate, Wooden Chest, fence and sign of build mode is a blow of the hammer. It can also take apart a frame not yet finished. | [[建造|Building]] |
| [[铲子]] | Digs up stumps and crops (a crop dug up goes back to seeds). Bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. | [[农耕|Farming]] |
| [[木桶]] | An empty bucket fills with water at a river or a well, becoming a “Water Bucket”; a Water Bucket waters crops and puts out burning things. An empty bucket can also milk a fed cow. | [[农耕|Farming]], [[动物|Animals]] |
| [[绳索]] | Mends the broken ferry rope at the Ford. | [[渡口]] |
| [[鱼竿]] | Fishes at the water's edge, casting up to 3 cells out; baited, its chance of a bite doubles. Made by hand from 2 sticks and 2 cut grass, or bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. | [[钓鱼|Fishing]] |

Left-click a built wall, door or floor, or a bed or workbench, or anything else that can be taken away, once with an axe, a pickaxe or a smith hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; then press {{键|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into the inventory by itself). See [[建筑件|Structures]].

The catalogue also holds forms such as the [[小刀|Knife]], [[火镰|Flint and Steel]], [[扫帚|Broom]] and [[开锁器|Lockpick]], which at present have no fixed source on the maps.

The bucket is not the only vessel for water: the [[空瓶|Empty Flask]] and the [[空水袋|Empty Waterskin]] can hold water too, and once full they can water things or be drunk (restoring a little health).

## Tool efficiency

The same blow works differently on different materials: an axe is most efficient on wood, and only a quarter as efficient on stone; a pickaxe is most efficient on stone and ore. A tool softer than the material does nothing to it, and says “Too hard.” A stone axe fells a tree in two blows.

## Tools used in crafting are not used up

Some recipes can be made only “carrying” a certain tool; splitting a log into planks, for instance, needs an axe carried. The tool just stays in the inventory and is not used up. In the crafting panel, a needed tool has a ✓ or ✗ beside it, with the tip “A tool it needs carried; not used up”.

## Where to get them

- **Made by hand** ({{键|C}}): the Stone Axe, Stone Pickaxe, Stone Hoe, Stone Sickle and Stone Smith Hammer (stone, sticks, cut grass), and the Bucket (3 planks and 1 cut grass). See [[制作|Crafting]].
- **On the maps**: the “Old Axe” by a stump at [[渡口|the Ford]], the “Old Pickaxe” at the mouth of [[旧矿坑|the Old Mine]]. The toolbox by the garden in [[初原村]] holds a Steel Hoe, a Bronze Sickle, a Steel Smith Hammer and a Water Bucket.
- **Quests**: [[乔斯]]'s quest [[第九根钉子（任务）|The Ninth Nail]] rewards a Smith Hammer.
