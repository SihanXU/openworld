**Tools** are items for changing the world: felling trees, cutting stone, tilling soil, reaping, finishing what you build, carrying and pouring water. Holding a tool, left-click the target to use it. Like weapons, tools have “power”, and their efficiency differs from one material to another.

## What each tool can do

The tooltip says in blue what a tool can do.

| Tool | What it can do | See |
|---|---|---|
| [[Axe]] | Fells trees and splits wood: chop a tree down for a [[Log|Log]], and click a log on the ground with the axe to split it into two [[Plank|planks]]. Carrying an axe, you can also split logs into planks in the crafting panel. | [[Woodcutting|Woodcutting]] |
| [[Pickaxe]] | Breaks rock, boulders and ore veins, for [[Stone|Stone]], [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and [[Coal|Coal]]. | [[Mining|Mining]] |
| [[Hoe]] | Turns grass, dirt, sand or snow into farmland. | [[Farming|Farming]] |
| [[Sickle]] | Reaps ripe crops, for 2 of the harvest and 2 seeds. | [[Farming|Farming]] |
| [[Smith Hammer]] | Finishes building: the last step of the crate, Wooden Chest, fence and sign of build mode is a blow of the hammer. It can also take apart a frame not yet finished. | [[Building|Building]] |
| [[Shovel]] | Digs up stumps and crops (a crop dug up goes back to seeds). Bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. | [[Farming|Farming]] |
| [[Bucket]] | An empty bucket fills with water at a river or a well, becoming a “Water Bucket”; a Water Bucket waters crops and puts out burning things. An empty bucket can also milk a fed cow. | [[Farming|Farming]], [[Animals|Animals]] |
| [[Rope]] | Mends the broken ferry rope at the Ford. | [[The Ford]] |
| [[Fishing Rod]] | Fishes at the water's edge, casting up to 3 cells out; baited, its chance of a bite doubles. Made by hand from 2 sticks and 2 cut grass, or bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. | [[Fishing|Fishing]] |

Left-click a built wall, door or floor, or a bed or workbench, or anything else that can be taken away, once with an axe, a pickaxe or a smith hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; then press {{key|E}} to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into the inventory by itself). See [[Structures|Structures]].

The catalogue also holds forms such as the [[Knife|Knife]], [[Flint and Steel|Flint and Steel]], [[Broom|Broom]] and [[Lockpick|Lockpick]], which at present have no fixed source on the maps.

The bucket is not the only vessel for water: the [[Empty Flask|Empty Flask]] and the [[Empty Waterskin|Empty Waterskin]] can hold water too, and once full they can water things or be drunk (restoring a little health).

## Tool efficiency

The same blow works differently on different materials: an axe is most efficient on wood, and only a quarter as efficient on stone; a pickaxe is most efficient on stone and ore. A tool softer than the material does nothing to it, and says “Too hard.” A stone axe fells a tree in two blows.

## Tools used in crafting are not used up

Some recipes can be made only “carrying” a certain tool; splitting a log into planks, for instance, needs an axe carried. The tool just stays in the inventory and is not used up. In the crafting panel, a needed tool has a ✓ or ✗ beside it, with the tip “A tool it needs carried; not used up”.

## Where to get them

- **Made by hand** ({{key|C}}): the Stone Axe, Stone Pickaxe, Stone Hoe, Stone Sickle and Stone Smith Hammer (stone, sticks, cut grass), and the Bucket (3 planks and 1 cut grass). See [[Crafting|Crafting]].
- **On the maps**: the “Old Axe” by a stump at [[The Ford|the Ford]], the “Old Pickaxe” at the mouth of [[The Old Mine|the Old Mine]]. The toolbox by the garden in [[Firstlight Village]] holds a Steel Hoe, a Bronze Sickle, a Steel Smith Hammer and a Water Bucket.
- **Quests**: [[Joss]]'s quest [[The Ninth Nail (quest)|The Ninth Nail]] rewards a Smith Hammer.
