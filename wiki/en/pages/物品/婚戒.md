The **Wedding Band** is the ring of the late husband of [[Edda]], of the East Road farmstead. She hung it on her gatepost, and when the knight in white rode by he took it along with everything that shone. It lies in the White Knight's kingdom on [[The East Road|the East Road]]. In the game this one is golden, and its name shows as “Golden Wedding Band”.

## Source

{{sources}}

There is only one in the world, lying in the ring of briars of the knight's kingdom on the East Road.

## Uses

{{uses}}

- **Quest**: give it to Edda to complete the side quest [[Edda's Ring]]. Edda keeps it.
- **Wearable**: it can be worn on a finger, but gives no bonus.

## Lore

Edda hides in the barn of the burned farmstead:

> The days were too short, and the wheat died in the field. Then the night things came. Then a knight in white rode through and took everything that shone: the hinges, the pans, my husband's ring off the gatepost.

When she asks the hero for help, she says: “If you're going up the road... my husband's ring. The knight took it with everything else. I don't care about the rest.” “A plain band. It's all I have of him.” When it is returned, she says:

> ...Thank you. I'll wear it now. I was keeping it on the gate so he could find his way home.
