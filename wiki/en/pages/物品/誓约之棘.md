**The Oath-Thorn** is a thorn still green in the briar where the White Knight fell. It is warm, and still hums very faintly. Hold it and left-click to use it on yourself, and you enter [[誓守之地]]; use it again inside the Held Ground and you return to where you stood before going in.

## Source

{{来源}}

You get it automatically when you defeat [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克|Alberic, the White Knight]]:

> (In the briar where the knight fell, one thorn is still green. It is warm, and humming very, very faintly, like a door not quite shut.)

[[露米|Lumi]] goes on: “It wants to be used. On you, I think — hold it and right-click yourself. I can hear a field on the other side of it. And… someone waiting.”

A save in which the White Knight was defeated before this thorn came into the game also gets it when loaded.

## Uses

{{用途}}

- **Entering and leaving the Held Ground**: hold it and left-click to use it on yourself; the key prompt reads “Enter / Leave the Held Ground”. The Held Ground lies on no cell of [[五印之地]], and it is the only way in.
- The Held Ground is the hero's camp: you can build, farm and keep livestock there. The first time you go in, [[阿尔贝里克|Alberic]], his crown taken off, is waiting there and becomes its steward. See [[扩建誓守之地 I|Expanding the Held Ground]].
- **Will not open in combat**: if in the last 5 seconds you struck or were struck, or an enemy is chasing you, it only says “Not now — still fighting.” You cannot bring pursuers into the Held Ground, nor use it to escape a fight.
- **Never leaves you**: press {{键|G}} on someone while holding it and it only says “This has to stay with me.” It is never handed to anyone else; putting it on the ground or in a chest works as usual.
- **Not dropped when you fall**: it counts as an important item. On Normal difficulty, when you fall, the other things on you are bundled as [[遗落的包裹|Lost Belongings]] and left on the spot, while the Oath-Thorn stays with you (see [[难度]]).
- The Oath-Thorn is of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality, but cannot be worn.

## Lore

The altar of [[守墓者圣所]] is engraved with the White Knight's vow: “I will hold until one comes to relieve me. — Alberic.” By defeating him the hero relieved him. In the Held Ground he keeps the vow turned the other way:

> I swore to hold until someone came to relieve me. You relieved me. So now I hold for you — this land, out to the mist. It's yours: build, sow, keep beasts, as you like.

He also says, from time to time: “The same vow, only turned around.” The boundary stones round the edge of the Held Ground are carved with a ring of thorns, “and no kneeling figure inside the ring”.
