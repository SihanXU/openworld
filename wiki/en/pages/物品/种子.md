**Seeds** are items that can be planted in farmland. Sow them and water them, and the crop grows stage by stage; once ripe, a harvest gives back seeds as well as the crop. For the rules of farming see [[Farming]]; for each crop see [[Crops]].

## Sowing

1. Turn grass, dirt, sand or snow into farmland with a [[Hoe|Hoe]].
2. Hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland to plant a “sprout”. The fourteen vegetables can also be chosen as “Sow X” on the “Farming” page of build mode ({{key|B}}).
3. Water it by left-clicking it with a container holding water. A watered crop grows one stage two game hours later: sprout → young plant → ripe. Each stage needs one watering.
4. Crops grow only in the open; those under a roof do not grow.
5. Once ripe, reap it with a [[Sickle|Sickle]] for 2 portions of crop and 2 of seed; a fertilized crop (with [[Ash|Ash]] or [[Fertilizer|Fertilizer]]) gives 1 more portion of crop. You can also press {{key|E}} to pick one portion of crop and one of seed by hand.
6. [[Tomato|Tomatoes]], [[Corn|corn]], [[Blueberries|blueberries]], [[Strawberry|strawberries]], [[Hot Pepper|hot peppers]], [[Eggplant|eggplants]] and berry bushes grow fruit again after a harvest, with no need to replant.

Crops do not wither, and there are no seasons.

## Where to get them

- **The vegetable garden in Firstlight Village**: the tool crate by the garden (“Garden Supplies”) holds 2 each of [[Carrot Seeds|Carrot Seeds]], [[Turnip Seeds|Turnip Seeds]], [[Tomato Seeds|Tomato Seeds]] and [[Pumpkin Seeds|Pumpkin Seeds]], plus a hoe, a sickle, a smith hammer and a bucket of water. The garden already grows these four crops at every stage.
- **The [[trading stall|trading stall]] at [[The Held Ground]]**: its “Farming” page sells the seeds of the fourteen vegetables, plus Wheat Seeds, Herb Seeds, Berry Seeds and acorns, for [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]. For prices see the crop table below and [[Money#What the stall sells|what the stall sells]]. Ten of the vegetables (parsnip, potato, radish, cauliflower, melon, corn, blueberries, strawberry, hot pepper, eggplant), along with Wheat Seeds, Herb Seeds and Berry Seeds, are found only here.
- **Harvesting**: every harvest gives seeds back.
- **[[Acorn|Acorns]]**: felling a tree has a 25% chance to drop one (after learning the skill “Woodcutter” it drops an extra log instead, and no acorn). An acorn need not go in farmland: plant it in any ground that can be dug, and it grows first into an oak sapling, then into a tree two game days later. The stall also sells acorns.

[[Wheat Seeds|Wheat Seeds]], [[Herb Seeds|Herb Seeds]] and [[Berry Seeds|Berry Seeds]] are not placed on any map and can only be bought at the stall.
