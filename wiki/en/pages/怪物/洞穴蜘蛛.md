The **cave spider** is a big red spider that runs very fast; it has little health but a nasty bite. There are some in [[荆棘谷]], the [[磨坊地窖]] and the [[旧矿坑]], and those in Thornwood Hollow and the Old Mine come back after you clear them.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **Thornwood Hollow**: 3 at the start. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up on the grass every 2 game hours.
- **The Mill Cellar**: 1. Once felled, it does not come back.
- **The Old Mine**: 3 at the start. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up in the tunnels every 2.5 game hours.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 4 to 5 tiles. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: fast.** About 0.4 seconds per tile, as fast as a [[狼|wolf]].
- **Element: none.** But its bite is venomous; see “Venom Fang” below.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Web Spit**: at 2–4 tiles away, it spits web at your feet, marking 3×3 tiles in purple, which land 0.8 seconds later: whoever is on those tiles is [[状态#怪物的技能|webbed]] for 2.5 seconds, each step taking 3 times as long as usual. While you are caught, it crawls over and bites. **The webs stay on the ground**: every marked tile except the one you stand on is left with a cobweb, and stepping into one catches you again; a single cut or a touch of fire breaks it, and it vanishes by itself after 12 seconds. The spider itself walks through cobwebs unhindered.
- **Venom Fang**: one in every 4 of its ordinary attacks leaves you [[状态#怪物的技能|poisoned]] for 3 seconds, losing 1 health per second, ignoring defense.

## Strategy
- If you cannot outrun it, stand and fight: it is an ordinary level 2 monster and goes down in five or six hits.
- Spiders felled in Thornwood Hollow count toward [[霍尔特]]'s [[清剿荆棘谷]].
- In the [[磨坊地窖]] it is with two [[绿史莱姆|green slimes]], guarding the lamp oil [[守夜之备]] needs.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
