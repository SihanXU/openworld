The **ice golem** is a golem of the ice element. It stands in the snow on the lower stretch of [[烬岭山道]], together with the [[霜狼|frost wolves]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **[[烬岭山道]]**: 1, in the snow on the lower stretch of the pass.

## Behavior
The following is its type data and how the rules treat it; its temper depends on where it is placed.
- **Speed: very slow.** About 0.9 seconds per tile.
- **Element: ice.** Its attacks carry ice: hitting someone who is {{状态|潮湿}} leaves them {{状态|冰冻}}.
- **Construct.** Lightning deals it only half damage and does not leave it {{状态|感电}}.
- **Ice cannot hurt it.** Ice-element hits instead restore 2 health to it.
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Ground Slam**: at 1–2 tiles away, it raises both fists and lights a red ring 2 tiles around itself, then slams down 1.6 seconds later: whoever is in the ring takes attack × 1.6 damage (carrying its own element) and is knocked back 1 tile. Its wind-up is the longest of all, a good moment to strike back: hit it while its fists are raised, or step out of the ring first.

## Strategy
- Use fire on it: it catches {{状态|燃烧}}. Do not use ice or lightning.
- While you are dry, its ice will not freeze you.
