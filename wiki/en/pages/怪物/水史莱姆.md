The **water slime** is a slime of the water element, with the most health of the ordinary slimes. It guards the sandspit at [[芦苇湾]], where Rolf's boat bell lies.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **[[芦苇湾]]**: 3 at the start, on the sandspit; when fewer than 2 remain, more come up on the sand.

## Behavior
The following is its type data and how the rules treat it; its temper depends on where it is placed.
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile.
- **Element: water.** Its attacks carry water: whoever it hits becomes {{状态|潮湿}}, and any fire on them is put out.
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Hop**: at 2–3 tiles away, it lights red marks on the 3×3 tiles around your feet, then 0.8 seconds later leaps over and lands beside you, crushing whoever is in the marked area for attack × 1.5 damage, carrying its own element. When you see the marks, step out of those 9 tiles.

## Strategy
- Once it has soaked you, lightning and ice are more dangerous to the player: wet, a lightning hit leaves you {{状态|感电}} and spreads to those around you, and an ice hit leaves you {{状态|冰冻}}.
- The other way round works just as well: wet it first, then hit it with lightning or ice. See [[元素]].
