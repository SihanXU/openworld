The **chick** is the young of the [[chicken|chicken]]. When two fed hens stand close together, one of them hatches a chick beside itself: “A chick hatches!” It uses the chicken's sprite, only smaller. A chick grows into a chicken after {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours, and only lays eggs once grown. See [[Animals]].

Chicks do not hatch from [[Egg|Eggs]]: an egg left lying never hatches; it can only be eaten, cooked or sold.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- Chicks are never placed directly on a map; they can only be hatched by chickens. Chickens on any map can hatch them: [[The Held Ground]], [[The Grassland]], [[Emberfall]], [[The Wild]], or chickens released from a [[Crated Hen|Crated Hen]].

## Behavior
- **How it hatches**: feed a chicken [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] or [[Wheat|Wheat]], and once fed it calls to the chickens near it. If a chicken within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down) that is already fed and has not hatched recently hears it, that chicken hatches a chick beside itself (if that tile has no room, on the nearest free tile). Both chickens are then {{status|bred}} and will not hatch again for {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours. As many such chickens as are nearby, that many chicks hatch.
- **Growing up**: {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours after it hatches, it becomes a [[chicken|chicken]], and from then on lays an egg every {{value|下蛋间隔}} game hours. Time still passes while the player is not on that map, and is made up when you return.
- **Before it grows up**: it does not lay eggs, does not hear the chickens' call and does not hatch chicks. It can be fed, and is happy when fed, but that does nothing else.
- **Temper: docile.** Like a chicken, it never attacks anyone; whoever hits it, it runs away from. It usually wanders near where it hatched. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: fast.** About 0.4 seconds per tile, faster than a chicken.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

## Strategy
- **Two hens are enough.** The Held Ground starts with no chickens; buy two [[Crated Hen|Crated Hens]] (40 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]] each) in the “Animal Husbandry” section of the [[trading stall|trading stall]], and put them together.
- **Feed one, then the other one next to it.** The two feedings must fall within {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours, and the two chickens must be no more than 3 steps apart when you feed the second. The one fed first hatches the chick. Chickens wander, so feed them while they are close together; you can also pen them together with a [[fence|fence]].
- **One portion of feed per animal.** One chick takes at least 2 portions of feed (5 portions for 6 Dawnmarks at the stall); wheat works too.
- **More chickens, more eggs.** Every grown chicken lays eggs on its own, fed or not; the trading stall buys eggs at 4 Dawnmarks each.

## Drops
{{drops}}

A chick is made of 1 [[Feather|Feather]] and leaves half of it when it falls: a 50% chance to leave 1 Feather, otherwise nothing. It does not leave [[Raw Chicken|Raw Chicken]]. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health, 2 points.
