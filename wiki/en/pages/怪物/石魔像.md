The **stone golem** is a moving statue of stone, of the earth element, with one of the highest defenses among the ordinary monsters. It guards the upper stretch of [[Cinder Pass]], not far from [[The Dragon's Lair]].

Cinder Pass belongs to the “Emberfall and the dragon” line after Chapter 1, which is placeholder content; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **Cinder Pass**: 1, near the top of the pass. Once felled, it does not come back.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 4 tiles, and goes back once 5 tiles from home. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: very slow.** About 0.9 seconds per tile.
- **Element: earth.** Its attacks carry earth: if the person it hits is {{status|frozen}}, the ice shatters and that hit does 1.5 times the damage.
- **Construct.** Lightning deals it only half damage and does not leave it {{status|electrified}}.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Ground Slam**: at 1–2 tiles away, it raises both fists and lights a red ring 2 tiles around itself, then slams down 1.6 seconds later: whoever is in the ring takes attack × 1.6 damage (carrying its own element) and is knocked back 1 tile. Its wind-up is the longest of all, a good moment to strike back: hit it while its fists are raised, or step out of the ring first.

## Strategy
- It has 3 defense: a low-attack weapon takes off very little health per hit, so come back with a better weapon.
- It is very slow and does not chase far: hit it and back off, wait for it to turn back, then go in again.
- Do not use lightning on it.
- Do not let yourself be frozen while fighting it.

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
