The **bat** is the fastest-moving monster in the game, with very little health. It lives in dark places: there are bats in [[古墓]], the [[荆棘骸骨堂]], the [[初晓礼拜堂]] and the [[旧矿坑]]. After nightfall it is also one of the two [[夜行生物|night creatures]] that come up in the wild.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Old Barrow**, **the Ossuary of the Thorn**, **the Chapel of the First Dawn**: 1 each. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **The Old Mine**: 2. When fewer than 2 remain, another appears in the tunnels every 3 game hours.
- **The wild at night**: on the outdoor maps of [[战斗区域|combat zones]], four in ten of the night creatures that come up after nightfall are bats. They vanish at daybreak. See [[夜行生物]].

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It swoops at anyone it sees within 4 to 5 tiles (8 tiles for those that appear at night). See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: fastest.** About 0.3 seconds per tile, as fast as the player walking slowly.
- **Flies.** Drops in height do not stop it, and it never falls.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Swoop**: in the same row or column, 2–3 tiles away, it dives along a red line 0.6 seconds later and bites the first enemy (attack × 1.2), draining back 1 health, then bounces back 2 tiles. It always swoops and leaves; rather than chase it, step aside when it comes at you next.

## Strategy
- If you cannot get away, fight: it has very little health.
- In dark places like the barrow and the mine, a [[火把|Torch]] in your hand does not keep it back; what does is something lit or burning, such as the lit braziers in the tunnels.
- At night in the wild, the safest places are beside a lit [[营火|campfire]] or shut indoors.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
