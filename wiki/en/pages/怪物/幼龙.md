The **dragon whelp** is the young of [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]], and carries the fire element. Whelps are placed on no map: once the dragon's health has fallen below half and it is hit again, it lets out a shriek and calls three whelps out of the dark. Wren has read about it: “The books say dragons call their young when they are hurt.”

The dragon belongs to the “Emberfall and the dragon” line after Chapter 1, which is placeholder content; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Dragon's Lair**: once the dragon's health is below half, the next hit it takes calls out 3, only once per fight.
- When the dragon dies, the whelps lose heart and, one after another, flee shrieking into the dark and vanish.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** As soon as they are called out they pounce on anyone within 6 tiles. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: fairly fast.** About 0.5 seconds per tile.
- **Element: fire.** Its attacks carry fire, and whoever it hits catches {{状态|燃烧}} (not when {{状态|潮湿}} or {{状态|防火}}).
- **Does not fear fire.** It carries fire itself, and fire does not keep it back.
- **Fire cannot hurt it.** Fire-element hits instead restore 2 health to it; water does it more damage.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Ember Breath**: at 1–3 tiles away, in the same row or column as you, it breathes a cone of fire at you (3 tiles deep), which burns 1 second later: whoever is in the cone takes attack × 1.2 fire damage and catches fire.

## Strategy
- A [[防火药剂|Fireproof Draught]] greatly reduces fire damage and keeps you from catching fire, against the whelps and the dragon alike.
- [[屠龙剑|Dragonsbane]] also deals double damage to whelps.
- You can also ignore the whelps and focus on the dragon: once the dragon dies, the whelps scatter.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health; whelps that flee give no experience.
