The **frost wolf** is a wolf of the ice element, sturdier than an ordinary [[wolf|wolf]] and with a harder bite. There are two in the snow on the lower stretch of [[Cinder Pass]], and more in the snowfields of [[The Wild]].

Cinder Pass belongs to the “Emberfall and the dragon” line after Chapter 1, which is placeholder content; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **Cinder Pass**: 2, on the lower stretch of the pass. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **The wild**: 1 on each snowfield map.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 5 tiles (4 tiles in the wild). See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: fast.** About 0.4 seconds per tile, as fast as a wolf.
- **Element: ice.** Its attacks carry ice: hitting someone who is {{status|wet}} leaves them {{status|frozen}}.
- **Ice cannot hurt it.** Ice-element hits instead restore 2 health to it.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire.
- **Flammable.** Like other beasts, when a fire starts nearby, the flames spread to it.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Lunge**: in the same row or column as you, 2–3 tiles away, it lights a red line between you, then 0.7 seconds later dashes along it and bites the first enemy it meets: damage is attack × 1.5, and the target is left [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|bleeding]] for 4 seconds. Step one tile aside and it misses.
- **Howl**: when its health is below 60% it howls (at most once a minute): it and every beast within 6 tiles become [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|frenzied]] for 8 seconds, moving faster with +1 attack.
- The frost wolf's Lunge carries ice: a bite on someone wet freezes them.

## Strategy
- Use fire on it: it fears fire, and it catches {{status|burning}} too. Halfway up the pass there is a lit [[campfire|campfire]] you can fall back to.
- Do not fight it with ice-element weapons.
- Do not let it bite you while you are wet.

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
