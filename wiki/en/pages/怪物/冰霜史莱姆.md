The **frost slime** is a slime of the ice element. There is one at [[渡口]]: by day it stays on the frosted patch at the north end of the riverbank, where the river's edge is frozen too; after dark it comes down to roam near the dock and attacks anyone it sees. There are frost slimes in the snowfields of [[荒野]] too.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Ford**: 1. Once felled, it does not come back. By day it is in the frosted corner at the north end of the riverbank; at night it moves to the dock.
- **The wild**: 1 on each snowfield map.

## Behavior
- **Temper:** the one at the Ford is **nocturnal**: it ignores people by day and attacks anyone it sees after nightfall (see [[昼夜]]). Those in the snowfields are **aggressive** and attack by day too. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile.
- **Element: ice.** Its attacks carry ice: hitting someone who is {{状态|潮湿}} leaves them {{状态|冰冻}}, unable to move for a while.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire, nor chase someone standing by a fire.
- **Ice cannot hurt it.** Ice-element hits instead restore 2 health to it.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Hop**: at 2–3 tiles away, it lights red marks on the 3×3 tiles around your feet, then 0.8 seconds later leaps over and lands beside you, crushing whoever is in the marked area for attack × 1.5 damage, carrying its own element. When you see the marks, step out of those 9 tiles.

## Strategy
- At night at the Ford you are safe by the camp's [[营火|campfire]], which is what [[赫斯珀]] tells you too: “Keep near the fire after dark.”
- On a rainy day or just after being in the water, you are wet, and a hit from it will freeze you. Dry off before fighting: with empty hands, press {{键|E}} beside a lit [[营火|campfire]] to warm up, and you are dry at once (see [[营火#取暖|warming up]]).
- A frozen body struck by a blunt weapon or the earth element shatters, taking more damage. This holds both for it and for the player. See [[元素]].
- Fire works best against it: it catches {{状态|燃烧}}.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
