The **giant crab** is a big crab with a hard shell on its back, guarding the riverbank south of [[The Reed Bend]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **[[The Reed Bend]]**: 1, on the riverbank to the south. It chases and attacks anyone it sees.

## Behavior
The following is its type data; temper: aggressive (the one at the Reed Bend).
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Hard shell.** It has 3 defense, taking 3 off every hit.
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Shell Up**: when its health is below half (at most once every 2.5 minutes), it pulls into its shell for 4 seconds: +4 defense, but it moves very slowly. While it is shelled, put some distance between you first, or knock on it with blunt weapons and elements.

## Strategy
Fight it with a high-attack weapon; otherwise every hit does only 1 damage.
