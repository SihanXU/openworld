The **storm wisp** is a floating wisp charged with lightning, drifting over the mountain path on the middle stretch of [[烬岭山道]]; when a thunderstorm breaks over the pass, a few more come up.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **[[烬岭山道]]**: 1 at the start, on the middle stretch of the path.
- **In a thunderstorm**: when a thunderstorm breaks over Cinder Pass, they come up on the snow and the mountain path, up to 2 at most (one try per minute).

## Behavior
The following is its type data and how the rules treat it; its temper depends on where it is placed.
- **Range 4 tiles.** When set up as a ranged type, it attacks once it and its target are in the same row or column, 4 tiles apart or less.
- **Speed: fast.** About 0.4 seconds per tile.
- **Floats.** Drops in height do not stop it, and it never falls.
- **Element: lightning.** Its attacks carry lightning, and whoever it hits is left {{状态|感电}}, briefly unable to move; if they are {{状态|潮湿}}, the current also jumps to other wet things within 2 tiles.
- **Charged.** The lightning element has no effect on it.
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Discharge**: at 2–4 tiles away, it marks a cross centered on your feet (2 tiles out above, below, left and right), and discharges 1 second later: whoever is on the cross takes attack × 1 lightning damage and is electrified; anyone wet passes the current to wet people beside them.

## Strategy
- Do not fight it while you are wet, nor in the rain.
- Lightning is useless against it. See [[元素]].
