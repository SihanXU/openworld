The **boar** is a thick-skinned beast with a little defense. There is one in the bushes on [[东大路]], and more in the deep woodland of [[荒野]]. Their tempers differ: the one on the East Road will not strike unless you provoke it, while those in the wild charge anyone they see.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The East Road**: 1, by the bushes south of the road. Once felled, it does not come back.
- **The wild**: 1 on each deep-woodland map.

## Behavior
- **Temper:** the one on the East Road is **wary**: only those who have hit it are chased and attacked. Those in the wild are **aggressive**: they charge anyone they see within 4 tiles. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: fairly fast.** About 0.5 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire.
- **Flammable.** Like other beasts, when a fire starts nearby, the flames spread to it.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Charge**: in the same row or column as you, 2–5 tiles away, it paws the ground for 1.2 seconds, with a red line marked out to 5 tiles, then lowers its head and charges: the first enemy it hits takes attack × 2 damage, is knocked back 2 tiles and is [[状态#怪物的技能|stunned]]. It cannot stop — if it runs headlong into a wall, tree or rock, it is the one stunned, for 2 seconds. Standing in front of something hard and waiting for it to charge is the easiest way to fight a boar.

## Strategy
- You can go round the one on the East Road.
- It has 1 defense: every hit does 1 less damage, but always at least 1. See [[战斗]].

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the [[生牛肉|meat]] and [[皮革|Leather Hide]] listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
The boar has no place in the story. Its sprite is borrowed from a reddish-brown tusked monster called “bear” in the asset pack: the pack has a separate boar sprite, but it has only two side-on frames, not four directions.
