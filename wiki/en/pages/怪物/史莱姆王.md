The **Slime King** is a huge slime, with far more health and attack than an ordinary slime. **It does not currently appear in the game**: no map, respawn rule or story event puts it into the world. It holds a slot on the monster page of the [[Codex|codex]], so for now the codex cannot be completed.

It carries the “boss” and “large” tags, but it is not one of the [[Bosses|bosses]]: it has no telegraphed skills and does not start a boss fight.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

It currently spawns nowhere.

## Behavior
The following is its type data; its temper depends on where it is placed.
- **Speed: very slow.** About 0.9 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

It is an elite among the minor monsters: it uses its moves by the minor monsters' rules (whenever their conditions are met), and it has two.
- **Royal Bounce**: at 2–4 tiles away, it lights red marks within two tiles of your feet (a 5×5 square without its four corners), and leaps over 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the marks takes attack × 1.5 damage. Its area is one ring wider than an ordinary slime's Hop.
- **Split**: when its health falls below half, 2 [[green slime|green slimes]] split off from it, only once per fight. Clear the small ones first, or strike fast before they close in.

## Strategy
It is far slower than the player: hit it and back off, and you avoid its counterattacks.
