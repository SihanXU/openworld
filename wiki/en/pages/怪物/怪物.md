The game's monsters belong to the “wild” faction and attack the “folk” faction (the player and all the residents); animals are counted among them too, but never attack. There are 25 kinds in all, plus 4 [[首领|bosses]]. This page lists them by kind, temper and place.

## Levels and roles
Every kind of monster has its own level (1–7, the same level as its [[专属掉落|trophy]] gear), and its health, attack and defense are all worked out from its level and its **role** along one curve: each level up adds 25% health, the same step by which gear grows stronger per level. So with gear of the same level as the monster, a level 7 monster takes about as long to fight as a level 1 one.

| Role | How it fights | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Swarm | Little health, down in three or four hits | [[绿史莱姆|Green slimes]] and the other slimes, [[蝙蝠|bats]] |
| Fighter | Five or six hits, with moves to dodge | [[狼|Wolves]], [[霜狼|frost wolves]], [[僵尸|zombies]], [[骷髅|skeletons]], [[洞穴蜘蛛|cave spiders]] |
| Caster | Hits as hard as a fighter, falls sooner | [[幽灵|Ghosts]], [[火焰小鬼|fire imps]], [[风暴鬼火|storm wisps]], [[幼龙|dragon whelps]] |
| Brute | Lots of health, high defense, a long fight | [[野猪|Boars]], [[石魔像|stone golems]], [[冰魔像|ice golems]], [[巨蟹|giant crabs]] |
| Elite | A fight of its own, three times a fighter's health | [[史莱姆王]], [[古墓守卫|the barrow warden]], [[独眼巨人|the cyclops]] |

The experience for felling one is still equal to its maximum health: the monsters are tougher, and every fight gives more experience too.

## Temper
The same kind of monster can have a different temper in different places (for example the [[野猪|boar]] and the [[冰霜史莱姆|frost slime]]). Temper is data carried by each monster. See [[阵营与脾性]].

| Temper | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Aggressive | Chases and attacks anyone it sees | Most monsters |
| Nocturnal | Ignores people by day, attacks anyone it sees after nightfall | The [[冰霜史莱姆|frost slime]] at [[渡口]] |
| Wary | Only chases and attacks whoever has hit it | The [[火焰史莱姆|fire slime]] at [[渡口]], the [[野猪|boar]] on [[东大路]] |
| Docile | Never attacks; when hit, runs away from whoever hit it | [[牛|Cows]], [[鸡|chickens]] |

Whatever its temper, whoever hits a monster becomes its foe. Each monster keeps near its own home, and goes back once a chase takes it a certain distance away. Someone knocked down is not attacked again.

The health bar overhead is red for those that will attack the player right now, and amber for the rest.

### Fire
Except for those that carry fire themselves ([[火焰史莱姆|the fire slime]], [[火焰小鬼|fire imps]], [[幼龙|dragon whelps]], [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]]), every monster fears fire: they will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire. So keeping watch beside a lit [[营火|campfire]] at night is safe. See [[营火]], [[昼夜]].

### Elements
A monster with an element attacks with that same element: fire sets people {{状态|燃烧}}, ice freezes those who are {{状态|潮湿}}, and lightning leaves people {{状态|感电}}. Fire monsters struck by fire and ice monsters struck by ice heal instead; fire monsters fear water, electric ones are immune to lightning, and golems take only half damage from lightning. See [[元素]].

## Reaching out empty-handed

Press {{键|E}} on a wild monster with empty hands (“Reach out to the wolf”), and it snaps at your hand: “The wolf snaps at the outstretched hand!” It hits you once with its own attack (defense counted as usual), an angry emote appears over its head, and from then on it treats you as a foe. Wary monsters (which only chase whoever has hit them) and nocturnal monsters by day will also come after you if you touch them like this.

[[动物|Animals]] (cows, chickens, calves, chicks) do not bite: pressing {{键|E}} on them empty-handed is something no rule covers (“Interact with the cow ?”); see [[世界会学]].

## Handing things to creatures

Holding something that is **not a weapon**, right-click a monster or animal, and instead of attacking you hand it the thing (“Hand the stone to the green slime”). {{物品|绳索}} is a tool, so a left click binds too. The creature takes it by its own rules:

| What you hand it | Result |
|---|---|
| Fiber: {{物品|绳索}}, {{物品|干草}} | “Bind the green slime with the rope”: “The green slime is lashed down with the rope.” For 3 seconds it cannot move or attack. The rope or cut grass stays on it. |
| Something edible (bread, raw meat, berries, herbs...) | “Feed the apple to the green slime”: “The green slime snaps up the apple.” A happy emote appears over its head. This does not heal it, nor change how it feels about you. What it swallows is gone. |
| Anything else | The creature's own kind answers: no rule covers what happens (“Hand the stone to the green slime ?”); see [[世界会学]]. |

- **Bosses take nothing**: a boss like [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]], and also the [[史莱姆王]], [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]], [[独眼巨人|cyclops]] and [[烬峰魔龙|the dragon of Cinder Peak]] that carry the boss mark: whatever you hand them, no rule covers it.
- **Something already frozen or bound**: handing it more fiber is something no rule covers.
- **Bound means frozen**: binding uses the {{状态|冰冻}} status; on screen it is wrapped in a shell of ice, and its status reads “frozen”. So it is just like being frozen: blunt weapons or the earth element deal it × 1.5 damage and free it at once; fire also frees it (leaving it wet).
- **Animals** can be bound too, and will swallow ordinary food, but only [[饲料|Animal Feed]] and [[麦子|Wheat]] count as feeding them (see [[动物#喂食|feeding]]).
- Things with their own use do that first: clothing and armor are put on it, a torch sets it alight, a container of water splashes it, and tools such as the pickaxe and hoe strike it.
- Only the right click (and the left click with a tool) hands things over: pressing {{键|G}} on a creature does nothing ({{键|G}} only gives things to people).

## Night creatures
After nightfall, [[僵尸|zombies]] (six in ten) and [[蝙蝠|bats]] (four in ten) come up on the outdoor maps of [[战斗区域|combat zones]]. They appear 6 to 10 tiles from the player, at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}} on one map at once, and crumble to dust at daybreak. [[和平区域|Peaceful zones]], underground and indoor maps never have them. After the White Knight falls the nights grow longer, and the time they roam grows with them. See [[夜行生物]].

## Monsters not currently in the game
The [[史莱姆王]] is already written into the game's data, but no map, respawn rule or story event puts it into the world, so you cannot meet it for now. It holds a slot on the monster page of the [[图鉴|codex]], so for now the codex cannot be completed.

## After the fight
- Felling a monster gives experience equal to its maximum health. See [[等级与经验]].
- Animals leave meat when they fall: a [[牛|cow]] leaves 2 pieces of [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]], a [[鸡|chicken]] 1 piece of [[生鸡肉|Raw Chicken]].
- Every kind of monster, once felled, leaves the materials of its own body: hides, meat, bones, stones, and the [[史莱姆凝胶|Slime Gel]], [[毒囊|Venom Sacs]] and [[灵质|Ectoplasm]] only monsters have, lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up. See [[怪物掉落]]. Those carrying things drop them too: the [[独眼巨人|cyclops]] drops 2 [[铁矿石|Iron Ore]], the [[古墓守卫|barrow warden]] drops “Tomas's Belt of Vigil”; rope or cut grass you bound it with also counts as something it carries and drops (what you fed it has been eaten and does not drop again).
- A [[持印者|Seal-bearer]] leaves 4 Iron Ore when it falls, and a [[传说武器|Legendary Arm]] if the player has a profession. See [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]].
- Things dropped on the ground lie still and do not fly into your bag by themselves; press {{键|E}} to pick them up.

## History
- 2026-09-30: The first monster types were added.
- 2026-10-02: Two slimes were placed at the Ford; cows and chickens were added with survival play.
- 2026-10-03: The night creatures, the barrow, and the monsters of the Emberfall line were added; the boss challenge added the Seven Sins bosses.
- 2026-10-04: Monsters appear in the wild.
- 2026-10-05: The Seven Sins setting was withdrawn, and the White Knight Alberic was added.
- 2026-10-07: The barrow grew to three levels; monsters and animals were placed in the Mill Cellar, Dawn Hill, the Chapel of the First Dawn and the Held Ground.
- 2026-10-09: Left-clicking a creature while holding something that is not a weapon hands it over: rope or cut grass binds it, edible things it swallows, bosses take nothing; reaching out empty-handed to a wild monster gets you bitten.
- 2026-10-21: The controls settled on four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to {{键|E}} (see [[操作]]).
