**The Crown Without a King** is a huge boss shaped like a peacock gargoyle: its head is a hollow crown, and mirror-like plates of armor float around it. It **appears only in the [[Boss Challenge|boss challenge]] and the boss test arena**; it has no place in the story and is not entered in the [[Codex|codex]]. Its name still carries the word “Pride”: it comes from the earlier “Seven Sins” boss setting, which has since been withdrawn.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **The [[Boss Challenge|boss challenge]]** and the boss test arena: the fight takes place in the clearing beside the road on [[The East Road]].
- It does not appear in the campaign.

## Skills
{{boss skills}}

It uses its three moves in turn. Each one winds up first, marking every tile it will land on; once locked, the marks no longer follow the player. Its damage is the highest of the four bosses, and its wind-ups are the longest.

| Skill | Area | Effect | Damage (phase 1 / phase 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Royal Rebuff | A cone straight in front of it, 7 tiles deep | Pushes you out one tile | 42 / 57 |
| Perfect Reflection | A cross centered on the player's locked position, each arm 7 tiles | — | 36 / 49 |
| Kneel Before the Crown | A square frame 11 tiles on a side centered on the player's locked position (exactly 5 tiles from the center) | Staying at the center or getting fully clear of the frame is safe | 44 / 59 |

All damage figures are before defense. When its health falls to {{value|首领二阶段}} it enters the second phase: wind-ups 20% shorter, the pause after each move 35% shorter, damage 35% higher.

## Behavior
- 2600 health and 3 defense, both the highest of the four bosses. Its body takes up 5×4 tiles, also the largest.
- It spots opponents within 12 tiles, and never strays more than 12 tiles from where it spawned.
- It has no ordinary attack; all its damage comes from its three skills.

## Strategy
- The cone only opens in front of it: get round to its side or back. When the Rebuff pushes you a tile, take care not to be pushed into the marks of the next move.
- The cross covers only the two straight lines: stand diagonally and you are safe.
- The frame of Kneel Before the Crown is drawn around the player's locked position: if you stay put, you are at the center of the frame, which is safe; if you dodge, get right out of it, and do not stop on the frame.
- Its defense is high; use a high-attack weapon.

## Drops
{{drops}}

The boss challenge saves nothing: even if you win, you cannot take the spoils with you.

## Lore
It was originally Pride in the “Seven Sins” setting. When that setting was withdrawn, the other four had their skills and code deleted; it, [[The Velvet Lure|the Velvet Lure]] and [[The Face Thief|the Face Thief]] stayed in the boss challenge. The bosses of the story are now the [[Seal-bearers|Seal-bearers]]; where these three will go later has not been decided (to be designed).

Its art was generated with image tools: a 4×5 action sheet (idle, walk, attack, cast, hit and fall, 96 pixels per frame), plus three skill effects. It is displayed at 2× scale. Its colors are lapis blue, ivory and gold.
