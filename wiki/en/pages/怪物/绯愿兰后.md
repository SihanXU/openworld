**The Velvet Lure** is a huge boss shaped like an orchid mantis, wearing a porcelain mask and a petal-like cloak, wound about with a covenant of thorns. She **appears only in the [[首领挑战|boss challenge]] and the boss test arena**; she has no place in the story and is not entered in the [[图鉴|codex]]. Her name still carries the word “Lust”: she comes from the earlier “Seven Sins” boss setting, which has since been withdrawn.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The [[首领挑战|boss challenge]]** and the boss test arena: the fight takes place in the clearing beside the road on [[东大路]].
- She does not appear in the campaign.

## Skills
{{首领技能}}

She uses her three moves in turn. Each one winds up first, marking every tile it will land on; once locked, the marks no longer follow the player.

| Skill | Area | Effect | Damage (phase 1 / phase 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Velvet Crescent | A cone straight in front of her, 7 tiles deep | — | 30 / 41 |
| False Promise | Five landing points: the tile the player is on when it locks, plus the tiles 4 away above, below, left and right of it | Pulls you one tile toward her, then leaves you {{状态|迷眩}} for 0.8 seconds | 24 / 32 |
| Thorn Covenant | A cross centered on the player's locked position, each arm 6 tiles | Leaves you {{状态|迟滞}} for 2.5 seconds: you walk slower (attacks are unaffected) | 32 / 43 |

All damage figures are before defense. When her health falls to {{数值|首领二阶段}} she enters the second phase: wind-ups 20% shorter (0.8 seconds at the least), the pause after each move 35% shorter, damage 35% higher.

## Behavior
- 1600 health, 2 defense. Her body takes up 5×3 tiles, the second largest of the four bosses.
- She spots opponents within 12 tiles, and never strays more than 12 tiles from where she spawned.
- She has no ordinary attack; all her damage comes from her three skills.

## Strategy
- The cone only opens in front of her: get round to her side or back.
- False Promise pulls you a tile closer and then holds you in place; being pulled into the area of the next move is the most dangerous thing. Leave as soon as you see the landing points.
- The cross covers only the two straight lines: stand diagonally and you are safe. Once you are Mired, do not try to run out of a large marked area.
- In the boss challenge, choose your profession before starting the fight; see [[首领挑战]].

## Lore
She was originally Lust in the “Seven Sins” setting. The bosses' design document says she expresses desire through impossible promises and the binding of flowers, not through a sexualized body.

When the Seven Sins setting was withdrawn, the other four (Greed, Gluttony, Sloth and Wrath) had their skills and code deleted, leaving only their art; she, [[窃面者|the Face Thief]] and [[无王之冠|the Crown Without a King]] stayed in the boss challenge. The bosses of the story are now the [[持印者|Seal-bearers]]; where these three will go later has not been decided (to be designed).

Her art was generated with image tools: a 4×5 action sheet (idle, walk, attack, cast, hit and fall, 96 pixels per frame), plus three skill effects. She is displayed at 1.75× scale. Her colors are wine red and ivory.
