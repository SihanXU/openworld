The **chicken** is a docile [[动物|animal]] that never attacks anyone and runs away when hit. Every so often it lays an [[鸡蛋|Egg]] at its feet. There are chickens on [[草原]], at [[誓守之地]], in [[余烬镇]], and on the open plains of [[荒野]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Grassland**: 4 at the start. When fewer than 5 remain, another appears on the grass every 90 game minutes.
- **The Held Ground**: none at the start; buy [[装箱的母鸡|Crated Hens]] at the [[货摊|trading stall]], and after that two chickens can also hatch chicks. They never appear by themselves.
- **Chicks grow up**: a [[小鸡|chick]] grows into a chicken after {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours.
- **Emberfall**: 2. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **The wild**: 1 on each open-plains map.

## Behavior
- **Temper: docile.** It never attacks. Whoever hits it, it runs away from. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Lays eggs.** It lays an egg every {{数值|下蛋间隔}} game hours, left where it stands; pick it up with {{键|E}}.
- **Speed: medium.** About 0.5 seconds per tile.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.
- **Chickens in the wild are different.** The chickens on the maps of the wild use another set of behavior: when hit, they do not flee but chase and peck you. Their attack is 0, though, so the pecks do no damage.

- **Feeding**: holding [[饲料|Animal Feed]] or [[麦子|Wheat]], right-click it (or press {{键|G}}), and it is {{状态|吃饱了}}. It lays eggs whether fed or not.
- **Breeding**: when two fed chickens that have not just bred stand close together (no more than 3 steps across and down added up), the one fed second calls, and the other hatches a [[小鸡|chick]]: “A chick hatches!”
- There is no henhouse. See [[动物]].

## Strategy
- If you want eggs, do not fell your chickens: stay nearby and wait for them to lay. [[玛尔塔]]'s [[给锅里添点东西]] asks for 2 eggs.
- Eggs can be eaten raw, or made into a cooked egg on a cooking spot such as a lit campfire. See [[烹饪]].

## Drops
{{掉落}}

A chicken is made of 2 portions of [[生鸡肉|Raw Chicken]] and leaves half of them when it falls, that is 1 piece. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
The chicken has no place in the story. Its sprite was drawn by this project itself and is not from the asset pack.
