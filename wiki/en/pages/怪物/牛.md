The **cow** is a docile [[动物|animal]] that never attacks anyone and runs away when hit. There is a herd on [[草原]] south of [[初原村]], looked after by the herder [[布琳]]; the cows at the player's camp, [[誓守之地]], have to be bought at the trading stall. Felling a cow gives you [[生牛肉|Raw Beef]]. Feed it [[饲料|Animal Feed]] or [[麦子|Wheat]] and it is fed: a fed cow can be milked with an empty bucket for a [[一桶牛奶|Bucket of Milk]], and two fed cows standing close together will have a [[小牛|calf]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Grassland**: 3 at the start. When fewer than 4 remain, another appears on the grass every 2 game hours.
- **The Held Ground**: none at the start; buy a [[装箱的小牛|Crated Calf]] at the [[货摊|trading stall]] (released, it is a calf that grows into a cow), and after that two cows can also have calves. They never appear by themselves.
- **Calves grow up**: a [[小牛|calf]] grows into a cow after {{数值|幼崽长大}} game hours.

## Behavior
- **Temper: docile.** It never attacks. Whoever hits it, it runs away from. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile. It usually ambles about within 6 tiles of home (4 tiles at the Held Ground).
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

- **Feeding**: holding [[饲料|Animal Feed]] or [[麦子|Wheat]], right-click it (or press {{键|G}}), and it “eats happily”, staying {{状态|吃饱了}} for {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours.
- **Milking**: left-click a cow that is fed and has not been milked within {{数值|喂饱时长}} game hours with an empty [[木桶|Bucket]], and you get a [[一桶牛奶|Bucket of Milk]].
- **Breeding**: when two fed cows that have not just given birth stand close together (no more than 3 steps across and down added up), the one fed second calls, and the other gives birth to a [[小牛|calf]].
- There is no cowshed, and cows do not follow people. See [[动物]].

## Strategy
- Cows are slower than the player; chase one and you can reach it.
- [[玛尔塔]]'s [[给锅里添点东西]] asks for 3 Raw Beef, and a cow leaves only 2, so you need at least two cows.
- Raw Beef can be eaten raw, or cooked into [[牛排|Steak]] on a cooking spot such as a lit campfire. See [[烹饪]].
- Cows on the grassland come back; the one at the Held Ground does not. To keep more cows at the Held Ground, feed them so they have calves, or buy them at the trading stall.
- If you want milk, do not fell your cows: feed one once a day and you can milk a bucket each day, which sells for 12 [[晨辉币|Dawnmarks]].

## Drops
{{掉落}}

A cow is made of 4 portions of Raw Beef and leaves half of them when it falls, that is 2 pieces. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
The first time you come to the grassland between 17:00 and 20:00, a scene plays of the herd kneeling to the east (only once): the cows kneel toward the White Knight's kingdom in the east. After the White Knight falls, Brin says they no longer kneel, only stand looking east. See [[布琳]], [[第一章]].

The cow sprite was drawn by this project itself and is not from the asset pack.
