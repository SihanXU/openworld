**The dragon of Cinder Peak** is the great dragon lairing in [[The Dragon's Lair|the Dragon's Lair]] atop Cinder Peak, the final foe of the “Emberfall and the dragon” line. It comes down from the mountain once every spring, and this year it came down twice: the claw marks in the ashes of the burned farmstead on [[The East Road]] and the half of [[Emberfall]]'s roofs that are newly laid are both its doing. Fighting it is a [[Bosses|boss]] fight: it breathes fire, hurls fireballs, sweeps its tail and dives, leaves embers on the ground, and calls up [[dragon whelp|dragon whelps]] at half health. Defeat it, and the line comes to an end.

> The Emberfall and dragon line was written before the [[The Five Sufferings|Five Sufferings]] setting and is placeholder content; it will later be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (to be designed). See [[Emberfall and the Dragon]], [[Chapter 2]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **The Dragon's Lair**: 1, on the scorched rock deep in the lair. When you walk into the mouth of the lair there is a stretch of narration, and the main quest [[The Dragon Road]] shows its last objective, “Defeat the dragon of Cinder Peak”.

## Behavior
- **Boss fight.** Once you enter the lair and come near it, its large health bar appears at the top of the screen, the camera pulls back, and the boss music starts. See [[Bosses]].
- **Its body takes up 2×2 tiles.** Collisions and hits count for the whole body; the wings, body and tail trailing behind it are only drawn.
- **Sight 10 tiles, at most 12 tiles from its nest.** If its target runs too far, it goes back.
- **Skills only.** It has no ordinary attack; all its damage comes from its skills, each a multiple of its attack (7 in the campaign, 22 in the boss challenge).
- **Does not fear fire.** It carries fire itself; fire-element hits instead restore 2 health to it, and water does it more damage.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

| Skill | Area | When it lands | How to answer it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cinder Breath | A cone 5 tiles out in front of it; **rock pillars block it**, and tiles behind a pillar are neither marked nor hurt | Fire (attack × 1.4), leaving [[embers|embers]] on the marked tiles | Step out of the cone, or hide behind a rock pillar; drink a Fireproof Draught |
| Threefold Fire | Three fireballs, each landing on the tile you are on at that moment and the ring around it (3×3), each aimed afresh | Fire (× 0.9), once per ball | Keep moving |
| Tail Sweep | An answering move: when you are within 2 tiles of it, the ring around it | Blunt (× 1.0), pushes you back 2 tiles | Hit once and back off |
| Bite | An answering move: when you are right in front of it, facing it | Piercing (× 1.5) | Do not stand straight in front of it |
| Call the Brood | Opens phase 2, marks no tiles | Calls out 3 dragon whelps (only once in the whole world) | Clear the whelps first, or fight while dodging |
| Searing Dive | From phase 2: charges along a line 3 tiles wide and 10 tiles long in your direction | Fire and blunt (× 1.6), pushes you back 1 tile, leaving embers on the tiles it crosses | Step to the side. **Trick it into a pillar**: when its charge hits rock, it stuns itself for 2 seconds |
| Ashfall | Opens phase 3: five patches of falling fire, 3×3 each (where you are and 4 tiles above, below, left and right), followed at once by a second wave aimed afresh | Fire (× 1.0) | Read both waves of marks and slip into the gaps |

**Embers** are fire on the ground: step in and you catch fire ({{status|burning}}); they go out after 4.5 seconds. The Fireproof Draught still helps.

**The three phases**:
- **Phase 1 (100%–50%)**: Cinder Breath → Threefold Fire → Cinder Breath → Threefold Fire, on a loop.
- **Health falls to half**: a shriek, and Call the Brood. Then phase 2: Searing Dive → Cinder Breath → Threefold Fire → Searing Dive, wind-ups 10% shorter.
- **Health falls to a quarter**: Ashfall. Then phase 3: Searing Dive → Cinder Breath (twice in a row, the second aimed afresh) → Threefold Fire, wind-ups 20% shorter, damage 20% higher.

## Strategy
For the full walkthrough see [[Main Quest Walkthrough]].
- **Get two things ready first.** [[Garrick]] forges [[Dragonsbane|Dragonsbane]] from 5 [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] and 3 [[Coal|Coal]], dealing double damage to the dragon and its whelps; [[Wren]] brews two [[Fireproof Draught|Fireproof Draughts]] from 3 [[Fireweed|Fireweed]] and 3 [[Frostcap|Frostcap]].
- **Drink at the mouth of the lair.** A Fireproof Draught leaves you {{status|fireproof}} for a while: fire damage drops to a quarter, and you cannot catch fire. Drink the second when the first is about to wear off.
- **Make good use of the four rock pillars.** Cinder Breath cannot pass a pillar; a dive into a pillar stuns the dragon, and that is your best chance to strike.
- **Do not linger on embers.** After Cinder Breath and the dive the ground is left burning; walk round it.
- **Once the whelps are out**, you can clear them first, or focus on the dragon: once the dragon dies, the whelps scatter.
- Halfway up the pass there is a lit [[campfire|campfire]] where you can rest.

## Drops
{{drops}}

It leaves nothing (it carries nothing on its body). Defeating it gives experience equal to its maximum health. When it falls, the ending scene plays with the closing card “The dragon of Cinder Peak”: “Everything the world learned from you is kept in the Threadbook (J).” See [[Threadbook]].

Once the dragon is dead, [[Mara]] wants to carve the player's name on the well.

## Lore
The dragon line is the old main plot from before the Five Sufferings setting was settled (“from the broken bridge to the dragon”), and for now it follows Chapter 1. According to the [[Lore|lore]], the chapter of the Second Seal will replace it; its content is still to be designed. See [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].
