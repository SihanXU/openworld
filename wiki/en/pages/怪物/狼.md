The **wolf** is a beast that runs very fast. There are wolves in [[Thornwood Hollow]], on [[Dawn Hill]] and on [[The East Road]], and in the rolling hills of [[The Wild]] too. It pounces on anyone it sees, and a player on foot cannot shake it off.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **Thornwood Hollow**: 2. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **Dawn Hill**: 2. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **The East Road**: 2, right inside the White Knight's kingdom of thorns. When fewer than 2 remain, another appears on the grass every 4 game hours.
- **The wild**: 1 on each rolling-hills map.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 5 tiles (4 tiles in the wild). It goes back once the chase takes it too far from home: 7 tiles in Thornwood Hollow, 6 on Dawn Hill and in the wild, 8 on the East Road. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: fast.** About 0.4 seconds per tile. The player runs a tile in 0.2 seconds, or 0.3 seconds walking slowly with {{key|Shift}} held.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire.
- **Flammable.** A beast's fur burns: when a fire starts nearby, the flames spread to it.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Lunge**: in the same row or column as you, 2–3 tiles away, it lights a red line between you, then 0.7 seconds later dashes along it and bites the first enemy it meets: damage is attack × 1.5, and the target is left [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|bleeding]] for 4 seconds. Step one tile aside and it misses.
- **Howl**: when its health is below 60% it howls (at most once a minute): it and every beast within 6 tiles become [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|frenzied]] for 8 seconds, moving faster with +1 attack.

## Strategy
- You can outrun it, but not by far; rather than flee, find a fire nearby or fight it head-on.
- Fire works on it: set alight, it keeps losing health while {{status|burning}}.
- The two wolves on the East Road are inside the White Knight's ring of thorns; deal with them before taking on the White Knight. See [[The White Knight Alberic]].
- Wolves felled in Thornwood Hollow count toward [[Clear the Hollow]].

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
