The **fire imp** is a small imp of the fire element. Fire imps haunt the ash on the upper stretch of [[烬岭山道]], and come back after you clear them.

Cinder Pass belongs to the “Emberfall and the dragon” line after Chapter 1, which is placeholder content; see [[余烬镇与魔龙]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **Cinder Pass**: 2 at the start. When fewer than 2 remain, another comes up on the ash every 4 game hours.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It comes after anyone it sees within 6 tiles. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Ranged.** Range 4 tiles. When it and its target are in the same row or column, 4 tiles apart or less, it stops and attacks; farther than that, it closes in first.
- **Speed: fairly fast.** About 0.5 seconds per tile.
- **Element: fire.** Its attacks carry fire, and whoever it hits catches {{状态|燃烧}} (someone {{状态|潮湿}} is only steamed dry).
- **Does not fear fire.** It carries fire itself, and a campfire does not keep it back.
- **Sets fires.** When it touches something dry and flammable, it sets it alight.
- **Fire cannot hurt it.** Fire-element hits instead restore 2 health to it; water does it more damage and leaves it wet.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Fireball**: at 2–5 tiles away, it throws a ball of fire at your feet, marking 3×3 tiles, which lands 1 second later: whoever is on those tiles takes attack × 1.2 fire damage and catches fire. Anyone wet does not catch fire.

## Strategy
- Do not fight it with fire; use water or a weapon with no element.
- When it stands in the same row or column as you it often attacks from where it is; use that moment to circle round and close in from the side.
- If it sets you alight, rain, walking into mud or being splashed with water all put the fire out. There is a lit [[营火|campfire]] on the pass, but it does not keep the imps back.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
