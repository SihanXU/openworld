The **sporecap** is a large mushroom that has grown into something that walks. In the story of the [[五苦|Five Sufferings]], when the First Night seeped into the world, the first things to change were the beasts and plants: in the thickest of the thorns of [[荆棘谷]], the mushrooms woke too.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **[[荆棘谷]]**: 2, one on the mud to the south and one in the grass to the northeast. When it sees someone, it shuffles slowly toward them.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It comes toward anyone it sees within 3 tiles.
- **Speed: slow.** Nearly 1 second per tile; you can outrun it.
- **Fears fire.** It is a plant and carries “flammable”: fire can set it alight.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Spore Cloud**: at 1–2 tiles, it lights purple marks within two tiles around itself, then puffs out spores 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the ring takes attack × 0.8 damage and is [[状态#怪物的技能|poisoned]] for 3 seconds. Its wind-up is long; when you see the purple ring, back off beyond two tiles. If you are poisoned, you can drink an [[解毒剂|Antidote]].

## Strategy
- It is slow and its wind-up is long: hit it twice and back off, wait for it to finish puffing, then go in again.
- If you are carrying a [[火把|Torch]], one touch sets it alight.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves one [[草药|Herb]]. It also has a very small chance to drop its own trophy gear, the Sporecap Hat (see [[专属掉落]]).
