The **cyclops** is a giant guarding the deepest chamber of [[The Old Mine]], with high health and attack. That chamber holds the best iron veins, and the cyclops has hoarded two pieces of [[Iron Ore|Iron Ore]] of its own. The smith [[Garrick]] warns you: “Don't let the cyclops see you at it.”

The Old Mine belongs to the “Emberfall and the dragon” line after Chapter 1, which is placeholder content; see [[Emberfall and the Dragon]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **The Old Mine**: 1, in the deep chamber at the north end of the main tunnel. Once felled, it does not come back.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It attacks anyone it sees within 5 tiles, and goes back once 6 tiles from the middle of the chamber. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: very slow.** About 0.9 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning. There are lit braziers in the main tunnel.

It carries the “boss” and “large” tags, but it is not one of the [[Bosses|bosses]]: it has no telegraphed skills, and fighting it is like fighting an ordinary monster.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

It is an elite among the minor monsters: it uses its moves by the minor monsters' rules (whenever their conditions are met), but it has three, and its stomp is followed by an aftershock.
- **Boulder Toss**: at 3–6 tiles away, it throws a boulder at your feet, marking 3×3 tiles, which lands 1.4 seconds later: whoever is on those tiles takes attack × 1.6 earth damage. Someone frozen who is struck by earth shatters, taking more damage.
- **Stomp**: at 1–2 tiles, it lights the 2 tiles around itself red and stomps 1.6 seconds later: whoever is in the ring takes attack × 1.5 damage and is knocked back 1 tile; **an Aftershock follows at once**: the ring 3 tiles out is hit again (attack × 1). After being knocked back, do not stay on that ring.
- **Baleful Glare**: in the same row or column, 2–6 tiles away, a purple line marks its gaze, and 1.2 seconds later it glares: whoever is on the line is [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|stunned]]. Leave that row to avoid it.

## Strategy
- It is very slow and does not chase far (6 tiles): hit it and back off, wait for it to go back to its chamber, then go in again.
- It has 2 defense, taking 2 off every hit.
- If you would rather not fight it, you can mine the veins in the side tunnels: the iron veins and coal seams there are enough for [[Garrick]] to forge the sword, and they slowly grow back after being worked out.

## Drops
{{drops}}

When it falls it drops the two pieces of Iron Ore it carries, which lie on the ground; pick them up with {{key|E}}. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
