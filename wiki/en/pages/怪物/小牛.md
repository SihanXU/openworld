The **calf** is the young of the [[cow|cow]]. When two fed cows stand close together, one of them gives birth to a calf beside itself: “A calf is born!” It uses the cow's sprite, only smaller. A calf grows into a cow after {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours; until then it cannot be milked and does not breed. See [[Animals]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- Calves are never placed directly on a map. They are born of cows, and cows on any map can give birth: [[The Held Ground]], [[The Grassland]], or a released calf once it has grown up.
- Releasing a “[[Crated Calf|Crated Calf]]” bought at the [[trading stall|trading stall]] gives you a calf.

## Behavior
- **How it is born**: feed a cow [[Animal Feed|Animal Feed]] or [[Wheat|Wheat]], and once fed it calls to the cows near it. If a cow within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down) that is already fed and has not given birth recently hears it, that cow gives birth to a calf beside itself (if that tile has no room, on the nearest free tile). Both cows are then {{status|bred}} and will not breed again for {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours. As many such cows as are nearby, that many calves are born.
- **Growing up**: {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours after it is born, it becomes a [[cow|cow]]. Time still passes while the player is not on that map, and is made up when you return.
- **Before it grows up**: it cannot be milked (no rule says what happens if you click it with a bucket), it does not hear the cows' call, and it does not breed. It can be fed, and is happy when fed, but that does nothing else.
- **Temper: docile.** Like a cow, it never attacks anyone; whoever hits it, it runs away from. It usually wanders near where it was born. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: medium.** About 0.6 seconds per tile, faster than a cow.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

## Strategy
- **Get two cows first.** The Held Ground starts with only one cow; buy a [[Crated Calf|Crated Calf]] (120 [[Dawnmark|Dawnmarks]]) in the “Animal Husbandry” section of the trading stall, and once the released calf grows up you have a second cow.
- **Feed one, then the other one next to it.** The two feedings must fall within {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours, and the two cows must be no more than 3 steps apart when you feed the second. The one fed first gives birth to the calf. Animals wander, so feed them while they are close together; you can also pen them together with a [[fence|fence]].
- **One portion of feed per animal.** One calf takes at least 2 portions of feed (5 portions for 6 Dawnmarks at the stall); wheat works too.
- **Wait for it to grow up.** After {{value|幼崽长大}} game hours the calf grows into a cow, which can be milked (see [[Bucket of Milk]]) and can have calves of its own. Two cows can breed again after {{value|喂饱时长}} game hours.
## Drops
{{drops}}

A calf is made of 1 portion of [[Raw Beef|Raw Beef]] and leaves half of it when it falls: a 50% chance to leave 1 piece, otherwise nothing. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health, 5 points.
