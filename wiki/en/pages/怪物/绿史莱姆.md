The **green slime** is the most common monster in the game: a blob of green jelly with no element that leaps at anyone it sees, but walks slowly and has little health. You can meet it among the thorns of [[Thornwood Hollow]], in the [[The Mill Cellar]], and in [[The Wild]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **Thornwood Hollow**: 3 at the start. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up on the grass every 90 game minutes.
- **The Mill Cellar**: 2. Once cleared, they do not come back.
- **The wild**: 2 on each open-plains map, 1 on each deep-woodland, rolling-hills and road map.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees (within 4 tiles in Thornwood Hollow and the cellar). It gives up and goes back once the chase takes it 7 tiles from home, 6 tiles in the wild. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile; the player runs a tile in just 0.2 seconds.
- **Element: none.** Its attacks carry no element.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Hop**: at 2–3 tiles away, it lights red marks on the 3×3 tiles around your feet, then 0.8 seconds later leaps over and lands beside you, crushing whoever is in the marked area for attack × 1.5 damage, carrying its own element. When you see the marks, step out of those 9 tiles.

## Strategy
- It walks far slower than the player: hit once and step back, and you open up the distance.
- It has no elemental resistance; [[Elements|fire]] sets it {{status|burning}}.
- After dark, stand beside a lit [[campfire|campfire]] and it will not come near.
- Green slimes felled in Thornwood Hollow count toward [[Holt]]'s [[Clear the Hollow]].

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
