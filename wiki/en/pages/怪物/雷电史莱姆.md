The **spark slime** is a slime of the lightning element. Spark slimes gather on the top of [[晨光丘]], where the storms strike first.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **[[晨光丘]]**: 2, on the hilltop. They chase and attack anyone they see.

## Behavior
The following is its type data and how the rules treat it; its temper depends on where it is placed.
- **Speed: slow.** About 0.7 seconds per tile, like the other slimes.
- **Element: lightning.** Its attacks carry lightning, and whoever it hits is left {{状态|感电}}, briefly unable to move; if they are {{状态|潮湿}}, the current also jumps to other wet things within 2 tiles.
- **Charged.** The lightning element has no effect on it.
- **Fears fire.** Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Hop**: at 2–3 tiles away, it lights red marks on the 3×3 tiles around your feet, then 0.8 seconds later leaps over and lands beside you, crushing whoever is in the marked area for attack × 1.5 damage, carrying its own element. When you see the marks, step out of those 9 tiles.

## Strategy
- Do not fight it while you are wet.
- Lightning is useless against it; use fire or a weapon with no element. See [[元素]].
