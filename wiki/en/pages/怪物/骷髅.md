The **skeleton** is one of the dead who will not rest, with a little defense. The three levels of [[The Old Barrow]] (the Old Barrow, the [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]] and the [[The Warden's Sanctum]]) are full of them, and there is one in the [[The Old Mine]] too. The skeletons in the barrow and the ossuary slowly come back after you clear them.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **The Old Barrow**: 3. When fewer than 2 remain, another comes up on the stone floor every 4 game hours.
- **The Ossuary of the Thorn**: 4. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up every 4 game hours.
- **The Warden's Sanctum**: 2, standing guard on either side of [[barrow warden|the Warden]]. Once felled, they do not come back.
- **The Old Mine**: 1, on the middle stretch of the main tunnel. Once felled, it does not come back.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 4 to 5 tiles, and goes back once the chase takes it about 7 tiles from home. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: medium.** About 0.6 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning. There are lit braziers in both the ossuary and the sanctum.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Bone Toss**: in the same row or column, 2–5 tiles away, it raises a bone, a red line marks the 5 tiles it will fly over, and it throws 0.8 seconds later: enemies on the line take attack × 1 damage. Leave that row to dodge it. Up close it only makes ordinary attacks.

## Strategy
- It has 1 defense, taking 1 off every hit.
- The skeletons in the barrow come back, so on trips in and out of the barrow, do not count on the way being clear forever.
- The 2 tiles around the braziers in the ossuary and the sanctum are ground it will not enter.

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
The skeletons in the ossuary are the dead of the Order of the Thorn: there are bones in every wall, and every one has a name carved over it. [[Lumi|Lumi]] says: “They're not sleeping either.”
