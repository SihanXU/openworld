The **ghost** is a floating undead whose attacks carry ice. There is only one in the whole game, in the hall of niches of the [[The Ossuary of the Thorn]].

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- **The Ossuary of the Thorn**: 1, in the hall of niches. Once felled, it does not come back.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 5 tiles, and goes back once the chase takes it 7 tiles from home. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: fairly fast.** About 0.5 seconds per tile.
- **Floats.** Drops in height do not stop it, and it never falls.
- **Element: ice.** Its attacks carry ice: hitting someone who is {{status|wet}} leaves them {{status|frozen}}.
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Wail**: at 1–2 tiles away, it lights a purple circle within 2 tiles around itself and wails 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the circle takes attack × 0.8 ice damage and is [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|stunned]] for 1.5 seconds; anyone wet is frozen. Its wind-up is long; when you see the circle, run out of it.

## Strategy
- While you are dry, its ice will not freeze you; it is just ordinary damage.
- The ossuary also holds four [[skeleton|skeletons]] and a [[bat|bat]]; do not stir them all up at once in the hall.

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
