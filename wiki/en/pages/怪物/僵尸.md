The **zombie** is one of the undead that roam the wild at night. It is placed on no map; it only comes up out of the dark after nightfall, one of the two [[夜行生物|night creatures]] (the other is the [[蝙蝠|bat]]). It is the slowest-walking monster in the game, and it crumbles to dust once day breaks.

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- It appears only **at night**, on the **outdoor** maps of [[战斗区域|combat zones]], such as [[草原]], [[森林]], [[荆棘谷]], [[山脚]], [[东大路]], [[晨光丘]], [[烬岭山道]], [[林间空地]] and [[荒野]]. [[和平区域|Peaceful zones]] ([[初原村]], [[渡口]] and the like) and underground and indoor maps never have any.
- Every so often during the night, a night creature comes up 6 to 10 tiles from the player, and six in ten of them are zombies; at most {{数值|夜行生物上限}} can be on one map at once.
- After daybreak they crumble to dust and vanish, one after another.

See [[夜行生物]]. As the Seal-bearers fall, the nights grow longer, and so does the time they roam; see [[昼夜]].

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 8 tiles, and gives up only once the chase takes it 14 tiles away. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: slowest.** About 1 second per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

- **Grasp**: when it is right next to you, it lights a purple cross on the four tiles above, below, left and right of itself, and grabs 1 second later: damage is attack × 1, and whoever it grabs is [[状态#怪物的技能|Mired]] for 2.5 seconds (moves slowly). It is slow; when you see the cross, step away diagonally.

## Strategy
- It is so slow that you always have time to back off when fighting it head-on. The real danger is several of them closing in at once.
- The safest place at night is beside a lit [[营火|campfire]], or shut inside a house you built yourself. See [[建造]], [[房间]].
- Two quests ask you to fight zombies: [[芬尼克]]'s [[老骑士]] asks you to put down 3 zombies; [[布琳]]'s [[草原守夜]] asks you to put down 3 undead on the grassland.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{键|E}} to pick them up (see [[怪物掉落]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
“The dead find no rest” is one of the signs that the [[极夜|Long Night]] is drawing near. [[霍尔特]] says: “The dead walk the wild at night. Not here, though. Never here.”
