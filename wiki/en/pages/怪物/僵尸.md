The **zombie** is one of the undead that roam the wild at night. It is placed on no map; it only comes up out of the dark after nightfall, one of the two [[Night Creatures|night creatures]] (the other is the [[bat|bat]]). It is the slowest-walking monster in the game, and it crumbles to dust once day breaks.

## Spawning
{{habitat}}

- It appears only **at night**, on the **outdoor** maps of [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|combat zones]], such as [[The Grassland]], [[The Forest]], [[Thornwood Hollow]], [[Mountain Foot]], [[The East Road]], [[Dawn Hill]], [[Cinder Pass]], [[The Clearing]] and [[The Wild]]. [[Peaceful and Combat Zones|Peaceful zones]] ([[Firstlight Village]], [[The Ford]] and the like) and underground and indoor maps never have any.
- Every so often during the night, a night creature comes up 6 to 10 tiles from the player, and six in ten of them are zombies; at most {{value|夜行生物上限}} can be on one map at once.
- After daybreak they crumble to dust and vanish, one after another.

See [[Night Creatures]]. As the Seal-bearers fall, the nights grow longer, and so does the time they roam; see [[Day and Night]].

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 8 tiles, and gives up only once the chase takes it 14 tiles away. See [[Factions and Temper]].
- **Speed: slowest.** About 1 second per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire.

## Skills
{{monster skills}}

- **Grasp**: when it is right next to you, it lights a purple cross on the four tiles above, below, left and right of itself, and grabs 1 second later: damage is attack × 1, and whoever it grabs is [[Statuses#Monsters' skills|Mired]] for 2.5 seconds (moves slowly). It is slow; when you see the cross, step away diagonally.

## Strategy
- It is so slow that you always have time to back off when fighting it head-on. The real danger is several of them closing in at once.
- The safest place at night is beside a lit [[campfire|campfire]], or shut inside a house you built yourself. See [[Building]], [[Rooms]].
- Two quests ask you to fight zombies: [[Fennick]]'s [[The Old Templar]] asks you to put down 3 zombies; [[Brin]]'s [[Night on the Grassland]] asks you to put down 3 undead on the grassland.

## Drops
{{drops}}

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press {{key|E}} to pick them up (see [[Monster Drops]]). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

## Lore
“The dead find no rest” is one of the signs that the [[The Long Night|Long Night]] is drawing near. [[Holt]] says: “The dead walk the wild at night. Not here, though. Never here.”
