The **barrow warden**, called “the Warden” in quests and dialogue, is a skeleton-like undead in the [[守墓者圣所]], the deepest part of [[古墓]]. It stands guard before an altar on which the vow of Alberic, the White Knight, is carved. Defeating it is the last objective of the main quest [[古墓里的失踪者]].

## Spawning
{{出没}}

- **The Warden's Sanctum**: 1, with 2 [[骷髅|skeletons]] beside it. Once defeated, it does not come back.

## Behavior
- **Temper: aggressive.** It attacks anyone it sees within 6 tiles, and goes back once it is 6 tiles from the altar. See [[阵营与脾性]].
- **Speed: fairly slow.** About 0.8 seconds per tile.
- **Element: none.**
- **Fears fire.** It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning. A lit brazier stands in each of the sanctum's four corners.

It carries the “boss” tag, but it is not one of the [[首领|bosses]]: it has no telegraphed skills, and fighting it is like fighting an ordinary monster.

## Skills
{{怪物技能}}

It is an elite among the minor monsters: it uses its moves by the minor monsters' rules (whenever their conditions are met), but it has three, and it chains them.
- **Vigil Sweep**: at 1–2 tiles, it sweeps the cone in front of it toward you, for attack × 1.4 damage, pushing you back 1 tile.
- **Shield Charge**: in the same row or column, 2–4 tiles away, it raises its shield and charges along a red line; whoever it hits takes attack × 1.6 damage and is [[状态#怪物的技能|stunned]]; right after the charge it **follows up with a Vigil Sweep**. When you see the red line, step aside; do not wait for it to reach you.
- **Hold the Post**: when its health is below half (at most once every 4 minutes), it lights a purple ring around itself (the ring 3 tiles out), which falls 1.4 seconds later: whoever is on the ring is cut and [[状态#怪物的技能|Mired]]. **Crowd control does not interrupt** this move. Stand right beside it or retreat outside the ring to avoid it.

## Strategy
- There are two skeletons in the sanctum as well; clear them first, then deal with the Warden alone.
- It is slow: hit once and step back, and you avoid most of its counterattacks. It has 1 defense.
- The 2 tiles around each corner brazier are ground it will not enter; retreat there if you are surrounded.

## Drops
{{掉落}}

When it falls it drops “Tomas's Belt of Vigil”: a leather belt of [[遗誓|Vowbound]] quality, with higher defense at night, carrying the vow “Vigil” (after nightfall, +20% critical chance and +15% movement speed). Defeating it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

As it falls apart it speaks its last words: “The night... is coming back. I could not... lie down.”

## Lore
In life the Warden was Brother Tomas of the Thorn, sworn brother of Alberic, Knight-Commander of the Order of the Thorn (a brother in the Order, not by blood; Alberic's brother by blood was named Hugh). About a hundred years ago he was charged with guarding the Order's dead buried beneath Thornwood Hollow, and was buried as their warden. His niche in the [[荆棘骸骨堂]] reads: “Brother Tomas of the Thorn, the Commander's brother. Warden of this house until the night comes back.” His memorial stone in the courtyard of the [[荆棘修道院]] reads: “Brother Tomas of the Thorn, sworn brother to the Commander, sent to ward the Order's dead under the hollow. He keeps them still.” On the altar behind him is carved Alberic's vow: “I will hold until I am relieved.” Pressed into the soot beneath the words is a fresh thumbprint of ash. See [[白骑士·阿尔贝里克]].

{{剧透|The inner door}}
[[莉娜]] says a grey man shut the inner door from the outside. What the Grey Man shut in was in fact this Warden who would not rest, not her and [[奥斯卡]]. See [[灰衣人]].
