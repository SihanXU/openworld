Difficulty

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Difficulty decides what happens after the player falls. When you start a new game you choose one of three, Easy, Normal or Hardcore; the default is Normal. Once chosen, difficulty cannot be changed, and it is kept with the save. Difficulty does not affect how strong monsters are, how fast you get hungry, or any other number; it only affects the price of death.

The three difficulties

Difficulty Description on the title page What you can do after falling
Easy Get up where you fell, with everything Get up · Load a save · Title screen
Normal (default) Respawn at your resting place; what you carried is bundled and left where you fell (the equipment you wear, the weapon in your hand and important items are not dropped); go back and press E on the bundle to take it all back Respawn · Load a save · Title screen
Hardcore One life Only Title screen; this save ends here

Old saves with no difficulty recorded are treated as Normal. The tutorial and the Boss Challenge have no difficulty to choose, and falling in the tutorial is treated as Normal.

Falling

When the player's health drops to 0, the character collapses (down) and the whole world stops:

  • The screen is washed with red, with “You died!” in the middle.
  • Below it is what felled you, for example “Felled by bat”; when it cannot be found out, it says “You fell”.
  • Below that are the buttons the difficulty allows. The death screen takes only mouse clicks on its buttons.

What can make the player fall: attacks, burning, bleeding, falls (6 points for every storey beyond the first) and so on. Hunger never takes the last point of health. See Health.

The game does not autosave while the player is down, so “Load a save” always loads a save from before the fall.

Respawning

Button Difficulty Result
Get up (with what you carry) Easy You stand up where you fell, with full health and everything still on you. The message reads “You get up again.”
Respawn (at your resting place; what you carried stays where you fell) Normal You return to your resting place with full health. The things in your inventory are bundled into Lost Belongings left where you fell; the Dawnmarks you carry do not go into the bundle but scatter beside it, to be walked back to and picked up; the equipment you wear, the weapon in your hand and important items (such as the Oath-Thorn) stay on you. The message reads “You rise again. What you carried lies bundled where you fell, your coin scattered beside it; what you wore and what matters most are still with you.”
Load a save (from before the fall) Easy, Normal Opens the load panel to choose a save to load. See Saving
Title screen All Returns to the title page

The bundle left on Normal lies quietly on the tile where you fell; it is not drawn into your inventory and does not vanish. Walk back and press E on it (“Take back your Lost Belongings”), and everything in it returns to your inventory at once: “You gather up your belongings.” The bundle then disappears.

Resting place

The resting place is where you respawn on Normal:

  • the bed you last slept in;
  • or the waypoint statue you last awakened;
  • whichever of the two is later. With neither, you return to where the game began (for a new game, the ruined outpost on Cloudtop).

You respawn standing on the bed; when the resting place is a statue, on the nearest empty tile beside it. If the resting place is on another map, you go straight back to that map. Once the bed is taken apart or burned, you return to where the game began. Pressing E on a statue already awakened (“Listen”) does not change the resting place; only awakening a new statue does.

Hardcore

  • You have one life. When you fall, the save is at once saved as “ended”.
  • The death screen reads “Hardcore has one life: this world has ended.”, with only a “Title screen” button.
  • On the title page an ended save shows as “Hardcore · ended · place · day N”. “Continue” skips it, and in the load panel it has no “Load” button, but it can still be exported and deleted.
  • The threads the world has learned are not lost by this: they belong to the rule library every save shares.

When others go down

Characters that “do not die” (villagers, Lumi and the player) all collapse instead of vanishing when their health reaches zero:

  • They stay down for 30 seconds, then get up with half their health.
  • No one attacks a character who is down.
  • While down, Lumi cannot follow; once up, she catches up again.

Unprotected characters such as monsters and animals are defeated at zero and break into their materials. See Combat.

Tips

  • On Normal, before a long trip, sleep or awaken the local waypoint statue to move your resting place closer.
  • When you fall somewhere dangerous, “Load a save” is often safer than respawning and running back for your things. Loading takes you back to the save before the fall.
  • Autosaves are not made within 15 seconds of being hurt or within 8 tiles of an enemy, so loading never drops you back into the fight you lost. See Saving.
