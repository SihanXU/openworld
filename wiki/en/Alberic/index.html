“I swore to hold until I was relieved. It was you who relieved me. So now I hold this ground for you. It is the same vow.”

— Alberic

Alberic is the White Knight with the Crown of Thorns taken off, the keeper of The Held Ground. The crown took most of him; what is left keeps the same vow turned another way: he holds a piece of land for the one who relieved him, and enlarges it with what the player brings. He cannot keep names in his head, so he asks the player's name.

He also owns the Held Ground's stall: “Alberic's stall” stands beside the spot where he keeps watch by day, with three shelves, Farming, Ranching and Fishing, selling seeds, fertilizer, shovels, feed, buckets, crated livestock, fishing rods and bait; its “Sell” page buys what the player grows, raises and catches. The money it takes is the Held Ground's own Dawnmarks; for prices see Money. Press E on the stall to buy and sell, with no need to talk to him first, whether or not he is nearby, by day or by night.

He is not in the world until the player defeats The White Knight Alberic. After defeating the White Knight, the player receives the Oath-Thorn: hold it and right-click yourself to enter the Held Ground, where he is. For the boss fight see The White Knight Alberic; this page is about the man.

Before the White Knight falls, he is nowhere the player can reach. After he falls, he lives at the Held Ground: he sleeps in his own tent from 22:00 to 06:00, works at the forge and anvil east of the sword from 06:00 to 14:00, and from 14:00 to 22:00 stands by the planted sword, keeping watch over the land.

He is also the Held Ground's smith: before he was a knight, his father was a smith. Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark. The forge is always lit and anyone may use the anvil (see Smithing); he also sells Smith Hammer, Coal, Iron Ingot and Steel Bar (see Trading).

Relationships

Fennick was once his squire, who carried his lance for six years as a young man; by then he had worn the Crown of Thorns for two hundred and fifty years. He can no longer recall Fennick's face. His brother by blood was named Hugh; his journal reads “My brother Hugh says it is a sentence”; the warden, Brother Tomas, was his sworn brother in the order, not his brother by blood (see barrow warden).

The first time the player enters the Held Ground, his welcome scene plays: “You are the one who took the crown from me. I could not see your face then. Now I can.” He asks the player to tell him their name: “This time, I would like to keep a name.” Finally he hands the player 30 Dawnmarks: “Take this, the coin of this ground. My stall keeps seeds, feed and bait, and buys back what you grow, raise and catch. There are fish in the pond.”

Once the player has taken on Fennick's The Old Templar, he speaks of a squire, once.

Quests

  • The Held Ground (Main)

All expansion of the Held Ground goes through him: talk to him and choose “The Held Ground…” to open the steward's panel, and hand him materials to put into the Held Ground's stock, to widen the land, open new grounds and put up working buildings (see Expanding the Held Ground). Each time the land is widened, he gives you a sum of Dawnmarks:

  • The first (the woodland): Logs and Stone. Reward 20 Dawnmarks.
  • The second (the meadow): Planks and Iron Ore. Reward 40 Dawnmarks.
  • The third (the ancient boundary stone): Planks, Stone and Gold Ore. Reward 60 Dawnmarks. Gold Ore is dug from gold veins deep in The Old Mine.

Each time one is finished, a ring of mist around the Held Ground disperses, revealing new ground. See The Held Ground.

Lore

Alberic is the First Seal-bearer and the Commander of the Order of the Thorn. On the roll in The Ossuary of the Thorn, his name is cut the deepest: “Knelt to children. Chosen by the Holy City to bear the Crown of Thorns. He holds.” His vow is carved on the altar of The Warden's Sanctum: “I will hold until I am relieved.” He was knighted at Abbey of the Thorn, and chosen there to wear the crown, with the promise that someone would relieve him after forty years; the Holy See's Writ of Relief was never sent, and he held for three hundred years (Chapter 1's design proposal). See The White Knight Alberic and Chapter 1.

What he does not say (click to show)

The crown took most of his self, and he can no longer remember names. He has turned his vow, “hold until relieved”, around: since the player relieved him, he holds for the player.

The Seal-bearers are among the fulcrums that hold up the vessel that is Astra. Defeating him took away one fulcrum, which is why the nights really did grow longer after he fell. At dusk he apologizes for it: “The sun sets early. Earlier than it did. I am sorry. I think I am sorry.” See Seal-bearers and Main Story.

Trivia

  • His age in the game data is 341.
  • His human form uses the same base sprite as Bram.
  • He says there is “nothing to kneel to” at the Held Ground; the Held Ground's boundary stone is carved with a ring of thorns, and inside the ring there is no kneeling figure.
