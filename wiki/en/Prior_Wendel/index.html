Prior Wendel

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Monster
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Prior Wendel, the Last Keeper is the last prior of the Abbey of the Thorn. After death he became a skeleton, keeping the northernmost part of the The Abbey Crypt, guarding the seal of the Order of the Thorn. Defeating him is one of the objectives of The First Seal: “Take the Order's seal from the last keeper of the Abbey Crypt”.

In the game he is a single skeleton of his own, counted as a skeleton in the codex and the monster list.

Spawning

  • The Abbey Crypt, north chamber: 1. Once defeated, he does not come back.

Behavior

  • 90 health, 5 attack, 2 defense, far sturdier than an ordinary skeleton.
  • He attacks anyone he sees within 5 tiles, and goes back once 5 tiles from his place.
  • He carries the “boss” tag, but he is not one of the bosses: he has no telegraphed skills, and fighting him is like fighting an ordinary monster.
  • The first time you walk into the deepest part of the crypt, he first speaks (a scene), and only then attacks.

Strategy

  • The middle chamber of the crypt also holds a skeleton and a ghost; clear them first, then go into the north chamber.
  • He has 2 defense, so bare hands make slow work of him; bring a weapon.

Drops

  • The Order's Seal: always dropped, where he falls.
  • He drops no monster spoils.

Lore

What he did (click to show)

Father Caius mentions that the Seal-bearer “was due relief, once”. The Writ of Relief in the abbey's The Chapter Hall is that writ of relief: forty years borne, another to be sent to relieve him. It was never sent, and on the back is written: “No one could be found who would bear it. Let him go on holding.” The one who held it back was Prior Wendel.

As he rises he says: “Who comes for the seal? ...The Crown went east, as was ordained. The relief stays here, with me. We hold, that others need not.” Lumi says: “He's getting up! Watch out!”

The forty-year term and the prior's holding back the writ are setting added by the Chapter 1 design beyond the original lore text (docs/chapter-1.md §14, marked as a proposal).
