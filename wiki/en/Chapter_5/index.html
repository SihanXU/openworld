Chapter 5

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Story
To be designed: this part is not designed yet; below is only what is settled. To make a proposal, click “Talk” at the top of the page.

Chapter 5, by the structure of the main story, is the chapter of the Fifth Seal: the protagonist faces the fifth and last Seal-bearer, the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar, and recovers the last Instrument of the Sufferings, the Lance That Pierced the Heart. After it comes the Final Chapter. The chapter's places, people, quests and boss fight have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

What is already decided

  • Seal-bearer: the Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar, holder of the Fifth Seal, modeled on the martyrs of the fifth seal in the Book of Revelation (§6).
  • Torment: the Fifth Suffering, the Piercing. A holy lance pierced Astra's chest, and at that very moment the sun rose again (§4).
  • Instrument of the Sufferings: the Lance That Pierced the Heart.
  • Chapter structure: one Seal-bearer per chapter; when the Seal-bearer falls, Lumi catches part of the Seal's power and gains a Boon; and the nights grow a little longer again. The Fifth Seal's Boon and how much longer the nights grow are both to be designed.
  • Once all five Seal-bearers have fallen, the protagonist carries the five holy instruments to the Holy City (§7).
What the White Witnesses bear (click to show)

The White Witnesses bear the wound to the soul the holy lance left (§12). What the Piercing really does: when the blade went through his heart, Astra's body became, for the first time, an “entrance” joining reality and the First Night, and the First Night began to pour into his body (§4).
