The plot for a second residents' tent is a staked-out patch of empty ground east of the sword on the Held Ground, the place where the “Second Residents' Tent” of Expanding the Held Ground is to be built. Until it is built it is just a ring of stakes; once built, after a while it becomes a tent.

Source

  • Placed on maps: The Held Ground
  • On the Held Ground from the start, east of the sword. You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

  • Building it: talk to Alberic, choose “The Held Ground…”, and find “Second Residents' Tent” on the “Structures” page. It requires land tier I (the Grove); materials: 8 Bolt of Cloth, 14 Plank and 6 Log, handed to him for the Held Ground's store. Once the store has enough, press “Build!”. When it is finished, Alberic also gives you 50 Dawnmark.
  • The card says what you get once it is built: “Two more beds”.
  • Once you press it, Alberic says: “Voices at the fire of an evening. I had forgotten that, too.” Every so often the plot checks whether the building is done; when it is, it turns into a tent with a flash of light.
  • What it becomes: one bed on each side in front of the tent, for the people you invite to live there (see Held Ground Residents).
  • The plot does not block the way, and you can walk over it; hold something and press G on it, and the thing is put down on its tile.
  • The stakes catch fire. A plot burned away is gone, and then the building will never appear here. See staked plot.

History

  • 2026-10-12: Added together with the Held Ground's residents; the Held Ground in older saves gets this plot staked out too.
