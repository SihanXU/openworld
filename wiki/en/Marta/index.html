— Marta

Marta keeps the fire and the pot of Firstlight Village, 44 years old. The whole village eats at her fire; since the storm, there has been little to put in the pot. Her daughter is Mia and her nephew is Pell; both children live with her.

Relationships

Mia is her daughter, and Pell calls her aunt. When Pell says he has seen the Grey Man, she does not believe him: she says he gets it from his father, who went east and never wrote again.

Quests

  • Something for the Pot (Side, giver)
  • Something for the Pot: she needs 3 Raw Beef and 2 Eggs to make a stew for the whole village. The cows and chickens are on The Grassland south of the village. Hand them in for Bread and “Marta's Kitchen Sash”.

Selling bread

Marta is a merchant of Firstlight Village: every morning she bakes 6 Bread, which you can buy from her (see Trading). Villagers who are hungry and have money come to buy too, so the bread is often sold out before evening (see People's Needs).

Her bread is carried on her person and is hers: taking it from her pocket is pickpocketing. When Vigo is hungry, he often has designs on that bread. She, Mia, Pell and Lina are one household (the cook's house); steal from any one of them and the whole family holds a grudge.

Lore

What Chapter 1 added to her is her worry over Pell's stories. The Grey Man Pell talks of turns out to be real; see The Grey Man.

Trivia

  • Her role in the game is “innkeeper”, but the village has no inn; what she does is cook for the village.
  • She shares a character sprite with Lina and Mara.
