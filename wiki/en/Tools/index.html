Tools

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Tools are items for changing the world: felling trees, cutting stone, tilling soil, reaping, finishing what you build, carrying and pouring water. Holding a tool, left-click the target to use it. Like weapons, tools have “power”, and their efficiency differs from one material to another.

What each tool can do

The tooltip says in blue what a tool can do.

Tool What it can do See
Axe Fells trees and splits wood: chop a tree down for a Log, and click a log on the ground with the axe to split it into two planks. Carrying an axe, you can also split logs into planks in the crafting panel. Woodcutting
Pickaxe Breaks rock, boulders and ore veins, for Stone, Iron Ore and Coal. Mining
Hoe Turns grass, dirt, sand or snow into farmland. Farming
Sickle Reaps ripe crops, for 2 of the harvest and 2 seeds. Farming
Smith Hammer Finishes building: the last step of the crate, Wooden Chest, fence and sign of build mode is a blow of the hammer. It can also take apart a frame not yet finished. Building
Shovel Digs up stumps and crops (a crop dug up goes back to seeds). Bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. Farming
Bucket An empty bucket fills with water at a river or a well, becoming a “Water Bucket”; a Water Bucket waters crops and puts out burning things. An empty bucket can also milk a fed cow. Farming, Animals
Rope Mends the broken ferry rope at the Ford. The Ford
Fishing Rod Fishes at the water's edge, casting up to 3 cells out; baited, its chance of a bite doubles. Made by hand from 2 sticks and 2 cut grass, or bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. Fishing

Left-click a built wall, door or floor, or a bed or workbench, or anything else that can be taken away, once with an axe, a pickaxe or a smith hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; then press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into the inventory by itself). See Structures.

The catalogue also holds forms such as the Knife, Flint and Steel, Broom and Lockpick, which at present have no fixed source on the maps.

The bucket is not the only vessel for water: the Empty Flask and the Empty Waterskin can hold water too, and once full they can water things or be drunk (restoring a little health).

Tool efficiency

The same blow works differently on different materials: an axe is most efficient on wood, and only a quarter as efficient on stone; a pickaxe is most efficient on stone and ore. A tool softer than the material does nothing to it, and says “Too hard.” A stone axe fells a tree in two blows.

Tools used in crafting are not used up

Some recipes can be made only “carrying” a certain tool; splitting a log into planks, for instance, needs an axe carried. The tool just stays in the inventory and is not used up. In the crafting panel, a needed tool has a ✓ or ✗ beside it, with the tip “A tool it needs carried; not used up”.

Where to get them

  • Made by hand (C): the Stone Axe, Stone Pickaxe, Stone Hoe, Stone Sickle and Stone Smith Hammer (stone, sticks, cut grass), and the Bucket (3 planks and 1 cut grass). See Crafting.
  • On the maps: the “Old Axe” by a stump at the Ford, the “Old Pickaxe” at the mouth of the Old Mine. The toolbox by the garden in Firstlight Village holds a Steel Hoe, a Bronze Sickle, a Steel Smith Hammer and a Water Bucket.
  • Quests: Joss's quest The Ninth Nail rewards a Smith Hammer.
