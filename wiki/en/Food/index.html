Food

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Food is any item that can be eaten or drunk. Eating restores satiety; drinking potions and drinks restores health. Raw meat, fish, eggs and mushrooms can be cooked over a fire, and cooked food is more filling.

Eating

  • Hold the food in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Eat X?”, and confirming eats it.
  • Eating restores only satiety, not health directly. With satiety at 90% or more, health slowly recovers by itself. See Satiety and Health.
  • Herbs are the exception: they can be eaten and also restore a little health.
  • Raw food does no harm when eaten; it is just less filling than cooked.
  • Food does not spoil.
  • Skills can make food more filling or make satiety drop more slowly; see Skills.

Drinking

  • Hold a potion or drink in your hand and left-click to use it on yourself; you are asked “Drink X?”, and drinking it restores health.
  • A Healing Potion restores the most; Wine Bottle, Mug of Ale, Antidote and Murky Potion restore less.
  • A Bucket, Empty Flask or Empty Waterskin holding water can be drunk too, restoring 2 health; the container is left behind empty.
  • A Bucket of Milk restores both 4 health and 4 satiety; once drunk, the bucket is left behind empty.
  • Buckets, flasks and waterskins are tools: their left click fills them with water, waters or splashes; to drink what is in one, hold it and right-click yourself.
  • A Fireproof Draught restores no health; for 5 game hours after drinking it, fire does only a quarter of its damage and cannot set you alight (the status “fireproof”).
  • Using a drink on someone else makes them drink it.

Cooking

Main article: Cooking
  • Holding raw food, right-click a lit cooking fire: a campfire, brazier, fireplace, forge or cauldron. The raw food in your hand is cooked: “It sizzles and cooks.”
  • Pressing G at a lit cooking fire while holding raw food does the same; letting the fire burn it cooks it too. Lamp posts, candles and lantern posts are not cooking fires; food put on them does not cook.
  • What can be cooked: Raw Fish → Grilled Fish, Raw Beef → Steak, Raw Chicken → Roast Chicken, Mushroom → Roasted Mushroom, Egg → Boiled Egg.
  • There is no cooking interface, and no recipes with several ingredients.

Where to get it

  • On the ground: Berries grow on Cloudtop and in the tutorial's Clearing; the ones in the Clearing slowly grow back.
  • Farming: fourteen vegetables: Carrot, Turnip, Tomato and Pumpkin, plus Parsnip, Potato, Radish, Cauliflower, Melon, Corn, Blueberries, Strawberry, Hot Pepper and Eggplant, whose seeds are bought at the trading stall on the Held Ground. See Farming, Crops and Seeds.
  • Animals: Cows on the Grassland and the Held Ground leave Raw Beef when knocked down, and chickens leave Raw Chicken; a hen lays an egg every 3 game hours; a well-fed cow can be milked for a Bucket of Milk (drinking it restores health and satiety too). See Animals.
  • Fishing: Raw Fish, Sunfish, Perch, Catfish, Eel, Pike, Golden Carp and Dawnscale, all of which cook into Grilled Fish. See Fishing.
  • Quests and people: Bread is the most common reward, and Hesper also gives you a loaf the first time you meet; Brin gives Cheese; Sister Ilse gives two Healing Potions in the quest The Pilgrim Before; the herbalist Wren brews Fireproof Draughts.
  • Dawn Hill: the dawnbloom there is a kind of herb.

Forms such as Apple, Pie, Honey Jar, wine and ale have no fixed source on the maps at present.

Giving it to others

Use G to hand food to a person, and they take it. Tobin is starving and will trade the Frost Wand on his cart for any one portion of food. Right-clicking a person while holding food feeds it to them (pressing E talks to them): their satiety goes up (and herbs restore a little health too).

Right-click a creature (a monster or animal) while holding something edible: “Feed Apple to green slime”, and it gulps it down (“The green slime snaps up the apple.”), with a happy emote over its head. Creatures have no satiety; swallowing does not heal them and does not change how they feel about you: an aggressive monster still attacks you after eating. What is swallowed is gone, and does not drop again if you knock the creature down. Bosses eat nothing. Animals also swallow ordinary food this way, but only Animal Feed and Wheat count as feeding them (see Feeding). Pressing G at a creature does nothing. See Handing things to creatures.

Harvests, eggs, milk and raw fish can be sold to the trading stall on the Held Ground; see Money.

History

  • 2026-10-09: Left-clicking a creature while holding something edible makes it gulp it down; animals swallowing ordinary food does not count as feeding them.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
