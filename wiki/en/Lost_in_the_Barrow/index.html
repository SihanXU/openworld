Lost in the Barrow is a Chapter 1 main quest given by Rowan, the elder of Firstlight Village. On the day of the storm, Lina and Oskar from the village took shelter in the barrow at the far end of Thornwood Hollow, and were trapped inside by the dead. The barrow goes down three floors: find the two of them, defeat the warden at the very bottom, and come back to Rowan.

The quest starts by itself the first time you enter Firstlight Village and have watched the welcome scene, with no need to talk to Rowan first; once you have found both and defeated the warden, you go back and hand it in to him.

  • To the barrow entrance: walk to the spot between the two standing stones at the bottom of Thornwood Hollow.
  • Find Lina: talk to her (E) in the western cell of the barrow (first floor).
  • Find Oskar: talk to him in the south-east cell of The Ossuary of the Thorn, the second floor.
  • Defeat the warden: defeat the barrow warden in The Warden's Sanctum, the third floor.

Apart from the first objective, which you must pass first, the other three can be done in any order. While the quest is under way, Lina and Oskar have a “?” over their heads, and the map markers point at them and at the barrow entrance. When the last three objectives are all complete, the journal reads “I found them both and put the warden down. Now back to Rowan.”, and one last objective appears, “Back to Rowan”.

Walkthrough

  1. Setting out: from the southern exit of Firstlight Village (where Holt stands guard) head south, through the Grassland, the Forest and Thornwood Hollow in turn. The path in the Forest first bends south-west, and the exit to Thornwood Hollow is in the Forest's south-west corner. The Grassland, the Forest and Thornwood Hollow are all combat zones, with zombies and bats after dark; Thornwood Hollow has green slimes, cave spiders and wolves even by day.
  2. The barrow entrance: the path through Thornwood Hollow slants all the way to the south-east, and between the two standing stones at the bottom of the hollow is the entrance; walk there and the first objective is complete. North of the entrance stands a waypoint statue; wake it and you can teleport straight back on the way home.
  3. The barrow (first floor): go down the steps on the north side; in the hall there are 3 skeletons and 1 bat. Head west into the cell and Lina's scene plays first; then press E on her, she says “Took you long enough. ...The way's clear? Fine. Tell Rowan I'm alive,” and the second objective is complete. The stairs down are in the eastern cell.
  4. The Ossuary of the Thorn (second floor): once down, go south and then east into the hall of burial niches, where there are 4 skeletons, 1 ghost and 1 bat. Go south-east into Oskar's cell; after his scene plays, press E on him and the third objective is complete. The stairs down are in the north-east corner.
  5. The Warden's Sanctum (third floor): a round stone chamber, with the warden standing before the altar and a skeleton on either side. Defeat the warden and the fourth objective is complete, and a scene plays; it drops Tomas's Belt of Vigil.
  6. Back to the village: go back the way you came, or teleport back to Firstlight Village from the world map (you must have woken the village's statue first). By day (7:00–21:00) Rowan stands on the porch of the middle hut on the north side, with a “?” over his head. Press E on him to hand in.

Lina and Oskar go back to the village only after you have handed in; until then they stay in their cells.

Things to pick up on the way

  • Thornproof Boots on the dry-grass slope of Thornwood Hollow (Fine, Defense 2).
  • The Rusted Helm of the Thorn (Crude) near Lina's cell in the barrow.
  • Brother Odo's Pauldrons and Sister Maren's Ring in front of the burial niches of the Ossuary of the Thorn (both Vowbound gear).
  • Tomas's Belt of Vigil, dropped by the warden (Vowbound).

If you defeat the White Knight first

If you cross the river and defeat The White Knight Alberic before handing this quest in, it is treated as “passed” and simply ends, and Lina and Oskar come home by themselves. If by then you had found both and defeated the warden, it still completes and pays its rewards (the journal reads “The road went on, and this was done along the way.”); otherwise there is no reward (the journal reads “The road has gone past this: the two gatherers found their own way home.”).

Rewards

  • Experience +60 (Levels and Experience)
  • Bread×2
  • Rowan's Old Hauberk (Fine): Breastplate, defence 7, level 2
  • Regard Rowan +15 (Friendship)
  • Regard Lina +20 (Friendship)
  • Regard Oskar +20 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

After you hand in: Lina and Oskar return to Firstlight Village, and the next main quest, The Vigil, begins by itself.
