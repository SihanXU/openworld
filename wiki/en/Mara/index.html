— Mara

Mara is the elder of Emberfall, 71 years old. She has buried two husbands and a season's harvest burned by dragonfire, and refuses to bury this town too. By day she keeps watch over the town square, counting the buckets by the well.

The line of Emberfall and the dragon of Cinder Peak was written before the Five Sufferings setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See Emberfall and the Dragon and Chapter 2.

Relationships

She trusts the smith Garrick and has given him the town gate to guard; she pays for the coal he smiths with. She taught the herbalist Wren to read.

Quests

  • The Dragon Road (Main)

Mara's part belongs to the main quest The Dragon Road: finding her is its second objective. The first time you press E on her, the “elder's council” scene plays:

  • A dragon lives on Cinder Peak, and has come down twice this spring;
  • The smith Garrick has the pattern of a sword that can cut through dragonhide, but lacks iron and coal, which are to be found in The Old Mine;
  • The herbalist Wren can brew a draught against fire;
  • She gives the player two Bread: “No one fights well on an empty stomach.”

The objectives after that are bringing materials to Garrick and Wren. Once the dragon is dead, she means to carve the player's name on the well.

Lore

Mara is the guide of the Emberfall line. That line is the old main story “from the broken bridge to the slain dragon”; see Emberfall and the Dragon.

Trivia

  • Her role is “village head”, and talking to her triggers the elder's council; the role of Rowan, the elder of Firstlight Village, is “villager”.
  • She shares a character sprite with Marta and Lina.
