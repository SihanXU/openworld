Boars in the Coppice is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Orrin, the charcoal burner of The Charcoal Dell. Boars have been rooting up the stumps in the coppices around the dell, so no new wood can grow; he asks you to defeat 4 boars in the Charcoal Dell.

Orrin only asks this of you once you reach level 2.

Objectives

  1. Boars felled in the dell
    Defeat boar×4 (The Charcoal Dell)
  2. Back to Orrin
    Talk to Orrin

Only boars defeated on the Charcoal Dell map after you take the quest count.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: from level 2, go to the Charcoal Dell and find Orrin, who keeps by the clamp by day (6:00–21:00); press E on him and choose “Accept ‘Boars in the Coppice’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Fight the boars: at the start there are 4 boars in the coppices north and south of the dell, and they charge at you; once defeated, another may appear every 1500 seconds, up to 3 at a time. A boar has 18 health, 4 attack, 1 defense and is fast, so do not go empty-handed. A defeated boar has a chance to drop the Tusked Belt (see monster trophies).
  3. Hand in: go back to Orrin and press E to hand in.

Dialogue

When offering
“Boars in the coppice again. Six years of growth, rooted up in one night. Put four of them down and the rest will keep to the deep woods. Mind the tusks.”
While unfinished
“Four boars, in the woods round the dell. They come out of the undergrowth at you. Stand your ground.”
On handing in
“Quieter. The stools might come back now. Take these, the boots I cut the coppice in before my knees went.”
