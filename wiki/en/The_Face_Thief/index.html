The Face Thief is a small masked assassin whose face is a stolen mirror, with a glass dagger in each hand. He appears only in the boss challenge and the boss test arena; he has no place in the story and is not entered in the codex. His name still carries the word “Envy”: he comes from the earlier “Seven Sins” boss setting, which has since been withdrawn.

He uses his three moves in turn. Each one winds up first, marking every tile it will land on; once locked, the marks no longer follow the player. His wind-ups are the shortest of the four bosses.

Skill Area Effect Damage (phase 1 / phase 2)
Borrowed Blades A cone straight in front of him, 3 tiles deep — 24 / 32
Shattered Faces Five landing points: the tile the player is on when it locks, plus the tiles 3 away above, below, left and right of it — 20 / 27
Stolen Footstep A circle of radius 2 centered on the player's locked position After the hit he flashes to a spot beside that position 24 / 32

All damage figures are before defense. When his health falls to 50% he enters the second phase: wind-ups 20% shorter (0.8 seconds at the least), the pause after each move 35% shorter, damage 35% higher.

Behavior

  • 1000 health and 1 defense, the least of the four bosses. His body takes up just 1 tile.
  • He spots opponents within 12 tiles, and never strays more than 12 tiles from where he spawned.
  • He has no ordinary attack; all his damage comes from his three skills.

Strategy

  • His moves come fast, but their areas are small: get out as soon as the marks appear.
  • After Stolen Footstep he appears beside where the player just stood, exactly the moment to strike back.
  • He is small and lightly defended; once you land hits, his health drops fast.

Drops

  • Iron Ore×4
  • A Legendary Arms of the slayer's profession (one not yet in the world)
  • Experience: equal to its maximum health

The boss challenge saves nothing: even if you win, you cannot take the spoils with you.

Lore

He was originally Envy in the “Seven Sins” setting. The bosses' design document says his small size is deliberate: quick moves, clear tells, thin armor and teleporting, in contrast to the two hulking others.

When the Seven Sins setting was withdrawn, the other four had their skills and code deleted; he, the Velvet Lure and the Crown Without a King stayed in the boss challenge. The bosses of the story are now the Seal-bearers; where these three will go later has not been decided (to be designed).

His art was generated with image tools: a 4×5 action sheet (idle, walk, attack, cast, hit and fall, 32 pixels per frame), plus three skill effects. He is displayed at 1.25× scale. His colors are emerald and bone white.
