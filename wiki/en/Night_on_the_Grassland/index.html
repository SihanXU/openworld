Night on the Grassland is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Brin, the herder. She has lost three cows this month; as soon as it gets dark, the dead walk on the Grassland, and the herd knows it. She asks you to keep watch with her on the Grassland for a night and defeat three of the dead.

The quest has no prerequisites.

Objectives

  1. The dead put down on Dusklea
    Defeat undead×3 (Dusklea)
  2. Back to Brin
    Talk to Brin

Only the dead defeated on the Grassland map after you take the quest count. The only dead that appear on the Grassland are the zombies that come after dark; the bats that appear alongside them do not count.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: by day (6:00–21:00) Brin watches the cattle by the bench in the middle of the Grassland. Press E on her and choose “Accept ‘Night on the Grassland’” in the dialogue box. The first time you are on the Grassland at dusk (17:00–20:00), you see a scene of the herd kneeling (it plays only once), and at the end of the scene Brin also asks for your help.
  2. Wait for dark: night begins at 21:00 (brought forward to 20:00 once the White Knight has fallen). Brin goes back to the village to sleep at 21:00; you stay on the Grassland.
  3. Fight zombies: after dark, a night creature may appear on the Grassland 6 to 10 tiles from you every 6 seconds, sixty percent of them zombies, with at most 4 at a time. Defeat three zombies. At daybreak they crumble to dust.
  4. Hand in: go back to the Grassland the next day and press E on Brin to hand in.

The Old Templar wants three zombies defeated after dark, anywhere. If you take both quests, every zombie you defeat on the Grassland counts for both. The commission The Night-born, which the guards sometimes give, also counts night creatures, so the same zombie counts for all three.

Dialogue

When offering
“Three cows gone this month. Not wolves; wolves leave something. The dead walk the grass after dark now, and the herd knows it. Will you stand a night out here with me?”
While unfinished
“They come up after dark from the south end, by the trees. Put three of them down and the herd will sleep.”
On handing in
“They're lying down. Lying down properly, not kneeling. ...Here, cheese from the last of the good milk. Thank you.”
The kneeling cattle (click to show)

On the Grassland at dusk, the cows bend their forelegs and kneel one after another, all of them facing east. Brin says it started after the bridge broke; Lumi says that east is the Ford, and past the Ford is that road, and whatever is on the road, the cows are kneeling to it. When you hand in, Brin says: “They lay down. Properly lay down, not kneeling.”
