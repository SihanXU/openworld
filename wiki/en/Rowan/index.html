— Rowan

Rowan is the elder of Firstlight Village, 67 years old. When the storm came, two of the village's foragers, Lina and Oskar, took shelter in The Old Barrow beyond Thornwood Hollow, and never came out. He asks for help from everyone who can lift a weapon; the main quest Lost in the Barrow is his request.

By day he stands on his porch, looking out over the village square.

Quests

  • Lost in the Barrow (Main, giver)
  • Lost in the Barrow (main): begins on its own once the player reaches the village. Find Lina and Oskar, defeat the warden at the very bottom, and come back to hand it in to Rowan. The rewards include Bread and “Rowan's Old Hauberk”. Once it is handed in, Lina and Oskar return to the village.

Lore

In Chapter 1, Rowan stands for the village's trust in the Church. On the night of thanks he leads the whole village in giving thanks at the holy flame; after the flame is stolen, he grows harder and harder toward that “heretic”: “And if you meet that thief in grey on the road, remember what he took from us.” When the player brings the Crown of Thorns back to the village, he says: “And that heretic still has our flame.” See Chapter 1.

At the end of Chapter 1, the Grey Man leaves the player a line meant to be passed on to Rowan: “Tell Rowan the light is safer with me than with any of you.”

Trivia

  • He is the elder, but his role in the game is “villager”. The role of “village head” belongs to Mara of Emberfall.
  • He says “The Church tells us to be patient. To be honest, I am running out of patience.” This is a line for after Chapter 1 ends.
