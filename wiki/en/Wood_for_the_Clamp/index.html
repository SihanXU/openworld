Wood for the Clamp is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Orrin, the charcoal burner of The Charcoal Dell. His back can no longer carry logs, and he needs 6 logs to stack his next clamp; in exchange he offers 4 lumps of coal from the last burn and a hat.

The quest has no prerequisites.

The logs are taken when you hand them in.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: cross the Forest and follow the fork on its west side to the Charcoal Dell. In the daytime (6:00–21:00) Orrin is by the clamp in the middle of the dell, with a “!” over his head. Press E on him and choose “Accept ‘Wood for the Clamp’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Fell trees: chop trees with an axe; each tree takes two blows and falls for 2 logs and 1 stick (see Woodcutting). The coppices north and south of the dell and the Forest are full of trees; Gus has ready-cut logs on his wood pile in Firstlight Village too, but those are Gus's.
  3. Hand in: bring the 6 logs back to Orrin and press E to hand them in.

Wood for the Fire also wants logs (4 a day). Take both quests together and one trip cutting 10 logs covers them both.

Rewards

  • Experience +25 (Levels and Experience)
  • Coal×4
  • Burner's Felt Cap (Fine): Hat, defence 1, level 2
  • Regard Orrin +10 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

Coal is the fuel for Smithing (steelmaking included); Orrin sells coal himself as well.

Dialogue

When offering
“Six logs and I can build another clamp. My back will not carry them any more, and Gus's is going the same way. I will pay you in coal. It burns hotter than wood.”
While unfinished
“Six logs. Any tree will do; the coppice is nearest.”
On handing in
“That is a clamp's worth. Four days and it is charcoal. Here, coal from the last burn, and a cap. The ash gets in your hair.”
