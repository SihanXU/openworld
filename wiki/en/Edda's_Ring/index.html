Edda's Ring is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Edda, the farm woman who survived at the burned farmstead on The East Road. When the White Knight passed by, he took everything that shone, including her late husband's wedding band, which she had hung on the doorpost. The ring is in his kingdom at the far end of the road.

The quest has no prerequisites, but you can only meet Edda once you have crossed the river.

    Carry Wedding Band

The quest only accepts things of the kind Wedding Band. It is taken when you hand it in.

Walkthrough

  1. Find Edda: after crossing the river, head east through The Pilgrims' Rest, The Pilgrim's Road and The Kneeling Field onto the East Road. Halfway along, north of the road, is the burned farmstead; walk into the farmstead, then into the barn in its south-west corner, and Edda comes out to talk to you. After that, press E on her and choose “Accept ‘Edda's Ring’” in the dialogue box. She hides in the barn all day.
  2. Find the ring: the wedding band is in the kingdom of thorns at the eastern end of the East Road, in the south-east part of the ring of thorns: a gold ring on the ground (called “Golden Wedding Band”). The White Knight Alberic stands in the ring; walking into the ring plays the kingdom's scene, and he will fight you. You can defeat him first and then pick it up, or slip round and pick it up during the fight.
  3. Hand in: go back to Edda and press E to hand in. Once the White Knight has fallen, Edda moves to Firstlight Village and works in the vegetable garden in the south-east of the village by day (7:00–21:00); find her there to hand in.

You do not need to have taken the quest to pick up the ring. If you pick it up first and take the quest afterwards, talk to her once more after accepting to hand it in.

Rewards

  • Experience +30 (Levels and Experience)
  • Egg×2
  • Bread
  • Edda's Work Gloves (Fine): Gloves, defence 2, critical chance 0.03, level 4
  • Regard Edda +20 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

When you hand in, Edda gives you two eggs, a loaf of bread and “Edda's Work Gloves” (Gloves, Defense 1). The eggs are raw; you can eat them as they are, or put them on the fire to make boiled eggs.

Dialogue

“A plain band. It's all I have of him.”
On handing in
“...Thank you. I'll wear it now. I was keeping it on the gate so he could find his way home.”
What she says in the barn (click to show)

In the barn Edda says: the days grew too short and the wheat all died; then the things of the night came; and later a knight in white rode by and took the door hinges, the pots and her husband's ring. On the night the barn caught fire, it was a man in grey who pulled her out of the smoke, and she asks you not to tell the village. When you hand in, she says: she had hung the ring on the door so that he could find his way home.
