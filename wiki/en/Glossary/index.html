One-sentence explanations of the lore's terms, grouped by theme. The explanations use only the first layer of understanding (the account everyone believes early in the game); terms that would spoil the reversals are gathered in the spoiler block at the end of this page. For the game's system terms (fullness, waypoint statues…), see their own pages.

Heaven, earth and calamity

Term Explanation See
The First Night The darkness that existed before the world was formed. The sun keeps light apart from it. The First Night
The Long Night The calamity in which the First Night seeps into reality, the sun vanishes, and the world sinks into a long night. The Long Night
The First Long Night What the game calls the first Long Night. The Long Night
The Second Long Night The Church's warning: once the five Seals fail completely, the Long Night will come again. The Long Night
The First Fire The fire of Firstlight Village, said to have been taken from the first ray of dawn after the First Long Night. In Chapter 1 the Grey Man takes it. Church of the Five Sufferings

Faith

Term Explanation See
The Church of the Five Sufferings The religion Astra's followers founded; people in the game usually just say “the Church”. Church of the Five Sufferings
The Five Sufferings The five holy torments Astra bore. The Five Sufferings
The Crowning The First Suffering: wearing the crown of thorns. The Church says it means giving up human pride and will. The Crowning
The Scourging The Second Suffering: being bound to a pillar and scourged. The Church says it means repaying everyone's sins with the flesh. The Scourging
The Starving The Third Suffering: giving up food and drink and being weighed on the scales against grain, sin and life. The Church says it means freedom from the desires of the flesh. The Starving
The Nailing The Fourth Suffering: being nailed to the holy wood. The Church says it means accepting death. The Nailing
The Piercing The Fifth Suffering: the holy lance pierces the chest. At that moment the sun rose again. The Piercing
The Great Rite of the Five Sufferings The end of the pilgrimage: the rite of the Five Sufferings held again in the Holy City. The Great Rite
The Instruments of the Sufferings The five instruments of torment used in the Five Sufferings, the Church's highest relics. Instruments of the Sufferings
The Crown of Thorns The first holy instrument, the instrument of the First Suffering. In the game, an item you can wear. Crown of Thorns
The Pillar of Scourging The second holy instrument. The Pillar of Scourging
The Scales of Hunger The third holy instrument. The Scales of Hunger
The Holy Wood of the Nails The fourth holy instrument. The Holy Wood of the Nails
The Lance That Pierced the Heart The fifth holy instrument. The Lance That Pierced the Heart
Suffering is not God's abandonment The first half of the core doctrine of the Church of the Five Sufferings; the second half is “Suffering is the proof the debt is being paid.” Doctrine
The Era of the Five Sufferings The calendar used by documents in the game. Timeline

People

Term Explanation See
Astra The saint who bore the Five Sufferings and ended the first Long Night. Astra
The First Martyr The title later ages gave Astra. Astra
Seal-bearer One of the five people who share Astra's pain, each holding one Seal and tied to one holy instrument. Seal-bearers
Seal What a Seal-bearer holds; there are five Seals, matching the Five Sufferings. The First Seal is the Crown of Thorns. Seal-bearers
Alberic, the White Knight The first Seal-bearer, the boss of Chapter 1. The White Knight Alberic
Matthias, Giver of War The second Seal-bearer. Matthias, Giver of War
Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale The third Seal-bearer. Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale
Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins The fourth Seal-bearer. Nacht, Holder of Death's Reins
The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar The fifth Seal-bearer. The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar
The Pilgrim The one the Church names to find the five Seal-bearers and recover the five holy instruments. In Chapter 1, the protagonist. The Pilgrimage and pilgrims
The Grey Man The man who turns up everywhere in Chapter 1 and takes the First Fire. In the setting he is Severin, the Greyvowed. The Grey Man
Lumi The fairy who follows the protagonist. Lumi

Orders and institutions

Term Explanation See
The Holy City The center of the Church of the Five Sufferings and the end of the pilgrimage. The Holy City
The Holy See The Church's highest body; it issues notices and has stationed a priest at the Pilgrims' Rest. The Holy City
The Pilgrimage The mission the Church calls when the Long Night returns. The Pilgrimage and pilgrims
The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage The document with which the Church calls the pilgrimage and names the pilgrim. Proclamation of the Pilgrimage
The Scallop Badge The pilgrim's mark; everyone who is named receives one. Pilgrim's Scallop
The Pilgrim's Vow The oath a pilgrim takes kneeling before the five holy wounds before setting out, led by the priest at the Pilgrims' Rest. Chapel of the Five
Crown-holder The reckoning of years on the kneeling stones, counted from when the first Seal-bearer put on the Crown of Thorns. He has held for three hundred years. The Kneeling Field
The Ashen Watch The old order that kept the fire taken from the first ray of dawn, dissolved ten years ago. The Ashen Watch
Heretic What the Church declared the last few brothers of the Ashen Watch; the people of Firstlight Village also call the Grey Man this. The Ashen Watch
The Order of the Thorn An order of knights buried beneath Thornwood Hollow; the White Knight was its Knight-Commander, and its abbey lies north of the Pilgrim's Road. The Order of the Thorn
The Sunwatch Post A post the Church once kept on Cloudtop to record the time of sunset. The Watchman's Log

Places and relics

Term Explanation See
Firstlight Village The village named for the first dawn after the First Long Night. Firstlight Village
The Chapel of the First Dawn The old chapel on top of Dawn Hill, where the First Fire was lit. Chapel of the First Dawn
Dawnstone The stone on Dawn Hill carved “HERE THE SUN ROSE AGAIN”. Dawn Hill
Five-wound shrine stone A roadside holy mark of the Five Sufferings carved with five wounds. On the one at the Mountain Foot, the first wound has cracked. Five-wound shrine stone
Kneeling stone A stone carved as a pilgrim kneeling, raised for pilgrims who would not kneel to the White Knight. Those in his kingdom are nameless; those on the Kneeling Field are carved with names and the year of the Crown-holder. The East Road, The Kneeling Field
Wayside shrine The five shrines on the Pilgrim's Road, one for each Suffering; each lights when what its Suffering asks for is laid on it. Wayside shrine
Writ of Relief The Holy See's document promising to send someone to relieve the first Seal-bearer after forty years; it was never sent. Writ of Relief
The Held Ground The land the Oath-Thorn opens after the White Knight falls; the protagonist's camp. The Held Ground
The Oath-Thorn The one thorn on the Crown of Thorns that is still green; with it you go in and out of the Held Ground. The Oath-Thorn
The Most Holy Hall The place of the main story's Final Chapter. The Most Holy Hall

Lore terms in the game's systems

Term Explanation See
Boon The part of a Seal's power that Lumi catches when a Seal-bearer falls. Boons
Crown's Insight The Boon of the First Seal: experience gained +100%. Boons
Vowbound Items left behind by those who swore vows; an item quality. Vowbound
Vow The special effect each piece of Vowbound gear carries: a condition and a reward; there are ten. Vowbound
Stigmata Items bearing one of the First Martyr's five wounds; the highest item quality (Stigmatic). Stigmata
Legendary Arms The weapon a Seal-bearer leaves, on falling, for the profession of the one who felled them. Legendary Arms

Term Explanation See

The Vessel A being able to carry the First Night. The Five Sufferings are the rite that remakes an ordinary person into a vessel. Nightbearer
