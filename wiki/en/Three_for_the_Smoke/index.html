Three for the Smoke is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Hilde, the fishwife of The Reed Bend. She smokes fish to sell and wants 3 fish for the smoking fire, any kind at all.

The quest has no prerequisites.

Objectives

  1. Fish
    Carry Fish×3
  2. Back to Hilde
    Talk to Hilde

Any kind of fish counts: Raw Fish, Sunfish, Perch, Eel, Pike, Catfish, Golden Carp, Dawnscale. The fish are taken when you hand them in, the first three she receives.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: cross the Forest and follow the path from the clearing on its east side to the Reed Bend. In the daytime (5:00–22:00) Hilde stands by the jetty, with a “!” over her head. Press E on her and choose “Accept ‘Three for the Smoke’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Make a fishing rod: two sticks and two handfuls of cut grass make a fishing rod by hand in the Crafting panel (C); Hilde also sells fishing rods and bait. Used on a fishing rod, bait makes fish bite twice as fast for the next five casts.
  3. Fish: with the rod in hand, stand on the bank or the jetty and left-click the water; see Fishing. By day it is mostly sunfish and perch; after dark (20:00–5:00) there are eels, and casting from the jetty into deep water brings pike.
  4. Hand in: bring the 3 fish back to Hilde and press E to hand them in.

You can also catch fish in the pond and the lake of The Held Ground, or buy them from Hilde (she sells 4 raw fish and 1 eel) — fish bought from her count when you give them back to her.

Dialogue

When offering
“You want to be useful? Three fish for the smoke, any kind. Make a rod from two sticks and two handfuls of grass, and cast off the pier. I sell bait, if you are in a hurry.”
While unfinished
“Three fish. The pier, the shallows, anywhere there is water. Eels come after dark.”
On handing in
“Those will smoke. Here, bait, and my spare oilskin. You will want it on this river.”
