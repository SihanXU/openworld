Trophies

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Trophies are the gear monsters drop. Every kind of monster has one trophy piece that belongs to it (one piece, not a set). Each time you fell one, there is a chance that piece appears straight in the bag of whoever felled it.

Rules

  • Who gets it: whoever fells it (the last blow). The piece never lands on the ground; it goes straight into the bag, while an icon flies into the bag on screen; when the player gets one, the lower left says “A trophy: X!”.
  • Drop rate: 4% for each ordinary monster, 10% for the stronger ones, and certain for the boss-like monsters (Slime King, cyclops, the dragon of Cinder Peak, barrow warden).
  • Pity: if 49 of one kind of monster are felled in a row without the piece dropping, the 50th surely drops it. Each person keeps their own count for each kind of monster; once it drops, the count for that kind starts over.
  • Main stats come from the level: every trophy has its own level (the stronger the monster, the higher the level), and its defense and attack are worked out from the level, not from its material or quality: a piece of a set counts as Fine (×1.25), a standalone piece as Masterwork (×1.5).
  • Random affixes: when it drops it rolls 4 affixes all the same, so the same trophy is different every time it drops.
  • Only from monsters: it cannot be forged, and no merchant sells it.
  • Tooltip: hover over it and it reads “✧ Trophy · dropped by X”.
  • Each monster's page names its trophy and drop rate in its “Drops” section.

Sets

The trophies of one family of monsters make up a set: a six-piece armor set (head, shoulders, body, hands, belt, feet), or a three-piece trinket set (necklace, earring, ring). Two pieces belong to no set; they stand alone, and their main stats are higher than those of any one piece of a set. For each set's bonuses, see monster sets.

Not yet obtainable

No map, respawn or story beat yet puts out Slime King, water slime, spark slime, giant crab, ice golem or storm wisp, so their trophies cannot be had for now. So at present you can gather at most 3 pieces of the Gelatin Armour, at most 5 of the Wild Hunt Armour, and at most 1 of the Elemental Court Trinkets.

Tips

  • A 4% drop rate gives one piece every 25 kills on average; if your luck is bad, pity makes the 50th certain.
  • There is only one each of the barrow warden, the cyclops and the the dragon of Cinder Peak, and each surely drops its piece when felled. The dragon whelp appears only in the dragon fight.
  • When gathering a set only the slot counts: two rings of the same set count as one piece; see Gear Sets.

History

  • 2026-10-12: Trophies added: one piece for every kind of monster, 4% for ordinary ones, 10% for stronger ones, certain for bosses, with pity at 50 of one kind; five monster sets and two standalone pieces.
  • 2026-10-13: Every piece got a level (the stronger the monster, the higher), with defense and attack worked out from it; stealth, guard reduction and perception replaced by luck.
