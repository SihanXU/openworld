The boar is a thick-skinned beast with a little defense. There is one in the bushes on The East Road, and more in the deep woodland of The Wild. Their tempers differ: the one on the East Road will not strike unless you provoke it, while those in the wild charge anyone they see.

Spawning

  • The Charcoal Dell: 4 at the start, respawns (up to 3, once every 150 s)
  • The Sunrise Road: 1 at the start
  • The East Road: 1, by the bushes south of the road. Once felled, it does not come back.
  • The wild: 1 on each deep-woodland map.

Behavior

  • Temper: the one on the East Road is wary: only those who have hit it are chased and attacked. Those in the wild are aggressive: they charge anyone they see within 4 tiles. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: fairly fast. About 0.5 seconds per tile.
  • Element: none.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire.
  • Flammable. Like other beasts, when a fire starts nearby, the flames spread to it.

Skills

  • Charge: in the same row or column as you, 2–5 tiles away, it paws the ground for 1.2 seconds, with a red line marked out to 5 tiles, then lowers its head and charges: the first enemy it hits takes attack × 2 damage, is knocked back 2 tiles and is stunned. It cannot stop — if it runs headlong into a wall, tree or rock, it is the one stunned, for 2 seconds. Standing in front of something hard and waiting for it to charge is the easiest way to fight a boar.

Strategy

  • You can go round the one on the East Road.
  • It has 1 defense: every hit does 1 less damage, but always at least 1. See Combat.

Drops

  • Raw Beef×2 (makeup 4 × 50%)
  • Leather Hide (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Tusked Belt (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 72 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves the meat and Leather Hide listed above lying where it fell; press E to pick them up (see Monster Drops). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

Lore

The boar has no place in the story. Its sprite is borrowed from a reddish-brown tusked monster called “bear” in the asset pack: the pack has a separate boar sprite, but it has only two side-on frames, not four directions.
