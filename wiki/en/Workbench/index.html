Workbench

The workbench is a wooden bench. Nothing of the game's own is made beside it any more: the Club, the Shield and the Bow used to be made at a bench, and are now crafted anywhere once the Workshop is built on the Held Ground (Expanding the Held Ground). The workbench itself can be made anywhere from 4 Plank, and what comes out is called a “wooden workbench”. Knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself) and you can carry it with you. Hold it and right-click a cell and it stands there, ready to use (putting it down with G only leaves it loose on the ground; it does not stand up).

Source

  • Placed on maps: Firstlight Village, The House in the Lane, The Mill
  • Crafting: Plank×4
  • Make one yourself: press C; 4 planks, no tool needed, anywhere.
  • Place one in Building mode: press B and choose “Wooden workbench”. If you carry one, it is put down directly; if not, one is first made from 4 planks you carry and then put down (see Building).
  • Ready-made: Firstlight Village has one, standing on the west side of the village.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Its panel

Press E on a workbench (“Work at the workbench”) and its own panel opens: what is made at a bench, from what you carry, into your bag. None of the game's own recipes is a bench's now, so the panel usually says “Nothing is made here yet”; what the world learns to make at a bench appears there.

The three things that used to be made beside it are made anywhere once the Workshop stands on the Held Ground (see Crafting):

Recipe Materials Notes
Wooden club 1 Log A blunt weapon anyone can use
Wooden shield 3 planks, 1 Stick Only a Warrior can wear it
Wooden bow 2 sticks, 3 Cut Grass Recipe level 2; only a Ranger can fight with it

Other uses

  • Bench top: hold something and press G at it, and the thing is put on the bench top.
  • Room use: a room with a workbench inside counts as a “workshop”. For now this use has no effect.
  • It can be taken away: knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and the whole bench comes loose where it stood; press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself). It is not damaged.

It cannot be broken, but it burns

A workbench has no durability, so hitting it with a weapon takes none off (an axe, pickaxe or hammer knocks the whole bench loose; see above). Whatever you do to it with something that the rules do not define is something the world has not seen. It is made of wood and catches fire: burnt out, it becomes Ash, and what was on the bench top falls where it stood.

History

  • 2026-10-04: Shields, clubs and bows have to be made beside a workbench; Firstlight Village got one (W4).
  • 2026-10-05: The workbench can be made from planks anywhere, picked up and carried, and placed directly in Building mode.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
  • 2026-10-21: The club, shield and bow are no longer made beside a workbench; they unlock with the Workshop on the Held Ground and are made anywhere. E on a workbench opens its panel.
