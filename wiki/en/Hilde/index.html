“Forty years on this water, and I never once saw a god in it. Fish, weed and fog, that's all.”

— Hilde

Hilde is the fisherwoman of The Reed Bend, 47 years old, and plain-spoken. She fishes from the jetty and smokes eels, and sells fish, bait and fishing rods. Nine winters ago her husband Rolf took the boat out into the mist; the boat came back on its own, and he did not. The boat bell still lies on the spit, among the water slimes, and she does not go there.

Her first words are: “If you want fish, I've got fish. If you want talk, the wood's full of trees.” She tells you which fish bite when, and how to make a fishing rod (see Fishing). She sells Raw Fish, Eel, Bait and Fishing Rods.

Quests

  • Three for the Smoke (Side, giver)
  • The Bell on the Spit (Side, giver)
  • Three for the Smoke: 3 fish of any kind, for 5 Bait and “Hilde's Oilskin”.
  • The Bell on the Spit: bring her Rolf's boat bell from the spit. The reward is 2 Grilled Fish and “Rolf's Net Ring”, with luck.

Lore

Hilde does not believe in gods, but she is not the kind who spreads unbelief: tell her “there's no god watching at all” and she only pauses; she does not get angry. After you have read the sunken stone on the shore, she says a line she says only once: that stone was there before there was a village, “before the Church came to teach us what to repent of”, and whoever carved it thought the gift of one fish worth recording, “and so do I”. See The Reed Bend and Lore.

When you bring back the bell she says: “It still sounds the same. I thought it would have changed.” She gives you Rolf's Net Ring, saying it only works on a hand that goes places.

Trivia

  • She is the first person to sell Fishing Rods. Making one yourself takes only two sticks and two handfuls of grass.
