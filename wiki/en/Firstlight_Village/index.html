Firstlight Village is a small village ringed by woods, west of the Ford. It is named for the first light of dawn after the First Long Night, and the fire on its square is said to have been taken from that very light. Most of Chapter 1's people live here, and most quests are taken here.

Firstlight Village is a peaceful zone; no monsters appear at night. It has an exit on each side and is the hub of the country round the Ford: the Ford to the east, Millbrook to the west, Dawn Hill to the north, and the Grassland to the south.

Map

The map is 60×40, ringed by woods two to four tiles deep. The road from the Ford enters the village from the east; north of the road stand the general store and the smithy; it goes on past Tobin's handcart to the flagstone square in the middle. North of the square, behind Astra's altar, stands Firstlight Chapel. Three cottages stand in a row to the north and one to the west; south of the square is the pond, west of the pond a hot spring lies in the woods, and the vegetable garden is in the southeast corner. On either side of the lane south toward the forest are the two houses in the lane. Other lanes lead to the west, north and south exits.

Areas

  • The square and the holy flame: the flagstone square in the middle of the village. A campfire burns on the square: the village's holy flame (also called the First Fire), which Sister Ilse keeps by day. Beside the fire is Astra's altar; press E to read: “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.” By the fire is a log bench, and on the ground a chalk drawing of a grey man (one of Pell's Drawings).
  • Firstlight Chapel: behind the altar and the holy flame is Sister Ilse's chapel (Firstlight Chapel); step out of its door and one step down is Astra's altar. The sign by the door reads: “FIRSTLIGHT CHAPEL. The flame and Astra's altar, kept by Sister Ilse. Skill books at the bookstand within; ask Brother Anselm.” Inside are an altar carved with the five holy wounds, candlestands and benches; Brother Anselm keeps the chapel bookstand and sells skill books. It is not the same place as the Chapel of the First Dawn on Dawn Hill.
  • The well, the notice board and the commission board: north of the square is a well, and the notice board beside it reads: “FIRSTLIGHT VILLAGE. East: the Ford. West: Millbrook. North: Dawn Hill. South: the grassland, and the forest beyond.” Left of the notice board is the commission board, listing the village's commissions no one has taken yet (see commissions). Two lit street lamps also stand on the square.
  • Waypoint statue: on the west side of the square stands a waypoint statue (named “Firstlight”).
  • Cottages: three to the north, one to the west, and two more in the lane to the south. You can walk into each one: walk up from the doorway to go in, and inside are a bed, table and chairs, a fireplace and a chest. The middle one to the north is the Elder's House (the elder Rowan stands before its porch by day), the northwest one is Marta's House, the northeast one the Watch House, the west one the Woodcutter's House, and to the south are the House in the Lane and the Little Cabin.
  • Tobin's handcart: by the road on the east side of the square. On the cart lie a Frost Wand and a Flask of Oil, both Tobin's. For trading food for the wand, see “Ways across” on the Ford's page.
  • The shops by the east road: on the north side of the road into the village from the Ford, two houses you can enter, west to east, each with a sign at its door, and two street lamps by the road:
    • General Store: Greta stands behind the counter, selling sundries, food and potions, and buying herbs, hides and eggs.
    • The Smithy: inside are a lit forge and an anvil anyone may use (see smithing); the smith Haldor works there by day.
  • The training ground: the open ground beside the northeast cottage, where the profession mentor Bram stands. Once you reach level 5, choose a profession beside him.
  • The carpenter's corner: south of the west cottage, with a workbench and an open wood pile. Joss planes boards at the workbench by day, and Gus stacks the wood he has cut on the pile in the afternoon. Beside the workbench you can make things that need a workbench (see crafting).
  • The pond: south of the square, where Mia skips stones by day.
  • The healing spring: a hot spring in the woods west of the pond; the sign beside it reads “Warm spring — sit a while, and it mends you.” Step into the spring and you regain 1 health at once, and 1 more every half second you stay in (the status “soaking”). The first time you come near, Lumi points it out to you.
  • The vegetable garden: a 4×4 plot of farmland in the southeast corner. Its four columns are carrots, turnips, tomatoes and pumpkins; the first three rows are at the sprout, young plant and ripe stages, and the last row is empty, ready for sowing. Beside it, the Garden Supplies (crate, 24 slots, durability 12) holds 2 portions of seed for each of the four crops, a steel Hoe, a bronze Sickle, a Water Bucket, a steel Smith Hammer, and 4 each of planks, sticks and stones. See Farming, Crops.
  • The lane south: the head of the lane to the Grassland, where the guard Holt keeps watch.

Lore

The village's name, “Firstlight”, means the first dawn after the First Long Night: the morning Astra's fifth suffering ended. Sister Ilse says the fire on the square was taken from that day's first light; in the Chapel of the First Dawn on Dawn Hill stands the fire altar that lit it. Behind the fire and Astra's altar is Sister Ilse's own chapel, where the villagers kneel before the five holy wounds and give thanks for the holy flame outside its door. Firstlight believes what the Church teaches: suffering is not God's abandonment.

What happens in the village in Chapter 1 (click to show)
  • The first time you come into the village, Sister Ilse welcomes you by the fire, Pell wants to draw Lumi, and the elder Rowan says two of the villagers have gone missing (Lost in the Barrow).
  • Once the two are rescued and The Vigil is handed in, at dusk (17:00–21:00) Ilse tells the villagers the story of the First Long Night and the Five Sufferings; after dark (21:00–24:00) the whole village gives thanks before the holy flame. As the thanksgiving ends, a man in a grey cloak scoops the holy flame into a lantern; Holt cries thief, and the man walks off east, the river freezing under his feet to let him cross. From then on the holy flame is out.
  • The next morning, the Holy City's courier comes to the village, reads out the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, names you a pilgrim, and leaves a copy of the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage.
  • When you come back with the Crown of Thorns, Rowan is furious that the heretic still holds the holy flame, Ilse says “He always did carry things that were too heavy for him,” and Nessa says the sun set four minutes early tonight. Chapter 1 ends here, and the chapter card appears.
