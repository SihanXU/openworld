The zombie is one of the undead that roam the wild at night. It is placed on no map; it only comes up out of the dark after nightfall, one of the two night creatures (the other is the bat). It is the slowest-walking monster in the game, and it crumbles to dust once day breaks.

Spawning

  • The Pilgrim's Road: respawns (up to 2, once every 240 s)
  • Night Creatures: outdoor maps in combat zones, after nightfall
  • It appears only at night, on the outdoor maps of combat zones, such as The Grassland, The Forest, Thornwood Hollow, Mountain Foot, The East Road, Dawn Hill, Cinder Pass, The Clearing and The Wild. Peaceful zones (Firstlight Village, The Ford and the like) and underground and indoor maps never have any.
  • Every so often during the night, a night creature comes up 6 to 10 tiles from the player, and six in ten of them are zombies; at most 4 can be on one map at once.
  • After daybreak they crumble to dust and vanish, one after another.

See Night Creatures. As the Seal-bearers fall, the nights grow longer, and so does the time they roam; see Day and Night.

Behavior

  • Temper: aggressive. It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 8 tiles, and gives up only once the chase takes it 14 tiles away. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: slowest. About 1 second per tile.
  • Element: none.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing by a fire.

  • Grasp: when it is right next to you, it lights a purple cross on the four tiles above, below, left and right of itself, and grabs 1 second later: damage is attack × 1, and whoever it grabs is Mired for 2.5 seconds (moves slowly). It is slow; when you see the cross, step away diagonally.

Strategy

  • It is so slow that you always have time to back off when fighting it head-on. The real danger is several of them closing in at once.
  • The safest place at night is beside a lit campfire, or shut inside a house you built yourself. See Building, Rooms.
  • Two quests ask you to fight zombies: Fennick's The Old Templar asks you to put down 3 zombies; Brin's Night on the Grassland asks you to put down 3 undead on the grassland.

Drops

  • Bone (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Bolt of Cloth (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Gravebell Earring (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 38 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press E to pick them up (see Monster Drops). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

Lore

“The dead find no rest” is one of the signs that the Long Night is drawing near. Holt says: “The dead walk the wild at night. Not here, though. Never here.”
