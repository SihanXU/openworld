Health

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Health is shown as ten hearts at the upper left of the toolbar. The player starts with 40 health; each heart stands for a tenth of maximum health (4 points each at the start), is drawn in quarters, and empties from right to left. Hover the mouse over the hearts to see “Health current / maximum”. When health reaches zero the player collapses and the death screen appears; see Difficulty.

Taking damage

Source Notes
Attacks The attacker's attack plus the weapon's power, minus your defense, at least 1 point. Dodge (capped at 50%) can make an attack miss entirely, and a shield in the off hand blocks attacks at its block rate (capped at 50%). See Combat for details
Elements An attack with an element also has that element's effect; elemental resistance reduces the damage by a percentage. See Elements
burning While you are on fire you lose about 1 point per second, and the fire spreads to flammable things nearby. Becoming wet (rain, a splash of water, walking into a mud pool) puts it out
bleeding 1 point per second, ignoring defense
Falls Falling from an upper storey: a drop of up to one storey does no harm; every storey beyond that costs 6 points. See Storeys. On the ground, you can only jump down a cliff one level high, and it does no harm (see Terrain)
Starving At 0 satiety you lose 1 point every 8 seconds, but it never takes the last point
Lightning In a thunderstorm, lightning strikes things nearby. See Weather

Recovery

Method Amount
Being well fed With satiety at nine tenths or more (18/20) and health not full, 1 point every 8 seconds
Sleeping Lying down at once restores half the missing health; in a bed in a bedroom, all of it
Healing spring The hot spring in Firstlight Village: stepping in restores 1 point, and you keep healing while you soak in it (soaking)
Healing Potion 16
Antidote 5
Wine Bottle 4
Mug of Ale 3
Murky Potion 1
Drinking water (Flask of Water, Waterskin, a Water Bucket) 2, and the container is emptied
Bucket of Milk 4, plus 4 satiety; the bucket is emptied
Herb Eaten, restores 4 points, plus 1 satiety
Revival On Easy and Normal difficulty, health is full again after revival
  • Ordinary food does not heal. Food such as Bread and Steak only restores satiety; once you are well fed, health comes back slowly through “being well fed”. See Food.
  • Drinks and herbs first ask “Drink the X?” or “Eat the X?” and are used up only once you confirm.
  • Hold a potion or drink and right-click someone else to give it to them; it restores their health.
  • Campfires do not heal.

Skills

Skill Effect
Second Wind Health regenerates on its own once satiety is above half
Regeneration Health regenerates on its own as long as satiety is at least a quarter
Adrenaline When an attack leaves you at 30% health or less, you regain 1 point per second for 5 seconds and move 30% faster; at most once a minute
Shadow Recovery (Assassin) Defeating an enemy with a dagger restores a tenth of your maximum health
Arcane Recovery (Mage) Defeating an enemy with a staff restores 3 points (wands do not count)

Weapons and Vowbound equipment with the “lifesteal” property draw back health with every hit; see Equipment.

Maximum health

Maximum health can only be raised through skills:

Skill Maximum health
Vitality +4
Hardy +6
Iron Will +8
Unbreakable (Warrior) +10

The extra health is filled in at once when you learn the skill. There are always ten hearts; the higher the maximum, the more health each heart stands for.

At zero

  • When the player's health reaches zero, they collapse, the whole world stops, and the death screen appears. See Difficulty.
  • Villagers and Lumi collapse for 30 seconds at zero, then get up with half their health.
  • Monsters and animals at zero are defeated, and whoever defeated them earns experience. See Levels and Experience.

Other health bars

A thin health bar appears above anything that is hurt (creatures hostile to you show it even unhurt):

Color Whose
Red Those who will attack you
Green People and Lumi
Amber Everything else: creatures that do not mean to attack you, and things such as a tree that has been chopped or a wall (showing durability)

A boss's health bar is shown at the top of the screen. See Bosses.

Tips

  • To recover health, first eat until satiety is at nine tenths or more, then find a safe place and wait a while.
  • A night's sleep in a bedroom restores all of it at once. Build a house with a bed, a door and a ceiling; see Rooms.
  • The healing spring in Firstlight Village costs nothing; when you are hurt, go back and soak in it.

History

  • 2026-10-02: Health became ten hearts above the toolbar; satiety and “being well fed” healing were added.
  • 2026-10-04: Damage taken is reckoned from the weapon's efficiency against the material and from elemental resistance; things that are defeated break into materials.
  • 2026-10-05: The health restored by sleeping depends on whether you are in a bedroom.
  • 2026-10-08: The Fireproof Draught lost its healing (drinking it only gives fireproofing); a Bucket of Milk restores health as well as satiety; Shadow Recovery only counts daggers, and Arcane Recovery only counts staves.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
