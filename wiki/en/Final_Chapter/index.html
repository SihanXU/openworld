Final Chapter

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Story
To be designed: this part is not designed yet; below is only what is settled. To make a proposal, click “Talk” at the top of the page.

The Final Chapter is the main story's last chapter, after all five Seal-bearers have fallen. The protagonist carries the five Instruments of the Sufferings into the Most Holy Hall and faces the final boss, Astra. The original lore text sets out what happens in this chapter and Astra's lines; the map, the walkthrough and the boss fight itself have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

What is already decided

  • The end of the pilgrimage is the Holy City, where the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings is held again (§7).
  • The protagonist carries the five holy instruments into the Most Holy Hall (§14).
  • The final boss is Astra, the First Martyr (§14).
  • The current version makes only the first ending, the preview ending (§16). See Ending.
What happens in the Final Chapter (§14–§15) (click to show)
  • At the center of the Most Holy Hall lies Astra's body. As the five holy instruments return to their places (the Crown of Thorns set on again; the scourge marks split open again; the Scales of Hunger rising once more; the holy nails fixing his body again; the Lance That Pierced the Heart entering his chest once more), Astra opens his eyes (§14).
  • Astra knows he can no longer go on bearing the First Night, but he cannot simply make the protagonist the new Nightbearer: if the new vessel cannot withstand it, the world will only be destroyed sooner. Only “one who can kill the present Nightbearer” is fit to succeed him. The final battle is not meant to stop the player from saving the world; it is “the last trial” (§15).
  • Early in the fight, Astra still demands that the player leave: “Leave this place.” As the fight goes on: “You do not know what you are asking for.” At last, he is satisfied the player is strong enough: “Good.” “At least this time...” “I can die.” (§15)

Not to be confused with the game's “Final Chapter”

In the game, the last main objective of the older storyline Emberfall and the Dragon used to be called “Final Chapter” as well: after you defeated the dragon of Cinder Peak, the objective box showed “Final Chapter”. Now that storyline is the main quest The Dragon Road, which is complete as soon as the dragon falls, and the last line of its log is “The dragon is dead. Emberfall can sleep at night again.” That storyline predates this setting and has nothing to do with the Final Chapter this page is about.
