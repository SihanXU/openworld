Chests and Containers

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

A container is a piece of furniture that holds things: chests, barrels, crates, clay pots, wardrobes, shelves, bookshelves, sacks and wood piles. Press E on a container to open it; the chest panel appears on screen, and in it you move things between the container and your inventory. A container stays put on the ground and cannot be carried off whole.

Kinds

Container Capacity (slots) Materials (half returned when broken)
Chest 12 4 Planks
Wardrobe 12 4 Planks
Wood pile 12 2 Logs
Shelf 8 3 Planks
Bookshelf 8 3 Planks
Crate 6 2 Planks
fixture/container/barrel (barrel) 4 2 Planks
Sack 4 1 Cloth
Clay pot 2 —

Some containers have their own capacity on the map: Hesper's chest at the Ford holds 8 things, and the Garden Supplies beside the kitchen garden in Firstlight Village hold 24 (its durability is also higher than an ordinary crate's, at 12).

Capacity counts pieces, not slots: a container holds at most that many pieces, and pieces of the same kind are shown stacked in one slot in the panel.

Opening and moving things

Action Result
E Opens the container and shows the chest panel; if the container is already open, just shows the panel
Closing the panel (Esc, ✕ or clicking outside it) The container closes with it
G on an open container Puts what is in your hand into it; when it is full, it says “It is full.”
G on a closed container It says “It is closed.”

Time stops while the panel is open. The panel closes by itself when the container is burned, broken, or closed by a rule.

The chest panel

The top of the panel holds the container's slots, with “pieces held / capacity” beside the title; below is your own inventory.

Action Effect
Click a slot Moves the whole stack to the other side
Shift+click Moves only one
Right-click a slot A menu: use what is in your hand on it, put what is in your hand into it, handle it empty-handed, move one
⇣ Add to existing stacks Puts in everything in your inventory of a kind the container already holds
⇅ Organize Organizes your inventory

Building your own container

In the “Buildings” section of build mode (B):

Name Needs Result
Crate 2 Planks, and a hammer on you A crate (6 pieces)
Wooden chest 4 Planks, and a hammer on you A chest (12 pieces)

The hammer only has to be carried; it is not used up. You can make a Stone Smith Hammer (2 Stone, 1 Stick) with your bare hands. You can also do without build mode: put two Planks together and you have a frame; strike it once with a hammer and it is nailed into a crate; see Building.

An axe can break crates and chests, giving back half the Planks. See Durability and Breaking.

Other people's containers

  • Many containers in the village, and the things in them, have owners. An item with an owner says “Belongs to X” in its tooltip.
  • Take something from someone's container in front of them and they say “Hey — that is mine!”, and the story goes round the village for two days. There is no other punishment. See Residents.
  • The rules have locking and unlocking with keys: a locked container cannot be opened, and a key can unlock or lock it. For now there are no locked containers on the map.

Tips

  • When your inventory is nearly full, put a chest somewhere you often go. A wooden chest holds twice as much as a crate for only 2 more Planks.
  • Back at the chest, press “Add to existing stacks” to put your materials away in one go.
  • Most containers are made of wood and are flammable; do not put them next to a campfire.
