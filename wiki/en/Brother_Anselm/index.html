“Sister Ilse keeps the flame; I keep the books.”

— Brother Anselm

Brother Anselm is an old brother of Firstlight Village, 68 years old, who serves in Sister Ilse's chapel (Firstlight Chapel). The chapel stands on the square, just behind the altar of Astra and the holy flame; Ilse keeps the flame outside the door, and he keeps the skill-book shelves inside. The skill books on the shelves were copied before the storm by the monastery over the mountain, and he sells them at what it cost to bring them down. He has read every one of them, yet cannot do a single thing in them; reading, he says, is not learning.

From 6 in the morning to 9 at night he stands by the bookstand, and at night he sleeps in the chapel.

The skill bookstand

Press E on the bookstand (“Trade at the skill bookstand”) to open the shop panel “Chapel Bookstand”, with two tabs: Skill Books (active skills anyone can learn, 60 Dawnmarks each) and Profession (an active skill of one profession, 120 each). Hold a book you have bought and left-click to read it, and you learn the skill in it (see Skills).

He also carries a few things of his own to buy and sell: Holy Water, candles, a book and a quill (see Trading).
