Something for the Pot is a side quest of Firstlight Village given by Marta, who minds the pot by the fire on the square. She wants 3 Raw Beef and 2 eggs to make a pot of stew for the whole village. Both the cows and the chickens are on the Grassland south of the village.

The quest has no prerequisites and can be taken at any time once you are in the village.

Objectives

  1. Raw beef
    Carry Raw Beef×3
  2. Eggs
    Carry Egg×2
  3. Back to Marta
    Talk to Marta

Both things are taken when you hand in. They must be raw: a roasted Steak or a cooked Boiled Egg does not count.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: by day (6:00–22:00) Marta minds the pot by the campfire on the square, with a yellow “!” over her head. Press E on her and choose “Accept ‘Something for the Pot’” in the dialogue box.
  2. To the Grassland: out through the village's southern exit is the Grassland. At the start there are 3 cows and 4 chickens; they do not attack, and run off when struck.
  3. Raw beef: a defeated cow gives 2 raw beef, so you need to defeat 2. Cows walk more slowly than you, so you can catch them; you can fight bare-handed too (see Combat). Once the Grassland's cows are gone they slowly come back: while you are on the Grassland, one may arrive every 120 seconds, up to 4.
  4. Eggs: a hen lays an egg every 3 in-game hours; the egg drops on the tile where the hen is standing at the time and does not jump into your backpack by itself, so press E to pick it up. A defeated chicken only gives Raw Chicken, never eggs, so do not fight the hens. The hens at the start lay their first eggs at staggered times, so there will be one before long.
  5. Hand in: go back to Marta, whose marker has turned to “?”, and press E to hand in.

Other ways

  • Raw beef and eggs from anywhere else in the world count too, as long as you have them on you when you hand in.
  • The Held Ground has a cow and two chickens, but livestock there does not come back once defeated.

Rewards

  • Experience +25 (Levels and Experience)
  • Bread×3
  • Marta's Kitchen Sash (Fine): Belt, defence 1, level 1
  • Regard Marta +10 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

Dialogue

When offering
“Everyone in Firstlight eats from this pot, and since the storm there has been little to put in it. Bring me three cuts of beef and two eggs, and the whole village eats tonight. The cows and hens are on Dusklea, south of here.”
While unfinished
“Three cuts of beef and two eggs. The pot is on already.”
On handing in
“Now that is a stew. Here, the first bowl is yours, and take some bread for the road.”
