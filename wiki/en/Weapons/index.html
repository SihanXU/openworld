Weapons

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

A weapon is an item held in the hand to attack with. Hold a weapon and left-click a target, or press Space to attack in front of you: that is one blow. Attack power = your own attack + the weapon's power. For the rules of fighting see Combat.

Profession weapons and common weapons

Some weapons belong to a single profession. Others can hold them, store them and give them away, but cannot attack with them; they are told “Only an X can fight with a Y”.

Profession Profession weapons
Assassin Dagger
Warrior Sword, Scimitar, and the off-hand Shield
Ranger Bow
Mage Staff

The other weapons are common weapons that anyone can use: Axe, Club, Mace, Warhammer, Spear, Halberd, Wand, Dragonsbane. A profession's talents work only on that profession's weapons; the tooltip of a common weapon says “Common weapon: no profession talent bonus”.

Before you have chosen a profession (which waits until level 5), only common weapons can be used to fight. When you first choose a profession you get a starting weapon: an Iron Dagger for the Assassin, an Iron Sword and a Wooden Shield for the Warrior, a Wooden Bow for the Ranger, a Wooden Staff for the Mage.

Attack range

How far a left-click attack reaches depends on the weapon in your hand:

Weapon Range
Most weapons, bare hands 1 tile
Spear, Halberd 2 tiles
Staff 4 tiles
Wand 5 tiles
Bow 6 tiles

A ranged attack can only go out along your own row or column. Bows, wands and staves fire a projectile in the direction you face. Some skills and Legendary Arms reach farther.

Bare hands

With nothing in hand, the left click strikes with the weapon in your main hand, or, if the main hand is empty too, with a punch. When you hold something that is neither a tool nor a weapon, such as food or a material, the left click uses it on yourself and does not swing. A fist can slowly break things softer than itself (grass, wood); hitting stone only says “Too hard.” See Durability and Breaking.

When your weapon is not drawn (X), left-clicking a friendly person first asks “Attack so-and-so?”, and strikes only on “Yes”, whatever you hold; with the weapon drawn, or once a fight is under way, it no longer asks (see Friendly people are asked about first).

Elements

A weapon that can carry an element gains a word in its name: of Embers (fire), of Tides (water), of Frost (ice), of Storms (lightning), of Gales (wind), of Stone (earth). When it hits, the element acts on the target: fire can set it alight, water wets it, ice freezes wet things, lightning jumps between wet things, wind blows the target back two tiles, earth can shatter brittle things. See Elements.

The ready-made element weapons in the game are the Wand of Frost on Tobin's cart (traded for food), and among the Legendary Arms Galestring, Emberheart and Rimecrown.

The axe is both weapon and tool

The axe is in the weapon catalogue, and is also the tool for felling trees and splitting planks. Your first axe is usually a Stone Axe you make yourself. See Tools.

Where to get them

  • Make them yourself: press C to open crafting. A Stone Axe can be made by hand; at a workbench you can make a Wooden Club, a Wooden Shield and a Wooden Bow (the bow needs level 2).
  • On the map: the “Old Axe” by the stump at The Ford.
  • Quests: Holt's quest Clear the Hollow gives a Spear.
  • Trade: Tobin trades the Wand of Frost for a portion of food.
  • Profession: the starting weapon when you first choose a profession.
  • Seal-bearers: a defeated Seal-bearer drops a Legendary weapon of your profession.
  • Old story line: Dragonsbane, forged by the smith Garrick.

Forms in the catalogue such as the Mace, Warhammer and Halberd have no fixed source on the maps for now.
