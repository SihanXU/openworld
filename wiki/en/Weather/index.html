Weather

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The weather changes once a day and is shown beside the clock face in the top right. There are five kinds: clear, cloudy, rain, storm and fog. Rain and storms soak people and creatures outdoors and put out burning things; storms also bring lightning. There is no weather forecast.

The five kinds of weather

Weather Icon Chance Effect
Clear ☀ (☾ at night) 50% None
Cloudy ☁ 25% The screen is a little darker
Rain ☂ 15% Soaks, puts out fires; the screen is darker, with streaks of rain
Storm ⚡ 5% Everything rain does, plus lightning strikes; the screen is darkest
Fog ≋ 5% Only a thin mist over the screen

Apart from the first two days, each day's weather is decided by the world's seed and stays the same all day. Hovering over the icon shows the weather's name.

The first two days

The weather on days 1 and 2 is fixed, the same in the tutorial and in a new game:

Day Time Weather
Day 1 00:00–17:00 Cloudy
Day 1 17:00–20:00 Storm
Day 1 20:00–24:00 Rain
Day 2 00:00–08:00 Fog
Day 2 After 08:00 Clear

Rain

In rain and storms, about every 6 seconds:

  • People and creatures out in the open become wet. Being wet puts out fire on you, and makes you more easily affected by lightning and freezing; see Elements. Pressing E bare-handed beside a lit fire to warm yourself dries you off at once (see Warming up).
  • Things out in the open that are burning are put out.
  • A lit campfire is not put out.
  • Standing on a cell covered by a ceiling, or on an indoor map or a dark underground map, you don't get rained on. See Rooms, Storeys.
  • Rain doesn't water crops for you; you still have to water them yourself. See Farming.

Storms and lightning

During a storm, a bolt of lightning falls about every 30 seconds:

  • Half the time, it strikes something tall within 12 cells of the player. At present the only thing that counts as “tall” is the dead tree at the Ford;
  • the rest of the time, it strikes a random spot near the player.
  • Lightning has a power of 4. What it strikes reacts by the rules of elements: something wet is electrified and passes the current to wet things next to it, something dry and flammable catches fire.

The overworld

On the overworld of the Lands of the Five Seals, each step takes half again as long in rain and storms. See World Map.

Villagers

Some villagers have a different schedule in rain and storms, and say things about the weather. The others, if they do not want to get wet, go indoors and stay there until it stops; farmers, guards, fishers, hunters, soldiers, sailors and ferrykeepers do not mind and work on. Walk onto a map while it is raining and those people are indoors from the start. See Rainy days, Residents.

Tips

  • There is a storm from 17:00 to 20:00 on the first day. Lightning falls near the player and can set dry grass and wood alight, so don't linger by haystacks.
  • Rain doesn't soak placed things like campfires, so you can still light a fire on a rainy day.
  • When you are on fire, rain is the best way to put it out.

History

  • 2026-10-01: Weather was added.
  • 2026-10-02: Weather can be fixed by time of day: the storm on the evening of day 1 was added. Rain soaks things outdoors and puts out burning things, and storm lightning seeks out tall things.
  • 2026-10-04: Travel on the overworld is slower in rain; covered cells don't get rained on.
  • 2026-10-09: If you are soaked you can warm yourself dry beside a lit fire.
  • 2026-10-28: Those who do not want to get wet go indoors when it rains (see Keeping Safe).
