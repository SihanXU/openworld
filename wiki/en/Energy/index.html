Energy

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

OpenWorld has no energy (stamina) system. Felling trees, breaking stone, tilling and swinging weapons cost nothing at all; you can keep at them as long as you like, and you are never forced home to sleep because you are tired.

The closest thing in the game is satiety: it falls slowly over time and has to be restored by eating; only at nine tenths or more does it heal you, and when it is empty you lose health. But satiety changes only with time, not with how much you do. The only limits on doing things back to back are their own cooldowns: at least 0.5 seconds between attacks, and a 4-second cooldown on Dash. Sleep only serves to skip the night, recover health and set your resting place; see Sleeping.

History

  • 2026-10-01: An early version modeled on Stardew Valley had a stamina bar, with every use of a tool costing stamina.
  • 2026-10-02: The team decided not to copy Stardew Valley's gameplay systems, and stamina was removed together with weeks, seasons and friendship points. Satiety was added the same day.
