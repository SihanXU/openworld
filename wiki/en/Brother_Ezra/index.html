“At the Rest they taught me that every stroke I take is one Astra need not. So every evening I take a few.”

— Brother Ezra

Brother Ezra is a penitent, 37 years old, who kneels year after year by the roadside halfway along The Pilgrim's Road, scourging himself with a rope; the Church says the faithful may take on a little of the Seal-bearer's pain this way. He has walked this road for eleven years, and has given his ropes to the shrines.

Story

  • When you come near, he tells of the one time he walked as far as The Kneeling Field: the knight was mending a banner, and when he saw Ezra his hands stopped, “as if he had forgotten what hands are for”, and then he bade him kneel.
  • Once all five shrines are lit, he says he has never seen them lit all at once, and sends you up the hill to the abbey.
  • He will not go into Abbey of the Thorn: “The dead in there still keep the hours.”
