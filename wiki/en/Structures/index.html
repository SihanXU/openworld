Structures

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List

Structures are the four parts houses are built from: the Wall, the Floor, the Stairs and the Door. Like other items they can be carried; once built, a part takes up a cell and becomes part of the house; left-click it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood, so it can be taken back in one piece. For how to build a house see Building, for the rules of several storeys Storeys, and for what counts as a room Rooms.

The four parts

Part Materials Once placed
Wall 2 planks Blocks passage and sight; holds up floors within two cells above it; a wall of the storey above can stand on a wall of the storey below
Floor 2 planks Laid on ground you can stand on, it covers the ground; laid on an upper storey, it needs walls to hold it up. Seen from below, it is the ceiling of the storey beneath
Stairs 3 planks A flight one storey high, placed in front of the higher level; the cell above the stairs must be left open
Door 2 planks Press E to open and close it. Shut, it blocks passage and sight like a wall; open, it can be walked and seen through; open or shut, it counts as a room's edge

Parts made of planks are called “Wooden Wall”, “Wooden Floor”…; the substance in the name follows the materials. All four parts are wood, and can burn.

How to make them

  • Press C to make them by hand in the crafting panel; the finished parts go into the inventory.
  • Or skip that: select one in build mode (B), and if you carry the materials it is made and placed on the spot.

How to place them

  • Holding one, right-click a cell to build it there, and hold and drag to build a whole row; or in build mode (B) place it with the left click or E, holding and dragging for a whole row too.
  • Holding one and pressing G only puts it on the ground, lying loose, not built; press E to pick it up again.
  • Each storey builds only on itself. In build mode, R switches between “floor” and “ceiling”, and also turns the stairs; with a floor in hand, R makes the right click lay a ceiling instead.
  • At most 4 storeys can be built.
  • When it cannot be placed, you are told why: “Something is there already.” “There is a floor there already.” “A floor cannot be laid on this ground.” “Stairs keep the storey over them open.” “Nothing would hold it up.” “Out of reach.”

How to take them down

  • Left-click a built part once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; then press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into the inventory by itself).
  • A floor with something on it can be taken down only once that thing is removed.
  • When a wall holding up a floor is taken down, the floor falls.
  • Struck with any other tool or a weapon (a sword and the like), a part only loses durability; broken, it gives back only half its materials. See Durability and Breaking.
