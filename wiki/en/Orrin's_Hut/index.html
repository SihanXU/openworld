Orrin's Hut is the inside of the thatched hut on the east side of the Charcoal Dell, entered by the hut's door. The charcoal burner Orrin sleeps here at night and keeps watch by the kiln by day.

The hut is indoors, and no night creatures appear in it. It hangs under the Charcoal Dell and has no place of its own on the Lands of the Five Seals or the world map.
