“It's safe inside the fence. Past the grassland, keep your blade out. After dark, keep it out everywhere.”

— Holt

Holt is the only guard of Firstlight Village, 38 years old. He watches the path south out of the village: the things of Thornwood Hollow crawl up through the gap the storm left, and he stands at the head of that path.

At night he warns the player that the undead roam the wilds, “but they never come into the village. Never.” This matches the game's rules: Firstlight Village is a peaceful zone, and night creatures do not appear in the village.

Quests

  • Clear the Hollow (Side, giver)
  • Clear the Hollow: available from level 2. Defeat 6 monsters in Thornwood Hollow (slimes, spiders and wolves all count). The reward is a Spear and the “Militia Pauldrons”.
  • The Night-born: on some evenings he (or Fennick) asks you to defeat 3 night creatures before dawn.

Guard

Holt keeps watch over Firstlight Village together with Fennick, one of Firstlight Village's guards:

  • When he sees a crime, he reports it to the watch on the spot; when he sees a killing or an assault, he moves to arrest the culprit himself (see Crime).
  • When he sees someone wanted, he calls “You there! In the name of the watch, stop!” and goes to stop them: pay the fine, go to jail, talk your way out, bribe him, or resist arrest.
  • Walk up to him with your weapon drawn and he tells you to put it away.
  • He fights: struck, he strikes back; when someone cries for help, he runs to them.

The cells are behind the guards' quarters.

Lore

In Chapter 1, Holt swears to catch the Grey Man. On the night of thanks he is the first to shout “Thief!”; the Grey Man walks across the river on the ice that forms under his feet, and Holt chases him as far as his spear can reach. After Chapter 1 ends, he says he wants to come along when the player goes further east. In the script this is a thread left for later.

Trivia

  • He says the Grey Man “looked at me like I was a child”.
