Chapter 2

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Story
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.
To be designed: this part is not designed yet; below is only what is settled. To make a proposal, click “Talk” at the top of the page.

Chapter 2, by the structure of the main story, is the chapter of the Second Seal: the protagonist faces the second Seal-bearer, Matthias, Giver of War, and recovers the second Instrument of the Sufferings, the Pillar of Scourging. The chapter's places, people, quests and boss fight have not yet been designed, and it is not in the game yet.

What is already decided

  • Seal-bearer: Matthias, Giver of War, holder of the Second Seal, modeled on the rider on the red horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation (§6).
  • Torment: the Second Suffering, the Scourging. Astra was bound to a pillar and scourged at length; legend says that even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall. The Church reads it as “repaying everyone's sins with the flesh” (§4).
  • Instrument of the Sufferings: the Pillar of Scourging.
  • Chapter structure: one Seal-bearer per chapter; when the Seal-bearer falls, Lumi catches part of the Seal's power and gains a Boon; and the nights grow a little longer again (docs/chapter-1.md §10; “Agreed additions”). What the Second Seal's Boon is and how much longer the nights grow are both to be designed.
  • The Grey Man: works against the pilgrimage from Chapter 1 onward; by the middle of the game the protagonist will come round to his side (“Agreed additions”). What he does in Chapter 2 is to be designed.
What Matthias bears (click to show)

Matthias bears the bodily pain of the scourging (§12). What the Scourging really does is let the flesh gradually adapt to the constant pain and transformation the First Night brings (§4).

The game's current “Chapter 2”

In the game, the main quest after Chapter 1 ends is The Dragon Road, whose first log line reads “I took the road east, toward Emberfall.” It continues into the older storyline Emberfall and the Dragon, which predates this setting: Emberfall, the Old Mine, Cinder Pass and the dragon of Cinder Peak. That storyline is only a placeholder and will later be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal. The claw marks in the burned farmstead on the East Road in Chapter 1 are also a clue left for that older storyline.
