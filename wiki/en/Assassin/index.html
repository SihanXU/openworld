Assassin

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

The Assassin is one of the four professions, and masters the Dagger. The Assassin's way of fighting is timing: the first blow after a quiet spell, a blow from behind, the third blow on the same target, all deal much more damage. Choose it from Bram in Firstlight Village from level 5.

Starting gear

The first time you become an Assassin, Bram gives you an Iron Dagger: power 3, range 1, no element.

The dagger is the Assassin's profession weapon: only an Assassin can fight with it, and the Assassin's talents work only with daggers. Fight with an axe, a mace or another common weapon and none of the Assassin's talents count.

Skills

The Assassin's first six talents are in the profession branch of the skill tree (K). The root is Short-Blade Training; two branches lead outward from it, ending in a 2-point ultimate. All of them work only with a dagger; Ambush, Relentless, Backstab and Death Dance count only the main target of a melee blow, not the neighbors caught by Sweep.

Skill Requires Points Effect
Short-Blade Training 1 Attacks with a dagger gain +1 power.
Ambush Short-Blade Training 1 After 4 seconds without attacking or taking combat damage, the next blow deals +50% damage. This is a gathering of strength, not invisibility.
Relentless Ambush 1 The 3rd blow on the same target deals +60% damage. No more than 2 seconds between blows; changing target, missing or a ranged attack breaks the chain.
Backstab Short-Blade Training 1 A blow from behind the target deals +40% damage. Judged by the way the target faces; the sides and front do not count.
Shadow Recovery Backstab 1 Felling a creature with a dagger restores 10% of your maximum health (rounded up).
Death Dance Relentless, Shadow Recovery 2 A blow that triggers Ambush or Backstab is always a critical hit.

Active skills

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with Shift+1–6 (see Active skills and the skill bar). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are an Assassin.

Skill Requires Points Cooldown Effect
Shadow Strike Short-Blade Training 1 10 s Flash behind the nearest enemy within 4 cells and strike (1.5× damage, can trigger Backstab).
Envenom Short-Blade Training 1 20 s Your next 5 attacks make the target bleed (1 per second for 4 seconds).
Smoke Bomb Shadow Strike 1 25 s For 3 seconds monsters cannot see you and will not attack you.
Flurry Envenom 1 8 s Stab the cell ahead 4 times in quick succession (0.6× damage each).

Learning all twenty-two (ten talents, five skill upgrades, seven active skills) takes 25 points. For the skill tree's rules, see Skills.

The profession branch grew twelve more nodes, twenty-two in all: three new active skills (the last a 2-point ultimate), five skill upgrades (once learned, they apply straight to their active skill: a shorter cooldown, more damage, a wider area), three profession talents, and a 2-point ultimate talent.

Legendary Arms

The Assassin's two Legendary weapons are Nightfang and Mirrorshard, both daggers. When an Assassin fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see Legendary Arms, Bosses).

How to play

  • The opening blow: hold off for 4 seconds before closing in, and the first blow takes Ambush's full +50%. With Death Dance learned, that blow is also a sure critical.
  • Going round the back: strike from the side opposite the way the enemy faces. An enemy chasing the player always faces the player, so a backstab is hard; an enemy busy fighting someone else, or one that has just turned, is the easiest prey.
  • The chain of three: strike the same target three times in a row, and the third blow gets +60%. Do not change target midway.
  • Healing: Shadow Recovery heals a little every time you fell something with a dagger, good for clearing a pack of small monsters in a row. Fell with another weapon (an axe, bare hands) and you heal nothing.
  • The dagger's power is low (3 for the Iron Dagger); the Assassin lives on bonuses. Critical skills of the shared branches such as Keen Eye and Lethal Edge suit the Assassin well.

Boss Challenge

In the Boss Challenge, the Assassin is level 20: health 114, attack 6, defense 1, holding an Iron Dagger, with all twenty-two Assassin skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with Space, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned.

History

  • 2026-10-03: The Assassin is the first profession, with six skills: Short-Blade Training, Ambush, Relentless, Backstab, Shadow Recovery, Death Dance.
  • 2026-10-05: The dagger became the Assassin's profession weapon, unusable by other professions; the Assassin's talents count only daggers. Two Assassin Legendary weapons added.
  • 2026-10-09: The profession branch grew to twenty-two nodes: the new active skills Throwing Knives, Fan of Knives and Vanishing Strikes, five skill upgrades, three talents and an ultimate talent (see “Advanced”).
  • 2026-10-08: Four active skills added: Shadow Strike, Envenom, Smoke Bomb, Flurry.
  • 2026-10-08: Shadow Recovery heals only when you fell something with a dagger, like the other Assassin talents.
