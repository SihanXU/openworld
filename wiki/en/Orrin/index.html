“The night the bridge went, the birds came. Every bird in the wood, all in my oak, and not a sound out of them since. I cut a notch every day. The board's nearly full.”

— Orrin

Orrin is the charcoal burner of The Charcoal Dell, 58 years old, who has burned charcoal for the smiths and hearths of Firstlight Village for forty years. A clamp takes four days to burn and is ruined if no one sleeps beside it, so he sleeps beside it. His back can no longer carry wood; while Gus's back still held, Gus brought him wood. Boars have rooted up the stools in his coppice, and no new shoots come. Since the night the little bridge broke, every evening at dusk the birds of the whole forest settle on the old oak at the west end of his dell, without a sound; every day he cuts a notch in a board.

Lore

Orrin is the other end of the clue that “there are no birds in the forest”: the first time you enter The Forest, Lumi says she cannot hear a single bird, and the answer is in his oak. After you have watched the old oak at dusk (see The Charcoal Dell), he says a line he says only once: he no longer cuts the notches for the birds, but “for what they're waiting for”. Like the kneeling cattle on The Grassland, the birds are looking east, toward The White Knight Alberic. See Chapter 1.

Trivia

  • He is the first person in the game to sell Coal; before him, Coal could only be dug in The Old Mine and The Quarry, or bought from Haldor.
