Trading stall

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item

CategoryFurniture

The trading stall is a stall on the Held Ground, named “Alberic's stall”. Press E to open the stall panel and buy seeds, fertilizer, a shovel, feed, buckets, crated livestock, bait and a fishing rod with Dawnmarks, or sell it what you grow, raise and catch in exchange for Dawnmarks. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, see Money.

Source

  • Placed on maps: The Held Ground
  • There is only this one, on the Held Ground, southeast of the sword planted in the ground, two tiles east of where Alberic stands by day.
  • You cannot make one, and it cannot be taken.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Trading

  • Opening: walk up to the stall and press E (label “Trade at Alberic's stall”). This opens the stall page of the menu, with four shelves: Farming, Ranching, Fishing and Sell, and above them how many Dawnmarks you carry.
  • Buying: click a thing on one of the first three shelves; its price is taken and what you bought goes straight into your pack (how many you get per purchase is in the tables below). Without enough money you cannot buy.
  • Selling: click a thing on the “Sell” shelf; one of it is taken from your pack or hands, and the Dawnmarks it fetches go into your pack. One at a time.
  • You must stand beside it: you can trade only while standing in the ring of 8 tiles around the stall. When you are not beside it, the stall page says “Stand beside the stall to trade.” and the buy and sell buttons are grey; a trade sent while not beside it does not go through, and you are only told “That has to be done at the stall.”
  • No one needs to mind it: the stall does its own trade; you can buy and sell even when Alberic is not nearby or has gone to bed for the night.

The stall buys only the things in this table; nothing else can be sold. It takes what you grow (wheat, vegetables, berries, herbs), what you raise (eggs, milk) and what you catch (every kind of fish, and the Soggy Boot too), but not the seeds, feed and tools it sells itself.

Other

  • Putting things on it: it is a counter. Holding something, press G at it and the thing is put on the counter; E first picks up what is on the counter, and the stall itself cannot be taken.
  • Blocks the way: the stall is solid.
  • Hitting: it has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
  • Fire: this stall on the Held Ground always has fireproof and will not burn. It is the only place in the game to buy and sell, so fire cannot touch it.
  • For anything else you do to it, the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-10-08: Added the trading stall and the Dawnmark: the Held Ground became a camp where you can farm, keep livestock and fish; harvests can be sold for money, and money buys seeds, livestock and bait.
  • 2026-10-08: The stall does not catch fire; the Farming page sells the shovel; a crated calf lets out a calf; trading while not beside the stall says “That has to be done at the stall.”
