“Oh! A visitor. I'm Wren; I make herbal remedies and salves. Mind the cauldron — now and then it goes off.”

— Wren

Wren is the herbalist of Emberfall, 23 years old, young and quick-handed, and the only one in town who has read about dragons rather than only feared them. Her cauldron is always burning.

The line of Emberfall and the dragon of Cinder Peak was written before the Five Sufferings setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See Emberfall and the Dragon and Chapter 2.

She tells the player where the materials are: “Fireweed grows on the open grassland; frostcaps grow in the forest shade.” She has also read that a wounded dragon calls its whelps.

Quests

  • The Dragon Road (Main)

Wren's part belongs to the main quest The Dragon Road: the fireweed and frostcaps you give her, and the Fireproof Draught she brews, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it she has a “?” over her head. After you have heard Mara's council:

  1. Give her Fireweed and Frostcaps one at a time (G), 3 of each. Fireweed grows in Firstlight Village, frostcaps in The Forest.
  2. Once she has enough of both, press E on her, and she brews two flasks of Fireproof Draught: drink one at the mouth of the lair, and the second when the first is about to wear off.

Trade

Wren is also a merchant (see Trading): she sells Healing Potion, restocking to 3 flasks every morning, and carries 40 Dawnmark to buy your goods.

Portrait

Lore

The Fireproof Draught makes you fireproof for a while: fire damage is greatly reduced, and you cannot be set alight. See the dragon of Cinder Peak.
