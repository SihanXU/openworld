Sleeping

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

To sleep, stand on a bed and press E. Once you lie down, time speeds ahead to 6:00 in the morning, and you wake with some of your health restored. A bed you have slept in becomes your rest point: on Normal difficulty, when you fall you get back up here. The game has no stamina (see Energy), so sleep is not required; it is the way to pass the night, recover health and set your rest point.

How to sleep

  • You have to stand on a bed to reach it: walk onto the bed and press E; the prompt is “Sleep”.
  • “You lie down.” Time immediately speeds ahead to 6:00 in the morning.
  • You can sleep at any time: lie down at 22:00 and you sleep 8 game hours; lie down at 7 in the morning and you sleep until 6 the next day.
  • While asleep you have the asleep status and cannot move.

The game has no bed lying ready; you make your own: 2 planks and 2 cut grass (the “wooden bed” in Crafting), or place one directly in Building mode. A bed can be carried: knock it with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself); when you get where you are going, hold it and right-click a cell, and it is set up for sleeping.

When you wake

  • Health restored: your missing health × the rest value, rounded up.
    • The bed is in a bedroom (a bed inside a house closed in on every side and covered overhead): rest value 1, everything you were missing is restored. “You sleep deeply and wake refreshed.”
    • The bed is anywhere else: rest value 0.5, half is restored. “You get some sleep, after a fashion.”
  • Rest point: this bed becomes your rest point. On Normal difficulty, when you fall you return to the bed you last slept in or the waypoint statue you last woke (see Difficulty, Waypoint statues).

Hunger slows while you sleep

While asleep, satiety falls 4 times more slowly, and this stacks with the 7.5 times of “hungry” (see Satiety). A night's sleep (8 game hours) on a full stomach costs about 2 to 3 satiety.

Being woken

While time speeds ahead, you wake at once whenever your health drops:

  • Satiety reaches 0 and health starts to go: “Hunger wakes you.”
  • Something hurts you (a monster out at night, say): “Pain wakes you.”

So do not sleep on an empty stomach, and do not sleep in the open: shut the door and sleep indoors (see Night Creatures).

How others sleep

Residents sleep by their own schedules, going back to where they sleep when it is time; rain and the story change their schedules too. They do not need beds to sleep.

Tutorial

The last lesson of the Tutorial: build a house with a bed, shut the door before dark, and go to bed. Lumi reminds you: “Don't go to sleep hungry, or you'll wake up starving in the middle of the night.”

Tips

  • Eat before you sleep: with satiety at 90% or more, you keep slowly regaining health after you wake too.
  • A night in a bedroom is as good as full health. Badly hurt? Going indoors for a sleep is the easiest cure.
  • Take a bed when you explore far away: a bed can be knocked loose and carried, set up with a right-click when you arrive, and slept in, and it becomes your new rest point.

History

  • 2026-10-04: Beds can be slept in: sleep until morning, with health restored by the room; a bedroom restores it fully (W5). The bed you slept in becomes your rest point, where you come back to life on Normal difficulty.
  • 2026-10-05: While asleep you have the “asleep” status, lying still, with satiety falling 4 times more slowly; only an empty stomach wakes you with hunger.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
