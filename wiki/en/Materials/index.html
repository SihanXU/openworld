Materials

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Materials are items for crafting, building and handing in for quests. The five most used are the Stick, Stone, Cut Grass, Log and Plank: the first axe, the campfire, walls and beds all begin with them.

Materials are a category of items; what a thing is made of (wood, stone, iron…) is its “substance”, see Substances.

Common materials

Log A tree felled with an axe drops 2 (1 more with Woodcutter learned) Split into planks; clubs; Gus's quest Wood for the Fire; expanding the Held Ground
Plank Click a log on the ground with an axe, or make them in the crafting panel carrying an axe: 1 log gives 2 planks Walls, floors, doors, stairs, beds, workbenches, buckets, shields, chests; mending the Ford's bridge
Iron Ore Break the iron veins of the Old Mine with a pickaxe (2 each); dropped when the cyclops or a Seal-bearer falls Garrick forging Dragonsbane; Land II of expanding the Held Ground; smelted into Iron Ingots in a lit forge
Iron Ingot Right-click a lit forge with iron ore, one smelted for one; or smelt them in batches beside the forge by a smithing recipe Forged at the anvil into weapons, armour, tools and jewelry; sold to merchants
Gold Ingot Right-click a lit forge with gold ore, one smelted for one; once you learn Goldsmith, also by recipe Forged into rings and amulets; sold to merchants (worth 75)

Herb The Dawnbloom on Dawn Hill, which slowly grows back; grow your own (seeds bought at the trading stall) Eaten (restores a little health); Ilse's quest The Vigil; sold to the trading stall
Fireweed On the grass of Firstlight Village, slowly growing back Wren brewing the Fireproof Draught
Frostcap In the Forest, slowly growing back Wren brewing the Fireproof Draught
Bone Two lie on the ground at the Ford —
Ash Left when something that burns has burned out As fertilizer: a fertilized crop reaped with a sickle gives 1 more
Fertilizer Bought at the trading stall on The Held Ground Fertilizes like ash
Wheat Grow your own (Wheat Seeds bought at the trading stall) Feeding animals; sold to the trading stall
Animal Feed Bought at the trading stall Feeding animals: a fed cow can be milked, and two fed animals of a kind have young

Sticks, stones and cut grass on the ground grow back one at a time every so often (15 seconds), until a map holds a certain number of them.

Materials from broken things

When something is broken, it leaves half the materials it is made of (50%): a tree is 4 logs and 2 sticks, and felled it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; a wooden wall is 2 planks, and broken it leaves 1. A thing you made yourself also gives back only half its materials when broken; knocking a wall, a floor or another structure loose with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer gets it back whole. See Durability and Breaking.

Materials with no source

Forms such as the Bolt of Cloth, Leather Hide, Wool, Wax and Salt have no fixed source on the maps at present.

  • Gold Ore is dug from the gold veins deep in the Old Mine; the third level of expanding the Held Ground needs 3; it can also be smelted into Gold Ingots in the forge.
  • Wheat has to be grown: Wheat Seeds are bought at the trading stall on The Held Ground.
  • A Feather is left only by a felled chick, with a one-in-two chance.
  • Fertilizer and Animal Feed are bought at the trading stall; see fertilizing and feeding.

History

  • 2026-10-09: Iron Ingots and Gold Ingots were added: ore is smelted into them in a lit forge.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
