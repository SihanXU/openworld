The Slime King is a huge slime, with far more health and attack than an ordinary slime. It does not currently appear in the game: no map, respawn rule or story event puts it into the world. It holds a slot on the monster page of the codex, so for now the codex cannot be completed.

It carries the “boss” and “large” tags, but it is not one of the bosses: it has no telegraphed skills and does not start a boss fight.

Spawning

Not in the game yet.

It currently spawns nowhere.

Behavior

The following is its type data; its temper depends on where it is placed.

  • Speed: very slow. About 0.9 seconds per tile.
  • Element: none.
  • Fears fire. Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

It is an elite among the minor monsters: it uses its moves by the minor monsters' rules (whenever their conditions are met), and it has two.

  • Royal Bounce: at 2–4 tiles away, it lights red marks within two tiles of your feet (a 5×5 square without its four corners), and leaps over 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the marks takes attack × 1.5 damage. Its area is one ring wider than an ordinary slime's Hop.
  • Split: when its health falls below half, 2 green slimes split off from it, only once per fight. Clear the small ones first, or strike fast before they close in.

Strategy

It is far slower than the player: hit it and back off, and you avoid its counterattacks.

Drops

  • Slime Gel×4 (makeup 8 × 50%)
  • Crown of the Slime King (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: always drops)
  • Experience 135 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)
