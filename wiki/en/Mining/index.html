Mining

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Mining is breaking stony things with a pickaxe (Pickaxe). Rocks and boulders give Stone; ore veins give Iron Ore, Coal and Gold Ore. The mining ground you can reach in Chapter 1 is Dawn Hill Mine on the west slope of Dawn Hill, north of Firstlight Village (iron and coal, with a stone pickaxe at the entrance); later there are The Old Mine and the Quarry on The Held Ground. Stones grow on the ground by themselves anyway (see Foraging); mining is a bigger, faster source of stone, and one of the only sources of iron ore.

Veins grow back: break open a vein and it drops 2 ore, leaving a spent face where it stood — bedrock that no tool can touch — which turns back into a vein after one game day (24 game hours, about 24 minutes), in the same place. The clock keeps running while you are off that map. Boulders and rocks are gone once broken.

How to mine

Hold a pickaxe, point the mouse at a rock and press the left button. The prompt bar at the top reads “Mine …”, and the rock's cell is outlined.

  • Each blow deals your own attack plus the pickaxe's power. A pickaxe's efficiency against both stone and ore is 1.
  • A new player has attack 2 and a stone pickaxe has power 4, so a blow does 6 points.
  • When durability reaches 0, it shatters and drops materials by “what it is made of × half” (see Durability and Breaking). What drops pops out onto the ground; walk close and it flies into your bag.

Fists cannot break stone. The body's material is softer than stone, so hitting it only shows “Too hard.” with no damage. An axe can break it, but its efficiency is only 0.25: 2 points a blow with a stone axe.

What can be mined

Boulder The north slope of The Ford and elsewhere 12 2 4 stones

Stone wall Stone-built walls on maps 24 4 2 stones
Iron vein The Old Mine 12 2 2 iron ore
Coal seam The Old Mine 12 2 2 coal
Gold vein The deepest part of The Old Mine 12 2 2 gold ore

The amount dropped is what it is made of times the salvage rate of 0.5: a boulder is made of 8 stones and drops 4 when broken.

The Old Mine

The Old Mine is north of Emberfall, a dark cave map.

  • The main tunnel has four coal seams and four iron veins along its sides, and a cyclops guards the iron vein at the deepest point. The deepest chamber also has two gold veins, just below the iron vein the cyclops guards; this is the only place in the whole world with gold.
  • On the ground at the mine's entrance lies an “Old Pickaxe” (an iron pickaxe); if you have no pickaxe, take that one for now.
  • Veins grow back: iron veins and coal seams up to 4 each, with one more appearing on empty cave floor every 3 game hours (3 real minutes); gold veins up to 2, with one more every 6 game hours. They grow only while someone is in the mine.
  • The mine has bat, cave spider and skeleton; the spiders and bats come back too.

Other sources of iron ore: the cyclops carries 2, which drop when you fell it; every Seal-bearer drops 4 when they fall (see Bosses).

What ore is for

  • Iron ore: Expand the Held Ground II needs 5; the smith Garrick in Emberfall wants 5 iron ore and 3 coal to forge you Dragonsbane (see Emberfall and the Dragon).
  • Coal: used only when Garrick forges Dragonsbane (3).
  • Gold Ore: Expand the Held Ground III needs 3. Mining out both gold veins gives 4, enough.
  • Smelting: hold iron ore or gold ore and right-click the lit forge in Emberfall, and each ore melts into one Iron Ingot or Gold Ingot (see Smelting). Ingots are hammered on an anvil into weapons, armor, tools and jewelry, or sold to merchants: an iron ingot is worth 15 and a gold ingot 75, more than the ore (6 and 20). Quests and Garrick want ore, so keep enough before you smelt.

How ingots are made into things is in Smithing; the skills of the Smithing branch smelt more and forge better.

Tips

  • To walk off the edge of a map onto The Lands of the Five Seals, you often have to break the rocks at the edge first.
  • A stone pickaxe breaks a rock in two blows, as fast as a stone axe fells a tree; but a cave wall takes three.
  • Bare hands against rock only get “Too hard”; make a stone pickaxe first: 2 stones, 2 sticks, 1 cut grass (see Crafting).

Related skills

Prospector and Deep Vein make veins drop more ore, Heavy Pick makes the pickaxe stronger, Stonecutter makes stone drop more, and Gem Finder sometimes digs up gems (see Gathering skills). The ore you dig can be taken to Smithing.

History

  • 2026-10-03: The Old Mine added with the main quest's maps, with iron veins, coal seams and a cyclops.
  • 2026-10-04: Rocks, veins and cave walls got durability and material; the old rule that a pickaxe could chip stone endlessly was removed, and broken things give back half of what they were made of (W2b). Veins grow back slowly by the “appearing” rule instead (W7).
  • 2026-10-08: Two gold veins added in the deepest part of the Old Mine, so gold ore can be mined.
  • 2026-10-09: Ore can be smelted into iron and gold ingots in a lit forge.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
