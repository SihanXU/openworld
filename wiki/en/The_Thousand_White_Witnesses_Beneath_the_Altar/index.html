The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Lore

The Thousand White Witnesses Beneath the Altar are the fifth of the five Seal-bearers, holder of the Fifth Seal, tied to the Fifth Suffering, the Piercing, and to the holy instrument the Lance That Pierced the Heart. The figure is modeled on the martyrs of the fifth seal in the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, the White Witnesses are the boss of Chapter 5, and the last Seal-bearer before the Final Chapter.

Lore

  • Seal: the Fifth Seal.
  • Torment: the Piercing. At last a holy lance pierced Astra's chest. At that very moment the sun rose again, and the Long Night ended.
  • Instrument of the Sufferings: the Lance That Pierced the Heart.
  • Like the other Seal-bearers, the White Witnesses share part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.
What the White Witnesses bear (click to show)
  • The White Witnesses are one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bear “the wound to the soul the holy lance left” (§12).
  • What the Piercing really does: when the blade went through his heart, Astra's body became, for the first time, an “entrance” joining reality and the First Night, and the First Night began to pour into his body (§4).
