The calf is the young of the cow. When two fed cows stand close together, one of them gives birth to a calf beside itself: “A calf is born!” It uses the cow's sprite, only smaller. A calf grows into a cow after 48 game hours; until then it cannot be milked and does not breed. See Animals.

Spawning

  • Breeding: born when two well-fed cow stand side by side
  • Put Crated Calf down: the animal inside walks out
  • Calves are never placed directly on a map. They are born of cows, and cows on any map can give birth: The Held Ground, The Grassland, or a released calf once it has grown up.
  • Releasing a “Crated Calf” bought at the trading stall gives you a calf.

Behavior

  • How it is born: feed a cow Animal Feed or Wheat, and once fed it calls to the cows near it. If a cow within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down) that is already fed and has not given birth recently hears it, that cow gives birth to a calf beside itself (if that tile has no room, on the nearest free tile). Both cows are then bred and will not breed again for 24 game hours. As many such cows as are nearby, that many calves are born.
  • Growing up: 48 game hours after it is born, it becomes a cow. Time still passes while the player is not on that map, and is made up when you return.
  • Before it grows up: it cannot be milked (no rule says what happens if you click it with a bucket), it does not hear the cows' call, and it does not breed. It can be fed, and is happy when fed, but that does nothing else.
  • Temper: docile. Like a cow, it never attacks anyone; whoever hits it, it runs away from. It usually wanders near where it was born. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: medium. About 0.6 seconds per tile, faster than a cow.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

Skills

No skills of its own: only plain attacks.

Strategy

  • Get two cows first. The Held Ground starts with only one cow; buy a Crated Calf (120 Dawnmarks) in the “Animal Husbandry” section of the trading stall, and once the released calf grows up you have a second cow.
  • Feed one, then the other one next to it. The two feedings must fall within 24 game hours, and the two cows must be no more than 3 steps apart when you feed the second. The one fed first gives birth to the calf. Animals wander, so feed them while they are close together; you can also pen them together with a fence.
  • One portion of feed per animal. One calf takes at least 2 portions of feed (5 portions for 6 Dawnmarks at the stall); wheat works too.
  • Wait for it to grow up. After 48 game hours the calf grows into a cow, which can be milked (see Bucket of Milk) and can have calves of its own. Two cows can breed again after 24 game hours.

Drops

  • Raw Beef (makeup 1 × 50%)
  • Experience 5 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

A calf is made of 1 portion of Raw Beef and leaves half of it when it falls: a 50% chance to leave 1 piece, otherwise nothing. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health, 5 points.

History

  • 2026-10-08: Added to the game: two fed cows standing together have a calf, and the calf grows into a cow.
  • 2026-10-08: Born and released calves are as docile as cows and no longer chase and butt people; a released Crated Calf is a calf; growing up now takes two game days, and the rest after being fed or giving birth is a full day.
