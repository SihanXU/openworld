Crafting

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Crafting turns the materials you carry into new things. Press C to open the crafting panel. The panel lists every recipe in the game; nothing has to be unlocked, and when you have the materials you can make it. It is not called “combining”.

Where recipes come from

Everything that can be made says two things: what it is made of, and where it is made. The recipe comes from these two; there is no separate recipe table:

  • Made of: a stone axe is 1 stone, 1 stick and 1 cut grass; a wall is 2 planks.
  • Where it is made: “by hand” is made anywhere, any time — that is this panel; “forge” and “anvil” recipes are made by pressing E on that thing to open its own panel (see Smithing).

A few “by hand” recipes wait for a building of the Held Ground; once it stands they too are made anywhere:

Building Recipes unlocked
Workshop Club, Shield, Bow
Workshop II (upgrade) Candle (1 honey jar, 1 cut grass make 2), Lantern (1 iron ingot, 1 torch); a Forge Builder's smith's forge, an Apiarist's bee box
Weaving Shed Bolt of Cloth (4 cut grass); the leather gloves, boots, helmet, pauldrons and breastplate of a Leatherworker

The simple tools and things — stone axe, pickaxe, hoe, sickle, fishing rod, smith hammer, torch, bucket, planks, bed, workbench, walls, floors, doors, stairs — are made by hand from the start, with no building. The skill recipes and the sets at an anvil wait for the Held Ground's smithy upgrades, and the arcane workbench's recipes for the Arcane Workshop: see Expanding the Held Ground.

Until then those recipes are greyed, with “needs the Workshop built” after their names.

A new thing the world learns (see the world learns) can be made in the panel from the moment it appears, as long as it says these two things as well.

Using the panel

Each row is a recipe:

  • On the left is what it makes. One marked “needs the Workshop built” or “needs the Weaving Shed built” waits for that building on the Held Ground; the label is red until it stands.
  • In the middle are the materials, written “on you / needed”; any you are short of turn red.
  • A tool you need to carry is drawn as its icon, with ✓ if you have it and ✗ if not. Hovering shows “A tool you need to carry; it is not used up.”
  • On the right is the “Craft” button. A recipe that makes more than one says “Craft ×2”.
  • When you can make several, an “All ×N” button appears: it keeps crafting from what you carry until it can make no more. Shift-clicking “Craft” does the same.

Rules:

  • Only what is in your bag and in your hands counts. Materials in a chest beside you do not.
  • Tools are not used up. The Plank recipe needs an axe on you, and the axe is not used up.
  • What you make goes into your bag, and “+1 name” floats up on the screen.

Material and quality

What you make:

  • Material follows the first ingredient. A tool that starts with stone is of stone (stone axe, stone pickaxe); one that starts with a log, plank or stick is of wood (wooden bow, wooden shield, wooden bed). The material sets the multiplier of a weapon's power and armor's defense; see Substances.
  • Quality depends on how far the maker's level is above the recipe's level:
Level − recipe level Quality Power, defense multiplier
Below 0 Crude ×0.8
0 to 2 Common ×1
3 to 5 Fine ×1.25
6 and above Masterwork ×1.5

Every recipe is level 1 except the Bow (recipe level 2). So a bow made at level 1 is a “Crude Wooden Bow”, from level 2 it is common, from level 4 tools are Fine, and from level 7 Masterwork. The two qualities Vowbound and Stigmatic cannot be made, only found (see Quality).

For example: a stone axe has power 5; a Fine one made at level 4 has 6, a Masterwork one made at level 7 has 8.

Structures and furniture

Wall, Floor, Door, Stairs, a wooden bed and a wooden workbench can be made in the crafting panel first and carried, then held and right-clicked to build them (putting one down with G only leaves it loose on the ground); or they can be placed directly in Building mode, which makes one from materials on the spot when you carry none. Campfires, chests, fences and signs are not in the crafting panel; they are built in Building mode.

Profession gear

Anyone can make a Shield or a Bow, but only a Warrior can wear a shield, and only a Ranger can fight with a bow (see Professions).

What you get back when it breaks

When something you made is broken, only half of what it was made of comes back (see Durability and Breaking). Things that can be picked up, such as walls, floors and beds: knock one with an axe, pickaxe or hammer (left click) and it comes loose whole where it stood; then press E to pick it up, and you get the whole piece back with nothing lost.

What there is not

  • No cooking recipes: food can only be cooked over a fire; see Cooking.
  • No smelting or smithing recipes. Smelting is not in the crafting panel: hold iron ore or gold ore and right-click a lit forge, and each ore melts into one Iron Ingot or Gold Ingot (see Smelting). No recipe uses ingots yet, and the Smithing branch has no skills yet.
  • Smelting and smithing are not in this panel: press E on a forge or an anvil and its own panel opens with what is made at it (see Smithing). The cauldron has no recipes of its own yet (lit, it cooks; see Cooking).
  • The trading stall on The Held Ground also counts as a “workstation” in the game: buying and selling are worked out the way a recipe is (what you give is used up, and what you get goes into your bag). But these trades are not in the crafting panel; they are in the stall's own panel; see Money.

Tips

  • The third lesson of the tutorial is making a stone axe: press C; 1 stick, 1 stone, 1 cut grass.
  • You need not put logs down to split them: carry an axe and click “All” on the plank row in the panel.
  • The club, the shield and the bow wait for the Workshop on the Held Ground (8 logs, 4 stones; it can be built from the start), see Expanding the Held Ground.

History

  • 2026-10-02: The survival loop added the crafting panel, opened with C.
  • 2026-10-04: Recipes are derived from “made of” and “where it is made” instead of being listed separately; what you make has a material and a quality (W4). Recipes in the panel for things that are not items (walls, stairs) show their own picture.
  • 2026-10-05: Logs can be split into planks right in the panel, with an axe on you; “All ×N” added.
  • 2026-10-08: A fishing rod can be made by hand (2 sticks, 2 cut grass). The trades of the stall on the Held Ground borrow the recipes' workings but appear only in the stall's panel.
  • 2026-10-09: Ore can be smelted into iron and gold ingots in a lit forge (not in the crafting panel).
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
  • 2026-10-21: Standing beside a workbench, forge or anvil is no longer asked. The forge and the anvil open their own panels with E; the workbench's recipes (club, shield, bow) unlock once the Workshop is built on the Held Ground and are made anywhere from the bag, as cloth and leather gear are once the Weaving Shed stands.
  • 2026-10-21: The Workshop can be raised to level II (candles, lanterns, a smith's forge, a bee box); the anvil's skill recipes and sets wait for the Held Ground's smithy upgrades; the arcane workbench was added (wands, staves, draughts, spell books).
