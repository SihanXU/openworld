Frostfang Coast

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Location
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Frostfang Coast is one of the six regions of the Lands of the Five Seals, in the northwest of the continent. It is the land of the First Seal, the kingdom of the White Knight Alberic, who bears the burden of the Crown of Thorns.

All the story you can play in the game today is here: from Cloudtop, the Ford and Firstlight Village to, east of the river, the Pilgrim's Road, the East Road, Emberfall and Cinder Pass. For every map, see Locations.

Landscape

A cold coast, pine forests and hills, with snowfields in the northernmost rows. The mountains north of the Ford and the river to the south enclose the river's west bank, and only the Ford crosses it (see the Lands of the Five Seals). Its wilds are the original five grounds (see The Wild).
