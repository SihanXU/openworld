Music

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The game's music and sound effects come from the asset pack Ninja Adventure (CC0). Music plays by map, and boss fights switch to boss music. In the Options, sound only has an on/off switch, with no volume; music is fixed at 35% volume.

Every map has its own track: the wilds and The Lands of the Five Seals play Field; The Clearing and The Forest play Forest; Firstlight Village, Millbrook, the Held Ground and Emberfall play Village; barrows, mines and cellars play Cave; The Dragon's Lair plays Battle; the Chapel of the First Dawn plays Temple. A new game starts with Field at Cloudtop.

Tracks

The tracks the asset pack includes and the game uses: Village, Village (Night), Field, Forest, Interior, Cave, Battle, Boss Battle, Shop, Temple, Dungeon, Dark Forest, Title. Those the maps actually use at present are Village, Field, Forest, Cave, Battle, Boss Battle and Temple.

History

  • 2026-10-08: every map's music name now matches a track: a new game is no longer silent, the Clearing and the Forest play the forest track, and the villages play the village track.

Sound effects

Footsteps, pick up, put down, hit, hurt, attack, cast, fire, water, ice, thunder, wind, explosion, door opening, eating, healing, coins, undefined (doing something the world has never seen), learned (the world weaves in a new thread), death.
