The Forest lies south of the Grassland: a very dense wood, with a path threading between a few clearings and running south to Thornwood Hollow. It is a good place for timber, and Frostcap grows here. In a clearing in the southeast corner is a camp no one has used in a long time.

The Forest is an open-air combat zone; after nightfall night creatures appear.

Map

The map is 40×30. The woods are thick all round, and inside them about two tiles in five hold a tree. The path comes in at the northern exit (columns 22–24), passes the northern clearing, bends southwest, skirts the middle and eastern clearings, then runs west to the clearing in the southwest corner and leaves the map southward from there (columns 12–14). Three side paths branch off: one west through the woods, leaving the map on the west side (rows 14–16) for The Charcoal Dell; one east from the eastern clearing, leaving the map on the east side (rows 16–18) for The Reed Bend; and one to the clearing in the southeast corner.

Areas

  • The northern clearing: the first clearing you reach coming in from the Grassland. Gus fells trees here every morning.
  • The middle clearings: the few forest clearings the path runs through, the ground strewn with fallen leaves, ferns and moss.
  • The Pilgrim's Camp: the clearing in the southeast corner. A rotted tent, a cold fire ring (a dead campfire), an old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage nailed to a tree (a notice board; press E to read it), and beside it a cairn with a pilgrim's staff leaning on it; on the ground by the cairn lies a Pilgrim's Scallop. The paper on the tree reads: “BY ORDER OF THE HOLY CITY. The Seal-bearers weaken. Let the pilgrimage be called. Brother Cassian of Firstlight is named pilgrim. — In the 380th year of the Five. (The paper is grey with age. The wording is almost the same as the courier's.)”
  • The way to Thornwood Hollow: the southwest corner, where the path leaves the Forest southward.

Residents

  • Guscomes by on schedule

No one lives in the Forest. Gus fells trees in the northern clearing every day from 6:00 to 12:00, then goes back to Firstlight Village to stack the wood.

  • Logs: the Forest has more trees than anywhere. Felling one gives 2 Logs and 1 Stick, and the stump it leaves grows back in 2 game days. See Woodcutting.
  • Frostcap: at the start 3 Frostcap grow in the shade; once taken, one may grow back every 90 seconds, up to 3 at a time. It is one of the ingredients Wren, the herbalist of Emberfall, asks for.
  • Sticks: they lie thickest on the ground here, up to 10 at a time. Stones reach at most 3, and cut grass at most 4.

Lore

The first time you enter the Forest, Lumi says that a forest this big ought to be full of noise in the afternoon, yet there is not a single bird. Where the birds went is answered in the Charcoal Dell to the west: since the night the little bridge broke, every bird in the Forest has flown each dusk to the old oak of the charcoal burner Orrin to roost, all of them facing east, not one making a sound (see Where the Birds Went).

The Pilgrim Before (click to show)

Walking into the camp in the southeast corner plays a scene: Lumi reads out the paper on the tree and finds it almost word for word the same as the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage the courier read to you, only very old, and the name on it is not yours. The scallop by the cairn is a pilgrim's token; Sister Ilse will know whose it is. After this you can take up the quest The Pilgrim Before: it was Brother Cassian, named thirty-one years ago, for whom Ilse prayed when she was still a novice.

Once you have found the camp, come back at night (20:00–04:00) and a pale shade kneels beside the cold fire ring, head bowed, and says: “Do not kneel to him.” Then it vanishes.
