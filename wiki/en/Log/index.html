Log

A Log is the raw timber you get from felling a tree. Its main use is becoming Plank: with an Axe on you, one log makes two planks, and the walls, floors, doors, beds and workbenches you build all need planks.

Source

  • Left when broken or harvested (makeup × 50%): wood pile, stump, Club, Plank, tree (terrain), pine tree (terrain), stump (terrain)
  • Felling trees: fell a tree with an Axe and it leaves 2 logs and 1 stick; with Woodcutter, 1 more. The stump grows back into a tree after two game days. See Woodcutting.
  • Wood pile: logs are stacked on the wood pile in Firstlight Village. The woodcutter Gus goes to the forest to fell trees every morning and stacks the logs there in the afternoon; anyone who needs them can take them.
  • Things made of wood, such as stumps and wood piles, also leave some logs when they are broken (see Durability and Breaking).

Uses

  • Made into: Club, Plank, Mace, Mace, Warhammer, Warhammer
  • Needed for quests: Wood for the Fire, Wood for the Clamp
  • Making planks: with the axe in your inventory, press C and choose “Plank”: 1 log makes 2 planks. Or lay the log on the ground and left-click it with the axe to split it into 2 planks.
  • Making a club: standing by a workbench, 1 log makes a wooden club.
  • Quests: Gus's daily quest Wood for the Fire asks for 4 logs; the first level of expanding the Held Ground asks for 10.
  • Burning: logs catch fire (the tooltip says “Flammable”). A campfire needs no feeding; a log thrown into a lit fire just burns to Ash.

History

  • 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue; a tree felled with an axe leaves logs.
  • 2026-10-03: Gus's daily quest asks for logs.
  • 2026-10-08: The tooltip no longer says “can be used as fuel”.
