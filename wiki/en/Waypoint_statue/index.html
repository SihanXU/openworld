Waypoint statue

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Waypoint statues are ancient stone figures scattered across the land. Press E on one to wake it, and it lights up; from then on you can click it on the world map (M) to travel there. The statue you woke last is also the player's resting place: on Normal difficulty you come back to life beside it after falling. Standing beside a woken statue, you can also have Lumi change to another boon.

There are six at present, one each at Mountain Foot, Firstlight Village, The Ford, Thornwood Hollow, Dawn Hill and The East Road. The first time a statue comes within 6 cells of you, Lumi says: “Look — a waypoint statue! Walk up and press E to wake it; then the map (M) can take you back to it from anywhere.”

Waking

Press E on a statue not yet woken, and the prompt reads “Wake X”:

  • The statue lights up and glows from then on, standing out especially at night. The lower left says: “The statue wakes under your hand. (M: the map — travel to any statue you have woken.)”
  • It becomes your resting place (replacing the bed you last slept in or the statue you last woke). See Resting place.
  • Each statue woken is recorded once in the story. Nessa Quill's side quest The Measure of Days asks you to wake three.
  • On the minimap, woken statues are bright cyan dots, and those not yet woken are dark cyan.

Listening

Press E on a statue already woken, and the prompt reads “Listen to X”:

  • The notice reads “The statue hums. Open the map (M) to travel to another one.”
  • Lumi's boon panel opens, where you can change the one she carries (talking to Lumi opens this panel too, but there you can only look, not change). See Boons.
  • Listening doesn't change your resting place.

Travel

  1. Press M to open the world map.
  2. Click a glowing statue (hovering over it says “click to travel”).
  3. You appear at once on the cell just below that statue, facing it. Lumi comes along too.
  • You can only travel to statues you have woken. Statues not yet woken are marked “not woken yet” on the map.
  • You cannot travel during a cutscene, while a yes/no question is open, or while the world is learning from your action.
  • Travel takes no game time.

Changing boons

Lumi's boons can only be changed within 3 cells of a woken waypoint statue: press E on the statue (listen) to open the panel, and choose one of five slots. At present four of the five slots are still sealed. When Lumi isn't with you (not on the same map), her boon has no effect.

Lore

The scholar Nessa Quill says these statues are older than the Church and hum at sunset; they all hum the same note, a little lower every week. She reads the statues to work out how fast the days are shortening. See Why the nights grow longer.

Tips

  • When you reach a new place, wake its statue first: you can travel back, and your resting place moves there too.
  • On Normal difficulty, wake the nearest statue before heading far out to fight, so the place you come back to life after falling is closer to the battle.
  • To move your resting place back to your bed at home, just go back and sleep.
