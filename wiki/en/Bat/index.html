The bat is the fastest-moving monster in the game, with very little health. It lives in dark places: there are bats in The Old Barrow, the The Ossuary of the Thorn, the Chapel of the First Dawn and the The Old Mine. After nightfall it is also one of the two night creatures that come up in the wild.

Spawning

  • Longsleep Barrow: 1 at the start
  • The Ossuary of the Thorn: 1 at the start
  • Dawn Hill Mine: 2 at the start, respawns (up to 2, once every 180 s)
  • Chapel of the First Dawn: 1 at the start
  • The Embervein Mine: 2 at the start, respawns (up to 2, once every 180 s)
  • Night Creatures: outdoor maps in combat zones, after nightfall
  • The Old Barrow, the Ossuary of the Thorn, the Chapel of the First Dawn: 1 each. Once felled, they do not come back.
  • The Old Mine: 2. When fewer than 2 remain, another appears in the tunnels every 3 game hours.
  • The wild at night: on the outdoor maps of combat zones, four in ten of the night creatures that come up after nightfall are bats. They vanish at daybreak. See Night Creatures.

Behavior

  • Temper: aggressive. It swoops at anyone it sees within 4 to 5 tiles (8 tiles for those that appear at night). See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: fastest. About 0.3 seconds per tile, as fast as the player walking slowly.
  • Flies. Drops in height do not stop it, and it never falls.
  • Element: none.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of a lit fire.

  • Swoop: in the same row or column, 2–3 tiles away, it dives along a red line 0.6 seconds later and bites the first enemy (attack × 1.2), draining back 1 health, then bounces back 2 tiles. It always swoops and leaves; rather than chase it, step aside when it comes at you next.

Strategy

  • If you cannot get away, fight: it has very little health.
  • In dark places like the barrow and the mine, a Torch in your hand does not keep it back; what does is something lit or burning, such as the lit braziers in the tunnels.
  • At night in the wild, the safest places are beside a lit campfire or shut indoors.

Drops

  • Fertilizer (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Batwing Slippers (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 18 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press E to pick them up (see Monster Drops). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
