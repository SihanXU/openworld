Woodcutting

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Woodcutting is chopping trees with an axe. A felled tree leaves a stump, two Log, one Stick, and sometimes an Acorn. Logs split into Plank, and planks are the material most used in building and crafting.

Chopping a tree

Hold an axe (Axe), point the mouse at a tree (a tree or a pine tree) and press the left button. The prompt bar at the top reads “Chop …”, and the tree's cell is outlined.

  • Every chop takes some durability off the tree and leaves an axe mark on it (status notched).
  • The mark lasts 10 seconds. If you do not chop again within 10 seconds, the tree heals and its durability is full again.
  • A tree has 10 durability. Each chop deals your own attack plus the axe's power, and an axe's efficiency against wood is 1 (see Durability and Breaking). A new player has attack 2 and a stone axe has power 5, so a chop does 7 points: two chops fell a tree.
  • When the tree falls you see “The tree falls!”, it turns into a stump where it stood, and it drops 2 logs and 1 stick, with a 25% chance of 1 acorn as well.
  • With Woodcutter learned, every tree drops 1 more log. After you learn it, trees no longer drop acorns.

What drops pops out onto the ground; walk close and it flies into your bag.

Without an axe

  • Bare hands: a punch on a tree does 1 damage, so ten punches fell a tree. Fists leave no axe mark, so the durability you knock off never grows back.
  • Other weapons: blades such as swords and daggers have an efficiency of 0.5 against wood, blunt weapons 0.25, much slower than an axe (efficiencies in Durability and Breaking).

Burnt trees

  • A tree that catches fire and burns out, or is chopped down while it burns, becomes a charred tree: it leaves no stump and drops no logs.
  • One chop of an axe on a charred tree breaks it into 1 Coal (“The charred tree crumbles into coal.”).
  • Cinder Pass has charred trees already standing.

Bushes and other plants

  • A bush has 4 durability and drops 1 stick when broken: four punches bare-handed, one chop with an axe.
  • A briar has 6 durability, is woody, and has a chance to drop 1 stick when broken.

Stumps

  • A stump in the open with no one standing on it grows back into a tree after two game days (48 game hours, 48 real minutes). While it is covered or someone stands on it, it looks again every game hour.
  • Chop the stump too (6 durability, one chop of a stone axe) and you get 1 log, and that cell never grows a tree again.
  • A Shovel digs the whole stump out (for 1 more stick); a dug-out stump never grows back into a tree. Shovels are sold at the trading stall on The Held Ground.

So, to keep a wood full of trees to chop, chop only the trees and leave the stumps.

Logs and planks

A log splits into two planks, in one of two ways:

  1. In the crafting panel: press C and choose Plank. You need an axe on you; it is not used up. Each craft makes 2 planks; “All ×N” splits every log you carry at once.
  2. On the ground: put a log on the ground, hold an axe and left-click it: “You split the log into planks.” Two planks pop out.

The uses of logs and planks are on their own pages. The most common:

Use Logs Planks
Walls, floors, doors 2 each
Stairs 3
bed, workbench 2, 4
Club (at a workbench) 1
Wood for the Fire (once a day) 4
Expand the Held Ground I 10

A plank put on shallow water lays a stretch of wooden bridge (see Terrain).

Acorns and oaks

  • Chopping a tree has a 25% chance to drop an Acorn (none after you learn Woodcutter).
  • Hold an acorn and right-click a cell that can be hoed (grass, dirt, sand, snow, farmland); if no other crop is on it, you plant it: “You plant an acorn.”
  • What you plant is an oak sapling. Like a stump, in the open with no one standing on it, it grows into a tree after two game days.

The dead tree at the Ford

Where the river at The Ford is narrowest stands a dead tree. Chop it three times; on the third chop it falls away from the player. If it falls across the river, its trunk becomes a stretch of wooden bridge, and a stump is left where it stood. This is one of the Ford's solutions; see The Ford.

Tips

  • The edge of a map is ringed with woods or rocks. To walk off the map onto The Lands of the Five Seals, you first have to chop (or dig) a way out.
  • A stone axe fells a tree in two chops. Do not wander off too long between them: the axe mark heals in 10 seconds.
  • Keep logs as they are and split them when you need them: the crafting panel splits logs without your putting them down.

Related skills

Woodcutter, Sharp Axe, Carpenter, Forester, Timber Master: chop faster, get more logs, and have every tree drop an acorn (see Gathering skills).

History

  • 2026-09-30: First version of woodcutting: an axe chops a tree; the tree falls and leaves a stump and logs.
  • 2026-10-04: Trees got durability and a material, and a stone axe fells a tree in two chops; stumps use a “growth stage” instead and grow back into trees after two days (W2b, W7).
  • 2026-10-05: In the crafting panel, an axe on you turns logs into planks; “All” splits them all at once.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
