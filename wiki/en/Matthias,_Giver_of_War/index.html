Matthias, Giver of War

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Lore

Matthias, Giver of War is the second of the five Seal-bearers, holder of the Second Seal, tied to the Second Suffering, the Scourging, and to the holy instrument the Pillar of Scourging. Matthias's figure is modeled on the rider on the red horse among the first five seals of the Book of Revelation. Following the structure of “one Seal-bearer per chapter”, Matthias is the boss of Chapter 2.

Lore

  • Seal: the Second Seal.
  • Torment: the Scourging. Astra was bound to a pillar and scourged at length; legend says that even when his blood had all run out, he still did not fall. The Church reads it as “repaying everyone's sins with the flesh”.
  • Instrument of the Sufferings: the Pillar of Scourging.
  • Like the other Seal-bearers, Matthias shares part of Astra's pain, and centuries of bearing it have twisted this Seal-bearer.
What Matthias bears (click to show)
  • Matthias is one of the five Fulcrums of the vessel that is Astra, and bears “the bodily pain of the scourging” (§12).
  • What the Scourging really does is let the flesh gradually adapt to the constant pain and transformation the First Night brings (§4).

In the game

Matthias is not yet in the game: there is no chapter, place, boss skill or dialogue for this Seal-bearer, and no Boon that Lumi receives when Matthias falls. The original lore text says nothing more about Matthias than the above.

After Chapter 1, what currently follows in the game is an older storyline that predates this setting, Emberfall and the Dragon, which will later be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal.
