Mage

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

The Mage is one of the four professions, and masters the Staff. The Mage casts spells from a few cells away: an elemental staff deals more damage, every fourth spell is a sure critical, you take fewer blows while holding a staff, and felling an enemy with a spell heals you. Choose it from Bram in Firstlight Village from level 5.

Starting gear

The first time you become a Mage, Bram gives you a Wooden Staff: power 4, range 4, no element.

The staff is the Mage's profession weapon: only a Mage can fight with it, and the Mage's talents work only with staves.

The wand is not the Mage's weapon. The Wand is a common weapon: anyone can use it, its range is 5, but it takes no Mage talent.

How to cast

  • Press Space and the spell flies the way you face, hitting the first person, the first creature or the first thing in the way along that row (or column).
  • When you click a distant target with the left button, it must be in the same row or column as you, and within range.
  • With an elemental staff, the target hit reacts to the element (see Elements): fire sets it burning, ice freezes what is wet, lightning shocks what is wet.

Skills

The Mage's first six talents are in the profession branch of the skill tree (K). One branch makes spells stronger, one keeps the Mage alive, and they end in a 2-point ultimate.

Skill Requires Points Effect
Arcane Focus 1 Staff power +1.
Elemental Attunement Arcane Focus 1 Spells cast with an elemental staff (fire, ice, lightning…) deal +25% damage.
Spell Echo Elemental Attunement 1 Every 4th spell that hits is a sure critical.
Mana Shield Arcane Focus 1 While holding a staff, direct combat damage taken −20%.
Arcane Recovery Mana Shield 1 Felling a creature with a staff restores 3 health (wands do not count).
Archmage Spell Echo, Arcane Recovery 2 Staff power another +2, range +1.

Active skills

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with Shift+1–6 (see Active skills and the skill bar). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are a Mage.

Skill Requires Points Cooldown Effect
Fireball Arcane Focus 1 6 s Hurl a fireball that bursts at the first target ahead, catching the 3×3 around it (1.5× fire damage).
Frost Nova Arcane Focus 1 15 s Enemies within 2 cells take frost damage (0.5×) and are frozen for 2 seconds.
Chain Lightning Fireball 1 10 s Lightning strikes the first enemy ahead, then jumps to 2 others nearby (1.2× damage each).
Blink Frost Nova 1 12 s Teleport up to 4 cells ahead, through creatures but not through walls.

Learning all twenty-two (ten talents, five skill upgrades, seven active skills) takes 25 points. For the skill tree's rules, see Skills.

Advanced (2026-10-09)

The profession branch grew twelve more nodes, twenty-two in all: three new active skills (the last a 2-point ultimate), five skill upgrades (once learned, they apply straight to their active skill: a shorter cooldown, more damage, a wider area), three profession talents, and a 2-point ultimate talent.

Thunderstorm Chain Lightning 1 20 s Call lightning down on the 3×3 round the first enemy ahead, one round a second for 3 seconds (0.7× lightning damage per round).
Meteor Fireball: Inferno, Frost Nova: Spreading 2 35 s Ultimate: call a meteor down on the first target ahead, burning the 5×5 around it (3.0× fire damage).

Legendary Arms

The Mage's two Legendary weapons are Emberheart (fire) and Rimecrown (ice), both elemental, with a longer range than the Wooden Staff. When a Mage fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see Legendary Arms, Bosses).

How to play

  • Keep your distance: the Wooden Staff's range is 4, 5 with Archmage. Do not let enemies get in your face.
  • Water first, then lightning; wet first, then freeze: elemental reactions can turn one spell into a whole area (see Elements).
  • Elemental Attunement needs an elemental staff: the starting Wooden Staff has no element, so this skill does nothing with it; wait until you have an elemental staff (a Legendary staff, for example).
  • Spell Echo counts by itself: every 4 hits, the 4th is a sure critical; you need not keep to the same target.
  • Mana Shield works only while you hold a staff in your hand; switch to anything else and it is gone.

Boss Challenge

In the Boss Challenge, the Mage is level 20: health 94, attack 5, defense 1, holding the “Challenge Ember Staff” (a Wooden Staff of fire), with all twenty-two Mage skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with Space, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned. The least health of the four professions.
