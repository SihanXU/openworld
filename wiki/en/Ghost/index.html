The ghost is a floating undead whose attacks carry ice. There is only one in the whole game, in the hall of niches of the The Ossuary of the Thorn.

Spawning

  • The Ossuary of the Thorn: 1 at the start
  • Abbey of the Thorn: 1 at the start
  • The Abbey Crypt: 1 at the start
  • The Kneeling Field: respawns (up to 1, once every 360 s)
  • The Ossuary of the Thorn: 1, in the hall of niches. Once felled, it does not come back.

Behavior

  • Temper: aggressive. It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 5 tiles, and goes back once the chase takes it 7 tiles from home. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: fairly fast. About 0.5 seconds per tile.
  • Floats. Drops in height do not stop it, and it never falls.
  • Element: ice. Its attacks carry ice: hitting someone who is wet leaves them frozen.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

Skills

SkillWhenMarked cellsMovementWind-upCooldown
Wail1–2 cells awaycircle, 2 cells, centred on itselfstays put1.2 s7 s
  • Wail: at 1–2 tiles away, it lights a purple circle within 2 tiles around itself and wails 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the circle takes attack × 0.8 ice damage and is stunned for 1.5 seconds; anyone wet is frozen. Its wind-up is long; when you see the circle, run out of it.

Strategy

  • While you are dry, its ice will not freeze you; it is just ordinary damage.
  • The ossuary also holds four skeletons and a bat; do not stir them all up at once in the hall.

Drops

  • Ectoplasm (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Wraith Locket (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 37 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press E to pick them up (see Monster Drops). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
