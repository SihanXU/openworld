The Vale of Scales

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Location

The Vale of Scales is one of the six regions of the Lands of the Five Seals, in the east of the continent. It is the land of the Third Seal, where Ehle, Priest of the Black Scale bears the Scales of Hunger: in the market cities everything is weighed, and the poor go hungry.

It is not yet open: you can walk into it, its towns, fortresses and caves are all there, and its wilds hold the monsters of these parts, but no one lives here yet, and there is no story and no commission. On the world map, beneath its name, it reads “Story not yet open”. Later versions will fill it with playable content.

Landscape

The withered great grain plains (fields), dust plains and salt flats, with rivers running through. Its wilds are the Barren Fields, the Dust Plain (cave spiders), the Salt Flat (giant crabs) and the Thin Grassland.

Locations

Its capital is Weighmarket. The Black Balance is the Seal-bearer's seat, sealed for now.
