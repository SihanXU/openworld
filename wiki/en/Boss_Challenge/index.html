Boss Challenge

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The Boss Challenge is a separate mode on a page of its own (the title page has no way in): pick a profession, pick a boss, and fight straight away. The challenge uses a temporary level-20 character; nothing is kept afterward, and no save is touched. All four bosses can be fought here, including the three not yet in the campaign.

How to enter

Open the page boss-gallery.html?challenge=1 beside the game. This page is in Chinese only.

Choosing yourself and your opponent

The page first shows the choice panel:

  1. Choose a profession: one of Assassin, Warrior, Ranger and Mage. Each card shows the weapon and the way of fighting, with health and defense.
  2. Choose an opponent: one of the four bosses, with how many cells it takes up beside it. Those you have beaten have a ✓ in front.
  3. Click “Start the challenge”.

The challenge character

Every profession is level 20. All six profession skills are learned, plus four shared skills: Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength, Vitality. The weapon is put in your hand automatically. You never get hungry.

Profession Health Attack Defense Weapon
Assassin 114 6 1 Iron Dagger
Warrior 174 6 2, plus the Wooden Shield's +2 Iron Sword
Ranger 104 5 1 Wooden Bow (range 7)
Mage 94 5 1 Challenge Ember Staff (a Wooden Staff of fire)

Health already includes Vitality (+4) and the Warrior's Unbreakable (+10), and attack already includes Strength (+1).

The bosses use their full numbers: Alberic, the White Knight, for example, has 1200 health, far more than the 300 he has in the campaign (see Bosses).

The battlefield

  • The battlefield is a clearing on the East Road, at 12 noon.
  • Only the player and the boss are on it: no residents, no other monsters, no night creatures rising at night, and the map's exits are sealed.

Controls

  • WASD or the arrow keys move, Space attacks. Melee needs you close to the boss; ranged attacks fly the way you face.
  • Esc pauses, and again resumes; switching to another window pauses too.
  • Profession talents work by themselves.

Results

  • The boss falls: “Challenge won”, with how many seconds it took and how many you have beaten this time. With all four beaten, it is “Every boss broken”.
  • You fall: “Challenge lost”, with the tip “Watch the warnings on the ground, leave the marked cells, then strike in the gap as the move ends.”
  • From the result panel you can “Challenge again” or “Change profession / opponent”, and after a win also “Challenge the next”.
  • “Beaten this time: N / 4” counts only while the page stays open, and resets when the page is closed. There are no achievements, and nothing goes into a save.

Boss · Test Stage

Developer feature: only in development builds, not in the released game.

Open the same page (boss-gallery.html) without ?challenge=1 and it is the “Boss · Test Stage”, for development and art review, with no way in either:

  • The left column: pick a boss (“Lord”) and see its collision size, health and defense.
  • “View mode”: plays the boss's animations one by one, with the fight paused. For the White Knight you can also pick the top-down facing (front, side, back, mirrored).
  • One button per skill: click it and the boss uses that move at once, to look at its marks and effects.
  • “Restart”, “Enter phase two” (takes the boss straight down to four-tenths of its health), “Pause”.
  • The test character has 200 health and 8 attack, and neither reads nor writes the game's saves.

Tips

  • For your first fight, choose the Warrior: the most health, Bulwark's damage reduction, and healing when struck.
  • The White Knight's “Thorns Bend the Knee” is a ring, and inside the ring is safe: walk in toward him.
  • Try all four professions here first, then decide which to choose in the campaign (see Professions).

History

  • 2026-10-03: The Boss Challenge and the boss test stage added: a level-20 character for each of the four professions, challenging bosses without touching saves.
  • 2026-10-04: The bosses cut from seven to three.
  • 2026-10-05: Alberic, the White Knight added; the Warrior's weapons changed to an Iron Sword and a Wooden Shield.
