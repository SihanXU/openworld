The Kneeling Field lies between the Pilgrim's Road and the East Road, on the outskirts of the White Knight's kingdom. Pilgrims would kneel on this field to pray, then go on east.

It is a combat zone.

Map

44×24. To the west it joins the Pilgrim's Road, to the east the East Road. A road runs west to east through the dry grass in the middle of the field.

Areas

  • The kneeling stones: a row on each side of the road, 8 in all, each carved as a kneeling pilgrim. Like the kneeling stones in the East Road's kingdom, they bear the names of those who would not kneel to the knight. Press E to read them, for example: “Aelis of Varrow, pilgrim. She would not kneel. The 212th year of the Crown.” The others are Brother Hal, Ysolde of the Salt Coast, Odile the Smith, Corin (“sent with the blessing of the Holy See”), the sisters Petra and Joan, an unnamed one (“A scallop and a child's shoe”), and the newest: “The carving is fresh. The name is not cut yet.”
  • Kneeling “subjects”: the 6 scarecrows between the stones. Press E to read: “A pilgrim's coat stuffed with straw, bent at the knees and tied there with briar. It faces east, in a row with the others. The briar is still green.”
  • At the field's east end grow the knight's thorns, and a sign reads “THE KINGDOM. KNEEL.”

Lore

  • The first time you enter, a scene plays and completes “Go on east to the Kneeling Field” in The First Seal. Two rows of stones carved as kneeling pilgrims stand across the field, the straw figures tied kneeling between them, all facing east. Lumi jumps: “Oh! That straw one moved — no. It was only the wind.”
  • The years on the stones are counted in the reckoning of the Crown, from the year Alberic put on the Crown of Thorns. The oldest stone is from the 212th year.
