Quality

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Quality is the grade of how well a weapon, tool or piece of gear is made, in six grades (a different thing from gear level: level 1–20 is how strong it is, quality how well made). The higher the quality, the higher its attack and defense, and the more affixes gear rolls. Quality is written before the name (such as “Fine Stone Axe”; Normal quality isn't written), and the color of the name in the tooltip also shows the quality. The first four grades can be crafted; the top two can only be found.

For each grade's multiplier see the quality table at the start of this section. A thing's attack and defense = the form's own stat × the substance's multiplier × the quality's multiplier × the level's multiplier, rounded, at least 1. The number of affixes gear rolls: Crude 2, Normal 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5, Stigmatic 6. So at the same level, gear of higher quality is always better than gear of lower quality.

Quality when crafting

When you make something, its quality is decided by how far the crafter's level is above the level the recipe requires. Most recipes require level 1; the Bow requires level 2.

Level − recipe requirement Quality made
Below 0 Crude
0–2 Normal
3–5 Fine
6 or more Masterwork

For example, at level 1 a bow you make can only be Crude; at level 4 a stone axe you make is Fine, and at level 7 Masterwork.

Vowbound

Things left by those who swore a vow, such as gear left behind by the Order of the Thorn or by pilgrims of old. Under the name in the tooltip it says: “Vowbound — left by one who swore a vow. It still remembers.” Besides a ×1.75 multiplier and 5 minor affixes, every piece of Vowbound gear carries a Vow: a special effect with a condition and a reward (Vigil, Dawn, the Held Ground, the Thorn, Mercy, Silence, the Road, the Shield, the Hunt, the Lone Road: ten in all), written in purple in the tooltip as “✦ Vow name · Vow.” Randomly rolled Vowbound gear carries one at random; the few named pieces in the story each have a set one. See Vowbound, Equipment.

Vowbound gear comes from quest rewards and certain enemies, such as Tomas's Belt of Vigil carried by the barrow warden. See Vowbound.

Stigmatic

Things that bear one of the First Martyr's five wounds. Only the Seal-bearers carry them; each is unique in the world and has a power of its own. The tooltip says: “Stigmatic — it bears one of the First Martyr's five wounds. Suffering brings one nearer to God.” See Stigmata, Legendary Arms.

Tips

  • If you want to make Fine tools and weapons, gain a few levels before making them.
  • A tool's power is multiplied by its quality too, so a Fine axe also fells trees faster. For how well tools work on different materials, see Durability and Breaking.

History

  • 2026-10-04: The Crude, Fine and Masterwork grades were added, with quality decided by level when crafting; the quality multiplier applies to attack and defense.
  • 2026-10-07: The Vowbound and Stigmatic grades were added, found only and never crafted; the tooltip tells where they come from.
  • 2026-10-13: Quality decides the number of affixes on gear (2 to 6); the stat formula includes gear level.
  • 2026-10-13: Vowbound gear's random minor traits were replaced by ten Vows, and it rolls minor affixes like other gear.
