The Old Templar is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Fennick, the old knight who sits on the bench on the square. He will not let anyone who has not survived a night in the wild challenge a Seal-bearer: after dark, defeat three of the wandering dead outside the village, then walk back on your own.

It can only be taken after the Church's letter (see The First Seal).

Objectives

  1. The dead put down
    Defeat zombie×3
  2. Back to Fennick
    Talk to Fennick

Only zombies count, on any map, but they must be defeated after you take the quest. Zombies are night creatures that appear after dark: they only appear in open-air combat zones, never in peaceful zones such as Firstlight Village or The Ford.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: by day (7:00–22:00) Fennick sits on the bench on the south side of the square in Firstlight Village. Press E on him and choose “Accept ‘The Old Templar’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Wait for dark: night begins at 21:00 (brought forward to 20:00 once the White Knight has fallen). The places nearest the village where zombies appear at night are the Grassland to the south and Dawn Hill to the north; Millbrook to the west and the Ford to the east are peaceful zones, where none appear.
  3. Fight zombies: after dark, in open-air combat zones a night creature may appear 6 to 10 tiles from you every 6 seconds, sixty percent of them zombies and forty percent bats, with at most 4 at a time. Defeat three zombies. At daybreak they crumble to dust.
  4. Hand in: go back to Fennick and press E to hand in. After 22:00 he goes off to sleep, and comes out again at 7:00 the next day.

Night on the Grassland wants three of the dead defeated on the Grassland. If you take both quests, every zombie you defeat on the Grassland counts for both. The commission The Night-born, which the guards sometimes give, also counts night creatures, so the same zombie counts for all three.

Rewards

  • Experience +40 (Levels and Experience)
  • Fennick's Squire Helm (Vowbound): Helmet, defence 4, level 3, dodge 0.1, vow “Vow of the Shield”
  • Regard Fennick +15 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

Fennick's Squire Helm is of Vowbound quality; besides defense and evasion +10%, it carries the Vow of the Shield: the first hit you take 10 seconds after leaving combat deals half damage.

Dialogue

When offering
“You mean to go against a Seal-bearer. Then stand a night out there first. The dead walk Dusklea after dark. Put three of them down and walk back here on your own feet.”
While unfinished
“Three of the dead. They come out after dark, past the fence. Keep moving.”
On handing in
“Still standing. Good. Take my old helm. I wore it as a squire, and it stopped worse than claws.”
