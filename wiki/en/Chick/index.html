The chick is the young of the chicken. When two fed hens stand close together, one of them hatches a chick beside itself: “A chick hatches!” It uses the chicken's sprite, only smaller. A chick grows into a chicken after 48 game hours, and only lays eggs once grown. See Animals.

Chicks do not hatch from Eggs: an egg left lying never hatches; it can only be eaten, cooked or sold.

Spawning

  • Breeding: born when two well-fed chicken stand side by side
  • Chicks are never placed directly on a map; they can only be hatched by chickens. Chickens on any map can hatch them: The Held Ground, The Grassland, Emberfall, The Wild, or chickens released from a Crated Hen.

Behavior

  • How it hatches: feed a chicken Animal Feed or Wheat, and once fed it calls to the chickens near it. If a chicken within 3 tiles (counting steps across and down) that is already fed and has not hatched recently hears it, that chicken hatches a chick beside itself (if that tile has no room, on the nearest free tile). Both chickens are then bred and will not hatch again for 24 game hours. As many such chickens as are nearby, that many chicks hatch.
  • Growing up: 48 game hours after it hatches, it becomes a chicken, and from then on lays an egg every 3 game hours. Time still passes while the player is not on that map, and is made up when you return.
  • Before it grows up: it does not lay eggs, does not hear the chickens' call and does not hatch chicks. It can be fed, and is happy when fed, but that does nothing else.
  • Temper: docile. Like a chicken, it never attacks anyone; whoever hits it, it runs away from. It usually wanders near where it hatched. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: fast. About 0.4 seconds per tile, faster than a chicken.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

Skills

No skills of its own: only plain attacks.

Strategy

  • Two hens are enough. The Held Ground starts with no chickens; buy two Crated Hens (40 Dawnmarks each) in the “Animal Husbandry” section of the trading stall, and put them together.
  • Feed one, then the other one next to it. The two feedings must fall within 24 game hours, and the two chickens must be no more than 3 steps apart when you feed the second. The one fed first hatches the chick. Chickens wander, so feed them while they are close together; you can also pen them together with a fence.
  • One portion of feed per animal. One chick takes at least 2 portions of feed (5 portions for 6 Dawnmarks at the stall); wheat works too.
  • More chickens, more eggs. Every grown chicken lays eggs on its own, fed or not; the trading stall buys eggs at 4 Dawnmarks each.

Drops

  • Feather (makeup 1 × 50%)
  • Experience 2 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

A chick is made of 1 Feather and leaves half of it when it falls: a 50% chance to leave 1 Feather, otherwise nothing. It does not leave Raw Chicken. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health, 2 points.

History

  • 2026-10-08: Added to the game: two fed chickens standing together hatch a chick, and the chick grows into a chicken.
  • 2026-10-08: Hatched chicks are as docile as chickens and no longer chase and peck people; growing up now takes two game days, and the rest after being fed or hatching is a full day.
