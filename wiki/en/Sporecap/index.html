The sporecap is a large mushroom that has grown into something that walks. In the story of the Five Sufferings, when the First Night seeped into the world, the first things to change were the beasts and plants: in the thickest of the thorns of Thornwood Hollow, the mushrooms woke too.

Spawning

  • Thornwood Hollow: 2 at the start
  • Thornwood Hollow: 2, one on the mud to the south and one in the grass to the northeast. When it sees someone, it shuffles slowly toward them.

Behavior

  • Temper: aggressive. It comes toward anyone it sees within 3 tiles.
  • Speed: slow. Nearly 1 second per tile; you can outrun it.
  • Fears fire. It is a plant and carries “flammable”: fire can set it alight.

Skills

SkillWhenMarked cellsMovementWind-upCooldown
Spore Cloud1–2 cells awaycircle, 2 cells, centred on itselfstays put1.2 s6 s
  • Spore Cloud: at 1–2 tiles, it lights purple marks within two tiles around itself, then puffs out spores 1.2 seconds later: whoever is in the ring takes attack × 0.8 damage and is poisoned for 3 seconds. Its wind-up is long; when you see the purple ring, back off beyond two tiles. If you are poisoned, you can drink an Antidote.

Strategy

  • It is slow and its wind-up is long: hit it twice and back off, wait for it to finish puffing, then go in again.
  • If you are carrying a Torch, one touch sets it alight.

Drops

  • Herb (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Sporecap Hat (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 38 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves one Herb. It also has a very small chance to drop its own trophy gear, the Sporecap Hat (see Trophies).

History

  • 2026-10-19: Added to the game, using the mushroom monster sprite from the asset pack that had never been used.
