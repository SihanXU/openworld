Emberfall and the Dragon

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Story
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Emberfall and the Dragon is the stretch of the main story that follows Chapter 1 in the game: the protagonist travels east along the East Road to Emberfall, helps the smith and the herbalist ready a dragon-slaying sword and a fireproofing draught, crosses Cinder Pass, and defeats the dragon of Cinder Peak in the Dragon's Lair.

This storyline was written on 2026-10-02, before the current Church of the Five Sufferings setting. The setting has no dragon, and this storyline has nothing to do with the Seal-bearers or the Five Sufferings. It is now only placeholder content, kept in the game so there is still a road to walk after Chapter 1; it will later be replaced by the chapter of the second Seal (see Chapter 2). This page records it as it is in the game.

Place in the main story

  • Until Alberic, the White Knight is defeated, the East Road's exit toward Emberfall is closed. Once he falls, it opens.
  • After you return to Firstlight Village with the Crown of Thorns and Chapter 1 ends, the main quest The Dragon Road begins. Its first log line is “I took the road east, toward Emberfall.”, and Lumi's hint is “The East Road is open. Follow it east to Emberfall.” The “Chapter 2” here is a name the old storyline kept, not the second Seal of the setting.
  • The burned farmstead on the East Road in Chapter 1 (claw marks longer than an arm in the ashes) is a clue left for this storyline.

Emberfall

On entering at the gate, the protagonist sees: “Half its roofs are new thatch; the rest are still black.” Talk to Mara: she tells you about the dragon on Cinder Peak, sends you to find the smith and the herbalist, and gives you two pieces of Bread: “And take this bread with you.”

The smith and the herbalist

  • Garrick wants 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal. The Old Mine is north of the town, and its ore veins must be broken open with a Pickaxe; the richest iron vein is at the very bottom, guarded by a cyclops, which drops 2 Iron Ore when it falls. There is an “Old Pickaxe” at the mine's mouth.
  • Wren wants 3 Fireweed (on the grass of Firstlight Village) and 3 Frostcap (in the Forest).
  • Hold each thing in your hand and press G to hand them over one at a time; once you have given enough, press E to talk to them. The quest tracker in the top right shows how many of each you have handed in.
  • Garrick forges Dragonsbane: “My family has waited twelve generations to forge that blade.” Wren brews two Fireproof Draughts: drink one at the lair's entrance, and the second when the first is about to wear off.

With both in hand, the protagonist gets ready in town and sets out, and the objective “Climb Cinder Pass to the dragon's lair” appears.

Cinder Pass

A snowy uphill road with more and more scorched rock and ash the higher it climbs. The lower stretch has frost wolves; the upper stretch has fire imps and a stone golem. Halfway up there is a lit campfire where you can roast food and rest.

The Dragon's Lair

When you step into the lair's entrance, “Something vast breathes in the dark,” and the last objective appears: “Defeat the dragon of Cinder Peak”.

  • The dragon has 700 health; it breathes fire from 4 cells away and also bites.
  • When it is hit while below half health, it gives a shriek and summons 3 dragon whelps (only once).
  • After you drink a Fireproof Draught, fire deals only a quarter of its damage, and you cannot be set alight.
  • Dragonsbane deals double damage to the dragon and its whelps.
  • Once the dragon is dead, the remaining whelps flee into the dark one by one.

When the dragon falls, an end card plays:

The dragon of Cinder Peak

The dragon is dead. Emberfall can sleep tonight, and the road east is open.

Everything the world learned from you is kept in the Threadbook (J).

The Dragon Road is complete when the dragon falls. There used to be an objective after this called “Final Chapter”, unrelated to the setting's Final Chapter; it is gone now.

People

  • Mara: Emberfall's elder.
  • Garrick: the smith, who forges Dragonsbane.
  • Wren: the herbalist, who brews the Fireproof Draught.

Items

  • Dragonsbane: the sword Garrick forges, of Stigmatic quality, with the power “Dragonslayer”.
  • Fireproof Draught: for a while after drinking it, fire damage is cut to a quarter and you cannot be set alight.
  • Materials to hand in: Iron Ore, Coal, Fireweed, Frostcap.
