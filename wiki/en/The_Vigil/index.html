The Vigil is a Chapter 1 main quest given by Sister Ilse. Firstlight Village keeps a vigil at the sacred flame for those who come home: she needs lamp oil from the cellar at Millbrook to the west, dawnbloom from Dawn Hill to the north, and someone to ring the old bell in Chapel of the First Dawn on top of the hill.

The quest begins by itself once Lost in the Barrow is handed in; with all three gathered, you go back and hand it in to Sister Ilse. After you hand in, the next main quest, The First Seal, begins at once, and the village holds the night of thanks that night.

Objectives

  1. Fetch the lamp oil from the mill cellar at Millbrook (west)
    Carry Flask of Oil×2
  2. Pick dawnbloom on Dawn Hill (north)
    Carry Herb×3
  3. Ring the bell in the Chapel of the First Dawn, on top of Dawn Hill
    after ringing the chapel bell
  4. Back to Sister Ilse
    Talk to Sister Ilse
  • Vigil oil: 2 flasks of oil, taken when you hand in.
  • Dawnbloom: 3 herbs, taken when you hand in. The quest only recognizes things of the kind “herb”: the herbs on Dawn Hill called “dawnbloom” are ready to hand, and herbs from anywhere else count just the same.
  • Ring the bell: walk into the bell tower in the north-east corner of the Chapel of the First Dawn.

The three objectives can be done in any order. The oil and dawnbloom counts look at how many you are carrying right now. With all three gathered, the journal reads “I have the oil and the dawnbloom, and the bell has been rung. Back to Sister Ilse.”, one last objective appears, “Back to Sister Ilse”, and a “?” appears over her head.

Walkthrough

  1. Lamp oil: take the western exit of Firstlight Village (rows 17–19) to Millbrook. The mill is the long building on the north side, and its door leads into The Mill Cellar. In the cellar there are 2 green slimes and 1 cave spider; the two flasks of vigil oil stand on either side by the north wall; press E to pick them up.
  2. Dawnbloom: take the northern exit of Firstlight Village (columns 24–26) to Dawn Hill. At the start there are 4 dawnblooms, all on the east slope: climb the stair, pass the waypoint statue and follow the road east; where it turns north, the four lie in the flower beds two or three cells to the right (east) of that northward stretch, between the corner and the ramp up to the crown. Once they are taken, every 120 seconds an ordinary herb may grow in the flower beds and the grass (3 at most), and these can be handed in too. There are 2 wolves at the foot of the hill.
  3. Ring the bell: follow the path up to the hilltop and go in through the chapel door. There is a bat inside. Walk to the bell tower in the north-east corner and you ring the bell; a scene plays, and the objective is complete.
  4. Hand in: go back to Firstlight Village, where by day (6:00–22:00) Sister Ilse keeps watch by the sacred flame on the square; press E on her to hand in. She takes 2 flasks of oil and 3 herbs.

On the top of Dawn Hill there is a waypoint statue; wake it and going back to the village and coming here again are easy; it also counts as one of the statues for The Measure of Days.

Other ways

  • There are only two flasks of oil in the cellar. Tobin's handcart holds one more flask of oil, but it is his, and if you are seen taking it, it counts as theft.
  • Wilds of the rolling-hills kind in the Wild also have herbs on the ground at the start.

If you defeat the White Knight first

If you defeat The White Knight Alberic before handing this quest in, it is treated as “passed” and ends; the night of thanks and the next day's letter no longer happen. If at that moment you had all three gathered (2 flasks of oil and 3 dawnblooms on you, and the bell rung), Ilse still takes the oil and the dawnbloom and the rewards are paid, and the journal reads “I went east before the vigil, but I had brought everything. Sister Ilse took the oil and the dawnbloom all the same.”; if not, it simply ends, the journal reads “The road has gone past this.”, there is no reward, and you keep what you are carrying.

Rewards

  • Experience +50 (Levels and Experience)
  • Cloak of the Dawn Vigil (Masterwork): Cloak, defence 1, level 3, night defence 1
  • Regard Sister Ilse +15 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

After you hand in, the vigil is ready: from then on, at dusk (17:00–21:00) in Firstlight Village the scene of Ilse telling a story plays, and after dark (21:00–24:00) the night of thanks plays in the village. If it is already dark when you hand in, the night of thanks begins at once and the storytelling scene no longer plays.

Dialogue

While unfinished
“The oil from Tam's cellar, the dawnbloom from the hill, and the bell. They say that bell was rung on the morning the sun came back. It ought to be rung for them, too.”
On handing in
“The lamps are trimmed, and the bell has been rung. Thank you for going so far for us. ...And this time I did not lose a single wick. Tonight after dark Firstlight gives thanks at the flame. Please stay for it.”

The quest starts by itself, so there is no line of request; talk to her before everything is gathered and she says “The oil from Tam's cellar, the dawnbloom from the hill, and the bell. They say that bell was rung on the morning the sun came back. It ought to be rung for them, too.”

The bell (click to show)

As you ring the bell, its sound rolls all the way down the hillside to Firstlight Village. Lumi says it sounds like morning, even though it isn't; she adds that Ilse once told her the First Fire was lit in this fire altar with the light of the sun itself, and now it is so cold, as if it has been waiting for something.
