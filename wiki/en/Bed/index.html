Bed

A bed is for sleeping. Stand on a bed and press E to lie down and sleep until 6 in the morning, and the bed you slept in becomes your resting place (see Sleeping). No map in the game has a ready-made bed; you have to make one: 2 Plank and 2 Cut Grass, no tool and no workbench, and it comes out named “Wooden Bed”. A bed can be carried: strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole bed comes loose and lies on the spot, then press E to pick it up; holding it, right-click a tile to set it up, and wherever you set it you can sleep.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Marta's House ×4, The Elder's House, The Watch House ×3, The Woodcutter's House ×2, General Store, The Smithy, Firstlight Chapel ×2, The House in the Lane, The Little Cabin, Orrin's Hut, Hilde's Cabin, The Mill, Chapel of the Five, The Pilgrims' Hostel ×5, The Chapter Hall, Cloudtop, The Hunter's Cabin, A House on the Square ×2, The Rebuilt House ×2, The Thatched House ×3, The Miners' House ×3, Mara's Teahouse, Garrick's Smithy, Wren's Hut, The Burned Farmhouse, The Ferry Camp Tent, Inside the Rotted Tent, Inside Alberic's Tent
  • Crafting: Plank×2 + Cut Grass×2
  • Crafting: press C and choose “Wooden Bed”: 2 planks and 2 cut grass, made on the spot.
  • Build mode: press B and choose “Wooden Bed”. If you carry a bed it is put down directly; if not, one is first made on the spot from the materials on you and then put down (see things put down).
  • Setting it up: holding a bed, right-click an empty tile. Pressing G only puts the bed on the ground loose, like an item, and you cannot sleep in it: pressing E on it picks it up.
  • Lesson six of the tutorial asks you to put a bed in the house.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Sleeping

  • A bed is only within reach when you stand on it: walk onto the bed and press E; the prompt is “Sleep”.
  • “You lie down.” Time skips ahead to 6:00 in the morning, and you wake with some health restored: fully if the bed is in a bedroom, half elsewhere.
  • While asleep your Satiety drops more slowly; being woken by hunger or by a blow wakes you at once.
  • For the detailed rules see Sleeping.

Resting place

The bed you slept in becomes your resting place. On Normal difficulty, when you fall, you get up again at the bed you last slept in or the waypoint statue you last woke.

Bedroom

A room closed all round and covered overhead with a bed in it is a bedroom. The bedroom is for now the only room use with a real effect: sleep a night in it and your health is fully restored.

Taking up and putting down

  • Taking up: strike it once with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer and the whole bed comes loose and lies on the spot; press E to pick it up (close by, it is drawn into your bag by itself), with no loss of material.
  • A bed does not block the way, and people can stand on it; nor does it take up a tile's space slot, so it can share a tile with a floor: putting the bed down first and laying the floor afterwards works too, the floor being laid under the bed (see Storeys).
  • Press G on a bed with something in hand to put the thing on the bed's tile.
  • Residents do not use beds to sleep; when the time comes they go back to where they sleep.

Cannot be broken, but burns

A bed has no durability. Axes, pickaxes and hammers only knock the whole bed loose; what happens if you hit it with a weapon is not written in the rules (see The World Learns). It is made of wood and cloth, can catch fire, and burns down to a portion of Ash. Do not put your bed next to a campfire.

History

  • 2026-09-30: The bed has been in the game since the first version.
  • 2026-10-04: Beds can be slept in: you sleep until morning, health is restored by the room, fully in a bedroom; the bed you slept in becomes your resting place (W5). Beds can be made on the spot from planks and cut grass, and carried.
  • 2026-10-05: Build mode can place beds directly; sleeping gives the status “asleep”, with hunger 4 times slower.
  • 2026-10-07: A bed put down is drawn as the whole bed.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were set as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
