The East Road runs east from The Kneeling Field to Emberfall. Coming from The Ford, you first head east through The Pilgrims' Rest, The Pilgrim's Road and the Kneeling Field. North of the road stands a burned farmstead, where the farmer Edda hides in the barn; the eastern stretch of the road is a field ringed with thorns, the “kingdom” of the First Seal-bearer, Alberic, the White Knight. He stands at its center, and none cross that do not kneel. Defeating him is the last objective of the quest The First Seal.

The exit to Emberfall opens only once the White Knight falls. The East Road is an open-air combat zone; after dark, night creatures appear.

Map

The map is 48×24, ringed with woods. A road two tiles wide enters from the west (rows 11–13, from the Kneeling Field), winds east and leaves by the east edge (rows 10–12). The burned farmstead lies north of the road's middle stretch; the road's eastern stretch is ringed by a circle of briars, and the road runs straight through the ring.

Areas

  • Signpost: a sign at the western junction reads “Emberfall →”.
  • The clearing south of the road: a patch of trodden earth south of the road. The Boss Challenge and the development boss sparring ground set up their arenas here.
  • The burned farmstead: north of the road's middle stretch. The house still stands, but the yard, the hay and the orchard have burned to ash; a few trees are charred stumps, and one haystack is still smoldering (lit: the fire does not spread and never burns itself out; a splash of water puts it out). Walking into the farmstead plays a scene. You can go inside the farmhouse: the room is thick with soot, and two plates sit on the table, one of them never used.
  • The barn: in the southwest corner of the farmstead, where Edda hides. Once you have seen the farmstead's scene, walk into the barn and she comes out to talk.
  • The road before the thorns: the stretch of road west of the kingdom.
  • Waypoint statue: by the road before the thorns stands a waypoint statue (named “The East Road”). You can wake it here before fighting the White Knight and change Lumi's powers beside it.
  • The Thorn Kingdom: the eastern stretch of the road, where a ring of “the knight's thorns” (briars) encloses a field, with gaps left only where the road passes. Walking in plays a scene; the White Knight Alberic stands inside the ring.
    • Kneeling stones: inside and outside the ring stand 5 stones carved as kneeling pilgrims (gravestones). Press E to read: “A stone carved as a pilgrim kneeling. No name. A scallop shell, the pilgrim's mark, worn almost away.” Like the kneeling stones of the Kneeling Field, they stand for the pilgrims before you who would not kneel to him. Beside one of them lies A Kneeling Pilgrim's Earring (Fine, experience +10%).
    • What the knight took: scattered in the ring are the Ninth Nail and a gold Wedding Band, the things sought by The Ninth Nail and Edda's Ring; there is also a pair of Gauntlets of the White Host (Masterwork, defense 2, attack 1).

Residents

  • Eddalives here
  • Edda hides in the barn all day. After the White Knight falls, she moves to Firstlight Village.
  • The White Knight Alberic stands at the center of the Thorn Kingdom; he is a boss, not a resident. See his page.
  • The Grey Man appears in the story on the road before the thorns and in the kingdom, but does not live here.

Lore

Scenes on the East Road (click to show)
  • On the road: the first time you set foot on the East Road after crossing the river, Lumi says the sun is lower than it was this morning, though it is barely past noon; and the thorns along the road were planted in a line by someone.
  • The burned farmstead: everything here has burned, and in the ash are claw marks longer than an arm. They are what the dragon left in the old main story (see Emberfall and the Dragon).
  • The barn: Edda says the days grew too short and the wheat died; then the things of the night came; and later a knight in white took everything that shone, her husband's ring hanging on the doorpost among it. The night the barn caught fire, it was a man in grey who pulled her out of the smoke, and she dares not tell the village.
  • The road before the thorns: once the Church's letter has come, walk onto this stretch and The Grey Man stands in your way and tells you to turn back. He says the knight beyond the thorns has held this road for three hundred years and is a good man; the Church wants you to take his crown, and he will not stop you, only when the crown comes off “something will come out of it. That, I will take with me.” He looks at Lumi for a long time, says nothing, and then leaves. You never fight him.
  • The kingdom: walk into the ring of thorns and the knight says: “None cross that do not kneel. Another pilgrim. They always send another. I hold. I... hold. I no longer remember what.”
  • The Crown of Thorns: when the knight falls, he kneels, lifts off the Crown of Thorns and holds it out to you: “Take it. It was never mine to hold.” A cold light streams from the crown to Lumi, and she gains her first Boon, Crown's Insight. The Grey Man appears and gathers the rest of the light into the holy fire he stole: “Every seal you break makes the night longer. Ask your Church why.” From then on night falls earlier (see Day and Night). Then, where the knight fell, you find a thorn still green, the Oath-Thorn, which leads to The Held Ground.
