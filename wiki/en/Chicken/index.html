The chicken is a docile animal that never attacks anyone and runs away when hit. Every so often it lays an Egg at its feet. There are chickens on The Grassland, at The Held Ground, in Emberfall, and on the open plains of The Wild.

Spawning

  • Dusklea: 4 at the start, respawns (up to 5, once every 90 s)
  • Emberfall: 2 at the start
  • Grows up from chick (48 in-game hours)
  • Put Crated Hen down: the animal inside walks out
  • The Grassland: 4 at the start. When fewer than 5 remain, another appears on the grass every 90 game minutes.
  • The Held Ground: none at the start; buy Crated Hens at the trading stall, and after that two chickens can also hatch chicks. They never appear by themselves.
  • Chicks grow up: a chick grows into a chicken after 48 game hours.
  • Emberfall: 2. Once felled, they do not come back.
  • The wild: 1 on each open-plains map.

Behavior

  • Temper: docile. It never attacks. Whoever hits it, it runs away from. See Factions and Temper.

  • Lays eggs. It lays an egg every 3 game hours, left where it stands; pick it up with E.

  • Speed: medium. About 0.5 seconds per tile.

  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

  • Chickens in the wild are different. The chickens on the maps of the wild use another set of behavior: when hit, they do not flee but chase and peck you. Their attack is 0, though, so the pecks do no damage.

  • Feeding: holding Animal Feed or Wheat, right-click it (or press G), and it is fed. It lays eggs whether fed or not.

  • Breeding: when two fed chickens that have not just bred stand close together (no more than 3 steps across and down added up), the one fed second calls, and the other hatches a chick: “A chick hatches!”

  • There is no henhouse. See Animals.

Skills

No skills of its own: only plain attacks.

Strategy

  • If you want eggs, do not fell your chickens: stay nearby and wait for them to lay. Marta's Something for the Pot asks for 2 eggs.
  • Eggs can be eaten raw, or made into a cooked egg on a cooking spot such as a lit campfire. See Cooking.

Drops

  • Raw Chicken (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Experience 4 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

A chicken is made of 2 portions of Raw Chicken and leaves half of them when it falls, that is 1 piece. Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.

Lore

The chicken has no place in the story. Its sprite was drawn by this project itself and is not from the asset pack.

History

  • 2026-10-02: Added with survival play, placed on the meadow west of the Ford (that map later became Firstlight Village), coming back over time.
  • 2026-10-03: Firstlight Village was built, and the chickens moved to the grassland south of the village; 2 were placed in Emberfall.
  • 2026-10-04: Chickens appear on the open plains of the wild.
  • 2026-10-07: 2 placed at the Held Ground.
  • 2026-10-08: Can be fed and bred; hatched chicks grow into chickens. The trading stall at the Held Ground sells Crated Hens and also buys eggs.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled on four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
