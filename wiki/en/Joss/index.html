“Joss. I build things: bridges, fences, benches. The footbridge at the Ford was mine and Aldo's.”

— Joss

Joss is the bridge-builder of Firstlight Village, 58 years old. Twenty years ago he and Aldo, Hesper's husband, built the little bridge at The Ford. Now someone has taken that bridge apart, neatly. By day he planes boards at the village workbench, and he teaches the player how to mend the bridge with planks.

The way he tells you to mend the bridge is exactly how it works in the game: “Axe the tree, axe the logs into planks. Three planks and the little bridge is whole.” See Woodcutting and planks. He has also seen that no storm broke the bridge: “The nails were pulled out straight and laid in a row. What storm pulls nails?”

Lore

In Chapter 1, Joss is the source of the clue that “the bridge was taken apart, not washed away”. See Chapter 1.

What he does not say (click to show)

Twenty years ago, the one who paid for that bridge was a man in grey, a boy at his heel carrying the purse, with old coins that bore no king's head (the coin of the Ashen Watch), who said he wanted “a road a cart could leave here by, quickly”. Joss never understood what he meant. After Chapter 1 ends, he tells this story, once.
