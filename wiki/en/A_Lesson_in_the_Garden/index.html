A Lesson in the Garden is one of Lumi's guide quests, teaching farming. The first time you walk into the vegetable garden in the south-east corner of Firstlight Village, she speaks up.

Walkthrough

  1. Start: in a proper game, walk onto the garden plot, Lumi speaks, and the quest begins by itself.
  2. Tools: in the east corner of the garden there are “Garden Supplies” (press E to open them), holding a Hoe, a Sickle, a bucket of water and two packets of seeds for each crop. Take the hoe, the sickle and the seeds, and three objectives are complete; take the water bucket while you are at it. When you track the quest, the marker points at this crate.
  3. Harvest: the garden's three rows of crops are sprouts, seedlings and ripe plants; hold the sickle and left-click a ripe one to get vegetables and seeds. Once there are vegetables in your bag, the objective is complete.
  4. Sow: the bottom row is empty farmland. Hold a seed and right-click an empty tile of farmland, and the seed becomes a sprout. Once a sprout stands on that row, the objective is complete.
  5. Water: hold the full bucket and left-click a growing crop; it becomes watered, and grows one stage every one to four in-game hours, as its seed's description says. The well by the square fills an empty bucket.
  6. Complete: once all three are done, the quest completes, and Lumi gives you two packets of Turnip Seeds.

The hoe can turn grass into farmland, so you can start a garden of your own; the harvest can be eaten, or sold at the trading stall in The Held Ground. For what can be grown, see Crops.

Dialogue

Stage 10
“This is the village garden! The crate at its corner has a hoe, a sickle, a bucket of water and seeds. Open it (E) and take the hoe, the sickle and some seeds. We can borrow them.”
Stage 20
“Good! See the rows? Sprouts, young plants, and the top row is ripe. Cut one with the sickle; you get the crop and its seeds back. Then sow a seed on the bare bottom row and water it with the bucket. A crop grows a stage every one to four hours, as its seed says, and needs water at each stage. Once you know Irrigation, one watering lasts two stages.”
On handing in
“You did it! Crops are food, and you can sell them too. With the hoe you can turn any grass into a garden of your own. Here, a few more seeds.”
