Pell's Drawings is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Pell, a boy who loves to draw. No one believes he has seen a grey man, so he made a chalk drawing at every place he saw him. Find all four drawings and press E to look at each.

The quest has no prerequisites. All four places lie on roads the main line takes you along, and most players come across them on the way.

Look at a drawing with E, standing within one tile of it. Pell says “I draw small, you have to look at the ground”: the drawings are on the ground and do not block the way.

Drawings you looked at before taking the quest count too. If you have seen all four before you take it, talk to him once more after accepting to hand it in.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: in the daytime (7:00–20:00) Pell is drawing on the north side of the square in Firstlight Village, near the notice board. Press E on him and choose “Accept ‘Pell's Drawings’” in the dialogue box.
  2. The four drawings:
    • The shrine stone: in the ring of flowers in the middle of Mountain Foot, beside the five-wound shrine stone. It is called “chalk drawing of a stone”.
    • The barrow door: at the northern entrance of the Old Barrow, near the top of the steps down from Thornwood Hollow. It is called “chalk drawing of a door”.
    • The little bridge: at the west end of the broken bridge at The Ford, on the ground beside the bridge. It is called “chalk drawing of a bridge”.
    • By the sacred flame: beside the campfire on the square of Firstlight Village. It is called “chalk drawing of a grey man”.
  3. Hand in: go back to Pell and press E to hand in.

Rewards

  • Experience +30 (Levels and Experience)
  • Warm Ember
  • Chalk-dusted Cloak (Fine): Cloak, defence 1, level 2
  • Regard Pell +15 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

The Warm Ember is what Pell calls “the other warm stone”.

Dialogue

When offering
“Nobody believes me about the grey man. So I drew him everywhere I saw him, four places! Go and find them all and you'll see. Then you'll believe me.”
While unfinished
“The shrine stone up the mountain, the barrow door, the little bridge, and here by the fire. I draw small, so look down low.”
On handing in
“You saw them? He was there every time, the night before something bad happened. He wasn't the one doing the bad thing. He just got there first. Here, you can have my other warm stone.”

What the four drawings show:

  • The shrine stone: “The shrine stone, with five marks on it, the first one coloured in black. A grey person has a hand pressed against it.”
  • The barrow door: “A closed door with a handprint on it. Behind the door, something wearing a crown of bone. In front of the door, two little people.”
  • The little bridge: “A bridge broken in the middle. A tall white figure stands beside it, and a smaller grey figure watches from the trees.”
  • By the sacred flame: “A hooded person carrying a lantern, standing by the fire. Underneath, in a child's writing: he came first.”
What Pell is trying to say (click to show)

When you hand in, Pell says: the grey man was there every time, always the night before something bad happened; the bad things were not his doing, he just got there first. Pell also says that the grey man once gave him a stone that grows warm at night, and since he has had it, the things of the night stay away from his window. As Chapter 1 is designed, that is an ember from the fire of the dawn; what he gives you at hand-in is “the other one”.
