Karma and Faith

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

You carry two numbers, both shown in the backpack's stats panel (below your character portrait): Karma and Faith. Both start at 0, and both lie between −100 and 100. Beside them is a third, Luck; see below.

Karma

The stats panel shows only its size, not its sign: Karma of 5 reads “Good +5,” dropping to −5 reads “Evil +5,” and 0 is “Neutral.” Good is green, evil dark red.

How it falls (doing wrong): every evil deed you commit lowers your Karma, whether or not anyone sees: whether people see only decides whether you are held in grudge or wanted; you yourself know what you did. It falls by a quarter of the deed's weight in people's eyes:

Evil deed Weight Karma
Murder 100 −25
Assault 35 −9
Arson 30 −8
Pickpocketing 25 −6
Theft 15 −4
Vandalism 10 −3
Lockpicking 8 −2

Hitting someone who struck you first is not assault (self-defense). Taking things that have no owner and fighting monsters are not evil deeds.

How it rises (doing good):

  • Completing a quest that helps someone (commissions, errands, anything after which they think better of you): +3.
  • Giving back what you took (“Give back what I took.” in conversation): +2.
  • Having an apology accepted: +1.

What it does: it decides how fast regard grows (see Friendship). Everyone also has their own leaning, decided by how honest they are: most villagers are good, light-fingered people like Vigo lean evil, and a boastful peddler like Tobin is in the middle.

  • When you are good, good people's regard for you grows faster (up to 60% faster), and wicked people's grows more slowly.
  • When you are evil it is the other way round: wicked people warm to you more easily, and good people less so.
  • It only affects how fast regard rises; when you do something that angers people, they lose just as much as before.

Faith

Faith is your relationship with the Church of the Five Sufferings. A positive number shows as “+12” (gold), a negative one as “−12” (greyish purple).

How it rises:

  • Prayer: pressing E on an altar is praying there, Faith +4; pressing E on a wayside shrine on the Pilgrim's Road, you first read the inscription on it and then pray, Faith +2. You can pray at each place only once a day; pressing again that day says “You have already prayed here today.”
  • Lighting a shrine: put down at a wayside shrine the thing its suffering asks for (see The Pilgrim's Road), Faith +5.

How it falls: talk to anyone and choose “Spread unbelief: There are no gods watching, you know.”, Faith −5. You can say it to each person only once a day. Devout people are angered by it, regard −8: Brother Anselm, Sister Ilse, Father Caius, Church Courier, Brother Ezra, Maud. Anyone else is only taken aback: “…No gods? Then who is keeping the sun up?” You can't say this to children under 18 (Mia, Pell, Wynn): the choice doesn't appear.

Where the story goes (Chapter 1, the Pilgrim's Road part):

  • Faith 30 or more (devout): when you return to the Pilgrims' Rest after taking the vow, Father Caius blesses you, gives you two flasks of Holy Water, and asks you to tell him what the Writ of Relief says once you find it.
  • Faith −30 or less (unbeliever): Father Caius has heard what you say and refuses you the Rest's blessing; when you set out on the Pilgrim's Road, the Grey Man appears and tells you what those shrines really are.
  • At the Kneeling Field, what Lumi says also differs with your Faith.
  • These things are all recorded in the story (the blessing, the refusal, the Grey Man's visit), and later chapters read them.

Luck

Luck comes after “Experience” in the stats panel and is a percentage. It raises the chance of monster drops:

  • The drop rate of a monster's trophy is multiplied by (1 + Luck): a 4% drop rate with Luck +10% becomes 4.4%. The pity count doesn't change.
  • The materials left to luck when a monster's body is broken down (the parts that don't come every time) have their chances multiplied by (1 + Luck) too.

Luck comes from gear: a random affix can roll Luck (+3% to 10%), and some monster trophies and sets carry Luck too (see Equipment).

History

  • 2026-10-12: Karma, Faith and Luck were added. Fortitude, Stealth and Perception are no longer shown in the stats panel, and those three stats on gear were all replaced by Defense, Dodge or Luck (gear in old saves is converted automatically).
