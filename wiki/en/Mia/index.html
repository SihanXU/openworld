Mia is Marta's daughter, 9 years old. She followed the foragers all the way to the briars of Thornwood Hollow, and lost her grandmother's silver ring there. By day she skips stones at the village pond.

Quests

  • Mia's Ring (Side, giver)
  • Mia's Ring: the ring is silver, and lies where the briars are thickest on the east side of Thornwood Hollow. She begs the player not to tell her mother. Once you bring it back, she gives the player her “best stone” (an ordinary Stone) and “Mia's Ribbon Earring”.

Lore

Mia's story belongs to the village alone, and never meets the main story.

Trivia

  • She gives the player an ordinary stone as a lucky charm: “It brings good luck. Probably.”
  • Her role in the game is “villager”; Pell, also 9, is a “child”.
