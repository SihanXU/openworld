Smithing

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Smithing is the craft of turning ore into gear, in two steps: first smelt ore and coal into ingots beside a forge, then stand beside an anvil with a Smith Hammer and hammer the ingots into things. What you make is of the ingot's metal: an iron ingot makes an iron sword, a Steel Bar a steel sword; every thing has one anvil recipe for iron and one for steel (jewelry is iron and gold). The smithies of Firstlight Village, The Held Ground and Emberfall have a forge and anvil ready to use; once you learn Forge Builder you can build your own at home. Ore comes from Dawn Hill Mine north of Firstlight Village: reachable in Chapter 1, its iron veins and coal seams grow back one game day after being mined out; see Mining.

Smithies

There are three places where you can smelt and forge; the forges are always lit, and anyone may use the anvils:

Place Smith What they sell
The smithy by the East Road in Firstlight Village Haldor Smith Hammer, Coal, Iron Ore, Iron Ingot
East of the sword on The Held Ground Alberic (the White Knight, at the anvil in the mornings) Smith Hammer, Coal, Iron Ingot, Steel Bar
The smithy in Emberfall Garrick Pickaxe, Hoe, Dagger, Sword, Helmet, Iron Ingot

A smith has an anvil-and-hammer mark over their head. Haldor is at the anvil 6:00–21:00, Alberic 6:00–14:00; when the smith is away, the forge and anvil can be used all the same. Once you learn Forge Builder you can also build a forge and anvil of your own at home.

Smelting (forge)

Recipe Materials Requires
2 Iron Ingot 2 Iron Ore, 1 Coal Nothing
2 Gold Ingot 2 Gold Ore, 1 Coal Goldsmith
2 Steel Bar 3 Iron Ore, 3 Coal Steelworking, level 3

Efficient Smelting doubles the ingots each smelting yields. You can also do without a recipe: hold one iron ore or gold ore and right-click a lit forge, one ore for one ingot, with no coal used (see Smelting).

Forging (anvil + smith hammer)

  • Anyone can forge: daggers, spears, swords, maces, helmets, shields.
  • Skills required: warhammers and halberds (Heavy Arms), pauldrons and breastplates (Plate Work), metal axes, pickaxes, hoes, sickles and shovels (Tool Smith), rings and amulets (Jeweler).
  • Level: the gear you forge has your level (no lower than the recipe's level, at most 20); see Gear level: a level 10 smith forges a level 10 sword.
  • Quality is set by your level plus your smithing skills (Apprentice Smith, Fine Craft, Grandmaster Smith) minus the recipe's difficulty: 3 above is Fine, 6 above Masterwork. Masterwork has a 15% chance of one grade higher, and Legendary Smith a 4% chance of coming out Vowbound outright.
  • Weaponsmith and Master Weaponsmith give forged weapons more power, and Keen Edge adds critical chance; Armorsmith, Reinforced and Master Armorsmith give armor more defense.

Gear sets

Main article: Gear Sets

Every profession has a set of forged gear covering the whole body: head, earring, necklace, shoulders, body, hands, ring, belt, feet, main hand and off hand, all forged at an anvil with a Smith Hammer in hand. The first material decides the material, smithing level and skills decide the quality, and the names follow the profession's gear (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave). Jewelry (earrings, necklaces, rings, circlets) needs Jeweler first. Head, shoulders, body, hands, belt and feet make up the armor set (bonuses at 2, 4 and 6 pieces worn); necklace, earring and ring make up the jewelry set (2 and 3 pieces); the main hand and off hand belong to the profession alone and are not part of a set. Each piece's materials and bonuses are in Gear Sets.

The newly added forms of piece: Jerkin (body), Robe (body), Parrying Dagger (Assassin's off hand), Quiver (Ranger's off hand), Tome (Mage's off hand). They can only be forged at an anvil as a piece of a set.

Everything forged rolls affixes: how many is set by its quality (Crude 2, Common 3, Fine 4, Masterwork 5, Vowbound 5), and the range of their values grows with the maker's level.

Selling

The stall buys iron ore, gold ore and coal; Ore Merchant sells them for more.

History

  • 2026-10-09: Smelting and smithing added.
  • 2026-10-12: After DNF, sets were split into armor sets (6 pieces, bonuses at 2/4/6) and jewelry sets (3 pieces, bonuses at 2/3), and the main hand and off hand no longer count toward a set; two pieces for the same slot count as one.
  • 2026-10-11: Four profession sets added (Shadowfang, Oathguard, Windrunner, Starweave), 12 pieces each covering the whole body, with set bonuses at 3/6/9/12 pieces; Cass's loom can weave cloth from cut grass.
  • 2026-10-11: Firstlight Village and the Held Ground each got a smithy, with Haldor and Alberic as the smiths.
  • 2026-10-10: Ingots split by metal (iron ingot, gold ingot, steel bar), one anvil recipe per metal; each smelting yields two.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled into four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swings, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
