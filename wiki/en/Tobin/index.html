Tobin is a peddler who works the road to the east, 34 years old. Twice a year he brings charms, lamp oil and sundries from the east, talking all the way. The storm stopped the ferry, so he moved his handcart to Firstlight Village. Nobody here buys what is on the cart, and he has nothing to eat himself.

On the cart are a Frost Wand and a flask of lamp oil, both his. Give him any food (G) and he gives the player the Frost Wand; from then on, taking the wand is not theft. It is one of the game's rare barters; see Money.

Appearance

Tousled copper-red hair under a soft mustard-yellow cap, a pencil behind his ear; green eyes with laugh lines, a dusting of freckles over his nose, a little stubble. A deep teal travelling coat to the knee with many pockets and patched elbows, worn open over a cream shirt and a brown waistcoat, a cream scarf with rust-red stripes knotted loosely at his neck. His belt carries a coin purse, a small ledger and strings of charms; the wooden pack frame on his back is hung with charms and bottles of oil, the blue Frost Wand tucked in.

He has a portrait of his own: when you talk to him, the dialogue box shows it. It has six expressions: neutral (an easy grin), happy (a laugh, eyes creased shut), sad (hangdog), angry (mock indignation), surprised (startled) and sheepish (a guilty, clenched-teeth grin). He blinks soon after each line begins, and his mouth opens and closes as he speaks (laughing, his eyes are already shut and his mouth open, so it neither blinks nor changes). His sprite on the map follows: red hair, green eyes, the teal coat. His design book: Art.

He sleeps by his cart from 22:00 to 06:00; from 06:00 to 09:00 and from 11:00 to 19:00 he watches the cart (not while the cart is on fire); from 09:00 to 11:00 he draws water at the well on the square; from 19:00 to 22:00 he warms his hands at the fire on the square. That fire is the village's holy flame; when it is out (after the flame is stolen, for example), he cannot warm his hands then. On stormy days he spends all day sheltering under the market awning.

Relationships

He owes Hesper at the Ford the fare for a crossing. He says the river owes them both; Hesper does not laugh.

Bram in the drill yard complains that he talks nonstop on the sidelines at every drill.

He sold Nessa Quill an old almanac from before the First Long Night, and last winter sold a frost charm to a man in grey.

He is happiest when given food: “Two days. Two whole days.” When he sees someone stealing, he says he is a peddler, and one always sees these things.

Quests

No quests involve this person.

Tobin gives no quests. Trading food for the Frost Wand is one of the ways to cross at the Ford: the wand's cold freezes the water's surface into thin ice, and you walk across on the ice. See The Ford.

Trade and fencing

Tobin is a merchant of Firstlight Village (see Trading): he sells Flask of Oil, Hood, Lockpick and Firecracker, restocks every morning, and carries 40 Dawnmark to buy your goods.

He is also the village's only fence: stolen goods other merchants will not take, he does, only at half the price again. The hood and lockpick he sells are just what that line of work needs (see Crime and Sneaking and Pickpocketing).

He minds his own business: when he sees others stealing, he does not shout or go to the watch. Steal from him, though, and he will still ask for it back.

Lore

Chapter 1 gave him two touches: the old almanac sold to Nessa, and the frost charm sold to the Grey Man. Only after the flame is stolen does he work out that the Grey Man crossed the river with that charm. See The Grey Man.

What he does not say (click to show)

He hopes Hesper has forgotten that fare; she has not. He is afraid of the slimes that crawl out of their burrows at night.

The old coins the Grey Man paid him bear no king; they are the coins of the Ashen Watch.

Trivia

  • Before 2026-10-03 he camped at the Ford and slept by his cart; his line “better than sleeping by the hole at the Ford with one eye open” is about those days.
  • In thunder he shouts not to stand under the trees, then mentions his uncle, and will not say more.
