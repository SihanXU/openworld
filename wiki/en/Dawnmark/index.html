Dawnmark

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item
Dawnmark
CategoryMiscellany
Chinese name晨辉币
IDitem/misc/thornmark

The Dawnmark is the world's common coin: a gold coin struck with half a rising sun, in memory of the first dawn after the First Night, the moment Astra bore the Five Sufferings and the sun rose again (see The Five Sufferings). The trading stall on the Held Ground sells seeds, feed, bait and livestock in crates, and buys what you grow, raise and catch; the general store in Firstlight Village and Brother Anselm's skill bookshelf take it; everyone trades in it (see Trading). The header line of the pack in the inventory panel shows how many you carry. See Money.

Source

  • Quest reward: What Was Taken, A Letter to Carry, The Night-born
  • Sell things to the Trading stall: Wheat, Carrot, Turnip, Tomato, Pumpkin, Parsnip, Potato, Radish, Cauliflower, Melon, Corn, Blueberries, Strawberry, Hot Pepper, Eggplant, Berries, Herb, Egg, Bucket of Milk, Raw Fish, Sunfish, Perch, Catfish, Eel, Pike, Golden Carp, Dawnscale, Soggy Boot, Iron Ore, Gold Ore, Coal, Leather Hide, Feather, Honey Jar, Cheese, Slime Gel, Venom Sac, Ectoplasm (see Money)
  • Alberic's welcome gift: the first time you enter the Held Ground, Alberic gives you 30 when he has finished speaking: “Take this, the coin of this ground. My stall keeps seeds, feed and bait, and buys back what you grow, raise and catch. There are fish in the pond.” He gives it only once.
  • Expanding the Held Ground: the three expansions pay 20, 40 and 60.
  • Selling things: stand by the stall and press E, and sell things one by one on the “Sell” shelf. What the stall buys and for how much: see trading stall.

Uses

  • Buy at the Trading stall: Wheat Seeds, Carrot Seeds, Turnip Seeds, Tomato Seeds, Pumpkin Seeds, Parsnip Seeds, Potato Seeds, Radish Seeds, Cauliflower Seeds, Melon Seeds, Corn Seeds, Blueberries Seeds, Strawberry Seeds, Hot Pepper Seeds, Eggplant Seeds, Berry Seeds, Herb Seeds, Fertilizer, Acorn, Shovel, Hoe, Sickle, Bucket, Animal Feed, Bucket, Crated Hen, Crated Calf, Bait, Fishing Rod (see Money)
  • Buying things: stand by the stall and press E to open it, then click what you want on the “Farming”, “Ranching” or “Fishing” shelf; the money is taken from your inventory, and what you buy goes straight into it. The stall panel shows how many Dawnmarks you carry. Step away from the stall (beyond the ring of 8 tiles around it) and you cannot buy; the panel says “Stand next to the stall to trade.”
  • Dawnmarks are ordinary items, kept one by one in the inventory: you can drop them, pick them up, give them to someone with G, or put them in a chest.
  • They are made of metal and do not catch fire. The rules do not say what else they do; the world may learn a new answer (see the world learns).

History

  • 2026-10-12: Renamed the Dawnmark (formerly the Thornmark); its icon became a gold coin struck with a rising sun.

  • 2026-10-08: Added to the game, together with the Held Ground's stall.
