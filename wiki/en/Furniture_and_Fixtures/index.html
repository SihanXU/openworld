Furniture and Fixtures

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Furniture and fixtures are the things that stand in the world: beds, workbenches, chests, campfires, lamps, wells, signs, altars… Most of them cannot be picked up; only beds and workbenches can be taken away and set down again: left-click one with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; press E to pick it up. Build mode (B) can also put up campfires, chests, fences and signs.

Furniture you can take away

Furniture Materials Use
bed 2 planks, 2 cut grass Stand on the bed and press E to sleep till morning; the bed becomes your resting place. A room with a bed in it is a bedroom, where you sleep better. See Sleeping.
workbench 4 planks Standing beside it, the crafting panel offers the things made at a workbench: Wooden Club, Wooden Shield, Wooden Bow.

Both are made by hand in the crafting panel (C), and can also be made on the spot and set down in build mode. There is a ready-made workbench by the wood pile in Firstlight Village.

Built in build mode

Fixture Materials Use
campfire 2 stones, 2 sticks Light it with a torch: warmth, cooking, light at night
crate 2 planks, finished with a hammer Small storage, 6 slots
Wooden Chest 4 planks, finished with a hammer Large storage, 12 slots
fence 2 sticks, finished with a hammer Blocks passage; several can be joined into a ring to fence a garden
Sign 1 stick, 1 plank, finished with a hammer A blank sign; carrying ink, you can write on it with a quill (the game has no source of ink or quills yet)

For those that need a hammer, put down the materials first, and finish by left-clicking once with a Smith Hammer in hand. One not yet hammered is “unfinished work”, which a tool can take apart. Left-click a crate or a Wooden Chest with an axe, a pickaxe or a hammer, and it comes loose whole and drops (what is inside spills out first); pick it up and it can be built somewhere else.

Containers

Press E to open one, and things can be put in and taken out (see Chests and Containers). How many slots each container holds:

Container Slots
chest 12
wardrobe 12
wood pile 12
shelf 8
bookshelf 8
crate 6
barrel 4
sack 4
clay pot 2

Barrels, crates and clay pots are flimsy and break in one blow. A room holding three or more containers counts as a storeroom.

Note: the storage barrel and the bucket for carrying water share one Chinese name in the game. The one for water is a tool.

Light sources

  • Campfire: gives light only once lit. Left-click it holding a Torch and it catches; with the skill Firemaker an ordinary stick lights it. A lit campfire can cook; rain does not put it out.
  • Braziers, lamp posts, candles, lantern posts, fireplaces: the fixed lights of the towns and the underground, most of them lit from the start. A lit brazier or fireplace can also cook; lamp posts, candles and lantern posts cannot.
  • Lights held in the hand: the Torch (1 stick, 1 cut grass, made by hand), the Candle, the Lantern. Held in the hand, they make you glow, as a visual effect only.

Within 2 cells of anything lit or burning, wild things that fear fire do not come near, and do not chase you in. So beside a lit campfire is a safe place at night. See Day and Night and Night Creatures.

A lit campfire, brazier, lamp post, candles, lantern post, fireplace, forge or cauldron can be warmed at by pressing E empty-handed: if you are wet, you dry at once. See warming up.

Other fixtures

  • Living: tables, chairs, benches, log benches, cooking racks, handcarts, wells (for drawing water), tents.
  • Workstations: the anvil, the forge, the cauldron (forge and cauldron can cook when lit; a lit forge can smelt ore into ingots, see smelting).
  • Readable: signs (readable even from 2 cells away), notice boards, gravestones, altars. See Documents.
  • Holy sites: five-wound shrine stones, waypoint statues.
  • Training: the training dummy, which wobbles back up by itself once knocked down; the tutorial uses it for attack practice.

Rooms

The fixtures in a room decide what room it is: a bed makes a bedroom, a cooking fire a kitchen, a workbench or forge a workshop, three or more containers a storeroom. See Rooms.

History

  • 2026-10-09: Lit lamps and cooking fires can be warmed at; the forge can smelt ore into ingots.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were fixed as four inputs: using a thing held on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
