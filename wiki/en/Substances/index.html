Substances

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Substance is what a thing is made of: wood, stone, iron, steel, silver, leather, cloth… The same form (a “sword”, say) can be made of different substances; the substance is written into the name (“Iron Sword”, “Silver Sword”) and also decides how strong and how hard it is.

“Materials” are a category of items (logs, stones…); “substance” is what a thing is made of. A Plank is a material; its substance is wood.

What the substance decides

  • Power and defense: each substance has an attack multiplier and a defense multiplier. A weapon's power = the form's power × the substance's attack multiplier × the quality's multiplier × the level's multiplier; an armour piece's defense likewise.
  • Hardness: a tool or weapon softer than its target does nothing to it, and says “Too hard.” A fist cannot break stone, for example, and a wooden thing cannot cut rock.
  • Class: substances fall into the classes wood, plant, stone, ore, metal, earth, fiber, hide, bone, organic, flesh and crystal. Tools work at a different efficiency on each class; see the efficiency table below.
  • Traits: wood, leather, cloth and silk burn; metal conducts, and lightning electrifies it; crystal, glass, clay and obsidian are brittle, and the earth element can shatter them. See Elements.

What substance crafted things are made of

A thing you make takes the substance of its main material: an axe made from stone is a Stone Axe, a shield made from planks a Wooden Shield, a bow made from sticks a Wooden Bow. When a material is itself of that substance (a plank is wood), the name does not repeat it.

The world's own substances

The things in the world are not only items: bodies are flesh, grass is plant, the ground is earth, ice is ice, ore veins are ore. They have a hardness too. Flesh has hardness 1: a bare-handed punch can slowly break grass and wood, but not stone. The ground itself cannot be broken.

Substances seen in the game

Most items obtainable in Chapter 1 are of these substances: the stone tools and wooden furniture you make yourself; the “Old Axe” at the Ford is iron; the pauldrons in the Ossuary are steel, the ring silver; Edda's Wedding Band is gold; gloves, boots and belts are leather, cloaks cloth. The garden toolbox in Firstlight Village holds a Steel Hoe, a Bronze Sickle and a Steel Smith Hammer. In the old storyline, the “Old Pickaxe” at the mouth of the Old Mine is iron.
