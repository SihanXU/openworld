Fence

A fence is a length of wooden fence that blocks the way; neither people nor creatures can get through. You have to build it yourself: in build mode choose “Fence”, 2 Stick, with a hammer on you; hold the left button and drag to build a whole row, fencing in a garden or a pen. At present no map has a ready-made fence.

Source

  • Build mode: Stick×2 + Hammer×1
  • Build mode: press B and choose “Fence”: 2 sticks, with a hammer on you (the Smith Hammer, not used up).
  • By hand: put a stick on the ground, hold another stick and press G on it: “Two sticks make stakes.” Knock the stakes with a hammer: “You nail up a fence.” (see building by hand)

Direction

A fence has a direction: across (running east–west) or upright (running north–south).

  • Build mode: the arrow on the preview is its facing; press R to turn it. When the arrow points left or right the fence stands north–south; when it points up or down, east–west. If you have not turned it, it runs between the player and the tile (its face toward the player).
  • By hand: the fence faces whichever way the player faced when setting the stakes, likewise with its face toward the player.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

  • Blocks the way: a fence is solid; people and creatures have to go around it. Animals cannot get out either.
  • A fence cannot be taken, and an axe, pickaxe or hammer cannot knock it loose; to remove it, break it.
  • Breaking: durability 4, wood. An axe cuts it open in one blow; a bare-handed punch takes off 1 point. Broken, it leaves 1 stick (half of its 2, see Durability and Breaking).
  • Fire: wooden, it catches fire and burns down to one Ash; along a row of fence, the fire may run on down the line.
  • Hold something and press G, and the thing is put down on its tile.

Crafting

Built in Build mode (B): Blocks passage. Place several to mark a garden.

History

  • 2026-09-30: The first version already had the fence: two sticks make stakes, and a hammer nails them into a fence.
  • 2026-10-03: Fences could be built from the build bar.
  • 2026-10-04: Gained durability and a makeup (2 sticks).
  • 2026-10-08: Fences gained a direction: press R in build mode to turn one, and an upright fence is drawn upright; unturned, its face is toward the player.
