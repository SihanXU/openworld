“Suffering is not God's abandonment.”

— Church Courier

The Church Courier is a mounted courier from the Holy City, 27 years old. On the morning after the holy flame is stolen, he rides into Firstlight Village, reads out the Holy City's command that the Pilgrimage begin, hands the player the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, and rides away.

He appears only in this one story scene, and the player cannot talk to him.

His table of lines holds only “Suffering is not God's abandonment.”, which the player never hears. What he says in the scene is:

  • “By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers weaken, and the Long Night returns. Let the pilgrimage be called.”
  • “One came down from the mountain with a fairy. The Church names that one the Pilgrim.”

When he is done, he stares at Lumi for a long time, and says nothing.

Quests

No quests involve this person.

He gives no quests. His letter is an objective of the main quest The First Seal (“Wait in Firstlight for the Church's letter the next morning”), and it makes Sister Ilse's The Pilgrim's Road (quest) available.

Lore

The courier formally names the player the Pilgrim, and from then on Chapter 1 turns from the village's troubles to the Seal-bearers. See Chapter 1 and Church of the Five Sufferings.

Why he stares at Lumi (click to show)

Lumi is “the Second Vessel”, the one who could not bear the Five Sufferings long ago. The courier staring at her in silence is the corner of her past that Chapter 1 lets show. See Lumi.

Trivia

  • Deep in The Forest, in the old camp, the same Proclamation is nailed up, grey with age; it names Brother Cassian, thirty-one years ago, in words identical to the ones the courier reads.
