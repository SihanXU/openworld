The Pilgrims' Rest stands on the east bank of the Ford, a stopping place the Church of the Five Sufferings keeps for pilgrims. Head east from the Ford's east exit and this is the first place you reach; farther east is the Pilgrim's Road. Father Caius, stationed here, welcomes the pilgrim named in the Church's letter.

The Rest is a peaceful zone. It is the only road east to Emberfall, so after defeating the White Knight, going back to the village and heading east again both take you through here.

Map

36×26. It joins the east bank of the Ford on the west and the Pilgrim's Road on the east. A dirt road runs through the Rest from west to east, with a flagstone square in the middle.

Areas

  • The Chapel of the Five: the stone chapel to the north, its door facing south. Inside are the altar of the five wounds and the bookstand with the Litany of the Five; see Chapel of the Five.
  • The Pilgrims' Hostel: the long house to the south. Inside are a row of beds and the pilgrims' book; see The Pilgrims' Hostel.
  • The square: the flagstones in the middle of the road. On its west side stands the Holy See's notice; press E to read: “BY THE HOLY SEE OF THE FIVE. The Seal-bearers, saints who bear what Astra bore, can bear it no longer. Pilgrims of the Five: seek out the five Seal-bearers, overcome them, and bring the five instruments home to the Holy City for the Great Rite. Before you go on, take the oath at the five wounds.”
  • Northeast of the square is a well; the signpost on the east reads “THE PILGRIM'S ROAD → Five wayside shrines along it, one for each suffering. Light each as you pass.”
  • South of the square are the pilgrims' fire (always lit) and two log benches.

Residents

  • Father Caius: in the morning he keeps the five holy wounds in the chapel, from noon to evening he watches the road from the chapel door, and at night he sleeps in the chapel.
  • Maud and her daughter Wynn: pilgrims bound for the Holy City who stopped here when they heard about the White Knight. By day Maud sits by the fire and Wynn plays by the well; at night they sleep in the hostel.

Lore

  • The first time you come here (after receiving the Church's letter and before the White Knight falls), Father Caius comes out to greet you: “You must be the pilgrim the letter named, the one with the fairy.” He asks you to the chapel to take the oath: “Every pilgrim the Holy See has sent east took the oath there first.”
  • For the taking of the vow before the altar of the five wounds, see Chapel of the Five.
  • After the White Knight falls, the welcome and the vow no longer appear. The first time you come to the Rest after that, Father Caius says: “You have come from the east. ...Then it is done, and the road is open. The Holy See will have the Crown, the first of the five.”
