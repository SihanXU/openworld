Cauliflower (crops)

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item
Cauliflower
CategoryCrops
Chinese name花椰菜
IDfixture/crop/cauliflower

A cauliflower (crop) is a cauliflower planted in farmland and still growing in the ground. Sow Cauliflower Seeds, water it twice, and once ripe harvest Cauliflower and seeds. For the harvested vegetable see Cauliflower: eaten raw it restores 5 satiety.

Source

  • There is no set way to get it in the game yet.
  • Sowing: holding Cauliflower Seeds, right-click an empty farmland tile: “You sow Cauliflower Seeds.” The seeds become a “cauliflower sprout”. “Sow Cauliflower” on the “Planting” page of Building mode works too.
  • The seeds are sold at the trading stall on the Held Ground (“Farming” shelf, 3 for 20 Dawnmarks). No map has cauliflowers already planted, and the vegetable garden in Firstlight Village has none either.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Growth

Stage Name What to do
1 cauliflower sprout Water once; 4 game hours later it grows into the next stage
2 young cauliflower Water again; 4 game hours later it ripens
3 ripe cauliflower Harvest
  • Watering: holding a container full of water, left-click it: “You water X.” It gets watered and grows one stage 4 game hours later. Watering again before the watered status ends does nothing.
  • Needs open sky: while something covers it overhead, it does not grow when the time comes; it checks again every game hour and grows only once open to the sky.
  • No water, no growth, but it never withers either. Rain does not count as watering.
  • Fertilizing: holding Fertilizer or Ash, right-click it before it is ripe: “You work ash into the soil.” (With fertilizer it is “You work fertilizer into the soil.”) The harvest then yields 1 more. Each plant can be fertilized only once.
  • All crops grow equally fast: at least 4 game hours and two waterings from sowing to ripeness. See Farming for the full rules.

Harvest

Method You get
Holding a Sickle, left-click it when ripe (“Harvest X”) 2 cauliflowers (3 if fertilized) and 2 Cauliflower Seeds, dropped on its tile
Press E to pick it when ripe (“Pick X”) 1 cauliflower straight into your hands, 1 Cauliflower Seeds dropped on the ground

After the harvest the plant is gone, leaving empty farmland that can be sown again at once.

Harvested cauliflowers can be sold at the trading stall for 18 Dawnmarks each.

Other

  • Crops do not block the way.
  • What happens if you hit it with a weapon or sickle before it is ripe is not written in the rules.
  • In the rules, digging it up with a shovel turns it back into one seed (“You dig up X.”).
  • Crops do not catch fire.

History

  • 2026-10-08: Added ten crops, each with three stages: parsnip, potato, radish, cauliflower and melon, and corn, blueberries, strawberry, hot pepper and eggplant, which bear again after a harvest; their seeds are sold at the trading stall on the Held Ground.
  • 2026-10-08: Fertilizing with fertilizer now says “You work fertilizer into the soil.”
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
