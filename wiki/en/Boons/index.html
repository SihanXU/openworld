Boons

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Boons are the powers Lumi carries for the player. When each Seal-bearer falls, the power of his Seal scatters, and Lumi takes up part of it. Lumi carries only one at a time; while she is by the player's side, that power is added to the player. In the game the panel is called “Lumi's Powers”.

The moment she takes one up is shown in a short scene: after the Seal-bearer falls and gives up his relic, a cold light flies out of him, trailing sparks along an arc, and comes down on Lumi; a ring of light bursts, the whole screen flashes, and Lumi glows all over and rises a little; at the same time the top of the screen shows “First Seal · Lumi takes up the seal's power” and the boon's name, fading after a few seconds. The story waits for the scene to finish before letting her speak.

Crown's InsightFrom the First Seal, the Crown of Thorns. Experience gained +100%.The White Knight Alberic

The other four of the five slots are sealed for now (to be designed).

There are five places in all, one for each Seal-bearer. For now there is only the first:

  • Crown's Insight: from the First Seal: the Crown of Thorns. Experience gained +100%.

The other four places show on the panel as locked, “Not yet found”: “Lumi takes up part of a seal's power when its bearer falls.”

The other four boons will come with the second to fifth Seal-bearers; what they are has not been designed yet (to be designed).

How to get them

Lumi gains a boon in the story: when a Seal-bearer falls and his power scatters, “Lumi takes up a new power: X.” If she is carrying nothing else at that moment, she carries this one at once.

Where Crown's Insight comes from (click to show)

After you defeat Alberic, the White Knight, he kneels, takes off the Crown of Thorns and hands it to the player. A cold light spills from the Crown of Thorns and flows to Lumi: she has taken up part of the First Seal's power. The rest of the light is gathered into the lantern of the Grey Man.

How to change them

  • Talking to Lumi (press E on her) opens her panel; its “The Seals' Powers” page holds the five Seals' places, those not yet found sealed. Press E beside a woken waypoint statue (the prompt is “Listen to the waypoint statue”) and the panel opens straight to this page.
  • The panel lists the five places: the one carried says “✓ Carried”, the others “Carry”; click one to change.
  • You can change only while standing within 3 cells of a woken waypoint statue. When you are farther away, the panel says “A power can be changed only beside a woken waypoint statue.”, and the buttons are greyed out.
  • Once changed: “Lumi now carries X.”

When they work

  • The boon rests on Lumi (the status Crown's Insight), and she passes it to whoever she follows.
  • Lumi must be on the same map as the player for the boon to count. She always follows the player, so you seldom need to think about it.

And the Crown of Thorns

The Crown of Thorns itself, worn on the head, gives an experience rate of −50%. The experience bonus adds to it: carrying Crown's Insight while wearing the Crown of Thorns, you gain only 50% more experience. The experience rate is never below the plain rate (×1); the Crown of Thorns cannot make it less. See Levels and Experience.

Lumi and these powers

Lumi and these powers (click to show)

Lumi is the Second Vessel: she could not bear the Five Sufferings, and lost all her power and memory. When each Seal-bearer falls, what she takes up is part of his power. See Lumi, Seal-bearers.

Tips

  • Experience +100% is well worth it: with Crown's Insight, you level up twice as fast.
  • To change your boon, first look on the map (M) for where there is a woken waypoint statue.

History

  • 2026-10-05: Lumi and her powers added: five places, one carried at a time, changed only beside a woken waypoint statue; the first is Crown's Insight, experience +100%.
