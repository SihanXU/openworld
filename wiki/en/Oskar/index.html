“Mending baskets. I lost two down in the barrow and the rest got crushed. My hands still shake, so it goes slow.”

— Oskar

Oskar is the oldest forager in Firstlight Village, 63 years old. He followed Lina into The Old Barrow to bring her out, and ended up shut in there with her. He went down the stairs looking for another way out, and the dead down there drove him “like sheep” into a cell of The Ossuary of the Thorn on the Barrow's second level. Once rescued, he mends baskets in the village.

Before he is rescued, he spends all day in the cell in the Ossuary of the Thorn. After the player hands in Lost in the Barrow to Rowan, he returns to the village: he sleeps at home from 21:00 to 07:00 and sits on a bench mending baskets from 07:00 to 21:00.

Lore

Oskar voices the side of Chapter 1 where “the dead will not rest”: “I've buried a fair few. They used to stay down.” See The Long Night and Chapter 1.

Trivia

  • His first words to the player are “About time”, and only then does he thank you.
