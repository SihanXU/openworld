A coal seam is a band of coal in the rock. Mine it with a Pickaxe and it drops 2 Coal. Dawn Hill Mine, open from Chapter 1, has 4; the galleries on both sides of The Old Mine have 4; The Quarry on the Held Ground has 3. Once mined, it leaves a spent coal seam in place, which grows back after one game day.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Dawn Hill Mine ×4, The Embervein Mine ×4, The Oathstone Quarry ×3
  • The Old Mine: 4, in the galleries on both sides.
  • Dawn Hill Mine: 4: 1 in the mine hall, 1 in the north chamber, 2 in the south chamber.
  • The Quarry: The Quarry on the Held Ground has 3, reachable once the Quarry is opened through Expanding the Held Ground.
  • Grows back: wherever the coal seam is, once mined it leaves a spent coal seam in place (bedrock, unbreakable), which turns back into a coal seam after one game day.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Mining it

  • Durability 12; its material is ore (hardness 2).
  • Hold a pickaxe and left-click it (“Mine the coal seam”): a stone pickaxe deals 6 points a blow, so two blows break it open. Punching it: “Too hard.”
  • Mined, it drops 2 coal (half of its 4). See Mining, Durability and Breaking.

What coal is for

The smith Garrick of Emberfall needs 3 coal (plus 5 iron ore) to forge Dragonsbane. Smelting in batches at a forge needs coal too: 2 ore and 1 coal make 2 ingots (see Smithing). Coal catches fire, and can be thrown into a fire to burn to ash.

Other

A coal seam is solid and blocks the way; the seam itself does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.

History

  • 2026-10-04: Gained durability and a makeup (4 coal); coal seams in the Old Mine grow back (W7).
  • 2026-10-11: 3 were placed in the Quarry on the Held Ground.
