The Ninth Nail is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Joss, the bridge-builder. The three planks of the little bridge at the Ford used nine nails in all, and whoever tore up the bridge laid the nails out on the jetty, but there were only eight. Joss wants the ninth back: it went east with the one who tore up the bridge.

It can only be taken after the Church's letter (see The First Seal). The page title carries “(quest)” to tell it apart from the item The Ninth Nail.

  1. The ninth nail
    Carry The Ninth Nail

    Talk to Joss

The nail is taken when you hand it in.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: by day (6:00–21:00) Joss is planing boards by the workbench on the west side of Firstlight Village. Press E on him and choose “Accept ‘The Ninth Nail’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Across the river and onto the East Road: cross the river at The Ford by any means, and from the eastern exit on the far bank head east, through The Pilgrims' Rest, The Pilgrim's Road and The Kneeling Field, onto The East Road.
  3. Find the nail: the nail is in the kingdom of thorns at the eastern end of the East Road, near the stretch where the road passes north of the ring of thorns: a square-headed nail on the ground. The White Knight Alberic stands in the ring; walking into the ring plays the kingdom's scene, and he will fight you. You can defeat him first and then pick it up, or slip round and pick it up during the fight.
  4. Hand in: go back to Joss and press E to hand in.

You do not need to have taken the quest to pick up the nail. If you pick it up first and take the quest afterwards, talk to him once more after accepting to hand it in.

Rewards

  • Experience +30 (Levels and Experience)
  • Smith Hammer
  • Joss's Tool Belt (Fine): Belt, defence 2, level 3
  • Regard Joss +15 (Friendship)
  • Karma +3 (Karma and Faith)

The Smith Hammer is a building tool: crates, wooden chests, fences, signposts and the like are first framed up with planks or sticks, then knocked together with the smith hammer (see Building).

Dialogue

When offering
“Eight nails on the pier. Eight. I drove nine. Whoever took my bridge kept one. Find it for me, if you go that way.”
While unfinished
“The ninth nail. Square-headed, pulled out straight. You'll know it.”
On handing in
“The ninth. ...Twenty years that bridge stood. Here, a proper hammer. You've earned it.”
What Joss didn't say (click to show)

When you hand in, Joss only says the bridge stood for twenty years. Talk to him again after Chapter 1 ends (once you bring the Crown of Thorns back to the village), and he tells you, once, what happened back then: “Twenty years ago, it was a man in grey who paid for that bridge, and a boy at his heel carried the purse. Old coins, with no king's head on them. He said: ‘I want a road that lets the carts get away from here quickly.’ I never did understand what he meant.” As Chapter 1 is designed, those are old coins of the Ashen Watch.
