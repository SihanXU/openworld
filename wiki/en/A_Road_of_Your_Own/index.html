A Road of Your Own is one of Lumi's guide quests: when your character reaches level 5 and still has no profession, she speaks up by herself and takes you to the profession trainer Bram in the drill yard of Firstlight Village.

Walkthrough

  1. Start: in a proper game, reach level 5 (level 5 is exactly where Bram takes people on; see Levels and Experience) with no profession, and Lumi speaks; the quest begins by itself. A character who has already chosen a profession never gets it.
  2. Find Bram: day and night he is in the drill yard beside the hut in the north-east corner of Firstlight Village, with a crossed-swords mark over his head, and when you track the quest there is a marker on the map too. Press E on him.
  3. Choose a profession: his panel lists four roads — Assassin (daggers), Warrior (sword and shield), Ranger (bow), Mage (staff) — each with its style of play and starting weapon. Pick one.
  4. Complete: once the profession is chosen, the quest is complete. The profession branch of the skill tree (K) opens with it; if you have no weapon for your profession, Bram sells them.

You can go back to Bram later to change profession; the price is that the profession branch's skill points are refunded and spent again. See Professions.
