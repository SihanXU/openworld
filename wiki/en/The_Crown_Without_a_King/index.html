The Crown Without a King is a huge boss shaped like a peacock gargoyle: its head is a hollow crown, and mirror-like plates of armor float around it. It appears only in the boss challenge and the boss test arena; it has no place in the story and is not entered in the codex. Its name still carries the word “Pride”: it comes from the earlier “Seven Sins” boss setting, which has since been withdrawn.

It uses its three moves in turn. Each one winds up first, marking every tile it will land on; once locked, the marks no longer follow the player. Its damage is the highest of the four bosses, and its wind-ups are the longest.

All damage figures are before defense. When its health falls to 50% it enters the second phase: wind-ups 20% shorter, the pause after each move 35% shorter, damage 35% higher.

Behavior

  • 2600 health and 3 defense, both the highest of the four bosses. Its body takes up 5×4 tiles, also the largest.
  • It spots opponents within 12 tiles, and never strays more than 12 tiles from where it spawned.
  • It has no ordinary attack; all its damage comes from its three skills.

Strategy

  • The cone only opens in front of it: get round to its side or back. When the Rebuff pushes you a tile, take care not to be pushed into the marks of the next move.
  • The cross covers only the two straight lines: stand diagonally and you are safe.
  • The frame of Kneel Before the Crown is drawn around the player's locked position: if you stay put, you are at the center of the frame, which is safe; if you dodge, get right out of it, and do not stop on the frame.
  • Its defense is high; use a high-attack weapon.

Drops

  • Iron Ore×4
  • A Legendary Arms of the slayer's profession (one not yet in the world)
  • Experience: equal to its maximum health

The boss challenge saves nothing: even if you win, you cannot take the spoils with you.

Lore

It was originally Pride in the “Seven Sins” setting. When that setting was withdrawn, the other four had their skills and code deleted; it, the Velvet Lure and the Face Thief stayed in the boss challenge. The bosses of the story are now the Seal-bearers; where these three will go later has not been decided (to be designed).

Its art was generated with image tools: a 4×5 action sheet (idle, walk, attack, cast, hit and fall, 96 pixels per frame), plus three skill effects. It is displayed at 2× scale. Its colors are lapis blue, ivory and gold.

History

  • 2026-10-05: The Seven Sins setting was withdrawn; it and two others stayed in the boss challenge.
