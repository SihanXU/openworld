Cinder Pass climbs from the east of Emberfall up to The Dragon's Lair on Cinder Peak. Rock walls line both sides; snow lies at the bottom, and the higher you go, the more the ground is burned earth and ash. Along the way are frost wolves, fire imps and a stone golem; there is an ice golem in the snow, and a storm wisp drifts over the open road. Halfway up burns a campfire, the last place to rest before the lair.

Cinder Pass is an open-air combat zone, and after nightfall night creatures appear as well. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see Emberfall and the Dragon.

Map

The map is 30×40, tall and narrow. Rock walls line both sides, the ground is snow, and the upper half holds a wide stretch of ash. A mountain road two tiles wide comes in from the west (rows 34–36), zigzags north and leaves by the north (columns 14–16). A few charred trees stand along the way.

Areas

  • The snow slope at the bottom: the lower half, where frost wolves roam and the ice golem keeps watch in the snow.
  • The last camp: a lit campfire halfway up. Apart from fire creatures, monsters will not come within two tiles of a lit fire, so you can catch your breath here and cook food (see campfire, Cooking).
  • The scorched earth: the ash ground of the upper half, where the fire imps are.
  • The summit: before the northern exit, guarded by the stone golem.

Lore

To be designed: this part is not designed yet; below is only what is settled. To make a proposal, click “Talk” at the top of the page.

In the old main story this pass was the road from Emberfall to the dragon's lair on Cinder Peak; Mara, the head of the town, said the dragon lived at the top of the mountain road east of town. This content predates the setting of the Five Sufferings and is planned to be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see Chapter 2).
