Brazier

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item

Brazier
CategoryLight sources
Chinese name火盆

A brazier is a metal fire basin standing on the ground. In the dark maps — The Old Barrow, The Ossuary of the Thorn, The Warden's Sanctum, The Old Mine and The Dragon's Lair — 21 of them burn in all along the passages and in the chambers. The 2 tiles around a lit brazier are a safe place: except for fire monsters, monsters will not come in.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Longsleep Barrow ×5, The Ossuary of the Thorn ×5, The Warden's Sanctum ×4, Dawn Hill, Dawn Hill Mine ×3, The Chapter Hall ×2, The Abbey Crypt ×3, The Priors' Tomb ×4, The Embervein Mine ×4, The Cinderpeak Lair ×4
  • 4 in the Old Barrow, 5 in the Ossuary of the Thorn, 4 in the Warden's Sanctum (at the four corners of the chamber), 4 in the Old Mine (along the main gallery) and 4 in the Dragon's Lair. All start out lit.
  • You cannot make one.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Monsters keep away

  • Except for fire monsters (fire slimes, fire imps, dragon whelps, the dragon of Cinder Peak and the like), monsters will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning, nor chase someone standing there (see Factions and Temper).
  • When something is hard on your heels, fall back beside a brazier. The barrow warden in the Warden's Sanctum and the cyclops in the Old Mine fear fire too.
  • At night, people and creatures within 3 tiles of a lit fire see as far as by day.

Lighting and putting out

  • Putting out: holding a container full of water (a Water Bucket, for instance), left-click it, and the fire goes out. Any water element will do.
  • Lighting: holding a Torch, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn Firemaker, right-clicking it with an ordinary Stick lights it too. If the brazier is wet, the first touch of fire only dries it.
  • Once lit it stays lit: it needs no fuel, and rain does not put it out.

Cooking

A brazier is a cooking fixture:

  • Holding raw food, right-click a lit brazier (“Cook X over the brazier”), or press G on it, and the raw food is cooked, staying with you (see Cooking).
  • A room with a brazier in it counts as a kitchen.

Warming yourself

Press E empty-handed on a lit brazier (“Warm yourself at the brazier”): if you are wet, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself at the brazier; the damp steams off you.”); if not, just “Warmth from the brazier seeps into your hands.”, with no health restored. The rules do not say what happens when you press E empty-handed on an unlit brazier (the prompt shows “?”). See Warming yourself.

Throwing things in

  • Hold something that burns (logs, sticks, cut grass…) and press G: “X goes into the fire.” It burns down to Ash.
  • Anything else, with G, is put down on the brazier's tile.

Unbreakable, and it does not burn

The brazier is metal and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire either. Hit by the lightning element, it becomes charged (electrified), which does nothing to it.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-09-30: The first version already had the brazier.
  • 2026-10-03: Braziers were lit in the Old Barrow, the Old Mine and the Dragon's Lair.
  • 2026-10-07: Braziers were lit in the Ossuary of the Thorn and the Warden's Sanctum below the barrow too.
  • 2026-10-08: The brazier counts as a cooking fixture: right-clicking it holding raw food cooks the food too, and a room with a brazier is a kitchen.
  • 2026-10-09: Pressing E empty-handed lets you warm yourself at a lit brazier.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
