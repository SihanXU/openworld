The Axe is a tool for felling trees, and a weapon too. Hold it and left-click a tree to fell it, or left-click a Log on the ground to split it into two Plank; with an axe on you, the crafting panel can turn logs into planks. It belongs to no profession: anyone can fight monsters with it.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Vesper Ford
  • Crafting: Steel Bar×2 + Stick
  • Make one yourself: the stone axe is the first thing you can make: 1 Stick, 1 Stone and 1 tuft of Cut Grass; press C and make it anywhere, with no workbench needed. A common-quality stone axe has attack 5. It is the first tool the tutorial teaches.
  • Ready-made: an Old Axe (iron, attack 6) lies by the stump in the camp at The Ford. Hesper mentions it when you first meet.

Uses

  • As a tool (carried, not used up): Plank

The tooltip gives the Axe's use as “Fells trees, splits logs”. What it can do under the rules:

  • Fell trees: left-click a tree: “The axe bites into the tree.” The tree becomes notched. Two blows of a stone axe fell it, leaving a stump, 2 logs and 1 stick, with a 25% chance of an extra Acorn; once you have learned Woodcutter, you get an extra log instead. Stop after one blow and the cut heals in 10 seconds, leaving the tree whole again. See Woodcutting.
  • Split logs: left-click a log lying on the ground: “You split the log into planks.” It becomes 2 planks.
  • Make planks: with the axe in your inventory, the crafting panel's “Plank” recipe can be made: 1 log makes 2 planks. The axe only has to be on you; it is not used up.
  • Charred wood: left-click a tree charred by fire: “The charred wood crumbles to coal.” You get 1 Coal.
  • The dead tree at the Ford: the dead tree at the narrows of The Ford takes three blows to fall, and falls away from you; the river lies on that side, so the trunk ends up across it as a bridge. See The dead tree at the Ford.
  • Taking things apart: left-click to knock an unlocked crate or chest, and what is inside spills out while the container comes loose whole and drops; pick it up and you can build it elsewhere. A wooden frame, stakes or a sign post that has not been nailed together yet comes apart with one blow of the axe, and the planks and sticks that went into it come back as they were (see Building).
  • Knocking built things loose: left-click a built wall, door, floor, stairs, bed, workbench or anything else of the kind you can carry off, and it comes loose whole and drops where it stood; press E to pick it up (close by it is drawn into your inventory by itself), and you can carry it elsewhere and build it again. See Structures.
  • Combat: the Axe is a general weapon anyone can use, with a base attack of 4. Hold it and press Space, or left-click a monster within 1 tile.

Trivia

  • In the item catalogue the Axe is a weapon, not a tool: it is a bladed weapon that also carries the “axe” tool ability. So when you click a tree or a log with it you are using a tool, and when you click a monster you are attacking.
  • An axe cannot dig up a stump; that takes a Shovel; see Stumps.

History

  • 2026-09-30: Added to the game's item catalogue; the axe can fell trees and split logs.
  • 2026-10-02: The stone axe can be made in the crafting panel, the game's first recipe.
  • 2026-10-05: The crafting panel can turn logs into planks, provided you carry an axe.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls settled on four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon swung, anything else used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
