World and rules

Item Status Notes
The event model: the only AI interface is the outcome of an event Implemented 2026-10-02. See The World Learns
A learned rule belongs to one kind of thing only and fills one gap only Implemented 2026-10-09. Other kinds each ask their own question, and cases left unanswered are asked later; rules once written for a whole class are no longer used. See The World Learns
G1: the general cases of the base rules Implemented 2026-10-09. Things handed to creatures (fiber ties them up, food is swallowed), swings into empty cells are all blunt blows, wild creatures bite a reaching hand, terrain is only touched, warming up beside a lit fire, ore smelted into ingots in a forge, a collapsed Lumi waiting where she fell. See The general cases the game knows by itself
World model v4: everything is a thing, only things and the clock, durability, makeup, stages, spaces, rooms, two levels of map Implemented 2026-10-04; all ten steps (W1–W10) done. See Durability and Breaking, Rooms, World Map
Four inputs: E, G, left click, right click, with reach written in data Implemented 2026-10-05; set as four inputs on 2026-10-21 (picking up loose things goes to E, using a held thing on something goes to the right click). See Controls
Key descriptions follow the target and the item; AI patches write descriptions too Implemented 2026-10-05
Difficulty and death Implemented 2026-10-05. See Difficulty
Multi-storey buildings: each storey builds only its own level, the ceiling excepted; stairs; 3/4 perspective Implemented 2026-10-05. See Storeys
Two speeds of satiety Implemented 2026-10-06. See Satiety
Layers and occlusion (floors under standing things, things that hide people become translucent) Implemented 2026-10-07
Digging into mountains (the ground below made into things) To be designed At present the ground itself cannot be broken
Food spoiling, iron rusting, campfires burning out, eggs hatching Planned The “stages” mechanism already supports it; no content yet
Floors laid on water as bridges Planned At present a plank put into water can become a wooden bridge tile
Encounters on the world map To be designed

Gameplay

Item Status Notes
Inventory, crafting, levels, satiety Implemented Since 2026-10-02. See Inventory, Crafting
Woodcutting, build mode, farming Implemented See Woodcutting, Building, Farming
Skill tree (four combat branches, four life branches) Partly implemented Swiftness, Survival, Damage, Profession and Gathering have skills; Agriculture, Animal Husbandry and Smithing are still empty (to be designed). See Skills
Active skills, the skill bar (Shift+1–6), the skill panel (U) Implemented 2026-10-08, twenty active skills. Skill books are sold on the bookshelf of Firstlight Village's chapel (2026-10-11); gear carrying skills has no content handing it out yet. See Active skills and the skill bar
Main-hand weapon slot, the bundle left after you fall Implemented 2026-10-08. See Equipment, Difficulty
Professions Implemented 2026-10-03. See Professions
Equipment slots and tiers Implemented 2026-10-07. See Equipment, Quality
Lumi's boons Partly implemented Only the first (Crown's Insight); the other four come with the later four Seals, to be designed. See Boons
Waypoint statues, the world map, the Codex Implemented
Tutorial (guided) Implemented 2026-10-07. See Tutorial
Boss challenge Implemented See Boss Challenge
Money and the Held Ground's trading stall Implemented 2026-10-08: Dawnmarks; the stall's three shelves for farming, animal husbandry and fishing, and its buying. See Money, Trading stall
General buying and selling with villagers and merchants Planned Press G on a merchant while holding something to sell it, press E on something on a shelf to buy it. See Money
Fishing Implemented 2026-10-08: fishing rods (handmade or bought), bait, seven kinds of fish by hour, weather and water depth. See Fishing
Feeding, milking, breeding Implemented 2026-10-08: animal feed, milk, two well-fed animals of a kind produce young, the young grow up; crated livestock. See Animals
More crops and fertilizer Implemented 2026-10-08: ten more vegetables (five bear again after harvest), fertilizer sold at the stall. See Crops, Farming
Every item has a use Planned Books, scrolls, instruments, lockpicks, soap, brooms and the like have no fixed use yet
Cooking extended (cauldron, stews, bread, salt) Planned At present you can roast; see Cooking
Smelting and smithing Partly implemented 2026-10-09: a lit forge smelts iron ore and gold ore into iron ingots and gold ingots (see Smelting). No recipe uses ingots yet; smithing at the anvil is to be designed. See Crafting
Shield blocking, gear wear, gear drawn on the body Planned
People changing their relations by what they see Planned
Free text input To be designed
