A Spear is a polearm whose attack reaches 2 tiles. It belongs to no profession: anyone can fight with it. The only one in the game comes from Holt's quest.

Source

  • Crafting: Steel Bar + Stick×2
  • Quest reward: Clear the Hollow
  • Quest: the side quest Clear the Hollow from Holt in Firstlight Village (taken from level 2): fell 6 monsters in Thornwood Hollow and turn it in, and you get a spear and a pair of “Militia Pauldrons”. This spear has no material, so its attack is just the base 4.
  • No spear is placed on any map, and it cannot be crafted.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

  • Combat: hold it and press Space, or left-click a monster within 2 tiles. An attack at 2 tiles can only go out along your own row or column; it cannot reach diagonally. For the rules of combat see Combat.
  • General weapons get no bonus from profession talents.
  • Thrust: a spear strikes with its point, and its edge is “piercing” (as with the pickaxe). Against things such as wood and stone it is reckoned by the efficiency of piercing (see the efficiency table). It is not a blunt weapon: hitting a frozen target does not shatter the ice, and it gets none of the blunt weapon's 1.5× damage.
