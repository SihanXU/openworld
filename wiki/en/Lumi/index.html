“You're awake! I'm Lumi. I'm a fairy, and from today I go where you go.”

— Lumi

Lumi is a hand-sized fairy in the shape of a winged young girl, and the player's companion: a glowing yellow flower pinned in her long lavender hair, violet eyes, a white dress, and two pairs of clear ice-blue wings (see Appearance). When the player wakes on Cloudtop (in the Tutorial, in The Clearing), she is right beside them, and from then on she follows the player through the whole game. She is cheerful, curious and bold, and has a remark for everything.

  • Following. She stays within a few cells behind the player, and when the player stands still she floats to a spot two cells behind them and stops; when the player turns to face her, she does not circle round behind again, and you can talk to her from two cells away. She flies, so she gets past things that block people on foot; when the player goes to another map, or gets too far from her, she follows at once.
  • Takes up no room, and does not fight. She never blocks the way, and attacks swung forward pass beneath her; but an attack aimed at her on purpose still hits. She attacks nothing.
  • Hints. Press E on her and she names the quest being tracked: “Now: the quest's name.” After it come the unfinished objectives and their progress, plus this stage's hint; when no quest is under way she says “Nothing presses now. Go where you like!”
  • Showing the way. When a quest is tracked and you stand five seconds doing nothing, she flies to where it goes next and calls out in a bubble over her head, and a ring of light breathes on the ground there (see Showing the way).
  • Remarks along the way. When you reach a new place or something new happens, she says something in a bubble over her head; these are the story's “beats”.
  • Witnessing that the world learns. The first time the player does something to a thing that the world has never seen, she notices. See The World Learns.
  • Boons. When a Seal-bearer falls, part of the power that spills out flows to her, becoming power she carries for the player. See below and Boons.

She is an essential character: when her health falls to zero she is down for a while, then wakes up; she does not die. On the minimap she is a yellow dot.

Appearance

As small as a hand (about 14 cm), yet not a child: a petite young girl. Long straight lavender hair falls to her knees, a few strands floating; a yellow five-petal flower is pinned in it, and it glows when a Seal-bearer's power flows to her. Violet eyes. Two pairs of translucent ice-blue wings, the upper pair larger, finely veined. A sleeveless white halter dress, its thin blue halter ribbon tied at her throat in a small bow, its skirt in layered petal-shaped panels; a blue satin sash at the waist tied in a bow, its two long tails trailing; bare feet, and a ribbon bracelet with a small white flower round her wrist.

She has a portrait of her own: when she speaks in a scene, or is spoken to, the dialogue box shows it. It has six expressions (neutral, happy, sad, angry, surprised, shy), changing with the mood of the line; every expression blinks, and her mouth moves as she speaks (the happy one already laughs open-mouthed, so it keeps its mouth). The panel that opens when you talk to her shows the same portrait on its left, with both pairs of wings; on The Seals' Powers her expression changes with the seal pointed at. The lines in the bubble over her head have no portrait. Her sprite on the map follows the design too: lavender hair, the yellow flower, ice-blue wings. Her design book: Art.

Showing the way

When a quest is tracked and you stand five seconds doing nothing (no step, no act), Lumi shows you the way herself:

  • Where next is the first unfinished objective of the tracked quest with a spot on this map, at the spot nearest you: for something to fetch (the dawnbloom, the vigil oil), the nearest one lying on the ground, not the middle of the place; for someone to find, that person; for a place to go, that place. With no spot on this map, it is the way off it toward the objective's map (a door or the map's edge).
  • Near (within eight cells): she flies to the cell just above it, turns to you and calls out — “Hi, here! This one is dawnbloom — not the little flowers round it. Pick three!” A ring of light breathes on that cell, with a few motes rising.
  • Farther: she flies eight cells out that way, stops and looks back: “This way! Dawnbloom is off to the east.” Walk after her and she keeps leading; once it is within eight cells she flies over it. For a way off the map she calls “Dawn Hill is through here!” and the like.
  • Her lines come in turn, never the same one twice running; an objective with a line of her own (the dawnbloom, the oil, the chapel bell) gets that one.
  • When you reach it, walk off another way, or she has shown it eight seconds, she flies back to you; if you still do nothing, she shows it again twenty seconds later. Doing anything starts those twenty seconds over.
  • She does not lead in a scene, while a yes/no question is open, during the tutorial's drills, in a fight, while you cannot move, while fishing, or with “Companion shows the way when you stand still” turned off in Options.
  • Her words are in the bubble over her head: they open no dialogue box and need no key to close.

Schedule

Lumi has no schedule; she only follows the player.

While she is down she cannot keep up with the player, and reports “cannot keep up” to the world, at most once per game hour. The outcome while she is down is defined: she stays where she fell, a sad face pops up over her head, and a notice at the bottom left reads “Lumi lies where she fell, too weak to keep up.” After 30 seconds she wakes with half her health and flies back to the player.

  • She stays on whichever map she fell on. If the player goes to another map meanwhile, the time she spends down stops with that map: only when you return to the map she fell on does she wake and follow again.
  • When something else keeps her from moving and she cannot keep up (being frozen, say), the outcome is still undefined, and is decided by the world that learns; with no AI connected, she waits where she is and follows again once she can move.

Relationships

The one she follows is the player. Whom she follows is a piece of data she carries, not a hard-coded “the player”.

  • Pell wants to draw her the first time he sees her.
  • The Church Courier, bringing the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage, stares at her for a long time and says nothing.
  • Before the thorns, the Grey Man looks at her for a long time and says nothing.
  • After reading the sun-watch post's log on Cloudtop, Nessa Quill asks her whether she remembers any watchman.

Nothing recorded in the game data.

Dialogue

No lines.

All she says is the story's beats and hints. A few representative moments:

Moment She says
Beside the five-wound shrine stone at Mountain Foot “It's so cold next to it. Five marks, and the first one's cracked... I don't like it. I don't know why I don't like it.”
After your first try at something the world has never seen “Did you see that? It answered! Whatever the world learns is kept in the Threadbook: press J.”
The first time you near the healing hot spring in Firstlight Village “Come back for a soak whenever you're hurt.”
Ilse tells of the lance piercing Astra's heart She starts trembling: “I'm not. It's the cold. Just the cold.”
The watchman's log on Cloudtop “It's so cold in here... Let's go.”
The White Knight falls, and the crown's light flows to her “It came to me! ...It's cold. Am I allowed to keep it?”
Stopped at a place not open yet (east of the Ford) “That way lies east of the Ford, and the road there isn't open yet. Let's come back and explore it another time!” Bumping into it again (a minute or more later): “East of the Ford — another time.”

When you are stopped at a place the story has not opened yet, she says so, in words that depend on what stops you:

  • East of the Ford: a locked place on the world map, the east exit on the far side of the Ford, or the road east off a map's edge.
  • Behind the White Knight: east of the East Road.
  • Places whose tale is not told yet: the Most Holy Hall of the Holy City, for one.
  • The prologue: the way down from Cloudtop, before it opens.
  • Anything else: “We can't go on that way yet. Let's come back and explore it another time!”

She says it at most once a minute for the same place, and shorter the second time. When she is not with you, the lower left says why instead.

Quests

No quests involve this person.

Lumi is the quests' guide: the objectives and hints of each stage of the quest being tracked (by default, the main quest under way) can all be heard again by pressing E on her. In the Tutorial, what she tells you is how to do each stage of the main quest Waking. See Quests.

Once out of the tutorial, she also speaks up on her own to teach you ways of playing you have not learned yet: these are the guide quests (see Quests). She picks her moment and says a line, the quest begins by itself, and the panel's “The Road” page lists it beside the main quest:

Guide When What it teaches
The First Skill Point You have an unspent skill point Press K to open the skill tree and learn a skill
A Road of Your Own You reach level 5 with no profession Go to the drill yard and see Bram to choose a profession
The First Cast The first time you walk up to the Firstlight Village pond Make a fishing rod and go fishing
A Lesson in the Garden The first time you walk into the Firstlight Village vegetable garden Pick up tools, harvest, sow, water (farming)

Boons

She can hold up to five boons, and carries only one at a time. Talking to her (pressing E on her) opens her panel: on the left her portrait, on the right two pages, “The Road” (in her own words, what you should do now, with the main quest's objectives and progress below, and the guide quests under way listed beside them) and “The Seals' Powers” (a slot for each of the five Seals, sealed until you have it). You can look at the powers at any time, but can only change them within 3 cells of an awakened waypoint statue (pressing E on a statue and choosing “Listen” also opens this panel). A boon works only while she and the player are on the same map.

For now there is only one boon: “Crown's Insight”, gained by defeating The White Knight Alberic, which doubles the experience you gain. The moment she catches it, a cold light flies from the kneeling knight to her, she glows and rises, and a banner reading “The First Seal” crosses the screen (see Boons). For all boons, see Boons.

Portrait

Her map sprite and pixel portrait are the project's own art: a four-frame wing-flapping animation, plus a portrait. The dialogue and her panel use her own portrait (see Appearance).

Lore

In the setting, Lumi is not a guide sent by the Church.

Who she is (click to show)

Lumi is what is left of the Second Vessel. Long ago, after Astra, someone tried to bear the Five Sufferings, and could not hold. The Five Sufferings stripped her of her power and her memory, leaving a little fairy who does not know what she once was. When and why that attempt happened is left for later chapters.

She can catch the power that spills from each Seal because what remains of her still answers to the Seals: when a Seal-bearer falls, the power that spills out flows to her “like water finding an old riverbed”. She thinks it is a gift.

Chapter 1 shows only a few corners of this: she trembles beside the cracked wound of the five-wound shrine stone; she does not know how old she is; the watchman on Cloudtop once found a little “sleeping light” in the snow, which woke and asked him how old it was; the Church Courier and the Grey Man both stare at her. Her lines are always light: the sadness is left for the player to find alone, and is never spoken by her. See Main Story and Nightbearer.

Trivia

  • Her name comes from “light” (lumen): when a Seal-bearer's power spills out, it flows to her the way water finds an old channel, and she glows as she catches it. In the ever-longer nights, she is a point of light at the player's side.
  • Before 2026-10-12 she was called “Pip” (小啾). When a save from before then is loaded, the name is changed automatically.
  • Before 2026-10-05 she was a red parrot from the asset pack. When a save from before then is loaded, the parrot becomes her as she is now.
  • The first time the player enters Firstlight Village, Pell asks whether she bites, and she answers: “Draw me? ...All right. But be quick. I'm no good at keeping still.”
  • She also has a card on the people page of the Codex.
  • Before 2026-10-24 she wore a star in her hair and her wings had a touch of pink; her panel showed another portrait, drawn in a different style.
