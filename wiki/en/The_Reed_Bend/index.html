The Reed Bend lies east of the Forest, at the end of the path from the Forest's eastern clearing. The river of the Ford bends here and flows south, taking up a whole strip along the east of the map: shallow water near the bank, deep water farther out. On the bank lives the fisherwoman Hilde; her cabin and jetty are by the shore, and a fire for smoking fish is always burning. South of the jetty a sandbar runs out into the water, where three water slimes guard a bronze ship's bell: nine winters ago Hilde's husband Rolf put out in the fog, and the boat came back by itself, but he did not. North of the jetty, at the water's edge, a sunken stone leans askew, its carvings older than the village.

The Reed Bend is an open-air combat zone; after nightfall night creatures appear. There are fish in the river for fishing.

Map

The map is 40×30. An opening on the west side at rows 16–18 leads to the Forest; a path comes in there, runs northeast to the cabin's door, then east to the jetty. The river flows from north to south, its bank lying between columns 28 and 32 and curving westward in the middle stretch. Along the bank are grass and patches of sand, with reeds growing.

Areas

  • The fisher's home: the cabin, the salt barrel by the door, the nets hung to dry, and the fire for smoking fish (a campfire that is always lit, on which you can cook). The cabin's door leads into Hilde's Cabin.
  • The jetty: three planks running out into the water at row 12. By day Hilde stands at the edge of the jetty fishing. Stand on the jetty and cast east, and you reach deep water.
  • The sandbar: a strip of sand running out into the river south of the jetty (rows 20–22). “Rolf's ship's bell” (a Bell) lies at the very tip of the sandbar, with three water slimes round it. The Bell on the Spit has you bring it back.
  • The sunken stone: a toppled standing stone at the water's edge north of the jetty (row 6); press E to read it: “A standing stone, tilted, the river up to its waist. The carving is worn soft: a boat, and a figure in it handing a fish down to a child on the bank. The five wounds are nowhere on it.”
  • The river: the river to the east. The three tiles nearest the bank are shallow water, and beyond that is deep water all the way to the edge of the map. The far bank is not on this map.

Residents

  • Hildelives here

Hilde is the only person here. From 5:00 to 22:00 she fishes from the jetty, and from 22:00 to 5:00 she sleeps in the cabin.

At the start there are 3 water slimes on the sandbar, which attack on sight; once they are felled, another may come onto the sand every 1800 seconds, up to 2 at a time. Farther south along the bank from the sandbar, 1 giant crab keeps guard, and does not return once felled. After nightfall there are also zombies and bats.

  • Fish: the river's fish come by the hour and the depth, as everywhere else: Sunfish by day, Eels after dark, Pike in deep water, Catfish on rainy days, the odd Golden Carp, and, very rarely, a Dawnscale at dawn. See Fishing. A fishing rod takes just two Sticks and two bundles of Cut Grass to make; Hilde sells bait and fishing rods.
  • Rolf's ship's bell: the Bell on the sandbar, a quest item.
  • Sticks and stones lie on the ground at the start, and afterwards the ground slowly grows sticks, stones and cut grass.

There is no road on the river's east bank. On the Lands of the Five Seals, the Reed Bend occupies the cell of the river itself: it is a camp on the river's west bank and is drawn as a camp, but that cell, like the river, cannot be stood on, and it can only be entered from the Forest — the river remains a barrier that can be crossed only at the Ford.

Lore

The first time you enter the bend, Lumi says: it's the river! It's the Ford's river; it bends round this way; even the water is quiet here.

The Sunken Stone (click to show)

Walk up to the sunken stone and a scene plays: carved on the stone is a boat, and the one aboard is handing a fish down to a child, the carving worn almost smooth. Lumi asks: “Who carved this? It looks older than the village.” Afterwards Hilde says the stone was there before there was a village, “before the Church came and told us what to be sorry for”.

This is the first place in the game that hints at another faith before the Church of the Five Sufferings: one that honored giving, not suffering. See Lore.

Quests

  • Three for the Smoke: give Hilde 3 fish of any kind, in exchange for bait and a cloak.
  • The Bell on the Spit: bring Rolf's ship's bell back to her from the sandbar.
