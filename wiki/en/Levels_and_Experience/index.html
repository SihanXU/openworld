Levels and Experience

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Levels and experience: defeating creatures and completing quests earns experience; when enough experience builds up you go up a level, and every level gives 1 skill point to spend on learning skills on the skill tree. The experience bar sits below the hearts and drumsticks and above the toolbar, showing your current level. Hover over it to see “Level N · Experience current / needed”, along with the number of unspent skill points if you have any.

Earning experience

Source Experience
Defeating a creature or animal Its maximum health, times your experience bonus
Completing a quest The experience stated in the quest's reward, times your experience bonus. See Quests
  • Only the character who lands the killing blow earns experience.
  • Characters that “do not die” (people, Lumi) are not defeated when they collapse, and give no experience.
  • Breaking things, such as felling trees, breaking stone or knocking down walls, gives no experience.
  • For the maximum health of each creature, see Monsters and Animals. For example, a bat has 6, a zombie 14, a wolf 14.

Leveling up

Going from level L to the next takes 20 × L experience. Experience left over carries into the next level.

Level Needed for the next level Total experience from level 1
1 20 0
2 40 20
3 60 60
4 80 120
5 100 200
6 120 300
7 140 420
8 160 560
9 180 720
10 200 900
  • When you level up, “★ Level up! Level N · +1 skill point” appears at the lower left. Gaining a lot of experience at once can raise several levels in a row.
  • There is no level cap.
  • Leveling up adds no health or attack in itself; you grow stronger through skill points and equipment.
  • Unspent skill points show as a red number on the skills button (K) at the lower right.

Experience bonuses

All the experience bonuses you carry are added together and multiplied onto the experience you gain. A negative total counts as 0, so you always get at least the original amount.

Source Bonus
The Boon “Crown's Insight” (while Lumi carries it) +100%
Earring accessories Mostly +10%; the Star-glass Earring from a quest reward gives +20%
Crown of Thorns (worn on the head) −50%: it only cancels other bonuses, never bringing experience below the original

What levels are for

  • Skill points: 1 per level; see Skills. The first time you have an unspent point, Lumi begins guiding you through the quest The First Skill Point.
  • Professions: from level 5 you can see the profession master Bram in Firstlight Village and choose a profession. See Professions. If you reach level 5 without a profession, Lumi begins guiding you through the quest A Road of Your Own, taking you to him.
  • Crafting quality: when you make something, being 3 levels above the recipe's requirement gives Fine, 6 levels above gives Masterwork, and below the requirement you can only make Crude. See Quality, Crafting.
  • Quest thresholds: a few quests can only be taken at a certain level; for example Clear the Hollow needs level 2.

Tips

  • At night in the wild of a combat zone, zombies and bats keep appearing: an early source of experience, and a danger. See Night Creatures.
  • After defeating the first Seal-bearer, remember to have Lumi carry “Crown's Insight” beside an awakened waypoint statue; from then on your experience is doubled. See Boons.

History

  • 2026-10-02: Levels and experience were added: defeating an enemy gives experience equal to its maximum health, and each level needs more experience than the last.
  • 2026-10-03: Every level gives 1 skill point; the skill tree and professions were added.
  • 2026-10-05: Experience bonuses were added: Lumi's “Crown's Insight” and the cost of the Crown of Thorns.
