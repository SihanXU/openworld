Altar

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item
Altar
CategoryShrines
Chinese name祭坛
IDfixture/sacred/altar

An altar is a stone-built altar that things can be put on. Every altar in the game has a name of its own and words carved on it; walk up to one and press E to read it (“Read X”). The planted sword at the center of the Held Ground is not an altar but a thing of its own.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Reading

  • Astra's altar: “ASTRA, THE FIRST MARTYR. He bore the Five, and the sun rose. Suffering is not God's abandonment. It is the proof the debt is being paid.” Sister Ilse keeps the sacred flame every day from 6:00 to 22:00; after the flame is stolen, she sweeps this cold altar every morning. See Astra.

  • The first hearth: “The chapel's first hearth, a ring of stone, cold for centuries. Old soot is still packed deep in its cracks.” On each side of the hearth stands an unlit set of candles.

  • The altar of the five wounds: “Astra on the holy wood, carved in white stone: a crown of thorns, the scourge's marks, the ribs of the fast, the nails, the lance in the side. Below: WHO BEARS, IS NEAR TO GOD.”

The three deep in the barrow and in the Abbey of the Thorn (click to show)
  • The roll of the Order: “THE ORDER OF THE THORN. Forty-one names, the oldest worn smooth. The last, cut deeper than the rest: ALBERIC, KNIGHT-COMMANDER, WHO KNELT TO CHILDREN. TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN. HE HOLDS.”
  • The Commander's vow: “Cut into the altar, in a knight's plain hand: "I WILL HOLD UNTIL I AM RELIEVED. — ALBERIC." Below it, newer, pressed into the soot with a thumb: a mark of ash.”
  • The statue in the thorn yard: “A young knight on one knee before a child, holding out a wooden horse he has mended. The face is worn smooth by hands. The plinth: ALBERIC, ON THE DAY HE WAS MADE A KNIGHT.”

Putting things on it

An altar is a surface: hold something and press G on it, and the thing is set on the altar (“You set X on the altar.”). Nothing else happens when you do.

Unbreakable, and it does not burn

An altar is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken. Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-09-30: The first version already had the altar.
  • 2026-10-06: Astra's altar was raised beside the sacred flame in Firstlight Village.
  • 2026-10-07: The Ossuary of the Thorn, the Warden's Sanctum, the Chapel of the First Dawn and the Held Ground each got an inscribed altar.
  • 2026-10-11: The center of the Held Ground went back to being the planted sword, no longer an altar; the altar in older saves turns back into the sword.
  • 2026-10-12: The Chapel of the Five at the Pilgrims' Rest and the Abbey of the Thorn each got an altar.
