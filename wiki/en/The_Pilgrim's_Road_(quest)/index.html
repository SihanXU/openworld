The Pilgrim's Road is a Chapter 1 side quest given by Sister Ilse. After the courier from the Holy City names you the Pilgrim, Ilse tells you to ask those who know the road before you set out: hear Fennick on the knight, ask Joss about the bridge, and ask Hesper what she saw.

It can only be taken after the Church's letter (see The First Seal). After the letter, the main quest The First Seal gains an optional objective, “Ask Sister Ilse where the pilgrimage begins”, which is completed when you accept this quest. Once the White Knight has fallen, Ilse no longer asks this of you.

Objectives

    Talk to Fennick
  2. Ask Joss about the bridge
    Talk to Joss
  3. Ask Hesper what she saw
    Talk to Hesper
  4. Back to Sister Ilse
    Talk to Sister Ilse

Each of the three counts as asked once they have said their piece, in any order. While the quest is under way, those not yet asked have a “?” over their heads, and the map markers point at them. Once all three are asked, the journal reads “All done. Back to Sister Ilse.”, and the objective “Back to Sister Ilse” appears.

Walkthrough

  1. Take the quest: after the letter, Sister Ilse is sweeping the altar by the cold sacred flame on the square by day (6:00–22:00), with a “!” over her head. Press E on her: she tells of the Five Sufferings and the Seal-bearers, and tells you to ask those who know the road; choose “Accept ‘The Pilgrim's Road’” in the dialogue box.
  2. Fennick: on the bench on the south side of the square (7:00–22:00). Press E on him and he tells you the White Knight's moves: “When he drives his sword into the ground, thorns grow up in a ring around him. Stand inside the ring, or well away from it — just not on it. When he draws his bow, don't stand straight in front of him.” Later, a scene plays in the village of him remembering Alberic.
  3. Joss: by the workbench on the west side of the village (6:00–21:00). He tells you about mending the bridge: three planks will close the gap, or think of some other way across.
  4. Hesper: at The Ford. She tells you what the rider looked like. Later, a scene plays at the Ford of her showing you the bridge posts.
  5. Hand in: go back to Ilse and press E to hand in. Handing in does not advance the main line: the river objective belongs to The First Seal, and only counts once you stand on the far bank.

If you defeat the White Knight first

Once the White Knight falls, this quest is treated as “passed” and ends. If by then you had asked all three, it still completes and pays its rewards (the journal reads “The road went on, and this was done along the way.”); if not, nothing is paid (the journal reads “The road has gone past this.”). If you have not taken it yet, Ilse will not ask it of you any more.

Dialogue

When offering
“The Church teaches that Astra bore the Five Sufferings so that the sun would rise again, and that five Seal-bearers carry them still. The nights are growing longer, and the Church says the Seal-bearers are failing. The first of them holds the road beyond our Ford. Before you go, speak with those who know that road: Fennick, Joss and Hesper.”
While unfinished
“Fennick by the bench, Joss at his workbench, Hesper at Vesper Ford. Each of them knows a piece of the road.”
When talking to Fennick
“Alberic. When he plants his sword in the ground, thorns come up in a ring round him. Stand inside the ring, or well outside it, but never on it. And when he draws his bow, don't be in front of him.”
When talking to Joss
“The gap is three planks wide. Fell a tree with an axe, split the logs into planks, and lay them on the water where the boards were. Or find some other way across.”
When talking to Hesper
“White, all of him. A crown of thorns grown through his helm. He pulled my bridge up board by board and laid the nails out on the pier, like a toll paid. He never looked at me once.”
On handing in
“Then go with the blessing of the Five. Suffering is not God's abandonment; remember that, out there. And come back, so that I may hear your step again.”
Fennick's and Hesper's memories (click to show)
  • Fennick: as a young man he carried Alberic's lance for six years. By then Alberic had worn that crown for two hundred and fifty years, yet he would still halt the whole column to kneel on one knee and talk to children. “But the crown kept taking. Every year I was at his side, there was a little less of him.” One morning he looked at Fennick and asked his name. Fennick left that night. “Pilgrim, if he asks your name — tell him. Someone ought to tell him.”
  • Hesper: the knight drove thorns into the bridge posts where the planks used to be, as if marking something as his own. That bridge was built by Aldo and Joss, and the year after it was finished, the river took Aldo. “Mend it. Then go and tell that knight whose bridge it is.”

History

  • 2026-10-06: Added, as a main quest at the time: ask the three people before setting out.
  • 2026-10-07: Became a side quest; the Amulet of the Five Wounds was added to the rewards. After the letter you can cross the river and advance the main line without handing this quest in.
  • 2026-10-08: Handing in after crossing the river no longer sends the main objective back to “mend the broken bridge”.
  • 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system: accept or decline in the dialogue box; the journal records every step; handing in no longer advances the main line, whose river objective is in The First Seal.
  • 2026-10-13: Fennick's memory was rewritten: when he was a squire, Alberic had already worn the crown for two hundred and fifty years.
