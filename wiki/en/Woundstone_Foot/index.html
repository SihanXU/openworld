Mountain Foot lies south of Cloudtop and north of the Ford. The path down the mountain comes from the upland in the north along a gentle slope, passes an ancient five-wound shrine stone in a ring of flowers, and goes on through the woods to the cliff steps north of the Ford.

The shrine stone is something the game leaves on purpose as “not yet learned by the world”: whatever you do to it, the outcome is undefined, and the world learns to answer. Here the prologue first teaches you this.

Map

The north of the map is upland two levels higher than the rest, edged by a cliff; only the two tiles the path crosses are stairs cut into the rock; the cliff is two levels high, so you can neither jump down it anywhere else nor climb it (see Terrain). Woods lie all round. The path comes down from the north exit (three tiles wide), passes the shrine stone, and leaves south by the two-tile exit on the map's south edge.

Areas

  • The shrine stone and the flowers: a broad bed of flowers in the middle of the map, with a five-wound shrine stone standing in it, inside an old close: its walls fallen to their last runs, its gate two columns where the path comes off the slope, broken columns and a saint toppled off its plinth within, a tower's stump to the east. Walk into this area and Lumi says it's cold near the stone and asks you to do something to it. On the ground beside the stone is a chalk drawing of a stone (one of Pell's Drawings; press E to look at it).
  • Waypoint statue: southeast of the shrine stone stands a waypoint statue (named “Mountain Foot statue”).
  • The hunter's lodge: a long-abandoned wooden cabin to the west, wood-walled and wood-floored, its door long gone. On the floor inside lies a pair of Hunter's Gloves.
  • Signpost: by the path to the south stands a sign reading “The Ford ↓”.
  • The cliff steps: the south exit joins the stone steps on the cliff north of the Ford.

Lore

The five marks on the shrine stone (click to show)

The shrine stone is cut with five wound-marks, standing for the Five Sufferings. Lumi says the first (the Crown of Thorns) is cracked. In Pell's chalk drawing this mark is colored black, and a grey figure has his hand on it. As Chapter 1 is designed, this is the Grey Man checking how far the First Seal has decayed, though the villagers take it for a heretic desecrating a holy thing (see Chapter 1).

In the prologue, before the shrine stone, Lumi first asks you to “try something the world has never seen”. Once you have, she says that what the world learns is kept in the Threadbook (J).
