From the OpenWorld Wiki · Lore
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

This page lists the events of the lore in order. The original lore text mostly gives no specific years, so only the times stated in the original text and in the game's text are given here; anything uncertain is marked “unknown”, and anything not yet decided “to be designed”. Events involving the reversals are in spoiler blocks.

Documents in the game are dated by the Era of the Five Sufferings (the old Proclamation of the Pilgrimage in the forest is dated “the 380th year of the Era of the Five Sufferings”); from what year the era is counted is not stated. The kneeling stones of the Kneeling Field use another reckoning, the years of the Crown, counted from when the first Seal-bearer, Alberic, the White Knight, put on the Crown of Thorns; he has held for three hundred years. The count of the years of the Crown and the few dates below tied to it are proposals of Chapter 1's design (docs/chapter-1.md §14); the original lore text says only that the Seal-bearers have borne it for “several hundred years”.

Ancient times

When Event Source
Before the world was formed An older darkness already exists: the First Night. §2
Creation (the Church's account) God did not create light; he only separated light from the First Night. The sun is the boundary that keeps them apart. §2

The first Long Night

When Event Source
Several hundred years ago The First Night seeps into reality: the sun gradually vanishes and the days grow shorter and shorter until the world sinks into a long night. Crops wither, beasts turn strange, and the dead find no rest. This is the first Long Night, called “the First Long Night” in the game. §1, §2
Before or during the First Long Night An almanac is written, which Tobin later sold to Nessa (Almanac of the First Night). Game text; docs/chapter-1.md §7
During the first Long Night People try war, magic, sacrifice and prayer, and none of it stops it. §3
At the worst of the first Long Night Astra appears and bears the Five Sufferings one after another: the Crowning, the Scourging, the Starving, the Nailing, the Piercing. §1, §4
When the fifth Suffering is complete The holy lance pierces Astra's chest, the sun rises again, and the Long Night ends. §4
The first dawn after the First Long Night At the fire altar on top of Dawn Hill, people light the First Fire from the dawn light and carry it down the hill; that morning the chapel bell is rung. Firstlight Village is named for this dawn. Game text (the Chapel of the First Dawn, the dawnstone, The Vigil); docs/chapter-1.md §1
Afterward Astra's followers found the Church of the Five Sufferings, and the five instruments of torment are enshrined as its highest relics. §5

The Church's centuries

When Event Source
Unknown Astra alone can hardly bear the whole strain, and the Church sets up the system of Seal-bearers, dividing the power of the five holy instruments among five Seal-bearers. §6
About three hundred years ago (the first year of the Crown) Alberic, Knight-Commander of the Order of the Thorn, is chosen at the Abbey of the Thorn to wear the Crown of Thorns and becomes the first Seal-bearer, on the promise that someone will relieve him after forty years. His journal: “The Prior says it is an honour. My brother Hugh says it is a sentence. ... I can hold for forty years.” After him, the order never makes another knight. Game text (the journal and the roll in the Chapter Hall); docs/chapter-1.md §14
The 40th year of the Crown The Holy See's Writ of Relief should have reached him this year. The abbey's Prior, unable to find a successor willing to bear it, holds the writ back and never sends it: “No one could be found who would bear it. Let him go on holding.” Game text (the Writ of Relief); docs/chapter-1.md §14
The 41st year of the Crown Alberic's journal: “The forty-first year. No one has come. ... Today I could not remember Hugh's face.” The last page holds only “Hold.” Game text (the journal)
Unknown The Order of the Thorn buries its dead beneath Thornwood Hollow. The roll holds forty-one names, the last being Knight-Commander Alberic, “TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN.” Game text (the Ossuary of the Thorn)
About a hundred years ago Brother Tomas of the Thorn, the Knight-Commander's oath-brother, is buried as the barrow's warden, charged with watching over the order's dead beneath Thornwood Hollow: “Warden of this house until the night comes back.” He is not Alberic's brother by blood (that was Hugh). Game text (the niche in the Ossuary of the Thorn, the memorial at the Abbey of the Thorn); docs/chapter-1.md §3, §14
From the 212th year of the Crown The pilgrims the Holy See sends go east one after another and do not come back. The oldest kneeling stone on the Kneeling Field is carved: “Aelis of Varrow, pilgrim. She would not kneel. The 212th year of the Crown.” Later stones are carved with the 240th, 251st, 263rd and 270th years. In the book at the Pilgrims' Hostel, beside many names is written “Went east. Did not come back.” Game text (the Kneeling Field, the Pilgrims' Hostel); docs/chapter-1.md §14
About the 250th year of the Crown As a young man, Fennick serves Alberic as squire for six years, when Alberic has already worn the crown for two hundred and fifty years. The crown keeps taking: one morning he asks Fennick his name, and Fennick leaves that night. Game text (Fennick's memory)
The 380th year of the Era of the Five Sufferings Brother Cassian of Firstlight Village is named pilgrim, makes camp deep in the Forest, and never comes back. Ilse says this was thirty-one years ago, when she was still a novice. Game text (The Pilgrim Before)
Thirty-one years ago Hesper begins keeping the Ford. Game text
Twenty years ago Aldo and Joss build the footbridge at the Ford. It is paid for by a man in grey, a boy at his heel carrying the purse, with old coins that bear no king's head. Game text
The spring after the footbridge is built The river takes Aldo. Game text (Hesper)
Ten years ago The Church dissolves the Ashen Watch and declares its last few brothers heretics. docs/chapter-1.md §5
Year 401 to year 411 The Sunwatch Post on Cloudtop records the midsummer sunset: 19:42 in year 401, 19:31 in year 405, 19:12 in year 409. In year 411, the reports go down the mountain and nothing comes back. Game text (The Watchman's Log)

Before the game begins

When Event Source
Recent years The sunset begins to come a few minutes early, and then ever more plainly: dusk earlier, dawn later, the days shorter every month, more monsters at night, and the dead find no rest. §7
Recent years Nessa Quill records the sunset times and finds the curve exactly matches the old almanac's. Game text
Last winter Tobin sells an amulet of frost to a man in grey, who pays in old coins. Game text
Not long ago Pell sees the Grey Man three times and is given a stone that grows warm at night. Game text
Not long ago A rider in white tears down the footbridge at the Ford in a single night and lays its nails out on the jetty. Along the East Road he takes everything that shines, including the ring of her late husband that Edda had hung on her doorpost. Game text
Not long ago The days are too short and Edda's wheat dies; the things of the night come; on the night her barn burns, a man in grey pulls her out of the smoke. Game text
Shortly before the game begins A storm in the night snaps the Ford's ferry rope. Lina and Oskar take shelter in the Old Barrow and are trapped inside; a man in grey pulls the inner door shut from outside and pushes a piece of bread through the gap. Game text
Before the game begins The Church of the Five Sufferings declares that the five Seal-bearers can no longer go on bearing the Five Sufferings: the five Seals are failing, and the Second Long Night will come. §7

During the game

When Event Source
Day 1, 13:00 The protagonist wakes on Cloudtop with Lumi at their side. See Prologue. Game code
Chapter 1 Lina and Oskar are rescued; on the night of thanks the Grey Man takes the First Fire; the next morning the courier brings the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage; the protagonist crosses the river, takes the oath at the Pilgrims' Rest, walks the Pilgrim's Road and the Kneeling Field, defeats the first Seal-bearer, Alberic, the White Knight, on the East Road, and wins the Crown of Thorns. See Chapter 1. docs/chapter-1.md §6, §11, §14
After the White Knight falls The nights come earlier: from then on night falls at 20:00 and day breaks at 05:30 (previously 21:00 and 05:00). Nessa says the sun set four minutes early. Game code
After Chapter 1 What follows in the game is the older storyline Emberfall and the Dragon, to be replaced later by the chapter of the second Seal. docs/main-quest.md
Chapters 2 to 5 The other four Seal-bearers are faced in turn. To be designed. §6, §7
The second half of the main story and the ending (click to show)
When Event Source
Mid-game The protagonist comes to believe Astra is a monster and hunts him together with the Grey Man; during the hunt they discover the second reversal. “Agreed additions”
Final Chapter The protagonist carries the five holy instruments into the Most Holy Hall. The instruments return to their places, Astra opens his eyes, and he fights the protagonist. §14, §15
Ending Astra dies, and the First Night runs out of control. The protagonist inherits the Five Sufferings and becomes the new Nightbearer and “the Second First Martyr”. The sun rises in full, and people celebrate that the Second Long Night was stopped. §16, §17
