The Holy City

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Lore
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The Holy City is the center of the Church of the Five Sufferings. The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage is issued “by order of the Holy City”; once the pilgrim has recovered the five Instruments of the Sufferings, they must “finally travel to the Holy City and hold again the Great Rite of the Five Sufferings” (§7).

In the game

The Holy City is the region at the very center of the Lands of the Five Seals (since 2026-10-18): the Most Holy Hall stands in the middle cell, the eight cells around it are the Holy City's eight quarters, and beyond them lie pilgrims' meadows and fields. Like the four other Seal-bearers' regions, it is not yet open: you can walk into the quarters, but no one lives there yet and there is no story; the Most Holy Hall is locked, with the message “The way in is shut. Its tale is not yet told.”

The Holy City also appears in these texts:

  • The Proclamation of the Pilgrimage: the first line the Church Courier reads out is “By order of the Holy City: the Seal-bearers weaken, and the Long Night returns. Let the pilgrimage be called.” See Proclamation of the Pilgrimage.
  • The almanac: Nessa Quill introduces herself with “I am Nessa Quill; I make the almanacs for the Holy City.”
  • The roll of the Order of the Thorn: the roll in the Ossuary of the Thorn says the White Knight was “TAKEN UP BY THE HOLY CITY TO BEAR THE CROWN.”
  • The previous pilgrim: when the quest The Pilgrim Before is completed, Ilse says the Church told Firstlight Village that Brother Cassian “reached the Holy City.” Yet his camp is deep in the Forest, and the quest says he “never came back.”
  • The quest hint “The Holy City has issued the Proclamation of the Pilgrimage” (Chapter 1, after the courier has come).
The Holy City and the Seal-bearers' power (click to show)
  • In the ruins the protagonist discovers many structures underground that carry power toward the Holy City, and that the pain the five Seal-bearers bear all ends up flowing to the same place (§9).
  • The Grey Man believes that the Church, by sending the Seals' power to the Holy City, is waking Astra; he gathers the power that scatters from the Seals precisely so that it never reaches the Holy City (docs/chapter-1.md §5).
