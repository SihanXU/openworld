Tent

Tent
CategoryFixtures
Chinese name帐篷
IDfixture/tent

A tent is a cloth tent, 3×3 tiles in size. It is solid, blocking the way and also the view. There are three in the game at the start: Hesper's tent at the camp at The Ford, the “rotted tent” left by the pilgrim before deep in The Forest, and “Alberic's tent” in The Held Ground.

All three can be entered: press E facing the tent (“Enter X”) to duck inside, where there is a small room (The Ferry Camp Tent, Inside the Rotted Tent, Inside Alberic's Tent); walk down out of the tent's mouth from inside and you are back in front of the tent.

Once the residents' tent, the second and the third residents' tents are built in Expanding the Held Ground, the Held Ground's plots become new tents, each with two beds in front, for the people who come to live there (see Held Ground Residents). These tents have no room and cannot be entered.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Vesper Ford
  • The Ford's camp: the tent of the ferrywoman Hesper. On stormy days she keeps to the tent's doorway during the day instead of going to the dock.
  • The Forest: in a clearing deep in the southeast, beside the “rotted tent” are a ring of cold fire, a Proclamation nailed to a tree and a cairn of stones. Once you walk into this camp, you can get the side quest The Pilgrim Before from Sister Ilse.
  • The Held Ground: northwest of the planted sword, “Alberic's tent”. Alberic sleeps in front of the tent at night.
  • Cannot be made.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

  • Blocks the way: a tent takes up 3×3 tiles, and people and creatures walk round it.
  • Blocks the view: what happens behind a tent cannot be seen by people on its other side. For example, taking something in front of its owner gets you scolded, but behind a tent you are not seen (see Residents).
  • Not a room: a tent itself does not count as a room, and does not keep off rain; the three that can be entered hold another small map inside.
  • G: press G on it with something in hand to put the thing on the tent's tile.
  • Hitting: a tent has no durability; what happens if you hit it is not written in the rules.
  • Fire: a tent is cloth and can catch fire; after burning for about 4.5 seconds it becomes a portion of Ash, and the tent is gone.

What happens when you fiddle with it bare-handed with E is not written in the rules (the label shows “?”). Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History
