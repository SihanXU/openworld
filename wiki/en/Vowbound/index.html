Vowbound

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

“Vowbound — left by one who swore a vow. It still remembers.”

— Item tooltip

Vowbound is one of the item qualities, above Masterwork and below Stigmatic; its names and tooltips are purple. Vowbound items are things left by people who swore vows: the dead of the Order of the Thorn, the pilgrims who came before. They can only be found, never made yourself (the 4% of Legendary Smith is the one exception).

Rules

  • Multiplier: power, attack and defense are counted at ×1.75 (Common is ×1, Masterwork ×1.5), then multiplied by the factor of the level. See Quality.
  • Minor affixes: like other gear, every piece of Vowbound gear rolls 5 minor affixes when it is made (Masterwork also gets 5, Common 3). The named pieces roll them too, so at the same level a Vowbound piece is always better than a Common one.
  • Vow: every piece of Vowbound gear also carries a vow, a special effect with a condition and a reward, written in purple in the tooltip as “✦ Vow name · Vow” followed by a line of description. Randomly rolled Vowbound gear carries one of the ten below at random; the pieces placed in the game have vows chosen on purpose. The same vow worn on two pieces does not stack; different vows do. A vow works wherever it is worn on the body, and a weapon held in the hand counts too.

The ten vows

VowEffect
Vow of VigilAt night: +20% critical chance and +15% movement speed.
Vow of DawnBy day: every blow that lands draws back 1 health, and you take 10% less harm.
Vow of the Held GroundStanding your ground (no step this second): your blows deal 25% more, and you take 30% less.
Vow of the ThornWhoever strikes you hand to hand is pricked for 3.
Vow of MercyA blow on a foe below a third of its health draws back 2 health.
Vow of SilenceSneaking slows you no more, and a blow struck while sneaking has +25% critical chance.
Vow of the RoadOn a road, +30% movement speed; on the move, +10% dodge.
Vow of the ShieldA fight's first blow against you is turned half aside (ten seconds out of the fray).
Vow of the HuntYour blow on an unhurt foe is always a critical.
Vow of the Lone RoadWith no companion at your side: your blows deal 15% more, and +10% dodge.
  • The Vow of Vigil and the Vow of Dawn are night and day: one who keeps vigil is surer and faster at night; one sworn to the dawn heals with every blow by day and takes less harm.
  • The Vow of the Held Ground asks you to stand still for a full second (a single step starts the count over), and suits shields and heavy armour; the Vow of the Road is the opposite: quicker on the road, and harder to hit while moving.
  • The Vow of the Thorn is the only one that hurts back: an enemy that hits you in melee is pricked and loses 3 health (its defense reduces it as usual). The prick is never reflected again by another Thorn.
  • The Vow of Mercy and the Vow of the Hunt look at the enemy's health: Mercy heals you when an enemy is near death, the Hunt makes every blow against an enemy at full health a critical hit.
  • The Vow of Silence goes with sneaking (V): sneaking no longer slows you, and blows struck while sneaking get +25% critical chance.
  • The Vow of the Shield governs the first blow of a fight: after 10 seconds without striking or being struck, the next damage you take is halved.
  • The Vow of the Lone Road: while no companion is following you, +15% attack and +10% dodge.

Each also has 5 random minor affixes on top (the named pieces get the same ones in every new game).

The first three belong to the Order of the Thorn, buried under Thornwood Hollow: Odo “held the gate at Varn three days. He did not kneel” (hence the Vow of the Held Ground), Maren “carried water to both armies” (Mercy), and Tomas was the Commander's oath-brother in the Order, “warden of this house until the night comes back” (Vigil). Fennick's Squire Helm is the old knight's own old helm: “It stopped worse than claws, once.” (the Shield). The scallop and the prayer cord belonged to Brother Cassian, the pilgrim of thirty-one years ago (the Road, Dawn). The seal is engraved “WE HOLD, THAT OTHERS NEED NOT”, inside a ring of thorns (the Thorn). The Oath-Thorn comes from the White Knight: he swore “I will hold until someone comes to relieve me”; once relieved, he uses this thorn to keep a piece of land for the one who relieved him.

Old saves

Vowbound gear got before 2026-10-13 had no vow, the named pieces had no minor affixes, and the numbers were the old handwritten ones. Loading a save fills these in automatically: named pieces get the vows in the table above, unnamed ones a random vow; main stats are raised to what Vowbound quality should have (only ever raised, never lowered); pieces with no affixes roll 5.

History

  • 2026-10-07: Added the Vowbound quality, given to the Order's relics in Chapter 1, the previous pilgrim's scallop and several quest rewards.
  • 2026-10-13: The random small traits of Vowbound gear (+5% critical, +6% dodge and the like) were replaced by the ten vows; named Vowbound gear also rolls minor affixes; old saves are filled in automatically.
  • 2026-10-13: Tomas was made the Commander's oath-brother, not his brother by blood.
