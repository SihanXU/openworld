The Wild

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Location
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

The Wild is the land cells of the Lands of the Five Seals that hold no story location. Each is a 40×30 map, shaped by that cell's ground and the world seed. In a given world, the same cell is the same map every time you go in; only the places the player has changed inside it are remembered.

Frostfang Coast has five kinds of wild, one for each of its five walkable grounds; the other regions' grounds each have their own wilds, and the same ground may also differ from region to region (see Wilds of the Other Regions below). On the world map, the location markers bear names like “The wild plains” and “The wild forest”; once you walk in, the map itself is called “Open Plains”, “Deep Woods” and so on.

Map

Wild maps have no fixed layout, so there is no map template. Every one is 40×30, open on all four sides with no exit tiles: walk off any edge and you are back on that cell of the world map. Within a radius of 3 tiles of the map's center no trees grow and no rocks are placed, so there is always open ground under your feet when you walk in from the world map.

The Wild is an open-air combat zone. After nightfall night creatures appear (at most 4).

Areas

The five kinds of wild each have their own base ground, patches and plant life:

Deep Woods The wild forest grass 2 patches of flowers tree 20%, pine tree 6%, bush 4%
Rolling Hills The wild hills grass 5 patches of dry grass, 2 of gravel rock 5%, bush 2%, tree 1.5%
Snowfield The wild snowfield snow 1 patch of ice pine tree 8%, rock 2%
The Road The wild road grass 2 patches of flowers tree 2%, bush 1%

Through the middle of the road wild runs a dirt road two tiles wide, from the west edge all the way to the east, bending at random.

The same ground in other regions: in the Pale Fens the forest is the Grey Woods (charred trees among the living, ghosts, bats) and the plains are the Fen Meadow (mud and pools, zombies, water slimes); in the White Altar Heights the plains are the Stony Pasture and the forest is the Highland Pines; in the Holy City the plains are the Pilgrim Meadows (flowers and fields, chickens, cows); in the Scarred March the hills are the Red Hills (dirt and gravel, wolves ×2); in the Vale of Scales the plains are the Thin Grassland.

Residents

No one lives in the Wild.

Creatures

The creatures on the map from the start, by kind:

Map name Creatures
Open Plains green slime ×2, chicken ×1
Deep Woods boar ×1, green slime ×1
Rolling Hills wolf ×1, green slime ×1
Snowfield frost wolf ×1, frost slime ×1
The Road green slime ×1

Chickens fight back only when struck; every other creature gives chase as soon as it sees someone (sight 4 tiles). Note that the boars of the Wild differ from those of the East Road: the East Road's boars fight back only when struck, while the Wild's attack on sight. These creatures do not come back once felled; the zombies and bats of the night appear as usual and crumble to dust at daybreak.

Items and Resources

What lies on the ground from the start:

Map name Items
Open Plains Berries ×3, Stick ×3, Stone ×2
Deep Woods Mushroom ×4, Stick ×4
Rolling Hills Stone ×4, Herb ×2
Snowfield Stone ×2, Stick ×2

Besides this, the ground of every wild slowly grows sticks, stones and Cut Grass: a chance of one more of each every 15 seconds, up to 4 sticks, 3 stones and 4 bundles of cut grass. Trees, pine trees, bushes and rocks can all be felled or broken (see Woodcutting, Mining, Durability and Breaking), and stumps grow back into full trees after 2 game days.

Connected Locations

The Wild connects only to the Lands of the Five Seals: walk onto a wild cell on the world map and press E to enter; walk off any edge of the wild and you are back on the world map. There are no direct exits between wilds, or between a wild and a story map.

Lore

The Wild is part of the world model's “two-tier map”: everywhere outside the story locations is wild, with the aim of building the world up to hundreds of maps (see Design Pillars, Roadmap). For now they hold no story content.

History

  • 2026-10-04: The two-tier map is made; the empty cells of the world map become wilds, of five grounds.
  • 2026-10-04: The wilds' ground yield (sticks, stones, cut grass) now goes through the shared “appearing” mechanism.
