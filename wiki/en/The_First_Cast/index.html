The First Cast is one of Lumi's guide quests, teaching fishing. The first time you come to the pond south of the square in Firstlight Village (the pool of water east of the hot spring), she speaks up.

Objectives

  1. Get a fishing rod (C: two sticks and two tufts of grass; or the general store)
    Carry Fishing Rod
  2. Catch a fish in the pond (hold the rod, left click the water)
    Carry Fish

Walkthrough

  1. Start: in a proper game, step onto the bank of the pond in Firstlight Village, Lumi speaks, and the quest begins by itself; when you track it, the marker points at the pond.
  2. The fishing rod: a Fishing Rod can be made by hand — press C, 2 Stick and 2 Cut Grass; the general store in Firstlight Village sells them too. As soon as you have a rod, the objective is complete and Lumi says another line.
  3. Fish: stand on the bank with the rod in hand and left-click the water (any tile of water within 3 tiles). Sometimes a fish bites, sometimes there is nothing; each cast muddies the water, so wait for the surface to settle before the next one. With Bait on (hold the bait and right-click the rod) the chance of a bite doubles. What you catch depends on the hour and the weather; see Fishing.
  4. Complete: once there is any fish in your bag, the quest is complete, and Lumi gives you 3 bait.

Dialogue

Stage 10
“Look, a pond! Something just jumped. Make a rod from two sticks and two tufts of grass; you can do it by hand (C). Then stand on the bank, hold it and left click the water.”
Stage 20
“You have a rod! Now to the water. Hold it and left click the pond; if nothing bites, cast again when the ripples settle. Bait on the hook makes them bite twice as fast: hold the bait and right click the rod.”
On handing in
“You caught one! Cook it on a fire, or eat it as it is. What bites depends on the hour and the weather: eels at night, catfish in the rain. Here, some bait for next time.”
