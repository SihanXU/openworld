Forge

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.
Forge
CategoryWorkstations
Chinese name熔炉
IDfixture/craft/forge

A forge is a stone-built furnace, where the first step of smithing is done: press E on it to open the forge's own panel, and smelt ore and coal into ingots there. There are three in the game, all lit from the start: Haldor's forge in the smithy of Firstlight Village, Alberic's forge east of the sword on the Held Ground, and the forge in front of the smithy in Emberfall. Holding Iron Ore or Gold Ore, right-click it, and one ore melts straight into one Iron Ingot or Gold Ingot; it is also a cooking fixture: right-click it holding raw food to cook it (see Cooking). The three smiths (Haldor, Alberic and Garrick) work at the anvil beside it, and can only keep working while the forge is lit.

Source

  • Placed on maps: The Smithy, Emberfall, The Held Ground
  • One each: in the smithy by the East Road in Firstlight Village (named “Haldor's forge”), east of the sword on the Held Ground (“Alberic's forge”), and in front of the smithy in Emberfall.
  • You cannot make this kind of forge; once you learn Forge Builder, you can make a smith's forge that can be moved, from stone and coal.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Smelting

  • Holding Iron Ore or Gold Ore, right-click a lit forge (“Smelt Iron Ore in the forge”): “The ore glows in the forge and runs into a bar.” The one ore in your hand becomes one Iron Ingot or Gold Ingot, and the forge puffs out smoke.
  • One at a time, with no fuel, no skill and no waiting.
  • Smelting in batches: press E on the forge (“Smelt at the forge”) to open the forge panel: 2 iron ore and 1 coal make 2 iron ingots; with Forge Builder a smith's anvil is made here from 3 iron ingots; learn Goldsmith to smelt gold ingots, and Steelworking to smelt Steel Bar. For details see Smelting.
  • When the forge has been put out, clicking it with ore only gives the message “The forge is cold. It wants a fire first.”, and the ore stays as it is. Light it first (see Lighting and putting out below).
  • Only the right click smelts: holding ore and pressing G on the forge only puts the ore down on the forge's tile (“Set Iron Ore down by the forge”).
  • Other fires cannot smelt ore: the rules do not say what happens when you click a campfire, a brazier or a cauldron with ore (the prompt shows “?”).
  • The ingots you smelt are made into gear at an anvil (see Smithing); but Garrick's Dragonsbane and expanding the Held Ground want the ore itself, so keep enough back before smelting.

The panel

  • Press E on the forge, lit or cold, and the forge panel opens: what is made in a forge (the smelting recipes, the smith's anvil), from what you carry; you need not stand beside it while you make. Closing the panel and walking off is fine.
  • To warm yourself, go to a campfire or brazier: E on the forge opens its panel.

Cooking and burning things

  • Holding raw food, right-click a lit forge and the food is cooked (“Cook X over the forge”); pressing G on it does the same.
  • Hold something that burns and press G, and the thing goes into the fire and burns to Ash.

The smiths' fire

  • All three smiths need the forge lit while they work at the anvil: Haldor every day 6:00–21:00, Alberic 6:00–14:00, Garrick 6:00–21:00.
  • After the forge has been put out, Garrick lights it again himself after a while (“Out again. Pass me the flint.”); he does not light it while it is wet.
  • Dragonsbane is obtained by handing Garrick 5 iron ore and 3 coal and then talking to him; in the rules, forging the sword does not use the forge.

Lighting and putting out

  • Putting out: holding a container full of water, left-click it. Rain does not put out a lit forge.
  • Lighting: holding a Torch, left-click it, or hit it with the fire element. Once you learn Firemaker, an ordinary stick works too.

Other

  • Kitchen and workshop: the forge is both a cooking fixture and a workstation, so a room with a forge in it counts as both a kitchen and a workshop (no effect at present).
  • Monsters keep away: except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit.
  • The forge is stone and has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it; it does not catch fire.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-09-30: The first version already had the forge.
  • 2026-10-03: A lit forge was placed in front of Emberfall's smithy, with Garrick working beside it.
  • 2026-10-09: A lit forge can smelt iron ore and gold ore into iron and gold ingots; pressing E empty-handed lets you warm yourself beside it. Added the batch smelting recipes (see Smithing).
  • 2026-10-11: Firstlight Village's smithy and the Held Ground each got a lit forge.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
  • 2026-10-21: Smelting no longer asks you to stand beside the forge and press C: E on the forge opens the forge panel. Warming your hands at the forge went with it.
