Candles

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Item
Candles
CategoryLight sources
Chinese name烛台
IDfixture/light/candles

Candles are a candlestand set with candles. One burns north of the entrance to the Mill Cellar; in the Chapel of the First Dawn there is one on each side of the hearth below the north wall, unlit. Lit candles, like any other fire, keep monsters from coming close.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Firstlight Chapel ×2, Longsleep Barrow, The Ossuary of the Thorn, The Warden's Sanctum ×2, The Mill, The Mill Cellar, Chapel of the First Dawn ×2, Chapel of the Five ×2, The Ferry Camp Tent
  • 1 in the Mill Cellar (lit), 2 in the Chapel of the First Dawn (unlit).
  • You cannot make them.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Lighting and putting out

  • Lighting: holding a Torch, left-click them, or hit them with the fire element. Once you learn Firemaker, right-clicking them with an ordinary stick lights them too (“You work the stick until the candles catch.”). If they are wet, the first touch of fire only dries them.
  • Putting out: holding a container full of water, left-click them.
  • Once lit they stay lit; the candles never burn down.

Monsters keep away

Except for fire monsters, monsters will not come within 2 tiles of something lit, nor chase someone standing there. The slimes and spiders in the Mill Cellar behave this way too. At night, people and creatures within 3 tiles of a lit fire see as far as by day.

Warming yourself

Press E empty-handed on lit candles (“Warm yourself at the candles”): if you are wet, the damp is dried off (“You warm yourself at the candles; the damp steams off you.”); if not, just “Warmth from the candles seeps into your hands.”, with no health restored. The rules do not say what happens when you press E empty-handed on unlit candles (such as the two in the Chapel of the First Dawn; the prompt shows “?”). See Warming yourself.

Throwing things in

Hold something that burns and press G on lit candles, and the thing goes into the fire and burns to Ash. Candles are not a cooking fixture and cannot cook anything: holding raw food and pressing G on them only puts the food down on their tile, still raw; the rules do not say what happens when you right-click them holding raw food. For cooking, see Cooking.

Unbreakable, and they do not burn

Candles have no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit them; the candlestand itself does not catch fire.

Do something else to them and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-09-30: The first version already had candles.
  • 2026-10-07: Candles were placed in the Mill Cellar and the Chapel of the First Dawn.
  • 2026-10-08: Raw food put on lit candles is no longer cooked: only cooking fixtures cook.
  • 2026-10-09: Pressing E empty-handed lets you warm yourself at lit candles.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held object on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
