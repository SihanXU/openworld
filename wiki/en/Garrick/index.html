Garrick is the smith of Emberfall, 46 years old. His family has been smiths for twelve generations, and every generation kept the pattern of a dragon-slaying sword, yet never had ore enough to forge it. At the anvil he is loud; away from it he says little.

The line of Emberfall and the dragon of Cinder Peak was written before the Five Sufferings setting. It is placeholder content, to be replaced later by the Second Seal's chapter (to be designed). See Emberfall and the Dragon and Chapter 2.

Quests

  • The Dragon Road (Main)

Garrick's part belongs to the main quest The Dragon Road: the iron ore and coal you give him, and the Dragonsbane he forges, are objectives of that quest, and while you track it he has a “?” over his head. After you have heard Mara's council:

  1. Give him Iron Ore and Coal one piece at a time (G). He wants 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal in all; once he has enough, give him more and he says “That's iron enough” or “That's coal enough”, and the piece stays in your hand.
  2. Once he has enough of both, press E on him and he forges Dragonsbane: “Twelve generations of my family have waited to forge this one.”

The Iron Ore and Coal are in The Old Mine: break open an ore vein with a Pickaxe and they are yours. The deepest chamber is guarded by a cyclops, which carries two Iron Ore of its own.

Trade

Over his head is an anvil-and-hammer mark, showing he is a smith.

Garrick is also a merchant (see Trading): he sells Pickaxe, Hoe and Dagger, restocks every morning, and carries 60 Dawnmark to buy your goods. He fights: struck, he strikes back, and he runs to anyone who cries for help.

  • He says “Two knocks of a pick open a vein. Don't let the cyclops see you knocking.” A pickaxe really does open a vein in two knocks.
