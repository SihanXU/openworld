Night Creatures

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

At night, on the open-air maps of combat zones, zombie and bat rise out of the dark. They go for anyone they see, and turn to ash after dawn. Staying in a peaceful zone, shutting yourself indoors, or keeping by a lit fire all keep you clear of them.

  • Zombies: six in ten. Health 14, attack 3, very slow.
  • Bats: four in ten. Health 6, attack 2, fly fast, and never fall from heights.

Felling one gives experience equal to its maximum health: 14 for a zombie, 6 for a bat. They drop nothing.

When and where

  • Time: at night. Night runs from 21:00 to 05:00; once the First Seal is broken, nights grow longer, from 20:00 to 05:30 (see Day and Night).
  • Places: only on open-air maps of combat zones. Never in caves, cellars, chapels and the like (see Peaceful and Combat Zones).
  • Only on the player's map: they do not pile up on other maps.
  • How fast, how many: one rises every 6 seconds, at most 4 on a map.
  • How far: they rise on open ground 6 to 10 cells from the player.

Their temper is aggressive: sight 8 cells, chasing anyone they see, up to 14 cells away. They also go for passing residents.

How to avoid them

  • Go back to a peaceful zone: no monsters rise at night in the Ford, Firstlight Village, Emberfall and such places.
  • Shut yourself indoors: walls and closed doors keep them out.
  • Keep to a fire: they never come within 2 cells of a lit campfire or anything burning, and do not chase anyone standing there.
  • Sleep till morning: sleep in a bed indoors and time skips straight to morning. Sleep in the open and a bite wakes you in pain (see Sleeping).

Dawn

After dawn, every night creature has an even chance of turning to ash every half second, so they are soon all gone once day breaks. The moment of dawn follows the night: 05:00 at the start of the game, 05:30 once the First Seal is broken, and they stay until then.

Tips

  • The tutorial's first night teaches exactly this: build your shelter before dark, shut the door and go to bed.
  • When traveling by night in a combat zone, carry the makings of a campfire (2 stones, 2 branches) and a torch: when they close in, build one, light it and stand beside it.
  • While you keep to a fire they stop 2 cells off and come no closer. With a bow or a staff, you can stand by the fire and strike outward.

History

  • 2026-10-03: Zombies and bats that rise at night added; they turn to ash at dawn, and never appear in towns or camps.
  • 2026-10-04: Changed to the world's one “appearing” rule: open-air maps of combat zones, at night, 6 to 10 cells from the player, at most 4 (W7).
  • 2026-10-06: Once the First Seal is broken nights grow longer (20:00–05:30), and night creatures start appearing earlier with them; the time they turn to ash did not follow.
  • 2026-10-08: Turning to ash now follows the current moment of dawn: with the longer nights, they stay until 05:30.
