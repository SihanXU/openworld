The Pilgrim's Road

The Pilgrim's Road runs east from the Pilgrims' Rest to the Kneeling Field. From the middle of the road a path goes north, up the hill to the Abbey of the Thorn; this path is there from the start. Five wayside shrines stand by the road, each for one of the Five Sufferings.

This is a combat zone: there are wolves on both sides of the road, and zombies appear after dark.

Map

56×24. It joins the Pilgrims' Rest on the west, the Kneeling Field on the east, and the Abbey of the Thorn in the middle of the north side (columns 26–28).

Areas

  • The penitent's camp: a little southeast of the road's middle, a lit campfire. Brother Ezra kneels by the road by day and sleeps by the campfire at night. The first time you come this way, his scene plays.

The Five Shrines

Each shrine is cut with the scripture of its suffering, which you can read with E. Hold what it asks and press G to lay it there; the shrine lights up and takes the thing (see wayside shrine).

Shrine What to lay on it Found along the road
Shrine of the Crown Something worn on the head A pilgrim's hat (in the grass on the west of the road)
Shrine of the Pillar A cord A penitent's plaited cord (east of the first shrine)
Shrine of the Scales Something to eat A pilgrim's loaf (on the abandoned cart south of the road)
Shrine of the Nails A piece of metal A dented helm (iron, north of the road's middle)
Shrine of the Lance A light: a torch or a candle A candle (south of the road's eastern stretch)

A wrong offering is not accepted, and the shrine says what it asks for. Holy relics and key items such as the Crown of Thorns and the Order's Seal, and tokens too, are refused by every shrine. Once all five are lit a scene plays, completing this stage of The First Seal.

Lore

  • The White Knight's traces: the signposts are bound with thorns. Three signposts, west to east, read: “EAST: THE KINGDOM. NONE CROSS THAT DO NOT KNEEL.” “THE KINGDOM IS HELD. GO BACK.” “HE HOLDS. KNEEL.” Three white pennants stand by the road: the western one “mended many times, in small, even stitches”, the middle one where the mending stops halfway, and the easternmost only rags: “A needle is still stuck through one of them, its thread wound round and round it.”
  • The first time you set foot on this road, Lumi says: “There are white flags all along this road. Whose are they?”
  • The penitent: Brother Ezra believes that every stroke of the scourge he takes is one Astra need not. He once went as far as the Kneeling Field and saw the knight mending a flag: “When he saw me his hands stopped, and he stood a long while holding the needle. Then he told me to kneel. I knelt, and I came back.”
  • All five lit: as the fifth mark lights, five points of light shine out together, and the middle one points to the path north to the abbey. Lumi says the crown shrine is the coldest: “The crown one is cold. Colder than the others...”
