“Pick a road and walk it. You can change, if you must; only the new road starts from the beginning.”

— Bram

Bram is the old drillmaster of Firstlight Village, 61 years old, whose role in the game is “profession trainer”. Before the storm he trained the wardens of the road; now he trains those who come down off the mountain and last long enough. He is in the drill yard at the village's north-east corner.

Press E on him to open the “Choose a profession” panel; you can only settle on one at level 5 or above, standing within 3 cells of him. The panel holds four professions: Assassin, Warrior, Ranger and Mage. The first time you choose, he gives you a starting weapon. Changing profession refunds the skill points of the profession branch, and the new profession is trained from the beginning. See Professions for details.

Day and night he keeps to the drill yard, standing in the same spot without moving and never going to sleep, so he can be found at any hour. Over his head is a bobbing sword-shaped mark, showing he is a profession trainer (a quest marker shows first when there is one).

Lore

Bram is where the profession system stands in the world: a profession is not a menu, but a matter of finding a trainer. See Professions and Skills.

Trivia

  • The four roads in his lines are “dagger, sword and shield, bow, or staff”, matching one for one the weapons of the game's four professions.
  • Alberic's human form, once the crown is off, was drawn by recoloring the same character sprite Bram uses.
