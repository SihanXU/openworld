“Every dusk the whole herd goes down on its knees, all facing east. My gran kept cows all her life and never once saw one kneel.”

— Brin

Brin is the herder of Firstlight Village, 24 years old, who looks after the cows and chickens on The Grassland south of the village. Week by week more of the things of the night crawl up out of the dark onto the grassland and take her cattle. By day she keeps to her bench on the grassland watching the herd, and at night she goes back to the village to sleep.

Lore

Come to the grassland between 17:00 and 20:00 and the scene of the kneeling herd plays: the light turns amber, and the cattle kneel one after another, all facing east. Brin says it began after the bridge broke. Lumi points out that east is the Ford, and beyond the Ford is that road: the cattle are kneeling to something on the road. That something is The White Knight Alberic.

After the White Knight falls, Brin says the cattle no longer kneel; “now they just stand and look east.” See Chapter 1.

Trivia

  • When you hand in the quest, she says the cattle are “lying down proper, not kneeling”.
  • She shares a character sprite with Gus and Joss.
