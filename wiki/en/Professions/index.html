Professions

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

Your profession decides what weapons you fight with. You start with none. At level 5, go to the drill yard of Firstlight Village and find the profession trainer Bram, and choose one of the four professions: Assassin, Warrior, Ranger, Mage. Once you choose, the profession branch of the skill tree (K) grows that profession's six skills, and Bram hands you a starting weapon. If you have not chosen by level 5, Lumi begins the guide quest A Road of Your Own of her own accord and leads you to Bram.

The four professions

WarriorSword and shield, theirs alone; takes blows, answers them, and grows fiercer with each foe felled.Sword (Iron Sword), Shield (Wooden Shield)

  • Assassin: masters the Dagger; strikes first from the dark and from behind, and finishes with chained blows. Starting weapon: Iron Dagger.
  • Warrior: masters the Sword and the Shield; takes a beating, always strikes back when hit, and grows fiercer as the fight goes on. Starting weapons: Iron Sword and Wooden Shield.
  • Ranger: masters the Bow; standing firm, shoots true and far, and moves on once the shot is loosed. Starting weapon: Wooden Bow.
  • Mage: masters the Staff; elemental spells echo, a mana shield guards the body, and felling a foe restores health. Starting weapon: Wooden Staff.

How to choose

  1. Reach level 5 (see Levels and Experience).
  2. Go to the drill yard in the northeast of Firstlight Village and find Bram: he stands on the drill yard day and night, with the sword-shaped mark of a profession trainer over his head.
  3. Walk up to him and press E to talk; the “Choose a profession” panel opens. When you click to choose, you must be within 3 cells of him.
  4. The panel shows four cards, each with the profession's description, its starting weapon and “10 skills”. Click “Choose”.

You can open the panel to look before level 5 too: at the top it says “Come back at level 5 (you are level N). Until then, look over the roads.”, and the “Choose” button is greyed out. In the skill tree, with no profession, hovering over the profession branch shows the tip: “No profession yet. At level 5, Bram the trainer sets you on one in the drill yard of Firstlight Village (crossed blades over his head): assassin, warrior, ranger or mage.”

Once you have chosen:

  • The profession branch of the skill tree becomes that profession's, and you can spend skill points on its twenty-two skills: ten talents, five skill upgrades and seven active skills (see Skills).
  • The first time you choose a profession, Bram hands you a starting weapon (the Warrior also gets a Wooden Shield, put on at once if the off hand is empty). Leave a profession and come back to it, and nothing more is handed out.

Profession weapons

Daggers, swords and sabres, shields, bows and staves are profession weapons, each belonging to one profession:

  • Only someone of that profession can fight with it. Anyone else who attacks with it is told “Only a(n) X can fight with Y.”, and the blow does not land. Only a Warrior can put on a shield (“Only a Warrior can wear X.”).
  • Profession talents work only with the profession's own weapons. The Assassin's talents count only daggers, the Warrior's only swords, the Ranger's only bows, the Mage's only staves.
  • Anyone can hold one, carry it, give it away or put it in a chest, and anyone can make a Wooden Shield and a Wooden Bow at a workbench (see Crafting).

The other weapons are common weapons: axes, clubs, maces, warhammers, spears, halberds and wands. Anyone can use them, but they take no profession talent, only the skills of the shared branches: Swiftness, Survival, Damage. The wand is a common weapon; the staff is the Mage's.

Before you choose a profession, you can fight only with common weapons and tools.

Changing profession

You can go back to Bram at any time and change profession:

  • On the panel your own profession is marked “Your profession”, and the others say “Change to this”.
  • Before the change you are asked once: the skill points spent in the profession branch come back, and the old profession's skills are reset.
  • After the change: every skill of the old profession is forgotten and every point spent on them comes back; the old profession's gear you wear is taken off (it stays in your bag). What you learned in Swiftness, Survival, Damage, Gathering and the other branches stays.
  • Apart from changing profession, there is no other way to respec.

Legendary Arms

Each profession has two Legendary weapons (see Legendary Arms). When a Seal-bearer falls, if whoever felled him has a profession, he drops the first of that profession's two that has not yet appeared in the world. Without a profession, nothing drops. See Bosses.

Profession Legendary weapons
Assassin Nightfang, Mirrorshard
Warrior Oathbrand, The Unfallen Wall
Ranger Galestring, Starfall
Mage Emberheart, Rimecrown

Tips

  • Before level 5, the best weapon is a Stone Axe you make yourself (power 5); see Combat.
  • Try the four professions first in the Boss Challenge: there every profession is level 20 with all six profession skills learned.
  • Profession talents count only their own weapons: once you choose Ranger, stop fighting with an axe.

  • 2026-10-08: Each profession got four more active skills; Bram keeps to the drill yard day and night, with a profession trainer's mark over his head. With no profession, the skill tree's tip points to Bram in Firstlight's drill yard.
