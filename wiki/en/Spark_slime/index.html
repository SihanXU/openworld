The spark slime is a slime of the lightning element. Spark slimes gather on the top of Dawn Hill, where the storms strike first.

Spawning

  • Dawn Hill: 2 at the start
  • Dawn Hill: 2, on the hilltop. They chase and attack anyone they see.

Behavior

The following is its type data and how the rules treat it; its temper depends on where it is placed.

  • Speed: slow. About 0.7 seconds per tile, like the other slimes.
  • Element: lightning. Its attacks carry lightning, and whoever it hits is left electrified, briefly unable to move; if they are wet, the current also jumps to other wet things within 2 tiles.
  • Charged. The lightning element has no effect on it.
  • Fears fire. Like most monsters, it will not come near a lit fire.

Skills

  • Hop: at 2–3 tiles away, it lights red marks on the 3×3 tiles around your feet, then 0.8 seconds later leaps over and lands beside you, crushing whoever is in the marked area for attack × 1.5 damage, carrying its own element. When you see the marks, step out of those 9 tiles.

Strategy

  • Do not fight it while you are wet.
  • Lightning is useless against it; use fire or a weapon with no element. See Elements.

Drops

  • Slime Gel (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Sparkcord Sash (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 18 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

History
