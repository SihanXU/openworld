The Old Mine is an abandoned mine in the mountain north of Emberfall. Its tunnels hold iron veins and coal seams; break them with a pickaxe for Iron Ore and Coal, just what the smith Garrick needs to forge Dragonsbane. The deepest chamber is richest in iron and also has two gold veins, guarded by a cyclops (the only other gold vein is in the Quarry of the Held Ground).

The Old Mine is a dark underground combat zone, the same by day and night. It belongs to the old main story from before Chapter 1; see Emberfall and the Dragon.

Map

The map is 40×30, cave wall on every side. The entrance is in the south (columns 19–21), and a main tunnel three tiles wide runs north to the deepest chamber, with four lit braziers along the way. From the middle of the main tunnel one tunnel branches west and one east, and there is a short side passage in the southwest; each tunnel ends in a small chamber.

Areas

  • The entrance: in the south. On the ground just inside lies an Old Pickaxe (iron pickaxe).
  • The west tunnel: leads to the northwest chamber, with coal seams and one iron vein.
  • The east tunnel: leads to the east chamber, with coal seams and an iron vein.
  • The southwest passage: a small chamber with one coal seam.
  • The deep chamber: at the north end of the main tunnel, with two iron veins and, below them, two gold veins, guarded by the cyclops.

  • At the start there are 2 bats, 3 cave spiders, 1 skeleton, and the cyclops in the deep chamber. The cyclops carries 2 Iron Ore, which drop when it is defeated.
  • While you are in the mine, a spider may come back every 150 seconds (up to 3), and a bat every 180 seconds (up to 2). The skeleton and the cyclops do not come back.

Fixtures and furnishings

coal seam×4 iron vein×4 brazier×4 gold vein×2

  • At the start there are 4 iron veins, 4 coal seams and 2 gold veins. Break an iron vein with a pickaxe for 2 Iron Ore, a coal seam for 2 Coal, and a gold vein for 2 Gold Ore. A stone pickaxe will do too, breaking a vein in two blows; strike one bare-handed and you are told “Too hard.” See Mining.
  • A vein broken open leaves a spent face in its place (which cannot be broken), and after one game day it turns back into a vein in the same spot.
  • Dragonsbane takes 5 iron ore and 3 coal. In Expanding the Held Ground, Land II takes 5 iron ore and Land III takes 3 gold ore.
  • Veins elsewhere: Dawn Hill Mine, open already in Chapter 1, has 5 iron veins and 4 coal seams; there are 3 iron veins beyond the third ring of mist in the Held Ground, and the Held Ground's Quarry has 3 iron veins, 3 coal seams and 1 gold vein. Veins grow back the same way everywhere.

Lore

To be designed: this part is not designed yet; below is only what is settled. To make a proposal, click “Talk” at the top of the page.

In the old main story, the iron and coal the smith needs for Dragonsbane are all in this old mine. Bats, spiders and dead miners live in the mine, and a cyclops holds the best iron ore at its deepest point. This content is older than the setting of the Five Sufferings and, as planned, will be replaced by the chapter of the Second Seal (see Chapter 2).
