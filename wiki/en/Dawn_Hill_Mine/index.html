Dawn Hill Mine opens into the mountainside of Dawn Hill's western slope, entered by the path on Dawn Hill's west side (rows 18–19). It is Chapter 1's mine: the Iron Ore and Coal that the forge and anvil of the Firstlight Village smithy need are dug closest to hand here. The tunnels hold 5 iron veins, 4 coal seams and 3 boulders, and a worn stone pickaxe lies on the ground at the entrance. There are no gold veins: the gold is deep in the Old Mine and in the Quarry of the Held Ground.

A vein broken open does not vanish: it leaves a spent face in its place, which no tool can break, and after one game day (24 game hours, about 24 minutes) it turns back into a vein. So the ore here is steady: come once a day, and each vein gives 2 ore (more if you have learned Prospector). See Mining.

The mine is a dark underground combat zone, the same by day and night, gear level 2.

Map

The map is 30×22, cave wall on every side. The entrance is in the east, and a tunnel three tiles wide runs west to a mining hall; from the hall one tunnel climbs north to a small chamber, and another drops south to a chamber in the south. Three braziers burn in the tunnels.

Areas

  • The entrance: rows 11–12 on the east side, joining the path on Dawn Hill's west side. On the ground just inside lies the “Worn Pickaxe” (stone pickaxe, power 4, breaks a vein in two blows). You can also make a pickaxe yourself: C opens crafting; 2 stones, 2 sticks and 1 handful of grass, made by hand.
  • The mining hall: the west end of the tunnel, with 2 iron veins and 1 coal seam.
  • The north chamber: 2 iron veins and 1 coal seam, and a bat.
  • The south chamber: 1 iron vein and 2 coal seams, and a bat.
  • Along the tunnels: 3 boulders, each giving 4 stones when broken (boulders do not grow back).

Using the Ore Here

  1. Break a vein with a pickaxe; the ore pops out and flies into your bag.
  2. Go back to the Firstlight Village smithy (see smithing): standing by the forge, smelt 2 iron ore and 1 coal into 2 Iron Ingots.
  3. Holding a Smith Hammer, stand by the anvil and forge weapons, armor and tools from the ingots.
  4. Come back the next day, and the veins have all grown back.
