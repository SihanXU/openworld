Seeds

From the OpenWorld Wiki · List

Seeds are items that can be planted in farmland. Sow them and water them, and the crop grows stage by stage; once ripe, a harvest gives back seeds as well as the crop. For the rules of farming see Farming; for each crop see Crops.

Sowing

  1. Turn grass, dirt, sand or snow into farmland with a Hoe.
  2. Hold the seeds and right-click an empty tile of farmland to plant a “sprout”. The fourteen vegetables can also be chosen as “Sow X” on the “Farming” page of build mode (B).
  3. Water it by left-clicking it with a container holding water. A watered crop grows one stage two game hours later: sprout → young plant → ripe. Each stage needs one watering.
  4. Crops grow only in the open; those under a roof do not grow.
  5. Once ripe, reap it with a Sickle for 2 portions of crop and 2 of seed; a fertilized crop (with Ash or Fertilizer) gives 1 more portion of crop. You can also press E to pick one portion of crop and one of seed by hand.
  6. Tomatoes, corn, blueberries, strawberries, hot peppers, eggplants and berry bushes grow fruit again after a harvest, with no need to replant.

Crops do not wither, and there are no seasons.

Where to get them

  • The vegetable garden in Firstlight Village: the tool crate by the garden (“Garden Supplies”) holds 2 each of Carrot Seeds, Turnip Seeds, Tomato Seeds and Pumpkin Seeds, plus a hoe, a sickle, a smith hammer and a bucket of water. The garden already grows these four crops at every stage.
  • The trading stall at The Held Ground: its “Farming” page sells the seeds of the fourteen vegetables, plus Wheat Seeds, Herb Seeds, Berry Seeds and acorns, for Dawnmarks. For prices see the crop table below and what the stall sells. Ten of the vegetables (parsnip, potato, radish, cauliflower, melon, corn, blueberries, strawberry, hot pepper, eggplant), along with Wheat Seeds, Herb Seeds and Berry Seeds, are found only here.
  • Harvesting: every harvest gives seeds back.
  • Acorns: felling a tree has a 25% chance to drop one (after learning the skill “Woodcutter” it drops an extra log instead, and no acorn). An acorn need not go in farmland: plant it in any ground that can be dug, and it grows first into an oak sapling, then into a tree two game days later. The stall also sells acorns.

Wheat Seeds, Herb Seeds and Berry Seeds are not placed on any map and can only be bought at the stall.
