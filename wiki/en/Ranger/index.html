Ranger

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

The Ranger is one of the four professions, and masters the Bow. The Ranger shoots from afar: more damage when standing firm, the farther the more it hurts, and faster on the feet after a hit. Choose it from Bram in Firstlight Village from level 5.

Starting gear

The first time you become a Ranger, Bram gives you a Wooden Bow: power 2, range 6, no element.

The bow is the Ranger's profession weapon: only a Ranger can fight with it, and the Ranger's talents work only with bows. You can also make a Wooden Bow yourself beside a workbench (2 branches, 3 bundles of hay, recipe level 2).

How to shoot

  • Press Space and the arrow flies the way you face, hitting the first person, the first creature or the first thing in the way along that row (or column).
  • When you click a distant target with the left button, it must be in the same row or column as you, and within range.
  • Range 6 cells; 7 with Archery learned.

Skills

The Ranger's first six talents are in the profession branch of the skill tree (K). One branch fights standing, one fights on the move, and they end in a 2-point ultimate. All work only with bows.

Skill Requires Points Effect
Archery 1 Bow power +1, range +1.
Steady Aim Archery 1 If you have not moved for 1 second, bow hits deal +30% damage.
Sniper Steady Aim 1 Hits on a target 3 or more cells away deal +25% damage.
Fleet Archer Archery 1 After a bow hit, movement speed +20% for 1.5 seconds.
Quick Draw Fleet Archer 1 Attack speed +20% while holding a bow.
Hunter's Mark Sniper, Quick Draw 2 The 3rd hit in a row on the same target is always a critical.

Active skills

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with Shift+1–6 (see Active skills and the skill bar). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are a Ranger.

Skill Requires Points Cooldown Effect
Multishot Archery 1 8 s Loose 3 arrows side by side, each hitting the first enemy on its line (0.8× damage).
Leap Shot Archery 1 10 s Leap back 2 cells while loosing an arrow ahead (1.2× damage).
Piercing Arrow Multishot 1 10 s One arrow passes through every enemy on its line, up to a wall (1.3× damage).
Arrow Rain Leap Shot 1 20 s Arrows rain on a 3×3 area round the first enemy ahead, one volley a second for 3 seconds (0.6× damage per volley).

Learning all twenty-two (ten talents, five skill upgrades, seven active skills) takes 25 points. For the skill tree's rules, see Skills.

Advanced (2026-10-09)

The profession branch grew twelve more nodes, twenty-two in all: three new active skills (the last a 2-point ultimate), five skill upgrades (once learned, they apply straight to their active skill: a shorter cooldown, more damage, a wider area), three profession talents, and a 2-point ultimate talent.

Legendary Arms

The Ranger's two Legendary weapons are Galestring and Starfall, both bows, with a longer range than an ordinary bow. When a Ranger fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see Legendary Arms, Bosses).

How to play

  • Stand firm first: stop for 1 second before shooting, +30% damage; 3 cells or more from the target, another +25%. The two add together.
  • Shoot and go: for 1.5 seconds after a hit you run 20% faster; use it to open the distance, then stop and shoot again.
  • Keep to one: Hunter's Mark wants three arrows in a row on the same target, and the third is a sure critical. Shoot something else midway and the count starts over.
  • The Wooden Bow's power is only 2, so damage is low early on; you rely on Archery and the bonuses for standing still.
  • An enemy can be hit only once it is in the same row or column, so line up first as you move.

Boss Challenge

In the Boss Challenge, the Ranger is level 20: health 104, attack 5, defense 1, holding a Wooden Bow (range 7), with all twenty-two Ranger skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with Space, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned.
