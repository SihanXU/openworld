Foraging

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay
The Chinese page has changed since this translation was made: some of this may be out of date.

Foraging is getting materials from the world. Things on the ground are picked up with E; trees are chopped (Woodcutting), rocks are broken (Mining), crops are harvested (Farming). At the start of the game you carry nothing, and your first tools are all made from Stick, Stone and Cut Grass picked up from the ground (Crafting).

Picking things up

  • Point the mouse at something lying loose on the ground and press E. You can reach your own cell and the ring around it, that is, 1 cell (see Controls).
  • Picking up is a physical rule, always defined, and never asks the AI (see The World Learns). Things that stand (doors, chests, beds…) and people are not picked up: pressing E at them handles them, or talks to the person.
  • When several things are stacked on one cell, holding E lists every layer of that cell, and you choose one to take (only the things on your own storey are listed; see Storeys).
  • Other people's things can be taken too, but pressing E first asks “Steal …?”, and only takes it once you confirm: this is theft; if the owner sees it, they say “Hey — that's mine.” (see Crime). Hovering the mouse over it, the item's description says “Belongs to …”.

Drawn in by itself

What a rule makes drop (the Log from a felled tree, the Plank from a smashed chest, the crops you cut) pops out and flies into your bag by itself when you come within 2 cells. With Long Reach from the Gathering branch, the range grows by 2 cells more.

Things that do not fly to you by themselves:

  • What a creature leaves when it is felled, and what a person drops when they fall, lie quietly where they are; press E to pick them up.
  • An Egg a chicken lays lies where it is too.
  • On Normal difficulty, after you come back to life, the belongings left where you fell are not drawn in either.

When your bag is full, a new kind of item you pick up drops at your feet; kinds you already have stack in as usual. Items have no weight and no stack limit (see Inventory).

What grows again

The ground of outdoor maps grows scattered materials by itself. Every so often, as long as the map has not reached its limit of that thing, one more appears on empty ground. They never appear under the player's feet or in the cells right beside the player.

Place What grows (most on the map) Interval
Most outdoor maps (The Ford, Firstlight Village, The Grassland, Mountain Foot, Cloudtop, The Clearing, Thornwood Hollow, The East Road, Emberfall, Millbrook, Dawn Hill, The Held Ground) Stick 6, Stone 5, Cut Grass 6 15 seconds
The Forest Stick 10, Stone 3, Cut Grass 4; Frostcap 3 15 seconds; Frostcap 90 seconds
Firstlight Village Fireweed 3 as well 90 seconds
The Clearing (Tutorial) Berries 6 as well 15 seconds
Dawn Hill Herb 3 as well 120 seconds
The Wild Stick 4, Stone 3, Cut Grass 4 15 seconds

Sticks and stones grow on grass, cut grass on grass and dry grass (in the Wild, sticks and stones grow on snow too). Time is game time: 15 seconds is 15 game minutes.

Only the map the player is on grows things. Maps you have left are frozen, and do not catch up on growing when you return.

The veins of The Old Mine also grow back slowly; see Mining. The cows and chickens of The Grassland come back; see Animals.

Things placed only once

Some things are on a map from the start, and once taken they are gone:

  • The scattered items in the Wild: berries, sticks and stones on the open plains, Mushroom and sticks in the deep woodland, stones and Herb in the rolling hills, stones and sticks on the snowfields, sticks on the roads.
  • Materials lying on maps from the start, such as the sticks under the trees west of The Ford, the stones on the slope and the cut grass on the grass.

Gathering skills

The Gathering branch has four skills, learned in a line down from Long Reach:

Skill Effect
Long Reach The range at which drops are drawn in grows by 2 cells.
Woodcutter A felled tree drops one more log (see Woodcutting).
Big Pack One more row in the bag (10 slots).
Firemaker An ordinary stick lights a campfire, brazier or candles (see Campfire).

Tips

  • The first tool to make is a stone axe: 1 stick, 1 stone, 1 cut grass.
  • When you are short of sticks, chopping trees is faster than waiting for the ground: a felled tree drops 1 stick too.
  • A new game begins on Cloudtop, where the yard already has 3 berries, 2 sticks, 2 stones and 2 cut grass lying about, enough for your first stone axe.
  • What grows on the ground has a limit; once you have picked it all, you wait for it to grow again slowly. To stock up on materials, stay on one map and pick things up as you work.

History

  • 2026-10-04: “Things that grow” unified into one rule: each map says what grows, how many at most and how often (W7).
