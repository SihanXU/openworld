The Charcoal Dell lies west of the Forest, at the end of the Forest's western side path. In the middle of the dell is a stretch of trodden mud where three earthen charcoal kilns stand; the charcoal burner Orrin keeps watch by the kilns by day and sleeps at night in his hut to the east. North and south of the dell grows a coppice, cut and grown back, full of stumps, where boars root in the ground. At the west end of the dell stands an old oak ringed by trees: since the night the little bridge broke, every bird of the Forest has flown each dusk to this tree to roost, without a sound.

The Charcoal Dell is an open-air combat zone; after nightfall night creatures appear.

Map

The map is 40×30. An opening on the east side at rows 14–16 leads to the Forest, and a path comes in there, passes north of the hut, crosses the middle of the dell and runs all the way west to the old oak. The middle of the dell (rows 9–19) is open ground and mud; the rest is coppice, with about a third of the tiles holding a tree and stumps scattered between them.

Areas

  • The dell: the mud in the middle, with the three kilns in a row and the ground before them burned to ash. The kilns are solid mounds of earth and cannot be entered. East of the kilns are two woodpiles waiting to be burned; at the south edge of the mud stands a crate (“Orrin's charcoal crate”, holding 3 Coal), and 3 more coal lie scattered on the ground.
  • Orrin's hut: the thatched hut on the east side of the dell, whose door leads into Orrin's Hut.
  • The roosting oak: at the west end of the dell, a ring of trees encloses a little patch of grass with the old oak at its center. Under the tree lie 4 Feathers, dropped by the birds as they fly off at daybreak. The sign in the southeast corner is “Orrin's tally board” (press E to read it): notches are cut into the board in groups of five, and beneath them, in charcoal, is written “how many days the birds have come”.
  • The coppice: the two stretches of woods north and south of the dell; among the stumps is the boars' ground.

Orrin is the only person here. From 6:00 to 21:00 he watches the kilns, and from 21:00 to 6:00 he sleeps in the hut.

Lore

The first time you enter the dell, Lumi says: there's smoke; someone is burning something slowly — it smells like a fire that's been going for days; and still no birdsong.

The Old Oak at Dusk (click to show)

Come to the old oak between 17:00 and 21:00 and a scene plays: the old oak is covered in birds, hundreds of them wing against wing, and not one makes a sound. Lumi says so this is where all the birds of the Forest have gone — they all face east, just like the cattle on the Grassland; birds know before people when something is wrong, and they will not spend the night in the Forest. After that Orrin says something he says only once: he keeps his tally now not for the birds, but for the thing they are waiting for.

This explains why no birdsong is heard in the Forest: the birds flew away together on the night the little bridge was taken apart, and it is the other face of the same thing as the cattle kneeling on the Grassland — all of them are watching the White Knight Alberic on the East Road to the east.

Quests

  • Wood for the Clamp: give Orrin 6 logs in exchange for 4 coal and a hat.
  • Boars in the Coppice (level 2): fell 4 boars here.
  • Where the Birds Went: ask Gus why the Forest is so quiet, then pick up 3 feathers from under the old oak and give them to Orrin.
