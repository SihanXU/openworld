“Suffering is not God's abandonment. I say it at the altar every morning, and on the hard mornings I say it twice.”

— Father Caius

Father Caius is a priest of the Church of the Five Sufferings, 61 years old. The Holy See posted him to The Pilgrims' Rest to bless the pilgrims it sends east, and to keep the Five Wounds in Chapel of the Five. He is gentle, tired, and firm in his faith. When the pilgrim named in the Church's letter reaches the Rest, he is the one who hears the Pilgrim's Vow.

After the vow he looks at your faith: at faith 30 or above he blesses you, gives you two flasks of Holy Water, and asks you to tell him what the Writ of Relief says; at faith −30 or below he has heard that you spread unbelief, and refuses you the Rest's blessing. He is devout: spread unbelief to him and he grows angry, regard −8.

The first time you speak to him, he says: “Peace on your road, pilgrim. I am Father Caius. The Holy See keeps me at this Rest to bless the pilgrims it sends east, and to keep the five wounds.” This greeting does not mention the White Knight, and is the same whether you meet him before or after the White Knight falls.

Ordinarily he tells you what to lay at each of the five wayside shrines along the road, and he also says: “They say the Seal-bearer on the East Road was once a gentle man. The Crown asks the most of gentle men.”

After the White Knight falls, he says: “Three hundred years, and in the end it took one pilgrim and one fairy. God forgive us for letting him keep it so long.” And also: “The road east is open. Go on when you are ready, pilgrim. Four more Seal-bearers wait, and the Crown is only the first of the five.”

Lore

What he speaks of is the mission the Holy See proclaimed: seek out the five Seal-bearers, defeat them, and bring the five Instruments of the Sufferings back to the Holy City.

  • The welcome (after the Church's letter and before the White Knight falls, on your first visit to the Rest): “You must be the pilgrim the letter named, the one with the fairy. Welcome to the Rest. I am Father Caius. The Holy See keeps me here to see its pilgrims on their way east.” “Before the road, the oath. Come into the chapel and kneel at the five wounds. Every pilgrim the Holy See has sent east took the oath there first.”
  • The Pilgrim's Vow: before the altar of the Five Wounds he has the pilgrim kneel, and recites the Five Sufferings one by one as the Church reads them (the fourth: “The fourth, the Nails: upon the holy wood he accepted death.”), and says the five shrines on the road stand one for each suffering: “Halfway along, a path goes north to the old abbey. The knight is further east.” At the end he says: “The Seal-bearer has held that road for three hundred years. The Holy See asks you to overcome him and bring home the Crown, the first of the five instruments. ...He was due to be relieved, once. That is not a pilgrim's concern.” The pilgrim receives the Sworn Scallop.
  • After the White Knight falls: the welcome and the vow at the Rest no longer happen. When you come to the Rest again, he comes out to meet you: “You have come from the east. From him. ...Then it is done, and the road is open. The Holy See will have its Crown of Thorns, the first of the five Instruments.” “Rest here as long as you like, pilgrim. The chapel is always open, and the Five Wounds are always there.”
What he does not say (click to show)

He knows the First Seal-bearer should have been relieved after forty years, and he knows the Writ of Relief was never sent. After the player finds the writ in Abbey of the Thorn, he admits it: “You found the writ. ...Yes. I knew. I was told it was not a pilgrim's concern, and I decided to believe it. Go on. Don't look at me like that.”
