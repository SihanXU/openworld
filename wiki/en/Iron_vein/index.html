An iron vein is a seam of iron ore in the rock. Broken open with a Pickaxe, it drops 2 Iron Ore. Dawn Hill Mine, open from Chapter 1, has 5; the Old Mine has 4; the outermost ring of the Held Ground has 3, and the Held Ground's Quarry another 3. Once broken open it leaves a spent iron vein in its place, which grows back after one game day. Iron Ore is what smithing, the Emberfall smith's forging of Dragonsbane and Expanding the Held Ground need.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Dawn Hill Mine ×5, The Embervein Mine ×4, The Held Ground ×3, The Oathstone Quarry ×3
  • Dawn Hill Mine: 5, in the mine hall and its two chambers. A worn stone pickaxe lies on the ground at the entrance. It can be visited as soon as you reach the village in Chapter 1.
  • The Old Mine: 4, in the tunnels on either side and in the deepest chamber; a cyclops guards the iron veins in the deepest chamber. An old pickaxe lies on the ground at the mine entrance.
  • The Held Ground: 3, outside the third ring of banks of mist. They can be reached only once the land has been widened to level III in Expanding the Held Ground and the last ring of mist has lifted.
  • The Quarry: 3. Reachable once the Quarry has been opened in Expanding the Held Ground.
  • Grows back: iron veins are the same everywhere: once broken open each leaves a spent iron vein in its place (bedrock, which no tool can chip), which turns back into an iron vein after one game day (24 game hours).

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Breaking it open

  • Durability 12, material ore (hardness 2).
  • Holding a pickaxe, left-click it (“Mine iron vein”): a stone pickaxe takes 6 points a blow, breaking it open in two. Punching it: “It's too hard.”
  • Broken open it drops 2 Iron Ore (half of 4), which pop out and fly into your pack when you walk near; a spent iron vein is left in its place. See Mining and Durability and Breaking.

What Iron Ore is used for

  • The Emberfall smith Garrick: 5 Iron Ore and 3 Coal forge Dragonsbane.
  • Land II, “The Meadow”, in Expanding the Held Ground: 5 Iron Ore.
  • Smelted into Iron Ingots in a forge, then smithed at an anvil.

Other

An iron vein is solid and blocks the way; it does not catch fire; it cannot be taken.
