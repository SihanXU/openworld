Warrior

From the OpenWorld Wiki · Gameplay

The Warrior is one of the four professions, and masters the Sword and the Shield. The Warrior stands in front and takes the blows: damage cut, more health, a hard sword stroke back for every hit taken, and fiercer with every enemy felled. Choose it from Bram in Firstlight Village from level 5.

Starting gear

The first time you become a Warrior, Bram gives you:

  • an Iron Sword: power 6, range 1, no element.
  • a Wooden Shield: defense 2, worn in the off hand. If the off hand is empty, it is put on at once.

Swords (and sabres) and shields are the Warrior's profession weapons: only a Warrior can fight with a sword or wear a shield, and the Warrior's attack talents work only with swords.

Skills

The Warrior's first six talents are in the profession branch of the skill tree (K). One branch defends, one attacks, and they end in a 2-point ultimate.

Skill Requires Points Effect
Arms Training 1 Attacks with a sword gain +1 power.
Bulwark Arms Training 1 Direct combat damage taken −15%, still at least 1; hunger and the surroundings' damage are not reduced. Any weapon.
Unbreakable Bulwark 1 Maximum health +10.
Retaliation Arms Training 1 Within 2 seconds of taking combat damage, your next sword hit deals +40% damage.
Rampage Retaliation 1 After felling a creature with a sword, sword damage +15% for 5 seconds, stacking up to 3 times.
Warlord Unbreakable, Rampage 2 Bulwark's reduction rises to 30%; every Retaliation hit restores 2 health.

Active skills

The active skills are in the profession branch too. Once learned, they go on the skill bar in the lower left and are cast with Shift+1–6 (see Active skills and the skill bar). A skill's damage is your attack plus the weapon's power, times its multiplier; they can be cast only while you are a Warrior.

Skill Requires Points Cooldown Effect
Shield Bash Arms Training 1 10 s Strike the cell ahead and stun it for 1.5 seconds.
Charge Arms Training 1 12 s Charge ahead up to 3 cells, ramming and striking the first enemy (1.5× damage, a brief stun).
War Cry Shield Bash 1 25 s For 5 seconds, damage dealt +25% and combat damage taken −30%.
Earthsplitter Charge 1 12 s Slam the ground: enemies in a 3×3 area two cells ahead are struck (1.3× damage) and stunned for a moment.

Learning all twenty-two (ten talents, five skill upgrades, seven active skills) takes 25 points. For the skill tree's rules, see Skills.

The profession branch grew twelve more nodes, twenty-two in all: three new active skills (the last a 2-point ultimate), five skill upgrades (once learned, they apply straight to their active skill: a shorter cooldown, more damage, a wider area), three profession talents, and a 2-point ultimate talent.

Shields

  • A shield is worn in the off hand (the “Off hand” slot of the equipment), and the weapon held in the hand is unaffected.
  • A worn shield adds its defense to you: the Wooden Shield +2.
  • Some shields also have a block chance: when struck, a chance to block the whole blow (“Blocked!”), at most 50%. Ordinary shields have no block chance; the Legendary shield The Unfallen Wall has 15%.
  • A shield can be made beside a workbench from 3 planks and 1 branch, but only a Warrior can wear it (“Only a Warrior can wear X.”).

Legendary Arms

The Warrior's two Legendary weapons are Oathbrand (a sword) and The Unfallen Wall (a shield). When a Warrior fells a Seal-bearer, he drops the first of them that has not yet appeared in the world (see Legendary Arms, Bosses).

How to play

  • Take one, give one back: the sword stroke within 2 seconds of being hit gets +40%. With Warlord learned, that stroke also heals 2. So the Warrior does not fear being hit first.
  • Fell them in a row: Rampage stacks up to 3 times, +15% each, lasting 5 seconds. Against a pack, they fall faster and faster.
  • Bulwark is always on: whatever you hold, you take 15% less (30% with Warlord).
  • The sword's base power is high (6 for the Iron Sword), and the shared branch's Sweep cuts both sides at once, well suited to a Warrior facing a crowd.

Boss Challenge

In the Boss Challenge, the Warrior is level 20: health 174, attack 6, defense 2 (plus the Wooden Shield's +2), holding an Iron Sword, with all twenty-two Warrior skills (the seven active skills among them; but in the Boss Challenge you can only move and attack with Space, so active skills cannot be cast) and the four shared skills Light Footed, Quick Hands, Strength and Vitality learned. The most health of the four professions.
