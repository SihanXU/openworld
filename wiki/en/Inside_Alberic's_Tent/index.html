Inside Alberic's Tent is the inside of the tent northwest of the sword on The Held Ground. Press E at the tent (“Enter Alberic's tent”) to crawl in. It is small inside: a camp bed, a stand holding the armor he used to wear, and a chest.

Map

The map is 8×6, wooden walls and a wooden floor, a single small room. The exit is in the south wall.

Areas

  • Alberic's camp bed: against the west end of the north wall.
  • His old armor, on a stand: against the east end of the north wall. Press E to read: “White plate, the crown's thorns still caught in the joints. He keeps it polished and does not wear it.”
  • The chest: a chest by the east wall.

Residents

No one lives here.

Alberic does not sleep inside the tent at night, but in front of it (22:00–6:00).

Lore

The White Knight's armor is white plate. Since laying down the Crown of Thorns, Alberic has never worn it again (see The White Knight Alberic).
