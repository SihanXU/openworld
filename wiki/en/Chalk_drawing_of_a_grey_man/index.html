Chalk drawing of a grey man
CategoryChalk drawings
Chinese name画着灰衣人的粉笔画

The chalk drawing of a grey man is a picture the child Pell drew in chalk on the ground in Firstlight Village, beside the sacred flame. It lies flat on the ground and does not block the way. Walk up to it and press E to see what it shows; once you have looked at it, the drawing counts as found.

Source

  • Placed on maps: Firstlight Village
  • There is only this one, drawn by Pell. You cannot draw one yourself.

Uses

The game data records no set use for it. Right-click something else while holding it, and the world may learn a new use (see The World Learns).

Looking at the drawing

  • Stand within 1 tile of the drawing and press E. What it shows: “A hooded man with a lantern, beside a fire. Underneath, in a child's letters: HE WAS HERE FIRST.”
  • Once you have looked at it, the objective “The flame” of the side quest Pell's Drawings is complete. Looking at it before taking the quest counts too.
  • Pell says he drew one in every place where he saw the Grey Man. The four drawings are: chalk drawing of a stone (by the shrine stone at the mountain's foot), chalk drawing of a door (at the barrow's door), chalk drawing of a bridge (by the footbridge at the Ford) and chalk drawing of a grey man (by the flame in Firstlight Village).

Other

  • The chalk drawing cannot be taken (“That stays where it is.”), nor wiped away: water only makes it wet, and fire cannot burn it.
  • It has no durability; the rules do not say what happens when you hit it.
  • Hold something and press G on it, and the thing is put down on the drawing's tile.

Do something else to it and the world may learn a new response (see The World Learns).

History

  • 2026-10-06: Added Pell's four chalk drawings and the side quest “Pell's Drawings” (Chapter 1).
