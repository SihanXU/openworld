The cave spider is a big red spider that runs very fast; it has little health but a nasty bite. There are some in Thornwood Hollow, the The Mill Cellar and the The Old Mine, and those in Thornwood Hollow and the Old Mine come back after you clear them.

Spawning

  • Thornwood Hollow: 3 at the start, respawns (up to 3, once every 120 s)
  • The Mill Cellar: 1 at the start
  • The Embervein Mine: 3 at the start, respawns (up to 3, once every 150 s)
  • Thornwood Hollow: 3 at the start. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up on the grass every 2 game hours.
  • The Mill Cellar: 1. Once felled, it does not come back.
  • The Old Mine: 3 at the start. When fewer than 3 remain, another comes up in the tunnels every 2.5 game hours.

Behavior

  • Temper: aggressive. It chases and attacks anyone it sees within 4 to 5 tiles. See Factions and Temper.
  • Speed: fast. About 0.4 seconds per tile, as fast as a wolf.
  • Element: none. But its bite is venomous; see “Venom Fang” below.
  • Fears fire. It will not come within 2 tiles of anything lit or burning.

Skills

  • Web Spit: at 2–4 tiles away, it spits web at your feet, marking 3×3 tiles in purple, which land 0.8 seconds later: whoever is on those tiles is webbed for 2.5 seconds, each step taking 3 times as long as usual. While you are caught, it crawls over and bites. The webs stay on the ground: every marked tile except the one you stand on is left with a cobweb, and stepping into one catches you again; a single cut or a touch of fire breaks it, and it vanishes by itself after 12 seconds. The spider itself walks through cobwebs unhindered.
  • Venom Fang: one in every 4 of its ordinary attacks leaves you poisoned for 3 seconds, losing 1 health per second, ignoring defense.

Strategy

  • If you cannot outrun it, stand and fight: it is an ordinary level 2 monster and goes down in five or six hits.
  • Spiders felled in Thornwood Hollow count toward Holt's Clear the Hollow.
  • In the The Mill Cellar it is with two green slimes, guarding the lamp oil The Vigil needs.

Drops

  • Venom Sac (makeup 2 × 50%)
  • Silkspinner Gloves (trophy, straight into the slayer's bag: 4% a kill; certain on the 50th if the 49 before gave none)
  • Experience 38 (its maximum health; see Levels and Experience)

Once felled, it leaves the materials listed above lying where it fell; press E to pick them up (see Monster Drops). Felling it gives experience equal to its maximum health.
