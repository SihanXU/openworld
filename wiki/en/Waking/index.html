Waking is the main quest of the tutorial. You come to in The Clearing with a fairy called Lumi beside you. Get through a day and a night here, then follow the path south down the mountain.

Each stage of the tutorial is a stage of this quest: the quest journal (L) and the tracker in the top-right corner show the current objective, and pressing E on Lumi tells you how to do this stage; the tutorial's lessons (the spotlight, the key icons, the locked keys) follow the stages too. See Lessons.

This quest exists only in the tutorial entered from the title screen. A proper new game begins on Cloudtop, and its first main quest is Down from the Mountain.

Walkthrough

  1. Waking: at 07:00 on day 1 you are lying in the clearing, out cold. The quest is there from the start, and the journal's first line reads “I woke in a clearing. I don't remember how I got here.” After about 5 seconds you wake, Lumi introduces herself, and the first objective, “Find something to eat”, appears.
  2. Eat: press E to pick up the berries on the ground, press the number of that slot on the toolbar to take them in hand, and left-click to eat.
  3. Gather: sticks, stones and cut grass lie scattered around the clearing, and slowly grow back once taken. Tab opens the backpack.
  4. Stone axe: C opens crafting; make a stone axe from a stick, a stone and a handful of cut grass.
  5. Planks: hold the axe and left-click the trees in the woods to the east; a stone axe brings one down in two blows, giving logs; with the axe on you, turn the logs into planks in the crafting panel.
  6. Campfire: B opens building; set down a campfire; C makes a torch (a stick and grass); hold it and left-click the campfire.
  7. The house: place walls within the outline drawn on the ground, close them in and fit a door, put a bed inside, and finally place a ceiling overhead (in building mode choose the floor and press R to switch it to a ceiling). Once a bedroom appears on the map, the objective is complete.
  8. The night: after dark night creatures come out. Shut the door, eat something before bed, and press E on the bed to sleep. On the first morning (05:00) after the house is built, the path to the south opens.
  9. Down the mountain: follow the path out of the south of the clearing; once you reach Mountain Foot, the quest is complete. The tutorial played on its own ends here, and you return to the title screen (see Start and end).

For the details of each step, and the keys and locks of each lesson, see Tutorial.

Rewards

  • Experience +10 (Levels and Experience)

The tutorial is not saved; the experience and things you get here do not carry over into a proper game.

Dialogue

This quest has no dialogue.

At each new stage, Lumi says a line:

  • Waking: “Hey! You're finally awake! I'm Lumi, I'm a fairy, and from today wherever you go, I go.”
  • Having eaten: “Better, right? To make things, we first need sticks, stones and grass.”
  • With planks: “Planks! It gets dark early in the woods, and after dark things come out to wander. Light a fire first.”
  • With the house built: “A home! Now we just have to get through tonight. Eat something before bed — the berries keep growing back. As soon as it's light, we set off.”
  • Daybreak: “It's morning! You made it. The path to the south is clear now. Let's go down to the foot of the mountain!”

Press E on Lumi and she says “Now: Waking.”, followed by the current objective and how to do this stage, for example “Hold the axe and left-click a tree; it falls and becomes logs. Then left-click the logs with the axe to split them into planks.”

History

  • 2026-10-04: The tutorial was added; at the time its stages were kept on the player and appeared only in what Lumi said.
  • 2026-10-09: Moved to the new quest system: the tutorial's stages became the stages of the main quest “Waking”, the objectives are shown in the quest journal and the tracker, each stage records a line in the journal, and the tutorial's lessons follow the stages.
  • 2026-10-20: Tab now opens the backpack, with a row of tabs above it switching to the other panels (the old keys such as I still work); changing the toolbar row became Shift+Tab or Shift+mouse wheel; every key can be changed in Options, with two presets, scheme A and scheme B.
  • 2026-10-21: The controls were settled as four inputs: using a held thing on something moved to the right click (the left click is the held thing's own use: a tool or weapon is swung, anything else is used on yourself), and picking up loose things moved to E (see Controls).
